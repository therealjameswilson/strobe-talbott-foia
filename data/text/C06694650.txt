SUBJECT: ARISTIDE DELIGHTED WITH PRESIDENT CLINTON'S

RELEASE IN FULL 
DECONTROLLED 
1. DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT ACCOMPANIED BY THE 
AMBASSADOR, GENERAL FISHER, VICTORIA NULAND, DEBORAH 
SCHWARTZ AND THE DCM, MET WITH PRESIDENT ARISTIDE AT 1:00 
P.M. MARCH 8. SECRETARY TALBOTT TOLD PRESIDENT ARISTIDE 
THAT PRESIDENT CLINTON HAD ACCEPTED HIS INVITATION TO 
VISIT HAITI MARCH 31. TALBOTT SAID THAT THERE NEVER HAD 
BEEN A DOUBT IN PRESIDENT CLINTON'S MIND THAT HE WOULD 
COME TO HAITI; THE ONLY QUESTION HAD BEEN WHEN. TALBOTT 
.MENTIONED THE VISIT WOULD COMBINE BOTH BILATERAL AND 
MULTILATERAL EVENTS. 
2. ARISTIDE SAID HE WAS DELIGHTED WITH THE GOOD NEWS; 
IN FACT HE HAD BEEN DREAMING OF THIS DAY WHICH WILL NOW 
BECOME REALITY. 
3. MISSION WILL HOST INTERNAL PLANNING MEETING MARCH 9 
AND WILL PROVIDE AN AGENDA FOR WHITE HOUSE ADVANCE TEAM 
VISIT ON MARCH 10.
