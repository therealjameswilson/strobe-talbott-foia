SUBJECT: THE SECRETARY'S MEETING WITH MALCOLM RIFKIND, 
BRITISH FOREIGN SECRETARY, FEBRUARY 19, LONDON, ENGLAND 
1. CLASSIFIED BY JOHN CAMPBELL, DEPUTY EXECUTIVE 
SECRETARY, 
REASON 1.5 D. 
2. (U) THIS CABLE REPORTS ON THE'SECRETARY'S MEETING 
WITH BRITISH FOREIGN SECRETARY RIFKIND ON FEBRUARY 19, 
1997, AT CARLTON GARDENS. 
3. (U) US PARTICIPANTS: THE SECRETARY, AMBASSADOR 
CROWE, DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE TALBOTT, CHIEF OF 
STAFF SHOCAS, SENIOR ADVISOR TO THE SECRETARY RUBIN, 
ASSISTANT SECRETARY OF STATE KORNBLUM, NSC SENIOR 
DIRECTOR VERSHBOW, DEPARTMENT SPOKESMAN BURNS, 
EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT TO THE SECRETARY HALE, DCM BRADTKE 
AND EMBASSY NOTETAKER. BRITISH PARTICIPANTS: FOREIGN 
SECRETARY RIFKIND, MINISTER BONSOR, AMBASSADOR KERR, 
POLITICAL DIRECTOR GREENSTOCK, DEPUTY UNDER SECRETARY 
BURNS, DEPUTY UNDER SECRETARY GALSWORTHY, PRESS 
SPOKESMAN SHINEWALD, POLICY PLANNING DIRECTOR LESLIE 
AND PRIVATE SECRETARY EHRMAN. 
4.--(14—SEGIN SUMMARY. THE SECRETARY AND RIFKIND 
DISCUSSED CYPRUS, THE MIDDLE EAST, HONG KONG AND THE 
UNHCR RESOLUTION ON CHINA. ON CYPRUS, RIFKIND 
SUGGESTED MOVEMENT TOWARD A UN LED U.S.-EU EFFORT. THE 
SECRETARY SAID WE WERE STILL CONSIDERING OUR APPROACH 
AND LOOKED FORWARD TO BILATERAL DISCUSSIONS IN LONDON 
IN A FEW WEEKS. THE SECRETARY AND RIFKIND AGREED THAT 
THE ISRAELIS HAD TO BE URGED TO LIFT COUNTER-PRODUCTIVE 
RESTRICTIONS ON THE PALESTINIAN ECONOMY. RIFKIND 
PRESSED FOR A STATEMENT ON OUR VIEW OF A POST-SADDAM 
IRAQ, FULLY REINTEGRATED INTO THE WEST, ARGUING THIS 
WOULD BE HELPFUL TO HUSSEIN. THE SECRETARY NOTED THAT 
WE HAD ALREADY MADE OUR VIEWS CLEAR AND WONDERED HOW WE 
COULD RESTATE THEM WITHOUT SUGGESTING A CHANGE IN 
POLICY TOWARD IRAQ. THE SECRETARY SAID WE WERE STILL 
AWAITING MORE EVIDENCE ON AL-KHOBAR AND WOULD CONSULT 
WITH BRITAIN AT THE APPROPRIATE TIME. RIFKIND 
EXPRESSED BRITISH GRATITUDE FOR U.S. SUPPORT ON HONG 
KONG AND SAID HMG WOULD SUPPORT A UNHCR RESOLUTION ON 
CHINA. THE SECRETARY SAID WE WANTED TO PREPARE THE 
GROUND FOR A POSSIBLE RESOLUTION. SHE WOULD ASSESS THE 
SITUATION DURING HER FORTHdOMING VISIT TO BEIJING. END
