2. ~ BEGIN SUMMARY: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY MET WITH
BRITISH AMBASSADOR SIR CHRISTOPHER MEYER ON MARCH 14 TO
DISCUSS RUSSIA. MEYER PROVIDED A READOUT OF PRIME
MINISTER BLAIR'S RECENT VISIT TO MOSCOW AND HIS MEETINGS
WITH ACTING PRESIDENT PUTIN. THEIR DISCUSSIONS CENTERED
ON CHECHNYA. PM BLAIR EMPHASIZED THE LACK OF
PROPORTIONATE RESPONSE BY RUSSIAN TROOPS. PUTIN
EMPHATICALLY REJECTED THIS VIEW. ALSO ATTENDING THE
MEETING WERE AMB. SESTANOVICH, UK POLMILCOUNS PETER
GOODERHAM, D CHIEF OF STAFF JOHN BASS AND DEPUTY UK
DESKOFF (NOTETAKER). END SUMMARY.
DECLASSIFIED
3. ~ MEYER PROVIDED A READOUT OF PRIME MINISTER BLAIR'S
RECENT TRIP TO MOSCOW. HE SAID BLAIR HAD BOTH A FORMAL
ROUND OF TALKS AND MANY INFORMAL DISCUSSIONS WITH ACTING
PRESIL'ENT PUTIN. MEYER SAID THE FORMAL EXCHANGE HAD A
.STILTED& "BREZHNEV" QUALITY TO IT. THE DISCUSSION WAS
MOSTLY ON CHECHNYA; NMD DID NOT COME UP. BLAIR SPOKE
ABOUT THE LACK OF PROPORTIONATE RESPONSE BY RUSSIAN
TROOPS. THE RUSSIANS NEEDED TO DEVELOP A TRANSPARENT
PROCESS TO LOOK INTO WHAT HAD TRANSPIRED. PUTIN RESPONDED
WITH A "VERY LONG AND DEFENSIVE" EXPLANATION OF RUSSIAN
ACTIONS. PUTIN CLAIMED THAT CHECHNYA WAS PART OF A LARGER
EFFORT COMING OUT OF AFGHANISTAN AND PAKISTAN TO
DESTABILIZE CENTRAL ASIA. HE ACKNOWLEDGED NO ATROCITIES
BY RUSSIAN TROOPS, AND SAID THEIR BEHAVIOR WAS COMPLETELY
PROPORTIONATE. BLAIR RETURNED SEVERAL TIMES TO THE POINT
THAT PUTIN HAD TO SET IN MOTION A SYSTEM FOR INVESTIGATING
ALLEGATIONS OF ATROCITIES IF HE WANTED WESTERN
UNDERSTANDING OF RUSSIA'S POSITION.
4.~MEYER SAID THERE WAS SOME DISCUSSION OF ECONOMIC
ISSUES. PUTIN EXPRESSED HIS COMMITMENT TO A FREE MARKET
ECONOMY AND ECONOMIC LIBERALIZATION, BUT THIS "HAD TO BE
DONE CAREFULLY." HE SAID RUSSIA'S POVERTY PROBLEM HAD TO
BE MANAGED LEST IT PROVOKE A "SOCIAL EXPLOSION." AT
DIFFERENT POINTS THROUGHOUT THE VISIT, PUTIN CONVEYED, THE
SENTIMENT THAT RUSSIA SHOULD BE MORE CENTERED CULTURALLY
AND ECONOMICALLY TOWARDS EUROPE. PUTIN SPOKE OF THE NEED
FOR RUSSIA TO DEVELOP "MATURE" POLITICAL PARTIES AND THE
NEED FOR A SPECTRUM OF PARTIES WITH NATIONAL BASES.. THE
OVERALI BRITISH IMPRESSION WAS THAT PUTIN WAS SAYING
THINGS THAT THE WEST WANTS TO HEAR FROM HIM.
5. ~ON KOSOVO/BALKANS, PUTIN STRESSED THAT HE HAD NO
LOVE FOR MILOSEVIC, ALTHOUGH HE BALANCED THIS SENTIMENT
WITH REMARKS ABOUT "ALBANIAN EXTREMISTS." NEVERTHELESS,
PUTIN MADE CLEAR THAT THESE WERE PRIVATE VIEWS, AND COULD
NOT BE SAID IN PUBLIC.
6. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RESPONDED THAT WHILE ALL OF
THIS REPRESENTED NOTHING NEW --WHAT IT CONFIRMED WAS BOTH
"CAUTIONARY AND SOBERING." THE U.S. WILL SEEK WAYS TO

CONVEY A SENSE OF RESERVE ABOUT PUTIN. ON CHECHNYA, THE
DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID IT SOUNDED LIKE PUTIN WAS ENGAGED IN
OVERLONG EXPRESSIONS OF "JE NE REGRETTE RIEN." AMB.
SESTANOVICH OBSERVED THAT PUTIN USED TO MAKE AT LEAST
TACTICAL CONCESSIONS TO THE IDEA OF "MISTAKES WERE MADE,"
BUT NO LONGER. TALBOTT SAID PUTIN IS RESORTING TO DENIAL,
THOUGH HE HAS TO KNOW THE TRUTH ABOUT RUSSIAN ACTIONS. HE
BELIEVES PUTIN HAS BASICALLY DECIDED TO "GUT IT OUT," AND
FIGURE OUT THE ABSOLUTE MINIMUM STEPS HE CAN TAKE ON
CHECHNYA, E.G., PERHAPS LETTING THE INTERNATIONAL
COMMITTEE. OF THE RED CROSS INTO THE REGION..TALBOTT SAID
THE WEST SHOULD NOT DECEIVE ITSELF IN THAT "WE ARE DEALING
WITH AN INDIVIDUAL WHO HAS RIDDEN CHECHNYA RIGHT INTO THE
PRESIDENCY."
7. ~ TALBOTT SAID RUSSIAN DEPUTY FONMIN MAMEDOV HAD
STRESSED IN THEIR RECENT MEETINGS IN ROME THAT THE
RUSSIANS WANT THE UPCOMING G-8 SUMMIT IN OKINAWA TO-BE A
"COMING OUT PARTY" FOR THE "NEW MEMBER OF THE BOARD."
JUST AS THE COLOGNE SUMMIT WAS AN OCCASION FOR
RAPPROCHEMENT AFTER THE KOSOVO CRISIS, THE RUSSIANS WANT
OKINAWA TO SERVE THE SAME PURPOSE FOLLOWING CHECHNYA.
TALBOTT SAID WE HAVE GOT TO BE CLEAR ABOUT THE CONDITIONS
REQUIRED TO MAKE A GOOD RECEPTION HAPPEN.
8. ~ TALBOTT STRESSED THAT HE DID NOT MEAN TO CALL INTO
QUESTION THE WISDOM OF THE BLAIR VISIT, BUT HE OBSERVED
THAT IT HAD CREATED A SENSE THAT "ALL ROADS LEAD TO PUTIN"
THESE DAYS. TALBOTT THOUGHT PUTIN'S FIRST FOREIGN TRIP
WOULD BE TO BEIJING. HE SAID PUTIN IS "WALKING A WALK.
THAT- IS DIFFERENT FROM THE TALK HE IS TALKING." MEYER
RESPONDED THAT THERE WERE NO "ROSE-TINTED GLASSES" IN
LONDON, BUT THAT PUTIN WAS A MAN THEY NEEDED TO DO
BUSINESS WITH. MEYER SAID HE BELIEVED PUTIN HAD RECEIVED
BLAIR'S MESSAGE ON CHECHNYA AND HUMAN RIGHTS LOUD AND
CLEAR STILL g PUTIN TH INKS IT IS ENOUGH TO APPOINT A
HUMAN RIGHT OMBUDSMAN FOR CHECHNYA, VLADIMIR KALAMANOV,
AND LET UN HIGH 'COMMISSIONER MARY ROBINSON VISIT CHECHNYA.
MEYER SAI D PUTI N IS WELL PREPARED FOR VI S ITORS g AND LAYS
ON THE FLATTERY. TALBOTT ASKED IF THE ISSUE OF JOURNALIST
ANDREI BABITSKY HAD COME UP. MEYER SAID HE HAD NO
INDICATION THAT IT HAD.
9.~ TALBOTT SAID IT WAS A HUGE QUESTION AS TO WHAT
PUTIN WOULD REVEAL AFTER THE ELECTION. THE PRAGMATIC CASE
SUGGESTED HE WOULD NEED TO INTEGRATE RUSSIA WITH THE WEST,
BUT A LOT OF INDICATORS THAT THE U.S. HAS SEEN SO'FAR
SUGGEST THINGS COULD GO IN A DIFFERENT DIRECTION. TALBOTT
SAID HIS OWN FEELING WAS THAT THESE WERE STILL "PANNING
OUT" IN PUTIN'S OWN MIND. TALBOTT CLOSED THE DISCUSSION
BY SAYING THAT IT SOUNDED LIKE BLAIR HAD GONE OUT OF HIS
WAY TO HAMMER HOME THE RIGHT MESSAGE.
