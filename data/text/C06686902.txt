September 20, 1993 
@WEE 
Thank you for your letter of July 7 requesting a meeting to 
discuss Sovlink-American Corporation sponsbrship of an 
investment fund for the New Independent States (NIS) of the 
former Soviet Union. 
I understand that members of my staff met with Roger Pyle 
of Sovlink on September 2 to discuss ways in which the U.S. 
Government can effectively network with the Fund. My staff 
believes this unique fund fills an important niche in promoting 
private sector development and demonstrating the profitability 
of investments with significant NIS participation and 
substantial Western content and technology. My staff has 
followed up with our Embassy in Moscow to ensure that our field 
staff is aware and supportive of Sovlink's activities. 
Thank you again for bringing Sovlink's activities to my 
attention. I look forward to learning about the Fund's 
investment successes in the NIS. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador At Large and 
Special Adviser to The Secretary 
On the New Independent States
