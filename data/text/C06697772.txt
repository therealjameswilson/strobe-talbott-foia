DECLASSIFIED 
2. 44) SUMMARY. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH 
BELARUSIAN PARLIAMENTARY SPEAKER SEMYON SHARETSKY ON MAY 
20. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ADDRESSED SHARETSKY'S CONCERNS 
ABOUT NATO EXPANSION AND STRESSED THE U.S. DESIRE TO 
REMAIN ENGAGED WITH BELARUS OVER THE LONG TERM, THROUGHOUT 
ALL LEVELS OF SOCIETY. HE ALSO NOTED U.S. CONCERN OVER
RECENT HUMAN RIGHTS VIOLATIONS IN BELARUS. END SUMMARY. 
3.-K-+ DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT SPENT NEARLY 40 MINUTES 
ON MAY 20 WITH SEMYON SHARETSKY, THE SPEAKER OF THE 
BELARUSIAN PARLIAMENT, WHO IS IN THE U.S. ON A USIA 
INTERNATIONAL VISITORS PROGRAM TO STUDY THE ROLE OF 
CONGRESS IN THE U.S. GOVERNMENT. ACCOMPANYING SHARETSKY 
WERE FELLOW PEOPLE'S DEPUTY MECHISLAV GIRUT, LEADER OF THE 
AGRARIAN BLOC; PARLIAMENTARY STAFFER VLADIMIR ULAKHOVICH, 
AMBASSADOR SERGUE1 MARTYNOV AND FIRST SECRETARY VALENTIN 
RYBAKOV. PRESENT ALONG WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WERE 
S/NI S DEPUTY DAN SPECKHARD, D SPECIAL ASSISTANT PHILIP 
GOLDBERG AND EUR/WST MARIA RUDENSKY. 
4. -(€1- - THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OPENED THE MEETING BY 
WELCOMING THE SPEAKER'S DELEGATION TO WASHINGTON AND 
PRAISING THE ',IMPORTANT WORK" THAT THE PEOPLE OF BELARUS 
AND THE PARLIAMENT IN PARTICULAR HAVE UNDERTAKEN AS THE 
COUNTRY ASSUMES ITS PLACE IN THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY. 
HE EXPRESSED FIRM U.S. SUPPORT FOR THE INDEPENDENCE, 
SOVEREIGNTY AND TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY OF ALL 12 NEW 
INDEPENDENT STATES, IN PARTICULAR BELARUS. DIFFICULTIES 
IN OUR BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP HAVE ARISEN FROM TIME TO 
,TIME,-HE SAID, BUT GOODWILL EXISTS THROUGHOUT ALL LEVELS 
OF GOVERNMENT IN THE U.S. AND BELARUS. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY POINTED TO THE HIGH LEVEL OF COOPERATION AMONG 
THE DEPARTMENT, THE MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS AND THE 
EMBASSY OF BELARUS IN WASHINGTON. HE ALSO NOTED U.S. 
CONCERN OVER RECENT HUMAN RIGHTS ABUSES IN BELARUS.

SUPPORT OF HIS "YOUNG GOVERNMENT, WHICH, LIKE A YOUNG 
PLANT, REQUIRES MUCH CARE." SHARETSKY SAID THAT WHILE 
BELARUS IS A PRESIDENTIAL REPUBLIC, PARLIAMENT NONETHELESS 
PLAYS A KEY ROLE. BY WAY OF EXAMPLE, SHARETSKY MAINTAINED 
THAT PRESIDENT LUKASHENKO NEGOTIATED THE APRIL 2 
BELARUS-RUSSIA INTEGRATION ACCORD ONLY AFTER RECEIVING 
GUIDING PRINCIPLES FROM PARLIAMENT, AND PARLIAMENTARY 
RATIFICATION WAS NECESSARY FOR THE TREATY TO TAKE EFFECT. 
IN ADDITION, SHARETSKY TOLD THE DEPUTY SECRETARY HE WOULD 
HEAD THE INTER-PARLIAMENTARY BODY CREATED BY THE TREATY, 
AND EMPHASIZED THAT THIS ENTITY WOULD NOT BE SUPRANATIONAL 
IN NATURE, AND THAT BELARUS WOULD REMAIN A SOVEREIGN, 
INDEPENDENT COUNTRY UNDER THE ACCORD. HE SAID PARLIAMENT 
HAD INSTRUCTED LUKASHENKO TO HAMMER OUT THE TERMS OF THE 
TREATY TO INCLUDE: PRESERVATION OF THE INDEPENDENCE OF 
BOTH BELARUS AND RUSSIA, ABSENCE OF "INTERFERENCE" IN THE 
INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF EITHER COUNTRY BY THE OTHER; 
PRESERVATION OF BOTH COUNTRIES' TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY; AND 
ASSURANCE OF MUTUALLY BENEFICIAL RELATIONS FOR BOTH 
COUNTRIES. REFERRING TO HIS OWN BACKGROUND, HE SAID THAT 
EVEN DURING THE SOVIET ERA, HE HAD SPOKEN OF THE NEED TO 
TRANSFORM THE ECONOMY FROM A COMMAND BASIS TO A MARKET 
SYSTEM. HE CLAIMED THAT HE IDENTIFIED THE DRAWBACKS OF 
THE SOVIET SYSTEM EARLY ON, DESPITE THE DANGERS THAT SUCH 
THINKING COULD HAVE POSED FOR HIM. 
BELARUS OPPOSES NATO'ENLARGEMENT 
6. -1-E-PROCEEDING TO THE TOPIC OF EUROPEAN SECURITY, THE 
SPEAKER STATED FLATLY THAT BELARUS OPPOSES NATO 
ENLARGEMENT. BELARUS'DOES NOT INTEND TO JOIN NATO AND 
BELARUS'BELIEVES NATO EXPANSION EASTWARD IS' A BAD IDEA, HE 
SAID. WITH THE WARSAW PACT DEFUNCT, NATO NO LONGER HAS A 
REASON 70 EXIST, HE DECLARED. THE FEAR OF NUCLEAR WEAPONS 
POSSIBLY' BEING DEPLOYED IN POLAND SEEMED TO WEIGH 
ESPECIALLY HEAVILY ON SHARETSKY'S MIND. HE CITED 
STATEMENTS BY POLISH OFFICIALS THAT THEY ARE READY TO 
DEPLOY NUCLEAR WEAPONS IN POLAND WHEN AND IF THE COUNTRY 
JOINS NATO. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RESPONDED THAT NATO HAS 
ALREADY FUBLICLY DECLARED THAT UNDER EXISTING AND 
FORESEEABLE CIRCUMSTANCES MICH INCLUDE NATO ENLARGEMENT) 
NATO HAS NO PLANS TO STATION NUCLEAR WEAPONS WHERE THEY 
ARE NOT NOW DEPLOYED.

NOT IN RESPONSE TO ANY THREAT FROM THE EAST BUT TO DEVELOP 
A BROAD SECURITY ARCHITECTURE THROUGHOUT THE NORTH
