SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S APRIL 5 MEETING WITH GREEK 
FOREIGN MINISTER PAPANDREOU 
CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR R. NICHOLAS BURNS. REASONS 1.5 (B,D). 
RELEASE IN FULL 
1. -44- SUMMARY: GREEK FOREIGN MINISTER GEORGE PAPANDREOU TOLD 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT APRIL 5 THAT GREECE WILL CONTINUE TO 
SUPPORT THE NATO BOMBING CAMPAIGN DESPITE FIERCELY NEGATIVE PRESS 
AND PUBLIC OPINION HERE. HE WARNED THAT GREECE COULD NOT AGREE 
WITH ANY CHANGE OF BORDERS IN THE BALKANS AND WAS RELIEVED TO 
HEAR THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S ASSURANCES THAT WE HAD NO SUCH 
INTENTION. PAPANDREOU COMMITTED THAT GREECE WOULD TAKE IN 
SEVERAL THOUSAND (WE BELIEVE A MINIMUM OF 5000) REFUGEES AND 
WOULD PERMIT TRANSIT OF GREECE BY THOSE REFUGEES DESTINED FOR 
TURKEY. 
2. -E-Si PAPANDREOU IS PREPARED TO ASSUME A LEADING DIPLOMATIC 
ROLE SHOULD THAT BE NECESSARY. HE PROPOSED A DE FACTO, 
UNANNOUNCED SUSPENSION OF NATQ BOMBING ON ORTHODOX EASTER THIS 
COMING APRIL 11, REMINDING TALBOTT THAT THE NAZIS LAUNCHED 
INFAMOUS BOMBINGS OF BELGRADE ON EASTER 1941. HE WARNED THE 
SERBS WOULD USE ANY EASTER BOMBINGS TO CONTINUE THEIR CAMPAIGN TO 
BRAND THE U.S. AS NEO-NAZIS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THANKED 
PAPANDREOU FOR HIS FIRM STATEMENTS AGAINST ETHNIC CLEANSING. 
THEY AGREED ON THE CRITICAL NEED TO PRESERVE THE U.S.-GREECE 
RELATIONSHIP WHICH HAS BEEN BUFFETTED IN THE CRISIS. END 
SUMMARY. 
3: -Hi- DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT MET FOR TWO HOURS OVER 
BREAKFAST IN ATHENS APRIL 5 WITH GREEK FOREIGN MINISTER GEORGE 
PAPANDREOU. AMBASSADOR BURNS AND PAPANDREOU'S CHIEF OF STAFF 
MARIALENA CONALIS ALSO ATTENDED. THE DISCUSSION FOCUSSED 
EXCLUSIVELY ON KOSOVO-RELATED ISSUES. 
4. -664 DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT BEGAN BY TELLING PAPANDREOU THAT 
WE VALUE THE CLOSE CONSULTATIONS BETWEEN GREECE AND THE U.S. 
SINCE THE CONFLICT BEGAN. WE WANT TO CONTINUE THAT COOPERATION 
AS WE PROSECUTE THE BOMBING CAMPAIGN AND LOOK FORWARD TO A 
RESOLUTION. HE CONTINUED THAT THE U.S. UNDERSTANDS THE GREEK 
GOVERNMENT'S DIFFICULTY IN COPING WITH PRESS AND PUBLIC OPINION 
WHICH IS NEARLY UNIVERSAL IN OPPOSITION TO THE NATO ACTION. WE 
VALUE THE SUPPORT GREECE HAS GIVEN TO NATO IN LIGHT OF THIS 
ENVIRONMENT AND ASSUME GREECE WILL PLAY AN EVEN GREATER ROLE WHEN 
THE DIPLOMATIC PHASE OF THE CONFLICT IS REACHED. 
5. i-s4 PAPANDREOU ACKNOWLEDGED THE FIERCE OPPOSITION OF THE 
GREEK PUBLIC TO THE CAMPAIGN. IN THIS SENSE, IT WAS CRITICAL 
THAT WE DID EVERYTHING POSSIBLE TO PRESERVE THE U.S.-GREECE 
RELATIONSHIP THROUGH THE CRISIS. HE NOTED THAT HIS OWN ROLE WAS 
SENSITIVE AND COMPLICATED--HE WAS BORN AND EDUCATED IN THE U.S. 
AND HIS FATHER HAD DIFFICULT RELATIONS WITH THE U.S. IN THE 
1980S--AND THAT HE HAD TO BE CAREFUL IN THE COMPLEX GREEK 
POLITICAL WORLD BECAUSE OF HIS PAST. NONETHELESS, HE WAS 
PERSONALLY DEDICATED TO PRESERVE OUR RELATIONSHIP AND TO PLAY A

6. -H3-.)- PAPANDREOU LISTED BRIEFLY THE COMPLICATIONS OF THE KOSOVO 
CONFLICT FOR GREECE--GREECE'S ACTIVE TRADE WITH BALKAN COUNTRIES 
WAS DISRUPTED; TOURISM, THE COUNTRY'S LARGEST MONEY EARNER, WAS 
IN JEOPARDY; THE STOCK MARKET HAD DECLINED AND SOME EVEN DOUBTED 
GREECE WOULD NOW REACH MEMBERSHIP IN EMU. FIGHTING IN KOSOVO HAD 
A DIRECT, NEGATIVE EFFECT ON GREECE. 
7 (S) MOST IMPORTANTLY, PAPANDREOU CONTINUED, GREECE WORRIED 
ABOUT POSSIBLE CHANGES IN BORDERS DUE IN LARGE PART TO GREECE'S 
OWN COMPLICATED HISTORY IN THE BALKANS IN THIS CENTURY. GREEK 
POPULAR FEELINGS WERE STRONGLY PRO-SERB, STRONGLY ANTI-WAR AND 
INFUSED WITH A STARK SENSE OF A DOUBLE STANDARD--NATO WAS WILLING 
TO FIGHT FOR KOSOVARS BUT NOT FOR CYPRIOTS OR KURDS. 
8. -+S+ IT WAS BECAUSE OF THESE INTERNAL CONCERNS THAT THE GREEK 
GOVERNMENT NEEDED TO SHOW ITS PEOPLE THAT IT WAS ACTIVE IN THE 
SEARCH FOR A SOLUTION TO THE KOSOVO CONFLICT. PM SIMITIS, 
CONSEQUENTLY, HAD ORDERED ITS MILITARY TO HELP THE RELIEF EFFORT 
IN ALBANIA AND, POTENTIALLY, IN MACEDONIA. IN ADDITION, GREECE 
WANTED TO BE ACTIVE DIPLOMATICALLY WHEN THE TIME WAS RIGHT. 
PAPANDREOU HOPED WE WOULD UNDERSTAND, IN THIS CONTEXT, HIS 
FREQUENT PUBLIC STATEMENTS FOR A PEACEFUL SOLUTION AND FOR 
VARIOUS INITIATIVES. HE NOTED THAT HE AND DEFENSE MINISTER 
TSOHATZOPOULOS HAD RECENTLY MADE STRONG STATEMENTS AGAINST ETHNIC 
CLEANSING. GREECE WAS TRYING HARD TO WORK COOPERATIVELY WITH 
TURKEY. HE SPOKE TO FM CEM FREQUENTLY ON REFUGEE ASSISTANCE 
ISSUES. 
9. 4..S.4. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT REPLIED THAT GREECE'S OPPOSITION 
TO THE ETHNIC CLEANSING WAS PARTICULARLY IMPORTANT GIVEN ITS 
HISTORIC RELATIONSHIP WITH SERBIA. HE ASSURED PAPANDREOU THAT WE 
UNDERSTOOD GREEK SENSITIVITIES ON BORDER CHANGES. WE WERE 
OPPOSED TO CHANGES OF BORDERS IN THE BALKANS, OPPOSED TO AN 
INDEPENDENT KOSOVAR STATE AND WANTED AN OUTCOME LEAVING BALKAN 
BOUNDARIES INTACT. MILOSEVIC, HOWEVER, HAD LOST MORAL AUTHORITY 
IN KOSOVO AND THE RIGHT TO RULE IT IN THE FUTURE. THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY MUST WORK TO EJECT THE SERBS FROM CONTROL 
OF KOSOVO AND TO ESTABLISH SOME SORT OF PROTECTION REGIME TO 
PERMIT EVENTUAL RETURN OF THE REFUGEES. THIS SORT OF OUTCOME 
WOULD PRESERVE THE CONCEPT OF SOVEREIGNTY AND TERRITORIAL 
INTEGRITY IN THE BALKANS BUT WOULD PREVENT THE SERBS FROM 
EXERCISING CONTROL OVER THE KOSOVARS IN THE FUTURE. 
10. 4.g4 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED GREECE TO DO AS MUCH AS IT 
COULD TO ACCEPT REFUGEES, UNDERSTANDING THAT GREECE HAD ALREADY 
TAKEN IN'UPWARDS OF 600,000 ALBANIANS IN THIS DECADE. TURKEY 
WOULD TAKE A MINIMUM OF 20,000 PEOPLE. WOULD GREECE PERMIT 
TRANSIT OF THOSE REFUGEES THROUGH GREECE EN ROUTE TO TURKEY? 
PAPANDREOU REPLIED THAT HE SAW NO REASON FOR GREECE NOT TO PERMIT 
THIS, ADDING IT WOULD BE POSITIVE SYMBOLISM FOR BOTH COUNTRIES. 
11. 4,&} THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THEN ASKED THAT GREECE FIND WAYS TO 
HELP MACEDONIA AT A CRITICAL TIME. IT WAS CLEARLY NOT IN 
GREECE'S NATIONAL INTEREST TO SEE MACEDONIA COME APART. 
AMBASSADOR BURNS EXPLAINED, FOR EXAMPLE, THAT A LARGE NUMBER OF 
HOWITZERS DESTINED FOR MACEDONIA HAD BEEN HELD UP IN.LEGALISMS AT 
THE PORT OF THESSALONIKI FOR THREE MONTHS. GREECE SHOULD PUT 
ASIDE THE BUREAUCRATICS AND RELEASE THEM IMMEDIATELY AS THEY WERE 
DESPERATELY NEEDED. PAPANDREOU SAID HE HAD RECEIVED THE 
AMBASSADOR'S LETTER AND WANTED TO HELP THIS WEEK.

12. 464- PAPANDREOU RESPONDED POSITIVELY TO THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY'S SUGGESTION THAT GREECE, AND SPECIFICALLY PAPANDREOU, 
MIGHT PLAY A SIGNIFICANT ROLE WHEN THE DIPLOMATIC PHASE IS 
REACHED. PAPANDREOU SAID HE WAS READY TO CONTRIBUTE PERSONALLY 
SHOULD WE AND OTHERS FIND THAT USEFUL (WE WILL PROVIDE FURTHER 
DETAILS ON THIS DISCUSSION SEPTEL). 
13. 4..B PAPANDREOU AND THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED THAT WE 
NEEDED TO WORK CLOSELY TOGETHER ON A LONG-TERM PLAN ON THE FUTURE 
OF THE BALKANS.- SPECIFICALLY, PAPANDREOU BELIEVED WE NEEDED TO 
THINK THROUGH WITH THE EU HOW TO DEVELOP THE REGION ECONOMICALLY. 
FIRST EFFORTS SHOULD BE DIRECTED TO ASSIST MACEDONIA TO COPE WITH 
THE REFUGEE PROBLEM. IN ADDITION, IT MIGHT BE POSSIBLE TO MAKE 
SOME HEADWAY ON THE MACEDONIA NAME ISSUE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
ENCOURAGED PAPANDREOU TO BE FLEXIBLE. "FYROM" AS A NAME HAD 
EXTREMELY NEGATIVE SYMBOLISM IN LIGHT OF THE CURRENT CONFLICT. 
14. 4-64 BRINGING THE MEETING TO A CLOSE, PAPANDREOU SUGGESTED 
THAT WE THINK HARD ABOUT CALLING OFF ALL AIR STRIKES ON ORTHODOX 
EASTER SUNDAY--APRIL 11. NATO NEED NOT STATE WHAT IT WAS DOING 
OR CALL IT A PAUSE. BUT WE SHOULD BE MINDFUL THAT THE NAZIS 
LAUNCHED AN INFAMOUS BOMBING RAID ON BELGRADE IN 1941. IF NATO 
BOMBED THIS COMING SUNDAY, WE WOULD HAND THE SERBS A PROPAGANDA 
TOOL TO CONTINUE THEIR CAMPAIGN TO BRAND US AS NEO-NAZIS. 
15. (-6-) AMBASSADOR BURNS CONCLUDED THE MEETING BY REQUESTING THE 
FASTEST POSSIBLE GREEK ACTION IN TAKING IN REFUGEES IN THE COMING 
DAYS, DRAWING ON CHRIS HILL'S BELIEF THAT THIS WOULD HELP BOOST 
MACEDONIAN MORALE. PAPANDREOU CONFIRMED THAT THIS WAS GREECE'S 
INTENTION. 
16. -(-ST DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT DID NOT HAVE AN OPPORTUNITY TO 
CLEAR THIS CABLE BEFORE DEPARTING GREECE.
