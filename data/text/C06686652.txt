This note is, I recognize, out-of-channels, but 
I trust not out of line. I'm writing to thank you for 
doing an unscheduled drop-by on the Ukrainian Foreign 
Minister in Tony Lake's office today -- and then getting 
the President to do the same. You really did help advance 
the cause. If we succeed in getting those nuclear weapons 
out of Ukraine, I'll try to arrange for one to be mounted 
on your wall as a trophy commemorating your help. Your 
secretary looked a tad astonished when I bulled my way 
into your office to make my impassioned and unauthorized 
appeal -- and you sure made me.glad I did so. But I 
promise not to make a habit out of it. 
By the way, let me use this note also to thank 
you for what you had to say on the subject of Nagorno-
Karabakh, the Minsk Group, CSCE and all that in the 
Kozyrev session in the Oval Office last week. That, too, 
helped a lot. 
A11 in all, it's terrific to have you as one 
of the captains of the FSU Team. 
RinoorAly
B6 
-----Strobe Talbott
