2. FOLLOWING IS A SUMMARY ON DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S AUGUST 
30 MEETING WITH PRD LEADER PENA GOMEZ. 
SUMMARY 
3. DURING AN AUGUST 30 MEETING WITH DOMINICAN REVOLUTIONARY 
PARTY (PRD) LEADER JOSE FRANCISCO PENA GOMEZ, DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT REAFFIRMED THE USG COMMITMENT TO DEMOCRACY THROUGHOUT THE 
HEMISPHERE. HE REITERATED OUR MESSAGE THAT THE USG WILL NEVER 
SEEK GODR SUPPORT ON HAITI IN RETURN FOR DOWNPLAYING DEFENSE OF 
DEMOCRACY ON THE DOMINICAN SIDE OF THE ISLAND. TALBOTT COMMENDED 
THE PRD'S ROLE IN AVOIDING POST-ELECTION VIOLENCE AND PROMISED TO 
FOLLOW EVENTS IN THE DR CLOSELY AS PART OF OUR SUPPORT FOR 
DEMOCRACY. PENA GOMEZ PRAISED THE ROLE OF THE USG IN THE 
DOMINICAN ELECTORAL CRISIS AND PROFESSED OPTIMISM THAT THE LATEST 
CONGRESSIONAL MOVES PORTEND COMPLIANCE WITH THE PACT FOR 
DEMOCRACY. END SUMMARY. 
DEFENSE OF DEMOCRACY 
4. IN THE FINAL EVENT OF DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S AUGUST 30 
VISIT TO THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC, HE HELD AN AIRPORT MEETING WITH 
PRD LEADER (AND PRESIDENTIAL CANDIDATE) JOSE FRANCISCO PENA 
GOMEZ. TALBOTT TOLD THE PRD LEADERS THAT DEFENSE OF DEMOCRACY IS 
A USG GOAL IN ALL OF LATIN AMERICA, ONE WHICH HAS HIS STRONG 
PERSONAL SUPPORT. HE TOLD PENA THAT AN HOUR EARLIER HE HAD 
REAFFIRMED TO PRESIDENT BALAGUER THE USG'S SUPPORT FOR DEMOCRACY 
AND ALSO URGED GODR DEFENSE OF DEMOCRACY IN HAITI AND AT HOME. 
PENA GOMEZ OPINED THAT U.S. ACTIONS HAD IMPROVED THE CLIMATE FOR 
DEMOCRACY IN THE DR. THE PRD LEADER ALSO APPLAUDED U.S. 
EXECUTIVE BRANCH AND CONGRESSIONAL STATEMENTS IN FAVOR OF 
DEMOCRACY AND PRAISED THE ROLE OF EMBASSY OFFICERS IN THE POST 
ELECTORAL CRISIS: PENA ACKNOWLEDGED THAT THE USG WAS NOT 
DEFENDING DEMOCRACY TO HELP HIM OR HIS PARTY, BUT FOR THE BENEFIT 
OF THE DOMINICAN PEOPLE. 
DEMOCRACY ON BOTH SIDES OF THE ISLAND 
5. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ALSO REITERATED THAT IT IS A MISTAKE TO 
BELIEVE THE U.S. HAS, OR WILL, STRIKE A "FAUSTIAN DEAL" IN WHICH 
WE WOULD SEEK GODR SUPPORT ON HAITI IN RETURN FOR MUTING OUR 
SUPPORT OF DEMOCRACY ON THE DOMINICAN SIDE OF HISPANIOLA. WE 
HAVE SENT THE MESSAGE THAT THERE IS NO DEAL, AND WE BELIEVE THE 
MESSAGE HAS BEEN RECEIVED, HE SAID. PENA COMMENTED THAT THE 
PERCEPTION OF A DEAL HAS EXISTED IN THE DR AS WELL AS THE UNITED 
STATES. (NOTE: AT THE END OF THE MEETING, ALVAREZ BOGAERT
