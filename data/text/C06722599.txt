WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, SULTAN QABOOS NOTED HIS 
ABIDING CONCERNS ABOUT THE MEDIA, ADVOCATED A VISIT BY 
UNSYG ANNAN TO THE GULF TO SPEAK ON IRAQ, AND AGREED

REACTIVATE THE MIDDLE EAST PEACE PROCESS AND TO GIVE

STRENGTH OF THE BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP: END SUMMARY.

5.  THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT THERE WAS REASON

SULTAN: WE SHOULD BE ONE CIVILIZATION 
6. -(C)- QABOOS AGREES THAT A CLASH OF CIVILIZATIONS IS 
NOT INEVITABLE. RATHER, HE SAID, CIVILIZATIONS HAVE 
BECOME MORE "INTERLINKED" OVER THE CENTURIES. THIS IS 
THE CASE IN ECONOMICS AND OTHER FIELDS, AND WE SHOULD 
LOOK FOR WAYS TO UNIFY THE WORLD FURTHER IN ONE 
CIVILIZATION. HE DID ACKNOWLEDGE THAT THERE CAN BE 
DIFFERENT WAYS OF THINKING (E.G., A MUSLIM WAY) WITHIN 
THAT ONE CIVILIZATION, AND ASSERTED -- FORESHADOWING HIS 
COMMENTS ON THE MEDIA -- THAT THOSE WANTING TO WIDEN 
SUCH GAPS SHOULD NOT BE GIVEN THE CHANCE TO WIN. 
THE MEDIA: WIDENING THE GAPS 
7. t'C',2 THE SULTAN POSITED THAT THE MEDIA OUGHT TO PLAY A 
MAJOR ROLE IN BRINGING CIVILIZATIONS TOGETHER BUT 
INSTEAD ARE WIDENING GAPS BY FOCUSING ON "THOSE WHO ARE 
AGAINST" OR THOSE TRYING TO "INVADE" OTHER CULTURES. HE 
WAS DISMISSIVE OF THE IDEA OF SUCH CULTURAL INVASION, 
SAYING THAT IT EXISTS ONLY IN THE OPINION OF 
"TROUBLEMAKERS" WHO SHOULD NOT BE GIVEN FREE REIN TO 
ACT. QABOOS PROCEEDED TO DRAW A DISTINCTION BETWEEN THE 
PRINT AND ELECTRONIC MEDIA. NEWSPAPERS, BOOKS AND 
MAGAZINES ARE RELATIVELY UNIMPORTANT, IN HIS VIEW, 
BECAUSE FEW PEOPLE ("IN OUR REGION") TAKE THE TIME TO 
READ IN DEPTH. TELEVISION ON THE OTHER HAND, GIVES 
WRONG-HEADED PEOPLE THE OPPORTUNITY TO "THROW BULLETS AT 
A WIDE AUDIENCE" AND CONFUSE ISSUES.

12. 1-64. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EMPHASIZED THAT PRESIDENT 
CLINTON AND SECRETARY ALBRIGHT TAKE THE MEPP AS 
SERIOUSLY AS ANY FOREIGN POLICY CHALLENGE THEY FACE, 
INCLUDING IRAQ. THEY DEEPLY ADMIRE OMAN'S BRAVE 
LEADERSHIP ON THIS ISSUE AND REJECT THE LINKAGE MANY IN 
THE REGION HAVE ATTEMPTED TO MAKE BETWEEN THE PEACE 
PROCESS AND THE SITUATION IN IRAQ. THE SULTAN REJOINED 
THAT THOSE WHO TALK ABOUT DOUBLE STANDARDS ARE NOT 
FAMILIAR WITH THE REAL ISSUES, AND AREN'T BEING HELPED 
TO UNDERSTAND EITHER. HE CALLED FOR FINDING A WAY TO 
MAKE U.S. EFFORTS "MORE OBVIOUS" Alb GETTING PLAINER 
WORDS OF EXPLANATION TO "SIMPLE PEOPLE" THROUGH CNN, THE 
BBC AND CHANNELS IN THIS REGION. HE ALSO WONDERED ALOUD 
WHETHER THERE SHOULDN'T BE A GROUP FORMED TO EVALUATE 
THE EXTENT TO WHICH SECURITY NEEDS ARE BEING MET ON BOTH 
THE PALESTINIAN AND ISRAELI SIDES. SECRETARY TALBOTT 
TOOK THE IDEA ON BOARD AND VOLUNTEERED THAT THE IDEAS 
THE U.S. HAS SHARED WITH THE PARTIES ARE DESIGNED TO 
PRODUCE SYNCHRONIZED, IF NOT SIMULTANEOUS, MOVEMENT ON 
ALL FOUR TRACKS. 
IRAN: ENCOURAGING SIGNS 
13:"..s*Ca. THE SULTAN ASSOCIATED HIMSELF WITH SECRETARY 
TALBOTT'S VIEWS ON IRAN, SAYING THAT THE COUNTRY IS 
MOVING INTO A NEW PHASE OF GOVERNANCE AND DEALING WITH 
THE REST OF THE WORLD. HE SAID HE FINDS THE SIGNS 
ENCOURAGING BUT ADVOCATED GIVING KHATEMI MORE TIME TO 
WORK HIS WAY THROUGH THE DIFFICULTIES HE FACES. NOT 
ENOUGH IS KNOWN ABOUT THE INTERNAL BALANCE OF POWER, 
THOUGH KHATEMI AND RAFSANJANI WORKED CLOSELY TOGETHER ON 
THE LATTER'S RECENT VISIT TO SAUDI ARABIA. AS FOR 
KHAMENEI, THE SULTAN CLAIMED IGNORANCE OF HIS VIEWS 
EXCEPT AS PROPOUNDED IN SERMONS AT MOSQUES. HE SAID 
THAT THE "AHL AL-BAIT" CONFERENCE (A GATHERING OF THE 
DESCENDANTS OF THE PROPHET MOHAMMED) WENT WELL THIS 
YEAR, WITH SPEECHES ADVISING IRAN'S LEADERSHIP NOT TO 
MAKE TROUBLE, E.G., BY "SUPPORTING CERTAIN GROUPS HERE 
AND THERE." HE ALSO NOTED THAT THE MARJA' (SPIRITUAL 
GUIDE) FOR OMAN'S SHI'A COMMUNITY WAS IN ATTENDANCE. 
TALBOTT ENCOURAGED THE SULTAN AND BIN ALAWI TO SHARE 
THEIR INSIGHTS WITH THE IRANIAN LEADERSIiIP AND REAFFIRM 
TO THEM (WITHOUT IMPLYING THAT OMAN WAS SERVING AS AN 
INTERMEDIARY) U.S. WILLINGNESS TO MOVE DOWN THE ROAD OF 
DIALOGUE SO LONG AS IT IS OPEN, OFFICAL AND DIRECT. 
QABOOS PLEDGED TO CONTINUE DOING SO.

SECRETARY STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF MAINTAINING A LEVEL

ALSO THANKED THE SULTAN FOR HIS HELP AND SUPPORT IN 
ESTABLISHING THE AMERICAN INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL - MUSCAT

SECRETARY ALBRIGHT. THE SULTAN STAYS ABREAST OF THE 
DEVELOPMENT OF THE NEW AMERICAN SCHOOL AND WAS OBVIOUSLY 
DELIGHTED WITH THIS GESTURE. SOUNDING A THEME OTHER 
RECENT VISITORS HAVE ALSO RAISED, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
ENCOURAGED THE SULTAN TO CONSIDER MAKING ANOTHER TRIP TO 
THE U.S., WHICH HE HAS NOT VISITED SINCE 1983. 
QABOOS BROUGHT THE OFFICIAL DISCUSSIONS TO A 
CLOSE BY STATING THAT OMAN VALUES ITS FRIENDSHIP WITH 
THE U.S. AND WILL CONTINUE TO BE FIRM ON ITS 
COMMITMENTS. SAYING THAT HE LOOKED FORWARD TO EVEN 
CLOSER RELATIONS, HE PRAISED THE EXCHANGE WITH THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY AS AN EVENT THAT MAKES CLEAR THAT "THE 
RELATIONSHIP IS IN VERY coop HEALTH AND WILL CONTINUE.

THIS WOULD BE HIS FOURTH -- BUT WAS NONCOMMITTAL ABOUT A 
DATE. REMINISCENCES ABOUT HIS FIRST TRIP (1963 OR 
1964), AND INFORMATION ABOUT AN UPCOMING EXHIBITION ON 
U.S.-OMANI RELATIONS ORGANIZED BY THE EMBASSY, ROUNDED 
OUT A VERY WARM, ALMOST TWO-HOUR MEETING.
