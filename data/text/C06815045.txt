1. ~ SUMMARY: THE PRESIDENT AND SECRETARY ALBRIGHT
VISITED ISLAMABAD ON MARCH 25 AT THE CONCLUSION OF THE
PRESIDENT'S TRIP TO SOUTH ASIA. IN THEIR MEETINGS WITH
GENERAL MUSHARRAF, THE PRESIDENT STRESSED THE NEED TO
RESTORE DEMOCRATIC, CIVILIAN RULE; URGED SIGNATURE OF THE
CTBT AND ADOPTION OF OTHER NONPROLIFERATION GOALS; CALLED
ON PAKISTAN TO USE ITS INFLUENCE WITH THE TALIBAN TO BRING
USAMA BIN LADEN TO JUSTICE AND AMELIORATE THE HUMAN RIGHTS
SITUATION IN AFGHANISTAN; AND SUGGESTED THAT INDO-
PAKISTANI RELATIONS BE BASED ON RESTRAINT, RESPECT FOR THE
LINE OF CONTROL IN KASHMIR, RENUNCIATION OF VIOLENCE AND A
RENEWAL OF DIALOGUE. RECIPIENT POSTS ARE REQUESTED TO
CONTACT HOST GOVERNMENT FOREIGN MINISTERS AND PRESENT THE
FOLLOWING POINTS AS AN ORAL MESSAGE FROM THE SECRETARY.
RELEASE IN FULL
DECLASSIFIED
END SUMMARY.
2. ~ PRESIDENT CLINTON MADE A SIX AND ONE HALF HOUR
STOP IN ISLAMABAD ON MARCH 25 AFTER VISITING INDIA AND
BANGI,ADESH. THE PRES1DENT AND SECRETARY ALBRIGHT MET WITH
GENERAL MUSHARRAF, FOREIGN MINISTER SATTAR AND OTHER
PAKISTANI OFFICIALS AND THE PRESIDENT MADE A LIVE
TELEVISION AND RADIO ADDRESS TO THE PAKISTANI PEOPLE.
(THE TEXT OF THE PRESIDENT'S REMARKS IS INCLUDED AT THE
END OF THIS CABLE).
3. ~ THE OBJECTIVE OF THE VISIT WAS TO KEEP OPEN OUR
LINES OF COMMUNICATION WITH .THE PAKISTANI LEADERSHIP.
AFTER THE VISIT, WE HOPE THAT COUNTRIES WELL DISPOSED TO
PAKISTAN WHO SHARE OUR CONCERNS ABOUT THE DIRECTION OF
PAKISTANI POLICIES CAN REINFORCE THE MESSAGE THE PRESIDENT
GAVE TO MUSHARRAF. WE REQUEST THAT RECIPIENTS PRESENT THE
FOLLOWING POINTS AS AN ORAL MESSAGE FROM THE SECRETARY
WITH THE TEXT OF'HE PRESIDENT'S REMARKS.
4. ~ MESSAGE
THE PRESIDENT WENT TO PAKISTAN TO DEMONSTRATE OUR
FRIENDSHIP WITH THE PAKISTANI PEOPLE, TO ESTABLISH LINES
OF COMMUNICATION WITH THE CURRENT PAKISTANI LEADERSHIP,
AND TO EXPRESS OUR CONCERN ABOUT THE DIRECTION PAKISTAN IS
TAKING.
WE HEARD PAKISTAN'S POSITIONS AND DIRECTLY AND FRANKLY
SHARED OUR OWN VIEWS, WITH THE PAKISTANI LEADERSHIP AND IN
THE PRESIDENT'S ADDRESS TO THE PAKISTANI PEOPLE. WE
STRUCTURED THE VISIT TO ENSURE THAT THE PRESIDENT'S TRAV"L
TO PAKISTAN COULD NOT BE CONSTRUED AS LEGITIMIZING THE
NON-DEMOCRATIC GOVERNMENT OF GENERAL MUSHARRAF.
THE PRESIDENT REITERATED U.S. SUPPORT FOR A PROMPT AND
ORDERLY RESTORATION OF DEMOCRATIC, CIVILIAN RULE AND URGED
MUSHARPAF TO DEVELOP A TIMETABLE AND ROAD MAP FOR
DEMOCRATIZATION AT THE NATIONAL LEVEL.

THE PRESIDENT DISCUSSED THE TRIAL OF FORMER PRIME MINISTER
NAWAZ SHARIF, EXPRESSING INTEREST IN A FAIR AND
TRANSPARENT PROCESS, AND UNDERSCORING OUR VIEW THAT THE
PRIME MINISTER SHOULD NOT BE EXECUTED IF HE IS CONVICTED
IN THE ONGOING HIJACKING TRIAL.
IN A LENGTHY DISCUSSION OF NONPROLIFERATION, HE URGED
PAKISTAN TO SIGN THE CTBT AND MEET OTHER NONPROLIFERATION
GOALS, SPELLING OUT THE DANGERS AND COSTS OF AN ARMS RACE
BETWEEN INDIA AND PAKISTAN.
THE PRESIDENT ASKED PAKISTAN TO USE ITS INFLUENCE WITH THE
TALIBAN TO SEE THAT USAMA BIN LADEN IS BROUGHT TO JUSTICE
AS SOON AS POSSIBLE.
THE PRESIDENT RAISED OUR HUMAN RIGHTS CONCERNS ABOUT THE
TALIBAN, PARTICULARLY ITS TREATMENT OF WOMEN AND
MINORITIES, AND PRESSED MUSHARRAF TO DO WHAT HE COULD TO
ASSIST IN THAT AREA.
DURING AN EXTENSIVE DISCUSSION OF KASHMIR AND INDO-
PAKISTANI RELATIONS, THE PRESIDENT ENCOURAGED PAKISTAN TO
SHOW RESTRAINT RESPECT THE LINE OF CONTROL 'IN KASHMIRg
RENOUNCE VIOLENCE AND RENEW DIALOGUE WITH INDIA.
GENERAL MUSHARRAF WAS LARGELY NONCOMMITTAL ON SPECIFIC
U.S. CONCERNS. HE REITERATED PAKISTAN'S EXISTING
POSITIONS ON THESE ISSUES IN A STRAIGHTFORWARD MANNER.
GENERAL MUSHARRAF DID PLEDGE THAT PAKISTAN WOULD NOT
EXPORT DANGEROUS TECHNOLOGIES OR WEAPONS OF MASS
DESTRUCTION AND SAID HE WOULD WORK WITH THE TALIBAN TO
RESOLVE THE ISSUE OF USAMA BIN LADEN.
PAKISTAN IS AT A CROSSROADS. I URGE YOU TO USE YOUR
INFLUENCE WITH GENERAL MUSHARRAF TO ENCOURAGE HIM TO
PURSUE POLICIES THAT WILL HALT PAKISTAN'S GROWING
ISOLATION AND PAVE THE WAY FOR DEVELOPMENT OF A MORE
STABLE, PROSPEROUS, AND DEMOCRATIC PAKISTAN AT PEACE WITH
ITS NEIGHBORS,
