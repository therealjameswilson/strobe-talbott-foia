4. -4-C} SUAMARY: THE SECRETARY AND LUZHKOV DISCUSSED WHAT 
LUZHKOV CITED AS "UNSATISFACTORY" ISSUES IN THE 
RELATIONSHIP, INCLUDING U.S. POLICY IN IRAQ, U.S. "DOUBLE 
STANDARDS" ON KOSOVO, AND U.S. SANCTIONS AGAINST MOSCOW-
BASED RESEARCH AND DESIGN INSTITUTES. THE SECRETARY 
STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF RESOLVING MISUNDERSTANDINGS AND 
UNDERSCORED PRESIDENT CLINTON'S DETERMINATION TO MAINTAIN 
A GOOD, FRIENDLY PARTNERSHIP WITH RUSSIA. SHE CITED 
TERRORISM AND PROLIFERATION OF WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION 
AS THE TWO BIGGEST PROBLEMS THREATENING GLOBAL SECURITY, 
AND CHALLENGED LUZHKOV'S CRITICISM OF U.S. POLICY IN IRAQ, 
UNDERSCORING THE THREAT IRAQ POSES TO GLOBAL SECURITY. 
REGARDING KOSOVO, SHE NOTED THAT THERE IS LITTLE 
DISAGREEMENT WITH RUSSIA THESE DAYS, BUT ON THE QUESTION 
OF U.S. ADMINISTRATIVE MEASURES WITH REGARD TO RUSSIAN 
INSTITUTES, THE SECRETARY REITERATED THAT THERE IS 
EVIDENCE THESE INSTITUTES ARE HELPING IRAN AND THAT IT IS 
IN RUSSIA'S INTEREST TO WORK WITH THE U.S. TO HALT THE 
TRANSFER OF DANGEROUS TECHNOLOGY AND KNOWLEDGE. THE 
SECRETARY URGED LUZHKOV TO ESTABLISH A MORE REGULAR 
DIALOGUE WITH AMBASSADOR COLLINS TO HELP RESOLVE SUCH 
ISSUES. END SUMMARY 
WORSENING RELATIONS 
5. -tel-LUZHKOV WELCOMED THE SECRETARY, EXPRESSING THE 
HOPE THAT HER TRAVEL TO MOSCOW WOULD GIVE HER A BETTER 
FEEL FOR THE PRESENT STATE OF BILATERAL RELATIONS. HE 
WARNED OF A TREND OF WORSENING RELATIONS BETWEEN THE U.S. 
AND RUSSIA, ADDING THAT THIS TENDENCY HAS BECOME RATHER 
OBVIOUS. 
6. _pc} THE SECRETARY RESPONDED BY NOTING THAT THIS WAS 
THE FIRST VISIT IN WHICH SHE HAD THE PLEASURE OF MEETING 
WITH LUZHKOV IN HIS HISTORIC OFFICE BUILDING. SHE ADDED 
THAT SHE HAD MORE PRESSING ISSUES ON THE AGENDA OF THIS 
TRIP THAN AT ANY PREVIOUS TIME. THE SECRETARY EMPHASIZED 
TO LUZHKOV THAT PRESIDENT CLINTON AND SHE WANTED TO KEEP 
RELATIONS WITH RUSSIA AT THE HIGHEST LEVEL OF FRIENDSHIP, 
SUPPORT, AND UNDERSTANDING. SHE ADDED THAT IT IS URGENT 
FOR HER TO TRY TO COUNTER THE REASONING THAT THE U.S. DOES 
NOT WANT TO BE SUPPORTIVE OF RUSSIA. THE U.S. AND RUSSIA 
ARE FRIENDS, BUT EVEN FRIENDS CAN HAVE DIFFERENCES.

LUZHKOV'S FRANK TALK ON "UNSATISFACTORY" ISSUES 
7. -0* RECOGNIZING THE SECRETARY'S FRANK AND DIRECT 
STYLE, THE MOSCOW MAYOR NOTED THE IMPORTANCE OF NOT 
LETTING THE PRESENT TENDENCIES MOVE U.S.—RUSSIAN RELATIONS 
IN AN UNDESIRABLE DIRECTION. HE ACKNOWLEDGED THAT HE IS 
NOT A DIPLOMAT AND ALSO BELIEVES IN FRANK DISCUSSION AND 
PROCEEDED TO LIST A NUMBER OF ISSUES HE CITED AS 
"UNSATISFACTORY" IN THE RELATIONSHIP, MANY OF WHICH, HE 
MAINTAINED, REPRESENT FLASHPOINTS IN THE WORLD TODAY. 
8. 444 U.S. "DIKTAT". NOTING THE INTERNATIONAL ORDER 
THAT HAS PREVAILED SINCE 1945 TO RESOLVE INTERNATIONAL 
DISPUTES, LUZHKOV ARGUED THAT THE UNITED NATIONS IS 
BEGINNING TO LOSE ITS AUTHORITY BECAUSE OF THE U.S. HE 
CHARGED THAT WORLD ORDER IS NOW DICTATED BY ONLY ONE SIDE 
AND CITED THE FOLLOWING EXAMPLES: 
-- IRAQ. LUZHKOV INSISTED THAT IT WOULD BE A MISTAKE FOR 
THE U.S. TO BELIEVE THAT RUSSIA LIKES SADDAM HUSSEIN. 
AGREEING THAT HUSSEIN IS PARANOID AND DISPLAYS "SCHIZOID" 
TENDENCIES, LUZHKOV ARGUED THAT A TOUGH LINE IS NEEDED 
AGAINST HIM FOR THE OBJECTIVES OF THE U.S. AND OTHER 
COUNTRIES TO BE MET. HOWEVER, THE ACTION SHOULD BE A 
MATTER FOR THE U.N. TO DECIDE, AND NOT THE ARBITRARY 
ACTION OF ONE SIDE. HE SUGGESTED THAT WHILE OPERATION 
"DESERT FOX" MAY HAVE BEEN SUCCESSFUL, IT HAD UNEXPECTED 
RESULTS IN THAT IT ACTUALLY HELPED STRENGTHEN SADDAM AND 
CONSOLIDATE THE ARAB WORLD AGAINST THE UNITED STATES. 
-- DOUBLE—STANDARDS. CLOSELY RELATED ARE A NUMBER OF 
DIFFERENT DECISIONS THE U.S. HAS MADE REGARDING THE USE OF 
FORCE -- IN KOSOVO, BOSNIA, AND OTHER COUNTRIES. SUCH 
DECISIONS AND ACTIONS AGAINST SMALL NATIONS TAKEN OUTSIDE 
OF THE INTERNATIONAL FORA ARE NOT RIGHT. WITHOUT GOING 
INTO FURTHER DETAIL, LUZHKOV MENTIONED TURKEY, CYPRUS, 
SPAIN, FRANCE, CORSICA AND THE U.K. AS OFFERING ADDITIONAL 
EXAMPI1ES OF TARGETS OF U.S. ACTION. 
9. 41:1* LUZHKOV EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT U.S. 
ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION AGAINST THE WORK OF RUSSIAN RESEARCH 
AND DESIGN BUREAUS. HE ARGUED THAT THE U.S. MEASURES ARE 
NOT BASED ON REALITIES AND NOTED THAT HE HAD SENT A LETTER 
TO AMBASSADOR COLLINS REQUESTING AN OPPORTUNITY TO REVIEW 
THE SITUATION. LUZHKOV ASSERTED THAT THE U.S. HAD 
PARTICIPATED IN THE BUILDING OF AN ATOMIC POWER PLANT IN 
IRAN TEN YEARS AGO UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE IAEA. 
LUZHKOV ARGUED THAT RUSSIA DOES NOT ALLOW PROLIFERATION OR 
COOPERATION BEYOND THE LIMITS ALLOWED BY THE IAEA AND 
SUGGESTED THAT U.S. ACTIONS MAY BE AIMED INSTEAD AT

SECRETARY'S RESPONSE ON PARTNERSHIP GOALS 
13. THE SECRETARY THANKED LUZHKOV FOR HIS FRANKNESS, 
AGREEING THAT POLITE TALK DOES NO ONE ANY GOOD. SHE 
STRESSED TO HIM THAT HER VISIT IS IMPORTANT BECAUSE OF THE 
NEED TO OVERCOME SO MANY MISUNDERSTANDINGS. THE SECRETARY 
REITERATED PRESIDENT CLINTON'S AND HER OWN DETERMINATION 
TO MAINTAIN GOOD, FRIENDLY RELATIONS WITH RUSSIA. SHE 
NOTED THAT, AFTER 50 YEARS AS ENEMIES, IT IS A DIFFICULT 
JOB AND REQUIRES CHANGE IN POLITICAL CULTURE AS WELL AS 
THINKING. THE SECRETARY NOTED, HOWEVER, THAT EVEN AS 
ENEMIES THE TWO COUNTRIES MANAGED TO WORK OUT AN ARMS 
CONTROL REGIME THAT IS GOOD FOR BOTH COUNTRIES. 
14. 444-CONTINUING, THE SECRETARY EXPLAINED THAT SINCE 
THE END OF THE COLD WAR THE U.S. HAS CONSISTENTLY TRIED TO 
FOCUS ITS ATTENTION ON GLOBAL THREATS AFFECTING EVERY 
COUNTRY. SHE NOTED THAT THE TWO BIGGEST PROBLEMS ARE THE 
PROLIFERATION OF WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION AND 
TERRORISM. BOTH THREATEN RUSSIA AS MUCH AS THEY THREATEN 
THE U.S. THE SECRETARY EMPHASIZED THAT THE U.S. EXPECTS 
RUSSIA TO HELP RESOLVE SUCH PROBLEMS IN THE 21ST CENTURY 
AND NEEDS A STRONG RUSSIA AS A PARTNER. THE U.S. HAS NO 
DESIRE TO HAVE A WEAK RUSSIA AS A PARTNER.

WANT TO RUN THE WORLD THEMSELVES. THE U.S. NEEDS PARTNERS

16. fe}-THE SECRETARY RESPONDED TO THE SPECIFIC ISSUES 
LUZHKOV RAISED AS FOLLOWS: 
-- IRAQ. THE SECRETARY AGREED WITH LUZHKOV/S 
CHARACTERIZATION OF SADDAM HUSSEIN, ADDING THAT HE IS A 
THREAT TO HIS OWN PEOPLE AND TO HIS NEIGHBORS. HE HAS 
USED HIS WEAPONS AGAINST HIS OWN PEOPLE AND IS DETERMINED 
TO ACQUIRE THE MEANS TO DEVELOP WEAPONS OF MASS 
DESTRUCTION. EMPHASIZING THAT THE U.S. AND RUSSIA NOW 
HAVE AN OPPORTUNITY TO WORK TOGETHER, THE SECRETARY TOLD 
LUZHKOV THAT SHE HAD DISCUSSED WITH FOREIGN MINISTER 
IVANOV A COOPERATIVE PROPOSAL TO WORK WITHIN THE U.N. 
SECURITY COUNCIL TO ESTABLISH AN ESCROW FUND FOR IRAQI OIL 
SALE FUNDS TO BE DIRECTED TO HELP ITS PEOPLE AND TO HELP 
REPAY IRAQIS DEBTS. 
"DESERT FOX." REGARDING "DESERT FOX," THE SECRETARY 
TOLD LUZHKOV THAT THE U.S. DID, IN FACT, ACHIEVE ITS GOALS 
-- IT DEGRADED SADDAWS ABILITY TO RECONSTITUTE HIS 
WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION AND SUCCEEDED IN ISOLATING HIM 
IN THE ARAB WORLD. SHE NOTED THAT SADDAM HAD INSULTED 
ARAB COUNTRIES OUT OF DESPERATION BY CALLING FOR THE 
POPULAR OVERTHROW OF ARAB LEADERS. SHE ADDED THAT THE DAY 
BEFORE, JANUARY 24, AT AN ARAB LEAGUE MEETING, IRAQ FAILED 
IN ITS EFFORTS TO GAIN SUPPORT AGAINST THE U.S. AND WALKED 
OUT.

OUT OF YUGOSLAVIA. SHE EXPRESSED CONFIDENCE THAT, BY 
WORKING TOGETHER, THE U.S. AND RUSSIA CAN SUCCEED IN 
GETTING ALL THE PARTIES TO THE TABLE. ON THE QUESTION 
LUZHKOV RAISED REGARDING SMALL NATIONS, THE SECRETARY DID 
NOT AGREE THAT THERE ARE SERIOUS PROBLEMS TO BE ADDRESSED. 
-- ARM. THE SECRETARY EXPLAINED TO LUZHKOV THAT THE ARM
