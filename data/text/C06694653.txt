2. FOLLOWING IS PROPOSED TEXT OF CABLE COVERING MEETING OF 
DEPUTY SECRETARIES TALBOTT AND DEUTCH WITH DOMINICAN PRESIDENT 
BALAGUER. 
3. BEGIN SUMMARY. ON AUGUST 30, A DELEGATION LED BY DEPUTY 
SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT AND DEPUTY SECRETARY OF DEFENSE 
JOHN DEUTCH MET FOR ONE HOUR WITH DOMINICAN PRESIDENT BALAGUER. 
TALBOTT TOLD BALAGUER THAT THERE IS NO LONGER ANY ALTERNATIVE TO 
MILITARY INTERVENTION IN HAITI. THE ONLY REMAINING QUESTION IS 
THE CIRCUMSTANCES OF THE INTERVENTION. IT CAN BE A VIOLENT 
CONFRONTATION IF GENERAL CEDRAS AND HIS SUPPORTERS TRY TO RETAIN 
POWER OR A PEACEFUL ENTRY IF THEY LEAVE VOLUNTARILY. BALAGUER 
AND THE DOMINICAN GOVERNMENT COULD HELP SHAPE WHICH SCENARIO 
UNFOLDS, TALBOTT SAID. HE ASKED THAT THE GODR CONVINCE CEDRAS OF 
THE INEVITABILITY OF A MILITARY INTERVENTION AND INCREASE THE 
PRESSURE ON THE HAITIAN MILITARY BY SIGNIFICANTLY STRENGTHENING 
THE ECONOMIC EMBARGO. 
4. BALAGUER AGREED TO BOTH REQUESTS, BUT SUGGESTED THE WARNING 
TO CEDRAS WOULD BE MORE EFFECTIVE IF IT CAME FROM A RANKING U.S. 
OFFICIAL. BALAGUER SAID CEDRAS STILL BELIEVES A MILITARY 
INTERVENTION IS UNLIKELY. REGARDING BORDER SMUGGLING, BALAGUER 
SOUGHT UNDERSTANDING FOR THE DIFFICULT CIRCUMSTANCES CONFRONTING 
BORDER COMMUNITIES AND THE MILITARY MEMBERS TASKED WITH 
IMPLEMENTING THE EMBARGO. 
5. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT ALSO STRESSED THAT DEMOCRACY IN THE 
DOMINICAN REPUBLIC IS JUST AS IMPORTANT AS IT IS IN HAITI. HE 
REITERATED OTHER US CALLS FOR FULL IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PACT FOR 
DEMOCRACY. BALAGUER OFFERED HIS ASSURANCES THAT THIS IS 
OCCURRING. END SUMMARY. 
6. THE U.S. DELEGATION TO THE MEETING WAS COMPRISED OF DEPUTY 
SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT, DEPUTY SECRETARY OF DEFENSE 
JOHN DEUTCH, NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR TO THE VICE PRESIDENT LEON 
FUERTH, U.S. AMBASSADOR TO THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC DONNA HRINAK, 
USACOM J-5 MG MICHAEL BYRON, MULTINATIONAL OBSERVER GROUP (MOG) 
COMMANDER COL. WILLIAM MCDONOUGH, D-SPECIAL ASSISTANT DEBBI 
SCHWARTZ, EMBASSY POLITICAL COUNSELOR DENNIS LINSKEY (NOTETAKER), 
AND STATE DEPARTMENT INTERPRETER GUILLERMO BASERVA. THE 
DOMINICAN DELEGATION WAS COMPRISED OF PRESIDENT JOAQUIN BALAGUER, 
FOREIGN SECRETARY CARLOS MORALES TRONCOSO, SECRETARY TO THE 
PRESIDENT DR. RAPHAEL BELLO ANDINO, SECRETARY OF THE ARMED FORCE 
LT. GEN. CONSTANTINO MATOS VILLANUEVA, DOMINICAN AMBASSADOR TO 
THE U.S. JOSE CARMEN ARIZA, AND MILITARY AIDE TO THE PRESIDENT 
MAJ. GEN. LUIS PEREZ BELLO. THE MEET/NG OCCURRED IN BALAGUER'S 
PRIVATE OFFICE.

7. AFTER INTRODUCTORY COMMENTS BY BOTH SIDES AND A BRIEF REVIEW 
OF THE CARICOM MEETING IN KINGSTON, UNDERSECRETARY TALBOTT 
INFORMED BALAGUER THAT THERE WILL BE A MULTINATIONAL DEPLOYMENT 
IN HAITI. HE STRESSED THIS NOT A THREAT BUT A FACT. TALBOTT 
REPORTED THAT IN HIS MEETING EARLIER THAT DAY IN KINGSTON THAT A 
NUMBER OF CARIBBEAN GOVERNMENTS COMMITTED THEMSELVES TO JOIN WITH 
THE UNITED STATES, THE UNITED KINGDOM AND ARGENTINA TO 
PARTICIPATE IN THE DEPLOYMENT. UNDERSECRETARY DEUTCH REINFORCED 
THESE REMARKS BY NOTING THAT PLANNING FOR THE INTERVENTION IS NOW 
COMPLETE. HE ADDED THAT THE FORCE USED WILL BE MASSIVE AND WILL 
STRIVE TO ACHIEVE ITS OBJECTIVES RAPIDLY WHILE HOLDING 
CAUSALITIES TO A MINIMUM. 
8. TALBOTT ADDED THAT THE ONLY REMAINING QUESTION WAS WHETHER 
THIS FORCE WOULD DEPLOY IN A HOSTILE ENVIRONMENT OR A PASSIVE ONE 
FOLLOWING THE VOLUNTARY DEPARTURE OF THE MILITARY LEADERSHIP. - 
HOWEVER, IRRESPECTIVE OF THE ENVIRONMENT, AN INTERVENTION WOULD 
OCCUR.
