While it pains me to add to what must be a horrendous amount of 
mail that you receive, I am, nonetheless, writing further to my 
letter to you of January 26 to update you on the Russian Officer 
Fellowship Programs of American Universities. We believe this 
program is a natural to be included in the Clinton-Yeltsin summit 
package. 
The goal is to place large numbers of middle level Russian 
officers in a situation in which they can focus on the role of 
the military in a modern society, including democratic 
institutions, free markets, business and financial practices, 
procurement and management, and the impact of new technology. 
Enclosed are background papers on the subject.. 
o A one page program summary: Russian Officer Fellowship 
Program of American Universities 
o A list of the Board of Directors of Intermilitary, Inc. (to 
be renamed International Military Fellows, Inc.), a 
not-for-profit corporation which will run the program. 
o A memo on the Status of Contacts_ 
o A memo on budget,
