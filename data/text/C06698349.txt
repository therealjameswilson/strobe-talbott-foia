FUNDAMENTAL CONCERN REGARDINGTHE GOT'S APPLICATION TO

SECRETARY SAID WHILE THE USG WOULD.NOT SECOND-GUESS THE 
GOT REGARDING THE DOMESTIC ASPECTS OF THE APPLICATION 
FOR RELIEF, THE USG WAS VERY CONCERNED ABOUT THE 
INTERNATIONAL ASPECTS OF THE APPLICATION. 
7. —46+ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THE USG WAS 
ESPECIALLY CONCERNED HOW WASHINGTON AND THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY WOULD RECEIVE THE APPLICATION. 
HE EXPRESSED HOPE THAT THE GOT COULD ADOPT A FORMULA TO 
SEEK RELIEF THAT WOULD NOT CARRY THE DANGERS PRESENTED 
BY THE CURRENT APPLICATION. HE NOTED THAT OYMEN HAD 
HEARD THESE CONCERNS FROM ADAIR, BUT THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY CONSIDERED OYMEN A GOOD FRIEND AND 
COLLABORATOR AND WISHED TO REITERATE THE CONCERNS. 
8. OYMEN REPLIED THAT THE PRIME MINISTER HAD 
COMMITTED HIMSELF TO MAKING THE APPLICATION FOR RELIEF, 
I.E., IT WAS THE PRICE THE PRIME MINISTER PAID HIS PARTY 
MEMBERS, GIVEN THE PARTY'S OPPOSITION TO THE EXTENSION 
OF OPERATION PROVIDE COMFORT. OYMEN COMMENTED THAT IN 
EXPLAINING TO PARLIAMENT THE GOT'S DECISION TO EXTEND 
OPERATION PROVIDE COMFORT, THE PM HAD EMPHASIZED THAT 
CONDITIONS WERE DIFFERENT. 
9. -.+EH- OYMEN CONTINUED, STATING THE GOT'S BOTTOM LINE 
WAS THAT TURKEY SHOULD NOT BE TREATED LESS FAVORABLY 
THAN JORDAN. HE SAID THE GOT HAD INCLUDED LANGUAGE IN 
THE PROPOSED APPLICATION THAT MADE CLEAR THE APPLICATION 
WAS NOT AN ATTEMPT BY THE GOT TO VIOLATE SANCTIONS. 
OYMEN SAID FM CILLER HAD EMPHASIZED THAT THE APPLICATION 
DID NOT MEAN THE GOT WOULD IMPLEMENT ANYTHING WITHOUT 
CONSULTING WITH THE USG AND ITS OTHER UN PARTNERS_ 
OYMEN TOLD THE DEPUTY SECRETARY HE CONTINUED TO SEEK 
COMMENTS AND SUGGESTIONS TO IMPROVE THE APPLICATION. 
10. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE HOPED AS 
CONSULTATIONS WENT FORWARD THE GOT WOULD FIND SOME WAY 
TO MEET ITS STATED OBJECTIVES WHILE AVOIDING THE 
PRINCIPAL PITFALLS IN THE CURRENT VERSION OF THE 
PROPOSED APPLICATION. HE EMPHASIZED THAT THE GOT SHOULD 
NOT UNDERESTIMATE WHAT THE GOT HAD ALREADY ACCOMPLISHED 
WITH THE EXTENSION OF OPERATION PROVIDE COMFORT AND THE 
PROGRESS THAT HAD BEEN MADE ON THE TEXT IN THE PREVIOUS 
SEVERAL HOURS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REITERATED THE 
DANGER THAT THE APPLICATION COULD BE MISINTERPRETED BY 
THE UN AND IN WASHINGTON AND PROMISED TO FOLLOW IT VERY 
CLOSELY. 
11. OYMEN THEN REQUESTED THE USG MAKE SOME 
POSITIVE STATEMENT REGARDING THE APPLICATION, E.G., THAT 
THE GOT HAD CONSULTED WITH THE USG PRIOR TO SUBMITTING 
THE APPLICATION, THE APPLICATION WAS NOT A VIOLATION OF 
THE SANCTIONS REGIME, AND IT SHOULD BE CONSIDERED BY THE 
UN. OYMEN AGREED THE GOT AND THE USG NEEDED TO 
ANTICIPATE ANY MISINTERPRETATION OF THE APPLICATION. 
12. (-84- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID ONE WAY TO AVOID ANY 
MISINTERPRETATION WAS TO CONSULT FURTHER BEFORE 
SUBMITTING THE APPLICATION. OYMEN REPLIED HE WAS READY 
TO HEAR FURTHER SUGGESTIONS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
WELCOMED OYMEN'S WILLINGNESS TO CONSULT FURTHER AND 
PROMISED THE USG WOULD PROVIDE ADDITIONAL COMMENTS
