4. G91 THE ACTING SECRETARY EXPLAINED HE WAS CALLING 
CONCERNING A MATTER OF GREAT IMPORTANCE. IN THE 
INTEREST OF ACHIEVING A PEACEFUL RESOLUTION TO THE 
CRISIS IN NAGORNO—KARABAKH, PRESIDENT CLINTON DECIDED 
U.S. DIPLOMATIC ENGAGEMENT WAS ESSENTIAL. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY EXPRESSED HIS UNDERSTANDING OF FRENCH 
SENSITIVITIES, BUT STATED U.S. CO—CHAIRMANSHIP OF THE 
MINSK GROUP WAS VITAL TO ITS SUCCESS. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY ACKNOWLEDGED FM COTTI'S SUGGESTION IN HIS 
DECEMBER 21 LETTER THAT THE U.S. CONTACT THE FRENCH 
DIRECTLY TO RESOLVE ANY DIFFERENCES, AND EXPLAINED THAT 
THE UNITED STATES IS DOING EVERYTHING POSSIBLE TO WORK 
WITH THE FRENCH GOVERNMENT. 
5. S,S,) THE ACTING SECRETARY STRESSED U.S. 
CO—CHAIRMANSHIP OF THE MINSK GROUP MAKES SENSE FOR TWO 
REASONS. FIRST, THE UNITED STATES HAS THE GREATEST 
FAMILIARITY WITH THE ISSUE. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
MENTIONED HIS OWN KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING OF THE KEY 
PLAYERS IN THE REGION AND THE FREQUENT TRIPS OF 
HIGH—LEVEL U.S. OFFICIALS TO THE REGION. SECOND, THE 
UNITED STATES COULD DEAL WITH RUSSIA AS AN EQUAL. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY EMPHASIZED THE RELEVANCE OF THIS FACTOR 
BECAUSE MANY OF THE PARTICIPANTS IN THE MINSK GROUP HAVE 
A CERTAIN AMOUNT OF MISTRUST FOR THE RUSSIANS.

8. (X) THE ACTING SECRETARY INDICATED HE WAS ACUTELY 
AWARE OF SWITZERLAND'S SENSITIVE POSITION AND PROPOSED 
TWO POINTS FOR FM COTTI'S CONSIDERATION. FIRST, RATHER 
THAN FOCUS ON THE EVENTS OF THE RECENT PAST, THE ACTING 
SECRETARY URGED FM COTTI TO THINK OF THE FUTURE. 
PRESIDENT CLINTON DETERMINED IT WAS ESSENTIAL FOR PEACE 
IN RUSSIA AND THE FORMER SOVIET REGION THAT 
NAGORNO-KARABAKH NOT BOIL OVER AGAIN. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY STATED THERE IS NO ALTERNATIVE TO U.S. 
ENGAGEMENT. SECOND, THE ACTING SECRETARY OFFERED FM 
COTTI ANOTHER OPTION FOR DEALING WITH FRENCH CONCERNS. 
THE SWISS GOVERNMENT COULD TAKE NO DECISION AT THIS 
TIME, AND LEAVE THE CHOICE TO THE DANES ONCE DENMARK 
ASSUMED THE CHAIRMANSHIP OF THE OSCE. 
9 (Z1 THE ACTING SECRETARY THANKED FM COTTI FOR

CALLING NOT ONLY AS ACTING SECRETARY OF STATE, BUT ON 
BEHALF OF PRESIDENT CLINTON, WHO HAS EXPENDED GREAT 
PERSONAL EFFORT TO KEEP PEACE IN THE'REGION ON TRACK.

SUGGESTIONS SERIOUS THOUGHT WHEN CONSIDERING THE 
QUESTION FURTHER.
