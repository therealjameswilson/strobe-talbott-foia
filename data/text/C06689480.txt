June 15, 1993 
Dear Mr. Gutman: 
Thank you for your letter dated May 5 regarding the 
provision of humanitarian assistance for orphans and abandoned 
children in the Russian Federation. While the concept of 
bringing Russian children, particularly the orphaned and the 
abandoned, to the United States for a summer stay or 
participation in family camps is one of merit, the United 
States Government currently does not have funds to support this 
type of activity. 
I recommend that you contact several oraanizations which 
provide humanitarian assistance as well as financial support 
directly to orphanages, children's home and summer campS in 
Russia. You may find these groups a useful resource to your 
organization. Several of these organizations are affiliates of 
Jewish and Protestant denominations. The names of the 
organizations, telephone numbers and points of contact are 
included on the enclosed sheet for your use. 
Again, thank you for your letter. Providing Critically 
needed assistance requires the good will and interest of 
individual citizens and organizations. We applaud your efforts 
and interest in assisting needy children in the former Soviet 
Union. 
Sincerely, 
Ny 
trobe Talbott 
Ambassador At Large and 
Special Advisor to The Secretary 
On the New Independent States 
Mr. Mikhail Gutman, 
Executive Director,

Illinois Chapter.
