2. SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT REVIEWED IN A 
PRIVATE SESSION WITH THE NAC AMBASSADORS APRIL 13 THE 
RESULTS OF HIS JUST-COMPLETED VISIT TO INDIA, PAKISTAN, 
POLAND AND SLOVAKIA. PERMREPS WERE PARTICULARLY 
INTERESTED IN TALBOTT'S DESCRIPTION OF INDIA-PAKISTAN 
RELATIONS IN THE POST-COLD-WAR ENVIRONMENT, ESPECIALLY IN 
THE CONTEXT OF U.S. EFFORTS TO CRAFT AN APPROACH TO 
NUCLEAR NONPROLIFERATION ON THE SUBCONTINENT. THE 
QUESTION OF RUSSIA'S ROLE AND INTENTIONS WAS AT THE 
FOREFRONT OF CONCERNS IN THE COURSE OF A NUMBER OF 
EXCHANGES ON CENTRAL AND EASTERN EUROPE. TALBOTT HELD 
THAT RUSSIA HAS NOT YET CROSSED THE "RED LINE" THAT WOULD 
MAKE ITS INCLUSION IN THE LARGER EUROPEAN CONCERT 
IMPOSSIBLE, BUT NOTED THAT SOME IN THE REGION, SUCH AS 
'WALESA, BELIEVE OTHERWISE. END SUMMARY.
