Thank you so much for your June 16 letter about the 
Inaugural Meeting of "The International Committee for 
Economic Reform and Cooperation in Kiev on June 7th and 
8th. 
I had very much hoped to stop by and say a few words 
of support, since I was in Kiev with Secretary Aspin that 
day. However, our negotiations with the Ukrainians on a 
variety of difficult and important security matters ended 
up leaving no time for me to make an appearance at your 
session. I am glad that our Charge, Shaun Byrnes, was 
able to attend, and I gather he conveyed my sentiments of 
greetings and support. 
I admire the important work that you are doing, and I 
wish you all the best in this very promising and valuable 
effort. 
Sincerely,
