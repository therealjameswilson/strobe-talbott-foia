1. (-cl) SUMMARY. IN A MARCH 4 BILATERAL MEETING PRIOR TO 
THE OPENING OF THE BARENTS EURO-ARCTIC COUNCIL, THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY PERSUADED THE NORWEGIAN FOREIGN MINISTER 
TO AGREE TO A SIGNING, RATHER THAN A MERE ADOPTION, OF A 
DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES ON A MULTILATERAL NUCLEAR 
ENVIRONMENTAL PROGRAM IN RUSSIA. VOLLEBAEK ALSO AGREED 
TO HELP BREAK AN IMPASSE WITH THE RUSSIAN DELEGATION OVER 
THE TEXT OF THE DECLARATION. (THE NEXT DAY THE 
DECLARATION WAS SIGNED.) ON KOSOVO, VOLLEBAEK DESCRIBED 
HIS LATEST ENCOUNTER WITH A DEFIANT MILOSEVIC. TALBOTT 
AND VOLLEBAEK AGREED ON THE ESSENTIAL POINTS FOR NEXT 
STEPS--GETTING THE KOSOVO ALBANIANS TO AGREE TO THE 
RAMBOUILLET AGREEMENT, AND DELIVERING A STRONG JOINT 
MESSAGE TO MILOSEVIC WHICH IS BACKED UP BY THE REAL 
THREAT OF FORCE. TALBOTT WARNED THAT IF WE LET IT BECOME 
A PURELY DIPLOMATIC EXERCISE, THEN MILOSEVIC KNOWS HE 
WILL WIN. THE PARADOX IS THIS WOULD LEAD TO EVEN MORE 
DISASTROUS MILITARY CONSEQUENCES FOR EVERYONE DOWN THE 
ROAD. IT WOULD LEAD TO MORE DEATHS AND A VICIOUS CYCLE 
WITH WAR SPREADING, AND THEN WE WOULD BE IN IT BIG TIME, 
MUCH BIGGER THAN WE ARE NOW. END SUMMARY.

MINISTER KNUT VOLLEBAEK ON MARCH 4 AT 4:30 P.M. FOR 
APPROXIMATELY 40 MINUTES AT THE RADISSON SAS HOTEL IN 
BODOE, NORWAY. VOLLEBAEK WAS HOST OF THE SIXTH 
MINISTERIAL MEETING OF THE BARENTS EURO-ARCTIC COUNCIL. 
IN THE BILATERAL MEETING, TALBOTT CONVEYED THE 
SECRETARY'S BEST WISHES AND REGRET AT NOT BEING ABLE TO 
ATTEND THE MINISTERIAL AND NOTED HER INTEREST IN 
VOLLEBAEK'S ROLE AS CHAIRMAN IN OFFICE (CIO) OF THE OSCE 
AND AS A NATO FOREIGN MINISTER. THE FIRST TOPIC 
DISCUSSED BEFORE MOVING ON TO KOSOVO, HOWEVER, CONCERNED 
ONE OF THE "DELIVERABLES" FOR THE BARENTS MINISTERIAL. 
DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES 
4. 4E4 BACKGROUND: THE COMMITTEE OF SENIOR OFFICIALS 
(CSO) OF THE BARENTS EURO-ARCTIC COUNCIL (BEAC) HAD MET 
ON MARCH 3 AND 4 TO PREPARE DOCUMENTS FOR THE BEAC 
MINISTERIAL. THEY WERE ABLE FINALLY TO AGREE ON THE TEXT 
OF THE COMMUNIQUE BUT GOT STUCK ON THE "DECLARATION OF 
PRINCIPLES REGARDING A MULTILATERAL NUCLEAR ENVIRONMENTAL 
PROGRAMME IN THE RUSSIAN FEDERATION." ONE OF THE GOALS 
OF THE U.S. AT THE BEAC MINISTERIAL WAS TO SIGN THIS 
DECLARATION WHICH HAD BEEN THE SUBJECT OF MONTHS OF 
MULTILATERAL NEGOTIATIONS. ONE OF OUR NORWEGIAN 
INTERLOCUTORS IN THE CSO INFORMED US ON THE SIDELINES 
THAT FOREIGN MINISTER VOLLEBAEK HAD INSTRUCTED THE 
NORWEGIAN DELEGATION THAT "ADOPTING" RATHER THAN 
"SIGNING" THE DECLARATION WAS SUFFICIENT FOR THE GON. 
SUCH AN "ADOPTION" WOULD HAVE HAD LITTLE MEANING FOR THE 
U.S. BECAUSE, AMONG OTHER THINGS, THE U.S. IS ONLY AN. 
OBSERVER IN THE BEAC. ANOTHER STUMBLING BLOCK WAS THAT 
THE RUSSIAN NEGOTIATORS IN THE CSO HAD BACKED AWAY FROM 
THEIR EARLIER AGREEMENT ON THE TEXT OF THE DECLARATION 
AND WERE INSISTING ON INCLUSION OF THE PHRASE "IN 
ACCORDANCE WITH RUSSIAN LAW" IN THE TEXT, WHICH WAS 
TOTALLY UNACCEPTABLE TO THE. U.S. END BACKGROUND. 
'5. -E-C—)- THE FIRST ISSUE RAISE DEPUTY SECRETARY RAISED 
WITH FOREIGN MINISTER VOLLEBAEK WAS THE SNAG IN THE 
DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES, THE CENTERPIECE OF THE BEAC 
MINISTERIAL, AND WHETHER TO SIGN OR ADOPT IT. CAROL 
KESSLER FURTHER DISCUSSED THE ISSUE, INCLUDING THE 
RUSSIAN INTRANSIGENCE ON THE WORDING OF THE TEXT. 
TALBOTT ADVISED OUR STRONG PREFERENCE THAT THE NORWEGIAN 
SIDE TO BE PREPARED TO SIGN RATHER THAN MERELY ADOPT THE 
DECLARATION. VOLLEBAEK AGREED TO THIS REQUEST AND SAID 
HE WOULD PUSH FOR A "SIGNING" OF THE DECLARATION. IN 
ADDITION, HE OFFERED TO HAVE A WORD WITH RUSSIAN DEPUTY 
' FOREIGN MINISTER SREDIN LATER THAT EVENING AND WOULD SEEK

KOSOVO 
7. 4Q4- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND VOLLEBAEK HAD A QUICKLY 
PACED EXCHANGE ON THE MOST RECENT DEVELOPMENTS IN KOSOVO. 
AS THE CHAIRMAN IN OFFICE (CIO) OF THE OSCE, VOLLEBAEK 
DESCRIBED HIS MOST RECENT MEETING WITH A VERY DEFIANT 
(AND SARCASTIC) MILOSEVIC WHO BRAGGED ABOUT STANDING UP 
TO STALIN, BREZHNEV AND NOW VOLLEBAEK, AND ENDED THEIR 
MEETING SAYING It() COME AGAIN, SO NICE TO TALK TO YOU." 
MILOSEVIC SAID HE "WANTED TO HELP" VOLLEBAEK. THE LATTER 
DESCRIBED THE SETTING AS HAVING THE APPEARANCE OF "A 
THIRD-RATE MAFIA MOVIE." MILOSEVIC HAD DELIVERED A FLAT 
"NO" TO THE POSSIBILITY OF HAVING AN INTERNATIONAL 
MILITARY PRESENCE IN KOSOVO. VOLLEBAEK SAID MILOSEVIC 
KNEW THERE WAS A DIVISION IN THE CONTACT GROUP. 
8. 4,a)-- VOLLEBAEK SAID IT WAS DIFFICULT TO PUT PRESSURE 
ON MILOSEVIC AS LONG AS THE KOSOVO ALBANIANS HAD NOT 
SIGNED ON TO THE RAMBOUILLET AGREEMENT. IN THE MEANTIME, 
VOLLEBAEK SAID THE MEETINGS WITH THE KOSOVO ALBANIANS HAD 
ALL BEEN POSITIVE AND EXPRESSED SOME OPTIMISM IN GETTING 
THEM ON BOARD. HE ALSO SAID THE SERB OPPOSITION IN 
KOSOVO HAD ALSO BEEN POSITIVE ON THE AGREEMENT. 
9. *G4- IN CONCLUSION, VOLLEBAEK SAID THE MAIN THING IS 
TO GET THE KOSOVO ALBANIANS ON BOARD THE RAMBOUILLET 
AGREEMENT AS SOON AS POSSIBLE AND TELL MILOSEVIC HE HAS 
TO ACCEPT IT AS A PACKAGE. HE THOUGHT MILOSEVIC WOULD 
DRAG IT OUT AS LONG AS HE CAN, BUT HE WAS NOT SURE HE 
WOULD RISK A STRIKE. 
10. 444 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED HIS APPRECIATION 
FOR VOLLEBAEK'S ACCOUNT AND TOTALLY AGREED WITH HIS 
BOTTOM LINE. HE INTRODUCED PHIL GOLDBERG AND DIRECTED 
THAT HE BE IN DIRECT CONTACT WITH VOLLEBAEK'S COUNTERPART 
AIDE ON KOSOVO MATTERS, JORG WILLY BRONNEBAKK, BECAUSE 
EVENTS WERE MOVING SO FAST. REFERRING TO SECRETARY 
ALBRIGHT'S STRONG VIEWS ON WHAT HAS TO BE DONE, TALBOTT 
OUTLINED A COURSE OF ACTION OVER THE NEXT DAYS IN ORDER 
TO DELIVER A STRONG JOINT MESSAGE TO MILOSEVIC WHICH IS 
BACKED UP BY THE REAL THREAT OF FORCE. HE SUMMED UP THE 
ESSENCE OF THE KOSOVO MATTER AS REQUIRING A "YES" FROM 
THE KOSOVO ALBANIANS AND USING THAT TOGETHER WITH THE 
THREAT OF THE "ACTORD" TO EXTRACT A "YES" FROM MILOSEVIC.

11. 4119 HE NOTED THE PRESIDENT AND THE SECRETARY HAD 
ASKED BOB DOLE TO GO TO PRISTINA, BUT THAT HE WAS HAVING 
A VISA PROBLEM WITH BELGRADE. VOLLEBAEK OFFERED TO CALL

12. 444 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND NORWEGIAN FOREIGN 
MINISTER ALSO DISCUSSED THE POSSIBILITY OF HAVING A 
SUCCESSION OF VISITS (GERMAN FOREIGN MINISTER JOSCHKA 
FISCHER, U.S. SPECIAL ENVOY RICHARD HOLBROOKE, AND THE 
CHAIRMAN OF THE CONTACT GROUP) TO BELGRADE IN THE COMING 
DAYS TO DELIVER A JOINT MESSAGE AND PUT THE PRESSURE ON 
MILOSEVIC AND GET HIM INTO COMPLIANCE. THERE WAS CONCERN 
ABOUT RECENT BORDER ACTIVITY AND THE POSSIBILITY OF A 
LAUNCH OF NEW AGRESSION BY THE SERBS AGAINST THE 
ALBANIANS. 
13.  TALBOTT AND VOLLEBAEK AGREED ON THE NEED FOR A 
UNITED FRONT. THEY ALSO DISCUSSED THE NEED TO REMOVE THE 
KOSOVO VERIFICATION MISSION (KVM) IN ADVANCE OF ANY 
BOMBING. VOLLEBAEK SUGGESTED WORKING ON THE ITALIANS WHO 
WERE SOMEWHAT HESITANT AND DOUBTFUL. TALBOTT SAID HE 
WOULD TALK TO FOREIGN MINISTER DINI AT HIS NEXT STOP IN 
ROME. VOLLEBAEK THOUGHT IN ORDER TO GET PEOPLE ON BOARD 
IT MIGHT NOT BE A BAD IDEA FOR DINI TO GO SEE FOR HIMSELF 
THE SITUATION. 
DIPLOMATIC VS MILITARY SOLUTION 
14. 444—IN CLOSING, TALBOTT RESPONDED TO A POINT 
VOLLEBAEK HAD MADE CONCERNING THE POLITICAL AND MILITARY 
ASPECTS OF THE KOSOVO SITUTATION. VOLLEBAEK SUGGESTED 
THAT EVEN THOUGH THERE WILL BE MILITARY CONSEQUENCES, IT 
SHOULD BE DECIDED AT THE POLITICAL LEVEL, NOT BY THE 
MILITARY. TALBOTT REPLIED, AS HE SAID THE SECRETARY 
WOULD, "YES, BUT." HE WARNED THAT IF WE LET IT BECOME A 
PURELY DIPLOMATIC EXERCISE, THEN MILOSEVIC KNOWS HE WILL 
WIN. THE PARADOX IS THIS WOULD LEAD TO EVEN MORE 
DISASTROUS MILITARY CONSEQUENCES FOR EVERYONE DOWN THE 
ROAD. IT WOULD LEAD TO MORE DEATHS AND A VICIOUS CYCLE 
WITH WAR SPREADING, AND THEN WE WOULD BE IN IT BIG TIME, 
MUCH BIGGER THAN WE ARE NOW. VOLLEBAEK APPEARED 
CONVINCED BY TALBOTT'S THESIS AND SAID HE AGREED. HE 
NOTED BRONNEBAKK AND GOLDBERG WOULD STAY IN TOUCH. 
15. (U) THIS CABLE HAS BEEN CLEARED BY THE OFFICE OF THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY.
