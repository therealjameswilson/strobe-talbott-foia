2. SUMMARY. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND DEPUTY 
NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR SANDY BERGER MET WITH A RANGE 
OF PARLIAMENTARIANS AND POLITICAL LEADERS DECEMBER 6 TO - 
DISCUSS LEGISLATIVE ELECTIONS IN HAITI. AMBASSADOR 
SWING, AMBASSADOR DOBBINS, MR. BEERS AND MR. PLEBANI ALSO 
ATTENDED. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED, AND MR. BERGER 
CONCURRED, THAT ELECTIONS MUST BE HELD AS SOON AS 
POSSIBLE, AND THAT STEPS NECESSARY TO THAT END, INCLUDI14G 
FORMATION OF AN ELECTORAL COUNCIL AND SETTING A DATE, 
MUST OCCUR IN THE IMMEDIATE FUTURE. THE PARLIAMENTARIANS 
WERE RECEPTIVE, INFORMING THE VISITORS THAT THEY HAD MET 
THE DAY BEFORE WITH PRESIDENT ARISTIDE ON THE ISSUE AND 
EXPECTED TO SEE HIM AGAIN THAT AFTERNOON. SEVERAL, 
HOWEVER, WARNED OF GRAVE CONCERNS ABOUT SECURITY FOR THE 
ELECTIONS, SUGGESTING THAT LAVALAS CONTENDERS ARE 
INTIMIDATING AND THREATENING THE OPPOSITION. END SUMMARY. 
THE MESSAGE: "ELECTIONS, ELECTIONS, ELECTIONS!" 
3. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OPENED THE MEETING BY SAYING HE 
HAD COME TO DELIVER ONE MESSAGE: "ELECTIONS, ELECTIONS, 
ELECTIONS" -- PREFERABLY IN JANUARY 1995. STRESSING THAT 
DEMOCRACY IS INSTITUTIONS AND PROCESS, NOT ONE MAN, HE 
EXPRESSED PRIDE AND OPTIMISM FOR HAITI'S FUTURE. HE --
ADDED, HOWEVER, THAT HE COULD BE MORE OPTIMISTIC WHEN 
THERE WAS AN ELECTORAL COUNCIL IN PLACE AND A DATE FOR 
ELECTIONS SET, LAYING EMPHASIS ON THE DOMESTIC POLITICAL 
NECESSITY FOR PROGRESS, WITH A NEW CONGRESS TAKING ITS 
SEAT JANUARY 4, AND ON THE ECONOMIC REQUIREMENT FOR 
DEMOCRATIC STABILITY IN ORDER TO ATTRACT DESPERATELY 
NEEDED INVESTMENT TO HAITI. 
4. DEPUTY NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR BERGER AGREED WITH 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MESSAGE, ADDING COMPLIMENTS FOR 
PARLIAMENT ON ITS EFFORTS THUS FAR TO KEEP THE PROCESS OF 
RESTORING DEMOCRACY IN HAITI MOVING. HE URGED THAT THERE 
BE NO INTERRUPTION IN PARLIAMENT'S OPERATIONS DUE TO 
DELAYED LEGISLATIVE ELECTIONS.
