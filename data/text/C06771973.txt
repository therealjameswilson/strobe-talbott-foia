1. -0,74.. SUMMARY. IN HIS APRIL 5 MEETING WITH DEFENSE MINISTER 
TSOHATZOPOULOS, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT EXPRESSED THANKS AND 
ADMIRATION FOR THE MINISTER'S EARLY AND STRONG STATEMENTS 
CONDEMNING ETHNIC CLEANSING IN KOSOVO AND FOR MAKING IT POSSIBLE 
FOR NATO TO USE THE SOUDA BAY AND ACT/ON MILITARY BASES IN THE 
FACE OF STRONG PUBLIC CRITICISM OF NATO AIR STRIKES AGA/NST 
YUGOSLAVIA. HE URGED GREECE TO TAKE A SIGNIFICANT NUMBER OF 
REFUGEES, EXPEDITE TRANSIT OF REFUGEES TO TURKEY, AND PLAY AN 
ACTIVE ROLE IN SUPPORTING ALBANIA. TSOHATZOPOULOS HIGHLIGHTED 
THE NEED FOR A POLITICAL SOLUTION IN KOSOVO. HE SAID THAT 
SOUTHEAST EUROPEAN COUNTRIES OPPOSED ETHNIC CLEANSING ON THE 
STRATEGIC GROUNDS THAT IT WAS LEADING TO THE PARTITION OF KOSOVO; 
NO COUNTRY OF THE REGION, THE MINISTER EMPHASIZED, COULD ACCEPT 
SUCH A REDRAWING OF BORDERS. IF NATO USED LAND FORCES IN KOSOVO, 
HE CONTINUED, THE ALLIANCE WOULD APPEAR TO BE PURSUING 
MILOSEVIC'S GOAL OF PARTITION. IN RESPONSE TO THE MINISTER'S 
STRONG VIEWS AGAINST CHANGING EXISTING BORDERS, THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY STATED THAT WASHINGTON WAS NOT TALKING ABOUT AN

2. (U) DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, HEADING AN INTERAGENCY 
DELEGATION, MET WITH GREEK DEFENSE MINISTER AKIS TSOHATZOPOULOS 
ON APRIL 5. ACCOMPANYING THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WERE AMBASSADOR 
BURNS, NSC SENIOR DIRECTOR FLANAGAN, CJCS ASSISTANT GEN. 
FOGLESONG, ASD KRAMER, EUR ACTING DAS SWIGERT, AND EMBASSY 
NOTETAKER. TSOHATZOPOULOS WAS ACCOMPANIED BY SPECIAL ADVISER 
NIKOS DIMADIS. 
3. -eel— DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT TOLD TSOHATZOPOULOS THAT HE HAD 
JUST VISITED MACEDONIA AND ALBANIA TO SEE THE KOSOVO REFUGEE 
SITUATION FIRST HAND. HE WOULD BE TRAVELING TO SOFIA AND 
BUCHAREST AFTER ATHENS. COMMENTING THAT HE HAD RECENTLY VISITED 
SKOPJE, BUCHAREST, AND SOFIA, TSOHATZOPOULOS ASKED FOR THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY'S IMPRESSIONS. 
KOSOVO 
4. -fei. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY BEGAN BY SAYING THAT HE WANTED TO 
OFFER SOME THOUGHTS FOCUSS/NG ON WHAT COMES NEXT, ESPECIALLY 
REGARDING WHAT WE AND GREECE CAN DO TOGETHER. FIRST, WE BELIEVE 
THAT MILOSEVIC IS USING REFUGEES AS AN INSTRUMENT OF WAR: HIS 
ETHNIC CLEANSING IS EMPTYING KOSOVO OF ALBANIANS AND AT THE SAME 
TIME DESTABILIZING NEIGHBORING STATES, ESPECIALLY MACEDONIA. 
NOTING THAT HE HAD BEEN VISITING MACEDONIA FOR THE LAST 25 YEARS, 
STARTING WHEN IT WAS PART OF YUGOSLAVIA, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
SAID THAT, DURING THIS VISIT, HE OBSERVED A "QUANTUM LEAP" IN THE 
ANXIETY LEVEL OF THE COUNTRY'S LEADERS REGARDING THE VIABILITY OF 
THEIR STATE. THE INFLUX OF ALBANIAN REFUGEES THREATENS TO UPSET 
THE BALANCE BETWEEN THE COUNTRY'S ALBANIAN AND SLAVIC 
POPULATIONS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REMARKED THAT HIS DELEGATION'S 
EXPERTS ON THE REFUGEE CRISIS HAD MET WITH THEIR GREEK 
COUNTERPARTS EARLIER THAT MORNING. 
5. --(1.," THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT MACEDONIAN LEADERS WANTED 
TO KNOW WHAT COMES AFTER THIS CRISIS. WILL NATO JUST WALK AWAY, 
LEAVING THEM AT THE MERCY OF A VENGEFUL SERBIA? HE TOLD 
TSOHATZOPOULOS THAT HE HAD TRIED TO ASSURE THE SKOPJE GOVERNMENT 
THAT NATO WOULD TRY TO HELP MACEDONIA STAND ON ITS OWN TWO FEET 
AS FAR AS ITS SECURITY WAS CONCERNED. 
6. -eel' TSOHATZOPOULOS REMARKED THAT THE ROMANIAN AND BULGARIAN 
GOVERNMENTS WERE VERY CONCERNED AT THE DEVELOPING CRISIS IN 
KOSOVO. BOTH HOPED FOR A POLITICAL SOLUTION BECAUSE BOTH FACED 
PRO-SERB PUBLIC SYMPATHIES AND A UNITED FRONT FORMED BY POLITICAL 
COMPETITORS UNITED BY OPPOSITION TO NATO AIR STRIKES AGAINST 
YUGOSLAVIA. NEITHER THE ROMANIAN NOR BULGARIAN GOVERNMENTS WANTS 
TO BE PART OF THIS PROBLEM, BUT THEY ARE USING THE SITUATION TO 
SEEK ASSURANCES ON NATO MEMBERSHIP. FYROM WANTS IMMEDIATE NATO 
MEMBERSHIP, HE SAID, OBSERVING THAT THERE WOULD BE A 19 PLUS ONE 
MEETING ON FRIDAY WITH THE NATO SECRETARY GENERAL. HE NOTED THAT 
THE POLITICAL OPPOSITION IN FYROM CLAIMED THAT KEEPING KOSOVO 
REFUGEES ON THE BORDER WILL LEAD TO CONFLICT.

7. -44— TSOHATZOPOULOS STATED THAT THE COUNTRIES OF SOUTHEAST 
EUROPE WANTED TO KEEP GOOD NEIGHBORLY RELATIONS IN THE REGION. 
ALL WANTED THIS CRISIS TO END SOON. NOW THEY SEE THE CRISIS 
ENTERING A NEW PHASE -- IT IS NO LONGER A LOCAL PROBLEM. IN 
SEPTEMBER, NATO AGREED IN PORTUGAL TO USE THE THREAT OF FORCE TO 
PUSH MILOSEVIC TOWARD A POLITICAL SETTLEMENT IN KOSOVO IN THE 
CONTEXT OF EXISTING BORDERS. NOW, AFTER TWO WEEKS OF AIR 
STRIKES, THE SITUATION IS DIFFERENT. MILOSEVIC BEGAN HIS ETHNIC 
CLEANSING, WHICH IS "A TRAGEDY." OVER 100,000 PERSONS HAVE BEEN 
PUSHED INTO NEIGHBORING COUNTRIES; 40,000 REFUGEES ARE IN FYROM 
AND ANOTHER 40,000 ARE APPROACHING THE BORDER. FYROM IS VERY 
CONCERNED AND WANTS HELP; IT HAS,GOOD RELATIONS WITH GREECE AND 
UNDERSTANDS THAT GREECE WILL HELP. GREECE HAD TWO C-130 AIRCRAFT 
READY YESTERDAY TO FLY REFUGEES FROM FYROM TO GERMANY AND NORWAY; 
NO REFUGEES CAME TO BOARD THE FLIGHTS THEN, BUT HE EXPECTED THE 
FLIGHTS TO OPERATE TODAY. 
8. fe)-- AT THE SAME TIME, THE MINISTER CONTINUED, FYROM DOES NOT 
WANT TO BE USED AS A PLATFORM FOR ATTACKS ON YUGOSLAVIA. THE 
MAJORITY OF THE POPULATION IS EITHER SERB OR PRO-SERB; THE 
ALBANIAN MINORITY, HOWEVER, WOULD BE HAPPY TO HAVE NATO LAUNCH 
ATTACKS ON YUGOSLAVIA FROM FYROM. 
9. -fel- TSOHATZOPOULOS CLAIMED THAT ALL THE COUNTRIES OF 
SOUTHEAST EUROPE ARE UNITED IN OPPOSITION TO ETHNIC CLEANSING 
BECAUSE IT AIMS AT THE PARTITION OF KOSOVO. NO COUNTRY OF THE 
REGION IS WILLING TO ACCEPT SUCH A REDRAW/NG OF BORDERS; IT 
WOULD OPEN A PANDORA'S BOX OF ETHNIC CLEANSING IN THE REGION. IF 
NATO WERE TO USE LAND FORCES IN KOSOVO, THE MINISTER ARGUED, IT 
WOULD APPEAR THAT NATO WAS PURSUING THE SAME GOAL AS MILOSEVIC --
PARTITION. ALBANIANS WHO DREAM OF A "GREATER ALBANIA" ARE 
PUSHING NATO TO WORK TOWARD THE SAME GOAL AS MILOSEVIC, THE 
MINISTER SAID; THE COUNTRIES OF SOUTHEAST EUROPE CANNOT ACCEPT 
THIS. 
10. 4.8+ TSOHATZOPOULOS SAID HE HAD ISSUED A STATEMENT IN SKOPJE 
ON SATURDAY AND SUBSEQUENTLY HAD RECEIVED TELEPHONE CALLS FROM 
PRESIDENT GLIGOROV AND FOREIGN MINISTER DIMITROV AGREEING WITH 
WHAT HE HAD SAID. TSOHATZOPOULOS POINTED OUT THAT HE HAD BEEN 
CRITICIZED IN THE PRESS FOR HIS REMARKS CRITICIZING ETHNIC 
CLEANSING. "IT IS MY RIGHT" TO SPEAK OUT ON THIS, HE MAINTAINED. 
HE ACKNOWLEDGED THAT THERE IS MUCH PRO-SERB SYMPATHY IN GREECE, 
BUT STATED THAT "WE CANNOT BE HOSTAGES TO VIOLENCE. NOW IS THE 
TIME TO STOP ETHNIC CLEANSING." 
11. tei- THE MINISTER ALSO ARGUED THAT IN FAVOR OF GUARANTEEING 
NO CHANGES IN EXISTING BORDERS; AGREEING ON NEW POLITICAL 
STRUCTURES IN KOSOVO WAS A SEPARATE ISSUE. AMBASSADOR HILL NEVER 
SAID THAT THERE MUST BE A REFERENDUM IN KOSOVO AFTER THREE YEARS, 
THE MINISTER STATED. WHY LEAVE OPEN THE POSSIBILITY OF CHANGING 
BORDERS IN THREE YEARS? TSOHATZOPOULOS SUGGESTED THAT NATO 
TROOPS COULD JOIN WITH TROOPS FROM NON-NATO COUNTRIES TO 
IMPLEMENT A PEACE AGREEMENT.

12. -(-69- HE WONDERED HOW, AT THE NATO SUMMIT LATER THIS MONTH, 
ALLIANCE MEMBERS WOULD EXPLAIN WHY THEY WERE AT WAR? IF NATO IS 
AT WAR, THEN IT MUST AIM TO DEFEAT MILOSEVIC. HOWEVER, AIR 
STRIKES WILL NOT DEFEAT ANYONE IN A FEW WEEKS. FYROM PRESIDENT 
GLIGOROV HAD STATED A CLEAR POSITION BASED ON SOUND ANALYSIS. 
GLIGOROV HAD ASSERTED THAT THE COUNTRIES OF SOUTHEAST EUROPE HAD 
TO UNITE IN A COMMON SECURITY SYSTEM AND THAT RUSSIA WOULD HAVE 
TO RESPECT IT. THE COUNTRIES OF SOUTHEAST EUROPE HAD TO END THE 
DIVISIONS WITHIN THEIR REGION AS THE COUNTRIES OF WESTERN EUROPE 
HAD DONE IN WESTERN EUROPE. TALBOTT COMMENTED THAT GLIGOROV HAD 
SAID SAME THE SAME THING TO HIM. THIS REPRESENTS BIG CHANGE IN 
THE THINKING OF THE MACEDONIANS, TSOHATZOPOULOS SAID. IF THE 
CRISIS CONTINUES, IT WILL BECOME TOO EXPENSIVE FOR ALL OF US. 
13. ..44,14- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED THANKS AND ADMIRATION

-- WE KNOW IT IS NOT EASY. THE MINISTER'S IMPORTANT ROLE IN 
KOSOVO-RELATED DECISIONS AND HIS STATURE IN GREECE MADE IT ALL 
THE MORE IMPORTANT THAT HE SPOKE OUT AS EARLY AS LAST YEAR 
AGAINST ETHNIC CLEANSING. 
14. -1€4- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASSURED TSOHATZOPOULOS THAT,

REDRAW THE MAP OF EUROPE. NATO AND THE OSCE ARE UNITED: WE MUST 
DO ALL WE CAN TO PRESERVE THE BORDERS AND TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY 
OF YUGOSLAVIA. IT IS IRONIC THAT MILOSEVIC HAS UNDERMINED BOTH. 
IT IS TOO EARLY TO SAY THAT RAMBOUILLET IS DEAD.. WE ARE NOT IN 
FAVOR OF AN INDEPENDENT KOSOVO. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ADDED HIS 
PERSONAL VIEW THAT WE HAVE TO LOOK FOR A SETTLEMENT IN KOSOVO 
THAT INCLUDES SELF-GOVERNANCE, RETURN OF REFUGEES, AND AN 
INTERNATIONAL PROTECTION FORCE FOR THEM, BUT WITHIN YUGOSLAV 
BORDERS. ON REFUGEES, HE URGED GREECE TO:
