Dear Mr. Derr: 
RELEASE IN FULL 
Thank you for your letter of April 30. I was very glad to 
meet and talk with you on April 22, and the conversation turned 
out to be doubly useful since it was good preparation for my 
recent visit to Almaty. Chevron has made great progress in 
working with Kazakhstan, and I hope that your hard currency 
problems will be resolved quickly so that this project can move 
forward. 
We are continuing our review of the pipeline issue, and my 
office has met with your Vice President for the CIS, Espy 
Price, and also with Edward Chow. My staff maintains good 
contact with your Washington office, and we are always 
interested in your thoughts and recommendations. 
If I can be of further assistance, please do not hesitate 
to contact me. 
Sincerely, 
atr be Talbott 
Ambassador At Large and 
Special Advisor to The Secretary 
On the New Independent States
Mr. KenT. Derr 
Chairman of the Board, 
Chevron Corporation,

San Francisco, CA 94104-4289
