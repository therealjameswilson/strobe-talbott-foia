4. ~ VOLLEBAEK INFORMED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY HE HAD MET
WITH INDIAN PM VAJPAYEE ON JANUARY 8, AND WOULD MEET WITH
INDIAN FM SINGH JANUARY 10. VOLLEBAEK WISHED TO CONSULT
WITH TALBOTT ON CTBT.
5. ~ TALBOTT EXPLAINED HE WOULD MEET WITH FM SINGH IN
LONDON ON JANUARY 18, FOR THE ELEVENTH ROUND OF A DIALOGUE
CONDUCTED SINCE INDIA LAST TESTED NUCLEAR WEAPONS.
TALBOTT RESPECTED SINGH~ AND REQUESTED VOLLEBAEK PASS HIS
REGARDS TO THE FOREIGN MINSTER.
6. ~j TALBOTT OUTLINED THE ESSENCE OF HIS TALKS WITH
SINGH.'HE U.S., ALONG WITH MANY ALLIES INCLUDING NORWAY,
FELT STRONGLY INDIA' DECISION TO TEST NUCLEAR WEAPONS WAS
A MISTAKE IN TERMS OF BOTH ITS OWN INTERSTS AND THE NON-
PROLIFERATION REGIME. THE U.S. WAS CLEAR'N THIS
POSITION, BUT REMAINED REALISTIC. INDIA IS DETERMINED TO
POSSESS A NUCLEAR DETERRENT, AND WILL NOT ALTER EITHER ITS
FUNDAMENETAL POLICY OR STRATEGIC POSTURE. AREAS FOR
U.S./INDIAN COOPERATION WILL ALWAYS BE LIMITED BY THIS
REALITY, PARTICULARLY W1TH REGARDS TO NUCLEAR. ENERGY. A
QUALITATIVELY BETTER U.S./INDIAN RELATIONSHIP COULD
DEVELOP IF INDIA COULD SEE IT AS BEING IN 1TS OWN
INTERESTS TO FIND A SOLUTION IN TERMS OF NON-
PROLIFERATION.
7. ~ TALBOTT' DISCUSSIONS. WITH SINGH REVOLVED AROUND
DEVELOPING FIVE STEPS INDIA COULD TAKE TOWARD IMPROVED
RELATIONS. FIRST, INDIA SHOULD SIGN CTBT. SECOND, INDIA
SHOULD MAKE A QUALIFIED BUT POSITIVE STATEMENT ABOUT
JOINING THE FISSILE MATERIALS CUTOFF TREATY. THIRD, INDIA
SHOULD ADOPT A FORCE POSTURE IN LINE WITH ITS RHETORIC ON
DETERRENCE. FOURTH, INDIA SHOULD BRING ITS EXPORT
CONTROLS IN LINE WITH THE INTERNATIONAL REGIME. THE FIFTH
BENCHMARK FOR PROGRESS IS AN IMPROVEMENT IN THE
INDIA/PAKISTAN BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP.
8. ~ TALBOTT INDICATED PRESIDENT CLINTON PLANNED TO
TRAVEL TO INDIA IN THE SPRING, AND THAT A POSITIVE 'TRIP
WOULD FOLLOW FROM INDIAN PROGRESS ON THESE STEPS,
PARTICULARLY CTBT, FM SINGH HAD MADE GOOD PROGRESS IN THE
PAST SIX WEEKS ON BUILDING A BETTER DOMESTIC ENVIRONMENT
FOR CTBT SIGNATURE, INCLUDING IN RELATIONS WITH
PARLIAMENT, THINK TANKS, AND THE FOREIGN POLICY ELITE.
TALBOTT REQUESTED THAT VOLLEBAEK RAISE CTBT WITH SINGH AND

9. + THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID INDIA HAD ADVANCED ITS
RELATIONSHIP WITH PAKISTAN UNTIL THE PAKISTANI INCURSION
INTO KASHMIR IN 1999. THE U.S. HAD HELPED TO DEFUSE THAT
CRISIS, BUT INDIA/PAKISTAN RELATIONS REMAINED WORSE THAN
BEFORE THE CONFLICT. THE PAKISTANI COUP AND RECENT INDIAN
AIRLINES HIJACKING MADE FM SINGH AND PM VAJPAYEE FEEL
VULNERABLE DUE TO THEIR PERSONAL INVOLVEMENT IN PUSHING
THE LAHORE PROCESS. TALBOTT HAD WORKED WITH SINGH TO HELP
DEFUSE THE HIJACKING CRISIS, AND HOPED INDIA WOULD FIND A
WAY TO DE-ESCALATE TENSIONS NOW THAT THE HOSTAGE SITUATION
HAD BEEN RESOLVED.
10. ~ VOLLEBAEK AGREED TO PASS TALBOTT'S REGARDS TO
SINGH AND TO PUSH CTBT WITH THE INDIAN FM. HE HAD RAISED
CTBT WITH PM VAJPAYEE ON JANUARY 8. VOLLEBAEK CONFIRMED
THAT IN TERMS OF THE PAKISTAN RELATIONSHIP VAJPAYEE FELT
BETRAYED BY THE COUP AND HIJACKING. IN THEIR DISCUSSION,
VAJPAYEE HAD REFERRED TO LAHORE AND HIS PERSONAL COURAGE
IN PROMOTING THE PROCESS, AND THEREFORE NOW FACED THE RISK
OF BEING VIEWED AS AN ACCOMPLICE TO THE CURRENT
INDIA/PAKISTAN TENSIONS'N CTBTr VAJPAYEE INDICATED THE
POSSIBILITY OF A NATIONAL CONSENSUS HAD BEEN HAMPERED BY
THE U.S. SENATE' VOTE AGAINST THE TREATY, A MOVE THAT
POSED PROBLEMS FOR HANDLING THE INDIAN PARL1AMENT.
VOLLEBAEK PLANNED TO RAISE THESE ISSUES WITH FM SINGH AND
SONIA GANDHI IN MEETINGS JANUARY 10.
11; (W) VOLLEBAEK INQUIRED ABOUT THE DIFFICULTY OF
ESTABLISHING AN INDIAN CONSENSUS ON CTBT. TALBOTT
SUSPECTED IT WOULD BE A BIT MORE PROBLEMATIC GIVEN THE
U.S. SENATE VOTE, BUT THOUGHT THE INDIANS WERE LOOKING FOR
EXCUSES TO JUSTIFY A FAILED CONSENSUS BID. WHILE THE
SENATE VOTE ON CTBT WAS A SETBACK, THE U.S. WAS URGING
INDIA TO BRING ITS POLICY IN PARALLEL WITH THE U.S.
POSITION — SIGNATURE OF CTBT BUT NOT ENTRY INTO FORCE OR
RATIFICATION. VOLLEBAEK'S MEETING WITH SONIA GANDHI WAS A
GOOD MOVE, SAID TALBOTT, AS THE CONGRESS PARTY HAD NOT
MADE IT EASIER FOR THE BJP GOVERNMENT TO ACT POSITIVELY ON
CTBT. TALBOTT SAID NORWAY WAS A SOLID INDIAN ALLY AND A
FORCEFUL ADVOCATE FOR NON-PROLIFERATION AND ARMS CONTROL.
THIS OFFERED VOLLEBAEK A USEFUL OPENING TO PUSH SINGH ON
CTBT, AND TO WISH SINGH LUCK ON CREATING THE NECESSARY
CONDITIONS FOR A FORWARD-LOOKING AND UPBEAT TRIP BY
