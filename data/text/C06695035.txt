2. SUMMARY: IN A JULY 25 MEETING WITH DEPSEC TALBOTT 
FOCUSED ON BILATERAL ISSUES, THAI PM CHUAN: 
-- INVITED THE PRESIDENT TO VISIT THAILAND; 
-- SAID LEGISLATION AMENDING THE STATE ENTERPRISE LABOR 
RELATIONS ACT COULD NOT BE ENACTED IN THE REMAINING DAYS 
OF THIS PARLIAMENTARY SESSION, BUT IT WOULD BE DONE IN 
THE SEPTEMBER SESSION; 
-- REAFFIRMED RTG "UNEQUIVOCAL” SUPPORT FOR THE 
CAMBODIAN GOVERNMENT; AND 
-- SUGGESTED THAT AMERICAN INVESTORS IN BURMA COULD HELP 
INTEGRATE THAT COUNTRY WITH-THE OUTSIDE WORLD. END 
SUMMARY. 
3. IN A 45-MINUTE CALL ON PRIME MINISTER CHUAN, DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT EXPRESSED GREETINGS FROM THE PRESIDENT 
AND REGRET BY THE SECRETARY THAT THE MIDDLE-EAST PEACE 
PROCESS HAD CAUSED HIM TO MISS THE ASEAN MEETINGS. PM 
CHUAN REFERRED REPEATEDLY AND POSITIVELY TO PRESIDENT 
CLINTON'S LETTER (WHICH CHUAN HAD MADE PUBLIC), 
EXPLAINING THE SECRETARY'S NEED TO BE IN WASHINGTON. 
CHUAN ASKED THAT DEPSEC TALBOTT CONVEY AN INVITATION TO 
THE PRESIDENT TO VISIT THAILAND, NOTING NO U.S.-PRESIDENT 
HAS VISITED IN 25 YEARS. 
ECONOMIC ISSUES 
4. NOTING THE PRESIDENT'-S RECENT LETTER PROPOSING A 
U.S.-THAI BUSINESS COUNCIL, CHURN SAID THAILAND'S 
RESPONSE HAS DELAYED BY THE RESIGNATION OF THE DEPUTY 
PRIME MINISTER WHO HAS IN CHARGE OF THE MATTER. CHUAN 
SPOKE POSITIVELY ABOUT THE IDEA, HOWEVER, SAYING THE 
COUNCIL WOULD ENHANCE TRADE BY BRINGING OUR TWO PRIVATE 
SECTORS CLOSER TOGETHER. TALBOTT AGREED THAT THE U.S.-
THAI ECONOMIC RELATIONSHIP HAS INCREASINGLY IMPORTANT, 
AND IN THIS CONTEXT STRESSED OUR HOPE THAT THE THAI 
PARLIAMENT WOULD ACT THIS SESSION TO AMEND THE STATE 
ENTERPRISE LABOR RELATIONS ACT IN ORDER TO ENSURE THAT 
THAILAND WOULD CONTINUE TO RECEIVE GSP BENEFITS. CHURN 
REPLIED THAT THE BILL HAD BEEN SUBMITTED TO THE 
PARLIAMENT AND WOULD BE CONSIDERED DURING THE SEPTEMBER 
SESSION, AFTER THE AUGUST RECESS. IN REPLY TO U/S
