PARTICIPANTS: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND GEORGIAN PRESIDENT
SHEVARDNADZE
ELECTION AND THE PIPELINE
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CONGRATULATED PRESIDENT
SHEVARDNADZE ON HIS REELECTION, CONVEYING GREETINGS AND
BEST WISHES FROM PRESIDENT CLINTON AND THE SECRETARY. HE
TOLD SHEVARDNADZE THE SECRETARY WAS VERY PLEASED TO HAVE
PARTICIPATED IN THE SIGNING OF THE BAKU-TBILISI-CEYHAN.
PIPELINE AGREEMENT. THE AGREEMENT WAS AN IMPORTANT STEP
IN SAFEGUARDING GEORGIAN ENERGY SECURITY. SHEVARDNADZE
THANKED HIM, EXPRESSING SATISFACTION THE PIPELINE DEAL HAD
BEEN INITIALED. HE NOTED THE ENGAGEMENT OF THE U.S.,
PART ICULARLY PRES IDENT CLINTON ~ WAS CRIT ICAL IN SEALING
THIS "DEAL OF THE CENTURY."
SECURITY AND CFE
5. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CHARACTERIZED FM
MENAGHARISHVILI' RECENT VISIT AS VERY PRODUCTIVE,
ESPECIALLY WITH REGARD TO CFE. REFERRING TO HIS MEETING
WITH THE FOREIGN MINISTER AND CFE SPECIAL ENVOY DUNKERLEY,
HE SAID THE U.S. AND GEORGIA APPEAR TO HAVE DEVELOPED A
STRATEGY FOR ENCOURAGING RUSSIA TO IMPLEMENT FULLY ITS
ISTANBUL COMMITMENTS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED
PRESIDENT CLINTON' JUNE VISIT TO MOSCOW WOULD PROVIDE AN
EXCELLENT OPPORTUNITY TO PRESS FOR PROGRESS ON THIS ISSUE.
HE EXPLAINED DUNKERLEY HAD PRESENTED THE RUSSIAN SIDE WITH
A NON-PAPER OUTLINING U.S. IDEAS AND SAID HE WOULD DISCUSS
THE MATTER WITH THEM FURTHER IN BRUSSELS MAY 5.
SHEVARDNADZE SAID HE HAD RECEIVED A READOUT ON THE FOREIGN
MINISTER'S WASHINGTON MEETINGS. HE CHARACTERIZED EFFORTS
TOWARD IMPLEMENTATION OF RUSSIA'S ISTANBUL COMMITMENTS AS
A "GOOD START." HE WOULD BE GRATEFUL IF THE PRESIDENTS
WOULD ADDRESS THIS ISSUE AT THE JUNE U.S.-RUSSIA SUMMIT.
ECONOMIC REFORM, CORRUPTION, ELECTION IRREGULARITIES
6. ~ TURNING TO ECONOMIC MATTERS, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY
EXPRESSED CONCERN GEORGIA'S MOUNTING ECONOMIC TROUBLES
COULD UNDERMINE THE COUNTRY'S SOVEREIGNTY AND ECONOMIC
STABILITY. HE HOPED SHEVARDNADZE'S NEW ADMINISTRATION
WOULD PUT TOGETHER THE STRONGEST POSSIBLE TEAM, WHICH

7 ~ ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD SHEVARDNADZE THE U.S.
WANTED TO MOVE FORWARD WITH DEBT RESCHEDULING FOR GEORGIA
AND A NEW IFI PROGRAM, BUT NOTED THERE MUST BE
DEMONSTRABLE PROGRESS IN ECONOMIC REFORM TO JUSTIFY
AMERICAN SUPPORT. HE SAID HE HAD DISCUSSED THIS ISSUE
WITH AMBASSADOR JAPARIDZE AND GIVEN HIM A MORE DETAILED
NON-PAPER COVERING BOTH ECONOMIC ISSUES AND THE QUESTION
OF ELECTION IRREGULARITIES IN GEORGIA.
8. ~ SHEVARDNADZE SAID HE WAS CONCERNED WITH THE
.ECONOMIC DIFFICULTIES CONFRONTING GEORGIA. HE NOTED
ELECTIONS HAD FORCED A SLOW-DOWN IN REFORM INITIATIVES,
INCLUDING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE ANTI-CORRUPTION
AGENCY. HIS NEW MANDATE, HOWEVER, WOULD ALLOW HIM TO
RENEW THE REFORM AGENDA. SHEVARDNADZE SAID HE INTENDED TO
MAKE THE FIGHT AGAINST CORRUPTION HIS GOVERNMENT'S FIRST
PRIORITY ONCE THE NEW ADMINISTRATION WAS IN PLACE. HE
ESTIMATED THIS WOULD TAKE ANOTHER SEVEN TO TEN DAYS. -HE
NOTED THE BLUEPRINTS FOR THE ANTI-CORRUPTION AGENCY WERE
COMPLETE. HE SAID THE MINISTRY OF REVENUE ENJOYED THE
ADMINISTRATION' FULL SUPPORT AND PROMISED TO CONTINUE
BACKING TAX REFORM.
9 ON THE QUESTION OF ELECTORAL IRREGULARITIES,
SHEVARDNADZE ADMITTED THE MOST RECENT POLLS HAD NOT BEEN
PERFECT. MOST IMPORTANT, HOWEVER, WAS THE FACT THAT A
CLEAR MAJORITY HAD SUPPORTED HIS REFORMIST AGENDA. THIS,
HE SAID, WAS NOT IN DISPUTE.
