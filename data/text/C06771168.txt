SUBJECT: TAJIKISTAN PRESIDENT RAHMONOV MEETING WITH DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT 
CLASSIFIED BY S/NIS ROSS WILSON. REASONS 1.5 (B)(D) 
1. tei. SUMMARY: IN AN APRIL 22 MEETING WITH THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY, TAJIKISTAN'S PRESIDENT IMOMALI RAHMONOV 
ANNOUNCED HIS INTENTION TO OPEN AN EMBASSY IN 
WASHINGTON BY SEPTEMBER OF THIS YEAR. HE EXPRESSED HIS 
REGRET THAT OPERATIONS AT THE U.S. EMBASSY IN DUSHANBE 
HAD BEEN CLOSED DOWN, AND SAID HOW IMPORTANT IT WOULD 
BE TO HAVE THE U.S. AMBASSADOR BACK AS TAJIKISTAN 
CARRIES OUT ITS REFERENDUM AND UPCOMING ELECTIONS. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED SUPPORT FOR GETTING 
AMBASSADOR FINN BACK INTO THE'COUNTRY IN WHICH HE IS 
ACCREDITED AS SOON AS POSSIBLE. RAHMONOV ASKED FOR 
ASSISTANCE IN FIGHTING NARCOTICS TRAFFICKING AND FOR 
USG POLITICAL SUPPORT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REMINDED 
THE PRESIDENT THAT THE USG'S ABILITY TO CARRY OUT 
BILATERAL RELATIONS WOULD DEPEND ON'PROGRESS IN THE 
PEACE PROCESS. HE URGED RAHMONOV TO KEEP UP THE 
MOMENTUM. END SUMMARY.

RAHMONOV REGRETS ABSENCE OF U.S. EMBASSY 
2. Itl- THE MAIN FOCUS OF THE APRIL 22 MEETING BETWEEN 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND TAJIKISTAN'S PRESIDENT IMOMALI 
RAHMONOV WAS BILATERAL RELATIONS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
SAID THE GOOD NEWS IS THAT THERE ARE DIPLOMATIC 
RELATIONS AND AMBASSADORS TRYING TO CARRY OUT 
DIPLOMACY; THE BAD NEWS IS THAT NEITHER STATE HAS AN 
OPEN, FUNCTIONING EMBASSY. HE AND RAHMONOV LOOKED 
FIRST AT THE QUESTION OF HOW TO ACCELERATE THE PROCESS 
OF GETTING EMBASSIES OPEN AND FUNCTIONING IN EACH 
OTHER'S COUNTRIES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REASSURED 
RAHMONOV THAT THE SECRETARY HAD CLOSED DOWN OPERATIONS 
AT THE U.S. EMBASSY FACILITY FOR REASONS RELATED 
EXCLUSIVELY TO SECURITY AND THAT THIS WAS NOT A 
POLITICAL SIGNAL. 
3. itt-THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT HE WAS EAGER TO 
GET AMBASSADOR FINN BACK INTO THE COUNTRY IN WHICH HE 
IS ACCREDITED AND INFORMED RAHMONOV THAT AN EXPERT WILL 
TRAVEL TO DUSHANBE IN MAY TO WORK ON THIS. (COMMENT: 
THIS WAS A REFERENCE TO EUR/EX - FRANK COULTER'S MAY 
12-28 TRIP TO CENTRAL ASIA. END COMMENT) THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY CONGRATULATED RAHMONOV ON THE IMPORTANT STEP 
THE TAJIKS HAD JUST TAKEN IN OPENING UP A CHAMBER OF 
COMMERCE IN WASHINGTON, AND EXPRESSED THE HOPE THE GOTI 
WILL FOLLOW THIS STEP BY OPENING AN EMBASSY AS SOON AS 
POSSIBLE.

TAJIKISTAN. A U.S. AMBASSADOR MUST BE THERE. RAHMONOV 
GAVE HIS PERSONAL ASSURANCE THAT THE GOTI COULD BE 
COUNTED UPON TO PROVIDE SECURITY GUARANTEES FOR OUR 
EMBASSY.
