6. -€9-)- MCKINNON TOUCHED ALSO ON RELATIONS WITH NIGERIA 
AND STATED HE UNFORTUNATELY DID NOT RAISE THE ISSUE WHEN 
HE WAS IN WASHINGTON. HE SAIDNEW ZEALAND WISHED TO 
ENCOURAGE NATIONS TO LOWER THEIR LEVELS OF DIPLOMATIC 
REPRESENTATION WITH NIGERIA. MCKINNON RELAYED BRITAIN'S 
CONCERNS THAT IT NOT BE THE ONLY ONE TO DO SO, ADDING 
THAT MANY NATIONS ARE NOT REPRESENTED IN NIGERIA AND A 
U.S. DRAWDOWN WOULD SEND A MESSAGE. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY COMMENTED HE WOULD HAVE TO DISCUSS THIS MATTER 
WITH A/S GEORGE MOOSE AND RESPOND TO MCKINNON THROUGH 
REGULAR CHANNELS. 
7. -(-&)- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THEN TURNED TO A 
DISCUSSION OF SECRETARY OF DEFENSE PERRY'S PLANS. HE 
CONFIRMED THAT PERRY IS AMENABLE TO A VISIT FOLLOWING 
THE AUSMIN TALKS. MCKINNON REPLIED HE THOUGHT THIS TO 
BE THE'CASE BUT WONDERED HOW PERRY WISHED TO PROCEED ON 
THE EXERCISES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STATED HE HAD 
CHECKED WITH DEFENSE A/S KURT CAMPBELL AND LEARNED PERRY 
IS STRONGLY INCLINED TO ARRIVE AT A SPECIFIC DATE FOR 
SCHEDULING PURPOSES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ALSO SAID 
PERRY HAD SOME IMAGINATIVE IDEAS CONCERNING THE TRIP'S 
FORMAT AND CONTENT.
