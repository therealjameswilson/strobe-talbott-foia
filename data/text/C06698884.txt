MEMORANDUM OF CONVERSATION 
SUBJECT: TELEPHONE CONVERSATION WITH RUSSIAN PRIME MINISTER 
VIKTOR CHERNOMYRDIN 
PARTICIPANTS: U.S. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT PM CHERNOMYRDIN 
DATE, TIME AND PLACE:' 
NOVEMBER 26, 1996, 6:08PM-6:20PM 
WEST WING OFFICE 
RUSSIA 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: HELLO, VICTOR STEPANOVICH? 
PM CHERNOMYRDIN: GOOD EVENING AL, I AM CALLING FROM PARIS 
AND EXTEND GREETINGS FROM MY FRENCH HOSTS. WE HAVE A JOINT 
RUSSIA-FRANCE COMMISSION SIMILAR TO OURS. I M GLAD TO HEAR
YOU AND GLAD I WILL BE SEEING YOU IN LISBON DECEMBER 1 AND 2. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: YES, I WILL BE IN LISBON AND AM LOOKING 
FORWARD TO MEETING WITH YOU. I WILL BE THERE ONLY MONDAY AND 
OUR STAFFS ARE WORKING AN AGENDA. I M HOPING TO USE OUR 
PRIVATE TIME TO TALK ABOUT EUROPEAN SECURITY, TO LOOK AHEAD 
TO OUR RELATIONSHIP IN THE COMING MONTHS. NOW THAT OUR 
ELECTIONS ARE OVER WE SHOULD START MAPPING OUT AREAS OUR 
PRESIDENTS MIGHT'FOCUS ON BETWEEN NOW AND THIS SUMMER.--
AREAS SUCH AS BILATERAL ARMS CONTROL, ECONOMIC COOPERATION.

PRESIDENT YELTSIN S RECENT LETTER TO PRESIDENT 0,INTON SPOKE ABOUT RE-ENERGIZING THE US-RUSSIAN RELATIONSHIP, WHICH PRESIDENT CLINTON WELCOMES. WE NEED TO KNOW WHAT YOUR THOUGHTS ARE ABOUT HOW WE CAN USE THE COMMISSION TO GREATEST EFFECT TO DO THIS. . WE ARE LOOKING FORWARD TO OUR NEXT MEETING IN EARLY FEBRUARY, AFTER DAVOS. ARE THERE ANY OTHER SPECIFIC TOPICS THAT YOU WOULD LIKE ME TO.BE PREPARED TO ADDRESS WITH YOU WHEN WE SEE EACH OTHER ON MONDAY? 
PM CHERNOMYRDIN: THANK YOU VERY MUCH, WE WILL DO AS YOU SAY. OUR EMBASSY WILL HELP PREPARE FOR OUR NEXT COMMIUION MEETING. WE CAN DISCUSS ALL THESE ITEMS CONCERNING FUTURE DIRECTIONS AND EUROPEAN SECURITY AS WELL. THANKS AGAIN FOR YOUR.ASSISTANCE IN RELEASING MR. GALKIN. EVERYONE IN RUSSIA KNOWS YOU DID A LOT TO HELP THIS, RIGHT AFTER OUR CONVERSATION; YOU CANNOT ESTIMATE THE GOOD THAT WILL COME FORM THIS. TURNING TO ANOTHER MATTER, REPORTS-I GET FROM - LISBON SAY THAT THE U.S. DELEGATION SEEMS RELUCTANT TO MOVE FORWARD ON PREPARING DOCUMENTS FOR THE SUMMIT AND APPEARS TO BE WAITING TO REACH AGREEMENT UNTIL NOVEMBER 29, WHEN ALL HEADS OF STATE WILL BE THERE. THIS GIVES US CONCERN AND OTHERS TOO ARE ASK/NG WHAT IS GOING ON. THEY SAY THESE ARE IMPORTANT DOCUMENTS AND DECISIONS, YET WE ARE NOT MAKING PROGRESS. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: YOU SHOULD NOT DOUBT OUR COMMITMENT TO OSCE AND OUR FIRM SUPPORT FOR THE ROLE OF THE OSCE IN FUTURE EUROPEAN SECURITY. WE VE TAKEN GREAT CARE TO ENSURE THE DOCUMENTS ARE DRAFTED WITH THE RIGHT TONE AND CONTENT TO MAKE SURE THE MEETING IS A SUCCESS, AND OUR DELEGATION IS FULLY ENGAGED. WE HAD SOME NARROW DIFFERENCES WITH THE EU, WHICH YOU MAY HAVE TAKEN FOR FOOT-DRAGGING, BUT THIS IS A MISINTERPRETATION. I UNDERSTAND THE SWISS CHAIR IS TO TABLE A TEXT BASED UPON THE EU AND U.S. TEXTS. WE WANT TO,HAVE A STRONG TEXT THAT IS SUITABLE FOR A SUMMIT DOCUMENT - YOU WILL SEE FULL EVIDENCE OF OUR COMMITMENT TO THIS. TELL YOUR PEOPLE NOT TO WORRY AND TO NOT READ TOO MUCH INTO OUR DISAGREEMENTS. OUR COMMITMENT IS THERE. 
PM CHERNOMYRDIN: THANKS, I M CERTAIN AND HAVE NO DOUBT; I WILL TALK TO MY PEOPLE. YOU MIGHT DISCUSS THIS WITH YOUR SECRETARY OF STATE TO ENSURE THESE MATTERS ARE UNDER CONTROL THERE IS NOT MUCH TIME LEFT AND THE QUESTIONS ARE MOST IMPORTANT. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: I SEE THE LISBON MEETING AS A SINGULARLY IMPORTANT OPPORTUNITY. THIS IS AN UNPRECEDENTED MOMENT -- A CHANCE TO FURTHER SHAPE OUR SHARED VISION FOR A SECURE, • STABLE, AND UNDIVIDED EUROPE. THIS IS THE FIRST TIME IN HISTORY THAT PEACE AND DEMOCRATIC VALUES REIGN FROM THE SHORES OF THE ATLANTIC TO THE URALS AND BEYOND. WHAT IS AT STAKE AT THE LISBON MEETING IS THE FOUNDATION FOR THE PEACE, PROSPERITY, AND SECURITY OF THE NEXT CENTURY. -- NOT JUST PERCEPTIONS OF COMPETING ORGANIZATIONS OR THEIR COMPETING MISSIONS. I WILL TELL YOU IN ADVANCE THAT IN MY PUBLIC COMMENTS I WILL MAKE CLEAR THE FUNDAMENTAL IMPORTANCE THAT THE US ATTACHES TO OSCE. WE NEED TO PROJECT A POSITIVE ATTITUDE TO THE PUBLIC, IN ORDER TO BE SURE THAT WE ESTABLISH POSITIVE, FORWARD MOMENTUM TOGETHER. BY THE WAY ARE THE FRENCH SERVING YOU, GOOD WINE? 
PM CHERNOMYRDIN: THANK YOU FOR BEING CONCERNED ABOUT ME BEING TAKEN CARE OF -- THE FRENCH HAVE BEEN PERFECT HOSTS AND THE RED WINE WAS FINE. THE SUMMIT IS EXTREMELY IMPORTANT. WE HAVE COVERED A LOT OF GROUND AND ARE BUILDING A FOUNDATOIN FOR AN UNDIVIDED EUROPE-WITH A STABLE SECURITY ARCHITECTURE. WE WILL DISCUSS THESE THINGS AND MORE WHEN WE MEET. BEST REGARDS FROM VALENTINA FEDOROVNA, SHE HAS BEEN WITH ME AT MY SIDE THIS EVENING. GIVE OUR BEST TO TIPPER AND YOUR
