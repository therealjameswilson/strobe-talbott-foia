2. SUMMARY: DURING A TWO-DAY VISIT TO WASHINGTON, 
GEORGIAN FORMIN IRAKLI MENAGHARISHVILI VISITED THE STATE 
DEPARTMENT AND MET WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE 
TALBOTT AND S/NIS COORDINATOR AMBASSADOR JAMES COLLINS, 
HE ALSO ATTENDED A WORKING LUNCH HOSTED BY 5/NIS 
ASSISTANCE COORDINATOR AMBASSADOR RICHARD MORNINGSTAR AND 
WAS BRIEFED BY PM DAS MICHAEL LEMMON ON THE CONCEPT OF A 
BILATERAL SECURITY DIALOGUE. THE DISCUSSIONS HELD DURING 
THESE MEETINGS FOCUSED ON REGIONAL DYNAMICS IN THE 
CAUCASUS AND PARTICULARLY GEORGIAN EFFORTS TO DEVELOP 
BILATERAL AND MULTILATERAL ARRANGEMENTS TO COUNTERBALANCE 
MOSCOW'S INFLUENCE. THE FORMIN SOUGHT ASSURANCES ON 
ISSUES OF CONCERN TO GEORGIAN PARLIAMENTARIANS WHO MUST 
RATIFY THE CFE FLANK AGREEMENT. HE WELCOMED U.S. INTEREST 
RELEASE IN FULL 
DECLASSIFIED 
IN EXPANDED COOPERATION AS WELL AS THE INVITATION FROM 
SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER TO BEGIN A REGULAR BILATERAL 
SECURITY AND ARMS CONTROL DIALOGUE EXPRESSING 
FRUSTRATION OVER THE LACK OF PROGRESS TOWARD A SETTLEMENT 
OF THE SITUATION IN ABKHAZIA, MENAGHARISHVILI ENCOURAGED 
THE U.S. TO PRESS MOSCOW AND TO BECOME MORE DIRECTLY 
INVOLVED. STRONGER TIES TO THE UgS AND WEST AND EXPANDED 
COOPERATION AND ASSISTANCE WERE THEMES THROUGHOUT HIS 
MEETINGS. END SUMMARY. 
2. FOREIGN MINISTER MENAGHARISHVILI'S VISIT TO 
WASHINGTON FOLLOWED A STOP IN NEW YORK WHERE HE DELIVERED 
A SPEECH TO THE UNGA AND.MET WITH SYG BOUTROS GAL1 AND 
OTHER UN OFFICIALS AS WELL AS WITH UNDERSECRETARY DAVIS 
(REFTEL). THE FOREIGN MINISTER WAS ACCOMPANIED TO THE 
MEETINGS BY AMBASSADOR TEDO JAPARIDZE AND POLITICAL 
COUNSELOR DAVID SOUMBADZE. OTHER AMERICANS IN THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY'S MEETING WERE JHERBST, 5/NIS DEPUTY 
COORDINATOR, AND EEDELMAN, EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT TO THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY. AMBASSADOR COLLINS' MEETING WAS 
ATTENDED BY SKOCH, OSD, AND JLAURENDEAU, EUR/PRM. 
INCLUDED IN THE WORKING LUNCH HOSTED BY AMBASSADOR 
MORNINGSTAR WERE WTAYLOR, S/NIS/C; DPRESSLEY, AID; DSTEIN, 
TDA; AND JMAYHEW, EUR/CASA. SROSENBERRY EUR/CASA SERVED 
AS NOTETAKER AND DZARECHNAK INTERPRETED AT EACH EVENT.

3. ...fel-DURING HIS MEETINGS WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT 
AND AMBASSADOR COLLINS, MENAGHARISHVILI FOCUSED ON 
GEORGIA'S EFFORTS TO SOLVE PROBLEMS AT HOME AND TO BUILD 
REGIONAL PARTNERSHIPS IN THE FACE OF SOMETIMES INTENSE 
PRESSURE FROM MOSCOW TO CONCENTRATE ON MAINTAINING A 
GEORGIAN-RUSSIAN "STRATEGIC" PARTNERSHIP." HE CITED 
RECENT VISITS TO AND/OR AGREEMENTS WITH KAZAKSTAN, 
UZBEKISTAN, TURKMENISTAN, UKRAINE, ARMENIA AND AZERBAIJAN 
AS POSITIVE EFFORTS, BUT HE ALSO NOTED MOSCOW'S NERVOUS 
REACTION TO THESE INITIATIVES. 
4. .fel DESCRIBING GEORGIA'S EFFORT TO ESTABLISH A 
CONSTRUCTIVE, BILATERAL DIALOGUE WITH MOSCOW ON A RANGE OF 
ISSUES, INCLUDING REGIONAL COOPERATION, MENAGHARISHVILI 
SAID THAT, GIVEN THE CONFLICTING CIRCLES AND DIRECTIONS OF 
RUSSIA'S CURRENT POLICY PROCESS, IT IS DIFFICULT TO FIND A 
CHANNEL TO THE CORE OF THE RUSSIAN POLICY PROCESS. EVEN 
ON THE ISSUE OF CIS, WHICH GEORGIA BELIEVES COULD PLAY A 
VERY POSITIVE ROLE, MOSCOW SEEMS UNABLE TO DEVELOP A CLEAR 
VISION OF HOW TO MOVE AHEAD. AT THE SAME TIME, THE 
RUSSIANS JEALOUSLY GUARD THEIR TRADITIONALLY DOMINANT ROLE 
IN THE REGION. 
5. (t) MENAGHARISHVILI POINTED TO ABKHAZIA AND 
NAGORNO-KARABAKH AS SPECIFIC PROBLEMS WHICH WILL CONTINUE 
TO FESTER UNTIL MOSCOW IS PREPARED TO FACILITATE A 
SOLUTION. THE FORMIN REPORTED THAT UN SYG BOUTROS-GALI 
HAD INFORMED HIM ON SEPTEMBER 25TH THAT, SINCE IT APPEARS 
NEITHER THE UN NOR THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY CAN PLAY A 
DIRECT ROLE IN SOLVING THE ABKHAZIA PROBLEM, THE UN 
INTENDS TO LEAVE THE ISSUE WITH RUSSIA. MENAGHARISHVILI 
APPEALED TO BOTH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND AMBASSADOR 
COLLINS FOR THE U.S. TO PUT PRESSURE ON MOSCOW TO PUSH FOR 
A SOLUTION CONSISTENT WITH GEORGIAN TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY 
AND TO BECOME DIRECTLY INVOLVED IF NECESSARY. IN HIS 
MEETING WITH AMBASSADOR COLLINS THE FORMIN ADDED THAT 
RESOLUTION OF N-K, WHICH CURRENTLY SERVES AS THE GREATEST 
OBSTACLE TO THREE-WAY COOPERATION IN THE CAUCASUS, IS EVEN 
MORE DEPENDENT THAN ABKHAZIA ON DIRECT RUSSIAN ACTION.

MOSCOW'S FOREIGN POLICY CONTEXT MAKE DIALOGUE DIFFICULT. 
HE OFFERED THAT THE RUSSIANS' INABILITY TO MAKE UP THEIR 
MIND ABOUT NATO EXPANSION MAY EXPLAIN THEIR HOLDING BACK 
ON OTHER ISSUES. AMBASSADOR COLLINS NOTED IN HIS MEETING 
THAT WHILE RUSSIAN SUPPORT WILL BE NECESSARY TO RESOLVE 
EACH OF THE REGIONAL CONFLICTS, IT IS NOT CLEAR THAT

10. IN EACH OF HIS MEETINGS AND AT THE WORKING LUNCH

INDEPENDENTLY PROTECTING GEORGIA'S BORDERS AND PORTS AND

EFFORT IS UNDERWAY TO DEVELOP A STRATEGY FOR PROVIDING 
ASSISTANCE IN THESE AREAS. IN ADDITION, DEPUTY SECRETARY

INTEREST IN THE PROCESS.
