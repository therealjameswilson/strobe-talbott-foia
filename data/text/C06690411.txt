Dear Mr. Brody: 
Thank you for your March 19 letter to Secretary Christopher 
and your April 6 letter to me regarding your contract with the 
Russian Central Bank for the design of a replacement for the 
interbank funds transfer system currently in use. 
Upon your return, I suggest that you meet with Mr.,Richard 
Kauzlarich, Principal Deputy to the Special Adviser for the New 
Independent States, and Mr. William Taylor, Office of the 
Coordinator, for Assistance to the New Independent States, to 
discuss your activities with the Central Bank and your 
proposals for future bank training programs. Please contact 
Ms. Robin Meyer, at (202) 736-4441, to arrange this meeting. 
I appreciate your commitment to RusSia as it undertakes 
this historic transition to a market-based economy. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador At Large and 
Special Adviser to The Secretary 
On the New Independent States 
Mr. Clifford L. Brody
