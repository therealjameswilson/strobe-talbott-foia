'.~SUMMARY: DURING A BREAKFAST IN HONOR OF DEPUTY
SECRETARY TALBOTT AND PARTY, SENIOR LDP LEADERS REINFORCED
THE IMPORTANCE OF THE U.S.-JAPAN SECURITY ALLIANCE, REVIEWED
NEXT STEPS IN RELATIONS WITH NORTH KOREA, AND EXCHANGED VIEWS
ON THE NEED FOR FURTHER ECONOMIC LIBERALIZATION. LDP POLICY
AFFAIRS RESEARCH COUNCIL CHAIRMAN SHIZUKA KAMEI EXPRESSED
SUPPORT FOR A RENEWAL OF THE SPECIAL MEASURES AGREEMENT (SMA)
BUT ASKED THE U.S. TO UNDERSTAND THE PRESSURES THE GOJ IS
FACING TO CUT CURRENT LEVELS OF HOST NATION SUPPORT (HNS).
ON KOREA, LDP MEMBERS DISAGREED ON THE VALUE OF EXTENDING
JAPANESE FOOD AID TO NORTH KOREA, ALTHOUGH ALL CONCURRED THAT
ANY JAPANESE INITIATIVE BE USED IN SUPPORT OF THE TRILATERAL
APPROACH. KAMEI AND DEPUTY NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR
STEINBERG ALSO ENGAGED IN AN EXCHANGE ON TRADE POLICY,
ANTI-DUMPING, AND THE WTO. END SUMMARY.

4. ~ EMPHASIZING THAT HE WAS SPEAKING ON BEHALF OF BOTH

POLICY CHIEF, KAMEI EXPRESSED STRONG SUPPORT FOR'RENEWING THE

ASSISTANCE TO U.S. FORCES. KAMEI REJECTED THOSE WHO
CHARACTERIZE JAPAN'S HOST NATION SUPPORT (HNS) AS "SYMPATHY"
OR "CHARITY, " ARGUING THAT GIVEN THE ALLIANCE'S UNEVEN
NATURE, FINANCIAL SUPPORT FOR U.S. FORCES WAS A JAPANESE
RESPONSIBILITY. HOWEVER, KAMEI REQUESTED THAT THE USG
UNDERSTAND THE PRESSURE THE GOJ WAS UNDER ON HNS, NOTING THAT
THE RELATIVE FISCAL CONDITIONS IN JAPAN AND THE UNITED STATES
IS THE OPPOSITE OF WHAT IT WAS THE LAST TIME THE TWO
COUNTRIES AGREED ON A CHANGE IN HNS LEVELS. ON ATSUGI, KAMEI
SAID THE GOJ IS DOING EVERYTHING IT CAN TO RESOLVE THE
PROBLEM AT AN EARLY DATE. KAMEI ACKNOWLEDGED THE IMPORTANCE
OF U.S. BASES IN OKINAWA NOT ONLY FOR REGIONAL SECURITY, BUT
ALSO FOR JAPAN'S DEFENSE. HE STATED THAT RESOLVING THE
FUTENMA AND OTHER BASE RELOCATION ISSUES WAS REALLY Ai
DOMESTIC GOJ RESPONSIBILITY.
