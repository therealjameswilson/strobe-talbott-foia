I want to thank you for introducing me at my Confirmation 
Hearing. I apologize for not writing to you sooner, but I have 
been caught up with the summit and its aftermath. You did me a 
great honor. I was. already grateful to you for having proposed

supporting reform in Russia, and I hope I can.rely on your good 
counsel as well as that of your staff. 
Thanks again for your support. I look forward to working 
with you.
