Thank you for your letter dated March 31 regarding the 
Center for Applied Biotechnology in Agriculture (CABA) and its 
Agricultural Initiative for Russia and the Commonwealth of 
Independent States. I apologize for the lateness of this 
response. 
The United States has been active in promoting both 
agricultural exchanges as well as agribusiness development in 
the New Independent States (NIS) of the former Soviet Union. 
Under its Food Systems Restructuring project, the Agency for 
International Development (AID) has awarded grants to three 
agribusiness trade associations to promote trade and investment 
activities in the NIS in order to increase the efficiency of 
the NIS food system. The three agribusiness trade associations 
are: Citizens Network Agribusiness Alliance (202-639-8889), 
Tri-Valley Growers (415-445-2014) and Agricultural Cooperative 
Development International (ACDI) (202-638-4661). These 
associations will provide funds to their member companies and 
possibly others to encourage private U.S. investment in 
agricultural production, processing, and marketing in the NIS. 
We have encouraged CABA to submit its proposal to these 
associations for potential subgrant funding. CABA may also 
contact the three associations for a list of the subgrant 
companies with which CABA may be able to develop joint 
activities. Additionally, CABA may wish to consider contacting 
the Eurasia Foundation. The Eurasia Foundation is a new, 
privately managed grantrnaking organization established with 
financing from US A.I.D. The Foundation supports technical 
assistance, training, educational and policy programs in the 
NIS covering a wide range of activities in economic and 
democratic reform. 
We look forward to the implementation of this agribusiness 
initiative as a key element in the U.S. effort to support the 
development of private food and agricultural systems in the 
NIS. We have encouraged CABA to contact these agribusiness
