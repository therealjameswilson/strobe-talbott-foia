6. --{1t-THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THE COMPOSITION AND 
AUSPICES OF THE INTERNATIONAL PRESENCE IN KOSOVO WAS 
CLOSELY LINKED TO THE TIMETABLE FOR WITHDRAWING FRY 
FORCES. THE KEY QUESTION WAS HOW TO ACHIEVE THE OBJECTIVE 
OF RETURNING THE REFUGEES. GIVEN THE EVENTS OF THE 
PREVIOUS 15 MONTHS, THE REFUGEES WOULD NOT RETURN UNLESS 
THE ARMED SERB PRESENCE WAS REDUCED TO AN ABSOLUTE 
MINIMUM, IF NOT ZERO. THE USG, HOWEVER, RECOGNIZED THE 
NEED FOR A TOKEN SERB PRESENCE TO DEMONSTRATE FRY 
SOVEREIGNTY OVER THE AREA. AVDEYEV AGREED THIS WAS 
IMPORTANT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID EVERYONE WANTED 
KOSOVO TO REMAIN PART OF THE FRY. THE USG MAS STUDYING 
HOW BEST IO USE THE UN SECURITY COUNCIL TO ACHIEVE A 
POSITIVE DIPLOMATIC OUTCOME, AS WELL AS HOW TO MAXIMIZE 
RUSSIA'S ROLE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WAS PLEASED THE EU 
HAD REFERRED TO A UNSC RESOLUTION IN ITS APRIL 15 MEETING.
