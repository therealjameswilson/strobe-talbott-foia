3. DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT CALLED IN INDIAN 
AMBASSADOR CHANDRA ON APRIL 11 TO DELIVER A STRONG DEMARCHE 
 /TALBOTT OPENED THE MEETING BY THANKING 
AMBASSADOR CHANDRA FOR HIS ROLE IN CONVINCING THE INDIAN 
DELEGATION TO THE CHEMICAL WEAPON CONVENTION (CWC) PREPCON 
MEETING IN THE HAGUE NOT TO CALL FINALLY FOR A POSTPONEMENT 
OF THE SCHEDULED APRIL 29 ENTRY INTO FORCE OF THE TREATY. 
HE PRAISED CHANDRA'S GOOD WORK ON THE MATTER, BUT SAID IT 
ESTABLISHED AN IRONIC CONTEXT FOR THE DISCUSSION AT HAND. 
THE CWC DEALS WITH WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION (WMD) AND 
PRITHVI MISSILES ARE A VEHICLE FOR THESE WEAPONS.

AS A SURPRISE. THERE HAD BEEN DISCUSSIONS ABOUT PRITHVI 
BETWEEN FRANK WISNER AND FOREIGN MINISTER GUJRAL AND 
PRINCIPAL SECRETARY CHANDRAN IN RECENT DAYS. WE ARE

10. AMBASSADOR CHANDRA TOOK THE OPPORTUNITY TO RAISE WITH 
TALBOTT SEVERAL POSSIBLE HIGH LEVEL VISITS. HE HOPED A 
FOREIGN MINISTER VISIT COULD BE RESCHEDULED SOON; HE 
PRESSED FOR HIGH LEVEL MEETINGS FOR THE VISIT OF VICE 
PRESIDENT NARAYANAN; AND HE URGED THAT THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
ENCOURAGE PRESIDENT CLINTON TO VISIT INDIA SOMETIME DURING 
THE FIFTIETH ANNIVERSARY CELEBRATIONS. TALBOTT REITERATED 
THE PERSONAL INTEREST OF THE SECRETARY IN IMPROVING 
RELATIONS IN THE REGION AND THANKED CHANDRA FOR COMING IN 
ON SUCH SHORT NOTICE.
