December 30, 1993 
In retrospect, the many times we sat around the table in the Pratt House 
begins to reveal a pattern. Though none of us could have foreseen or predicted what 
has happened, there is a sense of unconscious preparation for the future in our 
discussions. 
What you are now doing sounds fascinating and I am sure that you are 
reluctant to dilute it with other things. But the world is wide and diverse, and as 
Deputy Secretary there will be many things to arouse your interest and curiosity. 
Congratulations -- and all the best to you and Brooke for the New Year and 
thereafter.
