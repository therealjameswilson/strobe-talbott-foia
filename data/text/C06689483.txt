It was a great pleasure to have the oppôrtunity to meet you and your colleagues on 
April 22. We found your reflections on the recent Vancouver summit and insights into 
developments in Russia and associated states most illuminating. 
We also appreciated the interest you showed in Chevron's new joint venture in 
Kazakhstan for development of the Tengiz oil field. We continue to have a very positive.
view of this new project. Our people are continuing work on the logistics of generating 
hard currency for the exported oil, and as yet have no clear solution. We are hopeful 
that this will resolve itself within a few days, but if it does not we will bear in mind your 
generous expression of willingness to consider making available the Government's good 
offices. 
Once again, many thanks for the generous amount of time you spent with us on what was 
a very busy day. 
With best wishes and kind regards.
