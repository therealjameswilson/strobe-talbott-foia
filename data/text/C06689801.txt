RELEASE IN PART 
B6 
On the day the enclosed picture was taken, I think you were inside working on the translation 
of Khrushchev's manuscript. 
After two years as a Marshall Scholar at London School of Economics, I spent three years as 
an officer in the Navy; and I have been in commercial/investment real estate ever since. 
Much more conservative now than I was in the heady days of the McCarthy Campaign, when 
Taylor Branch and I were members of the Georgia Challenge Delegation, I have 
nevertheless kept up a strong interest in affordable housing and was, for example, the 
chairman of Mayor Maynard Jackson's Transition Team on Affordable Housing here in 
Atlanta. 
For some reason I studied Russian at the University of North Carolina all those years ago, 
and for the past two years I have been trying to figure out how to make money doing real 
estate development and brokerage in Russia and Ukraine. 
I edited and wrote major portions of the first, and as far as I know only, Real Estate 
Concepts course translated completely into Russian, which we gave to the fifty top managers 
of Moscapstroi eighteen months ago in Moscow. Enclosed is a copy of an article about that 
course, which is now owned by the non-profit Commercial Investment Real Estate Institute 
in Chicago (for which I am one of the part-time Senior Instructors). 
I have made eight trips to Moscow and Kiev in the past twenty-two months and brokered the.
land acquisition for the new Capital City Plaza office project in Moscow. I hope that 
through the Eastern European Real Property Foundation, funded by AID through the 
National Association of Realtors, we can teach this same real estate course to the city 
administration in Kiev, which has officially invited us.
