SUBJECT: AMBASSADOR HOLBROOKE'S RESTRICTED MEETING WITH RS 
PM DODIK 
(U) CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR RICHARD C. HOLBROOKE, PER 
REASONS 1.5 (B) AND (D). 
1.."*C,L,IN A RESTRICTED MEETING WITH DODIK FOLLOWING THE 
LARGER SESSION REPORTED SEPTEL, DODIK RAISED KARADZIC'S 
WHEREABOUTS AND WAYS THAT HE COULD ASSIST IN HIS 
APPREHENSION. 
2.-4C.L._"YOU MUST GET HIM AS SOON AS POSSIBLE," DODIK SAID. 
HE PARTICULARLY EMPHASIZED THE IMPORTANCE OF PICKING UP 
KARADZIC BEFORE THE SPRING ELECTIONS. (HE ALSO SAID THAT 
KRAJISNIK IS RUNNING WHAT AMOUNTS TO A MAFIA OPERATION, AND 
ENCOURAGED US TO GO AFTER HIM TOO.) 
3.-.4.C.L.DODIK INFORMED ME THAT HE THINKS KARADZIC IS IN THE 
BORDER AREA NEAR BIJELJINA, PERHAPS NEAR WHERE POPLASEN IS 
BELIEVED TO HANG OUT. HE FREQUENTLY TRAVELS BY CAR TO 
BELGRADE AND "CROSSES THE DRINA", BUT HE IS HARD TO SPOT 
BECAUSE HE CHANGES VEHICLES OFTEN.' HE IS GENERALLY 
ACCOMPANIED BY 30-50 ARMED MEN. DODIK OFFERED TO PROVIDE 
PEOPLE WHO WILL OBSERVE HIS MOVEMENTS AND HELP TRACK HIM. 
HE NOTED THAT HIS NEW INTELLIGENCE CHIEF AND CLOSE 
ASSOCIATE IELECEVIC WOULD BE COMING TO WASHINGTON IN EARLY 
'NOVEMBER FOR CONSULTATIONS, AND WOULD BE ABLE TO WORK ON' 
OPERATIONAL DETAILS. 
4. -K4 I POINTED OUT THAT WHILE SUCCESS WOULD LEAD TO 
DRAMATIC PROGRESS, A FAILED ATTEMPT AT CAPTURING KARADZIC 
WOULD BE A MAJOR PROBLEM. FIRST RATE INTELLIGENCE WOULD BE 
THE KEY. DODIK EMPHASIZED THAT HE WOULD WORK ON THIS ISSUE 
PERSONALLY. I URGED HIM TO DISCUSS THIS ISSUE IN 
CONFIDENCE WITH JIM DOBBINS AND CHRIS HILL. 
5. 7tt-DODIK ALSO MENTIONED AN APPROACH TWO MONTHS AGO 
THROUGH POPLASEN. KARADZIC ALLEGEDLY TOLD POPLASEN THAT HE 
WOULD BE PREPARED TO SURRENDER TO THE FRENCH ON CONDITION 
THAT HE WOULD BE TRIED-IN FRANCE RATHER THAN THE HAGUE, AND 
SERVE HIS SENTENCE IN A FRENCH PRISON. DODIK MADE CLEAR 
THAT HE DOES NOT TRUST KARADZIC, BUT EMPHASIZED THE 
IMPORTANCE OF GETTING HIM INTO A COURTROOM. "EVEN AN RS 
TRIAL WOULD BE GOOD." I REJECTED THAT PROPOSAL, MAKING IT
