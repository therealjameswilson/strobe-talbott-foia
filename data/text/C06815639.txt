PROCESS. SATTAR ASSURED PICKERING GOP PUBLIC STATEMENTS
WOULD SIMPLY CONDEMN THE ATTACKS (SATTAR PLANS A BBC

U.S. SHOCKED AND APPALLED AT MASSACRE
2. ~ PICKERING CALLED SATTAR AT 2:15 P.M. ON WEDNESDAY,
AUGUST 2 TO CONVEY U.S. CONCERN OUER THE LATEST VIOLENCE
IN KASHMIR AND THE NEED TO KEEP THE HIZBUL MUJAHIDEEN (HM)
CEASEFIRE GOING. PICKERING TOLD SATTAR THAT PRESIDENT
CLINTON HAD ALREADY CALLED INDIAN PM UAJPAYEE ABOUT THE
MASSACRE AND THAT WE WERE MAKING PUBLIC STATEMENTS
CONDEMNING THE ATTACKS. HE ALSO NOTED HIS CALL TO INDIAN
FOREIGN SECRETARY MANSINGH EARLIER TODAY (SEE SEPTEL)
ENCOURAGING INDIA TO "KEEP THE PROCESS GOING. " PICKERING
NOTED THAT WE HAVE REACHED A CRITICAL POINT, WHEN, AS IN
OTHER PARTS OF THE WORLD PURSUING PEACE, CERTAIN FORCES
ARE TRYING TO DESTROY THE PEACE PROCESS.
3. ~PICKERING TOLD SATTAR THAT WHILE WE DO NOT KNOW
FOR CERTAIN WHO THE PERPETRATORS ARE, THE GOI HAS TAKEN
MEMBERS OF THE LASHKAR-E-TOIBA (LT) INTO CUSTODY. HE
NOTED THAT IF EVIDENCE POINTS TO LT INVOLVEMENT, WE COULD
WELL MOVE TO DESIGNATE IT AS A FOREIGN TERRORIST
ORGANIZATION. WHILE THE GOP DOES NOT CONTROL ALL OF THE
MILITANTS, PICKERING NOTED THAT THE GOP CAN USE ITS
INFLUENCE TO STOP THEM FROM "KILLING THE PROCESS." SATTAR
TOLD PICKERING THAT THE GOP "WATCHES THE LT CAREFULLY" AND
THAT THEY CLAIM CREDIT FOR THINGS THEY HAVE NOT DONE NOR
HAVE THE CAPABILITY TO DO. HE SAID THEY ARE A "SELF-
SERVING GROUP WHO WANT CREDIT." SATTAR ADDED THAT IF THE
U.S. "DECLARES THEM TERRORIST, WE HAVE NOTHING TO SAY; GO-
AHEAD, DO SO, WE OFFER THEM NO PROTECTION." SATTAR SAID
THAT BASED ON THEIR SOURCES HE WILL PROVIDE US WITH A
READ-OUT TOMORROW ON WHO MIGHT BE RESPONSIBLE,
4. ~ PICKERING STRESSED GOP'S CRITICAL ROLE IN
PREUENTING THE HM CEASEFIRE FROM BEING DERAILED.

5. ~ WHEN ASKED BY PICKERING FOR HIS REACTION TO THE
MASSACRES, SATTAR SAID THAT THE GOP OFFICIALLY CONDEMNED
THE ATTACKS WITHOUT SPECULATING ON WHO THE PERPETRATORS
WERE. HE THEN PROCEEDED TO SPECULATE, RAISING THE
INCIDENT OF ATTACKS AGAINST THE SIKHS RIGHT BEFORE
PRESIDENT CLINTON'S VISIT (3/25-26)AND THAT THERE HAD
NEVER BEEN ANY GROUP CLAIMING CREDIT. HE SAID THAT "THERE
ARE A GROUP OF RENEGADES CULTIVATED BY INDIAN
INTELLIGENCE" TO ATTACK THE "FREEDOM STRUGGLE". HE ADDED
THAT "SOME PEOPLE DESCRIBED tTHEM] AS WEARING INDIAN
UNIFOPMS '"
6. ~ WITH REGARD TO YESTERDAY'S MASSACRES, SATTAR
STRESSED THAT THE KASHMIRI "FREEDOM FIGHTERS" "HAVE
NOTHING TO GAIN AND A LOT TO LOSE" BY PERPETRATING SUCH AN
ATTACK, THAT THEY COULD NOT HAVE "INDULGED IN SUCH A
HORRIBLE CRIME." SATTAR ADDED THAT HE WOULD BE ON THE BBC
TOMORROW AND WOULD REFRAIN FROM SPECULATING AND SIMPLY
CONDEMN THE CRIME. PICKERING NOTED THAT THE GOP HAD
ALREADY MADE PUBLIC STATEMENTS POINTING TO THE INDIANS.
SATTAR REPLIED THAT HE WOULD FORWARD THE FOREIGN OFFICE'S
STATEMENT TO THE EMBASSY FOR HIM. PICKERING URGED SATTAR
NOT TO SPECULATE SINCE IT WOULD NOT BE PRODUCTIVE. SATTAR
AGREED. HE LATER COMPLAINED THAT VAJPAYEE ACCUSED
PAKISTAN OF BEING INVOLVED IN THE MASSACRE.

MATTERS BETTER."
