SUBJECT: RUSSIAN PRESIDENTIAL FOREIGN AFFAIRS ADVISOR 
RYURIKOV: OCTOBER 23 SUMMIT ISSUES 
SECTION 01 OF 02, MOSCOW 33931 
1. SECRET - ENTIRE TEXT AS A RESULT OF INFORMATION 
RECEIVED FROM FOREIGN GOVERNMENT. 
2. SUMMARY: DEPSEC TALBOTT AND PRESIDENTIAL FOREIGN 
AFFAIRS ADVISOR RYURIKOV AGREED THAT BOSNIA AND 
EUROPEAN SECURITY ISSUES, WITH THEIR CLOSE 
INTER-LINKAGE, WOULD BE AT THE CENTER OF THE OCTOBER 23 
SUMMIT. TALBOTT PRESSED RYURIKOV ON TNE NEED FOR A 
SATISFACTORY SETTLEMENT ON THE CFE PROBLEM BEFORE 
NOVEMBER 17, AND STRESSED THAT THE HYDE PARK SUMMIT BE 
REFLECTIVE OF THE SPIRIT OF MAY, 1995, AND NOT BUDAPEST 
OF DECEMBER, 1994. END SUMMARY. 
3. AFTER AN EXCNANGE OF GREETINGS, RYURIKOV REMARKED 
THAT HE HAD JUST COME FROM TNE KREMLIN PALACE WHERE 
YELTSIN ON A TELE BRIDGE TO PARIS HAD RESPONDED TO A 
QUESTION THAT THE ESSENCE OF THE OCTOBER 23 SUMMIT 
WOULD BE TO PRESERVE ALL THAT HAS BEEN ACCOMPLISHED 
THROUGH RUSSIAN-AMERICAN COOPERATION. TALBOTT 
SUGGESTED THAT ITS GOAL BE NOT ONLY TO PRESERVE, BUT 
ALSO TO "BUILD UPON." RYURIKOV SAID THE RUSSIANS ALSO 
SAW IT THAT WAY. 
4. RYURIKOV SUGGESTED THAT PRESIDENT YELTSIN IS 
PREPARING ACTIVELY. TALBOTT EXPLAINED THAT PRESIDENT 
CLINTON WILL WANT TO TALK ABOUT PROSPECTS FOR RELATIONS 
IN GENERAL, SINCE HE IS ACUTELY INTERESTED IN AND 
ATTACNES MAJOR IMPORTANCE TO THE DEVELOPING 
RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE TWO STATES, PARTICULARLY IN 
THE COMING POLITICAL YEAR. IT WILL BE A KEY 
OPPORTUNITY FOR THE TWO PRESIDENTS TO EXCHANGE VIEWS ON 
THEIR ACCOMPLISHMENTS AND THE ISSUES WHICH LIE AHEAD 
FOR BOTH OF THEM IN 1996. TALBOTT STRESSED THE 
TREMENDOUS IMPORTANCE THAT WE PLACE ON THIS 
RELATIONSNIP AND THE FACT THAT BOTH POLITICAL LEADERS 
WILL NEED TO ASSURE THE SUCCESS OF OUR PARTNERSHIP 
AGAINST THE ATTACKS OF BOTH OPPONENTS AND 
CIRCUMSTANCES.

5. BOTH AGREED THAT BOSNIA AND EUROPEAN SECURITY WOULD 
BE CENTRAL QUESTIONS AND TAKE A SUBSTANTIAL AMOUNT OF 
TIME. THERE ALSO WOULD NEED TO BE SOME FOCUS ON CFE 
AND THE IRAN REACTOR QUESTION, WHICH THE VICE PRESIDENT 
AND PRIME MINISTER ALSO DISCUSSED IN MAINE. 
6. TALBOTT NOTED THAT EUROPEAN SECURITY AND PEACE IN 
THE BALKANS CERTAINLY WERE CONNECTED. RYURIKOV PUT AN 
EMPHASIS ON THE FACT THE TWO PRESIDENTS REALLY NEED TO 
TALK ABOUT THE LARGE ISSUES, THEY WERE IMPORTANT 
SERIOUS.POLITICAL LEADERS, AND THEY NEED TO EXCHANGE 
VIEWS ON THESE KEY QUESTIONS, NOT JUST ON SMALL SCALE 
POINTS. 
7. TALBOTT NOTED THAT CFE HAD TO BE WORKED OUT BY 
NOVEMBER 17, AND THAT WE WERE TRYING TO BUILD NOW ON A 
PROPOSAL THAT PRESIDENT YELTSIN HIMSELF HAD MADE TO 
.MODIFY AND ADJUST THE MAP. 
8. TALBOTT SAID EUROPEAN SECURITY NEEDS TO BE PURSUED 
AS WELL, AND PRESIDENT CLINTON WILL RENEW HIS 
ASSURANCES CONCERNING TNE DECEMBER NATO MINISTERIAL AND 
PLANS FOR NATO EXPANSION NEXT YEAR. TALBOTT SAID HE 
CERTAINLY HOPED THIS WOULD BE A CONVERSATION ALONG THE 
LINES OF MAY, 1995, AND WITHOUT THE SURPRISES OF LAST 
YEAR. 
9. RYURIKOV SAID THERE WOULD BE NO UNEXPECTED PROBLEMS 
RAISED. YELTSIN IS READY TO TALK ABOUT CFE AND IS 
INTERESTED IN A SPEEDY SOLUTION OF THE PROBLEM. MORE 
WORK CAN BE DONE TO HELP GET IT SOLVED BY NOVEMBER 17, 
AND THE RUSSIANS HAVE-A SERIOUS DESIRE TO PRESERVE THE 
RELATIONSHIP AND THEN-BUILD UPON IT, AND HAVE THE SAME 
ASSESSMENT OF OUR PARTNERSHIP, AS THE U.S. DOES, THAT 
IT IS NORMAL AND HEALTHY AND IT WILL BE A GOOD MEETING. 
10. ON BOSNIA, SECRETARY TALBOTT INDICATED WE HAD BEEN 
WORKING HARD SINCE GENEVA AND THAT WE HAD MIXED 
DELEGATION (MOD AND MFA) DISCUSSIONS YESTERDAY AND THIS 
MORNING, THERE STILL WERE'KEY DIFFERENCES AND A GREAT 
DEAL OF WORK NEEDED TO BE DONE. IT IS A DIFFICULT 
PROBLEM, BUT WE HAD GOOD TALKS AT THE MOD AND WE HAD 
MADE SOME PROGRESS. THERE ARE A COMBINATION OF VERY 
TECHNICAL ISSUES AND IMPORTANT POLITICAL PROBLEMS. EW . 
NEED TO GET THE TWO-MOD'S WORKING TOGETHER INTHAT -
REGARD. . _
