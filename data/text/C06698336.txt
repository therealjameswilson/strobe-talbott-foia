5. -(-51- ALTHOUGH MUCH REMAINED TO BE DONE, BILDT WAS 
CAREFULLY WATCHING KARADZIC'S PUBLIC APPEARANCES. HE 
SAID TELEVISION WAS THE WEAPON OF CHOICE WHEN 
APPROACHING AN ELECTION AND THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY 
WOULD NOT ALLOW KARADZIC TO EXPLOIT IT. HE ADDED THAT 
HE WAS ALSO USING MILOSEVIC TO PREVENT KARADZIC FROM 
APPEARING ON TELEVISION. HE NOTED KARADZIC HAD BEEN OFF 
THE AIR FOR A COUPLE OF.DAYS AND SAID IT SHOULD BE 
CLEARER IN A FEW DAYS WHETHER THAT ABSENCE WAS INDEED 
MEANINGFUL. HE STRESSED IT WAS ABSOLUTELY CRITICAL THAT 
KARADZIC NOT BE SEEN OR HEARD. HE ADDED HE WAS ALSO 
MONITORING WHETHER KARADZIC WAS EXERCISING POWER. HE 
SAID MS. PLAVSIC SHOULD BE MAKING IT CLEAR THAT SHE IS 
THE ONE EXERCISING POWER IN REPUBLIKA SRPSKA. 
6. -(131- BILDT COMMENTED ON THE DIFFICULTY OF ASSESSING 
THE EFFECTIVENESS OF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY'S
GRADUAL APPROACH, ARGUING THAT SUCCESS OR PROGRESS COULD 
ONLY BE EVALUATED OVER TIME. HE COMPLAINED ABOUT OSCE 
BOSNIA MISSION CHIEF FROWICK'S PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT 
REGARDING THE POSSIBLE BAN OF THE SERB DEMOCRATIC PARTY 
(SDS) FROM ELECTIONS IF KARADZIC WAS NOT REMOVED. BILDT 
SAID THE MOVE WAS UNWISE BECAUSE THE INTERNATIONAL 
COALITION WAS NOT LINED UP BEHIND IT. HE THEN STRESSED 
THE IMPORTANCE OF HAVING THE RUSSIANS ON BOARD, GIVEN 
THEIR INFLUENCE OVER THE BOSNIAN SERBS. HE SAID IF THE 
SERBS THOUGHT THE RUSSIANS WERE NOT ON BOARD WITH OSCE'S 
STRATEGY, THEY WOULD NOT TAKE THE INTERNATIONAL 
COMMUNITY SERIOUSLY. 
7. -t9i- BILDT SAID THE NEXT WEEK OR TEN DAYS SHOULD 
REVEAL WHETHER FROWICK'S STRATEGY REGARDING KARADZIC 
WILL SUCCEED. IF IT DOES NOT SUCCEED, THE INTERNATIONAL 
COMMUNITY MUST CONSIDER OTHER OPTIONS, INCLUDING BANNING 
THE SDS FROM ELECTIONS. HE BELIEVED, HOWEVER, IT WOULD 
BE VERY DIFFICULT FOR ELECTIONS TO BE HELD IN REPUBLIKA 
SRPSKA UNLESS THE SDS PARTICIPATED. 
8. -(11 BILDT STATED HIS BELIEF THAT THERE WAS A VERY 
HIGH LIKELIHOOD THAT THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY WOULD 
STUMBLE INTO THE NEED FOR MILITARY ACTION BY DEFAULT 
OTHER THAN BY DESIGN. HE NOTED HE HAD NOT BEEN AN 
ADVOCATE OF MILITARY ACTION, BUT HE BELIEVED A SITUATION 
COULD ARISE IN WHICH A MILITARY ACTION WOULD BE THE 
LEAST RISKY OPTION. HE SAID SUCH AN ACTION WOULD 
REQUIRE VERY SUBSTANTIAL PLANNING AND IMPLIED A 
SEMI-OCCUPATION MODE. HE ADDED THAT MLADIC WAS EVEN A 
MORE DIFFICULT PROBLEM THAN KARADZIC FROM A 
SECURITY/MILITARY POINT OF VIEW. 
9. 444 .BILDT SAID THE OPTIONS WERE DIFFICULT AND 
CALLED FOR STRATEGIC PLANNING WITH VARIOUS INTERNATIONAL 
ACTORS LINING UP TOGETHER. HE TOLD THE ACTING SECRETARY 
THE SWISS WERE DISTINCTLY UNHAPPY ABOUT FROWICK'S PUBLIC 
ANNOUNCEMENT AND WERE CAUGHT UNAWARE. HE ARGUED THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY SHOULD EITHER PUBLICLY ACCEPT A 
STRATEGY OF GRADUAL ACTION OR TAKE A BIGGER STEP, E.G. 
TAKE MILITARY ACTION AGAINST KARADZIC. BILDT 
ACKNOWLEDGED THE LATTER OPTION WAS HIGH RISK. 
10. -44H- THE ACTING SECRETARY TOLD BILDT GRADUALISM 
ITSELF CARRIED RISK AND A STRATEGY OF GRADUALISM WAS AT 
ODDS WITH THE URGENCY OF THE SITUATION AND THE RAPIDLY 
ARPROACHING ELECTIONS. HE COMMENTED THAT KARADZIC HAD 
PLAYED OFF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY'S GRADUALISM WITH 
SOME SUCCESS. WHILE THE ACTING SECRETARY TOOK BILDT'S 
CONCERNS SERIOUSLY, HE NOTED IT WAS HARD TO KNOW IF THE 
DAYTON ACCORDS AND THE ELECTIONS COULD BE TAKEN 
SERIOUSLY IN THE ABSENCE OF AN ULTIMATUM ON THE SDS FROM 
FROWICK.

13. THE ACTING SECRETARY ASKED WHETHER, TWO MONTHS 
AWAY FROM ELECTIONS, THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY COULD 
CONCEDE CREDIBILITY TO A PARTY WITH AN INDICTED WAR 
CRIMINAL AS ITS HEAD. HE SAID FROWICK'S ANSWER WAS NO. 
14. —WQ— BILDT RESPONDED BY SAYING THAT UNTIL FROWICK'S 
PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT, THE ANSWER HAD BEEN YES. HE 
EXPRESSED CONCERN THAT THE CHANGE IN POSITION MIGHT 
RESULT IN OSCE NOT BEING TAKEN SERIOUSLY. HE AGAIN 
STRESSED THE NEED FOR COORDINATED ACTION. 
15. --(-54- THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THE BOSNIAN SERBS 
HAVE A CLEAR CHOICE: THE SDS CAN PARTICIPATE WITHOUT 
KARADZIC OR IT WILL BE BANNED. THE BOSNIAN SERBS KNOW 
HOW TO KEEP SDS ON THE LANDSCAPE. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
AGREED WITH BILDT THAT SOLVING THE KARADZIC ISSUE, 
HOPEFULLY THROUGH POLITICAL AND DIPLOMATIC MEANS, STILL 
LEFT THE MLADIC ISSUE. HE SAID MLADIC WAS WORSE THAN 
KARADZIC, WITH MORE BLOOD ON HIS HANDS, BUT NOTED ONE 
IMPORTANT DISTINCTION BETWEEN THE TWO SERB LFDADERS. HE 
SAID MLADIC IS NOT ON THE ELECTION SCENE, WHILE KARADZIC 
REMAINS THERE. 
16. 441). THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID NO ONE WANTED TO SEE 
THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY STUMBLE INTO MILITARY 
ACTION. THE BEST WAY TO MINIMIZE THAT DANGER IS AN 
EFFECTIVE POLITICAL STRATEGY LEAVING KARADZIC WITH AS 
LITTLE ROOM AS POSSIBLE TO MANEUVER AGAINST THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE 
WAS TO MEET WITH HIS RUSSIAN COUNTERPART MAMEDOV LATER 
IN THE DAY AND HOPED TO KEEP THE RUSSIANS ON BOARD TO 
THE EXTENT POSSIBLE. 
17. -4g+ BILDT REITERATED HIS CONCERN THAT IF THE SERBS 
DETECT A MAJOR DIVERGENCE WITHIN THE INTERNATIONAL 
COMMUNITY, THE CHANCES FOR SUCCESS ARE MINIMIZED. 
18. --f-81 THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE WOULD RELAY
