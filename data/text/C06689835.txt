Dear Strobe: 
I returned to Washington this week and learned of your nomination as Deputy 
Secretary; I just wanted to send you a short note to say congratulations and to wish you the 
best. The country could not be better served. 
I hope that you will still have a chance to drop by the Council on Foreign Relations 
now and then; I've enjoyed having the chance to hear your views at our meetings and have 
appreciated the support that you have given the International Affairs Fellows program over 
the past several years. 
You are stepping into both a great challenge and a great opportunity—I look forward to 
watching you make the most of it. 
Sincerely, 
Bruce Berkowitz
