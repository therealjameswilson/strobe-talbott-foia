2. SUMMARY: The Deputy Secretary met with Mozambican 
Prime Minister Mocumbi September 20. He,used the occasion 
to express USG pleasure at the achievements Mozambique has 
made to date; to urge the Mozambicans to keep the economic 
reform program on track; and to stress the importance of 
local elections in the ongoing reconciliation process. He 
also cautioned them on their recent military contacts with 
Iran. Mocumbi said that his visit had found significant 
investor interest in Mozambique. Internally, the country 
was working to consolidate peace and planning for local 
elections next year. He specifically acknowledged the 
positive role Renamo leader Dhlakama was playing 
recently. As for contacts with Iran, MOcumbi said that 
they were well aware of IRAN'S positions and that they had 
only been interested in any material assistance 
desperately needed by the armed forces that Iran might 
provide. END SUMMARY. 
3. Deputy Secretary Talbott met with Mozambican Prime 
Minister Pascoal Mocumbi September 20. Mocumbi was 
accompanied by Trade Minister Oldemiro Baloi, Mozambican 
Ambassador Marcos Namashulua, Director of European and 
American Affairs GONCALVES Sengo, and Chief of Staff 
Silvestre Sechene. AF was represented by A/S Moose, AF/S 
Deputy Director Gallucci, and DeskOff Vann (notetaker). 
DEVELOPMENT AND RECONCILIATION 
4. The Deputy Secretary used the occasion to express USG 
support for the encouraging developments inside 
Mozambique. He noted that when he was last in Africa in 
1994, there was concern within SADC about the democratic 
process in Mozambique. Now, President Chissano is SADC 
Vice President.and Mozambique has gone from being a 
problem to a solution. The Prime Minister's mission to 
the U.S., reaching out to the private sector, was strongly 
in the right direction. Also, recent developments have 
given reason to be satisfied about reconciliation. The 
Deputy Secretary also pointed out that despite ongoing 
problems faced by our foreign assistance program, the USG 
remained interested in Mozambique.

5. Mocumbi commented that his mission to encourage 
investment had generated interest and that he hoped it 
would bear fruit. He mentioned the recent investments and 
potential investments of Seaboard, Edlow, Enron and Coca 
Cola as positive signs. He hoped the Seaboard investment 
in the Beira flour mill would eventually allow Mozambique 
to export flour. Likewise, Coca Cola planned to build two 
new plants (in BEIRA and Nampula), which could lead to 
regional exports. Concerning reconciliation, Mocumbi said 
there was reason to be more than satisfied. Renamo leader 
Dhlakama was "behaving excellently," he said, specifically 
citing Dhlakama's positive statements during his recent 
visit to Europe. Mocumbi said that despite the fragile 
economy, good things were happening. They were working to 
consolidate the peace. In preparation for municipal ' 
elections next year, they would be presenting a draft law 
on elections to parliament. He added that they would need 
support from external resources. 
REGIONAL PEACE: ROLE OF SADC 
6. The Deputy Secretary asked if SADC had taken a role or 
interest in Rwanda and Burundi. Mr. Moose, noting that he 
was departing for a trip concerning BURUNDI, expressed the 
hope that SADC would play an important role in these 
regional peace issues. Mocumbi said that SADC had 
discussed these issues and might be prepared to make a 
proposal if asked by Tanzania and others. He thought it 
could be possible to avoid a catastrophe In BURUNDI if all 
the regional neighbors could agree on a common agenda and 
if a commitment from the leadership in Burundi could be 
built. This would be delicate and would require a careful 
balance between pressures and incentives. 
RELATIONS WITH IRAN 
7. Noting the recent meetings between Mozambican defense 
officials and the Iranian Defense Minister, the Deputy 
Secretary underscored that the USG was concerned at such 
contacts and stressed that we would not want to see 
Mozambique entertaining the idea of a military 
relationship between Mozambique and Iran. Mocumbi 
understood the Deputy SECRETARY'S comments and explained 
that in the face of r,educed budgets, the defense sector 
had been looking at the possibility of Iranian assistance 
and could not close the doors to offers of aid. Mocumbi 
recounted that the GRM asked the Iranians for oil, 
hospitals, and other assistance to no avail. According to 
Mocumbi, the GRM remains aware of the need to be careful 
with Iran and appreciated our advice.
