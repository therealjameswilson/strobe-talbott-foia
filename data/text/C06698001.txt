the involvement of U.S. business and to promote 
investment. A couple of days ago there was a story in the 
Wall Street Journal about a couple of guys, a small group 
of investors, who are bringing modern telephone service to 
Uzbekistan. (Lucky for them that the small town where

it was a wonderful article because many small- and

I'd be curious to hear your thoughts: do you expect any

President Karimov: 
Yes. Regardless of who wins, pressures on the New 
Independent States will grow -- that is, independent of 
who wins in the second round. We have openly supported 
Yeltsin; he is the only one who can stand against the 
Communists. I personally like Yavlinskiy, but he can't 
stop the return of the Communists. 4C-4-
First of all, regarding Lebed: we consider him a 
proponent of aggressive nationalism. I don't know how 
long the alliance between him and Yeltsin will last. If 
it is long-lived, I am concerned. He is a supporter of an 
iron hand. His campaign had three slogans: restore 
order; root-out crirqe and corruption; and restore Russia's 
greatness. He doesn't have a party or a platform. Those 
who are unhappy with the state of affairs in Russia voted 
for him. This is the main thing: those who voted for him 
voted against Yeltsin. I don't think that Yeltsin will 
get many of Lebed's votes. (")-
The Communists have a permanent constituency: the 
nomenclatura; the military; and the elderly --
pensioners. Zyuganov engages in social demagoguery; this 
is his greatest weapon. And I must say that if those who 
carried out the reforms had not made so many mistakes, the 
Communists would not be where they are today. The other 
problem (for the government) is the lack of a strong 
social policy. Only recently did Yeltsin propose a 
realistic social policy; had he done this a year ago, it 
would have made a difference. {C-}-
All of the aims, all of Yeltsin's aims, are correct but 
they made a lot of mistakes with the mechanisms. There is 
also a great deal of corruption, which puts people off. 
Yeltsin should win. But even if he wins, this imperial 
ambition will persist and Russia's great-power interests
