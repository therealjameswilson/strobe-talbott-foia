SUMMARY 
2. '1.64- DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT UNDERSCORED U.S. SUPPORT 
FOR CAMBODIA AND EMPHASIZED CONCERNS ABOUT POLITICAL 
TENSIONS IN PHNOM PENH AND HUMAN RIGHTS PROBLEMS IN A JUNE 
5 MEETING WITH CAMBODIAN COMMERCE MINISTER CHAM PRASIDH. 
PRASIDH ACKNOWLEDGED THE IMPORTANCE OF KEEPING THE 
DEMOCRATIC TREND IN CAMBODIA UPWARD, BECAUSE IF IT TURNED 
DOWNWARD, CAMBODIA WOULD LOSE INTERNATIONAL CONFIDENCE AND 
SUPPORT. PRASIDH WAS OPTIMISTIC THAT THE TWO MAJOR 
PARTIES WOULD MAINTAIN THE COALITION GOVERNMENT; IN HIS 
WORDS, THERE WOULD BE NO "DIVORCE." END SUMMARY.

USAID MISSION IN PHNOM PENH. THE ADMINISTRATION WAS ALSO 
WORKING WITH CONGRESS TO GRANT CAMBODIA 
MOST-FAVORED-NATION STATUS.

TRUE, CORRECTIVE MEASURES WOULD HAVE TO BE TAKEN. THE 
IMPORTANT THING WAS TO KEEP THE DEMOCRATIC TREND MOVING

BE FUTILE TO TURN THE WHEEL AGAIN.
