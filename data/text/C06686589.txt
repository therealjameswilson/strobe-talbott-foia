Dear Mr. Deputy Prime Minister: 
Thank you for your letter describing the joint 
American-Russian IL-96 project. I congratulate you on the 
IL-96M's successful debut at the Paris Air Show this year. I 
understand that its introduction made the front pages of the 
major aviation periodicals. The U.S. government supports this 
joint U.S.-Russian project, which we hope can be a model for 
future commercial cooperation. 
Ilyushin, as well as its American partners, will need to 
work closely with the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) to 
certify this plane for the U.S. market. As you know, the FAA 
is currently working with your aviation authorities to conclude 
a bilateral airworthiness agreement, which would allow 
certification of the IL-96T, the cargo version of this plane. 
Once that agreement has been completed and the IL-96T has been 
certified, the FAA can do the further work necessary to certify 
the passenger version. The FAA has expended considerable time 
and energy on the joint Russian-American certification program 
and its efforts have the full support of the rest of the U.S. 
government. We believe that any technical problems can be 
worked out with the good will and close cooperation of your 
aviation authorities and ours. 
Once again, thank you for your letter and let me know if I 
can be of any further assistance.
