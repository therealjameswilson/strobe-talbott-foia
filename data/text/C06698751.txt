2. SUMMARY: IN SEPARATE MEETINGS WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT, DOD A/S KRAMER, AND NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR 
ANTHONY LAKE, PRESIDENT KABBAH URGED ADDITIONAL U.S. 
SUPPORT FOR SIERRA LEONE'S PEACE PROCESS, INCLUDING FOR 
IMPOSING A DEADLINE FOR THE REBELS TO SIGN AN AGREEMENT OR 
FACE UN-APPROVED MEASURES, AND FOR A MODEST UN MILITARY 
OBSERVER GROUP AFTER AN ACCORD IS SIGNED. KABBAH ALSO 
REQUESTED EXCESS MILITARY EQUIPMENT. KABBAH DECLINED TO 
RECOMMEND ANY POSSIBLE AFRICAN SYG CANDIDATE BEFORE 
AFRICA-WIDE CONSULTATIONS. HE NOTED THAT SIERRA LEONE 
WOULD LIKELY FOLLOW THE OAU'S LEAD. HE AND OTHER GOSL 
OFFICIALS EXPRESSED RESERVATIONS ABOUT THE APPARENT 
EXCLUSIVENESS OF THE PROPOSED ACRF, BUT SUPPORT THE FORCE 
IN PRINCIPLE. END SUMMARY. 
PEACE THROUGH PRESSURE 
3. DURING A FORTY MINUTE MEETING BETWEEN DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT AND PRESIDENT AHMAD TEJAN KABBAH IN WASHINGTON ON 
' OCTOBER 11, KABBAH SOUGHT U.S. SUPPORT FOR THE GOSL'S NEW 
INITIATIVES IN THE SECURITY COUNCIL TO MOVE SIERRA LEONE'S 
PEACE PROCESS FORWARD. KABBAH OUTLINED WHAT HAD BEEN 
EARLIER OBSTACLES TO REACHING A FINAL PEACE AGREEMENT 
BETWEEN THE GOSL AND FODAY SANKOH, LEADER OF THE 
'REVOLUTIONARY UNITED FRONT (RUF) -- REMOVAL OF THE SOUTH 
AFRICAN MERCENARY COMPANY EXECUTIVE OUTCOMES, AND 
MINISTERIAL POSITIONS FOR THE RUF. KABBAH SAID, HOWEVER, 
HE HAD ADDRESSED THOSE CONCERNS SEVERAL WEEKS AGO AND THE 
IVORIAN NEGOTIATORS ASSURED HIM SANKOH WAS PREPARED TO t
SIGN A FINAL AGREEMENT. SANKOH IS NOW STALLING AND THE 
DELAY IS LEADING GOSL HARD-LINERS TO PUSH FOR RENEWED 
MILITARY ACTION, OR RISK AN OFFENSIVE BY THE RUF. 
4. KABBAH URGED U.S. SUPPORT FOR THE PROPOSAL HE 
SUBMITTED INFORMALLY TO MEMBERS OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL IN 
NEW YORK ON OCTOBER 8, CALLING FOR A DECEMBER 1 DEADLINE 
FOR THE RUF TO SIGN A PEACE AGREEMENT OR FACE UN-APPROVED 
TRAVEL RESTRICTIONS, AND AN EVENTUAL WAR CRIMES TRIBUNAL 
(REFTEL). IN A SEPARATE MEETING WITH AF, KABBAH SAID THE

COUNCIL MEMBERS BELIEVED THE GOSL1S PROPOSALS WERE 
REASONABLE OPTIONS. HOWEVER, ONLY THE ITALIANS EXPRESSED 
ANY SUPPORT FOR THE IDEA OF A WAR CRIMES TRIBUNAL. DAS 
TWADDELL SUGGESTED CAUTION IN THIS REGARD, AS THE THREAT 
OF A TRIBUNAL COULD INCREASE SANKOn'S RELUCTANCE TO SIGN 
AN ACCORD FOR FEAR OF RETRIBUTION. KABBAH SAID THE 
TRIBUNAL IDEA ONLY RECENTLY EMERGED FOLLOWING THE ABUJA 
DISCUSSIONS ON USING A SIMILAR APPROACH TO BRING THE 
LIBERIAN WARLORDS TO THE TABLE. 
5. KABBAH SAID HE WOULD BE SEEKING A SECURITY COUNCIL 
RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF THE GOSL'S THREE-PHASED PEACE 
INITIATIVE. ADDITIONALLY, KABBAH NOTED THAT THE GOSL 
WOULD BE SEEKING A MODEST UN MILITARY OBSERVER FORCE --
ABOUT SIX PEOPLE -- AFTER A PEACE ACCORD WAS SIGNED AS A 
CONF/DENCE-BUILDING MECHANISM. 
6. KABBAH THANKED THE U.S. FOR SUPPORTING THE GOSL'S 
RECENT ELECTIONS AND THE ONGOING HUMANITARIAN PROGRAMS. 
HE SPECIFICALLY COMMENDED AID ASSISTANT ADMINISTRATOR 
STAFFORD AND OFDA DIRECTOR BORTON FOR RECENTLY TRAVELLING 
TO SIERRA LEONE TO FURTHER PROMOTE U.S. HUMANITARIAN 
ASSISTANCE. HOWEVER, KABBAH NOTED THE GOSL'S CONCERN OVER 
THE APPARENT LACK OF U.S. POST-ELECTIONS DEVELOPMENT AID. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE THE U.S. 
PLACES ON DEMOCRATIZATION GENERALLY, AND SPECIFICALLY IN 
AFRICA, AND NOTED THAT SIERRA LEONE WAS CONSIDERED A 
SUCCESS STORY IN THAT REGARD. HE NOTED THAT THE 
DEPARTMENT WOULD CONTINUE TO SEEK ASSISTANCE FOR COUNTRIES 
UNDERGOING DEMOCRATIZATION. 
U.S. AND IRAN: GOSL'S OLD AND NEW FRIENDS? 
7. KABBAH RELATED TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THE GOSL'S 
PARTICIPATION IN AN ANNUAL IRANIAN TRADE FAIR IN TEHERAN, 
AS ONE OF FIFTY-ONE COUNTRIES ATTENDING. HE EXPLAINED 
THAT HIS TRIP RESULTED FROM AN UNEXPECTED VISIT TO SIERRA 
LEONE BY THE IRANIAN DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER WHO ISSUED A 
PUBLIC INVITATION TO KABBAH. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
UNDERSCORED THAT THE U.S. SOUGHT TO ENSURE THAT KABBAH 
LIKE OTHER NATIONAL LEADERS AND OFFICIALS HE HAD RECENTLY 
BRIEFED (MALAWI, KYRGYSTAN, AND IVORY COAST) CLEARLY 
UNDERSTOOD THE BASIS FOR U.S. CONCERNS ABOUT IRAN'S 
SPONSORSHIP OF STATE TERRORISM AND UNABASHED OPPOSITION TO 
THE MIDDLE EAST PEACE PROCESS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ADDED 
THAT IF THE PRESIDENT REQUIRED ADDITIONAL INFORMATION 
ABOUT IRAN'S INVOLVEMENT IN STATE TERRORISM EMBASSY 
FREETOWN COULD ARRANGE A CONFIDENTIAL BRIEFING ON THIS 
SUBJECT.

8. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INQUIRED ABOUT THE GOSL'S VIEWS 
ON POSSIBLE AFRICAN CANDIDATES FOR THE SYG POSITION AND 
OUTLINED USG'S UNALTERABLE PREFERENCE FOR A LEADER READY, 
WILLING AND ABLE TO tFFECT MAJOR UN REFORMS. KABBAH 
RESPONDED THAT SIERRA LEONE'S POSITION WOULD LIKELY BE IN 
ACCORD WITH THAT OF THE OAU. KABBAH FURTHER STATED THAT 
THERE NEEDED TO BE A DISCUSSION BETWEEN ALL AFRICAN 
LEADERS BEFORE HE COULD SAY DEFINITIVELY WHAT SIERRA 
LEONE'S POSITION WOULD BE. HE ACKNOWLEDGED THE RISK OF 
AFRICANS MISSING AN OPPORTUNITY TO GET A SECOND TERM AT 
THE SYG POSITION BY FAILING TO ACT CAREFULLY AND JOINTLY. 
KABBAH JOKED ABOUT THE DELICATE POSITION HE WAS IN, AS 
SIERRA LEONE'S PERMREP JONAH WAS BEING DISCUSSED AS A 
POSSIBLE CANDIDATE, AND OFFERED TO CONSULT WITH THE U.S. 
FOLLOWING HIS AFRICAN TALKS. JONAH CAUTIONED ABOUT THE 
IMPORTANCE OF'TACTICS -- BAD TIMING IN PRESENTING A VIABLE 
AFRICAN CANDIDATE COULD PRODUCE A SYG WORSE THAN 
BOUTROS-GHALI ON THE ISSUE OF REFORM. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY CONCLUDED BY REITERATING U.S. DETERMINATION TO 
HAVE A NEW UN SYG AND AGREED TO LEAVE THIS AS AN OPEN 
ISSUE ON WHICH WE WOULD STAY IN CLOSE CONTACT. 
NEW FRENCH AND IVORIAN PEACE MOVES? 
9. IN THE SEPARATE AF MEETING, JONAH ASKED WHETHER THE 
U.S. HAD ANY RECENT CONSULTATIONS WITH THE FRENCH ON 
FURTHERING PEACE IN SIERRA LEONE. (NOTE: THERE HAVE LONG 
BEEN RUMORS OF FRENCH SUPPORT FOR SANKOH. END NOTE) DAS 
TWADDELL RESPONDED THAT WE HAD NOT HAD SPECIFIC DIALOGUE 
WITH THE FRENCH ON SIERRA LEONE, BUT NOTED AN APPARENT NEW 
FRENCH POLICY IN SUPPORT OF RESTRICTING ARMS FLOWS INTO 
,THE REGION. TWADDELL NOTED THE FRENCH HAD APPARENTLY 
ADOPTED THIS NEW POLICY FOLLOWING THE ABUJA TALKS ON 
LIBERIA. FM KALLON LATER SAID THE IVORIANS INTENDED TO 
WITHDRAW SANKOH'S IVORIAN PASSPORT BECAUSE HE WAS MISUSING 
IT BY ATTEMPTING TO TRAVEL TO BELGIUM TO DELAY SIGNING A 
PEACE AGREEMENT.

AFRICA'S FUTURE AND THE ACRF 
11. DURING HIS MEETING WITH NSC ADVISOR LAKE, KABBAH 
EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY'S 
TENDENCY TO MARGINALIZE AFRICA IN TERMS OF ALLOCATION OF.
RESOURCES AND INDICATED HIS GOVERNMENT'S INTENTION TO HELP 
CHANGE AFRICA'S NEGATIVE IMAGE. KABBAR ASKED ABOUT THE 
PROSPECTS OF CHANNELING AFRICA PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS 
THROUGH THE ORGANIZATION OF AFRICAN UNITY. JONAH THEN 
ADDED HIS CONCERN ABOUT WHAT HE CALLED "THE EXCLUSIVENESS1? 
OF THE PROPOSED AFRICAN CRISIS RESPONSE FORCE (ACRF). 
12. LAKE highlighted THE DIFFICULTIES in GETTING CONGRESS 
TO APPROVE RESOURCES FOR AFRICA, BUT STRESSED THAT FINDING 
SUCH SUPPORT WAS AT THE TOP OF PRESIDENT CLINTON'S FOREIGN 
POLICY AGENDA. LAKE ALSO UNDERSCORED THE URGENT NEED FOR 
UN REFORM WHICH REQUIRES A SYG WHO WILL WORK AGGRESSIVELY 
IN THIS REGARD. LAKE SAID HE SUPPORTED THE IDEA OF EACH 
REGION HAVING A SECOND TERM AND URGED AFRICANS TO PUT 
FORWARD POSSIBLE CANDIDATES. WITH RESPECT TO THE ACRF, 
THE GOAL IS TO HAND OFF TO AFRICANS, POSSIBLY THROUGH THE 
OAU OR SOME OTHER BODY, AS SOON AS POSSIBLE. ON THE NEXT 
STEPS IN THE PEACE PROCESS, LAKE NOTED THE DIFFICULTIES IN 
PROVIDING UN MILITARY OBSERVERS/PEACEKEEPERS AND URGED THE 
GOSL TO CONTINUE PURSING THIS ISSUE WITH THE OAU AND 
NON-GOVERNMENTAL ORGANIZATIONS.
