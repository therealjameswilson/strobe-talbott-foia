SUBJECT: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MEETING WITH BRAZILIAN MFA SECRETARY GENERAL REGO BARROS 
1. BEGIN SUMAARY: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND MFA 
GENERAL REGO BARROS DISCUSSED PERU=ECUADOR, UN SECURITY COUNCIL (UNSC) EXPANSION AND THE -NON-PROLIFERATION TREATY (NPT) DURING THEIR JULY 24 MEETING. ON PERU-ECUADOR, REGO BARROS SAID HE WAS OPTIMISTIC ABOUT A POSITIVE OUTCOME ON THE DEMILITARIZED ZONE (DMZ). 
IN A REVIEW OF UNSC REFORM, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED THAT UNSC EXPANSION IS A COMPLEX ISSUE THAT MUST BE CONSIDERED IN THE CONTEXT OF UN REFORM MORE GENERALLY RATHER THAN IN A VACUUM. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OBSERVED THAT THE UNITED STATES HAD LONG SUPPORTED UNSC MEMBERSHIP FOR GERMANY AND JAPAN BUT THAT ANY EXPANSION BEYOND THESE TWO MUST NOT DIMINISH THE EFFECTIVENESS OF THE UNSC. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT THE U.S. WANTS TO STAY IN CLOSE 
TOUCH WITH BRAZIL ON THIS ISSUE SINCE IT RELATES TO BRAZIL'S TRANSREGIONAL AND GLOBAL ROLE. REGO BARROS EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THIS APPROACH AND SAID THA? BRAZIL WANTED TO DO MORE INTERNATIONALLY. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE WOULD ALSO LIKE TO PROGRESS QUIETLY WITH BRAZIL ON NONPROLIFERATION. REGO BARROS SAID HE WAS OPEN TO DISCUSSION OF THIS ISSUE AND IT WAS AGREED THAT THE TWO COUNTRIES WOULD BEGIN AN INFORMAL, PRIVATE DIPLOMATIC DIALOGUE ON THE MATTER. REGO BARROS WELCOMED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S OFFER OF AN INFORMAL, CONTINUING HIGH-LEVEL DIALOGUE. END SUMMARY.

FOCUSSED ON THREE ISSUES: THE PERU/ECUADOR BORDER 
SITUATIONR UN SECURITY COUNCIL REFORM, AND NPT. 
3. PERU-ECUADOR: REGO BARROS LED OFF BY REPORTING THAT 
GENERAL DURAN, THE ECUADORIAN MILITARY LIAISON, HAD 
ARRIVED IN PERU AND THAT A MEETING WAS CURRENTLY UNDERWAY 
IN THE BRAZILIAN EMBASSY IN LIMA BETWEEN THE TWO MILITARY 
LIAISONS TO REACH AGREEMENT ON THE DEMILITARIZED ZONE. 
(DMZ). REGO BARROS SAID GOOD PROGRESS WAS BEING MADE AND 
WAS OPTIMISTIC ABOUT A POSITIVE OUTCOME ON THE DMZ. 
IN RESPONSE TO A QUESTION FROM THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, REGO 
BARROS SAID HE DOUBTED THAT THE WILLIAMSBURG DEFENSE 
MINISTERIAL WOULD PROVIDE ANY OPPORTUNITY TO FACILITATE 
THE TALKS, SINCE THE RIGHT PEOPLE WERE NOT PRESENT. 
HOWEVER, HE WAS OPEN TO MEETING WITH OTHER GUARANTOR 
COUNTRIES AND/OR PERU AND ECUADOR IF IT APPEARED USEFUL. 
(AGREEMENT ON DMZ WAS REACHED IN LIMA ON JULY 24). 
4. UN SECURITY COUNCIL: NOTING THE IMPROVEMENT IN 
US-BRAZILIAN TIES, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID WE HAVE THE 
BEGINNINGS ATMOSPHERICALLY AND SUBSTANTIVELY TO TURN A 
NEW PAGE Og NUMEROUS ISSUES PARTICULARLY IN THE GLOBAL 
ARENA. THE UN IS A PRIORITY GLOBAL ISSUE FOR BOTH 
SIDES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED HIS DESIRE TO 
BUILD ON THE CANDID CONVERSATION HE HAD ALREADY BEGUN 
WITH FOREIGN MINISTER LAMPRELA IN BUENOS AIRES AND WITH 
PRESIDENT CARDOSO AND REGO BARROS IN BRASILIA SO THAT 
BOTH SIDES CLEARLY UNDERSTOOD EACH OTHER'S POSITIONS. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THEN OUTLINED US VIEWS ON THE UNITED 
NATIONS AND SECURITY COUNCIL REFORM. HE STRESSED THAT 
UNSC EXPANSION WAS NOT COST-FREE FOR THE UNITED STATES, 
ESPECIALLY IN DOMESTIC TERMS. THE UN WAS NOW AT AN ALL 
TIME LOW IN US PUBLIC/CONGRESSIONAL OPINION. ATTITUDES 
WERE PARTICULARLY NEGATIVE ON UN PEACEKEEPING EFFORTS IN 
LIGHT OF THE BOSNIA CRISIS AND EXPERIENCE IN SOMALIA. 
THE UNITED STATES WAS FIGHTING TO PRESERVE FUNDING FOR 
THE UN. IT WAS NO EXAGGERATION TO SAY THAT THE FUNDING 
OUTCOME WOULD HAVE A MARKED INFLUENCE ON THE FUTURE OF 
THE ORGANIZATION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY URGED REGO BARROS 
TO SPEND SOME TIME WITH US MEMBERS OF CONGRESS TO GET A 
FULL APPRECIATION OF THE EXTENT OF THE PROBLEM. 
TURNING TO SECURITY COUNCIL EXPANSION THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY SAID THAT THE UNITED STATES HAD LONG SUPPORTED 
MEMBERSHIP FOR GERMANY AND JAPAN. HOWEVER, THE CLINTON 
ADMINISTRATION DID NOT HAVE ANY DOGMATIC FINAL ANSWER ON 
THE NUMBER OF NEW PERMANENT MEMBERS IT SHOULD BE ADDING. 
IT DID, HOWEVER, HAVE CONCERNS ABOUT EXPANDING THE UNSC 
IN A WAY THAT DIMINISHED THE EFFECTIVENESS OF THE BODY 
AND THE ROLE OF THE UN AS A WHOLE. THE UNITED STATES WAS 
FACED WITH THE GENERIC QUESTION, NOT BRAZIL-SPECIFIC, :OF 
WHAT TO DO ABOUT 11MEGA STATES" SUCH AS INDIA, BRAZIL, 
INDONESIA, AND SOUTH AFRICA, WITH INFLUENCES REACHING 
BEYOND THE REGION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT HE 
WOULD BE DISCUSSING UN REFORM WITH AMBASSADOR ALBRIGHT. 
HE CONCLUDED THAT THE UNITED STATES AND BRAZIL HAD TO 
FIND WAYS OF TALKING WITH EACH OTHER FREELY AND 
INFORMALLY. 
REGO BARROS RESPONDED FAVORABLY, CALLING THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY'S APPROACH PROFESSIONAL AND CONSTRUCTIVE AND 
THANKING HIM FOR OFFERING THIS KIND OF DIALOGUE. HE SAID 
BRAZIL NEEDS TO BE MORE ACTIVE INTERNATIONALLY AND TO DO 
MORE TO DEAL WITH WORLD PROBLEMS. IT WOULD DO SO EVEN IF 
SECURITY COUNCIL EXPANSION DID NOT TURN OUT AS BRAZIL 
HOPED. REGO BARROS ARGUED THAT EVEN THOUGH GEOGRAPHY NO 
LONGER HAS A CONTROLLING INFLUENCE IN THE UN, AN 
EXPANSION OF THE P-FIVE TO SEVEN WOULD RESULT IN A 
GEOGRAPHIC IMBALANCE WHICH, HE ASSERTED, WAS NOT IN US 
INTEREST (THREE EUROPEAN, TWO ASIAN, ONE EURASIAN, ONE 
WESTERN HEMISPHERE). HE SAID THE UNITED STATES SHOULD 
VALUE HAVING ANOTHER WESTERN HEMISPHERE COUNTRY AS A

WHICH WERE UNIQUE TO THE HEMISPHERE. FOR EXAMPLE, WE ARE 
ALL NATIONS OF IMMIGRANTS. THE KIND OF ETHNIC VIOLENCE 
NOW RAGING IN THE FORMER YUGOSLAVIA WAS FOREIGN TO US, TO

FOR THE UNITED STATES TO HAVE ANOTHER PERMANENT MEMBER ON

IN RESPONSE TO A QUESTION FROM REGO BARROS THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY SAID WE WOULD LIKE TO REACH AN ANSWER ON 
SECURITY COUNCIL EXPANSION SOONER RATHER THAN LATER. HE 
HOPED TO ADVANCE THE ISSUE OF UN REFORM THIS YEAR BECAUSE 
IT WAS TOO URGENT TO DELAY. REGO BARROS THEN SET OUT 
BRAZIL'S APPRECIATION OF THE SITUATION, SAYING THAT 
ITAMARATY WAS TALKING TO INDIA, JAPAN, AND GERMANY. HE 
SAID THE LATTER TWO AGREED THAT IT WOULD BE IMPOSSIBLE 
FOR THEM TO ENTER THE SECURITY COUNCIL ALONE. IN HIS 
VIEW, BRAZIL AND INDIA STOOD OUT AMONG POTENTIAL 
CANDIDATES, BUT IT WAS NOT IN THE CARDS FOR CANADA, 
MEXICO, OR ARGENTINA TO GAIN A PERMANENT SEAT. HE 
CONCLUDED THAT BRAZIL WAS SEEKING A PLUS-FIVE FORMULA, 
WITH PERMANENT MEMBERSHIP DOUBLING TO INCLUDE JAPAN, 
GERMANY, BRAZIL, INDIA, AND AN AFRICAN NATION, POSSIBLY 
SOUTH AFRICA. BRAZIL, HE STRESSED, WAS NOT INTERESTED IN 
MAGIC FORMULAS FOR ROTATION. TALBOTT REPLIED THAT PLUS-5 
SOUNDED LIKE IT CROSSED THE THRESHOLD OF EFFECTIVENESS 
AND WOULD BE TOO UNWEILDY, UNDERSCORING THE NEED FOR 
FURTHER THOUGHT AND DISCUSSION ON THE ISSUE. 
5. NONPROLIFERATION: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE WOULD 
LIKE TO PROGRESS QUIETLY WITH BRAZIL ON THE POSSIBILITY 
OF ADHERENCE TO THE NPT. BRASIL'S NEW POLICY OF 
OUTREACH, ITS ASPIRATIONS TO GLOBAL LEADERSHIP AND ITS 
RECORD OF INTERNATIONAL GOOD CITIZENSHIP ALL MAKE ITS 
REFUSAL TO JOINT THE NPT AN ANOMOLY. REGO BARROS REPLIED 
THAT HE HAD INSTRUCTIONS DIRECTLY FROM PRESIDENT CARDOSO 
THAT BRAZIL WAS PREPARED TO DISCUSS ANY SUBJECT FRANKLY 
AND OPENLY WITH THE UNITED STATES HE ASKED HOW IMPORTANT 
THIS ISSUE WAS FOR THE UNITED STATES. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY SAID IT WAS VERY IMPORTANT. A COUNTRY OF 
BRAZIL'S SIZE, POTENTIAL, AND INTERNATIONAL STANDING 
SHOULD BE PART OF THE NPT REGIME. OTHER MAJOR STATES 
STILL OUTSIDE THE TREATY, SUCH AS INDIA AND EGYPT, HAD 
REGIONAL SECURITY REASONS FOR HOLDING BACK, BUT BRAZIL 
HAS ALREADY ADHERED TO THE TREATY OF TLATELOLCO AND HAD 
NO SECURITY THREATS FROM ITS NEIGHBORS. THE TWO AGREED 
TO OPEN A QUIET INFORMAL AND PRIVATE DIALOGUE ON 
PROLIFERATION ISSUES USING TECHNICALLY QUALIFIED 
DIPLOMATS ON BOTH SIDES.
