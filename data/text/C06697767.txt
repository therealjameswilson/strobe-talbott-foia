FROM WASHINGTON HE TRAVELLED TO BRUSSELS, WHERE HE MET.WITH NATO SECRETARY GENERAL SOLANA AND EU REPRESENTATIVE SANTER, WITH WHOM HE HAD POSITIVE DISCUSSIONS. DRNOVSEK SAID IT SEEMS THAT HIS TELEPHONE CONVERSATION WITH ITALIAN PM PROD I ON THURSDAY, AND YESTERDAY'S VISIT OF ITALIAN MFA UNDERSECRETARY FASS/NO RESULTED IN THE RESOLUTION OF TECHNICAL ISSUES BETWEEN ITALY AND SLOVENIA. 6. 454-' THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT WAS EXTREMELY GOOD NEWS AND HE HAD ALREADY BEEN INFORMALLY TOLD THE ITALIANS WERE READY FOR DISCUSSIONS, AND AFTER AN EXCHANGE OF LETTERS, THE AGREEMENT WAS READY FOR SIGNATURE. 
7. -+S4- DRNOVSEK RESPONDED THAT THE ITALIANS CONFIRMED EVERYTHING WAS ON TRACK, AND THE EU AGREEMENT WILL BE DISCUSSED IN BRUSSELS TOMORROW. WHILE THE ITALIANS ARE ON BOARD, THE BRITISH POSITION COULD BE PROBLEMATIC. THE BRITISH MAY VETO ALL DECISIONS OF THE MINISTERIAL COUNCIL BECAUSE OF THE MAD COW PROBLEM. DRNOVSEK ASKED IF THE DEPUTY SECRETARY COULD WORK WITH THE BRITISH TO AVOID SUCH AN OUTCOME IN THE CASE OF THE EU AGREEMENT WITH SLOVENIA. DRNOVSEK SAID IT WOULD BE TRAGIC, AFTER 
TWO YEARS OF PROBLEMS WITH ITALY, FOR THE BRITISH TO VETO THE ASSOCIATION WITH SLOVEN IA BECAUSE OF THE MAD COW ISSUE. 
8.41:0- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED SURPRISE AT THE MAD COW DEVELOPMENT, AND STATED THAT EVEN THOUGH HE WAS NOT PERSONALLY AWARE OF THE PROBLEM, HIS COLLEAGUES CERTAINLY KNEW ABOUT IT. HE SAID WE SEE A REAL OPPORTUNITY BUT ALSO HAVE A SENSE OF URGENCY REGARDING THIS ISSUE, SINCE THERE ARE ONLY A COUPLE MORE WEEKS OF THE ITALIAN PRESIDENCY OF THE EU. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STATED HE WILL TAKE UP THE ISSUE WITH ASSISTANT SECRETARY KORNBLUM, AND WE WILL USE OUR GOOD OFFICES WITH THE BRITISH. 
9.-{131"DRNOVSEK THANKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND SAID THEY MUST BE CAREFUL: EVERYTHING IS READY AND THEY ALL WANT TO.WORK ON THE PROBLEM. HE REASSURED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ON THE OTHER ISSUES ABOUT WHICH THEY HAD SPOKEN IN WASHINGTON. DRNOVSEK SAID HE THOUGHT HIS GOVERNMENT REACTED POSITIVELY, INCLUDING ON THE IRAN ISSUE, WHERE THE GOS HAD ALREADY TAKEN SOME MEASURES. 
10.4E} THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RESPONDED WE ARE GRATIFIED BY THE PROMPTNESS AND CONTENT OF THE SLOVENIAN REPLY TO THE NON-PAPER AND TO THE COMMITMENT TO INVESTIGATE THE MATTER.
