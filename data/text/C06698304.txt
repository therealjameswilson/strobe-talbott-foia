SUBJECT: PERU/ECUADOR: VISIT OF ECUADORIAN PRESIDENT 
DURAN BALLEN 
1. (U) CLASSIFIED BY PERU DESKOFF LYNNSICAD2 PER 1.5 (B) 
AND (D). THIS IS AN ACTION MESSAGE (SEE PARA 2). 
2. (U) ECUADORIAN PRESIDENT DURAN BALLEN MET WITH 
PRESIDENT CLINTON ON JUNE 25 AND ACTING SECRETARY STROBE 
TALBOTT ON JUNE 26. FOREIGN MINISTER GALO LEORO MET WITH 
AMBASSADOR ElNAUDI JUNE 27. THIS CABLE OUTLINES THE MAIN 
POINTS DISCUSSED. BUENOS AIRES, SANTIAGO AND BRASILIA ARE 
REQUESTED TO SHARE PARAS 3 - 6 WITH HOST GOVERNMENTS AT 
APPROPRIATE LEVELS. 
3. PRESIDENT DURAN BALLEN SAID HIS CHIEF INTEREST WAS 
ACHIEVING PARITY WITH PERU AFTER THE MAY 21 FUJIMORI 
VISIT. HE SAID HIS RECEPTION BY PRESIDENT CLINTON "SAVED 
THE IMAGE OF THE U.S." 
4. 1S.4... WITH HIS CONCERNS FOR BALANCE FULFILLED, DURAN 
BALLEN CONCENTRATED ON THREE POINTS: 
-- HE STRESSED THE "GEOGRAPHIC PROBLEMS;" 
-- HE ASKED PRESIDENT CLINTON TO MAINTAIN MOMEP; AND, 
-- HE COMPLAINED BITTERLY THAT PERU HAD STALLED THE 
PROCESS. 
THE ECUADORIAN DESIRE FOR AN EMINENT PERSON TO ARBITRATE 
THEIR DIFFERENCES WITH PERU WAS INFERRED RATHER THAN STATED. 
5.-te} WE LISTENED CAREFULLY TO PRESIDENT DURAN BALLEN'S 
POINTS BUT GAVE NO SPECIFIC ASSURANCES ON EITHER THE 
GEOGRAPHIC PROBLEM OR THE CONTINUATION OF MOMEP. IT WAS 
CLEAR THAT ECUADOR WANTED TO BE ABLE TO SAY TO THE MEDIA 
THAT THE GUARANTORS WOULD ENSURE DISCUSSION OF THE 
GEOGRAPHIC ISSUE. THE ONLY ANSWER THEY RECEIVED WAS AN 
INDICATION FROM AMBASSADOR ElNAUDI THAT THE FINAL TALKS 
SHOULD BEGIN AS SOON AS POSSIBLE AND THAT THE GUARANTORS 
WOULD CONSIDER ISSUES ONLY AFTER THEY HAD BEEN RAISED IN 
THE TALKS. 
6. (U) PRESIDENT CLINTON TOLD DURAN BALLEN THAT QUOTE 
WITH THE NEGOTIATING APPROACH NOW IN PLACE, ECUADOR AND 
PERU ARE POISED TO MAKE REAL PROGRESS IN ADDRESSING THE 
PROBLEMS THAT HAVE DIVIDED THEM FOR MUCH OF THE PAST 
CENTURY AND A HALF UNQUOTE. HE CALLED ON BOTH COUNTRIES
