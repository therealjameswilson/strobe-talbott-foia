2.  (C) HEAD OF PRESIDENTIAL ADMINISTRATION ALEKSANDR 
VOLOSHIN PRAISED ONGOING U.S.-RUSSIAN EFFORTS AT 
REACHING A PEACEFUL SETTLEMENT OF THE YUGOSLAVIA 
CRISIS, UNDERSCORING RUSSIA'S NEGOTIATING ABILITY WITH 
PRESIDENT MILOSEVIC AS A VALUABLE RESOURCE AT THE 
WEST'S DISPOSAL. VOLOSHIN EMPHASIZED THE UTILITY OF 
THE GORE-CHERNOMYRDIN CHANNEL IN BILATERAL 
CONSULTATIONS ON A SETTLEMENT, BUT REPEATEDLY PROMOTED 
A NATO BOMBING PAUSE AS GIVING RUSSIAN NEGOTIATIONS 
WITH MILOSEVIC AN OPPORTUNITY TO MAKE RAPID PROGRESS. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INFORMED VOLOSHIN OF THE SUBSTANCE 
OF HIS CONVERSATIONS EARLIER IN THE DAY WITH FOREIGN 
MINISTER IVANOV AND SPECIAL REPRESENTATIVE 
CHERNOMYRDIN, AND URGED CLOSE U.S.-RUSSIAN COOPERATION 
IN ACHIEVING COMMON OBJECTIVES TO RESOLVE THE YUGOSLAV 
CRISIS. END SUMMARY. 
3.   VISITING DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT CALLED ON 
HEAD OF RUSSIAN PRESIDENTIAL ADMINISTRATION (PA) 
ALEKSANDR VOLOSHIN IN THE KREMLIN ON APRIL 27 TO REVIEW 
PROGRESS IN TALKS CONDUCTED EARLIER IN THE DAY WITH 
FOREIGN MINISTER IVANOV AND SPECIAL PRESIDENTIAL 
REPRESENTATIVE TO THE BALKAN CONFLICT VIKTOR 
CHERNOMYRDIN. THE 50-MINUTE MEETING WAS VOLOSHIN'S 
FIRST WITH U.S. OFFICIALS SINCE HIS MARCH 23 PROMOTION 
TO HEAD OF PA. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S DELEGATION WAS 
ACCOMPANIED BY AMBASSADOR COLLINS. VOLOSHIN WAS JOINED 
IN THE MEETING BY MFA SENIOR DEPUTY DIRECTOR OF THE 
NORTH AMERICAN DEPARTMENT VASILIY ISTRATOV, DEPUTY HEAD 
OF VOLOSHIN'S SECRETARIAT VIKTOR VEKSHIN, AND AN 
INTERPRETER.

YELTSIN, VOLOSHIN SAID HE INFORMED HIM OF THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY'S VISIT, NOTING THAT THE RUSSIAN PRESIDENT 
WAS BEING KEPT INFORMED OF ALL YUGOSLAVIA-RELATED 
DEVELOPMENTS. THE PA HEAD POSITIVELY ASSESSED THE 
APRIL 25 PHONE CONVERSATION BETWEEN PRESIDENTS CLINTON 
AND YELTSIN AS ADDING IMPETUS TO BILATERAL EFFORTS TO 
ACHIEVE A RESOLUTION OF THE YUGOSLAVIA CRISIS. 
VOLOSHIN FURTHER EMPHASIZED THE UTILITY OF THE 
COMMUNICATION CHANNEL OPENED BETWEEN CHERNOMYRDIN AND 
VICE PRESIDENT GORE, PARTICULARLY GIVEN THEIR LONG 
HISTORY OF WORKING TOGETHER. 
5_444— THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INFORMED VOLOSHIN OF HIS 
PRODUCTIVE MEETINGS EARLIER IN THE DAY WITH 
CHERNOMYRDIN AND IVANOV, NOTING THE BREADTH OF 
COMMONALITY IN THE U.S. AND RUSSIAN POSITIONS (NOTABLY 
IN THE AREA OF PRESERVING YUGOSLAVIA'S TERRITORIAL 
INTEGRITY WITHIN ITS CURRENT BOUNDARIES).AND THE 
SHRINKING AREAS OF DIFFERENCES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
DETAILED NATO'S CONDITIONS FOR A SETTLEMENT TO THE 
YUGOSLAVIA CRISIS AND STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF 
BILATERAL COOPERATION, FROM THE PRESIDENTIAL LEVEL 
DOWNWARD, TO BRINGING A SETTLEMENT NEARER. HE REPORTED 
TO VOLOSHIN THAT U.S. AND RUSSIAN EXPERTS WERE 
DISCUSSING IN DETAIL QUESTIONS RELATED TO THE 
INTERNATIONAL MILITARY PRESENCE AND CIVILIAN OBSERVER 
MISSION THAT WILL BE PART OF THE EVENTUAL SETTLEMENT 
AGREEMENT. 
6. -1€5,1- VOLOSHIN EXPRESSED SEVERAL CONCERNS. HE WARNED 
THAT THE YUGOSLAVIA CRISIS THREATENS TO UNDERMINE THE 
IMPORTANT ACCOMPLISHMENTS ACHIEVED IN THE BILATERAL 
RELATIONSHIP IN RECENT YEARS. WHILE EMPHASIZING 
RUSSIA'S WILLINGNESS TO USE ITS GOOD OFFICES TO BRING 
ABOUT A SETTLEMENT BETWEEN PRESIDENT MILOSEVIC AND 
NATO, VOLOSHIN REPEATEDLY SAID SUCH A PROCESS WOULD 
HAVE BEEN FAR EASIER TO ACHIEVE BEFORE NATO BEGAN ITS 
MILITARY OPERATIONS AGAINST THE FRY. THE DEPUTY 
VOLOSHIN 
SECRETARY RECOUNTED REPEATED EFFORTS ON THE PART OF 
BOTH THE WEST AND RUSSIA TO ACCOMPLISH JUST THAT, 
FRUSTRATED BY MILOSEVIC AT EVERY TURN. 
VOLOSHIN RECOGNIZED THAT MILOSEVIC IS VERY 
DIFFICULT TO NEGOTIATE WITH, PARTICULARLY IF PAINTED 
INTO A CORNER. HE NEVERTHELESS FEELS THAT THE NATO 
ACTIONS HAVE NOT ONLY ALLOWED MILOSEVIC TO CONSOLIDATE 
HIS SUPPORT WITHIN YUGOSLAVIA, BUT ALSO HAS MADE IT 
MORE DIFFICULT FOR MILOSEVIC TO RISK PUBLIC BACKLASH 
FOR ANY COMPROMISES HE MAY BE WILLING TO CONSIDER. IN 
VOLOSHIN'S VIEW, MILOSEVIC CAN ONLY BE BROUGHT TO HEEL 
IF PRESENTED WITH A MUTUALLY BENEFICIAL SETTLEMENT 
PROPOSAL. DESPITE THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S REPEATED 
EXPOSITION OF WHAT STEPS MILOSEVIC MUST TAKE TO BRING 
THE NATO STRIKES TO AN END, VOLOSHIN REITERATED THE 
EFFICACY OF A NATO BOMBING PAUSE TO GIVE RUSSIAN 
PEACEMAKING EFFORTS ROOM IN WHICH TO BLOSSOM.

ABLE TO RETURN TO THEIR RIGHTFUL HOMES. FAILURE TO 
ACHIEVE THIS, HE SAID, WOULD BE A COMMON DEFEAT.
