IDECLASSIFIED
1. ~ FORMER CROWN PRINCE HASSAN OF JORDAN CALLED ON
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT FEBRUARY 28 TO SEEK U.S. SUPPORT
FOR HIS CANDIDACY TO REPLACE SADAKO OGATA AS UN HIGH
COMMISSIONER FOR REFUGEES, WHOSE TERM OF OFFICE EXPIRES ON
DECEMBER 31, 2000. HASSAN OUTLINED HIS THINKING ON REFUGEE
ISSUES, INCLUDING THE IMPORTANCE OF INTERFAITH DIALOGUE AND
CRISIS AVOIDANCE. IN SUPPORT OF HIS CANDIDACY, HE NOTED
THREE POINTS: (A) THE RELATIVE LACK OF ARAB REPRESENTATION
AMONG TOP U.N. OFFICIALS; (B) THE FACT THAT MORE THAN 50
PERCENT OF REFUGEES CURRENTLY ARE OF THE MUSLIM FAITH; AND
(C) THE NEED FOR ARAB LEADERS TO TAKE A MORE ACTIVE ROLE IN
THE FUNDING OF MULTILATERAL ORGANIZATIONS.
2. ~ DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT THANKED HASSAN FOR HIS
EXPRESSION OF INTEREST AND NOTED THAT THE USG HAD BEEN
AWARE OF KING ABDULLAH'S NOMINATION OF HIM FOR THE UNHCR
POSITION. HE SAID THE USG HELD HASSAN IN HIGH ESTEEM AND
REGARDED HIM AS A SERIOUS CANDIDATE. HE WARNED HASSAN THAT
THERE WOULD BE STRONG COMPETITION FOR THE JOB, AND THAT THE
EUROPEANS, IN PARTICULAR, FELT THAT IT WAS THEIR "TURN" TO
TAKE IT- IN ADDITIONs HASSAN'S CANDIDACY WOULD BE
DISADVANTAGED BY THE FACT THAT JORDAN WAS NOT A SIGNATOPY
TO THE INTERNATIONAL CONVENTION ON REFUGEES AND DID NOT SIT
ON THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE; AND THAT THE UNHCR POST IS NOT
NORMALLY GIVEN TO A PERSON FROM A STATE WITH A LARGE
REFUGEE POPULATION. TALBOTT SAID THAT THE USG HAD NOT YET
TAKEN A POSITION FOR OR AGAINST ANY OF THE POSSIBLE
CANDIDATES FOR THE UNHCR POSITION, BUT ADDED THAT HASSAN
HAD MADE SOME COMPELLING POINTS. HE .PROMISED TO INFORM
SECRETARY ALBRIGHT OF THEIR CONVERSATION AND TO ENSURE
PROPER FOLLOW-UP TO HASSAN'S EXPRESSION OF INTEREST.
.3.~ ALSO PRESENT WERE JORDANIAN AMBASSADOR MUASHER, D
SPECIAL ASSISTANT DOHERTY, NEA/ARN DIRECTOR WIENER, AND
IO/ESA DIRECTOR BLODGETT (NOTETAKER}.
