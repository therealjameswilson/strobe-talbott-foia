WHEN HE HEARS MENTION OF CONTACTS WITH LIBYA, IRAQ 
OR IRAN. MINISTER ASSURED THE AMBASSADOR THAT THERE 
WAS NO REASON FOR SUCH CONCERNS. 
5. ON AUGUST 12, PRESIDENT MULUZI TOLD THE 
BRITISH HIGH COMMISSIONER THAT HE HAD RECEIVED A 
LIBYAN DELEGATION. THIS DELEGATION CONVEYED THE 
INVITATION FROM QUADHAFI (REF B) AND APPARENTLY 
PROBED FOR SOME FORM OF REPRESENTATION IN MALAWI. 
WE BELIEVE THE DELEGATION CAME AFTER THE 
AMBASSADOR'S MEETING WITH NTAFU AND THAT CIVIL 
SERVANTS IN THE FOREIGN MINISTRY WERE KEPT OUT OF 
THE LOOP BUT NTAFU'S SEEMINGLY CAREFUL WORDING OF 
HIS REPLY TO AMBASSADOR'S INQUIRY RAISES QUESTIONS 
ABOUT HIS VERACITY. 
6. --(43} MULUZI TOLD THE BRITISH HIGH COMMISSIONER 
THAT HE HAD REJECTED THE LIBYAN DELEGATION'S 
OVERTURES BUT THE HIGH COMMISSIONER TELLS US HE 
SENSED THAT MULUZI WAS DISSEMBLING. THE NEWEST 
STORY WE HAVE HEARD SAYS THAT LIBYANS (AND IRAQIS) 
HAVE BEEN GIVEN THE GO AHEAD TO ESTABLISH A 
RESIDENCE IN AN OUTLYING DISTRICT OF LILONGWE 
WITHOUT ANY OFFICIAL STATUS. 
7. Kt FROM A VERY SENSITIVE SOURCE, WE KNOW THAT 
MINISTER FOR WATER AND IRRIGATION (FORMERLY FOREIGN 
AFFAIRS) BWANALI SOUGHT AND WAS GRANTED A MEETING 
WITH THE IRAQI AMBASSADOR IN DAR ES SALAAM IN JULY. 
WE CANNOT CONFIRM THAT THE MEETING ACTUALLY TOOK 
PLACE AND HAVE NO KNOWLEDGE OF ITS PURPOSE. 
8. COMMENT: ALL OF THE ABOVE IS HARDLY 
CONCITSVE EVIDENCE THAT THE GOM IS PURSUING OR EVEN 
CONTEMPLATING ANY SIGNIFICANT OPENING TO LIBYA OR 
IRAQ. WE DISMISS THE IDEA THAT MULUZI HAS ANY 
ASPIRATIONS TO ISLAMIZE MALAWI. ABOUT 15 PERCENT OF 
THIS COUNTRY'S POPULATION IS MUSLIM, ALMOST 
EXCLUSIVELY IN MULUZI'S YAO ETHNIC GROUP AND IN THE 
SMALL BUT ECONOMICALLY INFLUENTIAL ASIAN COMMUNITY. 
OTHERWISE, THE ROOTS AND INFLUENCE OF VARIOUS 
CHRISTIAN CHURCHES ARE VERY DEEP. PERSONALLY, 
MULUZI WEARS HIS RELIGION QUITE LIGHTLY..' HE 
RECEIVED SOME OF HIS EDUCATION IN CHURCH
INSTITUTIONS AND HE OFTEN ATTENDS MAJOR CHRISTIAN 
CHURCH EVENTS AROUND THE COUNTRY. FEW OF HIS MOST 
IMPORTANT ADVISORS ARE MUSLIM. MALAWI'S RECENT 
OVERT OUTREACH TO MIDDLE EASTERNCOUNTRIES HAS BEEN 
TO KUWAIT, SAUDI ARABIA, AND THE UAE WITH ONE 
OBJECTIVE IN MIND - MORE RESOURCES TO FINANCE 
DEVELOPMENT ACTIVITIES.

9. 1.€4- COMMENT CONTINUED: HOWEVER, PRECISELY 
BECAUSE THE GOM IS SO FOCUSED ON FINDING ADDITIONAL 
FINANCIAL RESOURCES, WE CANNOT DISMISS THE 
POSSIBILITY THAT SOME WOULD FIND LIBYA BLANDISHMENTS

FOUND THE GOM TO HAVE EXPERIENCED SOME SIGNIFICANT 
PROBLEMS IN MAINTAINING FISCAL DISCIPLINE SINCE THE 
BEGINNING OF THE CURRENT FISCAL YEAR IN APRIL. SOME 
KEY CABINET MEMBERS ARE KNOWN TO BE CHAFFING UNDER 
THE CONSTRAINTS THAT ACCOMPANY TRADITIONAL DONOR 
ASSISTANCE. 
10. RECOMMENDATION: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
MEETING WITH BWANALI OFFERS AN EXCELLENT OPPORTUNITY 
TO IMPRESS UPON THE GOM THAT MULUZI'S MEETING WITH A 
LIBYAN DELEGATION IS A MATTER OF SERIOUS CONCERN TO 
THE USG AND HAS CAUSED US TO LOOK MORE SERIOUSLY AT 
OTHER REPORTS/RUMORS OF CONTACTS WITH LIBYA AND 
IRAQ. (UNDER NO/NO CONDITIONS SHOULD WE INDICATE 
KNOWLEDGE OF THE MEETING NOTED PARA 6.) HE SHOULD 
BE LEFT WITH NO DOUBT AS TO HOW SERIOUSLY WE VIEW 
THIS ISSUE AND HOW DAMAGING THE IMPACT WOULD BE ON 
OUR RELATIONS IF THE GOM WERE TO PURSUE THE 
DEVELOPMENT OF ANY RELATIONSHIP WITH LIBYA, IRAQ OR 
IRAN.
