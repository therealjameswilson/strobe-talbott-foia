2. ~ SUMMARY. ON THE MARGINS OF THE FOREIGN
MINISTERS'EETING
AT MIYAZAKI, G-8 FOREIGN MINISTERS AND POLITICAL
DIRECTORS MET FOR BREAKFAST ON JULY 13 WITH THE FOREIGN
MINISTER REPRESENTING THE NAM {SOUTH AFRICA), THE CHAIR OF
G-77 {NIGERIA) AND THE FOREIGN MINISTERS OF COLOMBIA,
BANGLADESH, AND UNCTAD CHAIR THAILAND. FOREIGN MINISTERS
DI SCUSSED GLOBAL IZATI ON S CHALLENGES I IN PART ICULAR
WIDENING ECONOMIC DISPARITY, AND CONFLICT PREVENTION. THE
DEVELOPING COUNTRY REPRESENTATIVES PRESSED FOR DEBT RELIEF
AND BRIDGING OF THE DIGITAL DIVIDE. G-8 MINISTERS NOTED
THE POTENTIAL OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY FOR PROMOTING.
DEVELOPMENT. THE U.S., UK AND RUSSIA SUPPORTED DEBT
RELIEF, THE U.S. LINKING IT TO POVERTY REDUCTION EFFORTS.
CANADA AND ITALY STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF SLOWING ARMS
FLOWS. FRANCE CALLED FOR MORE FOREIGN AID. RUSSIA
STRESSED THE NEED TO GIVE A "SOCIAL DIRECTION" TO
GLOBALIZATION. END SUMMARY.

3. ~JAPAN' FOREIGN MINISTER KONO INTRODUCED THE THEME
OF THE BREAKFAST. SOUTH AFRICA'S FOREIGN MINISTER DR.
NKOSAZANA CLARICE DLAMINI-ZUMA THANKED THE G-8 MINISTERS
FOR PROVIDING THIS FORUM F'R DEVELOPING COUNTRIES. SHE
STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF BUILDING A PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN
DEVELOPING AND DEVELOPED COUNTRIES IN THE AREAS OF: I)
REGIONAL STABILITY AND DEVELOPMENT; 2) INFO TECHNOLOGY
{IT) AND DIGITAL DIVIDE; AND 3) DEBT RELIEF FOR DEVELOPING
COUNTRIES.
4. ~COLOMBIA VICE MINISTER DR. GUSTAVO BELL LEMUS
NOTED THAT THE COLOMBIAN FOREIGN MINISTER COULD NOT ATTEND
AS HE WAS PROMOTING "PLAN COLOMBIA" TO "ERADICATE ILLICIT
CROPS" IN COLOMBIA. HE NOTED THAT ADVANCES IN THE AREAS OF
BIOTECHNOLOGY, PHARMACEUTICALS, AND COMMUNICATION AND
INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY WERE PROMISING AND COULD BE USED TO
OVERCOME DISPARITIES {DIGITAL DIVIDE) AMONG NATIONS. HE
STRESSED THAT NATIONS SHOULD NOT ALLOW INFORMATION
TECHNOLOGY TO BECOME A SOURCE OF CONFRONTATION AND
DISCREPANCY AMONG THE DEVELOPING AND DEVELOPED COUNTRIES,
AND SUGGESTED A "REVOLUTION" WAS NEEDED IN EDUCATIONAL
METHODOLOGY AND FORMAT OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY TO ENSURE
INDIVIDUALS AND COMMUNITIES EF'FICIENTLY INCORPORATE IT INTO
THEIR DAILY LIVES. LEMUS EXPRESSED HIS CONCERN THAT THE
TECHNOLOGY GAP BETWEEN DEVELOPING AND DEVELOPED COUNTRIES
WOULD CAUSE A "BOOMERANG EFFECT" IN THAT EFFECTS OF
TECHNOLOGY WOULD BE MINIMAL OR NEGATIVE TO DEVELOPING
COUNTRIES. IN ADDITION, LEMUS EXPRESSED HIS CONCERN THAT
AT SEVERAL VENUES SUCH AS ECOSOC LAST WEEK, THERE WAS FEAR
THAT DEVELOPED COUNTRIES WERE BLOCKING OR IMPEDING
TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER TO DEVELOPING COUNTRIES.

REDUCE THE NUMBER OF PEOPLE LIVING IN ABJECT POVERTY AND
PROVIDE BETTER ACCESS TO HEALTH CARE, EMPLOYMENT,
TECHNOLOGY, AND EDUCATION. HE SUGGESTED THAT INCREASED
MARKET ACCESS, MORE FOREIGN DIRECT INVESTMENT, AND DEBT

6. ~ NIGERIA'S FOREIGN MINISTER AHLAJI SULE LAMIDO
STATED THAT INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY AND GLOBALIZATION CAN
HAVE POSITIVE EFFECTS ON DEVELOPING COUNTRIES AND WOULD
CREATE A MORE STABLE, DEMOCRATIC AND PROSPEROUS
ENVIRONMENT. LAMIDO STATED INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY SHOULD
NOT BE A SOURCE OF CONFLICT BETWEEN THE "HAVES" AND "HAVE
NOTS" IN THE WORLD. ACCORDING TO LAMIDO, GLOBALIZATION
REQUIRES INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION, ESPECIALLY IN TERMS OF
DEBT RELIEF AND 1NTERNATIONAL TRADE. HE LAMENTED THE
DECLINE IN OFFICIAL DEVELOPMENT ASSISTANCE AND THE SIZEABLE
DEBT OVERHANG FACING MANY COUNTRIES. NIGERIA ADDED THAT AN
EFFECTIVE NORTH-SOUTH DIALOGUE IS IMPERATIVE FOR HEALTH
CARE ISSUES SUCH AS HIV/AIDS AND THAT DEVELOPING COUNTRIES
LACK RESOURCES TO CREATE DEMOCRATIC ENVIRONMENTS SO DEBT
RELIEF IS IMPORTANT.
7. ~ THAILAND'S FOREIGN MINISTER SURIN PITSUWAN STATED
THAT HE SUPPORTS AND ENDORSES HIS COLLEAGUES'IEWS ON DEBT
RELIEF ~ DEVELOPMENT ~ POVERTY AND INFECTIOUS DI SEASES ~ AND
THE DIGITAL DIVIDE. HE COMMENTED THAT GLOBAL COOPERATION
IS NEEDED BETWEEN "NORTH AND SOUTH" AS WELL AS "SOUTH AND
SOUTH" TO ENSURE THAT .INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY BENEFITS
EVERYONE. HE SAID THAT THREE YEARS AGO, THAILAND AND MANY
PARTS OF ASIA FELT THE NEGATIVE EFFECTS OF GLOBALIZATION
EVEN THOUGH THESE COUNTRIES WERE INTEGRATED INTO THE GLOBAL
ECONOMY AND FOLLOWED TEXTBOOK POLICIES OF OPEN, LIBERAL
MARKETS. THE ASYMMETRICAL AND IMBALANCED GLOBAL TRADE AND
FINANCIAL STRUCTURE LEFT THEM OPEN TO VOLATILITY. MANY
ASIAN COUNTRIES ARE NOW BACK ON TRACK WITH FORECASTED
GROWTH OF 4-8o. HE NOTED A NUMBER OF LESSONS LEARNED:
1) NEED FOR COHERENCE IN MACROECONOMIC POLICIES OF MAJOR
INDUSTRIALIZED COUNTRIES AND AMONG MAJOR MULTILATERAL
FINANCIAL, TRADE, AND DEVELOPMENTAL INSTITUTIONS;
2) CONCRETE, PRACTICAL, FAST REFORM OF INTERNATIONAL
FINANCIAL STRUCTURES, ESPECIALLY OF HEDGE FUNDS, SHORT TERM
CAPITAL FLOWS, CURRENCY SPECULATION, AND MONEY LAUNDERING;
3) MORE EQUITABLE MULTILATERAL TRADE REGIME THAT PROVIDES
MARKET ACCESS FOR DEVELOPING COUNTRIES, INCLUDING ANOTHER
ROUND OF WTO NEGOTIATIONS WITH A FOCUS ON THE DEVELOPMENT
IMPACT OF TRADE;
4) REGIONAL TRADE AND FINANCIAL ARRANGEMENTS SHOULD BE
ENCOURAGED TO COMPLEMENT MULTILATERAL ARRANGEMENTS;
5) INNOVATIVE FINANCIAL MODALITIES FOR DEVELOPMENT TO
COMPLEMENT ODA AND PRIVATE SECTOR INVESTMENT;
6) MAKING IT AVAILABLE AND ACCESSIBLE TO ALL, NOT ONLY IN
E COMMERCE BUT E E DUCAT ION ~ E SC IENCE ~ E TECHNOLOGY ~ E
HEALTH.
8. ~ OF THE G-8 MINISTERS, RUSSIAN FOREIGN MINISTER
IVANOV SPOKE FIRST, SAYING HE HOPED TODAY'S MEETING WAS NOT
SIMPLY PRO FORMA. HE NOTED THAT GLOBALIZATION IS A FACT OF
OUR TIME AND THERE SHOULD NOT BE A POOR/RICH DIVIDE.
GLOBALIZATION MUST ACCOUNT FOR THE IDIOSYNCRASIES OF EACH
REGION. HE ARGUED FOR A "SOCIAL DIRECTION" TO
GLOBALIZATION AND PROVIDING SOCIAL SAFETY NETS. RUSSIA, HE
CONCLUDED, SUPPORTS IMF AND WORLD BANK INITIATIVES ON DEBT
REDUCTION AND CONFLICT PREVENTION FOR POOR COUNTRIES.
IVANOV THEN MADE A STRONG PITCH TO ADDRESS INFORMATION
SECURITY GIVEN THE IT REVOLUTION AND INCREASING RELIANCE ON
INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY FOR RUNNING ECONOMIES AND SOCIETY.

10. (((,UK FOREIGN MINISTER COOK NOTED THAT WE HAVE MORE
IN COMMON TODAY THAN AT ANY OTHER TIME IN HISTORY. HE SAID
THAT GLOBALIZATION HAS BOTH CHALLENGES AND OPPORTUNITIES
AND THAT HE HOPES NEW OPPORTUNITIES WON,'T BECOME NEW

11. ~ FRENCH FOREIGN MINISTER VEDRINE COMMENTED THAT
TODAY'S MEETING HAS BOTH SUBSTANTIVE AND SYMBOLIC
IMPORTANCE. HE NOTED FRENCH SUPPORT FOR CONTINUED WTO
ROUNDS AND UNCTAD DISCUSSIONS. HE NOTED THAT SOME
COUNTRIES ARE GETTING RICHER EXPONENTIALLY WHILE OTHERS ARE
GETTING POORER, AND SUGGESTED THAT ODA SHOULD INCREASE TO
DEVELOPING COUNTRIES AND ASSURE HIGH CONTENT AND QUALITY SO
DEVELOPING COUNTRIES WILL NOT REMAIN IN DEPENDENCY MODE.
HE PREVIEWED THAT THIS WILL BE THE FOCUS OF FRANCE'S TERM
OF PRESIDENCY IN THE EU. HE URGED MORE ORGANIZATION AND
SOLIDARITY IN FIGHTING AIDS AND SUGGESTED MORE
INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCES AND MEETINGS TO DISCUSS THESE
ISSUES FURTHER.
12. ~ DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, TYING IN THE THEMES FROM
FINANCE MINISTERS MEETING IN FUKUOKA, NOTED THE U.S. IS
COMMITTED TO IMPROVING INTERNATIONAL FINANCIAL
INSTITUTIONS. WE ARE COMMITTED TO ENHANCED INITIATIVES ON
DEBT RELIEF (HIPIC) TIED TO IMPLEMENTATION OF LOCALLY
DEVELOPED POVERTY REDUCTION STRATEGIES. IN CONCURRENCE
WITH THE FRENCH, CANADIAN, RUSSIAN, BRITISH FOREIGN
MINISTERS, WE NEED TO COOPERATE ON FIGHTING DISEASE
(HIV/AIDS/MALARIA/TB) AND THE DIGITAL DIVIDE. THESE
TOPICS, HE NOTED, WOULD BE PRIORITIES FOR DISCUSSION BY THE
Ge HEADS IN OKINAWA.
