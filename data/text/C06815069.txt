SERBIAN SANCTIONS
8~ON THE ISSUE OF SERBIAN SANCTIONS, THE SECRETARY
TOLD COOK THERE WOULD BE STRONG USG RESISTANCE TO ANYTHING
APPEARING TO BE A STEP BACK. SHE NOTED THERE WAS INTEREST
IN STRENGHTENING ENFORCEMENT OF FINANCIAL SANCTIONS AND
EXTENDING THE VISA BAN TO IMMEDIATE FAMILIES; IF THOSE
STEPS COULD BE AGREED UPON, SHE THOUGHT A TIME-LIMITED
SUSPENSION OF THE FLIGHT EMBARGO MIGHT BE A POSSIBILITY,
BUT ADDED SHE COULD NOT PROMISE ANYTHING. THE SECRETARY
HOPED THE GAC WOULD DELAY FORMAL DECISION ON THESE ISSUES
AT LEAST UNTIL THE FEBRUARY 14 GAC MEETING. SHE WARNED
COOK ANY PRESS LEAKS FROM THE GAC ALLUDING TO A WEAKENING
OF SANCTIONS COULD BE VERY DAMAGING.
9. ~COOK SAID HE HOPED DECISION-MAKING WOULD BE
DELAYED UNTIL FEBRUARY 14 AS WELL, AND THOUGHT HIS EU
COLLEAGUES, PARTICULARLY THE DUTCH, MIGHTALSO SUPPORT
POSTPONEMENT. HE ADDED IT WOULD BE IMPORTANT THE EU BE
SEEN TO BE RESPONDING TO SERBIAN OPPOSITION LEADERS RATHER
THAN MAKING CONCESSIONS TO MILOSEVIC; DELAYING UNTIL
FEBRUARY 14 WOULD GIVE THEM ANOTHER OPPORTUNITY TO MEET
WITH THE OPPOSITION.
10.~COOK SAID HE HAD SPOKEN WITH FRENCH FM VEDRINE
WHO WAS RELUCTANT TO SUPPORT THE VISA EXTENSION AND
FINANCIAL SANCTIONS UNLESS THERE WAS FLEXIBILITY ON THE
FLIGHT BAN. THE SECRETARY NOTED THE FLIGHT BAN WAS A
CONCRETE SANCTION, WHEREAS FINANCIAL SANCTIONS WERE ONLY
AS EFFECTIVE AS EACH MEMBER STATE'S ENFORCEMENT OF THEM.
COOK AGREED, NOTING IT IS NOT THE EU THAT IS FAILING, BUT
MEMBER STATES. EU COMIVIISSIONER PATTEN IS ALSO EXERCISED
ABOUT THIS, HE ADDED. COOK SUGGESTED FEAR OF EXPOSURE ON
THIS ISSUE MIGHT ENCOURAGE MEMBER STATES TO ENFORCE THE
CURRENT FINANCIAL SANCTIONS REGIME.
11.~THE SECRETARY ADDED SHE HOPED THE EU PROPOSAL TO,
INCLUDE BRIDGE CONSTRUCTION AS PART OF THE DANUBE
CLEARANCE PROJECT COULD ALSO BE FORESTALLED. COOK SAID
THIS WAS A DIFFICULT ISSUE BECAUSE OF TRADE AND FLOOD
RISKS TO ROMANIA AND HUNGARY. HE SUGGESTED AN ACCEPTABLE
PACKAGE OF MEASURES COULD ADDRESS THE PROBLEM IF IT HAD
THE SUPPORT OF A UNITED SERBIAN OPPOSITION.
