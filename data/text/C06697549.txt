2. SUMMARY. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT NET ON MAY 9 WITH 
ARGENTINE DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER FERNANDO PETRELLA TO 
THANK HIM FOR THE EFFORTS OF THE GOA AND MERCOSUR MEMBER 
STATES IN HELPING RESOLVE THE PARAGUAYAN CONSTITUTIONAL 
CRISIS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED AN INTEREST IN 
MEETING JOINTLY WITH THE FOREIGN MINISTERS OF THE 
MERCOSUR COUNTRIES ON THE MARGINS OF THE ORGANIZATION OF 
AMERICAN STATES (OAS) GENERAL ASSEMBLY IN PANAMA. ON 
PERU-ECUADOR, THE DEPSEC NOTED THE NECESSITY OF REACHING 
AN ARBITRATED SETTLEMENT AND SOLICITED PETRELLA'S ADVICE 
ON WAYS TO MAKE PROGRESS. TALBOTT MADE CLEAR THAT THE 
USG WILL CONTINUE TO REMAIN ENGAGED IN HAITI AFTER OUR 
GROUND TROOPS DEPART, AND EXPRESSED HOPE THAT ARGENTINA 
WOULD PARTICIPATE IN A POST-JUNE 30 INTERNATIONAL 
SECURITY FORCE. 
3. PETRELLA INDICATED THAT IT WAS IN ARGENTINAO 
INTEREST THAT PARAGUAY REMAIN STABLE AND DEMOCRATIC, AND 
OBSERVED THAT THE MERCOSUR STATES WERE CONSIDERING THE 
ADDITION OF A PROTOCOL TO THEIR ENABLING TREATY TO 
ENSHRINE DEMOCRACY AS A GOVERNING PRINCIPLE OF THE 
REGIONAL BLOC. PETRELLA STATED THAT PROGRESS ON 
PERU-ECUADOR MUST AWAIT THE ECUADORIAN ELECTIONS, AND 
CONFIRMED THAT THE GOA WAS LOOKING INTO PARTICIPATING IN 
A POST-JUNE 30 FORCE IN HAITI. THE DEPUTY FOREIGN. 
MINISTER RAISED THE CONCERN THAT GREAT BRITAIN MAY USE 
ITS OBSERVER STATUS AT THE OASGA TO AGGRAVATE 
FALKLANDS/MALVINAS TENSIONS. END SUMMARY. 
PARAGUAY AND OASGA 
4. THE DERUTY SECRETARY OPENED THE MEETING BY THANKING 
PETRELLA PERSONALLY, AS WELL AS THE GOA AND OTHER 
MERCOSUR MEMBERS, FOR THEIR LEADERSHIP IN HELPING RESOLVE 
THE PARAGUAYAN CONSTITUTIONAL CRISIS. THE DEPSEC STATED 
THAT THE MANNER IN WHICH MERCOSUR RESPONDED TO THE 
SIThATION, IN COORDINATION WITH THE OAS, WAS A MODEL OF 
HOW SUCH CRISES SHOULD BE HANDLED. PETRELLA DESCRIBED 
THE PERSONAL ADVOCACY OF FONMIN DI TELLA AND HIS STAFF IN 
ASUNCION DURING THE CRISIS, AND COMMENTED THAT A STABLE 
PARAGUAY IS PARTICULARLY IMPORTANT TO ARGENTINA GIVEN THE . - 
LARGE NUMBER OF PARAGUAYANS RESIDING IN ARGENTINA. HE 
STATED THAT CONSULTATIONS ARE ON-GOING WITHIN MERCOSUR

ASUNCION (WHICH CREATED MERCOSUR) TO ENSHRINE DEMOCRACY 
AS A GOVERNING PRINCIPLE OF THE REGIONAL BLOC. 
5. TALBOTT TOLD PETRELLA THAT HE WOULD WELCOME THE 
OFPORTUNITY TO MEET WITH THE MERCOSUR FOREIGN'MINISTERS 
ON THE MARGINS OF THE OAS GENERAL ASSEMBLY IN PANAMA, AND 
THAT AMBASSADOR CHEEK WOULD PRESENT A FORMAL INVITATION 
TO THE ARGENTINE FOREIGN MINISTRY AS CURRENT PRESIDENT OF 
MERCOSUR. HE COMMENTED THAT HE REGARDED MERCOSUR AND 
NAFTA AS MUTUALLY REINFORCING RATHER THAN COMPETING 
ENTITIES. PETRELLA WAS RECEPTIVE BUT NON-COMMITTAL 
TOWARD THE DEPSECOS INVITATION, AND INDICATED THAT SHOULD 
THE MEETING TAKE PLACE ADDITIONAL TOPICS SUCH AS THE 
GUARANTORS' PROCESS COULD APPROPRIATELY BE CONSIDERED. 
PERU-ECUADOR 
6. THE DEPSEC REMARKED THAT PRESIDENT CLINTON IS 
PREPARED FOR UP TO A A SIX-MONTH EXTENSION OF U.S. 
PARTICIPATION IN AND SUPPORT FOR MOMEP, BUT THAT THIS 
WOULD BE THE FINAL EXTENSION. HE SOUGHT PETRELLA'S 
ADVICE ON RE-ENERGIZING EFFORTS TOWARD AN ARBITRATED 
SETTLEMENT, PARTICULARLY GIVEN REPORTS OF BRAZILIAN 
RELUCTANCE TO TAKE A MORE PROACTIVE POSTURE. PETRELLA 
RESPONDED THAT THE GOA IS INTERESTED IN ARRANGING FOR A 
"FACILITATOR" TO WORK TOWARDS A NEGOTIATED SETTLEMENT, 
BUT THAT ANY SOLUTION MUST AWAIT THE NEW ADMINISTRATION 
IN ECUADOR. 
HAITI 
7. TALBOTT WELCOMED REPORTS THAT THE GOA IS CONSIDERING 
CONTRIBUTING TO A POST—JUNE 30 INTERNATIONAL SECURITY 
FORCE IN HAITI. HE EMPHASIZED THAT THE USG WAS NOT 
WITHDRAWING FROM ITS COMMITMENT TOWARD HAITI, AND DESPITE 
THE FACT THAT GROUND TROOPS WILL SOON BE COMPLETELY 
WITHDRAWN, THE USG WILL REMAIN THOROUGHLY ENGAGED. THE 
DEPSEC INDICATED THAT SEVERAL HIGH-LEVEL USG DELEGATIONS 
WILL BE VISITING HAITI OVER THE NEXT SEVERAL MONTHS. HE 
REMARKED THAT THE ECONOMY WAS THE "MAKE OR BREAK ISSUE", • 
AND THAT THE USG WAS WORKING WITH THE WORLD BANK TO 
FURTHER ECONOMIC REACTIVATION EFFORTS. TALBOTT NOTED 
THAT ALTHOUGH DUE TO RUSSIAN AND CHINESE OPPOSITION TfiERE 
MAY BE NO FORMAL UNMIH EXTENSION, WE SHOULD NONETHELESS 
LOOK TOWARD OTHER OPTIONS WHICH MAY HAVE THE BLESSING IF 
NOT FORMAL PARTICIPATION OF THE UN. HE EMPHASIZED THAT 
THE NEED FOR AN INTERNATIONAL SECURITY PRESENCE REMAINS 
HIGH.
