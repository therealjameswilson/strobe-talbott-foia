4. -(-91- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INFORMED VOLLEBAEK THERE WAS A 
WRINKLE IN THE SECRETARY'S POSSIBLE VISIT TO OSLO. 
AMBASSADOR SESTANOVICH HAD DETECTED FROM RUSSIAN 
AMBASSADOR USHAKOV THAT RUSSIAN FM IVANOV WOULD WANT TO 
COME TO OSLO BUT WOULD NOT WANT A TRILATERAL MEETING. 
VOLLEBAEK INDICATED THIS WOULD NOT BE A PROBLEM. THE 
NORWEGIANS WISHED TO PROVIDE A VENUE AND ANY OTHER 
FACILITIES -- PUBLIC, PRIVATE, SECRET - AS REQUIRED. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THANKED THE FM AND SAID HE WOULD PASS 
THE INFORMATION TO THE SECRETARY. 
5. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1003 EDT.
