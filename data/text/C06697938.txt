SUMMARY 
3. HUNGARIAN DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER FOR COALITION 
AFFAIRS/INTERIOR MINISTER GABOR KUNCZE USED HIS 45-MINUTE 
MEETING WITH ACTING SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT ON APRIL 1 TO 
OUTLINE CONCERNS ABOUT EU AND NATO ENLARGEMENT, BOSNIA, 
AND BORDER RELATIONS. HE NOTED THAT HUNGARY SUFFERED 
SEVERE ECONOMIC LOSSES STEMMING FROM THE BOSNIAN CONFLICT 
BUT IS BEARING THE BURDEN TO ENSURE A LASTING PEACE. 
KUNCZE PREDICTED ROMANIA WILL NOT SIGN ITS BILATERAL 
TREATY WITH HUNGARY UNTIL AFTER ITS UPCOMING ELECTIONS. 
ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT SAID WE ANTICIPATED THAT CENTRAL 
EUROPEAN COUNTRIES WOULD BE AMONG THE FIRST ENTRANTS INTO 
NATO AND NOTED THE U.S. STANDS READY TO FACILITATE 
HUNGARY'S ONGOING TREATY NEGOTIATIONS WITH ROMANIA. HE 
ALSO ENCOURAGED KUNCZE TO INVITE BOSNIA AND CROATIA TO THE 
UPCOMING DEDICATION CEREMONY OF THE INTERNATIONAL LAW 
ENFORCEMENT ACADEMY IN BUDAPEST. END SUMMARY. 
BOSNIA, NATO, AND EU 
4. KUNCZE EXPRESSED HUNGARY'S DEEP APPRECIATION FOR THE 
U.S. ROLE IN BOSNIA. HE OBSERVED THAT HUNGARY'S ONGOING 
COOPERATION WITH IFOR IS SMOOTH,'AND EMPHASIZED THAT 
HUNGARY'S IFOR ACTIVITIES ARE PRACTICAL PROOF OF ITS 
STRONG COMMITMENT TO BECOMING A NATO MEMBER. KUNCZE ADDED) 
THAT THE GOH IS WORKING INTENSIVELY ON EVENTUAL EU 
ACCESSION. 
5. ON NATO ENLARGEMENT, ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT REFERRED 
TO THE SECRETARY'S MARCH 20 SPEECH IN PRAGUE NOTING THAT 
WE DID NOT INTEND TO KEEP "PEOPLE IN THE HOLDING ROOM." 
HE ADDED WE ALSO NEED TO WORK "QUIETLY AND CONSTRUCTIVELY" 
TO INCORPORATE SUCH STATES AS THE BALTICS AND UKRAINE IN 
BROAD EUROPEAN SECURITY STRUCTURES. "WE CANNOT DIMINISH 
THE SECURITY OF SOME COUNTRIES WHILE ENHANCING THE 
SECURITY OF OTHERS."

BORDER RELATIONS 
7. THE ACTING SECRETARY ENCOURAGED HUNGARY TO COMPLETE 
ITS TREATY NiGOTIATIONS WITH ROMANIA. HE SAID THE U.S. 
HAD WORKED INTENSIVELY TO FACILITATE A SIMILAR AGREEMENT 
BETWEEN HUNGARY AND SLOVAKIA, AND IS MONITORING THE 
TREATY'S IMPLEMENTATION. KUNCZE REPLIED THAT UPCOMING 
ELECTIONS IN ROMANIA HAVE POLITICIZED HUNGARIAN TREATY 
ISSUES; REAL PROGRESS IS LIKELY TO WAIT UNTIL AFTER THE 
ELECTIONS. THE ACTING SECRETARY EMPHASIZED THE IMPORTANCE 
OF THE TREATY TO REINFORCE STABILITY IN THE REGION AND 
NOTED THE U.S. STANDS READY TO FACILITATE ITS CONCLUSION. 
ILEA 
8. THE ACTING SECRETARY ENCOURAGED KUNCZE TO INVITE THE 
BOSNIAN AND CROATIAN INTERIOR MINISTERS TO THE APRIL 22 
DEDICATION CEREMONY FOR THE INTERNATIONAL LAW ENFORCEMENT 
ACADEMY (ILEA) IN BUDAPEST. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
UNDERSCORED THE IMPORTANCE OF INCLUDING THESE COUNTRIES AS 
FULL PARTICIPANTS IN THE INTERNATIONAL.COMMUNITY. KUNCZE 
REPLIED THAT THIS QUESTION REMAINS OPEN BUT TOOK THE 
ACTING SECRETARY'S POINT UNDER ADVISEMENT. (COMMENT: IN A 
FOLLOW-ON DISCUSSION WITH A/S GELBARD, KUNCZE UNDERTOOK TO 
INVITE THE MINISTERS. END COMMENT.)
