SUBJECT: 'PAKISTAN: D/S TALBOTT INDO-PAK WORLDNET 
DIALOGUE (NOV. 13): FINAL WRAP-UP 
REF: ISLAMABAD 08561 
1. BACKGROUND: ON NOVEMBER 13, DEPUTY SECRETARY 
OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT ENGAGED PAKISTANI AND 
INDIAN INTERLOCUTORS IN A PRODUCTIVE DISCUSSION 
OF U.S. NON-PROLIFERATIONPOLICIES IN SOUTH ASIA. 
POST DESIGNED THIS PROGRAM TO REACH AUDIENCES BY 
USING PAKISTANI PRINT AND ELECTRONIC MEDIA. FOR 
THE FIRST TIME, THE COMPLETE INTERACTIVE WAS TAPED 
AND AIRED BY THE GOVERNMENT-CONTROLLED TV (PTV) 
AND NATIONAL RADIO (PBC). LEADING ENGLISH- AND 
URDU-LANGUAGE NATIONAL DAILIES ALSO EXTENSIVELY 
REPORTED ON AND QUOTED FROM THIS INTERACTIVE. 
2. SETTING: THE PROGRAM HAD SPECIAL IMPORTANCE 
IN PAKISTAN BECAUSE IT TOOK PLACE IN THE RUN-UP TO 
P.M. NAWAZ SHARIF'S DECEMBER VISIT TO THE U.S. 
POST IS PLEASED THAT IT COULD ENGAGE LOCAL 
PARTICIPANTS WHO WERE LEADING EXPERTS ON 
SECURITY, DEFENSE, NON-PROLIFERATION AND FOREIGN 
POLICY-RELATED ISSUES AND THAT USIA WAS ABLE TO 
RECRUIT D/S TALBOTT TO ADDRESS SOUTH ASIAN POLICY 
ISSUES. USIS ISLAMABAD AND USIS NEW DELHI JOINTLY 
PROPOSED THIS PROGRAM WITH THREE PRIMARY 
OBJECTIVES IN MIND: A) TO CLEARLY RESTATE U.S. 
NON-PROLIFERATION POLICIES IN SOUTH ASIA; B) TO 
REMOVE MISCONCEPTIONS ABOUT U.S. INTENTIONS IN 
THE ONGOING DIALOGUE WITH PAKISTAN AND INDIA; AND 
C) TO DISSEMINATE INFORMATION ON U.S. POLICY TO 
THE LARGEST POSSIBLE AUDIENCE. THIS WORLDNET 
PROGRAM ACHIEVED ALL THREE OBJECTIVES. 
3. ENGAGING GOVERNMENT MEDIA: THIS WORLDNET WAS 
THE FIRST OF ITS KIND IN THE SENSE THAT POST 
PLACED IMPORTANT POLICY MATERIAL, WITHOUT 
SUBSTANTIVE EDITING, WITH THE GOVERNMENT-
CONTROLLED ELECTRONIC MEDIA OF PAKISTAN. 
GOVERNMENT TELEVISION AND RADIO GAVE MAXIMUM 
COVERAGE TO THE INTERACTIVE. - 
4. RADIO: TALBOTT'S PROGRAM WAS THE LEAD STORY 
OF RADIO PAKISTAN'S FOUR NEWS BULLETINS Nmg 
MORNING. RADIO SUBSEQUENTLY AIRED AN EDITED 
VERSION OF 35 MINUTES DURATION ON ITS NATIONAL 
NETWORK OF 21 TRANSMITTERS. IT ALSO MADE THE 
BROADCAST AVAILABLE TO INTERNET USERS THRU ITS 
OFFICIAL HOME-PAGE [WWW.PAK.RADIO.PK]. THE SAME 
PROGRAM WAS AIRED AGAIN ON AN ISLAMABAD ENGLISH-
LANGUAGE PROGRAM, "PAKISTAN-CALLING". 
5. PTV: IN ADDITION TO ITS NEWS BULLETINS IN 
ENGLISH AND URDU ON SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 
PAKISTAN TELEVISION AIRED A 48-MINUTE VERSION OF 
RELEASE IN FULL
