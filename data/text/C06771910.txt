4. TS4 VAN AARTSEN INFORMED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THERE HAD 
BEEN A VERY TOUGH, VERY INTERESTING GENERAL AFFAIRS 
COUNCIL MEETING. THE ENTIRE EU, INCLUDING SWEDEN AND 
AUSTRIA, ENDORSED NATO ACTIONS IN'THE FRY. THE EU 
REITERATED IN ITS DECLARATION THE FIVE NATO POINTS AND 
DEMANDS ON MILOSEVIC. IT WAS AN INTERESTING DISCUSSION 
AND A GOOD START FOR THE NATO MEETING ON APRIL 12. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY REPLIED THIS WAS GREAT NEWS; IT WAS 
ESPECIALLY SIGNIFICANT THAT THOSE WHO WERE TRADITIONALLY 
NONALIGNED SHOULD CLOSE RANKS WITH THE REST OF THE EU. 
VAN AARTSEN SAID IT WAS A USEFUL DISCUSSION. THE EU WOULD 
SHOW ITS SUPPORT FOR THE REFUGEES AND IDPS BY PROVIDING UP 
TO 250 MILLION EUROS IN AID. 
5. -1(43-1- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY BRIEFED VAN AARTSEN ON THE 
CONTACT GROUP MEETING. IT HAD NOT BEEN A PRODUCTIVE 
MEETING. GERMAN POLITICAL DIRECTOR PLEUGER AS CHAIR OF 
THE MEETING PRESENTED THE MINIMUM FIVE RED LINE POSITIONS 
TO MAYORSKIY, IN A WAY THE RUSSIANS COULD SUPPORT. IT 
BECAME EVIDENT MAYORSKIY WAS UNDER INSTRUCTIONS NOT TO 
COOPERATE, AS HE FOCUSED ONLY ON THE ISSUE OF ENDING THE 
BOMBING. UNFORTUNATELY, THE MEETING NEVER GOT OUT OF THAT 
RUT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD VAN AARTSEN HE HAD A 
BILATERAL MEETING WITH HIS COUNTERPART, AVDEYEV, WHICH AT 
LEAST IN TONE WAS POSITIVE. 
6. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY COMMENTED THAT WITH A UNITED 
EUROPEAN REACTION, THE RUSSIANS FACE A SIMPLE AND STARK 
CHOICE - JOIN EUROPE, OR BE ISOLATED AS DEFENDERS OF 
MILOSEVIC. VAN AARTSEN REMARKED IT WAS NOT IN THE RUSSIAN 
INTEREST TO GO FURTHER WITH MILOSEVIC. HE HAD THE 
IMPRESSION FROM IVANOV THE RUSSIANS WERE NOT HAPPY WITH 
MILOSEVIC - HE WAS EMBARRASSING THE RUSSIANS. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY SAID THAT FROM HIS CONVERSATIONS WITH AVDEYEV IT 
WAS CLEAR NATO BOMBING EXACERBATED THEIR ANXIETIES ABOUT 
NATO. THE RUSSIANS FEARED THIS WOULD BECOME A LICENSE TO 
INTERVENE EVERYTIME THERE WERE ETHNIC PROBLEMS, BUT AT THE 
SAME TIME THEY CONTINUED TO WANT INTEGRATION WITH EUROPE. 
7. 4,84- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY PREDICTED THE RUSSIANS WOULD 
GO ALL OUT IN EVERY FORUM, SUCH AS THE OSCE AND THE CG, TO 
PROMOTE A DIPLOMATIC SOLUTION TO END THE BOMBING. 
HOWEVER, MILOSEVIC WOULD NOT GIVE IN TO ANY OF THE 
ESSENTIAL REQUIREMENTS. IT WAS DIFFICULT FOR THE RUSSIANS

TO SUPPORT THE OBVIOUS LOGIC OF THE SITUATION, WHICH WAS 
IF MILOSEVIC DID NOT GIVE IN, THE RUSSIANS MUST PUNISH 
HIM. VAN AARTSEN ASKED IF THE RUSSIANS NEEDED MORE TIME. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REPLIED THE USG WOULD BE WILLING TO 
ALLOW MORE TIME, BECAUSE MORE TIME WAS NEEDED FOR THE 
BOMBING CAMPAIGN. HE ADDED THE ALLIES MUST THINK IN TERMS 
OF WEEKS, NOT DAYS FOR THE CAMPAIGN. HE MENTIONED HE WAS 
AT SHAPE HEADQUARTERS AND HAD RECEIVED AN EXCELLENT BRIEF 
FROM SACEUR CLARK. NATO WAS CAUSING SIGNIFICANT DAMAGE, 
BUT MILOSEVIC WAS A TOUGH CUSTOMER WHO WAS DUG IN. 
8. tRi- VAN AARTSEN INFORMED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THE DUTCH 
OPPOSED SENDING IN GROUND TROOPS. THE DUTCH BELIEVED THE 
AIR CAMPAIGN SHOULD BE INTENSIFIED IF AT ALL POSSIBLE. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED, STATING THAT PRESIDENT 
CLINTON HAD REITERATED WE WOULD NOT GO INTO A GROUND 
CAMPAIGN AT THIS TIME. VAN AARTSEN REPLIED THE NAC ON 
APRIL 12 SHOULD DEMONSTRATE ALLIANCE UNITY. NATO MUST 
REMAIN STRONG ON DEMANDS ON MILOSEVIC - THIS SHOULD BE THE 
MAIN GOAL. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CONCURRED. 
AARTSEN ASKED IF AFTER THE CAMPAIGN CONCLUDED, 
AND THERE WAS AN AGREEMENT, WAS THERE ANY TALK OF AN UNSC 
RESOLUTION TO BACK A PEACEKEEPING FORCE IN KOSOVO? THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY REMARKED THE RUSSIANS COULD BE EITHER A 
COMPLICATING OR FACILITATING FACTOR, DEPENDING ON WHAT 
POSTURE THEY TOOK. THERE WAS NO SOLUTION WITHOUT AN 
INTERNATIONAL SECURITY PRESENCE WITH NATO AT THE CORE. • AS 
WITH BOSNIA, NATO WOULD JOIN WITH PARTNER NATIONS - IT 
WOULD BE ESPECIALLY USEFUL IF IT COULD WORK IN COOPERATION 
WITH THE OSCE AND THE UN. HOWEVER, GIVEN RUSSIAN AND 
CHINESE VETO POWER, THE SECURITY COUNCIL COULD NOT BE THE 
ARBITRATOR. AN ISSUE THE ALLIES MUST ALL PURSUE WITH THE 
RUSSIANS WAS PERSUADING THE SERBS OF THE NECESSITY OF A 
POLITICAL SETTLEMENT, AND ADHERING TO THE SAFE RETURN AND 
SECURITY OF THE REFUGEES. 
10. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY MENTIONED PROTECTION FOR 
KOSOVO UNDER SOME FORM OF UN AEGIS HAD BEEN DISCUSSED. 
HOWEVER, WORDS SUCH AS PROTECTORATE AND TRUSTEESHIP 
CARRIED A GREAT DEAL OF HISTORICAL BAGGAGE. IT WAS CLEAR 
BELGRADE HAD LOST ITS AUTHORITY/WRIT ON KOSOVO. THE 
IMPORTANT ISSUE NOW WAS EVERYONE AGREED ON THE PRINCIPLE 
AND CIRCUMSTANCES OF THE KOSOVARS RUNNING THEIR OWN LIVES. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY UNDERSTOOD THE HIGH DEGREE OF ANXIETY 
IN THE REGION THAT NATO NOT FOSTER THE INDEPENDENCE OF 
KOSOVO. THE DIFFICULTY WOULD BE IN FINDING AN 
INTERNATIONAL AND LEGAL PROVISION TO PROTECT AND ALLOW FOR
