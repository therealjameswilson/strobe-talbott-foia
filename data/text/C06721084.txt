SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY'S CONVERSATION WITH YUSUF BIN 
ALAWI ON IRAN 
REF: MUSCAT 00944 
1. (U) CLASSIFIED BY FRANCES D. COOK, AMBASSADOR. 
REASON: 1.5(B) AND (D). 
2. -(f!-). SUMMARY: IN ONE-ON-ONE CONVERSATIONS WITH DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT, YUSUF BIN ALAWI PRONOUNCED IRANIAN 
PRESIDENT KHATEMI'S DESIRE FOR DIALOGUE GENUINE AND 
PRESENTED THE U.S. CONCERN WITH IRANIAN WMD ACQUISITION 
AS SOMETHING THAT COULD BE INTERPRETED AS TAKING AWAY A 
COUNTRY'S MEANS OF SELF-DEFENSE. END SUMMARY. 
3. -4-e+ DURING THE RIDE TO A MARCH 14 MEETING WITH SULTAN 
QABOOS, THE OMANI MINISTER RESPONSIBLE FOR FOREIGN 
AFFAIRS, YUSUF BIN ALAWI, PROVIDED DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT WITH HIS ANALYSIS OF DEVELOPMENTS IN IRAN. BIN 
ALAWI FEELS THAT PRESIDENT KHATEMI IS GENUINE AND 
REFLECTS THE DESIRE OF AN INCREASING MAJORITY IN IRAN TO 
SEE REAL CHANGES INSIDE THE COUNTRY AND IN ITS DEALINGS 
WITH OTHER NATIONS, ESPECIALLY THE U.S. BIN ALAWI 
OPINED THAT THE UPCOMING PARLIAMENTARY ELECTIONS WILL BE 
IMPORTANT FOR CONSOLIDATING KHATEMI'S FREEDOM OF MANEUVER.

RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN KHATEMI AND RAFSANJANI, BIN ALAWI 
ELICITED D/S TALBOTT'S VIEWS. TALBOTT REPLIED' THAT WE 
SEE THE SITUATION IN IRAN IN MUCH THE SAME TERMS AS HE 
HAD DESCRIBED, THAT WE HAVE NOTICED THE DIFFERENCE IN 
TONE FROM THE IRANIAN LEADERSHIP AND THAT WE TRUST THAT 
ALL CONCERNED HAVE TAKEN NOTE OF THE CHANGE IN OUR TONE 
AS WELL (E.G., IN PRESIDENT CLINTON'S 'EID MESSAGE). HE 
CONTINUED BY SAYING THAT THE USG IS PREPARED TO 
ENCOURAGE PEOPLE-TO-PEOPLE DIALOGUE BUT, GIVEN THE 
LATTER'S LIMITATIONS, WOULD PREFER DIRECT 
GOVERNMENT-TO-GOVERNMENT TALKS. WE HAVE MADE CLEAR VIA 
THE SWISS, OUR ONLY DIRECT LINK TO THE IRANIANS, THAT WE 
ARE NOT LOOKING FOR ANYONE -- INCLUDING OUR FRIENDS IN 
THE REGION -- TO SERVE AS INTERMEDIARIES. BIN ALAWI 
SAID HE UNDERSTOOD THE DISTINCTION. 
5. 4-€9- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY FURTHER NOTED THAT EVERYONE 
MUST UNDERSTAND THE IMPORTANCE TO US OF THE THREE 
TROUBLESOME ISSUES (SUPPORT FOR TERRORISM, PURSUIT OF 
WMD AND ACTIVE OPPOSITION TO THE MEPP), WHICH MUST BE 
PART OF ANY DISCUSSION. BIN ALAWI VOLUNTEERED THAT, IF 
THE IRANIANS WERE HEARING THAT OTHERS WERE TRYING TO 
TAKE AWAY THEIR MEANS OF SELF-DEFENSE, THERE WOULD BE 
NOTHING TO TALK ABOUT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY COUNTERED 
THAT U.S. CONCERNS ABOUT THE MATTER, WHILE STRONG, 
SHOULD NOT BE PORTRAYED AS A SHOWSTOPPER BEFORE A 
DIALOGUE GETS OFF THE GROUND. 
COMMENT 
ALAWI'S VIEWS ON KHATEMI WERE UNREMARKABLE, 
AND HIS COMMENT ON WMD DISINGENUOUS. (REFTEL REPORTS ON 
BIN ALAWI'S MOST RECENT VISIT TO IRAN.) THE IRAN 
PORTFOLIO HAS BEEN ENTRUSTED TO THE MINISTER OF 
INFORMATION -- WHO WAS PRESENT AT THE MORNING SESSION 
(SEPTEL) WHICH FOCUSED ON PUBLIC AFFAIRS RE: IRAQ 
CONTINGENCY -- AS HE KNOWS KHATEMI FROM A VISIT TO IRAN 
PRIOR SEVERAL YEARS AGO. SEE POST REPORTING ON THE 
SULTAN'S EXTENSIVE USE OF MINISTERS OTHER THAN BIN ALAWI 
TO HANDLE SENSITIVE REGIONAL PORTFOLIOS.
