United States Department of State

RELEASE IN FULL 
Your gracious note means a lot to me. I have few 
regrets about this past year, but one of them is that I 
didn't find more occasion to cross paths with you. I 
enjoyed the few times we were thrown together, and I've 
certainly profited from my contact with your staff --_ 
especially the two Toms and Steve and Charles. It 
saddens me that you won't be around as much now that 
I'll be just down the corridor from the S/P offices. 
But I hope we'll still find occasions to get together. 
Perhaps at some point during my "transition," you'll 
give me your advice on how I should approach the new 
job. 
Happy New Year. 
7/1A-/ Strobe Talbott
