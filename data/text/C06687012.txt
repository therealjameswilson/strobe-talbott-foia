Dear Strobe, 
Please let me know if you find it obnoxious that I keep sending you communications dealing with 
Russian affairs, but I have decided to do it again. 
Some time ago, I sent you a memo by a friend of mine, Alex Goodwin, chairman of Sovcap, inc. 
He is a former antitrust lame& at the Justice Department, a successful businessman and now one 
of the most inventive and realistic people involved in developing CIS markets. Goodwin's No.1 
project is putting together a major international investment package to develop Primorski krai as 
the Pacific gateway to Russia and Central Asia. Having observed his work for a few years, I believe 
he may well pull it off unless my sootechestvenniki screw it up. His sense of the political-strategic 
aspects of a US-Russian economic partnership is very keen. 
Having learned of the new Enterprise Fund that the President approved for Russia, I immediately 
thought of Goodwin who had been beating his drum for this idea for three years. I think he would 
be an excellent appointment for the Fund's Board, given his knowledge of the terrain out there, his 
energy and outlook. He is in Moscow now, and plans to come to DC on June 20th. I have corne to 
value his assessments of the situation there - usually they are borne out by later events, so it might 
be interesting for you to hear what he has to say. Needless to say, he would very much appreciate 
, an opportunity to meet you.
