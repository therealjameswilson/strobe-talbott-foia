ISSUES WITH UKRAINIAN FOREIGN MINISTER, JULY 26, 1996 (U) 
1. ..TS.L, SUMMARY: IN A JULY 26 MEETING, WITH UKRAINIAN 
FOREIGN MINISTER UDOVENKO, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REVIEWED 
SECURITY ISSUES, URGING STRONGLY THAT UKRAINE NOT DELAY 
PROGRESS ON ABM/TMD AT THE SCC. HE ALSO RE/TERATED NATO 
POLICY ON NUCLEAR WEAPONS DEPLOYMENT, PROMISED FURTHER 
EFFORT ON DEVELOPMENT OF THE BILATERAL (GORE/KUCHMA) 
COMMISSION, AND INDICATED THAT HIS PROMISED REVIEW OF THE 
U.S. POSITION ON MTCR WAS CONTINUING. UDOVENKO HANDED 
OVER A LETTER TO SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER PROPOSING A CENTRAL 
EUROPEAN NUCLEAR WEAPONS-FREE ZONE, REPORTED ON UKRAINE'S 
ACCELERATED EFFORTS IN PRIVATIZATION, AND ADVISED THAT 
TALKS WITH RUSSIA ON THE BLACK SEA FLEET WOULD RESUME AND 
THAT RUSSIA HAD AGREED TO UKRAINE'S PROPOSAL FOR TALKS ON 
BORDER DEMARCATION. END SUMMARY.

5. -(44- WORLD BANK/IMF CONSULTATIONS: 
REGARDING FULFILLMENT OF IMF OBLIGATIONS, UDOVENKO SAID 
DIRECTOR CAMDESSUS HAD PROMISED TO SEND A TEAM TO KIEV IN 
A FEW WEEKS TO REVIEW UKRAINE'S PROGRESS. ON THE CREATION 
OF A CURRENCY STABILIZATION FUND, UDOVENKO SAID CAMDESSUS 
ACCEPTED THE IDEA BUT SAID THIS WOULD REQUIRE SOME TIME TO 
PREPARE. PM LAZARENKO NOTED, IN THAT REGARD, THAT IT WAS 
IMPORTANT TO HAVE THE FUND IN PLACE BEFORE WINTER, WHEN 
ENERGY COSTS WERE LIKELY TO PUT EXTREME PRESSURE ON THE 
GOVERNMENT'S FINANCES. OVERALL, UDOVENKO SAID THE PRIME 
MINISTER'S VISIT HAD CREATED A RAPPORT AND A CERTAIN TRUST 
WITH IMF AND WORLD BANK LEADERS THAT WAS EXTREMELY 
IMPORTANT. IT ALSO DEMONSTRATED TO THE IFI LEADERS THAT . 
UKRAINE "WAS LED BY ONE TEAM, UNLIKE BEFORE," AND THAT 
LAZARENKO "HAS THE STRONG SUPPORT OF PRESIDENT KUCHMA." 
6. 44H1- ASSASINATION ATTEMPT: 
ASKED ABOUT THE ATTEMPT ON PM LAZARENKO'S LIFE, UDOVENKO 
SAID THE INVESTIGATION IS PROCEEDING AND HAS ESTABLISHED 
THAT THE ORGANIZERS WERE FROM THE DONETSK COAL INDUSTRY. 
HE NOTED THAT LAZARENKO HAD BEGUN TO REORGANIZE THE 
INDUSTRY AND HAD ANNOUNCED THE IMPENDING CLOSURE OF 40 
UNPROFITABLE COAL MINES. HE SAID IT WAS CLEAR THAT THE 
ROOTS OF THE ATTEMPT ON THE PM'S LIFE WERE IN THE 
DONBASS. ' 
7. -(4)- REGIONAL RELATIONS: 
ON RELATIONS WITH UKRAINE'S NEIGHBORS, UDOVENKO SAID IT 
WAS IMPORTANT NOT TO ISOLATE BELARUS. HE NOTEDCHAT, AT 
BELARUS' INITIATIVE, HE HAD RECENTLY MET WITH FOREIGN 
MINISTER SENKO AND THE POLISH FOREIGN MINISTER TO DISCUSS 
REGIONAL ISSUES, INCLUDING NATO ENLARGEMENT. EACH COUNTRY 
VIEWED THAT ISSUE DIFFERENTLY. BELARUS IS OPPOSED, 
UKRAINE SAYS "NO PROBLEM," AND POLAND WANTS TO JOIN 
QUICKLY. UDOVENKO HANDED OVER A LETTER ADDRESSED TO THE 
SECRETARY (TEXT.FOLLOWS SEPTEL) THAT OUTLINES PROPOSALS 
FOR NATO PRONOUNCEMENTS TO QUIET FEARS ABOUT NUCLEAR 
WEAPONS DEPLOYMENT.

COLLINS HOW THIS DIFFERED FROM BELARUS' NWFZ PROPOSAL,

9. —tet RELATIONS WITH RUSSIA, CIS, U.S.: 
ON RELATIONS WITH RUSSIA, UDOVENKO NOTED THAT HE WOULD BE 
SEEING PRIMAKOV IN MOSCOW AUGUST 1. UKRAINE HAS INVITED 
PRIME MINISTER CHERNOMYRDIN TO KIEV FOR AN OFFICIAL 
VISIT. ALSO, THERE ARE PLANS TO RENEW THE BLACK SEA FLEET 
NEGOTIATIONS AND TO CONVENE THE UKRAINE-RUSSIA 
COMMISSION. UDOVENKO SAID UKRAINE SEEKS TO RENEW 
NEGOTIATIONS ON ALL ISSUES. HE NOTED PROUDLY THAT RUSSIA 
HAS, AFTER DECLINING TO DO SO FOR FOUR YEARS, AGREED TO 
BEGIN TALKS ON A TREATY ON BORDER DEMARCATION. REGARDING 
RELATIONS WITH THE CIS, UDOVENKO SAID PRESIDENT KUCHMA HAS 
CONFIRMED THE POLICY OF DEEPENING TIES WITH EUROPEAN 
STRUCTURES, WHILE COOPERATING, BUT NOT INTEGRATING, WITH 
THE CIS. 
10. -Ka)- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INDICATED THAT THE U.S. 
VERY MUCH APPRECIATES UKRAINE'S POSITION ON NATO. HE 
PROMISED THAT WE WOULD LOOK VERY CAREFULLY AT THE LETTER 
TO THE SECRETARY. AS A MATTER OF PRINCIPLE, HOWEVER, HE 
SAID THE U.S. WOULD NOT FAVOR A POLICY THAT RULED OUT NATO 
MEMBERSHIP FOR ANY DEMOCRATIC COUNTRY. HE STRESSED THAT 
NATO EXPANSION IS PART OF A LARGER PROCESS THAT INVOLVES 
MANY EUROPEAN INSTITUTIONS. UDOVENKO CONFIRMED UKRAINE'S 
INTEREST IN THE GORE-KUCHMA COMMISSION. HE SUGGESTED THAT 
THE BILATERAL WORKING GROUPS CONTINUE TO PRESS FORWARD. 
11. 4Gi- SECURITY ISSUES: 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REITERATED HIS COMMITMENT TO REMAIN 
PERSONALLY ENGAGED IN U.S.-UKRAINE DELIBERATIONS ON MTCR. 
ON ABM, HE SAID REACHING AN AGREEMENT QUICKLY IS OF 
IMPORTANCE TO PRESIDENT CLINTON -- IT MUST NOT BE 
DELAYED. HE SAID SOME IN CONGRESS WERE CONTEMPLATING 
ACTIONS THAT WOULD BE VERY DAMAGING TO THE TREATY AND IT 
WAS ESSENTIAL THAT UKRAINE NOT BE THE CAUSE FOR DELAY. 
UDOVENKO SAID UKRAINE TAKES THE MATTER VERY SERIOUSLY AND 
HAD CONVENED A SPECIAL SESSION OF THE NATIONAL SECURITY 
COUNCIL TO DEAL WITH THE ISSUE.

REGARDING EXPORT CONTROLS, UDOVENKO SAID UKRAINE HAD 
DECIDED TO CREATE A NEW AGENCY RESPONSIBLE FOR APPROVING 
ALL ARMS SALES. HE SAID THE UKRAINIAN NSC HAD ALSO 
DECIDED THAT ALTHOUGH UKRAINE HAS NO DEALINGS WITH LIBYA, 
STEPS MUST BE TAKEN TO PROHIBIT ALL CONTACTS WITH THE 
MILITARY THERE. HE SAID THIS AGENCY IS BEING SET UP TO 
DEAL THIS PROBLEM. HE STATED THAT IT IS NOT THE POLICY OF 
UKRAINE TO HAVE DEALINGS WITH LIBYA, IRAN OR IRAQ. 
UDOVENKO NOTED THAT UKRAINE HAS A CONSISTENT POLICY OF 
SCRUPULOUS COMPLIANCE WITH THE RULES OF ALL REGIMES IT HAS 
JOINED. HE SAID THERE HAVE BEEN NO BREACHES OF THIS 
PRINCIPLE, BUT MENTIONED THAT DISCUSSIONS BETWEEN 
INDIVIDUALS DOES NOT MEAN THAT THE GOVERNMENT PERMITS OR 
SANCTIONS IT. TO BETTER CONTROL CONTACTS WITH FOREIGN 
GOVERNMENTS, UDOVENKO SAID THE GOVERNMENT HAD ALSO 
RECENTLY DIRECTED THAT ALL OFFICIAL'FOREIGN CONTACTS BE 
APPROVED BY THE MFA, THEREBY CONFIRMING THE AUTHORITY OF 
THE.FOREIGN MINISTER. HE SUGGESTED THAT THIS POLITICAL 
GESTURE WOULD STRENGTHEN HIS HAND WITH OTHER MINISTRIES 
THAT MIGHT PREFER TO ACT MORE INDEPENDENTLY.
