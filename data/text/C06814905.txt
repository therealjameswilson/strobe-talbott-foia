MINISTERS OF'USSIAN EFFORTS TO RESCUE INTERNATIONAL AID

CHECHNYA. HE POINTED TO RUSSIAN COOPERATION WITH THE
COUNCIL OF EUROPE INVESTIGATION OF HUMAN RIGHTS ABUSES AND

WAS ONLY TERRORIST ACTS.

ACTION AGAINST TERRORISTS, BUT WHEN CIVILIANS BEGIN PAYING
THE PRICE THERE ARE QUESTIONS THAT MUST BE RAISED.
AXWORTHY NOTED THAT IF THE MILITARY CONFLICT IN CHECHNYA
WAS OVER, AS THE RUSSIANS HAD CLAIMED, IT WAS TIME TO BEGIN
HOLDING THE RUSSIANS TO APPROPRIATE STANDARDS, INCLUDING
STANDARDS OF JUSTICE.

12, ~ WITH THE MAJORITY OF THE TIME ALLOTTED FOR THE SESSION

13. ~ AXWORTHY SAID THAT ENVIRONMENTAL ISSUES WILL TAKE
CENTER STAGE IN 2002, THE YEAR OF THE RIO PLUS TEN
CONFERENCE. HE NOTED THE RELUCTANCE OF THE DEVELOPING
WORLD TO ADOPT. THE KYOTO PROTOCOL WAS BASED ON CONCERNS
ABOUT THE INTRINSIC FAIRNESS AND THEIR PERCEPTION THAT THE
KYOTO ACCORD WOULD HARM THEIR ECONOMIES. AXWORTHY
SUGGESTED THE G8 TRY A DIFFERENT STRATEGY. INSTEAD OF
FOCUSING ON THE BENEFITS OF IMPROVED EMISSIONS THE
DEVELOPED WORLD SHOULD POINT OUT THE REAL IMPACT OF CLIMATE
CHANGE RELATED DISASTERS. THERE HAS BEEN AN INCREASE IN
DEATHS AND DAMAGES DUE TO NATURAL DISASTERS. HE SUGGESTED
THAT THE DEVELOPED COUNTRIES SHOULD FOCUS EFFORTS ON
DISASTER PREVENTION AND MANAGEMENT. AXWORTHY AND FISCHER
AGREED THAT WATER MANAGEMENT COULD BECOME A SOURCE OF
CONFLICT AND SUGGESTED THAT THE ISSUE BE ADDRESSED AT THE
NEXT G8 SUMMIT IN ITALY. FISCHER ALSO RAISED THE QUESTION
OF RISING OIL PRICES ON DEVELOPING COUNTRY PROSPERITY AND
THE NEED TO ADDRESS BOTH RAIN FOREST AND NORTHERN FOREST
ISSUES, AS WELL AS ENDANGERED SPECIES AT THE NEXT SUMMIT
MEETING.
