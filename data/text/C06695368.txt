1. DURING THE 30 AUGUS 1994.MEETING.BETWEEN DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOT AND PRESIDENT BALAGUER, DR FOREIGN 
MINISTER CARLOS MORALES TRONCOSO PRESENTED US WITH 
COPIES OF FOUR DIPLOMATIC NOTES THAT CONSTITUTE GODR 
INVITATIONS TO JO/N THE MOG..THE NOTES ARE ADDRESSED TO 
THE "EMBASSIES" OF JAMAICA, BARBADOS GUYANA AND 
ANTIGUA, THE NOTES DO NOT SPECIFY WHICH EMBASSIES ARE 
TO RECEIVE THE NOTES. 
2. HERE IS AN EMBASSY TRANSLATION OF THE NOTE TO
JAMAICA. ALL THE OTHER ARE IDENTICAL WITH THE EXCEPTION 
OF THE NAME OF THE CONCERNED COUNTRY. 
BEGIN TEXT. THE SECRETARIAT OF STATE FOR FOREIGN 
RELATIONS PRESENTS SALUTATIONS TO THE HONORABLE EMBASSY 
OF JAMAICA ON THE OCCASION OF ADVISING AND MAKING KNOWN 
THE POLITICAL WILL OF THE JAMAICAN GOVERNMENT TO 
PARTICIPATE WITH A MAXIMUM OF 15 BORDER POLICEMEN IN 
THE MULTINATIONAL GROUP OF OBSERVERS (MOG) THAT WILL BE 
DEPLOYED ON THE DOMINICAN-HAITIAN BORDER IN COMPLIANCE 
WITH ACTIVITIES RELATED TO THE EMBARGO IMPOSED ON HAITI 
BY THE SECURITY COUNCIL OF THE U.N. 
THE SECRETARIAT OF STATE FOR FOREIGN RELATIONS 
COMMUNICATES TO YOUR HONORABLE EMBASSY THAT THE 
GOVERNMENT OF THE UNITED STATES HAS DISCUSSED WITH THE 
GOVERNMENT OF THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC THE PARTICIPATION 
OF JAMAICA IN THIS MULTINATIONAL GROUP WITHOUT 
OBJECTION SO THAT IT BE EXTENDED TO THE GOVERNMENT OF 
JAMA/CA IN ACCORD WITH ARTICLE 11 OF THE MEMORANDUM OF 
UNDERSTANDING SIGNED BETWEEN REPRESENTATIVES OF THE 
GOVERNMENT OF THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC WITH THAT OF THE 
UNITED STATES THAT SAYS THE FOLLOWING: 
"THE PARTIES DISPATCHING AND RECEIVING CAN ON THE BASIS 
OF MUTUAL AGREEMENT INVITE OTHER DISPATCHING PARTIES TO 
PARTICIPATE IN THE MOG, CONVERTING THEMSELVES INTO 
PARTIES TO THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING. THE 
PARTIES THAT ARE SO INVITED WILL INDICATE TO BOTH THE 
DISPATCHING AND RECEIVING STATE, THROUGH DIPLOMATIC 
CHANNELS, THEIR ACCEPTANCE OF THIS MEMORANDUM OF 
UNDERSTANDING." PROTOCOLARY CLOSING. DATED 30 AUGUST 
1994. END TEXT. 
3. POST FAXED COPIES OF ALL FOUR NOTES TO THE HAITI 
WORKING GROUP (MARK CLARK) ON 31 AUGUST. 
4. LATER ON 31 AUGUST DR VICE MINISTER FOR FOREIGN 
AFFAIRS TRULLOLS CONTACTED DCM ASKING FOR ASSISTANCE IN 
DELIVERING THESE NOTES. IN THE INTEREST OF SPEEDING UP 
THE PROCESS, TRULLOLS SAID HE WOULD PROVIDE THE U.S. 
EMBASSY WITH THE ORIGINALS OF THE NOTES. WE WILL 
FORWARD THE ORIGNALS TO WASHINGTON BY THE FASTEST MEANS
