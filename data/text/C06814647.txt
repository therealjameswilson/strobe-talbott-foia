IN ANTICIPATION OF THIS MEETING HE WISHED TO SPEAK WITH 
HER ABOUT THE LANGUAGE LAW RECENTLY PASSED BY THE LATVIAN 
PARLIAMENT. HE SAID THE USG UNDERSTOOD THE VOLATILE 
POLITICAL SITUATION AND THE CONSCIENTIOUS MEASURES WHICH 
WOULD BE REQUIRED TO ENSURE THE LANGUAGE LAW BE MADE

USG MESSAGE WAS ONE OF COMPLETE SUPPORT. THE DEPUTY. 
SECRETARY EMPHASIZED HE WANTED THE PRESIDENT TO HEAR THAT 
MESSAGE FROM HIM PERSONALLY IN ADVANCE OF HIS MEETING WITH 
FM BIRKAVS. THE USG SUPPORTED OSCE CALLS FOR AMENDMENT TO 
THE LAW AND THE RETURN OF INCONSISTENT PROVISIONS TO THE 
PARLIAMENT. 
6. ON, THE PRESIDENT ASKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY IF THERE 
WERE CONCRETE PROVISIONS WHICH, IN HIS JUDGMENT, REQUIRED 
CHANGE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED OSCE LEGAL EXPERTS 
WERE EXPECTED TO ARRIVE IN RIGA ON JULY 13-AND PRAISED 
THESE CONSULTATIONS AS A LAUDABLE IDEA. HE SUGGESTED SHE 
CONSIDER OBTAINING LEGAL GUIDANCE FROM THE EU AS WELL. 
7. (0)- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED THE USG WOULD NOT 
ASK FOR CHANGES IN THE LAW BEYOND OSCE RECOMMENDATIONS. 
THE PRESIDENT EXPRESSED RELIEF AND CONFESSED TO CONCERN 
SUCH A MOVE WOULD MAKE HER UNPOPULAR AND RAISE THE 
POLITICAL STAKES. 
8. IS4.. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASSURED THE PRESIDENT HE 
UNDERSTOOD HER CONCERNS AND WAS.AWARE SHE HAD MANY 
CONSIDERATIONS TO BALANCE: THE GOAL POSTS SHOULD REMAIN 
WHERE THE OSCE SET THEM; THE USG DID NOT WISH TO SEE THEM 
MOVED. HE EMPHASIZED THE USG WOULD EXPRESS THOSE ' 
SENTIMENTS BOTH PUBLICLY AND IN DIPLOMATIC INTERCHANGE. 
STEADY AND FAIR PROGRESS WAS THE USG'GOAL BOTH FOR 
LATVIA'S SAKE AND FOR THE SAKE OF THE USG. 
9. *Q.1. THE PRESIDENT SAID THIS PLEDGE OF USG SUPPORT 
WOULD HELP HER MAKE THE DIFFICULT DECISION REQUIRED BY THE 
JULY 14 DEADLINE. THE AMBASSADOR SAID AN EU COMMISSION 
EXPERT COULD BE AVAILABLE BY'JULY 13 AND OFFERED TO 
ARRANGE SUCH A VISIT. PRESIDENT VIKE-FREIBERGA AGREED AND 
ASKED THE AMBASSADOR TO SERVE AS INTERMEDIARY.
