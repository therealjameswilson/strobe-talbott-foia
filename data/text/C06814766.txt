SUBJECT: ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT'S MEETING WITH PRC 
CHARGE ON TAIWAN STATEMENT 
REF: TAIPEI 2144 
(U) CLASSIFIED BY ACTING SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT. REASON 
1.5 (B/D). 
1. ISISUMMARY: ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT CALLED IN PRC 
EMBASSY CHARGE THE AFTERNOON OF JULY 13 TO DISCUSS LEE 
TENG-HUI'S RECENT STATEMENTS ON CROSS-STRAIT RELATIONS AND 
THE STATUS OF TAIWAN. TALBOTT PROVIDED REASSURANCES THAT 
OUR "ONE CHINA" POLICY HAD NOT CHANGED. IN RESPONSE THE 
CHARGE DREW HEAVILY ON THE CHINESE MFA SPOKESMAN'S 
STATEMENT ATTACKING LEE'S REMARKS. HE EXPRESSED 
APPRECIATION FOR WHITE HOUSE AND STATE DEPARTMENT PUBLIC 
STATEMENTS ON THE ISSUE, AND HOPED WE COULD ALSO PUBLICLY 
REITERATE OUR ADHERENCE TO THE THREE COMMUNIQUES AND THE 
"THREE NOS." CHARGE IS REQUESTED TO USE TALBOTTS POINTS 
IN DISCUSSION WITH CHINESE COUNTERPARTS. END SUMMARY.

2.A<ACTING SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT CALLED IN 
PRC EMBASSY CHARGE LIU XIAOMING JULY 13 TO ASSURE THE 
CHINESE GOVERNMENT THAT THE U.S. GOVERNMENT'S ONE CHINA 
POLICY HAD NOT CHANGED IN THE WAKE OF THE REPORTED 
STATEMENTS MADE BY TAIWAN PRESIDENT LEE TENG-HUI IN AN 
INTERVIEW WITH THE VOICE OF GERMANY RADIO (DEUTSCHE 
WELLE). LEE DESCRIBED RELATIONS BETWEEN TAIWAN AND THE 
PRC AS "STATE-TO-STATE RELATIONS." TALBOTT STRESSED THAT 
THE USG HAD RECEIVED NO ADVANCE WORD THAT LEE WAS GOING TO 
MAKE SUCH REMARKS AND THAT WE HAD NOT YET RECEIVED THROUGH 
UNOFFICIAL CHANNELS AN EXPLANATION OF THE MEANING OF THE 
REMARKS. HE OBSERVED THAT WE HAD, HOWEVER, RECEIVED WORD 
INFORMALLY FROM TAIPEI THAT TAIWAN DOES NOT SEEK TO ALTER 
THE FRAMEWORK OF CROSS STRAIT TIES AND REMAINS INTERESTED 
IN PROMOTING DIALOGUE. WE WILL BE SEEKING, THROUGH 
UNOFFICIAL CHANNELS, FURTHER CLARIFICATION. 
3. (el TALBOTT NOTED THAT WHILE THERE WERE A LOT OF 
QUESTIONS ABOUT THE MEANING OF LEE'S STATEMENT LEE HAD 
MADE WITHOUT WARNING, THERE WAS NO QUESTION ABOUT USG 
POLICY. HE THEN MADE THE FOLLOWING POINTS: 
— OUR "ONE CHINA" POLICY IS UNCHANGED. WE ABIDE BY THE 
PRINCIPLES OF THE THREE SINO-US COMMUNIQUES. WE DO NOT 
SUPPORT TAIWAN INDEPENDENCE. WE DO NOT SUPPORT "ONE 
CHINA, ONE TAIWAN" OR "TWO CHINAS". WE DO NOT SUPPORT 
TAIWAN'S ENTRY INTO INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS THAT 
REQUIRE STATEHOOD FOR MEMBERSHIP. 
— WE CONTINUE TO BELIEVE THAT THE TAIWAN ISSUE IS A 
MATTER FOR THE CHINESE PEOPLE, ON BOTH SIDES OF THE TAIWAN 
STRAIT, TO RESOLVE. WE RETAIN AN ABIDING INTEREST AND 
CONCERN THAT ANY RESOLUTION BE PEACEFUL. 
— THE UNITED STATES HAS ENCOURAGED BOTH SIDES TO ENGAGE 
IN MEANINGFUL, SUBSTANTIVE DIALOGUE. WE BELIEVE THAT. 
FACE-TO-FACE MEETINGS ARE THE BEST WAY TO CLARIFY 
MISUNDERSTANDINGS AND BUILD TRUST. 
— THROUGH UNOFFICIAL CHANNELS WE WILL BE REITERATING TO 
TAIPEI OUR DEEP CONCERN THAT THE DISCUSSIONS AND DIALOGUE 
BETWEEN BOTH SIDES OF THE STRAIT CONTINUE. WE WILL NOTE 
THAT NEITHER SIDE SHOULD TAKE STEPS WHICH WOULD MAKE 
HOLDING THIS TYPE OF DISCUSSION MORE DIFFICULT. 
4. 1S)..LIU EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THE OPPORTUNITY TO 
EXCHANGE VIEWS. HE EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THE 
ADMINISTRATION'S REITERATION OF SUPPORT FOR THE THREE 
COMMUNIQUES AND THE "THREE NOS." HE STATED THAT THE 
CHINESE GOVERNMENT WAS CONCERNED ABOUT LEE TENG-HUI'S 
REMARKS. HE ASSERTED THAT THE REMARKS CONSTITUTED A 
FLAGRANT ATTEMPT TO PUT TAIWAN ON A SEPARATIST PATH AND 
REPRESENTED AN OUT AND OUT NEGATION OF THE "ONE CHINA 
POLICY." HE STATED THAT LEE WAS DELIBERATELY ATTEMPTING 
TO DIVIDE CHINA'S SOVEREIGNTY AND TERRITORY. HE ASSERTED 
THAT SINCE ASSUMING OFFICE, LEE HAD DISREGARDED THE 
OPINION OF THE MAJORITY OF THE PEOPLE ON THE ISLAND AND 
THE MAINLAND AND EMBARKED A SEPARATIST PATH. HE WARNED 
THAT LEE'S REMARKS, WHICH IMPLIED TWO CHINAS OR ONE CHINA 
AND ONE TAIWAN AND DEFINED CROSS-STRAIT RELATIONS AS 
"STATE TO STATE RELATIONS," HAD GONE TOO FAR DOWN A 
DANGEROUS ROAD 'AND THREATENED TO OBSTRUCT THE IMPROVEMENT 
OF RELATIONS BETWEEN THE TWO SIDES. 
5. 18). LIU REFERRED TO THE PRC MFA SPOKESMAN'S COMMENT ON 
LEE'S REMARKS NOTING THAT THERE WAS ONLY ONE CHINA IN THE 
WORLD AND THAT TAIWAN WAS PART OF CHINA. CHINA'S 
TERRITORY AND SOVEREIGNTY ARE INDIVISIBLE. THE MAJORITY - 
OF COUNTRIES RECOGNIZE TAIWAN AS PART OF CHINA. ALTHOUGH

DIFFERENCE. HE ALSO SUGGESTED THAT THE QUALITY AND 
QUANTITY OF ARMS SALES TO TAIWAN HAD EMBOLDENED THE TAIWAN 
AUTHORITIES.

THERE WOULD BE A COMMUNICATION THROUGH CROSS-STRAIGHT 
CHANNELS. LIU SAID THAT WANG HAD CALLED FOR 
• CLARIFICATION, BUT SAID THAT THERE WOULD NOT BE ANY 
ADDITIONAL COMMUNICATION THROUGH THE CROSS-STRAIT CHANNEL. 
HE ADDED THAT THE MFA SPOKESMAN AND PARTY CENTRAL 
COMMITTEE SPOKESMAN WERE ALL ON RECORD. ALL SENT A STRONG 
CONSISTENT MESSAGE TO TAIWAN THAT LEE'S REMARKS WERE 
TOTALLY UNACCEPTABLE TO THE CHINESE PEOPLE AND THE CHINESE 
GOVERNMENT. 
8. 4.41-ALBOTT TOLD LIU THAT THE USG WOULD SEEK FURTHER

WITH CHINA ON THE MATTER IN ORDER TO AVOID 
MISUNDERSTANDING. EAP ACTING A/S RUST DEMING OBSERVED 
THAT IT WAS IMPORTANT FOR BOTH SIDES TO AVOID ACTIONS THAT
WOULD COMPLICATE THE CROSS-STRAIT DIALOGUE. 
9. 1.64-LIU RESPONDED THAT THE PRC ALWAYS ,HANDLED THE 
TAIWAN ISSUE WITH CAUTION AND STROVE FOR PEACEFUL 
REUNIFICATION. CHINA IS NOT TRYING TO "COMPLICATE" THE 
MATTER, ON THE CONTRARY, THE PRC GOVERNMENT HAD BEEN 
ENGAGED IN PREPARATIONS FOR WANG'S VISIT TO TAIWAN. LEE'S 
REMARKS ARE UNACCEPTBLE TO CHINA AND THE INTERNATIONAL 
COMMUNITY. HE ASSERTED THAT THE USG NEEDED TO REITERATE 
ITS COMMITMENT TO THE ONE CHINA POLICY, THE THREE JOINT 
COMMUNIQUES AND THE "THREE NOS" SO THAT LEE TENG-HUI WOULD 
NOT MISREAD THE MESSAGE.
