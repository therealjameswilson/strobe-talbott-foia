-{44 PARTICIPANTS: THE ACTING SECRETARY AND SOUTH 
AFRICAN DEPUTY PRESIDENT MBEKI 
SUBJECT: UNSYG ELECTION 
5.48+ THE ACTING SECRETARY PASSED ON THE SECRETARY'S 
REGRETS THAT HIS TRIP TO THE MIDDLE EAST PREVENTED HIM 
FROM CALLING MBEKI HIMSELF. THE ACTING SECRETARY NOTED 
PRESIDENTS CLINTON AND MANDELA HAD SPOKEN PREVIOUSLY 
ABOUT THE UN. BOTH AGREED ON THE IMPORTANCE OF THE UN 
AND WERE COMMITTED TO ENHANCING ITS EFFECTIVENESS. THE 
ISSUE OF WHO WOULD LEAD THE UN INTO THE TWENTY-FIRST 
CENTURY WAS VERY IMPORTANT AND THE U.S. BELIEVED IT IS 
TIME FOR A CHANGE. 
6. -Hi* THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THE U.S ADMIRED 
BOUTROS-GHALI'S ACCOMPLISHMENTS. HOWEVER, WHEN HE . 
ENTERED OFFICE FIVE YEARS AGO, BOUTROS-GHALI STATED HE 
WOULD ONLY HOLD OFFICE FOR A SINGLE TERM. OUT OF 
RESPECT FOR BOUTROS-GHALI, THE U.S AGREED TO SUPPORT AN 
EXTENSION OF ANOTHER YEAR TO HIS TERM, BUT 
BOUTROS-GHALI REJECTED THE OFFER. THE U.S. SUBSEQUENTLY 
WITHDREW THE OFFER OF AN EXTENSION AND TOLD 
BOUTROS-GHALI THAT, IF NECESSARY, THE U.S. WOULD VETO A 
SECOND TERM. 
7. 444- THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THE U.S. WAS 
CONSULTING WITH OTHER COUNTRIES ON THE SELECTION OF A 
NEW UNSYG AND ASKED IF THE SOUTH AFRICAN GOVERNMENT HAD 
ANY THOUGHTS ON THE MATTER. 
MBEKI SAID HE HAD NOT STARTED LOOKING AT THE 
ISSUE, BUT NOTED HE WOULD BE IN WASHINGTON D.C. ON OR 
ABOUT JULY 17-20 AND SUGGESTED HE COULD DISCUSS THE 
MATTER THEN. MBEKI SAID HE WOULD DISCUSS THE MATTER 
WITH PRESIDENT MANDELA AND SAID HE WAS SURE THE SAG 
WOULD HAVE SOME THOUGHTS ON THE ISSUE IN THE COMING 
WEEKS. 
9.-4174- THE ACTING SECRETARY WELCOMED THE OPPORTUNITY TO 
MEET WITH MBEKI IN JULY AND ASSURED MBEKI THAT THE U.S. 
WAS VERY SENSITIVE TO REGIONAL AND OTHER CONSIDERATIONS 
IN THE SELECTION OF A UNSYG AND WANTED TO TAKE AFRICAN 
ASPIRATIONS INTO ACCOUNT. 
10. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1207 EDT. 
11. END TEXT.
