February 14, 1994 
Dear Ed: 
Belated thanks for your kind letter. It didn't reach 
my office until February 3rd, and I have been swamped with 
confirmation and other matters since then. I am glad that 
you think our overall course is the right one, and I share 
your sense of some discouragement with the disarray of the 
democrats. I have passed along your thoughts about U.S. 
Assistance to Tom Simons and I'm sure he will look into 
the matter. By the time you receive this you will be back 
from Virgin Gorda. I hope you and Jill had a restful • 
vacation and that you are full of energy for the many good 
fights you are waging. 
A11 the best. 
Sincerely, 
Mr. Edward,Kline 
1165 Park Avenue, 
New York, NY 10128.
