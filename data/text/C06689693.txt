Dear Dick: 
Thanks so much for your kind letter. I will 
greatly miss Carnegie. It's one of the most 
gratifying associations I have ever had. I know a 
little bit about the work that you've been doing 
with the Russian-American Banking Forum. It's 
extremely important technical assistance if they are 
to have any hope at all of making it as a modern 
economy. I hope we'll find occasions from time to 
time to meet. In the meanwhile, thanks again for 
writing and the best of luck. 
Sincerely, 
A 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Adviser to The Secretary 
on The New Independent States 
Mr. Richard A. Debs, 
Morgan Stanley, 
1251 Avenue of the Americas, 
New York, NY.
