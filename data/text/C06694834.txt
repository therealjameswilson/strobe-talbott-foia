2. ,SUMMARY: PRESIDENT RAWLINGS IS LEANING TOWARD 
TRYING TO CONVINCE THE UNITED NATIONS TO ADOPT THE 
LIBERIA PEACEKEEPING EFFORT. IT WOULD BE A HYBRID 
OPERATION IN WHICH THE UN WOULD PAY THE BILLS AND ECOMOG 
WOULD DO THE PEACEKEEPING. HE PLANS TO CONVENE A 
MEETING OF THE COMMITTEE OF NINE IN ABOUT 10 DAYS TO 
DISCUSS THE LIBERIA PROCESS AND HOPES TO HOLD AKOSOMBO 
II IN 2-3 WEEKS. END SUMMARY. 
3. AMBASSADOR CALLED ON PRESIDENT RAWLINGS OCTOBER 27 
AT LATTER'S REQUEST TO DISCUSS LIBERIA. RAWLINGS OPENED 
BY ASKING WHAT HAD TRANSPIRED ON LIBERIA DURING DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT'S VISIT TO ABIDJAN. AMBASSADOR NOTED 
THE IVORIANS WOULD NOT SEND TROOPS TO PARTICIPATE IN 
ECOMOG AND WOULD BE UNLIKELY TO TAKE A HIGH PROFILE ROLE 
IN PEACE NEGOTIATIONS. THEY WERE WORRIED ABOUT THE 
SPILLOVER EFFECT OF THE WAR ON COTE D'IVOIRE, 
PARTICULARLY THE INCREASE IN THE NUMBER OF REFUGEES AND 
WERE SUPPORTIVE OF NEGOTIATIONS. RAWLINGS COMMENTED 
THAT AT LEAST THE GOCI HAD NOT COME OUT AGAINST THE 
AKOSOMBO AGREEMENT. 
4. IN A DISCUSSION OF POSSIBLE OPTIONS FOR INTER-
NATIONAL ACTION (OR INACTION) ON LIBERIA, RAWLINGS 
REITERATED THE VIEW HE HAD PRESENTED TO DEPSEC TALBOTT'S 
DELEGATION TO THE EFFECT THAT THE UN SHOULD NOT TAKE 
OVER THE ENTIRE LIBERIA PEACEKEEPING OPERATION. HE 
LEANED INSTEAD, HE SAID, TOWARD ONE OF THE OTHER OPTIONS 
MENTIONED BY A/S MOOSE--I.E., A HYBRID APPROACH IN WHICH 
THE UN ADOPTED THE LIBERIA OPERATION, WITH THE UN PAYING 
THE BILL AND ECOMOG DOING THE PEACEKEEPING. HE WOULD BE 
DISCUSSING THIS OPTION WITH FONMIN ASAMOAH AND OTHER 
ADVISERS AS WELL AS WITH ECOWAS LEADERS. AMBASSADOR 
AGREED THAT THIS COULD BE A GOOD APPROACH BUT WOULD FACE 
DIFFICULTY IN GAINING APPROVAL AT THE UN. 
5. ASKED FOR HIS THINKING ABOUT THE AKOSOMBO II MEETING 
HE HAS IN MIND, RAWLINGS SAID THAT IT WOULD INVOLVE 
THOSE WHO PARTICIPATED AT AKOSOMBO I PLUS THE 
SIGNIFICANT MORE RECENT FACTIONS. THE LNTG WOULD NEED 
TO BE REPRESENTED, PERHAPS IN A DELEGATION WHICH 
INCLUDED THE AFL. APPROPRIATE REPRESENTATION OF THE 
KRAHNS WAS TRICKY, HOWEVER, PARTICULARLY FOLLOWING THE 
ORDER FOR BOWEN TO STEP DOWN AS THE AFL COMMANDER. 
RAWLINGS PLANNED TO CONVENE A MEETING OF THE COMMITTEE 
OF NINE IN ABOUT 10 DAYS AND HOPED THAT AKOSOMBO II 
COULD TAKE PLACE IN TWO OR THREE WEEKS. IN THE NEARER
