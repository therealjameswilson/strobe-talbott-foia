2. SUMMARY: THE FRENCH BELIEVE RUSSIAN PARTICIPATION 
IN THE BOSNIA PEACE IMPLEMENTATION FORCE (IFOR) IS 
EXTREMELY IMPORTANT. THEY ARE INCLINED TO MAKE SOME 
CONCESSIONS TO RUSSIA, EITHER TO FISURE IT CAN 
PARTICIPATE IN IFOR OR AS POLITICAL COMPENSATION IF 
PARTICIPATION PROVES IMPOSSIBLE. THE FRENCH ARE 
CONCERNED THAT, IF THEY ARE NOT IN IFOR, THE RUSSIANS 
WILL RETALIATE BY TAKING A HARD LINE IN OTHER AREAS, 
E.G. NATO.ENLARGEMENT AND CFE. IN A MEETING OCT. 19 
WITH U.S. DELEGATION HEADED BY DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT, A FRENCH INTERAGENCY TEAM DID NOT OFFER MANY 
SPECIFICS OF THE CONCESSIONS THEY WOULD ENVISION, BUT 
DID DISCUSS AT SOME LENGTH THE IDEA OF OPENLY "SLOWING 
DOWN" THE NATO ENLARGEMENT PROCESS. THEY ALSO 
IDENTIFIED POLITICAL CONTROL OF IFOR AS A KEY AREA IN 
WHICH TO ACCOMMODATE RUSSIAN CONCERNS. THE TALBOTT 
DELEGATION, IN ADDITION TO BRIEFING THE FRENCH ON ITS 
JUST-COMPLETED MEETINGS IN MOSCOW, UNDERLINED NORTH 
ATLANTIC COUNCIL (NAC) CONTROL OF IFOR AS A SINE QUA 
NON FOR U.S. PARTICIPATION AND THE NEED FOR ABSOLUTE 
SOLIDARITY AMONG THE WESTERN ALLIES IN DEALING WITH 
RUSSIA ON IFOR. THE DELEGATION ALSO REBUTTED 
FORCEFULLY THE FRENCH SUGGESTION TO SLOW DOWN NATO 
ENLARGEMENT TO ASSUAGE RUSSIAN CONCERNS. END SUMMARY. 
3. U.S. DELEGATION HEADED BY DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT 
AND UNDER SECRETARY OF DEFENSE FOR POLICY SLOCOMBE HAD 
A MEETING IN PARIS OCTOBER 19 WITH A FRENCH INTERAGENCY 
TEAM HEADED BY THE PRIME MINISTER'S NATIONAL SECURITY 
ADVISOR BRUNO RACINE. THE FRENCH WERE KEEN TO HEAR 
ABOUT THE TALBOTT DELEGATION'S OCTOBER 17-18 MEETINGS 
IN MOSCOW ON NATO/RUSSIA COOPERATION IN IFOR. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY LAID OUT THE DIFFERENT SCENARIOS THE 
U.S. AND RUSSIAN SIDES WERE CONSIDERING, AND UNDERLINED 
THAT THE MEETINGS HAD BEEN USEFUL IN IDENTIFYING THE 
RED LINES FOR THE RESPECTIVE SIDES. HE NOTED, HOWEVER, 
THAT BRIDGING THE GAP, I.E. ENSURING THAT IFOR WAS 
NATO-LED WHILE FINDING A WAY TO SAVE RUSSIA'S FACE, 
WOULD REQUIRE FURTHER WORK. HE SAID WE COULD EXPECT 
RUSSIA TO CONVEY A GREAT SENSE OF URGENCY, PORTRAYING 
YELTSIN'S MEETINGS WITH PRESIDENTS CHIRAC AND CLINTON 
AS KEY DECISION POINTS. THE WESTERN PARTNERS NEED TO 
STAY COOL IN'THE FACE OF THAT PRESSURE. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY ALSO STRESSED THAT ANY DEPARTURE FROM A

5. MOD STRATEGIC AFFAIRS DIRECTOR JEAN-CLAUDE MALLET 
DID AGREE WITH THE TALBOTT DELEGATION THAT RUSSIAN 
ABSENCE FROM IFOR WOULD NOT PUT THE ENTIRE PEACE 
PROCESS AT RISK. HE ADDED, HOWEVER, THAT RUSSIA 
PROBABLY WOULD ATTEMPT TO RETALIATE AGAINST EXCLUSION 
FROM IFOR BY TAKING HARD LINES ON A WHOLE RANGE OF 
ISSUES, INCLUDING NATO ENLARGEMENT AND CFE. MALLET 
CALLED THIS THE "COHERENT STRATEGY OF THE RUSSIAN 
DEFENSE ESTABLISHMENT."

RUSSIAN ELECTIONS. MALLET SAID HE SAW TWO PRIORITIES:

COMMENT: CHIRAC FREE-LANCING ON RUSSIA POLICY? 
12. FOLLOWING THE MEETING, RACINE TOLD THE AMBASSADOR 
HE WAS VERY CONCERNED THAT CHIRAC MIGHT GO TOO FAR IN 
HIS MEETINGS WITH YELTSIN OCTOBER 20-21. RACINE SAID, 
TO OUR SURPRISE, THAT HE WOULD NOT BE INVOLVED IN THE 
YELTSIN VISIT, AND INDICATED THAT MAIN GOVERNMENT 
SECURITY POLICY EXPERTS FELT CHIRAC WAS CUTTING THEM 
OUT. IN EMBASSY'S VIEW, CHIRAC, WHO IS INTERESTED IN 
AND KNOWLEDGEABLE ABOUT RUSSIA, MAY BE FOLLOWING HIS 
OWN ADVICE. ANOTHER POSSIBILITY IS THAT NATIONAL 
ASSEMBLY PRESIDENT PHILIPPE SEGUIN, A KEY CHIRAC 
POLITICAL ALLY, HAS THE PRESIDENT'S EAR. SEGUIN VIEWS 
SECURITY NOT IN TRANSATLANTIC TERMS, BUT IN 
PAN-EUROPEAN TERMS. RUSSIA IS AN ESSENTIAL PART OF 
EUROPE IN SEGUIN'S EYES, WHILE HE IS VERY CRITICAL OF 
THE U.S. ROLE ON THE CONTINENT. END COMMENT.
