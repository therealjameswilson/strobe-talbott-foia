Dear Mr. Ambassador: 
RELEASE IN FULL 
My office has already informed yours that, with regret, I 
am unable to accept the invitation to speak to the luncheon , 
organized by the Canadian Institute of International Affairs. 
As it happens, that day and indeed most of that week, I will be 
tied up in intensive preparations for a trip that I am making 
to the former Soviet Union that weekend. 
I am sorry not to be able to accept the Institute's kind 
invitation. Canada has been an extremely important partner of 
the United States both in general and specifically on policy 
towards Russia and the New Independent States. Therefore, I 
would have very much liked to meet with this influential and 
interesting group. 
I am also looking forward to getting to know you better 
and working with you, and this would have perhaps been a good 
occasion to do that. But I trust there will be other chances 
in the near future. 
Sincerely, 
C I
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Adviser to The Secretary 
on the New Independent States 
His Excellen6r, 
General John de Chastelain, 
Ambassador of Canada.
