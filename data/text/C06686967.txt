the polls to answer 4 questions in a national referendum 
having no legal effect but which may well decide the fate 
of Boris Yeltsin and his economic program. 
These four questions are: 
Do you trust the President to lead the country? 
Do you approve his social and economic program? 
Are you in favor of early elections for a new 
Parliament? 
Are you in favor of early elections for a new 
President?
