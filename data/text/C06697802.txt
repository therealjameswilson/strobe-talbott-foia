5. -444— THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THANKED PETRELLA FOR 
HOSTING THE MERCOSUR LUNCH DURING HIS RECENT VISIT. HE 
SAID IT WAS A GOOD MOVE FOR THE GOA, BOTH SYMBOLICALLY 
AND SUBSTANTIVELY, AND EXPRESSED HIS APPRECIATION FOR 
ARGENTINE LEADERSHIP IN MERCOSUR MATTERS. HE COMMENTED 
THAT LUIGI EINAUDI WAS IN BUENOS AIRES FOR A GUARANTORS 
MEETING. PETRELLA REPORTED AGREEMENT ON A GOOD PAPER 
COVERING PROCEDURAL MATTERS AND NOTED THAT EINAUDI HAS 
BEEN AN ASSET. 
- 
7. 4S.4 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD PETRELLA THAT SINCE 
THEIR LAST MEETING, HE HAD FOCUSED ON RUSSIA AND THE 
RECENT ELECTIONS. HE SAID THAT THINGS WENT WELL DESPITE 
CONFLICT BETWEEN FACTIONS WITHIN THE YELTSIN CAMP. 
PETRELLA SAID DEVELOPMENTS IN RUSSIA WERE A GLOBAL ISSUE 
AND COULD NOT BE NEGLECTED. HE SAID PRESIDENT MENEM 
READ AN ARTICLE BY HENRY KISSINGER AND WAS INTERESTED IN 
U.S. VIEWS ON RUSSIA. HE SAID THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WAS 
AN ACKNOWLEDGED EXPERT ON RUSSIA, AND HE AS WELL AS 
PRESIDENT MENEM VALVED HIS PERSPECTIVE. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY REPLIED THAT HE WAS FLATTERED AND SUGGESTED 
THAT.AFTER THE JULY 3 RUNOFF ELECTIONS, THE TWO COMPARE 
NOTES ON RUSSIA, DIRECTLY OR THROUGH AMBASSADOR CHEEK. 
HE FURTHER ADDED THE U.S. WAS WILLING TO SHARE ITS VIEWS 
WITH THE GOA WITH SOME SORT OF NOTE FOR PRESIDENT 
MENEM'S REVIEW AFTER THE JULY 3 ELECTIONS. 
7. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1027 EDT.
