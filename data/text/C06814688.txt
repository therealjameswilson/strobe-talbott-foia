1.-4eT-SUMMARY: IN AN APRIL 28 MEETING WITH UNHCR OGATA, 
DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT BRIEFED ON HIS RECENT 
CONSULTATIONS IN BERLIN AND MOSCOW, REPORTING THAT HE IS 
CAUTIOUSLY OPTIMISTIC THAT THE KEY ACTORS, INCLUDING THE 
RUSSIANS AND THE UNSYG, ARE DOING EVERYTHING THEY CAN TO 
CREATE THE CONDITIONS FOR THE BOMBING CAMPAIGN TO STOP. HE 
EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT THE FRAGILE POLITICAL SITUATION IN 
MACEDONIA, NOTING THAT PUBLIC CRITICISM OF THE GOVERNMENT 
MIGHT BACKFIRE. OGATA SAID CAMP CONSTRUCTION IN BOTH 
ALBANIA AND MACEDONIA MUST ACCELLERATE URGENTLY. SHE 
COMPLAINED THAT THE EUROPEAN STATES STILL DO NOT SEE THE 
URGENCY OF THE HUMANITARIAN EVACUATION PROGRAM, AND ARE 
TREATING THE PROGRAM MORE LIKE RESETTLEMENT. END SUMMARY 
2.   ON APRIL 28, DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT MET WITH 
UNHCR SADAKO OGATA AT UNHCR HEADQUARTERS IN GENEVA. MRS. 
OGATA WAS ACCOMPANIED BY SPECIAL ENVOY DENNIS MCNAMARA AND 
SPECIAL ASSISTANT FILIPPO GRANDI. TALBOTT WAS ACCOMPANIED 
BY AMBASSADOR MOOSE, EUR DIRECTOR JAMES SWIGERT, EXECUTIVE 
ASSISTANT PHIL GOLDBERG AND MISSION NOTETAKER. 
3. (C)   TALBOTT BRIEFED MRS. OGATA ON HIS RECENT MEETINGS IN 
BERLIN AND IN MOSCOW, NOTING THAT ALTHOUGH THE DIPLOMATIC 
PICTURE REMAINS UNCLEAR, AT LEAST THE KEY ACTORS ARE NOW 
WORKING TOGETHER. HE DESCRIBED THE EXTRAORDINARY 
CONCENTRATION OF DIPLOMATIC PRESSURE ON THE FRY, A 
SIGNIFICANT PART OF WHICH IS FROM THE RUSSIANS. TALBOTT 
SAID THAT HE IS CONVINCED THE RUSSIANS WANT TO FIND AN 
ACCEPTIBLE POLITICAL SOLUTION AND THAT THEY NOW UNDERSTAND 
NATO'S BOTTOM LINES. CHERNOMYRDIN AND OTHERS ARE LOOKING 
FOR WAYS TO RECONCILE OUR POSITIONS, BUT THEIR ABILITY TO 
DELIVER MILOSEVIC REMAINS UNCERTAIN. 
4.-4G-)--RESPONDING TO A QUERY FROM OGATA, TALBOTT OUTLINED 
U.S. THINKING ON THE KIND OF INTERNATIONAL SECURITY FORCE 
WHICH WOULD BE REQUIRED IN KOSOVO, STRESSING THAT NATO MUST 
HAVE A CORE ROLE, BUT THAT OTHERS, LIKE THE UN/OSCE/RUSSIANS 
SHOULD PARTICIPATE. OGATA AGREED ON THE IMPORTANCE OF A UN 
ROLE AND ON RUSSIAN PARTICIPATION. 
5. (C)  OGATA EXPRESSED HER GROWING CONCERN OVER THE 
DETERIORATING SITUATION IN MACEDONIA AND SAID SHE HAD 
WRITTEN TO GLIGOROV URGIi\TG HIM TO KEEP THE BORDER OPEN AND

INSISTENCE ON USING LOCAL CONTRACTORS AT THE SITE HAD SLOWED 
CONSTRUCTION JUST AS A NEW WAVE OF REFUGEES CROSSED THE

SITUATION, AND ARE TREATING THE PROGRAM MORE LIKE 
TRADITIONAL RESETTLEMENT (INSISTING ON STRINGENT SELECTION

PRESSING THE EUROPEANS, BUT THAT PROGRESS IS MUCH TOO SLOW.
