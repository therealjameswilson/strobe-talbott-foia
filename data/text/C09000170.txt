Here are some reports and impressions on developments 
while you were away, a few of which you may want to 
discuss when we see each other. 
-- Russian Politics: Chernomyrdin is clearly 
ascendant, and he's looking for some sort of "third 
way" between pure reform of the kind that Gaidar and 
Fyodorov represent and the command system. At the same 
time, he is also trying to keep some of the 
personalities and policies of the pure reformers in his 
cabinet. As of yesterday, there were still 
negotiations going on that might result in Fyodorov 
remaining in the cabinet. 
There are at least two reasons for Chernomyrdin's 
growing strength. One is that his instincts, 
background, and contacts all make him better able than—
Yeltsin to deal with the nationalist-commtinist bloc. 
The other is the continuing uncertainty over Yeltsin's 
physical staying power. We've had a report that 
Yeltsin is back in the hospital. 1 The obvious question is whether the 
chernomyrdinization of the style and substance of the 
Russian leadership is disastrous, requiring an overhaul

/ -- Georgia On Our Minds: There's another side to 
the whole problem of Russian policy in the near abroad. 
Russia has real security concerns on its border, - 
especially along the soft underbelly. One way or 
another, Russia is going to carry out peacekeeping 
operations there. It's a question of whether they do 
so unilaterally, in a fashion that is perceived as 
proof of neo-imperialism, or whether they do so with an 
international blessing.of some kind, following 
international rules, and drawing from the international 
community for some of the peacekeepers. 
We keep telling them that they must come to the UN 
and the CSCE and multilateralize their peacekeeping-
activities. Now, in the UN on the subject of Georgia, 
they're saying, "Okay; here we are -- bless us; give us 
blue helmets (or at least berets), manpower from 
neutral states, and funding." 
To which our current reply is, Gulp. In short, 
they're calling our bluff. We have too problems, one 
political and one financial. We don't want to be in 
the position of providing international, or US, cover 
for Russian imperialism (the Rspublicans would_have a
