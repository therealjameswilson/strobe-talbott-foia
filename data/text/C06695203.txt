2. SUMMARY: IN A SEPTEMBER 13 MEETING WITH INCOMING OAS 
SECRETARY GENERAL CESAR GAVIRIA, DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT LAID OUT THE LATEST STATE OF PLAY ON HAITI, 
SUMMARIZING THAT WE ARE "AT THE MOMENT OF TRUTH". 
GAVIRIA STATED THAT THE BASIC FEAR FOR SOUTH AMERICAN AND 
CENTRAL AMERICAN NATIONS IS WHAT WILL HAPPEN IN THE YEAR 
AFTER ANY INTERVENTION -- WHAT W/LL ARISTIDE ÐO. 
RESPONDING TO A REQUEST FROM MR. TALBOTT, GAVIRIA AGREED 
TO SEEK OUT ARISTIDE TO SPEAK TO HIM DIRECTLY. TALBOTT 
ASKED GAVIRIA SEVERAL TIMES TO OPEN ANY CHANNELS HE MIGHT 
HAVE TO THE HAITIAN DICTATORS TO IMPART THE MESSAGE THAT 
THE THREE DICTATORS FACED THE CHOICE OF LEAVING 
UNCOMFORTABLY OR COMFORTABLY. END SUMMARY. 
3. IN A MEETING WITH INCOMING OAS SECRETARY GENERAL 
CESAR GAVIRIA, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT SET OUT THE 
LATEST CONCERNS ON HAITI, CALLING THIS THE MOMENT OF 
TRUTH." HE STATED THE US OBJECTIVE WAS RESTORATION OF 
DEMOCRACYY PEACEFUL MEANS, BUT THAT UNSCR 940, ' 
SANCTIONED BY LARGE PARTS OF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY 
AUTHORIZED THE USE OF ALL NECESSARY.MEANS TO REMOVE THE 
DICTATOR. HE FURTHER INFORMED GAVIRIA THAT PRESIDENT 
CLINTON WOULD DELIVER A SPEECH BY WEEK'S END ON THE 
SUBJECT OF DEMOCRACY AND HAITI THAT WE HOPED WOULD ACHIEVE 
A GREAT DEAL OF RESONANCE THROUGHOUT THE HEMISPHERE. 
4. GAVIRIA RESPONDED THAT HE THOUGHT RESTORING ARISTIDE 
WAS THE EASY PART, BUT THAT DEMOCRACY BUILDING WOULD BE 
VERY DIFFICULT, ESPECIALLY GIVEN THE POOR STATE OF BASIC 
INSTITUTIONS IN HAITI. IN ADDITION, IT WOULD TAKE MUCH 
TIME. HE COMPARED HAITI TO HIS NATIVE COLOMBIA, WHICH 
DESPITE ITS FAIRLY STRONG INSTITUTIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE, 
HE PREDICTED WOULD TAKE 25 YEARS TO MOVE BEYOND ITS 
CULTURE OF VIOLENCE. 
5. GAVIRIA WAS NOT SATISFIED WITH THE ROLE THE OAS HAS 
PLAYED IN THE HAITI ISSUE, WHICH HE DESCRIBED AS "SIMPLY 
PLACING IT IN THE LAP OF THE UN SECURITY COUNCIL." 
LOOKING AHEAD, GAVIRIA THOUGHT THE OAS COULD BE IN A 
POSITION TO ASSIST IN RECONCILIATION AND DEMOCRACY 
BUILDING, "AFTER THE FIRST PHASE" IN HAITI. HE SUGGESTED 
THE WORK MIGHT BE COMPARABLE TO THE CIAV WORK IN 
NICARAGUA, OR THE OAS'S SUCCESSFUL WORK IN SURINAME. 
ABOVE ALL, SAID GAVIRIA, THE OAS WOULD NEED TO DEPLOY 
GOOD PEOPLE. 
6. GAVIRIA OUTLINED THAT THE BASIC FEAR FOR SOUTH 
AMERICAN AND CENTRAL AMERICAN NATIONS IS WHAT WILL HAPPEN 
IN THE TWO OR THREE YEARS AFTER "THE INVASION" (HE
