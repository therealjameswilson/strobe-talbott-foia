May 5, 1993 
Dear Andrzej, 
Thanks for your kind and informative note. The notion of 
you wearing five hats fills me with admiration. The notion of 
you having only five people to help you with all those hats 
fills me with sympathy. 
As for the future of the radios, there is•no question that 
RFE and RL will be in some sense, consolidated into a single 
overall radio. However, I think that still leaves some 
flexibility for preserving the best that the radios have to 
offer. I will certainly try to use what influence I have 
.toward that end. 
You will not be surprised to know that I really do not 
think there is much merit in the criticism that the U.S. is 
going over board on Yeltsin. Keeping reform alive in Russia is 
critical to keeping it alive elsewhere in the former Soviet 
Union and, perhaps to a somewhat lessor extent, in Eastern, 
Europe as well. We in the G-7 are doing everything we can to 
assure that the $28 billion package of reform support announced 
in Tokyo will be usefully spent.
When I next get through Brussels and we have a chance to 
talk, I will look forward to talking to you on these points and 
to being persuasive as possible. 
A11 the best to Nika. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Adviser to The Sedretary 
on The New Independent States 
The Honorable 
Andrzej Krzeczunowicz,

1040 Brussels.
