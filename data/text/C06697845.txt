1. CLASSIFIED BY THE AMB. JAMES COLLINS. REASONS FOR 
CLASSIFICATION: 1.5. (B) . DECLASSIFY ON APRIL 30, 2001. 
2. -*et-SUMMARY: DURING MEETINGS APRIL 29 WITH DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT AND OTHER DEPARTMENT OFFICIALS, GEORGIAN 
FOREIGN MINISTER MENAGHARISHVILI REPEATEDLY STRESSED THE 
IMPORTANCE OF U.S. SUPPORT AS GEORGIA RESPONDS TO PRESSURE 
FROM MOSCOW ON SECURITY AND INTEGRATION, SEARCHES FOR A 
SOLUTION TO THE ABKHAZ DISPUTE, REACHES OUT TO ITS 
CAUCASUS NEIGHBORS, AND MOVES FORWARD WITH POLITICAL AND 
ECONOMIC REFORMS. HE DETAILED PROBLEMS THE GEORGIANS ARE 
FACING IN EACH AREA. EXPRESSING DEEP DISTRUST OF MOSCOW'S 
MOTIVES AND INTENTIONS, HE EMPHASIZED THE SITUATION IS NOT 
LIKELY TO IMPROVE, REGARDLESS OF THE OUTCOME OF RUSSIAN 
ELECTIONS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND OTHER DEPARTMENT 
OFFICIALS UNDERSCORED THE U.S. COMMITMENT TO CONTINUE 
SUPPORTING GEORGIAN EFFORTS. END SUMMARY 
3. (U) DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH GEORGIAN FOREIGN 
MINISTER IRAKLI MENAGHARISHVILI ON APRIL 29, 1996 AT THE 
DEPARTMENT. 
GEORGIAN - RUSSIAN RELATIONS AND CIS INTEGRATION 
4. -K4-MENAGHARISHVILI BEGAN THE,MEETING BY EXPRESSING 
APPRECIATION FOR THE SUPPORT THE U.S. HAS GIVEN TO GEORGIA 
AND STRESSING THAT THE UNCERTAINTIES FACED BY THE NIS AT 
PRESENT UNDERSCORE THE IMPORTANCE OF A CONTINUING U.S. 
ROLE, PARTICULARLY RELATING TO REGIONAL SECURITY. HE 
CITED THE EXAMPLE OF THE RECENT MEETING OF THE THREE 
'CAUCASUS HEADS OF STATE IN BRUSSELS, SPONSORED BY THE EU, 
AS AN EXAMPLE OF THE KIND OF SUPPORT THAT IS IMPORTANT IN 
STRENGTHENING THE IDENTITY AND SOVEREIGNTY OF THE NIS. 
5. -(-E-1. STRESSING THAT GEORGIA SEEKS A BALANCED, PEACEFUL 
RELATIONSHIP WITH RUSSIA, MENAGHARISHVILI ECHOED A COMMON 
SHEVARDNADZE THEME IN NOTING THAT, REGARDLESS OF THE 
OUTCOME OF RUSSIAN PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS, PRESSURE WITHIN 
RUSSIA FOR A STRONGER UNION WITH THE NIS WILL CONTINUE. 
HE CITED PLANS FOR NATO EXPANSION AS ONE OF THE FACTORS 
CONTRIBUTING TO THIS PRESSURE AND POINTED OUT THAT YELTSIN 
AND GRACHEV HAVE PUSHED GEORGIA TO SHARE ITS CFE QUOTA, TO 
AGREE TO MILITARY BASES IN POTI. AND TO SIGN ON TO A JOINT 
MILITARY COMMISSION. MENAGHARISHVILI ARGUED THAT THE

ABKHAZIA 
7. 461- THE FOREIGN MINISTER STRESSED THAT WHILE TBILISI 
IS WILLING TO WORK WITH THE RUSSIANS AND ABKHAZ'
REPRESENTATIVES TO FIND A SOLUTION TO THE SEPARATIST 
DISPUTE, NEITHER MOSCOW NOR SUKHUMI SEEM WILLING TO MAKE A 
COMMITMENT TO CONSTRUCTIVE NEGOTIATIONS AT THIS POINT. AS 
A RESULT, PROGRESS ON A SETTLEMENT IS AT A DEADEND. 
DISMISSING SYG SPECIAL ENVOY BRUNNER AS INEFFECTIVE, 
MENAGHARISHVILI SAID THAT HE WOULD BE ASKING THE UN 
LEADERSHIP IN NEW YORK TO BECOME MORE ACTIVE IN PRESSING 
FOR A SOLUTION, PERHAPS BEGINNING WITH AN INTERNATIONAL 
CONFERENCE ON THE ISSUE. HE REPORTED HE WAS NOT 
OPTIMISTIC ABOUT THE UN'S RESPONSE, HOWEVER, AND APPEALED 
TO THE U.S. TO BECOME DIRECTLY INVOLVED IN THE SEARCH FOR 
A SOLUTION. HE ARGUED THAT THE ABKHAZ DISPUTE MERITS THE 
ATTENTION WE ARE NOW GIVING NAGORNO-KARABAKH. HE ALSO 
CALLED ON THE FRIENDS OF GEORGIA (FOG) TO BECOME MORE 
INVOLVED, PARTICULARLY AFTER THE RUSSIAN ELECTIONS. 
8. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REASSURED MENAGHARISHVILI 
THAT WE ARE COMMITTED TO A COMPREHENSIVE SETTLEMENT 
CONSISTENT WITH GEORGIAN TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY, AS 
EVIDENCED BY OUR ACTIVE INVOLVEMENT IN THE UN AND FOG 
EFFORTS. HE INVITED THE FOREIGN MINISTER TO LET US KNOW 
IF THERE ARE SPECIFIC THINGS GEORGIA BELIEVES WE CAN DO 
BEYOND THESE EFFORTS, BUT STRESSED THAT WE BELIEVE WE CAN 
BEST HELP BY SUPPORTING THE WORK OF THE UN AND THE FOG. 
9. 4Qi-AMBASSADOR COLLINS ADDED THAT WASHINGTON SUPPORTS

TO OPEN UP AND ENERGIZE DIALOGUE ON ABKHAZIA. HE REPORTED 
ON THE TRIP OF THE EMBASSY TEAM TO SUKUMI OVER THE 
-WEEKEND, SAYING THAT IT APPEARED ARDZINBA HAS BECOME AWARE 
OF THE NEED TO TALK WITH RATHER THAN ONLY TO OTHER 
PARTIES. THE ABKHAZ LEADER HAD TOLD THE TEAM THAT, IN . 
CONTRAST TO SIX MONTHS AGO, HE BELIEVES THE UN AND THE FOG

REGIONAL COOPERATION 
10. -tet-MENAGHARISHVILI NOTED GEORGIA'S EFFORTS TO REACH 
OUT TO IT'S NEIGHBORS. HE RECALLED ALIYEV'S EARLY MARCH 
VISIT TO TBILISI AND SAID SHEVARDNADZE IS ALSO TALKING TO 
ARMENIA, TURKEY AND OTHER NEIGHBORS. A REGION-WIDE 
DIALOGUE LEADING TO COOPERATION ON A WIDE RANGE OF ISSUES 
--, HE MENTIONED TRANSPORT, COMMUNICATION, AND TRADE -- IS 
THE GOAL. THE FOREIGN MINISTER SAID HE WOULD BE MEETING 
THE AZERI AND ARMENIAN AMBASSADORS SEPARATELY DURING HIS 
VISIT TO WASHINGTON, AND HE ENVISIONED A MEETING OF 
REPRESENTATIVES OF THE THREE PARLIAMENTS SOMETIME DURING 
THE SUMMER. THESE ACTIVITIES MAY LEAD TO A TRILATERAL 
SUMMIT. 
11. —,K4 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED U.S. SUPPORT FOR 
INITIATIVES TO ENCOURAGE REGIONAL COOPERATION AND ASKED 
MENAGHARISHVILI TO USE WHATEVER LEVERAGE GEORGIA ENJOYS 
WITH ITS NEIGHBORS TO PUSH FOR RESOLUTION OF 
NAGORNO-KARABAKH. A SOLUTION TO THIS PROBLEM IS DOABLE; 
THE OBSTACLE IS A LACK OF POLITICAL WILL, DUE AT LEAST IN 
PART TO INCONSISTENT SIGNALS FROM VARIOUS PARTIES. RUSSIA 
APPEARS SO FOCUSED ON PRESERVING ITS PRESTIGE AS THE MAJOR 
FORCE IN THE REGION THAT IT FAILS TO RECOGNIZE THE DANGERS 
IN ALLOWING N-K AND OTHER REGIONAL CONFLICTS TO-PERSIST. 
IF ALL THOSE WITH LEVERAGE WERE WORKING TOGETHER, THE 
DISPUTES COULD BE RESOLVED. 
SHEVARDNADZE'S POSSIBLE VISIT TO WASHINGTON 
12. -tet IN RESPONSE TO MENAGHARISHVILI'S QUERY REGARDING 
A POSSIBLE DATE FOR PRESIDENT SHEVARDNADZE'S VISIT TO 
WASHINGTON; WHICH WILL BE SCHEDULED AROUND AN APPOINTMENT 
WITH PRESIDENT CLINTON, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THE 
DIFFICULTIES FACED IN GETTING A PRESIDENTIAL APPOINTMENT 
IN THE MONTHS LEADING UP TO THE ELECTION. HE NEVERTHELESS 
AŠSURED THE FOREIGN MINISTER THAT HE STRONGLY SUPPORTS THE 
REQUEST, WHICH IS NOW BEING WORKED AT THE WHITE HOUSE. 
13. (U) GEORGIAN AMBASSADOR TEDO JAPARIDZE AND DAVID 
SOUMBADZE, POLITICAL COUNSELOR, ALSO ATTENDED THE

WERE AMBASSADOR JAMES COLLINS, EUR/CASA DIRECTOR S. YOUNG; 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT V. NULAND; AND 
GEORGIA DESK OFFICER, S. ROSENBERRY.

15. 4C.) HERBST PROVIDED A BRIEFING ON THE STATUS OF CFE 
NEGOTIATIONS, NOTING THAT AMBASSADOR COURTNEY WOULD BE

MENAGHARISHVILI THE U.S. REMAINS SENSITIVE TO GEORGIAN 
CONCERNS. 
16. 444 BUILDING ON .HIS EARLIER CONVERSATION WITH TO THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY, MENAGHARISHVILI ACKNOWLEDGED THAT 
ARMENIA NEEDS TO BE WORKED INTO EFFORTS TO EXPAND 
COOPERATION WITHIN THE CAUCASUS. HE NOTED THAT 
TER-PETROSSIAN IS SCHEDULED TO VISIT GEORGIA IN EARLY JUNE 
AND THAT REGIONAL COOPERATION ISSUES WILL BE HIGH ON THE 
AGENDA. GIVEN REGIONAL CONFLICTS, SUCH BILATERALS BETWEEN 
THE CAUCASUS STATES ARE A USEFUL ADJUNCT TO THREE-WAY 
MEETINGS. HE ALSO NOTED THAT THE THREE CAUCASUS 
AMBASSADORS IN WASHINGTON HAVE MET SEVERAL TIMES TO 
DISCUSS REGIONAL ISSUES. HE WONDERED IF THE STATE 
DEPARTMENT MIGHT JOIN ONE OF THESE SESSIONS.
