SUBJECT: FM PAPANDREOU PROVIDES FURTHER INFORMATION ON AMERICAN

CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR R. NICHOLAS BURNS. REASONS 1.5 (B,D). 
RELEASE IN FULL 
1. 44- FOREIGN MINISTER GEORGE PAPANDREOU GAVE VISITING DEPUTY [DECLASSIFIED
SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT FURTHER INFORMATION ON THE THREE 
AMERICAN CAPTIVES IN YUGOSLAVIA. 
2. RH PAPANDREOU SAID THAT THE GREEK DOCTOR WITH THE 
ORGANIZATION MEDECINS SANS FRONTIERES HAD BEEN IN TOUCH AGAIN 
WITH THE GREEK GOVERNMENT OVER THE WEEKEND. THE DOCTOR REPORTED 
THAT THE TQICAN SERVICEMEN TAKEN CAPTIVE LAST WEEK WERE 
BEING HELD IN A HOSPITAL IN PRISTINA. HE TOLD THE GREEK 
GOVERNMENT THAT THE SOLDIERS WERE NOT SAYING MUCH TO THEIR 
CAPTORS. 
3. 4€4 MOST IMPORTANTLY, THE DOCTOR ADVISED THE GREEK GOVERNMENT 
THAT THERE WAS A CHANCE THE SERBS WOULD PERMIT ONE OF THE 
SERVICEMEN TO BE FREED AND TO TRAVEL TO GREECE WITH A DOCTOR FOR 
ORTHODOX EASTER--THIS COMING WEEKEND. 
4. 44 COMMENT: WE OBVIOUSLY ARE NOT IN A POSITION TO JUDGE THE 
CREDIBILITY OF THE GREEK DOCTOR IN QUESTION. PAPANDREOU CAN 
CONFIRM THAT THE DOCTOR IS IN SERBIA AND SEEMS TO BE WELL 
INTENTIONED. PAPANDREOU PROMISED TO PROVIDE US WITH ALL FURTHER 
INFORMATION FROM THE DOCTOR AS IT COMES IN.
