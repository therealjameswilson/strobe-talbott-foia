SUBJECT: SENIOR INDIAN OFFICIALS BRIEF THE PRESS ON THE 
TALBOTT/JASWANT SINGH TALKS 
REF: NEW DELHI 9619 
CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR RICHARD F. CELESTE, REASONS 
1.5(B) AND (D). 
1. -te4- SUMMARY: THE PRINCIPAL SECRETARY TO THE PRIME 
MINISTER AND THE INDIAN AMBASSADOR TO THE U.S. SEPARATELY 
BRIEFED INDIAN JOURNALISTS ON THE NOVEMBER 19-20 ROUND OF 
THEcTALBOTT/JASWANT SINGH TALKS. THE PRINCIPAL SECRETARY 
REPORTED THAT WHILE THE U.S. HAD ACCEPTED INDIA'S NEED 
FOR A MINIMUM DETERRENT, INDIA HELD FIRM AGAINST U.S. 
DEMANDS FOR RESTRAINTS ON ITS NUCLEAR PROGRAM AND A HALT 
IN FISSILE MATERIAL PRODUCTION. THE INDIAN AMBASSADOR 
INITIALLY FOCUSED ON THE "AREAS OF-COMMON AGREEMENT," 
THEN LAMBASTED THE U.S. ON THE ENTITIES LIST AND THE 
"DISCRIMINATORY" PARTIAL WAIVER OF SANCTIONS. THE GOI IS 
TRYING TO SUSTAIN DOMESTIC SUPPORT FOR THE INDO-U.S. 
DIALOGUE BY HIGHLIGHTING AREAS OF PROGRESS AND REASSURING 
CRITICS THAT INDIA WILL NOT CAVE TO,AMERICAN PRESSURE. 
END SUMMARY. 
MISHRA SAYS U.S. ACCEPTS INDIA'S MINIMUM DETERRENT 
2. (U) ON NOVEMBER 25, PRINCIPAL SECRETARY BRAJESH 
MISHRA BRIEFED A GROUP OF JOURNALISTS WHILE RETURNING 
FROM HYDERABAD ON THE PRIME MINISTER'S PLANE. MISHRA 
(QUOTED AS AN UNNAMED SOURCE IN THE ARTICLES) TOLD THE 
JOURNALISTS THAT THE U.S. HAD ACCEPTED, IN PRINCIPLE, 
THAT INDIA WILL KEEP A MINIMUM NUCLEAR DETERRENT. HE 
CONTINUED, "WE ARE FOR A MINIMUM DETERRENT. OUR NUCLEAR 
WEAPONS AND MISSILES CAPABILITY AND DEPLOYMENT WILL 
FOLLOW THE LOGIC FROM THIS DOCTRINE. BASICALLY NUCLEAR 
RESTRAINT IS THE STICKY ISSUE IN OUR BILATERAL TALKS WITH 
THE U.S. AND WE CAN NEVER GIVE UP OUR POSITION ON THE 
SUBJECT."' MOREOVER, HE INDICATED THAT INDIA HAD RULED 
OUT A MORATORIUM ON FISSILE MATERIAL PRODUCTION AHEAD OF 
AN AGREEMENT ON THE FISSILE MATERIAL CUT-OFF TREATY 
(FMCT).

3. (U) MISHRA -- THE NEWLY APPOINTED NATIONAL SECURITY 
ADVISOR -- ADDED THAT THE CREATION OF THE NATIONAL 
SECURITY COUNCIL (NSC) WILL MAKE THE NEXT ROUND OF TALKS 
"VERY INTERESTING." HE SAID THAT THE NSC WILL TAKE UP 
THE ISSUE OF WHAT CONSTITUTES A MINIMUM NUCLEAR DETERRENT 
BEFORE THE NEXT ROUND. THE NSC WILL ALSO PREPARE THE 
STRATEGIC DEFENSE REVIEW AND ADDRESS THE ISSUE OF 
DEPLOYMENT OF NUCLEAR WEAPONS.' ON EXPORT CONTROLS, 
MISHRA SAID THAT INDIA HAD OFFERED TO CONSIDER U.S. 
PROPOSALS TO STRENGTHEN ITS EXPORT REGIME, WITH THE 
EXCEPTION OF ADOPTING NUCLEAR SUPPLIERS GROUP (NSG)

4. (U)'BRIEFING INDIAN JOURNALITS IN WASHINGTON ON 
NOVEMBER 26, INDIAN AMBASSADR NARESH CHANDRA PAINTED A 
MORE POSITIVE PICTURE OF THE INDO-U.S. DIALOGUE. 
"CONTRARY TO THE GENERAL IMPRESSION, THE TALKS HAVE BEEN 
QUITE GOOD. COMMON AREAS OF AGREEMENT ON SUBJECTS LIKE 
EXPORT CONTROL, REDUCTION OF NUCLEAR WEAPONS AND CHECKS 
ON THE PRODUCTION OF FISSILE MATERIAL HAVE BEEN 
EXPANDED," HE REMARKED. 
5. (U) CHANDRA DID MAKE SURE, HOWEVER, TO EMPHASIZE THAT 
THE GOI PRESSED ITS CONCERNS ABOUT THE ENTITIES LIST AND 
REF: NEW DELHI 9619 
"DISCRIMINATORY" INTERNATIONAL FINANCIAL INSTITUTION 
(IFI) LENDING.. "OUR INITIAL SCRUTINY OF THE (ENTITIES) 
LIST SHOWS THAT IT IS UNDULY LARGE AND CERTAIN 
INSTITUTIONS HAVE BEEN INCLUDED WHICH HAVE NO BUSINESS TO 
BE THERE . . . THE ACTION GOES AGAINST THE PRINCIPLES 
THAT ALL COUNTRIES, INCLUDING THE U.S., HAVE ACCEPTED AT 
THE GATT OR WTO REGIME." HE SAID THAT THE GOI WAS 
CONSIDERING WHETHER TO ENTER NEGOTIATIONS WITH THE U.S. 
OVER THE LIST OR TO TAKE UP THE MATTER AT THE WTO. 
6. (U) FINALLY, CHANDRA DECRIED CONTINUED U.S. 
OPPOSITION TO WORLD BANK LOANS TO INDIA AS GOING "AGAINST 
THE WORLD BANK CHARTER THAT THEIR OPERATIONS HAVE TO BE 
IMMUNE FROM POLITICAL CONSIDERATIONS." "THE LOGICAL 
INCONSISTENCY IS VERY GLARING AND THIS . . . GETS EVEN 
MORE ACCENTUATED WHEN YOU FIND THAT IT IS FOR BASIC HUMAN 
NEEDS RANGE OF PROJECTS (SIC) THAT THE WORLD BANK LENDS 
IN THE FIRST PLACE," HE ADDED. 
COMMENT 
7. -(-e)--THE GOI IS ACUTELY AWARE OF THE GROWING 
IMPRESSION IN INDIA THAT THE TALBOTT/JASWANT SINGH 
DIALOGUE IS STALLED. BY GIVING ITS OWN VERSION TO THE 
PRESS, THE GOI SOUGHT TO PROJECT INDIAN SUCCESSES IN THE 
TALKS (U.S. ACCEPTANCE OF MINIMUM DETERRENCE, PROGRESS ON 
EXPORT CONTROLS) AND TO COUNTER CRITICS WHO CONTEND THAT 
THE GOVERNMENT IS BOWING TO AMERICAN PRESSURE. WHILE THE 
PICTURE PAINTED MAY NOT BE ENTIRELY ACCURATE, IT IS 
SIGNIFICANT THAT THE GOVERNMENT ACTIVELY CONTINUES TO 
SOLICIT PUBLIC SUPPORT FOR THE DIALOGUE. END COMMENT.
