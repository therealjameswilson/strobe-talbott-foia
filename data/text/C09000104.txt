Dear Strobe: 
As a member of the Program Committee of Rotary, I am pleased to extend an invitation to you to speak 
with our Rotary Club of Cleveland at a mutually agreeable date later this year, or early in 1995. We 
meet every Thursday at 12:00 o'clock noon on the Mezzanine Floor of the Statler Officer Building, and 
we can assure you that there would be great interest in having you share some of your thoughts and 
experiences with us. 
The men and women making up the Rotary Club of Cleveland represent a broad cross-section of business 
and professional people - committed to the positive growth and community interests of our city. As a 
former resident, and being in such a significant position in representing our Country, your comments and 
observations would be of great interest to our Rotary Group. 
As a suggestion, the Thursday noon dates of December 22nd, January 12th and 19th are currently 
available in our program schedule, and we'd be very pleased to have you select a date that would be 
convenient for you, or an alternative Thursday date. Our luncheon meetings start at 12:00 o'clock and 
end promptly at 1:30 p.m., with speaker remarks scheduled for approximately 12:50 to 1:30, including a 
few minutes for questions from the audience. 
As a part of Rotary International, our Rotary Club of Cleveland is deeply committed to keeping our 
members well informed, and we would welcome the chance to welcome your return to the Cleveland 
Community, and can assure you an interested audience.
