Dear Bill: 
RELEASE IN PART 
B6 
Belated thanks for your letter about Henry Bienen's 
invitation -- and apologies for the delay. What with the 
Gore trip to the FSU and preparations for the Clinton-
Yeltsin summit, I've been especially irresponsible about my 
correspondence of late. I don't think I've actually heard 
from Bienen. But at this point, I've got to hold off 
making any speaking commitments in the Near Year. There 
are several uncertainties (none of them too ominous) hover-' 
ing over me at the moment; maybe we can talk about them 
over the phone during the holidays. When I hear from 
Biehen -- or when I unearth an invitation that may be 
modering in my in-basket! -- I'll reply more artfully to 
him. 
Thanks for the report on George's address at the In-
stitute. What with the rather unsettling results of the 
Russian election (I can imagine how they would resonate 
with George's jaundiced view of democracy), I'-m thinking 
about inviting him back down to inject another shot of wis-
dom into our system soon after the summit. 
Delighted to hear about your connection with Shawn 
Byrnes. He's doing very good work for us in Kiev at a cru-
cial and difficult time.
