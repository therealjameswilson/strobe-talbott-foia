l. (U) FORMER PRIME MINISTER GUJRAL MET MARCH 7 WITH
ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT AT THE REQUEST OF THE INDIAN
EMBASSY. GUJRAL WAS ACCOMPANIED BY INDIAN DCM SREENIVASAN
WHILE TALBOTT WAS JOINED BY A/S INDERFURTH, SA/INS
DIRECTOR USREY, D STAFF CYNKIN AND DESKOFF AS NOTETAKER.
2. M REFERRING TO THE POSSIBILITY OF A POTUS STOP IN
PAKISTAN DURING HIS IMPENDING SOUTH ASIA TRIP, TALBOTT
POINTED OUT THAT THE PRESIDENT HAD TO BALANCE THE DESIRE
TO TURN TO A NEW PAGE, IF NOT A NEW CHAPTER, IN THE U.S.—
INDIA RELATIONSHIP, WHILE AT THE SAME TIME REMAINING
ENGAGED ON PRESSING ISSUES WITH PAKISTANI INCLUDING
DEMOCRACY AND NONPROLIFERATION. GUJRAL OBSERVED THAT
PAKISTAN WAS GIVING TWO CONTRADICTORY MESSAGES AT THE SAME
TIME. PAKISTAN WOULD HAVE TO MAKE A CHOICE; IT COULD GO
ON FIGHTING OR IT COULD OPEN TALKS WITH INDIA. HE SAID NO
INDIAN PM COULD OPEN A DIALOGUE WITH PAKISTAN WHILE
PAKISTAN WAS SUPPORTING TERRORISM. THERE WAS ALWAYS THE
POSSIBILITY OF USEFUL INDO-PAKISTAN DIALOGUE. HOWEVER,
THE DYNAMIC OF MILITARY RULE HAD BROUGHT TO PAKISTAN THE
LOSS OF HALF ITS TERRITORY AND THE DESTRUCTION OF ITS
ECONOMY. HE SUGGESTED THE ARMY AND THE ISLAMICISTS HAD
FEARED BEING SHUT OUT BX THE LAHORE PROCESS AND HAD
SABOTAGED IT THROUGH KARGIL.
3. ~ TALBOTT SAID PRESIDENT CLINTON WAS ACUTELY AWARE OF
THE CONSTRAINTS AND SENSITIVITIES THAT INDIA MUST CONSIDER
WITH RESPECT TO INTERNATIONAL INVOLVEMENT IN THE INDO-
PAKISTAN RELATIONSHIP. AT THE SAME TIME, THE PRESIDENT
HAD BEEN ABLE TO USE HIS PERSONAL RELATIONSHIP WITH PM
SHARIF TO DEFUSE A VERY DANGEROUS SITUATION AT KARGIL.
ONE STRONG ARGUMENT AGAINST CUTTING OFF THE U.S.
,RELATIONSHIP WITH PAKISTAN WAS THAT IT WAS POSSIBLE TO
IMAGINE FUTURE SCENARIOS IN WHICH SUCH A SPECIAL
RELATIONSHIP WITH PAKISTAN WOULD BE CRUCIAL. TALBOTT
NOTED THAT A POTUS STOP IN PAKISTAN WOULD ALSO AFFORD THE
OPPOR'UNITY TO DELIVER A VERY STRONG MESSAGE TO PAKISTAN
ABOUT DEMOCRACY, TERRORISM AND NONPROLIFERATION.
4. ~ INDERFURTH ASKED IF GUJRAL HAD ANY SUGGESTIONS FOR
PUTTING LAHORE BACK ON TRACK. THE USG HAD TAKEN THE VIEW
UP TO THIS POINT THAT IT WAS PAKISTAN'S RESPONSIBILITY TO
RESTART THE DIALOGUE. GUJRAL REPLIED THAT THE "UPSURGE"
IN TERRORISM MUST CEASE, AND MUSHARAFF MUST STOP THE
INFILTRATION OF MUJAHIDIN. IF THAT HAPPENED, ANYTHING WAS
POSSIBLE. UNTIL THAT HAPPENED, NO INDIAN PRIME MINISTER
WOULD HAVE THE OPTION OF OPENING TALKS BECAUSE PUBLIC
OPINION HAD BEEN HARDENED BY THE TV COVERAGE OF THE DEAD
SOLDIERS FROM KARGIL.
5. ~ TALBOTT CONCLUDED BY ASSURING GUJRAL THAT THE
"HYPHENATION" OF INDIA AND PAKISTAN WOULD STOP; THE
PRESIDENT'S VISIT WOULD UNDERSCORE THE U.S. COMMITMENT TO
DEVELOPING RELATIONS WITH INDIA IN ITS OWN RIGHT AND NOT
MERELY IN THE CONTEXT OF ITS RELATION TO PAKISTAN. ONE
