DECLASSIFIED 
1. SUMMARY. AT A JULY 29 MEETING, ACTING SECRETARY 
TALBOTT AND FRENCH DEFENSE MINISTER FRANCOIS LEOTARD— 
AGREED THAT THE CONTACT GROUP MUST SHOW THE SERBS' THAT 
THE GROUP HAS THE POLITICAL WILL TO CARRY THROUGH WITH OUR 
THREATS. LEOTARD SA/D THAT SANCT/ONS MUST BE TIGHTENED 
AND CURRENT UNSC RESOLUTIONS ENFORCED. LIFTING THE ARMS 
EMBARGO, HOWEVER, WOULD FORCE THE_EUROPEANS TO LEAVE 
UNPROFOR AND LEAD TO A REACWAR. THE ACTING SECRETARY WAS 
CAUTIOUSLY OPTIMISTIC THAT RUSSIA WOULD STAY ON BOARD. 
CONCERNING HAITI, LEOTARD AFFIRMED FRANCE'S SUFPORT FOR 
TWO-STEP APPROACH IN THE PROPOSED UNSC RESOLUTION. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY AND LEOTARD ALSO DISCUSSED RWANDA, NOTING 
THE EXTENSIVE COOPERATION BETWEEN OUR ARMED FORCES. ON 
JULY 31, LEOTARD WILL MEET WITH DEFENSE SECRETARY PERRY IN 
GOMA. END SUMMARY. 
BOSNIA 
2. LEOTARD AND THE ACTING SECRETARY AGREED THAT THE SERBS 
DO NOT THINK WE HAVE THE POLITICAL WILL TO CARRY THROUGH 
WITH OUR THREATS. LEOTARD CHARACTERIZED THE SITUATION AS 
"OUT OF CONTROL" AND THE LIKENED THE SITUATION IN SARAJEVO 
TO A "BAD MOVIE" IN THAT IT WAS GOING BACKWARDS. LEOTARD 
SAID THAT THE CONTACT GROUF COUNTRIES MUST REASSERT 
CONTROL BY TAKING MEANINGFUL ACTION TO TIGHTEN SANCTIONS 
AND ENFORCE THE CURRENT UNSC RESOLUTIONS. LIFTING THE 
ARMS EMBARGO SHOULD BE A LAST RESORT, BECAUSE IT WOULD 
FORCE THE EUROPEANS TO WITHDRAW T4EIR TROOPS FROM UNPROFOR 
AND WOULD LEAD TO A "REAL WAR." LEOTARD REPORTED THAT HE 
HAD MET WITH SEVERAL CONGRESSIONAL LEADERS (E.G., SENATORS 
NUNN, LEVIN, COHEN AND EXON) AND TOLD THEM THAT, IN HIS 
VIEW, LIFTING THE ARMS EMBARGO WOULD INVOLVE THE U.S. EVEN 
MORE DEEPLY, BECAUSE WE WOULD BE SEEN AS RESPONSIBLE FOR 
ARMING THE MUSLIMS: 
3. LEOTARD ALSO CRITICIZED UNPROFOR'S TEPID RESPONSES TO 
REPEATED SERBIAN PROVOCATIONS AND SAID THAT UNPROFOR 
FORCES SHOULD RESPOND WREN ATTACKED.

OF THE RUSSIAN STANCE. THE ACTING SECRETARY RESPONSDED

PEACEKEEPING 
9. THE ACTING SECRETARY NOTED THAT WE SHOULD CONSULT MORE 
CLOSELY ON HOW TO APPROACH THE PEACEKEEPING PROBLEM 
GENERALLY. THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY DOES NOT HAVE THE 
POLICIES OR INSTITUTIONS IN PLACE TO DEAL WITH "FAILED OR 
IMPLODING STATES." AT THIS POINT, LEOTARD BROUGHT UP AN 
IDEA THAT HE HAD DISCUSSED WITH AFRICAN CHIEFS OF STATE, 
THAT IS, AN UN/OAU STANDING PEACEKEEPING ARMY IN AFRICA 
THAT COULD DEAL WITH PROBLEMS SUCH AS RWANDA. LEOTARD 
REPORTED THAT PRELIMINARY REACTIONS FROM AFRICAN LEADERS 
WERE POSITIVE. THE ACTING SECRETARY NOTED THAT THIS WOULD 
BE A WORTHWHILE TOPIC FOR OUR FUTURE DISCUSSIONS ON 
PEACEKEEPING.
