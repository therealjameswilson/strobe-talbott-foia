Dear Yossi, 
Our meeting on Tuesday provided a useful opportunity to 
resume the dialogue we began in June and I look forward to 
similar occasions on which we can exchange views. Here are 
some additional thoughts on three of the subjects we 
discussed. 
I want to assure you that we will make every effort at 
the upcoming IAEA General Conference in Vienna to work with 
Israel for the restoration of IAEA technical assistance to 
Israel. I agree with you that, in light of the remarkable 
progress toward a comprehensive peace in the Middle East, 
now is the time to eliminate this residual, unhelpful issue 
from the. IAEA, and put it finally to rest. As you are 
aware, we have been urging IAEA member countries -- through 
our representatives in the IAEA in Vienna and our embassies 
in capitals -- to support an action restoring technical 
assistance to Israel. Our delegation will be working 
closely with yours during the conference as we coordinate 
our efforts on this issue. 
As I mentioned during our meeting, I have a strong 
interest in CSCE and other potential regional 
security/political mechanisms. This will be an important 
theme in an upcoming speech I will give at Oxford. 
Accordingly, I look forward to receiving your paper on 
CSCME. An evolving multilateral process covering security, 
economic and humanitarian areas can play a key role as part 
of the changing landscape brought about by a comprehensive 
peace in the Middle East. 
His Excellency 
Yossi Beilin, 
Deputy Foreign Minister of Israel.

Finally, I have checked with Ambassador Simons on our 
approach to cooperation with Israel and Turkey on 
assistance programs in Central Asia. Let me assure you 
that we remain highly committed to the process, both 
because of our close relationship with your government and 
the government of Turkey, and the potential benefits we see 
arising from our mutual cooperation. It is important to 
remember, however, that new resources are not likely to be 
forthcoming. Our immediate operational objectives include 
signing the draft HOU negotiated in Ankara and fielding a 
joint mission to discuss our plans with the Uzbekistan and 
Turkmenistan governments. We look forward to intensified 
cooperation over the coming months. 
Sincerely,
