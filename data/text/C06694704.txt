SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S MEETING WITH FCO 
MINISTER OF STATE ANTONY BALDRY 
1.   ENTIRE TEXT. 
2. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET OCTOBER 20 WITH FCO 
MINISTER OF STATE ANTONY BALDRY. BALDRY, WHOSE BRIEF ' 
INCLUDES THE AMERICAS, NOTED THAT HMG HAS BEEN PLEASED 
TO SEE THE PROGRESS BEING MADE IN HAITI. DEPSEC THANKED 
BALDRY FOR HMG'S SUPPORT AND ASSURED HIM THE USG WAS 
DOING EVERYTHING POSSIBLE TO ACHIEVE POSITIVE RESULTS. 
ON UNMIH, BALDRY EMPHASIZED HMG'S STRETCHED RESOURCES 
BUT SAID THE BRITISH WOULD LIKE TO HELP AND ARE STILL 
CONSIDERING POLICE TRAINING. 
3. BALDRY RAISED THE SIDE AGREEMENTS OF THE MOU ON SAFE 
HAVENS IN THE TURKS AND CAICOS, SAYING THAT THE 
GOVERNMENT OF TCI WAS MOST INTERESTED IN NEGOTIATING ON 
THE AIRPORT RUNWAY. DEPSEC ASSURED BALDRY THAT THE USG 
WOULD MOVE SPEEDILY AND REASONABLY ON CLOSURE OF THE 
MOU'S. 
4. ON CUBAN REFUGEES, BALDRY SAID HMG HOPED THE U.S. 
WOULD BE ABLE TO TAKE 1,000 REFUGEES FROM THE CAYMAN 
ISLANDS EITHER TO GUANTANAMO OR PANAMA. DEPSEC SAID HE 
UNDERSTOOD THIS ISSUE WAS UNDER DISCUSSION AND THAT THE 
U.S. WOULD BE AS HELPFUL AS POSSIBLE. 
5. BALDRY SAID HE PLANNED TO VISIT THE DOMINICAN 
REPUBLIC IN DECEMBER. DEPSEC ENCOURAGED HIM TO ALSO 
VISIT PORT-AU-PRINCE. 
6. THIS CABLE HAS BEEN CLEARED BY DEPSEC TALBOTT. CROWE
