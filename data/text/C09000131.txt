February 14, 1994 
Dear Frank: 
Thanks for your letter and sorry not to have gotten back 
to you more quickly. I have been extremely busy with my own 
confirmation process and with several fairly demanding 
developments on the foreign policy front. It is clear from 
your letter that you are doing important work in several 
areas. I wish I could be more directly helpful to you, but 
the responsibilities awaiting me in my new job will make it 
difficult for us to get together right away. I will, 
however, pass along your letter to my friend and colleague 
Tom Simons, the Coordinator of our Assistance Programs to 
the Former Soviet Union, with a note asking him to see if 
there is anything his shop could do to help. 
From my new post I will be paying quite a bit of 
attention to the Asia Pacific area. I intend to spend some 
time with Winston Lord and get caught up on his thinking on 
several of the subjects you mention in your letter. 
Very best wishes to you. 
Mr. Frank B. Gibney 
Encyclopedia Britannica, Inc., 
Britannica Centre, 
310 South Michigan Avenue, 
Chicago, Illinois.
