SUBJECT: THE ACTING SECRETARY'S NON-PROLIFERATION 
DISCUSSIONS WITH CHINESE VFM YANG 
CLASSIFIED BY THE ACTING SECRETARY PER 1.6X6. 
SUMMARY 
1. (3ECRET/NODIS/SILK) THE ACTING SECRETARY HELD A 40 
MINUTE MEETING (SEPTEL) WITH VISITING CHINESE VFM YANG 
JIECHI OCTOBER 23 TO DISCUSS OUTSTANDING BILATERAL ISSUES 
AND PREPARE FOR THE PRESIDENT'S MEETING WITH JIANG ZEMIN IN 
BRUNEI. THIS CABLE COVERS ONLY THOSE ELEMENTS DEALING WITH 
NON-PROLIFERATION. THE ACTING SECRETARY PRESSED FOR 
PROGRESS ON REACHING AN AGREEMENT ON NON-PROLIFERATION, 
REJECTING YANG'S STATEMENT THAT CHINA HAD AGREED TO FURTHER 
EXPERTS' CONSULTATIONS AS A FAVOR TO THE U.S. BUT THAT 
CHINA WOULD MAKE NO FURTHER CONCESSIONS. END SUMMARY. 
NON-PROLIFERATION 
2. iCECRET/NODIS/SILK). RAISING NON-PROLIFERATION AS A 
SHARED CONCERN, THE ACTING SECRETARY URGED THAT EXPERTS 
MEET IN EARLY NOVEMBER TO MAKE SUFFICIENT PROGRESS SO THAT 
THE ISSUE COULD BE BROUGHT TO CLOSURE IN BRUNEI. 
3. (jECREIVNODIS/SILK) YANG RESPONDED BY SAYING THAT THERE 
HAD BEEN A LONG PROCESS OF CONSULTATIONS AT THE EXPERT 
LEVEL AND THAT THE PRC, FOR ITS PART, BELIEVED THAT IT HAD 
ADDRESSED U.S. CONCERNS AND THAT "NON-PROLIFERATION PER. SE 
WAS NOT AN ISSUE." HE SAID THAT THIS QUESTION "SHOULD HAVE 
BEEN RESOLVED IN THE MIDDLE OF THE YEAR," AND THAT THE TWO 
SIDES SHOULDN'T STILL BE SPENDING TIME ON THE ISSUE. HE 
CLAIMED THAT, "AS BEFITS AN ANCIENT CIVILIZATION," CHINA 
HAD DEMONSTRATED PATIENCE AND AGREED TO HOLD ANOTHER ROUND 
OF CONSULTATIONS, BUT WARNED THAT THE U.S. SHOULD HAVE NO 
ILLUSIONS THAT THIS WOULD LEAD TO A CHANGE IN CHINA'S 
POSITION FROg THE LAST ROUND. 
4. (CCCRET/NODIS/SILK) THE ACTING SECRETARY RESPONDED BY 
STRESSING THAT THE PRESIDENT HAD WORKED HARD ON PNTR 
BECAUSE IT WAS IN THE U.S. INTEREST. NEITHER SIDE WAS IN 
THE HABIT OF GIVING GIFTS TO THE OTHER, HE STATED, BUT DID 
THINGS FOR REASONS OF NATIONAL INTEREST. THIS INCLUDED 
CHINA'S DECISION TO RESUME A NON-PROLIFERATION DIALOGUE 
WITH THE U.S., WHICH THE ACTING SECRETARY DID NOT CONSIDER 
TO BE A FAVOR. THE ACTING SECRETARY HOPED THAT A RENEWED 
HIGH-LEVEL IMPULSE TO THE DIALOGUE WOULD BE ONE RESULT OF 
THE VFM'S VISIT TO WASHINGTON. REITERATING HIS BELIEF IN 
THE IMPORTANCE OF THE NON-PROLIFERATION ISSUE BOTH IN AND 
OF ITSELF AS WELL AS TO AVOID ESCALATION IN SOUTH ASIA, THE 
ACTING SECRETARY SAID THAT PROGRESS WOULD ACCENTUATE 
POSITIVE POSSIBILITIES IN U.S.-CHINA RELATIONS.
