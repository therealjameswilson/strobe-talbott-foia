Dear Strobe: 
RELEASE IN FULL 
I really appreciated our talk this morning and confirm that, 
upon reflection and after some careful fact gathering including a 
most helpful call with Jeff Garten, I have concluded that it 
would be a mistake to continue our discussions further. 
As requested, I enclose the draft letter which I never sent 
to you. It,seems to me that, despite your willingness to 
consider variations on the theme, in fact too much has happened 
in the first eighteen months of your administration and that it 
would be a mistake to try and intrude on existing arrangements 
and assignments. It was for this reason that I suggested a six 
months review which I now realize is not practical. 
As The New York Times editorial page says this morning, the 
Clinton program seems to be on the right track with regard to 
Russia. You already have overall leadership and, on the 
international side, Larry Summers and Treasury are absolutely 
pivotal. Also, the role of the Vice President seems to be 
growing in importance with everything seemingly coming together 
effectively at the level of the NSC. 
I believe I should wait around for the unlikely possibility 
that there might be a clear mandate to do a senior level job. 
Should that opportunity occur in the months ahead, I would be 
happy to considdr it. In the interim, I am available to you 
personally at any time that I can be of help on special projects 
or just to kick around ideas. Now, having spent some time with 
you, I know that it would be a delight to work with you or just 
to get to know you better. 
Would you please thank Secretary Christopher for his 
interest, a sentiment which I extend also to you. 
With warmest wishes, 
Sincerely, 
:1)JAM 
James D. Wolfensohn
