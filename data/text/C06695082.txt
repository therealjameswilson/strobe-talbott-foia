2. SUMMARY. VENEZUELAN AMBASSADOR PEDRO LUIS ECHEVERRIA 
PRESENTED COPIES OF HIS CREDENTIALS TO DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT ON JULY 15. THE DEFUTY SECRETARY STRESSED THE 
VALUE OF OUR FRIENDSHIP, COOPERATION AND CLOSE RELATIONS 
WITH VENEZUELA. ECHEVERRIA PROVIDED A REVIEW OF THE 
ECONOMIC SITUATION IN VENEZUELA AND EXPLAINED THE GOV'S 
RECENT ACTIONS. THE'DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED CONCERN 
OVER THE CURRENT ECONOMIC SITUATION, HOPED THAT FOREIGN 
EXCHANGE AND PRICE CONTROLS WOULD BE LIFTED QUICKLY, 
URGED THAT A COMPREHENSIVE ECONOMIC PLAN BE IMPLEMENTED 
IN COORDINATION WITH THE APPROPRIATE INTERNATIONAL 
FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS AND OFFERED U.S. TECHNICAL 
ASSISTANCE TO HELP STRENGTHEN THE FINANCIAL SECTOR. HE 
ALSO EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT THE SUSPENSION OF 
CONSTITUTIONAL GUARANTEES. END SUMMARY. 
PRESENTATION OF CREDENTIALS 
3. VENEZUELAN AMBASSADOR PEDRO LUIS ECHEVERRIA ARRIVED 
IN WASHINGTON ON JULY 14 AND PRESENTED COPIES OF HIS 
CREDENTIALS TO DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT ON JULY 15. 
ECHEVERRIA THANKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY FOR ALLOWING HIM 
TO PRESENT HIS CREDENTIALS SO SOON AFTER HIS ARRIVAL SO 
HE CAN BEGIN HIS OFFICIAL DUTIES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
RESPONDED THAT WE ARE AWARE OF THE DIFFICULTIES VENEZUELA 
IS EXPERIENCING AND THAT OUR FRIENDSHIP WITH VENEZUELA 
LEADS US TO BE BOTH PROMPT AND CANDID. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY ALSO STRESSED THE VALUE OF OUR FRIENDSHIP 
COOPERATION AND CLOSE RELATIONS WITH VENEZUELA. HE NOTED 
THAT VENEZUELA IS AN IMPORTANT ALLY IN THE SUPPORT OF 
DEMOCRACY IN THE HEMISPHERE, A MAJOR TRADING PARTNER AND 
RELIABLE SUPPLIER OF OIL. 
ECONOMIC SITUATION IN VENEZUELA 
4. ECHEVERRIA PROVIDED A REVIEW OF THE ECONOMIC 
SITUATION IN VENEZUELA AND EXPLAINED THE GOV'S RECENT 
ACTIONS. VENEZUELA IS GOING THROUGH A DIFFICULT ECONOMIC
