Dear Mr. Ambassador: 
RELEASE IN FULL 
It is my pleasure to warmly congratulate you on the occasion of your nomination 
as Deputy Secretary of State. I sincerely enjoyed having substantial discussions 
with you on issues of mutual interest. Your professional approach and dedication 
to exploring peiceful resolutions to various regional problems have been 
specifically appreciated by my government. 
I am confident that the commitment of both the Armenian and the U.S. 
govemment to the values of democracy and human rights will greatly contribute to 
further strengthening the relations of good understanding and friendship, which 
happily exist between our two countries. 
I take this opportunity to extend to you, Mr. Ambassador, the renewed assurances 
of my highest consideration. 
Sincerely yours; 
Rouben Shugarian 
Ambassador

Deputy Secretary Nominee
