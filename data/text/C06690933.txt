RELEASE IN FULL 
January 7, 1994 
Who would have thought it—from the corridors of Hotchkiss to the 
seventh floor of State! Congratulations on your new appointment and 
congratulations to Secretary Christopher, who recognized the best talent 
available to him. 
Just to let you know, I will be moving back to Washington in February 
to become the new Director of the Institute for the Study of Diplomacy at 
Georgetown. I will hope to entice you there for a visit at some convenient 
time. I hope, as well, to pursue you for a Bilderberg meeting. The next one 
will be in Helsinki June 2-5 and your presence there would make an important 
statement of interest to our European colleagues. 
With best wishes. 
Sincerely, 
Casimir Yost
