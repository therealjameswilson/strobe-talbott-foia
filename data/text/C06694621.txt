1. SUMMARY. DEPUTY STROBE TALBOTT TOOK THE OCCASION OF 
RELEASE IN FULL 
DECLASSIFIED 
HIS MARCH 7-8 VISIT TO HAITI AS LEADER OF THE 
PRESIDENTIAL BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT MISSION (SEPTEL) .TO 
MEET WITH THE PROVISIONAL ELECTORAL COUNCIL (CEP) AND 
WITH UN SPECIAL REPRESENTATIVE TO HAITI LAKHDAR BRAHIMI. 
CEP PRESIDENT REMY HIGHLIGHTED HIS OPTIMISM THAT THE 
ELECTION IS ON SCHEDULE, BUT UNDERLINED THREE POTENTIAL 
PROBLEMS: LACK OF OFFICE SPACE, SECURITY, AND A BUDGET

THE ACTIVITY OF THE TRADE DELEGATION, AID PLANS-FOR-HAITI 
IN THE NEAR FUTURE, bRAHIMI'S POOR OPINION OF THE CEP, 
AND THE HOLDING OF THE OAS GENERAL ASSEMBLY IN HAITI IN 
JUNE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY JOINED COMMERCE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY DAVID BARRAM, GENERAL FISHER, REPRESENTATIVES 
MEEKS AND MCKINNEY AND AID'S MARK SCHNEIDER IN A TRIP TO

MEETING WITH PROVISIONAL ELECTORAL COUNCIL 
2. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, JOINED BY AMBASSADOR DOBBINS, 
NSC REP RAND BEERS, AID'S MARK SCHNEIDER, AMBASSADOR 
SWING AND USAID DIRECTOR LARRY CRANDALL, MET MARCH 7 WITH 
MEMBERS OF THE CEP. CEP PRESIDENT REMY GAVE THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY A SUCCINCT BRIEFING ON THE COUNCIL'S RECENT 
PROGRESS, NOTING THAT THE DEPARTMENTAL ELECTORAL OFFICES 
HAD BEEN ESTABLISHED AND THAT THE COMMUNAL AND LOCAL 
OFFICES (BECS AND BIVS) WOULD BE IN PLACE IN TIME TO 
START VOTER REGISTRATION MARCH 26. STRESSING HIS 
OPTIMISM THAT THE PROCESS WOULD END WITH AN ELECTION JUNE 
4, AS SCHEDULED, REMY NONETHELESS NOTED THREE CONCERNS: 
DIFFICULTY IN FINDING OFFICE SPACE, SECURITY AND A BUDGET 
DEFICIT. HE SAID HE HAD NO MAJOR PROBLEMS WITH AID OR 
THE UN'S ASSISTANCE ACTIVITIES, AND THAT IN ANY EVENT HE 
WAS IN CONSTANT CONTACT WITH THOSE ORGANIZATIONS TO 
DISCUSS ANY ISSUES. HE EXPLAINED THAT WITH REGARD TO THE 
EXECUTIVE'S ACTION IN DECREEING AN ELECTORAL LAW THAT 
AMENDED WHAT PARLIAMENT HAD PASSED, THE EXECUTIVE DID 
WHAT WAS NECESSARY TO BRING THE LAW INTO CONFORMITY WITH 
THE CONSTITUTION. HAD PARLIAMENT NOT WAITED UNTIL THE 
LAST DAY OF ITS MANDATE TO FORWARD THE LAW TO THE 
EXECUTIVE, HE IMPLIED, SUCH UNILATERAL ACTION MIGHT NOT 
HAVE BEEN NECESSARY.

FAIR ELECTIONS ARE THE MOST IMORTANT MANIFESTATION OF 
THAT PROCESS. HE MENTIONED THE EMBASSY WAS READY TO
