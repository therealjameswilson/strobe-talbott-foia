RELEASE IN FULL 
Dear Ms. Grubbs: 
Thank you for your letter notifYing me that Elena Bonner 
will receive an Honorary Doctor of Laws Degree from the 
University. Having written a letter on her behalf, I am 
obviously very gratified at the news. As a one-time member of 
the Corporation and the Honorary Degrees Committee, I derive 
particular pleasure from being able to participate in even this 
modest way all these years later. 
I will be in New Haven for my 25th reunion later that week, 
but unfortunately will not be able to make the commencement 
exercises on the 24th. My greetings to you and your colleagues. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Adviser to The Secretary 
on The New Independent States 
Ms. Diana T. Grubbs, 
Assistant Secretary, 
Yale University, 
P.O. Box 1303A Yale Station, 
New Haven, CT.
