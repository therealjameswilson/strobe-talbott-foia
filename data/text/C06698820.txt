-- YOU HAVE REGULARLY ASSURED US OF YOUR SUPPORT FOR THE 
PROPOSITION THAT IRAQ MUST FULFILL ITS OBLIGATIONS ON WMD 
UNDER UN RESOLUTIONS AND COOPERATE WITH UNSCOM 
UNCONDITIONALLY. 
-- WE WERE THEREFORE CONCERNED BY THE POSITION TAKEN BY 
RUSSIA AT THE SECURITY COUNCIL REGARDING THE COUNCIL'S 
CURRENT EFFORTS TO SUPPORT UNSCOM. 
-- AS YOU KNOW, IRAQ HAS REFUSED TO ALLOW UNSCOM TO REMOVE 
APPROXIMATELY 130 SCUD ENGINES AND OTHER MISSILE PARTS FROM 
IRAQ. 
-, THIS IS A CLEAR VIOLATION OF IRAQ'S LEGAL OBLIGATIONS. 
-- IRAQ'S BEHAVIOR HAS CONVINCED CHAIRMAN EKEUS THAT IRAQ 
MAY BE HIDING AN OPERATIONAL MISSILE FORCE CAPABLE OF 
DELIVERING CHEMICAL OR BIOLOGICAL WARHEADS. THIS IS A REAL 
AND IMMEDIATE THREAT TO INTERNATIONAL PEACE AND SECURITY. 
- UNSCOM CHAIRMAN ROLF EKEUS HAS EXPLAINED CLEARLY WHY IT 
IS NECESSARY FOR THESE PROSCRIBED MISSILE PARTS TO BE 
REMOVED FROM IRAQ FOR TESTING. 
-- IT IS UNSCOM'S RIGHT TO DETERMINE WHERE AND HOW TESTING 
SHOULD BE PERFORMED ON THESE PROSCRIBED MISSILE PARTS. 
IRAQ DOES NOT HAVE THE RIGHT TO DEMAND TO PARTICIPATE IN 
SUCH TESTING, NOR TO PLACE RESTRICTIONS ON UNSCOM'S ABILITY 
TO CARRY OUT SUCH TESTING IN A MANNER AND PLACE OF UNSCOM'S 
CHOOSING. 
-- WHILE WE MAY EACH PROVIDE TECHNICAL ADVICE, ONCE UNSCOM 
DECIDES WHAT IS REQUIRED, WE SHOULD NOT SECOND GUESS 
UNSCOM. OTHERWISE, WE WILL DRASTICALLY UNDERMINE ITS 
ABILITY TO FULFILL ITS MANDATE. 
- UNSCOM CHAIRMAN EKEUS HAS ACCURATELY DESCRIBED THE 
CURRENT STANDOFF AS A "WATERSHED EVENT," IN TERMS NOT ONLY 
OF COMING TO CLOSURE ON THE MISSILE ISSUE, BUT MORE 
IMPORTANTLY ON THE EFFECT ON UNSCOM'S AUTHORITY. 
-- THE COUNCIL HAS NEVER BEFORE REFUSED EKEUS' REQUESTS FOR 
ACTION, EVEN WHEN THEY WERE MUCH LESS SPECIFIC THAN THIS 
ONE. 
-- THIS URGENT MATTER MUST BE RESOLVED BEFORE THE END OF 
THE YEAR. FURTHER DELAYS IN RESOLVING THE STANDOFF WILL 
ONLY EMBOLDEN THE IRAQI REGIME AND WEAKEN UNSCOM'S 
AUTHORITY. 
-- THE SITTING SECURITY COUNCIL, WHOSE MEMBERS ARE ALL 
FAMILIAR WITH THE ISSUES AT HAND, SHOULD TAKE ACTION BEFORE 
THE END OF THE YEAR. PUTTING OFF A DECISION UNTIL THE NEXT 
SECURITY COUNCIL -- MANY OF WHOSE MEMBERS WILL BE NEW TO 
THIS SUBJECT -- WILL COMPLICATE THE ISSUE UNNECESSARILY. 
-- WE WANT TO ACHIEVE P-5 UNITY ON THE MOST IMPORTANT ISSUE 
UNSCOM HAS FACED THIS YEAR, BECAUSE IT WOULD DEMONSTRATE 
OUR CONTINUING COOPERATION ON THE KEY ISSUES OF PEACE AT 
THE UN. 
-- IF THE P-5 CANNOT QUICKLY AGREE ON A STATEMENT 
STRENGTHENING UNSCOM, WE WILL HAVE NO CHOICE BUT TO PROPOSE 
A RESOLUTION TO THE SECURITY COUNCIL -- WHILE THIS WOULD 
NOT BE OUR PREFERRED OUTCOME, WE ARE WILLING TO PROCEED ON 
THIS PATH IF NECESSARY. 
-- WE HAVE ALWAYS PREFERRED A PRESIDENTIAL STATEMENT TO A 
RESOLUTION. WE UNDERSTAND THAT ON DECEMBER 24, THE P-5 
CAME CLOSE TO AGREEING (AD REFERENDUM) TO A TEXT FOR A 
PRESIDENTIAL STATEMENT SUPPORTING UNSCOM. WE URGE YOU TO 
HAVE YOUR MISSION TO THE UN APPROVE THE AGREED TEXT 
QUICKLY.
