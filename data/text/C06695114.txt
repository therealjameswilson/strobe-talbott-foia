2. DURING A JUNE 29 MEETING WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
GERMAN NATIONA SECURITY ADVISOR JOACHIM BITTERLICH 
REVIEWED CHANCELLERY IMPRESSIONS OF LEADING RUSSIAN
POLITICAL FIGURES. OTHER TOPICS OF THE MEETING REPORTED 
SEPTEL. 
CHERNOMYRDIN 
RELEASE IN FULL 
DECLASSIFIED 
3. BITTERLICH VOLUNTEERED HIS SURPRISE AT THE WAY 
PRESIDENT YELTSIN HAD SPOKEN POSITIVELY (FOR 20 MINUTES) 
ABOUT PRIME MINISTER VIKTOR CHERNOMYRDIN DURING THE 
YELTSIN-KOHL MEETING AT THE EU CORFU SUMMIT. BITTERLICH 
SAID THIS WAS THE FIRST TIME THE CHANCELLOR HAD HEARD 
YELTSIN SPEAK THIS. WAY AND CONTRADICTED RUMORS THAT 
YELTSIN SAW CHERNOMYRDIN AS A POTENTIAL RIVAL. IN FACT, 
BITTERLICH SAID, CHERNOMYRDIN HAS BECOME A STALWART IN THE 
PRESIDENT'S INNER CIRCLE AND MUST NOW BE SERIOUSLY 
CONSIDERED AS A POSSIBLE SUCCESSOR TO YELTSIN. 
SHOKHIN 
4. BITTERLICH ALSO SAW EVIDENCE AT CORFU THAT DEPUTY 
PRIME MINISTER ALEKSANDR SHOKHIN WAS("STRONG WITH 
YELTSIN." SHOKHIN ATTENDED ALL THE KEY MEETINGS AND FELT 
FREE TO INTERJECT HIS OPINIONS AT ANY TIME. YELTSIN HAD 
TURNED TO.HIM FOR A PRESENTATION ABOUT THE ECONOMIC 
SITUATION IN RUSSIA. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT THE 
U.S. HAS SEEN SHOKHIN AS A REPRESENTATIVE OF "ECONOMIC 
NATIONALISM," AND YELTSIN'S MAN RATHER.THAN CHERNOMYRDIN'S. 
GRACHEV 
5. BITTERLICH SAID THAT HE WAS HAVING DIFFICULTY GETTING 
A FIX ON DEFENSE MINISTER PAVEL GRACHEV. GRACHEV (AND 
FOREIGN MINISTER KOZYREV) WERE "SABOTEURS" DURING THE 
PROCESS OF NEGOTIATING RUSSIA'S RELATIONSHIP WITH NATO. 
EVEN DURING THE RUSSIAN-GERMAN SUMMIT IN MAY GRACHEV HAD 
DEFENDED POSITIONS INCOMPATIBLE WITH THOSE OF YELTSIN. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RESPONDED THAT IN SUCH SITUATIONS,
