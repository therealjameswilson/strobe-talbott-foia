Dear Dr. Baranetsky: 
RELEASE IN FULL 
Thank you very much for your letter to me dated April 23. 
I regret that the press of business in Washington will make it 
difficult for me to accept your gracious speaking invitation 
for some time. 
The U.S. Government under. President Clinton is-committed to 
a wide-ranging program of humanitarian assistance and technical 
assistance to Ukraine and to the other new states. As you note 
in your letter, many of your members have cooperated with or 
are knowledgeable of some facets of the U.S. humanitarian 
assistance programs such as those involving Project Hope and 
the American International Health Alliance. 
You may be interested to know that a State Department 
officer who is closely involved in the NIS assistance effort, 
Ms. Christina Rufenacht, will be speaking to the Ukrainian 
National Women's League of America (UNWLA) at its convention 
May 28 in Newark, New Jersey. I would encourage you to 
consider attending that event for information on our 
partnership in assisting Ukraine. 
I am heartened by your group's dedication in aiding Ukraine 
in all aspects of medical needs. I wish you every great 
success. 
With warm regards.
