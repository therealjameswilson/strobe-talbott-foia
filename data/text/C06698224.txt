MEMCON: DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MEETING WITH UKRAINIAN 
NATIONAL SECURITY COUNCIL SECRETARY HORBULYN, JULY 17, 
1996, KIEV, UKRAINE 
1. (U) FOR D STAFF-VICTORIA NULAND ONLY. CLASSIFIED 
BY WILLIAM GREEN MILLER, AMBASSADOR, EMBASSY KIEV. 
REASONS: 1.5 (B) AND (D). 
2. -1434- AT A JULY 17 MEETING WITH UKRAINIAN NATIONAL 
SECURITY COUNCIL SECRETARY HORBULYN, THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY EMPHASIZED THE IMPORTANCE TO THE U.S. OF 
ENSURING THAT NATO EXPANSION ENHANCES UKRAINE'S 
SECURITY. HE EXPLAINED TO HORBULYN THAT AS EARLY AS 
THE SPRING OF 1997, DISCUSSIONS WITH POSSIBLE FUTURE 
MEMBERS OF THE ALLIANCE MAY BEGIN ON THE SUBJECT OF 
EXPANSION. IT IS CRITICAL THAT THIS PROCESS NOT ONLY 
NOT HARM UKRAINE'S SECURITY, BUT HELP IT. 
3. if:4s HORBULYN REPLIED THAT NATO IS ONE OF THE MAIN 
FOREIGN POLICY ISSUES ON UKRAINE'S AGENDA. UKRAINE HAS 
NO OBJECTIONS IN PRINCIPLE TO THE CONCEPT OF EXPANSION, 
BUT HOPES THAT THE PROCESS WILL BE EVOLUTIONARY. 
UKRAINE UNDERSTANDS THE FINANCIAL, MILITARY AND 
TECHNICAL.ISSUES THAT ARE, INVOLVED AND WILL SUPPORT 
EFFORTS TO INTEGRATE WITH WESTERN SECURITY STRUCTURES 
BY ALL OF ITS NEIGHBORS. THIS IS NOT ONLY IN UKRAINE'S 
INTEREST, BUT ALSO IN RUSSIA'S. EVEN RUSSIAN FOREIGN 
MINISTER PRIMAKOV RECENTLY STATED THAT HE SEES NATO AS 
AN INDISPENSABLE ELEMENT OF EUROPEAN SECURITY. 
UKRAINE'S ONLY CONCERN IS THAT THERE NOT BE TACTICAL 
NUCLEAR WEAPONS STATIONED ON ITS BORDERS. UKRAINE HAS 
A MORAL RIGHT TO INSIST ON THIS. UKRAINE GAVE UP ITS 
NUCLEAR ARSENAL AS IT PROMISED, AND COULD NOT NOW 
EXPLAIN TO THE UKRAINIAN PEOPLE THAT HAVING DONE SO, 
THERE WILL NOW BE NEW NUCLEAR WEAPONS ALONG ITS 
BORDERS. THIS IS AN ISSUE THAT UKRAINE NEEDS TO BE 
SURE IS ADDRESSED IN THE CONTEXT OF THE EXPANSION 
PROCESS. 
4. -(ey- TALBOTT REPLIED THAT HE IS CONFIDENT THAT THIS 
ISSUE CAN BE RESOLVED TO THE SATISFACTION OF ALL 
INVOLVED. UKRAINE HAS BEEN AN ACTIVE VOICE ON THIS 
SUBJECT, AND THE U.S. CERTAINLY HAS NOT FORGOTTEN AND 
WILL NOT FORGET THE COURAGEOUS AND CORRECT DECISIONS OF 
THE KRAVCHUK AND KUCHMA ADMINISTRATIONS ON NUCLEAR 
DISARMAMENT. IT IS ESSENTIAL THAT UKRAINIANS NEVER 
REGRET THOSE DECISIONS. FURTHERMORE, IT IS A GUIDING 
RELEASE IN FULL

PRINCIPLE OF U.S. RELATIONS WITH UKRAINE THAT THERE BE 
'FULL TRANSPARENCY IN REGARD TO THESE DECISIONS: 
UKRAINE MUST KNOW WHAT IS BEING DISCUSSED NOT ONLY WITH 
POTENTIAL NEW MEMBERS BUT ALSO WITH RUSSIA. WE WILL 
NOT DISCUSS WITH THE RUSSIANS BEHIND UKRAINE'S BACK THE 
QUESTION UKRAINE'S RELATIONS WITH NATO. IN THAT 
REGARD, WE ALSO BELIEVE THAT EXPANSION SHOULD NOT HAVE 
ANY NEGATIVE EFFECT ON RUSSIA'S SECURITY, BUT RATHER 
MUST ENHANCE THE SECURITY OF ALL COUNTRIES IN EUROPE, 
INCLUDING RUSSIA. IT ALSO MUST NOT HARM UKRAINIAN-
RUSSIAN RELATIONS. FINALLY, WE DO NOT AND WILL NOT 
EXCLUDE UKRAINE'S RIGHT TO APPLY FOR MEMBERSHIP IN NATO 
IF IT SHOULD CHOOSE TO DO SO. NOR WILL WE EXCLUDE 
UKRAINE'S RIGHT TO DECLINE TO APPLY FOR MEMBERSHIP. 
5. -K4- HORBULYN REPLIED THAT HE IS INTERESTED IN 
DISCUSSING THESE QUESTIONS IN GREATER DETAIL WITH 
NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR LAKE LATER THIS FALL IN 
WASHINGTON. THE U.S. SHOULD UNDERSTAND THAT PRESIDENT 
KUCHMA'S VIEWS ON EUROPEAN SECURITY ARE EVOLVING. NON-
BLOC STATUS, WHICH WAS INCLUDED IN UKRAINE'S 
DECLARATION OF STATE SOVEREIGNTY, IS NOT INCLUDED IN 
THE NEW CONSTITUTION, WHICH SUPERSEDES THE DECLARATION. 
IT IS NO LONGER DOGMA IN UKRAINIAN FOREIGN POLICY. 
KUCHMA HAS STATED PUBLICLY THAT EUROPE IS THE PRIMARY 
ORIENTATION OF UKRAINIAN FOREIGN POLICY, AND RELATIONS 
WITH NATO ARE A CRUCIAL ELEMENT OF THIS. TALBOTT 
REPLIED THAT WE NEED TO ENSURE THAT DIALOGUE ON THESE 
ISSUES IS FREQUENT, DETAILED, MULTI-DIRECTIONAL AND 
TRANSPARENT, AND SKETCHED THE'RANGE OF POTENTIAL 
CONSULTATIONS THAT THE U. 
S. BELIEVES SHOULD BE 
ENCOURAGED. HE CONCLUDED BY ASSURING HORBULYN THAT THE 
U.S. WILL REMEMBER THE CONFIDENCE THAT UKRAINE HAS 
PLACED IN IT, THROUGH THE TRILATERAL PROCESS AND ON 
OTHER ISSUES, AND ASSURED HORBULYN THAT UKRAINE WILL 
NOT REGRET HAVING DECIDED TO ENTRUST THE U.S. WITH THAT 
CONFIDENCE.
