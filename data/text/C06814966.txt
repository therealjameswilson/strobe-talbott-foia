CLASSIFIED BY S/NIS AMB. S.TEPHEN SESTANOVICH FOR REASONS 
1.5(B) AND (D). 
1. (U) MOLDOVAN PRIME MINISTER DUMITRU BRAGHIS LED A 
DELEGATION TO WASHINGTON OCTOBER 3-8 FOR MEETINGS WITH USG 
OFFICIALS, MEMBERS OF CONGRESS, REPRESENTATIVES OF THE IFIS 
AND LEADERS IN THE PRIVATE SECTOR. THE DELEGATION INCLUDED 
DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER IURIE LEANCA, DEPUTY MINISTER OF 
FINANCE VICTOR CHIRIAC, DEPUTY MINISTER OF ECONOMY AND 
REFORM PETRU VEVERITA AND NATIONAL BANK VICE GOVERNOR 
VERONIC BACALU. U.S. AMBASSADOR TO MOLDOVA RUDOLF PERINA 
ACCOMPANIED THE DELEGATION TO WASHINGTON. BRAGHIS MET WITH 
ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT AND HAD SEVERAL OTHER MEETINGS IN 
THE DEPARTMENT OCTOBER 4-5. THIS WAS BRAGHIS' FIRST VISIT 
TO WASHINGTON AS PRIME MINISTER AND THE FIRST VISIT OF A 
MOLDOVAN HEAD OF GOVERNMENT TO WASHINGTON IN FIVE YEARS. 
THE RESULTS OF A ROUNDTABLEBETWEEN BRAGHIS AND AMBASSADORS 
DAN FRIED, CAREY CAVANAUGH AND CRAIG DUNKERLEY WILL BE 
REPORTED SEPTEL. 
2. -tel. ACTING SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT CONVEYED SECRETARY 
ALBRIGHT'S GREETINGS AND DISAPPOINTMENT THAT SHE COULD NOT 
ATTEND THE MEETING WITH BRAGHIS. HE ALSO NOTED THAT IT HAD 
BEEN A LONG TIME SINCE A MOLDOVAN HEAD OF GOVERNMENT HAD 
COME TO WASHINGTON AND EXPRESSED PLEASURE AT BRAGHIS' 
VISIT. MENTIONING THE RECENT CHANGES IN MOLDOVA'S 
GOVERNMENT, TALBOTT ASKED WHETHER THE REFORMS WOULD 
INCREASE MOLDOVA'S POLITICAL STABILITY. THE IFIS ADMIRED 
BRAGHIS' EFFORTS TO REFORM MOLDOVA'S ECONOMY AND THE USG 
GREATLY APPRECIATED COOPERATION ON NON-PROLIFERATION 
ISSUES. DESPITE THE ECONOMIC PROGRESS AND OPTIMISM FOR THE 
FUTURE, HOWEVER, A LOT OF WORK STILL NEEDED TO BE DONE IN 
MOLDOVA. 
3. 444-BRAGHIS THANKED THE ACTING SECRETARY FOR U.S. 
ASSISTANCE AND SUPPORT. MOLDOVA WAS MOVING TO A 
PARLIAMENTARY POLITICAL SYSTEM, BUT WOULD DO SO LEGALLY AND 
DEMOCRATICALLY. CONTINUING ECONOMIC REFORM, HOWEVER, WAS 
MORE DIFFICULT. HE CONTENDED THAT REFORM HAD NOT 
IMMEDIATELY PRODUCED THE DESIRED ECONOMIC GROWTH OR 
INCREASE IN LIVING STANDARDS. THE MOST IMMEDIATE ISSUES TO 
'ADDRESS, THEREFORE, WERE THE CONCERNS OF PEOPLE SUFFERING 
FROM REFORM-RELATED DISLOCATIONS, PARTICULARLY PENSIONERS. 
BRAGHIS' GOVERNMENT WAS WORKING TO ADDRESS THESE CONCERNS 
AND HOPED TO RESTART IFI LENDING PROGRAMS SOON. AN IMF 
MISSION WOULD VISIT MOLDOVA NEXT MONTH TO DISCUSS POVERTY 
ALLEVIATION PROGRAMS. IN THE LONG-TERM, HOWEVER, 
INTEGRATION INTO THE EURO-ATLANTIC COMMUNITY WAS THE ONLY 
HOPE FOR SUSTAINED DEVELOPMENT IN MOLDOVA. BRAGHIS WAS 
APPRECIATIVE OF U.S. SUPPORT FOR MOLDOVA'S FULL MEMBERHSIP 
IN THE SOUTHEAST EUROPEAN STABILITY PACT AND WAS OPTIMISTIC 
ABOUT COMPLETING WTO ACCESSION NEGOTIATIONS BY THE END OF 
THE YEAR. HE ALSO SUGGESTED CREATION OF A JOINT p.s.-
MOLDOVAN TASK FORCE TO PROMOTE ECONOMIC COOPERATION.

PUBLICLY STATED VIEW THAT THE CONFLICT MUST BE RESOLVED IN

COMPLETE IMPLEMENTATION OF RUSSIA'S ISTANBUL CFE 
AGREEMENTS. THE RUSSIAN POLITICAL SETTLEMENT PROPOSAL WAS 
UNACCEPTABLE TO CHISINAU BECAUSE IT WOULD "FEDERALIZE" 
MOLDOVA. NEGOTIATIONS BETWEEN CHISINAU AND TIRASPOL WERE 
AT A STANDSTILL, BUT RUSSIA COULD FACILITATE AN AGREEMENT 
SIMPLY BY IMPLEMENTATING ITS CFE WITHDRAWAL COMMITMENTS, 
THEREBY FORCING THE TRANSNISTRIAN LEADERSHIP TO NEGOTIATE 
SERIOUSLY. FINALLY, BRAGHIS MENTIONED MOLDOVA'S ENERGY 
DEPENDENCE ON RUSSIA, STRESSING THAT ENERGY INDEPENDENCE 
AND SECURITY WERE IMPORTANT FOR MOLDOVA'S VIABILITY. 
THE ACTING SECRETARY RESPONDED THAT ECONOMIC REFORM, 
ENERGY INDEPENDENCE AND RESOLUTION OF THE TRANSNISTRIA 
CONFLICT WERE ALL INEXTRICABLY LINKED. ENERGY REFORM 
SHOULD BE MOLDOVA'S MAIN ECONOMIC PRIORITY. PRIVATIZATION 
WAS THE KEY, NOT GOVERNMENT DEALS THAT WOULD ONLY INCREASE 
MOLDOVA'S DEPENDENCE ON RUSSIA. AN IMPORTANT STEP WOULD BE 
NAMING INDEPENDENT CANDIDATES FOR THE ENERGY REGULATORY 
BOARD. THIS WOULD SEND A STRONG SIGNAL.TO INVESTORS AND 
INCREASED INVESTMENT WOULD IN TURN FACILITATE ECONOMIC 
GROWTH. S/NIS AMBASSADOR-AT-LARGE SESTANOVICH ADDED THAT 
WHILE ENERGY REFORM WAS CRUCIAL, CONTROLLING CORRUPTION WAS 
EQUALLY IMPORTANT FOR ATTRACTING INVESTMENT. TALBOTT 
AGREED, CALLING CORRUPTION THE WORST LEGACY OF THE SOVIET 
PERIOD AND THE MOST IMPORTANT TO OVERCOME. IT AFFECTED THE 
RULE OF LAW, GOVERNMENT TRUSTWORTHINESS AND THE EFFICIENCY 
OF WESTERN ASSISTANCE. 
6. THE ISSUE OF MOLDOVA'S MEMBERSHIP IN THE 
,STABILITY PACT, TALBOTT STRESSED THAT U.S. EFFORTS TO 
ADVOCATE ON MOLDOVA'S BEHALF WOULD ONLY BE SUCCESSFUL IF 
MOLDOVA WERE AS ACTIVE AS POSSIBLE IN ITS CURRENT OBSERVER 
STATUS. HE ALSO URGED QUICK CONCLUSION OF MOLDOVA'S WTO 
ACCESSION NEGOTIATIONS, ESPECIALLY THE U.S.-MOLDOVAN 
BILATERAL AGREEMENT. ON THIS LAST POINT, TALBOTT MADE 
REFERENCE TO THE AUDIO-VISUAL DISPUTE WITH THE EU BY 
STRESSING THE IMPORTANCE OF REACHING AN AGREEMENT THAT 
MEETS'THE CONCERNS OF BOTH THE U.S. AND MOLDOVA. 
7. WITH REGARD TO TRANSNISTRIA, TALBOTT MENTIONED THAT 
PRIMAKOV HAD TRIED TO GET HIM PERSONALLY ENGAGED ON THE 
ISSUE. HE HAD CHOSEN, HOWEVER, TO UTILIZE THE HELP OF 
SPECIAL ENVOY FOR CFE CRAIG DUNKERLEY AND SPECIAL 
NEGOTIATOR FOR NIS REGIONAL CONFLICTS CAREY CAVANAUGH TO 
REDIRECT PRIMAKOV TO THE OSCE, THE PROPER LOCUS FOR 
DISCUSSIONS ON TRANSNISTRIA. TALBOTT MENTIONED THAT THE 
PRESIDENT.HAS MET PRESIDENT PUTIN THREE TIMES, AND EACH 
TIME HAS AFFIRMED THE IMPORTANCE THE U.S. PLACES ON RUSSIA 
MEETING ITS ISTANBUL COMMITMENTS IN MOLDOVA. FOR U.S. 
SUPPORT TO BE EFFECTIVE, HOWEVER, MOLDOVA NEEDED TO BE 
CREATIVE YET FIRM ON FUNDAMENTALS DURING THE UPCOMING 
TRANSNISTRIA DISCUSSIONS IN VIENNA. 
8. -f-C-3-THE MEETING CONCLUDED WITH TALBOTT REAFFRIMING U.S. 
SUPPORT FOR MOLDOVA AS IT CHARTED A FUTURE COURSE OF 
DEMOCRATIC REFORM AND EURO-ATLANTIC INTEGRATION. THE 
DISCUSSION THEN CONTINUED DURING A LUNCHEON HOSTED BY AMB. 
SESTANOVICH AND NSC MARK MEDISH. NANCY LEE, DEPUTY 
ASSISTANT SECRETARY OF TREASURY FOR EURASIA, ALSO ATTENDED 
THE LUNCHEON. THE PARTICIPANTS AGREED TO LAUNCH THE "JOINT 
TASK FORCE ON ECONOMIC COOPERATION AND INVESTMENT" 
SUGGESTED BY BRAGHIS DURING THE MEETING WITH THE ACTING 
SECRETARY.

NIS AMBASSADOR BILL TAYLOR. HE PRAISED U.S.-MOLDOVAN 
COOPERATION ON LAND PRIVATIZATION AND ASKED FOR CONTINUED 
ASSISTANCE ON POST-PRIVATIZATION ISSUES. HE MENTIONED THAT 
TWO REMAINING STATE-OWNED POWER DISTRIBUTION PLANTS WOULD 
SOON BE PRIVATIZED ALONG WITH THE HEAT GENERATION FACILITY 
"THERMOCOM." HE RAISED THE NEED FOR WINTER HEATING 
ASSISTANCE FOR THE MOST VULNERABLE SECTORS OF SOCIETY AND 
SUGGESTED THE U.S. ALSO CONSIDER PROVIDING ASSISTANCE FOR 
INSTALLATION OF RESIDENTIAL METERS. 
10. -4-Et-TAYLOR RESPONDED THAT IT WOULD BE EASIER TO 
CONVINCE CONGRESS TO ALLOCATE FUNDS FOR ASSISTANCE IF THOSE 
FUNDS WERE PROVIDED ONLY TO PRIVATIZED ENTITIES. IF THE 
GOM NEEDED WINTER HEATING ASSISTANCE, IT SHOULD MOVE ' 
QUICKLY TO FINISH PRIVATIZATION OF THE TWO POWER 
DISTRIBUTION PLANTS AND THERMOCOM. ASSISTANCE WITH 
INSTALLATION OF METERS WAS ALSO A POSSIBILITY AND WOULD BE 
EXAMINED AT THE WORKING LEVEL. AFTER FURTHER DISCUSSION, 
BRAGHIS AGREED WITH TAYLOR THAT ENERGY ASSISTANCE TO 
VULNERABLE GROUPS WOULD ONLY BE PROVIDED IN THE CONTEXT OF 
,PRIVATIZED ENTITIES. 
11. -re+ ON THE ISSUE OF FOOD ASSISTANCE, BRAGHIS THANKED 
TAYLOR FOR THE ADDITIONAL WHEAT PROVIDED BY USDA TO 
AMELIORATE THE EFFECTS OF THIS SUMMER'S DROUGHT IN MOLDOVA. 
HIS GOVERNMENT, HOWEVER, WOULD LIKE TO HAVE MORE OVERSIGHT 
OF THE PVO RESPONSIBLE FOR DISTRIBUTING THE WHEAT. THAT 
WAY DISTRIBUTION WOULD NOT BECOME A POLITICAL ISSUE AND THE 
GOVERNMENT COULD ENSURE THE WHEAT REACHED THOSE FOR WHOM IT 
WAS INTENDED. HE ALSO INQUIRED ABOUT THE POSSIBILITY OF 
BUYING SEEDS FOR FARMERS WHOSE INCOMES HAVE BEEN REDUCED BY 
THE DROUGHT. TAYLOR RESPONDED THAT ANY CONCERNS OVER 
DISTRIBUTION OF THE WHEAT SHOULD BE DIRECTED TO OUR EMBASSY 
IN CHISINAU. HE ALSO SAID THAT VIA A PVO, ADDITIONAL WHEAT 
COULD BE SOLD IN MOLDOVA AND THE PROCEEDS USED TO BUY SEEDS 
FOR FARMERS. 
12. 444- VICTOR CHIRIAC, DEPUTY MINISTER OF FINANCE, 
MENTIONED THAT THE DELEGATION HAD DISCUSSED RESTRUCTURING 
OF MOLDOVA'S EXTERNAL DEBT WITH TREASURY OFFICIALS EARLIER 
THAT DAY. CHIRIAC QUERIED TAYLOR ON THE POSSIBILITY OF 
SUPPORTING TREASURY'S PROPOSAL TO SEND ADVISORS TO MOLDOVA 
TO WORK ON DEBT RESCHEDULING AND CREATION OF AN INTEGRATED 
COMPUTER SYSTEM TO CONNECT THE MOLDOVAN TREASURY, CUSTOMS 
SERVICE, TAX AGENCY AND NATIONAL BANK. VERONICA BACALU, 
VICE GOVERNOR OF THE NATIONAL BANK, ADDED THAT SHE WOULD 
LIKE THE INTEGRATED COMPUTER SYSTEMS USED BY OTHER EUROFEAN 
GOVERNMENTS TO SERVE AS MODELS FOR INTEGRATION OF MOLDOVA'S 
SYSTEMS. TAYLOR RESPONDED THAT HE WAS AWARE OF BRAGHIS' 
DISCUSSIONS AT TREASURY AND SUPPORTED THE PROPOSAL TO SEND 
ADVISORS TO MOLDOVA. TAYLOR SAID THAT FUNDS WOULD ALSO BE 
PROVIDED, VIA WORLD BANK, FOR INSTALLATION OF A COMPUTER 
SYSTEM TO CONNECT THE MOLDOVAN CUSTOMS SERVICE TO A SECT-
SPONSORED SOUTHEAST EUROPEAN CUSTOMS NETWORK. 
13. 4-re-)- BRAGHIS CONTINUED DISCUSSION OF SECI INITIATIVES 
DURING A MEETING WITH SECI COORDINATOR AMB. RICHARD 
SCHIFTER. BRAGHIS THANKED SCHIFTER FOR HIS SUPPORT OF 
MOLDOVA'S INTEGRATION INTO EURO-ATLANTIC STRUCTURES. SECI 
WAS AN IMPORTANT INITIATIVE THAT HELPED MOLDOVA BRING ITS 
GOVERNMENT STANDARDS UP TO THE EUROPEAN LEVEL, ONE OF THE 
MOST IMPORTANT PRIORITIES FOR BRAGHIS' GOVERNMENT. THE 
CUSTOMS, ANTI-CRIME AND ENERGY INITIATIVES OF SECI WERE 
PARTICULARLY IMPORTANT FOR MOLDOVA. BRAGHIS ALSO ASKED FOR 
U.S. SUPPORT FOR MOLDOVA'S FULL MEMBERSHIP IN THE SOUTHEAST 
EUROPE STABILITY PACT.

14. -K-)- SCHIFTER INFORMED BRAGHIS THAT THE WORLD BANK WAS 
READY TO MOVE QUICKLY TO INSTALL THE SECI-SPONSORED CUSTOMS 
NETWORK. THE NEXT STEP, HOWEVER, WAS FOR MOLDOVA TO 
INCLUDE THE SYSTEM IN ITS WORLD BANK COUNTRY ASSISTANCE 
REQUESTS FOR FY02 AND TO ADHERE TO AN MOU PREVIOUSLY SIGNED 
BY SIX OTHER COUNTRIES PARTICIPATING IN THE PROGRAM. ONCE 
INSTALLED, THE U.S. CUSTOMS LIAISON-IN CHISINAU WOULD 
ARRANGE TRAINING FOR MOLDOVAN CUSTOMS OFFICIALS TO USE THE 
NEW SYSTEM. ON THE ISSUE OF CRIME, IN ADDITION TO ONGOING 
EFFORTS TO COORDINATE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES IN THE 
REGION, SECI PLANNED TO COORDINATE EFFORTS AMONG 
PROSECUTORS OF INDIVIDUAL COUNTRIES TO PREPARE CASES 
AGAINST ORGANIZED CRIME SYNDICATES. GIVEN THE SPECIAL 
NEED TO COMBAT TRAFFICKING IN HUMAN BEINGS, SOMETHING THAT 
HAS BECOME A SERIOUS PROBLEM FOR MOLDOVA, IT WOULD BE 
HELPFUL TO HAVE MOLDOVA'S PROSECUTORS COOPERATE WITH THIS 
INITIATIVE. FINALLY, SECI WAS WORKING ON INTEGRATION OF 
THE ELECTRICAL GRIDS IN SOUTHEAST EUROPE. SECI COULD ALSO 
ASSIST THE GOM TO ENCOURAGE ENERGY CONSERVATION AND 
REPLACEMENT OF ANTIQUATED EQUIPMENT AS A MEANS OF REDUCING 
ENERGY COSTS.
