2. STROBE: AS JIM HAD REQUESTED, I ASKED MAMEDOV 
PRIVATELY THIS AFTERNOON FOR HIS VIEWS ON HOW BEST TO 
HANDLE YOUR DISCUSSION WITH YELTSIN IN THE CAR ENROUTE 
FROM ANDREWS. HE WARMED TO THE SUBJECT QUICKLY, AND 
PROVIDED A NUMBER OF INSIGHTS THAT ARE WORTH BEARING 
IN MIND BOTH FOR YOUR CONVERSATION AND THE PRESIDENT'S 
TALKS WITH YELTSIN. 
3. MAMEDOV'S MAIN POINT WAS THAT YOU SHOULD CONVEY 
THE PRESIDENT'S PERSONAL COMMITmENT TO HIS FRIENDSHIP 
WITH YELTSIN. HE ADVISED YOU TO BE FRANK ABOUT THE 
DIFFICULTIES THE ADMINISTRATION FACES; HE SAID YELTSIN 
WILL BE EQUALLY HONEST ABOUT HIS OWN DILEMMAS. WHAT 
MATTERS MOST TO YELTSIN ABOUT HIS TRIP TO WASHINGTON, 
MAMEDOV SAID, IS REINFORCING HIS PERSONAL RELATIONSHIP 
WITH THE PRESIDENT AND REESTABLISHING HIMSELF AS A 
WORLD-CLASS LEADER. 
4. BEYOND THAT, MAMEDOV ENCOURAGES YOU TO ENGAGE IN 
SOME "PREEMPTIVE DIPLOMACY." CITING PUBLIC REMARKS BY 
KOZYREV YESTERDAY WHICH WERE BROADLY CRITICAL OF 
CONTINUING COLD WAR TRADE RESTRICTIONS AND OF AMERICAN 
RESERVATIONS ABOUT RUSSIAN POLICY IN THE NEAR ABROAD, 
MAMEDOV SAID THIS WAS "JUST THE TIP OF THE ICEBERG." 
A LOT OF PEOPLE AROUND YELTSIN, MAMEDOV SUGGESTED, ARE 
FILLING HIM WITH STORIES THAT THE AMERICANS ARE OUT TO 
UNDERCUT RUSSIA IN THE CAUCASUS AND SPRING UNWELCOME 
SURPRISES ON A VARIETY OF ISSUES EITHER AT THE SUMMIT 
OR PRIOR TO THE NOVEMBER ELECTIONS. HE SUGGESTS THAT 
YOU PREEMPT THIS BY GETTING ACROSS A FEW GENERAL 
POINTS UP FRONT, BEFORE THE TWO PRESIDENTS BEGIN THEIR 
MEETINGS: 
-- HAITI: MAMEDOV SAID YELTSIN HAS NOT ENGAGED VERY 
MUCH ON THIS ISSUE. HE ADVISES YOU NOT TO DWELL ON 
IT, BUT SIMPLY TO NOTE THAT THE U.S. HAS HAD TO MAKE 
SOME DIFFICULT CHOICES, UNDER A VARIETY OF DOMESTIC 
AND INTERNATIONAL PRESSURES (SOMETHING YELTSIN CAN 
RELATE TO). 
- BALTICS: MAMEDOV SUGGESTS THAT IT CERTAINLY WON'T 
HURT TO REITERATE THE PRESIDENT'S APPRECIATION FOR 
COMPLETION OF BALTIC TROOP WITHDRAWAL, WHICH WAS NOT 
AN EASY THING FOR YELTSIN TO DO. 
-- CAUCASUS: IT WOULD BE USEFUL, MAMEDOV THINKS, TO 
STRESS THAT OUR APPROACH TO NAGORNO-KARABAKH IS 
DESIGNED TO PROMOTE OUR MUTUAL INTEREST IN REGIONAL 
STABILTY -- NOT TO "PUT RUSSIA IN ITS PLACE" OR
