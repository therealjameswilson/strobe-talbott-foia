SUBJECT: SHEVARDNADZE SUPPORTS NATO'S ACTIONS IN KOSOVO 
CLASSIFIED BY: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, REASONS 1.5 (B,D) 
SUMMARY 
1. 4G-)- SUMMARY: DURING A VERY WARM AND CANDID ONE-HOUR 
MEETING APRIL 23 IN WASHINGTON, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT 
AND GEORGIAN PRESIDENT SHEVARDNADZE DISCUSSED REGIONAL 
VIEWS TOWARD THE KOSOVO CONFLICT AND NATO. SHEVARDNADZE 
SPOKE AT LENGTH ABOUT KOSOVO -- THE REQUIREMENTS HE SEES 
FOR A S(ETTLEMENT; THE CHERNOMYRDIN MISSION AND ROLE; HOW 
THE KOSOVO SITUATION PLAYS OUT IN RUSSIAN DOMESTIC 
POLITICS; AND THE IMPORTANCE OF THE PRECEDENT OF KOSOVO FOR 
THE GEORGIA/ABKHAZIA CONFLICT. END SUMMARY

CHERNOMYRDIN'S NEW ROLE,
3. -(..G4 SHEVARDNADZE EXPLAINED THAT CHERNOMYRDIN HAD COME TO 
GEORGIA (AND AZERBAIJAN AND UKRAINE) TO SEE WHAT THE 
RESPONSE WOULD BE IF MILOSEVIC ACCEPTED AN INTERNATIONAL 
PRESENCE. SHEVARDNADZE TOLD CHERNOMYRDIN THAT THERE WAS NO 
OTHER WAY. SHEVARDADZE ASSESSED CHERNOMYRDIN'S MOTIVES FOR 
VISITING GEORGIA, AZERBAIJAN AND UKRAINE AS A DESIRE TO 
REVIEW HIS OWN THINKING AND SEE HOW RUSSIA'S VIEWS WOULD 
FLY. HE THOUGHT CHERNOMYRDIN WAS COUNTING ON HIM PASSING 
ALONG THIS MESSAGE TO THE USG. SHEVARDNADZE COMMENTED THAT 
CHERNOMYRDIN'S APPOINTMENT WAS A SIGN OF PRIMAKOV'S LACK OF 
SUCCESS. SHEVARDNADZE ALSO NOTED THAT YELTSIN'S APPROACH 
ON KOSOVO MAY BE BEGINNING TO CHANGE, AND THAT IT WAS NOT 
BY ACCIDENT THAT HE CALLED CHERNOMYRDIN BACK. SHEVARDNADZE 
THOUGHT THAT YELTSIN HAD MADE THE RIGHT CHOICE IN 
APPOINTING CHERNOMYRDIN - HE WAS A MAN WITH EXPERIENCE WHO 
COULD SIT DOWN WITH USG OFFICIALS AND WORK THINGS OUT. 
KOSOVO 
4. te) THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CAUTIONED THAT PART OF THE DARK 
LESSON OF KOSOVO WAS THAT STATES CAN FALL APART UNDER BAD 
LEADERSHIP. NATO AND UN SYG ANNAN ARE DISCUSSING AN 
INTERNATIONALLY PROTECTED AREA WITHIN YUGOSLAVIA, WITH THE 
WITHDRAWAL OF ALL ARMED SERBIAN FORCES. ON THE MAP IT ALL 
WOULD BE YUGOSLAVIA, BUT BELGRADE WOULD NOT BE IN CHARGE OF 
THIS AREA; THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY WOULD. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY ASKED SHEVARDNADZE HOW GEORGIA AND THE REGION 
WOULD VIEW SUCH A SOLUTION IN YUGOSLAVIA. SHEVARDNADZE 
REPLIED THAT GEORGIA WOULD FIND IT ACCEPTABLE, AND IMPLIED 
HE FOUND IT A HELPFUL PRECEDENT VIS-A-VIS ABKHAZIA. AT 
VARIOUS POINTS HE MADE CLEAR HE SEES PARALLELS BETWEEN THE 
BEHAVIOR OF ARDZINBA AND MILOSEVIC AND HOPES THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY WILL TREAT THEM THE SAME WAY.

KOSOVO AND ABKHAZIA 
5. +.04- SHEVARDNADZE SAID HE AND HIS KEY AIDES HAVE GIVEN A 
LOT OF THOUGHT TO THE IMPLICATIONS OF KOSOVO FOR ABKHAZIA. 
HE SKETCHED BRIEFLY THE POSITIVE FEATURES HE SEES IN THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY'S APPROACH: (1) IT UNDERSCORES 
OPPOSITION TO ETHNIC CLEANSING AND HOLDS THE POSSIBILITY 
THAT OFFENDERS WILL BE BROUGHT TO JUSTICE IN THE HAGUE OR 
ELSEWHERE; (2) IT LEGITIMIZES INTERNATIONAL INTERVENTION TO 
PREVENT AND REVERSE ETHNIC CLEANSING; (3) IT ARGUES FOR 
ENHANCED U.N. ATTENTION (HE FORESHADOWED A COMING GEORGIAN 
DIPLOMATIC EFFORT TO WIN "MUCH STRONGER, TOUGHER" LANGUAGE 
ON ABKHAZIA AND ARDZINBA DURING JULY UNSC DEBATE ON UNOMIG 
MANDATE RENEWAL); AND (4), MOST IMPORTANTLY, IT BOLSTERS A 
CASE THAT TBILISI HAS FOR SOME TIME BEEN MAKING THAT THE 
ONLY WAY TO GUARANTEE ADEQUATELY THE SECURITY OF IDPS 
RETURNING TO GALI IS BY MEANS OF JOINT TEAMS UNDER AN 
INTERNATIONAL BANNER. SHEVARDNADZE INDICATED THAT THE 
GEORGIANS WOULD BE MAKING A RENEWED PUSH ON THIS LAST ITEM, 
AND LOOKING FOR SUPPORT FROM THE U.S. AND OTHER "FRIENDS" 
IN FIGURING OUT A WAY TO ACHIEVE SUCH 
PATROLLING/INVESTIGATING/SAFEGUARDING IN GALI UNDER A U.N. 
BANNER. 
6. 4G} ON THE CIS (RUSSIAN) PEACEKEEPERS, PRESIDENT 
SHEVARDNADZE SEEMED RELAXED. HE SAID THAT HE SAW NO SIGNS.

UNCONDITIONED LEGAL STATUS IN GEORGIA TO THE PEACEKEEPERS.
