Dear Mrs. Moynihan: 
My apologies for not responding more quickly to your 
letter. As yo.0 can imagine the last week or so has been very 
hectic, in part because of the confirmation process but also 
because of intense activity on several foreign policy fronts. 1 
I was interested to learn about the Indo-U.S. Subcommission 
on Education and Culture. It came into being not long after my 
first visit to Delhi, which occasioned an opportunity for me to 
travel with you and Nancy Kissinger to the Taj Mahal. I have 
more then a pasting interest in India, since my wife Brooke 
lived there for four months in the summer of 1968 as a 
participant in the Experiment International Living. As soon as 
I get past confirmation, I will do what I. can to follow up on 
your letter. In the meantime I will pass it along to Robin 
Raphel, my colleague in the department who oversees our policy 
toward India. 
As I told your husband when I made my courtesy call on him, 
I certainly agreed that we should move with all possible haste 
in getting an Ambassador to Delhi.- I feel personal - --
responsibility for the unfortunate gap in that assignment in 
one sense; I had something to do with yanking Tom Pickering out 
of there a year ago to represent us in Moscow, where he has 
done an absolutely splendid job. 
z 
I hope that our paths will cross soon, and I'll assure you 
I will do whatever I can from my new post to support the good 
work you are doing for U.S. India relations. 
Sincerely, 
B6 
Mrs. Elizabeth B. Moynihan
