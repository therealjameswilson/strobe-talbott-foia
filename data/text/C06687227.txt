The Chern4orskoye Joint Venture 
On April 15, 1993, Eximbank approved its first project 
financing transaction in Russia, a guarantee of $44,052,865 to 
support the sale of U.S. oilfield equipment and services to the 
Chernogorskoye Joint Venture in Nizhnevartovsk, Russia. A variety 
of U.S. exporters will benefit from the financing, including the 
Halliburton Company, National Oilwell and National Engineers and 
Constructors, Inc. Morgan Grenfell and Company, Ltd., of London 
will be the lender for the Eximbank-guaranteed loan, which is being 
extended on the basis, of limited recourse project risk. An 
additional $30 million in financing to the project will be provided 
by the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development. 
The Chernogorskoye Joint Venture is a 50/50 joint venture 
between Anderman/Smith International-Chernogorskoye Partnership, a 
Colorado general partnership, and a private Russian oil production 
company. The joint venture will be developing a new oil field 
adjacent to the Samotlor oil field in Western Siberia. The oil 
produced by the project will be purchased by Itochu Corporation of 
Japan. The revenues from such sales will be deposited in an 
offshore bank account and used for debt service and approved 
project expenses.
