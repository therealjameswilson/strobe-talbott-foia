I am writing to ask you to help the Antitrust Division to 
provide badly-needed technical assistance to the new Russian

on earth. The Russians have repeatedly requested our help, and I 
would like to respond with a comprehensive assistance program. 
Given our current budgetconstraints, however, I need funding 
from the Agency for International Development ("AID") in order to 
provide such a program.
