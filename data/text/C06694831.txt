3. SUMMARY. IN ADDITION TO PRIVATE DISCUSSIONS WITH 
IVORIAN PRINCIPALS (SEPTELS) DURING HIS OCTOBER 25-26 
VISIT TO ABIDJAN, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT-USED TWO-
HIGH-PROFILE PUBLIC EVENTS TO PROMOTE THE USG'S DUAL 
OBJECTIVES OF INCREASED IVORIAN INVOLVEMENT IN 
PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS ABROAD AND CONTINUED IVORIAN 
DEMOCRATIZATION AT HOME. IN AN OCTOBER 25 ADDRESS TO 
THE IVORIAN NATIONAL ASSEMBLY, THE DEPSEC OUTLINED 
AMERICA'S EFFORTS IN SUPPORT OF MULTILATERAL PEACE 
INITIATIVES AND URGED AFRICANS TO REINFORCE THE 
CAPACITIES OF REGIONAL GROUPINGS LIKE THE OAU AND 
ECOWAS. AT A RECEPTION WITH POLITICAL PARTY AND CIVIL 
SOCIETY LEADERS LATER THAT EVENING, THE DEPSEC FOCUSED 
HIS FORMAL REMARKS ON DEMOCRATIZATION. DURING A 
RECEPTION PULL-ASIDE WITH INTERIOR MINISTER BOMBET, 
DEPSEC UNDERSCORED USG INTEREST IN FREE AND TRANSPARENT 
ELECTIONS IN 1995, URGED INTERNATIONAL ELECTION 
OBSERVER PARTICIPATION, AND LISTENED TO AN UPDATE ON 
ELECTIONS PREPARATION. THESE EVENTS AND THE VISIT AS A 
WHOLE SERVED TO HEIGHTEN IVORIAN SENSITIVITIES TO BOTH 
PEACEKEEPING AND DEMOCRACY, ALTHOUGH GOCI OFFICIALS 
REMAIN RELUCTANT TO ENTERTAIN PUBLICLY DIRECT 
INVOLVEMENT IN LIBERIA OR INTERNATIONAL ELECTIONS 
OBSERVATION. END SUMMARY. 
NATIONAL ASSEMBLY ADDRESS FOCUSES ON PEACEKEEPING

ASSEMBLY, CHARLES DONWAHI, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT

FOREIGN AFFAIRS COMMISSION, WAS EXPANDED TO INCLUDE ALL 
INTERESTED DEPUTIES. APPROXIMATELY 120 OF THE 175 
DEPUTIES ATTENDED, AN UNUSUALLY LARGE SHOWING FOR StJCH AN 
EVENT.
