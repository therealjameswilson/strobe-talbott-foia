SECRETARY GENERAL GAVIRIA 
—e3i— SUBJECT: OAS RESOLUTION 1080 AND POSSIBLE 
FOREIGN MINISTERS MEETING 
5. *Si- THE ACTING SECRETARY PRAISED OAS SECRETARY 
GENERAL GAVIRIA FOR HIS EFFORTS TO RESOLVE THE SITUATION 
IN PARAGUAY. THE ACTING SECRETARY NOTED HE HAD TALKED 
WITH THE SECRETARY, WHO ALSO ADMIRED GAVIRIA'S 
WILLINGNESS TO PLUNGE INTO THIS VENTURE WITH FULL ENERGY. 
6. -4-9+- GAVIRIA THANKED THE ACTING SECRETARY, NOTING 
THAT HE WAS JUST DOING HIS JOB. HE COMMENTED THAT THE 
SITUATION IN ASUNCION WAS BETTER, ALTHOUGH NOT TOTALLY 
RESOLVED. HE OBSERVED THE TRANSITION WOULD MOVE FORWARD 
APRIL 24, AS PRESIDENT WASMOSY HAD DEMANDED GENERAL 
OVIEDO RELINQUISH HIS MILITARY COMMAND BEFORE BECOMING A 
CABINET MEMBER. GAVIRIA OBSERVED THE GOVERNMENT'S 
POSITION SEEMED REASONABLE, ALTHOUGH IT MIGHT BE AN 
UPSETTING COMPROMISE FOR OVIEDO. GAVIRIA ADDED THAT ALL 
SHOULD GO WELL, AS OVIEDO SEEMED TO BE EXERCISING GOOD 
JUDGEMENT. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THIS WAS 
-ENCOURAGING AND STRESSED GAVIRIA'S PRESENCE HAD BEEN A 
POSITIVE FACTOR. 
7. -Fei— THE ACTING SECRETARY THEN RAISED HIS CONCERNS 
ABOUT THE APRIL 23 OAS MEETING IN WASHINGTON. HE SAID 
HE HAD SPENT THREE HOURS AT THIS EMERGENCY SESSION OF 
THE PERMANENT REPRESENTATIVES TO THE OAS. THE MEETING 
FOCUSED ON WHETHER THE COMMUNIQUE FROM THE MEETING WOULD 
INVOKE RESOLUTION 1080 AND HOLD OPEN THE POSSIBILITY OF 
A FOREIGN MINISTERS MEETING IN THE NEAR FUTURE. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY UNDERSTOOD AMBASSADOR MONTANARO, 
PARAGUAY'S REPRESENTATIVE TO THE OAS, INITIALLY HAD 
INSTRUCTIONS TO SEEK ACTION UNDER RESOLUTION 1080.• 
HOWEVER, THE ACTING SECRETARY HAD LEARNED ONLY FIVE 
MINUTES AGO THAT AMRASSADOR MONTANARO HAD RECEIVED NEW, 
CONTRARY INSTRUCTIONS, FROM HIS GOVERNMENT. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY STRESSED THE USG SAW THIS AS A BIG MISTAKE. 
8. 464- GAVIRIA AGREED AND OFFERED TO IMMEDIATELY CALL 
THE PARAGUAYAN FOREIGN MINISTRY, ADDING THAT DURING AN 
EARLIER-MEETING WITH GAVIRIA, THE GOP HAD AGREED TO MOVE 
AHEAD WITH RESOLUTION 1080 AND A FOREIGN MINISTERS 
MEETING WITHOUT A DATE. 
9. -(-9-)- THE ACTING SECRETARY THANKED GAVIRIA FOR THIS 
GOOD NEWS, ADDING GAVIRIA WAS WELCOME TO INVOKE THE NAME 
OF THE USG IN THE STRONGEST TERMS WITH THE GOP. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY EMPHASIZED HE HAD MADE A STRONG 
STATEMENT, NOT JUST IN GENERAL TERMS, BUT ON THE 
SPECIFIC QUESTION OF RESOLUTION 1080 AND THE FOREIGN 
MINISTERS MEETING. MOREOVER, THE SECRETARY HAD 
AUTHORIZED THE ACTING SECRETARY TO INFORM THE PERMANENT 
COUNCIL THAT THE SECRETARY WOULD PERSONALLY ATTEND A 
FOREIGN MINISTERS MEETING ON THE SUBJECT. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY ASKED GAVIRIA TO DO WHAT HE COULD TO KEEP 
PRESSURE ON THE.GOP AND TO GET THE ISSUE RACK ON TRACK. 
GAVIRIA AGREED, NOTING THE SITUATION CONTINUED TO EVOLVE 
AND A SEARCH FOR A SOLUTION HAD JUST BEGUN. 
10. {9-} THE ACTING SECRETARY CONTINUED THAT HE HAD 
SPOKEN WITH HIS COUNTERPARTS FROM BRAZIL AND ARGENTINA, 
UNDERSCORING USG SUPPORT FOR THE MERCOSUR MISSION 
ALREADY UNDERWAY. GAVIRIA NOTED HE HAD BEEN 
COORDINATING CLOSELY WITH BRAZILIAN, ARGENTINE, AND 
URUGUAYAN OFFICIALS IN ASUNCION, WHOSE ROLES HAD BEEN 
KEY TO SOLVING THE CURRENT SITUATION. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY CONCLUDED BY WISHING GAVIRIA WELL IN HIS 
EFFORTS TO GET NEW INSTRUCTIONS TO AMBASSADOR MONTANARO 
SO THAT RESOLUTION 1080 AND A FOREIGN MINISTERS MEETING 
REMAINED THE PRIME OBJECTIVE. 
(U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1650 EST.
