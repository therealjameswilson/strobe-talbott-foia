RELEASE IN FULL 
2. SUMMARY. IN A MEETING WITH QIAN QICHEN IN BANGKOK ON 
JULY 24, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REITERATED US POLICY OF 
COMPREHENSIVE ENGAGEMENT WITH CHINA, HIGHLIGHTING UPCOMING 
HIGH-LEVEL EXCHANGES. QIAN OFFERED TO VISIT WASHINGTON 
AFTER THE UNGA FOR MORE DETAILED DISCUSSIONS BUT ASKED TO 
CONSIDER THE PROPOSED OCTOBER 3 DATE. QIAN AND THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY AGREED THAT THE SITUATION ON THE KOREAN PENINSULA 
SHOWED SOME PROMISING SIGNS, PARTICULARLY THE APPARENT 
WILLINGNESS OF NORTH KOREA TO CONTINUE ITS DIALOGUE WITH 
THE US AND SOUTH KOREA. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REVIEWED US. 
EFFORTS REGARDING HAITI AND NOTED THAT COOPERATION AMONG 
THE PERMANENT MEMBERS OF THE UNSC WOULD INCREASE.CHANCES 
FOR A PEACEFUL SOLUTION. QIAN EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT THE 
USG POLICY REVIEW ON TAIWAN, WHICH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
STRESSED WOULD REMAIN CONSISTENT WITH OUR COMMITMENTS UNDER 
THE THREE COMMUNIQUES. END SUMMARY. 
COMPREHENSIVE ENGAGEMENT 
3. EXCHANGES: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THE PRESIDENT'S 
DETERMINATION TO HAVE AS MUCH CONTACT AS POSSIBLE BETWEEN 
OUR TWO COUNTRIES AND CITED RECENT VISITS BY USCINCPAC

ENGAGEMENT. VICE PREMIER AND FOREIGN MINISTER QIAN QICHEN 
ACCEPTED IN PRINCIPLE THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S PROPOSAL FOR 
A MEETING WITH SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER IN THE US, BUT ASKED 
FOR TIME TO CONSIDER THE OCTOBER 3 DATE. QIAN WELCOMED 
VISITS TO CHINA BY SECRETARIES BROWN AND PERRY, AND NOTED 
THE IMPORTANCE OF THE JOINT COMMISSION ON DEFENSE 
CONVERSION AND THE US VISIT OF THE DEPUTY CHIEF OF THE PLA 
GENERAL STAFF. QIAN SAID THAT, WHILE THE END OF THE COLD_ 
WAR HAD NOT CREATED TOTAL SECURITY, DEFENSE INDUSTRIES 
COULD BE CONVERTED TO CIVILIAN USE. QIAN ADDED THAT 
SECRETARY PERRY'S VISIT WOULD HELP TO INCREASE CONTACTS AND 
COOPERATION BETWEEN THE MILITARIES OF BOTH COUNTRIES. A/S 
LORD FURTHER NOTED THAT U/S TARNOFF'S VISIT WOULD PROVIDE 
INTER ALIA THE OPPORTUNITY FOR A MEANINGFUL DISCUSSION ON 
NON-PROLIFERATION. (NOTE: TIME CONSTRAINTS PROHIBITED
