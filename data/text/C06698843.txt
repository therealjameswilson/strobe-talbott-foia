THE VICE PRESIDENT: WE ARE PLEASED WITH THE PROGRESS THAT WE HAVE MADE THIS FALL ON NATO ADAPTATION. 
PRIME MINISTER GUTERRES: WE APPRECIATE THE SUPPORT YOII HAVE GIVEN US ON IBERLANT. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: DID YOU EVER HAVE ANY DOUBT? 
PRIME MINISTER GUTERRES: NO, BUT YOUR SUPPORT HAS BEEN VERY HELPFUL. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: OUR BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP HAS BEEN VERY IMPORTANT TO US. YOUR SUPPORT FOR THE U.S. POSITION ON MANY TRICKY ISSUES IS SOMETHING FOR WHICH WE ARE VERY GRATEFUL. WE HAVE A SHARED VISION OF WHAT THE FUTURE OF EUROPE SHOULD LOOK LIKE. I THINK WE BOTH SUPPORT PARALLEL PROGRESS ON NATO ENLARGEMENT, NATO ADAPTATION, AND NATO-RUSSIAN RELATIONS. 
PRIME MINSTER GUTERRES: I KNOW YOU MET WITH THE RUSSIAN PRIME MINISTER THIS MORNING. I HAD A LONG CONVERSATION WITH HIM YESTERDAY. IT IS CLEARLY GOING TO BE A VERY TOUGH ISSUE. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: YES,.CHERNOMYRDIN IS AN OLD FRIEND. THERE WERE SOME ISSUES WE DRALT WITH IN THE PAST ON WHICH HE BEGAN WITH A POSITION OF FIERCE RESISTANCE. AS WE TOOK HIS CONCERNS INTO ACCOUNT AND EXPLAINED OUR APPROACH CAREFULLY, HOWEVER, HIS RESISTANCE SOFTENED. I HOPE AND EXPECT THAT THIS WILL BE THE CASE THIS TIME. BOSNIA PROVIDES A GOOD EXAMPLE. WHO WOULD HAVE PREDICTED THAT OUR TROOPS AND RUSSIANS TROOPS WOULD BE WORKING SIDE-BY-SIDE? WE NEED TO ENGAGE WITH THE RUSSIANS IN THE SAME COOPERATIVE SPIRIT BEFORE THE INVITATIONS TO THE NEW MEMBERS GO OUT IN JUNE OR JULY. 
PRIME MINISTER GUTERRES: THE RUSSIANS DON T WANT UNILATERAL DECISIONS ON NATO ENLARGEMENT BEFORE SOMETHING IS DONE WITH THEM. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: THAT IS TRUE, BUT WE CANNOT LET THEM BELIEVE THAT WE CAN ACCEPT A SEQUENTIAL APPROACH. THEY WOULD ONLY BE TEMPTED TO VETO NATO ENLARGEMENT BY DELAYING THE NATO-RUSSIA AGREEMENT. 
PRIME MINISTER GUTERRES: I BELIEVE WE NEED TO GO FURTHER WITH THE IDEAS THAT WE HAVE CONCEIVED FOR THE NATO-RUSSIA RELATIONSHIP. AN ALLIANCE WITH NATO MIGHT GIVE THE RUSSIANS MORE CONFIDENCE. YESTERDAY, I MET WITH CHERNOMYRDIN FOR. OVER AN HOUR. OUR DISCUSSION WAS VERY WARM, BUT I COULD ALSO SENSE A DEEP CONCERN. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: THE LOSS OF THE SOVIET UNION WAS A BLESSING FOR RUSSIA BUT IT ALSO CAUSED A CERTAIN FEELING OF HUMILIATION. THIS NEW SENSE OF VULNERABILITY IS WHAT YOU WERE SENSING FROM CHERNOMYRDIN YESTERDAY. •• 
PRIME MINSTER GUTERRES: YOU SHOULD USE THE GERMAN CHANCELLOR. CHANCELLOR KOHL HAS A GOOD PERSONAL RELATIONSHIP WITH YELTSIN AND CAN PLAY A MAJOR ROLE IN SOFTENING THE RUSSIAN POSITION. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: AGAIN, THANK YOU FOR YOUR SUPPORT ON NATO ADAPTATION AND THE FOLLOW-ON FORCE IN BOSNIA. I KNOW YOU HAVE BEEN SUPPORTING US ON AFSOUTH.
