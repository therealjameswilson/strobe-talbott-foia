2. SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S JULY 26 
MEETING IN BANGKOK WITH FOREIGN MINISTER KONO FOCUSED 
PRIMARILY ON THE ECONOMIC FRAMEWORK, WITH KONO 
STRESSING THE IMPORTANCE OF SOLVING BILATERAL ECONOMIC 
ISSUES SO THAT THEY DO NOT HAMPER COOPERATION ON 
POLITICAL AND STRATEGIC ISSUES. HE URGED THAT LINES OF 
COMMUNICATION REMAIN OPEN -- INCLUDING A VISIT BY 
DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER HAYASHI TO WASHINGTON THIS WEEK 
-- BUT OFFERED NOTHING NEW IN SUBSTANCE. IN 
PARTICULAR, HE SHOWED CONTINUED STRONG RESISTANCE TO 
PROSPECTIVE OBJECTIVE INDICATORS. TALBOTT VOICED U.S. 
DISAPPOINTMENT WITH THE HAYASHI-BARSHEFSKY MEETING IN 
HONG KONG, AND, WHILE AGREEING TO FORWARD THE PROPOSAL 
FOR A HAYASHI TRIP, QUESTIONED THE ADVISABILITY OF 
FURTHER MEETINGS UNLESS JAPAN HAS NEW PROPOSALS THAT 
MEET OUR RESULTS-ORIENTED REQUIREMENTS. HE NOTED THE 
TIMETABLE FOR TITLE VII ACTION ABSENT SATISFACTORY 
JAPANESE PROPOSALS, A PROSPECT THAT KONO OBVIOUSLY 
FOUND DISQUIETING. AT BREAKFAST AND AT A LATE MEETING 
IN THE AFTERNOON, TALBOTT PRAISED KONO FOR HIS CAUTIOUS 
STANCE ON EAEC AND FOR MURAYAMA'S STATEMENTS REGARDING 
THE CONSTITUTIONALITY OF THE SELF-DEFENSE FORCES AND 
FOREIGN POLICY COMMONALITY. HE URGED JAPAN TO 
RECONSIDER SUPPORT FOR IRAN'S KARUN DAM. KONO PLEDGED 
CONTINUED CLOSE COOPERATION ON NORTH KOREA.
