SUBJECT: Telephone Conversation with Albanian 
President Meidani 
PARTICIPANTS: The President 
Albanian President Meidani

AND PLACE: 
Notetakers: Tony Campanella, Matt Sibley, 
Robert Ford 
Interpreter: Lindita Inami 
April 18, 1999, 1:56 - 1:59 p.m. EDT 
The Residence 
The President: Hello? (U) 
President Meidani: President Meidani here. (U) 
The President: This is President Clinton. Thank you for 
taking my call. (U) 
President Meidani: Thank you very much. It is a great 
pleasure to hear your voice. (U) 
The President: I wanted to call to express my appreciation 
for your support of NATO actions. I heard Yugoslavia broke 
diplomatic relations. I am very concerned about your 
security and any action by Serbia would be met with a strong 
response from me and the Allies. I just wanted to assure 
you of that and to say I am doing all I can to help with 
refugee problems. -Itt-
President Meidani: Thank you very much. I have been 
following everything, from your speeches and actions. Any 
support from your side is a great thing for the Albanian 
people arid the Albanian state. I am trying to coordinate 
with NATO here to organize refugee camps supported by 
different countries. The number of deported Albanians is 
growing. We already have an additional 50,000 deported 
Albanians. I have to say thank you.
