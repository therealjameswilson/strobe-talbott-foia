2. ~ SUMMARY: IN A HALF-HOUR MEETING ON AUGUST 7 WITH UK
AMBASSADOR SIR CHRISTOPHER MEYER, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND
MEYER DISCUSSED THE STATE OF PLAY ON NMD AND ONGOING
BILATERAL CONTACTS WITH RUSSIAN PRESIDENT PUTIN. THE
DEPUTY SECRETARY INDICATED THE NORTH KOREANS'OFFER" HAD
TNTERESTING ELEMENTS BUT THE EXACT NATURE OF THE PROPOSAL
REQUIRED FURTHER CLARIFICATION, MEYER STRESSED THAT,
CRITICAL PRESS AND PARLIAMENTARY VIEWS NOTWITHSTAIDING,
THE PRIME MINISTER REMAINED "VERY FORWARD-LEANIN? ON NMD.
REGARDING ONGOING U.S./UK TALKS ON EXTENDING ITAR
LICENSING EXEMPTIONS TO THE UK, MEYER MADE A PITCH FOR
ALLOWING THE UK TO EMPLOY ITS OWN ENFORCEMENT MECHANISMS
RATHER THAN ADOPT U.S. PROCEDURES. MEYER ALSO RAISED
BERMUDAN CLAIMS FOR ADDITIONAL CLEAN-UP AT'HE FORMER U.S.
BASE THERE AND FOR REPAYMENT OF OUTSTANDING BRIDGE
MAINTENANCE FEES. HE ALSO COMPLAINED ABOUT THE TONE AND
TERMS OF A RECENT U.S. DRAFT AGREEMENT TO OPEN THE USAF

NMD
3. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT THE PRESIDENT HAD
NOT YET MADE A DEPLOYMENT DECISION ON NMD AND REVIEWED
AGAIN THE FOUR CRITERIA THE PRESIDENT WOULD CONSIDER. HE
STRESSED THAT ALLIED CONCERNS WERE WEIGHING HEAVILY IN THE
DELIBERATIONS. HE REFERRED IN PASSING TO THE RECENT SPATE
OF NEGATIVE ARTICLES IN THE UK PRESS ON NMD AS WELL AS THE
HOUSE OF COMMONS'OREIGN AFFAIRS SELECT COMMITTEE'S
CRITICAL REPORT.
4. ~ TALBOTT INDICATED THAT THE NORTH KOREANS'OFFER"
COULD BE RELEVANT TO THE DEPLOYMENT DECISION, BOTH IN
TERMS OF THREAT AND IN TERMS OF ITS EFFECT ON THE ATTITUDE
OF ALLIES AND OTHER RELEVANT COUNTRIES, INCLUDING RUSSIA
AND CHINA, BUT THE PRECISE NATURE OF THE PROPOSAL REMAINED
UNCLEAR. AMBASSADOR SHERMAN WOULD BE TRAVELING TO MOSCOW
TOWARD THE END OF AUGUST TO PROBE THE RUSSIANS FURTHER AND
OFFER ADDITIONAL EXPLANATION OF THE U.S. POSITIOIN. THE
DEPUTY SECREARY NOTED THAT CLINTON AND PUTIN HAD HAD VERY
SUBSTANTIVE DISCUSSIONS IN OKINAWA ON STRATEGIC STABILITY
AND ON NON-PROLIFERATION ISSUES. PUTIN HAD ACKNOWLEDGED
RUSSIA'S EXPORT CONTROL PROBLEM, PARTICULARLY WITT REGARD
TO IRAN, AND PROMISED TO RECTIFY IT. THE TWO LEAADERS
WOULD FOLLOW.UP ON THESE ISSUES, AMONG OTHERS, A'T THE
MILLENIUM SUMMIT.

REMAINED "VERY FIRMLY 1N DOWNING STREET, " AND THE PRIME

ITAR WAIVER
6. ~ REGARDING ON-GOING U.S./UK DISCUSSIONS ON EXTENDING
ITAR LICENSING EXEMPTIONS TO THE UK, MEYER INSISTED HIS
SIDE WANTED TO MAKE PROGRESS QUICKLY AND ESTABLISH "REAL
CONTROLS THAT ARE SATISFACTORY TO YOU AND US;" BUT HE
EXPRESSED CONCERN THAT THE U.S. APPEARED INTENT ON
EXPORTING ITS WAY OF CONTROLLING TRADE IN SENSITIVE ITEMS
ACROSS. THE ATLANTIC. HE WAS ESPECIALLY CONCERNED, HE
SAID, THAT THE APPROACH TAKEN TO DATE BY THE U.S. IMPLIED
THAT NEW UK LEGISLATION WOULD BE REQUIRED. (COMMENT: A
KEY U.S. NEGOTIATING OBJECTIVE IS FOR THE UK TO ADOPT
CONTROLS ON THE "INTANGIBLE" TRANSFER OF CONTROLLED
DEFENSE TECHNOLOGY, E.G., THROUGH INTERNET OR EMAIL
TRANSMISSION. THE UK CURRENTLY LACKS LEGAL AUTHORITY TO
CONTROL SUCH TRANSFERS. END COMMENT.) U.S. PROPOSALS
WOULD ALSO PUT BIC BUREAUCTRIC OBSTACLES IN THE WAY OF UK
COMPANIES, MEYER CLAIMED. HE ASKED FOR THE U.S. TO ACCEPT
THAT THERE COULD BE "DIFFERENT WAYS OF SKINNING A CAT,"
I.E.THAT THE UK "CAN PROVIDE ADEQUATE PROTECTION IN OTHER
WAYS."
7. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT THE U.S. IS NOT
TRYING TO "CLONE" U.S. PRACTICES BUT ADDRESS OUR
SUBSTANTIVE CONCERN FOR CLOSING ANY GAP IN PRACTICES SO WE
CAN BE CERTAIN THAT CONTROL PROCEDURES IN THE UK PREVENT
UNDESIRABLE TRANSFERS OF TECHNOLOGY. TO MEYER'S COMMENT
THAT THE TWO SIDES SHOULD IN PRINCIPLE BE ABLE TO REACH
AGREEMENT QUICKLY, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SUGGESTED BOTH
SIDES WORK TO GET THEMSELVES, AS WITH ASCENSION (SEE
BELOW), FROM AGREEMENT IN PRINCIPLE TO AGREEMENT IN FACT.
BILATERAL ISSUES
8. ~ ON THE BERMUDA BASE CLEAN-UP ISSUE, MEYER SAID THE
UK IS ACTING AS AN "HONEST BROKER" IN REPRESENTING
BERMUDA'S CONCERNS. HE COMPLAINED THAT THE UK SIDE WAS
"GETTING ABSOLUTELY NOWHERE" WITH DOD AND IN FACT FELT IT
HAD BEEN "STRUNG ALONG," WITH DOD SUGGESTING CONGRESS WAS
THE PROBLEM WHEN THAT DID NOT APPEAR TO BE THE CASE. HE
CONTENDED THAT PRECEDENT WAS NOT A PROBLEM, SINCF THE
BASE, LIKE THOSE IN CANADA, DERIVED FROM THE LEND LEASE
AGREEMENT. WHAT HAD BEEN A "FIST-SIZE CLOUD" IN THE U.S.—
UK BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP A YEAR AGO HAD BECOME
DARKENING SKY~ WITH MP S NOW RAISING QUESTIONS WE
REALLY WANT TO MOVE THIS FORWARD."
9. ~ HE BROUGHT UP CLAIMS AGAINST THE U.S. FOR
MAINTENANCE OF LONGBIRD BRIDGE AS WEL'L, NOTING THAT "YOU
APPAR'NTLY DON'T DISPUTE THE OBLIGATION." MEYER SAID HE
WANTED TO SENSITIZE THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TO THE NEED FOR
THE U.S. TO REPAY THE BERMUDANS FOR THE MONEY THEY HAD
SPENT SO FAR ON "TEMPORARY REPAIRS."
