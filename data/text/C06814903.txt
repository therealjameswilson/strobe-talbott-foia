SUBJECT: KOSOVO: DEMARCHE IN LONDON ON FEBRUARY 18 NAC 
1. (U) CLASSIFIED BY: ROBERT BRADTKE, DEPUTY CHIEF OF 
MISSION, AMEMBASSY LONDON. REASON: 1.5(B) AND (D). 
2. .t..6:4 IN RESPONSE TO DCM'S DEMARCHE DRAWING ON REFTEL 
POINTS, FCO POLDIR JONES PARRY AGREED THAT NATO MUST BE 
PREPARED TO LAUNCH AIR STRIKES AFTER THE EXPIRATION OF THE 
TIMEFRAME ESTABLISHED FOR AGREEMENT ON AN INTERIM SETTLEMENT. 
THERE WAS NO QUESTION OF EXTENDING THE DEADLINE, ALTHOUGH THE 
CLOCK MIGHT BE STOPPED TEMPORARILY SHOULD THERE BE SUFFICIENT 
PROGRESS IN THE TALKS AT RAMBOUILLET ON SATURDAY TO INDICATE 
AN AGREEMENT WAS IMMINENT. 
3. 1-E4— REFERRING TO A RECENT CONVERSATION WITH THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY, JONES PARRY UNDERSCORED HMG STRONGLY BELIEVED THAT 
AIR STRIKES INTENDED TO PERSUADE MILOSEVIC TO ACCEPT A 
POLITICAL SETTLEMENT SHOULD BE DESIGNED WITH A PAUSE TO ALLOW 
MILOSEVIC TO COME TO THAT CONCLUSION. ACCORDINGLY, THE 
BRITISH POSITION WAS THAT THE NAC HAS AUTHORIZED SOLANA TO 
DECIDE ON THE UTILITY OF A PAUSE DURING OR AFTER THE FIRST 
LIMITED AIR RESPONSE AND BEFORE THE PHASED AIR CAMPAIGN. 
MINISTERS (COOK) WERE INTERESTED IN THE POSSIBILITY OF A BREAK 
EVEN DURING THE LIMITED AIR RESPONSE PHASE TO ENSURE THAT THE 
FIRST STRIKES DO NOT RULE OUT A MILOSEVIC RETURN TO THE TABLE: 
THE UK SAW IT AS "INDISPENSABLE" THAT THERE BE ROOM FOR A 
PAUSE AFTER THE FIRST SET OF STRIKES. THIS NEED NOT BE A 
FORMAL BREAK, BUT COULD BE AUTHORIZED BY THE SYG TO EXPLORE 
THE FRY'S WILLINGNESS TO ACCEPT THE AGREEMENT. SOLANA WOULD 
RETAIN THE AUTHORITY TO RESUME STRIKES WITHOUT NEEDING AN 
ADDITIONAL'NAC DECISION. 
4. -4.e*- JONES PARRY EMPHASIZED THAT THE DESIRE TO BUILD IN A 
PAUSE DID NOT REFLECT ANY LACK OF RESOLVE ON THE PART OF THE 
UK. HE AND HIS GOVERNMENT HAD MADE IT CLEAR TO MILOSEVIC AND 
THE SERBS THAT IF THEY DID NOT ACCEPT THE AGREEMENT ON OFFER 
AT RAMBOUILLET, BRITISH PLANES WOULD BE BOMBING THE FRY WITHIN 
THE NEXT 10 DAYS. BUT IT WAS IMPORTANT THAT WE KEEP CONTACT 
GROUP UNITY OF PURPOSE AND UNITY ON THE GOAL TO BE ADVANCED BY 
MILITARY MEANS. JONES PARRY REFERRED TO COHEN-ROBERTSON 
DISCUSSIONS OF 2/17 AND INDICATED HIS BELIEF THAT THE TWO HAD 
REACHED AN UNDERSTANDING ABOUT BUILDING IN A "POLITICAL AS 
WELL AS A MILITARY ELEMENT" TO THE AIR STRIKES. 
5. ON EVACUATION PLANNING, THE BRITISH ARE PUTTING OUT A 
CONSULAR NOTICE TODAY (2/18) THAT BRITISH CITIZENS SHOULD NOT 
TRAVEL TO THE FRY AND THOSE IN COUNTRY SHOULD LEAVE BY 2/19. 
THE BRITISH PLAN TO BEGIN EVACUATING NON-ESSENTIAL PERSONNEL 
FROM THEIR BELGRADE MISSION TOMORROW (2/19). THE BRITISH HAVE 
TAKEN NO DECISION AS YET ABOUT CLOSING THEIR MISSION. THEY 
CONSIDER AT LEAST SOME KVM AS ESSENTIAL STAFF WHO SHOULD STAY 
UNTIL ESSENTIALS ARE PULLED OUT, ALTHOUGH THE DRAWDOWN OF KVM 
COULD START 2/19.
