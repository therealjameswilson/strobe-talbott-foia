1. ~ STEVE, ROSS, JOHN AND JIM: I APPRECIATE THE POINTS
ON STROBE'S DISCUSSIONS WITH GEM THAT YOU PROVIDED FOR MY
USE IN THE NAC FEBRUARY 2 (REF A). I'M ALSO GRATEFUL FOR
THE POINTS ON THE SECRETARY'S DISCUSSIONS WITH FM IVANOV
OF NATO-RUSSIA (REF B), AND THE READOUT ON THE ALBRIGHT-
PUTIN MEETING (REF C), WHICH WERE VERY MUCH WELCOMED BY
PERMREPS. WE ARE FIGHTING UPHILL BATTLES HERE ON SEVERAL
RUSSIA-BELATED ISSUES, INCLUDING NMD AND ABM. TIMELY
AND SUBSTANTIVE READOUTS OF HIGH-LEVEL U.S.-RUSSIAN
MEETINGS ON THESE ISSUES — AS WELL AS CHECHNYA AND THE
BALKANS — ARE VITAL IF WE ARE GOING TO CONVINCE ALLIES OF
THE EXISTENCE OF A CREDIBLE NMD THREAT AND OF THE NEED TO
TAKE MEASURES — IN CONSULTATION WITH THEM — TO PROVIDE
FOR LIMITED DEFENSE WITHIN AN ADAPTED ABM TREATY REGIME.
2. ( ) I URGE YOU ALL TO PROVIDE US HERE WITH AS MUCH
INFORMATION AS YOU CAN AS EARLY AS YOU CAN ON MEETINGS
JUST CONCLUDED IN MOSCOW AND FUTURE HIGH-LEVEL U.S.—
RUSSIA MEETINGS. OFFERING THIS INFORMATION IN THE NAC
HELPS US TO REINFORCE THAT SMALL AND LARGE ALLIES ARE
EQUALLY IMPORTANT TO US. WHEN THE TIME COMES FOR US TO
LINE UP POLITICAL SUPPORT FROM THEM ON NMD AND OTHER
ISSUES, WE WILL BE ABLE TO REMIND ALLIES THAT WE
CONSIDER THEM TO BE PARTNERS IN PURSUIT OF BROADER
SECURITY GOALS — FOREMOST AMONG THEM BEING OBJECTIVES
INVOLVING RUSSIA.
