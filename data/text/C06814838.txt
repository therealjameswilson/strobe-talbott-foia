SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND PRESIDENT MBEKI

CLASSIFIED BY DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT. REASON 1.5 (D) 
PRIORITY 
1. (U) SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT ON MAY 9 MET WITH 
SOUTH AFRICAN PRESIDENT THABO MBEKI IN HIS OFFICE AT STATE 
HOUSE IN PRETORIA FOR A FRANK, HOUR-LONG DISCUSSION THAT 
NOT ONLY INCLUDED THE VARIOUS ASPECTS OF SADC BUT ALSO THE 
COUNTRIES OF ANGOLA, SIERRA LEONE, ETHIOPIA/ERITREA, DROC, 
AND ZIMBABWE. ALSO PRESENT WERE SA DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER 
AZIZ PAHAD, SA CHIEF OF STAFF FRANK CHIKANE, MBEKI LEGAL 
ADVISOR MAJANKU GUMBI, AF ASSISTANT SECRETARY SUSAN RICE, 
AMBASSADOR LEWIS, DOD PDAS BERND MCCONNELL, AND DAS 
SCHNEIDMAN. END SUMMARY. 
2. (C)  PRESIDENT MBEKI, FOLLOWING SECRETARY TALBOTT'S 
OPENING, UNDERSCORED HIS APPRECIATION FOR THE U.S.HOLDING 
ITS SECOND FORUM WITH SADC. MBEKI SAID THAT SADC HAD 
SERIOUS STRUCTURAL PROBLEMS THAT NEEDED TO BE ADDRESSED, 
AS DID A NUMBER OF CAPACITY-RELATED ISSUES. AS AN 
EXAMPLE, ONE OF THE REASONS THAT THE SADC TRADE PROTOCOL, 
NOW SCHEDULED TO BE IMPLEMENTED ON SEPTEMBER 1, HAD BEEN 
SO DELAYED WAS THE NEED TO EXPLAIN TO OTHER COUNTRIES WHAT 
RULES-OF-ORIGIN ISSUES WERE ABOUT. THERE WAS ALSO THE 
NEED TO UNDERSTAND HOW LOWER TARIFFS WOULD IMPACT ON THE 
PRODUCTION OF MANUFACTURED GOODS IN SOUTH AFRICA. AS 
MBEKI PUT IT, "WE HAD TO GET THEM TO UNDERSTAND THEIR 
INTERESTS." MOREOVER, SADC WAS NOT GEARED TO ADDRESS 
TECHNICAL ISSUES. 
3. t%)...A SECOND EXAMPLE THAT MBEKI GAVE OF SADC'S 
STRUCTURAL WEAKNESS WAS A MARKETING PLAN THAT SOUTH AFRICA 
HAD DEVELOPED TO HIGHLIGHT THE REGION AS A TOURIST 
DESTINATION. WITH SOME DISMAY, MBEKI SAID THAT IT WAS 
UNLIKELY THAT THE GOVERNMENTS IN THE REGION, OR SADC, 
WOULD DO WHAT WAS NECESSARY TO TAKE THE PLAN FORWARD. 
SUMMARIZING, MBEKI COMMENTED THAT THE SADC'S STRUCTURE WAS 
PROBLEMATIC WHEN A COUNTRY LIKE ANGOLA WAS IN CHARGE OF 
THE ENERGY SECTOR BUT HAD NOT CALLED A MEETING OF ENERGY 
MINISTERS IN EIGHT YEARS. SIMILARLY, SWAZILAND HAD DONE 
LITTLE TO MOVE FORWARD ITS AGENDA FOR HUMAN RESOURCE 
DEVELOPMENT. AT THAT POINT, MBEKI URGED DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT WHILE IN MAPUTO TO ENCOURAGE SADC TO ADDRESS 
SERIOUSLY THE ISSUE OF RESTRUCTURING. HE SAID THAT 
REGIONAL INTEGRATION WAS CRITICALLY NEEDED. IN 
CONCLUSION, THE U.S. AND SOUTH AFRICA SHOULD PUSH TO 
STRENGTHEN SADC. 
4. l'.5)..,AT THAT POINT, THE CONVERSATION TURNED TO ZIMBABWE, 
AND MBEKI SAID HE HAD TALKED TO SEVERAL OF THE ZIMBABWEAN 
DELEGATES WHO HAD BEEN IN LONDON AND INDICATED THE MEETING 
HADN'T PRODUCED MUCH. THE KEY POINT FOR THE ZIMBABWEANS 
WAS WHEN THEY ASKED IF THE BRITISH HAD ANY MONEY TO PAY 
FOR LAND REFORM, AND FOREIGN SECRETARY ROBIN COOK SAID 
"NO." THE ISSUE, IN MBEKI'S UNERSTANDING, REVOLVES AROUND 
118 FARMS THE GOVERNMENT WOULD LIKE TO KEEP THAT WOULD 
REQUIRE 9 MILLION POUNDS. IN MBEKI'S VIEW, IT WAS 
IMPORTANT TO GET THAT MONEY. AS HE PUT IT, "IF THE 
BRITISH SAID YES, IT WOULD SOLVE THE PROBLEM." A/S RICE
