JOAN, WINSTON AND I WENT TO BANGKOK WITH THREE BROAD 
OBJECTIVES: 
TO REPRESENT YOU AT THE ANNUAL ASEAN 
POST-MINISTERIAL CONFERENCE AND HELP LAUNCH THE 
ASEAN REGIONAL FORUM, A PROMISING NEW INSTITUTION 
OF THE POST-COLD WAR ORDER; 
TO REAFFIRM THE THEMES OF THE PRESIDENT'S VISION OF 
A NEW PACIFIC COMMUNITY, THUS HELPING TO PREPARE 
THE-WAY FOR HIS VISIT TO INDONESIA IN NOVEMBER FOR 
THE APEC SUMMIT; 
TO PRESS SPECIFIC GOALS IN BILATERAL SESSIONS WITH 
OTHER DELEGATIONS. 
DURING OUR VISIT, WE PLAYED ON OUR TWO MAJOR ASSETS: 
OUR MILITARY AND DIPLOMATIC PRESENCE IN THE AREA, WHICEJIS 
CENTRAL FOR ASIAN SECURITY, AND OUR MARKET, WHICH IS CENTRAL 
TO ASIAN PROSPERITY. WE USED THE OPPORTUNITY TO AFFIRM OUR 
STAKES AND OUR STAYING POWER IN THE WORLD'S MOST DYNAMIC 
REGION. 
WE FOUND EVIDENCE .OF MOMENTUM FROM A SERIES OF POSITIVE 
DEVELOPMENTS IN THE PAST TWO MONTHS -- MFN RENEWAL WITH 
CHOA,-THE THIRD ROUND WITH NORTH KOREA, PROGRESS WITH

ASEAN REGIOML FORUM 
IN YOUR RECENT APPEARANCE BEFORE THE SFRC, YOU 
EMPHASIZED, AS A MAJOR THEME OF THE CLINTON FOREIGN POLICY, 
THE ESTABLISHMENT OF NEW, POST-COLD WAR INSTITUTIONS THAT 
WILL REINFORCE THOSE THAT HAVE SERVED US WELL DURING THE 
POST-WORLD WAR II ERA. THE ASEAN REGIONAL FORUM IS AN 
EXCELLENT EXAMPLE. IT BRINGS TOGETHER A NUMBER OF POWERS 
THAT HAVE, IN VARIOUS COMBINATIONS, BEEN ANTAGONISTS IN THE 
NOT TOO DISTANT PAST: THE U.S., CHINA, VIETNAM, AND RUSSIA, 
THERE WAS A PALPABLE SENSE BOTH OF OPPORTUNITY AND OF THE 
NEED TO BUILD CONFIDENCE IN ORDER TO AVERT CONFLICTS DOWN 
THE ROAD. THE INFORMAL ATMOSPHERICS WERE SURPRISINGLY GOOD 
AND THE CONVERSATION GENERALLY SPONTANEOUS. PREDICTABLY, 
CONSENSUS ON SOME ISSUES DID NOT COME EASILY, BUT THE GROUP 
NONETHELESS CLOSED RANKS BEHIND SOME STATEMENTS THAT ARE 
HELPFUL TO OUR POLICY GOALS. ON THE QUESTION OF HOW 
ADVENTUROUS AND OUTSPOKEN TO BE WHEN ITS SPEAKS IN CHORUS, 
THE FORUM IS ROUGHLY DIVIDED INTO TWO CAMPS: ON THE ONE HAND 
ARE THE ASEANS THEMSELVES, LED BY AUSTRALIA, PLUS THE U.S. 
AND KOREA, WHO FAVOR MOVING FORWARD BRISKLY; ON THE OTHER 
HAND ARE CHINA, VIETNAM AND TO SOME EXTENT RUSSIA, WHO ARE 
MORE CAUTIOUS AND SUSPICIOUS. THAT SAID, IT WAS NO SMALL 
ACCOMPLISHMENT TO PRODUCE AN AMBITIOUS WORK PROGRAM ON 
CONFIDENCE BUILDING MEASURES AND A BLUEPRINT FOR INFORMAL 
MEETINGS TO FLESH THEM OUT BETWEEN NOW AND NEXT YEAR'S 
MEETING IN BRUNEI. 
THE ASEAN REGIONAL FORUM HAS CONSIDERABLE LONG-TERM 
POTENTIAL. IT IS, TO BE SURE, BY NO MEANS AN ASIAN 
EQUIVALENT OF CSCE --WHICH IS WHAT GARETH EVANS AND OTHERS 
HAD CALLED FOR -- BUT IT POINTS IN THE GENERAL DIRECTION OF 
A REGIONAL INSTITUTION THAT WILL ENHANCE COLLECTIVE 
SECURITY. THERE IS MUCH TO BUILD ON HERE, AND OUR "DIALOGUE 
PARTNERS" ARE CLEARLY LOOKING TO US FOR IDEAS AND 
LEADERSHIP. JUST AS THE SEATTLE SUMMIT MAY BE SEEN IN 
RETROSPECT AS A MILESTONE ON REGIONAL ECONOMICS, SO TOO WE 
MAY LOOK BACK ON THIS INITIAL CONCLAVE OF THE A.R.F. AS THE 
CORNERSTONE OF A NEW SECURITY STRUCTURE IN ASIA. THE 
AMERICAN ROLE IN PUTTING IN PLACE THESE TWO BUILDING BLOCKS 
OF A PACIFIC COMMUNITY APEC AND A.R.F. -- MAY PROVE TO BE 
A SIGNIFICANT LEGACY OF THE CLINTON ADMINISTRATION.

KEY ISSUES 
IN THE POST-MINISTERIAL CONFERENCE PLENARIES AND A 
STEADY\STREAM OF BILATERALS AND PULL-ASIDES, WE GAINED SOME 
GROUND ON A NUMBER OF KEY ISSUES. 
EMU,: NON-PROLIFERATION GENERALLY, AND THE NORTH 
KOREAN NUCLEAR CHALLENGE IN PARTICULAR, LOOMED LARGE. 
THERE WAS UNIVERSAL APPRECIATION OF BOTH THE HIGH STAKES 
AND THE WISDOM OF OUR OWN COURSE, WHICH COMBINES PATIENT 
DIPLOMACY WITH A WILLINGNESS, IF NECESSARY, TO GO TO THE 
UN FOR SANCTIONS. WE USED THE CLOSED-DOOR AND PUBLIC 
EVENTS TO UNDERSCORE OUR SOLIDARITY WITH SOUTH KOREA AND 
JAPAN. WE SHOULD AUGMENT OUR EFFORTS TO KEEP ALL THE 
NATIONS OF THE REGION FULLY INFORMED AS WE MOVE THROUGH 
THE NEXT CRITICAL PHASE. 
BAEC: WE CONTINUED TO REGISTER OUR LOW-REY SKEPTICISM 
ABOUT MALAYSIA'S PROPOSAL FOR AN "EAST ASIAN ECONOMIC 
CAUCUS." WE MADE CLEAR THAT WE'RE NOT SO MUCH TRYING TO 
BLOCK THE FORMATION OF A SUBGROUPING WITHIN APEC AS WE 
ARE RAISING HONEST QUESTIONS ABOUT HOW THIS ENTITY WOULD 
WORK IN PRACTICE AND WHETHER IT MIGHT BECOME AN 
EXCLUSIONARY TRADE BLOC, INIMICAL TO THE INTERESTS OF 
THE U.S. AND TO THE CAUSE OF GLOBAL FREE TRADE. WITHOUT 
SQUANDERING THE GOODWILL FROM THE PRESIDENT'S MEETING 
WITH MAHATHIR, WE MANAGED, WITH A LITTLE HELP FROM OUR 
FRIENDS, TO APPLY THE BRAKES TO THIS JUGGERNAUT. THE 
JAPANESE, KOREAN, AND (SURPRISINGLY) CHINESE MINISTERS 
MADE CLEAR TO THEIR ASEAN HOSTS THAT OTHER NATIONS 
(I.E., AUSTRALIA AND U.S.) MUST BE MADE COMFORTABLE WITH 
THE EAEC IF THE IDEA IS GOING TO GO ANYWHERE WITHIN 
APEC. AT SOME POINT, I SUSPECT WE WILL NEED TO SETTLE 
ON A RELATIVELY BENIGN DEFINITION OF THE CAUCUS AND 
COUNT ON OUR FRIENDS WHO ARE AT THE TABLE WITH MALAYSIA 
ON THIS ISSUE -- PARTICULARLY JAPAN -- TO LOOK OUT FOR 
OUR INTERESTS. 
CALWRIA: THE ABORTIVE COUP AND OUTLAWING OF THE KHMER 
ROUGE MAY HAVE ENERGIZED THE CAMBODIAN COALITION. THESE 
EVENTS CERTAINLY CALLED FORTH PLENTY OF SUPPORT -- IN 
THE FORM OF ECONOMIC, HUMANITARIAN, AND DIPLOMATIC 
BACKING -- AT OUR MEETINGS FOR THE PHNOM PENH 
GOVERNMENT. THAT SUPPORT, HOWEVER, IS NOT UNQUALIFIED: 
THE CAMBODIANS WILL HAVE TO UNDERTAKE REFORM OF THE ARMY 
IF THEY ARE GOING TO GET MUCH IN THE WAY OF MILITARY 
ASSISTANCE. WE SHOULD STEP UP OUR COORDINATION IN PHNOM 
PENH WITH THE AUSTRALIANS, MALAYSIANS, FRENCH, AND 
OTHERS. WE MADE SURE THAT THE THAIS UNDERSTAND HOW 
IMPORTANT IT IS FOR THEM TO STOP ANY ACTIVITY BY THEIR 
MILITARY THAT COULD BE CONSTRUED AS SUPPORT FOR THE 
KHMER ROUGE.

BOTH CHINA SU: HOTLY DISPUTED TERRITORIAL CLAIMS, 
RICH RESOURCES, AND CHINA'S PROPENSITY FOR THROWING ITS 
WEIGHT AROUND COMBINE TO MAKE THE SPRATLEY ISLANDS ISSUE 
POTENTIALLY EXPLOSIVE. BEIJING MOVED TO DEFUSE THE 
ISSUE'SOMEWHAT ON THE EVE OF THE CONFERENCE BY AGREEING 
TO TALK TO VIETNAM, BUT THE SOUTHEAST ASIAN NATIONS ARE 
CLEARLY WORRIED ABOUT CHINA'S TOUGH BEHAVIOR, AMONG 
OTHER THINGS BECAUSE IT IS SEEN AS A HARBINGER OF 
BEIJING'S INCREASING ASSERTIVENESS ON OTHER FRONTS. I 
MADE CLEAR OUR NEUTRALITY ON THE CLAIMS THEMSELVES BUT 
ALSO STRESSED OUR STRONG OPPOSITION TO THE USE OF FORCE 
AND OUR WILLINGNESS TO BE HELPFUL. WE HAVE LEVERAGE 
HERE WHICH WE SHOULD USE. WE ALSO HAVE SEVERAL LAYERS 
OF INTEREST IN AVOIDING CONFLICT, SINCE OUR OWN 
COMPANIES ARE INVOLVED IN DRILLING PROJECTS IN THE AREA. 
BURMA: THIS IS ONE ISSUE WHERE WE AND OUR ASIAN 
PARTNERS ARE AT ODDS. WE ARE HOLDING TO THE LINE THAT 
BURMA'S REPRESSIVE REGIME HAS ISOLATED ITSELF AND IT'
MUST LOOSEN UP BEFORE IT CAN BE WELCOMED INTO THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY THE ASEANS PREFER 
"CONSTRUCTIVE ENGAGEMENT." IN A CLEAR SHOT ACROSS OUR 
BOW, ASEAN, LED BY HOST THAILAND, INVITED THE BURMESE 
FOREIGN MINISTER TO BE A GUEST AT ITS MINISTERIAL 
MEETING, WE WERE QUITE LONELY IN ARGUING THAT BURMA 
SHOULD MAKE MORE THAN COSMETIC GESTURES BEFORE IT IS 
WELCOMED AT SUCH GATHERINGS. THIS SPECIFIC DISAGREEMENT 
BETWEEN US AND ASEAN SYMBOLIZES A LARGER ONE: OUR 
EMPHASIS ON HUMAN RIGHTS VERSUS THEIR INSISTENCE THAT WE 
SHOULD BE MORE RESPECTFUL OF ASIAN "UNIQUENESS" AND 
SOVEREIGNTY, AND LET THEM HANDLE SUCH ISSUES IN THEIR 
OWN WAY. THIS TENSION WILL BE JUST BELOW THE SURFACE OF 
THE PRESIDENT'S APEC TRIP IN NOVEMBER. INDEED, IF 
INDONESIA CONTINUES TO BAN MAGAZINES AND PLAY ROUGH ON 
EAST TIMOR, THE TENSION COULD BE VERY MUCH ON THE 
SURFACE. 
APEC: ASIDE FROM THAT POTENTIAL PROBLEM, PROSPECTS FOR , 
THE APEC MEETING ARE GOOD. THE EUPHORIA FROM THE SEATTLE 
HAS TRANSLATED INTO GENUINE ENTHUSIASM AND SOLID 
MOMENTUM TO MAKE APEC THE CORE OF THE REGION'S ECONOMIC 
FUTURE. SUHARTO IS PUSHING, FOR FREE TRADE IN THE AREA 
BY A DATE CERTAIN AGAINST THE DESIRES OF THE MORE 
CAUTIOUS AND NATIONALISTIC ALATAS. HE IS POSITIONED TO 
BE THE BEST ADVOCATE FOR BRINGING ALONG MORE CAUTIOUS 
ASIAN PARTNERS. WE WILL HAVE TO HOPE THAT HIS 
SELF-INTEREST IN THE SUCCESS OF THE EVENT WILL INDUCE 
RESTRAINT ON THE PART OF HIS GOVERNMENT IN THE 
PRESS-FREEDOM AND HUMAN-RIGHTS FIELDS BETWEEN NOW AND 
NOVEMBER.

OVERALL, I WAS IMPRESSED BY THE ASIAN MINISTERS AND THE 
QUALITY OF THEIR INTERVENTIONS. THIS IS A SELF-CONFIDENT 
GROUP WITH AN EASY CAMARADERIE, REFLECTING BOTH THE DYNAMISM 
OF THEIR NATIONS AND A GROWING SENSE OF A VIBRANT AND

THERE IS NO QUESTION YOU WERE SORELY MISSED, BUT THE 
DRAMATIC CNN COVERAGE OF THE RABIN-HUSSEIN VISIT TO 
WASHINGTON MADE CLEAR THAT YOU COULD HARDLY HAVE HAD A 
BETTER EXCUSE. AFTER I REPORTED ON YOUR BEHALF ABOUT THE 
MIDDLE EAST TO THE POST-MINISTERIAL CONFERENCE, A NUMBER OF 
YOUR COUNTERPARTS SPOKE UP TO EXPRESS THEIR GRATITUDE AND 
ADMIRATION FOR YOUR ROLE IN THE PEACE PROCESS. 
THE LOW POINT OF MY MISSION TO BANGKOK WAS 
UNQUESTIONABLY THE MUSICAL (AND I USE THE WORD LOOSELY) 
EVENT THE LAST NIGHT. I'M SORRY TO REPORT THAT OUR 
DELEGATION ACQUITTED ITSELF ABYSMALLY. DAVID LAMBERTSON, 
OUR VERY ABLE AMBASSADOR TO BANGKOK, AND HIS STAFF HAD 
COMPOSED A WITTY VARIATION OF "DOWN BY THE RIVERSIDE." 
HOWEVER, WE MANAGED TO BUTCHER IT BEYOND BELIEF IN 
PERFORMANCE, RENDERING THE FUNNY LINES INCOMPREHENSIVE AND 
THE TUNE UNRECOGNIZABLE. JUDGING FROM THE READING ON THE 
GROAN METER, WE TIED FOR LAST PLACE WITH THE KOREANS, WHO

AND THE COUNTRY'S -- THAT YOU WERE 11,000 MILES AWAY.
