1. ~ SUMMARY: ZIMBABWE WAS THE MOST EXTENSIVELY DISCUSSED
SUBJECT DURING A/S RICE'S APRIL 19 DISCUSSIONS WITH SENIOR
FOREICN AND COMMONWEALTH OFFICE (FCO) OFFICIALS (OTHER
SUBJECTS REF A). ON THE LAND QUESTION, FCO MINISTER OF STATE
PETER HAIN AND AFRICA DIRECTOR ANN GRANT STRESSED THAT HMG
WILL NOT PROVIDE ZIMBABWE WITH ANY FURTHER ASSISTANCE FCR
LAND REFORM UNTIL THE CURRENT WAVE OF FARM OCCUPATIONS ARE
BROUGHT TO AN END. THEY ADDED, HOWEVER, THAT HMG REMAINS
PREPARED TO HELP WITH LAND REFORM. THE UK OFFICIALS BELIEVE
THE GOZ WILL Go AHEAD WITH PARLIAMENTARY ELECTIONS THIS YEAR,
AND ARE CONSIDERING SENDING BRITISH MONITORS, BUT REMAIN
SKEPTICAL ABOUT HOW FAIR THE ELECTIONS WILL BE. HAIN AND A/S
RICE DISAGREED ON THE QUESTION OF HOW MUCH CONTROL ZIMBABWE
PRESIDENT MUGABE RETAINS OVER THE WAR VETERANS, BUT AGREED
THAT THIS 'WAS A VERY WORRYING QUESTION. HAIN AND GRANT
EXPRESSED CONCERN FOR THE SAFETY OF OPPOSITION LEADER
TSVANGIRAI, BUT DENIED THAT HMG IS MAKING ANY SPECIAL PLANS
TO EVACUATE BRITISH NATIONALS FROM ZIMBABWE. THEY SAID
BRITAIN IS WORKING TO GET OTHER AFRICAN LEADERS, PARTICULARLY
NIGERIAN PRESIDENT OBASANJO, TO INTERVENE WITH MUGABE. END
SUMMARY.
2. ~ ON APRIL 19, AF ASSISTANT SECRETARY SUSAN E. RIC'",
SPECIAL ASSISTANT PHIL DROUIN AND AFRICA WATCHER (NOTETAKER)
HELD TWO BACK-TO-BACK MEETINGS WITH SENIOR UK OFFICIALS
DEALING WITH AFRICA. THIS CABLE REPORTS ON DISCUSSIONS
CONCERNING ZIMBABWE (OTHER SUBJECTS SEPTEL). THE FIRST
MEETING WAS HOSTED BY FCO MINISTER OF STATE PETER HAIN, AND
INCLUDED FCO AFRICA DIRECTOR ANN GRANT, AFRICA DEPARTMENT
(SOUTHERN) DEPUTY HEAD FRANCES MACLEOD AND DEPARTMENT FOR
INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT AFRICA COORDINATION DEPARTMENT HEAD
BARBARA KELLY. A SECOND MEETING WAS HOSTED BY GRANT, AND
ALSO II'JCLUDED MACLEOD AND FCO CONFLICT MANAGEMENT ADVISOR
MARK BOWDEN.
RELEASE IN FULL
IDECLASSIFIED
THE LAND QUESTION
3. ~ PETER HAIN BEGAN BY DISCUSSING HMG'S PLANNED APPROACH
TO THE LAND QUESTION IN THE FORTHCOMING UK-,GOZ MEETINGS. HE
SAID FOREIGN SECRETARY COOK HAD ALREADY MADE CLEAR THAT
BRITAIN WOULD NOT DISCUSS THE ISSUE WHILE THE ILLEGAL FAPA
OCCUPATIONS CONTINUED. ANN GRANT UNDERLINED THAT BRITAIN HAD
ALWAYS SUPPORTED LAND REFORM IN ZIMBABWE, AND HAD CONTRIBUTED
A CONSIDERABLE AMOUNT TO IT OVER THE YEARS. WHILE IT DID NOT
WANT TO BE SEEN AS REWARDING MUGABE FOR HIS CURRENT BEHAVIOR,
HMG REMAINED COMMITTED TO THE "SCRIPT" AGREED AT THE 1998
LAND CONFERENCE. IF THE GOZ COULD BE BROUGHT BACK TO THIS,
AND THE OCCUPATIONS ENDED, IT MIGHT BE POSSIBLE, AS A NEXT
STEP, TO LOOK INTO PUTTING-TOGETHER A MULTI-LATERAL PROGRAM
WITH "A LOT OF DONORS" TO LAUNCH A "SENSIBLE PROGRAM" OF LAND
REFORM. HAIN REITERATED THAT BRITAIN WOULD PROVIDE NO HELP
UNTIL THE CURRENT CAMPAIGN OF OCCUPATIONS AND INTIMIDATION
WAS ENDED.

4. ~ WHILE NOTING THAT U.S. FUNDS COULD NOT BE USED TO
PURCHASE LAND FOR REDISTRIBUTION, A/S RICE SAID THE USG
REMAINED WILLING TO HELP WITH OTHER ASPECTS OF LAND REFORM.
INDEED, SHE NOTED, BY STRESSING OUR WILLINGNESS TO HELP WITH
LAND REFORM WE WERE ACTUALLY INCREASING THE PRESSURE ON
MUGABE OVER HIS CURRENT POLICIES. THAT SAID, WE WERE NOT IN
A POSITION TO DO ANYTHING NEW UNTIL THE SITUATION ON THE
GROUND IN ZIMBABWE CHANGED, AND INDEED HAD ALREADY SUSPENDED
OUR $ 1 MILLION LAND SURVEY PROGRAM.
ELECTIONS
5. ~ HAIN NOTED THAT THE REAL REASON FOR THE CAMPAIGN OF
FARM OCCUPATIONS WAS NOT AN UPSURGE IN DEMAND FOR LAND
REFORM, BUT MUGABE'S EFFORT TO WIN THE FORTHCOMING
PARLIAMENTARY ELECTIONS. HE SAID HMG WAS CONSIDERING SENDING
OBSERVERS, AND NOTED THAT MUGABE HAD TOLD NIGERIAN PRESIDENT
OBASANJO THAT HE WOULD BE PREPARED TO ACCEPT OBSERVERS FROM
COMMONWEALTH COUNTRIES. NONETHELESS, HAIN WAS NOT AT ALL
CONVINCED THAT THEY WOULD DO MUCH GOOD, ESPECIALLY IF MUGABE
WAS PPEPARED TO RIG THE ELECTORAL REGISTER AND INTIMIDATE THE
OPPOSITION.
6. ~ A/S RICE ACCEPTED THAT THE ELECTORAL PROCESS IN
ZIMBABWE WAS LIKELY TO BE "DEEPLY FLAWED," BUT ARGUED THAT
HAVING OBSERVERS ON THE SCENE WOULD STILL HELP TO REDUCE THE
LEVELS OF VIOLENCE AND CHEATING, AND ENABLE US. TO HAVE A
CLEAR PICTURE OF WHAT WAS REALLY HAPPENING ON THE GROUND. WE
WERE THEREFORE BOTH TRAINING ZIMBABWEAN OBSERVERS AND
PREPARING TO FUND INTERNATIONAL MONITORS, INCLUDING
AMERICANS, AS WELL.
7. ~ A/S RICE ASKED WHAT THE FCO'S TAKE WAS ON WHEN THE
ZIMBABWEAN ELECTIONS TWOULD TAKE PLACE. GRANT SAID SHE
THOUGHT MUGABE WOULD PREFER THEM TO BE AS SOON AS POSSIBLE,
SINCE THE COUNTRY'S ECONOMIC PROBLEMS WOULD ONLY GET WORSE
WITH TIME. SHE BELIEVED, HOWEVER, THAT HE WAS PREPARED TO
WAIT UNTIL THE DELIMITATION COMMISSION FINISHED ITS WORK
BEFORE PROCEEDING ~ WH ICH WOULD MEAN ELECTIONS NO SOONER THAN
JULY. MACLEOD SAID HMG HAD AVOIDED COMMENT ON THE DATE
QUESTION SO FAR SO AS TO AVOID MAKING IT YET ANOTHER ISSUE
BETWEEN THE UK AND THE GOZ; SHE NOTED THAT MUGABE WAS STILL
WELL WITHIN THE PROVISIONS OF THE ZIMBABWE CONSTITUTION ON
THIS SCORE.
READING THE POLITICAL SITUATION
8. +} A/S RICE ASKED WHETHER THE BRITISH THOUGHT THERE WERE
SPLITS WITHIN ZANU-PF. HAIN THOUGHT THERE HAD BEEN EVID'NCE
OF CONSIDERABLE DISSATISFACTION FOLLOWING THE DEFEAT OF THE
REFERENDUM, BUT DOUBTED THAT THIS WOULD LEAD TO A REAL
DIVISION, GIVEN HOW TIGHT AN ORGANIZATION ZANU WAS, AND HOW
MUCH MUGABE STILL DOMINATED THE PARTY. .THAT MIGHT CHANGE IN
THE FUTURE, ESPECIALLY IF THE PARLIAMENTARY ELECTIONS WERE
CLOSE. RICE AGREED THAT MUGABE RETAINED A TIGHT GRIP ON THE
PARTY AND THE POLICE'S FOR THE MILITARY'E JUST DID NOT
KNOW.
9. ~( A/S RICE SAID IT REMAINED UNCLEAR HOW MUCH CONTROL
MUGABE HAD OVER THE WAR VETERANS ORGANIZATION. HE HAD
CLEARLY INSTIGATED THEIR ACTIONS IN THE FIRST PLACE, BUT IT
WAS FAR FROM CERTAIN THAT HE COULD STILL CONTROL THEM; THIS
WAS THE MOST FRIGHTENING SCENARIO. HAIN DISAGREED, ARGUING
THAT, IF MUGABE SAID "STOP," THEY WOULD STOP. HE ALSO
BELIEVED THAT THE CAMPAIGN OF INTIMIDATION AGAINST THE
OPPOSITION WAS BEING ORCHESTRATED FROM THE TOP.

10. ~ HAIN SAID HE WAS PARTICULARLY CONCERNED FOR THE
SAFETY OF OPPOSITION LEADER MORGAN TSVANGIRAI; HE ONLY HAD
ONE S CURITY GUARD, AND HIS FORMER DRIVER HAD BEEN MURDERED
OVER THE WEEKEND. GRANT COMMENTED THAT THE ONLY PRACTICAL
SOLUTION IN THE SHORT TERM MIGHT BE FOR TSVANGIRAI'S BACKERS
INSIDE ZIMBABWE TO GET HIM MORE SECURITY. A/S RICE SAID SHE
BELIEVED THE BEST WAY'OR OUTSIDERS TO HELP PROTECT
TSVANGIRAI WAS TO GIVE HIM AS MUCH PROMINENCE AS POSSIBLE.
SHE AGREED THAT THIS WAS "NO SILVER BULLET," AND THOUGHT WE
MIGHT ALSO'RY TO "HAVE A PRIVATE DIALOGUE" WITH SOME OF THE
MORE REASONABLE PEOPLE WITHIN ZANU-PF ABOUT HOW UNACCEPTABLE
ANY MOVE TO HARM TSVANGIRAI WOULD BE.
11. ~DESPITE CONCERNS ABOUT THE SECURITY SITUATION IN
ZIMBABWE, GRANT DISMISSED HYPERBOLIC PRESS REPORTS THAT HMG
WAS MAKING PLANS TO EVACUATE UP TO 20,000,0FITS NATIONALS
FROM THE COUNTRY. AS IN MANY PLACES, BRITAIN HAD PLANS. FOR
PROTECTING ITS CITIZENS SHOULD THE NEED ARISE, BUT IT HAD NO
EXPECTATION THAT ANY SUCH EVACUATION WAS LIKELY. IN ANY
CASE, SHE ADDED, HMG HAD NO SCENARIO FOR MOUNTING AN
INTERVENTION BY ITS FORCES INTO ZIMBABWE.
NEXT STEPS
12. ~ HAIN SAID HMG RECOGNIZED THAT DIRECT BRITISH
PRESSURE ON ZIMBABWE WOULD ONLY PLAY INTO MUGABE'S HANDS, AND
MORE THAN LIKELY MAKE MATTERS WORSE FOR THE OPPOSITION. HE
BELIEVED A BETTER APPROACH WOULD BE TO TRY TO MOBILIZE
INTERNATIONAL OPINION, ESPECIALLY AFRICAN OPINION, TO
INTERVENE WITH MUGABE. FOR THIS REASON, FORSEC COOK HAD
ASKED NIGERIAN PRESIDENT OBASANJO TO SPEAK TO MUGABE, AND HAD
SOUGHT THE HELP OF UNSYG ANNAN AS WELL. ANNAN SUBSEQUENTLY
PHONED MUGABE, AND OBASANJO HAD SENT FOREIGN MINISTER LAMIDO
TO SPEAK TO HIM. HE ASKED FOR A/S RICE'S VIEWS ON WHOM ELSE
HMG MIGHT APPROACH FOR HELP IN INFLUENCING MUGABE.
13.~ A/S RICE AGREED WITH THIS GENERAL IDEA, BUT
CAUTIONED THAT MUGABE WAS UNLIKELY TO GIVE MUCH HEED TO
ANYONE OTHER THAN A HEAD OF STATE LIKE HIMSELF'REFERABLY
SOMEON:" OF HIS OWN GENERATION. HE HAD GOOD RELATIONS WITH
OBASAN JO~ BUT WAS MUCH LESS LIKELY TO BE SWAYED BY LANI DO
AS FOR OTHER POSSIBILITIES, SHE SUGGESTED MOZAMBIQUE
PRESIDENT CHISSANO, WHO HAD A LONG-ESTABLISHED RELATIONSHIP
WITH MUGABE. HAIN ASKED ABOUT SOUTH AFRICAN PRESIDENT MBEKI,
WHO WAS DUE TO VISIT LONDON IN MAY. RICE BELIEVED MBEKI WAS
VERY CONCERNED BY DEVELOPMENTS IN ZIMBABWE, BUT WAS NOT
CERTAIN WHETHER MUGABE WOULD LISTEN TO HIM.
14. ~ THIS MESSAGE WAS CLEARED BY A/S RICE.
