SUMMARY 
2. PAKISTANI PRES/DENT LEGHARI TOLD DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT 
APR/L 9 THAT THE U.S. SHOULD TAKE INTO ACCOUNT PAKISTAN'S 
"GENU/NE FEARS" OF INDIA IN FORMULATING ITS NON-PROLIFERATION 
STRATEGY. PAKISTAN, HE ARGUED, CAN PLAY A POSITIVE ROLE IN 
SOUTH ASIK, THE MIDDLE EAST, AND CENTRAL ASIA. TALBOTT SAID 
THAT PAKISTAN IS IMPORTANT TO THE U.S. FIRST BECAUSE IT IS AN 
EXAMPLE OF AN "ISLAMIC DEMOCRACY" WHICH THE U.S. WANTS TO SEE 
SUCCEED, AND SECOND BECAUSE PAKISTAN IS CLOSE TO OTHER AREAS OF 
INTEREST OR CONCERN TO THE U.S.: IRAN, CHINA, CENTRAL ASIA. 
TALBOTT ASKED THAT PAKISTAN REASSERT PUBLICLY THAT IT IS 
NOT/NOT NOW PRODUCING FISSILE MATERIAL, BUT LEGHARI, THOUGHT 
SUCH A STATEMENT WOULD FOCUS UNDUE ATTENTION ON THE ISSUE. 
LEGHARI SAID PAKISTAN AND INDIA COULD WORK OUT A SOLUTION TO 
KASHMIR, BUT ONLY WITH U.S. "MEDIATION." 
3. LEGHARI ASKED THAT AID RESCISSIONS ON TWO PROJECTS IN 
PAKISTAN BE RECONSIDERED, BUT TALBOTT SAID THE U.S. HAS HAD TO 
MAKE DIFFICULT AND PA/NFUL DECISIONS FOLLOWING THE CALIFORNIA 
EARTHQUAKE. LEGHARI SAID THAT PAKISTAN UNDER PM BHUTTO IS 
REGAINING SOME OF THE POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC STABILITY LOST IN 
RECENT YEARS, BUT THAT THE SOCIAL SECTOR -- ESPECIALLY 
EDUCATION, HEALTH, AND WOMEN'S DEVELOPMENT -- LAG FAR BEHIND. 
END SUMMARY 
U.S.-PAX RELATIONS: "REDRESS WHATEVER WENT WRONG" 
4. PRESIDENT LEGHARI SAID THAT U.S.-PAKISTAN RELATIONS SHOULD 
BE VIEWED IN ALL THEIR ASPECTS AND IN HISTORICAL PERSPECTIVE, 
AND SHOULD NOT/NOT BE JUST BASED ON ONE OR TWO ISSUES. 
PAKISTAN AND THE U.S. IN THE PAST HAVE BEEN "THE MOST ALLIED OF 
ALLIES," AND PM BHUTTO AND THE CURRENT GOVERNMENT WANT TO 
REVIVE THOSE CLOSE TIES. "WE SHOULD REDRESS WHATEVER WENT 
WRONG BETWEEN THE U.S. AND PAKISTAN," HE DECLARED. 
INDO-PAK RELATIONS: "A CLEARER UNDERSTANDING OF OUR FEARS"

5. LEGHARI HOPED THAT THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WOULD GAIN A 
"CLEARER UNDERSTANDING OF OUR FEARS" ON HIS TRIP. KASHMIR, THE 
BIGGEST IRRITANT IN INDO-PAK RELATIONS, COULD BE OVERCOME 
THROUGH "MEDIATION," AS INDIA AND PAKISTAN HAVE SOLVED THE 
INDUS WATER DISPUTE AND THE RAN OF KUTCH PROBLEM IN THE PAST. 
THE U.S., AS THE "SOLE SUPERPOWER," WAS THE ONLY MEDIATOR FOR 
THE DISPUTE. THE U.S. HAS MORE LEVERAGE OVER INDIA THAN IT 
THINKS: INDIA SEEKS FOREIGN INVESTMENT, AND THE U.S. WILL BE A 
MAJOR SOURCE OF THAT INVESTMENT. 
NON-PROLIFERATION INITIATIVE 
6. LEGHARI SAID THAT THE GOP VIEWS THE USG'S NON-PROLIFERATION 
INITIATIVE AS A "GENUINE EFFORT TO IMPROVE U.S.-PAKISTAN 
RELATIONS" AND "TO PUT THE PRESSLER AMENDMENT BEHIND US." 
PAK/STAN'S VIEW OF NUCLEAR NON-PROLIFERATION, LEGHARI ARGUED, 
IS DIFFERENT FROM THAT OF THE U.S. PAKISTAN IS TOLD THAT IT 
MUST TAKE THE FIRST NON-PROLIFERATION STEP, THEN INDIA WILL BE 
ASKED TO FOLLOW SUIT. PAKISTAN, WHICH HAS FOUGHT THREE WARS 
AGAINST ITS MUCH LARGER NEIGHBOR AND SEEN ITSELF "RENT ASUNDER" 
(WHEN BANGLADESH SECEDED FROM PAKISTAN IN 1971), SEES THE 
PROBLEM THE OTHER WAY AROUND. MANY PAKISTANIS "FEAR THAT OUR 
VERY EXISTENCE WILL BE SNUFFED OUT" BY IND/A. "WE HAVE GENUINE 
FEARS, AND THOSE FEARS HAVE TO BE ADDRESSED." PAKISTAN AGREES 
FULLY WITH THE U.S. GOALS OF NON-PROLIFERATION, BUT ASKS THAT 
ITS SECURITY CONCERNS BE TAKEN-INTO ACCOUNT BY THE LEADERSHIP 
OF BOTH THE U.S. ADMINISTRATION AND THE CONGRESS. 
7. TALBOTT SAID HE HOPED DURING HIS-VISIT TO PUT IN PLACE A _ 
PROCESS WHICH WOULD INVOLVE QUIET DIPLOMACY TO TAKE THE 
NON-PROLIFERATION DISCUSSION OUT OF THE LIMELIGHT. GETTING THE 
U.S.-PAKISTAN RELATIONSHIP PAST THE CURRENT IMPASSE, HOWEVER, 
WOULD LIKELY REQUIRE MORE TIME AND CAREFUL DISCUSSION. TALBOTT 
SAID THAT HE ACCEPTED PAKISTAN CHIEF OF ARMY STAFF GEN. ABDUL 
WAHEED'S ARGUMENT THAT PAKISTAN MUST BE LEFT WITH SOME DEGREE 
OF AMBIGUITY ABOUT THE EXACT STATUS OF ITS NUCLEAR PROGRAM AS A 
DETERRENT TO INDIA. THE USG UNDERSTANDS THE DOMESTIC 
SENSITIVITIES OF THE NUCLEAR-ISSUE IN PAKISTAN, AND DOES NOT 
WANT TO DO ANYTHING TO WEAKEN THE BHUTTO GOVERNMENT. THE U.S. 
IN THIS INITIATIVE IS NOT ASKING PAKISTAN, AT THIS POINT, TO 
ROLL BACK ITS NUCLEAR PROGRAM. THE U.S. IS ASKING PAKISTAN TO 
CAP ITS PRODUCTION OF FISSILE MATERIAL TO GET PAST THE CURRENT 
IMPASSE IN OUR RELATIONS AND MOVE FORWARD NON-PROLIFERATION 
GOALS. 
GOP SHOULD REASSERT NO PRODUCTION OF FISSILE MATERIAL.
