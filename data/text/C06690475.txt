THE ASIA SOCIETY January 27, 1994 
RELEASE IN FULL 
The Honorable 
Strobe Talbot 
Deputy Secretary of State 
United States Department of State 
Washington DC 20520 
Dear Secretary Talbot: 
I am honored to invite you and Mrs. Shearer to be guests at the 
seventh annual dinner, Asia in Washington, of the Washington 
Center of The Asia Society, to be held Thursday, April 14, 1994.-
The diplomatic host of this year's event will be His Excellency 
Li Daoyu, Ambassador of the People's Republic of China. 
As in previous years, we are inviting other Ambassadors from 
Asian and Pacific countries and U.S. government officials with 
U.S. Asia responsibilities to be guests of The Asia Society. 
Together with Asia Society members and a large Washington 
corporate audience the attendance is expected to be 350 - 400 
persons. 
The black tie dinner will be held at the ANA Hotel, 2401 M 
Street, N.W., Washington,D.C. with a reception at 7 p.m. and 
dinner at 8 p.m. Asia in Washington, promises to be a relaxed 
evening with entertainment, dinner favors and remarks from 
special guests. 
Our annual dinner is the most important event in our program 
year, and your attendance means a great deal to us. Proceeds 
from this dinner support the more than fifty programs about Asia 
which are presented by the Washington Center throughout the year. 
We hope very much that you will join us. Joan Hubbard will be 
calling you in the near future to confirm your presence at Asia 
in Washington. Our invitation card together with dinner tickets 
will be sent subsequently. 
Sincerely, 
Philip C. Jessup, Jr. 
Chairman, Advisory Committee
