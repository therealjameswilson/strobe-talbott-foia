3. DURING A BRIEF MEETING AT THE AIRPORT WITH PRESIDENT 
RAWLINGS OCTOBER 25, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT APOLOGIZED 
FOR THE LACK OF APPROPRIATE TREATMENT OF MRS. RAWLINGS 
WHEN SHE ARRIVED IN NEW YORK. ASKED FOR RAWLINGS' VIEWS 
ON COMMUNICATING WITH THE IVORIANS ON LIBERIA. RAWLINGS 
EXPRESSED INTEREST IN BEING HELPFUL TO PRESIDENT 
CLINTON'S POLICIES ABROAD AND STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF 
THE U.S. ACTING IN SUCH A WAY AS TO EVOKE RESPECT RATHER 
THAN FEAR, AND COOPERATING WITH OTHERS RATHER THAN BEING 
PERCEIVED AS IMPOSING AMERICAN WILL. HE ALSO NOTED THE 
IMPORTANCE OF TREATING AFRICANS AS EQUALS, END SUMMARY. 
MRS. RAWLINGS' VISIT 
4. IN A RARE GESTURE TO A FOREIGN VISITOR, PRESIDENT 
RAWLINGS SAW DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT OFF AT ACCRA 
AIRPORT OCTOBER 25. DURING A BRIEF MEETING BEFORE 
DEPARTURE, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY APOLOGIZED FOR THE LACK 
OF VIP TREATMENT FOR MRS. RAWLINGS WHEN SHE ARRIVED AT 
JFK AIRPORT FOR HER CURRENT VISIT TO THE U.S. THE HEAVY 
WORKLOAD OF PROTOCOL OFFICIALS WAS A PARTIAL EXRLANATION 
BUT NOT AN EXCUSE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT HE 
WOULD TAKE STEPS FOR THE USG TO MAKE AMENDS. 
U.S. INVOLVEMENT

WAS IMPORTANT FOR THE U.S. TO ACT IN THE WORLD IN SUCH A 
WAY AS TO BE LOVED AND RESPECTED, NOT REVILED OR 
FEARED. IN SO DOING, IT SHOULD WORK IN COOPERATION WITH 
OTHERS AND NOT BE PERCEIVED AS IMPOSING ITS WILL. IN
