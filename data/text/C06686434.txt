As you may know already, I am going to be talking over 
the editorial pages here at the end of the month, and wanted 
to begin with a splash in the first couple of weeks of 
January. 
If I were running foreign policy I might put up a sign 
saying "it's Russia; stupid," and I think we lose track of 
that with all the other, lesser, issues battering us for.

Any chance you would be willing to write an op-ed

My best to Brook.
