-em- SUBJECT: THE SITUATION IN PARAGUAY 
5. 46* THE ACTING SECRETARY TOLD THE ACTING FM HE WAS 
CALLING TO COORDINATE A RESPONSE TO THE TRAIN OF EVENTS 
IN ASUNCION. HE SAID HE HAD JUST SPOKEN TO THE 
SECRETARY IN DAMASCUS, FOR WHOM HE IS ACTING, AND WANTED 
TO DISCUSS THREE POINTS WITH THE ACTING FM. FIRST, HE 
UNDERSTOOD THAT THERE HAD BEEN SOME HYPOTHETICAL 
DISCUSSIONS ABOUT CLOSING THE BORDER WITH PARAGUAY. HE 
EXPRESSED THE USG'S HOPE THAT BRAZIL AND ARGENTINA WOULD 
DO SO NOW IN ORDER TO INCREASE PRESSURE ON OVIEDO AND 
HIS SUPPORTERS. 
6. {-64-- SECONDLY, THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE HOPED 
SOMEONE FROM THE GOB WOULD CONSIDER JOINING THE USG IN A 
BILATERAL OR TRILATERAL DELEGATION TO ASUNCION. HE SAID 
HE PLANNED TO SPEAK TO OAS SECRETARY GENERAL GAVIRIA 
TODAY, AND IF IT IS HIS ADVICE THAT SUCH A DELEGATION 
WOULD BE HELPFUL, THE USG PLANS TO DESIGNATE A 
PRESIDENTIAL ENVOY TO ASUNCION. THE USG DELEGATION • 
COULD STOP IN BRASILIA ON THE WAY TO ASUNCION AND PICK 
UP SOMEONE FROM THE GOB. 
7. -(-9-)- THE ACTING SECRETARY ADDED THAT IF THE 
DEMOCRATIC PROCESS IN PARAGUAY IS INTERRUPTED, IT WILL 
BE VERY IMPORTANT TO ISOLATE PARAGUAY, INCLUDING 
EXPELLING IT FROM MERCOSUL. HE SOLICITED THE ACTING 
FM'S REACTION AND ADVICE. 
8. --(-S* ACTING FM REGO RARROS REPLIED THAT AN IMNEDIATE 
BORDER CLOSURE WAS NOT A POSSIBILITY WHICH HAD OCCURRED 
TO HIM. HE PROMISED TO STUDY THE SUGGESTION. HE 
INFORMED THE ACTING SECRETARY THAT THE GOB HAD 
ESTABLISHED CONTACT WITH GENERAL OVIEDO, WHO HAD STUDIED 
IN BRAZIL AND HAD MANY FRIENDS THERE. THE ACTING El 
SAID THE GOB WAS TRYING TO CALM OVIEDO AND WAS URGING 
HIM TO ACCEPT THE PRESIDENT'S DECISION AND FIND AN 
INSTITUTIONAL SOLUTION. REGO BARROS SAID HE WOULD TAKE 
THE BORDER CLOSURE SUGGESTION TO THE PRESIDENT AND 
MEMBERS OF THE CABINET. 
9. -(131- REGARDING THE SUGGESTION OF A JOINT DELEGATION, 
THE ACTING FM SAID HE UNDERSTOOD OAS SYG GAVIRIA WAS TO 
ARRIVE IN ASUNCION TUESDAY AFTERNOON AND THE GOVERNMENTS 
OF BRAZIL, URUGUAY AND ARGENTINA WERE CONSIDERING 
SENDING A VERY HIGH-LEVEL MERCOSUL DELEGATION TO 
ASUNCION. HE SAID THE GOB BELIEVED MAKING AN APPROACH 
VIA MERCOSUL FIRST WOULD BE MOST HELPFUL. IT MIGHT 
LATER BE NECESSARY TO ESCALATE APPROACHES. 
10. 1-5)- NOTING THAT AMERICAN INTERVENTION SOMETIMES 
STIRRED STRONG FEELINGS, THE ACTING FM WARNED THAT IF 
BRAZIL JOINED A USG EFFORT IN THIS FIRST STAGE, IT MIGHT 
DIMINISH ITS POTENTIAL TO BE CONSTRUCTIVE. HE COMMENTED 
THAT BRAZIL AND PARAGUAY ARE NEIGHBORS WITH LOTS OF 
COMMUNICATION AND THE GOB HAD NOT YET EXHAUSTED THESE 
LOCAL POSSIBILITIES. HE ADVISED THAT A USG EFFORT COULD 
BE UNDERTAKEN IN A WAY TO GIVE MERCOSUL COUNTRIES SOME 
TIME TO MAKE AN INITIAL SEPARATE APPROACH. IF 
NECESSARY, THEY COULD JOIN THE USG AT A LATER STAGE. HE 
EMPHASIZED THE GOB WAS NOT RULING OUT PARTICIPATION IN A 
DELEGATION WITH A USG REPRESENTATIVE. 
11. 444.- REGARDING THE INTERRUPTION OF THE DEMOCRATIC 
PROCESS, REGO BARROS SAID HE HAD NO DOUBTS THAT BRAZIL 
WOULD ISOLATE PARAGUAY, ALTHOUGH SUCH ACTION WOULD BE 
VERY COSTLY. HE SAID A HALF-MILLION BRAZILIANS LIVE IN 
PARAGUAY AND THE PRESIDENT'S POSITION IS THAT BRAZIL 
CANNOT ACCEPT A NON-DEMOCRATIC PARAGUAY. THE GOB WILL 
DO EVERYTHING IT CAN, BILATERALLY, WITH MERCOSUL, AND 
WITH THE USG, TO RESOLVE THE SITUATION.

12. -Hi- THE ACTING SECRETARY THANKED THE ACTING FM FOR 
CONSIDERING HIS BORDER CLOSURE SUGGESTION. HE 
REITERATED HIS POINT THAT MAXIMUM TOUGHNESS NOW WILL 
INCREASE THE LIKELIHOOD OF SUCCESS AND OBVIATE TOUGHER 
MEASURES LATER WHICH COULD BE MORE COSTLY TO PARAGUAY'S 
NEIGHBORS. THE ACTING SECRETARY CONCURRED IN REGO 
BARROS' RECOMMENDATION CONCERNING SEPARATE DELEGATIONS. 
HE SAID THE U.S. WILL STRONGLY SUPPORT THE MERCOSUL 
DELEGATION AND SPEAK OUT PUBLICLY IN ITS FAVOR. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY ASKED HOW QUICKLY THE MERCOSUL PARTNERS 
COULD MOUNT SUCH A DELEGATION. 
13. -44- ACTING FM REGO BARROS SAID THE GOB MIGHT BE 
ABLE TO ANNOUNCE IT WITHIN AN HOUR. THEY CONTINUED TO 
MAKE CONTACTS WITH OVIEDO, WHO SAID HE WOULD RECEIVE A 
MERCOSUL DELEGATION. HE STRESSED IT WAS IMPORTANT TO 
GIVE OVIEDO SOME WAY OUT OF THE CURRENT SITUATION. 
14. 444- THE ACTING SECRETARY AGREED OVIEDO SHOULD BE A 
GIVEN AN EXIT AND SHOULD USE IT. NOTING THE USG HAS 
SUSPENDING MILITARY-TO-MILITARY ASSISTANCE TODAY, HE 
ASKED WHETHER A SEPARATE U.S. MILITARY ENVOY WOULD BE 
HELPFUL. 
15. -HH- REGO BARROS SAID HIS OFF-THE-CUFF REACTION WAS 
THAT A MILITARY ENVOY MIGHT BE MORE HELPFUL IN A SECOND 
STAGE. IT WOULD DEPEND ON MERCOSUL'S CAPACITY TO ACT 
PROMPTLY. IF SUCH ACTION WAS NOT PROMPT, THE NAMING OF 
A USG MILITARY ENVOY MIGHT PROVE USEFUL. IF THE GOB 
ANNOUNCED THE SENDING OF A MERCOSUL DELEGATION WITHIN 
THE NEXT SIXTY MINUTES, HOWEVER, HE HOPED THE USG COULD 
WAIT TO ANNOUNCE A DECISION TO SEND A MILITARY ENVOY. 
16. (S) THE ACTING SECRETARY AGREED. HE ASKED IF THE 
USG COULD DO ANYTHING HELPFUL TO SUPPORT THE MERCOSUL 
INITIATIVE. 
17. -4;4- THE ACTING FM RESPONDED THAT GOB MILITARY 
PERSONNEL INDICATED OVIEDO REACTED NEGATIVELY TO USG 
INVOLVEMENT. HE REFUSED TO RECEIVE THE BRAZILIAN 
AMBASSADOR IN ASUNCION BECAUSE HE FELT HE WAS MEDDLING 
IN BRAZIL'S INTERNAL AFFAIRS AND WORKING TOO CLOSELY 
WITH AMBASSADOR SERVICE. HE WARNED THAT BRAZIL MUST BE 
VERY SUBTLE IN ITS EFFORTS TO RESOLVE THE CURRENT 
CONFLICT. 
18. 464- THE ACTING SECRETARY NOTED THAT COOPERATION 
BETWEEN AMBASSADOR SERVICE AND THE BRAZILIAN AMBASSADOR 
WAS A GOOD THING, BUT ACCEPTED REGO BARROS' POINTS. 
19. 4E+ REGO BARROS REPEATED HIS REQUEST THAT THE 
MERCOSUL COUNTRIES BE GIVEN AN OPPORTUNITY TO RESOLVE 
THE CONFLICT, BUT RECOGNIZED THAT IT WAS IMPORTANT FOR 
THE USG TO REACT. SOME DELAY, HOWEVER, WOULD BE HELPFUL 
TO THE MERCOSUL INITIATIVE. 
20. -(-St THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE ACCEPTED REGO 
BARROS' POSITION AND THE GOB'S PROPOSED COURSE OF ACTION 
WAS CONSISTENT WITH USG HOPES. HE REQUESTED 
CLARIFICATION REGARDING WHAT MERCOSUL WOULD DO TO 
ISOLATE PARAGUAY IF THE CURRENT IMPASSE WAS NOT RESOLVED. 
21. 46+ REGO BARROS REPLIED THAT BRAZIL WOULD CALL FOR 
MERCOSUL TO SUSPEND PARAGUAY FROM THE TRADING GROUP AND 
SAID HE BELIEVED THAT WOULD BE THE URUGUAYAN POSITION AS 
WELL. HE STATED IT WOULD BE SIMPLY UNACCEPTABLE NOT TO 
DO THAT. RETURNING TO THE BORDER CLOSURE ISSUE, HE 
NOTED MOST PARAGUAYAN IMPORTS COME THROUGH BRAZILIAN 
PORTS AND IT WOULD NOT BE DIFFICULT "TO SUFFOCATE 
THEM." HE SAID HE HOPED IT WOULD NOT COME TO THAT, BUT 
THE GOB HAD ALREADY CONVEYED TO OVIEDO IT WAS PREPARED 
TO TAKE SUCH ACTION IF NECESSARY.
