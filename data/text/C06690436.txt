HOTEL AND TRAVEL 
The Third Global Structures Convocation is being held at the Hyatt Regency 
Crystal City at National Airport located near Washington, DC, USA at 2700 
Jefferson Davis Highway, Arlington, VA 22202. Room rates of $92 single or double 
occupancy and $112 triple or quadruple occupancy are available. Reservations 
may be made by calling (800) 233-1234 or by calling the hotel directly at (703) 
418-1234. Please be sure to request the Global Structures group rate to receive the 
discount. Reservations must be made by January 13, 1994. 
American Airlines is the official carrier of for the Third Global Structures 
Convocation Discounted airfares have been arranged. To make reservations please 
call (800) 433-1790 The group identification number is 0514FD. 
REGISTRATION 
Registration fees and sponsorship contributions provide less than half of 
the funds necessary to organize and produce the Global Structures 
Convocations, even though our budgets are very modest for the size of our 
conferences. We simply could not produce the Convocations without generous 
contributions and rnatching funds from Stanley and Martha Platt, WFA and 
many others.. 
This year we have added a Patron category to our registration fees for those 
individuals and organizations whose extra contributions will go to enabling 
the participation of international delegates, students, others with limited 
resources, speakers, and follow-up activity. 
Volunteer positions are available. Please contact.the Global Structures Ofhce 
for more information.
