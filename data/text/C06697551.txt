SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND DFM REGO BARROS 
MEET ON ECUADOR-PERU 
1. CLASSIFIED BY DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, DEPARTMENT 
OF STATE; REASON 1.5 (X-5). 
2.  L ENTIRE TEXT. 
3. SUMMARY: TALBOTT, DAVIDOW AND BABBITT MET JUNE 2 
WITH BRAZILIAN DFM SEBASTIAO REGO BARROS TO UNDERLINE 
USG INTEREST IN USING THE JUNE 18 BUENOS AIRES MEETING 
TO SET IN MOTION A DEFINITIVE SOLUTION TO THE 
ECUADOR-PERU BORDER DISPUTE. REGO BARROS EXPRESSED 
SKEPTICISM THAT A LAME-DUCK DURAN BALLEN GOVERNMENT 
WOULD BE ABLE TO UNDERTAKE BINDING COMMITMENTS OF THE 
SORT NECESSARY TO END THE DISPUTE AND AGREED TQ TAKE 
BACK TO BRASILIA BABBITT'S SUGGESTION OF USING THE OASGA 
TO ANNOUNCE THE EXTENSION OF MOMEP. TALBOTT EXPLAINED 
THE LARGER PEACEKEEPING EQUITIESTHAT OBLIGE US TO STICK 
WITH PLANS TO WITHDRAW U.S. LOGISTICAL SUPPORT FOR MOMEP 
AT THE END OF THE YEAR. END SUMMARY. 
4. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT OPENED THE MEETING BY 
NOTING CONTINUING HIGH-LEVEL USG INTEREST IN THE 
ECUADOR-PERU PEACE PROCESS AND EXPRESSING OUR STRONG 
HOPE THE FOREIGN MINISTERS•OF ECUADOR AND PERU WILL 
CAPITALIZE ON THE OPPORTUNITY PROVIDED BY THE UPCOMING 
BUENOS AIRES MEETING. REGO BARROS STATED THAT PROGRESS 
IN BUENOS AIRES IS UNLIKELY. THIS IS A CONFLICT WITH 
MORE THAN 100 YEARS OF HISTORY. INDEED, HE COMMENTED 
THAT TO A CERTAIN EXTENT THE GUARANTORS "ARE BECOMING 
HOSTAGES OF THE CONFLICT.'1 ANY PERMANENT SOLUTION, HE 
SUGGESTED, WILL REQUIRE A SEPARATE STATUS FOR THE 
DISPUTED TERRITORY -- PERHAPS A NATIONAL PARK OR SOME 
SPECIAL ECONOMIC PROJECT. 
5. ARA ACTING A/S DAVIDOW EXPLAINED OUR TACTIC OF USING 
DURAN BALLEN'S LAST WEEKS IN OFFICE TO LOCK-IN•A 
NEGOTIATING FRAMEWORK WHICH WILL BE ACCEPTED BY THE NEW 
ECUADOREAN GOVERNMENT. OTHERWISE, HE ADDED, THE FIRST 
MONTHS OF THE NEW PRESIDENT'S TERM WILL BE LOST AS FAR 
AS THE PEACE PiOCESS IS CONCERNED. REGO BARROS - 
EXPRESSED CONCERN THAT ANY SUBSTANTIVE INITIATIVE BY THE 
DURAN BALLEN GOVERNMENT WOULD BE REJECTED BY THE NEW 
ECUADOREAN ADMINISTRATION FOR POLITICAL REASONS. IN 
RESPONSE, DAVIDOW SAID WE ARE ENCOURAGING THE GOE TO 
UNDERTAKE INFORMAL CONVERSATIONS WITH NEBOT AND HIS 
RUNNING MATE TO AGREE ON PROCEDURES THAT WOULD HELP MOVE 
THE BORDER CONFLICT TOWARDS A SOLUTION. REGO BARROS 
RELEASE IN FULL

ARGUED THAT TO BE POLITICALLY SUSTAINABLE ANY PROCEDURE 
SHOULD BE FREE OF DURAN BALLEN'S FINGERPRINTS. 
6. OAS AMBASSADOR BABBITT RAISED'WITH REGO BARROS OUR 
IDEA OF ANNOUNCING THE EXTENSION OF MOMEP ON THE MARGINS 
OF THE OASGA IN ORDER TO BUILD MOMENTUM FOR BUENOS 
AIRES. REGO BARROS ASKED TO THINK ABOUT IT, AND LATER 
TOLD BABBITT HE WOULD REFER THE IDEA TO ITAMARATY FOR 
GUIDANCE. 
7. REGO BARROS EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT THE COINCIDENCE 
BETWEEN THE DECEMBER WITHDRAWAL OF U.S. MILITARY SUPPORT 
FOR MOMEP AND THE JANUARY ANNIVERSARY OF THE CONFLICT. 
TALBOTT EXPLAINED THE CONNECTION BETWEEN OUR WITHDRAWAL 
PLANS AND THE BROADER DEBATE OVER U.S. PARTICIPATION IN 
PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS. WITHOUT AN EXIT STRATEGY AND A 
DATE CERTAIN FOR TERMINATING OUR PARTICIPATION, HE 
NOTED, WE CANNOT DEFEND U.S. INVOLVEMENT IN ANY SUCH 
OPERATION. ALTHOUGH OUR ON THE GROUND LOGISTICAL ROLE 
WILL END IN DECEMBER, WE INTEND TO REMAIN ACTIVE 
PARTICIPANTS IN MOMEP. AS EVIDENCE OF OUR ONGOING 
ENGAGEMENT, TALBOTT CITED PRESIDENT CLINTON'S MAY 21 
MEETING WITH FUJIMORI. REGO BARROS WAS UNAWARE OF THE 
MAY 21 MEETING, AND ASKED HOW WE FOUND FUJIMORI'S 
ATTITUDE. DAVIDOW SAID FUJIMORI WAS GENERALLY 
CONSTRUCTIVE, BUT NOTED THE PERUVIANS ARE STILL SENDING 
MIXED MESSAGES. REGO BARROS REPORTED THAT HIS 
AMBASSADOR IN LIMA THINKS THE PERUVIANS ARE SERIOUS, BUT 
CAUTIONED THAT WE MUST ALL BE SURE THAT FUJIMORI WANTS 
TO FIND A SOLUTION. 
8. PARTICIPANTS IN THE JUNE 2 MEETING ON THE MARGINS OF 
THE OASGA IN PANAMA WERE TALBOTT, REGO BARROS, DAVIDOW, 
BABBITT AND D SPECIAL ASSISTANT PYATT.
