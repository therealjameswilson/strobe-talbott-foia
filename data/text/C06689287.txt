Dear Jim: 
Thanks very much for your kind note about the Round-
table lunch. It's I who should -- and do -- thank you for 
the chance to make the pitch for congressional support of 
our NIS policy. 
You ask me about others who might meet with your 
group from time to time: I'd urge you consider Tom Simons, 
our Assistance Coordinator (and former Ambassador to 
Poland); also, David Lipton, who is the chief specialist on 
Russia and the NIS for Treasury (a DAS under Larry Sum-
mers). There are several others who are going to be work-
ing with me on the crucial issue of how to preserve (or re-
store!) peace and stability around the periphery of the old 
USSR. If and when that subject seems compelling to your 
group, let me know and we'll make sure you've got the right 
person. 
Let's keep in touch. Your advice and-support-will be---
important to me as we proceed. 
Best regards, 
Strobe Talbott
