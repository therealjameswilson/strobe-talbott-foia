1. ~SUMMARY. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY MET WITH FRENCH FOREIGN
MINI STER HUBERT VEDRINE ON JULY 1 2 I 2000 IN MI YAZAKI ~ JAPAN
ON THE MARGINS OF THE G-8 FOREIGN MINISTERS'EETING. THE
FRENCH FOREIGN MINISTER BEGAN BY EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR
U.S. EFFORTS TO ADVANCE THE MIDDLE EAST PEACE PROCESS. THE
SITUATION IN RUSSIA DOMINATED THE DISCUSSION, ESPECIALLY
THE COMPATIBILITY OF PRESIDENT PUTIN'S EFFORTS TO FORGE A
STRONG STATE WITH WESTERN DEMOCRATIC VALUES AND HOW TO DEAL
WITH CHECHNYA. IN DISCUSSIONS ABOUT THE FORMER REPUBLIC OF
YUGOSLAVIA (FRY), THE TWO DISCUSSED THE POSSIBILITY OF
HOLDING TALKS AT THE UNITED NATIONS ABOUT THE LEGAL STATUS
OF THE FRY AND HOW TO TREAT MILOSEVIC'S CONSTITUTIONAL
CHANGES IN THE G-8 COMMUNIQUE. THE U.S. REQUESTED FRANCE'
SUPPORT AND ASSISTANCE REGARDING MAURITIUS CANDIDACY FOP.
THE UN SECURITY COUNCIL. END SUMMARY

MIDDLE EAST PEACE PROCESS
3.~ VEDRINE OPENED BY ASKING ABOUT THE MIDDLE EAST PEACE
PROCESS AND WHETHER THE DEPUTY SECRETARY HAD HEARD ANYTHING
FROM CAMP DAVID. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REPLIED THAT HE
HOPED HE WOULD HAVE SOME NEWS TO CONVEY BEFORE THE FOREIGN
MINISTERS MEETING FINISHED, BUT THAT IT WAS A DIFFICULT
PROCESS. VEDRINE NOTED THAT THE FRENCH WERE CONSCIOUS OF
THE DIFFICULTIES AND ADDED THAT FRANCE FELT BARAK SHOULD BE
MORE FLEXIBLE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT BARAK WAS
BALANCING A NUMBER OF COMPETING IMPERATIVES BUT EXPRESSED
HOPE THAT THE MOMENT HAD ARRIVED FOR BOLD ACTION ON ALL
SIDES.
MI LOSEV IC I THE FRY~ AND IVANOV
4. Q VEDRINE THEN STATED THAT HE WOULD LIKE TO DISCUSS
RUSSIA BUT THAT HE FIRST WAS WONDERING IF THE U.S. HAD ANY
SPECIFIC COMMENTS ON THE G-8 MEETING. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY
RAISED THE ISSUE OF THE COMMUNIQUE LANGUAGE ON YUGOSLAVIA,
NOTING THE URGENCY OF FINDING COMMON LANGUAGE WITH THE
RUSSIANS THAT WOULD CONDEMN MILOSEVIC'S LATEST
CONSTITUTIONAL MANIPULATIONS AND INDICATE SUPPORT FOR THE
MONTENEGRIN GOVERNMENT. TALBOTT SAID HIS NEXT MEETING

5. ~ VEDRINE AND ERRERA CONSULTED BRIEFLY, AND AGREED IT
WOULD BE-IMPORTANT TO SEND A UNITED MESSAGE TO MILOSEVIC.
ERRERA CONCLUDED THAT, IF NECESSARY, IT WOULD BE BETTER TO

CONCURRED~ SAYING THAT GOAL OF A FIRM~ UNITED G 8 MESSAGE
JUSTIFIED SOME CONCESSIONS BUT THE TONE WAS IMPORTANT: WE
SHOULDN'T "BE IN A TRANCE AND SHOW IMPOTENCE."
RUSSIA
6. ~ VEDRINE THEN TURNED TO HIS MAIN THEME, PRESIDENT
PUTIN AND PUTIN'S VIEW OF RUSSIAN'TATEHOOD. VEDRINE NOTED
THAT HE WANTED TO ELABORATE ON WHAT HE HAD SAID TO THE
SECRE'ARY IN A CALL ABOUT FRENCH SUPPORT FOR A "MODERN,
EFFECTIVE" RUSSIAN STATE. HIS CONCERN, VEDRINE SAID, WAS
THAT BY EMPHASIZING "DEREGULATION" AS A PRIORITY OF
RUSSIA'S TRANSITION, THE WEST HAD CONTRIBUTED TO
DISMANTLING REPRESSIVE CONTROLS WITHOUT ANYTHING TO REPLACE
THEM --AND CRIME AND CHAOS FOLLOWED. THE WEST MUST
ACKNOWLEDGE THAT RUSSIA ALSO NEEDS TO BE A
FUNCTIONING'TATE.
VEDRINE CLAIMED HE WAS CONCERNED THAT THE SECRETARY
INFERRED IN THIS ACKNOWLEDGEMENT A LICENSE FOR A MORE
REPRESSIVE RUSSIAN STATE.
7. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RESPONDED THAT THE U.S. UNDERSTOOD
THE FRENCH CONCEPT OF MODERN STATEHOOD, BUT THAT PUTIN'S
ABILITY TO DIFFERENTIATE BETWEEN STRONG LEADERSHIP AND
REPRESSION HAD YET TO BE FULLY TESTED. VEDRINE WAS
UNDETERRED, STRESSING AGAIN THAT THE IFI CONDITIONS IMPOSED
ON RUSSIA DO NOT SUFFICIENTLY TAKE INTO ACCOUNT THE NEED TO
ASSIST RUSSIA TO BECOME AN EFFECTIVE STATE BY BUILDING A
FUNCTIONING JUDICIARY, CUSTOMS SERVICE, AND EFFECTIVE TAX
SYSTEM. THE F'RENCH BELIEVE THIS SHOULD BE DISCUSSED IN THE
EU, IN THE G-7, AND IN THE IF'IS. TALBOTT AGREED THAT
ECONOMIC CONDITIONALITY DOES NOT MEET ALL THE NEEDS OF
RUSSIA, BUT WARNED THAT DEMOCRATIC VALUES MUST NOT BE
OVERLOOKED IN THE EFFORT TO BUILD AN EFFECTIVE MODERN
STATE: SOME ITEMS IN PRESIDENT PUTIN'S SPEECH TO THE DUMA,
TALBOTT COMMENTED, SOUNDED LIKE A SOCIAL CONTRACT
REQUESTING LATITUDE FOR A LITTLE REPRESSION NOW TO BE PAID
BACK WITH DEMOCRACY AND CIVIL SOCIETY LATER --AND GAVE
REASON FOR CAUTION.
CHECHNYA
8. ASKED BY VEDRINE FOR HIS VIEWS ON CHECHNYA, TALBOTT
SAID WE SAW NO SIGNS OF A CHANGE IN THE RUSSIAN APPROACH:
PUTIN WAS STILL RIDING THE TIGER. CRITICISM BY FRANCE, THE
U.S. AND OTHERS HAD LED HIM TO MODERATE HIS APPROACH, BUT
RUSSIA HAD YET TO LEARN THE LESSONS OF ITS EARLIER
ADVENTURES IN THE CAUCASUS, SPECIFICALLY GEORGIA/ABKHAZIA
IN 1993. VEDRINE FELT THAT MAYBE PUTIN UNDERSTOOD THE

THE LEGAL STATUS OF THE FRY AT THE UN
9. ((S4 DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT POINTED OUT THAT RUSSIA WANTS
TO BE BOTH MORE SELF-ASSERTIVE AND ALSO APPEAR TO WORK
COOPERATIVELY AND ELICIT RESPECT FROM THE INTERNATIONAL
COMMUNITY. HE NOTED THAT RUSSIA WAS STILL TRYING TO DEFEND
MILOSEVIC BUT THAT RUSSIA'S DESIRE TO BE PART OF THE
INTERNAT10NAL COMMUNITY MIGHT PROVIDE LEVERAGE FOR GETTING
MOSCOW TO BE TOUGHER WITH MILOSEVIC. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY
NEXT BROUGHT UP THE QUESTION OF THE LEGAL STATUS OF THE FRY
AT THE UN. ERRERA ASKED WHETHER WE WANT TO OUST THE FRY IN
NEW YORK. U/S PICKERING EXPLAINED THAT WE BELIEVE THERE
ARE GROUNDS TO REMOVE THE FRY REPRESENTATION, WHICH HAS NO
LEGAL STATUS AS THE SUCCESSOR STATE TO YUGOSLAVIA, BUT THAT
IT WAS A QUESTION FOR THE APPROPRIATE LEGAL EXPERTS TO
EXPLORE IN GREATER DETAIL. FRENCH UN REPRESENTATIVE
LEVI TTE ~ HOWEVER ~ WAS NOT ALLOWI NG EVEN PRELI MI NARY LEGAL
EXPLORATION TO PROCEED, HE SAID. ERRERA PROMISED HE WOULD
INSTRUCT LEVITTE NOT TO STAND IN THE WAY OF A PRELIMINARY
DISCUSSION, BUT WARNED THAT WE NEED TO BE VERY CAREFUL IN
THE WAY WE APPROACH THIS ISSUE.
MAURITIUS AS CANDIDATE FOR UN SECURITY COUNCIL SEAT
10. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED UNDER SECRETARY PICKERING
TO UPDATE FRANCE ON THE QUESTION OF THE CANDIDACY FOR THE
AFRICAN SLOT ON THE SECURITY COUNCIL. PICKERING RECOUNTED
THAT ON JULY 11, ALGERIA ATTEMPTED TO PUSH SUDAN'S
CANDIDACY THROUGH AT THE OAU SUMMIT MEETING, BUT THAT THIS
ATTEMPT WAS BLOCKED BY SOUTH AFRICAN PRESIDENT MBECKI,
JOINED BY THE SENEGALESE AND THE NIGERIANS. PICKERING
STRESSED THAT NONE OF US SHOULD WANT A VIOLATOR OF UN
SANCTIONS IN THE SECURITY COUNCIL. VEDRINE STATED THAT HE
BELIEVED THE UN CHARTER WAS MUTE ON THE ISSUE; PICKERING
COUNTERED THAT THE CHARTER DOES REQUIRE THAT STATES BE
QUALIFIED. HE REQUESTED FRENCH ASSISTANCE IN SUPPORTING
MAURITIUS'ANDIDACY, AND NOTED THAT MAURITIUSS STATUS AS
A COMMONWEALTH AND A FRANCOPHONE COUNTRY STRENGTHENED ITS
CANDIDACY. VEDRINE SAID HE WOULD CONSIDER THE REQUEST.
