RELEASE IN FULL 
Dear Bob; 
I was deeply touched by your letter and am sorry not to have 
been more prompt in replying. I never expected, nor aspired to, 
a job like the one I'm now leaving, much less the one into which 
(Senate permitting) I'm heading. But now that I've had this 
work land on me, I do find myself specially grateful to those 
friends I've had over the years who set the standard for public 
service --and you're certainly in the front rank of those. 
I hope, from time-to time, you'll be able to give me the 
benefit of your counsel. I'll be tackling some issues to which 
you've already given a lot of your own talents and wisdom, and 
I'd like to be able to tap into your experience and thinking. 
-- It •made-me a bit wistful-to read that-you were in Aspen for 
the holidays. I hope the snow conditions were good. I'm hoping 
to get off on a SCUBA trip with my wife and boys and some other 
family members‘in February. 
Happy New Year -- and here's hoping our paths do cross soon 
and often; I, for one, will try to make sure that happens. 
Cheers. 
Sinceely, 
Strobe Talbott 
Robert S. 
1455 Pennsylvania Ave, NW, 
Washington, DC.
