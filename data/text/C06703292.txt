 / DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT WELCOMED THE NEW U.S.-
UZBEK JOINT COMMISSION; SOUGHT GOU HELP AGAINST 
DISINFORMATION ABOUT U.S. POLICY IN AFGHANISTAN AND 
CONTINUED SUPPORT FOR UN EFFORTS; AND SAID THE U.S. WILL 
CONSIDER THE GOU'S CENTRAL ASIAN NUCLEAR WEAPON FREE ZONE 
(CANWFZ) PROPOSAL WITHIN OUR BROADER APPROACH ON ARMS 
CONTROL AND WILL PARTICIPATE AT THE EXPERT LEVEL IN THE 
GOU'S SEPTEMBER CONFERENCE ON 'CANWFZ. END SUMMARY.

3. DROPPING BY AT THE BEGINNING OF THE MEETING, THE 
SECRETARY WELCOMED U.S.-UZBEK COOPERATION ON ISSUES OF 
MUTUAL CONCERN AND UZBEKISTAN'S CLOSE COLLABORATION IN THE 
UN. UPON HER DEPARTURE, TALBOTT AND KAMILOV NOTED BOTH 
PRESIDENTS' INTEREST IN THE BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP AND THE 
IMPORTANCE OF THE NEWLY-AGREED JOINT COMMISSION. THE 55-
MINUTE DISCUSSION THEN COVERED REGIONAL ISSUES, UZBEKISTAN 
IN EURO-ATLANTIC STRUCTURES, AND THE GOU'S PROPOSAL FOR A 
CENTRAL ASIAN NUCLEAR WEAPON FREE ZONE (CANWFZ). OTHER 
ATTENDEES BELOW, PARA 10. 
4. AFGHANISTAN: KAMILOV NOTED THE BASIC CORRESPONDENCE OF 
USG AND GOU INTERESTS IN STABLILITY, REGIONAL SECURITY, AND 
CROSS-AFGHAN TRANSPORTATION ROUTES WHICH WOULD MAKE CENTRAL 
ASIA MORE INDEPENDENT OF RUSSIA. 
THUS THE GOU IS 
READY TO COOPERATE WITH THE USG IN INFORMATION-SHARING AND 
CONSIDERATION OF NEXT STEPS, SUCH AS AN INTERNATIONAL 
CONFERENCE OR RETURN OF KING ZAHIR SHAH. 
5. NOTING THE FUNDAMENTAL SIMILARITY IN USG AND GOU VIEWS, 
TALBOTT HIGHLIGHTED THREE POINTS. FIRST IS USG CONCERN AT 
DISINFORMATION ABOUT U.S. POLICY IN AFGHANISTAN AND THE 
BROADER REGION. AS KAMILOV INTERJECTED THAT THE GOU DOES 
"ALL IT CAN" TO COUNTER SUCH DISINFORMATION, TALBOTT 
CONTINUED BY UNDERSCORING THE CENTRAL IMPORTANCE OF GOU 
HELP IN WORKING AGAINST SUCH DISTORTIONS. 
TALBOTT SAID WE'VE MADE THE SAME CLARIFICATION TO RUSSIAN 
OFFICIALS AND POLITICIANS. SECOND IS THE IMPORTANCE OF 
CONTINUED GOU AGREEMENT TO SUPPORT KEEPING THE PROCESS 
UNDER UN AEGIS. THIRD, THE KEY IS FINDING CONCRETE 
FORMULAS FOR MOVING AHEAD; WE LOOK FOR GOU SUGGESTIONS. 
6. RUSSIA IN CENTRAL ASIA: TALBOTT NOTED THAT, IN THE CASE 
OF NAGORNO-KARABAKH, WE'VE SEEN SOME SIGNS OF RUSSIAN 
WILLINGNESS TO COOPERATE WITH US. HOWEVER, RUSSIA HAS BEEN 
TEMPTED TO CONTINUE TO WORK FROM THE IDEA THAT TROUBLE ON 
ITS PERIPHERY BENEFITS RUSSIAN INTERESTS; RUSSIA HAS 
STRONGLY RESISTED U.S. INVOLVEMENT IN SOLVING OTHER 
REGIONAL CONFLICTS. IN THIS LIGHT, DOES THE GOU SEE 
OPPORTUNITES FOR GREATER U.S. DIPLOMATIC INVOLVEMENT, 
INCLUDING WITH RUSSIA ON THE PERIPHERY?

7. IRAN: IN RESPONSE TO TALBOTT'S REQUEST FOR A GOU 
ASSESSMENT, KAMILOV OPINED THAT VELAYATI CAN'T TURN 180 
DEGREES FROM THE ISLAMIC REVOLUTION. IRAN WOULD NEED TIME 
 I THE ESSENTIAL POINT IS THAT THE USG AND 
GOU SHOULD STAY IN VERY CLOSE TOUCH ON ALL ISSUES AFFECTING 
REGIONAL SECURITY. 
8. UZBEKISTAN IN THE EURO-ATLANTIC COMMUNITY: TALBOTT 
URGED A CONTINUED VERY ACTIVE GOU ROLE IN TRANS-ATLANTIC 
AND EUROPEAN STRUCTURES. WE HOPE THE GOU WILL PARTICIPATE 
IN THE NEW EAPC AT A HIGH LEVEL SINCE THERE IS SUBSTANTIAL 
WORK TO DO TOGETHER TO DEVELOP THE COUNCIL. IN THE RELATED 
VEIN OF BILATERAL SECURITY COOPERATION, TALBOTT WAS PLEASED 
AT VCJCS RALSTON'S ENTHUSIASTIC READOUT OF KAMILOV'S JUNE 
10 MEETING WITH DOD DEPSEC WHITE. AT THE SAME TIME, WE 
URGE CONTINUED PROGRESS ON HUMAN RIGHTS AND ACCELERATION OF 
ECONOMIC REFORM, INCLUDING A RESOLUTE MOVE BACK TOWARD FULL 
CONVERTIBILITY. IN THIS CONTEXT THE USG WILL INVEST GREAT 
ENERGY IN ENSURING SUCCESS FOR THE NEWLY-AGREED JOINT 
COMMISSION. KAMILOV REPLIED THAT UZBEKISTAN HAS REORIENTED 
ITSELF SERIOUSLY IN A EURO-ATLANTIC DIRECTION. THE U.S. IS 
NOW IN FIRST PLACE AS A TRADING PARTNER. THE GOU WILL 
DEFINITELY PARTICIPATE IN EAPC, WANTS TO ATTEND MADRID AT A 
SENIOR LEVEL, WILL CONTINUE TO FULFIL 100 PERCENT OF ITS 
PFP OBLIGATIONS, AND WILL PURSUE FURTHER CENTRAL ASIAN 
INTEGRATION THROUGH THE CENTRAL ASIAN PEACEKEEPING 
BATTALION.' REGARDING THE PACE OF REFORMS, THE GOU WILL 
STRIVE TO DO BETTER, BUT MUCH HAS CHANGED IN A POSITIVE WAY 
SINCE TALBOTT'S LAST VISIT IN 1993.

9. CANWFZ: KAMILOV SAID THE GOU UNDERSTANDS THE USG'S 
CAREFUL APPROACH ON NUCLEAR WEAPON FREE ZONES BUT EXTENDS A 
FORMAL INVITATION TO THE SECRETARY OR OTHER HIGH-LEVEL USG 
OFFICIALS TO PARTICIPATE AT THE SEPTEMBER CANWFZ CONFERENCE 
IN TASHKENT. TALBOTT RESPONDED THAT THE U.S. POSITION ON 
NWFZ'S IS EXTREMELY COMPLEX AND SOPHISTICATED. IT INVOLVES 
SEVEN CRITERIA AND PUTTING NWFZ'S IN THE CONTEXT OF A 
MULTI-LEVEL APPROACH TO ARMS CONTROL, INCLUDING COOPERATIVE 
THREAT REDUCTION, OTHER NON-PROLIFERATION AND ANTI-WMD 
EFFORTS, START II RATIFICATION BY RUSSIA AND MOVES TOWARD 
START III. THE U.S. WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE CONFERENCE AT 
THE EXPERT LEVEL; WE WILL CONSIDER THE UZBEK INITIATIVE IN 
THE BROADER CONTEXT OF U.S. ARMS CONTROL POLICY.
