(B,D) 
1. DURING HIS APRIL 5 VISIT TO ATHENS, DERUTY• 
SECRETARY TALBOTT INITIATED DISCUSSION ON AN ACTION • 
PROGRAM TO BE UNDERTAKEN BY COUNTRIES IN THE BALKAN 
REGION ON THE SUBJECT OF THE ECONOMIC FUTURE OF THE 
REGION AFTER THE FIGHTING STOPS. IN PARTICULAR, 
ALBANIA AND FYROM WILL REQUIRE SUBSTANTIAL ASSISTANCE 
IN THE AFTERMATH OF THE FIGHTING. THE GREEK GOVERNMENT 
WANTS TO PUSH THE EU TO MAKE A GREATER COMMITMENT TO 
THE REGION IN TERMS OF AID AND LONG-TERM ASSOCIATION. 
DEPUTY.SECRETARY TALBOTT-SUGGESTED THAT THE FOREIGN
MINISTERS OF GREECE AND ROMANIA LEAD A DISCUSSION AT 
THE NATO SUMMIT ON THIS ISSUE. GREECE WANTS TO ADD 
BULGARIA, AND POSSIBLY EVEN TURKEY, TO THIS INITIAL 
EFFORT.
