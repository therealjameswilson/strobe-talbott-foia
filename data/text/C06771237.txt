HALONEN THANKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND RETURNED 
L4REGARDS FROM HERSELF AND THE PM TO THE SECRETARY. SHE 
4,,ISAID FINLAND HAD PREPARED A STATEMENT ON KOSOVO FOR 
RELEASE AT THE EU SUMMIT IN BERLIN, AND THE EUROPEAN 
4117M5CN' COUNCIL WAS PREPARING A LONGER, MORE COMPREHENSIVE 
STATEMENT AS WELL. FINLAND PLANNED TO'ISSUE A SHORT 
FOLLOW-UP STATEMENT AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF NATO 
AIRSTRIKES. SHE CAUTIONED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THAT MANY 
MILITARILY NON-ALIGNED NATIONS IN THE EU, AND OTHER 
NEUTRAL EU MEMBERS, HAD CONCERNS ABOUT THE SITUATION'IN 
KOSOVO AND THE NATO RESPONSE. EVERYONE UNDERSTOOD WHY 
NATO FELT ACTION WAS NECESSARY, BUT CONCERNS REMAINED. 
6. HALONEN TOLD THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THAT FINLAND'S 
STATEMENT WOULD CONFIRM STRONG SUPPORT FROM FINLAND AND 
THE EU FOR THE EFFORTS OF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY IN 
KOSOVO, AND CONCERN FOR INTERNALLY DISPLACED PERSONS AND 
THE HUMANITARIAN CRISIS IN KOSOVO. -THE FINNISH STATEMENT 
WOULD DEPLORE THE DETERIORATION OF THE SITUATION IN KOSOVO 
DUE TO SERE STUBBORNNESS, AND WOULD EXPRESS HOPE THAT A 
PEACEFUL SOLUTION COULD BE FOUND AS SOON AS POSSIBLE. 
7. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED APPRECIATION THAT 
FINLAND EXPRESSLY ACKNOWLEDGED SERB STUBBORNNESS AS THE 
ROOT OF THE PROBLEM. STUBBORNNESS JUST SCRATCHED THE 
SURFACE OF SERB RESPONSIBILITY, THOUGH, AS MILOSEVIC HAD 
USED EVERY DAY OF DELAY TO SLAUGHTER MORE INNOCENT 
CIVILIANS IN KOSOVO. HALONEN AGREED, NOTING THE LONGER EU 
STATEMENT WOULD ADDRESS THIS ISSUE MORE COMPLETELY. 
FINLAND, FOR ITS PART, HAD TRIED TO VIEW KOSOVO 
OBJECTIVELY SINCE IT HAD TO BE SENSITIVE TO RUSSIAN 
CONCERNS. 
8. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED THAT RUSSIAN CONCERNS 
WERE AN IMPORTANT ELEMENT TO SOLVING THE CRISIS. HE NOTED 
PRESIDENT CLINTON HAD JUST COMPLETED AN EXTREMELY TOUGH 
CONVERSATION WITH RUSSIAN PRESIDENT YELTSIN, RUSSIAN PM 
PRIMAKOV HAD CANCELED A TRIP TO THE U.S. AFTER VP GORE 
INFORMED HIM AIRSTRIKES WERE IMMINENT, AND THE SECRETARY 
HAD A NUMBER OF TOUGH CONVERSATIONS WITH RUSSIAN FM 
IVANOV. THE RUSSIAN DOMESTIC POLITICAL DIMENSION WAS AN 
IMPORTANT ASPECT OF THE CRISIS AND THE DECISION TO USE 
FORCE. ONE REASON THE USG REGRETTED THE NECESSITY OF 
USING FORCE WAS THE COMPLICATIONS THESE ACTIONS WOULD 
RAISE IN U.S.-RUSSIAN RELATIONS, NATO-RUSSIAN RELATIONS, 
AND RUSSIAN INTERNAL POLITICS. BECAUSE OF THESE 
COMPLICATIONS, IT WOULD BE CRITICAL FOR ALL OF RUSSIA'S 
MOST IMPORTANT INTERLOCUTORS, INCLUDING THE U.S. AND 
FINLAND, TO WORK CLOSELY WITH RUSSIA AS THE KOSOVO 
SITUATION DEVELOPED. IF THE RUSSIAN PEOPLE AND GOVERNMENT 
SAW THAT FINLAND COULD TAKE AN OBJECTIVE VIEW AND STILL 
ASSIGN BLAME WHERE IT BELONGED, THESE ACTIONS WOULD HAVE A 
CALMING EFFECT ON THE RUSSIAN INTERNAL POLITICAL 
SITUATION. MANAGING THE RUSSIAN DIMENSION OF THE CURRENT 
CRISIS REMAINED ONE OF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY'S 
BIGGEST CHALLENGES, AND WAS SOMETHING IN WHICH PRESIDENT 
CLINTON WAS PERSONALLY ENGAGED. 
9. 444- HALONEN AGREED WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
ASSESSMENT OF THE RUSSIAN DIMENSION TO THE CRISIS. SHE 
SAID FINLAND, ON BEHALF OF THE EU, HAD CONVINCED RUSSIA TO 
CONTINUE TO COOPERATE WITH THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY TO 
MITIGATE THE CRISIS IN KOSOVO, BECAUSE FINLAND WAS 
COGNIZANT OF SINCERE, BUT ULTIMATELY UNSUCCESSFUL, RUSSIAN 
EFFORTS TO CONVINCE MILOSEVIC TO BACK DOWN. 
10. 464— THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REITERATED THE USG'S OFFER 
TO BRIEF FINLAND AT ANY TIME ON DEVELOPMENTS IN KOSOVO.
HALONEN AND THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED TO REMAIN IN CLOSE 
CONTACT ON THE SUBJECT. 
11. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1352 EST. 
12. (U) END TEXT.
