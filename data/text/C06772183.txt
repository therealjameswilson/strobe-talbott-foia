PARTICIPANTS: THE ACTING SECRETARY AND PRESIDENT 
AHTISAARI 
4. +6+ THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE HAD JUST SPOKEN TO 
RUSSIAN SPECIAL ENVOY ON KOSOVO CHERNOMYRDIN, WHO WOULD 
SOON BE CALLING AHTISAARI. THE ACTING SECRETARY BELIEVED 
THE NATO COUNTRIES AND RUSSIA WERE MAKING PROGRESS TOWARD 
A POSSIBLE SOLUTION TO THE KOSOVO CRISIS. CHERNOMYRDIN 
HAD WANTED TO ACT QUICKLY, BUT THE U.S. WAS TRYING TO 
PERSUADE HIM NOT TO TRAVEL TO BELGRADE UNTIL THE U.S. AND 
RUSSIA HAD MADE MORE PROGRESS TOWARD WORKING OUT THEIR 
DIFFERENCES. OTHERWISE, A CHERNOMYRDIN TRIP MIGHT NOT 
ONLY FAIL TO PRODUCE A SOLUTION, BUT ALSO HIGHLIGHT 
DIFFERENCES BETWEEN RUSSIA AND NATO. HE HAD TOLD THE 
RUSSIAN ENVOY U.S. OFFICIALS WERE WORKING DAY AND NIGHT TO 
PROVIDE DETAILED ANSWERS TO QUESTIONS CHERNOMYRDIN HAD 
RAISED ON HOW A SERB WITHDRAWAL FROM KOSOVO WOULD BE 
CARRIED OUT AND HOW AN INTERNATIONAL SECURITY FORCE WOULD 
BE STRUCTURED. THE ACTING SECRETARY WOULD BRING A TEAM OF 
EXPERTS, INCLUDING HIGH-LEVEL MILITARY OFFICIALS, WITH HIM 
TO MOSCOW TO HELP EXPLAIN. HE HAD TOLD CHERNOMYRDIN IT 
WOULD ONLY MAKE SENSE FOR AHTISAARI TO ACCOMPANY THE 
RUSSIAN ENVOY TO BELGRADE IF RUSSIA AND THE U.S. HAD 
RESOLVED MAJOR DIFFERENCES. HE HAD SAID AHTISAARI WAS NOT 
PREPARED TO CONDUCT SHUTTLE DIPLOMACY. 
5.-654 AHTISAARI SAID HE HAD NOT BEEN AWARE THAT HE MIGHT 
BE ASKED TO ACCOMPANY CHERNOMYRDIN TO BELGRADE. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY SAID THE RUSSIANS BELIEVED THEY COULD 
APPLY PRESSURE ON SERBIAN PRESIDENT MILOSEVIC, BUT DID NOT 
THINK MILOSEVIC WOULD HAND OVER HIS SWORD TO A RUSSIAN. 
THEY BELIEVED AN'INTERNATIONAL PERSONAGE MIGHT BE MORE 
ACCEPTABLE TO BELGRADE. AHTISAARI WAS MENTIONED AS 
SOMEONE WHO COULD FILL THAT ROLE. IF AHTISAARI WERE TO GO 
TO BELGRADE, HE WOULD HOPEFULLY WEAR THE HATS OF AT LEAST 
TWO ORGANIZATIONS: THE EU, AS INCOMING EU PRESIDENT; AND 
THE UN, WHICH WAS PREPARED TO CONSIDER GIVING SUCH A 
MISSION ITS BLESSING. CHERNOMYRDIN WAS ENTHUSIASTIC ABOUT 
THE IDEA OF AHTISAARI'S INVOLVEMENT, AS WERE THE GERMANS. 
UNSYG ANNAN'S ONLY RESERVATION WAS THAT HE WAS PLANNING TO 
NAME TWO UN ENVOYS TO BELGRADE, PROBABLY SLOVAK FM KUKAN 
AND FORMER SWEDISH PM BILDT. BUT ANNAN HAD AGREED NOT TO 
DISPATCH THE ENVOYS UNTIL AHTISAARI AND CHERNOMYRDIN HAD 
HAD A CHANCE TO CONDUCT THEIR MISSION. IN ADDITION, ANNAN 
WOULD AVOID OVERLAP BY HAVING HIS ENVOYS FOCUS ON 
IMPLEMENTING PEACE ACCORDS RATHER THAN CEASING 
HOSTILITIES.

6. 4} AHTISAARI SAID HE DID NOT UNDERSTAND WHAT ROLE THE 
UN ENVOYS COULD PLAY ON PEACE IMPLEMENTATION. AND WHY DID 
THE UN NEED TWO ENVOYS? THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID ANNAN 
WANTED TWO ENVOYS IN ORDER TO BALANCE THE TICKET, WITH ONE 
ENVOY FROM A NEUTRAL COUNTRY AND ONE FROM A CENTRAL 
EUROPEAN NATION OUTSIDE OF NATO. HE SAID HE DID NOT FAVOR 
THE NAMING OF THE ENVOYS, AND COULD NOT DEFINE THEIR ROLE. 
BUT IT DID NOT REALLY MATTER WHAT MISSION THE UN ENVOYS 
WERE GIVEN. THE IMPORTANT THING WAS TO GET NATO AND 
RUSSIA TO AGREE ON A PROCESS FOR CEASING HOSTILITIES. ANY 
AGREEMENT WOULD HAVE TO REQUIRE ALL SERB FORCES, MUP AND 
VJ, TO LEAVE KOSOVO. SOME FORCES COULD PERHAPS BE ALLOWED 
BACK IN TO PROTECT HOLY SITES AND OTHER SENSITIVE AREAS. 
BUT IF ARMED SERBS WERE LEFT IN PLACE THE REFUGEES WOULD 
NOT COME BACK, EXCEPT FOR KLA MEMBERS WHO WOULD COME TO 
FIGHT. THERE WOULD ALSO HAVE TO BE AN INTERNATIONAL FORCE 
WITH NATO AT ITS CORE, BUT INCLUDING NON—NATO COUNTRIES 
LIKE RUSSIA. IN ADDITION, THEY WOULD HAVE TO DISCUSS HOW 
TO RESPOND IF MILOSEVIC CONTINUED TO REJECT AN 
INTERNATIONAL FORCE IN KOSOVO. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID 
HE WOULD BE ABLE TO EXPLAIN IN MORE DETAIL DURING HIS TRIP 
THE FOLLOWING WEEK TO MOSCOW AND HELSINKI. 
7. --fE4 AHTISAARI SAID HE NEEDED TIME TO CONSIDER WHETHER 
HE WOULD BE WILLING TO ACCOMPANY CHERNOMYRDIN TO BELGRADE, 
UNDER THE RIGHT CONDITIONS. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID 
CHERNOMYRDIN WOULD PROBABLY RAISE THIS ISSUE WITH 
AHTISAARI. HE SAID HE HAD NOT COMMITTED AHTISAARI TO THIS 
PROPOSAL, SO AHTISAARI WAS FREE TO MAKE UP HIS OWN MIND ON 
WHETHER TO DO IT. AHTISAARI SAID IT WAS NOT CLEAR TO HIM 
WHAT HIS ROLE IN SUCH A TRIP WOULD BE, IF CHERNOMYRDIN 
WOULD ACTUALLY BE RUNNING THE SHOW. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
SAID CHERNOMYRDIN WOULD NOT BE RUNNING THE SHOW. HE SAID 
AMBASSADQR EDELMAN WOULD CALL AHTISAARI BACK ON A SECURE 
LINE TO EXPLAIN FURTHER. HE REITERATED THAT AHTISAARI 
WOULD PROBABLY NOT WANT TO GO TO BELGRADE WITH 
CHERNOMYRDIN UNLESS NATO AND RUSSIA HAD REACHED FURTHER 
AGREEMENT. 
8. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 0955 EDT.
