Bertelsmann, the German-based parent company of such powerful U.S. communications 
companies as the Bantam Doubleday De11 Publishing Group and BMG Music, is the second

venue returns to the United States as German journalists gather to meet ten prominent 
American decision-makers from politic, business, and the arts. These exchanges have proven 
immensely successful in the expansion of cornmunications within the American-German

U.S., the new Germany, and Europe in transition. Featured speakers in prior meetings have

As President Clinton's new administration begins to take root, we are watching the American 
system of government undergo a crucial period in its key policy-making areas. 
With the United States looking more towards its European allies as a model for domestic 
changes and for their deeper cooperation on foreign affairs, American-German relations are 
once again taking on a greater significance. These evolving policies require even closer 
contact between the two nations, begging for a strengthening of the ties between their 
respective opinion makers. 
Bertelsmann is currently planning the 1994 conference for February 27 through March 1 in 
New York. Appropriately, the focus of this important session will be on the theme: 
"Clinton's America: A Midterm Preview." The Chairman for the event is Dr. Werner 
Weidenfeld, Coordinator of German-American Cooperation, and Foreign Policy Advisor to 
German Chancellor Kohl. 
A cross-section of our distinguished guests already scheduled to participate include David 
Gergen, Hodding Carter, Jim Hoge, Spike Lee, Frank Biondi, President and Chief Executive 
Officer of Viacom, and Reverend Calvin Butts of the Harlem Baptist Church. 
Among the pertinent topics to be addressed are the direction of American domestic programs, 
the position of the U.S. economy around the world, the defining of America's role in foreign 
policy, and the revolution in the media industry.
