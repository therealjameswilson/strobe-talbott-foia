1. te--4- SUMMARY AND ACTION REQUEST: THE UKRAINIAN PRIME 
MINISTER HELD A SERIES OF MEETINGS IN WASHINGTON THE WEEK 
OF OCTOBER 5 TO ASSURE U.S. OFFICIALS UKRAINE REMAINED 
COMMITTED TO IMPLEMENTING ITS IMF PROGRAM AND TO PURSUING 
ECONOMIC REFORM. UKRAINE ALSO RAISED ONGOING CONCERNS THAT 
EU EXPANSION WILL CREATE A NEW DIVIDING LINE IN EUROPE AT 
UKRAINE'S WESTERN BORDER. POSTS IN EU CAPITALS ARE ASKED 
TO DEMARCHE APPROPRIATE HOST GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS DRAWING 
ON POINTS IN PARAGRAPH 5 BELOW IN ANTICIPATION OF THE EU-
UKRAINE SUMMIT ON OCTOBER 16. END SUMMARY. 
2.   BACKGROUND: UKRAINIAN PRIME MINISTER VALERlY 
PUSTOVOYTENKO LED UKRAINE'S DELEGATION TO IMF/IBRD MEETINGS 
THIS WEEK TO HIGHLIGHT UKRAINE1S COMMITMENT TO IMPLEMENTING 
ITS IMF PROGRAM AND CONTINUING ECONOMIC REFORM. IN 
ADDITION TO THE BANK/FUND MEETINGS, PUSTOVOYTENKO MET WITH 
VICE PRESIDENT GORE, ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT, ENERGY 
SECRETARY RICHARDSON, DEPUTY NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISER 
STEINBERG, AND TREASURY UNDER SECRETARY LIPTON. ALL USG 
INTERLOCUTORS HIGHLIGHTED THE NEED FOR UKRAINE TO CONTINUE 
ITS ECONOMIC REFORM PROGRAM AND REMAIN IN COMPLIANCE WITH 
ITS IMF AGREEMENT. PUSTOVOYTENKO'S MESSAGE THROUGHOUT THE 
MEETINGS WAS THAT UKRAINE HAS DONE ALL THAT HAS BEEN ASKED 
OF IT BY THE IMF, DESPITE THE PRESSURES BROUGHT ON BY THE 
RUSSIAN CRISIS, AND THAT UKRAINE WILL STICK TO ITS IMP 
PROGRAM, BUT THAT UKRAINE WILL CONTINUE TO NEED HELP TO GET 
OUT OF ITS CURRENT ECONOMIC SITUATION. 
3. +g)- USG OFFICIALS ALSO EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT 
POTENTIAL PITFALLS IN ENERGY SECTOR REFORM. A PENDING 
CONSTITUTIONAL COURT DECISION COULD IMPEDE THE GOVERNMENT'S 
ABILITY TO SET ELECTRICITY TARIFFS, THREATENING AN 
IMPORTANT CRITERION FOR CONTINUED IBRD AND IMP LENDING. IN 
ADDITION, THIS WOULD CREATE SERIOUS DIFFICULTIES FOR 
UPCOMING EBRD CONSIDERATION OF FUNDING FOR THE COMPLETION 
OF TWO UKRAINIAN NUCLEAR REACTORS (RIVNE AND KHLEMENITSKY), 
WHICH THE UKRAINIANS ARE INSISTING ON AS A PREREQUISITE FOR 
CLOSURE OF CHERNOBYL. 
4. -tet DURING WASHINGTON MEETINGS, UKRAINIAN OFFICIALS 
ALSO EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT EU EXPANSION, ARGUING THAT 
THIS RISKS CREATING A NEW DIVIDING LINE IN EUROPE ON THE 
POLAND/UKRAINE BORDER. UKRAINE SEEKS US SUPPORT WITH THE 
EU TO MINIMIZE THE IMPACT OF EXPANSION.

A SENIOR UKRAINIAN DELEGATION LED BY PRIME MINISTER 
VALER1Y PUSTOVOYTENKO HELD A SERIES OF MEETINGS IN 
WASHINGTON THE WEEK OF OCTOBER 4. 
- AMONG OTHERS, PUSTOVOYTENKO MET WITH VICE PRESIDENT GORE, 
ACTING SECRETARY OF STATE TALBOTT, SECRETARY OF ENERGY 
RICHARDSON, DEPUTY NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISER STEINBERG, AND 
TREASURY UNDER SECRETARY LIPTON. 
- WITH THE EU-UKRAINE SUMMIT COMING UP OCTOBER 16, WE 
WANTED TO SHARE WITH YOU OUR ASSESSMENT OF THE VISIT. 
- THE PRIME MINISTER'S MAIN MESSAGE WAS THAT UKRAINE-IS 
COMMITTED TO IMPLEMENTING ITS IMF PROGRAM AND CONTINUING 
ITS REFORM EFFORTS. 
- THIS TASK HAS BECOME SIGNIFICANTLY MORE DIFFICULT FOR 
UKRAINE DUE TO THE RUSSIAN CRISIS. PUSTOVOYTENKO, 
EMPHASIZED, HOWEVER, THAT UKRAINE HAS HAD SOME SUCCESS IN 
FENDING OFF THE EFFECTS OF THE RUSSIAN SITUATION, 
SUCCESSFULLY RENEGOTIATING FOREIGN DEBTS AND MANAGING A 
CONTROLLED DECLINE IN THE HRYVNIA. 
- PUSTOVOYTENKO PRESSED HARD ON THE NEED TO DIFFERENTIATE 
UKRAINE FROM RUSSIA, AND SOUGHT U.S. HELP IN TAKING THIS 
MESSAGE TO PUBLIC OPINION AND THE FINANCIAL MARKETS. 
- U.S. OFFICIALS EMPHASIZED THE NEED FOR FULL 
IMPLEMENTATION OF THE IMF PROGRAM AND CONTINUED UKRAINIAN 
EFFORTS ON REFORM. 
- ON ENERGY, U.S. OFFICIALS HIGHLIGHTED THE IMPORTANCE OF 
FULL IMPLEMENTATION OF UKRAINE'S FINANCIAL RECOVERY PLAN 
ANa ITS G-7 MOU COMMITMENT TO CLOSE CHERNOBYL BY THE YEAR 
2000. 
- UKRAINIAN OFFICIALS ALSO RAISED CONCERNS OVER EU 
EXPANSION, FEARING THAT REQUIREMENTS IMPOSED ON POLAND AND 
HUNGARY WILL CREATE NEW BARRIERS AGAINST THE FLOW OF GOODS 
AND PEOPLE BETWEEN UKRAINE AND ITS NEIGHBORS. 
- UKRAINIAN OFFICIALS INDICATED THIS WILL BE A CENTRAL 
POINT OF DISCUSSION AT THE UPCOMING EU-UKRAINE SUMMIT 
OCTOBER 17. 
- WE HOPE THAT THE EU AND UKRAINE CAN DEVELOP A FRAMEWORK 
OF ONGOING COOPERATION TO MITIGATE THE IMPACT OF EU 
EXPANSION ON UKRAINE. 
- WE URGE THE EU TO TAKE STEPS THAT AVOID ISOLATING UKRAINE 
FROM THE EU POLITICALLY AND ECONOMICALLY. 
- WE LOOK FOWARD TO DISCUSSING WITH YOU DURING THE U.S.-EU 
SENIOR LEVEL GROUP MEETING IN NOVEMBER WAYS IN WHICH WE CAN 
ADVANCE OUR JOINT COMMITMENTS TO COOPERATE IN UKRAINE IN 
THE AREAS OF NUCLEAR SAFETY, POWER SECTOR REFORM, CIVIL 
SOCIETY DEVELOPMENT AND OBSTACLES TO TRADE AND INVESTMENT. 
- WE SHOULD ALSO DISCUSS AT THE NOVEMBER MEETING WAYS TO 
BETTER COORDINATE OUR ASSISTANCE TO REDUCE THE EFFECTS OF 
• EU EXPANSION ON UKRAINE. 
ALBRIGHT
