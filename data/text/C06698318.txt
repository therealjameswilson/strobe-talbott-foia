SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S AUGUST 27 
MEETING WITH CANADIAN ACTING FOREIGN MINISTER 
GORDON SMITH: SALMON AND WHALES 
I. SUMMARY: DURING HIS AUGUST 27 MEETING WITH CANADIAN 
DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER GORDON SMITH, DEPUTY SECRETARY 
STROBE TALBOTT EXCHANGED V/EWS ON PRESSED U.S. CONCERN 
ABOUT THE RECENT KILLING OF TWO ENDANGERED BOWHEAD 
WHALES, WHILE SMITH UNDERSCORED THE NEED TO FIND A 
SOLUTION TO THE PACIFIC SALMON PROBLEM. END SUMMARY 
PACIFIC SALMON 
2. FOLLOWING DISCUSSION OF OTHER ISSUES (SEPTELS) 
CANADIAN DEPUTY F.M. GORDON SMITH TURNED BRIEFLY TO THE 
ISSUE OF PACIFIC SALMON, STATING "YOU KNOW OUR 
CONCERNS." HE ADDED THAT THINGS WERE QUIET FOR THE 
MOMENT, BUT THAT THE PROBLEM WILL START UP AGAIN NEXT 
YEAR. "IT IS IMPORTANT TO FIND A SOLUTION% EVEN THOUGH 
IT WILL NOT BE EASY." 
3. D/S TALBOTT RESPONDED BY ASSURING SMITH THAT HE HAD 
BECOME PERSONALLY ENGAGED IN THE PACIFIC SALMON ISSUE. 
HE RECOGNIZED THAT IT WAS AN ISSUE THAT "MATTERED" TO 
CANADA, AND THAT ITS RESOLUTION WAS IMPORTANT TO THE 
HEALTH OF AN IMPORTANT BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP. TALBOTT 
ASSURED SMITH THAT HE HAD DISCUSSED THE ISSUE WITH OES 
ASSISTANT SECRETARY CLAUSSEN, AND THAT HE HAD ASKED EUR 
ASSISTANT SECRETARY KORNBLUM TO BECOME "PERSONALLY" 
INVOLVED; HE HAD ALSO SPENT TIME RECENTLY DISCUSSING 
THE SALMON /SSUE WITH UNDER SECRETARY TIM WIRTH.
