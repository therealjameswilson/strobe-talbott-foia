2. SUMMARY: IN FRANK BUT CONSTRUCTIVE DISCUSSION, GEN. 
WAHEED INITIALLY CAME ON STRONG IN OPPOSITION TO THE 
PROPOSED NONPROLIFERATION PACKAGE FOR PAKISTAN, TERMING IT 
"POLITICAL SUICIDE" FOR ANY GOP AND SAYING PAKISTAN DID 
NOT WANT F-16S, BUT LATER SUGGESTINGYTHAT IT MIGHT BE 
POSSIBLE TO REWORK THE PROPOSAL DISCREETLY TO MAKE IT MORE 
ACCEPTABLE. KEY PROBLEMS CENTERED ON VERIFICATION 
PROCEDURES INVOLVING THE HUMAN ELEMENT AND THE RISK TO THE 
"AMBIGUITY" OF PAKISTAN'S CAPABILITY, THE UNILATERAL 
NATURE OF THE PROPOSAL, AND SKEPTICISM THAT THE U.S. WOULD 
BE ABLE TO GET THE INDIANS,TO TAKE A SIMILAR STEP. 
HOWEVER, IF THE PROPOSAL COULD BE MADE A "STEP" AND NOT A 
"LEAP" FOR PAKISTAN, HE'WOULD THEN WORK TO BRING ALONG THE

DISCUSSIONS, ESPECIALLY ON VERIFICATION, BE HELD WITH 
RAKISTANI EXPERTS DURING HIS UPCOMING VISIT TO ISLAMABAD.
