RELEASE IN FULL 
March 3, 1993 
The Honorable Strobe Talbot 
Special Envoy for the NIS 
Dear Ambassador Talbot: 
My purpose in writing is to apprise you of the activities of the "Coal 
Project" in Russia and invite your participation in a high level seminar on the 
Russian coal industry scheduled for the week of March 29, in Moscow. 
For the last several years I have been working with leaders of U.S. labor 
and management on the development and implementation of a program to 
assist labor and management of the former Soviet Union to make the transition 
to a market economy. Our project, "The Coal Project", grew directly out of the 
reform movement in the former USSR, in which the independent miners' 
unions played such a significant role. In the latter part of 1991, we established 
a joint organization, "Partners In Economic Reform", as a vehicle for 
promoting and assisting in the democratic development of their coal industries 
and coal mining regions. 
Thus far, assisted by a grant from US AID, we have established 
coordinating offices in Moscow and Kiev, and Cooperation and Development 
Centers in the Kuzbass Region of Russia and the Donbas Region of Ukraine. 
We hope to make these Centers focal points for the democratic reform and 
development of the respective coal industries. In additional to technical 
training, it is our intention that our Centers establish an American presence in 
the politically, socially and economically important coal mining regions. 
I would welcome an opportunity to meet with you prior to your 
departure for Moscow to discuss . our program and its objectives. Members of 
our Board would also be interested in participating. My office will be in touch 
with the appropriate office at the State Departnent to see when such a meeting 
can be arranged.

Moscow during the week of March 29, 1993. This seminar is intended as the
