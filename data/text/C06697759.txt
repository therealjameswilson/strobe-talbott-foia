SUBJECT: TURKISH REPRESENTATIVE TO NAGORNO-KARABAKH 
SHARES ASSESSMENT OF RECENT TRIP WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
1. CLASSIFIED BY THE DEPUTY SECRETARY. REASON, 1.5 (B AND 
D). 
SUMMARY:./THE TURKISH SPECIAL REPRESENTATIVE FOR 
THE N-K CONFLICT BRIEFED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ON MARCH 28 
ON THE RESULTS OF THE LATEST TURKISH VISIT TO THE REGION. 
THE TURKS CONCLUDED A SETTLEMENT WAS FARTHER AWAY THAN 
THEY HAD PREVIOUSLY BELIEVED. TURKEY BELIEVES THAT 
ARMENIA IS NOT BEING ENTIRELY FORTHCOMING IN THE 
NEGOTIATIONS, AND THAT IT IS SEEKING MORE AUTONOMY FOR 
NAGORNO-KARABAKH THAN AZERBAIJAN CAN ACCEPT. TURKEY 
REMAINS COMMITTED TO WORKING WITH THE UNITED STATES TO 
SEEK A SOLUTION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WELCOMED THE RECENT 
STATEMENT BY TURKISH PM YILMAZ ON OPENING THE ARMENIAN - 
BORDER ONCE A DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES HAS BEEN SIGNED. 
END SUMMARY. 
3. -(-G,)- ON MARCH 28, AMB. AYHAN KAMEL, TURKISH SPECIAL 
REPRESENTATIVE FOR THE NAGORNO-KAAABAKH CONFLICT, CALLED 
ON THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TO SHARE HIS ASSESSMENT OF H/S 
RECENT TRIP TO BAKU AND YEREVAN. 
DECLASSIFIED 
4.-4Q+ KAMEL SAID HE INITIALLY THOUGHT ARMENIA AND 
AZERBAIJAN HAD MADE A GREAT DEAL OF PROGRESS TOWARDS A 
SETTLEMENT. HIS RECENT TRIP CONVINCED HIM THAT SERIOUS 
PROBLEMS REMAIN. HE BELIEVED ARMENIA WAS HOLDING BACK ITS 
FINAL POSITION IN THE NEGOTIATIONS, SEEKING TO ACHIEVE DE 
FACTO INDEPENDENCE FOR NAGORNO-KARABAKH UNDER THE GUISE OF 
SELF RULE. TURKEY UNDERSTANDS ARMENIA'S SECURITY 
CONCERNS, AND BELIEVES NEITHER ARMENIA NOR THE N-K 
ARMENIANS WANT TO RESPECT THE PRINCIPLE OF TERRITORIAL 
INTEGRITY UNTIL THEY KNOW THE FINAL STATUS OF N-K. 
KAMEL BELIEVED ALIYEV WOULD ACCEPT CONSIDERABLE 
AUTONOMY FOR N-K, INCLUDING THE FREEDOM TO CONCLUDE 
CULTURAL AND COMMERCIAL AGREEMENTS WITH OTHER COUNTRIES, 
AND THE DESIGNATION OF BOTH AZERI AND ARMENIAN AS OFFICIAL 
LANGUAGES. HOWEVER, ALIYEV WOULD REJECT PROPOSALS THAT 
N-K COULD CONCLUDE AGREEMENTS ON SECURITY AND POLITICAL 
MATTERS OR THAT ITS CONSTITUTION COULD CONTRADICT THE . 
AZERI CONSTITUTION. KAMEL SUGGESTED THE EXAMPLE OF 
TATARSTAN MIGHT BE RELEVANT.

ALIYEV WAS WAITING FOR THE RUSSIAN ELECTIONS, FEELING HE 
COULD GET A BETTER DEAL IN N-K UNDER A DIFFERENT RUSSIAN 
PRESIDENT. KAMEL SAID ALIYEV BLUNTLY TOLD HIM THAT HE HAD 
NO REASON TO WAIT, AS RUSSIA WOULD HAVE THE SAME POSITION 
AFTER THE ELECTION. KAMEL SAID RUSSIA WANTS TO REGAIN AS 
MUCH INFLUENCE IN THE CAUCASUS AS POSSIBLE. HOWEVER, 
KAMEL SAID AZERBAIJAN CANNOT ACCEPT A SOLUTION AT ANY 
COST. KAMEL CONVEYED ALIYEV'S FRUSTRATION THAT, DESPITE 
INVITING THE UNITED STATES TO PLAY A ROLE IN THE REGION, 
THE U.S. HAD YET TO CONVINCE THE ARMENIANS.TO AGREE TO A 
SETTLEMENT. 
7.-4;4- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED U.S. PLEASURE WITH 
TURKISH PRIME MINISTER YILMAZ'S RECENT STATEMENT THAT 
TURKEY WOULD OPEN ITS LAND BORDER WITH ARMENIA ONCE IT HAD 
SIGNED A DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES WITH AZERBAIJAN. KAMEL 
RESPONDED THAT TURKEY IS DISAPPOINTED AT THE LACK OF 
POSITIVE REACTION FROM ARMENIA. HE LAMENTED THAT TURKEY 
MADE A SIMILAR GESTURE TO ARMENIA LAST YEAR BY OPENING THE 
AIR CORRIDOR. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SUGGESTED THAT ARMENIA 
MAY IN FACT BE PLAYING ITS HAND CLOSE TO THE VEST, AND 
NOTED THAT WE ARE VERY PLEASED WITH THE TURKISH 
ANNOUNCEMENT. 
8.-.K!t-THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED OUR IMPRESSION THAT THE 
REGIONAL PARTIES BELIEVE THEY CAN PLAY THE U.S. OFF THE 
RUSSIANS. THEY UNDERESTIMATE THE DEGREE OF CONSULTATION 
AND COOPERATION BETWEEN THE UNITED STATES AND RUSSIA. MR. 
TALBOTT ASKED KAMEL TO STRESS TO THE REGIONAL PARTIES THAT 
THIS COMMUNICATION TAKES PLACE. IF IT IS NOT POSSIBLE TO 
REACH A SETTLEMENT BY APRIL 20, WE HOPE TO USE THE 
PRESIDENT'S MEETING WITH YELTSIN TO NARROW DIFFERENCES. 
9.-tet THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REASSURED KAMEL THAT THE , 
UNITED STATES IS URGING ARMENIA TO BE FORTHRIGHT IN ITS 
NEGOTIATIONS WITH AZERBAIJAN AND NOT TO PLAY ANY SEMANTIC 
OR PROCEDURAL GAMES, BUT THE PARTIES MUST MAKE MORE 
PROGRESS BILATERALLY. ONCE AZERBAIJAN AND ARMENIA REACH 
AGREEMENT ON THE ESSENTIAL ELEMENTS OF A SETTLEMENT, 'THE 
UNITED STATES AND TURKEY CAN HELP THEM BRIDGE ANY 
REMAINING PROBLEMS. ARMENIA MUST RECOGNIZE TERRITORIAL 
INTEGR/TY IN A WAY THAT THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY 
RECOGNIZES, AND MUST STOP SHORT OF DE FACTO INDEPENDENCE 
FOR NAGORNO-KARABAKH. AZERBAIJAN MUST OFFER THE HIGHEST 
POSSIBLE DEGREE OF AUTONOMY FOR N-K SHORT OF 
INDEPENDENCE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD KAMEL WE SUSPECT 
ARMENIA IS NOT YET WILLING TO BE AS REASSURING TO 
AZERBAIJAN AS IT HAS BEEN TO THE U.S. HE ADVISED KAMEL 
THAT A SETTLEMENT TO THIS CONFLICT MUST BE SU1 GENERIS, 
RATHER THAN DERIVED FROM OTHER GEOGRAPHIC EXAMPLES WHICH 
ALL HAVE PROBLEMS AND ARE NOT EXACTLY ANALOGOUS.
