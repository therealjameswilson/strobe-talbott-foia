2. SUMMARY: IN A SEPTEMBER 30 MEETING--IN NEW YORK, DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT TOLD ACTING PRESIDENT RAKHMONOV THAT THE 
USG SUPPORTS THE RECENT CEASE-FIRE AGREEMENT. TALBOTT SAID 
THE NOVEMBER 6 ELECTIONS ARE AN OPPORTUNITY FOR THE TAJIK 
GOVERNMENT (GOTI) TO SHOW ITS COMMITMENT TO DEMOCRACY AND 
NATIONAL RECONCILIATION. HE ALSO PRESSED THE TAJIK 
PRESIDENT ON HUMAN RIGHTS ISSUES. RAKHMONOV WELCOMED THE 
UN DECISION TO DISPATCH OBSERVERS AND DEFENDED RUSSIA'S 
ROLE IN TAJIKISTAN. HE PINNED RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE 
CONFLICT ON ISLAMIC FUNDAMENTALISTS SUPPORTED BY IRAN AND 
OTHERS. END SUMMARY 
3. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY MET WITH PRESIDENT RAKHMONOV AT THE 
LATTER'S HOTEL SUITE IN NEW YORK SHORTLY AFTER RAKHMONOV'S 
SPEECH AT THE UNGA. RAKHMONOV WAS ACCOMPANIED BY FOREIGN 
MINISTER ALIMOV, PRESIDENTIAL ADVISOR YULDASHOV, AND UN 
PERMREP KAYUMOV. USUN AMB. INDERFURTH, S/NIS SENIOR 
COORDINATOR COLLINS, S/NIS REGIONAL COORDINATOR PRESEL, 
D SPECIAL ASSISTANT WITHERS, EUR/ISCA DESKOFF JACKSON, AND 
AN INTERPRETER SAT IN ON THE U.S. SIDE. 
CEASE-FIRE AND UN OBSERVERS 
4. TALBOTT WELCOMED THE SEPTEMBER 17 CEASE-FIRE AGREEMENT 
BETWEEN THE GOTI AND TAJOPP AS AN /MPORTANT STEP TOWARD A 
BROADER POLITICAL SETTLEMENT. RAKHMONOV SAID THE GOTI IS 
DETERMINED TO BUILD ON THE AGREEMENT AND AFFIRMED THE 
GOTI'S COMMITMENT TO THE INTER-TAJIK DIALOGUE AND THE 
PROCESS OF NATIONAL RECONCILIATION. 
5. INDERFURTH OUTLINED ACTION THE UNSC HAD TAKEN TO SUPPORT 
THE CEASE-FIRE AGREEMENT. HE NOTED IN PARTICULAR THE UNSC 
PRESIDENT'S SEPTEMBER 29 LETTER TO THE UNSYG (REFTEL) WHICH 
NOTED THE LATTER'S DECISION TO EXTEND THE MANDATE OF THE UN 
SPECIAL ENVOY, TO SEND 15 OBSERVERS TO MONITOR THE 
CEASE-FIRE, AND TO DISPATCH AN ASSESSMENT TEAM TO EXAMINE 
POSSIBLE ESTABLISHMENT OF A BROADER UN MISSION. INDERFURTH 
SAID THE UNSC HOPES THAT THE ARRIVAL OF THE 15 OBSERVERS 
WILL PERMIT THE FORMAL ENTRY INTO FORCE OF THE PROVISIONAL 
CEASE-FIRE. 
6. RAKHMONOV SAID HE HAD URGED UN OFFICIALS TO SEND THE 
CEASE-FIRE OBSERVERS QUICKLY SO THAT THE NEXT ROUND OF 
PEACE TALKS COULD PROCEED. THE PRESIDENT HAD ALSO SAID
