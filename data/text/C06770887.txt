SUBJ: DEPUTY SECRETARY AND PAKISTANI FOREIGN MINISTER DISCUSS NONPROLIFERATION ROAD MAP AND SINO-INDIAN TIES 
(U) CLASSIFIED BY STROBE TALBOTT, DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE. REASONS: 1.5 (C) AND (D). 
1. 4.S-) SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT AND GENERAL RALSTON MET FEBRUARY 2 WITH PAKISTANI FOREIGN MINISTER SARTAJ AZIZ 
AND FOREIGN SECRETARY AHMED. SARTAJ UNDERSCORED THAT THE U.S. AND 
PAKISTAN SHOULD MOVE BEYOND THE NONPROLIFERATION AGENDA AND BUILD 
A DYNAMIC RELATIONSHIP THAT DEALS WITH ISSUES OF MUTUAL CONCERN. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED WITH THE BROAD THRUST OF SARTAJ'S COMMENTS. HE OUTLINED THE WAY AHEAD, WHICH INVOLVED A ROAD MAP, 
WHICH HAD PAKISTAN SIGNING THE CTBT BY MAY. THE FOREIGN MINISTER STRESSED THAT FOR PAKISTAN THE SANCTION REGIMES PROVIDED FOR BY 
U.S. LEGISLATION HAD TO BE DONE AWAY WITH; THIS WOULD HELP THE 
PRIME MINISTER DOMESTICALLY. TALBOTT REPLIED THAT IF PAKISTAN 
WANTS THESE SANCTION REGIMES OUT OF THE WAY IT SHOULD MAKE PROGRESS ON ISSUES OF PROLIFERATION CONCERN, SUCH AS CTBT. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY ALSO RAISED SINO-INDIAN TIES, COMMENTING THAT PAKISTAN 
COULD PLAY A USEFUL ROLE BY HELPING CONVINCE CHINA TO IMPROVE THE.
TONE OF ITS TIES TO INDIA. END SUMMARY.
