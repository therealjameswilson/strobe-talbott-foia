CHINESE WHITE PAPER
3. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT ALMOST IMMEDIATELY
AFTER PUBLICATION OF THE CHINESE WHITE PAPER, THE USG HAD
SENT CLEAR MESSAGES TO CHINESE OFFICIALS THAT THE PAPER
HAD A DAMAGING EFFECT. CINCPAC ADMIRAL BLAIR HAD CONVEYED
THE SAME MESSAGE TO SENIOR PRC MILITARY LEADERS. SOME
SIGNS ARE EMERGING THAT THIS MESSAGE MAY BE GETTING
THROUGH. THREE OBJECTIVES UNDERLIE THE RAPID, FIRM U,S.
RESPONSE: TO PERSUADE THE PRC THAT ITS ACTION HAS BEEN
COUNTERPRODUCTIVE, TO LESSEN THE ODDS THE PRC WILL DO
SOMETHING SIMILARLY UNSETTLING IN THE FUTURE, AND TO
ENSURE THAT THE ADMINISTRATION SETS A QUICK, FIRM, BUT
CONSTRUCTIVE TONE FOR THE U.S. RESPONSE. WHILE THERE IS
NO CHANCE THE PRC WILL FORMALLY BACK AWAY FROM THE WHITE
PAPER, IT COULD MOVE TAKE OTHER CORRECTIVE ACTIONS BY, FOR
EXAMPLE, INCREASING .ITS EMPHASIS ON THE COMMON AGENDA'.

NMD/TMD
7. ~ AMBASSADOR THAWLEY NOTED THAT THE GOA HAS TOLD THE
CHINESE WITH RESPECT TO NMD AND TMD THAT IT IS NOT
POSSIBLE FOR AUSTRALIA TO ASK ANY COUNTRY, TAIWAN
INCLUDED, TO LEAVE ITSELF DEFENSELESS. LATE LAST YEAR,
THE AUSTRALIAN CABINET CONSIDERED NMD/TMD AND DETERMINED
THAT AUSTRALIA WOULD SUPPORT THE CONCEPT, AND WOULD LIKELY
BE INVOLVED IN SOME WAY TECHNICALLY, BUT REMAINED
CONCERNED ABOUT HOW TO HANDLE THIS ISSUE WITH CHINA.
8. ~ TALBOTT NOTED THAT HIS RECENT DISCUSSIONS IN CHINA
WERE VERY MUCH DRIVEN BY NMD, WITH THE OBJECTIVE OF
PROVIDING SUFFICIENT INFORMATION ABOUT NMD TO CLEAR AWAY
AT LEAST SOME CHINESE SUSPICIONS AND MISUNDERSTANDINGS.
THE PRC MAY NOW HAVE ACCEPTED THE US VIEW THAT NMD IS NOT
DESIGNED AGAINST CHINESE FORCES, BUT STILL INSISTS THAT
ITS DEPLOYMENT WOUID UNDERMINE THE ABM TREATY AND REGIONAL
STABILITY. THE CHINESE ALSO REMAIN UNCONVINCED THAT NMD
WILL NOT BE LINKED TO TMDr AND IS OBSESSED WITH THE LATTER
BECAUSE THEY WANT TO RETAIN THE OPTION TO THREATEN THE USE
OF FORCE AGAINST TAIWAN. TALBOTT EMPHASIZED TO THE CHINESE
THAT TMD IS DISTINCT FROM NMD, WITH THE FORMER BEING
DEPLOYED ONLY IN THE CONTEXT OF PROTECTING AMERICAN
FORCES. NO DECISION HAD BEEN MADE REGARDING TAIWAN, AND
ALL THESE TECHNOLOGIES ARE STILL UNDER DEVELOPMENT.
9. ~ THAWLEY INDICATED AUSTRALIA WOULD TRY TO BE HELPFUL
IN ENCOURAGING A POSITIVE VIEW OF NMD IN ASIA. BRIEFINGS
ON THE PROGRAM WOULD BE HELPFUL IN THIS ENDEAVOR. THE
DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED, SAYING THE U.S. WOULD CONTINUE TO
SEEK OPPORTUNITIES TO BRIEF THE GOA ON NMD AND TMD
PROGRAMS.
NEW ZEALAND
10. ~ DURING A BRIEF DISCUSSION OF DEVELOPMENTS IN NEW
ZEALAND, AMBASSADOR THAWLEY NOTED THAT PRIME MINISTER
HOWARD WOULD SOON MEET NEW ZEALAND PRIME MINISTER CLARK;
AUSTRALIA WILL HAVE TO APPROACH DEFENSE ISSUES CAREFULLY
DURING THIS DISCUSSION, BUT THE PM WILL ALMOST CERTAINLY
MAKE A GENERAL POINT THAT AUSTRALIA EXPECTS NEW ZEALAND TO
DO ITS FAIR SHARE IN TERMS OF DEFENSE SPENDING AND
CAPABILITIES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT AS THE
KIWIS TRY TO PREPARE THE U.S. FOR A NEGATIVE DECISION ON
THE F-16 PURCHASE, THEY ARE TRYING TO MAKE THE ARGUMENT
THAT THEY WILL MAINTAIN SPENDING LEVELS AND USE THE F-16

THAT HE DOES NOT FIND THESE ARGUMENTS ESPECIALLY
PERSUASIVE, PARTICULARLY GIVEN THE PROGRESS THAT HAS BEEN
MADE IN BILATERAL RELATIONS DURING THE PAST SEVERAL YEARS.
THE NZ DECISION TO PURCHASE THE F-16'S HAD REALLY HELPED
THE ATMOSPHERE OF THE BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP. REVERSING
