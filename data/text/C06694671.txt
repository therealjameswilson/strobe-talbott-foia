SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S MEETING ON AFRICAN 
PEACEKEEPING WITH BRITISH OVERSEAS ASSISTANCE MINISTER 
1. CONFIDENTIAL ENTIRE TEXT. 
2. SUMMARY: IN HIS OCTOBER 19 MEETING IN LONDON WITH 
BRITISHMINISER FOR OVERSEAS ASSISTANCE, BARONESS 
CHALKER, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT EMPHASIZED THAT HIS 
RIP TO AFRICA IS INTENDED TO CREATE A CLIMATE CONDUCIVE 
TO REGULAR PEACEKEEPING ACTIVITIES IN AFRICA THAT CAN 
AMPLIFY THE WORK OF THE UNITED NATIONS. 
-- CHALKER WELCOMED THE EFFORT, AND INDICATED BRITAIN 
WILL SPONSOR AN ANGLOPHONE AFRICAN CONFERENCE ON 
PEACEKEEPING (IN ACCRA IN NOVEMBER), FOLLOWED LATER BY A 
FRANCOPHONE CONFERENCE IN CAIRO. SHE NOTED BRITAIN IS 
CONTEMPLATING ASSISTING GHANA IN ESTABLISHING A 
PEACEKEEPING FORCE, AND HELPING THE ORGANIZATION OF 
AFRICAN UNITY (OAU) IN SETTING UP AN OPERATIONS CENTER. 
IN REPLY TO CHALKER'S REMARKS ON THE UN, THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY SAID HE WOULD SEEK TO SUPPORT BRITAIN AT THE 
UN IN EFFORTS TO "PEP UP" THE UN PEACEKEEPING DEPARTMENT. 
- A/S GEORGE MOOSE, ACCOMPANYING THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, 
AGREED WITH CHALKER THAT THE OAU PROVIDES AN APPROPRIATE 
LOCUS FOR PEACEKEEPING EFFORTS, BUT REQUIRES EXTENSIVE 
REFURBISHING IN ORDER TO FUNCTION IN THAT CAPACITY. A/S 
BENNET, ALSO AT THE MEETING, SAID THAT -- GIVEN OAU 
WEAKNESSES -- THE USG PLANS TO EMPHASIZE DEVELOPMENT OF 
BILATERAL, PEACEKEEPING-TRAINING PROGRAMS WITH AFRICAN 
STATES. 
-- FOLLOWING DISCUSSION OF POTENTIAL FUTURE INSTABILITY 
IN BURUNDI, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT UNDERTOOK TO RAISE 
THESE CONCERNS WITH BURUNDIAN OFFICIALS DURING HIS BRIEF 
FUELLING STOP IN BUJUMBURA. END SUMMARY. 
PEACEKEEPING PRINCIPLES 
RELEASE IN FULL

3. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH BRITISH MINISTER 
FOR OVERSEAS DEVELOPMENT BARONESS CHALKER OF WALLASEY IN 
LONDON ON OCTOBER 19 BEFORE BEGINNING HIS TRIP TO 
AFRICA. AFTER INITIAL GREETINGS, TALBOTT OPENED BY 
EXPLAINING THAT HE IS TRAVELLING TO AFRICA AT THE 
REQUEST OF SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER IN ORDER TO HELP 
DEVELOP A MORE SYMPATHETIC APPROACH TO PEACEKEEPING 
EFFORTS THERE. DESPITE HEROIC WORK CARRIED OUT BY THE 
UNITED NATIONS, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CONTINUED, THE UN 
CAN NOT DO IT ALL, AND ITS PEACEKEEPING EFFORTS SHOULD 
BE AMPLIFIED WITH REGIONAL ORGANIZATIONS FOR THE 
PURPOSE. ALTHOUGH THERE ARE TROUBLED AREAS IN MANY 
PARTS OF THE WORLD, AFRICA IS WHERE THE PROBLEMS ARE 
WORST. TALBOTT PRAISED FOREIGN MINISTER HURD'S RECENT

5. CHALKER REPLIED THAT THE U.S. AND UK APPROACHES ARE 
SIMILAR. SHE URGED THE U.S. AND OTHER COUNTRIES TO 
DEVELOP MORE RAPID RESPONSE MEASURES TO PREVENT 
HUMANITARIAN DISASTERS, NOTING THAT IN HER FIRST VISIT 
TO RWANDA IN JULY, SHE BROUGHT WITH HER A CIVIL ENGINEER 
FROM THE MINISTRY OF DEFENSE TO PREPARE PRACTICAL 
RESPONSES TO ALLEVIATE HUMAN SUFFERING RESULTING FROM 
THE CRISIS. CHALKER ADDED THAT PROBLEMS OF LOGISTICS 
AND LACK OF POLITICAL WILL REMAIN IMPEDIMENTS TO 
INDIGENOUS SOLUTIONS TO OVERCOMING SUCH CRISES IN AFRICA. 
6. CHALKER SAID BRITAIN IS CONSIDERING HELPING 
PRESIDENT RAWLINGS OF GHANA IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF A 
PEACEKEEPING FORCE. ASKED BY THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WHAT 
BRITAIN HAD IN MIND, CHALKER SAID HMG MAY SUPPLY 
VEHICLES AND TRAINING. SHE EMPHASIZED THAT WHAT AN 
AFRICAN PEACEKEEPING FORCE NEEDS IS LOGISTICAL SUPPORT 
AND MAINTENANCE CAPABILITIES, AS WELL AS NON-LETHAL 
EQUIPMENT, AND TRAINING IN SUCH ISSUES AS- RIOT-CONTkOI; 
AND EMERGENCY FOOD SUPPLY. BRITAIN IS ALSO CONSIDERING 
HELPING THE OAU SET UP AN OPERATIONS CENTER, SHE SAID.
