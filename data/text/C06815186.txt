HIS OFFICE AS WELL AS A LUNCH FOR FCO PERMANENT UNDER

RUSSIA

HE HAS CONCLUDED 'THAT THERE IS LITTLE REASON TO CONTINUE
TO LOOK FOR H IDDEN DEPTH TO PUT IN RATHER~ WHAT YOU SEE
IS WHAT YOU GET." OVER A SERIES OF INTERACTIONS, PUTIN
HAS COME ACROSS AS PREDICTABLE AND HIS BRIEFING BOOK
(WHICH, HOWEVER, HE MASTERS WELL); MOREOVER, HE DOES NOT
SEEN TO HAVE FRESH IDEAS FOR SOLVING RUSSIA'S PROBLEMS AND
APPEARS SUSCEPTIBLE TO THE SERIOUSLY FLAWED ANALYSES OF
THOSE AROUND HIM, SOMETIMES WITH SERIOUS NEGATIVE
CONSEQUENCES.

SAID THE OBJECT WILL BE TO TRY TO BREAK THROUGH THE
NOTION, SHARED BY PUTIN AND IVANOV, THAT FUNDAMENTALIST
VIOLENCE IN THE CAUCASUS IS MAINLY INSTIGATED FROM WITHOUT
RATHER THAN BEING INDIGENOUS.
8. ~ TALBOTT ASKED KERR WHETHER THE PRIME MINISTER
WOULD BE PREPARED TO PRESS PUTIN AGAIN ON THE FRY, THOUGH
AT THIS POINT EVERYONE WAS AWAITING THE OUTCOME OF THE
ELECTIONS AND THE IMPACT ON THE SITUATION IN MONTENEGRO.

PRESS IN RUSSIA AND GOVERNMENT CONTROLS OVER THAT
COUNTRY'S NASCENT CIVIL SOCIETY. BLAIR COULD PLAY A
HELPFUL ROLE IN DELIVERING A MESSAGE ON THAT. THE PUBLIC
AND PRESS REACTION IN RUSSIA TO THE KURSK INCIDENT GIVES
REASON FOR SOME MEASURE OF HOPE. KERR NOTED SIMPLY THAT
BLAIR HAD WEIGHED IN PREVIOUSLY WITH PUTIN REGARDING THE
FRY AND COULD BE COUNTED ON TO DO SO AGAIN.

ASSERTING SUPPORT FOR THE DEMOCRATIC OPPOSITION

MILOSEVIC WOULD BE TOO WARY OF STREET DEMONSTRATIONS TO
DISPATCH HIS FORCES FOR ANY IMMEDIATE MOVE AGAINST
MONTENEGRO. HIS CONCERN FOCUSED MORE ON THE POSSIBILITY
OF PRECIPITATE ACTION BY DJUKANOVIC.

MILOSEVIC 'TO HOLD HOSTAGE HIS RELATIONS WITH THE WEST.

RUSSIANS BUT INDICATED THE PRESIDENT HAD PRESSED PUTIN IN
NEW YORK/ OKI NAWA~ AND MOSCOW ON TH IS SCORE W ITH NO
POSITIVE RESULT APPARENT.
SEA KING HELICOPTER PARTS
12. ~PREEMPTING KERR, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RAISED THE
UK REQUEST FOR PERMISSION TO RE-TRANSFER SEA KING
HELICOPTER PARTS TO INDIA, DESPITE CONTINUING U.S.
SANCTIONS. TALBOTT CAUTIONED THAT GRANTING THE REQUISITE
NATIONAL SECURITY WAIVER WOULD BE HARD TO JUSTIFY, GIVEN
THE TOTAL LACK OF MOVEMENT BY THE INDIANS ON NUCLEAR
NONPROLIFERATION. HE SAID THAT THE JUST CONCLUDED VISIT
BY PRIME MINISTER VAJPAYEE HAD GONE WELL -- EXCEPT ON THE
SUBJECT OF NONPROLIFERATION, WHERE THE INDIANS HAD REFUSED
TO ENGAGE.
13. ~KERR COUNTERED WITH THREE ARGUMENTS. FIRST, WE
DO NEED LEVERS TO MOVE THE INDIANS AND PAKISTANIS TO SIGN
UP TO THE CTBT, HE SAID, BUT THESE PARTICULAR PARTS DO NOT
PROVIDE MUCH OF A LEVER IN THAT REGARD. SECOND, THERE HAD
BEEN A CAREFUL REVIEW OF PENDING ARMS TRANFERS AND AT THE
LEVEL OF THE PRIME MINISTER THERE HAD BEEN A DECISION THAT
NAVAL ITEMS LIKE THIS WOULD NOT AFFECT PAKISTAN ANDc WHAT
IS MORE, COULD PLAY A NEEDED ROLE IN LEGITIMATE AIR/SEA
RESCUE MISSIONS, PARTICULARLY IN THE BAY OF BENGAL.
THIRD, THE U.S. STANCE DID NOT PLAY WELL AT A TIME WHEN
THE FUTURE OF TRANS-ATLANTIC DEFENSE COOPERATION IS UNDER
SO MUCH SCRUTINY.
14. ~ TALBOTT ACKNOLWEGED KERR'S POINTS AND AGREED WITH

ESDI
15. re IN A BRIEF EXCHANGE, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY
CHARACTERIZED PROGRESS ON ESDI AS GENERALLY GOOD, WITH
NATO/EU LINKS AND CAPABILITIES THE TWO MAIN ISSUES
REQUIRING A CONTINUED WATCHING BRIEF. KERR SAID THE
FRENCH ARE EAGER TO FINISH THE "WIRING DIAGRAM" BY NICE,
BUT AS FAR AS THE UK IS CONCERNED, THAT CAN WAIT IF NEED
BE UNTIL THE SWEDISH PRESIDENCY. THE CENTRAL ISSUE IS
CAPABILITIES, AND HE URGED THE U.S. TO ASSESS WHAT THE
EUROPEANS WOULD BE COMING UP WITH AND LET THEM KNOW SOON
IF THERE WERE PROBLEMS. IF, ON THE OTHER HAND, THE U.S.
APPROVES OF WHAT EMERGES FROM THE CAPABILITIES CONFERENCE,
WE SHOULD SAY AS MUCH PUBLICLY. "YOU WILL NOT .BE ABLE TO
SAY THE EUROPEANS ARE INCREASING THEIR DEFENSE
EXPENDITURES," HE CAUTIONED (EXCEPT IN SOME ISOLATED CASES
LIKE THE UK AND THE NETHERLANDS). SPENDING LEVELS
PROBABLY WOULD, HOWEVER, END UP HIGHER THAN THEY WOULD
HAVE BEEN WITHOUT ESDI/P, AND AVAILABLE RESOURCES WOULD
END UP BEING MORE INTELLIGENTLY SPENT. HE SUGGESTED THE
DECEMB"R NAC COULD SAY "SOMETHING SUPPORTIVE," BUT HE
ARGUED AGAINST ANY NEW DECLARATION NEGOTIATED UNDER THE
FRENCH. HE CALLED FOR ADDITIONAL "FRIENDLY AMERICAN
REASSURANCE" FOR THE TURKS.
UK OVERSEAS TERRITORIES
16. ~KERR SAID HE WANTED TO "TOUCH LIGHTLY" ON THE
OUTSTANDING U.S./UK ISSUES RELATING TO SEVERAL BRITISH
OVERSEAS TERRITORIES. HE NOTED RECEIPT OF THE U.S. DRAFT
FOR AN AGREEMENT TO OPEN UP THE USAF AIRFIELD ON ASCENSION
ISLAND TO UK CHARTER FLIGHTS BUT SUGGESTED THE U.S. SIDE
WOULD "NEED TO GIVE A LITTLE MORE." HE SAID THE REALLY
"HIGH PROFILE" ISSUE IS BERMUDA, GIVEN THE INVOLVEMENT OF
THE BERMUDANS AS A THIRD PARTY. KERR SAID HE UNDERSTOOD
THE PROBLEM DOD HAD WITH SETTING PRECEDENT BY AGREEING TO
ADDITIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL CLEAN-UP AT THE FORMER U.S.
BASES, BUT "YOU NEED TO FIND A QUID PRO QUO IN ANOTHER
DOSSIER." HE WENT ON TO INSIST THAT HE "WOULDN'T DREAM,
OF COURSE, OF DRAWING A LINK" BETWEEN U.S. CONCESSIONS ON
THOSE FRONTS AND UK APPROVAL OF PENDING U.S. REQUEST TO
UNDERTAKE SOME CONSTRUCTION ON DIEGO GARCIA. HE CONCEDED
THAT DOING SO WOULD BE "CRUDE AND UNPLEASANT" BUT, HE
REMARKED WRYLY, IT WAS A FACT THAT THE SAME PART OF THE
FCO IS DEALING WITH ALL THREE OF THE ISSUES.
