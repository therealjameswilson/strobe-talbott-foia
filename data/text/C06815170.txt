SUBJECT: INDIAN PM VAJPAYEE'S CONCERNS REGARDING

I
(U) CLASSIFIED BY DEPUTY CHIEF OF MISSION ALEXANDER
MARGULIES. REASON: 1.5(B,D).
1. ~ THE AMBASSADOR WAS PRESENTED TO INDIAN
PRIME MINISTER ATAL BEHARI VAJPAYEE AT A RECEPTION AT
THE INDIAN HIGH COMMISSIONER'S RESIDENCE THE EVENING
OF MARCH 12 (THE PRIME MINISTER WAS IN THE MIDST OF A
THREE-DAY VISIT TO MARK MAURITIUS'NDEPENDENCE DAY).
VAJPAYEE, WHO WAS CLEARLY EXHAUSTED AFTER A FUI L DAY
OF CEREMONIES, MUMBI.ED EXTENSIVELY BUT, GIVEN THE
RECEPTION'S DIN, INCOHERENTLY TO THE AMBASSADOR.
2. tS) THE NEXT DAY, MAURITIAN PRIME MINISTER
NAVINCHANDRA RAMGOOLAM, WHO WAS PARTY TO THE PREVIOUS
EVENINGS "CONVERSATION," TOLD THE AMBASSADOR THE
INDIAN PRIME MINISTER HAD STATED THAT:
PRESIDENT CIJNTON HAD CALLED VAJPAYEE BEFORE
DECIDING TO VISTT PAKTSTAN:
VAJPAYEE INITIALLY TRIED TO TALK PRESIDENT
CLINTON OUT OF SUCH A VISIT, BUT WAS CONVINCED BY THE
PRESIDENT THAT THE TRIP WAS A GOOD IDEA; AND
VAJPAYEE REMAINS VERY/VERY CONCERNED ABOUT
PRESIDENT CLINTON'S SECURITY IN PAKISTAN.
3. ~ COMMENT: WE HAVE NO REASON TO DOUBT PRIME
MINISTER RAMGOOLAM'S RECOLLECTION OF THE DISCUSSION
BETWEEN VAJPAYEE AND THE AMBASSADOR. VAJPAYEE
CLEARLY WANTED TO MAKE A POINT IN HIS COMMENTS TO THE
AMBASSADOR, BUT GIVEN THE CIRCUMSTANCES HIS MESSAGE
ONLY CAME TO US IN INDIRECT FASHION. END COMMENT.
