3. SUMMARY. DURING HIS OCTOBER 25-26 VISIT TO ABIDJAN, 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT USED PUBLIC EVENTS TO PROMOTE 
THE USG'S DUAL OBJECTIVES OF INCREASED IVORIAN 
INVOLVEMEilT IN PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS ABROAD AND 
CONTINUED IVORIAN DEMOCRATIZATION AT HOME. IN AN 
OCTOBER 25 ADDRESS TO THE IVORIAN NATIONAL ASSEMBLY, THE 
DEPSEC OUTLINED AMERICA'S EFFORTS IN SUPPORT OF 
MULTILATERAL PEACE INITIATIVES AND URGED AFRICANS TO 
REINFORCE THE CAPACITIES OF REGIONAL GROUPINGS LIKE THE 
OAU AND ECOWAS. AT A RECEPTION WITH POLITICAL PARTY AND 
CIVIL SOCIETY LEADERS LATER THAT EVENING, THE DEPSEC 
FOCUSED HIS FORMAL REMARKS ON DEMOCRATIZATION. DURING A 
RECEPTION PULL-ASIDE WITH INTERIOR MINISTER BOMBET, 
DEPSEC UNDERSCORED USG INTEREST IN FREE AND TRANSPARENT 
ELECTIONS IN 1995, URGED INTERNATIONAL ELECTION OBSERVER 
PARTICIPATION, AND LISTENED TO AN UDATE ON ELECTIONS 
PREPARATION. NEVERTHELESS, GOCI OFFICIALS REMAIN 
RELUCTANT TO ENTERTAIN PUBLICLY DIRECT INVOLVEMENT IN 
LIBERIA OR INTERNATIONAL ELECTIONS OBSERVATION. END

9. THE PUBLIC REACTION TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
MESSAGE WAS OVERWHELMINGLY POSITIVE. MEDIA COVERAGE, 
ELECTRONIC AND PRESS, WAS EXTENSIVE. NEWSPAPER ARTICLES 
AND TELEVISION REPORTS FOCUSED ON THE REGIONAL NATURE OF 
THE TRIP AND HIGHLIGHTED THE USG'S TWIN CONCERNS-OF 
REGIONAL PEACEKEEPING AND DEMOCRACY. THE SPEECH AT THE 
NATIONAL ASSEMBLY WAS WELL/COVERED; FOR EXAMPLE, AN 
ARTICLE PUBLISHED IN GOCI-OWNED DAILY "FRATERNITE MATIN" 
ENTITLED "PLEA FOR AFRICAN ENGAGEMENT" TOUCHED ON ALL OF 
THE DEPSEC'S MAJOR POINTS. THE ISSUE OF DEMOCRATIZATION 
WAS ALSO EMPHASIZED, WITH T.V. COVERAGE OF SEVERAL OF 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S STATEMENTS CONCERNING THE NEED 
FOR COTE D'IVOIRE'S 1995 ELECTIONS TO SERVE AS AN 
EXAMPLE FOR AFRICA.
