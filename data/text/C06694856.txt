1. SUMMARY. CHILEAN AMBASSADOR JOHN BIEHL PRESENTED A 
COPY OF HIS CREDENTIALS TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY IN AN 
AUGUST 5 MEETING IN THE DEPARTMENT. BIEHL STATED THAT 
PRESIDENT FREI WAS "EXTREMELY PLEASED" BY THE RESULTS OF 
HIS RECENT VISIT TO WASHINGTON AND THAT A RECIPROCAL 
VISIT BY PRESIDENT CLINTON TO SANTIAGO WOULD BE 
WELCOMED. BIEHL EXPRESSED CONCERN THAT FAST TRACK WAS IN 
TROUBLE AND THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, WHILE ACKNOWLEDGING 
THAT IT WAS AN UPHILL BATTLE, STATED THAT THE 
ADMINISTRATION WAS DEVOTING ITS FULL ENERGY TO PERSUADING 
CONGRESS TO PASS FAST TRACK. ON HAITI, BIEHL REITERATED 
CHILE'S SUPPORT FOR U.S. POLICY AND THE GOC'S DESIRE TO 
CONTRIBUTE TO PHASE II OF THE UN OPERATION. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY THANKED THE AMBASSADOR FOR CHILETS SUPPORT ON 
HAITI AND EXPRESSED HOPE THAT CHILE COULD ASSIST WITH 
POLICE TRAINING IN PHASE II. END SUMMARY. 
2. CHILEAN AMBASSADOR BIEHL PRESENTED A COPY OF HIS - 
CREDENTIALS TO DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT ON AUGUST 5. 
JOINING THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WERE ARA/BSC DIRECTOR DON 
PLANTY, D STAFFER DEBORAH SCHWARTZ AND ARA/BSC DESK 
OFFICER DAN BELLEGARDE. CHILEAN DCM JOAQUIN MONTES 
ACCOMPANIED AMBASSADOR BIEHL. 
3. FREIVISIT. 
AMBASSADOR BIEHL OPENED THE MEETING BY SAYING THAT 
PRESIDENT FREI WAS "EXTREMELY PLEASED" WITH THE RESULTS 
OF HIS RECENT WASHINGTON VISIT AND THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
RESPONDED THAT WE SHARED THIS ASSESSMENT OF THE VISIT. 
LATER IN THE CONVERSATION BIEHL COMMENTED THAT AVISIT BY 
PRESIDENT CLINTON TO CHILE WOULD BE WELCOMED BY THE FREI 
GOVERNMENT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT THE 
ADMINISTRATION WOULD BE "OPEN" TO DISCUSSING A POSSIBLE 
CLINTON VISIT AT AN APPROPRIATE FUTURE TIME. 
4. FREE TRADE.

5. HAITI. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY COMMENTED THAT WITHOUT A CREDIBLE 
THREAT OF FORCE, THE CURRENT HAITIAN GOVERNMENT WOULD 
NEVER LEAVE. HE THANKED CHILE FOR ITS SUPPORT OF OUR 
HAITI POLICY IN INTERNATIONAL FORA, PARTICULARLY AT THE 
RECENT OAS MEETINGS IN BELEM. WHILE ACKNOWLEDGING THE 
POLITICAL CONSTRAINTS IN CHILE, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
EXPRESSED THE HOPE THAT CHILE WOULD BE ABLE TO 
PARTICIPATE IN PHASE II OF THE UN OPERATION, CITING 
POLICE TRAINING AS A POSSIBLE CONTRIBUTION. AMBASSADOR 
BIEHL SAID THAT THE GOC REGARDED HAITI AS A SERIOUS 
PROBLEM, WAS FOLLOWING IT "VERY CLOSELY" AND WANTED TO 
WORK WITH US TO BRING ABOUT A SUCCESSFUL RESOLUTION. HE 
ADDED THAT THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY HAS TO TAKE ACTION 
THAT WILL MAKE IT CLEAR THAT THE FORCIBLE,OVERTHROW_OF_____ 
GOVERNMENTS IS SOMETHING THAT WILL NOT BE ALLOWED -- PAST 
PRACTICES CANNOT BE ALLOWED TO RETURN. AFTER THE 
MEETING, BIEHL TOLD ARA/BSC DIRECTOR PLANTY THAT HE 
AGREED COMPLETELY WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S REMARKS ON 
THE NEED TO MAKE THE USE OF FORCE CREDIBLE. BIEHL SAID 
IT WAS THE CREDIBLE THREAT OF FORCE WHICH HAD EVENTUALLY 
BROUGHT PEACE TO CENTRAL AMERICA.
