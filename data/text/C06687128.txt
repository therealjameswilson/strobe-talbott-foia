A Two-Part Package 
An interagency policy steering group, under the 
auspices of the NSC, is developing a package of proposals in 
two areas: 
1) programs of economic engagement and assistance to 
accelerate Russia's transformation from a communist command 
system to a free market; 
2) measures to reduce levels of nuclear weaponry 
within Russia itself, hasten the de-nuclearization of Ukraine 
and other non-Russian former republics of the USSR, curb arms_ 
proliferation, and enhance security and stability in Edrasia. 
In the first area -- economic engagement and as-
sistance -- we have been concentrating on,initiatives of 
three.types: 1) emergency humanitarian food and medical as-
sistance; 2) technical economic assistance for the transition 
to a modern market economy; and 3) trade and investment pro-
grams, all of which will, in the long run, benefit American 
industries and workers.
