SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY RAISES M-11S WITH PM B 
1. -seete? - ENTIRE TEXT. 
2. IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING THE APRIL 9 WORKING LUNCH AT THE 
PRIME MINISTER'S RESIDENCE, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET 
PRIVATELY WITH PM BHUTTO TO RAISE THE SENSITIVE ISSUE OF M-11 
MISSILES. HE NOTED THAT ALTHOUGH THE U.S. HAD IMPOSED 
RELATIVELY LIGHT SANCTIONS AGAINST CHINA AND PAKISTAN IN AUGUST 
1993 FOR TRANSFERRING M-11-RELATED ITEMS, THE U.S. REMAINS 
CONCERNED THAT ACTUAL M-11 MISSILES MAY HAVE,BEEN TRANSFERRED. 
3. TALBOTT EXPLAINED THAT IF THE U.S. ACQUIRES CONVINCING 
INFORMATION THAT THE MISSILES THEMSELVES HAVE BEEN TRANSFERRED 
AND ARE IN PAKISTAN, THIS WOULD TRIGGER SEVERE TRADE SANCTIONS 
UNDER U.S. LAW, MAKE,THE PROPOSED EXCEPTION TO THE PRESSLER 
AMENDMENT POLITICALLY INCONCEIVABLE, AND PLACE ANOTHER DARK 
CLOUD OVER OUR BILATERAL RELATIONS. OUR GOAL, TALBOTT SAID, 
WOULD NOT BE TO EMBARRASS BHUTTO OR DO HER ANY POLITICAL HARM. 
IT WOULD ONLY BE TO MEET THE REQUIREMENTS OF U.S. LAW AND OUR 
NONPROLIFERATION POLICY, WHILE PROTECTING OUR BILATERAL 
RELATIONSHIP. HE ENDED BY EMPHASIZING THAT PAKISTAN SHOULD NOT 
PRETEND THIS PROBLEM WILL SIMPLY GO AWAY. 
4. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT U.S. OFFICIALS HAVE 
SUGGESTED TO THE GOP A DISCLOSURE PROCEDURE UNDER.WHICH ANY 
M-11S CURRENTLY IN PAKISTAN —.IF DISCLOSED TO THE U.S. SOON --
WOULD NOT BE SUBJECT TO SANCTIONS. IF THE GOP DESIRED, HE 
OFFERED TO HAVE U.S. EXPERTS EXPLAIN THIS FURTHER TO THE GOP. 
IF M-11S ARE IN PAKISTAN, TALBOTT SAID, WE WOULD HAVE TO THINK 
VERY CAREFULLY ABOUT HOW TO DEAL WITH THE MATTER. HE URGED THE 
PRIME MINISTER TO WORK COOPERATIVELY WITH US VERY QUIETLY TO 
RESOLVE THE PROBLEM. 
5. IN REPLY, THE PRIME MINISTER INITIALLY RESPONDED BY 
CLAIMING THAT PAKISTAN DOES NOT HAVE M-11 MISSILES. SHE LATER 
ASKED TALBOTT TO KEEP HER PERSONALLY APPRISED OF ANY SPECIFIC 
INFORMATION THE U.S. DEVELOPS ON THE M-11S. SHE SAID SHE WOULD 
PREFER TO USE A VERY DISCREET CHANNEL TO COMMUNICATE ON THIS 
SENSITIVE ISSUE. IN ISLAMABAD, SHE PREFERRED THAT THE 
AMBASSADOR DEAL DIRECTLY WITH HER AND COAS GENERAL WAHEED. IN 
WASHINGTON, BHUTTO SAID HER CHANNEL WOULD BE AMBASSADOR LODHI. 
6. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY HAS CLEARED THIS CABLE. MONJO
