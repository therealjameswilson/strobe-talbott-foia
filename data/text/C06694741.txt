2. SUMMARY: IN HALF-HOUR BILATERAL OCTOBER.4 AT UN 
(SHORTENED BY LATE ARRIVAL OF PAKISTANIS), GOP FONMIN 
ASSEF ALI TOLD DEPSEC TALBOTT THAT PAKISTAN IS DETERMINED 
TO PRESS FOR UN INVOLVEMENT IN THE KASHMIR DISPUTE TO SPUR 
MOVEMENT ON THE ISSUE. SPECIFICALLY, ALI HAS PROPOSED IN 
A LETTER TO UNSC PRESIDENT THAT UNMOGIP POST OBSERVERS ON 
BOTH SIDES OF LINE. SECOND THE GOP WANTS INDIA TO MAKE 
SOME "SMALL GESTURES," E.G. RELEASING MORE KASHMIRIS, 
WITHDRAWING SOME TROOPS, TO GET BILATERAL NEGOTIATIONS 
UNDERWAY. TALBOTT RESPONDED THAT WHILE THE U.S. SHARES 
THE GOAL OF A BILATERAL SETTLEMENT, WE DO NOT BELIEVE 
MULTILATERAL, PUBLIC PRESSURE IS LIKELY TO BE PRODUCTIVE. 
WE TAKE THIS LINE WITH BOTH SIDES. TIME CONSTRAINTS AND 
ALI'S FOCUS ON KASHMIR FORECLOSED DISCUSSION OF OTHER 
ISSUES. END SUMMARY.

"BRING.INDIA BACK TO THE TABLE," HE SAID.
