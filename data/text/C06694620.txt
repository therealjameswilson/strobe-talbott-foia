SUBJECT: CONTRIBUTION TO KEDO 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THANKED. FM VAN MIERLO FOR SPEAKING 
WITH HIM AND SAID HE WAS CALLING ON THE SUBJECT OF KEDO. 
VAN MIERLO SAID HE HAD SPOKEN PREVIOUSLY WITH THE 
SECRETARY ON THE SUBJECT OF KEDO, AND HAD INDICATED THE DUTCH WANTED TO PARTICIPATE ON A COMMERCIAL BASIS 
THROUGH THE SUPPLY OF TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE. VAN MIERLO THEN SAID, HOWEVER, HE AND THE ECONOMIC MINISTER HAD PREPARED A PROPOSAL FOR A $500,000 CONTRIBUTION TO KEDO,

WHICH HAS READY TO BE SUBMITTED TO THE DUTCH CABINET. VAN MIERLO ADDED HE COULD NOT, NATURALLY, GUARANTEE THE RESULTS OF THE CABINET'S CONSIDERATION OF THE PROPOSAL. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THEIR OFFER IS GPATIFYING, NOT ONLY QUANTITATIVELY, BUT FOR ITS POLITICAL IMPLICATIONS AS WELL. HE TOLD VAN MIERLO THE BERLIN ROUND OF TECHNICAL TALKS WITH THE DPRK WENT BETTER THAN ANTICIPATED, AND THE RELATIVE SUCCESS OF THE TALKS WOULD BE FURTHERED BY SIGNIFICANT INTERNATIONAL PARTICIPATION IN KEDO. 
VAN MIERLO SAID THEIR OFFER IS AN EXPRESSION OF SOLIDARITY, WHICH THEY HOPE WILL ENLARGE THE POSSIBILITIES OF POLITICAL SUPPORT IN OTHER AREAS. HE ACKNOWLEDGED THE AMOUNT OF $500,000 WAS NOT EXTENSIVE, ADDING THE SECRETARY HAD SAID EVEN A SYMBOLIC CONTRIBUTION WOULD BE HELPFUL. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THE CONTRIBUTION WOULD BE HELPFUL IN THE AREA OF HEAVY FUEL OIL. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY COMMENTED_THAT ALTHOUGH HE COULD MAKE NO GUARANTEES, HE HOPED COMMERCIAL CONTRACTS WOULD DEVELOP FOR THE DUTCH FROM KEDO. VAN MIERLO ALLOWED THEY EXPECTED SOME RESULTS FROM THEIR CONTRIBUTION ALTHOUGH HE UNDERSTOOD NO PROMISES WERE POSSIBLE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INDICATED THE SECRETARY WOULD BE GRATIFIED BY THE DUTCH CONTRIBUTIO14, AND THEY AGREED TO KEEP IN TOUCH ABOUT THE OUTCOME OF THE CABINET MEETING. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED CONDOLENCES TO VAN MIERLO ON THE DEATH OF A.DUTCH PEACEKEEPER IN BOSNIA. VAN MIERLO SAID THE SOLDIER WAS HIT DIRECTLY AS HE MOVED 
INTO A SHELTER AND THAT HIS DEATH MARKED THE -FIRST REAL CASUALTY ON THE DUTCH SIDE FROM AN ACT OF WAR. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID-WE HATE TO SEE THE TRAGEDY OF THE BALKANS BROUGHT HOMEITO THE DUTCH PEOPLE IN SUCH A WAY. 
THE CALL CONCLUDED AT 1111 EST.
