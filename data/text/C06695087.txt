2. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, ACCOMPANIED BY AMBASSADOR 
LAMBERTSON, MET JULY 25 WITH CAMBODIAN FOREIGN MINISTER, 
PRINCE SIRIVUDH, AND RCG AMBASSADOR TO BANGKOK, ROLAND 
ENG. (THE BRIEF MEETING TOOK PLACE IN THE SHANGRI-LA 
HOTEL LOBBY PRIOR TO THE DEPARTURE FOR A JOINT CALL ON 
PRIME MINISTER CHUAN BY VISITING MINISTERS.) 
3. SIRIVUDH BEGAN BY EXPRESSING THANKS FOR STRONG U.S. 
SUPPORT FOR'CAMBODIA, WHICH HAD BEEN ESSENTIAL FOR THE 
SUCCESS OF THE PEACE PROCESS AND WAS STILL NEEDED. HE 
ALSO SAID RCG APPRECIATED USG-EFFORTS TO ENCOURAGE 
STRONGER THAI EFFORTS VIS-A-VIS THE KR. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY SAID WE HAD BEEN ENCOURAGED BY UNEQUIVOCAL THAI 
STATEMENTS OF SUPPORT FOR PHNOM PENH AND NON-SUPPORT FOR 
THE KR, AS ALSO EXPRESSED IN THE FINAL STATEMENT OF THE 
ASEAN MINISTERIAL MEETING. IN OUR MEETINGS WITH PRASONG 
AND OTHERS, WE WERE URGING THE THAI TO CONTINUE THE 
EFFORT TO ISOLATE THE KR. SIR/VUDH SAID HIS OWN MEETING 
WITH'PRASONG HAD BEEN QUITE POSIT/VE AS WELL, WITH 
PRASONG SAYING THE RTG WOULD TRY TO BE HELPFUL. 
4. SIRIVUDH AGAIN THANKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY FOR THE 
CONSISTENT SUPPORT WE HAD PROVIDED, INCLUDING THE HELPFUL 
AND ACTIVIST ROLE OF AMBASSADOR TWINING. OUR DEMINING 
ASSISTANCE WAS ESPEC/ALLY APPRECIATED. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY NOTED THAT WE WOULD CONTINUE TO HELP CAMBODIA, : 
ADDING THAT THERE WAS WIDESPREAD CONGRESSIONAL INTEREST 
IN ASSISTING THE RCG. DEMINING ASSISTANCE ENJOYED 
PARTICULARLY STRONG CONGRESSIONAL SUPPORT. 
DECLASSIFIED 
5. SIRIVUDH SAID THE CAMBODIAN ECONOMY WOULD BENEFIT - 
GREATLY IF CAMBODIA WERE TO BE GIVEN MFN TREATMENT AND 
MADE EL/GIBLE FOR GSP. HE UNDERSTOOD THAT DECISIONS ON 
BOTH THESE ISSUES WERE PENDING AND HOPED BOTH WOULD SOON-
BE APPROVED. SIRIVUDH ADDED THAT, WHILE IT WAS 
EMBARRASSING TO RAISE THE ISSUE IN VIEW OF THE AID THE US 
WAS GIVING CAMBODIA, THERE WERE UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS OF 
CLAIMS AND FROZEN ASSETS WHICH NEEDED TO BE SORTED OUT. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE WOULD FOLLOW UP ON BOTH 
THESE QUESTIONS (MFN/GSP AND CLAIMS/ASSETS). TALBOTT
