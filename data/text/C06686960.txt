Dear Mr. Tymann: 
It was a pleasure to meet with you and hear about the 
Westinghouse Company's project to modernize Russia's air 
traffic management system. We strongly support Russia's ATM 
modernization program, a win-win proposition. The Russians 
will benefit from hard currency and from an improved 
transportation system. The U.S. will benefit from new and 
safer international air routes as well as increased business 
opportunities. 
Several U.S. government agencies are already actively 
helping Russia improve its ATM system. As you know, the Trade 
and Development Agency has funded two ATM projects in the NIS
