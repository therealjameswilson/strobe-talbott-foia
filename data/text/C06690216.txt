RELEASE IN FULL 
Dear Alfred: 
Thanks for the kind note. I appreciate the good wishes. 
One of the reminders I occasionally have that my place of work 
is a friendly place is when Andrew's quite, confident presence 
lightens the atmosphere of otherwise somber and elegant rooms 
where I find myself (his arrival officially signals that the 
meeting is over --but to me it means more than that). 
I've been so preoccupied with the Ukraine angle of the 
President's trip (which of course has involved a lot of dealings 
with your predecessor Bill Miller) that I haven't gotten into 
the details of the Moscow stop. By the time you get this (I'm 
writing it in a hotel room in Brussels), the reception will have 
come and gone, but I'll be on the alert for the people you 
recommended to Will Itoh. 
One of my regrets (few) about the past year is that I 
haven't seen as much of good friends as I'd have liked. But I 
hope very much to see you soon and often now that the year and 
my job have changed. 
A11 the best.
