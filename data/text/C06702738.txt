AND A UNITED AND DEMOCRATIC EUROPE UNDER THE SECURITY 
UMBRELLA OF AN ENLARGED AND RESTRUCTURED NATO (FULL 
STATEMENT RELEASED PUBLICLY). SHE EXPRESSED CONFIDENCE 
THAT NATO WOULD REACH ITS GOALS OF INTERNAL ADAPTATION, 
ENLARGEMENT AND CREATING ENHANCED RELAT/ONSHIPS WITH 
UKRAINE AND RUSSIA. SHE URGED NATO TO COMPLETE 
MEMBERSHIP NEGOTIATIONS BY THE END OF 1997 50 THAT THE 
PROCESS OF RATIFICATION COULD BEGIN, ASKED THAT ALL 
PARTNERS BE INVITED TO THE SUMMIT, AND PROPOSED 
LAUNCHING THE APC AT OR BEFORE,THE SUMMIT. THE 
SECRETARY EXPRESSED CONFIDENCE THAT RUSSIA WOULD 
UNDERSTAND THAT ITS BEST HOPES FOR A SECURE FUTURE 
INCLUDE A PARTNERSHIP WITH A TRANSFORMED NATO. SHE 
PROPOSED CREATING A NATO-RUSSIA JOINT COUNCIL AND A 
JOINT BRIGADE, AND REAFFIRMED THE ALLIANCE HAD "NO 
PLAN, NO NEED, AND NO INTENTION TO STATION NUCLEAR 
WEAPONS ON THE TERRITORY OF NEW MEMBERS." PRAISING 
NATO'S ROLE IN BOSNIA, THE SECRETARY REMINDED MINISTERS 
OF THE NEED FOR FURTHER EFFORTS TO DIMINISH THE 
EXTERNAL MILITARY PRESENCE.

THE SECRETARY'S CONCLUDING REMARKS 
28. 4ft-SECRETARY ALBRIGHT THANKED HER COLLEAGUES AND 
EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THE RECEPTION AND SUPPORT 
FROM NATO. ALTHOUGH THE AMOUNT OF REMAINING WORK WAS 
GREAT AND TIME WAS SHORT, SHE REMINDED HER COLLEAGUES 
THAT "NOTHING CONCENTRATES THE MIND BETTER THAN A HEAVY 
SCHEDULE." THERE SHOULD BE NO DETOURS TO MADRID. THE 
SECRETARY SAID SHE WAS PLEASED AT THE BROAD SUPPORT FOR 
THE SECRETARY-GENERAL'S EFFORTS WITH RUSSIA, AND 
PROMISED THAT AMBASSADOR HUNTER WOULD DEBRIEF PERMREPS 
ON HER TRIP TO MOSCOW.
