SUBJECT: UN ENVOY BILDT MEETS DEPUTY SECRETARY AND U/S 
PICKERING: PLANNING FOR POST-MILOSEVIC ERA 
CLASSIFIED BY IO/UNP OFFICE DIRECTOR WILLIAM A. STANTON. 
REASONS: 1.5 (B) AND (D). 
1. SUMMARY: UN SPECIAL ENVOY FOR THE BALKANS AND 
FORMER SWEDISH PRIME MINISTER CARL BILDT MET SEPARATELY 
WITH UNDER SECRETARY PICKERING AND DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT 
IN WASHINGTON MAY 4. BILDT, ASSERTING THAT, IT IS ONLY A 
MATTER OF TIME BEFORE MILOSEVIC LEAVES THE SCENE, STRESSED 
MILOSEVIC'S DEPARTURE WOULD INCREASE THE REGION'S PROBLEMS 
BY FOSTERING DESIRE FOR INDEPENDENCE AMONG THE CONSTITUENT 
PIECES OF THE FEDERAL REPUBLIC OF YUGOSLAVIA (SERBIA AND 
MONTENEGRO). ONCE MILOSEVIC WAS GONE, BILDT THOUGHT THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY WOULD HAVE A BRIEF OPPORTUNITY TO 
PROVIDE AN ALTERNATIVE -- A "LOOSER" FEDERATION OF THREE 
REPUBLICS (SERBIA, MONTENEGRO AND KOSOVO) THAT MIGHT AVOID 
INSTABILITY. THIS WOULD'PERMIT FRY INTEGRATION INTO 
EUROPE. BILDT URGED THE U.S. TO ASK THE RUSSIANS TO 
ENCOURAGE MILOSEVIC'S DEPARTURE FROM POWER, IN EXCHANGE FOR 
A U.S. COMMITMENT NOT TO SUPPORT INDEPENDENCE FOR KOSOVO 
AND MONTENEGRO. IN RESPONSE, PICKERING INDICATED THAT 
WHILE WE SHARED MANY OF BILDT'S VIEWS, SUCH AN APPROACH TO 
THE RUSSIANS WAS PROBLEMATIC. INSTEAD, THE UNDER SECRETARY 
PREFERRED TO ENCOURAGE INDICATIONS THAT THE RUSSIANS ARE 
STARTING TO SEE MILOSEVIC AS THE CENTRAL PROBLEM. 
2. 1.E4 IN A SUBSEQUENT SHORTER MEETING WITH DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT, BILDT REITERATED THE SAME POINTS. IN 
RESPONSE THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ALSO EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT 
BILDT'S SUGGESTED APPROACH TO THE RUSSIANS, AND STRESSED 
THAT KOSOVAR ALBANIANS (AND OTHERS) WERE UNLIKELY TO ACCEPT 
A NEW, "LOOSER" CONSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENT. DISCUSSION OE 
THIS ISSUE SHOULD NEVERTHELESS CONTINUE, TALBOTT CONCLUDED. 
END SUMMARY. 
MEETING WITH UNDER SECRETARY PICKERING 
3. (U) UN SPECIAL ENVOY FOR THE BALKANS CARL BILDT VISITED 
WASHINGTON mAy 3-5, MEETING SENATORS WARNER (R.-VIRGINIA) 
AND HAGEL (R-NEBRASKA), LEON FUERTH AT THE NSA, AND UNDER 
SECRETARY OF DEFENSE FOR POLICY SLOCOMBE AT THE PENTAGON, 
AMONG OTHERS. BILDT MET WITH UNDER SECRETARY PICKERING AND 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT IN THE DEPARTMENT ON MAY 4. 
ACCOMPANYING THE SPECIAL ENVOY WERE SENIOR ADVISOR LISA 
BUTTENHEIM, SPECIAL ASSISTANT CHRISTOPHER GEIDT, AND AN 
ADVISOR FROM THE UN INFORMATION CENTER, LINDA JAMISON. 
ALSO AT THE MEETING WITH UNDER SECRETARY PICKERING WERE 
S/SA AMBASSADOR PARDEW AND EUR DAS SWIGERT. 
4. 1"5.) FOLLOWING GREETINGS, BILDT TOLD PICKERING THAT, IN 
A DISCUSSION OF THE BALKANS THE PREVIOUS DAY, SENATOR 
WARNER HAD INDICATED THAT HE WANTED TO REAFFIRM THE ROLE OF 
THE SENATE IN FOREIGN POLICY. BILDT HAD URGED SENATOR 
WARNER "NOT TO DO ANYTHING THAT WOULD CAUSE TROUBLE," AND 
TO CONCENTRATE ON RESOLVING THE POLITICAL PROBLEMS OF THE 
BALKANS, NOT TO FOCUS PURELY ON FUNDING ISSUES. BILDT DID 
NOT FORESEE PROBLEMS ARISING FROM THE WARNER AMENDMENT.

THE POST-MILOSEVIC ERA 
5. -(-6+ EXPLAINING THAT SECRETARY-GENERAL ANNAN HAD ASKED 
HIM TO FOCUS ON LONG-TERM ISSUES IN THE BALKANS, BILDT 
INDICATED THAT IT WAS NOW CLEAR THAT SLOBODAN MILOSEVIC 
WOULD EVENTUALLY DEPART FROM POWER. WHEN THAT HAPPENED 
THERE WOULD BE A BRIEF WINDOW OF OPPORTUNITY FOR THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY -- PERHAPS ONLY A COUPLE OF WEEKS 
-- TO PROVIDE SOME NEW CONSTITUTIONAL DISPENSATION IN ORDER 
TO AVOID INSTABILITY. BILDT EXPECTED THAT MONTENEGRO AND 
KOSOVO WOULD SEEK INDEPENDENCE AFTER MILOSEVIC LEFT THE 
SCENE. A LOOSER ARRANGEMENT COULD ACCOMMODATE THOSE 
DESIRES; BILDT PREFERRED THAT KOSOVO, MONTENEGRO AND SERBIA 
BECOME AUTONOMOUS REPUBLICS WITHIN A NEW STATE. THIS WOULD 
PREVENT IRREDENTIST EFFORTS THAT COULD DESTABILIZE 
NEIGHBORING COUNTRIES, PARTICULARLY MACEDONIA. 
6. --1$4.,BILDT THOUGHT THE TIME WOULD BE RIGHT FOLLOWI.NG 
KOSOVAR ELECTIONS TO BEGIN CLARIFYING KOSOVO'S "INTERNAL 
CONSTITUTIONAL POSITION." PICKERING AGREED ON THE NEED FOR 
SUCH A CLARIFICATION, AND SUGGESTED THAT RAMBOUILLET WOULD 
BE USEFUL IN THE EFFORT. BILDT AGREED. 
7. )N.,BILDT FORESAW A SERIES OF PROBLEMS FOLLOWING 
MILOSEVIC'S DEPARTURE. KOSOVO WOULD BE ONLY A PART OF THE 
PROBLEM. FOR INSTANCE, MONTENEGRIN SEPARATIST AMBITIONS 
WOULD COME TO THE FORE AFTER MILOSEVIC LEFT. THE PREVLAKA 
PENINSULA COULD ALSO BE A POINT OF CONFLICT THAT•COULD 
INVOLVE CROATIA. BILDT BELIEVED A POST-MILOSEVIC BELGRADE 
WOULD SEEK A "STABILIZING PROCESS," AND WOULD PROBABLY 
WELCOME A "THREE REPUBLIC SOLUTION," WITH SERBIA, 
MONTENEGRO AND KOSOVO POSSESSING GREATER AUTONOMY THAN EVER 
BEFORE INSIDE A NEW FEDERAL REPUBLIC OF YUGOSLAVIA (FRY). 
PICKERING NOTED THAT THE KOSOVARS WERE STRONGLY OPPOSED TO 
REMAINING IN THE FRY; BILDT SUGGESTED THE INTERNATIONAL 
COMMUNITY WOULD HAVE TO IMPOSE SUCH A SOLUTION 
EUROPEAN INTEGRATION AND THE FRY 
8. l'ISNL EUROPE WOULD HAVE A KEY ROLE IN PROVIDING A 
PLATFORM FOR STABILIZATION OF A POST-MILOSEVIC FRY IN 
BILDT'S VIEW. FIRST, FOLLOWING MILOSEVIC'S DEPARTURE, 
EUROPE SHOULD OFFER THE FRY EVENTUAL ACCESSION TO THE EU. 
THE BREAKTHROUGH ON TURKISH ACCESSION LAST YEAR HELD OPEN 
SUCH A POSSIBILITY, PERHAPS IN 10 OR 20 YEARS, BILDT SAID. 
SECOND, EUROPE WOULD NEED TO EMPHASIZE THAT REFORMING THE 
COUNTRY'S INSTITUTIONS FOR ACCESSION WOULD MAKE THEM A 
"CONVEYOR BELT" TO THE EU, NOT "A WAITING ROOM." FINALLY, 
THE STABILITY PACT WOULD HELP TO PREPARE THE COUNTRY FOR 
INTEGRATION INTO EUROPE RATHER THAN INDEPENDENCE, BILDT 
SAID. PICKERING AGREED THAT THE STABILITY PACT SHOULD HELP 
TO PREPARE FOR EU MEMBERSHIP. INTEGRATION INTO EUROPE, IN 
PICKERING'S VIEW, WOULD SUBLIMATE INTER-ETHNIC ANTAGONISM. 
PICKERING NOTED THAT THE U.S. SOUGHT TO SUPPORT DJUKANOVIC 
IN MONTENEGRO WHILE AVOIDING A CONFLICT WITH SERBIA; BUT

A RUSSIAN APPROACH 
9. Mi-BILDT STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF A UNITED 
INTERNATIONAL EFFORT IN THE POST—MILOSEVIC ERA THAT 
INCLUDED THE RUSSIANS. HE KNEW RUSSIAN FOREIGN MINISTER 
IVANOV FROM THE 1995 DAYTON CONFERENCE. IF THE RUSSIANS NO 
LONGER SUSPECTED "A HIDDEN U.S. AGENDA" OF SUPPORTING 
INDEPENDENCE FOR KOSOVO "OR A DRIFT TO INDEPENDENCE," EILDT 
CONTINUED, THEN THEY COULD DO MORE•TO INFLUENCE BELGRADE, 
ESPECIALLY TO INFLUENCE THE SERBIAN MILITARY. IN RESPONSE, 
PICKERING INDICATED THAT, WHILE THE RUSSIANS PROBABLY COULD 
BE MORE HELPFUL IF REASSURED ON INDEPENDENCE, IT WAS NOT 
CLEAR THAT THEY COULD DELIVER ON MILOSEVIC. MOREOVER, IT 
WAS NOT CLEAR THAT THE U.S. COULD GUARANTEE KOSOVAR 
COOPERATION IN VIEW OF KOSOVAR DESIRES FOR INDEPENDENCE. 
PICKERING PREFERRED TO ENCOURAGE INDICATIONS THAT THE 
RUSSIANS ARE STARTING TO SEE THAT MILOSEVIC IS THE CENTRAL 
PROBLEM; RE—STARTING THE CONTACT GROUP WAS PART OF AN 
EFFORT TO DO SO. PICKERING INDICATED THAT THE U.S. WAS 
SENSITIVE TO RUSSIA'S CONCERNS ABOUT KOSOVAR INDEPENDENCE, 
AND THAT WE HAD CONCERNS OF OUR OWN ABOUT IT. 
10. BILDT MENTIONED THAT HE WOULD SOON BE TALKING TO 
THE CHINESE. BOTH PICKERING AND DAS SWIGERT SAW THE 
CHINESE AS HAVING A "MALIGN INFLUENCE" ON THE REGION BY 
PROMOTING THEIR SHORT—TERM COMMERCIAL INTERESTS TO 
MILOSEVIC'S BENEFIT. SWIGERT POINTED OUT THAT IN PRIVATE 
DISCUSSIONS, A BROAD RANGE OF SERB OPPOSITION 
REPRESENTATIVES MADE CLEAR THAT WHEN THEY CAME TO POWER, 
THEY WOULD NOT RESPECT MILOSEVIC'S SHADY BUSINESS DEALS. 
PICKERING NOTED THAT AS A CONSEQUENCE THE CHINESE NOW ALSO 
FACED THE PROSPECT OF BAD LOANS IN THE FRY. BILDT REPLIED 
THAT HE SOUGHT TO CLOSE OFF SUPPORT FOR MILOSEVIC FROM ALL 
SIDES, INCLUDING THE CHINESE. 
REGIONAL IMPACT 
11. 11SL TURNING TO A DISCUSSION OF THE IMPACT OF THE 
FUTURE DETARTURE OF MILOSEVIC ON THE REGION, BILDT 
COMMENTED THAT THE FUNDAMENTAL PROBLEM FOR THE FORMER 
YUGOSLAVIA HAS BEEN UNCERTAINTY OVER THE COUNTRY'S 
POLITICAL FRAMEWORK. WHEN YUGOSLAVIA DISSOLVED IN THE 
EARLY 1990'S, THE CYCLE OF CONFLICT BEGAN. SETTING A NEW 
FRAMEWORK "IN CEMENT" WAS NECESSARY TO STOP THAT CYCLE, 
BILDT SAID. PICKERING REPLIED THAT A SHORT TRANSITION 
WOULD BE HARD FOR THE BALKANS, WHICH WERE NOT AMENABLE TO A 
"MECHANISTIC" APPROACH. PICKERING RECOMMENDED OFFERING A 
SERIES OF GOALS INSTEAD OF EMPHASIZING JUST THE LIMITS 
PREFERRED BY THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY. BILDT AGREED THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY NEEDED A COMMON SET OF GOALS, 
BECAUSE MANY IN THE FRY WOULD BE UNHAPPY WHEN MILOSEVIC 
LEFT. BILDT SAID HE WAS ESPECIALLY CONCERNED ABOUT THE 
STABILITY OF MACEDONIA AND BOSNIA. PICKERING NOTED HE WAS 
WORRIED ABOUT ALBANIA'S PROSPECTS AS WELL. BILDT REPLIED 
THAT ALBANIA "AS A FAILED STATE" WOULD BE A SOURCE OF 
"MISCHIEF," BUT KOSOVAR ALBANIAN IRREDENTISM COULD 
DESTABILIZE MACEDONIA, TRIGGERING A WAR. BILDT SAID HE 
WOULD DISCUSS THESE VIEWS WITH SECRETARY—GENERAL ANNAN ON 
MAY 5. ANNAN WANTED BILDT TO MAKE A REPORT TO THE SECURITY 
COUNCIL ON THESE QUESTIONS.

SUPPORTED TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY. REASSURING THE RUSSIANS

RESPONSE IF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY, IN ORDER TO 
REASSURE THE RUSSIANS, APPEARED TO PRE-JUDGE THE QUESTION

15."-rthi,..SENIOR ADVISOR O'BRIEN ASKED HOW BILDT PROPOSED TO

ERA. BILDT RESPONDED THAT THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY 
SHOULD FIRST AGREE ON ITS GOALS; HE WAS LESS CONCERNED WITH 
PUBLIC PRESENTATION THAN WITH ACHIEVING AN INTERNAL 
AGREEMENT ON WHAT SHOULD BE DONE. TALBOTT NOTED THE 
LIKELIHOOD OF SUCH A DISCUSSION SOON BECOMING PUBLIC. 
BILDT ACKNOWLEDGED THAT POSSIBILITY, ADDING THAT WHEN 
MILOSEVIC LEFT -- WHETHER IN FIVE YEARS OR FIVE DAYS -- THE 
PEOPLE OF THE FRY WOULD ASK FOR OUR VIEWS. WE WOULD HAVE A 
BRIEF CHANCE FOR SOME INFLUENCE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
RESPONDED THAT WE WOULD NEED TO MOVE "BRISKLY" TOWARD. 
ELECTIONS, THE KEY PRINCIPLES OF WHICH WOULD BE AUTONOMOUS 
AND DEMOCRATIC SELF-GOVERNANCE. TALBOTT HAD HOPED THE 
PROCESS OF RADICALIZATION WOULD BE REVERSED IF THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY INTERVENED TO STOP BELGRADE'S 
DEPREDATIONS; SOME REVERSAL HAD INDEED OCCURRED, BUT THE 
KOSOVAR LEADERSHIP REMAINED SKEPTICAL AND UNLIKELY TO 
APPRECIATE THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A CONSTITUENT AND AN 
AUTONOMOUS REPUBLIC IN A POST-MILOSEVIC-FRY. IN CLOSING, 
BILDT NOTED THE POTENTIAL FOR REGIONAL INSTABILITY 
FOLLOWING MILOSEVIC'S DEPARTURE, AND THE NEED FOR CONTINUED 
LONG-TERM PLANNING.
