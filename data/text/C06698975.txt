4-64- SUBJECT: BOSNIA AND OTHER BILATERAL CONCERNS 
4. +St MAMEDOV BEGAN BY TELLING THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, 
THAT HE WAS SENDING A PERSONAL MESSAGE THROUGH RUSSIAN 
AMBASSADOR VORONTSOV AND ASKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TO 
GIVE IT SPECIAL ATTENTION. HE SAID THE MESSAGE DEALT 
WITH THE NUCLEAR DIMENSION OF EUROPEAN SECURITY. MR. 
TALBOTT ASSURED MAMEDOV HE WOULD LOOK AT THE MESSAGE 
CAREFULLY. 
5. 464- MAMEDOV CONTINUED BY RAISING THE ISSUE OF 
U.S.-RUSSIAN COOPERATION IN BOSNIA. MAMEDOV SAID HE 
KNEW PRESIDENT CLINTON CONSIDERED THE TWO NATIONS' WORK 
IN THE REGION TO BE ONE OF THE MORE PROMISING ELEMENTS 
IN THE EUROPEAN LANDSCAPE AND THE GOR WAS CONCERNED THAT 
AMBASSADOR FROWICK WAS NOT BRINGING UNITY TO THE CONTACT 
GROUP BY HIS ACTIONS. THEREFORE, FOREIGN MINISTER 
PRIMAKOV HAD ASKED MAMEDOV TO ASK MR. TALBOTT TO TAKE A 
PERSONAL INTEREST IN RUSSIAN CONCERNS. 
6. ** THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REPLIED THAT HE TOOK GREAT.
INTEREST IN BALKAN ISSUES AND BELIEVED AMBASSADOR 
FROWICK WAS DOING A GOOD JOB ON BEHALF OF THE OSCE. 
MAMEDOV REPEATED THAT HE BELIEVED FROWICK MAY UNDERMINE 
THE IMPORTANT WORK OF THE CONTACT GROUP IF HE DOES NOT 
FULFILL THE AGREEMENTS PREVIOUSLY DISCUSSED. MR. 
TALBOTT ASKED MAMEDOV TO CONVEY TO FM PRIMAKOV HIS 
ASSESSMENT OF AMBASSADOR FROWICK ALONG WITH HIS 
ASSURANCE THAT HE WOULD TAKE A CLOSE LOOK AT RUSSIAN 
CONCERNS. BOTH MR. TALBOTT AND MAMEDOV AGREED TO STAY 
IN CLOSE TOUCH ON BOSNIA THROUGH MESSRS. TARNOFF AND 
KORNBLUM AND THEIR RUSSIAN COUNTERPARTS. 
7. 44+ MAMEDOV THEN TURNED TO ARMS CONTROL TALKS IN 
GENEVA. HE SAID THE GOR WAS NOT HAPPY WITH THE COURSE 
OF TALKS ON ABM DEMARCATION. ALTHOUGH THE GOR DID NOT 
EXPECT SMOOTH SAILING, RUSSIA BELIEVED THAT THERE WAS AN 
UNDERSTANDING THAT PHASE I WOULD BE IMPLEMENTED BEFORE 
PHASE II NEGOTIATIONS BEGAN. IF SUCH AN EXPLICIT 
ARRANGEMENT IS NOT ON PAPER, MAMEDOV SAID PHASE I WOULD

8. —(4+ FINALLY, MAMEDOV TURNED TO AFGHANISTAN. HE 
SAID FM PRIMAKOV RECEIVED A SYMPATHETIC RESPONSE FROM 
THE PRESIDENT WHEN HE RAISED THE ISSUE OF U.S. SUPPORT 
FOR RUSSIAN INITIATIVES IN THE REGION. MAMEDOV SAID HE 
HOPED THE U.S. WOULD USE ITS INFLUENCE WITH CERTAIN 
COUNTRIES IN THE REGION TO PREVENT THE FIGHTING FROM 
CONTINUING INDEFINITELY. MR. TALBOTT REPLIED THAT THERE 
WAS ALREADY A LONG HISTORY OF CONFLICT IN THE REGION AND 
THAT IT WAS NOT IN THE INTEREST OF EITHER THE U.S. OR 
RUSSIA TO PROLONG IT. 
9. -4-St IN CLOSING, MAMEDOV SAID HE HOPED TO MEET MR. 
TALBOTT SOMETIME AFTER THE U.S. ELECTION. HE ALSO 
SUGGESTED MR. TALBOTT GET A READOUT ON THE MEETING 
BETWEEN SECDEF PERRY AND PM CHERNOMYRDIN IN MOSCOW. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY ASSURED MAMEDOV HE WOULD BE BRIEFED AND 
LOOKED FORWARD TO RECEIVING HIS LETTER FROM AMBASSADOR 
VORONTSOV ON OCTOBER 21.
