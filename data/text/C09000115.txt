B6 
Thank you for your letter of December 6 regarding the 
Fincom Group's efforts to develop a financial institution in 
Russia. 
As you may know, the USG has established two funds to 
assist private sector development in Russia. These 
organizations represent the best opportunity sector initiative 
in the former Soviet Union. Moreover, they have autonomy to 
decide whether or not to cooperate with other financial 
institutions. 
,Accordingly, I would suggest you contact either the Russian 
American Enterprise Fund (212) 902-8770, attention Mr. Gerald 
Corrigan, Chairman; or the Fund for Large Enterprises in Russia 
(212) 632-6176, attention Mr. Michael Blumenthal, Chairman. 
I wish you good luck in your venture.
