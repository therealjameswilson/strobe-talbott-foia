Dear Jerry: 
I am replying to your letter of April 16th inviting me to 
take part in the Academy Program this summer. I am still 
working out some travel plans that will take me to the former 
Soviet Union during the sumrner, so I hope you will give me a 
little time to get back to you on dates. Thank you for being 
available to talk on the phone in recent weeks. I trust you 
got the material we sent you on various G-7 matters. I gather 
I am going to be seeing you in the next week and hope we will 
have a chance for some quiet conversation on that occasion. 
Best regards. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Taatt 
Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Adviser to The Secretary 
on The New Independent States 
Mr. E. Gerald Corrigan, 
President,

New York, New York.
