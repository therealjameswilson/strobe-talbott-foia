I deeply' regret not being with you this evening. As you 
know, the President and Secretary of State asked me to represent 
them at the annual awards dinner of the Appeal of Conscience 
Foundation, which is presenting a special award to Patriarch 
Aleksey II; I also have to deliver a speech outlining 
Administration policy on freedom of religion. 
Please convey my greetings to President Brazauskas. I
attended a meeting he and his two Baltic colleagues held with 
President Clinton on Monday afternoon. President Clinton told 
me afterwards that the meeting made a powerful impression on 
him. As he told the three Baltic Presidents, he is determined 
to use American influence to the utmost to achieve the goal we 
share -- a peaceful, prosperous, secure future for Lithuania, 
Latvia and Estonia. As President Clinton stressed in his 
meeting Monday with the three Baltic Presidents, this is a major 
objective of our Administration. President Clinton has 
underscored his personal commitment to achieving this objective 
in both his face-to-face meetings with President Yeltsin as well 
as in phone calls and correspondence.
