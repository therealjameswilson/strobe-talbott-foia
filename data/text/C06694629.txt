SUBJECT: NEW ZEALAND'S APEC POLICY FOR 1995 
1. SUMMARY: NEW ZEALAND INTENDS TO BE ACTIVE IN 
IMPLEMENTING THE BOGOR DECLARATION, AND WANTS TO WORK 
CLOSELY WITH THE UNITED STATES. WHILE AVOIDING SMALLER 
SUB-APEC GROUPS IN THE PAST, NEW ZEALAND NOW WANTS TO 
WORK WITH THE U.S. IN LIKE-MINDED SUB-GROUPS. THE GNZ 
BELIEVES THAT THE USG IS MODERATELY ENCOURAGED BY 
INDICATIONS THAT JAPAN TAKES SERIOUSLY ITS 
RESPONSIBILITY TO ADVANCE THE BOGOR COMMITMENTS. END 
SUMMARY. 
2. AT A DINNER ON JANAURY 30, DURING THE VISIT OF 
DEPSEC TALBOTT, SECRETARY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS AND TRADE 
RICHARD NOTTAGE PASSED A GNZ APEC POLICY PAPER TO 
SPECIAL ASSISTANT TO THE PRESIDENT ROTH, SAYING THAT 
RATHER THAN SUMMARIZE THE PAPER, HE WOULD JUST GIVE IT 
TO THE USG DELEGATION. THE PAPER COMMENTS, INTER ALIA, 
ON GNZ VIEWS OF USG POLICY, AND WHILE NOTTAGE CLEARLY 
DIDN'T MIND IT BEING PASSED TO WASHINGTON, WE DOUBT IF 
THE MFAT DRAFTERS INTENDED THAT IT BE SO USED. IT 
APPEARS TO EMBASSY THAT EXCEPT FOR THE FIRST THREE-POINT 
SECTION, "KEY POINTS TO GET ACROSS TO THE UNITED 
STATES," THE REST OF THE PAPER WAS INTENDED TO BE 
BACKGROUND FOR NOTTAGE, ESPECIALLY THE THREE POINT 
SECTION, "US VIEWS."
