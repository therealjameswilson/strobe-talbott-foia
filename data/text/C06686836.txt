Thank you for your letter of August 26, 1993, seeking support for 
a Justice Department effort to provide technical assistance to 
the Russian antitrust agency. Antitrust is clearly an important 
component of economic reform in Russia. 
Your Deputy Assistant Attorney General-gesignate, Diane Wood,'did 
meet with Ambassador Tom Simons, the Coordinator of U.S. 
Assistance to the NIS, earlier this month to discss this issue. 
As I am sure Diane briefed you, Tom is impressed 'with the work 
you have done in Central and Eastern Europe and interested in 
seeing the antitrust project move forward. As you know, A.I.D. 
has an ongoing program that will have to form the framewqrk for a 
Justice (and FTC) contribution. Tom and Diane talked about ways 
that this could happen. I trust this effort will result in 
complementary efforts. 
Thank you for your interest. I look forward to continued 
cooperation.
