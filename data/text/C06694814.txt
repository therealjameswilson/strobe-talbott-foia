2. DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT, DEPUTY NATIONAL 
SECURITY ADVISOR SANDY BERGER, VICE PRESIDENTIAL 
NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR LEON FUERTH, DEPUTY SPECIAL 
ADVISOR FOR HAITI AMBASSADOR JAMES DOBBINS, AND 
ACCOMPANYING DELEGATION HELD TWO MEETINGS WITH 
HAITIAN PARLIAMENTARIANS AND LEADING POLITICIANS 
DURING A ONE-DAY VISIT TO PORT AU PRINCE ON OCTOBER 7 
(REFTEL). 
3. IN THE FIRST MEETING WITH PARLIAMENTARY DEPUTIES, 
PARTY LEADERS AND THE MAYOR OF PORT AU PRINCE, THE 
U.S. DELEGATION EMPHASIZED THE USG'S COMTTMENT TO 
STRENGTHENING THE FULL RANGE OF DEMOCRAT/C 
INSTITUTIONS IN HAITI. THE DELEGATION ENCOURAGED THE 
WORK OF THE PARLIAMENTARIANS, NOTING THAT THE U.S. 
ROLE IS IMPORTANT BUT LIMITED, AND THAT IT IS HAITI'S 
LEGITIMATE POLITICAL REPRESENTATIVES WHO MUST 
DETERMINE HAITI'S FUTURE. SOME HAITIAN POLITICAL 
FIGURES NOTED THE NECESSITY OF HOLDING FREE AND FAIR 
ELECTIONS SOON. OTHERS EMPHASIZED THAT HAITI'S 
INSTITUTIONS MUST BE STRENGTHENED, NOT TO SERVE ONE 
MAN'S INTERESTS, BUT THE NATION AS A WHOLE. THE 
POLITICIANS COMMENTED THAT THE MNF COULD BE SEEN AS A 
POSITIVE PRESENCE THAT ENSURES PEACE OR, IF WRONGLY 
USED, AS AN OCCUPATION FORCE THAT CREATES DIVISION. 
THE POLITICIANS NOTED THAT ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT IS 
CRITICAL FOR POLITICAL CHANGE. 
4. THE DELEGATION HELD A BRIEF, SEPARATE MEETING 
WITH HAITIAN SENATORS, WHO HAD JUST SUCCEEDED IN 
PASSING THE CRUCIAL AMNESTY LAW. THE DELEGATION 
CONGRATULATED THE SENATORS FOR DEMONSTRATING THAT 
HAITI HAS FUNCTIONING DEMOCRATIC INSTITUTIONS. THE 
SENATORS AGREED THAT THE NEXT STEP IN RESOLUTION OF 
THE POLITICAL CR/SIS SHOULD BE THE DEPARTURE OF DE 
FACTO PRESIDENT JONASSAINT. THE DELEGATION DID NOT 
HAVE AN OPPORTUNITY TO CLEAR THIS MESSAGE BEFORE 
DEPARTING PORT AU PRINCE. END SUMMARY. 
MEETING WITH HAITIAN PARLIAMENTARIANS
