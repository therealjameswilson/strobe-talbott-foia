Yesterday's Bretton Woods meeting at the International Club 
was most informative and instructive. Our Government, the World 
Bank, and the IMF certainly have a slew of irons in the fire, good% 
many of which are not fully comprehended in the hinterland. Your 
briefing was superb, right on target. 
Since you mentioned that you are meeting with Ukrainian mili-
tary big-wigs soon, I am taking the liberty of enclosing a paper 
prepared by Col. Yury I. Grynenko, Ukrainian Armed Forces, who com-
leted on June 12, 1993, a year of duty at the U.S. War College in 
Carlisle Barracks, Pa. A number of us in the Ukrainian American com-
munity in NYC and in North Port, F1. had the pleasure of meeting the 
Colonel, his wife, and daughter during the Xmas holiday period. They 
shaped up well. I hope you find the Colonel's paper interesting. 
The Institute and the Ukrainian American Veterans (30 Posts in 
eastern USA) at the request of Lt. Col. David Grob, Military Attache 
in Kiev, and with approval of Ambassador Roman,Popadiuk, sponsored 
the travel costs of the families of Col. Grynenko and Colonel 
Alexander Volynsky, who was attending the Command' and General Staff 
College in Leavenworth, KS. They have all returned safely to their 
homeland. 
We are in the process of trying to help three new officers who 
arrived last month. They are Col. Alexander Shemets at Ft.Leavenworth, 
Col. Sergiy Mokrenets at Carlisle Barracks, and Col. Anatoly Gritcenko 
at the Air Force College, Maxwell Air Base, Montgomery, Alabama. I'm 
not sure of how much we can do, but we are trying. 
Looking forward to hearing you again in the,near future. Stay 
healthy, lot of good luck, and beaucoup success in all your activities. 
Sincerely,
