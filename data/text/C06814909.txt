4. ~ THE MOLDOVANS TOLD US THAT WHEN PUTIN RAISED THE
ISSUE OF RUSSIAN "PEACEKEEPERS," DURING HIS VISIT TO
CHISINAU, LUCINSCHI SOUGHT TO DEFLECT RUSSIAN IDEAS FOR A
RUSSIAN-DOMINATED CIS FORCE BY INSISTING ON A MULTILATERAL
FORCE, OF LIMITED DURATION AND PRECISE MANDATE WITH
MULTINATIONAL PARTICIPATION AND INTERNATIONAL OBSERVERS
UNDER OSCE AUSPICES. THE MOLDOVANS ALSO LAID. DOWN CLEAR
PUBLIC MARKERS TO THIS EFFECT, BOTH AT THE RPC AND AT THE
JULY OSCE PARLIAMENTARY ASSEMBLY IN BUCHAREST. WE
INDICATED OUR SUPPORT, IN PRINCIPLE, FOR ACTIVE
CONSIDERATION WITHIN THE OSCE, LED BY THE C-I-0 TOGETHER
WITH INTERESTED DELEGATIONS, OF A POSSIBLE OSCE OPERATION
IN SUPPORT OF AN EVENTUAL SETTLEMENT. WE ARGUED THAT ANY
DETAILED DISCUSSION OF SUCH A FORCE SHOULD ONLY TAKE PLACE
IN THE CONTEXT OF AN ACTUAL SETTLEMENT AND BE SHAPED BY ITS
REQUIREMENTS. IT IS PREMATURE TO PREJUDGE QUESTIONS OF
NUMBERS, COMPOSITION AND COMMAND ARRANGEMENTS. EFFORTS
SIMPLY TO "EXEMPT" RIGHT NOW A SIGNIFICANT PORTION OF
RUSSIAN FORCES AND EQUIPMENT IN TRANSNISTRIA (AND TO ASSERT
THEIR CONTINUED PRESENCE AS A PREREQUISITE FOR ANY SORT OF
POLITICAL SETTLEMENT) WOULD BE THE REVERSE OF THIS PROCESS
AND WOULD BE CONTRADICTORY WITH THE ISTANBUL COMMITMENTS
FOR COMPLETE WITHDRAWAL. (NOTE: WE WILL BE LOOKING TO
FOLLOW UP ON EXAMINING MODALITIES FOR AN OSCE-LED
STABILIZATION FORCE IN VIENNA AND OSCE CAPITALS TO ENSURE
THAT THIS OPTION REMAINS A VISIBLE AND VIABLE ALTERNATIVE
TO A RUSSIAN-ONLY PRESENCE, END NOTE);
PUTIN'S VISIT AND RUSSIAN INTIMIDATION
5 ~ WE UNDERSTAND~ FROM A VARI ET Y OF SOURCES ~ THAT
PUTIN'S VISIT TO MOLDOVA INCLUDED BLUNT EXPRESSIONS OF
HOSTILITY TO FOREIGN MINISTER TABACARU, INCLUDING IMPLIED
DEMANDS FOR HIS REMOVAL. IT ALSO APPEARS TABACARU HAS BEEN
TOLD TO MAKE HIMSELF SCARCE DURING PRIMAKOV'S VISIT.
TOGETHER, THESE DEVELOPMENTS CREATE AN UNPLEASANT
ATMOSPHERE OF RUSSIAN INTIMIDATION. AMBASSADOR SHOULD BE
AWARE OF THIS IN HIS MEETING WITH PRIMAKOV. IT IS POSSIBLE
THAT ADVANCE CONSULTATION WITH US WILL BE USED BY PRIMAKOV
TO PUT FURTHER PRESSURE ON THE MOLDOVANS; FOR THIS REASON,
POINTS BELOW ON MOLDOVAN SOVEREIGNTY AND ON THE
UNACCEPTABILITY OF A RUSSIAN-IMPOSED "STABILIZATION FORCE"
ARE ESPECIALLY IMPORTANT.
