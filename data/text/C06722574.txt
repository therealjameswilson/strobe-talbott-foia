ST.IMMARY 
1. et-DURING A FEBRUARY 2? WORKING LUNCH WITH UZBEKISTANI 
FOREIGN MINISTER KAMILOV, ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT REVIEWED 
U.S. POLICY TOWARD IRAN, EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR 
UZBEKISTAN'S PUBLIC SUPPORT FOR OUR IRAQ POLICY, AND URGED 
THE UZBEK SIDE TO WORK VIGOROUSLY WITH US TO ADDRESS 
EXISTING OBSTACLES TO AMERICAN TRADE AND INVESTMENT. 
KAMILOV SPOKE OF UZBEK CONCERNS OVER AGGRESSIVE RUSSIAN 
PROMOTION OF THE CIS; TALBOTT RESPONDED THAT USG POLICY WAS 
TO VIEW THE CIS AS AN INSTITUTION CAUTIOUSLY AND SEEK THE 
VIEWS OF THE OTHER NIS AS EVENTS UNFOLD. COMING AT THE END 
OF THE INAUGURAL U.S.-UZBEKISTAN JOINT COMMISSION, THIS 
SESSION ALLOWED THE ACTING SECRETARY TO ENGAGE IN AN 
OVERVIEW OF THE BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP. END SUMMARY

KAMILOV ON RUSSIA AND THE CIS 
V. ('6.;.) BORIS YELTSIN, CONTINUED KAMILOV, HAS DECLARED THIS 
"THE YEAR OF THE CIS.', ACCORDING TO AN OFFICIAL RUSSIAN 
DOCUMENT ON NATIONAL SECURITY PUBLISHED IN DECEMBER (NOTE: 
THE RUSSIAN NATIONAL SECURITY BLUEPRINT, PUBLISHED IN 
DECEMBER, 1997) RELATIONS WITH THE NIS WERE DISCUSSED NOT 
UNDER THE SECTION ON RUSSIA AND THE INTERNATIONAL

THAT THE MORE THE NIS DEMONSTRATE THEIR SOVEREIGNTY AND 
INDEPENDENCE, THE GREATER THE THREAT TO RUSSIA. THIS HAS 
BEEN ACCOMPANIED BY MUCH RUSSIAN EXHORTING OF THE NIS TO 
STRENGTHEN THE CIS, BOTH MILITARILY AND POLITICALLY.

TOURISM IN UZBEKISTAN 
16.. THE FIRST LADY HAD SPOKEN OF HER VERY FOND 
IMPRESSIONS OF UZBEKISTAN WITH THE ACTING SECRETARY, AND 
HAD ASKED WHAT THE PROSPECTS FOR TOURISM WERE THERE. 
KAMILOV CITED TWO HANDICAPS: THE LACK OF WESTERN-QUALITY 
INFRASTRUCTURE (ESP. HOTELS) AND THE NEED TO ESTABLISH 
DIRECT AIR LINKS FROM THE WEST TO THE MAIN TOURIST SITES OF 
SAMARKAND AND BUKHARA. STILL, AMB. SAFAEV REPORTED, 
OVERALL TOURISM LEVELS HAD RISEN FROM 120,000 IN 1993 TO 
700,000 LAST YEAR. 
EAST-WEST ENERGY TRANSPORT CORRIDOR 
17. '0Q) THERE WAS A BRIEF DISCUSSION OF EAST-WEST ENERGY 
TRANSPORT LINES, INCLUDING A CROSS-CASPIAN PIPELINE. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY RECALLED DISCUSSING THESE ISSUES WITH 
PRESIDENT KARIMOV IN 1993, WHEN HE LAST VISITED TASHKENT. 
KARIMOV HAD PULLED OUT A MAP THEN TO DEMONSTRATE 
UZBEKISTAN'S GEO-POLITICAL IMPORTANCE. THE U.S. ALSO 
REMAINED VERY CONCERNED THAT IRAN NOT GAIN A DOMINANT ROLE 
IN THIS PROCESS. HE WAS CONFIDENT TASHKENT'S LEADERS WELL 
UNDERSTOOD THE IMPORTANCE OF PREVENTING TEHRAN FROM 
OBTAINING A STRANGLE-HOLD ON THE FLOW OF CASPIAN ENERGY. 
POLITICAL REFORM/DEMOCRACY
