Dear Mr. Shelkovnikov: 
I regret that you had to postpone your visit to Washington 
the week of August 9. We are most interested in talking with 
you soonabout your government's plans to modernize your Air 
Traffic Management (ATM) system. 
We strongly support Russia's plans to modernize and upgrade 
its infrastructure, particularly in the civil aviation area 
where we can both benefit. The increase in air traffic which 
such Modernization makes possible creates opportunities for 
both Russian and American airlines as well as hard currency 
revenues for your country. For example, the opening of new 
overflight routes In the Russian Far East has already brought 
benefits to both our countries and, with improvements in the 
ATM system, capacity on these routes will increase. 
The U.S. private sector has a vital role, to play in 
modernization of Russia's ATM system. U.S. companies are 
recognized leaders in ATM technology throughout the world and 
can provide Russia with the most advanced and cost-effective 
equipment and services in all aspects of air traffic 
management. I urge you to give fullest consideration to 
proposals from U.S. companies and I_hope we can meet during 
your next trip to Washington to discuss how we can_work 
together to make modernization of_the_Russian ATM system,a.
success.
