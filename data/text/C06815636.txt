2. ~ SUMMARY: IN AUGUST 14 MEETING WITH ACTING
SECRETARY TALBOTT, JAPANESE AMBASSADOR YANAI OUTLINED GOJ
POLITICAL CONCERNS WITH DEPLOYMENT OF CH-53D HELICOPTERS
TO IWAKUNI, REPEATING THE POINTS MADE BY FM KONO AUGUST 2.
TALBOTT ASSURED HIM THAT STATE, DOD AND NSC OFFICIALS WERE
CAREFULLY CONSIDERING JAPANESE CONCERNS, BUT WARNED THAT
PUBLIC PERCEPTIONS ABOUT THE CHANGING ASIA SECURITY SCENE
SHOULD NOT BE ALLOWED TO AFFECT DAY TO DAY DECISIONS.
THIS ISSUE SHOULD BE CONSIDERED IN THE CONTEXT OF OUR
LONG-TERN SECURITY ALLIANCE, HE NOTED. END SUMMARY
DECLASSIFIED
3. ~ AMBASSADOR YANAI INTRODUCED HIS CONCERNS BY NOTING
THAT THE JAPANESE WERE WELL AWARE THAT THIS WAS A
POLI T ICAL IS SUE I NOT A LEGAL IS SUE HE REPEATED THE THREE
REASONS FM KONO GAVE AMBASSADOR FOLEY AUGUST 2: THAT THE
GOJ OPPOSES THIS DEPLOYMENT BECAUSE 1) IT UNDERMINES THE
CRITICAL PREMISE FOR OKINAWAN ACCEPTANCE OF FUTENMA
RELOCATION WITHIN OKINAWA -- THAT MARINES AND THEIR AIR
ASSETS MUST BE CO-LOCATED; (2) IT IS UNACCEPTABLE TO THE
COMMUNITIES SURROUNDING IWAKUNI, WHICH MADE CLEAR WHEN
THEY ACCEPTED THE RELOCATION OF SIX KC-130 TANKERS FROM
FUTENMA AS PART OF SACO THAT THEY WOULD DO NO MORE; AND
(3) FORCE AUGMENTATION IN JAPAN GOES AGAINST THE DPRK-
RELATED TREND TOWARD RELAXING TENSIONS IN THE REGION. IN
SUM, HE INDICATED THAT OPPOSITION TO THE HELICOPTERS COULD
UNDERMINE THE FUTENMA RELOCATION AND COME AT A VERY HIGH
POLITICAL COST, (COMMENT: YANAI DID NOT SPECIFICALLY
CIITE THE FOREIGN MINISTER, NOR DID HE BRING UP THE PRIME
M1NISTER'S NAME.)
4. ~ ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT REPLIED THAT HE HAD A
GOOD UNDERSTANDING OF THE ATMOSPHERE SURROUNDING THIS
ISSUE GIVEN HIS RECENT VISITS TO MIYAZAKI AND OKINAWA.
ALSO, HE HAD WORKED CLOSELY WITH AMBASSADOR FOLEY TO MEET
FM KONO'S CONCERNS ON THE OKINAWA CURFEW. WHILE HE WAS
NOT IN A POSITION TO GIVE A DEFINITIVE ANSWER, HE ASSURED
YANAI THAT WE WERE SERIOUSLY CONSIDERING THE POINTS THE
GOJ HAS RAISED, AND THAT INTERAGENCY DISCUSSIONS WERE
UNDERWAY, HE SAID THE USG WAS DETERMINED TO WORK CLOSELY
WITH THE GOJ TO FIND THE CORRECT APPROACH THAT WOULD DEAL
WITH THE MILITARY EQUITIES AND BE SENSITIVE TO GOJ
CONCERNS ABOUT LOCAL REACTION. A CONTINUING FRANK
DIALOGUE WITH THE GOJ WOULD BE NECESSARY. REGARDING
YANAI'S THIRD POINT, TALBOTT WARNED THAT WE SHOULD NOT
ALLOW CONCERNS ABOUT PUBLIC PERCEPTIONS TO FREEZE OUR
FORCES IN PLACE; DAY TO DAY ADJUSTMENTS WERE STILL NEEDED.
IN FACT, IT WAS LIKELY THAT THE DPRK, ROK AND OTHERS IN
ASIA WOULD STUDY OUR OPERATIONAL DECISIONS IN DETAIL, AND
ANY SUGGESTION THAT POLITICAL PRESSURES WERE RESTRAINING
OPERATIONAL CHANGES COULD ENCOURAGE THE WRONG INSTINCTS IN
PYONGYANG. WHILE WE ARE NOT PLANNING ANY ESCALATION OF
FORCES,'WE SHOULD CONTINUE -BUSINESS AS USUAL, AND BALANCE
SOUND MILITARY REQUIREMENTS WITH LOCAL AND POLITICAL
