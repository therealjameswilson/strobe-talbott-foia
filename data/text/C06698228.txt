Subject: Jakarta Meeting 
John, 
I'm not sure what you may have heard about the Jakarta meeting, 
or from whom. Following for what it may be worth are my rough 
notes from Toria's "quick dump" based on her phone conversation 
with Jim Steinberg. 
The Secretary and Primakov met July 23 for 90 minutes, 
1800-1730. Also present were Markaryan, Tom Donilon, and Jim 
Steinberg. 
YMP: we support a joint statement on CTBT; we support the 
Ramaker text. 
WC: what can you tell me about Lebed and Rodionov? 
YMP: I talked with Lebed about the comments on religion; 
he's inexperienced. Foreign policy doesn't fall under 
him. Rodionov's good -- a rare, thoughtful general. 
BNY is back at work. I told him to rest, but he wants 
to work. 
(Discussion of CTBT. YMP agrees to approach Indians.) 
(ABM/TMD: "Bad." YMP insisted that the Presidents agreed in 
Moscow in April that Part 1 and Part 2 had to be completed 
before the GOR puts START-2 before the Duma. This was not 
true; our guys were not certain of that during the meeting 
Strobe tasked materials which can be faxed out so that the 
Secretary can follow up with Primakov. Strobe also suggested 
that we make clear we're willing to go to Chernomyrdin. There 
was some agreement between Primakov and the Secretary that 
Davis and Mamedov would meet again.) 
("Security of correspondence": Primakov raised. He was 
convinced that Gertz's leaker of his ABM/TMD letter works in 
DOD. He made repeated disparaging references to DOD. After a 
non-diScussion, it was agreed that there would be a closed 
"Primakov to Pickering to Christopher" channel. Not clear that 
this is anything new.)
