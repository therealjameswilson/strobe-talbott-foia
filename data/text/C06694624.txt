2. SUMMARY: IN CANBERRA, DEPSEC TALBOTT TWICE 
REGISTERED STRONG USG CONCERN ABOUT FM EVANS'S MID-JANUARY VISIT TO CUBA, STATING THAT AUSSIE PRAISE FOR ECONOMIC AND SOCIAL PROGRESS WOULD "GIVE HEART TO THE WRONG PEOPLE" AND ENCOURAGE CASTRO TO HOLD OUT. 
EVANS DENIED ANY SUCH INTENTION AND TRIED TO EXPLAIN THAT HE HAD PUSHED CUBAN LEADERS TO IMPROVE THEIR 
BEHAVIOR ON INTERNATIONAL ISSUES AND TO OPEN UP ON POLITICAL AND WORKER RIGHTS. EVANS'S BILATERAL AGENDA IN CUBA INCLUDEDDEBT REPAYMENT, MINING PARTICIPATION, . AND THE GOA UNSC CANDIDACY. HE SAID THERE IS MUCH TALK

4. LATER, EVANS PORTRAYED THE STOP IN HAVANA AS MORE THE FILLING OF A SCHEDULING GAP BETWEEN VISITS TO CALIFORNIA AND EUROPE.
