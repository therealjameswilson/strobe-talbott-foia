Dear Strobe, 
RELEASE IN FULL 
It was good to see you again at the Belorus Embassy unveiling. You mentioned a 
possible trip to Tallinn next month. Forgive the plug (it's my job) but a brief stopover in 
Riga could be worthwhile. We have elections in June, which will determine our next 
government and policies toward the CIS in the coming two years. President Gorbunovs, who 
I know remembers you from Chautauqua, will be a major figure in those elections as well, 
and would be delighted to meet with you. And depending upon the kind of publicity you're 
looking. for, you could be hailed as a returning hero. (That can be arranged.) Most 
importantly, you could pick up some useful information about our continuing interchange 
with our rather large neighbor. 
If it's feasible, let me know. 
Sincerely, 
O'ars Kalnins 
Ambassador
