2. DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT, DEPUTY NATIONAL 
SECURITY ADVISOR SANDY BERGER, VICE PRESIDENTIAL 
NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR LEON FUERTH, AMB. DOBBINS 
AND ACCOMPANYING DELEGATION MET OCT. 7 WITH THE 
HAITIAN TRANSITION TEAM (HTT) LED BY GEN. JEAN 
BELIOTTE, COMPRISING PLANNING MINISTER JEAN-MARIE 
CHERESTAL, FR. ANTOINE ADRIEN AND MIKE LEVY. AMB. 
SWING AND LTG SHELTON ATTENDED. 
3. DEPSEC TALBOTT AND MR. BERGER AFFIRMED PRESIDENT 
CLINTON'S DEEP-SEATED AND CONS/STENT COMMITMENT TO 
THE RESTORATION OF HAITI'S DEMOCRATICALLY-ELECTED 
GOVERNMENT AND PRESIDENT ARISTIDE. THEY ASSURED THE 
HTT THAT THE U.S. WANTS TO WORK CLOSELY WITH THEM TO 
ENSURE THE SUCCESSFUL, PEACEFUL AND SMOOTH RETURN OF 
PRES. ARISTIDE ON OCTOBER 15. WHILE THE U.S./MNF CAN 
CREATE A SECURE AND STABLE ENVIRONMENT, WE LOOK'TO 
THE HTT TO DO EVERYTHING POSSIBLE TO ENSURE THAT THE 
STRUCTURES OF EFFECTIVE GOVERNMENT ARE IN PLACE 
BEFORE OCT. 15. 
4. MINISTER CHERESTAL POINTED OUT THAT HE HAD MET 
WITH THE EMBASSY AND USAID THIS MORNING TO PRESENT A 
LIST OF NEEDS TO BE ADDRESSED FOR PRES. ARISTIDE'S 
RETURN. ANOTHER MEETING, TO INCLUDE APPROPRIATE 
SECURITY OFFICIALS, WILL BE HELD SATURDAY MORNING. 
THE HA/TIAN SIDE, HE ANNOUNCED, IS HUDDLING TODAY TO 
PREPARE FOR THAT SESSION. AMBASSADOR SWING 
EMPHASIZED THAT THE U.S. MISSION IS AT THE HTT'S 
DISPOSAL TO WORK THROUGHOUT THE WEEKEND. 
5. FR. ADRIEN APPEALED FOR QUICK ACTION TO FILL THE 
POWER VACUUM HE PERCEIVES AT PRESENT. MIN. CHERESTAL 
STATED THAT THE ACTING GOVERNMENT OF PM MALVAL IS 
FULLY CAPABLE OF GOVERNING, BUT IS BLOCKED SO LONG AS 
THE DE FACTOS RETAIN CONTROL OF THE MINISTRIES. HE 
REPEATEDLY INSISTED THAT THE DE FACTOS BE EVICTED AT 
ONCE. DEPSEC TALBOTT UNDERSTOOD PM MALVAL'S 
PRINCIPLED POSITION THAT THE LEGITIMATE GOVERNMENT 
SHOULD OCCUPY NO MINISTRY UNTIL THE DE FACTOS HAVE 
VACATED ALL MINISTRIES. 
6. DEPSEC TALBOTT AND MR. BERGER NOTED THAT PROMPT 
PASSAGE OF A SATISFACTORY AMNESTY LAW WOULD HELP 
SPEED THE TRANSITION, AND CALLED ON THE HTT TO 
FACILITATE THIS TO THE UTMOST. MINISTER CHERESTAL 
EXPRESSED SATISFACT/ON THAT THE CHAMBER OF DEPUTIES

7. REGARDING PRESIDENT ARISTIDE'S WISH TO MEET IN 
NORFOLK ON OCTOBER 8 WITH SENIOR FAD'H OFFICERS, 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT OFFERED TO TAKE DESIGNATED 
OFFICERS BACK TO WASHINGTON WITH HIM. GENERAL 
BELIOTTE EXPLAINED THAT THE OFFICERS WILL NEED 
APPROVAL FROM THE CINC, WHO IS STILL LTG CEDRAS. 
AMBASSADOR SWING AND LTG SHELTON OFFERED TO TAKE THE 
LIST OF NAMES TO LTG CEDRAS IMMEDIATELY AND ASK HIM TO 
APPROVE THEIR TRAVEL. CINCUSACOM ADMIRAL MILLER WILL 
SEND AN AIRCRAFT TO TRANSPORT THEM. 
8. FATHER ADRIEN SAID THE PEOPLE ARE WORRIED ABOUT 
VETTING SOME FADH MEMBERS INTO THE NEW POLICE FORCE. 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT STRESSED THE INTERIM NATURE 
OF THIS ARRANGEMENT, THE CLOSE CONSULTATIONS WITH 
PRESIDENT ARISTIDE THAT PRECEDED IT, AND THE DESIRE OF 
THE U.S. TO AVOID BECOMING THE POLICE FORCE OF HAITI. 
LTG SHELTON DESCRIBED THE SHORT-TERM PLAN TO INSTALL A 
STOPGAP SECURITY FORCE IN CAP HAITIEN, WITH THE_ 
SUPPORT OF ICITAP AND INTERNATIONAL POLICE MONITORS. 
8. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT DID NOT SEE OR CLEAR THIS 
MESSAGE BEFORE HIS DEPARTURE.
