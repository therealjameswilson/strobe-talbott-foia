2. SUMMARY. A U.S. DELEGATION HEADED BY DEPUTY 
SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT MET WITH UKRAINIAN PARLIAMENT 
(RADA) SPEAKER OLEKSANDR MOROZ ON JULY 17 IN A POSITIVE 
DISCUSSION FOCUSSED ON TECHNICAL.COOPERATION WITH THE 
U.S. CONGRESS, THE NEW UKRAINIAN CONSTITUTION, AND 
UPCOMING RADA LEGISLATION TO IMPLEMENT THE 
CONSTITUTION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WAS ACCOMPANIED ON 
THE U.S. SIDE BY AMBASSADOR-AT-LARGE FOR THE NIS JIM 
COLLINS, COORDINATOR OF ASSISTANCE TO THE NIS 
AMBASSADOR RICHARD MORNINGSTAR, U.S. AMBASSADOR TO 
UKRAINE WILLIAM GREEN MILLER, NSC DIRECTOR CARLOS 
PASCUAL, S/NIS SPECIAL ASSISTANT GEORGE KROL, D SPECIAL 
ASSISTANT MARK WONG, AND KIEV POLOFF MONICA EPPINGER. 
MOROZ WAS JOINED BY FIRST DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER ANTON 
BUTEYKO AND PYOTR DATSENKO OF THE U.S.A. AND CANADA
DESK OF THE UKRAINIAN FOREIGN MINISTRY. 
MOROZ PRAISES COOPERATION WITH CONGRESS 
3. —EG4— MOROZ LEAD OFF BY NOTING THAT THE REGULAR 
CONTACT HE HAD HAD WITH TALBOTT WAS CHARACTERISTIC OF 
THE CLOSE U.S.-UKRAINE RELATIONSHIP. HE PRAISED THE 
TIES BETWEEN THE RADA AND THE U.S. CONGRESS, NOTING AS 
PARTICULARLY IMPORTANT THE ASSISTANCE THE RADA HAS 
RECEIVED FROM THE CONGRESSIONAL RESEARCH SERVICE, WHICH 
AMONG OTHER,THINGS HAD PROVIDED DEPUTIES WITH A 
TCONNECTION O THE INTERNET. HE DESCRIBED THE INTERNET 
CONNECTION AS A VERY USEFUL TOOL, OFTEN USED BY MEMBERS 
AS A SOURCE OF RELIABLE INFORMATION. MOROZ, ALSO 
DESCRIBED A SPECIAL COMMITTEE OF 40 DEPUTIES, HiADED BY 
BUTEYKO, AIMED AT BROADENING COOPERATION WITH THE U.S. 
CONGRESS. FUTURE PROMISING POSSIBILITIES FOR RADA-
CONGRESS COOPERATION, ACCORDING TO MOROZ, INCLUDE 
STUDYING U.S. EXPERIENCE IN LEGISLATION ON BUDGET 
FORMATION, TAX LAW,• AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT.
