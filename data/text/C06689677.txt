RELEASE IN FULL 
January 1, 1994 
Dear Strobe: 
I was delighted to learn of your nomination 
to be Deputy Secretary, by the way one of the 
better kept secrets in the annals of recent 
high-level personnel actions. Perhaps that, in 
itself, is a good omen. 
To say the least, you take on your new 
responsibilities at an extraordinarily important 
time, and I have to admit to envying you the 
opportunity to have such a major impact on our 
country's national security interests. Needless 
to say, if there is anything my staff and. I can do 
to support you in these tasks, you need only ask. 
Best regards, 
omas M.T. Niles 
Ambassador 
The Honorable Strobe Talbott 
Deputy Secretary of State - Designate 
Department of State 
Washington, D.C.
