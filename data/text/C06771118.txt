5. -(1.11 VOLLEBAEK THANKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, NOTING HE 
SHOULD SHARE CONGRATULATIONS WITH AMBASSADOR WALKER AND 
GENERAL CLARK. VOLLEBAEK WAS IMPRESSED WITH THE WORK OF 
THE KVM AND WITH THE EXCELLENT JOB GENERAL CLARK HAD DONE 
ASSEMBLING AND TRAINING THE EXTRACTION FORCE. IT WAS 
IMPORTANT TO VOLLEBAEK TO HAVE SEEN THE EXTRACTION FORCE 
PERSONALLY, SO THAT AS OSCE CHAIRMAN HE COULD ATTEST TO 
THEIR PREPAREDNESS TO CONDUCT RESCUE OPERATIONS. 
VOLLEBAEK THANKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY FOR THE USG'S OFFER 
TO ASSIST THE OSCE IN ITS WORK IN KOSOVO, AND PROMISED TO 
SPEAK TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGAIN JANUARY 19 TO DISCUSS 
DETAILS OF FURTHER ACTION IN THE REGION. 
6. 4.s.) VOLLEBAEK SAID THE OSCE TROIKA WOULD BE MEETING 
JANUARY 20. IT WOULD BE IMPORTANT DURING THAT MEETING TO 
DISCUSS POLITICAL BENCHMARKS THAT SHOULD BE REITERATED TO 
MILOSEVIC. GENERAL CLARK HAD AGREED WITH VOLLEBAEK THAT 
THE OSCE COULD MOVE INTO THE POLITICAL ARENA AND ASSIST 
AMBASSADOR HILL AND THE KVM WITH THEIR WORK IN KOSOVO. 
VOLLEBAEK NOTED HE HAD MADE CLEAR TO MILOSEVIC THE DEMANDS 
OF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY REGARDING KOSOVO, AS WELL 
AS WHAT THREATS COULD BE BROUGHT TO BEAR AND WHAT OPTIONS 
REMAINED FOR MILOSEVIC. HE HAD UNDERSCORED TO MILOSEVIC 
THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY WAS SCRUTINIZING HIS 
PERFORMANCE. IT WAS IMPORTANT NOW FOR COUNTRIES INVOLVED 
IN KOSOVO TO DECIDE WHAT NEW BENCHMARKS TO SET FOR 
MILOSEVIC AND TO MONITOR HIS PROGRESS IN ATTAINING THEM. 
7. -(-84- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED WITH VOLLEBAEK, NOTING 
NATO SYG SOLANA HAD MADE THE SAME SUGGESTIONS TO HIM 
EARLIER. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED HE HOPED TO TRAVEL TO 
OSLO WELL BEFORE THE NATO SUMMIT IN ORDER TO GET NORWAY'S 
PERSPECTIVE AND ADVICE ON.KEEPING NORWAY, THE U.S., AND 
THE OSCE IN SYNCH IN PREPARATION FOR THE SUMMIT. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF MANAGING 
BROADER EUROPEAN
