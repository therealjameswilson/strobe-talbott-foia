On behalf of Secretary Christopher, thank you for your 
letter enclosing a copy of your response to Senator Mack 
concerning Russian credits to Cuba. In light of U.S. concerns 
regarding outside assistance to Cuba, we are pleased that our 
two governments can continue to address this matter together 
openly and constructively. 
I appreciate your willingness to discuss this matter. 
Further consultations between our two governments on Russia's 
economic relations with Cuba will be useful in confirming the 
commercial nature of those relations.
