April 27, 1993 
Dear Peter: 
It is an extraordinary coincidence, but I am getting 
around to replying to your fax on the very day that I 
gave a 20 minute interview over WORLDNET to Mr. 
Kisilyov. I was very impressed by the sensitivity and 
intelligence of his questions. I hope that my attempt to 
answer them met with his approval. The interview is 
going to be broadcast on "ITOGI" on the day of the 
referendum. I hope all is going well with you. I miss 
my association with Carnegie, but I hope I will see you 
from time to time on my visits to Moscow. 
Sincerely,
