—6.-)7 SUBJECT: TURKISH-ISRAELI RELATIONS 
4. -4.81- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE WANTED TO 
ELABORATE ON ONE OF THE ISSUES DISCUSSED WHEN FM CILLER 
MET WITH UNDER SECRETARY TARNOFF: TURKISH SUPPORT FOR 
THE MIDDLE EAST PEACE PROCESS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
SAID HE HAS AWARE THAT PM ERBAKAN ASSURED U/S TARNOFF OF 
GOT SUPPORT FOR THE MEPP. HE ADDED THAT PEACE IN THE 
REGION IS OF PARAMOUNT IMPORTANCE TO THE U.S., NOTING 
THAT PRESIDENT CLINTON AND THE SECRETARY RECENTLY MET 
WITH ISRAELI PM NETANYAHU. COMMENTING ON THE AGREEMENT 
BETWEEN TURKEY AND ISRAEL, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THE 
USG HOPES GOOD RELATIONS DEVELOP BETWEEN THE TWO NATIONS 
AND ENCOURAGED THE GOT TO ISSUE A POSITIVE PUBLIC 
STATEMENT INDICATING ITS WILLINGNESS TO FORGE AHEAD ON 
GOT/GOI RELATIONS. 
5. 4911- FM CILLER RESPONDED THAT HER SUPPORT FOR THE 
GOI IS BASIC AND SOLID. SHE SAID SHE EARLIER SPOKE WITH 
THE ISRAELI PM, WHO WAS INTERESTED IN THE OUTCOME OF 
EGYPTIAN PRESIDENT MUBARAK'S VISIT TO TURKEY. CILLER 
SAID MUBARAK SOUGHT A REVISION OF TURKEY'S MILITARY 
AGREEMENT WITH ISRAEL. HOWEVER, THE GOT PUBLICLY 
ANNOUNCED IT WILL ABIDE BY THE EXISTING AGREEMENT. SHE 
CONSIDERS THE ANNOUNCEMENT A SUCCESS STORY AND WILL DO 
HER BEST TO MAINTAIN GOOD RELATIONS WITH ISRAEL. SHE 
COMMENTED THAT PM ERBAKAN DOES NOT FAVOR SOLIDARITY WITH 
ISRAEL AND SAID HER PARTY NOW REQUIRES USG 'SUPPORT FOR 
THE DECISION TO STAY THE COURSE. CILLER SAID SHE IS 
DEDICATED TO PEACE AND SOLIDARITY WITHIN THE REGION, NOT 
JUST IN TURKEY, AND INTENDS TO MOVE FORWARD AND 
WESTERNIZE IN ORDER TO SUSTAIN DEVELOPMENT. 
6. -(44- TURNING TO OTHER ISSUES, CILLER COMPLAINED THAT 
GREECE AND ITS "INFLEXIBLE POLICIES" CONTINUE TO BLOCK 
TURKEY'S EU INTEGRATION AND RECENTLY PREVENTED THE GOT'S 
PARTICIPATION IN THE JULY 16 EU ASSOCIATION COUNCIL 
MEETING. SHE ALSO REQUESTED USG SUPPORT REGARDING THE 
ONGOING AEGEAN CONFLICT. CILLER PRAISED USG 
INTERVENTION WITH THE UNITED ARAB EMIRATES, NOTING THAT 
THE UAE WILL INITIATE PAYMENTS FOR TURKEY'S LOSSES 
DURING THE GULF WAR. 
7. -(4} FM CILLER ALSO INFORMED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
SHE IS CONCURRENTLY DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER AND ASSERTED 
THAT SHE SHARES "EQUAL AUTHORITY" WITH PM ERBAKAN. SHE 
EXPLAINED THAT THE COALITION GOVERNMENT DOES NOT SHARE 
VIEWS ON SOME ISSUES AND HER SUCCESS IS DEPENDENT ON 
U.S. SUPPORT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE HAS A BETTER 
UNDERSTANDING OF TURKEY'S INTERNAL CONFLICTS AS A RESULT 
OF THE CALL AND PROMISED USG SUPPORT AS LONG AS THERE IS 
NO BACKSLIDING ON BASIC ISSUES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
SAID HE WILL FORWARD HER CONCERNS TO PRESIDENT CLINTON, 
AMBASSADOR GROSSMAN, AND THE SECRETARY, WHO LOOKS 
FORWARD TO WORKING WITH HER. 
8. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1158 EDT. 
9. (U) END TEXT.
