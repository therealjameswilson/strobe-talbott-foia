2. SUMMARY. DURING HIS OCTOBER 25-26 VISIT TO 
ABIDJAN, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT USED PUBLIC EVENTS TO 
PROMOTE THE USG'S DUAL OBJECTIVES OF INCREASED IVORIAN 
INVOLVEMENT IN PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS ABROAD AND 
CONTINUED IVORIAN DEMOCRATIZATION AT HOME. IN AN 
OCTOBER 25 ADDRESS TO THE IVORIAN NATIONAL ASSEMBLY, 
THE DEPSEC OUTLINED AMERICA'S EFFORTS IN SUPPORT OF 
MULTILATERAL PEACE INITIATIVES AND URGED AFRICANS TO 
REINFORCE THE CAPACITIES OF REGIONAL GROUPINGS LIKE THE 
OAU AND ECOWAS. AT A RECEPTION WITH POLITICAL PARTY 
AND CIVIL SOCIETY LEADERS LATER THAT EVENING, THE 
DEPSEC FOCUSED HIS FORMAL REMARKS ON DEMOCRATIZATION. 
DURING A RECEPTION PULL-ASIDE WITH INTERIOR MINISTER 
BOMBET, DEPSEC UNDERSCORED USG INTEREST IN FREE AND 
TRANSPARENT ELECTIONS IN 1995, URGED INTERNATIONAL 
ELECTION OBSERVER PARTICIPATION, AND LISTENED TO AN 
UPDATE ON ELECTIONS PREPARATION. NEVERTHELESS, GOCI 
OFFICIALS REMAIN RELUCTANT TO ENTERTAIN PUBLICLY DIRECT 
INVOLVEMENT IN LIBERIA OR INTERNATIONAL ELECTIONS 
OBSERVATION. END SUMMARY. 
NATIONAL ASSEMBLY ADDRESS FOCUSES ON PEACEKEEPING 
3. AT THE INVITATION OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE NATIONAL 
ASSEMBLY, CHARLES DONWAHI, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT 
ADDRESSED THE IVORIAN NATIONAL ASSEMBLY ON THE SUBJECT 
OF PEACEKEEPING ON OCTOBER 25. APPROXIMATELY 120 OF 
THE 175 DEPUTIES ATTENDED, AN UNUSUALLY LARGE SHOWING 
FOR SUCH EVENTS. 
4. BOTH ASSEMBLY PRESIDENT DONWAHI AND FOREIGN AFFAIRS 
COMMISSION PRESIDENT SIAKA BAMBA GAVE BRIEF 
INTRODUCTORY REMARKS. BAMBA EMPHASIZED COTE D'IVOIRE'S 
DEMOCRATIC INSTITUTIONS IN HIS WELCOMING SPEECH. HE 
NOTED THAT ALTHOUGH COTE D'IVOIRE HAS ONLY ENJOYED 
MULTIPARTY DEMOCRACY SINCE 1990, ITS BASIC THRUST HAS 
ALWAYS BEEN TOWARDS DEMOCRATIC STRUCTURES. HE ALSO 
MADE MENTION OF THE ACCOMPLISHMENTS OF THE BEDIE 
ADMINISTRATION, INCLUDING RESTRUCTURING THE ECONOMY AND 
REINFORCING THE RULE OF LAW IN COTE D'IVOIRE. 
5. AFTER INTRODUCING THE MEMBERS OF HIS DELEGATION, THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TOOK UP THE THEME OF DEMOCRACY AND 
EMPHASIZED THE MOVEMENT TOWARDS STABLE DEMOCRATIC 
INSTITUTIONS IN AFRICA. THE DEPSEC THEN TURNED TO 
ANOTHER OF THE PRINCIPAL THEMES OF HIS TRIP: 
PEACEKEEPING. AFTER HIGHLIGHTING THE INCREASED 
PEACEKEEPING ROLE WHICH HAS FALLEN TO,THE UNITED
