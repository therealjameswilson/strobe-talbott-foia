1. SUMMARY: SYG REPRESENTATIVE FOR HAITI ENRIQUE TER 
HORST VISITED WASHINGTON MAY 6 BEFORE HIS MAY 7 MEETING 
WITH THE FRIENDS OF HAITI IN NEW YORK. HE MADE A CASE FOR 
LONG-TERM INVOLVEMENT IN HAITI AND STRATEGIC PLANNING OVER 
THE NEXT FIFTEEN YEARS BY AN EXPANDED FRIENDS ii GROUP IN 
ORDER TO KEEP HAITI A PRIORITY FOR INTERNATIONAL 
ORGANIZATIONS. USG AND OTHER INTERLOCUTORS TOLD TER HORST 
THAT THEY WOULD CONSIDER THIS IDEA, BUT THEIR INTEREST 
CENTERED FOR NOW ON THE IMMEDIATE SITUATION IN HAITI, THE 
POSSIBLITY OF A UNSC RESOLUTION CONTINUING A UN PRESENCE 
IN HAITI, AND THE ROLE OF UNMIH CIVPOL. END SUMMARY. 
2. TER HORST MET PRIVATELY WITH THE PRESIDENT OF THE 
INTER-AMERICAN DEVELOPMENT BANK, ENRIQUE IGLESIAS. HE 
ALSO MET WITH OAS SECRETARY-GENERAL GAVIRIA; NSC DEPUTY 
DIRECTOR BERGER; DEPUTY SECRETARY OF DEFENSE WHITE; DEFUTY 
SECRETARY OF STATE TALEOTT; ACTING 10 A/S WILLIAMSON AND 
AID ADMINISTRATOR SCHNEIDER; ICITAP DIRECTOR STROMSEN AND 
HAITI/WORKING GROUP DIRECTOR LEONARD; AND U.S. COORDINATOR 
FOR SECURITY AND JUSTICE IN HAITI KOZAK. 
TER HORST'S MESSAGE 
3. IN HIS MEETINGS WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND 
DEPUTY DIRECTOR BERGER, TER HORST BROUGHT UP THE IDEA OF 
AN EXPANDED "FRIENDS" GROUP, WHICH WOULD WORK WITH THE 
GOVERNMENT OF HAITI TO ELICIT THE HAITIANS' VIEW OF WHAT 
KIND OF COUNTRY THEY WANT IN FIFTEEN YEARS, AND WHAT IS IN 
FACT FEASIBLE. THE GROUP, WHICH WOULD INCLUDE MORE LATIN 
AMERICAN AND EUROPEAN NATIONS THAN THE CURRENT FRIENDS, 
WOULD KEEP HAITI ON THE FRONT BURNER FOR POLICYMAKERS AND 
HELP THE HAITIANS ACHIEVE THEIR GOALS. ACCORDING TO TER 
HORST, IADB PRESIDENT IGLESIAS AGREED WITH HIM BUT SAID 
THAT, REALISTICALLY, HELP FOR HAITI WOULD COME FROM A FEW 
DEVELOPED NATIONS. TER HORST ALSO STATED TO DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT THAT IT WOULD BE ADVISABLE TO "LOCK IN" 
THE POSITIVE REFORMS UNDERWAY IN HAITI'S POLITICS AND 
ECONOMY, TO MAKE BACKSLIDING LESS LIKELY AFTER PRESIDENT 
PREVAL LEAVES OFFICE.

THE U.S. FOCUS: HAITI'S CONDITION AND A CONTINUED UN 
PRESENCE 
5. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT EXPRESSED HIS GRATITUDE TO 
TER HORST FOR TAKING ON THE ROLE OF SRSG, AND SAID THAT 
TER HORST SHOULD ASK FOR HELP FROM THE U.S. WHENEVER HE 
NEEDS IT. ASKED BY TALBOTT ABOUT THE CURRENT SITUATION IN 
HAITI, TER HORST SAID THAT HAITI'S MOST PRESSING PROBLEM 
IN THE SHORT TERM WAS THE RAPIDLY DECLINING LEVEL OF ITS 
RESERVES, WHICH ARE SHRINKING BY DOLLARS g MILLION PER 
MONTH AND ARE AT ABOUT DOLLARS 100 MILLION AT PRESENT. 
EVEN IF THE IFI AGREEMENT IS SIGNED SOON1 THE 
INSTITUTIONAL BOARDS WILL NOT MEET UNTIL JULY, AND HAITI'S 
RESERVES COULD BE PERILOUSLY LOW BY THEN. 
6. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT SAID THE U.S. HAS CAUTIONED 
PRESIDENT PREVAL ON THE NEED TO BALANCE HAITI'S RELATIONS 
WITH TAIWAN WITH ITS NEED FOR PRC SUPPORT AT THE UN, BUT' 
"WILL NOT TELL THE HAITIANS WHO THEY SHOULD OR SHOULDN'T 
SEND" TO THE MAY 20 TAIWANESE PRESIDENTIAL INAUGURATION.' 
IN RESPONSE, TER HORST SAID PREVAL HAD TOLD HIM MAY 3 THAT 
HAITI WILL SEND ONLY ITS RESIDENT AMBASSADOR TO THE 
INAUGURATION, RATHER THAN A HIGH-LEVEL DELEGATION FROM 
HAITI. MEANWHILE, HAITI HAS SPOKEN TO THE CHINESE ABOUT 
OPENING A COMMERCIAL OFFICE IN BEIJING, AND HAS INVITED 
THE CHINESE PERMREP TO VISIT HAITI. TER HORST NOTED IN 
PASSING THAT WHILE PREVAL IS AN IMPRESSIVE LEADER, WHO 
APPEARS TO HAVE A BROAD VIEW OF WHERE HAITI SHOULD GO, HE 
DOES NOT SEEM TO HAVE MANY CLOSE ADVISORS. HE IS ALWAYS 
ALONE WHEN TER HORST HAS HIS WEEKLY MEETING WITH HIM, AND 
"THAT WORRIES ME A LITTLE", TER HORST SAID. SMARCK MICHEL 
AND LESLIE VOLTAIRE ARE NOT AS INFLUENTIAL OR INVOLVED AS 
THEY HAD BEEN, ACCORDING TO TER HORST. 
7. TALBOTT SAID THAT THE U.S. FAVORS A CONTINUED UN 
PRESENCE IN HAITI, ALTHOUGH EXTENDING THE CURRENT FORCE 
UNDER THE EXISTING UN MANDATE WOULD BE VERY DIFFICULT. A 
UN PRESENCE AFTER JUNE 30 SHOULD CONTAIN A MIX OF POLICE 
AND MILITARY, AND THE U.S. WAS LOOKING AT ITS ABILITY TO 
FUND PART OF THE MILITARY ELEMENT. TER HORST SAID HE 
WANTS SECURITY COUNCIL NEGOTIATIONS IN NEW YORK ON A 
RESOLUTION FOR A POST-JUNE 30 PRESENCE TO BEGIN AS SOON AS 
POSSIBLE, EVEN "THE DAY AFTER THE TAIWAN INAUGURATION" 
(MAY 21). HE IS TRYING TO SPEED UP THE PREPARATION OF.THE

IN-DEPTH DISCUSSION ON POLICE ISSUES 
9. IN A MEETING WITH LEONARD, KOZAK AND STROMSEN, THE 
U.S. SIDE NOTED THE RECENT ATTACKS ON POLICEMEN AND THE 
CONTINUING THREAT POSED BY HEAVILY ARMED GROUPS SUCH AS 
THE SIN AND THE MINISTERIAL SECURITY GUARDS, AND ASKED 
WHAT UNMIH/CIVPOL WAS DOING TO ADDRESS THESE PROBLEMS. 
TER HORST RECOUNTED THE INVESTIGATIVE ASSISTANCE CIVPOL 
WAS PROVIDING ON THE POLICE KILLINGS AND SAID HE WAS 
WILLING TO HAVE UNMIH PROVIDE A "BACK-UP" TO THE HNP IN 
THEIR EFFORT TO DISBAND THE ARMED GROUPS, BUT EMPHASIZED 
THAT THE GOH AND HNP HAD TO TAKE THE LEAD. THE U.S. SIDE 
ALSO EMPHASIZED THE IMPORTANCE OF CIVPOLSERVING AS FIELD 
TRAINERS AND OF TRAINING ON THE BASIS OF COORDINATED 
WRITTEN CURRICULA VICE AD HOC TRAINING. TER HORST AGREED 
WITH OUR VIEWS, SAID HE THOUGHT THE SITUATION HAD IMPROVED 
SOMEWHAT, AND SAID HE WOULD BE PREPARED TO HOLD A 
COORDINATION MEETING WITH THE US, CANADA AND FRENCH POLICE 
TRAINING CONTINGENTS. (EMBASSY MAY WISH TO FOLLOW UP ON 
THIS.) 
10. IN A SUBSEQUENT CONVERSATION WITH KOZAK REGARDING THE 
NON-POLICE PARTS OF THE ADMINISTRATION OF JUSTICE PROGRAM 
(INCLUDING THE INACTION OF UNDP ON THE PRISONS SEGMENT), 
TERHORST OPINED,THAT A "MOgE RADICAL" APPROACH MAY BE 
REQUIRED AND THAT THE MINISTER OF JUSTICE WAS NOT, IN HIS 
VIEW, UP TO THE TASK. KOZAK SAID THE U.S. IS CONSIDERING 
AN INTER-AGENCY VISIT TO REVIEW THE PROGRAM, AND TER HORST 
ASKED THAT WE SCHEDULE THE VISIT TO COINCIDE WITH THE 
PRESENCE OF HIS CHIEF AOJ OFFICER, TO BETTER COORDINATE 
OUR EFFORTS.
