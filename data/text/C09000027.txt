I want to thank you and your staff on behalf of The University of 
Tulsa for taking a second look at the denial of a visa to Chief Justice 
Bello of Nigeria to corpe to the United States. As you know, our 
invitation to Chief Justice Bello was motivated solely by our scholarly 
V and educational interests. We were pleased that he had agreed to 
present a paper at our conference on the Warren Court, on the 
international significance of the Supreme Court's decisions in the 
Warren era. Unfortunately, his visa came through too late for JustiCe 
Bello to make his travel arrangements. 
I understand from Nick Rostow of our College of Law faculty that 
Ms. Nuland and Mr. Withers in your office, Ms. Brown in the Legal 
Adviser's Office, and Ms. Barks-Ruggles in the Bureau of West Africa 
Affairs could not have been more helpful, responsive, and professional 
in this instance. All of us here are grateful to them. The country is 
lucky to have them at the Department of State. 
With every good wish,
