Thank you for your kind letter. My apologies for the 
delay in this reply. The last several weeks have, as you 
can imagine, been very hectic. Until confirmed, I don't 
have a staff, which means I've fallen behind in cor-
respondence. 
However, you can be assured that I've given a great 
deal of thought to the challenge of how best to assist eco-
nomic reform in Russia and the other new independent 
states. You're quite right about micro-economic assistance 
being a key part of the program. While assistance will come 
under my purview, the person working on the matter full-
time will be Tom Simons, currently our ambassador in War-
saw, who will be replacing Rich Armitage shortly. I'm going 
to ask Tom to study the material you sent me and to be in 
touch about how to follow up on the ideas you suggest in 
your letter.
