RELEASE IN FULL 
Dear Bill and Mary: 
I was deeply touched to receive your letter. I should have 
replied earlier, but my life has been so crazed of late that it 
has required me to set off across the ocean and hole up in a 
hotel room (in Brussels, while Clinton is doing his NATO thing) 
in order to get any of my correspondence done. God kngws when 
you'll get this; I'm writing this in a hotel room in Brussels.
(while the President does his NATO thing), and you want get this 
until after my return from the grand tour. 
I'm afraid I agree with you about the impossibility of the 
job. I might not feel that way if it weren't for Bosnia etc., 
which is the only problem I/we face that strikes me as truly 
intractable. But I guess I now have no excuse for not facing up 
to it. I'd like, from time to time, to be able to call you and 
talk it through. 
Thanks for the kind words on the NATO expansion issue. I'm 
fairly confident we're right not to plunge into fast-track 
admission for the East Europeans. There's little doubt in my 
mind that if we were to go that route, we'd commit the fallacy 
of the self-fulfilling prophecy in Russia (the Zhirinovsky-
Zyuganov types would relish proof that the West is anti-Russian 
-- anti-Russian Westerners and anti-Western Russians have always 
fed off of each other); I also suspect that neither the West 
Europeans nor the U.S. Senate would accept the Visegrad 
countries in NATO any time soon even if the Administration 
called for it. 
Anyway, I'll try to keep my sense of humor, my sense of 
perspective, My sense of history -- and my sense, of being 
blessed with friends like the two of you. Cheers! 
S' erely, 
Strobe _Talbott 
Mr. and Mrs. Willia affn 
1087 Great Road, 
Princeton, NJ.
