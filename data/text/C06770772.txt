1. {G} SUMMARY EUROPEAN PUBLIC OPINION IS COMPELLING EU 
GOVERNMENTS TO ASSUME THEIR RESPONSIBILITIES IN KOSOVO AND IN 
EUROPEAN DEFENSE, BELGIAN FOREIGN MINISTER ERIK DERYCKE TOLD 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT IN AN EXCHANGE OF VIEWS FEBRUARY 24. 
"ALL THE PIECES TO THE PUZZLE ARE ON THE TABLE" FOR FURTHER 
DEVELOPMENT OF ESDI, BUT IT WILL TAKE YEARS BEFORE EUROPE 
FINALLY WORKS OUT HOW IT WILL FIT THEM ALL TOGETHER. THE 
ESDI EFFORT IS NOT ANTI-AMERICAN BUT REFLECTS A WIDESPREAD 
PUBLIC DESIRE TO CREATE DEFENSE CAPABILITIES COMMENSURATE 
WITH EUROPE'S NEW-FOUND ECONOMIC POWER FOLLOWING INTRODUCTION 
OF THE EURO. 
2. +ei-- DERYCKE AGREED THERE WAS A GROWING UNDERCURRENT OF 
DISCOMFORT WITH THE U.S.; EUROPEAN LEADERS INCREASINGLY 
BRISTLE AT PERCEIVED U.S. UNILATERALISM. WHILE CURRENT EURO-
DISPLEASURE HAS BEEN BROUGHT ON BY ISSUES (BANANAS) DREDGING 
UP OLDER DIFFERENCES (LEGISLATION PERCEIVED TO BE EXTRA-
TERRITORIAL), DERYCKE PREDICTED THAT PROBLEMS OVER BANANAS 
WILL PALE WHEN COMi)ARED TO APPROACHING TRANSATLANTIC 
DIFFERENCES OVER "CULTURE." ON KOSOVO, DERYCKE INDICATED 
BELGIAN SUPPORT FOR THE U.S. VIEW THAT NATO MUST MAINTAIN 
PRESSURE ON MILOSEVIC AND SAID THAT BELGIUM WAS READY TO 
PROVIDE ITS CONTRIBUTION TO KFOR. END SUMMARY.

SOME CONTEXT (WHICH HE DESCRIBED AS "BELGIAN AND EUROPEAN 
FEELINGS ON WHAT IS GOING ON") TO THE ISSUES LIKELY TO BE 
DISCUSSED, "BECAUSE EVERYTHING IS LINKED TO EVERYTHING ELSE.".

6. (C) HOWEVER, THE NEW CRISIS IN KOSOVO GAVE IMPETUS TO 
THE NEW DISCUSSIONS AND "BLAIR AND CHIRAC MADE IT HAPPEN." 
DERYCKE QUICKLY EMPHASIZED THAT ST. MALO HAD "NOTHING TO DO 
WITH ANTI-NATO OR ANTI-AMERICAN SENTIMENTS," BUT RATHER, THE

COHERENCE." WHILE THE EU IS "NOT YET THERE," EUROPE'S 
OBJECTIVES IN ESDI ARE "COMPLEMENTARY TO WHAT BELGIUM IS AND 
HOW WE HAVE ACTED SINCE 1949 IN NATO." 
7. (44 ,DERYCKE REITERATED THAT WITH THE CRISIS IN KOSOVO, 
EUROPE FINALLY UNDERSTOOD THAT IT COULD NO LONGER FAIL TO 
SHOULDER ITS RESPONSIBILITIES. THE CRISIS IN KOSOVO WAS MUCH 
MORE TANGIBLE FOR EUROPEANS THAN BOSNIA, FOR THREE REASONS: 
THE CRISIS HAD OCCURRED LATER IN THE EVOLUTION OF EU 
INSTITUTIONS; IT WAS MORE VISIBLE TO EUROPE; AND THE PUBLIC 
IN EUROPE IS "FED UP -- IT WANTS A SOLUTION." DERYCKE 
REITERATED THAT "THERE IS A HUGE AWARENESS IN EUROPE OF THE 
PROBLEMS IN KOSOVO; EUROPEAN PUBLIC OPINION IS SAYING THAT 
EU GOVERNMENTS HAVE TO ASSUME THEIR RESPONSIBILITIES." 
FURTHERMORE, IN DERYCKE'S VIEW; THIS IS "NOT JUST AN 
INTELLECTUAL DEBATE; IT IS HUGELY POLITICAL." 
8. -441;)- DERYCKE ADDED THAT HE HOPED A SOLUTION FOR KOSOVO 
COULD BE FOUND BEFORE THE WASHINGTON NATO SUMMIT, "BECAUSE 
THIS CAN INFLUENCE THE AIMS AND DESIRES OF WHAT WE CAN DO IN 
THE NEW NATO CONTEXT." NEVERTHELESS, EUROPEAN INTEREST IN 
ESDI "WILL NOT STOP WITH THE SUMMIT. IT WILL TAKE YEARS." 
"ALL THE PIECES OF THE PUZZLE ARE ON THE TABLE BUT WE HAVE TO

ANTI-NATO, ANTI-AMERICAN, OR ANTI-CANADIAN." EUROPE IS

9. -(-e+- DERYCKE CONCEDED THAT THE DEBATE ON HOW MUCH EUROPE

10. 4-e4- IN RESPONSE, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY UNDERSCORED THAT 
THE U.S. WANTS THE WASHINGTON SUMMIT TO BE POSITIVE AND 
ENSURE NATO IS EQUIPPED TO FACE THE NEW RANGE OF SECURITY 
CHALLENGES WE FACE; THAT WAS IN LARGE MEASURE WHY WE WERE 
CONDUCTING PRE-SUMMIT CONSULTS IN AS MANY NATO CAPITALS AS 
POSSIBLE. ON ESDI, THE U.S. SUPPORTED INCREASED EUROPEAN 
DEFENSE CAPABILITIES AND WELCOMED FURTHER DEVELOPMENT OF ESDI 
-- PROVIDED THE "THREE D'S" WERE RESPECTED FULLY. NON-
DISCRIMINATION WAS ESPECIALLY IMPORTANT; THE RANKS OF NON-EU 
ALLIES WERE GROWING. DERYCKE AGREED WITH THE FINAL POINT, 
SAYING THAT THIS QUESTION "SEEMS TO BE THE MOST IMPORTANT 
PART OF THE DEVELOPMENT OF ESDI." HE ALSO CONCEDED THAT THE 
QUESTION OF WHAT EUROPE WILL BE WILLING TO PAY IS IMPORTANT, 
SAYING "TO BE CANDID," THAT HE HAD TO ADMIT THAT WHILE SOME 
OF THE WORK ON ESDI IS FOCUSING ON INSTITUTIONS, THE REST IS 
ON CAPABILITIES, "AND CAPABILITIES ARE RELATED TO RESOURCES." 
KOSOVO AND RAMBOUILLET 
11. -(44- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THANKED DERYCKE FOR BELGIAN 
WILLINGNESS TO CONTRIBUTE TO KFOR. HE UNDERSCORED THAT, POST-
RAMBOUILLET, WE MUST ASSUME MILOSEVIC ONLY UNDERSTANDS THE 
THREAT OF FORCE. NATO MUST THEREFORE KEEP THE THREAT OF 
FORCE IN PLACE, ALTHOUGH THIS WAS NOT A UNIVERSALLY HELD 
VIEW. 
12. DERYCKE ASKED FOR THE RUSSIAN VIEW. TALBOTT SAID 
U.S. DISCUSSIONS IN MOSCOW INDICATED RUSSIA OPPOSED THE USE 
OF FORCE AND NATO'S PREEMINENT ROLE, BUT WAS INTERESTED IN 
PARTICIPATING IN AN IMPLEMENTATION FORCE PROVIDED COMMAND 
ARRANGEMENTS WERE WORKED OUT, SOME SORT OF OVERARCHING OSCE 
"COVER" WAS IN PLACE AND A CLEAR EXIT STRATEGY IDENTIFIED. 
HOWEVER, THE SERBS ARE INDICATING THAT THEY DO NOT WANT THE 
RUSSIANS, AT WHICH DERYCKE EXPRESSED PROFOUND SURPRISE.

13. 444- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INDICATED WE WERE AWARE OF 
WHAT SEEMED TO BE RISING UNEASE, INCLUDING ON THE PART SOME 
OF OUR BEST FRIENDS, WITH HOW THE U.S. CONDUCTS ITSELF AND 
WITH OUR ROLE AS THE "INDISPENSABLE NATION." AFTER SOME 
REFLECTION, DERYCKE CONFIRMED THESE SENTIMENTS AND SOUGHT TO 
EXPLAIN THEIR ROOTS. "A VERY COMPLEX MIX OF MANY FACTORS" 
CONTRIBUTED TO THIS PERCEPTION. THE LARGER EUROPEAN POWERS 
FOUND AMERICA'S RELATIVE POWER DIFFICULT TO ACCEPT. THE 
LAUNCH OF THE EURO HAD BOTH GENERATED NEW CONFIDENCE WITHIN 
EUROPE AND PROMPTED EUROPEAN PUBLICS TO ASK WHY EUROPE DID 
NOT WIELD POLITICAL AND MILITARY POWER EQUAL TO ITS ECONOMIC 
HEFT. FINALLY, THERE WERE ECONOMIC PROBLEMS WITH THE U.S. 
THAT HAVE CAUSED "OLD FORGOTTEN PROBLEMS" TO RESURFACE -- IN 
PARTICULAR, HELMS-BURTON AND D'AMATO. THUS, THE "NEW ANTI-
AMERICANISM," AS DERYCKE DESCRIBED IT, WAS NOT MAINLY A 
DEFENSE/SECURITY PROBLEM; IT HAD MORE TO DO WITH ECONOMIC 
DIFFERENCES. THAT SAID, EUROPE DID HAVE "BIG AMBITIONS IN 
THE MILITARY FIELD," BECAUSE EUROPE'S FAST GROWTH MEANS 
PRESSURE IN THE DEFENSE FIELD. 
14. -171!* DERYCKE SUGGESTED A NEW EU COMMISSION COULD DEAL 
MORE QUICKLY WITH PROBLEMS ON THE U.S.-EU TRADE AGENDA. S;IR 
LEON'S HANDLING OF HELMS-BURTON HAD CREATED A REACTION THAT 
WAS VERY COMPLICATED, BECAUSE IT WAS A "MIX OF ALL KINDS OF 
IRRITANTS." THE MANNER IN WHICH SIR LEON USED HIS MANDATE IN 
DEALING WITH THE U.S. WAS AN "UNRESOLVED FAILURE" AND 
ILLUSTRATED THE ANALYSIS HE WAS TRYING TO MAKE, DERYCKE SAID. 
ONE MAY SAY IT IS A PROBLEM OF EUROPE: THERE HAS TO BE 
CLOSER POLICY COORDINATION BETWEEN THE MEMBER STATES AND'THE 
COMMISSION. THIS IS "PART OF THE EU'S GROWING PAINS." 
15. 44'4 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THE EXPRESSION 
"UNILATERALISM" HAD BEEN USED AT THE NAC EARLIER IN THE DAY, 
IN THE CONTEXT OF THE U.S. BEING THE MOST VOCAL PROPONENT OF 
'THE CREDIBLE THREAT OF FORCE IN KOSOVO. BUT IT WAS IMPORTANT 
THAT AMERICA'S FRIENDS UNDERSTAND THE OTHER SIDE OF THE 
DILEMMA. IF THE U.S. WERE TO FOLLOW THEIR ADVICE, WE WOULD 
BE DRIVEN TO LOWEST,COMMON DENOMINATOR AND END IN PARALYSIS. 
IF WE FOLLOW THE RULE THAT IF ANYONE HAS MISGIVINGS ON THE 
USE OF FORCE, IT WILL NOT BE USED. THE IRONY -- AND 
DANGER -- IN THIS IS THAT IT WOULD BE TERRIBLE FOR THE U.S., 
BELGIUM, AND NATO, AND IT WOULD BE GOOD ONLY FOR THOSE LIKE 
MILOSEVIC AND SADDAM. TALBOTT NOTED THAT IF MILOSEVIC THINKS 
THAT NATO, THE CONTACT GROUP AND THE OSCE CANNOT FOLLOW 
THROUGH, HE WILL CONCLUDE THAT HE HAS A FREE HAND. WE HAVE 
TO FIND A WAY TO COPE WITH THIS PROBLEM.

THE AMBASSADOR'S VIEW, CULTURAL PROTECTION ARGUMENTS ARE MORE 
USED FOR REASONS OF ECONOMIC PROTECTIONISM THAN FOR CULTURAL .

EMBASSY COMMENT 
17. DERYCKE'S THOUGHTFUL, IF RAMBLING, RESPONSE TO THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY'S REQUEST FOR VIEWS ON HIGH-LEVEL U.S. 
PERCEPTIONS OF EUROPEAN DISCOMFORT WITH THE U.S. UNDERSCORED 
THAT THERE IS INDEED A GROWING UNEASE, EVEN ON THE PART OF 
SOME OF AMERICA'S BEST FRIENDS, WITH HOW IT ACTS.. SUCH 
EXCHANGES THEMSELVES ARE VALUABLE IN ADDRESSING THIS MALAISE 
-- AND ARE EXTREMELY USEFUL IN OUR EFFORTS TO OVERCOME 
RECURRING BELGIAN RESENTMENT OF BEING LEFT OUT OF DECISIONS 
THAT THEY, AS DUTIFUL MEMBERS OF THE ALLIANCE, WILL NEED 
SUBSEQUENTLY TO IMPLEMENT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S COMMITMENT 
TO DERYCKE TO CONTINUE NURTURING U.S. RELATIONSHIPS WITH 
SMALLER ALLIES -- WHO MAY HAVE BEEN NEGLECTED BECAUSE OF THE 
ATTENTION PAID TO THE "HIGH-ENERGY" RELATIONSHIPS OF SOME OF 
AMERICA'S LARGER PARTNERS -- DIRECTLY ADDRESSED SOME OF 
DERYCKE'S FREQUENTLY-RAISED CONCERNS. MFA CONTACTS TELL US 
THAT DERYCKE'S EXPRESSIONS' OF ENTHUSIASM REGARDING THE 
EXCHANGE WERE GENUINE AND THAT HE PUT HIS HEART INTO THE 
ANALYSIS HE GAVE THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ON DEVELOPMENTS IN 
EUROPE.
