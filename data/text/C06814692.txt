SUBJECT: TFSR02: DEPSEC AND FISCHER: KEEP RUSSIA 
ENGAGED; REVIEW SPECIFICS TO MAINTAIN UNITED FRONT 
/ 
CLASSIFIED BY POLITICAL MINISTER COUNSELOR BRIAN FLORA FOR 
REASONS 1.5 (B) AND (D). 
SUMMARY 
1. -HI+ DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT BRIEFED GERMAN FOREIGN 
MINISTER FISCHER APRIL 27 IN BERLIN ON HIS TALKS EARLIER 
THAT DAY IN MOSCOW. THE DEPSEC NOTED SOME POSITIVE MOVEMENT 
IN RUSSIAN THINKING, BUT EMPHASIZED THAT THIS DID NOT 
NECESSARILY TRANSLATE INTO MOVEMENT ON MILOSEVIC'S PART. HE 
EMPHASIZED THAT NATO'S UNITED STAND AT THE WASHINGTON SUMMIT 
MAY HAVE HELPED PRODUCE MOSCOW'S MORE PRAGMATIC TONE. 
FISCHER URGED THAT NATO WORKING-LEVEL EXPERTS REVIEW NATO 
"BOTTOM LINES" ON VARIOUS SPECIFIC ISSUES, SUCH AS HOW FRY 
WITHDRAWAL FROM KOSOVO WOULD BE MEASURED, AND NATO'S 
POSSIBLE RESPONSE TO A FRY WITHDRAWAL TO JUST OVER THE 
BORDER, IN THE RUN-UP TO A POSSIBLE G-8 MINISTERIAL IN MID-
MAY. FISCHER NOTED THAT IVANOV, DURING A PHONE CONVERSATION 
WITH HIM EARLIER THAT DAY, HAD EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT A 
POSSIBLE PERMANENT INTERNATIONAL PROTECTORATE IN KOSOVO. 
IVANOV SAID THAT RUSSIA IS INTERESTED IN A "RAMBOUILLET 
SOLUTION" PROVIDING AUTONOMY AS QUICKLY AS POSSIBLE FOR 
KOSOVO, PREFERABLY BEFORE THE RETURN OF MOST REFUGEES. 
FISCHER SAID HE HAD REJECTED THIS AS UNWORKABLE. HE TOLD 
THE DEPSEC THAT, IN HIS VIEW, YELTSIN IS MORE FOCUSED ON A 
POLITICAL SOLUTION THAN ARE EITHER PRIMAKOV OR IVANOV, AND 
AGREED WITH THE DEPSEC THAT CHERNOMYRDIN'S APPOINTMENT AND 
RUSSIAN POLICIES ON KOSOVO ALSO HAVE AN INTERNAL RUSSIAN 
POLITICAL ANGLE. FISCHER CONCLUDED THAT THE WEST SHOULD 
REVIEW HOW IT WILL DEAL WITH THE "FRY" AND WITH MILOSEVIC 
HIMSELF AFTER THE CONFLICT ENDS. BOTH AGREED ON THE NEED TO 
KEEP RUSSIA ENGAGED. PARTICIPANTS ARE LISTED IN LAST PARA. 
END SUMMARY. 
RUSSIA: TRYING TO FIND COMMON GROUND WITH NATO ... 
2. THE DEPSEC BRIEFED FISCHER ON HIS APRIL 27 TALKS IN 
MOSCOW WITH, INTER ALIA, CHERNOMYRDIN AND IVANOV. HE NOTED 
RUSSIAN ATTEMPTS TO FIND SOME COMMON DENOMINATOR WITH NATO 
ON KOSOVO. IN THIS VEIN, IVANOV AND OTHERS ARE TRYING TO 
INTERPRET AS LOOSELY AS POSSIBLE THE WASHINGTON SUMMIT 
COMMUNIQUE ON KOSOVO. HOWEVER, THERE WAS NEITHER AN 
INDICATION THAT MILOSEVIC IS MOVING TO MEET NATO'S
CONDITIONS NOR ANY EVIDENCE THAT RUSSIA CAN DELIVER HIM.

3.   THE DEPSEC NOTED HIS INSISTENCE IN MOSCOW THAT ALL 
FRY FORCES, INCLUDING MILITARY, PARAMILITARY, AND POLICE, 
WITHDRAW, AND HIS REVIEW OF WHY NATO SHOULD BE THE CORE OF 
AN INTERNATIONAL SECURITY PRESENCE AND WHY THE U.S. MUST BE 
PART OF THAT FORCE. HOWEVER, RUSSIAN THINKING CONTINUES TO 
FOCUS ON WHAT MILOSEVIC MIGHT BE ABLE TO ACCEPT, RATHER THAN 
ON WHAT HE MUST ACCEPT. 
FISCHER REJECTS IVANOV'S "RAMBOUILLET FORMULA" 
4.  FISCHER DESCRIBED HIS LENGTHY PHONE CALL EARLIER 
THAT DAY (APRIL 27) WITH IVANOV. IVANOV SAID THAT THE 
RUSSIAN GOVERNMENT WILL DECIDE APRIL 28 ON CHERNOMYRDIN'S 
NEXT TRAVEL ITINERARY (NOTE: SUBSEQUENTLY, THE PRESS 
REPORTED THAT CHERNOMYRDIN WILL VISIT BONN APRIL 29). 
IVANOV EXPRESSED SERIOUS CONCERN ABOUT A POSSIBLE "PERMANENT 
INTERNATIONAL PROTECTORATE" IN KOSOVO. HE STRESSED THAT 
RUSSIA FAVORS A "RAMBOUILLET SOLUTION" WHICH WOULD GRANT 
KOSOVO AUTONOMY QUICKLY, WITH AN ELECTION WITHIN SEVERAL 
MONTHS OF AN AGREEMENT. IVANOV ARGUED THAT THIS SCENARIO 
WOULD BE EASIER TO IMPLEMENT BEFORE A MAJORITY OF REFUGEES 
RETURNED TO KOSOVO. FISCHER REJECTED THIS AS UNWORKABLE. 
HE TOLD IVANOV THAT KOSOVO WOULD NEED AN INTERNATIONALLY 
CONTROLLED CIVIL ADMINISTRATION WHOSE MOST PRESSING TASK 
WOULD BE TO ADDRESS THE DAILY PRACTICAL NEEDS OF THE 
RETURNED REFUGEES. 
5. -HE+ ACCORDING TO FISCHER, IVANOV INDICATED THAT 
MILOSEVIC MIGHT REDUCE HIS TROOP LEVEL IN KOSOVO TO THAT 
AGREED UNDER THE HOLBROOKE AGREEMENT. FISCHER REJECTED THIS 
AS WELL, OPINING THAT BOTH THE KLA AND THE ALBANIAN REFUGEES 
WOULD NOT ACCEPT SUCH A DEAL. IT WOULD ONLY CREATE A 
"CLIMATE OF BLOOD REVENGE," FISCHER SAID. HE TOLD THE 
DEPSEC THAT, SHOULD SOME FRY FORCES REMAIN IN KOSOVO, A MUCH 
STRONGER INTERNATIONAL FORCE WOULD BE NEEDED THERE. 
G-8 MINISTERIAL: START ADDRESSING SPECIFICS NOW 
6. —(-69- FISCHER INDICATED THAT THE RUSSIANS HAVE SAID THAT 
THEY WOULD "WORK WITH US" ON A G-8 MINISTERIAL. HE 
SUGGESTED MAY 11 AS A POSSIBLE DATE CITING SECRETARY 
ALBRIGHT'S TRIP TO GERMANY DURING THAT PERIOD. THE DEPSEC 
REPLIED THAT THE U.S. WAS READY TO ENTERTAIN THE IDEA OF A 
MEETING IF THE GERMANS THOUGHT IT WOULD BE PRODUCTIVE, AND 
THAT HE WOULD DISCUSS THE ISSUE WITH THE SECRETARY SO SHE 
COULD CONSIDER IT. FISCHER UNDERSCORED THE NEED TO START 
EXAMINING HOW THE WEST WILL DEAL WITH BOTH THE "FRY" AND 
WITH MILOSEVIC HIMSELF SHOULD A SETTLEMEN't BE REACHED. IN 
THIS CONNECTION, NATO NEEDS TO IDENTIFY WHICH SERBIAN 
INTERESTS COULD BE DEEMED LEGITIMATE AND WHAT NATO COULD 
OFFER BELGRADE IF IT ACCEPTS THE FIVE CONDITIONS. THE 
DEPSEC AND FISCHER CONCLUDED THAT NATO WORKING-LEVEL EXPERTS 
SHOULD INTENSIFY THEIR WORK ON SPECIFIC NATO RESPONSES TO 
VARIOUS ISSUES, SUCH AS THE PRECISE PARAMETERS OF A FRY 
WITHDRAWAL, FOR BOTH REVIEW BY POLITICAL LEADERS AND TO HELP 
ENSURE NATO CONTINUES TO HAVE FIRM COMMON POSITIONS ON 
SPECIFIC QUESTIONS BEFORE A G-8 MINISTERIAL.
