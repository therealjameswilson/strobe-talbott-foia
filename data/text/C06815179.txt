2 . (U) SUMMARY.. THE DEPUTY 'SECRETARY' SUBSTANTIVE AND
CANDID MEETING WITH FOREIGN MINISTER ERDENECHULUUN
SEPTEMBER 13 FEATURED DISCUSSION OF MONGOLIA' ONGOING
DEMOCRATIC DEVELOPMENT, THE NEW GOVERNMENT ACTION'ROGRAM,
THE IMPORTANCE OF CIVIL SOCIETY AND THE MONGOLIAN PRIME

TO DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT TO VISIT MONGOLIA. THE
FOREIGN MINISTER SOUGHT TO ASSURE US OF THE NEW
GOVERNMENT'S COMMITMENT TO DEMOCRACY AND MARKET REFORM.
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY PRAISED MONGOLIA'S PROGRESS TO DATE
WHILE EMPHASIZING THE IMPORTANCE OF PRESS FREEDOMS. END
SUMMARY.

3. (U) FOREIGN MINISTER LUVSANGIIN ERDENECHULUUN
EXTENDED HIS STAY IN WASHINGTON AN EXTRA DAY IN ORDER TO
MEET WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT ON SEPTEMBER 13. THE
FOREIGN MINISTER WAS ACCOMPANIED BY AMBASSADOR CHOINHOR,
O. ENKHTSETSEG, HEAD OF THE MFA'S INTERNATIONAL
ORGANIZATIONS DEPARTMENT, AND MONGOLIAN EMBASSY OFFICIALS-
DCM TULGA AND POLOFF BAASAN. U.S. PARTICIPANTS WERE EAP
AlS ROTH EAP ACTING DAS KEITH AND EAP/CM MCCONNELL
NOTETAKER.
4. (U) FM ERDENECHULUUN TOLD DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT
THAT HE WAS VERY PLEASED WITH A WASHINGTON PROGRAM THAT
INCLUDED MEETINGS WITH STATE DEPARTMENT UjS LIEBERMAN AND
EAP A/S ROTH, AND OFFICIALS FROM THE NSC, DOD AND USAID.
HE NOTED THAT DURING THIS VISIT HE WAS ESPECIALLY PLEASED
TO HAVE RESOLVED THE ISSUE OF FOREIGN MILITARY FINANCING
ELIGIBILITY FOR THE MONGOLIAN BORDER TROOPS COMMUNICATION
PROJECT (RAISED DURING HIS MEETING WITH UNDER SECRETARY
FOR DEFENSE POLCIY SLOCOMBE).
l
MPRP FULLY COMMITTED TO DEMOCRACY
5. (U) FOLLOWING THE MONGOLIAN PEOPLE'S REVOLUTIONARY
PARTY'S (MPRP) SWEEPING VICTORY IN THE JULY PARLIAMENTARY
ELECTIONS, THE FOREIGN MINISTER SAID .HE HAD BECOME AWARE
THAT SOME MONGOLIA WATCHERS WERE SKEPTICAL ABOUT THE
WILLINGNESS OF THE FORMER COMMUNIST PARTY, NOW IN POWER
WITHOUT A VIABLE OPPOSITION, TO PROCEED WITH DEMOCRATIC
AND ECONOMIC REFORMS. ERDENECHULUUN SAID HE WANTED TO
ASSURE WASHINGTON POLICYMAKERS THAT THE MPRP EMBRACED
DEMOCRATIC IDEALS AND WOULD NOT BACKSLIDE FROM THE
DEMOCRATIC GAINS AND MARKET REFORMS OF THE PAST TEN YEARS.
6. (U) THE FOREIGN MINISTER REFERRED TO "THE GOVERNMENT
ACTION PROGRAM RECENTLY APPROVED BY PARLIAMENTS WHICH
OUTLINED THE MPRP GOALS FOR THE NEXT FOUR YEARS AND WHICH
WILL BE READY FOR TNTERNATIONAL DISTRIBUTION AFTER
TRANSLATION. THIS WILL BE THE BASIS FOR THE GOM'S COURSE
OF ACTION TO TACKLE CHALLENGES OF POVERTY REDUCTION AND
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT TO IMPROVE LIVING STANDARDS FOR THE
MONGOLIAN PEOPLE.

7. (U) RECOGNIZING THAT HE WAS ONLY LAYING A MARKER FOR
THE NEXT ADMINISTRATION, ERDENECHULUUN ASKED DEPUTY
SECRETARY TALBOTT TO CONSIDER A U.S.. VISIT BY MONGOLIAN
PRINE MINISTER {PN) ENKHBAYAR IN FEBRUARY, 2001.
ENKHBAYAR IS PLANNING HIS FIRST OFFICIAL VISIT AS PN. TO
TOKYO IN THE SECOND HALF OF FEBRUARY, AND WOULD LIKE TO
CONE TO,WASHINGTON TO NEET WITH USG OFFICIALS AFTER
VISITING JAPAN,'RDENECHULUUN COMMENTED THAT THIS WOULD
BE AN IMPORTANT OPPORTUNITY FOR THE PN TO DISCUSS HIS
GOVERNMENT'S REFORM EFFORTS AND PROGRESS IN IMPLEMENTING
THE GOVERNMENT ACTION PROGRAM, AS WELL AS AN OCCASION TO
ESTABLISH PERSONAL CONTACTS. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT
INDICATED THAT HE OBVIOUSLY WAS NOT IN A POSITION TO
COMMIT THE NEXT ADMINISTRATION, BUT THAT HE WOULD
CERTAINLY RECOMMEND THIS TO HIS SUCCESSOR.
USG SUPPORTS MONGOLIA' ONGOING TRANSITION
8. ~ DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT REFLECTED ON HIS LONG-
HELD INTEREST IN NONGOLIA AND THE DEVELOPMENT OF BILATERAL
RELATIONS IN THE EARLY STEPS TOWARD RAPPROCHEMENT IN THE
1970',FOLLOWED BY THE DECADE THAT SAW MONGOLIA'
PEACEFUL DEMOCRATIC REVOLUTION AND GENUINE INDEPENDENCE.
HE STRESSED THE BENEFITS OF U.S.-MONGOLIA BILATERAL
CONSULTATIONS ON REGIONAL SECURITY ISSUES, AND PRAISED
MONGOLIA' PARTICIPATION IN THE ASEAN REGIONAL FORUM AS A
GOOD EXAMPLE OF SUCH COOPERATION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY
SAID THAT HE LOOKED FORWARD TO STAYING IN CONTACT WITH THE
FOREIGN MINISTER ON DEVELOPMENTS IN CHINA AND RUSSIA, AS
WELL AS IN SUPPORTING PROGRESS ON THE KOREAN PENINSULA.
ACCORD PRIORITY TO CIVIL SOCIETY AND FREEDONS
9. (U) DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT OBSERVED THAT NEITHER
HISTORY NOR NOTHER NATURE HAVE BEEN KIND TO MONGOLIA,, AND
HE EXPRESSED CONCERN FOR MONGOLIA'S RECOVERY FROM THE
1999-2000 SEVERE WINTER DISASTER. ON THE SUBJECT OF
MONGOLIA'S TRANSFORMATION OF ITS POLITICAL CULTURE, DEPUTY
SECRETARY TALBOTT EMPHASIZED THAT HE HOPED THE NEW
LEADERSHIP WOULD ACCORD THE HIGHEST PRIORITY TO CIVIL
SOCIETY, INCLUDING FREEDOM OF THE PRESS.
