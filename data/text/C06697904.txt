2. 444 SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT RECEIVED COPIES OF 
CREDENTIALS JUNE II FROM ALGERIA'S APPOINTED AMBASSADOR, 
RAMTANE LAMAMPA, AND REVIEWED WITH HIM CURRENT U.S.-
ALGERIAN BILATERAL RELATIONS: DEPSEC TALBOTT TOLD LAMAMRA 
THAT, AS PRESIDENT ZERDUAL MOVES AHEAD WITH INTERNAL 
DIALOGUE AND OPENNESS, THE U.S. WILL BE SUPPORTIVE AND SEEK 
ADDITIONAL AREAS OF COOPERATION. TALBOTT ALSO EXPRESSED 
INTEREST IN PRESS FREEDOM IN ALGERIA. END SUMMARY. 
3. —Fe* TALBOTT OPENED THE MEETING BY NOTING THE OBVIOUS 
IMPORTANCE THE GOA ATTACHES TO BILATERAL RELATIONS WITH THE 
U.S. IN NOMINATING A DIPLOMAT OF LAMAMRA'S STATURE. 
REGRETTING THAT HE DID NOT HAVE FIRSTHAND KNOWLEDGE OF 
ALGERIA, TALBOTT SAID THAT IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENTS APPEAR TO 
BE UNDERWAY THERE, AND THE U.S. WANTS TO BE SUPPORTIVE. 
LAMAMRA RESPONDED THAT A/S PELLETREAU'S MARCH VISIT 
TO ALGIERS AND MEETINGS WITH THE PRESIDENT, PRIME MINISTER 
AND FOREIGN MINISTER HAD EXPANDED OUR BILATERAL DIALOGUE 
WHICH HAD PREVIOUSLY BEEN LESS THAN DESIRED. THERE WAS 
GOOD POTENTIAL TO IMPROVE U.S.-ALGERIAN RELATIONS. WHILE 
AT THE UN, HE PERSONALLY HAD BUILT CONSENSUS WITH THE U.S. 
AROUND ISSUES SUCH AS INDEFINITE EXTENSION OF NPT AND WOULD 
CONTINUE TO DO SO. HIGH LEVEL DIALOGUE SHOULD BE 
CONTINUED. 
5. 44;.) LAMAMRA THEN PROVIDED A BRIEF OVERVIEW OF ECONOMIC, 
POLITICALAND SECURITY DEVELOPMENTS. ECONOMIC ANp 
FINANCIAL COOPERATION WITH THE U.S. WAS INCREASING, HE 
SAID. THERE WERE GOOD PROSPECTS, NOT JUST IN THE ENERGY 
SECTOR, BUT WELL BEYOND. PRESIDENT ZEROUAL AND HIS 
GOVERNMENT HAVE A STRONG COMMITMENT TO BOTH PEACEFUL AND 
DEMOCRATIC GOVERNMENT AND TO ECONOMIC REFORM. THE GOA 
NEEDS TO WORK TOWARD CREATION OF A SYSTEM OF CHECKS AND 
BALANCES AND TO MAKE THE MARKET ECONOMY IRREVERSIBLE. 
FOLLOWING HIS ELECTION, ZEROUAL IS IMPLEMENTING NEW IDEAS, 
SIMILAR TO THOSE IN THE U.S., LIKE A BICAMERAL LEGISLATURE 
AND FIXED TWO-TERM PRESIDENCY. HE NOW NEEDS BACKING FROM 
ALGERIA'S POLITICAL CLASS AND POPULAR SUPPORT FOR THESE 
REFORMS IN A REFERENDUM. IN THE SECURITY AREA, THE

6.-{e}-TALBOTT POINTED TO THE LINKAGE BETWEEN OUR BILATERAL 
RELATIONS AND INTERNAL DEVELOPMENTS IN ALGERIA. IF 
PRESIDENT ZEROUAL AND HIS GOVERNMENT CONTINUE TO MAKE 
PROGRESS IN THE INTERNAL DIALOGUE, THE AREAS IN WHICH WE 
CAN WORK TOGETHER WILL ALSO INCREASE. A/S PELLETREAU NOTED 
THAT, IN A SUBSEQUENT MEETING WITH LAMAMRA, HE WOULD BE 
DISCUSSING RESTRICTIONS ON THE ALGERIAN PRESS AND THE 
IMPORTANT ROLE OF THE MEDIA IN A DEMOCRACY. TALBOTT 
INDICATED THAT, AS A FORMER JOURNALIST, HE WAS PARTICULARLY 
CONCERNED ABOUT THIS AND HOPED TO BE KEPT INFORMED. 
LAMAMRA SAID THAT HE HAD DISCUSSED THE SUBJECT WITH 
AMBASSADOR NEUMANN AND THAT, RELATIVE TO OTHER ARAB 
COUNTRIES, ALGERIA'S PRESS HAS BEEN THE MOST OUTSPOKEN 
SINCE 1989 (REFTEL). 
7.-Eei-AFTER RECEIVING FROM LAMAMRA COPIES OF HIS LETTERS 
OF RECALL AND CREDENCE, AS WELL AS PLANNED WHITE HOUSE 
REMARKS, TALBOTT CONCLUDED THE MEETING WITH A TRIBUTE TO A 
PERSONAL FRIEND AND PROFESSIONAL COLLEAGUE, FORMER GOA
FOREIGN MINISTER LAKHDAR BRAHIMI. BRAHIMI'S SUCCESS AS THE 
UNSYG'S REP IN HAITI HAD GIVEN US A HIGH REGARD FOR 
ALGERIAN DIPLOMATIC ABILITIES, AND TALBOTT HOPED BRAHIMI 
WOULD ACCEPT NEW RESPONSIBILITIES. DCM AMINE KHENE

DIRECTOR JACKSON, MARIA SOTIROPOULOS OF PROTOCOL, AND GEOFF
