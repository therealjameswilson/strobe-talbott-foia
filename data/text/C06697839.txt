2. *el- SUMMARY. IN A MAY 8 MEETING, DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT URGED SLOVENIAN PRIME MINISTER DRNOVSEK TO PUSH 
AHEAD WITH PROPERTY LEGISLATION AIMED AT RESOLVING 
SLOVENIA'S DISPUTE WITH ITALY AND PAVING THE WAY FOR 
SLOVENIA'S ASSOCIATION AGREEMENT WITH THE EUROPEAN UNION. 
HE ASKED DRNOVSEK TO LOOK INTO REPORTS THAT SLOVENIAN 
COMPANIES WERE SELLING ARMS-RELATED EQUIPMENT TO IRAN. 
DRNOVSEK EXPRESSED SLOVENIA'S INTEREST IN BEING AMONG THE 
FIRST NEW MEMBERS OF NATO, AND ASKED FOR RENEWED ACTION ON 
THE PROPOSED DOUBLE TAXATION TREATY WITH THE U.S. END 
SUMMARY. 
RELATIONS WITH ITALY 
3. l'et- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD PRIME MINISTER DRNOVSEK 
THAT IT HAS IMPORTANT FOR SLOVENIA TO PUT THE BILATERAL 
DISPUTE WITH ITALY BEHIND IT BY MOVING AHEAD WITH THE 
LIBERALIZATION OF SLOVENIA'S PROPERTY LAWS. TALBOTT NOTED 
THAT THE U.S. WAS ALSO URGING ITALY TO PUT THIS ISSUE TO 
REST, AND THAT HE HAD PREVIOUSLY PLAYED A ROLE IN 
PROVIDING GOOD OFFICES TO HELP THE TWO SIDES RESOLVE THE 
DISPUTE. HE EXPRESSED SYMPATHY FOR THE POLITICAL 
PRESSURES FACING DRNOVSEK IN THE LATTER'S EFFORTS TO PUSH 
THE PROPERTY LEGISLATION THROUGH PARLIAMENT. IN RESPONSE 
TO DRNOVSEK'S ASSERTION THAT THE SLOVENIAN SIDE HAD 
ALREADY PASSED LEGISLATION STATING ITS WILLINGNESS TO 
RESOLVE THE DISPUTE AND IT IS NOW UP TO THE ITALIAN SIDE 
TO RESPOND, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT HE HOPED 
NEITHER SIDE WOULD VIEW THE ISSUE AS A TENNIS MATCH, WHERE 
EACH SIDE CAN ONLY TAKE ACTION IF THE OTHER SIDE HAS 
RESPONDED. 
4. -4G4.- DRNOVSEK SAID THAT HIS GOVERNMENT HOPED TO AVOID 
AN IMMEDIATE PARLIAMENTARY VOTE ON THE POLITICALLY 
SENSITIVE PROPERTY ISSUE BY PLACING LANGUAGE ON PROPERTY 
RIGHTS IN SLOVENIA'S EU ASSOCIATION AGREEMENT. UNDER THE 
SLOVENIAN CONSTITUTION, SUCH AN AGREEMENT WOULD HAVE THE 
FORCE OF LAW, ACCORDING TO THE PRIME MINISTER, AND WOULD 
ALLOW SLOVENIA TO RESOLVE ITS DISPUTE WITH ITALY WITHOUT

UP TO NATIONAL ELECTIONS. REFERRING TO THE PROPERTY 
LEGISLATION NOW BEFORE PARLIAMENT, DRNOVSEK SAID "THERE IS

5. IN RESPONSE TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S QUESTION AS 
TO HOW THE U.S. COULD BE OF ASSISTANCE IN HELPING THE TWO 
SIDES RESOLVE THE DISPUTE, DRNOVSEKTOINTED TO PRESIDENT 
CLINTON'S RECENT LETTER (I.E., PRAISING PARLIAMENT'S 
STATEMENT OF INTENT ON THE ISSUE AND CALLING ON THE 
GOVERNMENT TO MOVE AHEAD WITH IMPLEMENTING LEGISLATION) AS 
AN.EXAMPLE OF THE POSITIVE ROLE WE COULD PLAY. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY ASKED DRNOVSEK TO STAY IN TOUCH THROUGH OUR 
EMBASSY AND LET US KNOW IF THERE WERE OTHER, SPECIFIC 
ACTIONS'WE COULD TAKE TO HELP MOVE THE PROCESS FORWARD. 
NATO ENLARGEMENT 
6. DRNOVSEK SAID THAT SLOVENIA WANTED TO BE 
CONSIDERED AMONG THE FIRST IN LINE FOR ENTRY INTO AN 
ENLARGED NATO. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT NATO HAD 
NOT YET TAKEN ANY DECISION ON THE "WHO" AND "WHEN" OF 
ENLARGEMENT. HE SAID THAT COUNTRIES WOULD BE EVALUATED ON 
THEIR MERITS AND ON A CASE-BY-CASE BASIS. THE NAC 
MINISTERIAL IN DECEMBER WOULD TAKE STOCK OF THE CURRENT 
"INTENSIFIED DIALOGUE" PHASE OF THE ENLARGEMENT PROCESS 
AND LAY OUT NEXT STEPS. TALBOTT WELCOMED SLOVENIA'S 
ACTIVE PARTICIPATION IN THE PARTNERSHIP FOR PEACE, CITING 
IT AS AN EXAMPLE OF THE "SELF-SELECTION" PROCESS THAT 
COUNTRIES WISHING TO JOIN NATO MUST DEMONSTRATE. 
ARMS TO IRAN 
7. -(44- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT 
REPORTS THAT SLOVENIAN COMPANIES HAD SOLD OR PLANNED TO 
SELL ARMS-RELATED EQUIPMENT TO IRAN. NOTING THAT THE ARMS 
ISSUE HAD ALSO BEEN RAISED IN DRNOVSEK'S MEETINGS WITH THE 
VICE PRESIDENT AND SECRETARY PERRY, TALBOTT UNDERSCORED 
THE IMPORTANCE OF STRICT EXPORT CONTROLS. HE ADDED THAT 
THE USG'S RECENT EXPRESSIONS OF CONCERN ON THIS SUBJECT 
WERE NOT TIMED TO COINCIDE WITH THE PRIME MINISTER'S 
VISIT, BUT WERE THE RESULT OF NEW INFORMATION.

DOUBLE-TAXATION TREATY 
8. -(1:1,1- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT IT IS IMPORTANT TO 
TAKE ADVANTAGE OF THE ROOM FOR GROWTH IN BILATERAL TRADE 
AND INVESTMENT. DRNOVSEK AGREED, SAYING HE HOPED U.S. 
FIRMS WOULD PARTICIPATE IN THE UPCOMING PRIVATIZATION OF 
SLOVENIA'S BANKS. HE THEN ASKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TO 
LOOK INTO THE STATUS OF THE DOUBLE-TAXATION TREATY SOUGHT 
BY SLOVENIA. TALBOTT AGREED TO LOOK INTO THE QUESTION, 
AND TO CALL HIS COUNTERPART AT THE TREASURY DEPARTMENT TO 
SEE WHETHER THE TREATY COULD BE MOVED ALONG. 
DAYTON PEACE PROCESS 
9. -(•C-)- IN RESPONSE TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S QUESTION, 
DRNOVSEK PRAISED THE DAYTON AGREEMENT AND THE ROLE OF 
IFOR. HOWEVER, HE NOTED THAT MORE TIME WAS NEEDED TO 
CONSOLIDATE GAINS ON THE CIVILIAN SIDE, AND SAID THAT ONE 
YEAR WAS NOT LONG ENOUGH FOR THIS CONSOLIDATION TO TAKE 
PLACE. HE SAID THAT THE SERBS AND THE CROATS STILL SEE 
THE SITUATION IN BOSNIA AS TEMPORARY. "I AM NOT SURE THEY 
ACCEPT A UNITED BOSNIA-HERZEGOVINA," HE SAID. 
10. -(-C-)- TALBOTT REPLIED THAT THE U.S. AND ITS ALLIES 
WERE LOOKING INTO WAYS TO ENSURE CONTINUED SECURITY ON THE 
GROUND AFTER IFOR DEPARTS.
