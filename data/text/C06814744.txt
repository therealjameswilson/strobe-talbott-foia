2. —M.- SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE 
TALBOTT AND PERUVIAN FOREIGN MINISTER FERNAND6DE 
TRAZEGNIES MET ON THE MARGINS OF THE 54TH U.N.,GENERAL 
ASSEMBLY ON SEPTEMBER 20, 1999. TALBOTT AND DE TRAZEGNIES 
AGREED TO FURTHER COOPERATION ON COUNTERNARCOTICS AND 
REGIONAL SECURITY ISSUES, ESPECIALLY ON EFFORTS TO SECURE 
PEACE IN COLOMBIA, WHILE TALBOTT STRESSED THE NEED FOR 
PERU TO DEMONSTRATE ITS COMMITMENT TO HUMAN RIGHTS AND 
FAIR ELECTIONS. DE TRAZEGNIES EXPRESSED CONFIDENCE IN 
EXPLORING OPTIONS FOR A CIVILIAN TRIAL FOR IMPRISONED 
AMCIT LORI BERENSON, AND WELCOMED TALBOTT'S INTEREST IN 
TRAVELING TO PERU. END SUMMARY. 
3. -4-&)- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, WHA DAS WILLIAM BROWFIELD, 
D SPECIAL ASSISTANT AND PERU DESKOFF (NOTETAKER) MET FOR 
APPROXIMATELY THIRTY MINUTES AT THE UN GENERAL ASSEMBLY ON 
SEPTEMBER 20 WITH PERUVIAN FOREIGN MINISTER DE TRAZEGNIES.

THE MINISTER WAS ACCOMPANIED BY PERU'S AMBASSADOR TO THE 
U.S. ALFONSO RIVERO AND AMBASSADOR TO THE OAS JOSE EMILIO 
ROMERO. TALBOTT TOLD DE TRAZEGNIES THAT HE BELIEVES THE 
ARRIVAL OF NEW U.S. AMBASSADOR JOHN HAMILTON PROVIDES AN 
OPPORTUNITY TO WORK TOGETHER AND MANAGE, EVEN BETTER, OUR 
MULTIFACETED RELATIONSHIP. HE CONGRATULATED DE TRAZEGNIES 
ON PERU'S SUCCESSFUL PEACE ACCORD WITH ECUADOR AND 
REASSURED HIM THAT WE WILL KEEP THE GOP FULLY INFORMED ON 
OUR STRATEGIES FOR ASSISTING THE GOVERNMENT OF COLOMBIA IN 
ITS INTERNAL CONFLICT, WHICH THE USG REALIZES IS A MATTER 
OF GREAT CONCERN TO PERU. 
4. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT THERE ARE AREAS 
WE NEED TO WATCH AND WORK VERY CAREFULLY AS WE DEVELOP OUR 
RELATIONSHIP, NOTABLY IN HUMAN RIGHTS AND DEMOCRACY, AND 
MENTIONED THE CASE OF AMCIT LORI BERENSON. ON NEXT YEAR'S 
PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS, TALBOTT EXPRESSED THE HOPE THAT, 
FOR THE SAKE OF PERUVIAN DEMOCRACY, THEY PLAY OUT IN A WAY 
THAT DEMONSTRATES TO THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY THAT THEY 
ARE FAIR, TRANSPARENT, AND CONSTITUTIONALLY CORRECT. 
5. (C) DE TRAZEGNIES THANKED TALBOTT FOR HIS REMARKS 
AND COMMENTED THAT RELATIONS HAVE BEEN MOST "EFFICIENT AND 
PRODUCTIVE" WHEN HANDLED THROUGH•QUIET DIPLOMATIC 
CHANNELS, CITING PERU'S COUNTERNARCOTICS SUCCESS AS AN 
EXAMPLE. HE HIGHLIGHTED PERU'S TREMENDOUS PROSRESS ON 
COCA CULTIVATION REDUCTION AND STATED THAT WITH "A GOOD 
RESPONSE" FROM THE U.S., THE GOP'S PROPOSED FIVE-YEAR 
STRATEGIC ALLIANCE TO ELIMINATE COCA COULD BE ACHIEVED. 
6. THE FOREIGN MINISTER EXPRESSED CONCERNS OVER 
COLOMBIAN PRESIDENT PASTRANA'S ABILITY TO MANAGE THE PEACE 
PROCESS, SAYING PASTRANA "CANNOT NEOGITATE WHEN THE ----
TERRORISTS ARE STRONG", AND WORRIED THAT PASTRANA LACKS A 
"PLAN B" TO FALL BACK UPON WHEN "PLAN A" PEACE TALKS FALL 
THROUGH. COLOMBIA REPRESENTS A "NEW DANGER" TO PERU, HE 
SAID, AN INTERNAL CONFLICT OF ANOTHER COUNTRY WHICH 
THREATENS TO SPILL OVER INTO PERU (THUS THE MOVE TO 
REINFORCE PERU'S NORTHERN BORDER). THE GOP AND GOC HAVE 
VERY GOOD RELATIONS AND PERU IS WILLING TO SHARE 
EXPERIENCES FROM ITS BATTLES WITH TERRORISM, BUT PERU IS 
NOT PREPARED TO TAKE ANY ACTION AT THIS POINT TO GET 
INVOLVED IN COLOMBIA. DAS BROWNFIELD SUGGESTED THE GOP 
REVIEW PASTRANA'S NEWLY UNVEILED STRATEGIC PLAN FOR PEACE 
AND SHARE COMMENTS AND SUGGESTIONS WITH US AND THE 
COLOMBIANS, WHICH DE TRAZEGNIES UNDERTOOK TO DO.

7. 4-el— ON HUMAN RIGHTS, DE TRAZEGNIES REPEATED THE GOP 
POSITION THAT THE RULING OF THE INTER-AMERICAN COURT OF 
HUMAN RIGHTS ON THE CHILEAN MRTA MEMBERS WAS A DIRECTIVE 
PERU COULD NOT CONSTITUTIONALLY FULFILL, AND THUS FORCED 
PERU TO WITHDRAW FROM THE COURT'S JURISDICTION ON 
CONTENTIOUS CASES. HE CITED AS PROOF OF PERU'S ADHERENCE 
TO HUMAN RIGHTS THE FACT THAT ALL JAILED TERRORIST LEADERS 
HAD NOT ONLY RECEIVED WHAT HE BELIEVES WERE FAIR TRIALS, 
THEY WERE ALSO SPARED THE DEATH PENALTY. THE FOREIGN 
MINISTER DISMISSED OUTRIGHT THE POSSIBILITY OF FRAUD IN 
NEXT YEAR'S ELECTIONS, STATING CATEGORICALLY THERE WAS NO 
REASON TO QUESTION WHETHER THEY WOULD BE DEMOCRATIC. 
8. -(f4.- REGARDING THE BERENSON CASE, DE TRAZEGNIES

PLAYED A "LEADERSHIP ROLE" IN MRTA. DAS BROWNFIELD TOLD 
THE PERUVIANS THE U.S. WOULD LOOK FURTHER INTO THIS 
POSSIBILITY.
