2. ~SUMMARY: BULGARIAN FM MIKHAYLOVA MET WITH ACTING
SECRETARY TALBOTT MARCH 7. MIKHAYLOVA BRIEFED THE ACTING
SECRETARY ON A RECENT MEETING OF THE FOREIGN MINISTERS OF
TURKEY, GREECE, AND BULGARIA. THE ACTING SECRETARY
DISCUSSED NATO AND LATER PULLED ASIDE THE FM FOR A TETE-A-
TETE IN WHICH HE ENGAGED HER ON ILLEGAL ARMS SALES AND THE
FRY OIL EMBARGO. THE ACTING SECRETARY OFFERED TO INTERVENE
TO HELP SECURE A WHITE HOUSE MEETING FOR PM KOSTOV IN
RETURN FOR POSITIVE BULGARIAN ACTION ON CURBING ILLEGAL
ARMS TRANSFERS OR REDUCING OIL DELIVERIES TO THE FRY. END
SUMMARY.
3. ~ FM MIKHAYLOVA REPORTED ON A THREE-WAY MEETING HELD
MARCH 5 FOR THE FOREIGN MINISTERS OF GREECE, TURKEY AND
HERSELF. MOST IMPORTANTLY F'R THE BULGARIANS, GREECE AND
TURKEY DECLARED THEIR SUPPORT FOR BULGARIAN ACCESSION TO
NATO. MIKHAYLOVA INDICATED THAT SUCH TRILATERAL MEETINGS
COULD BE ORGANIZED IN THE FUTURE AS A MEANS OF REDUCING
TENSIONS. SHE MENTIONED THAT INTERIOR MINISTERS OF GREECE,
TURKEY AND BULGARIA INTENDED TO MEET TO DISCUSS ISSUES SUCH
AS ORGANIZED CRIME AND CORRUPTION. THE BULGARIAN PRESS
CHARACTERIZED THE MEETING AS A "PACT OF NON-VIOLENCE" WHICH
MIKHAYLOVA FELT MISREPRESENTED THE NATURE OF THEIR
DISCUSSIONS.
4. ~ NATO: THE ACTING SECRETARY ASSURED THE FM THAT
ALTHOUGH THE CLINTON ADMINISTRATION WOULD BE OUT OF OFFICE
BEFORE THE ISSUE OF ACCESSION BY ASPIRANT STATES TO NATO
WAS COMPLETED, THE ADMINISTRATION WOULD WORK TO ENSURE
CONTINUITY AND MOMENTUM IN THE NEXT ADMINISTRATION. TO
MAKE BULGARIAN MEMBERSHIP IN NATO A REALITY, SPECIFIC
MEASURES WOULD HAVE TO BE TAKEN BOTH WITHIN BULGARIA AND
EXTERNALLY. BOTH BULGARIA AND OTHER ASPIRANT STATES AND
U.S EXECUTIVE AGENCIES SHOULD CONTINUE TO WORK WITH THE
U.S. CONGRESS AND POLITICAL ELITE AS WELL AS EUROPEAN
MEMBER NATIONS. PUBLIC OPINION WOULD ALSO CONTINUE TO PLAY
AN IMPORTANT ROLE IN THE PROCESS. THE ACTING SECRETARY
NOTED THAT WHILE THE U.S. PRESIDENTIAL CAMPAIGN HAD NOT
FOCUSSED ON FURTHER NATO EXPANSION AS A PROBLEM, POSITIVE
SUPPORT WOULD NEED TO BE GENERATED FOR THE FUTURE.
5. ~TALBOTT-MIKHAYLOVA TETE-A-TETE: IN HIS MEETING
WITH BULGARIAN FM MIKHAYLOVA, THE ACTING, SECRETARY PULLED
HER ASIDE FOR A PRIVATE DISCUSSION. KNOWING THAT THE
BULGARIANS ARE EXTREMELY INTERESTED IN OBTAINING A WHITE
HOUSE MEETING FOR PM KOSTOV, TALBOTT OFFERED HELP IN TRYING
TO MAKE IT HAPPEN. HE TOLD HER, HOWEVER, THAT IT WOULD BE
HARD, ESPECIALLY IN THIS CAMPAIGN YEAR. HE SAID HE WOULD
NEED SOMETHING NEW TO GIVE HIS APPEAL SOME RESONANCE. HE
SUGGESTED POSITIVE BULGARIAN ACTION ON CURBING ILLEGAL ARMS
TRANSFERS, ESPECIALLY IN AFRICA, OR CUTTING BACK OIL
DELIVERIES TO THE FRY AS THINGS THAT MIGHT MAKE A
DIFFERENCE.
