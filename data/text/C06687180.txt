Dear Mr. Carlsson: 
Thank you for your letter to Sir Peter Ustinov, President of the World 
Federalist Movement (WFM.) We would like to congratulate you on the 
establishment of the commission on issues of global governance. 
You may not recall. but I had the pleasure of dining with you in your rooms 
in Ixtapa. Mexico during the SuMmit of the Five Continent Peace Initiative 
some years ago when you were Prime Minister and I was Chairman of 
Parliamentarians Global Action. 
WF)i welcomes the fact that the Commission,is receptive to NGO input. As the 
principal global NGO working to strengthen the UN. WFM would be happy to 
coordinate NGO input into the Commission's work. _ 
The ability of WFM to undertake such a task is witnessed by the fact that 
WFM is already co-convening the International NGO Task Group on Legal and 
Institutional Matters (INTGLIM.) WFM offered to establish INTGLIM early in 
the UNCED process in order to gather and mobilize NGO and individual experts' 
contributions into Working Group III of the Conference. INTGLIM is now a 
network of over 350 governmental and non-governmental contacts from both 
North and South whose top priority during UNCED. the establishment of a 
IIIcommission on sustainable development. has been approved in principle by the 
Earth Summit and will soon be discussed in the UN General Assembly. 
We look forward. Sir. to your response. We wish you much success with the 
work of the Commission. 
Yours sincerely.
