44)- PARTICIPANTS: THE ACTING SECRETARY AND FM AGNELLI 
48* SUBJECT: LINKAGE BETWEEN BRUSSELS CONFERENCE 
AND BALKAN PRISONER RELEASE 
464- AGNELLI SAID SHE CONTACTED HER EUROPEAN COLLEAGUES 
ABOUT CARL BILDT'S POSITION REGARDING LINKAGE BETWEEN 
THE BRUSSELS CONFERENCE AND BALKAN PRISONER RELEASE. 
THE FRENCH ARE BEING DIFFICULT, WHILE THE BRITISH AND 
GERMANS ARE WILLING TO GO AHEAD WITH BILDT'S APPROACH. 
THE EU POLITICAL DIRECTORS WILL MEET WITH BILDT TO 
DISCUSS THE ISSUE. AGNELLI EXPECTS MOST OF THEM WILL 
PRESS BILDT AS TO WHY HE CHANGED HIS MIND ON THE ISSUE.
