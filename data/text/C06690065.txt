May 5, 1993 
Dear Ojars: 
I'm somewhat tardy in replying to your kind letter. I am 
moved to reply as promptly as possible however by the happy 
coincidence of running into you at the opening of the Belarus 
Chancery Wednesday evening. It was a pleasure to see you 
again. As I told you, I vividly remember our trip to Riga 
together and also seeing you on several occasions on the 
Carnegie Endowment after that. 
Thank you for your kind words about At the Highest Levels. 
I'm glad that our account squares with what you know, and I am 
gratified to think that it might be read in Riga. 
I look forward to spending some time with you talking about 
the prospects before your own country, particularly relate to 
those of the countries that make up my new diplomatic beat. 
Best regards. 
Sincerely 
S robe_Talbo ---, 
Ambassador-at-Large and _ 
Special AdliiSer to The Secretary 
on The New Independent States 
His Excellency 
Ojars Erik Kalnin, 
Ambassador of Latvia.
