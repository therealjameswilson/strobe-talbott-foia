1, ~ IN A FEBRUARY 16 MEETING, DEPUTY NATIONAL SECURITY
ADVISOR JAMES STEINBERG DISCUSSED RELATIONS WITH CHINA AND
PREPARATIONS FOR THE G-8 SUMMIT WITH NORESHIGE ANAMI, THE
PRIME,MINISTER'S CHIEF CABINET COUNCILOR FOR EXTERNAL
AFFAIRS. DCM, DIRECTOR JAMES KEITH, SPECIAL ASSISTANT SHEBA
CROCKER& AND POLOFF NOTETAKER ALSO PARTICIPATED.
2. , STEINBERG REVIEWED FOR ANAMI TOPICS TO BE DISCUSSED
BY DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, STEINBERG, AND DELEGATION DURING
THEIR UPCOMING VISIT TO BEIJING. STEINBERG PLEDGED THAT THE
TALBOTT DELEGATION WOULD BE CLEAR IN ITS DISCUSSIONS IN
BEIJING THAT THE U.S.-JAPAN BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP IS
ESSENTIAL TO THE REGION. ANAMI DESCRIBED THE CHINESE
LEADERSHIP AS MORE ENGAGED IN ADDRESSING SERIOUS INTERNAL
POLITICAL PROBLEMS (SMUGGLING SCANDAL, FALUN GONG) THAN
EXTERNAL ONES. HE INFORMED STEINBERG THAT THE GOJ HAD
INVITED PREMIER ZHU RONGJI TO VISIT JAPAN IN THE FIRST HALF
OF THIS YEAR BEFORE THE SUMMITc BUT TO DATE HAD NOT RECEIVED
A RESPONSE. ANAMI ANTICIPATED THAT VICE PREMIER QIAN QICHEN
WOULD TAKE A HARD-LINE, DEFENSIVE POSITION WITH THE U.S.
DELEGATION.

3. (((ANAMI JUDGED THAT BEIJING WOULD WQRRY ABOUT THE
OUTCOME OF THE TAIWAN PRESIDENTIAL ELECTION BUT NOT DO

FUTURE WITH TAIWAN AND HOW TO ACHIEVE IT. KEITH POINTED OUT
THAT NONE OF THE PRESIDENTIAL CANDIDATES IN TAIWAN WERE IN A
POSITION TO RETRACT PRESIDENT LEE TENG-HUI'S DECLARATION OF
STATE"TO-STATE RELATIONS. STEINBERG EMPHASIZED THAT WE
NEEDED, TO MOVE THE CHINESE TOWARD THE CONCLUSION THAT IT
WOULD BE SUFFICIENT IF THE NEW TAIWAN PRESIDENT REFRAINED
FROM REPEATING THE DECLARATION. ANAMI EXPRESSED APPRECIATION
FOR U,S. DIPLOMATIC EFFORTS IN URGING BOTH SIDES TO EXERCISE
RESTRAINT. HE NOTED THAT THE GOJ REFRAINED FROM MAKING A
PUBLIC STATEMENT OUT OF CONCERN ABOUT A POSSIBLY FIERCE
RESPONSE.
4. ~ ON G-8 SUMMIT PREPARATIONS, ANAMI SAID HE WOULD BE
INVOLVED MOST DIRECTLY IN LOGISTICAL ARRANGEMENTS. HE NOTED
THAT DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER NOGAMI WAS SOUNDING OUT THE
OTHER SHERPAS ON THE POSSIBILITY OF ASIAN PARTICIPATION.
STEINBERG RESPONDED THAT THE U.S. WOULD BE SUPPORTIVE IF
ASIAN PARTICIPATION WAS OUTSIDE OF THE FORMAL SUMMIT, AND
THAT THE IDEA OF INVOLVING KEY ASIAN POWERS WAS A GOOD ONE.
HE EXPRESSED SOME CONCERN ABOUT JAPANESE PRESS COMMENTARY
BUILDING UP EXPECTATIONS THAT CHINA WOULD ACTUALLY BE IN THE
SUMMIT ITSELF, STRESSING THAT WE WANT TO AVOID SENDING A
NEGATIVE MESSAGE TQ BEIJING. ANAMI ASKED WHETHER THE
AMBASSADOR'S REMARKS ON THIS TOPIC HAD BEEN ACCURATELY
REPORTED. THE DCM RESPONDED THAT IN FACT THE AMBASSADOR HAD
BEEN TRYING TO GET AROUND THE QUESTION BY ADDRESSING THE
PROCESS.

COMMENTARY ABOUT THE POSSIBILITY OF CHINESE PARTICIPATION IN
THE G-8 SUMMIT. ANAMI EXPRESSED DOUBT THAT BEIJING WOULD
COME FOR A SEPARATE DIALOGUE. ANAMI HOPED TO DISCUSS THIS
WITH FM TANG JIAXUAN, BUT NOTED THAT TANG SAID HE WOULD MEET
ANAMI ON ANY TOPIC BUT THIS ONE. ANAMI SUGGESTED THE PRC
FOREIGN MINISTRY WAS NOT CONFIDENT OF ITS INFLUENCE OVER THE
TOP LEADERSHIP ON THIS QUESTION. IN PARTICULAR, THE CHINESE
MFA LACKS CLOSE ACCESS TO ZHU, ON WHOM THE DECISION MIGHT
DEPEND. ANAMI THOUGHT THE RISK TAKEN BY A CHINESE LEADER IN
VISITING OKINAWA TO DISCUSS A RANGE OF TOPICS (WITHOUT ANY
GUARANTEE THE EUROPEANS WOULD NOT RAISE HUMAN RIGHTS) MIGHT
BE TOO GREAT FOR A POSITIVE DECISION. ANAMI SUGGESTED THAT
WE HAVE TO TELL THE CHINESE THAT PARTICIPATING IN A SEPARATE
DIALOGUE WOULD BE GOOD FOR CHINESE DIPLOMACY AND THAT THEIR
ABSENCE WOULD BE A LOSS. ANAMI BEMOANED THE LACK OF
DISTINCTION IN PRESS COMMENTARY BETWEEN PARTICIPATION IN THE
G-8 SUMMIT ITS ELF AND IN A SEPARATE DIALOGUE. HE EXPRESSED
CONCERN ABOUT THE IMPACT ON PM OBUCHI IF THE PRESS DEFINED
THE EVENT AS A FAILURE WITHOUT CHINESE PARTICIPATION.
6. ~ STEINBERG EMPHASIZED THAT THE U.S. ALSO WANTS TO
AVGID PUTTING PM OBUCHI IN A DIFFICULT POSITION. TURNING TO
BILATERAL ISSUES IN THE SUMMIT CONTEXT, HE POINTED OUT THESE
TOO WERE DELICATE ON BOTH SIDES AND THAT CLOSE COMMUNICATION
WAS NEEDED. WHILE ACKNOWLEDGING. DIFFERENCES BETWEEN SEATTLE
AND OKINAWAI STEINBERG STRESSED THAT WE NEED TO HANDLE ANY
DEMONSTRATIONS IN SUCH A WAY THAT WE PROTECT THE INTEGRITY OF
THE SUMMIT MEETING WITHOUT GIVING THE SENSE WE WERE CUTTING
EVERYONE OUT. STEINBERG AND ANAMI CONCLUDED BY AGREEING THAT
CHANCES OF CHINESE PARTICIPATION IN A SEPARATE DIALOGUE IN
OKINAWA WERE SLIM, BUT THAT IT WAS IMPORTANT TO TRY. IN
CLOSING, STEINBERG PROMISED THE GOJ A REPORT ON THE TALBOTT
DELEGATION MEETINGS IN CHINA.
