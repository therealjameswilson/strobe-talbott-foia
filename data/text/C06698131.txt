TALBOTT, THE SWISS FLOATED NAMES OF POSSIBLE SWISS 
CANDIDATES FOR THE POSITION OF HUMAN RIGHTS OMBUDSMAN FOR

ROBERT FROWICK AS THE HEAD OF THE OSCE MISSION IN BOSNIA.

3. 441:4 SWISS FOREIGN MINISTER COTTI USED HIS MEETING 
WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT TO FLOAT THE NAMES OF 
POSSIBLE SWISS CANDIDATES FOR THE POSITION OF OSCE HUMAN 
RIGHTS OMBUDSMAN IN BOSNIA. COTTI EMPHASIZED THAT THE 
SWISS DO NOT WANT TO OFFER CANDIDATES UNLESS THEY CAN BE 
SURE THEY WOULD RECEIVE SUPPORT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
ASSURED COTTI THAT THE U.S. WOULD GIVE THIS MATTER 
SERIOUS CONSIDERATION AND WOULD GET BACK TO THE SWISS 
SHORTLY, NOTING THAT IT WOULD BE BETTER FOR THE HUNGARIAN 
CHAIRMAN TO NOMINATE A SWISS CANDIDATE THAN FOR THE SWISS 
TO DO IT AFTER TAKING OVER AS OSCE CHAIRMAN IN OFFICE.

SARAJEVO. (LATER, THE SWISS AMBASSADOR TO THE OSCE 
APPROACHED D STAFF TO SAY THAT THE SECOND POSSIBLE SWISS 
CANDIDATE WOULD BE A MR. BIRCHLER, NOT MR. KLEINER. MR. 
BIRCHLER IS CURRENTLY THE DEPUTY ADMINISTRATOR IN 
MOSTAR.)
