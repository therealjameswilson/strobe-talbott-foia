Dear Mr. Usery: 
I read with interest your letter of March 3 detailing "The 
Coal Project" and the work of Partners in Economic Reform You 
and your organization are to be commended for undertaking a 
project that will ultimately benefit the Russian and Ukrainian 
coal industries and assist those countries in the 
transformation to a market economy. 
I appreciate your kind invitation to participate in the 
coal industry seminar in Moscow scheduled for the week of March 
29. I regret that I will be unable to accept. As you may 
know, that while I have been designated Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Advisor to the Secretary of State on the New 
Independent States I have not yet been confirmed by the 
Senate. Until I am confirmed I cannot represent the Department 
of State in an official capacity. In addition, your seminar 
convenes shortly before the récently announced Summit betwe-n 
Presidents Clinton and Yeltsin to be held in early April. I am 
afraid that during the upcoming weeks most of my attention will 
be directed toward assisting the President prepare for the 
Summit. 
May I suggest that you invite members of the Economic 
section and of the USAID mission of the U.S. Embassy in Moscow 
to participate in your seminar. 
I am sorry that I could not respond posit'ively to your 
request. I wish you success as you C2velop the seminar and in 
your endeavors in the former Soviet Union. I wiiL look forward 
to learning more about your organization and will follow its 
progress.
