+G.4- PARTICIPANTS: THE ACTING SECRETARY AND CHERNOMYRDIN 
4. -(44- THE ACTING SECRETARY TOLD RUSSIAN SPECIAL ENVOY FOR 
KOSOVO CHERNOMYRDIN HE AND THE SECRETARY HAD BOTH TALKED 
TO UNSYG ANNAN. ANNAN WAS CONSIDERING GIVING 
CHERNOMYRDIN'S MISSION, AND FINNISH PRESIDENT AHTISAARI'S 
PART IN HIS MISSION, SHOULD HE ACCEPT, A UN BLESSING. 
ANNAN HAD SAID HE WOULD LIKE TO DISCUSS DETAILS AND 
SEQUENCING FURTHER. THE ACTING SECRETARY URGED 
CHERNOMYRDIN TO SPEAK WITH AHTISAARI. CHERNOMYRDIN 
RESPONDED ENTHUSIASTICALLY. HE WAS ANXIOUS TO GET GOING 
AND WANTED TO ENSURE AHTISAARI'S PARTICIPATION IN THE 
NEGOTIATIONS. 
5. tS.). THE ACTING SECRETARY CLARIFIED AHTISAARI'S ROLE. 
AT THE APPROPRIATE MOMENT, AHTISAARI WOULD ACCOMPANY 
CHERNOMYRDIN ON THE "CLIMACTIC AND BREAKTHROUGH VISIT" TO 
BELGRADE. THE ACTING SECRETARY NOTED THIS WOULD BE THE 
CEREMONIAL CLOSER. AHTISAARI WAS NOT PREPARED TO ENGAGE 
IN SHUTTLE DIPLOMACY OVER A PERIOD OF WEEKS OR MONTHS. 
6.-{&} THE ACTING SECRETARY NOTED THE SECRETARY.HAD MET 
WITH RUSSIAN FM IVANOV IN BONN MAY 6. IT WAS IMPORTANT TO 
IDENTIFY AS MUCH COMMON GROUND AS POSSIBLE BETWEEN 
RUSSIA'S CONCEPT OF HOW A PEACE SHOULD BE IMPLEMENTED AND 
THE UNITED STATES'. SUCH A CONCEPT WOULD INCLUDE THE 
MILITARY DIMENSION. THE U.S. WAS WORKING DAY AND NIGHT ON 
THIS ISSUE, NOT JUST IN THE U.S., BUT IN BRUSSELS AS WELL. 
THE ACTING SECRETARY OFFERED TO HAVE IN-DEPTH 
CONVERSATIONS WITH CHERNOMYRDIN'S COLLEAGUES THE FOLLOWING 
WEEK DURING•HIS VISIT TO MOSCOW. 
CHERNOMYRDIN WONDERED ABOUT THE STATUS OF THE UN 
ENVOYS. THE ACTING SECRETARY EXPLAINED ANNAN'S IDEA OF 
APPOINTING TWO ENVOYS WHOSE JOB WOULD BE TO BEGIN WORK 
IMMEDIATELY ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF A PEACE AGREEMENT, 
WITH PARTICULAR FOCUS ON THE ROLE OF THE UN. ANNAN HAD 
NOT MADE A FINAL DECISION ON WHO HE WOULD APPOINT, BUT IT 
WAS THE ACTING SECRETARY'S EXPECTATION HE WOULD APPOINT 
SLOVAKIAN FM KUKAN AND FORMER SWEDISH PM BILDT. 
8. 4.81 THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID ANNAN HAD AGREED 
CHERNOMYRDIN WAS DOING IMPORTANT WORK WITH BELGRADE, AND.
THERE WAS A FEELING THAT CHERNOMYRDIN DESERVED SOME SPACE 
IN WHICH TO WORK. ANNAN ALSO UNDERSTOOD, AS DID THE U.S., 
THAT WHEN CHERNOMYRDIN WAS PREPARED TO MAKE A CLIMACTIC 
VISIT TO BELGRADE IT WOULD BE HELPFUL TO BE ACCOMPANIED BY 
ANOTHER INTERNATIONAL FIGURE. ALL AGREED AHTISAARI WOULD
