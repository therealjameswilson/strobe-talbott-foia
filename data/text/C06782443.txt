SUBJECT: D/S TALBOTT MAKES CONVINCING CASE VIA 
WORLDNET FOR U.S. NON-PROLIFERATION POLICY IN 
SOUTH ASIA 
1. SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT 
CONDUCTED A WORLDNET INTERACTIVE ON NOVEMBER 13 ON 
PEACE AND SECURITY IN SOUTH ASIA WITH PANELISTS IN 
ISLAMABAD AND NEW DELHI. EIGHTEEN PRINT 
JOURNALISTS AS WELL AS SEVERAL MEMBERS OF THE 
FOREIGN AND INFORMATION MINISTRIES ATTENDED THE 
WORLDNET IN ISLAMABAD, PAKISTAN TV FILMED IT FOR 
AIRING ON ITS PRIME7TIME CURRENT EVENTS SHOW, AND 
PAKISTAN STATE RADIO TAPED IT FOR SIMULTANEOUS 
BROADCAST'DURING THE TELECAST. ALL MAJOR URDU-
AND ENGLISH-LANGUAGE NEWSPAPERS GAVE EXTENSIVE 
FRONT-PAGE COVERAGE TO THIS EVENT. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY'S CONCISE OVERVIEW OF U.S. POLICY GOALS 
RECEIVED THE MOST INTENSIVE COVERAGE OF A USIS 
PUBLIC DIPLOMACY EVENT IN PAKISTAN IN MANY YEARS, 
THEREBY DEMONSTRATING TRANSPARENCY IN AMERICAN 
FOREIGN POLICY. END SUMMARY. 
2. USING "PEACE, SECURITY AND PROSPERITY IN SOUTH 
ASIA" AS THE WORKING TITLE FOR THE PROGRAM, 
ISLAMABAD AND NEW DELHI HOSTED A TWO-COUNTRY, TWO-
POST WORLDNET INTERACTIVE WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY 
OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT ON NOVEMBER 13. THE 
PROGRAM ELICITED GREAT INTEREST, AS SHOWN BY THE 
FACT THAT WITH LESS THAN 9 HOURS NOTICE A 
DISTINGUISHED PANEL WAS PUT TOGETHER AND 
JOURNALISTS REPRESENTING EVERY MAJOR DAILY 
NEWSPAPER WERE IN ATTENDANCE ON A FRIDAY EVENING. 
ISLAMABAD PANELISTS WERE SENATOR AKRAM ZAKI, 
CHAIRMAN OF THE SENATE STANDING COMMITTEE ON 
FOREIGN AFFAIRS; AMBASSADOR TANWIR AHMED KHAN, 
FORMER FOREIGN SECRETARY AND INFLUENTIAL OP-ED 
WRITER; AND LT. GEN. (RETD) KAMAL MATINUDDIN, 
FORMER CHAIRMAN OF THE INSTITUTE OF STRATEGIC 
STUDIES AND REGULAR OP-ED COLUMNIST.

6. -- "TALBOTT'S MEDIATION OFFER," AN EDITORIAL 
IN THE ISLAMABAD'S RIGHTIST ENGLISH DAILY, 
"PAKISTAN OBSERVER" (16 NOV.) 
QUOTE: THE UNITED STATES DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE 
MR. STROBE TALBOTT HAS REITgRATED HIS COUNTRY'S 
OFFER OF MEDIATION FOR THE SETTLEMENT OF KASHMIR 
DISPUTE BETWEEN INDIA AND PAKISTAN.... 
THE FAILURE OF SERIES OF ROUNDS OF INDO-PAKISTAN 
TALKS ON KASHMIR ISSUE OVER THE PAST HALF A 
CENTURY PRIMARILY DUE TO THE INDIAN BELLIGERENCE 
HAS BROUGHT TO FOCUS THE FACT THAT THE TWO 
COUNTRIES CANNOT RESOLVE THEIR DIFFERENCES ON 
THEIR OWN.... THE U.S. OFFER OF MEDIATION IN 
THESE ENVIRONMENTS, THEREFORE, APPEARS TO BE THE 
ONLY FEASIBLE WAY OUT TO END THE NUCLEAR AND 
CONVENTIONAL WEAPONS RACE AND TO PROMOTE PEACE AND 
SECURITY IN SOUTH ASIA. WE ARE CERTAINLY NOT 
UNMINDFUL OF THE INDIAN RIGIDITY OVER THE ISSUE, 
WHICH HAS, AS A MATTER OF FACT, IMPEDED ITS 
SOLUTION IN THE PAST FIVE DECADES. YET THE 
CLINTON ADMINISTRATION'S RESOLVE AND PERSEVERANCE 
IN BROKERING ACCORDS FOR BOSNIA AND MIDDLE EAST 
PROVIDES US ENCOURAGEMENT TO HOPE FOR A SIMILAR 
EXERCISE FOR DURABLE PEACE AND STABILITY IN SOUTH 
ASIA.... END QUOTE.

SHOULD BE BORNE IN MIND THAT CTBT HAD BEEN 
DELIBERATED SPECIFICALLY TO RETARD THE NUCLEAR 
DESIGNS OF THE THRESHOLD STATES ONLY.... END 
QUOTE.
