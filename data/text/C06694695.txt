2. SUMMARY: RWANDAN REFUGEES AND ZAIRIAN EFFORTS TO 
COPE WITH ILLEGAL MONETARY TRANSACTIONS WERE THE FOCUS 
OF THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S BRIEF MEETING WITH PRIME 
MINISTER LEON KENGO WA DONDO DURING HIS KINSHASA 
REFUELING STOP OCTOBER 24. KENGO SAID HE WOULD SIGN AN 
ACCORD WITH THE RWANDAN PRIME MINISTER IMMEDIATELY 
FOLLOWING HIS MEETING WITH DEPSEC TALBOTT (WHO HAD 
UNDERSCORED THE BURUNDIAN CONCERN ABOUT RWANDAN REFUGEES 
IN EASTERN ZAIRE). KENGO REPEATED HIS COMPLAINT ABOUT 
THE ILLEGAL PRINTING OF ZAIRIAN CURRENCY AND DECLARED 
THAT THE PRACTICE THREATENS ECONOMIC REFORM. - 
NONETHELESS, KENGO WAS UPBEAT ABOUT HIS MADRID MEETINGS 
WITH THE IMF AND WORLD BANK, BOTH OF WHICH WOULD BE 
SENDING'DELEGATIONS TO ZAIRE WITHIN THE NEXT SEVERAL 
DAYS. END SUMMARY. 
3. MONETARY CHICANERY: IN RESPONSE TO THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY'S INQUIRY, KENGO RECOUNTED THE STEPS HE HAD 
TAKEN LAST WEEK TO REMOVE DEFINITIVELY THE GOVERNOR OF 
THE CENTRAL BANK. HE HAD PRESENTED,EVIDENCE OF ILLEGAL 
TRANSACTIONS TO THE PARLIAMENT. IN ADDITION TO THE 
IMPORTATION OF ILLEGAL CURRENCY FROM ARGENTINA, TEN 
PROMISSORY NOTES, EACH FOR FIFTY MILLION DOLLARS SIGNED 
BY THE HEAD OF THE CENTRAL BANK IN CONCERT WITH FORMER 
PRIME MINISTER BIRINDWA'S MINISTER OF FINANCE, HAD 
SURFACED. THESE NOTES WERE COVERED BY LETTERS FROM 
PRESIDENT MOBUTU ATTESTING TO THE SIGNERS' FULL POWERS. 
4. A/S MOOSE ASKED HOW THIS HAD ALL PLAYED OUT IN 
KENGO'S RELATIONSHIP WITH PRESIDENT MOBUTU. KENGO SAID 
MOBUTU DENIES KNOWING ABOUT THE INSTRUMENTS IN HIS 
NAME. HE HAD, HOWEVER; SOUGHT TO BLOCK THE HCR-PT 
ACTION ON THE GOVERNOR'S SUSPENSION. KENGO SAID, 
HOWEVER, THE PARLIAMENT IS WITH HIM, AND WOULD BE 
MEETING TODAY FOR FINAL RESOLUTION. ONE OF THE 
PERPETRATORS OF THE FIFTY MILLION DOLLAR PROMISSORY 
NOTES HAS FLED TO SOUTH AFRICA. COMMENT: IT WAS CLEAR 
FROM KENGO'S RESPONSE THAT HE WAS GETTING.NO COOPERATION 
FROM MOBUTU IN ATTEMPTING TO CONTROL ZAIRE'S 
CATASTROPHICALLY HAPHAZARD MONETARY TRANSACTIONS. 
5. RWANDAN REFUGEES: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RELAYED THE 
CONCERNS OF THE BURUNDIAN FOREIGN MINISTER DURING HIS 
BRIEF STOP IN BUJUMBURA OCTOBER 21. HE SAID THE 
BURUNDIANS FEEL THEIR STABILITY IS THREATENED BY THE 
EX-RWANDAN ARMY'S PRESENCE IN ZAIRE. KENGO SEEMED MORE 
POSITIVE ON THIS ISSUE NOTING THAT WHEN HE DEPARTED THE 
AIRPORT HE WOULD BE GOING TO SIGN AN AGREEMENT WITH
