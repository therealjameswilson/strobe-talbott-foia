Dear Strobe, 
You probably won't be able to put a face to my name, but I met 
you [and your wife] during one of your visits to Singapore when I 
was working there for TIME with Nigel Smith [previously with Marc 
Weinberger in London]. After seven years in Lee's Republic, I 
went to Hong Kong for another four and a half years [though took 
a break from TIME, instead designing magazines and did some of my 
own writing], returning to the UK in May 93. Slight shock to the 
system after so long in Asia! 
I felt prompted to drop you a note of congratulations on your 
elevated position, having seen recent press articles [enclosed, 
though am sure you have your own clipping service]. TIME's loss 
is definitely the Administration's gain. It's comforting to know 
there is someone in there who understands what's happening beyond 
the confines of the US boundaries. 
With the current mess in the UK government, it's a pity John 
Major doesn't have someone of your calibre to advise him! 
As was the case with your articles in TIME, I shall continue to 
look for your name with interest in the world arena. At the next 
US elections will it be "Strobe for President"??! 
With kind regards, 
4.„ 
Gil ian Ashworth
