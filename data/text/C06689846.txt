RELEASE IN FULL 
January 19, 1994 
Dear vlad: 
Thanks for your letter. As always, it was a tonic to hear 
from you. Believe me, I'm not letting the yelling and screaming 
about our Russia policy get to me. It's quite understandable 
after the setback of December 12 -- understandable, but not very 
persuasive. As for the NYTimes disclosure that there's no 
unanimity in our shop -- well, that's exaggerated to say the 
least. The Times misinterpreted my now-famous "less shock, more 
therapy" line and then got caught having to reconcile their 
misinterpretation with a clearer understanding of the policy. 
It's no big deal. 
I'm on my way home from the summit, which provided plenty of 
evidence for both the optimists and the pessimists. 
Hope this finds you well. 
Cheers. 
Sincerely, 
--' 
. I 
Strobe Talbott 
Mr. Vladimir Petrov 
The George Washington University, 
Institute for Sino-Soviet Studies, 
Washington, DC.
