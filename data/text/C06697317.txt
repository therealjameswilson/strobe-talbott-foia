-(-Er)- FM AGNELLI BEGAN BY NOTING THAT HER TENURE AS 
ITALIAN FOREIGN MINISTER IS ABOUT TO END. SHE SAID THE 
REASON FOR THE CALL WAS TO ASK A FAVOR. SHE NOTED THE 
HUNGARIAN CANDIDATE FOR SYG OF THE OSCE HAD BEEN VETOED 
BY ANOTHER OSCE MEMBER, PERHAPS ROMANIA. THE ACTING ' 
SECRETARY SAID HE BELIEVED THE SLOVAKS HAD OBJECTED TO 
THE HUNGARIAN. 
AGNELLI STATED THE EU HAD NOW DECIDED THE HEAD OF 
THE OSCE SHOULD COME FROM ONE OF ITS MEMBER STATES. 
WITH THAT IN MIND, AGNELLI CONTINUED, SHE WISHED TO 
SUGGEST GIANCARLO ARAGONA, HER CHIEF OF STAFF FOR THE 
LAST 18 MONTHS, FOR THE JOB. AGNELLI ADDED SHE HAD 
ALREADY SPOKEN WITH OTHER EU MEMBERS, WHO WANTED THE 
CURRENT EU AMBASSADOR TO KENYA FOR THE OSCE SYG JOB. 
AGNELLI SAID WHEN A NEW ITALIAN GOVERNMENT IS FORMED, 
BOTH SHE AND'MR. ARAGONA WILL LEAVE THE FOREIGN MINISTRY 
AND HE IS THE BEST CANDIDATE'THE ITALIANS HAVE TO 
OFFER. AGNELLI ADDED SHE WOULD BE VERY PLEASED IF MR. 
ARAGONA RECEIVED THE ONE-YEAR OSCE JOB. 
-**- THE ACTING SECRETARY REPLIED HE ATTACHED GREAT 
IMPORTANCE TO AGNELLI'S PERSONAL REQUEST AND HE WOULD 
FORWARD IT TO SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY ADDED HE KNEW FROM HIS OWN EXPERIENCE THAT THE 
WORLD OF MULTILATERAL ORGANIZATIONS IS A JUNGLE. THE.
ACTING SECRETARY THEN PROMISED AGNELLI HE WOULD DISCUSS 
MR. ARAGONA'S CANDIDACY WITH ASSISTANT SECRETARY JOHN
