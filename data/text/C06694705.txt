RELEASE IN FULL 
Date: November 4, 1994 
Participants: The Deputy Secretary 
Steven Solarz 
Debbi Schwartz, Notetaker 
Solarz said he was working on an initiative spearheaded by Mort 
Abramowitz and the Carnegie Endowment. They have received a 
$200,000 grant from George Soros to establish a new NGO which 
would facilitate more effective responses to man-made disasters 
like Rwanda and Bosnia. 
They are assembling-a cadre of experts who have dealt with 
various aspects of such disasters, such as peacekeeping, human 
rights, health, etc. These teams would be dispatched to the 
scene early on to develop a comprehensive set of 
recommendations, which they would then bring to the attention 
of relevant international organizations, governments and NGOs. 
They will be backed up by a prestigious board consisting of 
Nobel Prize winners, former presidents, etc, who will use their 
pressure on governments. They will also use public advocacy 
where necessary. 
The objective of this organization is to use an early warning 
system to prompt early action toward effective responsés. 
A steering committee has been set up, which will meet in 
mid-January in London. Members include Mexican President 
Salinas, former Costa Rican president Oscar Arias, former 
Australian PM Fraser, former Nigerian president Obasanjo, 
former Canadian PM MacDougal, Desmond Tutu, Bob McNamara, 
Vernon Jordon, Paul Simon, etc. 
Solarz has been asked to travel, meet with government leaders 
and get reactions. They anticipate an annual budget of $10-12., 
million annually, and would hope to get $1 million from each of 
10-12 governments. He is optimistic that governments will want 
to give, to get "greater humanitarian bang for their crisis 
management buck." He recently visited the Nordic countries 
(encouraging responses), Saudi Arabia (virtual commitment), Abu 
Dhabi and Kuwait (positive). Next week he will go to London, 
Bonn, the Hague and Brussels; in December he will go' to Asia.
