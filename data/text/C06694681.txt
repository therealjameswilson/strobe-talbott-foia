INCREASING IVORIAN SUPPORT FOR PKO, DIPLOMATIC EFFORTS 
3. IN HIS OCTOBER 25 MEETING WITH DEFENSE 
MINISTER/ACTING FOREIGN MINISTER LEON KONAN KOFFI, 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT RAISED THREE ISSUES, CENTERED 
ON COTE D'IVOIRE'S POTENTIAL INVOLVEMENT IN RESOLVING 
THE LIBERIAN CONFLICT: THE ROLE OF THE UN AND ECOWAS 
IN PEACE-KEEPING OPERATIONS (PKO), RENEWAL OF IVORIAN 
DIPLOMATIC EFFORTS TO END THE LIBERIAN CIVIL WAR, AND 
THE OFFER OF USG TRAINING IN PEACE-KEEPING. THE 
IVORIAN MILITARY AND POLITICAL LEADERSHIP HAD RECENTLY 
BEGUN TO CONSIDER A POSSIBLE ROLE IN PKO, OBSERVED THE 
DEPSEC. FOREIGN MINISTER ESSY'S PRESENCE IN NEW YORK 
TO PRESIDE OVER THE 49TH UNGA UNDERSCORED THE 
IMPORTANCE OF AFRICA IN THAT BODY. 
4. COMMENTING ON THE KEY ROLES OF ECOWAS AND ECOMOG IN 
REGIONAL CRISIS RESOLUTION, THE DEPSEC ASKED THAT COTE 
D'IVOIRE SUPPORT GHANAIAN PRESIDENT RAWLINGS' ENDEAVORS 
TO IDENTIFY AN ECOWAS SOLUTION TO THE LIBERIAN 
CONFLICT. THE DEPSEC ALSO SUGGESTED THAT THE GOCI 
"HARNESS THE ENERGY" OF YAMOUSSOUKRO I-IV, THE COTONOU 
ACCORD, AND THE AKOSOMBO AGREEMENT IN ORDER TO 
REVITALIZE ITS OWN DIPLOMATIC PEACEMAKING EFFORTS. ONE 
SHOULD NOT BELIEVE TALK ABOUT FRANCOPHONE/ANGLOPHONE 
WEST AFRICAN DIFFERENCES, COMMENTED THE DEPSEC. COTE 
D'IVOIRE AND GHANA WERE UNITED IN THE QUEST FOR PEACE 
IN LIBERIA.

5. MINISTER KOFFI DID NOT COMMENT DIRECTLY ON IVORIAN

1,000 IVORIAN TROOPS POSTED ALONG THE IVORO-LIBERIAN 
BORDER AND THESE TROOPS NEEDED ADDITIONAL LOGISTICAL 
SUPPORT (INCLUDING VEHICLES AND TENTS).
