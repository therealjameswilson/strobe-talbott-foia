Dear Bruce: 
I must begin by apologizing to you for my delay in 
responding to your. letter. Since the announcement of my 
appointment life has been very hectic. If confirmed and 
installed in the Deputy Secretary's suite I hope to become 
better organized. 
Thanks for your words of support. They mean a lot to me. 
The future responsibilities are both daunting and exciting. Be 
sure to let me know when you think I'm out of line. 
I hope all is well with you. Thanks again for writing.
