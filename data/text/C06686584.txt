Dear Archbishop McCarrick: 
Thank you for your kind letter. I very much enjoyed 
meeting you at the Appeal of Conscience Foundation dinner. 
I greatly appreciate your comments about my remarks. I 
feel very strongly that freedom of religion is a fundamental 
right. Tolerance is the basis of freedom and human dignity. 
Your prayers and support are most appreciated. We have a 
great task before us, and a little divine intervention now and 
then can only help. 
Strobe Talbott 
The Most Reverend Theodore E. McCarrick
