2. DURING A MID-ROUND BRIEFING ON LATEST 
RUSSIAN-BROKERED TALKS WITH THE PARTIES ON 
NAGORNO-KARABAKH, RUSSIAN SPECIAL NEGOTIATOR-
KAZIMIROV PRESENTED NUMEROUS RUSSIAN IDEAS FOR 
RESOLVING THORNY ISSUESLIKE LACHIN, SHUSHA AND 
THE DEPLOYMENT OF MONITORING AND PEACEKEEPING 
FORCES. BUT HE ADMITTED THE PARTIES TO THE 
CONFLICT REMAINED SHARPLY OPPOSED ON THE 
TERRITORIAL QUESTIONS. KAZIMIROV GAVE A LUKEWARM 
RESPONSE TO THE U.S. PROPOSAL FOR A FOUR-PARTY 
LEADERSHIP GATHERING LATER THIS MONTH IN NEW YORK 
ON NAGORNO-KARABAKH AND SOMEWHAT TESTILY DEFENDED 
HIS OWN NEGOTIATING RECORD INDEPENDENT OF THE 
MINSK GROUP. KAZIMIROV CONFIRMED THAT THE 
RUSSIANS WOULD BE UNABLE TO ATTEND ANY MINSK 
GROUP MEETING NEXT WEEK IN VIENNA, AND CALLED FOR 
FLEXIBILITY BY ALL PARTIES IN SUPPORTING RUSSIAN 
EFFORTS TO MAINTAIN MOMENTUM IN THE SEARCH FOR 
PEACE. END SUMMARY. 
INTRODUCTION 
3. IN A SEPTEMBER 10 BRIEFING FOR VISITING S/NIS 
SENIOR COORDINATOR COLLINS, RUSSIAN SPECIAL 
NEGOTIATOR FOR NAGORNO-KARABAKH KAZIMIROV 
PROVIDED A DETAILED BRIEFING ON THE ON-GOING

PARTICIPATED IN THE SESSION, ARRANGED BY DFM

4. KAZIMIROV BEGAN BY COMPLAINING ABOUT THE 
MANEUVERINGS OF THE PARTIES TO THE CONFLICT OVER 
PRESS COVERAGE AND KOCHARYAN'S PARTICIPATION IN 
THE TALKS. BUT HE CONFIRMED THAT ALIYEV, TER-
PETROSYAN, KOCHARYAN AND KOZYREV, ALONG WITH 
THEIR DEPUTYIES, HAD HELD TALKS ON SEPTEMBER 8 
AND 9 AND THAT THE TALKS WOULD CONTINUE LATER IN 
THE DAY. KAZIMIROV INDICATED THE ATMOSPHERE OF 
THE TALKS HAD IMPROVED ONCE MODALITIES WERE 
GOTTEN OVER AND THE SIDES HAD SAT DOWN TOGETHER.
