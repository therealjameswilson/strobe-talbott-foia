1~ SUMMARY. MOLDOVAN FM TABACARU MET IN WASHINGTON
WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, S/NIS AMBASSADOR
SESTANOVICH, CFE SPECIAL ENVOY DUNKERLEY AND EUR/WST
OFFICE DIRECTOR WARLICK. TABACARU WAS ACCOMPANIED BY
MOLDCiVAN AMB. CIOBANU, POLOFF GRIGORE AND U.S. DESK
OFFICER GALBUR. DISCUSSIONS CONTINUED/AT A FOLLOW ON
LUNCH HOSTED BY S/NIS AMBASSADOR SESTANOVICH. A RANGE
OF ISSUES WERE DISCUSSED AT THE MEETINGS, INCLUDING
STRATEGY FOR ADVANCING PROGRESS ON RUSSIAN
IMPLEMENTATION OF ITS CFE OBLIGATIONS ON WITHDRAWAL
FROM TRANSNISTRIA, CONTINUING CQNTACTS BETWEEN THE
MOLDOVAN FIRM CUANTA AND THE IRANIAN MISSILE PROGRAM
AND MQLDOVAN INTEGRATION INTO EURO-ATLANTIC STRUCTURES.

2, ~ DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE TALBOTT MET WITH
MOLDOVAN FM NICOLAE TABACARU IN WASHINGON ON MARCH 15.
TABACARU DESCRIBED HIS MEETINGS THE PREVIOUS DAY WITH
SEVERAL MEMBERS OF CONGRESS, INCLUDING SONNY CALLAHAN,
CHAIRMAN OF THE HOUSE SUBCOMMITTEE ON FOREIGN
OPERATIONS. TABACARU SAID CALLAHAN CALLED MOLDOVA THE
"MODEL" OF SUCCESSFUL U.S. ASSISTANCE AND SAID HE WOULD
SUPPORT A REQUEST FROM THE ADMINISTRATION FOR AN
INCREASE IN ITS ASSISTANCE PROGRAM. TABACARU ASKED
THAT THE DEPARTMENT SUBMIT SUCH A REQUEST.
3. ~WITH AN EYE TO POST-ELECTION RUSSIAN POLICIES
TOWARD THE CIS, TABACARU NOTED THAT MOLDOVA IS FACING
THE QUESTION OF "TO BE QR NOT TO BE." HE ASSERTED THAT
RUSSIA IS USING GAS AS A POLITICAL LEVER TO KEEP
MOLDOVA IN THE CIS SPHERE AND TO PREVENT ITS FURTHER
INTEGRATION WITH THE WEST. THE RECENT GAZPROM SHUTOFF
IN MQLDOVA DID NOT AFFECT TRANSNISTRIA, HOWEVER,
CLEARLY INDICATING THAT RUSSIA IS WILLING TO USE
MOLDOVA'S ENERGY DEPENDENCY FOR POLITICAL PURPOSES.
TRANSNISTRIA WAS ALSO PROTECTED FROM GAS SHUT OFFS
BECAUS" TRANSNISTRIANS WITH RUSSIAN CITIZENSHIP WILL BE
ALLOWED TO VOTE IN THE UPCOMING RUSSIAN ELECTIONS AND
PUTIN IS COUNTING ON THEIR SUPPORT.
4.~TABACARU THANKED TALBOTT FOR U.S. ASSISTANCE
DURING THE ISTANBUL SUMMIT, AND AMBASSADOR DUNKERLEY'.S
CONTRIBUTIONS IN PARTICULAR. NOTING THE CONTINUED
EFFORTS OF THE TRANSNISTRIAN LEADERSHIP TO BLOCK TROOP
WITHDRAWAL'ND THE WORK OF THE OSCE ASSESSMENT TEAM
UNDER FRENCH GENERAL AUSSEDAT, HE STRESSED THAT SMIRNOV
SHOULD NOT BE ALLOWED TO INTERFERE WITH RUSSIAN
IMPLEMENTATION OF ITS. CFE COMMITMENTS. AN OSCE
ASSESSMENT MISSION WOULD BE KEY TO GETTING THE
EUROPEANS TO FOLLOW THE U.S. LEAD IN PROVIDING SUPPORT
.FOR RUSSIAN WITHDRAWAL. TABACARU NOTED FAVORABLY
SPECIAL NEGOTIATOR CAREY CAVANAUGH'S RECENT MEETING
WITH SMIRNOV, BUT INSISTED THAT ONLY MOSCOW CAN DELIVER
SMIRNOV. NOW IS THE TIME TO PRESS RUSSIA TO BRING
SMIRNOV ON BOARD, AS PUTIN WILL TRY TO CONSOLIDATE

THE UKRAINIAN SIDE OF THE UKRAININAN-TRANSNISTRIAN

5. (g TALBOTT RESPONDED THAT THE U.S. POSITION IS VERY
CLEAR: WE SUPPORT THE INDEPENDENCE AND SOVEREIGNTY OF
MOLDOVA AND RECOGNIZE THAT THESE WILL BE COMPROMISED AS
LONG AS THE COUNTRY IS DIVIDED. ACCORDINGLY, WE WILL
ACTIVELY CONTINUE TO SEEK A COMPREHENSIVE POLITICAL
SETTLEMENT FOR TRANSNISTRIA AND ENCOURAGE THE PROMPT
AND CRDERLY WITHDRAWAL OF ALL RUSSIAN FORCES FROM THE
COUNTRY. IN TURN, MOLDOVA'S FIRST ORDER OF BUSINESS
MUST BE TO RESOLVE THE CURRENT ECONOMIC AND POLITICAL
CRISIS. CURBING THE FLOW OF TECHNOLOGY WITH
NONPROLIFERATION IMPLICATIONS IS ALSO A SERIOUS ISSUE.
OUR BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP COULD BE CLOUDED IF THE GOM
DOES NOT ACT NOW TO STOP CONTACT BETWEEN CUANTA AND THE
IRANIAN MISSILE PROGRAM. CUANTA'S CONTACTS WITH IRAN
ARE AS SERIOUS AS. CONTACTS BETWEEN RUSSIAN ENTITIES AND
IRAN, AND IF THEY CONTINUE THE U.S, WILL IMPOSE
SANCTIONS ON CUANTA SIMILAR TO THOSE IMPOSED ON RUSSIAN
ENTITIES. IF THIS ACTIVITY IS NOT STOPPED, MOLDOVA
COULD ALSO LOSE SUPPORT IN CONGRESS FOR ITS ASSISTANCE
PROGRAMS.
6. ~ TALBOTT AGREED WITH THE MINISTER THAT PUTIN HAS
TALKED ABOUT RUSSIA WANTING TO BE PART OF EUROPE AND
NATO~ AS A POLITICAL NOT MILITARY ORGANI ZATION THE
U.S. HAD MADE CLEAR, HOWEVER, THAT IF COOPERATION IS
RUSSIA'S GOAL, IT MUST UNDERSTAND THE NEED TO PLAY BY
THE RULES, INCLUDING RULES ON RESPECT FOR THE
SOVEREIGNTY AND INDEPENDENCE OF NEIGHBORING STATES.
CONTRARY TO THE MINISTER'S FEAR OF DEALS MADE AT
MOLDOVA'S EXPENSE& THE U.S. WOULD TAKE ADVANTAGE OF
UPCOMING U.S.-RUSSIAN CONTACTS AT THE PRESIDENTIAL
LEVEL --A POSSIBLE BILATERAL SUMMIT, THE G-7 OKINAWA
SUMMIT, AND THE UNGA SESSION -- TO REINFORCE THIS
MESSAGE. IN HIS LATEST MEETING WITH RUSSIAN DEP FM
MAMEDOV IN ROME, IN THE PRESENCE OF RUSSIAN GENERAL
ZLENKO, TALBOTT HAD STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF RUSSIA
FULFILLING ITS CFE OBLIGATIONS IN GEORGIA AND MOLDOVA.
THE U.S. WILL CONTINUE TO ENCOURAGE THE RUSSIANS TO SEE
THE BENEFIT OF VISIBLE PROGRESS IN IMPLEMENTING THEIR
ISTANBUL COMMITMENTS AND WILL URGE THE WEST EUROPEANS
TO SEND A SIMILAR MESSAGE. TALBOTT POINTED OUT THAT,
FOR THE NEW RUSSIAN LEADERSHIP, TOLERANCE FOR CONTINUED
OBSTRUCTION BY TRANSNISTRIAN SEPARATISTS COULD BE CAST
AS DIRECTLY CONTRADICTING THEIR FUNDAMENTAL CONCERN
ABOUT SEPARATISM IN CHECHNYA AND ELSEWHERE IN THE NORTH
CAUCASUS.

APPROACHES ON THIS AND THAT MOLDOVA SHOULD TAKE MAXIMUM
ADVANTAGE OF ITS CURRENT STATUS BY BEING AS ACTIVE AS
POSSIBLE IN THE PACT.
8. ~ DURING A BRIEF PULL-ASIDE WITH TALBOTT, TABACARU
ASKED ABOUT THE STATUS OF A VISIT FOR PRESIDENT
LUCINSCHI TO WASHINGTON, STATING THAT HIS OWN FUTURE IS
AT STAKE. HE ASKED IF EITHER HE OR LUCINSCHI WERE
"DOING SOMETHING WRONG," SINCE THE PRESIDENTS OF ALL
THE OTHER COUNTRIES IN SOUTHEASTERN EUROPE HAD MET WITH
THE PRESIDENT IN WASHINGTON. TALBOTT POINTED OUT THE
DIFFICULTY OF FINDING TIME ON THE PRESIDENT'S SCHEDULE,
ESPECIALLY DURING AN ELECT ION YEAR~ BUT SAI D HE WOULD
TRY TO ADVANCE THIS DURING HIS UPCOMING TRIP TO SOUTH
ASIA WITH THE PRESIDENT. TABACARU, AT A 'LUNCH
IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING THE MEETING WITH TALBOTT, RAISED
THE SAME ISSUE WITH SESTANOVICH.
9. ((( DURING THE FOLLOW ON LUNCH HOSTED BY AMBASSADOR
SESTANOVICH, TABACARU PRESSED FURTHER ON EXPANDING THE
U.S. ASSISTANCE PROGRAM FOR MOLDOVA, INCLUDING THE
POSSIBILITY OF REPROGRAMING SOME OF THE ETRI FUNDS.
SESTANOVICH RESPONDED THAT WHILE SOME SLIGHT INCREASES
MIGHT BE POSSIBLE, U.S. ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS THROUGHOUT
THE NIS HAD BEEN REDUCED THIS YEAR DUE TO CONGRESSIONAL
ETRI REQUIREMENTS.
10. ~ SESTANOVICH ALSO EMPHASIZED THE PROBLEMS WITH
CUANTA'S ACTIVITIES, NOTING THE PROBLEM WAS NOT SOLVED
AND GETTING WORSE. WE UNDERSTOOD CUANTA WAS MOSTLY
OWNED BY THE GOM. U.S. LAW REQUIRED THE IMPOSITION OF
SANCTIONS IF TRANSFERS WERE BEING MADE TO IRAN'S
MISSILE PROGRAM. THE U.S. HAD SENT A TEAM TO MOLDOVA
EARLIER THIS YEAR TO ADVISE ON ITS EXPORT CONTROL LAW.
WE WERE ALSO DEVELOPING A ROADMAP IN THE NEXT COUPLE OF
WEEKS. IF WE HAD TO IMPOSE SANCTIONS, THIS WOULD
BECOME A BIG BILATERAL PROBLEM AND ENDANGER U.S.
ASSISTANCE TO MOLDOVA. WE WERE TRYING TO HELP, BUT SAW
NO RESULTS. TABACARU PROFESSED NOT TO BE AWARE OF THE
GOM'S CONTROLLING STAKE IN CUANTA AND ASKED FOR
EVIDENCE OF ITS ACTIVITIES WITH IRAN. SESTANOVICH
NOTED WE HAD SHARED INFORMATION IN THE PAST. THIS
COULD EITHER BECOME AN EVEN BIGGER PROBLEM OR A REAL
SUCCESS, DEPENDING ON THE GOM'S RESPONSE. MOREOVER,
REAL PROGRESS IN HALTING THIS ACTIVITY COULD HELP MAKE
THE GOM'S CASE FOR THE HIGH LEVEL VISIT IT WAS SEEKING.
ll.~ ON THE ECONOMIC AND POLITICAL CRISIS IN
MOLDOVA, TABACARU SAID THERE WAS'NO REAL ARGUMENT OVER
WHETHER TO PRIVATIZE THE TOBACCO AND WINE INDUSTRIES,
JUST A DISAGREEMENT OVER IMPLEMENTATION. HE WAS
CONFIDENT THAT THIS WOULD BE RESOLVED BY MARCH
31.'ABACARU

12. ~ AMB. DUNKERLEY, ALSO AT LUNCH, ASKED FOR THE
MINISTER'S THOUGHTS ON A MOLDOVAN GAMEPLAN FOR DEALING
WITH THE RUSSIANS THIS YEAR IN ORDER TO MAKE BEST USE
OF VARIOUS EVENTS AND HIGH-LEVEL MEETINGS AS FORCING
MECHANISMS FOR DECISIONS OUT OF MOSCOW AND PROGRESS ON
THE GROUND. HE WELCOMED THE MOLDOVAN INITIATIVE TO
PROPOSE THAT BILATERAL TALKS WITH THE RUSSIANS BEGIN AS
EARLY AS MARCH 30 BUT INQUIRED ABOUT THE MOLDOVAN
STRATEGY AND SCRIPT FOR THESE TALKS. TABACARU
RESPONDED THAT HE EXPECTED LITTLE SUCCESS FROM
BILATERAL TALKS ALONE. DUNKERLEY NOTED THAT ACTION WAS
CLEARLY NEEDED ON ALL TRACKS. SMIRNOV'S BEHAVIOR LAST
WEEK, FOR EXAMPLE, HAD GIVEN THE U.S.. AND MOLDOVA A
GOOD OPPORTUNITY TO HIGHLIGHT THE PROBLEM OF PROGRESS
ON WITHDRAWAL IN THE OSCE PERMANENT COUNCIL IN VIENNA.
SESTANOVICH EMPHASIZED THE IMPORTANCE OF INTRODUCING A
SENSE OF URGENCY INTO THE PROCESS IN ORDER TO ADVANCE
PROGRESS ON THE COMMITMENTS MADE AT ISTANBUL.
13.~ TABACARU SAID IT WOULD BE IMPORTANT TO SEE
EARLY SIGNS OF REAL MILITARY WITHDRAWALS (I.E.,TANKS,
NOT BULLDOZERS). HE WAS HESITANT ABOUT RAISING THE
WITHDRAWAL ISSUE AT THE JULY CIS MEETING ON
TRANSNISTRIA'S POLITICAL STATUS, HOWEVER, FOR FEAR THE
RUSSIANS WOULD FIND AN EXCUSE TO INSIST ON
"SYNCHRONIZATION" OF THE ISSUES. DUNKERLEY AGREED THAT
ANY EFFORT AT AUTOMATIC LINKAGE SUCH AS
"SYNCHRONIZATION" IMPLIED NEEDED TO BE RESISTED. BUT
IT WOULD BE IMPORTANT TO PLAN IN ADVANCE IN ORDER TO
TAKE ADVANTAGE OF ALL UPCOMING SUMMIT LEVEL MEETINGS
AND OTHER OPPORTUNITIES THIS YEAR IN ORDER TO ADVANCE
THE WITHDRAWAL ISSUE IN A SYSTEMATIC WAY. SUCH A
STRATEGY WOULD / AMONG OTHER TH INGS ~ MAKE MORE DI FF ICULT
THE USE OF TRANSNISTRIAN RECALCITRANCE AS AN EXCUSE FOR
RUSSIAN INACTION. SESTANOVICH SUGGESTED THE MINISTER
STAY IN CLOSE TOUCH WITH DUNKERLEY IN DEVELOPING JUST
SUCH A SENSE OF GAMEPLAN AND CALENDAR OVER THE COMING
WEEKS.
14.~AMBASSADOR SESTANOVICH ASKED ABOUT MOLDOVA'S
STRATEGY FOR REDUCING ITS ENERGY DEPEPENDENCY ON RUSSIA
AND GAZPROM. TABACARU NOTED THAT RUSSIA WAS LIKELY TO
REMAIN MOLDOVA'S PRINCIPAL SUPPLIER OF GAS AND OIL FOR
THE FORESEEABLE FUTURE, BUT MOLDOVA WAS TAKING STEPS TO
RENEGOTIATE ITS CONTRACT WITH GAZPROM ON MORE FAVORABLE
TERMS AND ENCOURAGE FOREIGN INVESTMENT IN THE OIL, GAS
AND POWER GENERATING SECTORS. AN OIL TERMINAL WAS
BEING BUILT ON THE DANUBE AND MOLDOVAN CONNECTIONS TO
'HE
CASPIAN PILELINES MIGHT ALSO HELP. PROGRESS WOULD

URGED THAT FOLLOW UP ACTIONS BE TAKEN ON A NUMBER OF
ISSUES THAT HAD COME UP FOR DISCUSSION DURING HIS AND
