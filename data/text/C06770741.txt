SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MARCH 10 CALL ON DOWNING STREET 
DIPLOMATIC ADVISER SAWERS DECLASSIFIED 
1. (U) CLASSIFIED BY: JUDITH R. JOHNSON, POLITICAL MINISTER-
COUNSELOR, AMEMBASSY LONDON. REASON: 1.5(B) AND (D) 
2. 4Q4 SUMMARY: IN A MARCH 10 MEETING AT DOWNING STREET, 
THE PM'S DIPLOMATIC ADVISER JOHN SAWERS URGED THAT WE 
CAREFULLY THINK THROUGH NOW WHAT OUR OBJECTIVES WOULD BE FOR 
THE USE OF FORCE IN THE KOSOVO DILEMMA BEYOND COERCING 
MILOSEVIC TO ACCEPT THE RAMBOUILLET AGREEMENT. THE BRITISH 
PUBLIC wopup NOT SUSTAIN A PROLONGED BOMBING CAMPAIGN. IT 
WOULD BE IMPORTANT TO ARTICULATE A MORE LIMITED SECONDARY 
OBJECTIVE, SUCH AS REDUCING THE ABILITY OF SERB FORCES TO 
REPRESS THE ALBANIAN KOSOVARS. WE SHOULD ALSO KEEP IN MIND THE 
EFFECT OF CONCURRENT ACTIONS IN IRAQ ON PUBLIC OPINION. 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND SAWERS AGREED TO STRESS WITH THE 
RUSSIANS THAT THE INTERIM SOLUTION AND KFOR DEPLOYMENT WOULD 
BE THE BEST HEDGE AGAINST THE BREAK-UP OF YUGOSLAVIA. ON 
RUSSIA, SAWERS SAID BLAIR SAW THE NEED, NOT TO DROP 
CONDITIONALITY ON ADDITIONAL LENDING, BUT TO BROADEN THE 
CONTEXT IN WHICH WE LOOKED AT RUSSIA'S PROBLEMS. TALBOTT AND 
SAWERS ALSO REVIEWED NORTHERN IRELAND AND PROGRESS TOWARDS THE 
WASHINGTON NATO SUMMIT. END SUMMARY. 
KOSOVO - THINKING THROUGH NEXT STEPS 
3. +e)- TALBOTT FORESAW THE POSSIBILITY THAT WE WOULD HEAD 
INTO THE NEXT ROUND OF KOSOVO TALKS WITH A CLEAR-CUT YES FROM 
THE KOSOVAR ALBANIANS AND AN EQUALLY CLEAR NO FROM MILOSEVIC. 
WE WOULD NEED TO BE TOUGH-MINDED ABOUT WHAT HAPPENED NEXT. 
NATO COULD NOT AFFORD TO BLINK. WE ACCEPTED THE PARADOX THAT 
THE MORE CREDIBLE OUR THREAT OF FORCE, THE MORE WE DIMINISHED 
OUR NEED TO USE IT. BUT IT WAS IMPORTANT THAT ALLIES FACED UP 
NOW TO OUR COMMITMENTS AND WERE PREPARED TO ACT ON THEM. 
4. 4-G-} SAWERS SAID PRIME MINISTER BLAIR AGREED WITH THE 
PRESIDENT THAT WE MIGHT BE COMPELLED BY MILOSEVIC'S OBSTINACY 
TO USE FORCE. HE AGREED THAT NATO COULD NOT AFFORD TO BLINK. 
BUT IT WAS CRUCIAL THAT WE NOW THINK THROUGH WHAT OUR GOALS 
WOULD BE IN ANY USE OF FORCE. OF COURSE, WE WANTED MILOSEVIC 
TO ACCEPT THE RAMBWILLET AGREEMENT. SOME ANALYSTS BELIEVED 
MILOSEVIC NEEDED A SHOW OF FORCE BEFORE HE COULD CAPITULATE. 
AT THE SAME TIME, A SUSTAINED BOMBING CAMPAIGN COULD LIMIT HIS 
OPTIONS TO BACK DOWN. WE NEEDED TO ARTICULATE A MORE LIMITED 
SECONDARY OBJECTIVE, SUCH AS REDUCING THE ABILILTY OF THE SERB 
FORCES TO REPRESS THE KOSOVARS. SUCH AN OBJECTIVE WOULD ALLOW 
US TO INFLICT DAMAGE ON MILOSEVIC'S SECURITY FORCES BUT END 
AIR STRIKES BEFORE WE LOST PUBLIC SUPPORT. THE BRITISH PUBLIC 
WOULD NOT SUSTAIN A PROLONGED COERCIVE BOMBING CAMPAIGN.

5. 44.;)- REFERRING TO THE OUTCRY IN THE BRITISH PRESS AND PARLIAMENT OVER THE RECENT INTENSIFIED BOMBING IN IRAQ, SAWERS CAUTIONED THAT AN ATTACK NOW ON THE SERBS SIMILARLY RISKED 
EXCITING DOMESTIC AND INTERNATIONAL CRITICS OF "TRIGGER-HAPPY 
ANGLO-SAXONS." TO AVOID FUELING THEIR CHARGES, WE SHOULD 
REASSURE THE PUBLIC IN NATO COUNTRIES THAT WE HAD GONE THE 
EXTRA MILE WITH MILOSEVIC BEFORE ANY STRIKES. WE WOULD HAVE 
TO KEEP IN MIND THE PERIPHERAL EFFECT OF CONCURRENT ACTIONS IN IRAQ ON ALLIED AND PUBLIC OPINION. 
DEALING WITH THE RUSSIANS ON KOSOVO 
6. TALBOTT UNDERSCORED THAT WE ALSO NEEDED TO WORK ON 
THE RUSSIANS TO AVOID A MELTDOWN OF THE NATO-RUSSIA RELATIONSHIP OVER KOSOVO. RUSSIA SAW POSSIBLE NATO AIR STRIKES ON KOSOVO THROUGH THE PRISM OF CHECHNYA. THEY FEARED THE FURTHER BREAK-UP OF YUGOSLAVIA FOR ITS POSSIBLE EFFECTS ON THEIR OWN FEDERATION. WE NEEDED TO CONVINCE THE RUSSIANS THAT KFOR (EVEN A KFOR INSERTED AFTER AIR STRIKES) WAS THE BEST 
HEDGE AGAINST THE BREAK-UP OF YUGOSLAVIA. CONVERSELY, PARALYSIS IN THE CONTACT GROUP AND THE RESUMPTION OF THE VICIOUS CYCLE OF FIGHTING IN KOSOVO INCREASED THE LIKELIHHOD OF A SEPARATE KOSOVO STATE AND OF SPREADING WAR IN THE 
BALKANS. 
7. 4g4.- TALBOTT AND SAWERS AGREED ON THE NECESSITY OF THOROUGHGOING CONSULTATIONS WITH THE RUSSIANS AT ALL LEVELS TO 
TRY TO GET SOME DEGREE OF UNDERSTANDING OF OUR KOSOVO POLICY. 
IT WOULD BE HARDER THAN,OVER IRAQ, BUT THE RUSSIANS UNDERSTOOD THAT THERE WAS MUCH MORE AT STAKE IN THEIR RELATIONS WITH THE 
WEST THAN KOSOVO. THEY COULD PERHAPS BE PERSUADED TO PUT THIS PROBLEM IN THE LARGER CONTEXT. COOK'S TALKS WITH IVANOV AND 
PRIMAKOV HAD GONE RELATIVELY WELL. TALBOTT NOTED PRIMAKOV WOULD BE IN WASHINGTON FOR THE FIRST MEETING OF THE GORE-
PRIMAKOV COMMISSION MARCH 23-24. SAWERS AND TALBOTT THOUGHT PRIMAKOV WOULD WANT TO CONTAIN THE SPILLOVER OF KOSOVO ONTO THESE TALKS, BUT HE HAD TO MANAGE DOMESTIC POLITICAL ANGLES IN MOSCOW. 
AND THE LARGER RUSSIAN CONTEXT 
8. -(,G)- IN HIS MOST RECENT CONVERSATION WITH PRIME MINISTER 
BLAIR, DURING RAMBOUILLET, PRIMAKOV HAD ASKED BLAIR TO ENCOURAGE THE IMF TO ACCEPT WHAT WAS ON OFFER IN THE RUSSIAN 
BUDGET. BLAIR SAW THE NEED FOR SOME CONDITIONALITY, BUT 
BELIEVED THAT THE WEST NEEDED TO LOOK AT RUSSIA'S ECONOMIC DIFFICULTIES AND THE IMF'S ROLE IN A WIDER CONTEXT, SAWERS SAID. THE WEST SHOULD STAY ENGAGED TO TRY TO SUPPORT PRIMAKOV 
AS HE WAS THE LEAST BAD OF THE POSSIBLE OPTIONS FOR SUCCESSION

DIRECTION, EVEN IF THE ROAD WERE SLOW AND BUMPY. TALBOTT 
BELIEVED THIS WAS STILL THE CASE, BUT UNDERSTOOD THAT 
PERCEPTIONS TO THE CONTRARY WERE PART OF THE REALITY WITH 
WHICH WE MUST DEAL. 
PREPARATIONS FOR THE WASHINGTON NATO SUMMIT 
10. -(4) THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REVIEWED BRIEFLY HIS AND 
ASSISTANT SECRETARY GROSSMAN'S MARCH 9-10 TALKS WITH BRITISH 
AND OTHER ALLIED OFFICIALS ON THE OUTSTANDING ISSUES TO BE 
RESOLVED BEFORE THE WASHINGTON NATO SUMMIT. HE AND SAWERS 
CONCURRED THAT THE DEAL WE HAD REACHED AT FOUR ON OPEN DOOR 
LANGUAGE WAS NOT IDEAL BUT WAS ACCEPTABLE, SO LONG AS WHAT WAS 
AGREED HELD, BOTH AT FOUR AND AT NINETEEN. 
11. TALBOTT WORRIED THAT THE MANDATE QUESTION WAS STILL 
SO FAR FROM RESOLUTION. SAWERS RESPONDED THAT WE ALL 
PREFERRED UNSC RESOLUTIONS WHEN WE COULD GET THEM, BUT IT WE 
COULDN'T, WE ACTED ANYWAY. THE FRENCH WERE DIFFICULT IN THE 
THEORY, BUT NOT IN THE PRACTICE, ON THIS ISSUE. 
12. (C) SAWERS HOPED THAT THE USG WOULD SOON BE IN A 
POSITION TO SHARE WITH ALLIES OUR VIEWS ON THE VISION 
STATEMENT, WHICH WOULD NEED A SIGNIFICANT DEGREE OF CO-
OWNERSHIP WITHIN NATO. HE AND HIS DEPUTY PHILIP BARTON ASKED 
WHETHER THE USG ENVISAGED THE VISION STATEMENT, OR SOME OTHER 
DOCUMENT, BEING SIGNED AT THE SUMMIT. 
13. 4-G4- ON ESDI, TALBOTT SAID THE U.S. HAD CONFIDENCE IN THE 
BRITISH AS CO-DRIVERS OF THIS INITIATIVE. DID THE BRITISH 
BELIEVE THAT THEY COULD BRING OTHERS ALONG ON ESDI ELEMENTS, 
AS OUTLINED-IN THE HATFIELD PAPER, BEFORE THE SUMMIT? 
14. 4.G4- SAWERS ACKNOWLEDGED THE FRENCH WERE LESS KEEN TO GET 
LANGUAGE ON BERLIN PLUS INTO THE WASHINGTON SUMMIT. FOR THEM 
THE MANDATE ISSUE WAS MORE IMPORTANT THAN EUROPEAN DEFENSE. 
AND IT WAS NOT SO ESSENTIAL FOR THEM THAT ESDI SHOULD REMAIN 
CLOSELY GEARED TO NATO. THE FRENCH (INCLUDING ERRERA IN THE 
LONDON CLOSE-ALLIES MEETINGS) CONTINUED TO EMPHASIZE A HIGH 
DEGREE OF AUTONOMY FOR EUROPEAN FORCES BECAUSE THEY DIDN'T 
WANT TO BE BOXED INTO A POSITION IN WHICH THE ONLY LOGICAL 
OUTCOME FOR THEM WAS TO REJOIN NATO'S MILITARY STRUCTURES.

NORTHERN IRELAND 
15. +Ei SAWERS EMPHASIZED THAT THE NORTHERN iRELAND ISSUE

"IRISH TIMES" .HAD NOW CRITICIZED THE IRA FOR BEING UNWILLING
