2. SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY. TALBOTT AND PARTY 
MET OCTOBER 22 WITH ZIMBABWE FOREIGN MINISTER 
SHAMUYARIRA AND DEFENSE MINISTER MAHACHI TO 
EXCHANGE VIEWS ON PEACEKEEPING ISSUES. THE 
MINISTERS SAID THE GOZ FULLY SUPPORTS THE OAU'S 
CONFLICT RESOLUTION MECHANISM, AND THE EXPANDED 
ROLE OF SADC. ZIMBABWE WILL HOST AN OCTOBER 25 
SADC MEETING TO DELIVER A STRONG MESSAGE TO THE 
MOZAMBICANS THAT THIS WEEK'S ELECTIONS MUST BE 
FREE AND FAIR, AND THE RESULTS RESPECTED BY THE 
GOVERNMENT AND RENAMO BOTH. MAHACHI PRAISED U.S. 
INTERVENTION IN HAITI AS ENCOURAGING DEMOCRACIES 
TO OPPOSE THOSE, LIKE THE MILITARY IN LESOTHO, WHO 
THREATENED ELECTED GOVERNMENTS. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THE GOZ'S 
PARTICIPATION IN INTERNATIONAL PEACEKEEPING 
OPERATIONS IN SOMALIA, RWANDA, AND ANGOLA. HE 
RECOGNIZED ZIMBABWE'S RESOURCE LIMITATIONS, BUT 
HOPED THE GOZ WOULD CONTINUE TO STEP FORWARD WHEN 
FORCES WERE REQUIRED. SHAMUYARIRA SAID ZIMBABWE 
WOULD PARTICIPATE, FOR EXAMPLE, IN A NEW ANGOLA 
PEACE OPERATION, BUT ONLY UNDER UN AND REGIONAL 
AUSPICES. END SUMMARY. 
3. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S OCTOBER 22 
DISCUSSIONS WITH FOREIGN MINISTER SHAMUYARIRA AND 
DEFENSE MINISTER MAHACHI CENTERED ON HOW BEST TO 
USE PEACEKEEPING MECHANISMS TO PREVENT AND RESOLVE 
CONFLICTS IN THE REGION, AND ELSEWHERE IN AFRICA. 
THE FOREIGN M/NISTER SAID THE GOZ BACKS THE OAU'S 
CONFLICT RESOLUTION MECHANISM, BUT SUPPORT FROM 
THE U.S., BRITAIN, AND OTHER POWERS IS NEEDED TO 
MAKE IT EFFECTIVE. A/S MOOSE NOTED THAT THE U.S. 
HAS ALREADY PROVIDED USD 1.5 MILLION TO THE OAU 
FOR THAT PURPOSE, AND THAT OTHER NATIONS, 
INCLUDING BRITAIN SEEM SUPPORTIVE. HOWEVER, WE 
NEED TO BALANCE WHAT WE PROVIDE WITH WHAT AFRICA 
CAN OFFER. IT IS IMPORTANT THAT AFRICANS ASSUME 
PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY FOR PROTECTING THE PEACE ON 
THE CONTINENT.

ENDANGER THE MOZAMBICAN ELECTION PROCESS. THE 
MINISTERS SAID THE GOZ IS ALSO CONCERNED AND IS 
DETERMINED TO DO EVERYTHING POSSIBLE TO ENSURE 
THAT THE ELECTIONS GO WELL. SHAMUYARIRA EXPLAINED 
THAT SADC HAS ESTABLISHED A NEW BODY TO OPERATE
