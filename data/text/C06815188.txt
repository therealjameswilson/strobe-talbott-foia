1. ~ SUMMARY: OVER DINNER AT THE AMBASSADOR'S
RESIDENCE SEPTEMBER 27, FOREIGN MINISTER GOFF EXPRESSED
SATISFACTION WITH HIS U.S. VISIT, NOTING THAT SEVERAL
INTERLOCUTORS CITED NEW ZEALAND'S EAST TIMOR DEPLOYMENT
IN FAVORABLY COMMENTING ON NZ'S CONTRIBUTION TO REGIONAL
SECURITY. GOFF WAS DOWNBEAT ON INDONESIA, ANTICIPATING
NEW TROUBLE IN REGIONS SUCH AS WEST PAPUA AND RATING THE
WAHID GOVERNMENT'S ABILITY TO COPE WITH ITS PROBLEMS AS
POOR. THE AMBASSADOR TOOK THE OCCASION TO UNDERSCORE
OUR STRONG INTEREST IN, AND THE IMPORTANCE OF, ACHIEVING
REVISION OF THE UN RATES OF ASSESSMENT. END SUMMARY.

2. (U) THE AMBASSADOR HOSTED FOREIGN MINISTER PHIL
GOFF AT THE RESIDENCE SEPTEMBER 27 TO REVIEW HIS RECENT
VISIT TO THE U,S, AND TO DISCUSS REGIONAL ISSUES AND UN
REFORM. GOFF WAS ACCOMPANIED BY HIS SPECIAL ASSISTANT,
DAVID SHEARER. ALSO ATTENDING WERE FORMER SECRETARY
OF'EFENSE
AND MRS. GERALD HENSLEY, DCM AND POL/ECON
COUNSELOR.
U.S. VISIT
3. ~ GOFF WAS VERY UPBEAT ON HIS RECENT VISIT TO THE:
U,S. GOFF CLAIMED THAT HIS INTERLOCUTORS ACROSS THE
BOARD WERE POSITIVE ABOUT NEW ZEALAND'S CONTRIBUTION TO
REGIONAL SECURITY, CITING NZ'S COMMITMENT IN EAST TIMOR.
(NOTE: GOFF DID NOT MENTION, IN THIS CONNECTION, HIS
EXCHANGES WITH DEPSECDEF DE LEON OR DEPUTY CINCPAC.)
GOFF ASSERTED THAT THE KIWI PRESS WENT OVER THE TOP IN
REPORTING HIS OPTIMISTIC COMMENTS REGARDING REMAINING
U.S. RESTRICTIONS STEMMING FROM THE UNFINISHED BUSINESS
OF NEW ZEALAND'S ANTI-NUCLEAR LEGISLATION. HE STATED
THAT QUOTATIONS ATTRIBUTED TO HIM WHICH HAD A POSITIVE
SPIN WERE CORRECT, BUT THAT HEADLINES SUCH AS "GOFF SAYS
REMAINING SANCTIONS TO GO" WERE NOT HIS WORDS, BUT THE
RESULT OF EDITORIAL LICENSE.
INDONESIA
GOFF WAS DECIDEDLY PESSIMISTIC ON INDONESIA.
HE SAW STRONG PRESSURE FOR INDEPENDENCE IN REGIONS SUCH
AS WEST PAPUA, A PROSPECT THAT HE NOTED WAS TOTALLY
UNACCEPTABLE TO JAKARTA. GOFF THOUGHT THE BEST SOLUTION
TO THIS "VERY UNSTABLE AND UNCERTAIN SITUATION" WOULD BE
FOR JAKARTA TO GRANT A DEGREE OF AUTONOMY TO SUCH
REGIONS, BUT SAID IT WAS UNCLEAR WHETHER THAT WOULD
HAPPEN. GOFF SAID PR'ESIDENT WAHID'S HEART WAS IN THE
RIGHT PLACE, BUT QUESTIONED WHETHER HE HAD STRONG ENOUGH
LEADERSHIP AND ENOUGH CONTROL OVER THE TNI TO DEAL WITH
INDONESIA'S PROBLEMS. FM GOFF DISMISSED THE CURRENT
MILITARY COMMANDER AS UNABLE TO CONTROL THE ARMY,
CONTRASTING HIM WITH WIRANTO, WHO "WITH ALL HIS FAULTS"

PACIFIC ISLANDS
5. ~ SPECIAL ASSISTANT SHEARER, WHO RECENTLY PENNED
A PAP R ENTITLED "THE AFRICANIZATION OF THE PACIFIC,"
DID MOST OF THE TALKING ON PACIFIC ISLANDS. THE CONCEPT
OF NATIONHOOD, HE OBSERVED, HAD AN EVEN SHORTER HISTORY
IN THE SOUTH PACIFIC ISLAND STATES THAN IN AFRICA. AND
AS IN AFRICA, MANY PACIFIC ISLANDERS PLACE PRIMARY
IMPORTANCE ON TRIBAL AND CLAN RELATIONS, AND HAVE LITTLE
SENSE OF LOYALTY TO A NATIONAL GOVERNMENT. SHEARER
QUESTIONED WHETHER SMALL ISLAND COUNTRIES IN THE SOUTH
PACIFIC WITH GROWING POPULATIONS CAN BE ECONOMICALLY
VIABLE, SINCE MOST (UNLIKE MANY AFRICAN NATIONS) HAVE
LITTLE OR NO NATURAL RESOURCES.
6. ~ FM GOFF CITED NEW ZEALAND'S EFFORTS TO STEM THE
VIOLENCE IN BOUGANVILLE AND MORE RECENTLY IN THE SOLOMON
ISLANDS, NOTING THAT IN THE LATER CASE "95 PERCENT OF
THE PEOPLE WANT PEACE, BUT THE OTHER FIVE PERCENT HAVE
ALL THE GUNS."
UN RATE REASSESSMENT
7. ~ THE AMBASSADOR USED THE OCCASION TO HIGHLIGHT
OUR INTEREST IN, AND THE IMPORTANCE OF, ACHIEVING
REVISION OF THE UN RATES OF ASSESSMENT THIS YEAR.
DRAWING ON REFTEL POINTS, THE AMBASSADOR EMPHASIZED THAT
WE WANTED TO WORK TOGETHER WITH NEW ZEALAND AND OTHERS
REGIONAL ISSUES, UN REFORM
TO SCLVE THIS ISSUE. SHE NOTED THAT THE U.S. CONTINUED
TO BE ~ BY FAR~ THE LARGEST CONTRI BUTOR TO THE UN IN BOTH
ABSOLUTE AND PER CAPITA TERMS. THE AMBASSADOR
UNDERLINED THE NEED TO CHANGE ASSESSMENT RATES THAT, FOR
HI STC'RICAL AND OTHER REASONS I HAD NEVER BEEN AD JUSTED
FAILURE TO DO SO, THE AMBASSADOR CONCLUDED, WOULD ONLY
PLAY INTO THE HANDS OF THOSE WHO ARE OPPOSED TO THE
UNITED NATIONS. GOFF COUNTERED THAT ASSESSMENT BASED ON
GDP WAS A MORE APPROPRIATE BENCHMARK THAN PER CAPITA.
HE ADDED THAT, WHILE SOME REFORM WAS NEEDED, OUR
STRATEGY "WAS WRONG," WHICH WE INTERPRET AS GOFF'S
OPPOSITION TO NON-PAYMENT OF ARREARS UNTIL REASSESSMENT
IS ACHIEVED. MOSELEY BRAUN
