RELEASE IN FULL 
May 26, 1993 
Dear Les: 
I just wanted to drop a belated note to thank you for 
making Brigadier General John Gordon available to accompany us 
on our recent interagency trip to Russia, Ukraine, Armenia, 
Azerbaijan and Estonia. 
As John has certainly reported to you by now, we had an 
excellent opportunity to see first hand the new political 
dynamic at work in the former Soviet Union and to do some 
useful work on the U.S. security agenda as well. Because John 
was such a key part of our inter'agency teams, I hope that you 
will be able to make him available to travel with us as the OSD 
representative when we visit Kazakhstan, Moldova, Georgia and 
Belarus in early June. 
Thank you so much for your help on this and many other 
matters. 
Sincerely„ 
re,/ 
Strobe Talbott 
The Honorable 
Les Aspin 
Secretary of Defense. 
BC: BG John Gordon
