2. SUMMARY: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S APRIL 12 CALL ON 
TURKISH GENERAL STAFF (TGS) DEPUTY CHIEF, GENERAL AHMET 
COREKCI, FOCUSED ON THE TURKISH CROSS-BORDER OPERATION 
IN NORTHERN IRAQ AND ON RUSSIA/CFE. A TGS BRIEFER GAVE 
UPDATED CASUALTY FIGURES (417 PKK KILLED, 13 CAPTURED, 
40 TURKS DEAD, 136 WOUNDED) AND NOTED CAPTURE OF OVER 
USD 10 MILLION WORTH OF PKK SUPPLIES, WEAPONS AND 
AMMUNITION. COREKCI NOTED THAT THE AREA OF OPERATIONS 
HAD BEEN DIVIDED INTO FOUR SECTORS. ONE SECTOR HAD BEEN 
CLEARED AND THE FORCES WITHDRAWN, WHILE ANOTHER WAS SOON 
TO BE CLEARED, LEAVING THE IMPLICATION THAT MORE FORCES 
COULD DEPART SOON. HE ALSO DECLARED THAT THE 
OPERATION'S DURATION WOULD BE AN UNSPECIFIED MATTER OF 
WEEKS, NOT MONTHS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED WITH 
COREKCI ON THE NEED TO PRESERVE THE TERRITORIAL 
INTEGRITY OF STATES IN lHE REGION AND TO OPPOSE CREATION 
OF AN INDEPENDENT KURDISH STATE. ON CFE, THE TGS 
WELCOMED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S STATEMENT ON THE NEED 
FOR RUSSIAN COMPLIANCE WITH THE TREATY AND H/S 
ASSURANCES THAT CFE TREATY ADJUSTMENTS MUST BE 
ACCEPTABLE TO ALL SIGNATORIES. COREKCI ALSO REITERATED 
LONGTIME TURKISH CONCERNS REGARDING RUSSIAN HEGEMONY 
WHILE THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED THE NEED FOR 
U.S./TURKISH CONSULTATION AND COOPERATION. END SUMMARY. 
OPERATION STEEL 
3. MGEN ORHAN YONEY (J-3) BRIEFED ON TURKEY'S 
CROSS-BORDER OPERATION INTO NORTHERN IRAQ - "OPERATION 
STEEL." AS BACKGROUND, HE CLAIMED THAT GOT SECURITY 
FORCES INCREASED THE PACE OF OPERATIONS IN THE SUMMER OF 
1994 AND OBTAINED "FULL CONTROL" OF SOUTHEASTERN TURKEY 
FROM THE PKK. THE GOT KEPT UP THE PACE IN THE WINTER, 
FORCING MANY PKK INTO NORTHERN IRAQ. THERE, SOME 
2400-2800 PKK DEPLOYED AT EIGHT MAJOR CAMPS, THUS 
CREATING THE CIRCUMSTANCES FOR THE GOT'S PREEMPTIVE 
STRIKE. YONEY STRESSED THAT OPERATION STEEL WAS "NOT A 
MANHUNT" AND ITS SUCCESS SHOULD NOT BE MEASURED BY THE 
NUMBER OF PKK CAPTURED OR KILLED. THE 35,000 TURKISH 
TROOPS DEPLOYED IN NORTHERN IRAQ HAD BEEN ORDERED TO TRY 
TO AVOID CIVILIAN CASUALTIES OR INTERFERING WITH NGOS'S 
AND RELIEF ACTIVITIES. YONEY DECLARED THAT THE PKK HAD 
SUFFERED 417 KILLED AND 13 CAPTURED WHILE THE GOT HAD 
TAKEN CASUALTIES OF 40 DEAD AND 136 WOUNDED. ALSO, USD 
10 MILLION WORTH OF PKK EQUIPMENT HAD BEEN CAPTURED. 
THERE HAD BEEN ONLY ONE CIVILIAN CAS(JALTY REPORTED SO 
FAR AND MINIMAL PROPERTY DAMAGE.
