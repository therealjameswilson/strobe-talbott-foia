RELEASE IN FULL 
I am glad I caught up with you in the Moscow 
Radisson breakfast line two weeks ago. Thanks for 
writing out the 80th birthday greeting to Tom Watson. It' 
will mean a lot to him. 
On the other hand, your visit to Moscow with Vice 
President Gore preempted the meeting Mark Garrison and I 
had set with Vice Minister Georgy Mamedov. We were to 
discuss all aspects of the Foreign Ministry's (and 
Mamedov's personal) participation in the Carter-Brezhnev 
project. (My former colleague Jan Kalicki, who is now 
counselor for Secretary of Commerce Brown, did bring up 
the project with Mamedov, during the same Moscow visit.) 
Mark and I,ran into Amb. Pickering and Acting DCM Louis 
Sell in the Foreign Ministry after meeting with Mamedov's 
deputy for Archival Administration, Igor Lebedev, and 
they confirmed it: you guys did steal Mamedov from the 
historical discussion we were to have with him. (I 
enclose the letter I sent

Here is what we need from Mamedov: (1) a prod for 
Yeltsin to answer President Carter's letter, requesting 
that Yeltsin open up Russian archives for the Carter-
Brezhnev project; (2) Mamedov's authorization to Lebedev

hosted.by the Foreign Ministry. (Only with authorization

conference. Otherwise, we will look elsewhere, even 
though holding it in Moscow makes substantive sense.)
