AND PRESIDENT CLINTON TO DISCUSS THE POSITION OF UNSYG. 
THE ISSUE IS ONE OF SOME URGENCY BECAUSE IT WILL BE 
RAISED AT THE OAU SUMMIT IN YAOUNDE. WHEN BOUTROS-GHALI 
ACCEPTED THE POSITION, HE SAID HE WOULD SERVE ONLY ONE 
TERM, BUT NOW HE HAS DECIDED HE WANTS A SECOND TERM. 
FOLLOWING MUCH REFLECTION, AND WITHOUT REFLECTING ILL ON 
BOUTROS-GHALI, PRESIDENT CLINTON OFFERED BOUTROS GHALI 
AN EXTENSION THROUGH HIS 75TH BIRTHDAY, BUT HE 
DECLINED. AS THE U.S. LOOKS AHEAD AT THE ISSUES TO BE 
FACED BY THE UN IN THE NEXT CENTURY, IT WAS DECIDED 
SOMEONE WITH MORE ENERGY WAS NEEDED. 
5. 4Z.).--THE ACTING SECRETARY ASSURED MUGABE THE U.S. HAS 
NO SPECIFIC CANDIDATE IN MIND. A PROFILE OF THE NEW 
UNSYG WOULD BE A RESPECTED PERSON WITH INTERNATIONAL 
STATURE AND EXTENSIVE DIPLOMATIC AND ADMINISTRATIVE 
EXPERIENCE. THE ACTING SECRETARY EMPHASIZED THE U.S. 
UNDERSTANDS THAT TRADITION AND HISTORY SUGGEST SPECIAL 
CONSIDERATIONS FOR QUALIFIED AFRICAN CANDIDATES. 
6.-44_ MUGABE SAID HE READ THE STATEMENT EXPRESSING THE 
.VIEWS OF PRESIDENT CLINTON CONCERNING BOUTROS-GHALI'S 
SECOND TERM. THIS WAS THE FIRST INDICATION THAT THE 
U.S. HELD A DIFFERENT VIEW THAN THAT HELD BY 
BOUTROS-GHALI'S SUPPORTERS. MUGABE AFFIRMED HE HAD 
TAKEN FOR GRANTED THAT BOUTROS-GHALI WOULD SERVE A 
SECOND TERM. HIS GOVERNMENT HAD NOT EVEN CONSIDERED THE 
MATTER UNTIL THE U.S. AND MALAYSIA EXPRESSED THEIR 
CONCERNS. WHILE MEETING LAST WEEK IN GABORONE, THE SADC 
HEADS OF GOVERNMENT DECIDED IT WAS BETTER TO DISCUSS THE 
MATTER AT THE OAU SUMMIT. ZIMBABWE WILL GO ALONG WITH 
THE AFRICAN POSITION. MUGABE SAID HE HAD NO PROBLEMS 
WITH BOUTROS-GHALI, BUT IF VALID REASONS WERE PRESENTED 
FOR CONSIDERING OTHER CANDIDATES, HE WOULD LISTEN TO 
THEM AND TRY TO BE OBJECTIVE. 
7. NL THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID MUGABE'S VIEWPOINT WAS 
LUCID AND CONSISTENT WITH SEVERAL OAU LEADERS. THE U.S. 
HOPES THE OAU WILL REFRAIN FROM ENDORSING BOUTROS-GHALI 
OR ANY OTHER CANDIDATE AT THIS TIME. SUCH AN 
ENDORSEMENT COULD MAKE IT DIFFICULT TO IDENTIFY A 
MUTUALLY ACCEPTABLE AFRICAN CANDIDATE FOR UNSYG. A 
CLEAN SLATE IS THE BEST WAY TO PROCEED AND UNNECESSARY 
CONFLICT AMONG UN MEMBERS MUST BE AVOIDED. THE U.S. IS 
OPEN-MINDED CONCERNING THE UNSYG REPLACEMENT, BUT 
PRESIDENT CLINTON HAS CONCLUDED FIRMLY THAT 
BOUTROS-GHALI SHOULD NOT SERVE A SECOND TERM. FRESH 
LEADERSHIP IS NEEDED IN THE UN. IT WOULD NOT BE HELPFUL 
FOR THE OAU TO ENDORSE A CANDIDATE WHOM THE U.S., AS A 
MEMBER OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL, ALREADY DECIDED TO 
OPPOSE. 
8. PRESIDENT MUGABE SAID THE OAU WOULD ONLY BE 
EXPRE SING ITS VIEWS, MUCH LIKE THE U.S. ALREADY HAS 
DONE. HE QUESTIONED WHETHER OR NOT AN EXPRESSION OF 
SUPPORT BY THE OAU FOR BOUTROS-GHALI OR SOMEONE ELSE 
WOULD CONSTITUTE AN ENDORSEMENT. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
REPLIED HE BELIEVED SUCH SUPPORT WOULD BE AN 
ENDORSEMENT. PRESIDENT MUGABE REPLIED THAT INDIVIDUAL 
STATES AT THE TIME OF A UNSYG VOTE WOULD BE FREE TO PART 
COMPANY FROM THE GROUP'S DECISION. BASED UPON 
DISCUSSIONS WITH SEVERAL OAU MEMBERS, MUGABE BELIEVES 
THE MAJORITY WILL SUPPORT BOUTROS GHALI FOR A SECOND 
TERM. 
9. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1459 EST.
