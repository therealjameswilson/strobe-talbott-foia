RELEASE IN FULL 
1. SUMMARY. DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE TALBOTT ADDRESSED A 
BREAKFAST MEETING OF THE CORPORATE COUNCIL ON AFRICA 
OCTOBER 1. HE DISCUSSED PLANS FOR SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER'S 
OCTOBER 7-15 TRIP TO AFRICA AND FIELDED SEVERAL QUESTIONS 
FROM CORPORATE COUNCIL MEMBERS. END SUMMARY. 
2. THE CORPORATE COUNCIL ON AFRICA HOSTED A BREAKFAST 
MEETING FOR DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT ON 
TUESDAY, OCTOBER 1, 1996 AT THE CLUB AT FRANKLIN SQUARE, 
1300 EYE STREET, N.W., WASHINGTON, D.C. OTHER SENIOR STATE 
DEPARTMENT OFFICIALS WHO ALSO PARTICIPATED WERE ASSISTANT 
SECRETARY FOR ECONOMIC AND BUSINESS AFFAIRS ALAN LARSON, 
DEPUTY ASSISTANT SECRETARY FOR AFRICA AFFAIRS REGINA BROWN, 
DEPUTY ASSISTANT SECRETARY/SPEECHWRITER BENNETT FREEMAN, 
DIRECTOR OF POLICY AND PLANNING, JIM STEINBERG, AND 
AMBASSADOR JOSEPH SALOOM, DIRECTOR OF THE ECONOMIC POLICY 
STAFF OFFICE IN THE AFRICA BUREAU. 
3. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT OPENED THE MEETING WITH AN 
OVERVIEW OF SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER'S PLANS FOR THE OCTOBER 
7-15 TRIP TO SUB-SAHARAN AFRICA AND WENT ON TO DISCUSS THE 
DEPARTMENT'S EFFORTS TO ENCOURAGE ECONOMIC REFORMS IN 
AFRICA AND TO HELP CREATE AN ENABLING ENVIRONMENT WHICH 
WILL ATTRACT U.S. TRADE AND INVESTMENT AND LESSEN THE NEED 
FOR U.S. ASSISTANCE. HE FIELDED SEVERAL QUESTIONS ON 
BUSINESS ISSUES IMPORTANT TO THE CORPORATE COUNCIL ON 
AFRICA FOLLOWING HIS REMARKS (SEE PARA 11 FOR THE TEXT OF 
HIS REMARKS AS PREPARED FOR DELIVERY). 
4. JAMES ANDREWS OF M.W. KELLOGG OPENED THE DISCUSSION 
WITH A COMMENT THAT IT WAS TIME TO LOOK AT AFRICA IN THE 
SAME WAY WE LOOKED AT RUSSIA AND THE NEW INDEPENDENT 
STATES, THAT IS WITH A FOCUSED PROGRAM FOR GOOD PERFORMERS. 
HE NOTED THAT THIS IDEA HAS STRONG CONGRESSIONAL AND 
CORPORATE SUPPORT. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT RESPONDED THAT 
THE CORPORATE COUNCIL HAD DONE SOME EXCELLENT WORK IN 
FORMULATING NEW APPROACHES TO AFRICA AND THEY COULD EXPECT 
US TO PLAGIARIZE MANY OF THEIR IDEAS. HE SAID AFRICA PAID 
A DOUBLE PRICE FOR THE END OF THE COLD WAR. NOT ONLY DID 
SOVIET ASSISTANCE DISAPPEAR, BUT MANY U.S. AID PROGRAMS 
WHICH WERE STARTED WITH AN EYE TO THE GEOPOLITICAL 
SITUATION BECAME HARDER TO JUSTIFY AND HAVE BEEN CUT. EB 
ASSISTANT SECRETARY LARSON ADDED THAT THE WORLD BANK NEEDED 
TO BE MORE SELECTIVE IN THE PROGRAMS AND PROJECTS IT 
SUPPORTS. MR. ANDREWS RESPONDED THAT MUCH OF THE DEBT

5. CATERPILLAR REPRESENTATIVE JERRY SCHMEIL COMMENTED THAT 
FINANCING DRIVES BUSINESS. AMERICAN FIRMS HAVE ENORMOUS 
DIFFICULTIES OPERATING IN AFRICA WITHOUT SOME SORT OF - 
FINANCIAL SECURITY BLANKET. IF THE SECRETARY WANTS TO 
BUILD CONFIDENCE AND MAKE A DIFFERENCE IN AFRICA, HE SHOULD 
FIND A WAY TO MAKE MORE FUNDING AVAILABLE. DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT RESPONDED THAT AFRICA HAS AN IMAGE 
PROBLEM WHICH MUST BE ADDRESSED TO FACILITATE INTERNATIONAL 
INVESTMENT AND FINANCING. 
6. J.P. MORGAN REPRESENTATIVE LORETTA BRUNO SAID AFRICA 
OFFERED WORLD CLASS PROJECTS, PARTICULARLY IN NATURAL , 
RESOURCES. SHE SAID J.P. MORGAN HAD BID ON AND LOST AN 
OPPORTUNITY TO PARTICIPATE IN A GOLD MINING PROJECT IN 
MALI. SHE ENCOURAGED US TO FOCUS OUR EFFORTS AND RESOURCES 
ON THOSE SECTORS IN WHICH AFRICAN COUNTRIES COULD DEVELOP 
WORLD CLASS PROJECTS. 
7. DR. MIMA NEDELCOVYCH OF F.C. SCHAFFER ARGUED THAT THE 
U.S. GOVERNMENT MUST BECOME SELECTIVE IN ITS SUPPORT FOR 
U.S. FIRMS. PART OF THE PROBLEM HE SAID WAS THAT U.S. 
FIRMS COMPETE AGAINST NATIONAL FIRMS FROM BRITAIN, FRANCE, 
GERMANY, SWEDEN, NORWAY, AND JAPAN THAT HAVE THE FULL AND 
EXCLUSIVE SUPPORT OF THEIR GOVERNMENTS WHILE THE USG 
PROVIDED GENERIC ADVOCACY FOR MULTIPLE U.S. BIDDERS. 
8. AMBASSADOR RUSH TAYLOR SAID EQUATOR BANK WAS OPTIMISTIC 
ABOUT PROJECTS IN AFRICA. THEIR BANK WAS OPEN IN SEVERAL 
COUNTRIES. HIS CONCERN WAS THAT WE NEEDED--MORE PEOPLE.ON 
THE GROUND TO IDENTIFY OPPORTUNITIES FOR AMERICAN BUSINESS. 
9. DAVID MILLER, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF THE CORPORATE 
COUNCIL ON AFRICA, ARGUED THAT THE U.S. NEEDS TO WORK ON 
ITS OWN IMAGE IN AFRICA. WE NEED TO DO MORE TO MEET OUR 
IDA AND AFDB OBLIGATIONS. A/S LARSON ACKNOWLEDGED THE 
CRISIS IN FUNDING FOR THE MDB'S IN GENERAL AND SAID THAT 
WHILE WE ARE TRYING TO RESOLVE OUR ARREARS PROBLEM WE ARE 
STILL WAY BEHIND. MILLER WENT ON TO SAY THERE IS A 
PERCEPTION WE DON'T TREAT AFRICA LIKE A PREMIER CONTINENT. 
WE NEED TO BE MORE SOPHISTICATED IN OUR ASSISTANCE 
PROGRAMS. AFRICANS WANT TELECOMMUNICATIONS AND INFORMATION 
TECHNOLOGY AND WE OFFER THEM FOOD PROGRAMS AND CONDOM 
DISTRIBUTION. MILLER ALSO CITED LIBERIA AS ANHAREA OF 
SPECIAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE UNITED STATES AND SAID WE 
NEEDED TO DO MORE TO ADDRESS PROBLEMS IN THAT COUNTRY.

DIPLOMACY TOWARD AFRICA DURING MY TIME AS DEPUTY SECRETARY. 
IN NOVEMBER, 1994, I TRAVELED TO BURUNDI, ZAIRE, MALAWI, 
ZIMBABWE, GHANA AND THE IVORY COAST. 
I'VE ALSO WORKED ON THE SECRETARY'S BEHALF ON ISSUES 
SUCH AS HELPING BRING DEMOCRACY,TO MOZAMBIQUE, TRYING 
AGAINST THE ODDS TO RESOLVE THE CONFLICT IN LIBERIA, AND 
ADDRESSING THE TRAGIC PROBLEMS IN RWANDA AND BURUNDI. 
BUT BACK TO SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER'S TRIP: LET ME 
SKETCH THE ITINERARY. HE WILL BE THE FIRST SECRETARY OF 
STATE EVER TO VISIT MALI, A COUNTRY WHERE DEMOCRATIC 
INSTITUTIONS AND ECONOMIC REFORMS ARE TAKING ROOT. MALI 
DESERVES OUR ENCOURAGEMENT. IT OFFERS A POSITIVE MODEL TO 
NEIGHBORING STATES IN WEST AFRICA. 
THEN, IN ADDIS ABABA, THE SECRETARY WILL MEET WITH 
ETHIOPIA'S LEADERS TO EMPHASIZE THE IMPORTANCE OF THE 
GREATER HORN OF AFRICA INITIATIVE. HE WILL ALSO MEET WITH 
THE LEADERSHIP OF THE OAU. 
IN ARUSHA, SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER WILL MEET WITH THE 
PRESIDENTS OF TANZANIA, KENYA, AND UGANDA TO FOCUS ON 
EFFORTS TO LESSEN TENSIONS IN BURUNDI AND RWANDA -- AND TO 
SUPPORT REGIONAL ECONOMIC INTEGRATION IN EAST AFR/CA. 
IN PRETORIA AND JOHANNESBURG, THE SECRETARY WILL GET A 
GLIMPSE OF THE REMARKABLE PROGRESS OF THE PEOPLE OF SOUTH 
AFRICA. HE WILL MEET WITH THEIR LEADERS TO DISCUSS OUR 
GROWING REGIONAL AND GLOBAL COOPERATION. 
AND IN A BRIEF STOP IN LUANDA, HE WILL REINFORCE THE 
COMMITMENT OF THE UNITED STATES TO SUPPORT THE PROCESS OF 
NATIONAL RECONCILIATION. HE WILL ALSO ASSESS THE LOCAL 
IMPACT OF OUR INTERNATIONAL EFFORT TO END THE SCOURGE OF 
ANTI-PERSONNEL LAND MINES. 
IN EVERY COUNTRY HE VISITS, SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER WILL 
REAFFIRM OUR COMMITMENT TO SUPPORT AFRICAN SOLUTIONS TO 
MEET AFRICAN CHALLENGES - FROM THE BUILDING OF DEMOCRACY, 
TO STEMMING ETHNIC CONFLICTS, TO ACHIEVING SUSTAINABLE 
DEVELOPMENT. 
AND HE WILL EMPHASIZE THE NEED TO ACCELERATE THE PACE 
OF ECONOMIC REFORM IN ORDER TO DEVELOP THE PRIVATE SECTOR 
AND TO ATTRACT FOREIGN INVESTMENT. 
WE BELIEVE THAT THIS IS THE ONLY PATH THAT WILL 
ULTIMATELY LEAD AFRICA TO HIGHER LIVING STANDARDS AND TO 
INTEGRATION INTO THE GLOBAL ECONOMY.

THE SECRETARY WILL ALSO, OF COURSE, MAKE THE CASE 
EVERYWHERE HE GOES THAT AFRICA MATTERS TO THE UNITED 
STATES. 
IT IS A PREMISE OF OUR POLICY THAT AS DEMOCRATIC AND 
ECONOMIC REFORM GAIN STRENGTH, WE HAVE AN OPPORTUNITY TO 
EXPAND AMERICAN TRADE AND INVESTMENT, AND THAT EXPANDING 
THOSE TIES IS PROFOUNDLY IN AMERICA'S INTEREST AS WELL AS 
IN AFRICA'S. 
WE ALSO BELIEVE THAT OUR ENGAGEMENT IN AFRICA WILL HELP 
UNDERGIRD PEACE AND STABILITY IN A VAST NEIGHBORHOOD OF THE 
GLOBAL COMMUNITY OF' WHICH WE ARE A PART - INDEED, OF WHICH 
WE ARE THE LEADER. 
BUT TO DO ALL THIS, WE MUST BUILD MORE OF A 
CONSTITUENCY FOR AMERICAN ENGAGEMENT IN AFRICA HERE AT 
HOME. THAT ISN'T EASY, ESPECIALLY THESE DAYS. 
TOO MANY MEMBERS OF THE OUTGOING CONGRESS FAVOR TURNING 
INWARD, TURNING AMERICA'S BACK ON THE WORLD. OVER THE LAST 
SEVERAL YEARS, THE CONGRESS HAS TAKEN A MEAT AX TO OUR 
INTERNATIONAL AFFAIRS BUDGET, SLASHING THE ABILITY OF THIS 
DEPARTMENT AND OUR MISSIONS AND PROGRAMS OVERSEAS TO 
PROTECT AND ADVANCE AMERICAN INTERESTS. AFRICAN POLICY AND 
PROGRAMS HAVE BEEN PARTICULARLY HARD HIT. 
THAT'S A FACT OF LIFE, BUT IT'S NOT ONE THAT WE'RE 
GOING TO ACCEPT LYING DOWN; WE'RE GOING TO KEEP DOING 
BATTLE AGAINST THE PENNY-WISE, POUND-FOOLISH BUDGET CUTTERS 
AND THE NEO1SOLATIONISTS WHO WOULD PUT AMERICAN LEADERSHIP 
ON A STARVATION DIET. 
OUR UPHILL STRUGGLE FOR RESOURCES MAKES IT ALL THE MORE 
IMPORTANT THAT WE, IN THE PUBLIC SECTOR, FORM ALLIANCES AND 
PARTNERSHIPS WITH ENLIGHTENED INTERNATIONALISTS IN THE 
PRIVATE SECTOR. . 
WHICH BRINGS ME TO THE CORPORATE COUNCIL ON AFRICA. 
YOU'VE ALREADY DONE MUCH TO ADVANCE EXACTLY THE GOALS THAT 
ARE UPPERMOST IN•THE SECRETARY'S MIND AS HE LOOKS AHEAD TO 
HIS TRIP NEXT WEEK. THE SECRETARY ASKED ME TO TELL YOU HOW 
MUCH HE PERSONALLY VALUES YOUR EFFORTS. 
AS MOST OF YOU REMEMBER, I'M SURE, OUR LATE FRIEND AND 
COLLEAGUE RON BROWN WAS PRESENT AT THE OFFICIAL LAUNCH OF 
THE COUNCIL AT THE STATE DEPARTMENT THREE YEARS. AGO. RON'S 
COMMITMENT TO YOUR ENTERPRISE IS PART OF HIS HUGE LEGACY, 
AND, IT IS AN INSPIRATION OF ALL OF US. 
WE COMMEND YOU FOR YOUR SUCCESSES TO DATE IN EXPANDING 
U.S. TRADE AND INVESTMENT TIES WITH THE SUB-SAHARAN REGION.
