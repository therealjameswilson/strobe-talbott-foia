RELEASE IN FULL 
I am writing you regarding a proposal by Science Applications 
International Corporation (SAIC) which I learned about during a Senate 
Armed Services Committee on May 7. The subject of the hearing was a 
mechanism established in previous law to promote a two-way exchange on 
the subject of environmental technologies between the Departments of 
Defense and Energy on the one hand and the scientific and commercial 
worlds on the other. SAIC has been intensely interested in the 
opportunitiee created by this legislation and appeared in order to 
testify on this matter. 
In the'course of their testimony, they presented an idea of 
their own called the °Commonwealth Environmental Research and 
Development Program" (=ADP). The Central notion in CERDP is that_it 
would attempt to stimulate the conversion of defense technology of 
former Soviet Union through the use of private US intermediaries.-
Specifically, private industry under U8 government contract would hire ---
former Soviet defense scientists and purchase former Soviet defense 
technology and data to apply that knowledge to environmental research - 
and develOpment. 
SA1C envisages itself in the role of the private US enterprise 
which would be the driver in CERDP. Since SAIC is a very successful-= 
employee-owned company, its management believee that they will have  
much to offer for groups of former soviet scientists who may be • 
attempting to create analogous types of employee.owned enterprises. 
SAIC thinks that CERDP could play a role in the context of the  
International Science and Technology Center (ISTC), with which you are-= - 
deeply involved. I azt sufficiently interested in their proposal to 
Isk you whether a concept such as CERDP might serve as the model, if 
not the actual instrument, for the ISTC's privatization outreach 
initiative.
