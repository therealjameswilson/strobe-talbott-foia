SUBJECT: DEPSEC TALBOTT/DEPUTY UNDER SECRETARY OF 
DEFENSE LODAL MEET WITH TURKISH GENERAL STAFF 
SUMMARY: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S CALL ON TURKISH GENERAL 
STAFF (TGS) DEPUTY CHIEF, GENERAL AHMET COREKCI, FOCUSED 
ON TURKEY' CROSS-BORDER OPERQ IRAQ AND 
ON RUSSIA/CFE. A TGS BRIEFER GAVE UPDATED CASUALTY 
FIGURES (417 PKK KILLED, 13 CAPTURED, 40 TURKS DEAD, 136 
WOUNDED) AND NOTED CAPTURE OF OVER USD 10 MILLION WORTH 
OF PKK SUPPLIES, WEAPONS AND AMMUNITION. COREKCI NOTED 
THAT THE AREA OF OPERATIONS HAD BEEN DIVIDED INTO FOUR 
SECTORS. ONE SECTOR HAD BEEN CLEARED AND THE FORCES 
WITHDRAWN, WHILE ANOTHER WAS SOON TO BE CLEARED, LEAVING 
THE IMPLICATION THAT MORE FORCES COULD DEPART SOON. HE 
ALSO DECLARED THAT THE OPERATION'S DURATION WOULD BE AN 
UNSPECIFIED MATTER OF WEEKS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
AGREED WITH COREKCI ON THE NEED TO PRESERVE THE 
TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY OF STATES IN THE REGION AND OPPOSE 
CREATION OF AN INDEPENDENT KURDISH STATE. ON CFE, THE 
TGS WELCOMED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S STATEMENT ON THE 
NEED FOR RUSSIAN COMPLIANCE WITH THE TREATY AND HIS 
ASSURANCES THAT CFE TREATY ADJUSTMENTS MUST BE 
CCEPTABLE TO ALL SIGNATORIES. COREKCI ALSO REITERATED 
LONGTIME TURKISH CONCERNS REGARDING RUSSIAN HEGEMONISM 
WHILE THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED THE NEED FOR 
U.S./TURKISH CONSULTATION AND COOPERATION. END SUMMARY. 
OPERATION STEEL 
MGEN ORHAN YONEY (J-3) BRIEFED ON TURKEY'S 
CROSS-BORDER OPERATION INTO NORTHERN IRAQ - "OPERATION 
STEEL." AS BACKGROUND, HE CLAIMED THAT GOT SECURITY 
FORCES INCREASED THE PACE OF OPERATIONS IN THE SUMMER OF 
1994 AND OBTAINED "FULL CONTROL" OF THE SOUTHEASTERN 
TURKEY FROM THE PKK. THE GOT KEPT UP THE PACE IN THE 
WINTER, FORCING MANY PKK INTO NORTHERN IRAQ. THERE, 
SOME 2400-2800 PKK DEPLOYED AT EIGHT MAJOR CAMPS, THUS 
CREATING THE CIRCUMSTANCES FOR THE GOT'S PREEMPTIVE 
STRIKE. YONEY STRESSED THAT OPERATION STEEL WAS "NOT A 
MANHUNT" AND ITS SUCCESS SHOULD NOT BE MEASURED BY THE 
NUMBER OF PKK CAPTURED OR KILLED. THE 35,000 TURKISH 
TROOPS DEPLOYED IN NORTHERN IRAQ HAD BEEN ORDERED TO TRY 
TO AVOID CIVILIAN CASUALTIES OR INTERFERING WITH NGOS'S
