4. SUMMARY: CHINESE AMBASSADOR LI ZHAOXING STRESSED THAT 
DESPITE MEDIA REPORTS THAT HAD CHARACTERIZED CHINA'S RECENT 
"WHITE PAPER" ON TAIWAN AS A DRAMATIC CHANGE IN POLICY, 
THERE WAS NO CHANGE TO THE PRC'S POLICY OF PEACEFUL 
REUNIFICATION OF CHINA. ASKED ABOUT A RUMOR THAT THE PRC 
WOULD SOON SET A DEADLINE FOR CHINESE REUNIFICATION, LI 
EXPRESSED SURPRISE AND SAID HE HAD HEARD NO SUCH RUMOR. IN 
A BRIEF ONE-ON-ONE, LI TOLD THE ACTING SECRETARY THAT HE 
WAS STILL UNHAPPY ABOUT NOT KNOWING ABOUT THE RELEASE OF 
THE WHITE PAPER PRIOR TO ITS RELEASE AND WAS ATTEMPTING TO 
ASCERTAIN WHICH OF THE OFFICIALS WITH WHOM THE ACTING 
SECRETARY HAD MET IN BEIJING HAD KNOWN ABOUT THE WHITE 
PAPER IN ADVANCE. END SUMMARY.

HIS MEETING THE PRESIDENT. OTHERWISE, VPM LI WOULD VISIT

MEETINGS WITH SENIOR CHINESE LEADERS.
