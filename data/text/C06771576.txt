SUMMARY 
2. -29- DURING HIS MARCH 8 VISIT TO ROME, 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH MFA 
SECRETARY GENERAL UMBERTO VATTANI. 
(DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MEETINGS WITH FOREIGN 
MINISTER DINI AND MOD SCOGNAMIGLIO ARE 
REPORTED SEPTELS.) THE DISCUSSION FOCUSED 
ON NATO SUMMIT ISSUES AND KOSOVO. THE 
ITALIANS PRESSED FOR A CREDIBLE OPEN DOOR 
POLICY THAT WOULD NOT DISCOURAGE POTENTIAL 
ASPIRANTS, A MORE ROBUST AND COHERENT NATO 
MEDITERRANEAN STRATEGY, AND GREATER 
EMPHASIS ON MULTINATIONAL SPECIALIZED 
UNITS (MSU) IN PLANNING FOR NATO MISSIONS. 
THE GOI STRONGLY SUPPORTS THE NATO SUMMIT 
WMD INITIATIVE, AND WANTS'TO USE THE 
INITIATIVE TO HIGHLIGHT ITALY'S CONCERNS 
ABOUT DELIVERY SYSTEMS. IN THAT REGARD, 
THE ITALIANS REAFFIRMED THE IMPORTANCE OF 
THE MEADS THEATER MISSILE DEFENSE PROGRAM 
TO ITALY. THE DISCUSSION CONFIRMED THAT 
U.S. AND ITALIAN VIEWS ON THE MANDATE OR 
"LEGITIMACY OF ACTION" ISSUE, ON THE 
IMPORTANCE OF DEVELOPING ESDI WITHIN THE 
ALLIANCE, AND ON NATO NUCLEAR POLICY ARE 
IN SYNCH. ON KOSOVO, THE ITALIANS 
STRESSED THAT GOI VIEWS ON KOSOVO COINCIDE 
PERFECTLY WITH THOSE OF NATO, I.E., THAT 
ITALY IS NOT "PRE-SERB," AND CALLED FOR 
MORE U.S.-EUROPEAN CONSULTATION ON KOSOVO. 
END SUMMARY.
