IRAQ PUBLIC DIPLOMACY , 
5. BARELY IN HIS SEAT AT THE START OF THE MEETING, 
FOREIGN MINISTER BIN ALAWI LAUNCHED INTO A LENGTHY 
CRITIQUE OF THE PUBLIC DIPLOMACY FAILURES OF THE 
COALITION DURING THE MOST RECENT IRAQ CRISIS. 
CONFIRMING THAT OMAN SHARES THE U.S. VIEW THAT THE 
CREDIBLE THREAT OF FORCE WAS A PREREQUISITE FOR A 
SUCCESSFUL DIPLOMATIC SOLUTION TO THE CONFRONTATION, 
ALAWI AGREED WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THAT THE CRISIS 
IS STILL NOT BEHIND US, BUT IN FRONT OF US. WHILE THE 
EXPANSION OF THE OIL-FOR-FOOD PROGRAM WILL HELP 
ALLEVIATE SUFFERING IN IRAQ, IT WON'T ERADICATE. IT, AND 
THAT SUFFERING IS AFFECTING POPULAR ATTITUDES SHAPED BY 
A MEDIA THAT IS INTENT ON "AMERICA BASHING." WE NEED TO 
BE BETTER PREPARED, ALAWI ARGUED, TO CONFRONT THE 
PROBLEM THE NEXT TIME A CRISIS ERUPTS WITH IRAQ. WE 
NEED JOINT EFFORTS TO IDENTIFY THE STRENGTHS AND 
WEAKNESSES OF OUR POSITION AND WE NEED TO DEVELOP NEW 
IDEAS FOR IMPROVING IT. 
6. 44P IN HIS REPLY, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT 
HIS VISIT TO OMAN COMES AGAINST THE BACKDROP OF THE 
OVERALL U.S.-OMANI RELATIONSHIP, WHICH IS CHARACTERIZED 
BY CLOSE CONSULTATION, AS WITNESSED RECENTLY IN THE 
PRESIDENT'S CALL TO THE SULTAN, IN THE SECRETARY'S CALL 
TO BIN ALAWI, AND IN SECRETARY COHEN'S VISIT. WE 
WELCOME THE .OPPORTUNITY TO SHARE OUR THINKING ON THE 
ISSUES OUTLINED BY THE MINISTER, BUT ALSO WANT TO HEAR 
HOW THE OMANIS WOULD RESPOND TO THESE QUESTIONS 
THEMSELVES. SECRETARY TALBOTT NOTED THAT THERE HAS BEEN 
A TENDENCY TO PORTRAY THE U.S. AS A "SCHOOLYARD BULLY" 
LOOKING FOR A FIGHT WITH THE OTHER BULLY, BUT THIS IS 
NOT THE CASE -- THE U.S. DOES NOT UNDERESTIMATE THE 
COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH A MILITARY CONFRONTATION WITH 
IRAQ. WE WANTED A PEACEFUL SETTLEMENT, AND WE SUPPORTED 
KOFI ANNAN'S EFFORTS TO ACHIEVE A PEACEFUL RESOLUTION, 
ALLEGATIONS OF TENSION BETWEEN THE U.S. AND THE UNSYG 
NOTWITHSTANDING. WE UNDERSTAND THAT THERE IS A DILEMMA 
IN CHOOSING BETWEEN SHORT AND LONG-TERM DANGERS. BUT WE 
HAVE TO WEIGH THE SHORT-TERM COSTS AGAINST THE LONG-TERM 
DANGER THAT SADDAM POSSESSES A WMD CAPABILITY, AND HAS 
DEMONSTRATED BOTH THE CAPABILITY AND WILLINGNESS TO USE 
IT. THIS IS A VERY BIG PROBLEM, AND WE BELIEVE THAT 
WE'VE BEEN HARD-HEADED IN ANALYZING THE SITUATION AND 
COMMITTING ALL OF OUR INTELLECTUAL, POLITICAL, AND 
MILITARY RESOURCES TO DIMINISHING THE LONG-TERM THREAT.
