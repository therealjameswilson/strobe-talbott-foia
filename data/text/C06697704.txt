SUBJECT: BELARUS AMBASSADOR SEEKS TO EXPLAIN AWAY THREAT 
TO U.S. DIPLOMAT 
REF: 96 MINSK 764 
1. classified by james f. collins, ambassador=at-large 
and special adviser to the secretary for the new 
independent states, reasons 1.5 (b) and (d). 
2. begin summary. in an april lst meeting with acting 
secretary talbott, belarus ambassador martynov portrayed 
the implied gob threat to minsk poloff as a 
"misunderstanding" between embassy officers and persons in 
the gob who were not authorized to make such statements. 
the acting secretary voiced the department's outrage over 
the threat to the officer, and by implication, to his 
colleagues in belarus, that their safety could not be 
vuaranteed. the acting secretary offered to put the 
incident behind us if the gob agrees to take it off the 
books. end summary. 
3. the acting secretary described the incident in which a 
minsk poloff's presence at an opposition rally in minsk 
had been characterized.as "participation" in the 
demonstration -- and particularly the subsequent threats 
to the poloff -- as "a potentially significant setback in 
our relations....the fact that your competent organs 
couldn't guarantee his or others' safety" is completely 
unacceptable, he asserted. "We regard this as nothing 
short of an outrage....a word we consider carefully." 
4. the poloff is an american diplomat doing what was 
consistent with the functions of a diplomat, the acting 
secretary continued, adding that "covering a political 
demonstration" falls squarely within such a rubric.

martynov's spin: "a misunderstanding; unofficial

6. "i understand the legitimate concerns you may have," 
the ambassador began, adding that he hesitated calling the 
matter "this incident" and opting for "this thing." 
7. the matter arose from a "misperception," martynov 
said. what was conveyed to the embassy minsk officer was 
"not a'formal demarche, not a formal meeting, not a formal 
request," and did not originate at the ministry of foreign 
affairs. "what took place was an unofficial working 
contact....and the goal of this contact was to resolve 
this in an amicable way....the aim was not to have a 
problem." 
8. martynov skirted the heart of the issue concerning the 
threat to the poloff and the request that his tour be 
shortened. instead, he highlighted "the circumstances" of 
the contact with the embassy minsk officer over the 
weekend, who as martynov said, was in belarus temporarily,. 
was not an accredited officer of the embassy, and 
represents a "different branch of your government." 
9. "whatever transpired was unofficial communication, 
without consultation with the ministry of foreign affairs, 
the official foreign policy body of belarus," he stated, 
adding that the first he learned of the incident was in a 
telephone call march 30 from the office of s/nis collins. 
"i've confirmed that this request was not under mfa 
approval...therefore we consider that no request exists." 
he then asked to meet the acting secretary one-on-one to 
convey several other items. 
10. s/nis collins reiterated that what the u.s. found 
"most disturbing" was that the threat was made, and that 
it was done in a manner that conveyed the imprimatur of 
authority. "that was one of the reasons we took it so 
seriously," he explained. 
11. while mentioning that all his statements made during 
the meeting still stand, the acting secretary said, "we 
hope this incident will be aberrational...because we view 
it as a throwback to the old days." he again declared 
that the u.s. holds the gob responsible for the safety of 
u.s. diplomats in belarus, and that no threat should be 
hanging over the diplomats preparing to cover the 
demonstration set for april 2, or any other activity in 
which they may legitimately engage. by the same token, he 
stated, the u.s. has undertaken to assure the safety of 
ambassador martynov and his staff during the conduct of 
their official functions in the u.s. 
in private martynov invites talbott to minsk 
12. in a private meeting requested by martynov, the 
acting secretary said that while he is encouraged by the 
statement which martynov conveyed, he asked that the 
ambassador confirm to s/nis collins april 2 that this is 
the/the position of the gob. martynov rater added that 
foreign minister senko asked him to convey to the acting 
secretary the hope that the deputy secretary could stop in 
minsk sometime in the near future for discussions.
