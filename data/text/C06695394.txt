2. DURING A LUNCHEON MEETING WITH ASSISTANT SECRETARY 
LORD, NSC SENIOR DIRECTOR BOTH AND EAP DAS HUBBARD JULY 24 
IN BANGKOK, ROK FOREIGN MINISTER HAN SEUNG JOO PROVIDED A 
READ OUT ON HIS EARLIER MEETING WITH CHINESE FOREIGN 
MINISTER QIAN QICHEN IN BANGKOK AND THE JULY 23 VISIT TO - 
SEOUL BY JAPANESE PRIME MINISTER MURAYAMA AND FOREIGN 
MINISTER KONO. HAM WAS ACCOMPANIED BY AMB. LEE, ASSISTANT 
SECRETARY FOR PLANNING, AND OTHER SENIOR OFFICIALS. THE • 
DISCUSSION COVERED BOTH NORTH KOREA AND -ISSUES RELATED TO 
THE ASEAN MEETINGS IN BANGKOK (SEE SEPTEL). 
3. HAN SAID MURAYAMA AND KONO HAD "SAID ALL THE RIGHT 
THINGS" IN SEOUL, ALTHOUGH THEY WERE NOT AS FORTHCOMING AS 
HOSOKAWA HAD BEEN ON SUCH THINGS AS APOLOGIES FOR THE PAST. 
MURAYAMA, ACCORDING TO HAN, HAD SHOWN FEW OF HIS LEFT-WING 
ANTECEDENTS IN DISCUSSING NORTH KOREA AND SEEMED TO RELY 
HEAVILY ON KONO DURING THE TALKS. HAN SAID THEY HAD NOT 
DISCUSSED LIGHT WATER REACTOR CONVERSION IN DETAIL. KONO, 
HOWEVER, SEEMED SOMEWHAT CONCERNED ABOUT THE PACE OF U.S. 
PREPARATIONS FOR THE THIRD ROUND, WONDERING WHY WE WERE 
"RUSHING" TO PUT TOGETHER A LIGHT WATER REACTOR PACKAGE 
WHEN THE PICTURE IN NORTH KOREA FOLLOWING KIM II SUNG'S 
DEATH IS NOT YET CLEAR. 
4. HAN SAID HE HAD RESPONDED THAT THERE WERE THREE BASIC 
REASONS FOR URGENCY: 1) THERE WAS THE TECHNICAL NEED TO DO 
SOMETHING QUICKLY WITH THE SPENT FUEL; 2) IF NORTH KOREA 
WERE TO REPROCESS WE COULD QUICKLY BE BACK ON THE ROAD TV-
SANCTIONS; AND 3) 'THERE WOULD BE STRONG POLITICAL PRESSUAE 
IN BOTH THE US AND THE ROK WITHOUT EARLY PROGRESS. HAN --
SAID HE HAD POINTED OUT THAT THE OBJECTIVE IS TO SOLVE THE 
NUCLEAR PROBLEM AND URGED KONO NOT TO GET TOO CAUGHT UP IN 
THE COMPLEXITIES OF JUST HOW LIGHT WATER REACTORS COULD BE 
PROVIDED. HE HAD ALSO ARGUED THAT THE SPENT FUEL DID NOT 
REPRESENT A ONE-WAY ULTIMATUM; THE NEED TO DISPOSE OF THE 
SPENT FUEL IN A MANNER THAT DOES NOT FORCE SANCTIONS ALSO 
PUT PRESSUE ON THE DPRK TO MAKE A DEAL. (AT THIS POINT 
AMB. LEE, FORMERLY ROK AMBASADOR TO THE IAEA, INTERJECTED 
HIS OWN STRONG DOUBTS AS TO THE TECHNICAL URGENCY OF 
REMOVING THE SPENT FUEL RODS FROM THE STORAGE POND; HUBBARD 
MADE THE STANDARD ARGMENTS TO THE CONTRARY.)
