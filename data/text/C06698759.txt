PROVIDE CERTAIN ITEMS OF EXCESS MILITARY EQUIPMENT. HE 
WILL SEEK TO DEVELOP AN AFRICAN CONSENSUS FOR AN 
ALTERNATIVE TO BOUTROS GHALI. END SUMMARY.

3. 52.1 PRESIDENT KABBAH SAID HE WOULD FOLLOW UP 
PROMPTLY WITH HIS UNDERTAKING TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TO SEEK AN AFRICAN CONSENSUS ON THE SYG ELECTION. HE 
WILL WRITE CAMEROON PRESIDENT BIYA URGING HIM TO 
UNDERTAKE CONSULTATIONS TO REACH EARLY AFRICAN 
AGREEMENT ON AN ALTERNATIVE CANDIDATE TO BOUTROS GHALI. 
KABBAH UNDERSTANDS THAT THE U.S. POSITION WILL NOT 
CHANGE AFTER THE ELECTION, AND BELIEVES IT IMPORTANT 
FOR THE AFRICANS TO SELECT ANOTHER CANDIDATE SO AS NOT 
TO'RISK THE LOSS OF THE SECOND TERM TO A NON-AFRICAN 
CANDIDATE. EN ROUTE BACK HE CONSULTED WITH MALI 
PRESIDENT KONARE ON THIS MATTER DURING THE STOPOVER IN
