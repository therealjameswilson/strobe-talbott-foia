Dear Bill: 
I'm writing you about my friend Lanny Davis, whom I have 
encouraged to seek a high position in your administration in the 
White House or at the sub-cabinet level. I believe you and 
Hillary have known Lanny for many yeara, and are aware of how 
hard he worked for you from he earliest days in the campaign --
not only in Maryland, but as a member of the DNC and on the 
finance council. Recently he has been working with Jim Hamilton 
and Warren Christopher as head of a vetting team. 
I'm enclosing a letter I wrote recently to Chris, as well as 
Lanny's resume and other materiale. I am sure you can appreciate 
the essential point I made to Chris about what Lanny Davis can 
contribute to your administration -- the unique combination of 
substantive and professional credentials plus ample political 
experience at the national and local level. 
Lanny would be interested in serving in the Administration 
as General Counsel for several departments, notably Defense, 
Transportation and Labor. He would also be well qualified for a 
high level position at Justice -- as Deputy or an Assistant AG. 
Lanny tells me his first choice would be Deputy.or Associate 
Counsel in the White House -- and helping you on the national 
service proposals. 
I've known Lanny as a close friend ever since our days at 
Yale together more than 20 years ago. He served as Chairman of 
the Yale Daily News several years after me, followed me by a few 
years at the law school, worked in my early campaigns, and I am 
God-father to his son. I hope you will find a way to make use of 
Lanny's talents, professional skills, and political experience in 
your administration. 
. Lieberman
