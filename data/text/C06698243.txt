SUBJECT: TALBOTT-LIBARIDIAN MEETING REVISITS "TILT" 
CHARGES, DISCUSSES NEXT STEPS ON NK 
REF: STATE 150240 
1. CLASSIFIED BY DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT. REASONS 
1.5(b) , (d). 
SUMMARY 
2. DURING A JULY 23 MEETING WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT, ARMENIAN PRESIDENTIAL ADVISOR LIBARIDIAN 
REPEATED HIS GOVERNMENT'S CONCERN OVER THE U.S. PUBLIC 
STATEMENT REAFFIRMING AZERBAIJAN'S TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY 
LAST MONTH, ARGUING THAT THIS HAD MADE AZERI PRESIDENT 
ALIYEV MORE INTRANSIGENT ON NAGORNO-KARABAKH PEACE. 
TALBOTT AGAIN FORCEFULLY REFUTED THE PERCEPTION OF AN 
AMERICAN "TILT" TOWARD AZERBAIJAN AND PROBED ARMENIAN 
THINKING ON OUR ROLE IN THE NEGOTIATING PROCESS. 
LIBARIDIAN AFFIRMED HIS PRESIDENT'S SUPPORT FOR AN 
ACTIVE U.S. ROLE AND INDICATED HE EXPECTED TO MEET WITH 
AZERI NEGOTIATOR QULUZADE IN MID-AUGUST. END SUMMARY 
3. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET JULY 23 WITH ARMENIAN 
PRESIDENTIAL ADVISOR LIBARIDIAN TO DISCUSS 
NAGORNO-KARABAKH AND BILATERAL RELATIONS. LIBARIDIAN 
BEGAN BY REVIEWING THE STATE OF PLAY IN THE DIRECT 
DIALOGUE BETWEEN ARMENIA AND AZERBAIJAN. THOUGH HIS 
JULY BONN MEETING WITH PRESIDENT ALIYEV HAD BEEN A VERY 
GOOD ONE, HE HAD A SENSE BAKU WAS RETHINKING ITS 
APPROACH TO PEACE. 'ALIYEV, LIBARIDIAN SUGGESTED,, WAS 
CONCERNED NOT TO BE PEGGED WITH HISTORIC RESPONSIBILITY 
FOR A COMPROMISE ON NK. PERHAPS HE WAS NOW THINKING OF

ARMENIA WAS CONSIDERING HOW TO DEAL WITH THIS. 
LIBARIDIAN REPORTED HE HAS AUTHORIZATION FROM HIS 
PRESIDENT TO WORK ON A STATEMENT WHICH MIGHT BE 
CHARACTERIZED AS "LUXEMBURG-PLUS," GOING FURTHER THAN 
THE LUXEMBURG JOINT STATEMENT BUT ATTEMPTING TO BE A 
MORE MODEST DOCUMENT THAN THE COMPREHENSIVE STATEMENT OF 
PRINCIPLES EARLIER CONTEMPLATED. 
4. ONE PROBLEM FOR THE AZERI SIDE, LIBARIDIAN 
CONTINUED, WAS ITS SENSE THAT ARMENIAN EFFORTS TO DEFINE 
NK STATUS WERE IN EFFECT NOTHING SHORT OF DE FACTO 
INDEPENDENCE. TALBOTT ASKED HOW YEREVAN COUNTERED THIS 
PERCEPTION. LIBARIDIAN STATED THE ARMENIAN SIDE IN 
EFFECT DID SEEK SOMETHING VERY CLOSE TO THIS, THOUGH 
PROVIDING THE FIGLEAF OF TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY TO 
AZERBAIJAN. THIS WOULD SOLVE THE ARMENIAN SECURITY 
PROBLEM AND PROVIDE FOR THE FUTURE OF NK ARMENIANS. BUT 
THIS DID NOT EXCLUDE THE POSSIBILITY THAT A MORE 
PROSPEROUS FUTURE AZERBAIJAN MIGHT IN TIME DEVELOP A 
DIFFERENT RELATIONSHIP WITH THE PEOPLE OF NK. WHAT WAS 
CLEAR WAS THAT THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY WOULD NOT 
COUNTENANCE THE COMPLETE INDEPENDENCE OF NK AT THIS 
TIME, SOMETHING LIBARIDIAN QUIETLY LAMENTED. 
5. LIBARIDIAN CONTENDED THAT ALIYEV DOESN'T REALLY KNOW 
WHAT SORT OF AUTONOMY HE COULD OFFER NK. TALBOTT 
ENCOURAGED THE ARMENIANS TO DEFINE THIS AUTONOMY FOR THE 
AZERI SIDE AS SOMETHING SUI GENERIS, A CASE UNTO 
ITSELF. THIS WAS PRECISELY THE ARMENIAN APPROACH, 
LIBARIDIAN AGREED. BUT THERE WERE PROBLEMS ON THE AZERI 
SIDE. HASANOV WAS THE BAD COP; QULUZADE WAS THE GOOD 
COP; ALIYEV WAS PRESERVING HIS OPTIONS. WITH OBVIOUS 
REFERENCE TO THE USG JUNE 12 STATEMENT, LIBARIDIAN 
OPINED THAT WHEN THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY OFFERS 
ALIYEV SUPPORT ON TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY, HE TAKES THIS 
AS AN INDICATION HE DOES NOT.HAVE TO NEGOTIATE SERIOUSLY. 
6. TALBOTT SAID HE WANTED TO DISCUSS THE ARMENIAN 
PERCEPTION OF A "TILT." REFERRING TO HIS JULY 9 MEETING 
WITH SHUGARYAN, TALBOTT MADE THE FOLLOWING GENERAL 
POINTS. FIRST, THERE WAS NO U.S. "TILT." WE WOULD 
CONTINUE STRONG BILATERAL RELATIONS WITH YEREVAN. 
NOTHING HAD CHANGED. SECOND, DESPITE ARMENIAN 
UNHAPPINESS WITH TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY, THIS WAS A 
GLOBAL PRINCIPLE FOR US WHICH HAD PARTICULAR 
APPLICABILITY TO THE ENTIRE FORMER SOVIET UNION. SO IT 
WAS NOT INTENDED AS REFERRING SPECIFICAT,LY TO NK AND WAS 
IN NO WAY MEANT TO TAKE SIDES IN THE CONFLICT. 
7. TALBOTT HOPED THE U.S. COULD CONTINUE TO USE ITS 
GOOD OFFICES TO WORK WITH THE PARTIES ON NK. PERHAPS 
THERE WOULD BE A WAY TO SQUARE THE CIRCLE, PRESERVING 
THE PRINCIPLE OF TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY WITH A PRACTICAL 
DEFINITION OF THE "HIGHEST DEGREE OF AUTONOMY" AS 
REFERRED TO BY ALIYEV. HE ASKED LIBARIDIAN'S ASSESSMENT 
OF WHAT COULD BE EXPECTED IN THE NEXT FEW MONTHS, AND 
WHAT ROLE HE SAW FOR THE U.S. 
8. LIBARIDIAN FIRST REEMPHASIZED FROM THE ARMENIAN 
PERSPECTIVE THE PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCES FOR THE 
NEGOTIATIONS OF U.S. AFFIRMATION OF TERRITORIAL 
INTEGRITY. IT MADE ALIYEV MORE INTRANSIGENT. BUT IN 
FACT THE ARMENIAN SIDE, FROM THE PRESIDENT ON DOWN, 
PRIVATELY DISCUSSED WAYS TO ADDRESS THE AZERI CONCERN 
OVER TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY, WITHIN THE CONTEXT OF A 
TRADEOFF ON NK STATUS. 
9. TALBOTT REITERATED HIS UNDERSTANDING OF THE ARMENIAN 
POSITION. BUT, HE UNDERSCORED, THERE WAS A REASON WHY 
WE HAD MADE THE JUNE 12 STATEMENT. THE PORTER AMENDMENT 
GAVE US REAL CONCERN. IT COULD HAVE DONE GREAT DAMAGE 
TO THE PEACE PROCESS. SO WE HAD ISSUED OUR PUBLIC
