Dear Strobe: 
RELEASE IN 
PART B6 
Congratulations on your new position. I can't think of anything more exciting, but I 
don't think your family is going to see much of you. What will you, do next? I 
guess we all grew up and luckily for you, you kept your connections with the 
President. He couldn't have hired a more qualified person. 
This has been an interesting year for me also. I gave up my stationery business on 
Long Island and am now in the travel business here in Greenwich. I am still 
writing my monthly newsletter, The Diplomat, on international business. I was 
fortunate to travel to Chile in October and to Romania, as a guest of their Minister 
of Foreign Affairs in November. 
I know your schedule is horrendous; but did want to send you a copy of The 
Diplomat on Chile and the report I prepared on Romania. The newsletter is 
presently at the printer. 
I'm proud to say the Cleveland Council on World Affairs 
has kept the John Hay Forum going. Hopefully, you might remember you were 
our first speaker. 
Good luck to you and I hope our paths will cross again. 
Sinc rely, 
Katherine B. Holmes
