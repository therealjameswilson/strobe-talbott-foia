SUBJECT: SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER'S JULY 10 BREAKFAST FOR 
POLISH PRESIDENT KWASNIEWSKI 
1. CLASSIFIED BY DIRECTOR JONATHAN B. RICKERT. REASON: 
1.5 (B) (D). 
2. SUMAARY. THE SECRETARY HOSTED A BREAKFAST ON 
JULY 10 FOR VISITING POLISH PRESIDENT ALEKSANDER 
KWASNIEWSKI. KWASNIEWSKI EMPHASIZED POLAND'S DESIRE TO 
ENTER NATO AS QUICKLY AS POSSIBLE, EXPLAINING THAT ITS 
INTEREST WAS EASED NOT ON ANY FEAR OF RUSSIA BUT ON ITS 
COMMITMENT TO PROMOTING EUROPEAN INTEGRATION. THE 
SECRETARY COMPLIMENTED KWASNIEWSKI ON POLAND'S SIGNIFICANT 
PROGRESS IN REFORM AND SAID POLAND WOULD BE A LEADING 
CANDIDATE TO JOIN AN ENLARGED NATO. OTHER TOPICS COVERED 
DURING THt HOUR-LONG DISCUSSION WERE THE RECENT RUSSIAN 
ELECTION (BOTH SIDES AGREED THAT THE OUTCOME HAD BEEN 
POSITIVE); POLISH-UKRAINIAN RELATIONS (KWASNIEWSKI 
DESCRIBED HIS EFFORTS TO BUILD MUCH CLOSER BILATERAL, 
POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC TIES, WHICH THE SECRETARY 
APPLAUDED); KWASNIEWSKI'S OWN DECEMBER 1995 ELECTION 
VICTORY; POLISH-JEWISH RELATIONS (KWASNIEWSKI SAID A MAJOR 
GOAL OF HIS VISIT WAS TO IMPROVE UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN THE 
TWO PEOPLES); OUTREACH TO POLISH-AMERICANS (ANOTHER OF THE 
PRESIDENT'S GOALS WAS TO MEET AS MANY POLISH-AMERICANS AS 
POSSIBLE AND EXPLAIN HIS ADMINISTRATION'S POLICIES); AND 
ARMS SALES TO PARIAH STATES (WITHOUT IMPLYING THAT SUCH 
TRANSACTIONS WERE A CURRENT ISSUE, THE SECRETARY 
UNDERSCORED THE IMPORTANCE OF ABSTAINING FROM SUCH 
TRADE). PRESIDENT KWASNIEWSKI CONCLUDED THE MEETING BY 
INVITING THE SECRETARY TO VISIT POLAND. END SUMAARY. 
NATO ENLARGEMENT 
3.).t4, THE SECRETARY COMPLIMENTED PRESIDENT KWASNIEWSKI 
FOR POLAND'S PROGRESS TOWARD NATO MEMBERSHIP AND EXPRESSED 
APPRECIATION FOR THE POLISH PRESIDENT'S RESTRAINT IN HIS 
PUBLIC REMARKS ON THE SUBJECT. THE U.S. WANTED THE 
ENLARGEMENT PROCESS TO BE, AND BE SEEN TO BE, OPEN AND 
DELIBERATE. KWASNIEWSKI AGREED THAT POLAND WAS ON TRACK 
FOR NATO MEMBERSHIP. WITH THE RUSSIAN PRESIDENTIAL 
ELECTION SUCCESSFULLY BEHIND US, NOW WAS THE TIME, HE 
SAID, TO SET A FIRM SCHEDULE FOR THE NEXT STEPS. 
PROMOTING POLAND'S EARLY ENTRANCE INTO NATO WAS A PRIMARY 
GOAL OF KWASNIEWSKI'S VISIT. POLAND WANTED TO BECOME A 
MEMBER AS SOON AS POSSIBLE, NOT OUT OF FEAR OF RUSSIA OR 
ANYONE ELSE BUT TO PROMOTE FASTER EUROPEAN INTEGRATION.

ORGANIZATION). THE SECURITY OF THOSE STATES NOT ADMITTED 
AMONG THE FIRST GROUP AND THE FULL TRANSPARENCY OF THE 
ENLARGEMENT PROCESS ALSO MUST BE MAINTAINED, HE SAID.
