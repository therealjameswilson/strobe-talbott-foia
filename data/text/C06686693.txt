RELEASE IN FULL 
sally Lilienthal 
sally Lilienthal is president and founder of the Ploughshares 
Fund, a public grantmaking foundation dedicated to preventing 
nuclear war and building global security. Since its founding in 
1981, Ploughshares has made grants totaling more than ten million 
dollars. 
She is a member of the council on Foreign Relations, and 
currently serves on the boards of the Center for International 
Policy and International Student Pugwash. Lilienthal was on the 
board of the Northern California American Civil Liberties Union, 
and was founder and chair of the Northern California Committee of 
the NAACP Legal Defense and Education Fund from 1971 through 
1977. she has also served on the boards of the Women's campaign 
Fund, the San Francisco Bar Association and Sarah Lawrence 
College. 
In 1973 Lilienthal was acting chair of Amnesty International/USA 
after five years as the national vice chair. For seven years she 
served as coordinator of the Western regional office in San 
Francisco. 
In March of 1990 Lilienthal was honored for her commitment to 
peace and human rights with the Eleanor Roosevelt Humanitarian 
Award from the United Nations Association. She also received the 
Robert w. Scrivner Award for outstanding-creativity-by-4h- -----
individual grantmaker from the Council on Foundations in 1987, an 
award for Exceptional Commitment to Working-for-Social Change - 
from the National Organization for Women in 1985, and the Phoebe 
Apperson Hearst Distinguished Woman Award in 1965. 
Ms. Lilienthal was born in 1919 in Portland, Oregon and has lived 
in san Francisco for more than fifty years. She graduated from 
Sarah Lawrence College in 1940 and is the mother of five children.
