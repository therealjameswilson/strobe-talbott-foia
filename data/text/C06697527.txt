THIS AD HOC AND INFORMAL MEETING PROVIDED AN OPPORTUNITY

TO THE INTERRUPTION OF DEMOCRACY IN PARAGUAY. THE OTHER

DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER FERNANDO PETRELLA NOTED THAT AT 
THE NEXT MERCOSUR MEETING IN SAN LUIS, ARGENTINA, THE

6. -(E4 MR. MCLARTY MADE A PITCH FOR CONTINUED CLOSE 
COOPERATION ON SUMMIT OF THE AMERICAS FOLLOW-UP ISSUES, 
AND ASKED HOW MERCOSUR DEALT WITH ENVIRONMENTAL

INFRASTRUCTURAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS. 
7.44;)- USDEL ASKED MERCOSUR FOR SUPPORT IN THREE SPECIFIC 
AREAS: HAITI, HUMAN RIGHTS, AND MEASURES TO FIGHT 
CORRUPTION. TALBOTT NOTED IMPROVEMENTS IN HUMAN-RIGHTS 
CONDITIONS IN HAITI, BUT SAID THAT HAITIAN DEMOCRACY STILL 
REQUIRED INTERNATIONAL SUPPORT, AND ASKED THAT THE 
MERCOSUR COUNTRIES SUPPORT THE OAS RESOLUTION THAT HAITI 
WOULD ITSELF INTRODUCE. MR. DAVIDOW CITED BRAZIL'S NEW 
DOMESTIC EMPHASIS ON PROTECTION OF HUMAN RIGHTS, AND 
THANKED THE BRAZILIAN DELEGATION FOR COOPERATING ON THIS 
ISSUE IN SUMMIT OF THE AMERICAS MEETINGS. THE BRAZILIANS 
REPLIED THAT COUNTRIES OF THE REGION WERE LEARNING TO DEAL 
IN CONSTRUCTIVE WAYS WITH UGLY ISSUES SUCH AS CORRUPTION, 
HUMAN RIGHTS, DRUG TRAFFICKING, AND MONEY LAUNDERING. 
AMBASSADOR BABBITT SAID THE OAS COUNTRIES' EFFORTS TO 
FIGHT CORRUPTION WERE A SOURCE OF PRIDE, AND COULD FORM 
THE BASIS OF A CONSTRUCTIVE EXCHANGE WITH THE EUROPEANS. 
8. -tel- THE MEETING ENDED WITH AN OBSERVATION BY TALBOTT 
THAT THE MEETING HAD UNDERLINED THE BASIC HUMAN AND 
POLITICAL'VALUES THAT WE AS A HEMISPHERE SHARE. HE NOTED 
THAT WE WOULD LOOK FORWARD TO HEARING MORE ABOUT 
MERCOSUR'S POLITICAL INITIATIVES IN ORDER TO COORDINATE 
INDIVIDUALLY, WITH MERCOSUR AS AN ORGANIZATION, AND 
INTERNATIONALLY.

10. (C) COMMENT: THE MEETING HAD THE GENERAL ATMOSPHERE 
OF A "FIRST DATE," WITH DELEGATIONS DEMONSTRATING MORE 
RESERVE THAN IS TYPICAL OF US BILATERAL DEALINGS WITH 
THESE COUNTRIES. ARGENTINA, MERCOSUR'S CURRENT CHAIRMAN 
AND THE FORCE BEHIND THE IDEA OF U.S.-MERCOSUR TALKS, DID 
MOST OF THE TALKING, WHILE THE, OTHERS TENDED TO SIMPLY 
CHIME IN, UNLESS ADDRESSED DIRECTLY BY USDEL. BRAZIL 
SUMMIT COORDINATOR BAHADIAN TOLD US COORDINATOR BROWN 
AFTER THE MEETING THAT HE DID NOT THINK BRAZIL WAS AS 
INTERESTED IN A MERCOSUR POLITICAL DIALOGUE WITH THE US AS 
WERE THE ARGENTINES.
