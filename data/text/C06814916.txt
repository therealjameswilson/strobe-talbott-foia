SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MEETING WITH RUSSIA'S 
YABLOKO FACTION LEADER AND DUMA DEPUTY GRIGORIY 
YAVLINSKIY ON FEBRUARY 22, 1999. 
1. (U) CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR JAMES F. COLLINS; 
REASON 1.5 (B) AND (D). 
2. Tu...3 SUMMARY: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY MET WITH 
GRIGORIY YAVLINSKIY, DUMA DEPUTY AND YABLOKO FACTION 
LEADER, ON FEBRUARY 22, IN MOSCOW, RUSSIA. YAVLINSKIY 
SHARED HIS CONCERNS ABOUT NATO AIRSTRIKES IN KOSOVO, 
THE NEED FOR A JOINT U.S.-EUROPEAN-RUSSIAN ABM TREATY, 
A PROPOSED IMF POLICY TOWARD RUSSIA ("TOUGH LOVE"), HIS 
PLAN FOR RUSSIA'S ECONOMIC RECOVERY, AND HIS THOUGHTS 
ON UPCOMING RUSSIAN PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS. END 
SUMMARY. 
KOSOVO 
3. (-C-14- YAVLINSKIY STATED THAT THE SITUATION IN KOSOVO 
IS LIKELY TO HAVE A NEGATIVE AFFECT ON U.S.-RUSSIAN 
RELATIONS. ACCORDING TO YAVLINSKIY, THE RUSSIAN 
POLITICAL ELITE ARE PREPARED TO REACT TO NATO 
AIRSTRIKES IN A TOUGH, ALMOST "HYSTERICAL" MANNER. 
ALTHOUGH HE DOESN'T BELIEVE THAT RUSSIA WILL START A 
WAR OVER KOSOVO, HE DOES BELIEVE THAT IF NATO BOMBS 
KOSOVO, HE WILL LOSE THE BULK OF THE POLITICAL SUPPORT 
HE CURRENTLY ENJOYS IN THE DUMA. YAVLINSKIY SUGGESTS 
THAT THE CONTACT GROUP SLOW DOWN AND DEEPEN MUTUAL 
UNDERSTANDING WITH RUSSIA ON THE ISSUE OF KOSOVO. HE 
FEELS THAT KOSOVO COULD BECOME A SERIOUS POLITICAL 
ISSUE FOR RUSSIA THAT SUPPORTERS OF KPRF LEADER 
GENNADIY ZYUGANOV, LDPR LEADER VLADIMIR ZHIRINOVSKIY, 
FORMER INTERIOR MINISTER ANATOLIY KULIKOV, AND PM 
PRIMAKOV MIGHT CONSENT TO SENDING "VOLUNTEERS" 
(MERCENARIES) TO THE REGION. 
4. --(-€-) THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD YAVLINSKIY THAT 
NATO'S DECISION TO USE FORCE IS AVOIDABLE BUT CLOSELY 
DEPENDENT ON GOR POSTURING IN THE 48 HOURS FOLLOWING 
THE MEETING. IF RUSSIA PUBLICLY OPPOSES AIRSTRIKES, 
MILOSEVIC MAY THINK HE HAS INTERNATIONAL SUPPORT FOR 
HIS CURRENT ACTIONS, THEREBY FORCING NATO TO USE 
AIRSTRIKES TO STOP HIM. IF RUSSIA SHOULD CHOOS,E TO 
WEIGH IN WITH MILOSEVIC IN FAVOR OF THE CURRENT 
AGREEMENT, IT COULD PREEMPT NATO AIRSTRIKES ALTOGETHER. 
YAVLINSKIY STATED HIS BELIEF THAT PRIMAKOV, IF 
PERSUADED TO CARRY THIS MESSAGE TO MILOSEVIC, CAN SOLVE 
THE PROBLEM.

ARM UMBRELLA 
5. Zt"4. YAVLINSKIY PROMOTED HIS U.S.-EUROPEAN-RUSSIAN 
ABM UMBRELLA ORGANIZATION AS A WAY TO JUMP-START 
RUSSIA'S ECONOMY AND CURB THE FLOW OF MILITARY AND 
NUCLEAR TECHNOLOGY TO ROGUE STATES. ACCORDING TO 
YAVLINSKIY, THE SITUATION IN RUSSIA IS "SERIOUS," AND 
ADDITIONAL COOPERATION BETWEEN THE U.S. AND RUSSIA WILL 
POSITIVELY AFFECT POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC ISSUES, AS 
WELL AS STOP WHAT HE CALLED "SHADOW COOPERATION" 
BETWEEN RUSSIA AND IRAN. YAVLINSKIY CALLED HIS IDEA A 
"MODIFIED MARSHALL PLAN FOR RUSSIX.," ONE WHICH WOULD 
CREATE A DEMAND FOR RUSSIAN MILITARY PRODUCTS AND 
PROVIDE RUSSIA WITH CLOSE STRATEGIC COOPERATION WITH 
NATO AND THE EUROPEAN UNION. 
6.s-1-S4 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REITERATED PRESIDENT 
CLINTON'S DEDICATION TO THE IDEA OF TECHNOLOGY SHARING, 
AND THE BELIEF THAT THIS WOULD BUILD ON RUSSIA'S 
STRENGTHS. IT COULD ALSO DILUTE RUSSIAN SUSPICION THAT 
NMD IS A PLOT TO WEAKEN THE RUSSIAN FEDERATION BY 
PROVIDING THE INCENTIVE FOR COOPERATION. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY STRESSED THAT THE U.S. IS FIRMLY AGAINST 
NUCLEAR/MILITARY PROLIFERATION ("SHADOW COOPERATION"), 
AND EXPLAINED THAT ABM IS AN ISSUE FOR THE U.S. BECAUSE 
ROGUE STATES SUCH AS IRAN RECEIVE DANGEROUS TECHNOLOGY 
FROM COUNTRIES SUCH AS RUSSIA. YAVLINSKIY STATED THAT 
ALTHOUGH HE BELIEVES THE RUSSIAN GOVERNMENT WOULD BE 
RECEPTIVE TO THIS IDEA, THE U.S. MUST TAKE THE 
INITIATIVE TO GET BOTH SIDES TO THE TABLE. 
THE IMF AND "TOUGH LOVE" 
7. >n,l, YAVLINSKIY SUGGESTED THAT THE IMF POLICY TOWARD 
RUSSIA BE ONE OF "TOUGH LOVE." HE EXPLAINED HIS VIEW 
THAT RUSSIA'S ECONOMIC SITUATION IS "DISASTROUS." HE 
IS UNHAPPY.WITH THE "DO NOTHING" POLICIES OF PRIMAKOV 
YABLOKO FACTION LEADER AND DUMA DEPUTY GRIGORIY 
YAVLINSKIY ON FEBRUARY 22, 1999. 
AND MASLYUKOV, AND CONCERNED ABOUT CORRUPTION. "TOUGH 
LOVE" FOR YAVLINSKIY MEANS THE IMF SHOULD EXPLAIN TO 
RUSSIA THAT THERE ARE "X" NUMBER OF GOALS THEY MUST 
ACHIEVE IN ORDER TO BE ELIGIBLE FOR FUTURE ASSISTANCE, 
AND THE IMF SHOULD NOT BACK DOWN. YAVLINSKIY STATED 
THE IMF COULD NEGOTIATE MORE LENIENTLY WITH RUSSIA ON 
DEBT RELIEF, BUT THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPLAINED THAT AN 
IMF PROGRAM MUST BE IN EFFECT IN THE COUNTRY BEFORE 
DEBT CAN BE RESTRUCTURED. 
8. 1-6.1. PASCUAL REITERATED PRESIDENT CLINTON'S SUPPORT 
FOR DEBT RESTRUCTURING FOR RUSSIA, BUT POINTED OUT THAT 
THE PRESIDENT FEELS DEBT RESTRUCTURING MUST GO HAND IN 
HAND WITH ECONOMIC RESTRUCTURING IN ORDER TO RESULT IN( 
REAL PROGRESS. PASCUAL STATED THAT BUDGET, TAXATION, 
AND FINANCIAL SECTOR REFORM WOULD FURTHER U.S. INTEREST 
IN DEBT RESTRUCTURING. YAVLINSKIY SAID THAT TAXES MUST 
BE LOWERED IN ORDER TO SHRINK THE SIZE OF RUSSIA'S 
SHADOW ECONOMY AND INCREASE THE TAX BASE. ACCORDING TO

9. -Tle*-YAVLINSKIY FEELS THE COLLAPSE OF THE. STATE 
FINANCE SYSTEM IS A DIRECT RESULT OF THE COLLAPSE OF 
THE STATE. ACCORDING TO YAVLINSKIY, THERE IS NO 
FUNCTIONING CUSTOMS SYSTEM IN RUSSIA, AND THERE IS NO 
FINANCIAL OVERSIGHT OF THE 50 PERCENT OF THE FEDERAL 
BUDGET WHICH IS DISBURSED TO RUSSIA'S REGIONS. 
YAVLINSKIY IS PROMOTING A SYSTEM IN WHICH NATURAL 
RESOURCE MONOPOLIES, SUCH AS GAZPROM, ARE SUBJECTED TO 
STRICT NATIONAL CONTROL AND OVERSIGHT. CONTINUING 
ECONOMIC COLLAPSE, YAVLINSKIY WARNED, IS DRIVING MORE 
OF THE RUSSIAN ECONOMY UNDERGROUND, AND-AWAY FROM THE 
LEGAL REALM. PASCUAL AGREED THAT THE CURRENT TAXATION 
SYSTEM IS STRANGLING THE RUSSIAN ECONOMY, BUT ADDRESSED 
THE QUESTION OF INCREASING REVENUE THROUGH VALUE ADDED 
TAXES, EXCISE TAXES, AND MORE EFFECTIVE TAX COLLECTION 
FROM MONOPOLIES. YAVLINSKIY STRESSED THAT RUSSIA MUST 
BREAK THE "BARTER CYCLE." 
PRESIDENTIAL CONTENDERS 
10. (C) THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED YAVLINSKIY FOR HIS 
VIEWS OF PRESIDENTIAL CONTENDERS' PROSPECTS. 
YAVLINSKIY STATED THAT IT IS NOT CLEAR TO HIM WHAT WILL 
HAPPEN IN THE NEXT PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS. HE POINTED 
OUT THAT IF ELECTIONS REMAIN ON SCHEDULE, MOSCOW MAYOR 
YURIY LUZHKOV WILL HAVE TO DECIDE WHETHER TO RUN FOR 
MAYOR OR PRESIDENT, AS BOTH ELECTIONS ARE SCHEDULED TO 
BE HELD ON THE SAME DAY. IN RESPONSE TO INQUIRIES 
ABOUT A RUMORED YAVLINSKIY-LUZHKOV ALLIANCE, YAVLINSKIY 
SAID HE HAS GOOD PERSONAL RELATIONS WITH LUZHKOV AND 
WILL MEET LATER THIS WEEK TO DECIDE WHETHER TO FORM AN 
OFFICIAL ALLIANCE OR NOT. YAVLINSKIY'S OFFICIAL 
POSITION REMAINS THAT HE WILL RUN IN THE PRESIDENTIAL 
ELECTIONS AND HOPES'TO COME IN THIRD DURING THE FIRST 
ROUND. 
11. l'Eq.-YAVLINSKIY STATED HIS BELIEF THAT EITHER 
LUZHKOV OR PRIMAKOV WILL RUN IN THE ELECTIONS, BUT 
THERE IS LITTLE CHANCE THEY WILL BOTH STEP INTO THE 
RING. ACCORDING TO YAVLINSKIY, LUZHKOV AND-PRIMAKOV 
FEED FROM THE SAME BASE OF SUPPORT POLITICALLY, AND IF 
LUZHKOV BELIEVES HE WILL BE THIRD, LUZHKOV WILL NOT 
PARTICIPATE AT ALL. YAVLINSKIY UNDERSCORED THAT FACT 
HE AND LUZHKOV DISAGREE ON THE FOREIGN POLICY ISSUES OF 
SEVASTOPOL AND THE BALTICS, AND HE IS HESITANT TO LEND 
HIS CLEAN IMAGE TO LUZHKOV'S DIRTY ONE. SESTANOVICH 
ASKED YAVLINSKIY IF U.S. ACTIONS COULD AFFECT THE 
RUSSIAN ELECTIONS, AND YAVLINSKIY CAUTIONED THAT 
SUPPORTING LUZHKOV PUBLICLY COULD SERVE TO ABSOLVE HIM 
OF CRIMINAL CONNECTIONS AND LEGITIMIZE HIS FOREIGN - 
POLICY STANCES WHICH CONTRADICT OUR OWN.
