THE POTENTIAL CONFRONTATION BETWEEN WASMOSY AND OVIEDO,

--4&]- THE ACTING SECRETARY CITED REPORTING THAT 
SIGNIFICANT MILITARY ELEMENTS IN THE AIR FORCE, NAVY, 
AND EVEN THE ARMY HAVE COME OUT IN SUPPORT OF DEMOCRATIC 
RULE. THEY SEE OVIEDO AS DAMAGED GOODS. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY ADDED, HOWEVER, THAT HE DID NOT WANT TO 
DOWNPLAY THE DANGERS. OVIEDO STILL HAS LOYAL FORCES AND 
COULD CAUSE BLOODSHED. THE OVERALL TREND IS IN THE 
RIGHT DIRECTION, HOWEVER. DI TELLA AGREED WITH THE 
ACTING SECRETARY'S ASSESSMENT. 
-1(1!”- THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THAT MERCOSUR HAS BEEN 
THE BEST CHANNEL TO SIGNAL TO OVIEDO THAT HE SHOULD 
ACCEDE TO THE WILL OF ALL THE PARTIES, INCLUDING 
PARAGUAY'S NEIGHBORS. HE MENTIONED THE EXCELLENT 
MILITARY TIES BRAZIL HAS WITH PARAGUAY THROUGH THEIR 
ATTACHE IN ASUNCION AND THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE 
PARAGUAYAN COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF OF THE ARMED FORCES WITH 
THE BRAZILIAN DEFENSE MINISTER. 
--H1”- DI TELLA SAID HE SPOKE WITH OVIEDO THE NIGHT BEFORE 
HIS RESIGNATION AS ARMY CHIEF. OVIEDO SAID HE WAS 
WILLING TO HONOR HIS DEAL WITH WASMOSY SO THERE WAS NO 
MORE DANGER OF A MILITARY RESPONSE. DI TELLA TOLD 
OVIEDO THAT IF HE MAINTAINED HIMSELF WITHIN THE LAW, 
THEN HE WOULD HAVE A POLITICAL FUTURE. IF NOT, HE WOULD 
BE OUT. ACCORDING TO DI TELLA, OVIEDO WANTS A POLITICAL 
FUTURE LESS AND LESS NOW. DI TELLA SUGGESTED THAT 
MERCOSUR WOULD RELEASE A PRESS ANNOUNCEMENT SUPPORTING ' 
WASMOSY'S DECISION. THE ACTING SECRETARY WELCOMED THIS 
INITIATIVE. THE USG WILL TAKE'MERCOSUR'S CUE AND WILL 
SYNCHRONIZE ITS EFFORTS WITH MERCOSUR. DI TELLA SAID 
THERE WOULD BE A STATEMENT RELEASED THIS AFTERNOON. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY REITERATED THE GREAT COORDINATION THE 
USG HAS HAD WITH MERCOSUR AND THE OAS. 
--Hi ON ANOTHER SUBJECT, DI TELLA ASKED THE ACTING 
SECRETARY IF THE PROPOSED APRIL 28 MEETING BETWEEN 
PRESIDENT CLINTON AND PRESIDENT MENEM WAS STILL ON IN 
FLORIDA. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THE PRESIDENT'S 
SCHEDULE IS ESPECIALLY COMPLICATED THESE DAYS, BUT NOTED 
HE WAS EN ROUTE TO THE WHITE HOUSE AND WOULD RAISE THE 
ISSUE. THE ACTING SECRETARY CLOSED BY THANKING DI TELLA 
FOR MAKING THE TRIP TO PARAGUAY AND FOR THE SUCCESSFUL 
RESULT.
