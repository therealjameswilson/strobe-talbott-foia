2. DESPITE HIS EARLIER CRITICISMS OF U.S MILITARY 
INTERVENTION IN THIRD WORLD COUNTRIES AS BEING "TOO 
INTIMIDATING," GHANAIAN PRESIDENT JERRY RAWLINGS ASKED 
FOR U.S. HELP TO "STEP-UP OUR PRESENCE" IN LIBERIA, 
"NOT TO AN INTIMIDATING LEVEL, BUT CLOSE TO IT." THE 
PROBLEM /N RAWLINGS' MIND WAS WHAT HE CALLED ECOMOG'S 
"MILITARY POISE" OR "PREPAREDNESS." IF THE U.S. WANTS 
TO GET INVOLVED, HE OBSERVED, IT WON'T HAVE TO SEND 
TROOPS JUST HELICOPTERS. HE MADE HIS PITCH: "YOUR 
GLOBAL PRESENCE IS PICKING UP NOW." (HAITI, IRAQ, 
NORTHERN KOREA HAD PREVIOUSLY BEEN DISCUSSED.) "DON'T 
SHY AWAY FROM MONROVIA. CAN YOU PROVIDE US WITH A FEW 
HELICOPTERS -- 3 OR 4 -- WITH OR WITHOUT AMERICAN 
CREWS." "I CAN ENFORCE DISARMAMENT IF YOU (THE U.S.) 
CAN HELP US BE SUBTLY INTIMIDATING," RAWLINGS DECLARED. 
3. RAWLINGS SWITCHED HIS MUSINGS ABOUT THE NEED FOR 
INTIMIDATION TO RWANDA, WHERE GHANA HAS A PEACEKEEPING 
BATTALION. REPEATING HIS EARLIER ANTI-FRENCH THEME, HE 
SAID THAT HE WISHED IT HAD BEEN ANY COUNTRY OTHER THAN 
FRANCE WHICH HAD FIRST GONE INTO RWANDA. HE SAID THAT 
THE RPF WAS BETTER EQUIPPED THAN HIS OWN FORCES. IF THE 
GHANA BATTALION WINDS UP BEING "DEMYSTIFIED," THEN 
CONVENTIONAL ARMIES WILL HAVE CREDIBILITY PROBLEMS 
EVERYWHERE.

THAT SEEMS TO BE COMPLEMENTARY OR SUPPLEMENTARY, AS 
OPPOSED TO TAKING OVER" THE WHOLE OPERATION. HE FEARED 
THAT A UN TAKEOVER NOW WOULD JUST "STEAL THE CREDIT" 
FROM ECOMOG AND ECOWAS. HE INVITED THE U.S. TO "SHARE 
THE FINAL VICTORY WITH US." HE ADDED THAT ECOMOG CAN 
ACHIEVE A SOLUTION ON ITS OWN, BUT COULD DO IT MORE 
QUICKLY W/TH U.S. HELP.
