Dear Mr. Halliwell: 
Thank you for your letter congratulating me on my 
confirmation as Ambastador-At-Large and Special Advisor for the 
Newly Independent States. The creation of this position 
demonstrates the high priority which President Clinton has 
given to U.S. relations with these states. The job is 
challenging, but exciting and I am looking forward to 
implementing the new ideas of this Administration. 
Congratulations to you on successfully completing your 
Enoject to supply $70 million dollars worth of U.S. 
agricultural equipment and supplies to Ukraine. The 
Administration strongly supports U.S. private trade and 
investment in the newly independent states. We recognize that 
official, government-to-government aid cannot begin to meet the 
vast needs of these states. Even if it could, governrnent 
assistance could not supply the entrepreneurial know-how which 
U.S. business can provide. I am looking forward to receiving 
your op-ed piece on the transaction. 
Congratulations again and good luck with your future 
ventures. 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Advisor to the Secretary on 
the New Independent States
