RELEASE IN PART 
B6 
In writing this letter, I overcome a reluctance borne of more or less equal parts: 
fearing to trade on a friendship not recently renawed; feeling a bit intimidated by your 
celebrated status; and some general self-doubt -- the latter probably encompasses the 
other two, actually. In part, the approaching reunion encouraged me to write. 
Though I've mostly kept a distance from Yale, I attended the 20th class reunion and 
was struck by how natural rnost friendships still felt, despite years of disuse. And I'm 
confident you're still the same reliably genuine guy I knew 25 years ago. (I also 
consider us lucky to have you as part of the government.) 
Apparently I have some possibility of appointment to the lower reaches of the 
Administration, as Regional EPA Administrator for Region III, in Philadelphia. This is 
thanks to the sponsorship of Sen. Rockefeller, as well as Governor Caperton, Sen. 
Byrd and the West Virginia House delegation. Here at home, I have support from both 
the business community and the local Sierra Club. West Virginia is just small fry in 
EPA Region III, however, which also includes Pennsylvania, Maryland, Delaware, 
Virginia, and D.C. 
I'm currently general counsel (and effectively deputy) to the state cabinet secretary 
for commerce, labor, and environment. My focus has been on rulemaking and 
legislative policy, and using conflict resolution and consensus-building to address 
issues between industry and the environmentalist community. I'd love a chance to 
work on such problems in' the Clinton administration. think I have the political and 
management preparation to do a good job. 
I realize this is a far cry from your bailiwick. And I realize it might be unrealistic to ask 
for your support, in view of our limited recent contact. Still, I'd be grateful for any 
help you feel comfortable Iending. Despite the passage of years, like most of our 
classmates, I'm still basically the same guy you knew back-when'---just older, wiser, 
and more chastened. 
I look forward to seeing you in New Haven. Thanks for anything you're able to do on 
this. 
B6
