SUMMARY 
1. -(-66)- ON JUNE 7, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND USUN 
AMBASSADOR INDERFURTH MET WITH UN U/SYG FOR PEACEKEEPING 
KOFI ANNAN TO DISCUSS HAITI, BURUNDI, BOSNIA, AND 
EASTERN SLAVONIA. , 
-- ON HAITI, TALBOTT EMPHASIZED U.S. SUPPORT FOR 
CONTINUED PEACEKEEPING IN HAITI; ANNAN SAID THAT THE 
SYG'S REPORT WOULD RECOMMEND A PEACEKEEPING OPERATION 
FOR SIX MONTHS AFTER JUNE 30. TALBOTT ALSO STRESSED THE 
NEED FOR INTERNATIONAL SUPPORT FOR THE HAITIAN NATIONAL 
POLICE'. 
-- ON BURUNDI, INDERFURTH ADVISED THE UN OF U.S. 
WILLINGNESS TO PROVIDE LOGISTICAL SUPPORT TO A CHAPTER 
VI OR VII MISSION AND ADDITIONAL PLANNING SUPPORT. HE 
AND ANNAN AGREED THAT INTERESTED DELEGATIONS SHOULD MEET 
AGAIN SOON, AFTER THE ARRIVAL OF THE U.S. PLANNING 
CONCLUSIONS FROM THE CARLISLE SEMINAR. 
-- ON BOSNIA, TALBOTT STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE AND 
POPULARITY OF ELECTIONS IN BOTH BOSNIAN ENTITIES. 
-- ON EASTERN SLAVONIA, TALBOTT AND ANNAN AGREED THAT 
THE SITUATION WAS GOING WELL. ANNAN STRESSED THE NEED 
TO PROVIDE RESOURCES TO THE LOCAL POLICE, AS WELL AS TO 
KEEP PRESSING TUDJMAN TO OFFER A BETTER AMNESTY AND TO 
TAKE STEPS TO REASSURE THE SERBS. END SUMMARY.

7. INDERFURTH SAID THAT THE NEED FOR PREVENTIVE

8. -tel- DPKO AFRICA DIVISION DIRECTOR HEDI ANABI SAID 
THAT THE SECRETARIAT HAD RECEIVED OTHER RESPONSES. FOR 
A CHAPTER VII OPERATION, THE FINNS HAD DECLINED TO 
ASSIST BUT THE BELGIANS HAD INDICATED WILLINGNESS TO 
PROVIDE ASSISTANCE IN PLANNING, LOGISTICAL ARRANGEMENTS, 
AND FINANCING. FOR A CHAPTER VI OPERATION, THE BELGIANS 
HAD MADE THE SAME OFFER AND THE BANGLADESHIS HAD OFFERED 
IN PRINCIPLE A LONG LIST OF PERSONNEL AND EQUIPMENT, 
"VIRTUALLY AN ARMY." THE AUSTRIANS, SENEGELESE, AND 
FINNS HAD DECLINED TO ASSIST. ANNAN ADDED THAT THE 
RUSSIANS ALSO EXPRESSED SUPPORT FOR A CHAPTER VI 
ACTION. TALBOTT EXPRESSED DISAPPOINTMENT AT FINNISH 
RELUCTANCE.
