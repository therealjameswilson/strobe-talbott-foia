4.-igi4 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD PETERSEN THE FIRST 
MUNITIONS HAD EXPLODED OVER TARGETS IN SERBIA MOMENTS 
EARLIER. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, SPEAKING ON THE 
SECRETARY'S BEHALF, SAID IT WAS EXTRAORDINARILY IMPORTANT 
THAT WASHINGTON AND THE EU REMAINED IN UNITY AS A ' 
COMMUNITY AND AN ALLIANCE. PETERSEN RESPONDED THAT THE EU 
HAD ISSUED A STATEMENT WITHIN THE PAST HOUR CONTAINING A 
FINAL REQUEST FOR MILOSEVIC'S COMPLIANCE. HE ADDED THAT 
THE EU MINISTERS WOULD ISSUE ANOTHER STATEMENT LATER IN 
THE EVENING WHICH WOULD BE BRIEF AND CLEAR AND NOT LACKING 
FOR UNITY. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED THANKS. 
5. (S) THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT MEMBERS OF THE EU 
AND THE ALLIANCE HAD SEPARATE SETS OF SENSITIVITIES, 
ESPECIALLY THE TURKS. HE COMPLIMENTED THE EU ON GREAT 
SKILL AND STATESMANSHIP AND SAID WASHINGTON HOPED MILITARY 
ACTION WOULD BE RESTRICTED IN TIME SO THE PARTIES COULD 
GET BACK TO DIPLOMACY. 
6. 4.64 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RECALLED THAT HE AND PETERSEN 
HAD SPENT MUCH TIME DISCUSSING RUSSIA. THE RUSSIAN 
DIMENSION COULD BE A PROBLEM NOW. PRESIDENT CLINTON SPOKE 
WITH YELTSIN EARLIER IN THE DAY, BUT THERE WAS STILL MUCH 
WORK TO DO THERE. PETERSEN COMMENTED THAT THE FIRST 
REACTIONS FROM THE RUSSIANS WERE TOUGH. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY HOPED THE RUSSIANS BE ON THE SIDE OF THE 
ALLIANCE WHEN DIPLOMACY RESUMED. PETERSEN CONCURRED. 
7. (-g-) PETERSEN SAID HE WANTED TO DISCUSS THE OPENING FOR 
UNDP ADMINISTRATOR. UNSYG ANNAN WAS QUOTED AS SAYING THE 
U.S. OPPOSED THE DANISH CANDIDATE FOR THE POSITION. 
PETERSEN WANTED TO WHY THE U.S.'TOOK THAT STANCE AND HOPED 
IT COULD BE DISCUSSED. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED TO 
LOOK.INTO THE MATTER. 
8. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1407 EST.
