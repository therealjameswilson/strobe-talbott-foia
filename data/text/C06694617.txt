2. SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH GOC 
FOREIGN MINISTER OUELLET ON OCTOBER 27 IN WINNIPEG ON THE 
MARGINS OF THE G-7 CONFERENCE ON PARTNERSHIP FOR ECONOMIC 
TRANSFORMATION IN UKRAINE. OUELLET RAISED BOSNIA, 
EXPRESSING CONCERN ABOUT BOSNIAN SERB INTENTIONS, 
PARTICULARLY AS THE WINTER APPROACHES. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY HOPED THE BOSNIAN SERBS WOULD REALIZE TIME IS 
NOT ON THEIR SIDE AND, CONSEQUENTLY, THAT THEY WOULD BE 
INDUCED TO COME TO THE TABLE. HE THANKED OUELLET FOR 
CANADA'S EXTENSION OF ITS UNPROFOR COMMITMENT. OUELLET 
AFFIRMED THAT HE HAD DISCUSSED BOSNIA AT LENGTH WITH 
RUSSIAN FOREIGN MINISTER KOZYREV EARLIER IN THE DAY AND 
AGREED TO SEND HIS NOTES ON THE MEETING TO AMBASSADOR 
BLANCHARD FOR TRANSMISSION TO THE SECRETARY. OUELLET 
RAISED TWO POINTS ON RAITI: TOO MANY ARMS ARE FLOATING 
AROUND THE ISLAND AND FRAPH MUST BE DISMANTLED. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED SUBSTANTIAL SUCCESS OF THE WEAPONS 
BUYBACK AND CONFISCATION PROGRAMS, AGREED COMPLETELY WITH 
OUELLET ON FRAPH, AND ASSURED HIM THAT ARISTIDE AGREED. 
3. REGARDING THE OECD, OUELLET APPRECIATED THE 
SECRETARY'S SUPPORT, INDICATED GOC DETERMINATION TO PRESS 
JOHNSON'S NOMINATION, AND NOTED ITS DOMESTIC IMPORTANCE 
GIVEN THE AMMUNITION WHICH A CANADIAN FAILURE MIGHT 
PROVIDE TO QUEBEC SEPARATISTS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID 
THAT NEW, VIGOROUS LEADERSHIP IS IMPERATIVE FOR OECD AND 
ASSURED OUELLET THAT THE SECRETARY HAS TAKEN A VERY 
HANDS-ON ATTITUDE TOWARD THIS PROBLEM. HE ADDED THAT 
CANADA'S DOMESTIC SITUATION IS UNDERSTOOD. THE MEETING 
CONCLUDED WITH BRIEF DISCUSSION OF POSITIVE DEVELOPMENTS 
IN THE MIDDLE EAST; OUELLET SAID THAT KOZYREV HAD BEEN 
OPTIMISTIC REGARDING SYRIA. END SUMMARY. 
4. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH GOC FOREIGN 
MINISTER ANDRE OUELLET ON THE FRINGES OF THE G-7 
CONFERENCE ON PARTNERSHIP FOR ECONOMIC TRANSFORMATION IN 
UKRAINE, HELD ON OCTOBER 27 IN WINNIPEG. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY COMPLIMENTED OUELLET ON THE EXCELLENT 
ORGANIZATION AND EXECUTION OF THE G-7 CONFERENCE. 
OUELLET THANKED THE U.S. FOR A SIGNIFICANT CONTRIBUTION 
AND SAID KOZYREV HAD INDICATED THAT MORNING A WILLINGNESS 
TO "PLEDGE SOMETHING."
