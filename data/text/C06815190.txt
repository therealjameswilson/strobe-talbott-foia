2. (U) SUMMARY. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S SUBSTANTIVE AND
CANDID MEETING WITH FOREIGN MINISTER ERDENECHULUUN
SEPTEMBER 13 FEATURED DISCUSSION OF MONGOLIA'S ONGOING
DEMOCRATIC DEVELOPMENT, THE NEW GOVERNMENT ACTION PROGRAM,
THE IMPORTANCE OF CIVIL SOCIETY AND THE MONGOLIAN PRIME
MINISTER'S INTEREST IN VISITING THE U.S. IN THE SECOND
HALF OF FEBRUARY, FM ERENDECHULUUN EXTENDED AN INVITATION
TO DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT TO VISIT MONGOLIA. THE
FOREIGN MINISTER SOUGHT TO ASSURE US OF THE NEW
GOVERNMENT'5 COMMITMENT TO DEMOCRACY AND MARKET REFORM.
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY PRAISED MONGOLIA'S PROGRESS TO DATE
WHILE EMPHASIZING THE IMPORTANCE OF PRESS FREEDOMS. END
SUMMARY.

7. (U) RECOGNIZING THAT HE WAS ONLY LAYING A MARKER FOR
THE NEXT ADMINISTRATION, ERDENECHULUUN ASKED DEPUTY
SECRETARY TALBOTT TO CONSIDER A U.S. VISIT BY MONGOLIAN
PRIME MINISTER (PM) ENKHBAYAR IN FEBRUARY, 2001.
ENKHBAYAR IS PLANNING HIS FIRST OFFICIAL VISIT AS PM TO
TOKYO IN THE SECOND 'HALF OF FEBRUARY, AND WOULD LIKE TO
COME TO WASHINGTON TO MEET WITH USG OFFICIALS AFTER
VISITING JAPAN. ERDENECHULUUN COMMENTED THAT THIS WOULD
BE AN IMPORTANT OPPORTUNITY FOR THE PM TO DISCUSS HIS
GOVERNMENT'S REFORM EFFORTS AND PROGRESS IN IMPLEMENTING
THE GOVERNMENT ACTION PROGRAM, AS WELL AS AN OCCASION TO
ESTABLISH PERSONAL CONTACTS. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT
INDICATED THAT HE OBVIOUSLY WAS NOT IN A POSITION TO
COMMIT THE NEXT ADMINISTRATION, BUT THAT HE WOULD
CERTAINLY RECOMMEND THIS TO HIS SUCCESSOR.

8. ~DEPUTY SECRETARY,TALBOTT REFLECTED ON HIS LONG-
HELD INTEREST IN MONGOLIA AND THE DEVELOPMENT OF BILATERAL
RELATIONS IN THE EARLY STEPS TOWARD RAPPROCHEMENT IN THE
1970'S, FOLLOWED BY THE DECADE THAT SAW MONGOLIA'S
PEACEFUL DEMOCRATIC REVOLUTION AND GENUINE INDEPENDENCE.
HE STRESSED THE BENEFITS OF U.S.-MONGOLIA BILATERAL
CONSULTATIONS ON REGIONAL SECURITY ISSUES, AND PRAISED
MONGOLIA'S PARTICIPATION IN THE ASEAN REGIONAL FORUM AS A
GOOD EXAMPLE OF SUCH COOPERATION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY
SAID THAT HE LOOKED FORWARD TO STAYING IN CONTACT'WITH THE
FOREIGN MINISTER ON DEVELOPMENTS IN CHINA AND RUSSIA, AS
WELL AS IN SUPPORTING PROGRESS ON THE KOREAN PENINSULA.
ACCORD PRIORITY TO CIVIL SOCIETY AND FREEDOMS
9, (U) DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT OBSERVED THAT NEITHER
HISTORY NOR MOTHER NATURE HAVE BEEN KIND TO MONGOLIA, AND
HE EXPRESSED CONCERN FOR MONGOLIA'S RECOVERY FROM THE
1999-2000 SEVERE WINTER DISASTER. ON THE SUBJECT OF
MONGOLIA'S TRANSFORMATION OF ITS POLITICAL CULTURE, DEPUTY
SECRETARY TALBOTT EMPHASIZED THAT HE HOPED THE NEW
LEADERSHIP WOULD ACCORD THE HIGHEST PRIORITY TO CIVIL
SOCIETY, INCLUDING FREEDOM OF THE PRESS.
