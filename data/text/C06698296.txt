OPERATION PROVIDE COMFORT (OPC) 
-- WE ARE DISCUSSING IN ANKARA TURKISH,CONCERNS ABOUT 
OPC. OPC IS A SUCCESS FOR YOU AND FOR US. IT MUST BE 
RENEWED. 
-- WE ARE READY TO RESTATE OUR STRONG SUPPORT OF TURKEY 
AND FOR YOUR FIGHT AGAINST TERRORISM. WE ARE STARTING 
TALKS ON ENHANCING INTELLIGENCE SHARING. TALKS HAVE 
ALSO BEGUN BETWEEN OUR MILITARIES ON THE IMPORTANCE OF 
THE MILITARY COORDINATION COMMISSION (MCC). 
-- THE MCC MUST STAY IN ZAKHO. WE KNOW THIS IS 
DIFFICULT FOR TURKEY. WE ARE WILLING,TO INCREASE THE 
NUMBER OF TURKISH MILITARY PEOPLE THERE SO IT REMAINS 
CLEAR THAT THE MCC IS WHOLLY IN TURKEY'S INTEREST. 
-- WE LOOK FORWARD TO COORDINATING CLOSELY WITH YOU AND 
YOUR MINISTRY AS THE DATE NEARS FOR PARLIAMENT TO DEBATE 
AND VOTE ON RENEWING THE MANDATE FOR OPC. 
GREECE/AEGEAN

ANKARA, U/S TARNOFF WAS ABLE TO OBTAIN THE CONCURRENCE 
OF THE GREEK GOVERNMENT TO THE RE-INSTITUTION OF THE 
1988 ACCORDS BANNING EXERCISES IN THE AEGEAN DURING JULY 
AND AUGUST. 
IT IS IMPORTANT NOW TO BUILD ON THIS STEP. 
-- OUR GOAL, LIKE YOURS, IS TO OBTAIN THE LIFTING OF 
THE GREEK VETO ON CUSTOMS UNION ASSISTANCE FOR TURKEY AS

-- I HOPE YOU WILL CONSIDER MAKING A DEFINITIVE

LIFTING OF THE VETO WHICH WE BOTH SEEK.
