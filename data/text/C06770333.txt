1. te4.- SUMMARY: ELYSEE ADVISORS LEVITTE AND DANA MET WITH 
DEPSEC TALBOTT ON FEBRUARY 3, DISCUSSING INDIA, PAKISTAN, 
ISSUES RELATING TO THE NATO SUMMIT, PRESIDENT CHIRAC'S 
FEBRUARY 19 VISIT TO WASHINGTON, AND KOSOVO. MOST OF THE 
DISCUSSION CENTERED ON THE NATO-UNSC MANDATE ISSUE, WITH 
FRANCE FAVORING LANGUAGE FROM THE 1994 SUMMIT DECLARATION 
("UNDER THE AUTHORITY OF THE UN SECURITY COUNCIL"), TO WHICH 
THE U.S. OBJECTED BECAUSE IT WOULD SUGGEST THAT NATO IS 
SUBORDINATE TO THE UN. BOTH SIDES AGREED TO CONTINUE WORKING 
TOWARD CLOSURE ON SUMMIT-RELATED ISSUES. END SUMMARY. 
2. ELYSEE DIPLOMATIC ADVISOR JEAN-DAVID LEVITTE AND HIS 
DEPUTY FOR STRATEGIC AFFAIRS THIERRY DANA MET ON FEBRUARY 3 
WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, AMBASSADOR ROHATYN, DCM 
PEARSON, EUR DAS RON ASMUS, NSC SENIOR DIRECTOR FOR EUROPE 
ANTONY BLINKEN, D SPECIAL ASSISTANT JOHN BASS, AND EMBASSY 
NOTETAKER. THE SESSION FOLLOWED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
MEETING AND LUNCH WITH MFA OFFICIALS (SEPTEL). 
INDIA/PAKISTAN 
3. (474- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REVIEWED HIS VISITS TO INDIA AND 
PAKISTAN, AND SAID HE AND ERRERA HAD CLOSED SOME GAPS DURING 
AN EARLIER SESSION ON THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN IFI LENDING 
AND INDIAN COMMITMENTS ON CTBT (SEPTEL). LEVITTE SAID THAT 
INDIAN PRINCIPAL SECRETARY MISHRA HAD VISITED PARIS RECENTLY. 
FRANCE, LEVITTE SAID, IS PUTTING A "CERTAIN PRESSURE" ON 
INDIA AND PAKISTAN, AND IS HAPPY THAT U.S. AND FRENCH VIEWS 
ON SANCTIONS ARE CONVERGING. OUR VIEWS ON SOUTH ASIA, HE 
NOTED,' ARE NOW "MORE IN PARALLEL." 
U.S.-FRANCE 
4. EEL} THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT HE TRIED ALWAYS TO BE 
CANDID ABOUT U.S.-FRANCE PROBLEMS, NOT ALL OF WHICH HAVE BEEN 
RESOLVED. HE ALLUDED TO DIFFERENCES THE PREVIOUS WEEKEND 
CONCERNING THE KOSOVO ACTORD AT NATO. BUT THESE WERE 
OVERCOME IN PART BECAUSE OF THE CLOSE PERSONAL CONTACT 
BETWEEN SENIOR USG AND GOF OFFICIALS. LEVITTE SAID THERE HAD 
BEEN A MISUNDERSTANDING, WE IDENTIFIED IT, AND SOLVED IT. HE 
APOLOGIZED FOR FM VEDRINE'S INACCESSIBILITY; HE WAS STUCK IN 
A MEETING DIFFICULT TO LEAVE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RELAYED 
THE U.S. VIEW THAT MATTERS WERE "BACK ON TRACK" AFTER THE 
WEEKEND CONTRETEMPS.

KOSOVO/RAMBOUILLET 
5. (4)-- THE KOSOVO TALKS AT RAMBOUILLET COULD GO OFF-TRACK, 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OBSERVED, BECAUSE OF THE SERB AND 
KOSOVAR "PASSENGERS." HE COMMENTED THAT THE FRENCH AND U.S. 
SEEM TO BE APPROACHING THE NEGOTIATIONS WELL, REFERRING TO 
THE TALKS HE AND A/S GROSSMAN HAVE WITH ERRERA. LEVITTE 
AGREED, SAYING THAT THE CONTACT GROUP MECHANISM WAS 
FUNCTIONING WELL. THE LONDON CG MINISTERIAL HAD ALSO GONE 
WELL, RESPONDING TO SOME OF THE SECRETARY'S CONCERNS. RUSSIA 
HAS ALSO BEEN HELPFUL IN PRESSURING MILOSEVIC. RUSSIA DOES 
NOT WANT TO SEE NATO BOMB THE SERBS. THE BEST WAY TO AVOID 
THAT IS TO PRESS THE SERBS TO COMPLY, AND THIS HAS PRODUCED 
RESULTS, LEVITTE OBSERVED. RUSSIA IS ON BOARD WITH.US AND WE 
CAN USE THE RUSSIANS TO APPLY PRESSURE. LEVITTE ADDED THAT 
THE EUROPEANS AND AMERICANS HAD DISPLAYED UNANIMITY 
CONCERNING OBJECTIVES AND HOW TO ACHIEVE THEM CONCERNING 
KOSOVO 
6. (-Ei THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED WITH THIS ASSESSMENT. 
HE CAUTIONED THAT WORKING OUT DETAILS WOULD REMAIN 
COMPLICATED. IN BROADER TERMS, MINISTERS AND POLITICAL 
DIRECTORS ARE CLOSE TO RESOLVING QUESTIONS CONCERNING 
SEQUENCING, PROCEDURES, TRIGGERS, AND OTHER ISSUES. THESE 
ARE IMPORTANT FOR THE U.S. NATO SYG SOLANA WILL CONSULT WITH 
THE CONTACT GROUP BUT WE MUST MAKE SURE THAT THERE IS NO 
VETO. WE NEED TO MOVE FORWARD THROUGH CONSENSUS. LEVITTE 
AGREED WITH THESE POINTS. HOWEVER, WE SHOULD AVOID A 
SITUATION LIKE THE ONE IN IRAQ, WHERE UNSCOM CHIEF BUTLER 
MADE CERTAIN DECISIONS WITHOUT CONSULTING THE UN SECURITY 
COUNCIL, HE ADVISED. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THIS WAS NOT 
LIKELY, KNOWING SOLANA AS WE DO, AND HIS DEDICATION TO 
CONSENSUS. 
7. 447..x)- LEVITTE SAID THAT THERE•ARE EXTREMISTS IN KOSOVO ON 
BOTH SIDES WHO MAY FIGHT AGAINST A GOOD POLITICAL SOLUTION. 
HE NOTED THE RISK OF KOSOVAR EXTREMISM AND THE DANGER OF 
HOSTAGE-TAKING. THIS COULD CAUSE SERBS TO REJECT AN ACCORD, 
ON THE BASIS THAT IT HAD NOT RESTRAINED THE KOSOVAR SIDE. IF 
ALL THIS HAPPENS DURING THE RAMBOUILLET TALKS, THE 
NEGOTIATIONS COULD BE SCUTTLED, PRESENTING NATO WITH A TOUGH 
CHOICE. WE MUST CONSIDER SUCH A SCENARIO, LEVITTE ADVISED.

ESDI 
10. {-154- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REITERATED OUR DISSATISFACTION 
WITH THE MANNER IN WHICH ST. MALO HAD BEEN CRAFTED AND THE 
PAUCITY OF)CONSULTATIONS BEFORE, DURING AND AFTER. LEVITTE 
DESCRIBED THE LATE-NIGHT NATURE OF THE FINAL NEGOTIATIONS AND 
HOW THE RESULT HAD GONE FARTHER THAN EITHER THE FRENCH OR UK 
HAD EXPECTED. LEVITTE SAID THE ORIGINAL FRENCH TEXT WAS MORE 
EXPANSIVE THAN THE ORIGINAL UK TEXT. THROUGH A LONG 
DIALOGUE, AGREEMENT WAS REACHED AND THE RESULT WAS MORE 
AMBITIOUS AND SURPRISING THAN EITHER SIDE EXPECTED IT TO BE. 
11. 4C-4- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE HAD EXPRESSED U.S. 
CONCERNS ABOUT ESDI AT THE MFA. HE SAID THAT THE BERLIN 
FORMULA REMAINS THE U.S. TEMPLATE FOR DEVELOPMENT OF ESDI 
THE U.S. WANTS THE ESDI PROCESS TO SUCCEED. HE CAUTIONED 
THAT SOME IN THE U.S. WANT ESDI TO SUCCEED FOR THE WRONG 
REASONS -- A SUCCESSFUL ESDI WOULD JUSTIFY U.S. DIS-
ENGAGEMENT FROM EUROPE IN THEIR VIEW. 
OPEN DOOR/ENLARGEMENT 
12. --K34- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THE U.S. WAS WORKING WITH 
A FRENCH DRAFT ON OPEN DOOR ISSUES. THE U.S. MAY SOON OFFER 
ALTERNATE LANGUAGE IN ONE OR TWO PLACES. A U.S. BOTTOM LINE, 
HE STATED, IS THAT THERE BE NO PRE-DESIGNATION OF FUTURE 
MEMBERS. WE ARE MOVING IN A CERTAIN DIRECTION BUT 
CIRCUMSTANCES CHANGE AND TODAY'S PROMISING ASPIRANTS MAY NOT 
BE AS IMPRESSIVE LATER. LEVITTE RAISED THE MADRID FORMULA, 
NOTING THAT SLOVENIA AND ROMANIA BELIEVED THEY HAVE REACHED A 
CERTAIN POINT. PULLING BACK WOULD BE UNFORTUNATE. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT ROMANIA WAS A DIFFICULT ISSUE --
THE ROMANIAN POSITION HAS DETERIORATED. HE SAID THAT THE 
U.S. UNDERSTOOD THE FRENCH POINT ABOUT NOT RETREATING FROM 
MADRID AND IS SEARCHING FOR SUITABLE LANGUAGE THAT ALSO 
MAINTAINS THE CREDIBILITY OF THE ENLARGEMENT PROCESS.

THE MFA ON MANDATES AND THE U.S. DISCOMFORT .WITH THE WORD 
"AUTHORITY" AS I'T APPEARS IN 1994 BRUSSELS SUMMIT LANGUAGE, 
WHICH THE FRENCH FAVOR. LEVITTE RESTATED FRENCH SUPPORT FOR 
THE 1994 LANGUAGE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT THE 
ORIGINAL TREATY OF WASHINGTON LANGUAGE ("PRINCIPLES AND 
PURPOSES OF THE UN") MIGHT BE APPROPRIATE. 
14. 4-eor- LEVITTE ASKED WHY THE U.S. RISKED OPENING A "BITTER 
DIALOGUE" ON THIS MATTER. THE 1994 SUMMIT LANGUAGE IS 
SUITABLE. IF WE GO BEYOND THAT, THE ISSUE BECOMES VERY 
DIFFICULT. HE PREDICTED WE WOULD NOT SUCCEED IN FINDING A 
BETTER FORMULA. HE SAID THAT FRANCE HAD ALREADY INDICATED A 
WILLINGNESS TO PROCEED CASE-BY-CASE, AND HAD DONE SO IN.
KOSOVO AND COULD DO SO AGAIN. 
15. -17E4 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT "AUTHORITY" AS USED 
IN 1994 IMPLIES SUBORDINATION, THAT NATO WOULD BE SUBORDINATE 
TO THE UN SECURITY COUNCIL. THAT WOULD CROSS A BASIC U.S. 
RED LINE, HE STRESSED. MOREOVER, THE 1994 LANGUAGE WAS 
INTENDED TO BE BOSNIA-SPECIFIC. IT SHOULD NOT BE VIEWED AS A 
"CANONICAL TEXT." THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE WAS CONFIDENT 
THAT WE CAN FIND WORDS TO PRESERVE THE FRENCH PRINCIPLE AS 
WELL AS AVOID U.S. CONCERNS. LEVITTE SAID HE "SUSPECTED THAT 
THIS WILL BE DIFFICULT." THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED BUT 
SAID THAT WE CAN OVERCOME DIFFICULTIES. THE ISSUE IS 
IMPORTANT -- WE CAN ALL IMAGINE SCENARIOS WHERE AN ILL-
INTENDED ASSERTION OF NATO'S SUBORDINATE STATUS COULD 
PARALYZE THE ALLIANCE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY COMMENTED THAT 
IF THE RAMBOUILLET TALKS ARE SUCCESSFUL, THIS dOULD EASE THE 
WAY FOR THE MANDATE ISSUE AT THE SUMMIT.

SHOULD PAY ATTENTION TO THE UNSC IMPLICATIONS OF KOSOVO AND 
IRAQ. THE PRC FM SAID THAT IF FRANCE THOUGHT IT COULD "GET 
AWAY FROM THE UNSC, CHINA WILL HAVE TO THINK ABOUT HOW IT 
VIEWS THE INTERNATIONAL ARCHITECTURE." LEVITTE SAID THAT WE 
MUST PREVENT CHINA FROM DOING THIS.

UNSC MEMBERS. WE ARE COVERED AS FAR AS THE UN IS CONCERNED.

CHINESE WILL NOT EXERCISE THEIR VETO POWER. THE DEPUTY
