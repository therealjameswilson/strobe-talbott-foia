SUBJECT: NSC DEPUTY DISCUSSES TAIWAN, WTO, WITH SHANGHAI

CLASSIFIED BY HENRY A. LEVINE, CONSUL GENERAL. REASON 1.5(B). RELEASEIN FULL
l. ~iSUMMARY: SHANGHAI ACADEMICS TOLD NSC DEPUTY STEINBERG
THAT DEPSEC TALBOTT'S DELEGATION'S U.S.-CHINA POLICY DIALOGUE
WAS AN IMPORTANT EXERCISE IN CRISIS PREVENTION AND A STRENGTH TO
THE BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP. TAIWAN REMAINS A GREAT CONCERN,
STEINBERG URGED THEM TO SEE THE ELECTION OF A NEW TAIWAN
PRESIDENT AS AN OPPORTUNITY AND TO TAKE A MORE SELF-CONFIDENT
VIEW TOWARD POSSIBLE MOVES TOWARD TAIWAN INDEPENDENCE--IF THE
REST OF THE WORLD WERE TO IGNORE SUCH DECLARATIONS, WHY NEED
CHINA TAKE ACTION? THE U.S. COULD NOT STAND BY IF MILITARY
FORCE WAS USED. CHINA'S ENTRY INTO WTO SHOULD LEAD TO INCREASED
NETWORKS OF INTERNATIONAL TIES, AND A STRONGER CHINA--WITH A
GREATER STAKE IN GLOBAL STABILITY--WOULD BE WELCOMED BY THE U.S.
END SUMMARY.
DECLASSIFIED'.
~ DEPUTY NSC ADVISER JAMES STEINBERG, EAP DAS SHIRK, AND
PARTY MET WITH INFLUENTIAL SHANGHAI ACADEMICS (PARTICIPANTS
LISTED LAST PARAGRAPH) ON FEBRUARY 21 FOR AN OPEN DISCUSSION OF
BILATERAL ISSUES.
TALBOTT, SLOCOMBE, STEINBERG VISIT VALUABLE
3. ~ THE FEBRUARY 16-18 VISIT OF DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT,
UNDERSECRETARY SLOCOMBE AND NSC.DEPUTY STEINBERG TO CHINA WAS
WELL-TIMED AND SERVED AS AN EXERCISE IN CRISIS PREVENTION, IN
THE ACADEMICS'IEW. THEY OUTLINED SEVERAL PROBLEMS WHICH STILL
BEDEVIL THE RELATIONSHIP: THE PERCEPTION OF CHINESE TECHNOLOGY
THEFT, HUMAN RIGHTS, TAIWAN, TMD, AND TRADE IMBALANCES; BUT
POINT OUT THAT THE VISIT DEMONSTRATED THAT DESPITE OUR
DIFFERENCES WE CAN STILL MAINTAIN A DIALOGUE. THE TIMING WAS
PARTICULARLY VALUABLE IN LIGHT OF THE. UPCOMING TAIWAN ELECTIONS.
STEINBERG APPRECIATED THAT PERCEPTION AND SAID THAT HE HOPED THE
CHINESE LEADERSHIP ALSO SAW THE VISIT IN THAT LIGHT.
NEED TAIWAN HAVE POTENTIAL TO UNDERMINE THE U.S.-CHINA BILATERAL
RELATIONSHIP?
4. ~ THE ACADEMICS STRESSED THAT TAIWAN REMAINS THE MOST
SERIOUS POTENTIAL PROBLEM IN THE U.S.-CHINA RELATIONSHIP. THEY
FEAR THAT LEE TENG-HUI WILL REMAIN INFLUENTIAL FOLLOWING THE
ELECTION AND THAT THE PERIOD BETWEEN THE ELECTION AND THE MAY 20
INAUGURATION WILL BE A CRITICAL PERIOD. LEE, BY VIRTUE OF HIS
LEADERSHIP OF THE KMT WILL REMAIN IN CONTROL OF THE NATIONAL
CONGRESS AND THE POSSIBILITY OF REVISING THE CONSTITUTION TO
INCLUDE THE TWO-STATE THEORY. SUCH CONSTITUTIONAL REVISION
WOULD "DESTROY THE LEGAL BASIS" FOR REUNIFICATION. TAIWAN IS
ALSO CONSIDERING THE DEVELOPMENT OF LONG-RANGE "OFFENSIVE"
MISSILES. THUS, WHILE CHINA IS TAKING A WAIT-AND-SEE ATTITUDE
TOWARD TAIWAN'S PRESIDENTIAL ELECTION, IT MUST PREPARE FOR A
WORST CASE SCENARIO.
5. +) STEINBERG EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT SUCH AN ATTITUDE AND
ASKED WHETHER CHINA'S INTERESTS WOULD BE ADVANCED IF MILITARY
ACTION WERE TAKEN AGAINST TAIWAN. HE NOTED THAT ALMOST ALL
COUNTRIES NOW SUPPORT ONE FORM OR ANOTHER OF "ONE CHINA, " BUT

STRAIT RELATIONS MAY WELL BE A FUNCTION OF THE PATIENCE THAT
CHINA HAS SHOWN SINCE LAST JULY'S TWO-STATE ANNOUNCEMENT. THE
ACADEMICS RESPONDED THAT IF THE CHINESE LEADERSHIP WEPE TO BE
PERCEIVED AS HAVING "LOST" TAIWAN, THE DOMESTIC POLITICAL FALL-

6. +6+ STEINBERG, DAS SHIRK AND NSC ASIA DIRECTOR KEITH PRESSED
THE ACADEMICS To THINK, THROUGH THE CONSEQUENCES OF ANY MILITARY
RESPONSE TO TAIWAN MOVES TOWARD INDEPENDENCE. STEINBERG ASSURED
THE GROUP THAT THE U,S. WOULD DO WHAT IT COULD TO PROMOTE A
CONSTRUCTIVE ATMOSPHERE IN TAIWAN. NEVERTHELESS,'HINESE
MILITARY ACTION AGAINST TAIWAN IS AN ABSOLUTE "RES LINE" FOR THE
U.S. IN ITS APPROACH TO THE CROSS-STRAIT SITUATION. AS A METHOD
OF PROMPTING DISCUSSION, STEINBERG ASKED THE GROUP TO CONSIDER
THAT'IF TAIWAN WERE TO DECLARE INDEPENDENCE BUT THE U.S., THE
EU, JAPAN AND THE REST OF THE WORLD WERE TO SIMPLY IGNORE THE
I
DECLARATIONS WHY NEED CHINA FEEL FORCED TO TAKE ACTION? HOW
WOULD THE WORLD HAVE CHANGED? IN RESPONSE, ONE ACADEMIC
SUGGESTED THAT A RESPONSE COULD BE THAT CHINA BACK OFF FROM ITS
POLICY OF PEACEFUL REUNIFICATION AND SAY THAT IT RETAINS THE
RIGHT TO ACHIEVE REUNIFICATION BY ANY MEANS--BUT SET NO
TIMETABLE FOR WHEN MILITARY ACTION MIGHT BE TAKEN./
7. ~ STEINBERG PAINTED FOR THE ACADEMICS THE U.S. VISION OF A
U.S.-CHINA RELATIONSHIP THAT IS SO STRONG THAT ACTION BY A THIRD
PARTY, EVEN TAIWAN, WOULD NOT UNDERMINE IT. 'HE NOTED THAT WE
CONTINUALLY HAVE DISPUTES WITH THE EU, OVER BANANAS OR BEEF
HORMONES, BUT THERE IS NO QUESTION OF THE UNDERLYING STRENGTH OF
THE U.S.-EU RELATIONSHIP.
CHINA: A GROWING, RESPONSIBLE INTERNATIONAL PLAYER?
8. ~.THE ACADEMICS WERE ASKED ABOUT CHINA'S INTERNATIONAL ROLE
IN THE FUTURE. THEY COMMENTED THAT ENTRY INTO WTO IS AN
IMPORTANT STEP TOWARD GREATER INTERNATIONAL INVOLVEMENT IN,,AND
RESPONSIBILITY FOR, INTERNATIONAL AFFAIRS. ALTHOUGH STILL ONLY
A "REGIONAL" POWER, THEY SEE CHINA IN 20-30 YEARS BECOMING MORE
DEMOCPATIC AND A FORCE FOR PEACE AND STABILITY IN THE WORLD.
STILL, CHINA WILL NEVER BECOME AN "INTERVENING" WORLD POWER.
9. ~ STEINBERG RESPONDED THAT IT SHOULD BE CLEAR THAT THE U.S.
HAS NO TERRITORIAL DESIGNS BEYOND ITS BORDERS AND THAT FOREIGN
INTERVENTIONS HAVE BEEN TO MAINTAIN FUNDAMENTAL,
INTERNATIONALLY ACCEPTED'ALUES KEEPING MOLOSIVEC FROM
WANTONLY ENGAGING IN MURDEROUS BEHAVIOR IN KOSOVO IS ONE
EXAMPLE. SHEN DINGLI SAID THAT HE HOPED CHINA WILL INCREASINGLY
SEE THE VALUE IN INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION TO UPHOLD IMPORTANT
VALUES. HE SAID HE THOUGHT CHINA WOULD NEVER UNDERTAKE
INTERVENTION LIKE THE U.S. INVASION OF PANAMA AND ARREST OF
NORIEGA. HE THEN CITED THE 1994 "YINHE" INCIDENT, SAYING THAT
WHILE HE HAS BEEN DISTURBED BY THE OUTCOME OF'HAT EVENT, IN THE
FUTURE HE HOPED CHINA COULD BE "HEGEMONIST" IN THIS SAME WAY.
HE HOPED THAT CHINA WOULD WORK WITH OTHER COUNTRIES TO TAKE
ACTIONS SUCH AS THE YINHEc EVEN IF CHINA'S INTERESTS WERE NOT
DIRECTLY AFFECTED BECAUSE, HE SAID, WITHOUT SUCH ACTIONS "THERE
ARE NO NORMS." STEINBERG SAID THAT THE U.S. SUPPORTS THE
DEVELOPMENT OF A SELF-CONFIDENT CHINA ENGAGED IN THE
INTERNATIONAL SYSTEM. THOUGH ACCUSED OF BEING HEGEMONIST, THE
U.S. IN FACT WELCOMES A STRONG CHINA WITH GROWING INTERESTS IN
INTERNATIONAL STABILITY. GREATER CHINESE SELF-CONFIDENCE WOULD

WTO: KEY TO REFORM?
10. ~ THE ACADEMICS STRESSED THE KEY ROLE WTO ENTRY WILL PLAY
IN SPURRING CHINA TO UNDERTAKE NEEDED REFORMS OF ITS ECONOMIC
SYSTEM. "IT IS A TEST OF WHETHER WE CAN BE A REAL MEMBER OF THE
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY." FURTHERMORE, PARTICIPATION IN THE WTO
WILL LEAD TO AN EVER-GROWING NETWORK OF ECONOMIC TIES THAT WILL
BIND CHINA AND THE REST OF THE WORLD~ INCLUDING TAIWAN ~ TOGETHER
AND THEREBY BE A FORCE FOR STABILITY.
11. ~ STEINBERG ASKED HOW REFORM MIGHT FAIL. THEY CITED FOUR
POTENTIAL CRITICAL WEAKNESSES: FAILURE OF THE LEGAL SYSTEM,
UNMANAGEABLE UNEMPLOYMENT, UNTENABLE INCOME DISPARITIES (BOTH
AMONG INCOME QUINTILES AND COASTAL'NLAND AREAS)c AND COLLAPSE
OF THE FINANCIAL SYSTEM. THEY PROMISED "BIG CHANGES" IN THE
FINANCIAL SYSTEM, BUT CAUTIONED THAT IT WILL TAKE 5-10 YEARS FOR
THE NECESSARY REFORMS TO TAKE HOLD. THEY ALSO EXPRESSED CONCERN
THAT THE U.S. "BUBBLE" ECONOMY COULD BURST CAUSING A SEVERE BLOW
TO CHINA'S EXPORTS. STEINBERG ACKNOWLEDGED THAT RISK/ BUT NOTED
THAT AS ASIAN COUNTRIES BOUNCE BACK FROM THE ECONOMIC CRISIS,
THE INTERNATIONAL ECONOMY WILL BECOME LESS VULNERABLE TO A
DOWNTURN IN THE U.S. "THE BEST THING FOR BOTH CHINA AND THE
U.S.,"HE OFFERED, "WOULD BE A RESURGENCE OF GROWTH IN JAPAN."
1 2 +E+ ON THE BRIGHT S IDE ~ THE ACADEMICS NOTED THAT CHINA HAS
PRETTY WELL MOVED BEYOND A PREOCCUPATION WITH FEEDING AND
CLOTHING ITS 1.2 BILLION PEOPLE AND THE CHINESE, AT LEAST IN
SHANGHAI AND OTHER COASTAL AREAS, ARE INCREASINGLY ANXIOUS TO
BUY CARS, HOUSES AND COMPUTERS. STEINBERG OBSERVED THAT THE
MULTIPLIER EFFECTS OF SUCH A CONSUMER MOVEMENT COULD FUEL
CHINA'S GROWTH FOR YEARS TO COME.
NEW FACES IN CHINESE LEADERSHIP?
13. (~ IN THE ABSENCE OF REAL DEMOCRACY, IT IS UNLIKELY THERE
WILL BE SURPRISES IN CHINA'S LEADERSHIP, THE ACADEMICS OBSERVED.
IT IS A LOT EASIER TO PREDICT THE NEXT LEADERS IN CHINA THAN IT
IS IN THE U.S. OR TAIWAN, THEY POINTED OUT WITH A CHUCKLE. THE
NEXT (FOURTH) GENERATION LEADERSHIP WILL STILL BE MADE UP OF
TECHNICIANS--MORE OF A GLOBAL ORIENTATION, BUT STILL WITHOUT A
BROAD EDUCATION. WITH THE FIFTH GENERATION, HOWEVER, CHINA'S
LEADERSHIP WILL UNDERGO REAL CHANGE WHEN THOSE THAT HAVE BEEN
EDUCATED IN THE U.S. AND EUROPE TAKE THE REINS.
CAN CHINESE STUDENT ATTITUDES TOWARD THE U.S. BE SALVAGED?
14.~CHINESE STUDENTS ARE INCREASINGLY LOOKING FOR NEWS AND
INFORMATION BEYOND THE "PARTY LINE," BUT THEY REMAIN SHALLOW IN
TERMS OF REAL UNDERSTANDING AND ARE STILL "EMOTIONAL, " ACCORDING
TO THE ACADEMICS. SINCE THE BELGRADE BOMBING, STUDENT
PERCEPTIONS OF THE U.S. HAVE REMAINED DECIDEDLY NEGATIVE--A FACT
THAT REFLECTS BOTH THEIR UNWILLINGNESS TO ACCEPT U.S.
EXPLANATIONS AND THE UNREALISTIC PERCEPTIONS THEY HAD OF THE
U.S. PRIOR TO THE BOMBING. THE ACADEMICS STRESSED THE NEED FOR
CONTINUING, AND MORE SOPHISTICATED, EDUCATION EFFORTS AND FOR
STRENGTHENING U.S.-CHINA MEDIA EXCHANGE PROGRAMS. STEINBERG
NOTED THAT U.S. LEADERS HAVE RECENTLY SPOKEN OUT ON THE
IMPORTANCE, OF U.S.-CHINA RELATIONS AND ASKED IF CHINESE LEADERS
COULD NOT DO THE SAME. THE ACADEMICS SAID THAT DESPITE THEIR
NEGATIVE ATTITUDES, CHINESE STUDENTS STILL WANT TO STUDY IN THE
U.S. AND STILL RETAIN THE AMERICAN DREAM.
