11/07/94 02:25 pm Page: 1 
2. WHEN I SAW SHAMUYARIRA AND CHIGWEDERE THE 
MORNING OF NOVEMBER 5 (ON ANGOLA),. THEY WANTED TO 
TALK ABOUT HOW THE USG COULD CONCRETELY HELP 
ZIMBABWE/THE FLS BEEF-UP THEIR PEACEKEEPING/MAKING 
CAPABILITIES AS DISCUSSED DURING THE DEPSECtS 
RECENT VISIT. 
3. SHAMUYARIRA OBSERVED WHAT A GROWTH BUSINESS 
PEACEKEEPING IS, NOTING ZIMBABWE'S ACTIVITIES ON 
MOZAMBIQUE, LESOTHO AND NOW ANGOLA IN JUST THE, 
PAST TWO WEEKS. IN SPECIFIC TERMS, HE SAID THE 
GOZ NEEDED A "SMALL" JET TO SUPPORT "ENVOYS" ON 
SHORT-NOTICE TRIPS (AN AIR ZIMBABWE PLANE HAD TO 
BE TAKEN OFF A COMMERCIAL ROUTE TO SUPPORT 
MUGABE'S TRIP TO PRETORIA LAST WEEK FOR THE 
LESOTHO TROIKA MEETING). I EXPRESSED DOUBTS ABOUT 
A DEDICATED AIRCRAFT AND SUGGESTED THE GOZ LOOK AT 
CHARTERING AIRCRAFT AS NEEDED WITHIN THE REGION. 
4. SHAMUYARIRA WENT ON TO SAY HE AND DEFMIN 
MAHACHI ARE BOTH KEEN ON INCREASING ZIMBABWE'S 
(AND THE FLS') CAPACITY FOR PEACEKEEPING 
OPERATIONS. THE MINISTRY OF DEFENSE HAS BEEN 
ASKED TO "QUANTIFY" WHAT IT NEEDS. I OBSERVED 
THAT THE ZNA STAFF COLLEGE PROVIDED A GOOD BASIS 
AS IT ALREADY INCLUDED PEACEKEEPING IN ITS 
CURRICULUM AND HAD AS STUDENTS OFFICERS FROM OTHER 
FLS. SHAMUYARIRA RESPONDED ASKING IF WE COULD 
PROVIDE AN EXPERT TO ANALYZE AND RECOMMEND HOW THE 
STAFF COLLEGE, THE MOD AND HIS MINISTRY COULD BE 
ENHANCED TO DEAL WITH PEACEKEEPING. 
5. I DON'T KNOW WHETHER WE CAN HELP ON EITHER 
REQUEST, BUT, HAVING ENCOURAGED THE ZIMBABWEANS/FLS 
TO PLAY A MORE ACTIVE ROLE, WE NEED TO GIVE THEM 
AN ANSWER. IS THERE ANY MONEY IN THE OAU 
PEACEKEEPING ACCOUNT THAT COULD BE CHANNELED THIS 
WAY? LANPHER
