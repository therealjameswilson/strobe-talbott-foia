2. -4-e-t— SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET FOR 45 MINUTES LATE 
ON MARCH 6 WITH CHUBAYS. THE DEPSEC PROVIDED AN OVERVIEW OF THE 
DAY'S DISCUSSIONS ON ARMS CONTROL (START ABM/TMD 
DEMARCATION) AND NATO-RUSSIAN RELATIONS. THE DEPSEC CHARACTERIZED 
THE TALKS AS PROMISING, AND ON TRACK FOR A PRODUCTIVE SUMMIT IN - 
HELSINKI. CHUBAYS RAISED THE ECONOMIC DIMENSION, PARTICULARLY 
RUSSIAN MEMBERSHIP IN WESTERN ORGANIZATIONS AS "COMPENSATION" FOR-
NATO ENLARGEMENT. THE DEPSEC TOOK ISSUE WITH THE NOTION OF 
"COMPENSATION," SAYING THAT THE U.S. WAS COMMITTED TO BRINGING - 
RUSSIA CLOSER TO THESE ORGANIZATIONS ON THE MERITS AND REGARDLESS 
OF THE OUTCOME OF THE NATO-RUSSIA DISCUSSIONS. THE DEPSEC ALSO 
TOLD CHUBAYS THAT A SENIOR ECONOMIC TEAM WOULD BE VISITING MOSCOW 
TO REACH CLOSURE ON AN ECONOMIC STATEMENT FOR THE SUMMIT, AND TO 
MAKE SUBSTANTIVE PROGRESS ON OUR BILATERAL ECONOMIC AGENDA -- AN 
AGENDA TO WHICH WE ATTACHED GREAT IMPORTANCE AND WHICH WE 
EMPHASIZED IN CONTACTS WITH THIRD COUNTRIES. END SUMMARY
