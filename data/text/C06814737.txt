1. SUMMARY: IN AN APRIL 22 MEETING WITH THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY, TAJIKISTAN'S PRESIDENT IMOMALI RAHMONOV 
ANNOUNCED HIS INTENTION TO OPEN AN EMBASSY IN 
WASHINGTON BY SEPTEMBER OF THIS YEAR. HE EXPRESSED HIS 
REGRET THAT OPERATIONS AT THE U.S. EMBASSY IN DUSHANBE 
HAD BEEN CLOSED DOWN, AND SAID HOW IMPORTANT IT WOULD 
BE TO HAVE THE U.S. AMBASSADOR BACK AS TAJIKISTAN 
CARRIES OUT ITS REFERENDUM AND UPCOMING ELECTIONS.. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED SUPPORT FOR GETTING 
AMBASSADOR FINN BACK INTO THE COUNTRY IN WHICH HE IS 
ACCREDITED AS SOON Aa POSSIBLE. RAHMONOV ASKED FOR 
ASSISTANCE IN FIGHTING NARCOTICS TRAFFICKING AND FOR 
USG POLITICAL SUPPORT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REMINDED 
THE PRESIDENT THAT THE USG'S ABILITY TO CARRY OUT 
BILATERAL RELATIONS WOULD DEPEND ON PROGRESS IN THE 
PEACE PROCESS. HE URGED RAHMONOV TO KEEP UP THE 
MOMENTUM. END SUMMARY. 
RAHMONOV REGRETS ABSENCE OF U.S. EMBASSY

POLITICAL SIGNAL.
