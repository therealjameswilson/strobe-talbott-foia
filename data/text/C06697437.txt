5. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE UNDERSTOOD THE GOA 
AND ITS MERCOSUL PARTNERS WOULD SEND A DELEGATION TO 
ASUNCION SHORTLY. HE OFFERED HIS ASSISTANCE AND 
PROMISED THAT THE USG WOULD DO EVERYTHING POSSIBLE TO 
SUPPORT THE MERCOSUL INITIATIVE. 
6. -RH- DEPUTY FM PETRELLA CONFIRMED THAT FOREIGN 
MINISTER DI TELLA HAS LEAVING BUENOS HIRES FOR ASUNCION 
AT 1500 HOURS (LOCAL TIME) AND WOULD BE JOINED BY THE 
FOREIGN MINISTER OF URUGUAY AND THE DEPUTY FOREIGN 
MINISTER OF BRAZIL. HE SAID HE HOPED THE ASUNCION 
DIPLOMATIC CORPS WOULD SUPPORT THE EFFORT AND ASKED THAT 
AMBASSADOR SERVICE BE AT THE AIRPORT WHEN THE DELEGATION 
ARRIVES. 
7 -4S4-- THE ACTING SECRETARY AGREED, AND EXPRESSED HIS 
HOPE THAT IF AN INTERRUPTION IN THE DEMOCRATIC PROCESS 
OCCURRED, MERCOSUL WOULD ISOLATE PARAGUAY BY EXPELLING 
OR SUSPENDING IT FROM THE GROUP. 
8. —(-8-)- PETRELLA STATED MERCOSUL HAD ALREADY ISSUED A 
COMMUNIQUE MONDAY EVENING DECLARING THAT PARAGUAY'S 
PARTICIPATION IN MERCOSUL WOULD BE UNSUSTAINABLE IF 
THERE WERE ANY BREAK IN DEMOCRACY IN ASUNCION. 
9. —(-31- THE ACTING SECRETARY EXPRESSED HIS AGREEMENT 
AND MENTIONED THAT AMBASSADOR CHEEK WOULD STAY IN CLOSE 
TOUCH WITH THE FOREIGN MINISTRY. HE AGAIN OFFERED USG 
ASSISTANCE TO SUPPORT OR COORDINATE EFFORTS IF NEEDED. 
HE PROMISED TO CONSULT WITH THE GOA REGARDING FUTURE USG 
EFFORTS TO RESOLVE THE CONFLICT. HE NOTED THE USG WAS 
SUSPENDING MILITARY-TO-MILITARY ASSISTANCE AND WAS 
CONSIDERING THE IDEA OF NAMING A PRESIDENTIAL OR 
MILITARY ENVOY TO BE DISPATCHED TO ASUNCION. THE USG 
WOULD WAIT, HOWEVER, UNTIL THE MERCOSUL DELEGATION HAD 
AN OPPORTUNITY TO PURSUE ITS INITIATIVE. 
10. —(Q4- PETRELLA AGREED THAT MERCOSUL SHOULD TAKE THE 
LEAD FOR NOW. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE WOULD 
DELIVER A VERY TOUGH STATEMENT TO A MEETING OF THE OAS 
PERMREPS THAT AFTERNOON. PETRELLA SAID THE ARGENTINE 
REPRESENTATIVE HAD INSTRUCTIONS TO BE VERY VOCAL AGAINST 
ANY THREAT TO DEMOCRACY IN PARAGUAY. 
11. -RH- THE ACTING SECRETARY ASKED PETRELLA TO CONVEY 
THE SECRETARY'S GREETINGS TO FM DI TELLA, IN ADDITION TO 
HIS OWN. 
12. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1107 EDT. 
13. END TEXT.
