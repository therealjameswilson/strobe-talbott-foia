Dear Mr. Ambassador: 
RELEASE IN PART 
B6 
Just prior to my trip earlier this month you sent me a 
very warm invitation to visit Latvia. On this most recent 
trip, I made a brief visit to Tallinn, but I am certainly 
interested in taking you up on your invitation on one of 
my next journeys to the region. 
I have kept up a very active interest in the Baltics, 
and the question of Russian troop withdrawals is always on 
my agenda in my discussions with Russian officials. It is 
hard for me now to give you an idea about the timing of 
such a visit, but I will be sure to get in contact with 
you once my travel plans become more definitive. I would 
also be grateful if you could pass along my regards to 
President Gorbunovs, whom I have not seen again since the 
Chautauqua Conference. 
Sincerely,
B6 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador At Large and ' 
Specia-1 Advisor to the Secretary 
on the New Independent States
