THE VICE PRESIDENT: I AGREE THAT THE DIALOGUE BETWEEN THE UNITED STATES AND RUSSIA AND BETWEEN NATO AND RUSSIA MUST ADOPT GREATER INTENSITY AND ENGAGE IN DISCUSSION OF ALL THE DETAILS SO THAT THERE IS A FULL UNDERSTANDING. I HAVE FELT FROM THE BEGINNING THAT THE QUESTION OF PARALLELISM WAS IMPORTANT. WHAT I MEAN IS THAT THE NATO-RUSSIA RELATIONSHIP MUST BE DEEPENED -- AS MUST THE U.S.-RUSSIA RELATIONSHIP -- AS ENLARGEMENT MOVES FORWARD. THIS IS DECEMBER, BUT WE ONLY KNOW ABOUT NATO ENLARGEMENT. I DO NOT THINK WE WILL BE ABLE TO DO ANYTHING ON THIS.

THE VICE PRESIDENT: FIRST, THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN PARALLELISM AND SEQUENTIAL DEVELOPMENT IS A MATTER ON WHICH WE DISAGREE. I BELIEVE THAT THE PARALLEL APPROACH IS THE BEST. IF WE PROCEED SEQUENTIALLY, THAT COULD GIVE RISE TO A VETO IN EFFECT. AN INABILITY TO COME TO MUTUALLY ACCEPTABLE RESOLUTION ON THE NATO-RUSSIA RELATIONSHIP MIGHT APPEAR TO 
STOP NATO ENLARGEMENT. THE MORE IMPORTANT POINT FOR ME IS THE ONE RAISED WHEN YOU ASKED. WHY NATO SHOULD ENLARGE. YOU AND I, PRESIDENTS CLINTON AND YELTSIN, HAVE DISCUSSED THIS. THE PROSPECT OF INSTABILITY IN EASTERN OR CENTRAL EUROPE IS-A CAUSE FOR CONCERN FOR ALL OF EUROPE. THERE IS A FEAR IN THESE PEOPLE BORN OF THEIR BITTER EXPERIENCE OF THIS CENTURY. THERE IS A STRONG DESIRE TO BELONG TO NATO. IT HAS NOTHING TO DO WITH HOSTILITY TOWARD RUSSIA. IT IS DUE TO A DESIRE TO. ACHIEVE THE PEACE AND STABILITY THAT NATO HAS HAD FOR 47 YEARS. IN THEORY, WE DO NOT EXCLUDE RUSSIA FROM JOINING NATO. THIS STRIKES SOME AS AN ABSURDITY, BUT NOT US AS THE PURPOSE OF NATO ENLARGEMENT IS TO PROMOTE STABILITY, PEACE AND CONFIDENCE. THE DESIRE OF NATIONS TO ACHIEVE THIS STABILITY IS THE REASON FOR THEIR DESIRE TO JOIN NATO. 
PM CHERNOMYRDIN: WE WILL-NOT PROHIBIT ANYONE FROM JOINING.NATO. WE HAVE NO RIGHT TO A VETO. BUT WHEN WE BEGIN DISCUSSIONS, THOSE WHO WANT TO JOIN NATO EXPLAIN WHY -- THEY TALK OF 'PEACE AND STABfLITY, BUT WE IN RUSSIA UNDERSTAND THAT THEY SEE THE THREAT AS RUSSIA. POLAND THINKS IT MIGHT BE THREATENED BY RUSSIA. WHO DO THEY SEE AS A THREAT -- SWEDEN? HUNGARY? THE POLES ARE SEEKING SECURITY GUARANTEES AGAINST SOMEONE, AND WE CANNOT EXPLAIN THIS. LET•US COOPERATE ON THIS OR BE SWEPT ASICE.

THE VICE PRESIDENT: LET US DO THIS INTENSELY AND IN PARALLEL. WE NEED TO DO IT IN A WAY THAT MAKES CLEAR TO RUSSIA AND EUROPE THAT NATO IS NOT AGAINST RUSSIA. THE PURPOSE OF THOSE COUNTRIES SEEKING TO JOIN IS NOT FEAR OF RUSSIA OR A DESIRE TO ARRAY MILITARY FORCE AGAINST RUSSIA, BUT TO ESTABLISH THE CONFIDENCE AND STABILITY THAT COMES'WITH BEING PART OF THE ALLIANCE. THIS IS A NEW ERA -- THERE ARE NO ENEMIES AND NO COLD WAR. DURING THE GREAT DEPRESSION, PRESIDENT ROOSEVELT SAID THAT THERE WAS NOTHING TO FEAR EXCEPT FEAR ITSELF. IN THE EUROPEAN CONTEXT, THE FEAR OF INSTABILITY CAN TAKE ON A LIFE OF ITS OWN. BUT ALLIANCE MEMBERSHIP CAN GIVE THEM STABILITY AGAINST CENTRIFUGAL FORCES. OR THE TENSIONS THAT ARISE WHEN DIFFERENT ETHNIC GROUPS LIVE ACROSS BORDERS. PARTICIPATION IN THE ALLIANCE WOULD PROVIDE CONFIDENCE TO 
WITHSTAND SUCH PRESSURES. THE PROCESS OVER THE NEXT SIX 
MONTHS BETWEEN YOU AND ME, BETWEEN PRESIDENTS CLINTON AND YELTSIN, AND BETWEEN RUSSIA AND NATO MUST BE AN INTENSE, SINCERE EFFORT TO RESOLVE PROBLEMS AND BUILD CONFIDENCE,IN A WAY THAT IS GOOD FOR RUSSIA AND EUROPE. 
PM CHERNOMYRDIN: I AGREE. WE CANNOT AVOID THE ISSUE. FOR THE UNITED STATES, THERE IS AN IDEAL FORMULA. BUT FOR RUSSIA AND EUROPE, IT MAKES US WARY. RUSSIA MIGHT UNDERSTAND YOU AND YOUR IDEAS, BUT THE POLES SEE NATO AS A COUNTERBALANCE FOR RUSSIA AND THE WARSAW PACT. FOR YEARS THERE WAS A NATO-WARSAW PACT BALANCE, BUT NOW THE WARSAW PACT IS GONE. NATO REMAINS, AND ITS GOALS AND PURPOSES ARE THE SAME. YOU CANNOT EXPLAIN THIS TO THE RUSSIANS. THEY.HAVE LONG MEMORIES, AND THEY'HAVE NOT YET BURIED THEIR COLD WAR . MEMORIES. THIS WILL BE OF CONCERN IN RUSSIA. WE WILL WORK WITH YOU AND NATO, AND I AM CONFIDENT WE WILL BE ABLE TO FIND SOMETHING. WE MUST WORK OUT SOMETHING. IT IS MUCH BETTER TO 
HAVE PEACE WITH RUSSIA AND A WORRIED POLAND THAN PEACE WITH POLAND AND A WORRIED RUSSIA. WE ARE DISCUSSING THIS WITH THE FRENCH AND GERMANS. THEY SAY THERE IS NO DANGER SINCE THEY ARE IN NATO BUT THEN POINT TO THE RUSSIANS AS A WORRY. THAT IS 
OUR CONCERN. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: YOU HAVE MADE AN IMPORTANT AND POWERFUL STATEMENT. THE DISCUSSION OF THE INTERNAL ADAPTATION OF NATO SHOULD BE SEEN AS MORE FAR-REACHING AND SIGNIFICANT THAN IT HAS BEEN DESCRIBED TO DATE. THERE IS .AN OPPORTUNITY FOR NATO TO DEFINE ITS PURPOSE AND MISSION IN NEW WAYS, TAKING ACCOUNT OF THE END OF THE COLD WAR AND THE EMERGENCE OF DEMOCRACY IN RUSSIA. THIS INTERNAL ADAPTATION MUST DEFINE NATO S PURPOSES IN WAYS INFORMED BY OUR DIALOGUE AS WELL AS THAT BETWEEN THE UNITED STATES AND RUSSIA AND NATO AND RUSSIA. WE HAVE A LOT OF WORK TO DO. 
PM CHERNOMYRDIN: THANK YOU FOR THE DISCUSSION. WE HAVE MUCH TO TALK ABOUT IN THE FUTURE. 
END OF CONVERSATION --
