Mamedov: What do you need? You will never get inspectors into NPO Trud. 
Talbott: Primakov tried to persuade Albright in New York that Russia had done nothing. We will need to have a 
third Koptev-Wisner meeting in the week of October 27. 
Courtney: It is important that Russia offer greater transparency on these issues. 
Mamedov: Some people think the U.S. is pushing this question because it has problems with Israel and others 
think the U.S. is trying to gain access to secrets of our defense complex. 
Talbott: Greater transparancy is the key. We do not want this issue to stop our promising relationship. 
Mamedov: Primakov is annoyed by the publicity on this case. 
Talbott. Our standard is that Yeltsin assurance twice given In person to President Clinton, "I promise you Bill 
nothing has happended,"-that things are in fact happening. 
Mamedov: Nobody is perfect. We wanted renewal of START III experts' meetings and you have given them-to 
us. What the U.S. has done is enough for START II ratification-Helsinki, New York, and these talks. We have 
gotten what we wanted and now it is our responsibilityt to ratify START II. The timing of the MIRACL test was 
not perfect. People are now crying that we shoud restart our ASAT program. Today, Duma speaker Seleznoz 
appeared at the CWC discussion. We expected a big fight today from Gen. Rocklan (Chairman of Duma -- 
defense committee) but he did not come. I am now cautiously optimistic on CWC ratification. We should puih 
ahead on START II ratification after CWC ratification to take advantage of good spirit. We must do START II 
ratification before Christmas. 
Courtney: What did Yeltsin have in mind in his letter when he talked about a joint document for the next summit

Mamedov: He would like a roadmap for the future on strategic issues, regional issues, economic relations,
