1. CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR WILLIAM J. HUGHES. REASON: 1.5 (D). 
2. BEGIN SUMMARY. DEPSEC TALBOTT AND COUNSELOR TO THE PRESIDENT 
MCLARTY MET JUNE 2 WITH OAS SECGEN CESAR GAVIRIA BEFORE THE 
OPENING OF THE OAS GENERAL ASSEMBLY TO RECOGNIZE THE SECGEN'S 
EFFORTS IN PARAGUAY AND THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC AND TO SOLICIT 
GAVIRIA'S IDEAS REGARDING THE NEXT SUMMIT OF THE AMERICAS. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY ALSO SHARED IMPRESSIONS ABOUT HIS RECENT TRIP TO 
HAITI AND VENEZUELA. GAVIRIA PREDICTED A STRAIGHTFORWARD SECOND 
ROUND OF THE ELECTIONS IN THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC AND EXPRESSED 
SUPPORT FOR A CONTINUED INTERNATIONAL PRESENCE IN HAITI. TALBOTT 
AND MCLARTY TOLD GAVIRIA THAT THE US STOOD READY TO HELP IN ANY 
WAY POSSIBLE WITH REGARD TO THE KIDNAPPING OF GAVIRIA'S BROTHER. 
END SUMMARY. 
PARAGUAY 
3. THE DEPSEC COMMENDED GAVIRIA FOR HIS LEADERSHIP IN HELPING TO 
THWART THE ATTEMPTED COUP IN PARAGUAY IN APRIL. GAVIRIA 
EMPHASIZED THAT SWIFT ACTION IS CRITICAL WHEN DEMOCRACY IS 
THREATENED. HE COMMENDED BRAZIL AND ARGENTINA IN PARTICULAR FOR 
THEIR ROLE IN PREVENTING THE OVERTHROW OF PRESIDENT WASMOSY. THE 
SECGEN NOTED THAT BRAZIL'S FOREIGN MINISTRY ISSUED A STRONG 
STATEMENT IN SUPPORT OF PARAGUAYAN DEMOCRACY AND THAT BRAZILIAN 
GENERALS CALLED GEN. LINO OVIEDO TO PRESSURE HIM TO DESIST IN HIS 
COUP ATTEMPT. GAVIRIA PRAISED THE ARGENTINE FOREIGN MINISTER FOR 
CALLING OVIEDO AND TELLING HIM THAT IF WASMOSY WERE OVERTHROWN, 
PARAGUAY WOULD HAVE PROBLEMS WITH ITS NEIGHBORS. THE SECGEN NOTED 
THAT A FOREIGN MINISTER FROM ONE COUNTRY DELIVERING SUCH A MESSAGE 
TO A GENERAL IN ANOTHER WAS NOVEL IN LATIN AMERICA. 
4. THE SECGEN SAID THE SUCCESSFUL OUTCOME IN PARAGUAY 
DEMONSTRATED THE IMPORTANCE OF MAINTAINING CONTINUOUS CONTACT WITH 
A COUNTRY'S LEADER DURING A CRISIS. WASMOSY, THE SECGEN SAID, HAD 
FULL .CONFIDENCE IN THE DIPLOMATIC COMMUNITY IN ASUNCION. IN 
CONTRAST, GAVIRIA SAID, HE FEELS IN RETROSPECT THERE WAS NOT 
ENOUGH DIRECT CONTACT WITH THE PRESIDENTS OF PERU AND' ECUADOR 
DURING THE BORDER CONFLICT BETWEEN THOSE TWO COUNTRIES. 
DOMINICAN REPUBLIC 
5. THE DEPSEC THANKED GAVIRIA FOR LEADING THE OAS DELEGATION TO 
THE FIRST ROUND OF THE DOMINICAN ELECTIONS. ARA A/S AMB. JEFF 
DAVIDOW INFORMED GAVIRIA THAT IT APPEARS PRESIDENT BALAGUER IS 
SUPPORTING LEONEL FERNANDEZ, WHO FINISHED SECOND TO JOSE FRANCISCO

WILL HELP FERNANDEZ MUCH. GAVIRIA DESCRIBED BALAGUER AS
"UNPREDICTABLE" AND SAID THAT WHILE THE FIRST ROUND OF THE

PROCESS. OVERALL, HOWEVER, HE IS GENERALLY OPTIMISTIC THAT 
NOTHING UNTOWARD WILL HAPPEN IN THE SECOND ROUND. 
HAITI 
6. THE DEPSEC SAID HE CONCLUDED FROM HIS RECENT TRIP TO HAITI 
THAT THE COUNTRY IS MOVING IN THE RIGHT DIRECTION, THOUGH HE NOTED 
THAT PROGRESS WILL BE RESTRAINED BY HAITI'S POVERTY AND WEAK 
INSTITUTIONS. TALBOTT SAID PRESIDENT PREVAL IS COMMITTED TO 
PRIVATIZATION, BUT THAT THE PARLIAMENT -- NOW A VIBRANT FORUM FOR 
DEBATE -- NEEDS TO BE PERSUADED REGARDING THE IMPORTANCE OF 
ECONOMIC REFORMS. TALBOTT NOTED THAT HUNDREDS OF MILLIONS OF 
DOLLARS IN POTENTIAL ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE ARE HELD UP, PENDING 
ECONOMIC REFORM. 
7. THE DEPSECEXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT THE SECURITY SITUATION IN 
HAITI. HE SAID THE HAITIAN NATIONAL POLICE IS TOO SMALL AND 
INEXPERIENCED TO CONTEND WITH THE INCREASE IN CRIME AND THAT THE 
USG WILL PROVIDE WHATEVER SUPPORT WE CAN TO MAKE THE FORCE MORE 
EFFECTIVE. TALBOTT PRAISED NEW HNP DIRECTOR DENIZE AS A MAN OF 
INTEGRITY WHO IS HELD IN HIGH REGARD BY HAITIANS. GAVIRIA SAID HE 
IS NOT SURPRISED BY THE INCREASE IN CRIME IN HAITI. HE NOTED THAT 
THE SAME OCCURRED IN EL SALVADOR WHEN NEW SECURITY INSTITUTIONS 
WERE BEING CREATED. 
8. TALBOTT SAID HE WOULD MEET IN PANAMA WITH RUSSIAN DFM MAMEDOV 
TO DISCUSS, AMONG OTHER THINGS, THE EXTENSION OF THE UNMIH 
MANDATE. TALBOTT SAID SUPPORTERS OF UNMIH FACE AN UPHILL BATTLE 
TO EXTEND ITS MANDATE BECAUSE OF OPPOSITION BY RUSSIA AND CHINA IN 
THE UN SECURITY COUNCIL. THE SECGEN SAID THAT LATIN AMERICAN 
AMBASSADORS AT THE UN OFTEN CALL HIM TO DISCUSS HAITI AND THAT HE 
BELIEVES UNMIH'S MANDATE SHOULD BE EXTENDED. HE SAID HE WILL 
CONVEY THAT VIEW TO OAS MEMBERS. 
9. TALBOTT PRAISED THE HEAD,OF THE OAS-UN INTERNATIONAL CIVILIAN 
MISSION IN HAITI, COLIN GRANDERSON, AND EXPRESSED HOPES HE WOULD 
REMAIN IN HAITI. GAVIRIA SAID GRANDERSON'S GOVERNMENT, TRINIDAD & 
TOBAGO, MAY WANT HIM TO RETURN TO OTHER DIPLOMATIC DUTIES BUT THAT 
GAVIRIA WOULD LIKE HIM TO WORK AT THE OAS WHEN HE COMPLETES HIS 
WORK IN HAITI. 
SUMMIT OF THE AMERICAS 
10. MCLARTY NOTED THAT TRADE WILL CONTINUE TO BE THE MOST 
IMPORTANT SUMMIT-RELATED ISSUE IN 1997 BUT THAT THE MOMENTUM OF 
INTEGRATION WILL CONTINUE WITH OR WITHOUT GOVERNMENT ACTION

BECAUSE OF PRIVATE SECTOR EFFORTS. GAVIRIA COMMENDED THE USG FOR 
KEEPING THE SUMMIT PROCESS ALIVE AND NOTED THAT CHILE, THE HOST OF 
THE NEXT SUMMIT, WOULD NOT HAVE THE SAME BUREAUCRATIC CAPACITY TO 
OVERSEE PLANNING OF AND FOLLOW-UP TO THE NEXT SUMMIT. HE SAID 
THAT IN RECOGNITION OF CHILE'S ROLE, COMMUNICATION AMONG THE 
HEMISPHERE'S GOVERNMENTS ABOUT THE NEXT SUMMIT WOULD HAVE TO BE 
REVISED SO IT IS NO LONGER CENTRALIZED IN WASHINGTON. GAVIRIA 
SAID THE USG IS THE ONLY GOVERNMENT WITH THE "INSTITUTIONAL 
MEMORY" OF THE MIAMI SUMMIT PROCESS AND HE SUGGESTED USING THE OAS 
TO PROVIDE SECRETARIAT SERVICES AND TO MAINTAIN THE INSTITUTIONAL 
RECORD FOR THE NEXT SUMMIT. DAVIDOW NOTED THAT THE HANDOFF OF 
SUMMIT MANAGEMENT FROM WASHINGTON TO SANTIAGO WILL BE A 
SIGNIFICANT DIPLOMATIC CHALLENGE. 
VENEZUELA 
11. THE DEPSEC TOLD THE SECGEN HE IS ENCOURAGED BY THE ECONOMIC 
REFORMS THE CALDERA GOVERNMENT HAS IMPLEMENTED AND RECALLED HIS 
VISIT TO VENEZUELA TWO YEARS AGO WHEN VENEZUELANS WERE IN DESPAIR 
OVER THE COUNTRY'S ECONOMIC PROSPECTS. TALBOTT SAID THE USG IS 
SUPPORTING THE GOV THROUGH THE IFIS. 
GAVIRIA'S BROTHER 
12. TALBOTT AND MCLARTY TOLD GAVIRIA THAT THE US STOOD READY TO 
HELP IN ANY WAY WITH REGARD TO THE KIDNAPPING OF GAVIRIA'S BROTHER. 
13. USG PARTICIPANTS IN THE MEETING IN ADDITION TO TALBOTT, 
MCLARTY AND DAVIDOW WERE ARA/USOAS AMB. HARRIET BABBITT, ARA/SCO 
AMB BROWN, ARA/USOAS SPECASS MARK FEIERSTEIN (NOTETAKER), D 
SPECASS GEOFFREY PYATT AND ERIC FARNSWORTH, SPECIAL ADVISOR TO 
MCLARTY. GAVIRIA WAS JOINED BY HIS CHIEF OF STAFF, RICARDO AVILA, 
AND TOMMY BRUCE, A SENIOR ADVISER. MR. MCLARTY AND AMBASSADORS 
BABBITT AND DAVIDOW HAVE CLEARED THIS CABLE.
