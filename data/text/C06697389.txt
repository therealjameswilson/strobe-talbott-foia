7. 4E4- REGO BARROS ASKED THE ACTING SECRETARY TO 
ADVISE AMBASSADOR SERVICE OF THE PLANS DESCRIBED. HE 
NOTED WHILE THE U.S. AMBASSADOR WORKED VERY CLOSELY. WITH 
THE BRAZILIAN AMBASSADOR IN ASUNCION, IT WOULD BE BETTER 
AT THIS JUNCTURE IF THE MERCOSUL DELEGATION HAD AN 
OPPORTUNITY TO PURSUE ITS INITIATIVE INDEPENDENTLY WITH 
THE UNITED STATES IN THE BACKGROUND. 
8. -484- THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE UNDERSTOOD REGO 
BARROS' POSITION AND THEN ENQUIRED WHETHER FM LAMPREIA 
WAS RETURNING FROM SINGAPORE TO ATTEND THESE MEETINGS. 
9. --(-84- REGO BARROS CONFIRMED THAT THE FM WAS RETURNING 
TO BRASILIA FROM SINGAPORE, BUT EXPLAINED THAT BECAUSE 
THE TRIP WOULD TAKE ABOUT 40 HOURS, THE FM WOULD NOT BE 
A PART OF THE MERCOSUL DELEGATION. IN ORDER TO AVOID 
DELAYING THE DELEGATION AND WASTING TOO MUCH TIME, REGO 
BARROS WILL ATTEND IN THE FM'S PLACE. 
10. —(131- THE ACTING SECRETARY EXPRESSED HIS AGREEMENT 
AND NOTED THAT HE WOULD ENSURE AMBASSADOR SERVICE WAS 
BRIEFED, AS WELL.
