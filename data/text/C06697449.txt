PRESIDENTS CLINTON AND MANDELA HAD SPOKEN PREVIOUSLY 
ABOUT THE UN. BOTH AGREED ON THE IMPORTANCE OF THE UN 
AND WERE COMMITTED TO ENHANCING ITS EFFECTIVENESS. THE 
ISSUE OF WHO WOULD LEAD THE UN INTO THE TWENTY-FIRST 
CENTURY WAS VERY IMPORTANT AND THE U.S. BELIEVED IT IS 
TIME FOR A CHANGE. 
6. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THE U.S ADMIRED 
BOUTROS-GHALI'S ACCOMPLISHMENTS. HOWEVER, WHEN HE 
ENTERED OFFICE FIVE YEARS AGO, BOUTROS-GRALI STATED HE 
WOULD ONLY HOLD OFFICE FOR A SINGLE TERM. OUT OF 
RESPECT FOR BOUTROS-GHALI, THE U.S AGREED TO SUPPORT AN 
EXTENSION OF ANOTHER YEAR TO HIS TERM, BUT 
BOUTROS-GHALI REJECTED THE OFFER. THE U.S. SUBSEQUENTLY 
WITHDREW THE OFFER OF AN EXTENSION AND TOLD 
BOUTROS-GHALI THAT, IF NECESSARY, THE U.S. WOULD VETO A 
SECOND TERM.

CONSULTING WITH OTHER COUNTRIES ON THE SELECTION OF A 
NEW UNSYG AND ASKED IF THE SOUTH AFRICAN GOVERNMENT HAD 
ANY THOUGHTS ON THE MATTER.
