RELEASE IN FULL 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE 
WASHINGTON 
November 9, 1994 
Lynn, 
I understand Walt Slocombe has asked that we 
reconsider State's opposition to return of "like 
item" repair parts to Pakistan.' I hope we can 
resolve this problem in a way that does not 
exacerbate the problem. Narrow interpretations 
of the Pressler Amendment send the wrong signal 
to the Paks about our intentions. 
I would like for you and me to sit down with 
the Senator for an informational exchange -- and 
call his attention to the absurd degree to which 
his original intentions have been subverted. 
Let's see if he would agree it is irrational to 
say we cannot give back to Pakistan broken plane 
parts identical to those they turned in.
