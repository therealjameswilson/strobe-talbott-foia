SUBJECT: Finns ask Deputy Secretary for 
Seat at G-7 Table as Euroland Rep 
(U) CLASSIFIED BY POLITICAL SECTION CHIEF 
STEPHEN CRISTINA. REASON: 1.5, B AND D. 
RELEASE IN 
FULL 
DECLASSIFIED 
1. (U) This is an action request. Please see 
paragraph 5. 
2. 4Q+ Finnish Prime Minister Paavo Lipponen pulled 
aside the Deputy Secretary at a meeting on Kosovo in 
Helsinki May 12 to ask for the U.S. to agree to a 
seat for Finland at the G-7 during its upcoming EU 
presidency. The PM said that Finland, as the EU 
presidency July 1 - December 31, will represent the 
EUROZONE and the Euro-11 and would like to be 
present at the G-7. He claimed, in that context, 
that the European Commission would give [up] its 
seat at the G-7 to the Euro-11. (We defer to USEU 
Brussel's view on the likelihood of this.) 
3. —4;.)-It was unclear from the distussion whether 
the Commission's yielding a seat at the G-7 to the 
EURO-11 would be permanent or a one-off event. 
Lipponen told the Deputy Secretary that he 
recognized that the US is the primary opponent of 
increased G-7 representation, but urged the Deputy 
Secretary to consider Finland,,s arguments. 
4. (U) Comment: Finnish Finance Minister Sauli 
Niinisto has raised this matter periodically with 
the Ambassador dating back to last Fall and recently 
said that Finland intends to bring it up at the 
Cologne Summit in June. Finland,, like other smaller 
EU member states in the EURO-11, are not convinced 
that their interests will be appropriately 
represented under the current G-7 arrangement. 
5. Action Request: Please provide guidance to 
respond to PM's request for support. 
6. (U) Deputy Secretary Talbott departed before 
this cable was drafted and was unable to review it. 
EDELMAN
