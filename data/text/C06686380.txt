Re: Fincom Group Industrial/Commercial/Financial Initiative 
For The Russian Federation. Chairman Lee Hamilton & 
House Committee On Foreign Affairs Helpful Support. 
Dear Mr. Ambassador, 
1. Some Welcomed Congressional Support For Fincom Group And 
Your And Our Common Efforts To Build Free Markets In RF 
Wirth &-Co is pleased to.advise as to some helpful additional 
support for our mutual efforts to get Fincom Group's ready-to-go 
private industrial/commercial/financial initiative for the Russian 
Federation, executed and underway. This support is in Letter from 
Chairman Lee Hamilton and House Committee on Foreign Affairs 
11/30/93, copy attached. 
As the Letter notes in supporting your and our work to date: 
"The United States has no more important foreign 
policy objective than promotin4 the economic and 
political transformation of Russia and the independent 
states. A key mechanism for pursuing that objective is 
promotion of-U.S. commercial presence and investment in 
the NIS.... 
"I appreciate being informed of the plans for Fincom 
and hope the endeavor comes to fruition. 
"With best regards, 
Sincerely, 
Lee H. Hamilton 
Chairman"

Wirth & Co is pleased that this Letter will be helpful to you 
and to everyone in this common U.S. effort. Chairman Hamilton and 
Committee's knowledge and support for Fincom Group is a valued 
weight on your side of the scale. Namely, for breaking through the 
levels of bureaucracy and getting the immediate U.S./Russian 
Projects underway and benefitting the Russian economy and society, 
now when most needed. 
2. W&Co Private Business Initiative To Obtain Additional Official 
Support 
Chairman Hamilton's supportive Letter is a result of Wirth & 
Co's prudent business approach. This is to be certain of full U.S. 
legislative and executive branch knowledge and support, and the 
broadest U.S. and Russian official consensus. for what will be an 
extremely difficult W&Co Fincom Group undertaking. A11. before W&Co 
starts. In Fincom Group, U.S./Russian/EC Investing Institutions are 
being mobilized by W&Co in part to back U.S. foreign policy. They 
do this by building free markets and some 9-12 major free 
businesses in a most difficult foreign environment, and where there 
is no tradition for same. 
W&Co as a business courtesy presented the Fincom Group 22-page 
Summary 11/1/93 and a polite 7-page Explanatory Letter 11/9/93 to 
Chairman Hamilton and Committee, copy attached. In the Letter you 
will note, W&Co praised your, State, Embassy, RAEF's good work. And 
also the outstanding support and cooperation provided to our U.S. 
private enterprise,  Fincom Group initiative. This is not to be nice. 
But because it is a fact. W&Co requested the Chairman and 
Committee's support for Fincom Group, noting it could not possibly 
be accomplished by U.S. private enterprise acting alone under 
extremely difficult RF conditions. 
Chairman Hamilton and Committee have looked dt Fincom Group' 
details. And have now provided that support. This is another key 
U.S. policy element. It helps all of us move forward on a sound 
basis. 
3. Fincom Group Is Ready To Go. Request For U.S. Executive 
Decision. 
How then do we go out and get Fincom Group done, given this 
welcomed added support. And why? 
Once done, Fincom Group becomes a working/living/real world 
symbol of what can be accomplished by a U.S. private/public team 
effort. And a stimulant to others in the RF and U.S. Which is 
exactly what we all want.

W&Co has patiently developed Fincom Group in close cooperation 
with Embassy and U.S. agencies, over an extended number of months. 
W&Co has patiently adjusted and readjusted Fincom Group to meet all 
major U.S. criteria. There is no need for additional change, out in 
the real world. It is just further delay, in terms of the business 
and economic\ realities. However, W&Co as a further sign of 
cooperation will make whatever immediate additional adjustments 
that may be absolutely essential, providing always these do not 
cause further delay, affect feasibility, or substance. 
The value-added here is not in U.S. official micromanagement 
or endless Project.adjustment. Fincom Group works as-is, given the 
proper Investing Institutions which W&Co will bring in alongside 
RAEF's critical, one-time, bell cow input. The value is the exact 
opposite. The favorable psychological value to the Russian people, 
the major U.S. foreign policy value, of a U.S,/Russian/EC 
initiative like Fincom Group, is clearly right now. And in its 
immediate activation and startup on the ground. 
Fincom Group value is now, when we need to show immediate 
viable new companies, new tangible industry, new production, new 
progress and new hope for the Russian people, all stemming from a 
new infant free market system. This exactly what Fincom Group and 
its 9-12 new Russian Operating Companies are structured to do. And 
to do in*the most constructive, largely Russian-owned, Russian-
managed, international-backed form. 
W&Co therefore repeats its respectful request in its Letter to 
you of November 9. Everything in this Letter still applies. That 
you and Ambassadors Pickering and Simons, now join _forces and _lead 
the way in making a simple U.S. executive branch decision: Whether 
this Fincom Group private enterprise initiative is to be pursued bv 
the U.S. executive branch, right now, and on a time priority basis. 
Or it is not. 
- - 
If there is a clear yes, W&Co will make the necessary 100% 
commitment to Fincom Group on its side. It will take all the work, 
'time, business risks of executing financing and proceeding with 
same. 
If silence, or neutral, or further-hedges constraints and time 
delays, W&Co will accept this in good spirits. W&Co will express no 
complaints to anyone. It will promptly drop Fincom Group as too 
uncertain and unfeasible. This is by no means the end of the world. 
But it is an unnecessary waste of a valuable vehicle. W&Co will 
then proceed with its vastly easier, normal, international 
undertakings. One of these, W&Co has developed in cooperation with 
institutions in the Peoples Republic of China over the past years, 
has in integrity advised RAEF of, is being pressed to act 
immediately on. There is obviously much more for responsible U.S. 
enterprise to do, than Fincom Group.
