2. ~ SUMMARY: THE CORNERSTONE OF DEPUTY SECRETARY
TALBOTT'S VISIT TO ESTONIA ON JANUARY 22 TO 24 WAS
HIS DELIVERY OF THE ROBERT C. FRASURE MEMORIAL
LECTURE ENTITLED QUOTE A BALTIC HOME-COMING UNQUOTE.
THE GOE STARTED THIS ANNUAL TRADITION LAST YEAR IN
HONOR OF FRASURE, WHO WAS THE FIRST AMERICAN
AMBASSADOR TO ESTONIA AFTER REINDEPENDENCE, AND WHO
DIED IN BOSNIA.. FOREIGN MINISTER TOOMAS HENDRIK
ILVES AND DEFENSE MINISTER JURI LUIK USED THEIR
MEETINGS WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT TO UNDERSCORE
THE IMPORTANCE THEY ASCRIBE TO THE FULL INTEGRATION
OF'STONIA INTO WESTERN INSTITUTIONS AND ALLIANCES,
PARTICULARLY NATO, AND TO ASK FOR U.S. SUPPORT FOR AN
EXIT STRATEGY FOR THE OSCE MISSION HERE, AN
INSTITUTION WHOSE PRESENCE HAS LONG IRRITATED THE
GOE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REITERATED THROUGHOUT HIS
VISIT U.S. SUPPORT IN ASSISTING THE BALTICS IN
CREATING THE CONDITIONS FOR NATO ACCESSION, AS WELL
AS SUPPORT FOR THE WORK THE OSCE HAS ACCOMPL'ISHED IN
ITS MEMBER-STATES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED THE
HOPE THAT THE OSCE WOULD GIVE DUE RECOGNITION TO THE
STRIDES ESTONIA HAS MADE IN THE-INTEGRATION OF ITS
RUSSIAN-SPEAKING MINORITY, AND THAT THOSE STRIDES ARE
REFLECTED IN THE OSCE'S FUTURE ROLE IN ESTONIA. END
SUMMARY.
MOD JURI LUIK: NATO, FIRST AND FOREMOST

JANUARY 24 MEETING WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TO
EMPHASIZE THAT THE GOE HAS "CHANGED GEARS" NOW THAT
IT HAS ITS EU ACCESSION INVITATION POCKETED (ALBEIT
WITH MUCH WORK TO DO) AND IS NOW CONCENTRATING ITS
EFFORTS ON READYING ITSELF FOR NATO ACCESSION. IT IS
'A "CHALLENGE" TO TRANSFORM THE MILITARY INTO A PROPER
EUROPEAN FORCE. THE GOE IS PROVIDING THE FINANCING
NEEDED TO DO SO, 1.6 PERCENT OF THE BUDGET, DESPITE
MAKING CUTBACKS IN VIRTUALLY EVERY OTHER 'MINISTRY.
4. ~ THE GOVERNMENT WILL RAISE THE MILITARY BUDGET
TO TWO PERCENT OF GDP. THIS SPENDING WILL NOT BE ON
"FANCY STUFF, " BUT ON BASICS SUCH AS INFRASTRUCTURE
AND QUALITY OF LIFE. LUIK WAS HEARTENED THAT THE
ALLIANCE TOOK THE MAP PROCEDURE SERIOUSLY. ESTONIA
HAD RECEIVED FROM NATO A CONCRETE LIST OF TASKS. HE
HOPED I HOWEVER F THAT THE DEC IS ION MAKING ON NATO

6. ~ LUIK RESPONDED WITH A QUESTION: HOW MUCH
DOES THE WIDE RANGING SUPPORT FOR THE FIRST THREE
COUNTRIES TRANSFORM INTO THE IDEA OF A WIDER
ALLIANCE? THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INTRODUCED SOME
"SOBERING POINTS," I.E.,THAT THE FIRST TRANCHE WAS
NOT AS POPULAR AS THE SENATE VOTE WOULD SUGGEST, AND
THAT AT LEAST AS MANY IN THE FOREIGN POLICY ELITE IN
THE U.S. WERE AGAINST ENLARGEMENT AS FOR. THIS IS
DUE IN PART TO NEO-ISOLATIONISM, HYPERSENSITIVITY To
RUSSIA, AND OPPOSITION BY THE PROFESSIONAL MILITARY
FOR PRACTICAL AND TECHNICAL REASONS. THE PRESIDENT
WAS THE DRIVING FORCE IN THE FIRST TRANCHE, AND
PRESIDENT CLINTON'S SUCCESSOR WOULD HAVE TO ASSUME
THE SAME ROLE TO REALIZE A SECOND TRANCHE. WHILE A
TIERED DISTINCTION HAS EXISTED FOR POSSIBLE NEW
ENTRANTS -- RANGING FROM THE NEUTRALS ON THE TOP OF
THE LIST TO THE FORMER NIS ON THE BOTTOM -- THAT
DISTINCTION HAS BROKEN DOWN AFTER. THE MADRID SUMMIT.
THE DYNAMICS OF THE DEBATE ON ENLARGEMENT HAVE
CHANGED, AND THE ISSUE OF THE BALTICS, WHICH ARE
STABLE AND HAVE PERFORMED WELL, IS UP FRONT.
7. ~ LUIK WAS CONCERNED ABOUT THE STRATEGIC
EQUATION, I.E.,HOW CAN ESTONIA BE TAKEN IN WITHOUT
FINLAND OR LATVIA IN THE ALLIANCE. THE DEPUTY
SECRETARY ANSWERED THAT HUNGARY WAS TAKEN IN WITHOUT
GEOGRAPHIC "CONTIGUITY," SO A PRECEDENT HAS ALREADY
BEEN ESTABLISHED. AS FOR FINLAND, IT IS KEEPING ITS
OPTIONS OPEN ON MILITARY ALLIANCES AT THIS POINT. IT
WAS ONE OF OUR BEST ALLIES ON KOSOVO. AS FOR THE
RUSSIAN FACTOR, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID IT COULD
CUT BOTH WAYS FOR ESTONIA. IF RUSSIA IS IN A HAPPIER
MODE, IT COULD FORTIFY ONE SET OF ARGUMENTS, I.E.,IF
THINGS ARE GOING WELL, WHY MESS THEM UP, WHILE IF
MATTERS ARE NOT SO BRIGHT, IT COULD REINFORCE OTHER
ARGUMENTS.
8. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID A POSITIVE CASE
COULD BE MADE FOR "LOCKING IN" PROGRESS TO FINALLY
MAKE NORTHEASTERN EUROPE STABLE. HE ACKNOWLEDGED THE
INHERENT DIFFICULTY IN SYNCHRONIZING EU AND NATO
ACCESSION AND SAID THE U.S. WOULD LIKE TO HELP SO
THAT ESTONIA IS NOT "MOUSETRAPPED" BETWEEN THE TWO.

FM ILVES — OFFENSIVE AGAINST PERCEIVED OSCE OFFENSES
10. ~THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S FORMAL MEETING WITH
FM TOOMAS HENDRIK ILVES WAS PRECEDED BY AN ILVES-
HOSTED DINNER ON JANUARY 23 AT WHICH THE MAIN ITEM ON
ILVES'GENDA --AN EXIT STRATEGY FOR THE OSCE
MISSION -- WAS DISCUSSED. THE ESTONIAN PARTICIPANTS
AT THE DINNER WERE EMOTIONAL IN THEIR CONDEMNATION OF
THE OSCE MISSION. THEY SAID ITS PRESENCE TARRED
ESTONIA'S INTERNATIONAL REPUTATION, ITS MANDATE IS
VAGUE, IT HAS "RUN OUT OF THINGS TO DO," AND THAT IT
IS ENGAGING IN QUESTIONABLE ACTIVITIES LIKE
REGISTERING DISSATISFACTION AT THE PRESUMABLY SMALL
NUMBER OF'NTER-MARRIAGES BETWEEN ETHNIC ESTONIANS
AND RUSSIANS'. ILVES ASKED RHETORICALLY, "CAN YOU
IMAGINE HOW CONGRESS WOULD REACT TO SUCH A CRITICISM
HAD IT BEEN LODGED. AGAINST THE U.S.?" THE ESTONIANS
WANT THE MISSION OUT, AND WOULD LIKE U.S. SUPPORT FOR
AN EXIT STRATEGY. (NOTE: ILVES WAS BEHIND THE OUSTER
OF THE LAST HOM TO WHOM HE TOOK A PARTICULAR DISLIKE,
GERMAN BERND BRAUN, AND HELD UP AGREEMENT ON HIS
SUCCESSOR, ANOTHER GERMAN, UNTIL JANUARY 24.
RELATIONS WITH THE GERMANS HAVE DAMPENED DUE TO THE
OUSTER OF THE PREVIOUS OSCE HOM, AND THERE IS CONCERN
THAT RELATIONS COULD CHILL FURTHER IF THE NEXT HOM
PROVED DIFFICULT FOR THE ESTONIANS. END NOTE) THE
DEPUTY SECRETARY COUNTERED THAT HE WAS NOT CONVINCED
THAT THE MISSION'S WORK WAS OVER IN ESTONIA, THAT IT
NEEDED ANOTHER TWO YEARS, THAT THE U.S. VIEWS OSCE
MISSIONS IN A WIDER EURASIAN CONTEXT, I.E.
ESTABLISHING A MISSION IN CHECHNYA, AND THAT HE HOPED
THE ESTONIANS WOULD NOT FORCE US TO "MAKE A CHOICE."
HOWEVER, THE OSCE SHOULD RECOGNIZE THE PROGRESS
ESTONIA HAS MADE TO DATE WITH THE INTEGRATION OF-
RUSSIAN-SPEAKERS, AND THAT PROGRESS SHOULD BE
REFLECTED IN THE OSCE'S FUTURE ROLE.
11. ~ ILVES SUBSEQUENTLY SOFTENED HIS TONE ON THE
OSCE IN JANUARY 24'S FORMAL MEETING. HE EXPLAINED
THAT HE WAS "FRUSTRATED" WITH PARTNERS WHO WOULD NOT
'DISCUSS THE ISSUE.
