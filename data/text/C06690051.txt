RELEASE IN FULL 
A-,
Dear Sid, 
Thank you for your kind words concerning my appointment; 
they were most welcome. I hope that you too will be able to 
become a member of the Clinton team, despite the frustration 
you currently feel. 
Your letter and appended paper troubled me, so I consulted 
with Tom Graham in an attempt to get to the bottom of the issue 
to see what could be done. Tom made it very clear that, as he 
explained to you, the decision was made by White House counsel 
after serious consideration. The crux of the issue was the 
potential ethical question that would arise by having your wife 
working in a position in which she would report to you whenever 
you were in Geneva. 
Even in the 1990s, U.S. Government personnel regulations 
must be fair to all concerned -- including those who serve 
together with married couples. For this reason, Department of 
State regulations,'for example, do not allow for situations in 
which one spouse reports directly to another. In many cases, 
this has resulted either in foreign service couples' having to 
serve at different posts or in one of the officer's resigning 
or going on leave without pay. 
Tom Graham stressed, and asked that I pass on to you, that 
he still considers you the best candidate to be the U.S. 
Commissioner to the SCC, that you clearly are the most 
qualified for the job, and that he would like to have you 
working with him. Sid, the ABM Treaty has been under 
relentless attack for the past ten years; we need someone now 
with your experience and talent to revitalize the treaty. I 
hope you will reconsider and accept the position. 
Sincerely, 
A 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Adviser to the Secretary on 
the New Independent States
