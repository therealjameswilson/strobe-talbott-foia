2. SUMMARY: DURING HIS OCTOBER 24 MEETING IN ACCRA 
WITH DEFENSE MINISTER IDDRISU, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT 
EMPHASIZED THE USG'S DESIRE TO WORK CLOSELY WITH 
REGIONAL AND SUB-REGIONAL GROUPS IN AFRICA ON 
PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS. HE NOTED SADCC IN SOUTHERN 
AFRICA AS A PROMISING EXAMPLE OF SUCH A GROUP. IDDRISU 
REVIEWED THE AKOSOMBO AGREEMENT ON LIBERIA; CALLED FOR 
LOGISTICAL AND RESOURCE SUPPORT FOR AFRICAN PEACEKEEPING 
OPERATIONS; AND DECRIED THE LACK-OF SUPPORT FROM 
FRANCOPHONE COUNTRIES FOR ECOWAS INITIATIVES. MINISTER 
IDDRISU AND AIR MARSHAL DUMASHIE ALSO APPEALED FOR 
CHANGES IN UN RULES REGARDING THE EQUIPMENT USED IN 
PEACEKEEPING. THE IMPORTANCE OF A CLEAR DEFINITION OF 
OBJECTIVES IN PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS WAS UNDERSCORED. 
A/S BENNET NOTED THE IMPORTANCE OF TIMELY CONSULTATIONS 
ON EMERGING CRISIS SITUATIONS. END SUMMARY.

3. NOTING THE ADMIRABLE RECORD OF GHANAIAN FORCES IN 
PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS OVER THE YEARS, THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY UNDERLINED THE DESIRE OF THE USG TO WORK MORE 
CLOSELY WITH GHANA AND OTHER STATES TO ACHIEVE PEACE IN 
THE REGION, PARTICULARLY IN LIBERIA. DEFENSE MINISTER 
IDDRISU REVIEWED THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE AKOSOMBO 
AGREEMENT AND ITS ATTEMPT TO EXPAND, NOT REPLACE THE 
COTONOU ACCORD. UNFORTUNATELY, SUBSEQUENT EVENTS, 
INCLUDING THE RECENT ABORTIVE COUP IN MONROVIA, HAD 
DEMONSTRATED THAT THE•WARRING PARTIES ARE UNWILLING TO 
DISARM WITHOUT A GUARANTEED SEAT ON THE GOVERNING 
COUNCIL. MOREOVER, IDDRISU NOTED, THE LARGE NUMBERS OF 
YOUNG SOLDIERS IN THE LIBERIAN CONFLICT NEED 
PROFESSIONAL OR VOCATIONAL TRAINING TO ENABLE THEM TO 
PURSUE PEACEFUL OCCUPATIONS IF DISARMAMENT IS TO BE 
LASTING. A/S MOOSE SAID THAT FROM THE USG PERSPECTIVE 
THE SITUATION IN LIBERIA HAS BECOME MORE CONFUSED THAN 
EVER AND ASKED WHAT IS LIKELY TO HAPPEN IN THE NEXT 
SEVERAL WEEKS. IDDRISU STRESSED THAT THE NEWLY-EMERGED 
WARRING FACTIONS MUST BE ENGAGED IN THE NEGOTIATIONS. 
IN RESPONSE TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S OBSERVATION THAT 
SOME ECOMOG STATES SEEM READY TO GIVE UP AND WITHDRAW, 
IDDRISU SAID ONLY THAT GHANA COULD NOT SPEAK TO THE 
INTENTIONS OF OTHER PARTICIPATING STATES.

4. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT WHILE THE USG HAS 
FAITH IN THE UN'S PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS, IT NEEDS THE 
ASSISTANCE OF REGIONAL ORGANIZATIONS LIKE THE OAU. IN 
TURN, THE OAU NEEDS SUB-REGIONAL ORGANIZATIONS. HE__ 
NOTED THAT SADCC IN SOUTHERN AFRICA HAS POTENTIAL AS A 
SECURITY FORCE. THE WEST AFRICAN ANALOGUE APPEARS TO BE 
ECOWAS; LIBERIA IS CLEARLY ITS TEST CASE. HOWEVER, THE 
USG TAKES PEACEKEEPING EFFORTS IN AFRICA SERIOUSLY; THE 
GEOGRAPHICAL SCOPE AND INTENSITY OF U.S. INTEREST HAS 
INCREASED. WHILE THE END OF THE COLD WAR MEANS THAT THE 
UNITED STATES IS MORE LIKELY TO FOCUS ON AFRICAN ISSUES, 
USG RESOURCES ARE LIMITED AND ITS RESPONSIBILITIES ARE 
GLOBAL. MOREOVER, THE UW SYSTEM IS OVEREXTENDED IN 
TERMS OF ITS FINANCIAL AND OPERATIONAL COMMITMENTS.
