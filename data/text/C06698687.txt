SUBJECT: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S NOVEMBER 1 MEETING WITH 
ROK MINISTER OF NATIONAL DEFENSE KIM DONG JIN 
REF: Seoul 6230 
1. Classified by EAP A/S Winston Lord; Reason 1.5(b) (d) 
2. Summary. Deputy Secretary Talbott met with ROK 
Defense Minister Kim Dong Jin November 1, following Kim's 
participation in the 28th annual U.S.-ROK Security 
Consultative Meeting (SCM). The Deputy Secretary 
reaffirmed the U.S. commitment to consult with the ROK on 
issues important to it, and laid down a firm, marker that 
he expected the same from the ROK, 
  Kim did not respond. The two discussed the 
outcome of the SCM, and Talbott complimented Kim on his 
handling of questions about Team Spirit in the post-SCM 
press conference. The two also discussed Kim's upcoming 
visit to Moscow, which Kim described as a courtesy visit. 
End Summary.

year's SCM was particularly useful as it allowed the U.S. 
and ROK, in the wake of the submarine incident, to

SCM and Team Spirit 
5. Turning to the SCM, Talbott noted that Kim obviously 
made a very persuasive case for referring to Team Spirit 
in the SCM communique in his.discussions with Secretary 
Perry. Talbott complimented Kim on his handling of 
questions on this issue, which was "extremely sensitive," 
in the post-Ministerial press conference, and noted the 
importande of keeping our disagreements over tactics out 
of the public eye. He then expressed concern about recent 
press stories about US-ROK disagreements sourced to MND. 
Kim expressed gratitude to the U.S. for accepting mention 
of Team Spirit in the SCM communique; said that its 
inclusion would send a clear signal to the North; and 
agreed on the importance of avoiding a public perception 
of a difference of opinion about Team Spirit. 
Kim's Trip to Moscow 
G. Kim then raised the subject of his upcoming trip to 
Moscow, to which Talbott had referred earlier in the 
conversation. Kim explained that he was unhappy with the 
timing of the visit, which was in return for a visit to 
Seoul by (then) Russian Defense Minister Grachev. Kim 
said he had decided that it was too late to cancel, but 
indicated he had shortened the visit to one working day 
(from three). During his time in Russia, Kim said he 
hoped to meet with Defense Minister Rodianov, Foreign 
Minister Primakov, and Prime Minister Chernomyrdin to 
discuss the situation on the Korean peninsula and seek 
Russian help in easing tensions there. Kim also indicated 
that he would likely sign a memorandum of understanding on 
military exchanges while in Moscow, but denied rumors that 
he was going to buy military hardware. Talbott indicated 
that Defense Minister Rodianov was a relative unknown to 
the U.S., but expressed the view that he was likely to be 
preoccupied by the serious problems facing Russials 
military. Kim agreed to brief Embassy Seoul on the 
outcome of of his trip. 
Participants

of MND's American Affairs Division Choe Jong-ho. USG 
participants included Ambassador Laney, EAP PDAS Kartman, 
EAP/K Director Minton, D Staff Wong, and EAP/K desk 
officer Rank (notetaker).
