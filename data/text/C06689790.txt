Dear Mr. Ambassador: 
Congratulations on your nomination to be Deputy Secretary of State. 
I was looking forward to meeting with you to discuss Peace Corps' 
programs in the NIS, and to thank you for the terrific support you 
and Tom Simons have given Peace Corps in that region. ln light of 
yesterday's very good news, I hope we will soon have a chance to 
discuss not only these important programs, but also Peace Corps' 
work in other regions, and some of the broader issues related to the 
good relations between the State Department and the Peace Corps. 
I wish you every success in your new responsibilities, and send my 
best wishes to you and your family for a very happy new year. 
Sincerely, 
Carol Bellamy 
Director 
1990 K Street, N.W., Washington, D.C. 20526
