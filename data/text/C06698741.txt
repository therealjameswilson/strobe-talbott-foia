AREAS. HE ARGUED FOR SIGNS FROM THE U.S. OF A WILLINGNESS 
TO COOPERATE WITH BELARUS IN ORDER TO ENCOURAGE ECONOMIC 
AND STRUCTURAL REFORM IN BELARUS AND ELSEWHERE.

REASSURANCE ABOUT THE PROCESS. SENKO SAID BELARUS FEELS 
ITSELF ISOLATED AND IS DEEPLY CONCERNED BY REFERENCES TO 
"WESTERN EUROPE" AND "THE WEST" THAT SUGGEST A REDRAWING 
OF CONFRONTATIONAL LINES IN EUROPE.
