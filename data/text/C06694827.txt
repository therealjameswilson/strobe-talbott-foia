2. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED ME TO WRITE UP THE BRIEF 
MEETING HE HAD WITH PRESIDENT RAWLINGS AT THE AIRPORT. 
THAT REPORT FOLLOWS. PLEASE SHARE WITH A/S MOOSE AND 
ADVISE IF THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WANTS THE DRAFT CHAMGED 
OR GIVEN ANY OTHER DISTRIBUTION. WARM REGARDS. 
SUMMARY 
3. DURING A BRIEF MEETING AT THE AIRPORT WITH PRESIDENT 
RAWLINGS OCTOBER 25, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT APOLOGIZED 
FOR THE LACK OF APPROPRIATE TREATMENT OF MRS. RAWLINGS 
WHEN SHE ARRIVED IN NEW YORK. HE EXPLAINED THE 
IMPORTANCE OF DISCRETION REGARDING GOG INTEREST IN MORE 
U.S. INVOLVEMENT IN LIBERIA AND ASKED FOR RAWLINGS' 
VIEWS ON COMMUNICATING WITH THE IVORIANS ON LIBERIA. 
RAWLINGS EXPRESSED INTEREST IN BEING HELPFUL TO 
PRESIDENT CLINTON'S POLICIES ABROAD AND STRESSED THE 
IMPORTANCE OF THE U.S. ACTING IN SUCH A WAY AS TO EVOKE 
RESPECT RATHER THAN FEAR, AND COOPERATING WITH OTHERS 
RATHER THAN BEING PERCEIVED AS IMPOSING AMERICAN WILL. 
HE ALSO NOTED THE IMPORTANCE OF TREATING AFRICANS AS 
EQUALS. 
MRS. RAWLINGS' VISIT 
4. IN A RARE GESTURE TO A FOREIGN VISITOR, PRESIDENT 
RAWLINGS SAW DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT OFF AT ACCRA 
AIRPORT OCTOBER 25. DURING A BRIEF MEETING BEFORE 
DEPARTURE, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY APOLOGIZED FOR THE LACK 
OF VIP TREATMENT FOR MRS. RAWLINGS WHEN SHE ARRIVED AT 
JFK AIRPORT FOR HER CURRENT VISIT TO THE U.S. THE HEAVY 
WORKLOAD OF PROTOCOL OFFICIALS WAS A PARTIAL EXPLANATION 
BUT NOT AN EXCUSE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THAT HE 
WOULD TAKE STEPS FOR THE USG TO MAKE AMENDS. 
U.S. INVOLVEMENT ABROAD
