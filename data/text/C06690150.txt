Dear Mr. Brody: 
Thank you for your faxed note of February 24 regarding the 
potential for some commercial Russian banks to lend to small-
and medium-sized U.S. firms operating in Russia. , 
Once I am confirmed by the' Senate, I will look forward to 
learning more about your proposal to work with these commercial 
banks and .the Export-Import Bank to promote U.S. foreign 
investment and exports in Russia. You may wish to provide more 
detailed information about your proposal to the Office of 
Ambassador Armitage, Coordinator for U.S. Assistance to the New 
Independent States, at (202) 647-2626. 
I appreciate your interest in exploring ways for the U.S. 
Government to support both our own economy and those of the New 
Independent States through the promotion of U.S. foreign 
investment and exports. 
Sincerely,
Strobe Talbott--
