The Newsletter for International Business and Protocol 
Putnam Travel of Greenwich, Connecticut is pleased to announce Katie Barney Holmes has joined as a travel 
representative. Ms. Holmes is the Editor of The Diplomat; former President of the International Visitor Centers 
in Cleveland and Newport; and has served on served international boards. 
The Diplomat is a monthly newsletter on international business protocol and social etiquette. Each month a 
different country is profiled. This includes a country briefing, doing business in that country, its customs - 
dining, giftgiving, etiquette, holidays/festivals - and important facts and addresses. 
The Diplomat is designed for the business and social traveler to give an insight into the country featured. Each 
issue folds into a convenient size. The newsletter may be ordered by yearly subscription and/or individual issue. 
The editor, a former president of the international visitor centers in Cleveland, Ohio and Newport, Rhode 
Island, lectures on protocol and etiquette. Consultation for companies and individuals may be arranged as well 
as customized coverage for countries not yet covered. 
Back issues when available are three U. S. dollars per copy, which includes first class postage.
