B6 
I was so happy for you, and the country, at your appointment. Your contribution already 
has been immense and now you are in even a better position to help guide us through the 
complexities of the post-cold war world. 
On a personal note, I have refrained from ever asking for your help regarding my serving 
in the Administration because I know you must be blitzed by many other friends. But just 
recently I got fired up once again and I wrote the President and Hillary. I think you know how 
early, and loyal, I was in supporting Bi11. And I do believe I could be helpful--somewhere in the 
White House, at Justice, or at Defense (my law practice has been heavily in the defense 
procurement area). 
I've enclosed a letter I recently wrotelo the President and Hillary. If you have a few 
minutes, I would love to get your reaction to my letter and any other thoughts you might have. If 
there is any way you could say a few words of support to the President and Hillary, I would of 
course be very grateful. 
Best regards, 
P.S. I've enclosed a piece about the Harvard-Yale game which my son Seth wrote for the New 
Haven Register which I thought you would enjoy.
