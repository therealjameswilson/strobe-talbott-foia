SUBJECT: SLOVAKS CONTINUE TO OPPOSE GYARMATI FOR 
OSCE SECRETARY GENERAL; EXPECT TO SIGN NATO TRANSIT 
AGREEMENT SOON 
1. (U) CLASSIFIEDBY DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT. 
REASON: 1.5 (B) AND (D). 
RELEASE IN FULL 
DECLASSIFIED 
2. -(44- SUMMARY: DURING A BLIATERAL WITH SLOVAK 
FOREIGN MINISTER SCHENK ON THE MARGINS OF THE OSCE 
MINISTERIALS, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT INFORMED THE 
SLOVAK DELEGATION OF THE U.S. DECISION TO SUPPORT 
HUNGARIAN CANDIDATE ISTVAN GYARMATI FOR OSCE 
SECRETARY GENERAL. SCHENK MADE IT CLEAR THAT SLOVAKIA 
OPPOSED GYARMATI'S CANDIDACY, CLAIMING THAT GYARMATI WAS 
UNACCEPTABLE AS A PERSON, NOT BECAUSE OF THE COUNTRY H 
REPRESENTED. IT WAS CLEAR, HOWEVER TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
PARTY THAT THE GOS IS LETTING SLOVAK/HIJNGARIAN BILATERA 
TENSION COLOR ITS POICY IN MULTILATERAL FORA. ON A MORE 
POSITIVE NOTE, SCHENK INFORMED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THAT THE 
NATO TRANSIT AGREEMENT WITH SLOVAKIA IS VERY CLOSE TO BEIN 
SINGED. END SUMMARY. 
SLOVAKS CONTINUE TO OPPOSE GYARMATI.. 
3. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OPENED THE MEETING BY TELLING 
FOREIGN MINISTER SCHENK WHY THE USG HAS DECIDED TO 
SUPPORT THE HUNGAR/AN CANDIDATE FOR OSCE SECRETARY 
GENERAL (LIKELINESS OF BUILDING A CONSENSUS; NEED TO 
RESOLVE THE ISSUE QUICKLY, PREFERABLY BEFORE THE END OF 
THE BUDAPEST MEETING DECEMBER 8). SCHENK THEN REITERATED 
THE GOS POSITION THAT GYARMATI IS NOT AN ACCEPTABLE 
CANDIDATE FROM SLOVAKIA'S PERSPECTIVE. WHAT'S MORE, THE 
SLOVAKIAN GOVERNMENT DOES NOT AGREE THAT THE ISSUE NEEDS 
TO BE RESOLVED QUICKLY, NOTING THAT THE CURRENT SECRETARY 
GENERAL'S MANDATE DOES NOT EXPIRE UNTIL JUNE 1996. 
SCHENK IS NOT CONVINCED THAT A CONSENSUS WOULD EMERGE 
AROUND GYARMATI, NOTING THAT THE POLES ALSO HAVE A 
CANDIDATE AND THAT OTHER COUNTRIES OPPOSE GYARMATI.• 
(ALTHOUGH HE MENTIONED THIS LATTER POINT TWICE, SCHENK 
DID NOT STATE WHICH OTHER COUNTRIES MIGHT OPPOSE THE 
HUNGARIAN CANDIDATE.) 
...ALLOWING BILATERAL TENSIONS 
TO INFLUENCE OSCE POLICIES

4. SCHENK SAID HE IS NOT CONVINCED THAT GYARMATI WILL BE 
ABLE TO "MANAGE THE JOB IN A BALANCED WAY....GYARMATI 
WILL REPRESENT A COUNTRY, NOT AN ORGANIZATION." IN 
RESPONSE TO THE DEPSEC'S QUERY THAT THE GOS OPPOSITION 
WAS NOT WITH GYARMATI BUT WITH THE COUNTRY HE REPRESENTS, 
SCHENK RESPONDED IN THE AFFIRMATIVE. IT WAS CLEAR TO THE 
USDEL THAT THE GOS WAS LETTING BILATERAL TENSION BETWEEN 
SLOVAKIA AND HUNGARY COLOR ITS POLICY IN THE OSCE. 
DEPSEC TALBOTT POINTED OUT TO SCHENK THE NEGATIVE SIGNAL 
THE SLOVAK POSITION WOULD SEND TO THE WEST ABOUT THE 
STATE OF RELATIONS BETWEEN STATES IN CENTRAL EUROPE. 
NEVERTHELESS, SCHENK REITERATED THAT HIS MANDATE IS TO 
OPPOSE THE GYARMATI CANDIDACY AND SUPPORT THE SLOVAK 
CANDIDATE, JAN KUBIS. 
NATO TRANSIT AGREEMENT READY TO SIGN 
5. TRYING TO END THE MEETING ON A POSITIVE NOTE, SCHENK 
INFORMED THE DEPSEC THAT THE SLOVAKS WERE VERY CLOSE TO 
SIGNING THE NATO TRANSIT AGREEMENT, NOTING THAT THERE ARE 
ONLY A FEW "TECHNICAL ISSUES" THAT STILL NEED TO BE 
RESOLVED.
