SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S 
VISIT TO ALBANIA 
(U) CLASSIFIED BY AMB. M. R. LINO FOR REASONS 
1.5 (B) AND (D) . 
1. 4e} DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND HIS PARTY 
VISITED ALBANIA APRIL 3. THE GROUP MET WITH 
PRESIDENT MEIDANI, PRIME MINISTER MAJKO, AS WELL 
AS REPRESENTATIVES OF THE INTERNATIONAL 
ORGANIZATIONS, THE NGO COMMUNITY, AND OSCE. 
THEY VISITED A REFUGEE CAMP ON THE OUTSKIRTS OF 
TIRANA AND DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT GAVE AN 
EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW TO ALBANIAN STATE TELEVISION 
(WHICH IS REGULARLY BEAMED INTO KOSOVO). 
2. 4E1137 IN HIS MEETING WITH MAJKO, THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY STRESSED HE WAS IN ALBANIA TO ASSESS 
THE SITUATION AND PROVIDE CONCRETE ACTION ON THE 
PART OF THE USG. HE ANNOUNCED 6 MILLION USD OF 
NEW MONEY FOR THE U.S. ASSISTANCE PROGRAM IN 
ALBANIA. HE ALSO REITERATED THE U.S. CONCERN 
FOR THE TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY OF ALBANIA AND OF 
ALL THE BORDERS IN THE REGION. MAJKO SAID HE 
HAD JUST RECEIVED WORD OF APPROVAL OF A NATO 
"AIR CORRIDOR" FOR HUMANITARIAN ASSISTANCE IN 
ALBANIA, BUT HE DID NOT HAVE MORE DETAILS. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED GENERAL FOGLESONG, DOD 
A/S KRAMER, AND A/S TAFT TO SAY A FEW WORDS 
ABOUT THEIR AREAS OF OPERATIONS BEFORE THE 
MEETING CONCLUDED. 
3. --(44— PRESIDENT lqIDANI FOCUSSED ON THE 
PLIGHT OF KOSOVAR ALBANIANS STILL INSIDE KOSOVO 
HE ASKED ABOUT THE POSSIBILITY OF AIR DROPS OF 
FOOD SUPPLIES INSIDE KOSOVO. GENERAL FOGLESONG 
REPLIED IT WAS NOT FEASIBLE TO DO SO, ALTHOUGH 
ALL OPTIONS WERE BEING EXAMINED. MEIDANI 
THANKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY FOR THE COMMITMENT 
OF THE UNITED STATES WITH REGARD TO KOSOVO AND 
IN ASSISTANCE TO THE REFUGEES. 
4. THERE WAS NO EMBASSY NOTETAKER IN THE 
MEETING A/S TAFT HAD WITH THE INTERNATIONAL 
ORGANIZATION REPRESENTATIVES, NGO'S AND OTHERS. 
CONCURRENTLY, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TAPED A 
LENGTHY INTERVIEW WITH ALBANIAN STATE 
TELEVISION. THE INTERVIEW, AS WELL AS THE VISIT 
AS A WHOLE, RECEIVED FAVORABLE AND EXTENSIVE 
PRESS PLAY IN ALBANIA (SEE SEPTEL MEDIA REACTION 
REPORT).
