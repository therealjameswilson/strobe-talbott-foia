1. ~ SUMMARY: THE AUSTRALIANS WERE "DELIGHTED" AT THE
QUALITY OF THE DIALOGUE AT THIS YEAR'S ASEAN REGIONAL FORUM
MEETING, FOR WHICH THEY GAVE THAI .FM SURIN HIGH MARKS. THEY
WELCOMED NORTH KOREAN FM PAEK'S PRESENCE, AND THOUGHT THE
DEPU'Y SECRETARY DID A GOOD JOB OF RESPONDING TO CRITICISMS
ON NATIONAL MISSILE DEFENSE. THE GOA PLANS TO FLESH OUT THE
IDEA OF ADAPTING THE ASEAN TROIKA INITIATIVE TO THE ARF AND
WILL DISCUSS THIS WITH US PRIOR TO THE OCTOBER
INTEPSESSIONAL MEETING IN SEOUL. END SUMMARY.

2. ~ IN AN AUGUST 17 MEETING, DEPARTMENT OF FOREIGN
AFFAIRS AND TRADE (DFAT) ACTING ASSISTANT SECRETARY FOR
STRATEGIC POLICY AND INTELLIGENCE BRUCE MILLER TOLD POLOFF
THAT THE GOA WAS PLEASED WITH THE LATE JULY ASEAN REGIONAL
FORUM MEETING IN BANGKOK. MILLER PRAISED THAI FM SURIN'S
HANDLING OF THE EVENT, WHICH FOCUSED DISCUSSION ON MANY
IMPOR1'ANT REGIONAL ISSUES THAT SOME ARF MEMBERS PREFERRED
NOT TO DISCUSS. SURIN DID NOT TRY TO FORCE THROUGH A FORMAL
AGENDA, MILLER SAID, BUT CLEARLY HAD A PLAN IN'IND AND
MOVED FROM ONE ISSUE TO THE NEXT SMOOTHLY. DISCUSSION WAS
REMARKABLY FREE-FLOWING AND ANIMATED. 'ILLER NOTED IN
PARTICULAR INDONESIA'S "REFRESHING WILLINGNESS TO DISCUSS
INTERNAL AFFAIRS," PROVIDING A REPORT ON TROUBLES IN ACEH,
WEST PAPUA, MALUKU, AND TIMOR AND INCLUDING CONSIDERABLE
DETAIL ON WHAT THE GOI HAD DONE AND WHERE IT HAD SUCCEEDED
OR FAILED.
3, ~ MILLER NOTED THAT THE PRESENCE OF NORTH KOREAN
FOREIGN MINISTER PAEK NAM SUN --ALSO LARGELY AS A RESULT OF
THAI EFFORTS -- PROVIDED "POSITIVE THEATER" FOR THE ARF
MEETING, BUT IT DIDN'T ENTIRELY DOMINATE THE AFFAIR. PAEK
MADE A PREDICTABLY DOCTRINAIRE INTERVENTION, BUT IT WAS
SOFTER THAN MANY (INCLUDING THE AUSTRALIANS) HAD EXPECTED.
DISCUSSION ON KOREAN PENINSULA ISSUES WAS NOT QUITE AS FRANK
AS IT OTHERWISE MIGHT HAVE BEEN, MILLER SAID, BUT PAEK
PRESUMABLY FULLY BRIEFED BY THE THAIS ON WHAT TO EXPECT
SAT STOICALLY THROUGH A DISCUSSION THAT INCLUDED CONCERNS
ABOUT NORTH KOREAN MISSILE PROLIFERATION.
4. ~ THE DISCUSSION INCLUDED THE EXPECTED CHINESE AND
RUSSIAN ATTACKS ON NATIONAL MISSILE DEFENSE, BUT MILLER SAID
THE AUSTRALIANS WERE MORE STRUCK BY CANADIAN FM AXWORTHY"S
SHARP CRITICISM. JAPANESE FM KONO WAS QUITE SUPPORTIVE OF
THE U,S., HOWEVER, POINTING OUT THAT MISS1LE PROLIFERATION
WAS REALLY THE PROBLEM, NOT MISSILE DEFENSE. MILLER
COMMENTED THAT THE DEPUTY SECRETARY HAD BEEN LOW KEY BUT
PERSUASIVE IN RESPONDING TO CRITICISM OF MISSILE DEFENSE,
AND HE THOUGHT THIS INTERVENTION HAD BEEN WELL RECEIVED BY
MOST DELEGATIONS.
5. ~ THE BURMA DISCUSSION WAS LESS EMOTIVE THAN USUAL,
MILLER SAID. HE,THOUGHT IT NOTEWORTHY THAT CHINA,'AOS AND
INDIA HAD ALL SPOKEN UP ABOUT THE TRANSNATIONAL SECURITY
THREAT TO THEIR NATIONS REPRESENTED BY NARCOTICS TRAFFICKING

'6. (® CHINA AND MALAYSIA REPEATED THEIR REFRAIN THAT THE
SOUTH CHINA SEA AND THE CODE OF CONDUCT NEGOTIATIONS WERE NO

7. ~ILLER REPORTED THAT THE AUSTRALIANS WERE DELIGHTED
THAT THE GROUP HAD FAILED TO REACH A CONSENSUS ON PAKISTANI
MEMBERSHIP. THEY FEARED THAT THIS ISSUE WOULD NOT GO AWAY,
HOWEVER& AND THAT THQSE OPPOSED TO PAKISTAN'S MEMBERSHIP
MIGHT HAVE TO TAKE A MORE NEGATIVE POSITION IN THE FUTURE.
FOR NOW, EVERYONE CITED THE ARF CONSENSUS ON APPROPRIATE
MEMBERSHIP CRITERIA: IN THE EAST ASIAN GEOGRAPHIC FOOTPRINT,
WITH STRATEGIC INTERESTS AT STAKE AND/QR THE CAPACITY TO
INFLUENCE THAT REGION. PAKISTAN DIDN'T FIT THOSE CRITERIA.
THEY ALSO WERE TO SOME EXTENT HIDING BEHIND THE LACK OF
ASEAN CONSENSUS ON PAKISTAN'S ENTRY. IF THAT CONSENSUS
GELLED AT SOME POINT, MILLER SAID, THE NQN-ASEAN ARF MEMBERS
MIGHT HAVE TO TAKE A MUCH MORE UP-FRONT STAND ON PAKISTAN.
8.~ WHILE THEY WERE DISAPPOINTED AT THE VERY LIMITED
DISCUSSION ON THE FUTURE OF THE ARF (PREVENTIVE DIPLOMACY,
ENHANCING THE ROLE OF THE ARF CHAIR), MILLER STRESSED THAT
THE AUSTRALIANS WERE DELIGHTED AT THE QUALITY OF THE
DIALOGUE THAT HAD GONE OVERTIME AND CAUSED THE FINAL SESSION
TO BE TRUNCATED.
9. ~ FINALLY, MILLER SAID THAT FM DOWNER HAD BEEN
ENCOURAGED BY THE POSITIVE RECEPTION FOR HIS SUGGESTION TO
ADAPT THE ASEAN TROIKA INITIATIVE TO THE ARF, WITH NON-ASEAN
COUNTRIES BEING INCLUDED AS "FRIENDS OF THE CHAIR." HE
THOUGHT THE THAIS, IN PARTICULAR, SEEMED TO LIKE THE IDEA.
MILLER NOTED THAT THE GOA INTENDED TO COME UP WITH A MORE
COMPLETE PROPOSAL TO PRESENT AT THE OCTOBER INTERSESSIONAL
MEETING IN SEOUL, AND PLANNED TO APPROACH THE U.S. TO
DISCUSS THAT INITIATIVE IN MORE DEPTH PRIOR TO THE SEOUL
MEETING.
