COMMON ELEMENTS FOR THE CONDUCT OF A NATO-RUSSIA DIALOGUE 
AND AN EVENTUAL NATO-RUSSIA CHARTER 
NATO and the Government of the Russian Federation agree to work 
toward a Charter on the overall character and structure of the 
relationship with the goal of signing such an agreement at a 1997 
NATO-Russia Summit. 
In the continuing development of NATO's doctrine and strategic 
concept, NATO.and Russia will both reaffirm that they do not view 
any nation in Europe as an adversary and will encourage 
cooperation between NATO and Russia. 
• These steps would include building on the Rome 1991 NATO 
"New Strategic Concept" and other appropriate NATO 
documents which deal with the new challenges facing 
European security. 
NATO and the Government of the Russian Federation recognize that 
there will be further command and force structure changes. Such 
changes, when they occur, will be implemented in a manner that 
builds confidence between NATO and all European states. 
NATO and the Government of the Russian Federation, in addition, 
agree to explore the possibility of establishing a NATO-Russia 
Standing Consultative Committee. The Standing Consultative 
Committee would provide a forum for European political and 
security consultations and cooperation. The Standing 
Consultative Committee could consider possible cooperation in a 
full spectrum of political-military issues of mutual interest, 
including European security developments, as well as possible 
Russian participation in planning non-Article 5 contingencies and 
other joint military activities. 
NATO and the Government of the Russian Federation agree that the 
Charter should specify both the procedures and subject matter for 
other consultations and joint action.

mechanisms. 
• Some issues will continue to be addressed at 16 plus 1. 
including WMD proliferation, counter-proliferation, arms 
control, civil emergencies, as well as economic and 
scientific issues. 
NATO and the Government of the Russian Federation agree to 
explore cooperation between individual allies and Russia and 
NATO, as appropriate, with Russia in the area of Theater Missile 
Defense (TMD). 
NATO and the Government of the Russian Federation agree to 
Russian participation in armaments-related cooperation through 
the NATO Conference of National Armaments Directors (CNAD). 
NATO and the Government of the Russian Federation agree to work 
toward enhancing structures like PFP and NACC in which Russia 
participates directly. 
NATO and the Government of the Russian Federation agree that 
permanent liaison structures should be established between them 
including: 
• Russian military liaison officers at SHAPE and major 
subordinate commands; 
A Russian politico-military liaison office at NATO 
Headquarters; 
• A NATO military liaison office with the Russian General 
Staff in Moscow and;
