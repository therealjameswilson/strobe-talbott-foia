2. ...4a). IN A FRIENDLY EXCHANGE OF VIEWS WITH THE ACTING 
SECRETARY, ALEKSANDR LEBED SAID HE REGARDS THE U.S. AS 
RUSSIA'S LONG-TERM STRATEGIC PARTNER. HE GAVE THE GOR LOW 
MARKS ON ITS MANAGEMENT OF THE ECONOMIC REFORM PROCESS AND 
CHARACTERIZED RUSSIA AS A MOCK DEMOCRACY THAT HAS BARELY 
STARTED MAKING THE PSYCHOLOGICAL CHANGES REQUIRED FOR TRUE 
DEMOCRACY. HE CALLED ON MOSCOW TO DEVOLVE AUTHORITY TO 
THE REGIONS, NOTING THAT NO PART OF RUSSIA -- INCLUDING 
CHECHNYA -- WOULD LEAVE IF GIVEN SUFFICIENT FREEDOM TO 
DEVELOP ECONOMICALLY. HE SAID HIS TOP PRIORITY IN COMING 
MONTHS (IN AN UNSPECIFIED CAPACITY) WILL BE TO IMPROVE THE 
DISMAL CLIMATE FOR FOREIGN INVESTMENT IN RUSSIA. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY PRAISED LEBED FOR ENDING THE WAR IN 
CHECHNYA AND EXPRESSED HOPE THAT THE PEACE WILL ALLOW 
RUSSIA TO DEVELOP A GENUINELY MULTI-ETHNIC AND MULTI-FAITH 
DEMOCRACY. END SUMMARY.
