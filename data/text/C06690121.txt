Dear Kevin: 
RELEASE IN PART 
B6 
I'm sorry it took so long to get back to you. Between my 
nomination hearing, the summit, and now the G-7 meeting in 
Tokyo, I haven't had a moment. I hope you're off in your 
prognosis that history is moving in the wrong direction. I'm a 
bit more optimistic, especially after what I heard and sensed 
in Vancouver. 
Thank you for the impassioned yet thoughtful plea on behalf 
of the radios. This is a tricky issue, not so much on the 
merits of keeping the radios, as on their cost. I am finding 
more and more that good ideas and thoughtful initiatives often 
fall under the weight of the budget ax. I don't mean to imply 
that an ins,titution like the radios can be characterized merely 
as a good idea, but there is sentiment that under present 
budgetary constraints, consolidation may be necessary. 
There are certainly allies on the Hill for keeping them 
open. Senators Biden and Lugar discussed this issue with me 
during my confirmation hearing. Their arguments were much like 
your own-- the need for an independent voice du-ring this time 
of transition and turmoil. 
A National Security Council review is under way of what to 
do about the future of RFE/RL. I have also asked my staff to 
look into it. Your arguments are compelling. I will make sure 
your voice is heard. 
It was good to hear from you. Keep in touch. 
Sincerely, 
B6 
Strobe Talbott
