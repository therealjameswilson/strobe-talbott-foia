4. ±51- THE ACTING SECRETARY APOLOGIZED TO PRESIDENT 
AHTISAARI FOR HIS EARLIER LACK OF CLARITY. HE HAD 
RECEIVED A FULL READ-OUT FROM AMBASSADOR EDELMAN AND HAD 
UNDERSTOOD AHTISAARI HAD DONE BETTER WITH CHERNOMYRDIN 
THAN THE U.S. HAD. HE THOUGHT AHTISAARI HAD CONSIDERABLY 
IMPROVED THE STRATEGY TACTICALLY, AND PERHAPS EVEN 
STRATEGICALLY. HE WAS PLEASED THE ISSUE OF THE JOINT 
MISSION HAD NOT COME UP. IT WAS CORRECT OF AHTISAARI TO 
URGE CHERNOMYRDIN TO REFRAIN FROM A VISIT TO FINLAND UNTIL 
AFTER THE DETAILS WERE FINALIZED. 
AHTISAARI SAID HE WAS PREPARED TO HAVE A BILATERAL 
WITH CHERNOMYRDIN BUT FIRST WANTED TO HAVE A TRILATERAL 
MEETING INCLUDING UNSYG ANNAN. HE HAD USED THE EXCUSE 
WITH CHERNOMYRDIN THAT ANNAN HAD NOT YET RAISED THE 
MISSION WITH HIM. AHTISAARI TOLD THE ACTING SECRETARY HE 
HAD TENTATIVELY SET MAY 13 AS THE DATE FOR THE TRILATERAL. 
HE WAS CONCERNED CHERNOMYRDIN MIGHT HAVE TO GO BACK TO 
MOSCOW MAY 13 BECAUSE OF FRENCH PRESIDENT CHIRAC'S VISIT. 
AHTISAARI SUGGESTED THE ACTING SECRETARY COME TO HELSINKI 
MAY 12. 
6. -434- AHTISAARI ASKED THE ACTING SECRETARY TO FEEL FREE 
TO PUT ANY ADDITIONAL THOUGHTS ON PAPER. HE HAD NOT BEEN 
INVOLVED IN THE WHOLE EXERCISE WITH THE DEPTH HIS 
PROFESSIONAL DUTIES REQUIRED. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
PROMISED TO PROVIDE AHTISAARI WITH ALL THE INFORMATION. 
HE THANKED AHTISAARI FOR HIS PART IN THE CONVERSATION AND 
ASKED AHTISAARI TO SHARE ANY TALKING POINTS OR 
CONTINGENCIES WITH EDELMAN. ' 
7. -(43+ AHTISAARI SAID THAT AT THE MOMENT HIS PUBLIC 
POSITION WAS THAT HE HAD BEEN APPROACHED BY ANNAN IN LIGHT 
OF HIS PAST EXPERIENCE. CHERNOMYRDIN HAD TOLD HIM RUSSIAN 
PRESIDENT YELTSIN WAS PLEASED HE WOULD HAVE A ROLE. 
AHTISAARI HOPED WHATEVER ANNOUNCEMENT ANNAN WOULD MAKE 
WOULD ALLOW FOR FLEXIBILITY. HE HOPED ANNAN WOULD STATE 
HE HAD REQUESTED AHTISAARI'S ASSISTANCE IN THE PROCESS OF 
FINDING A PEACEFUL SETTLEMENT. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
REPLIED IT WAS INCONCEIVABLE ANNAN WOULD MAKE AN 
ANNOUNCEMENT BEFORE CONSULTING WITH AHTISAARI. HE 
CAUTIONED AGAINST THE GREATER LIKELIHOOD OF LEAKS AND SAID 
THE U.S. WOULD REMAIN IN SYNCH WITH AHTISAARI'S POSITION.
