Thank you for sending me a copy of the very thoughtful 
letter you sent to the President. It is clear that you have 
given this issue a great deal of thought. I was sorry you 
weren't able to make it to my hearing before the House Foreign 
Affairs Committee. I'm sure there will be other opportunities 
in the future. 
I think your reasoning that we need to make better and more 
innovative use of the international financial institutions is 
right on target. That is precisely what we tried to do in 
Tokyo last week. 
I am heartened that you feel confident that at least some 
of our assistance program for Russia is politically 
supportable. I agree, however, that the transformation process 
must be accompanied by a transformation in attitudes in the 
region, which I believe is taking place. 
I am sorry that it has taken so long to get back to you, 
but I trust that I can continue to rely on your advice as we 
move ahead with our support program for Russia and the other 
NIS.
