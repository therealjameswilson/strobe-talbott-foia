Lynne: 
A note on the background of my call to you 
about Charlie Thomas and burden-sharing... 
An old acquaintance, he came by to'talk about 
several things (primarily his new job in P working on 
CEE), but in the course of it he mentioned what he sees 
as the importance of maintaining some heft for the burden-
sharing job. He's worried that any perceived downgrading 
of the job 'would drastically cut the amount of money the 
US could get out of those-whom we're trying to induce to 
share the burden. (He told me that we've gotten $4 billion 
out of the'Japanese.) Within literally minutes after 
Charlie left, Sir John Weston, the British ambassador to 
NATO, came in and joked gleefully about how glad he and 
his government were that Charlie was out of the burden-
sharing job and that -- so help me! -- "your Department 
is downgrading the post." 
Anyway, since the slot'is in PM and therefore 
in yobr purview, I wanted to pass this along and see if 
you', felt there was merit in finding a way to maintain 
the rank and clout associated with it. 
Cheer's,
