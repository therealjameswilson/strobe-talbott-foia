RELEASE IN FULL 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE 
WASHINGTON • 
March 23, 1994 
Dear Senator Pell: 
When I mentioned my forthcoming trip to 
Bratislava during the hearing today, you mentioned 
your having served there. It occurs to me that it 
might be a meaningful grace note to my visit if i 
were to carry with me -- and perhaps read on the 
appropriate occasion -- a paragraph or so of 
greetings from you to the Slovak people and 
government, recalling your own experience there 
early in your distinguished career. 
Just a thought... If it has merit, please 
get something to me before Easter weekend, since I 
leave the Monday afterward. 
Best regards,/
