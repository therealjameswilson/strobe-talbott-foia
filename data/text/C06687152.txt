RELEASE IN FULL 
I apologize for the delay in responding to your letter of 
April 29 regarding SAIC's CERDP program. I understand that 
SAIC began discussing this project with Tom Simons' 
predecessor, Richard Armitage, last spring and submitted a 
formal, unsolicited proposal in September 1992. According to 
the records of the Coordinator's office, an inter-agency panel 
was convened especially to review this proposal, and in 
November 1992, opposed funding it because of serious 
reservations about the costs and nature of these programs and 
the viability of these new firms once funding from the U.S. 
Government assistance program ended. 
At this time, we have exhausted our 1993 funds and must 
defer any new funding decisions until Congress provides 
additional assistance monies. I have passed on your request 
for an appointment to Tom Simons, Coordinator of U.S. 
Assistance to the NIS. I understand'he has a similar request 
in from your colleague, Bill Chadsey. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott, 
Ambassador at Large and
