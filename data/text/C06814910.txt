1. -(41- THIS CABLE PROVIDES BACKGROUND AND TALKING POINTS 
FOR DEPSEC DISCUSSION WITH SYG SOLANA ON 24 FEBRUARY, TO 
RE-INVIGORATE A PROCESS WHEREBY CONFIDENCE BUILDING 
MEASURES BETWEEN GREECE AND TURKEY MAY BE CREATED. 
2. -(7e)- BACKGROUND. SYG SOLANA'S FEBRUARY 4, 1999, VISIT 
WITH GREEK OFFICIALS IN ATHENS DID NOT PRODUCE SUBSTANTIVE 
PROGRESS' ON CBMS. AMBASSADOR BURNS 5 AND ASD FRANK 
KRAMER 5 SUBSEQUENT 'FLEETING ON FEBRUARY 10, 1999, ALSO 
DISCUSSED CBMS WITH GREEK DEPUTY MOD AND CHOD. AT THAT 
MEETING, GREEK INTERLOCUTORS SAW LITTLE CHANCE FOR CBM 
PROGRESS BEFORE THE SUMMIT. 'NOW, AFTER THE OCALAN AFFAIR, 
NEAR-TERKPROSPECTS MAY BE EVEN LESS PROMISING. 
NONETHELESS, THE EFFORT IS WORTHWHILE. WE SHOULD SUGGEST 
TO SOLANA WAYS TO FACILITATE A PROCESS WHICH MIGHT BE 
ANNOUNCED AT THE SUMMIT. 
A. (C) IN EARLY FEBRUARY, EMBASSY ATHENS ENDORSED SOLANA 
RAISING SEVERAL CBMS SUGGESTED BY WASHINGTON, AND BELIEVED 
THE GREEKS WOULD BE FAVORABLY DISPOSED TO THEM. EMBASSY 
ATHENS ALSO ADVISED NECESSITY OF KEEPING SEPARATE THE 
POLITICAL EFFORTS UNDER SOLANA'S AUSPICES, AND THE SECRET 
MILITARY-TO-MILITARY DISCUSSIONS BETWEEN THE GREEKS AND 
TURKS; THE LATTER CHANNEL MORE FOCUSED ON PRACTICAL 
OPERATIONAL MATTERS, HAS PROVED USEFUL IN REDUCING 
TENSIONS, AND REMAINS USEFUL SO LONG AS IT DOES NOT RECEIVE 
PUBLIC ATTENTION. 
B. -1(4)- EVENTS SINCE THEN, INCLUDING THE OCALAN AFFAIR, 
MAKE IT EXTREMELY UNLIKELY THE TURKS AND GREEKS WOULD BE 
INCLINED TO DISCUSS CBMS, LET ALONE AGREE ANYTHING, BEFORE 
THE APRIL SUMMIT. HOWEVER, THE EFFORT IS WORTHWHILE AND 
SHOULD BE PURSUED AFTER THINGS COOL DOWN BETWEEN THE TWO 
NEIGHBORS, PERHAPS AFTER THE SUMMIT. 
3. -1'7E9- RECOMMENDED ACTION. DURING YOUR VISIT WITH SYG 
SOLANA (LUNCHEON), YOU MAY WISH TO ENDORSE HIS EFFORTS TO 
RE-INVIGORATE THE CBMS PROCESS, SOUND HIM OUT ON THE 
PROSPECTS FOR NEAR TERM PROGRESS, AND CULTIVATE A CBM 
DEVELOPMENT PROCESS FOR THE FUTURE. SUGGESTED TALKING 
POINTS FOLLOW. 
4. +e..)- TALKING POINTS FOR MEETING WITH SYG SOLANA - AEGSAN
