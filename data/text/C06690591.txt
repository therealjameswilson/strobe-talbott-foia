RELEASE IN FULL 
Dear Andrzej: 
Thanks for your kind note. It means a lot to me. One of 
the reasons that I'm looking forward to the new job is that it 
will give me a chance to get more involved in policy toward 
Europe as a whole -- and East/Central Europe in particular 
(Poland very much in particular). In the coming months, we need 
to give a lot of attention to that region and its needs. I have 
a special feeling for Eastern Europe -- and you (and Leszek and 
my other RFE friends from that era of my life 20+ years ago) are 
in no small measure responsible for that. 
Anyway, I now have more in the way of formal bureaucratic 
pretext to see you from time to time, and I hope there will be a 
number of such times in '94. 
Brooke sends her very best. 
Cheers! 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott
