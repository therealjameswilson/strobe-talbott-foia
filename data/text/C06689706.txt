Dear Mr. Ambassador: 
I would like to congratulate you on your 
confirmation as Ambassador-at-Large to the new states of the 
former Soviet Union. It is encouraging to see the United 
States and the other members of the G-7 group commit their 
support to the democratic reformers in Russia. 
Our organization, The Ukrainian Medical Association of 
North America (UMANA) was organized in 1950 and consists of 
American and Canadian doctoral degree conferred health care 
professionals who are generally of ukrainian descent. We have 
approximately 3000 members, with 17 affiliated chapters 
throughout the United States and Canada. In the New York City 
Metropolitan area alone, there are over 450 ukrainian-
american physicians and dentists. 
Among its other professional activities, UMANA has a 
strong interest in aiding Ukraine in all aspects of medical 
needs. Several of °our members have individually travelled to 
the former Soviet Union and have established contact with 
many of their physicians. Some work directly with the 
American International Health,Alliance, Project Hope, the 
Peace Corps and other humanitarian groups which are funded 
by the United States Government Agency for International 
Development. Most frequently, we try to act as liasons 
between American humanitarian or medical organizations and 
their respective counterparts in the former Soviet Union.
