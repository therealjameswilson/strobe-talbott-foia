THE NATO COUNTRIES AND RUSSIA WERE MAKING PROGRESS TOWARD 
A POSSIBLE SOLUTION TO THE KOSOVO CRISIS. CHERNOMYRDIN 
HAD WANTED TO ACT QUICKLY, BUT THE U.S. WAS TRYING TO

HOW A SERB WITHDRAWAL FROM KOSOVO WOULD BE CARRIED OUT AND 
HOW AN INTERNATIONAL SECURITY FORCE WOULD BE STRUCTURED. 
THE ACTING SECRETARY WOULD BRING A TEAM OF EXPERTS, 
INCLUDING HIGH-LEVEL MILITARY OFFICIALS, WITH HIM TO 
MOSCOW TO HELP EXPLAIN U.S. POSITIONS. HE HAD TOLD 
CHERNOMYRDIN IT WOULD ONLY MAKE SENSE FOR AHTISAARI TO 
ACCOMPANY THE RUSSIAN ENVOY TO BELGRADE IF RUSSIA AND THE 
U.S. HAD RESOLVED MAJOR DIFFERENC-ES. HE HAD SAID 
AHTISAARI WAS NOT PREPARED TO CONDUCT SHUTTLE DIPLOMACY. 
AHTISAARI SAID HE HAD NOT BEEN AWARE THAT HE MIGHT 
BE ASKED TO ACCOMPANY CHERNOMYRDIN TO BELGRADE. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY SAID THE RUSSIANS BELIEVED THEY COULD 
APPLY PRESSURE ON SERBIAN PRESIDENT MILOSEVIC, BUT DID NOT 
THINK MILOSEVIC WOULD HAND OVER HIS SWORD TO A RUSSIAN. 
THEY BELIEVED AN INTERNATIONAL PERSONAGE MIGHT BE MORE 
ACCEPTABLE TO BELGRADE. AHTISAARI WAS MENTIONED AS 
SOMEONE WHO COULD FILL THAT ROLE. IF AHTISAARI WERE TO GO 
TO BELGRADE, HE WOULD HOPEFULLY WEAR THE HATS OF AT LEAST 
TWO ORGANIZATIONS: THE EU, AS INCOMING EU PRESIDENT; AND 
THE UN, WHICH WAS PREPARED TO CONSIDER GIVING SUCH A 
MISSION ITS BLESSING. CHERNOMYRDIN WAS ENTHUSIASTIC ABOUT 
THE IDEA OF AHTISAARI'S INVOLVEMENT, AS WERE THE GERMANS. 
UNSYG ANNAN'S ONLY RESERVATION WAS THAT HE WAS PLANNING TO 
NAME TWO UN ENVOYS TO BELGRADE, PROBABLY SLOVAK-FM KUKAN 
AND FORMER SWEDISH PM BILDT. BUT ANNAN HAD AGREED NOT TO 
DISPATCH THE ENVOYS UNTIL AHTISAARI AND CHERNOMYRDIN HAD 
HAD A CHANCE TO CONDUCT THEIR MISSION. IN ADDITION, ANNAN 
WOULD AVOID OVERLAP BY HAVING HIS ENVOYS FOCUS ON 
IMPLEMENTING PEACE ACCORD& RATHER THAN CEASING 
HOSTILITIES. 
6.4S+ AHTISAARI SAID HE DID NOT UNDERSTAND WHAT ROLE THE 
UN ENVOYS COULD PLAY ON PEACE IMPLEMENTATION, AND WHY DID 
THE UN NEED TWO ENVOYS? THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID ANNAN 
WANTED TWO ENVOYS IN ORDER TO BALANCE THE TICKET, WITH ONE 
ENVOY FROM A NEUTRAL COUNTRY AND ONE FROM A CENTRAL 
EUROPEAN NATION OUTSIDE OF NATO. HE SAID HE DID NOT FAVOR 
THE NAMING OF THE ENVOYS, AND COULD NOT DEFINE THEIR ROLE. 
BUT THE IMPORTANT THING WAS TO GET NATO AND RUSSIA TO 
AGREE ON A PROCESS FOR CEASING HOSTILITIES. ANY AGREEMENT 
WOULD HAVE TO REQUIRE ALL SERB FORCES, MUP AND VJ, TO 
LEAVE KOSOVO. IF ARMED SERBS WERE LEFT IN PLACE THE 
REFUGEE'S WOULD NOT COME BACK, EXCEPT FOR KLA MEMBERS WHO 
WOULD COME.TO FIGHT. THERE WOULD ALSO HAVE TO BE AN, 
INTERNATIONAL FORCE WITH NATO AT ITS CORE, BUT INCLUDING 
NON-NATO COUNTRIES LIKE RUSSIA. IN ADDITION, THEY WOULD 
HAVE TO DISCUSS HOW TO RESPOND IF MILOSEVIC CONTINUED TO 
REJECT AN INTERNATIONAL FORCE IN KOSOVO. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY SAID HE WOULD BE ABLE TO EXPLAIN IN MORE DETAIL 
DURING HIS TRIP THE FOLLOWING WEEK TO MOSCOW AND HELSINKI. 
7.-*S+-AHTISAARI SAID HE NEEDED TIME TO CONSIDER WHETHER 
HE WOULD BE WILLING TO ACCOMPANY CHERNOMYRDIN TO BELGRADE, 
UNDER THE RIGHT CONDITIONS. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID 
CHERNOMYRDIN WOULD PROBABLY RAISE THIS ISSUE WITH 
AHTISAARI. HE SAID HE HAD NOT COMMITTED AHTISAARI TO THIS 
PROPOSAL, SO AHTISAARI WAS FREE TO MAKE UP HIS OWN MIND ON 
WHETHER TO DO IT. AHTISAARI SAID IT WAS NOT CLEAR TO HIM 
WHAT HIS ROLE IN SUCH A TRIP WOULD BE, IF CHERNOMYRDIN 
WOULD ACTUALLY BE RUNNING THE SHOW. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
SAID CHERNOMYRDIN WOULD NOT BE RUNNING THE SHOW. HE SAID 
AMBASSADOR EDELMAN WOULD CALL AHTISAARI BACK ON A SECURE 
LINE TO EXPLAIN FURTHER. HE REITERATED THAT AHTISAARI 
WOULD PROBABLY NOT WANT TO GO TO BELGRADE WITH 
CHERNOMYRDIN UNLESS NATO AND RUSSIA HAD REACHED FURTHER 
AGREEMENT. 
8. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 0955 EDT.
