SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY'S ONE-ON-ONE APRIL 23 MEETING 
WITH PRESIDENT SHEVARDNADZE 
(U) CLASSIFIED BY Stephen Sestanovich, S/NIS Ambassador-at-
Large, REASON: 1.6. 
1. (S) IN A ONE-ON-ONE MEETING FOLLOWING THE BROADER APRIL 
23 MEETING, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD PRESIDENT 
SHEVARDNADZE THAT WE TAKE THE RUMORS OF COUPS AND PLOTS 
SERIOUSLY, INCLUDING IN OUR REPRESENTATIONS TO RUSSIANS. 
WE WANT TO KEEP RUMORS FROM BECOMING REALITY. SHEVARDNADZE 
REASSURED THE DEPSEC THAT GEORGIA IS DOING FINE, BUT WARNED 
THAT IF THE "REDS" DO BETTER IN THE FALL ELECTIONS, THE 
CLIMATE WILL GET WORSE. 
2. (C) PICKING UP ON THIS OPENING, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
NOTED THAT AMONG THE THINGS WE ADMIRE MOST ABOUT GEORGIA IS 
ITS RECORD OF CONDUCTING FREE AND FAIR ELECTIONS. TALBOTT 
SAID, "WHATEVER THE PRESSURES, WE COUNT ON YOU TO UPHOLD 
THE HIGHEST STANDARDS OF FREE AND FAIR ELECTIONS." 
SHEVARDNADZE REPLIED, "ABSOLUTELY, I UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU 
ARE SAYING."
