1. —4et- SUMMARY 
DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT VISITED PORT AU 
PRINCE MAY 29-30, MEETING WITH PRESIDENT PREVAL, FORMER 
PRESIDENT ARISTIDE, MEMBERS OF GOVERNMENT, PARLIAMENT, AND 
THE POLITICAL CLASS, AS WELL AS WITH PRIVATE SECTOR 
REPRESENTATIVES. HE ALSO VISITED THE NATIONAL POLICE 
TRAINING CENTER WHERE HE MET WITH POLICE DIRGEN DENIZE, 
PARTICIPATED IN A PUBLIC AFFAIRS EVENT DEMONSTRATING USG 
SUPPORT TO EQUIP THE POLICE AND NASCENT COAST GUARD, AND 
VISITED THE SPECIAL INVESTIGATIVE UNIT (SIU). KEY THEMES 
IN ALL OF THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MEETINGS WERE USG SUPPORT 
AND RESPECT FOR ALL OF HAITI'S DEMOCRATIC INSTITUTIONS, 
AND OUR COMMITMENT TO DO ALL WE CAN TO HELP THEM BRING 
HAITI THROUGH ITS CURRENT ECONOMIC AND SECURITY 
DIFFICULTIES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ALSO CONSISTENTLY 
EMPHASIZED USG COMMITMENT TO HELP HAITI'S NEW POLICE 
BECOME A PROFESSIONAL, APOLITICAL, AND EFFECTIVE FORCE. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S INTERLOCUTORS EXPRESSED CONCERN 
ABOUT SECURITY; THERE WAS RECOGNITION BY MOST 
INTERLOCUTORS OF THE URGENCY TO ACT ON THE ECONOMIC REFORM 
PACKAGE, BUT ALSO A SENSE OF UNEASE THAT THE GOVERNMENT 
MAY BE SACRIFICING TOO MUCH TO WIN INTERNATIONAL FINANCIAL 
SUPPORT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EMPHASIZED USG READINESS TO 
WORK WITH THE HAITIAN GOVERNMENT TO HELP PROMOTE POPULAR 
AND PARLIAMENTARY SUPPORT FOR THE PROGRAM.
