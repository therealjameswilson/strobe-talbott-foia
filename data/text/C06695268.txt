2. SUMMARY: WITH DEPUTY FONMIN AITMATOV IN ATTENDANCE, 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT TOOK ADVANTAGE OF THE SWEARING-IN 
CEREMONY FOR THE NEW U.S.-AMBASSADOR TO KYRGYZSTAN TO 
OUTLINE U.S. VIEWS ON THE CENTRAL ASIAN COUNTRY. WHILE 
HAILING KYRGYZSTAN'S PROGRESS TOWARD DEMOCRACY AND A MARKET 
ECONOMY, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT RECENT 
DEVELOPMENTS IN BISHKEK HAD GENERATED CONCERN REGARDING THE 
FUTURE. EROSION OF KYRGYZSTAN'S REPUTATION AS A DEMOCRACY 
WOULD UNDERMINE THE POLITICAL BASIS FOR LARGE-SCALE U.S. 
AID TO THAT COUNTRY, HE SAID. .THE USG HOPED THE AKAYEV 
-GOVERNMENT WOULD REVALIDATE ITS COMMITMENT TO DEMOCRACY 
DURING THE UPCOMING CONSTITUTIONAL REFERENDUM AND 
LEGISLATIVE ELECTIONS. 
3. PRIOR TO THE SWEARING-IN CEREMONY, S/NIS SENIOR 
COORDINATOR COLLINS MET SEPARATELY WITH AITMATOV. COLLINS 
PREVIEWED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MESSAGE AND SOLICITED 
DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER'S VIEWS ON POLITICAL CONDITIONS IN 
KYRGYZSTAN AND ON CIS INTER-STATE RELATIONS. END SUMMARY. 
4. KYRGYZSTAN DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER ASKAR AITMATOV IS IN 
THE U.S. TO PREPARE FOR UNGA, AT WHICH HE WILL SERVE AS 
KYRGYZSTAN'S PERMREP. HIS BRIEF VISIT TO WASHINGTON MADE 
IT POSSIBLE FOR HIM TO ATTEND EILEEN MALLOY'S SWEARING-IN 
AND TO MEET WITH THE NIS SENIOR COORDINATOR. 
MEETING WITH S/NIS 
5. KYRGYZ CHARGE D'AFFAIRES ALMAS CHUKIN ACCOMPANIED 
AITMATOV TO THE 45-MINUTE MEETING WITH COLLINS. S/NIS 
SPECIAL ASSISTANT WONG AND EUR/ISCA KYRGYZ DESK OFFICER 
HART ALSO ATTENDED. 
6. AITMATOV, SPEAKING THROUGHOUT IN ENGLISH, UNDERSCORED 
THE IMPORTANCE OF U.S. ASS/STANCE TO KYRGYZSTAN. HE STATED 
THAT THE LAST TWO YEARS HAVE DEMONSTRATED THAT U.S.-KYRGYZ 
AGREEMENT ON DEMOCRACY AND MARKET REFORM HELPED TO PROMOTE 
RELATIONS IN A VARIETY OF AREAS. THE KYRGYZ PEOPLE HIGHLY 
APPRECIATED U.S. HUMANITARIAN ASSISTANCE DURING THE LAST, 
SEVERE WINTER. KYRGYZSTAN'S RELATIONSHIP WITH THE U.S. WAS 
ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT FOR GOK FOREIGN POLICY, AITMATOV 
COMMENTED. THIS RELATIONSHIP WAS FRIENDLY AND BASED ON 
SIMILAR POSITIONS WITH REGARD TO MAJOR ISSUES. KYRGYZSTAN 
BELIEVED THERE WERE GOOD PROSPECTS FOR FURTHER DEVELOPMENT 
OF TIES.
