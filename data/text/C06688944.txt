Dear Terry: 
Thanks for your letter of June 14th. Sorry for the long 
delay in replying. I've been travelling quite a bit the past 
couple weeks. I'm glad to learn about Joe Fox and his very 
worthy program. When he writes to me, I will make sure that he 
ends up talking to the most appropriate people in our 
assistance operation who deal with exchanges. I will try to 
give Mr. Fox some of my own time, although the period ahead 
promises to be extremely busy for me. Perhaps I could do a 
drop by in whatever meeting he has with our exchange mavens.
