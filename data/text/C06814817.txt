2.~ SUMMARY: PRIME MINISTER OBUCHI EMPHASIZED TO DEPUTY
SECRETARY TALBOTT AND HIS DELEGATION ON FEBRUARY 16 THE
IMPORTANCE HE ATTACHED TO US-JAPAN RELATIONS IN GENERAL, AND
TO PRESIDENT CLINTON'S PARTICIPATION IN THE UPCOMING G8
SUMMIT IN PARTICULAR. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASSURED THE PM THE
US WOULD SUPPORT JAPAN'S EFFORTS TO STAGE A SUCCESSFUL
SUMMIT, WHILE NOTING WE HAD A NUMBER OF ISSUES TO ADDRESS
BEFOREHAND. THESE INCLUDED HOST NATION SUPPORT, AND THE
ATSUGI INCINERATOR PROBLEM; WE ALSO HOPED TO SEE FURTHER
PROGRESS BY JAPAN ON DEREGULATION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY

AND BRIEFLY REVIEWED US THINKING CONCERNING THE NEW RUSSIAN
LEADERSHIP, AND THE PRESIDENT'S UPCOMING SOUTH ASIA TRIP.
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND NSC DEPUTY STEINBERG STRESSED THE
IMPORTANCE OF THE G8 SUMMIT SENDING A POSITIVE MESSAGE ON
GLOBALIZATION AND FREE TRADE. OBUCHI APPLAUDED US EFFORTS TO
FURTHER IMPROVE RELATIONS WITH CHINA; HE REPORTED ON THE
SUCCESS OF HIS FIRST TRILATERAL MEETING IN MANILA WITH HIS
CHINESE AND SOUTH KOREAN COUNTERPARTS AND HOPED TO FURTHER
DEVELOP THIS DIALOG. HE ALSO HOPED TO FORGE A RELATIONSHIP
WITH NEW RUSSIAN LEADER PUTIN BETWEEN HIS LIKELY ELECTION AS
PRESIDENT IN MARCH AND THE G8 SUMMIT IN JULY. OBUCHI PLEDGED
HIS ADMINISTRATION WAS DOING ITS UTMOST TO SOLVE THE ATSUGI
INCINERATOR PROBLEM. END SUMMARY.
