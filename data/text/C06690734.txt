RELEASE IN FULL 
December 29, 199 
Hearty congratulations on your appointment as Deputy 
Secretary of State and welcome to a very exclusive club! Elliot 
Richardson has described it as the best job in Washington and I 
certainly agree. If I can ever help, you have only to ask. 
I know Cas Yost has written you about attending the 
Bilderberg conference in Helsinki on the June 2-5 weekend. I think in 
your new office this is even more important. I attended several times 
during my period in office and found it immensely helpful. 
With warm regards and best wishes from Nancy and from me. 
Sin ely,
