1.-{6-) SUMMARY: DURING THEIR OCTOBER 7 MEETING, FOREIGN MINISTER GRANIC ASSURED SPECIAL ADVISOR FOR DAYTON IMPLEMENTATION AMBASSADOR JAMES DOBBINS THAT HE EXPECTED "NO SURPRISES" FROM THE SUPREME COURT IN ITS HANDLING OF THE EXTRADITION CASE AGAINST MLADEN NALETILIC "TUTA", AND INDICATED THAT THE GOC HAD A PLAN ALREADY DEVELOPED TO DEAL WITH ANY MISCHIEF "THE LAWYERS" MIGHT MAKE AFTER THE COURTS DECISION. WHILE APPARENTLY RECEPTIVE TO THE POSSIBILITY OF WORKING ON TWO-WAY RETURNS WITH THE REPUBLIKA SRPSKA, GRANIC DECLINED TO COMMIT TO THREE-WAY TALKS, INDICATING HE FIRST WANTED TO ENSURE THAT "DODIK IS REALLY SERIOUS THIS TIME." AMB. DOBBINS RAISED THE ROLE OF GENERAL CESIC ROJS IN THE CROATIAN MILITARY GIVEN HIS CONTINUED ANTI-DAYTON ACTIVITIES IN BOSNIA, GRANIC MADE CLEAR THAT CESIC ROJS WAS A QUESTION FOR THE PRESIDENT'S OFFICE, DECLINING FIRMLY TO'TAKE ANY POSITION AT ALL. GRANIC PRESSED FOR ACTIVE U.S. ENGAGEMENT AT THE U.N. REGARDING THE SECRETARY GENERAL'S UPCOMING REPORT ON PREVLAKA AND A MODIFICATION OF THE BLUE ZONE, NOTING THAT THE GOC SHOULD NOT BE FURTHER PENALIZED FOR ITS SUPPORT OF U.S. POLICY VIS-A-VIS THE FRY. END SUMMARY.

ON THE TUTA CASE, GRANIC SAID THAT HE HAD TALKED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SUPREME COURT, AND DIRECTLY WITH PRESIDENT TUDJMAN TWICE. HE NOTED THAT THE SAKIC TRIAL HAD "GONE AS EXPECTED" AND HE HAD NO DOUBTS ABOUT THE SUPREME COURT'S HANDLING OF THE TUTA APPEAL. ONE SHOULD EXPECT MANEUVERING FROM THE LAWYERS ONCE THE COURT ISSUES ITS DECISION, PARTICULARLY MENTIONING A POSSIBLE APPEAL ON THE BASIS OF TUTA'S POOR HEALTH, GRANIC SAID, BUT HE REITERATED PREVIOUS STATEMENTS THAT IN THE CASE OF SUCH AN APPEAL, THE GOC WOULD IMMEDIATELY REQUEST AN ADVISORY MEDICAL OPINION FROM THE HAGUE. GRANIC EMPHASIZED MOREOVER, THAT MOST LEGAL SCHOLARS SUPPORTED THE INTERPRETATION THAT A CONSTITUTIONAL COURT APPEAL SHOULD NOT DELAY EXTRADITION, AND NOTING THAT PUBLIC SUPPORT IN FAVOR OF EXTRADITION OF TUTA WAS STRONG, SAID IT MUST TAKE PLACE SOON. 4. -(9} GRANIC NOTED THAT ELECTION PREPARATIONS WERE 
WELL UNDERWAY ON THE BASIS OF THE RULING PARTY'S 
PROPOSED ELECTION LAW AND HE SAID THE LAW WOULD BE 
FINALIZED DURING THE NEXT WEEK. GRANIC CONFIRMED PRESS 
REPORTS THAT SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE PAVLETIC WOULD INVITE 
THE OPPOSITION PARTIES TO JOIN THE RULING HDZ PARTY IN 
THE COMING WEEK IN A FORUM TO DISCUSS FAIR ACCESS TO THE 
MEDIA. HE MADE CLEAR THAT THE DATE OF THE ELECTIONS WAS 
STILL NOT DECIDED BY THE PRESIDENT AND WITHIN THE HDZ, 
BUT STATED UNEQUIVOCALLY THAT THEY WOULD BE HELD BETWEEN 
DECEMBER 15 AND THE 31. THE CHURCH IS NOT PLEASED WITH 
THE IDEA OF ELECTIONS BETWEEN CHRISTMAS AND NEW YEARS 
EVE -- APPARENTLY TUDJMAN'S FIRSTCHOICE -- SO THE PARTY 
REMAINS IN A QUANDRY ABOUT WHAT TO DO. A KEY QUESTION 
NOW BEING DEBATED IN LIGHT OF REPORTS OF TUDJMAN'S 
FAILING HEALTH, HE NOTED, WAS THE PROCESS OF SUCCESSION 
SHOULD THE PRESIDENT DIE OR BE INCAPACITATED AFTER THE 
PARLIAMENT IS DISSOLVED (NO LATER THAN NOV. 28), BUT 
BEFORE ELECTIONS ARE HELD. GRANIC INDICATED THAT THE 
GOC AND THE CONSTITUTIONAL COURT WOULD HAVE TO REACH 
CLOSURE ON THIS QUESTION BEFORE THE LAW IS FINALIZED. 
(NOTE: THE CONSTITUTION PROVIDES FOR THE PRESIDENT OF 
THE SABOR TO SUCCEED THE PRESIDENT AND CALL ELECTIONS 
WITHIN 60 DAYS. HOWEVER, ONCE DISSOLVED FOR ELECTIONS • 
THE PARLIAMENT'S MANDATE IS UP AND IT CANNOT LEGALLY BE 
RECONSTITUTED.

OF RESPONSIBILITY. AMB. DOBBINS RETORTED THAT WHEN 
CROATIAN GENERALS WERE OBSTRUCTING DAYTON IMPLEMENTATION
