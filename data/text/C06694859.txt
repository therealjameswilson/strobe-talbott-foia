SUBJECT: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MEETING WITH JAVIER PEREZ 
DE CUELLAR: JULY 29, 1994, WASHINGTON, D.C. 
DECLASSIFIED 
1. SUMMARY: IN A MEETING WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, 
JAVIER PEREZ DE CUELLAR SAID HE HAS NOT YET DECIDED TO RUN 
FOR THE PRESIDENCY IN PERU ALTHOUGH HE IS UNDER PRESSURE 
FROM THE OPPOSITION TO DO SO. HE EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT 
THE FAIRNESS OF THE ELECTORAL PROCESS IN PERU AND ASKED 
WHAT HELP THE U.S. MIGHT PROVIDE FOR THE ELECTIONS. HE 
ALSO SAID THAT THE OAS MONITORING IN PREVIOUS ELECTIONS 
HAD NOT BEEN AS GOOD AS EXPECTED. HE ASKED ABOUT A 
POSSIBLE UN-OAS AGREEMENT FOR ELECTION SUPPORT AND ALSO 
SAID HE WAS GOING TO INVITE PRESIDENT CARTER TO PUT 
TOGETHER A GROUP OF ELECTION MONITORS. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY REITERATED U.S. GOVERNMENT SUPPORT FOR FREE AND 
FAIR ELECTIONS AND OUTLINED THE TYPE OF SUPPORT THE U.S. 
WAS PREPARED TO PROVIDE. ON HAITI, PEREZ DE CUELLAR 
COMMENTED THAT, ALTHOUGH ARISTIDE WAS THE ELECTED LEADER 
AND, THEREFORE, HAD TO BE SUPPORTED HE BELIEVED THAT 
ARISTIDE SHOULD PERSONALLY DECIDE TO STEP DOWN "FOR THE 
GOOD OF HIS COUNTRY." END SUMMARY. 
PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS AND ELECTION ASSISTANCE 
2. DR. PEREZ DE CUELLAR OPENED BY ASKING FOR TALBOTT'S 
ASSESSMENT OF THE SITUATION IN PERU, SAYING THAT HE WAS 
UNDER CONSIDERABLE PRESSURE TO RUN FOR THE PRESIDENCY IN 
THE UPCOMING NATIONAL ELECTIONS, BUT THAT HE HAD NOT YET 
MADE UP HIS MIND. "IT IS A DECISION THAT NEEDS TIME FOR 
REFLECTION." 
3. PEREZ DE CUELLAR EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT THE HONESTY 
OF THE ELECTORAL PROCESS IN PERU. HE SAID THAT IT WAS 
IMPORTANT TO OBTAIN AS MUCH ASSISTANCE AS POSSIBLE FROM 
THE U.S. FOR THE ELECTIONS. HE DOES NOT BELIEVE OAS 
MONITORS WOULD BE AS EFFECTIVE AS GROUPS FROM THE UN OR 
THE CARTER CENTER. PEREZ DE CUELLAR INQUIRED WHETHER A 
UN/OAS ARRANGEMENT MIGHT BE POSSIBLE, SAYING THAT THE OAS 
DID NOT HAVE MANY RESOURCES TO PUT INTO ELECTION 
MONITORING. HE IS ALSO CONSIDERING INVITING PRESIDENT 
CARTER TO HEAD A DELEGATION TO OBSERVE THE ELECTIONS. 
PEREZ DE CUELLAR TOLD THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THAT OAS 
MONITORING OF THE THREE PREVIOUSLY NELD ELECTIONS (FOR THE 
CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY IN NOVEMBER 1992, MUNICIPAL ELECTIONS 
IN JANUARY 1993 AND THE CONSTITUTIONAL REFERENDUM IN 
NOVEMBER 1993) HAD NOT BEEN AS GOOD AS EXPECTED. "THE

TRANSPARENCY OF THE PROCESS AND THE EXTENT OF
