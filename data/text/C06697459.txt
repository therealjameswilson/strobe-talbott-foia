CHRISTOPHER AND AXWORTHY; NAMELY, THE ONGOING DISPUTE 
OVER SALMON FISHING IN THE PACIFIC, ESPECIALLY ALONG THE 
COASTLINE NEAR WASHINGTON STATE. 
5. -15+- DEPUTY FM SMITH NOTED THE CANADIAN DELEGATION 
WHICH VISITED WASHINGTON IN LATE MARCH RAISED THIS ISSUE 
WITH NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR LAKE AND SECRETARY 
CHRISTOPHER. THIS WAS FOLLOWED BY A CONVERSATION LAST 
WEEK BETWEEN ASSISTANT SECRETARY-DESIGNATE FOR EUROPEAN 
AFFAIRS JOHN KORNBLUM AND CANADIAN AMBASSADOR RAYMOND 
CHRETIEN IN WHICH KORNBLUM REPORTED OUR CONCLUSION THAT 
THERE IS NO LEGAL BASIS TO ARBITRATE THE DISPUTE. SUCH 
A POSITION IS NEITHER VALID NOR ACCEPTABLE IN THE 
CANADIAN VIEW. WITH THE FISHING SFASON ABOUT TO BEGIN 
IN TEN DAYS, IT IS IMPERATIVE THAT BOTH GOVERNMENTS MOVE 
TO DEVELOP AN ACCEPTABLE RESPONSE ON THIS ISSUE. 
HOWEVER, IT SHOULD BE NOTED THAT UNLESS THE U.S. AND 
CANADIAN NEGOTIATORS CAN MOVE BEYOND WHAT A/S-DESIGNATE 
KORNBLUM AND AMBASSADOR CHRETIEN DISCUSSED, GROWING 
DOMESTIC PRESSURE WILL FORCE THE CANADIAN GOVERNMENT TO 
INITIATE ACTIONS DESIGNED TO HARASS AMERICAN FISHERMAN 
IN DISPUTED WATERS OF THE PACIFIC, A STEP DEPUTY FM 
SMITH INDICATED HIS GOVERNMENT IS RELUCTANT TO TAKE. 
WITH PROVINCIAL ELECTIONS IN BRITISH COLUMBIA AND 
INCREASING POPULAR OPPOSITION TO THE ACTIONS OF U.S. 
FISHERMEN, THE CANADIAN GOVERNMENT WILL HAVE NO CHOICE 
BUT TO MAKE THE SITUATION DIFFICULT FOR AMERICAN FISHING 
BOATS IN THE REGION. 
6. -411- DEPUTY FM SMITH NOTED THIS WAS THE FIRST TIME 
HE HAD EVER SPOKEN WITH THE ACTING SECRETARY CONCERNING 
AN ONGOING U.S.-CANADIAN PROBLEM. NOTING THE SHARED 
CONCERNS OF BOTH SIDES IN LIGHT OF UPCOMING NATIONAL 
ELECTIONS IN THE UNITED STATES AND PROVINCIAL ELECTIONS 
IN BRITISH COLUMBIA, DEPUTY FM SMITH INDICATED MEMBERS 
OF HIS GOVERNMENT HAVE EVEN RAISED THE POSSIBILITY OF 
CANCELING THE UPCOMING NANOOSE SUBMARINE EXERCISES, AN 
ACTION WHICH THE CANADIAN GOVERNMENT HAS DECIDED NOT TO 
PURSUE. HOWEVER, WITHOUT SOME FORM OF COMPROMISE BY THE 
AMERICAN SIDE, THE CANADIAN GOVERNMENT WILL INITIATE 
HARASSMENT ACTION AGAINST AMERICAN FISHING BOATS. 
7.-111 THE DEPUTY FM CONTINUED THAT THE PROBLEM IS THE 
USG HAS FAILED TO UNDERSTAND THE POLITICAL SENSITIVITY 
OF THIS ISSUE. THIS WAS PARTICULARLY EVIDENT IN THE 
RECENT KORNBLUM-CHRETIEN CONVERSATION. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY ASSURED DEPUTY FM SMITH HE WOULD'ENSURE A 
PROMPT RESPONSE TO THE CANADIAN CONCERNS, NOTING HE 
FULLY UNDERSTOOD THE URGENCY, THE HIGH LEVEL ATTENTION 
AND THE POLITICAL SENSITIVITY OF THIS MATTER. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY NOTED HE HAD JUST RETURNED FROM A TRIP 
WITH PRESIDENT CLINTON AND, HENCE, WAS NOT FULLY 
APPRISED OF ALL THE FACTS CONCERNING THE FISHING ISSUE 
AND THE KORNBLUM-CHRETIEN CONVERSATION. PROMISING TO 
FLAG THIS ISSUE FOR BOTH A/S-DESIGNATE KORNBLUM AND 
UNDER SECRETARY PETER TARNOFF, THE ACTING SECRETARY 
ADVISED DEPUTY FM SMITH HE WOULD CONTACT HIM AGAIN 
SHORTLY THROUGH THE CANADIAN EMBASSY IN WASHINGTON OR 
VIA A DIRECT CALL TO THE MINISTRY IN OTTAWA. 
8. -R:0- DEPUTY FM SMITH CONVEYED HIS REGRET AT NOT 
HAVING SEEN THE ACTING SECRETARY IN MOSCOW. DEPUTY FM
SMITH NOTED YELTSIN LOOKED VERY WELL, GIVING AN 
EXCELLENT PERFORMANCE AT THE SUMMIT. HOWEVER, ZYUGANOV 
CONTINUES TO LEAD AND WILL BE A FORMIDABLE OPPONENT IN 
THE UPCOMING ELECTION. 
9. -4464- THE ACTING SECRETARY AGREED, NOTING AT THE 
PRESIDENT'S MEETING AT SPASO HOUSE ON APRIL 21 WITH 
REGIONAL LEADERS AND PROVINCIAL GOVERNORS AS WELL AS 
OPPOSITION LEADERS, IT WAS THE AMERICAN SIDE'S 
IMPRESSION THAT, WHILE REFORMERS AND CENTRISTS ARE 
BEGINNING TO COALESCE, THEY ARE DOING SO LESS IN SUPPORT 
OF YELTSIN THAN IN OPPOSITION TO ZYUGANOV. DESPITE THE 
COMMON INTEREST OF BOTH THE U.S. AND CANADIAN
