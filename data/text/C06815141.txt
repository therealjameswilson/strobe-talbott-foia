SUBJECT: S. IVANOV: "I AM READY: YOU SHOULD SEE THE
TWO VOLUMES THEY HAVE GIVEN ME. I HAVE A LETTER."
CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR JAMES F. COLLINS. REASONS:
1.5 (B) AND (D) .
1. ~ FOLLOWING A BRIEFING BY SERGEY IVANOV FOR THE
DIPLOMATIC CORPS ON RUSSIA'S SECURITY CONCEPT, HE ASKED
ME TO JOIN HIM AND STAFF BRIEFLY IN THE BACK ROOM. THE
MEETING WAS IN RESPONSE TO MY REQUEST TO FOLLOW UP ON
POINTS FROM WASHINGTON REGARDING HIS VISIT.
2. ~ I WENT OVER WITH HIM THE OUTLINE OF THE
SCHEDULE FAXED BY WASHINGTON MONDAY NIGHT'. I NOTED
THAT THERE WAS A ONE-ON-ONE BREAKFAST THURSDAY MORNING
WITH YOU AND A SMALL GROUP LUNCH YOU WOULD HOST. I
THEN SKETCHED OUT THE OTHER ELEMENTS OF THE SCHEDULE
WITHOUT DETAILS AS TO TIME, BUT INDICATING ON WHICH DAY
THE MAJOR ELEMENTS OF THE SCHEDULE WOULD OCCUR. IVANOV
RESPONDED THAT THE SCHEDULE SOUNDED VERY GOOD TO HIM.
HE HOPED THERE WOULD BE A LITTLE TIME TO SEE SOMETHING
OF WASHINGTON AS HE HAS NEVER BEEN THERE. (NOTE.: IF
THERE IS-ANY WAY TO INCLUDE A BIT OF SIGHT SEEING
HOSTED EVEN BY OUR SIDE, I THINK IT WOULD STRIKE A
RESPONSIVE CORD.}

3. ~ I NOTED THAT THE ISSUE OF PROLIFERATION WOULD
BE THE ISSUE FOR THURSDAY EUENING'S PLENARY AND
UNDERSCORED THE SPECIAL IMPORTANCE OF THIS PART OF THE
AGENDA FOR THE VISIT. I THEN ASKED WHETHER IT WOULD BE
POSSIBLE TO ARRANGE SIDE MEETINGS FOR KOPTEV AND RAPOTA
WITH THEIR COUNTERPARTS TO GO OVER DETAILS ON THE
MISSILE AND IRAN ISSUES. IUANOV RESPONDED THAT THIS
SHOULD BE POSSIBLE TO DO WHEN HE IS IN THE ONE-ON-ONE
WITH YOU OR ANOTHER RESTRICTED SESSION THAT WOULD NOT
INVOLUE KOPTEV AND RAPOTA. , HE REMINDED THAT. HE IS NOT
BRINGING EXPERTS TO ADDRESS NUCLEAR PROLIFERATION
ISSUES, HOWEVER; THAT HIS GUIDANCE AND MATERIAL WILL BE
IN RESPONSE TO THE LETTER YOU SENT TO HIM. I SAID I
WOULD ENSURE THERE IS NO CONFUSION 'ON THIS SCORE, AND
THAT I KNEW THERE WAS SERIOUS INTEREST IN HAVING THE
SESSIONS WITH KOPTEV AND COMPANY AS EARLY AS POSSIBLE
DURING THE UISIT.
4. — 4g-IVANOV THEN SAID "I HAVE A LETTER FOR PRESIDENT
CLINTON FROM (PUTIN)." AN AIDE CHIMED IN AT THAT
POINT, SAYING HE WONDERED IF THERE MIGHT BE AN
OPPORTUNITY FOR A PROTOCOL MEETING WITH THE PRESIDENT
TO DELIVER IT. I GAVE NO ASSURANCES, BUT SAID I KNEW.
THE LETTER WOULD BE WELCOME AND THAT AS WE ALL KNOW
SCHEDULES ARE UNKNOWN THINGS FOR PRESIDENTS BUT THAT
ASSISTANTS TO PRESIDENTS HAVE THEIR WAYS OF DEALING
WITH SUCH ISSUES. (NOTE: IT IS QUITE CLEAR THAT SERGEY
BORISOVICH IS VERY MUCH HOPING TO HAND THIS LETTER TO
THE PRESIDENT HIMSELF. IF THAT IS A POSSIBILITY, IT
AGAIN IS SOMETHING, I SUSPECT, THAT WILL LEAVE THE
BRIGHT AND BEST IMPRESSION).
5. ~ IVANOV LOOKS A BIT TIRED. ON AT LEAST THREE
OCCASIONS HE REFERRED TO THE TWO LARGE VOLUMES OF
BRIEFING MATERIAL PREPARED FOR HIM. HE SAID RATHER
WISTFULLY THAT HE HAD NOT YET STARTED READING, HIS
COMMENTS ON THE, SCHEDULE SUGGESTED THAT HE IS GIRDED
FOR A SET OF PRESENTATIONS MEANT TO CONVERT HIS
THINKING ON ITEMS LIKE MISSILE DEFENSE (THE BRIEFING IN
THE TANK) OR IRAN (AT THE AGENCY) . I TOLD HIM THAT THE
BRIEFINGS WERE CERTAINLY GOING TO TOUCH THESE ISSUES
BUT WERE GOING TO BE BROADER IN NATURE. THEY WOULD
COVER OUR SENSE OF CHALLENGES WE WILL BOTH FACE IN THE
FUTURE AND OUR PERCEPTION OF GLOBAL THREATS. REGARDING
HIS MEETINGS AT LANGLEY HE ALSO SAID HE HOPED THERE
WOULD BE AN OPPORTUNITY FOR AN EXCHANGE ON THE RANGE OF
INTELLIGENCE COOPERATION ISSUES.
