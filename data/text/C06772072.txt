PARTICIPANTS:THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND AMBASSADOR USHAKOV 
4.--(-8.} THE DEPUTY SECRETARY BRIEFED USHAKOV ON INFORMATION 
HE HAD RECEIVED ABOUT AN EXPLOSION IN YEKATERINBURG. A 
CAR BOMB EXPLODED IN FRONT OF THE BUILDING HOUSING THE 
CONSULATE GENERAL AT APPROXIMATELY 0400 LOCAL TIME APRIL 
24 (1800 EDT APRIL 23). NO AMERICANS WERE BELIEVED TO BE 
INJURED IN THE BLAST, ALTHOUGH WINDOWS IN THE BUILDING 
WERE BLOWN OUT AND A LARGE CRATER LEFT IN THE PARKING LOT. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED THE USG HAD NO COMPLAINTS 
REGARDING COOPERATION FROM LOCAL AUTHORITIES, WHOM HE 
UNDERSTOOD HAD RESPONDED PROMPTLY AND TAKEN APPROPRIATE 
MEASURES. THE CONSULATE GENERAL HAD RECEIVED A SERIOUS 
BOMB THREAT WHEN THE NATO AIRSTRIKES AGAINST YUGOSLAVIA 
BEGAN IN MARCH, PROVIDING CIRCUMSTANTIAL EVIDENCE THAT 
THIS INCIDENT MAY HAVE BEEN AIMED AT USG INTERESTS. HE 
SUGGESTED IT WOULD BE HELPFUL IF USHAKOV COULD SEND A 
MESSAGE TO MOSCOW EXPRESSING THE USG'S DEEP CONCERN AT 
THIS SERIOUS AND POTENTIALLY TRAGIC INCIDENT. USHAKOV 
SAID HE WAS UNAWARE OF THE INCIDENT AND THANKED THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY FOR THE BRIEFING. HE PROMISED TO ALERT MOSCOW 
IMMEDIATELY AND TO DO ALL HE COULD TO ASSIST IN THIS 
MATTER. 
5. —erzi- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD USHAKOV NATIONAL SECURITY 
ADVISOR BERGER HAD BEEN SATISFIED WITH HIS MEETING' WITH 
THE AMBASSADOR, AND THAT HE UNDERSTOOD THERE HAD BEEN 
EQUALLY POSITIVE MEETINGS WITH AMBASSADOR SESTANOVICH AND 
MS. NULAND. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OFFERED TO MEET USHAKOV 
APRIL 26 TO PROVIDE A READOUT OF THE,SUMMIT PROCEEDINGS. 
THE ALLIES HAD DISCUSSED AT LENGTH OUSSIAN DIPLOMACY ON 
KOSOVO. THE OVERALL TENOR WAS SUPPORTIVE OF MOSCOW'S 
INITIATIVES, W 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY HOPED RUSSIAN DIPLOMACY WOULD SUPPORT 
ALLIANCE OBJECTIVES. 
6. 4-81 USHAKOV AGREED TO MEET THE DEPUTY SECRETARY APRIL 
26 AND SAID THE RUSSIANS WERE STILL STUDYING NATO 
STATEMENTS COMING OUT OF THE SUMMIT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
OFFERED TO PROVIDE MORE COMPLETE INFORMATION AT THEIR 
MEETING. HE REGRETTED RUSSIA WAS ABSENT FROM THE SUMMIT, 
BUT UNDERSTOOD THE GOR'S REASONS FOR NOT TAKING PART. 
7. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 2123 EDT.
