2. SUMMARY: DURING A MEETING WITH DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT AND H/S DELEGATION DECEMBER 15, 
PRESIDENTIAL FOREIGN POL/CY ADV/SOR DMITRY 
RYURIKOV EXPLAINED YELTS/N'S REACTION TO THE NAC 
COMMUNIQUE ON NATO EXPANSION AS THAT OF A
