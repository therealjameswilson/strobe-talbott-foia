5. .4.%)- ROMANIAN FOREIGN MINISTER MELESCANU CALLED THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY WITH PRELIMINARY RESULTS OF ROMANIA'S 
NOVEMBER 3 PARLIAMENTARY AND PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS. HE SAID THE GOVERNMENT WOULD RELEASE OFFICIAL FIGURES ON 
NOVEMBER 7, BUT THE FINAL OUTCOME WAS FAIRLY CERTAIN. 
THE OPPOSITION DEMOCRATIC CONVENTION (CDR) APPEARED TO HAVE WON 30 PERCENT OF THE SEATS IN PARLIAMENT, 
PRESIDENT ILIESCU'S RULING SOCIAL DEMOCRATS 23 PERCENT, 
FORMER PM ROMAN'S UNION OF SOCIAL DEMOCRATS (DP-FSN) 13 
PERCENT, THE DEMOCRATIC UNION OF HUNGARIANS IN ROMANIA 
(UDMR) SEVEN PERCENT, THE NATIONAL UNITY PARTY OF 
ROMANIA 4.5 PERCENT, AND THE GREATER ROMANIA PARTY 4.3 
PERCENT. ONLY THESE SIX PARTIES WOULD BE REPRESENTED IN 
PARLIAMENT. 
6. —(-51 IN DISCUSSIONS WITH HIS COLLEAGUES, MELESCANU 
SAID THERE WAS A CLEAR ACCEPTANCE OF ELECTION RESULTS / 
DESPITE SOME MINOR PROBLEMS. HOWEVER, SUCH 
IRREGULARITIES WOULD NOT AFFECT THE OUTCOME. HE SAID 
THE NEW GOVERNMENT WOULD TAKE POWER IN EARLY DECEMBER 
AND BE IN OFFICE BY DECEMBER 15. MELESCANU ASSURED THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY THAT HIS PARTY HAD ACCEPTED THE CDR'S 
NEW ROLE IN PARLIAMENT. HE SAID THE CDR APPEARED TO 
HAVE ADOPTED A CONSTRUCTIVE POSITION ON FOREIGN AFFAIRS 
WHICH SUPPORTED CONTINUITY, ESPECIALLY ON NATO 
ENLARGEMENT AND EUROPEAN UNION ACCESSION ISSUES.

7. -69i- MELESCANU REGRETTED THAT HE COULD NOT PROVIDE 
POSSIBLE NAMES OF HIS SUCCESSOR AS NEGOTIATIONS STILL 
CONTINUED. HOWEVER, HE REITERATED THAT STNNG SUPPORT 
EXISTED IN THE PARLIAMENT FOR A CONTINUATION OF CURRENT 
FOREIGN POLICY GOALS. HE ASKED WHETHER A PUBLIC 
REAFFIRMATION OF ROMANIA'S INTENTIONS OR A VISIT BY A 
JOINT DELEGATION TO WASHINGTON WAS NEEDED. MELESCANU 
PROMISED TO RAISE THE ISSUE WITH AMBASSADOR MOSES. 
8. -(-8-}- ALTHOUGH THE DIFFERENCES BETWEEN CANDIDATES 
REMAINED NARROW, MELESCANU SAID THAT ILIESCU APPEARED TO 
HAVE A GOOD CHANCE OFrWINNING AS HE HAD A CLEAR, STATED 
POLICY. MELESCANU SAID PREDICTIONS CONCERNING THE OTHER 
CANDIDATES WERE MORE DIFFICULT. HE NOTED CONSTANTINESCU 
LACKED EXPERIENCE AND HIS INTENTIONS AND GOALS WERE 
UNCLEAR. ALTHOUGH CONSTANTINESCU'S PUBLIC STATEMENTS 
HAD BEEN POSITIVE, MELESCANU COULD OFFER NO FURTHER 
OPINIONS. MELESCANU SUGGESTED THAT POLLS AND 
DEVELOPMENTS IN THE COMING DAYS WOULD OFFER A BETTER 
PICTURE. 
9. -ttl- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THANKED MELESCANU FOR HIS 
VIEWS, ADDING THAT HE HAD PREVIOUSLY INTERVIEWED FORMER 
PRESIDENT CEAUCESCU, WHEN THE DEPUTY SECRETARY YEARS AGO 
TRAVELED IN THE REGION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE 
COULD NEVER HAVE KNOWN THEN THAT HE WOULD HAVE THE 
PLEASURE OF TALKING WITH A FOREIGN MINISTER OF ROMANIA 
WHO HAD GARNERED SUCH IMMENSE RESPECT IN WASHINGTON, 
ESPECIALLY AFTER THE COUNTRY'S FREE AND FAIR ELECTIONS. 
HE NOTED THAT BOTH THE U.S. AND ROMANIA HAD JUST 
COMPLETED DEMOCRATIC EXERCISES. ALTHOUGH THE U.S. HAD 
MORE PRACTICE, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY APPLAUDED ROMANIA 
FOR ITS EFFORTS IN TERMS OF THE PROCESS. WHILE THE U.S. 
DOES NOT COMMENT ON THE OUTCOME OF FOREIGN ELECTIONS, 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID ROMANIAN ELECTIONS APPEARED TO 
HAVE GONE SMOOTHLY. ROMANIAN DEMOCRATIC INSTITUTIONS 
APPEARED ALIVE AND WELL. HE WAS GLAD TO HEAR 
MELESCANU'S PROGNOSIS FOR CONTINUITY DESPITE CHANGES IN 
RATIOS AND PERSONALITIES, AND FORESAW GOOD PROSPECTS FOR 
CONTINUED COOPERATION ON BILATERAL AND MULTILATERAL 
ENDEAVORS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY PLEDGED TO WORK CLOSELY 
WITH MELESCANU'S SUCCESSOR.

10. —(-M- WHEN ASKED ABOUT HIS FUTURE ROLE IN 
PARLIAMENT, MELESCANU NOTED THAT HIS ELECTION TO THE 
SENATE FROM A DISTRICT NEAR BUCHAREST WAS NEARLY 
CONFIRMED. HE PLANNED TO STAY INVOLVED IN FOREIGN 
AFFAIRS AND WELCOMED THE CHANCE TO OBSERVE THE PROCESS 
FROM THE OTHER SIDE. HE URGED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TO 
CALL ON HIM IF NEEDED. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REPLIED 
THAT HE LOOKED FORWARD TO WELCOMING MELESCANU TO 
WASHINGTON IN THE FUTURE. ON MORE PRACTICAL ISSUES OF 
THE TRANSITION, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED MELESCANU TO 
ADVISE AMBASSADOR MOSES OF THEIR DISCUSSION, NOTING THAT 
WASHINGTON AWAITED THE EMBASSY'S ADVICE AND GUIDANCE. 
11. —(7ft MELESCANU THANKED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND 
SAID HE LOOKED FORWARD TO MEETING.WITH HIM IN HIS FUTURE 
CAPACITY. HE TOLD THE DEP.UTY SECRETARY THAT HE HAD 
OFFERED HIS SERVICES TO HIS SUCCESSOR TO ENSURE A SMOOTH 
TRANSITION.
