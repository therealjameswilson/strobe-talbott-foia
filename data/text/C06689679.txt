RELEASE IN FULL 
Dear Mr. Andrews: 
President Clinton recently shared with me the March 25 
letter you sent him advancing the idea of establishing a 
telemedicine satellite link between the Texas Children's . 
Hospital and the Tyumen Province in Western Siberia. As you 
may know, through our on-going Hospital Partnership Program we 
are extending and improving healthcare services throughout the 
NIS. Your proposal is an intriguing one and I have forwarded 
it to U.S.A.I.D. NIS Task Force for review. Until we are 
certain about future year funding levels for assistance 
programs in the NIS, I cannot speculate on the extent to which 
we will be able to support new proposals such as the 
telemedicine satellite link. I encourage you to continue your 
efforts to promote this concept. 
If I may be of further assistance, please do not hesitate 
to contact me. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador At Large and 
Special Adviser to The Secretary 
On the New Independent States 
The Honorable 
Michael A. Andrews, 
House of Representatives.
