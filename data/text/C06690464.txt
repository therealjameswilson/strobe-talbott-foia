RELEASE IN FULL 
PERSONAL AND CONFIDENTIAL 
March 1, 1993 
To: Tony Lake 
From,: Strobe Talbott 
Subject: Policy Steering Group on the Former Soviet Union 
Tony, I think we're off to a fairly good start on 
this effort. Toby and I will be getting to you shortly a memo 
for the President reporting on the progress that we're making 
in assembling options for the summit. 
As always with such ventures, we're learning -- and 
fine-tuning -- as we go. One thing I've learned is that it 
would help immensely in assuring the smooth and productive 
functioning of the Group if there were to be a presidential 
charter, to be promulgated as soon as I'm confirmed. Attached 
is a draft. I've discussed it with Peter, and he agrees this 
is the way to go. 
Provision No. 2 -- the one about my representing the 
President -- is intended to deal with a problem that has al-
ready come up several times. Some participants in the group 
(one in particular) are acutely sensitive to what might be 
called inter-department parity; they keep trying to "count" 
me as a representative of the State Department and thus to 
limit others from those foggy precincts. It would serve a 
couple of interrelated purposes if it were explicit that, 
when sitting in the chair of the group, I'm representing the 
President or, alternatively, yourself. The point should be 
that I'm there in a WH-blessed interagency capacity.
