Dear Mr. Talbott: 
July 21, 1993 
RELEASE IN FULL 
It was a great and most memorable pleasure to meet you at the recent 
International Club reception and to hear your supportive words for 
World Federalism. Our mutual friend Norman Cousins would have been 
immensely pleased with your message. 
An idea has just occurred to me: if President Clinton would invite _ 
the Stockholm Initiative members to hold the next meeting of their 
International Commission in Washington and to be his guests at the 
White House, it could be one of the best moves yet to strenthen world 
ties as well as to bring the need for UN Charter revision to public 
attention in this country. 
Would you be willing to make this suggestion to him? 
Thank you again, Mr. Talbott, for what you are and for all the good 
work you are doing. 
Most sincerely. 
Cleo Michelsen
