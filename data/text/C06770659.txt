5. 444 THE ACTING SECRETARY ASSURED FISCHER HE TOOK THE 
ISSUE VERY SERIOUSLY AND RECOGNIZED THE IMPORTANCE OF THE 
SITUATION TO THE GERMAN GOVERNMENT. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
TOLD FISCHER THAT FOLLOWING THEIR EARLIER CONVERSATION HE 
HAD DISPATCHED HIS STAFF TO ENSURE ALL APPROPRIATE ACTION 
HAD BEEN TAKEN AT THE DEPARTMENT OF STATE AND THE 
DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE. 
6. -(-211 FISCHER EXPRESSED HIS CONCERN A REQUEST FOR A STAY 
OF EXECUTION MIGHT NOT REACH THE GOVERNOR OF ARIZONA 
BEFORE THE SCHEDULED EXECUTION. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
EXPLAINED HE WOULD NOT BE IN TOUCH WITH THE GOVERNOR 
BECAUSE IT WOULD BE COUNTERPRODUCTIVE. HOWEVER, HE 
PROMISED FISCHER HE WOULD CONTACT HIS COUNTERPART AT THE 
DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE TO ENSURE HE WAS AWARE OF THE 
'COURT'S APPEAL AND THE FRG'S REQUEST.
