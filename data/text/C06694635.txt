2. SUMMARY: IN HIS JANUARY 28-29 VISIT TO 
CAMBODIA, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT EXPRESSED USG 
SUPPORT AND ADMIRATION FOR THE PROCESS OF CHANGE 
IN CAMBODIA. HE TOLD THE CAMBODIANS THAT THE 
USG WILL CONTINUE TO PROVIDE ASSISTANCE TO THE 
ROYAL CAMBODIAN GOVERNMENT (RCG) AS LONG AS THE 
COUNTRY REMAINS HEADED IN THE RIGHT DIRECTION. 
DEVELOPMENT OF DEMOCRATIC INSTITUTIONS AND 
FREEDOM OF EXPRESSION, HE SAID, ARE KEY TO 
CONTINUED PROGRESS. CAMBODIAN LEADERS, 
INCLUDING KING SIHANOUK, THE TWO PRIME 
MINISTERS, AND THE FOREIGN MINISTER ALL MADE THE 
POINT THAT, WHILE MANY PROBLEMS REMAIN TO BE 
SOLVED, STABILITY IN THE COUNTRY HAS IMPROVED 
GREATLY AND THE KHMER ROUGE (KR) ARE LARGELY A 
SPENT FORCE, REDUCED TO BANDITRY. SOME 
PARLIAMENTARIANS AND NGO WORKERS COMPLAINED'THAT 
THE GOVERNMENT DOES NOT TOLERATE OPPOSITION 
VIEWS, BUT OTHERS URGED US TO HAVE PATIENCE WITH 
THE NEW DEMOCRACY, SAYING THAT THE SITUATION 
WILL IMPROVE OVER TIME AS LONG AS THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY CONTINUES TO PROVIDE 
SUPPORT. CAMBODIAN OFFICIALS PRESSED FOR THE 
PASSAGE OF MFN. END SUMMARY. 
3. DURING HIS TRIP TO CAMBODIA ON JANUARY 
28-29, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH KING 
SIHANOUK, WITH THE TWO PRIME MINISTERS IN A 
JOINT MEETING, AND WITH FOREIGN MINISTER UNG 
HUOT. HE ALSO ATTENDED A DINNER HOSTED BY UNG 
HUOT AND A LUNCH HOSTED BY AMBASSADOR TWINING 
FOR PARLIAMENTARIANS, CAMBODIAN NGO LEADERS, AND 
OTHER INFLUENTIAL CAMBODIANS OUTSIDE THE 
GOVERNMENT. IN THESE MEETINGS, THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY EXPRESSED ADMIRATION FOR CAMBODIA'S 
SUCCESS IN PUTTING ITS TRAGIC PAST BEHIND IT. 
THE PROCESS OF CHANGE MAY TAKE SOME TIME, HE 
SAID, BUT AS LONG AS CAMBODIA CONTINUES TO MOVE 
IN THE RIGHT DIRECTION, THE USG WILL PROVIDE 
SUPPORT. AMBASSADOR LORD TOLD THE CAMBODIANS 
THAT, WHILE FORMIDABLE PROBLEMS REMAIN, THE 
SITUATION NOW IS BETTER THAN IT EVER HAS BEEN IN 
HIS LONG ASSOCIATION WITH CAMBODIA. HE SAID 
CAMBODIANS HAVE DISPROVED THE MYTH THAT ASIANS 
DO NOT SEEK FREEDOM AND DEMOCRACY. NSC SENIOR 
ASIA DIRECTOR STANLEY ROTH SAID CAMBODIA IS A 
SUCCESS STORY WHICH PROVES THAT INTERNATIONAL 
PEACEKEEPING EFFORTS CAN WORK.
