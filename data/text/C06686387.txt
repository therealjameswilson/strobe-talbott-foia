3. Fincom Group 
Fincom Group is a W&Co proposed, new, state-of-the-art, 
industrial/commercial/financial holding company. Its headquarters 
is Moscow. From this central base it will create over time some 9-
12 majority-owned Russian Operating Companies in defined key 
economic development sectors in the Russian Federation. Both Fincom 
Group and each of its Operating Companies are majority private 
companies, some with minority public participation. They are 
designed to create viable new business operations in priority 
economic sectors, as a stimulus to expanded free markets in the 
Russian Federation. Each of the Operating Companies is to be a 
long-term member of Fincom Group. Each is to be built up, from an 
initial small to medium-size entity, into one of the top 1-4 
national leaders in its market sector, and an example to other 
private firms in the Russian Federation.

4. Investing Institutions 
Fincom Group is to be co-owned by a grouping of Russian and 
U.S. financial institutions, the majority private. This consists 
of: (i) 3 major U.S. Banks through their investment affiliates, 
(ii) 2 major Russian Federation Semi-Official Financial Insti-
tutions, (iii) the U.S. Government's publicly-funded but privately-
managed Russian-American Enterprise Fund (collectively the 6 
Investing Institutions), and (iv) Fincom Group's original founder 
Wirth & Co (W&Co). 
The 6 Investing Institutions above will be the senior equity 
investors in Fincom Group. They will each have a Senior and 
Preferred ownership position. Wirth & Co is the founder, present 
100% owner, and sole original venturerequity investor in Fincom 
Group. W&Co agrees to dilute its 100% ownership, and become the 25% 
minority owner and junior investor to the Institutions - in order 
to facilitate the Private Placement financing. 
The 6 Investing Institutions will together purchase the U.S. 
$100 million Senior Equity Units in the Private Placement. Each 
Investing Institution will be represented on the Board of Directors 
of Fincom Group along with W&Co. Each Investing Institution will 
play an active role in building the Group. The purpose is to create 
an outstanding mix of international and Russian, private and public 
Institutions - all linked together in a common Russian industry-
building effort.

Fincom Group's symbolic and leadership value at this delicate 
turning point in Russia's move to freedom, is what is of overriding 
immediate importance. 
In addition to this immediate symbolic value, Fincom Group 
offers major longer-term economic benefits to both the U.S. and the 
Russian Federation. These are analysed in the Placement Memorandum. 
W&Co presents the Summary of these long-term economic benefits 
below: 
"(1) Fincom Group is already fully structured by 
W&Co. It requires no multiyear bricks and mortar 
construction to get underway. No massive plant and 
staffing startup cycle. It has everything in place and 
ready to go, immediately after this Private Placement, 
financing. So it can be executed with no delays, and 
basically at once. It can demonstrate immediate U.S. and 
Russian Government action and immediate private action
