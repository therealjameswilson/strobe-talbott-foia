Since coming to work for you, I have 
read --and written -- many, many thousands of 
words on US-Russian relations. Hundreds of 
documents have passed before my eyes; dozens 
have rolled out of my wordprocessor (many of 
them addressed to you). It is no exaggeration 
to say that your night note to the President on 
your Vladivostok conversation is perhaps the 
single most trenchant and insightful -- moreover: 
useful and encouraging -- that I've seen. You 
advanced both conceptually and operationally on 
the points that Toby, Jim and I were impressing 
upon you in our various, pre-Vladivostok musings. 
Until reading your night note, I hadn't seen 
clearly what Yeltsin and Kozyrev are trying to do 
with their foreign policy vis a vis their 
domestic constituents and critics. Your note also 
implies some guidelines for our own approach from 
here on out that make very good sense and will 
increase the chances of long-term success. A11
