SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MEETING WITH FRENCH MOD 
OFFICIALS, FEBRUARY 3, 1999 
RELEASE IN FULL 
DECLASSIFIED 
CLASSIFIED BY DEPUTY CHIEF OF MISSION W. ROBERT PEARSON FOR 
REASON 1.5 (B/D). 
1. (C) SUMMARY: IN A FEBRUARY 3 MEETING WITH FRENCH MOD 
OFFICALS, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPLAINED THAT THE USG HAD NOT 
YET TAKEN DECISIONS ON'COMMITTING GROUND FORCES TO A POSSIBLE 
KOSOVO IMPLEMENTATION FORCE, ASSUMING THE RAMBOUILLET TALKS 
YIELD AN AGREEMENT. HE SUGGESTED THAT KOSOVO WILL BE AN 
EXCELLENT OPPORTUNITY FOR THE EUROPEANS TO DEMONSTRATE ESDI IN 
PRACTICE. HE AND DAS ASMUS STRESSED THE NEED TO FIND COMMON 
GROUND ON NATO SUMMIT LANGUAGE REGARDING THE NATO/UN 
RELATIONSHIP, SUGGESTING THE ORIGINAL WASHINGTON TREATY 
LANGUAGE COULD BEST SERVE AS THE STARTING POINT FOR 
DISCUSSION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CRITIQUED THE LACK OF 
TRANSPARENCY SURROUNDING THE ST. MALO DISCUSSIONS ON ESDI BUT 
REITERATED OVERALL U.S. SUPPORT FOR THE CONCEPT. THE MOD, IN 
A SHORT EXPLANATION OF ITS DEFENSE INDUSTRIAL PHILOSOPHY, SAID 
IT SUPPORTS FRENCH COMPANIES PARTNERING WITH U.S. COMPANIES. 
END SUMMARY. 
2. -IFE)- AT A FEBRUARY 3 MEETING AT THE FRENCH MOD, DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT RECOUNTED TO CABINET DIRECTOR DOMINIQUE 
COMBLES DE NAYVES AND DIPLOMATIC COUNSELOR JACQUES AUDIBERT 
HIS TALKS EARLIER THAT DAY WITH QUAI AND ELYSEE OFFICIALS 
(SEPTELS), NOTED PREPARATIONS FOR THE UPCOMING RAMBOUILLET 
TALKS AND ENCOURAGED THE FRENCH TO TRY TO INFLUENCE THE 
ALBANIAN/KOSOVAR DIASPORA IN FRANCE TO, IN TURN, PRESSURE THE 
UCK TO PARTICIPATE SERIOUSLY IN PEACE EFFORTS. 
NO DECISION YET ON COMMITING U.S. GROUND TROOPS TO KOSOVO 
3. (C) RESPONDING TO FRENCH QUESTIONING, THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY EXPLAINED THAT NO DECISION HAS YET BEEN TAKEN BY THE 
USG REGARDING WHETHER U.S. GROUND TROOPS WOULD BE COMMITTED TO 
A KOSOVO IMPLEMENTATION FORCE, SHOULD THE RAMBOUILLET PROCESS 
RESULT IN AGREEMENT. THE USG, HE STATED, IS SEIZED WITH THIS 
ISSUE, BUT THE PRESIDENT HAS NOT YET COMMITTED THE U.S.
DEFERRING TO DOD ON COMMAND ARRANGEMENTS, HE THOUGHT THE SIZE 
OF A U.S. CONTINGENT WOULD NECESSARILY DEPEND UPON THE SIZE OF 
THE TOTAL FORCE. THERE ARE FOUR OPEN QUESTIONS: WHETHER THE 
U.S. SENDS GROUND FORCES; HOW MANY; WHAT THE RATIO TO NON-U.S. 
FORCES WILL BE; AND THE NATURE OF THE COMMAND STRUCTURE. 
HOWEVER, WHETHER OR NOT THERE ARE, U.S. TROOPS ON THE GROUND, 
THE USG BELIEVES EUROPEANS SHOULD PROVIDE THE LARGER 
PROPORTION OF TROOPS. THIS IS AN OPPORTUNITY FOR EUROPEANS TO 
DEMONSTRATE THAT THEY ARE PREPARED TO SHOULDER MORE OF THE 
DEFENSE BURDEN. THIS IS ESPECIALLY SO, SINCE THE U.S. ALREADY 
HAS SUBSTANTIAL FORCES IN MACEDONIA, BOSNIA, IN THE ADRIATIC 
AND IN THE KOSOVO AIR VERIFICATION MISSION. ASKED BY COMBLES

4. fei THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THE FRENCH-LED EXTRACTION 
FORCE (XFOR) WAS A WELCOME DEVELOPMENT, BUT IT IS CONFINED TO 
THE SINGLE TASK OF EXTRACTING THE OSCE VERIFIERS. OTHER 
MISSIONS MAY BE NECESSARY. • AUDIBERT HIGHLIGHTED THE FACT THAT 
XFOR WAS MAINLY EUROPEAN BUT UNDER AN AMERICAN NATO COMMANDER 
AND SUGGESTED THIS WAS A LARGE STEP FOR THE GOF IN TERMS OF 
PUBLIC PERCEPTION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ACKNOWLEDGED THIS, 
BUT ADDED THE U.S. ALSO WOULD HAVE TO DEAL WITH A PUBLIC 
PERCEPTION PROBLEM IF IT SENT GROUND FORCES INTO KOSOVO. 
ASMUS REITERATED THAT THE XFOR IS A LIMITED FORCE; THE U.S. 
MIGHT STILL HAVE TO COME TO THE RESCUE IF SOMETHING GOES 
WRONG. EXPRESSING CONCERN ABOUT WHAT HE TERMED THE "POLITICAL 
CHOREOGRAPHY" OF ESDI, HE SAID THIS MUST BE FOUND IN THE 
CONTEXT OF THE THREE 'D'S (NO DECOUPLING, DUPLICATION OR 
DISCRIMINATION) AND MUST REINFORCE STRATEGIC LINKAGE. HE' 
RECOGNIZED THAT THE ST. MALO DECLARATION WAS WRITTEN FOR 
EUROPEAN AUDIENCES, BUT THE LANGUAGE WAS NOT HELPFUL FOR OUR 
MUTUAL ESDI EFFORTS. THE USG HAD TAKEN THE HIGH ROAD AND DID 
NOT CRITICIZE THE FR/UK EFFORT, BUT THE OUTCOME WAS NOT IDEAL 
FROM THE U.S. PERSPECTIVE. 
ST. MALO: LESS THAN TRANSPARENT 
5. 4-Ei- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ADDED THAT, BEFORE ST. MALO, THE 
USG HAD ASKED THE TWO GOVERNMENTS WHAT WAS ENVISIONED AND THE 
RESPONSES WERE LESS THAN TRANSPARENT. WE RECOGNIZED THAT 
THERE WAS UNCERTAINTY ON THE PART OF THE TWO COUNTRIES AS TO 
WHAT WOULD COME OUT OF THE MEETING, BUT THE USG FELT IN THE 
DARK EVEN PAST THE LAST MINUTE. SOME AMBIGUOUS ELEMENTS, 
E.G., THE INTEGRITY OF THE ALLIANCE AND ACCESS TO COMMON 
ASSETS, REMAINED EVEN AFTER THE UK AND FRANCE EXPLAINED ST. 
MALO. BUT, TO ACCENTUATE THE POSITIVE, THE USG IS NOT AFRAID 
OF ESDI; IT SUPPORTS IT. IT IS NECESSARY, HOWEVER, TO 
UNDERSTAND WHERE IT IS GOING AND TO ENSURE DANGEROUS RESULTS 
ARE AVOIDED. 
6. -(-e-)- THERE ARE LESSONS FROM THE BALKANS: IF A CONFLICT IS 
ALLOWED TO FESTER UNTIL THE POINT WHERE THE ,U.S. BECOMES 
ENGAGED, IT NECESSARILY WOULD HAVE ADVANCED TO A VERY SERIOUS 
STATE AND THAT WOULD BE BAD FOR ALL. THUS, IF WE CAN LOWER 
THE INTERVENTION THRESHOLD, THIS WOULD BE FOR THE BETTER. AND 
IF EUROPEANS TAKE THE INITIATIVE EARLY, THIS THRESHOLD CAN BE 
PUSHED DOWN. KOSOVO MAY ENHANCE PROSPECTS FOR REAL ESDI IF 
ALL GOES WELL ON THE GROUND.

7. 442-F COMBLES DE NAYVES, TURNING TO PREPARATIONS FOR THE 
NATO SUMMIT, SAID THE MOD HAS BEEN HEAVILY ENGAGED IN THIS 
PROCESS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY DESCRIBED THE RELATIONSHIP 
BETWEEN NATO AND THE UN AS ONE OF THE CORE PROBLEMS. "WE NEED 
TO BRIDGE THE DIFFERENCES BETWEEN US ON THIS," PERHAPS WITH 
SOME SYNTHESIS OF OUR POSITIONS. THE U.S. DOES NOT WANT AN 
ECHO OF 1994 BRUSSELS SUMMIT LANGUAGE, WHICH INVITED PEOPLE TO 
CONCLUDE THAT NATO TAKES ORDERS FROM THE SECURITY COUNCIL. 
COMBLES DE NAYVES INTERVENED "BUT WE ARE ALL MEMBERS OF THE 
SECURITY COUNCIL." ASMUS EXPLAINED THAT THE WORD "AUTHORITY" 
IN THE 1994 LANGUAGE WAS ALREADY A COMPROMISE. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY RECALLED THAT THE COQS BOSNIA AND THE 
DISCUSSION WAS CASE-SPECIFIC. THE U.S. WANTS TO MOVE FORWARD. 
YES, ALL NATO MEMBERS ARE MEMBERS OF THE UN, AND ARE GUIDED BY 
THAT ORGANIZATION. SO ALREADY THERE IS A BRIDGE BETWEEN NATO 
AND THE UN, BUT NOT SUBORDINATION. IT IS THE DIFFERENCE 
BETWEEN NATO WORKING WITH THE UN OR FOR THE UN. THIS WOULD 
NOT MEAN THE UN COULD NOT SUPPORT NATO UNDERTAKINGS BY 
RESOLUTIONS OR STATEMENTS. ASMUS SUGGESTED RETURNING TO 
LANGUAGE IN THE ORIGINAL WASHINGTON TREATY AS BETTER THAN THE 
1994 BRUSSELS LANGUAGE AS A BASIS FOR RESOLUTION. AUDIBERT 
INTERJECTED THAT THE WORD "AUTHORITY" HAS NEVER INTERFERRED 
WITH NATO DOING WHAT IT HAS SOUGHT TO DO. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY ANSWERED, "BUT IT COULD DO SO." WE WILL NOT ALWAYS 
BE SO LUCKY AS WE HAVE BEEN SO FAR WITH RUSSIA, AND WE DO NOT 
WANT RUSSIA AND CHINA TO BE ABLE TO TELL US WHAT WE CAN DO. 
HE CONCLUDED HE DID NOT THINK THERE WAS A CHASM SEPARATING THE 
U.S. AND FRENCH POSITIONS. WE CAN FIND THE RIGHT WORDS; 
ASMUS' RECOMMENDATION TO RETURN TO THE WASHINGTON TREATY FOR A 
STARTING POINT IS A GOOD APPROACH. 
FRENCH DEFENSE INDUSTRIAL STRATEGY 
8. 444- STEPHANE CAINE, INDUSTRIAL ADVISOR WITHIN THE MOD 
CABINET, DESCRIBED THE MOD'S PERCEPTION REGARDING THE 
RESTRUCTURING OF DEFENSE INDUSTRIES IN EUROPE, WHICH COMBLES 
DE NAYVES ADDED WAS A CRUCIAL ELEMENT OF DEVELOPING A TRUE 
ESDI. CAINE SAID FRANCE HAS TOO MANY SMALL COMPANIES AND THE 
LEVEL OF TECHNOLOGICAL SOPHISTICATION MUST BE INCREASED TO 
DEVELOP BALANCED PARTNERSHIPS. THEREFORE, THE IDEA IS FOR 
THEM TO MERGE ON A NATIONAL LEVEL TO BECOME MIDDLE-SIZED 
(DESCRIBED AS AROUND TEN BILLION USD TURNOVER) AND THEN ALLY 
WITH OTHER EUROPEANS. IN ELECTRONICS AND AEROSPACE, THIS 
FIRST STEP HAS BEEN ACCOMPLISHED. HE EMPHASIZED THAT "IF WE 
CAN, WE WANT MERGERS TO INCLUDE U.S. COMPANIES." COMBLES DE 
NAYVES NOTED A PARALLEL WITH ESDI IN GENERAL, THE OBJECTIVE•IS
NOT TO DIVIDE, BUT TO DEVELOP GLOBAL PARTNERSHIPS. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY, RECALLING THAT DEPSECDEF JOHN HAMRE HAD ALSO HAD 
RECENT MEETINGS AT THE MOD, CONCLUDED THAT OUR TWO COUNTRIES 
NEED TO DO MORE TO FIND LANGUAGE FOR THE WASHINGTON SUMMIT ON 
DEFENSE INDUSTRIAL COOPERATION. BUT THERE HAS TO BE SOME
