SUMMARY 
DECLASSIFIED 
DECONTROLLED 
THE MAY 10-11 U.S.-SADC FORUM IN MAPUTO 
GENERATED GENUINE DIALOGUE AND AGREEMENT ON FOLLOW-ON 
ACTIONS, AND ALLOWED USDEL TO HELP SHAPE SOUTHERN 
AFRICA'S APPROACH TO ISSUES OF INTEREST TO THE U.S. 
SADC'S ACTING EXECUTIVE SECRETARY TOLD US THAT HE AND 
MEMBER STATES BELIEVED THAT THE PARTICIPATION, INTEREST 
AND DIALOGUE WERE EVEN BETTER THAN LAST YEAR; A DOZEN 
MINISTERS ATTENDED FROM THE SADC SIDE, INCLUDING SIX 
FOREIGN MINISTERS. THE POLITICAL ROUNDTABLE, WHICH 
CONTINUED WELL BEYOND ITS ALLOTTED TIME AND INCLUDED A 
REMARKABLY FRANK DISCUSSION ON ZIMBABWE'S LAND ISSUE, 
AFFIRMED THE UTILITY OF HAVING A REGIONAL POLITICAL 
DIALOGUE WITH SADC MEMBER STATES. THE ECONOMIC WORKING 
GROUP -- WITH STRONG PRIVATE SECTOR PARTICIPATION FROM 
BOTH SIDES -- FOCUSED ON BUILDING U.S.-SADC TRADE AND 
INVESTMENT. THE SADC FREE TRADE AREA, WHICH WILL BE 
LAUNCHED SEPTEMBER 1ST, AND THE AFRICA GROWTH AND 
OPPORTUNITY ACT, WHICH THE SENATE PASSED THE DAY THE 
FORUM ENDED, WERE FOCAL POINTS OF THE ECONOMIC 
DISCUSSION. THE FORUM ALSO MAPPED THE WAY FORWARD FOR 
CLOSER U.S.-SADC COOPERATION ON HIV/AIDS, AND WELCOMED 
THE PRESIDENT'S MAY 10 EXECUTIVE ORDER MAKING AIDS-
RELATED DRUGS MORE AFFORDABLE IN AFRICA. SADC ALSO 
WELCOMED THE U.S. OFFER TO CONVENE A POST-FLOOD SEMINAR 
TO HELP SHAPE PLANS FOR STRENGTHENING DISASTER 
MANAGEMENT CAPABILITIES IN THE REGION. RECOGNIZING THE 
FORUM AS THE PRIMARY VEHICLE FOR THE U.S. TO ENGAGE 
REGIONALLY WITH SOUTHERN AFRICA, THE PARTICIPANTS AGREED 
TO CONTINUE THIS AS AN ANNUAL EVENT, WITH THE NEXT FORUM 
TO TAKE PLACE IN WASHINGTON IN THE SECOND HALF OF 2001. 
END SUMMARY.

3. (U) PRESIDENT CHISSANO OPENED THE FORUM, DESCRIBING 
IT AS A SHINING EXAMPLE OF U.S.-SADC PARTNERSHIP. HE 
NOTED THE "SIGNIFICANT PROGRESS" MADE IN FOLLOWING UP 
ACTIONS EMANATING FROM THE 1999 FORUM IN THE AREAS OF 
REGIONAL INTEGRATION, TRADE AND INVESTMENT, AND 
TRANSNATIONAL SOCIAL ISSUES SUCH AS HIV/AIDS. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY, IN HIS INTRODUCTORY REMARKS, SAID SUB-
REGIONAL ORGANIZATIONS LIKE SADC ARE "NECESSARY 
ADDITIONAL BUILDING BLOCKS" IN THE ARCHITECTURE FOR 
PEACE. HE ALSO SET THE TONE FOR THE HIV/AIDS PLENARY BY 
CALLING AIDS "TOO IMPORTANT A SUBJECT FOR JUST NARROW

POLITICAL ROUNDTABLE SETS FORUM ABUZZ 
4. -4H- THE POLITICAL ROUNDTABLE SESSION IGNITED DURING 
AN ELECTRIFYING DISCUSSION OF ZIMBABWE'S LAND ISSUE. 
A/S RICE'S RAISING OF THE ISSUE DREW AN IMPASSIONED 
RESPONSE FROM ZIMBABWEAN DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER GOCHE, 
WHO SPOKE OF THE NEED FOR JUSTICE, NOT JUST PEACE. WHAT 
FOLLOWED WAS A HEARTFELT, NO-HOLDS-BARRED DISCUSSION OF 
THE LAND ISSUE, THE COLONIAL LEGACY IN SOUTHERN AFRICA, 
AND HOW DEMOCRACY AND GOVERNANCE ISSUES TIE INTO THAT. 
A/S RICE STRESSED THAT THE USG WAS COMMITTED TO 
EQUITABLE AND LAWFUL LAND REFORM AND LOOKED FORWARD TO 
FINDING A SOLUTION IN THE CONTEXT OF THE RULE OF LAW 
MINISTERS FROM SOUTH AFRICA, MOZAMBIQUE, ANGOLA AND 
NAMIBIA SPOKE CANDIDLY OF THE RACISM THEY SAW BEHIND THE 
ZIMBABWEAN LAND ISSUE.

BOOSTING TRADE AND INVESTMENT 
6. (U) THE ECONOMIC WORKING GROUP -- WITH STRONG 
PRIVATE SECTOR PARTICIPATION FROM BOTH SIDES -- FOCUSED 
ON BUILDING U.S.-SADC TRADE AND INVESTMENT. THE SADC 
FREE TRADE AREA, WHICH WILL BE LAUNCHED IN SEPTEMBER, 
AND THE AFRICA GROWTH AND OPPORTUNITY ACT (AGOA), WHICH 
THE SENATE PASSED THE DAY THE FORUM ENDED, WERE FOCAL 
POINTS OF THE ECONOMIC DISCUSSION. DURING THE SESSION 
ON THE SADC FREE TRADE AREA, THE PRIVATE SECTOR 
PARTICIPANTS WEIGHED IN VERY STRONGLY THAT THE EIGHT-
YEAR PHASE-DOWN PERIOD FOR TARIFF REDUCTIONS IS FAR TOO 
LONG. THE U.S. REITERATED ITS COMMITMENT TO SUPPORT 
SADC IN IMPLEMENTING ITS FREE TRADE AREA, AND OFFERED 
TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE IN AREAS SUCH AS STREAMLINING 
BORDER CROSSING PROCEDURES, HARMONIZING CUSTOMS 
ADMINISTRATION, AND ELIMINATING NON-TARIFF BARRIERS..
7. (U) DELEGATES FROM LESS DEVELOPED SADC MEMBER STATES 
EXPRESSED THE FEAR THAT, UNDER THE SADC FREE TRADE AREA, 
THE LARGER COUNTRIES IN THE REGIN =- PRIMARILY SOUTH 
AFRICA -- WOULD OVERWHELM TE SMALLER COUNTRIES. THIS 
CONCERN WAS COUNTEREDBY THE ASSISTANT USTR FOR AFRICA, 
WHO DESCRIBED MXICO'S BENEFITS UNDER NAFTA IN TERMS OF 
INCREASE TRADE, INVESTMENT AND JOB CREATION. SHE ALSO 
DSCRIBED THE PROVISIONS OF THE AGOA, WHICH WAS PASSD 
BY THE SENATE DURING THE CLOSING MOMENTS OF TH FORUM. 
SADC PARTICIPANTS EMBRACED THE AGOA AS A REAKTHROUGH IN 
U.S.-AFRICA RELATIONS, AND EXPRESED A DESIRE FOR MORE 
INFORMATION ABOUT THE BILL' ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS.

COMMENT 
117-tt-)- ONE OF OUR GOALS GOING INTO THE FORUM WAS TO 
ENGAGE IN GENUINE DIALOGUE WITH MINISTERS AND OTHER TOP 
OFFICIALS IN SOUTHERN AFRICA. THE ZIMBABWE DISCUSSION 
EXCEEDED ALL EXPECTATIONS OF THIS. A/S RICE TOLD 
PARTICIPANTS THAT THE POLITICAL ROUNDTABLE WAS ONE OF 
THE MOST PRODUCTIVE, HONEST AND OPEN EXCHANGES THAT SHE 
HAD BEEN PART OF IN ANY DIPLOMATIC SETTING. THE 
EXCHANGE WAS EQUALLY EXHILARATING TO OUR SADC 
INTERLOCUTORS -- AS AN ANGOLAN DELEGATE PUT IT, "WE 
DON'T EVEN TALK TO EACH OTHER LIKE THIS IN THE OAU."
