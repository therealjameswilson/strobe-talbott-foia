2. ~ ITALIAN FM DINI AND DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET IN
ROME ON MARCH 10. THEY DISCUSSED DINI'S RECENT TRIP TO
IRAN, HIS UPCOMING VISIT TO PYONGYANG AND TOUCHED UPON
ESDI. DINI FOUND IRANIAN MODERATES "EXUBERANT" AND "VERY
CONFIDENT" IN THE WAKE OF THE MAJLES ELECTIONS AND URGED
THE U.S. TO REOPEN A DIALOGUE WITH IRAN. DINI COUNSELED
THE U.S. TO SET ASIDE OUR FOCUS ON IRANIAN SUPPORT FOR
HIZBOLLAH AND BEGIN BY ADDRESSING IRANIAN CONCERNS ABOUT
ITS FROZEN ASSETS. TALBOTT ASSURED DINI THAT U.S.
LEADERSHIP WAS SEEKING WAYS TO HAVE A DIALOGUE WITH
TEHERAN. TALBOTT WARNED THAT DESPITE APPARENT SHIFTS IN
IRAN'S POLITICAL ORIENTATION, IRAN WILL CONTINUE TO DEVELOP
ITS WMD PROGRAMS. TALBOTT SAID THAT ITALY'S NEW RELATIONS
WITH 'HE DPRK COULD BE USEFUL. DIN1 SAID THAT THE DPRK
REGIME IS FEARFUL OF OPENING UP, YET DESPERATE TO RESOLVE
N. KOREA'S ECONOMIC PROBLEMS. HE SUGGESTED AN
INTERNATIONALLY-GUARANTEED NON-AGGRESSION PACT AS A
POSSIBLE WAY OF ASSUAGING N. KOREA'S FEARS. ON ESDI, DINI
SAID IT WAS "TOO EARLY TO WORRY" AND ASSURED US THAT THE
EUROPEANS WOULD INCLUDE STRONG ESDI/NATO LINKS IN FINAL
LANGUAGE. END SUMMARY.

4. ~TALBOTT ASKED ABOUT DINI'S MARCH 4-6 TRIP TO IRAN.
DINI PRAISED PRESIDENT CLINTON'S DECISION TO RECOGNIZE
IRANIAN ELECTION RESULTS AS "EXTRAORDINARY, " AND SHARED HIS
OWN IMPRESSIONS OF WHERE IRAN WAS HEADED IN THE WAKE OF THE
MAJLES ELECTIONS.'N DINI'S OPINION, THE OVERWHELMING
'REFORMIST VICTORY WOULD ALLOW KHATAMI AND THE NEW
PARLIAM NT TO MOVE AHEAD WITH REFORM LEGISLATION. WHILE
ADMITTING THAT IMPORTANT LEVERS OF POWER REMAINED IN THE
HANDS OF CONSERVATIVE CLERICS, DINI ALSO THOUGHT THAT
POPULAR PRESSURE TO OPEN UP THE ISLAMIC STATE WOULD RESULT
IN CONSERVATIVES'RADUALLY ADOPTING MORE MODERATE VIEWS ON
THE FUTURE SHAPE OF IRAN. DINI DETECTED NO FEARS ABOUT A
CONSERVATIVE BACKLASH IN HIS INTERLOCUTORSl ON THE
CONTRARY, HE FOUND KHATAMI AND KHARAZI "EXUBERANT" AND
"VERY CONFIDENT" AFTER THE ELECTIONS.

IRANIANS WERE RELIEVED THAT HIGH OIL PRICES WOULD ENABLE

6. (~ TURNING TO MORE TROUBLESOME TOPICS, DINI SAID
ALTHOUGH THE IRANIANS ARE SUPPORTING HIZBOLLAH, HE BELIEVED
THIS SUPPORT WOULD DECLINE, OR EVEN DISAPPEAR, ONCE ISRAEL
WITHDRAWS FROM SOUTHERN LEBANON. HE ALSO SAID ITALY WAS
AWARE OF IRAN'S EFFORTS TO DEVELOP ITS NUCLEAR WEAPONS
PROGRAM (WHICH, HE CLAIMED, WAS NOT VERY FAR ADVANCED). HE
CHIDED THE U.S. FOR ITS APPROACH TO IRAN, SAYING THAT
CONFRONTING IRAN ABOUT ITS SUPPORT FOR HIZBOLLAH WILL NOT
WORK. HE SAID THE IRANIANS ARE READY FOR A DIALOGUE WITH
THE U.S., BUT BECAUSE THE U.S. FROZE IRANIAN ASSETS AND
IMPOSED SANCTIONS, IRANIANS INSIST THE U.S. MAKE THE FIRST
.CONCILIATORY GESTURE. POINTING OUT THAT THE VALUE OF
IRAN'S FROZEN ASSETS COMPRISES A SMALL PART OF ITS TOTAL
GNP, DINI SAID THE IMPORTANCE OF RECUPERATING THE ASSETS
WAS MOSTLY SYMBOLIC.
7. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY LED DINI INTO A DISCUSSION
ABOUT IRAN'S SYSTEMATIC OPPOSITION TO THE MIDDLE EAST PEACE
PROCESS. DINI RECALLED PAST IRANIAN STATEMENTS THAT IRAN
WOULD NOT OPPOSE A SETTLEMENT THAT SATISFIES THE
PALESTINIANS. NEVERTHELESS, IRAN WAS NO FRIEND OF ISRAEL
AND JUSTIFIES ITS NUCLEAR PROGRAM AS A COUNTERWEIGHT TO
ISRAEL'S PURPORTED NUCLEAR CAPABILITIES. DINI SUGGESTED
THIS PROBLEM COULD BE "TACKLED" WITH THE RESUMPTION OF A
U.S./IRAN DIALOGUE. DINI LISTED IRAN'S THREE MOST
NEURALGIC ISSUES REGARDING THE U S FROZEN IRANIAN
ASSETS'HE
HUMAN RIGHTS RESOLUTION, AND U.S.-IMPOSED OBSTACLES TO
IRAN'S PETROLEUM INDUSTRY DEVELOPMENT (SPECIFICALLY,
ROUTING PIPELINES AROUND IRAN). DINI URGED US TO ENGAGE
THE IRANIANS ON THESE TOPICS, AND TO SET ASIDE ACCUSATIONS
ABOUT IRAN'S SUPPORT FOR HIZBOLLAH.
8. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REMINDED DINI THAT IRAN HAS
RESISTED U.S. INVITATIONS TO OPEN A DIALOGUE — POSSIBLY
BECAUSE THE MODERATES FEAR THAT CONTACT WITH THE U.S.
EQUALS THE "KISS OF DEATH" FOR THEM. DINI AGAIN URGED THE
U.S. TO MAKE A GESTURE. TALBOTT ASSURED HIM OUR LEADERSHIP
WAS SERIOUSLY LOOKING AT WAYS TO HAVE A DIALOGUE.
"MARXIST CONTRADICTIONS" IN RUSSIA'S IRAN POLICY
9. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RAISED IRAN'S "ROBUST" PROGRAMS
TO DEVELOP WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION (WMD) AND BALLISTIC
MISSILE CAPABILITIES. TALBOTT SAID THAT NO MATTER WHAT
CHANGES OCCUR IN THE ORIENTATION OF DOMESTIC IRANIAN
POLITICS, OR IN IRAN'S OVERALL APPROACH TO THE REST OF THE
WORLD, EVIDENCE INDICATES THAT THE INTENTION TO DEVELOP
BOTH PROGRAMS IS DEEPLY ROOTED IN IRAN'S MILITARY
ESTABLISHMENT.
10. ((( TALBOTT TOLD DINI THE GOVERNMENT OF RUSSIA ADMITS
THAT SOME RUSSIANS ARE FLOUTING THEIR OWN LAWS TO
CONTRIBUTE TO IRANIAN WMD/MISSILE PROGRAMS. THE U.S. WILL
BE WORKING CLOSELY WITH WHAT WE HOPE WILL BE A STABILIZED
RUSSIAN GOVERNMENT TO CLAMP DOWN ON THIS ACTIVITY, IRAN'S
ATTEMPTS TO ADVANCE THESE PROGRAMS ARE OBVIOUSLY A THREAT
TO WESTERN EUROPE AND RUSSIA. DINI SAID THAT BOTH RUSSIA
AND IRAN FEARED THEIR NEIGHBORS'UCLEAR CAPABILITIES AND
OPINED THAT RUSSIA WOULD COOPERATE FULLY WITH US. HE WRYLY
NOTED THE "MARXIST CONTRADICTION" BETWEEN OFFICIAL GOR IRAN

11. ~ TALBOTT ASSURED DINI THAT THE U.S. THOUGHT ITALIAN
POLICY IN N. KOREA COULD BE USEFUL. DINI LOOKED FORWARD
TO CONFERRING WITH COUNSELOR WENDY SHERMAN BEFORE HIS
HISTORIC TRIP TO PYONGYANG AT THE END OF MARCH AND OFFERED
TO DEBRIEF US UPON HIS RETURN IN DINI'S OPINIONi THE
CURRENT DPRK REGIME IS AFRAI D TO OPEN UP AND BE SWEPT AWAY ~
BUT IS DESPERATELY SEEKING A WAY OUT OF THE CURRENT
SITUATION. DINI SAID HE PLANNED TO EXPLAIN TO HIS N.
KOREAN INTERLOCUTORS ITALY'S EVOLUTION FROM A COUNTRY WITH
THE LARGEST COMMUNIST PARTY IN WESTERN EUROPE TO A MODERN
NATION LED BY AN EX-COMMUNIST PRIME MINISTER. HE WOULD
ALSO ENCOURAGE N. KOREA TO USE ITS RESOURCES DIFFERENTLY
AND WOULD ANNOUNCE A MODEST FOODSTUFFS ASSISTANCE PROGRAM.,
DINI THEN SUGGESTED THAT MAJOR WORLD POWERS SUCH AS RUSSIA,
CHINA, JAPAN, AND THE U.S. WORK ON A SYSTEM OF GUARANTE S
FOR THE DPRK~ PERHAPS IN THE FORM OF A NON AGRE SS ION PACT ~
AS A MEANS OF ASSUAGING N. KOREA'S THREATS TO ITS
TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY. TALBOTT QUESTIONED HOW TO GUARANTEE
THE DPRK'S SAFETY WHEN THERE WAS NO OUTSIDE THREAT. DINI
THOUGHT N. KOREA COULD PERCEIVE U.S. TROOPS IN THE ROK AS A
THREAT.
12. ~ DINI ASKED ABOUT N. KOREA'S LONG DISTANCE MISSILE
CAPABILITIES. TALBOTT SAID U.S. ANALYSTS ESTIMATE THAT, BY
THE YEAR 2005, THE CONT'INENTAL U.S. WILL BE VULNERABLE TO
N. KOREAN ICBMS. REFERRING TO THE IMMINENT VISIT TO THE
U.S. BY A HIGH-LEVEL DPRK DELEGATION, TALBOTT SAID WE HOPED
TO FORMALIZE A FREEZE ON N. KOREAN MISSILE TEST LAUNCHES.
WE HOPE THE FREEZE WILL LEAD TO AN EVENTUAL AGREEMENT TO
END THE ICBM PROGRAM ALTOGETHER.
DINI: TOO EARLY TO WORRY ABOUT ESDI
13. ~ TALBOTT SAID THE U.S. WANTS A STRONG ESDI. HE
SUGGESTED USING THE MAY NAC IN FLORENCE TO DISCUSS CLOSING
THE GAP IN THINKING — BOTH ACROSS THE ATLANTIC AND BETWEEN
EU AND NON-EU EUROPEAN COUNTRIES — ON ESDI. DINI SAID IT
WAS "TOO EARLY TO WORRY." HE ASSURED TALBOTT THAT NATO WAS
THE CORNERSTONE OF ANY EUROPEAN DEFENSE SYSTEM AND EUROPE
UNANIMOUSLY SUPPORTED STRONG ESDI/NATO LINKS. DINI WAS
CONFIDENT FINAL ESDI LANGUAGE WOULD IMPROVE UPON THE
ALREADY GOOD HELSINKI DRAFT. DINI SAID HE HAD ALSO SPOKEN
TO PUTIN ABOUT REESTABLISHING RUSSIA'S TIES TO NATO AND
HOPED FM IVANOV WOULD AGREE TO ATTEND THE FLORENCE NAC.
