2. (U) THE NEW SPANISH AMBASSADOR TO THE U.S., ANTONIO 
DE OYARZABAL MARCHESI, PRESENTED COPIES OF HIS CREDENTIALS 
TO ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT ON AUGUST 28, ALLOWING HIM TO 
ASSUME HIS DUTIES AS THE APPOINTED AMBASSADOR UNTIL HE 
PRESENTS HIS CREDENTIALS TO THE PRESIDENT. 
3 -(-e+ IN A BRIEF DISCUSSION ON NATO, THE ACTING 
SECRETARY EXPRESSED HOW PLEASED WE WERE TO HAVE JAVIER 
SOLANA AS NATO SECRETARY GENERAL, AND THAT SOLANA WAS
PROVIDING INTELLIGENT LEADERSHIP TO THE 
ADAPTATION/ENLARGEMENT PROCESSES. AMBASSADOR OYARZABAL 
AGREED, SAYING THAT SOLANA WAS MUCH MORE USEFUL TO SPAIN 
AT NATO THAN HE WOULD HAVE BEEN HAD HE'SUCCEEDED GONZALEZ 
AS HEAD OF THE SOCIALIST PARTY. OYARZABAL SAID THAT NATO 
INTEGRATION HAS AND CONTINUES TO PLAY A VERY IMPORTANT 
STABILIZING PSYCHOLOGICAL ROLE FOR THE ARMY AND THE 
SPANISH PUBLIC. 
4. /*COYARZABAL URGED THE U.S. NOT TO FORGET SPAIN 
DURING THE NATO ENLARGEMENT PROCESS. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY RESPONDED THAT WE CITE SPAIN AS AN EXAMPLE OF 
HOW NATO MEMBERSHIP PROMOTES REGIONAL STABILITY AND THE 
CONSOLIDATION OF DEMOCRACY. HE URGED SPAIN TO SPREAD THE 
WORD IN CENTRAL AND EASTERN EUROPE ON THE ROLE WHICH NATO 
MEMBERSHIP HAS PLAYED IN SPAIN ON STABILITY AND 
DEMOCRATIZATION. OYARZABAL NOTED THAT BEFORE DEPARTING TO 
THE U.S. FROM COPENHAGEN (WHERE HE WAS ALSO ACCREDITED TO 
LITHUANIA), THE LITHUANIAN FOREIGN MINISTER HAD MET WITH 
HIM TO PRESS LITHUANIA'S CASE FOR NATO MEMBERSHIP. IN 
THIS CONTEXT, TALBOTT MENTIONED U.S. EFFORTS TO ADDRESS 
RUSSIAN CONCERNS ON NATO ENLARGEMENT, AND THAT HE SAW SOME 
PROSPECT FOR ENGAGING THE RUSSIAN SIDE.
