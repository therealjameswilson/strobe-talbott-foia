RELEASE IN PART 
B6 
Thank you for your letter and for your articles. 
have, of course, heard about you from Diane. I'm fas-
cinated by your work. I've only been to Moldova once --
back in the summer. I took a group of government officials 
there for a round of talks with President Snegur and 
several of his ministers. He entertained us in a cave 
about 300 feet below ground which housed a vast supply of 
Moldovan wine. I've spent quite a bit of time since coming 
into my present job, especially in recent weeks, on the 
Transdniester issue and the doings of General Lebed. What 
you call "the politics of identity" interests me not only 
in the context of Moldova but of several other New Indepen-
dent States (as we call them). Ethnicity in all its dimen-
sions, notably including the linguistic one, is turning out 
to be a curse of the post-Cold War era. 
If ever you find yourself in Washington, I would very 
much like to get you together with_my_colleagues who_work 
on Moldova. They could learn a lot from you. In the _-
meantime, I will share with them your articles_-__,__ 
Very best regards -- and give my regards to St 
Antony's. I spent three wonderful years hanging around 
there -- my thesis supervisor, Max Hayward, was a fellow --
and living for one of those years just a few blocks away on 
Leckford Road. 
Sincere
