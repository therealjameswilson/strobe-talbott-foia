THIS EXTENSION IS NEEDED TO KEEP THE AGREEMENT FROM'UNRAVELING; WE HOPE YOU WILL SUPPORT IT. .THE FLANK AGREEMENT IS CRITICAL TO THE VIABILITY OF THE CFE TREATY. THE VICE PRESIDENT: I UNDERSTAND VERY WELL. THE FLANK AGREEMENT IN NO WAY COMPROMISES UKRAINE S INTERESTS. EXTENSION ALLOWS TIME TO CONSIDER APPROVAL NEXT YEAR IN BETTER CIRCUMSTANCES. FINALLY, LINKING THE FLANK AGREEMENT TO THE BLACK SEA FLEET WILL NOT HELP TO ENSURE THE VIABILITY OF THE CFE TREATY, WHICH IS IN OUR COMMON INTEREST. WITHOUT THE TREATY, RUSSIA COULD PROCEED ON BOTH MATTERS ACCORDING TO ITS OWN WISHES. I UNDERSTAND YOUR CONCERNS ON BOTH ISSUES. SO THANK YOU FOR NOT OBJECTING TO EXTENSION OF THE FLANK.
