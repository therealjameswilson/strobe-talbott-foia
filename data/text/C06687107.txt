Dear Strobe: 
[RELEASE IN FULL 
Franny and I follow with interest your activities as Ambassador and I 
hope that it is proving a satisfying assignment. Is there a chance that we could get 
you back to Cleveland for a joint meeting of the Cleveland International Program 
and the Council on World Affairs? CIP's annual meeting is tentatively scheduled for 
December 15, but we would gladly make any adjustment necessary to fit your 
schedule. I know that your schedule is probably impossible to predict; we are willing 
to take a chance if you think you might be able to do it. Drop me a line at your 
convenience or feel free to call. 
With best regards, 
Seth Taft
