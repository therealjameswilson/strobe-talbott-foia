The Center for American-Eurasian Studies 
and Relations (CAESAR) has been an important 
hub of activity: a meeting place in Washington 
DC for officials, diplomats, businessmen, schol-
ars, and citizens to promote the ideals of peace, 
democracy, economic cooperation and security 
between the United States and the nations of 
Europeand Asia, CAESAR, an independent non-
profit organization, is an association of specialists 
working on Soviet and East European affairs who 
have joined together to provide a forum for debate 
through public education, in order to promote a 
greater understanding and build better relations 
between the nations of East and West. 
The Center has firmly supported the forces of 
democracy within Eastern Europe and the former 
Soviet Union as they struggled for freedom. 
These goals have not been completely fulfilled, 
for our real work is just beginning, We are 
providing the peoples of these nations with the 
material, information, and guidance that citizens 
in new democracies desperately need in order for 
the seeds of democracy to grow and flourish,
