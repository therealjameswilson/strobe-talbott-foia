THE TIME IS NOW! 
Once again the nation and the world are watching history unfold in Russia. 
And once again our leaders in Washington are acknowledging the importance 
of events in Moscow for our own security and prosperity. They should know 
that thousands of Americans and Russians are already engaged in 
significant cooperation through The Friendship Force. The personal 
relationships that are being established through this citizen exchange 
movement represent a valuable asset in the quest for meaningful linkages 
between the two societies. 
The Friendship Force is now prepared to launch a major program that can 
bring 2,000 Russians to the United States this year for short-term 
business training. By combining The Friendship Force's national network 
of volunteers with the training experience of Global Business, Inc., the 
total cost can be kept to between two and three million dollars. This 
will include travel from Russia, all expenses in America, a one-month 
practical training program, and most important, a four-week homestay with 
an American counterpart. In addition to receiving well-targeted practical 
training, The Russians will return home with permanent links in .the U.S. 
in the form of personal and professional friendships. Friendships that 
can be relied upon to provide ongoing assistance and support in the long 
and difficult process of establishing a democratic, free-market society.
