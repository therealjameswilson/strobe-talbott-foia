Dear Mr. Carteron: 
. Thank you for your letter of March 18 inviting me to 
participate in the Crans-Montana Forum between June 18-20. The 
program which you describe responds to many of the most 
important questions facing Europe today: 
I appreciate very much your including me in this seminar; 
however, I regret that the pressure of my new duties will 
prevent me from attending this worthwhile event. As you can 
imagine, my present position as Ambassador at Large and Special 
Advisor to the Secretary on the New Independent States will be 
both challenging and time-consuming. 
Again I thank you for thinking of me. I wish you every 
success in your future endeavors at Fondation du Forum 
Universale.
