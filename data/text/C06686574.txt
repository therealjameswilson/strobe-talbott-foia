Dear 
I want to thank you for your considerable help insecuring 
the passage of this year's foreign aid bill. The assistance 
program for the New Independent States (NIS) contained in that 
bill is crucial to the success of our policy toward the NIS. 
This was not an easy year to pass foreign aid legislation, 
and,I am convinced that your efforts played a very important 
role in ensuring the passage of the bill. The transformation 
of the NIS into democratic nations with market economies is the 
President's number one foreign policy priority. Your hard work 
has been key to the success of our policy. 
Again, thank you for your support. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott
