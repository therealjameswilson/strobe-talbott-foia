B6 
As Director of the Center for American-Eurasian Studies and Relations, formerly the 
Center for Soviet-American Relations here in Washington, I believe it would be 
important if an Administration official such as yourself, submit a short article on the new 
U.S. foreign policy with Russia for publication the Center's Eurasian Reports. , 
The Center is an association of specialists of Eurasian affairs, which includes some our 
nations top specialists Adam Ulam and Dmitri Simes. In this issue of the Eurasian 
Reports,, entitled "What is Russia?" the Center will examine the profound change which 
is confronting the Russian Federation as it attempts to forge a new constitution and 
build a new democratic base. 
Having assisted the Clinton-Gore Campaign and Transition Team efforts, I believe it 
would be important for an Adthinistration official to submit an article on the new 
partnership being built with Russia as well as the dangers of nuclear proliferation. As a 
non-profit organization, dedicated to public education, the Center would be honored to 
have the Adininistration's perspectives incorporated in the body of the report. An 
interview with you on this topic could be arranged as well. Thank you very much for 
your time and efforts. 
Respectfully, 
B6
