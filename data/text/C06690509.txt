February 28, 1993 
To: Harry Schnabel, Jr. 
Curator, Diplomatic Reception Rooms 
From: Strobe Talbott, Transition Office 
RELEASE IN FULL 
Belated thank for your kind note to me and for writ-
ing Page as well. I should have written to Bill Davis and to 
you in appreciation of the tour that Page and my parents got 
when they were here. My only excuse -- or at least explana-
tion -- is that my time has not been my own. Our colleagues 
in Congressional Relations have been running me ragged (for 
the good cause of getting me confirmed). 
I will surely take you up on your offer to arrange 
tours from time to time. The Diplomatic Reception Rooms are a 
wonderful treasur and, not incidentally, a great credit to 
their curator. 
Next time you're on the first floor, stop by and make 
sure I'm still alive. Eventually, Senate permitting, I'll be 
on the 7th floor, in what used to be Amb. Kennedy's suite of 
offices. Stop by there too. 
Chee
