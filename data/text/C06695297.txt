2. SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND 
PRESIDENTIAL ADVISER RYURIKOV REVIEWED IN SOME DETAIL 
THE SUMMIT SCHEDULE. FOLLOWING THAT, THEY DISCUSSED 
BROAD ISSUES COMING UP AT THE SUMMIT INCLUDING THE 
NEED FOR ATTENTION TO EUROPEAN SECURITY BY THE TWO 
PRESIDENTS, THE SIGNING OF THE TIMAN PECHORA AGREEMENT 
AT THE SUMMIT, THE U.S. PROPOSAL FOR A FOUR-POWER 
SUMMIT ON NAGORNO-KARABAKH, AND THE GENERAL THEME OF 
THE INTEGRATION OF RUSSIA INTO THE WORLD COMMUNITY ON 
A BROAD BASIS. END SUMMARY. 
3. TALBOTT, ACCOMPANIED BY PICKERING, S/NIS COLLINS, 
AND NSC BURNS MET WITH RYURIKOV, WHO WAS ACCOMPANIED 
BY KREMLIN PROTOCOL CHIEF SHEVCHENKO AND A STAFF 
OFFICER. DISCUSSION FOCUSED ON SUMMIT SCHEDULE AND 
SUMMIT ISSUES. 
4. THE FIRST PART OF THE DISCUSSION, *IN WHICH 
SHEVCHENKO PLAYED A ROLE, WAS DEVOTED TO THE REVIEW OF 
AND CLEARING UP A SERIES OF SCHEDULE QUESTIONS RELATED 
TO THE SEPTEMBER 27 AND 28 SUMMIT. FOR EXAMPLE, 
SHEVCHENKO MADE CLEAR PRESIDENT YELTSIN WOULD NOT BE 
ABLE TO DO A "60 MINUTES" TV INTERVIEW ON SUNDAY.
EVENING THE 25TH DUE TO SCHEDULE PROBLEMS. YELTSIN 
TENTATIVELY IS SCHEDULED TO HAVE DINNER WITH BOUTROS 
GHALI, THE UN SECRETARY GENERAL. THE RUSSIAN SIDE 
ASKED WHETHER PRESIDENT CLINTON MIGHT ATTEND.' BURNS 
SAID IT WAS NOT POSSIBLE, NOTED THAT A LUNCHEON THE 
NEXT DAY WITH BOUTROS GHALI AND OTHER HEADS OF STATES 
WAS A POSSIBILITY AND THEN LATER CONFIRMED, DURING THE 
VISIT TO THE MFA, THAT PRESIDENT CLINTON WOULD BE 
AVAILABLE TO ATTEND THE LUNCHEON. 
5. THE RUSSIANS WERE INTERESTED IN THE SCOPE AND 
PURPOSE OF THE LUNCH BEING OFFERED BY THE VICE 
PRESIDENT ON THE 27TH, AND SEEMED TO BE PLEASED WITH 
THE IDEA. THEY REVIEWED TENTATIVE RUSSIAN ATTENDANCE 
FOR THE EXPANDED SUBSTANTIVE MEETINGS. THE ISSUE OF 
RUSSIAN ATTENDANCE AT THE AMERICAN STATE DINNER WAS 
RESOLVED SATISFACTORILY. THE RUSSIANS EXHIBITED 
CURIOSITY ABOUT WHICH CE&S WOULD BE AT THE SEPTEMBER 
28 ROOSEVELT ROOM SESSION FOLLOWING THE EXPANDED 
MEETING ON ECONOMIC ISSUES. THEY WERE ASSURED THAT 
THESE WOULD BE PEOPLE WHOM PRESIDENT YELTSIN HAD MET, 
WHO WERE THERE TO TALK ABOUT THEIR CONTRIBUTION TO 
TRADE AND INVESTMENT, AND THAT IT WOULD NOT TURN INTO 
A SITUATION IN WHICH BUSINESSMEN WERE SOLICITING 
SPECIAL CONSIDERATION FOR THEIR OWN PROJECTS.
