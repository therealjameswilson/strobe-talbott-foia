Dear Strobe: 
With my congratulations to you on being designated Deputy Secretary, I enclose 
the text and also another, earlier version including the discussion that followed of a 
presentation I have just made to a conference on NATO sponsored by the Fellows 
Program of the Center for International Affairs at Harvard University. 
In' this presentation, I made a strong argument for rapid inclusion of Hungary, the 
Czech Republic, and Poland in the North Atlantic Alliance and also for conclusion of a 
UN Charter Article 43 "special agreement" between the NATO alliance (or particular 
allies, perhaps beginning with Canada) and the UN Security Council. 
I believe these proposals could be decisively advanced at the forthcoming NATO 
Summit meeting, and therefore I hastpn to send them to you. 
With best wishes for a successful meeting, 
Yours sincerely, 
Alan K. Henrikson 
Director
