2. -(.E9.-SUMMARY: BELGIAN DEFENSE MINISTER FLAHAUT SPENT HIS 
MAY 22 MEETING WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT IN RECEIVE 
MODE, ALLOWING THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TO OFFER AN ASSESSMENT 
OF RUSSIAN PRESIDENT PUTIN, ASSESS WHERE TALKS WITH RUSSIA 
ON ABM ADAPTATION STOOD, AND OFFER VIEWS ON START III, THE,
BALKANS, AND ZIMBWABWE. END SUMMARY. 
3. DURING A SEVERAL-DAY VISIT TO WASHINGTON, BELGIAN 
MINISTER OF DEFENSE ANDRE FLAHAUT MET MAY 22 AT THE 
DEPARTMENT WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT FOR A DISCUSSION 
OF EUROPEAN SECURITY ISSUES. FLAHAUT WAS ACCOMPANIED BY 
MOD ADVISER MGEN VANKEIRSBILCK, MOD ADVISER REGIBAUD, 
BELGIAN AMBASSADOR REYN, AND EMBASSY POLOFF. JOINING THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY WERE EUR SENIOR ADVISER SIMMONS, D SPECIAL 
ASSISTANT KAIDANOW, AND EUR/UBI•DESK OFFICER. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TOLD FLAHAUT HE WOULD BE DEPARTING FOR MOSCOW THE 
FOLLOWING DAY TO DISCUSS SUMMIT PREPARATIONS, HIS TALKS 
FOCUSING ON SECURITY ISSUES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ALSO 
NOTED THAT HE HAD RECENTLY RETURNED FROM AFRICA, AND WAS 
WILLING TO DISCUSS RUSSIA, AFRICA, OR WHATEVER OTHER TOPICS 
INTERESTED FLAHAUT. 
4. r'Ci.FLAHAUT ASKED FIRST FOR THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
THOUGHTS ON RUSSIAN PRESIDENT PUTIN. PUTIN WAS STILL 
LARGELY AN UNKNOWN QUANTITY, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT 
OFFERED. THE PRESIDENT HAD MET HIM TWICE, TALBOTT HAD MET 
PUTIN THREE TIMES. SECRETARY ALBRIGHT HAD MET HIM MOST 
RECENTLY, IN FEBRUARY, WHEN HE WAS ACTING PRESIDENT. 
THOUGH THE RELATIONSHIP WITH PUTIN WAS STILL EVOLVING, 
CERTAIN TRENDS WERE BEGINNING TO EMERGE: 
PUTIN IS THE FIRST REAL POST-SOVIET PERSONALITY• 
(YELTSIN WAS A REVOLUTIONARY, BUT STILL ALSO A PRODUCT OF 
CAREER COMMUNIST PARTY MEMBERSHIP). EXPECT PUTIN'S 
SUBSTANCE AND STYLE TO DIFFER FROM HIS PREDECESSORS'. 
PUTIN WANTS TO BE RUSSIA'S LEADER FOR A LONG TIME, AND,
UNDER HIS DIRECTION WANTS TO MAKE RUSSIA A GREAT POWER 
AGAIN. HIS INSTINCTS ARE TO USE A HARD FIST WHERE NEEDED 
TO GET THAT DONE. 
-- BUT HE IS ALSO QUITE PRAGMATIC, WANTS RUSSIA TO DEVELOP 
AS A DEMOCRACY (UNDER HIS LEADERSHIP) AND TO PLAY BY 
INTERNATIONAL RULES. PUTIN KNOWS ALSO THAT TO BECOME GREAT 
AGAIN, RUSSIA MUST PLUG INTO THE GLOBAL ECONOMY, WITH ALL 
THAT ECONOMIC INTEGRATION ENTAILS. 
-- THE WEST CAN AFFECT THE WAY PUTIN EVOLVES AS RUSSIA'S 
LEADER, AND PERHAPS EXERCISE SOME INFLUENCE ON THE MARGINS • 
ON HOW HE DEVELOPS.

6. .."'"(15.4,RESPONDING TO AMBASSADOR REYN, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
DECLINED TO PREDICT HOW CURRENT TREATY MODIFICATION TALKS 
WITH RUSSIA MIGHT CONCLUDE. HE NOTED THAT NATIONAL 
SECURITY ADVISER SANDY BERGER HAD JUST RETURNED FROM MOSCOW 
MEETINGS WITH PUTIN AND WITH BERGER'S COUNTERPART. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY POINTED OUT THAT THERE IS STRONG DOMESTIC 
RUSSIAN OPPOSITION IN SOME QUARTERS TO NMD, NOTABLY FROM 
THE MOD AND THE COMMUNIST PARTY. PUTIN HAD CAUTIONED 
BERGER THAT NMD WAS A DIFFICULT ISSUE BUT PROMISED THAT 
DISCUSSION WOULD CONTINUE. IN MAKING THE DECISION WHETHER 
TO GO AHEAD WITH NMD, THE PRESIDENT WOULD CERTAINLY LOOK AT 
RELATIONS WITH ALLIES AND WITH RUSSIA AS IMPORTANT 
COMPONENTS OF ONE OF THE KEY DECISION CRITERIA, ALONG WITH 
OTHER FACTORS INCLUDING A NEW INTELLIGENCE THREAT 
ASSESSMENT. BOTH BILATERALLY AND AT NATO, BELGIUM HAS 
HEARD OUR NMD VIEWS FROM U/S HOLUM AND A/S BOHLEN AND 
OTHERS, THE SECRETARY MAY RAISE NMD IF SHE SEES BELGIAN 
FOREIGN MINISTER MICHEL AT FLORENCE, AND THE NATO DEFMINS 
MEETING IN TWO WEEKS OFFERS ANOTHER OPPORTUNITY TO DISCUSS. 
MOREOVER, SECDEF COHEN MIGHT RAISE IT WITH FLAHAUT IN THEIR 
MEETING THE NEXT DAY. 
7. 1)&1 AMBASSADOR REYN ASKED ABOUT THE STATUS OF START III 
TALKS WITH RUSSIA. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ANSWERED THAT THE 
USG IS NOT PREPARED YET TO BEGIN FORMAL START III 
NEGOTIATIONS, BUT WAS PREPARED TO INTENSIFY INFORMAL 
DISCUSSIONS WITH RUSSIA ON START III ISSUES. WE HAVE 
ALREADY BEEN TALKING TO RUSSIA INFORMALLY ABOUT START III 
FOR SOME TIME, HE ADDED. 
8. FLAHAUT THEN ASKED FOR THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
THOUGHTS ON THE BALKANS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REPLIED THAT 
IN KOSOVO, KFOR HAS BEEN DOING AN OUTSTANDING JOB IN 
DIFFICULT CIRCUMSTANCES, AND BELGIUM'S KFOR FORCES HAVE 
MADE A PARTICULARLY NOTEWORTHY CONTRIBUTION. KFOR'S WORK 
WILL CONTINUE FOR SOME TIME, BUT THE REAL EFFORT NOW MUST 
BE TO PROVIDE UNMIK THE SUPPORT AND RESOURCES NEEDED TO 
ACCELERATE CIVILIAN IMPLEMENTATION. REYN ASKED FOR 
TALBOTT'S THOUGHTS ON THE WARNER AMENDMENT. TALBOTT NOTED 
THAT THE SENATE VOTE WAS A GOOD OUTCOME, BUT THAT CONGRESS' 
WOULD CONTINUE TO TAKE AN ACTIVE INTEREST IN OUR ROLE IN 
THE BALKANS. OUR ARGUMENT AGAINST THE WARNER AMENDMENT HAD 
PROVED EFFECTIVE: PASSAGE WOULD EMBOLDEN MILOSEVIC AND 
THOSE WHO WANTED INSTABILITY TO FLOURISH IN THE REGION. IN 
SERBIA, MILOSEVIC WOULD CONTINUE TO HANG ON AS LONG AS' 
POSSIBLE. THE OPPOSITION HAD BEEN ENERGIZED BY THE 
EMERGENCE OF THE STUDENT MOVEMENT BUT WAS STILL WEAK AND 
DIVIDED. THE RUSSIANS ARE GETTING FED UP WITH MILOSEVIC, 
BUT THEIR INSTINCTS ARE TO CONTINUE TO REACH OUT TO HIM. 
THE SECRETARY WILL DISCUSS THE SITUATION IN SERBIA'WITH
