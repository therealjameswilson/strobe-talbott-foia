2. FOLLOWING IS A SUMMARY COVERING MEETING OF DEPUTY SECRETARIES 
TALBOTT AND DEUTCH WITH DOMINICAN PRESIDENT BALAGUER. * 
3. BEGIN SUMMARY. ON AUGUST 30, A DELEGATION LED BY DEPUTY 
SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT, DEPUTY SECRETARY OF DEFENSE JOHN 
DEUTCH AND NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISER TO THE VICE PRESIDENT LEON 
FUERTH MET FOR ONE HOUR WITH DOMINICAN PRESIDENT BALAGUER. TALBOTT 
TOLD BALAGUER THAT THERE IS NO LONGER ANY ALTERNATIVE TO MILITARY 
INTERVENTION IN HAITI. THE ONLY REMAINING QUESTION IS THE 
CIRCUMSTANCES OF THE INTERVENTION. IT CAN BE UNDER HOSTILE 
CIRCUMSTANCES IF GENERAL CEDRAS AND HIS SUPPORTERS TRY TO RETAIN 
POWER OR A PEACEFUL ENTRY IF THEY LEAVE VOLUNTARILY. BALAGUER AND 
THE DOMINICAN GOVERNMENT COULD HELP SHAPE WHICH SCENARIO UNFOLDS, 
TALBOTT SAID. HE ASKED THAT THE GODR CONVINCE CEDRAS OF THE 
INEVITABILITY OF A MILITARY INTERVENTION AND INCREASE THE PRESSURE 
ON THE HAITIAN MILITARY BY SIGNIFICANTLY STRENGTHENING THE ECONOMIC 
EMBARGO. 
4. BALAGUER AGREED TO BOTH REQUESTS, BUT SUGGESTED THE WARNING TO 
CEDRAS WOULD BE MORE EFFECTIVE IF IT CAME FROM A RANKING U.S. 
OFFICIAL. BALAGUER SAID CEDRAS STILL BELIEVES A MILITARY 
INTERVENTION IS UNLIKELY. REGARDING BORDER SMUGGLING, BALAGUER 
SOUGHT UNDERSTANDING FOR THE DIFFICULT CIRCUMSTANCES CONFRONTING 
BORDER COMMUNITIES AND THE MILITARY MEMBERS TASKE WITH 
IMPLEMENTING THE EMBARGO. 
5. DEPUTY SERETARYTALBOTT ALSO STRESSED THAT DEMOCRACY IN THE 
DOMINICAN REPUBLIC IS JUST AS IMPORTANT AS IT IS N HAITI. HE 
REITERATED OTHER US CALLS FOR FULL IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PACT FOR 
DEMOCRACY. BALAGUER OFFERED HIS ASSURANCES THAT THIS IS OCCURRING. 
END SUMMARY. 
INEVITABILITY OF HAITIAN INTERVENTION 
6. AFTER INTRODUCTORY COMMENTS BY BOTH SIDES AND A BRIEF REVIEW OF 
THE CARICOM MEETING IN KINGSTON, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT INFORMED 
BALAGUER THAT THERE WILL BE A MULTINATIONAL DEPLOYMENT IN HAITI. 
HE STRESSED THIS NOT A THREAT BUT A FACT. TALBOTT REPORTED THAT IN 
HIS MEETING EARLIER THAT DAY IN KINGSTON THAT A NUMBER OF CARIBBEAN 
GOVERNMENTS COMMITTED THEMSELVES TO JOIN WITH THE UNITED STATES, 
THE UNITED KINGDOM AND ARGENTINA TO PARTICIPATE IN THE DEPLOYMENT. 
DEPUTY SECRETARY DEUTCH REINFORCED THESE REMARKS BY NOTING THAT 
PLANNING FOR THE INTERVENTION IS NOW COMPLETE. HE ADDED THAT THE 
FORCE USED WILL BE OVERWHELMING IN ORDER TO ACHIEVE ITS OBJECTIVES 
RAPIDLY WHILE HOLDING CAUSALITIES TO A MINIMUM.
