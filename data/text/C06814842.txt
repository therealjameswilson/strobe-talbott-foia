SUBJECT: UKRAINIAN AMBASSADOR-DESIGNATE ANTON BUTEIKO:

DECLASSIFIED 
1. N UKRAINIAN AMBASSADOR-DESIGNATE ANTON BUTEIKO 
PRESENTED COPIES OF HIS CREDENTIALS TO ACTING SECRETARY 
TALBOTT IN A MEETING AT THE DEPARTMENT ON DECEMBER 7. 
BUTEIKO (NOW "APPOINTED AMBASSADOR") IS ALREADY SCHEDULING 
INITIAL CALLS WITH STATE DEPARTMENT OFFICIALS; HE WILL HAVE 
HIS FIRST MEETING WITH COORDINATOR FOR ASSISTANCE TO THE 
NIS BILL TAYLOR ON DECEMBER 11. S/CPR IS REQUESTING A DATE 
IN JANUARY FOR BUTEIKO AND SEVERAL OTHER AMBASSADORS TO 
FORMALLY PRESENT THEIR CREDENTIALS AT THE WHITE HOUSE. 
TALBOTT NOTED WE WOULD DO WHAT WE CAN'TO MOVE THIS ALONG. 
BUTEIKO WAS ACCOMPANIED BY UKRAINIAN EMBASSY ACTING DCM 
NATALIA ZARUDNA. TALBOTT WAS JOINED BY ACTING S/NI S ROSS 
WILSON, D SPECIAL ASSISTANT JOHN BASS, PROTOCOL OFFICER 
MARIA SOTIROPOULOS AND EUR/WST DIRECTOR MARY WARLICK 
(NOTETAKER). 
2. 1.64.. TALBOTT TOOK THE OPPORTUNITY TO RAISE WITH BUTEIKO 
THE SUBJECT OF CERTIFICATION OF U.S. ASSISTANCE TO UKRAINE. 
HE EMPHASIZED THE NEED FOR UKRAINE, IN VIEW OF THE FEBRUARY 
18 DEADLINE, TO FOCUS ON MAKING PROGRESS WITH THE IMF ON 
ECONOMIC REFORMS AND WITH U.S. INVESTORS ON OUTSTANDING 
BUSINESS DISPUTES. LITTLE PROGRESS HAD BEEN MADE ON THESE 
DISPUTES SINCE LAST APRIL, AND EVEN UKRAINE'S FRIENDS IN 
CONGRESS WERE CONCERNED, TALBOTT SAID. BUTEIKO NOTED HIS 
CONCERN WITH LANGUAGE IN THE APPROPRIATIONS BILL WHICH 
CALLS FOR "REFORM AND EFFECTIVE ENFORCEMENT OF COMMERCIAL 
AND TAX CODES," POINTING OUT THAT IN AN ELECTION YEAR, IT 
WILL BE DIFFICULT FOR THE ADMINISTRATION TO ENCOURAGE THE 
RADA TO ADOPT SUCH LEGISLATION. HE ALSO NOTED THAT WHILE 
UKRAINE HAS AN AGREED UPON PROGRAM WITH THE IMF, THE GOU 
WOULD LIKE TO LOOK AT HOW THIS PROGRAM CAN BE ADAPTED TO 
NEW CONDITIONS PREVAILING IN UKRAINE SINCE THE ONSET OF THE 
RUSSIAN ECONOMIC CRISIS. TALBOTT SAID HE WOULD PASS ALONG 
THESE CONCERNS TO TREASURY DEPPUTY SECRETARY LARRY SUMMERS. 
3. BUTEIKO NOTED SEVERAL OTHER ITEMS ON HIS AGENDA FOR 
THE ,NEXT YEAR: A JANUARY VISIT BY PRESIDENT KUCHMA TO 
WASHINGTON; UKRAINE'S PARTICIPATION IN THE APRIL NATO 
SUMMIT AND DESIRE FOR A NATO-UKRAINE EVENT; UKRAINE'S 
DESIRE FOR A POLISH-UKRAINE-U.S. TRILATERAL SUMMIT ON THE 
MARGINS OF THE NATO SUMMIT; A DECISION ON DATES FOR THE 
NEXT MEETING OF THE GORE-KUCHMA COMMISSION; A VISIT BY THE 
PRESIDENT OR VICE PRESIDENT TO UKRAINE IN EARLY OCTOBER 
1999 (BEFORE THE PRESIDENTIAL ELECTION); AND, A VISIT BY 
TALBOTT TO KIEV IN SEPTEMBER. UKRAINE WOULD ALSO LIKE TO 
BE FORMALLY REMOVED FROM JACKSON-VAN 1K AND RECEIVE MFN 
STATUS. BUTEIKO WELCOMED U.S. CONSULTATIONS WITH THE EU ON 
UKRAINE AND SUBMITTED PROPOSED LANGUAGE ON THIS FOR
