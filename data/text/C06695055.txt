SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY'S MEETING WITH NEW ZEALAND 
FOREIGN MINISTER MCKINNON 
1.   ENTIRE TEXT. 
DECLASSIFIED 
2. IN A BRIEF MEETING WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT ON THE 
MARGINS OF THE ASEAN PMC MEETING ON JULY 26, NEW ZEALAND 
FOREIGN MINISTER MCKINNON MADE A PLEA FOR AN EARLY 
INVITATION FOR PRIME MINISTER BOLGER TO MEET WITH PRESIDENT 
CLINTON AT THE WHITE HOUSE. MCKINNON SAID HE UNDERSTOOD 
THAT A FULL PRESIDENTIAL SCHEDULE LEADING UP TO NOVEMBER 
ELECTIONS WOULD PRECLUDE A MEETING THIS FALL. HOWEVER, AN 
EARLY MEETING IN WASHINGTON WAS ESSENTIAL TO DEMONSTRATE 
THAT THE U.S. AND NEW ZEALAND HAD TURNED OVER A NEW LEAF IN 
THEIR RELATIONS. A WHITE HOUSE MEETING WOULD SHOW THAT, 
WHILE DIFFERENCES EXIST IN THE DEFENSE AREA, THE U.S. AND 
NEW ZEALAND RAVE A POS/TIVE RELATIONSH/P IN OTHER KEY AREAS 
AND THE U.S. DOES NOT SEE NEW ZEALAND AS A PARIAH. HE SAID 
THE WASHINGTON VENUE WAS ESSENTIAL FOR SYMBOLIC pURPOSES; 
A MEETING IN NEW YORK DURING THE UNGA WOULD NOT SUFFICE. 
3. MCKINNON REFERENCED SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER'S ADMONITION 
TO HIM THAT NEW ZEALAND MUST STILL DO MORE ON ITS OWN TO 
CREATE A POSITIVE ENVIRONMENT FOR THE U.S.-NEW ZEALAND 
RELATIONSHIP. MCKINNON SAID HE HAD AND WOULD CONTINUE TO 
PRESS FOR SUCH PROGRESS (UNSPECIFIED). 
4. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CONFIRMED THE BUSY WHITE HOUSE 
SCHEDULE THIS FALL, WAS NONCOMMITTAL ON PROSPECTS FOR.A 
MEETING AND AGREED TO CONVEY HIS MESSAGE.
