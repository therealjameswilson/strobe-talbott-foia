2. SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH 
PRESIDENT ARISTIDE FOR ONE HOUR AFTERNOON OF MARCH 7. 
THIS FOLLOWED A LONGER TETE-A-TETE OVER LUNCH 
IMMEDIATELY BEFOREHAND. ARISTIDE REITERATED HIS 
STRONG DESIRE THAT PRESIDENT CLINTON VISIT HAITI. 
DISCUSSION OF U.S. CONCERNS ABOUT GOH'S ABILITY TO 
HOST THE OAS GENERAL ASSEMBLY IN JUNE CULMINATED IN 
AGREEMENT FOR A TECHNICAL TEAM FROM WASHINGTON (USG 
AND OAS) TO VISIT HAITI WEEK OF MARCH 13 FOR 
DISCUSSIONS WITH GOH ON THE OAS/GA. THERE WAS ALSO A 
DISCUSSION OF ORGANIZATION AND STRUCTURAL ISSUES WITH 
THE DEMISE OF THE MILITARY FAD'H. ARISTIDE APPEALED 
FOR ASSISTANCE IN CREATING JOBS. END SUMMARY. 
3. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH PRESIDENT 
ARISTIDE MARCH 7, ACCOMPANIED BY AMBASSADORS SWING, 
BABBITT AND DOBBINS, AND NSC'S RANDY BEERS. SECRETARY 
OF STATE FOR JUSTICE LEON JEUNE AND PALACE CHIEF OF 
STAFF LESLIE VOLTAIRE JOINED FROM THE HAITIAN SIDE. 
4. PRESIDENT CLINTON VISIT TO HAITI 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT INFORMED PRESIDENT ARISTIDE 
THAT NO DECISION HAD YET BEEN MADE ON A VISIT BY 
PRESIDENT CLINTON FOR THE MARCH 31 MNF/UNMIH 
TRANSITION CEREMONY. HE ASSURED ARISTIDE THAT THE 
PRESIDENT IS EAGER TO COME -- "IT'S JUST A SCHEDULING 
QUESTION." ARISTIDE SAID THAT IN ANY CASE "IT WILL BE 
A GREAT HONOR WHEN HE COMES AND HAITIANS WOULD HAVE AN 
OPPORTUNITY TO THANK HIM." HE ADDED, JOKINGLY, 
HOWEVER, THAT "HE MUST NOT RUN FOR OFFICE HERE BECAUSE 
HE'S TOO POPULAR.
