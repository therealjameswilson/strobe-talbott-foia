2. ~ SUMMARY. IN A LUNCHEON JULY 12 HOSTED BY RUSSIAN
FOREIGN MINISTER IVANOV ON THE FRINGES OF THE G-8 MIYAZAKI
MINISTERIAL, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT REVIEWED U.S.
PRIORITIES FOR THE UPCOMING PRESIDENTIAL BILATERAL IN
OKINAWA (STRATEGIC COOPERATION AND IRAN NON PROLIFERATION
ISSUES) AND U.S. CONCERNS ABOUT CONTINUING RUSSIAN SUPPORT
FOR THE MILOSEVIC REGIME IN SERBIA. IVANOV SAID RUSSIAN
PRESIDENT PUTIN ALSO PLANNED TO RAISE THE NEXT STEPS IN THE
U.S.-RUSSIAN ECONOMIC RELATIONSHIP, INCLUDING THE OUTLOOK
FOR U,S. INVESTMENT IN RUSSIA. ON SERBIA, TALBOTT PRESSED
IVANOV TO JOIN A UNITED G-8 FRONT IN CONDEMNING MILOSEVIC'S
ATTEMPTS TO USE EXTRA CONSTITUTIONAL MEANS TO EXTEND HIS
TERM IN OFFICE AND UNDERMINE MONTENEGRO'S DEMOCRATICALLY
ELECTED GOVERNMENT. IVANOV AGREED TO REEXAMINE THE

3. ~ IVANOV OPENED THE MEETING WITH A BRIEF REVIEW OF THE
JULY 10 CALL BETWEEN THE PRESIDENTS, WHICH HAD PREVIEWED
THE BILATERAL IN OKINAWA. QUESTIONS OF NON PROLIFERATION
AND IRAN WOULD CLEARLY BE A MAJOR TOPIC, IN LIGHT OF THE
VICE-PRESIDENT'S LETTER TO PM KASYANOV. IVANOV SAID PUTIN
WOULD ALSO WANT TO DISCUSS HIS ECONOMIC PROGRAM, AND NEXT
STEPS IN OUR BILATERAL ECONOMIC RELATIONSHIP, INCLUDING THE
OUTLOOK FOR INVESTMENT. ON STRATEGIC ISSUES, THE RUSSIAN
SIDE WOULD SEEK PROGRESS ON START III.HE PROMISED THAT
MORE CONCRETE TOPICS FOR DISCUSSION WOULD BE TRANSMITTED
PRIOR TO THE SUMMIT MEETING. IVANOV NOTED PROGRESS ON
ESTABLISHING THE WORKING GROUP ON AFGHANISTAN, WHERE THE
COUNTERPART TO UNDER SECRETARY PICKERING WOULD BE FIRST
DEPUTY FM TRUBNIKOV.
4. (~ TALBOTT RESPONDED THAT PUTIN'S CALL WAS VERY
FAVORABLY RECEIVED: THE TONE WAS POSITIVE AND THE IMPETUS
FOR THE CALL WAS ENCOURAGING. FROM THE U,S. PERSPECTIVE,
THE CALL HAD HIGHLIGHTED A NUMBER OF PRIORITY AREAS FOR THE
UPCOMING SUMMIT MEETING, INCLUDING CONTINUED WORK ON
COOPERATIVE STABILITY. WE WERE LOOKING FOR MODEST BUT REAL
STEPS FORWARD FROM THE UPCOMING MEETING; ALTHOUGH
S IGNI FICANT PROGRESS ON START III MIGHT BE DIFFICULT~ WE
WERE WITHIN REACH OF A PUBLIC STATEMENT ON THE COOPERATIVE
STABILITY INITIATIVE. PUTIN SHOULD ALSO EXPECT THE
PRESIDENT TO RAISE IN DETAIL U.S. NON PROLIFERATION
CONCERNS WITH RESPECT TO IRAN. TALBOTT ENCOURAGED IVANOV
TO REVIEW CAREFULLY THE VICE PRESIDENT'S LETTER TO KASYANOV
ON THIS ISSUE. HE ADDED THAT THE PRESIDENT WOULD LIKE TO
TAKE A COOPERATIVE AND NON-CONFRONTATIONAL APPROACH IN HiS
MEETING WITH PUTIN, BUT ACKNOWLEDGED THAT THE IRAN ISSUE
HAD THE POTENTIAL TO TURN INTO A MAJOR IRRITANT IN THE
BILATEFAL RELATIONSHIP.

5 ~ LOOKING AHEAD TO THE G 8 FM S MEETING~ IVANOV
FOCUSSED ON THE IMPASSE IN NEGOTIATIONS OVER COMMUNIQUE
LANGUAGE ON SERBIA, SUGGESTING THAT WHILE THE EIGHT SHOULD
EXPRESS WISHES/INTENTIONS ABOUT THE MILOSEVIC REGIME, IT
SHOULD REFRAIN FROM MAKING A (NEGATIVE) ASSESSMENT OF
MILOSEVIC, A LA "UNSC-STYLE" RESOLUTIONS. TALBOTT WARNED
OF THE CONSEQUENCES SHOULD MILOSEVIC SUCCEED IN
MANIPULATING REALITY AND APPEARANCES TO THE ADVANTAGE OF
HIS INTERNATIONAL REPUTATION AND DISADVANTAGE OF THE
SERBIAN PEOPLE.'E ASKED IF THE U.S. AND RUSSIA COULD NOT
FIND A WAY FORWARD IN THE COMMUNIQUE THAT MAINTAINED A
SOLID G-8 CONDEMNATION OF MILOSEVIC AND HIS ATTEMPTS TO
CLING TO POWER AND MUSCLE ASIDE THE DEMOCRATIC LEADERSHIP
OF MONTENEGRO. DON'T LET MILOSEVIC DIVIDE THE U.S. AND
RUSSIA ON THIS ISSUE, HE) ADDED: WE HAVE WORKED TOO HARD TO
FIND COMMON GROUND IN THE BALKANS, INCLUDING A GREAT DEAL
OF PERSONAL EFFORT BY IVANOV.
6. ~ IVANOV SOUGHT TO SET THE COMMUNIQUE DISCUSSION IN A
BROADER CONTEXT, ASKING HOW THE EIGHT HOPED TO INFLUENCE
THE LONG-TERM SITUATION IN THE BALKANS. HE GAVE A NEGATIVE
ASSESSMENT OF THE STATUS QUO, NOTING THAT SHOULD ELECTIONS
GO FORWARD IN THE FRY, MILOSEVIC WOULD CERTAINLY WIN IN THE
FACE OF WEAK OPPOSITION, AND THAT CURRENT SIGNALS SUGGEST
THAT EITHER A NEW WAR OR THE DISINTEGRATION OF THE FRY WERE
POSSIBLE. HOW MIGHT THE EIGHT, AND THE U.S. AND RUSSIA
MORE SPECIFICALLY, POSITIVELY INFLUENCE THE COURSE OF THESE
EVENTS? MORE SANCTIONS SEEMS AN UNPROFITABLE PATH, AND
NATO HAS INDICATED IT HAS LITTLE STOMACH FOR A SECOND
BOMBING CAMPAIGN. THE PRESIDENTS HAD DISCUSSED THE FRY
SITUATION IN MOSCOW, AND OUR ASSESSMENTS WERE NOT
DISSIMILAR. PUTIN HAD SAID THAT RUSSIA MAY NOT LIKE
MILOSEVIC EITHER, BUT THE QUESTION WAS WHAT WE COULD DO TO
AVOID FURTHER INTERNAL CONFLICT. TODAY KFOR CANNOT CONTROL
THE SITUATION IN KOSOVO -- MUCH LESS IN MONTENEGRO — AND
ITS EFFORTS ARE NOT "SOLVING" THE UNDERLYING PROBLEMS.
IVANOV COMMENTED THAT CONDITIONS NEED TO BE CREATED THAT
WOULD ALLOW FOR THE EMERGENCE OF NEW FORCES INSIDE THE FRY.
THE CURRENT OPPOSITION IS UNABLE TO GET ONTO ITS FEET
BECAUSE OF THE GENERAL ISOLATION OF THE FRY. WHEN BOSNIA
WAS BROUGHT OUT OF ISOLATION, HE SAID, DEMOCRACY BLOOMED
AND OPPOSITION PARTIES TOOK HOLD.
7. ( TALBOTT AGREED THAT NO ONE IN NATO WANTS TO SEE
RENEWED CONFLICT IN THE BALKANS, BUT SPECULATION ABOUT HOW
THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY MIGHT RESPOND TO FUTURE
MILOSEVIC UNDERTAKINGS IS NOT AS IMPORTANT RIGHT NOW AS
WHAT THE EIGHT SAY ABOUT MILOSEVIC OVER THE NEXT ..TWO WEEKS.
WE NEED TO DRAW ATTENTION TO MILOSEVIC AND HIS INTENTIONS
HE IS TRYING TO WHITTLE AWAY THE DEMOCRATIC PROGRESS MADE
IN MONTENEGRO. HE IS CREATING THE CONDITIONS FOR FUTURE
FRY TROOP INTERVENTION IN MONTENEGRO. WE NEED TO USE THE
FOREIGN MINISTERS'EETING AND NEXT WEEK'S SUMMIT TO SEND A
SIGNAL TO MILOSEVIC THAT "WE SEE WHAT YOU ARE UP TO, AND
WILL NOT TOLERATE IT."

9. ~ IVANOV AGREED To REVIEW THE COMMUNIQUE LANGUAGE, BUT
AGAIN RETURNED TO HIS BROADER POINTS, SAYING THE QUESTION
WAS NOT REALLY ONE OF COMMUNIQUE WORDING, BUT RATHER HOW WE
CAN AFFECT THE OUTCOME IN THE REGION AND WHAT THE REACTION
TO THE G-8 STATEMENT WILL BE. HE CONTINUED THAT THERE WAS
OPPOSITION TO ADDRESSING THE UNDERLYING PROBLEMS IN THE
BALKANS EVEN BEFORE THE NATO BOMBING — NOW THE SITUATION IS
THAT MUCH MORE INTRACTABLE. IN KOSOVO, THE SITUATION HAS
DETERIORATED, AND EVEN NATO ADMITS THIS. THERE IS NO WAY
OUT OF THE PROBLEMS THERE -- EVEN WITH ELECTIONS — AND
KOSOVO IS BECOMING A SERIOUS SOURCE OF TENSION IN EUROPE.
IN THE FRY, ONE NEEDS TO PUT ONESELF IN MILOSEVIC'S SHOES.
THE WAR CRIMES INDICTMENT GIVES HIM NO WAY OUT. MOREOVER,
MILOSEVIC KNOWS THAT AS LONG AS HE IS IN POWER HE IS SAFE
FROM THE REACH OF THE HAGUE.
10. ~TALBOTT RESPONDED THAT WE DID NOT SEE THE SITUATION
AS UTTERLY HOPELESS AS IVANOV HAD DESCRIBED, AND WE
SHOULDN'T GlUE UP NOW. HE SUGGESTED THAT THE U.S. AND
RUSSIA GET THEIR RESPECTIVE EXPERTS TOGETHER TO LOOK FOR A
WAY FORWARD IN THE BALKANS. HE INVITED IVANOV TO SEND A
DELEGATION TO WASHINGTON FOR THIS PURPOSE AS EARLY AS THE
LAST WEEK OF JULY. IVANOV AGREED TO CONSIDER THE OFFER.
ON THE COMMUNIQUE LANGUAGE IMPASSE, TALBOTT AND IVANOV
AGREED TO HAVE UNDER SECRETARY PICKERING AND DFM MAMEDOV
WORK TOGETHER TO COME UP WITH LANGUAGE ACCEPTABLE TO ALL
