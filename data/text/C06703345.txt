2. 1.6,1,„... DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET IN PARIS MARCH 4 WITH 
GERMAN POLITICAL DIRECTOR WOLFGANG ISCHINGER. DISCUSSION 
CENTERED ON NATO/RUSSIA RELATIONS AND TURKEY. TALBOTT 
SAID HIS UPCOMING MEETINGS IN MOSCOW WOULD FOCUS ON A 
NATO/RUSSIA ACCORD AND ON LAYING THE GROUNDWORK FOR A 
SUCCESSFUL HELSINKI SUMMIT. THE BEST TIMING AND FORMAT 
FOR SEALING A NATO/RUSSIA ACCORD, TALBOTT SUGGESTED, 
APPEARED TO BE IN LATE MAY AT A NATO/RUSSIA MEETING "AT 
17." ISCHINGER QUESTIONED WHETHER SOLANA SHOULD TABLE A 
DRAFT NATO/RUSSIA CHARTER NOW IN MOSCOW. HE THOUGHT IT 
WOULD REDUCE NATO'S "FLEXIBILITY." TALBOTT DISAGREED: 
PRIMAKOV NOW SEES SOLANA AS HIS MAIN INTERLOCUTOR. WE 
NEED TO REINFORCE THAT IMPRESSION. ALSO, NATO HAS AGREED 
ON THE ELEMENTS OF A CHARTER; SOLANA'S TABLING A DRAFT NOW 
WILL NOT REDUCE OUR FLEXIBILITY LATER. ISCHINGER ACCEPTED 
THIS AND ENDORSED THE IDEA OF SOLANA PRESENTING A DRAFT 
CHARTER TO THE RUSSIANS. 
ISCHINGER IDENTIFIED STATIONING OF FORCES AND NATO 
INFRASTRUCTURE IN NEW NATO MEMBERS AS THE KEY ISSUES FOR 
RUSSIA. HE WARNED OF THE DOMESTIC POLITICAL RISKS (IN 
GERMANY) OF "CONCEDING TOO MUCH" TO RUSSIA ON THESE' 
POINTS. TALBOTT AGREED AND POINTED TO THE NEED TO 
EDUCATE" RUSSIA FURTHER. ON POSSIBLE DUMA RATIFICATION OF 
A NATO/RUSSIA ACCORD, ISCHINGER SAID THE GERMANS ARE 
STRONGLY ADVISING THE RUSSIANS AGAINST IT. TALBOTT AGREED 
THAT DUMA RATIFICATION COULD STRIP THE NATO/RUSSIA ACCORD 
OF ANY REAL MEANING.
