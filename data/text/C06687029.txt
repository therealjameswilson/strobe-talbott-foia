Re: Russia's Outstanding Commercial Debt to U.S. Companies 
Dear Ms. Fabrizio: 
Thank you for your "fax" of last Friday requesting up-to-date information on 
the failure of the Russian Government to make any progress at all on its outstanding 
debt to INDSPEC Chemical Corporation, the employee-owned company which 
manufactured and shipped product to Russia at the request of its government in early 
1990. 
It was good to hear that Secretary Ron Brown intends to, make this an 
important issue in trade discussions with the Russians, since we had so little success 
with the previous Administration. 
Frankly, more than "talk" with the Russians seems necessary. In the case of
