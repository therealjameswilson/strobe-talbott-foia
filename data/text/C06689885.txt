Dear Itamar: 
Thank you for your kind letter -- and for passing 
along Mr. Beilin's message. One of my few regrets about 
the past year is that I haven't had more of a chance to 
work with you. I hope the new job will permit me to do 
that from time to time. I'm pecking out this note en 
route home from the summit. I left the President's party 
in Minsk; they flew on to see President Assad in Geneva, 
while I went to Brussels to brief NATO. 
Let's plan to get togehter soon -- and often.
