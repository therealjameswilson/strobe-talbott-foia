RELEASE IN FULL 
Enclosed with this letter is a Memorandum regarding a possible American role, 
undertaken within the context of forthcoming Group of Seven meetings, in helping Japan 
to resolve its "Northern Territories" dispute with Russia. This could facilitate Japan's 
cooperation, including increased financial and technical assistance, in addressing the 
difficult problem of stabilizing Russia today. 
The ideas described therein I are ones that I earlier sent to Tony Lake's office, 
just before President Clinton went to Vancouver for his .Summit meeting with President 
Yeltsin. I have subsequently sent them to Secretary Christopher as well. 
Enclosed, in addition, is a longer paper based on the presentation that I gave to 
Mr. Motoo Shiina's Policy Study Group in. Tokyo. The policy proposals are in the last 
section (V), pp. 19-27. You will also note the references to your recent work, At the 
Highest Levels. 
It is my hope that these ideas, and supporting information, will be useful in 
planning our part in consolidating the post-Cold War order, especially in northeast Asia. 
With best regards, 
Yours sincerely, 
Alan K. Henrikson
