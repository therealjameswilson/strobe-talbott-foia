RELEASE IN FULL 
Dear Bob: 
You're name came up in Brussels just before the President 
arrived for the NATO Summit. Stu Eizenstat mentioned that 
you're regarded as something of a guru on that side of the 
Atlantic because of your ideas on job retraining and other ways 
of making the transition to an internationally competitive, but 
still socially compassionate, modern state. 
Listening to Stu sing your praises and speak about how 
much influence you have over here, it occurred to me that you 
could.make a big impact on the Russian reformers as well. As 
you know, part of our message to them is "reform with a human 
face." Job retraining is a critical part of the challenge they 
face. 
Would there be any chance at all that you could find some 
time in '94 to make a visit to Russia? I really find this quite 
an exciting prospect and would love to get together with you and 
brainstorm on it. If nothing else, that will give me a chance 
to see you during working hours, which is one of-my greatest 
ambitions in life. 
Thanks, by the way, for your support on the aluminum 
matter. I'll make sure that Dan Speckhard, my economics deputy, 
stays in close touch with Larry Katz on that issue. 
Let me know when you've got a chance to discuss the above. 
Sincerely, 
The Honorable 
Robertdrt-i 
Secretary of Labor, 
Washington, DC.
