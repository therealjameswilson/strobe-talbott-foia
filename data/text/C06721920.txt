4. ( ) THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE UNDERSTOOD GEREMEK WAS 
TRA LING TO BELGRADE MARCH 27 AND WANTED TO FOLLOW up ON 
THEIR CONVERSATION REGARDING KOSOVO. GEREMEK CONFIRMED HE 
WAS GOING TO THE FRY FOR ONE DAY, FIRST TO BELGRADE TO SEE 
PRESIDENT MILOSEVIC AND FOREIGN MINISTER JOVANOVIC, THEN TO 
PRISTINA TO SEE KOSOVAR ALBANIAN LEADER RUGOVA, AND THEN TO 
MONTENEGRO. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED IT WOULD BE A BUSY 
DAY, AND LIKELY A FRUSTRATING ONE AS WELL. HE SAID SPECIAL 
REPRESENTATIVE GELBARD HAD BEEN WORKING THOSE SAME BASES IN-
RECENT DAYS: HE WAS IN BELGRADE NOW AND ALREADY HAD SEEN 
SERBIAN PRESIDENT MILUTINOVIC AND FM JOVANOVIC. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY COMMENTED THAT GELBARD HAD SENSED ZERO MOVEMENT 
AND ZERO FLEXIBILITY. IN PRISTINA, GELBARD HAD SEEN AND 
HEARD THE YUGOSLAV AUTHORITIES WERE CONTINUING TO BEAR DOWN 
HARD ON THE KOSOVAR ALBANIANS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD 
GEREMEK HE WAS MEETING WITH THE SECRETARY IN AN HOUR FOR A 
READOUT ON THE BONN CONTACT GROUP MEETING. HE WANTED TO 
COMPARE NOTES WITH GEREMEK BOTH IN HIS CAPACITY AS THE 
POLISH FOREIGN MINISTER AND AS THE OSCE CHAIRMAN-IN-OFFICE 
TO ENSURE COMPLETE COORDINATION. 
5. 0 GEREMEK SAID HE HAD HEARD THE BONN MEETING WAS 
FRUSTRATING. HE STATED THAT HE UNDERSTOOD THE SECRETARY 
SUPPORTED HIS TRAVEL TO BELGRADE TO SPEAK TO MILOSEVIC AND 
TO PRISTINA TO SPEAK TO RUGOVA. GEREMEK SAID HE INTENDED 
TO PROPOSE THE REINSTALLATION OF THE OSCE PERMANENT MISSION 
AND TO SUPPORT DIALOGUE BETWEEN THE TWO PARTIES. HE NOTED 
HE WAS SPEAKING ON BEHALF OF THE OSCE, BUT ALSO WOULD 
PREPARE A REPORT FOR THE CONTACT GROUP IN FOUR WEEKS. 
GEREMEK SAID HE WOULD TRY TO OBTAIN A PROMISE OF DIALOGUE 
FROM MILOSEVIC, BUT HE WAS NOT HOPEFUL. HE COULD NOT, 
HOWEVER, REFUSE THE INVITATION TO GO WHEN MILOSEVIC HAD 
GIVEN HIM THE RIGHT TO SEE RUGOVA IN PRISTINA, WHICH HAD 
BEEN A CONDITION OF HIS TRIP. GEREMEK ADDED THAT HE WOULD 
BE IN TOUCH WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AFTER HIS VISIT. 
6. ,(.1n THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REPLIED THAT HE AND THE 
SECRETARY WERE GLAD GEREMEK WAS GOING TO THE FRY. HE ASKED 
GEREMEK IF HE COULD SHARE A FEW THOUGHTS WITH HIM ON THE 
ISSUE OF STATUS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THE CONTACT 
GROUP HAD NOT REACHED CONSENSUS ON WHETHER THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY SHOULD ACQUIESCE ON MILOSEVIC'S 
INSISTENCE ON KEEPING KOSOVO IN SERBIA AS OPPOSED TO IN THE 
FRY. HE SAID THE U.S. THOUGHT IT WOULD BE A MISTAKE TO 
CONCEDE THIS POINT THIS EARLY ON. THIS DID NOT MEAN KOSOVO 
WOULD OR SHOULD BE REMOVED FROM SERBIA AT A LATER DATE, BUT 
RATHER THAT KOSOVO'S CONSTITUTIONAL AND JURISDICTIONAL 
STATUS SHOULD BE NEGOTIATED BETWEEN THE KOSOVAR ALBANIANS

7. GS4 THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID MILOSEVIC WOULD TELL 
GERENEK THAT DIALOGUE WOULD TAKE PLACE ONLY IF KOSOVO 
REMAINED PART OF SERBIA. MILOSEVIC'S CONCERNS WERE BASED 
ON HIS EXPERIENCE OVER THE LAST SEVEN TO EIGHT YEARS: IF 
KOSOVO BECAME A FEDERAL REPUBLIC, IT COULD SECEDE BY 
REFERENDUM. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT ALL PARTIES 
AGREED SECESSION WAS NOT THE BEST OUTCOME. HE EMPHASIZED 
THAT IN OUR DIALOGUE WITH MILOSEVIC WE MUST LEAVE OPEN ALL 
OPTIONS FOR THE ULTIMATE CONSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENT. 
8.419T-GEREMEK SAID THEY NEEDED TO RESPECT THE TERRITORIAL 
INTEGRITY OF THE FRY. HE HOPED IT WOULD BE DISCUSSED 
BETWEEN THE KOSOVAR ALBANIANS AND BELGRADE AUTHORITIES - 
THE ULTIMATE CONSTITUTIONAL SOLUTION WAS UP TO THEM. 
GEREMEK NOTED THAT HE WAS NOT SUPPORTING KOSOVO'S NATIONAL 
INDEPENDENCE, NOR WAS HE SUPPORTING THE MOVEMENT FOR , 
CULTURAL OR ETHNIC IDENTITY. THE REST OF IT, HE SAID, 
DEPENDED ON BOTH PARTIES. 
9. ;SI' THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STATED THAT HE BELIEVED THEIR 
POSITIONS WERE IDENTICAL, WHICH HE FOUND ENCOURAGING. HE 
SAID HE DID NOT WANT TO GIVE GEREMEK TALKING POINTS, BUT 
WANTED TO PASS ON AN IDEA OF HIS OWN FOR GEREMEK'S 
CONSIDERATION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID MILOSEVIC WAS AT 
A CROSSROADS. IF HE STAYED ON HIS CURRENT COURSE - 
OPPRESSING THE KOSOVAR ALBANIANS AND REDUCING THEM TO THIRD 
CLASS CITIZENS - HE WOULD GUARANTEE THE LOSS OF KOSOVO. 
MILOSEVIC WOULD ENSURE THAT THE SPORADIC VIOLENCE WOULD 
TURN INTO A FULL-FLEDGED CIVIL WAR AND THE FRY WOULD LOSE 
KOSOVO BY FORCE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID IF MILOSEVIC 
ULTIMATELY WANTED TO KEEP KOSOVO AS PART OF YUGOSLAVIA, IT 
WAS IN HIS INTEREST TO FIND. A POLITICAL SOLUTION THAT WOULD 
GIVE THE KOSOVAR ALBANIANS GENUINE CITIZENSHIP IN THE 
STATE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED GEREMEK IF THE LOGIC OF 
THIS ARGUMENT APPEALED TO HIM. 
10. k£r) GEREMEK SAID YES, AND IT WAS HIS INTENTION TO USE 
THIS ARGUMENT WITH MILOSEVIC. HE SAID HE DID NOT LIKE 
MILOSEVIC, BUT HE WOULD TRY TO CONVINCE HIM. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY WISHED GEREMEK THE BEST FOR HIS TRIP, ADDING THAT 
HE WOULD APPRECIATE A READOUT IF GEREMEK HAD THE 
OPPORTUNITY TO CALL HIM. GEREMEK SAID HE WOULD CALL THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY ON MARCH 28. 
11. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1400 EST.
