Dear George: 
RELEASE IN PART 
B6 
It is now the weekend, and another slightly manic 
work week is past. But there was something very special 
about this particular one -- not just for me, but for the 
Secretary of State4and all my other colleagues who work on 
relations with Russia and its neighbors. That is thanks to 
you. The Monday you spent with us was a wonderful experi-
ence in many ways. You did us a great honor by taking the 
time to. think and talk through with us the larger context 
of the issues we are grappling with. We came away with a 
heightened sense of.where these problems come from and 
where the solutions must lead (if there are solutions!). - 
Also, while you are by no stretch of the imagination a P91-
lyanna, you conveyed a basic sense of gratification about 
what has happened in the past several years and optimism 
about what may happen in the coming period, if cool heads 
and steady hands prevail in Moscow (and, one hopes, in 
Washington). 
I can't tell you how much comment I've heard from 
those who were at the afternoon and dinner sessions about 
what it meant to them. 
On Brooke's behalf as well as my own, please also 
thank Annelise for joining us for dinner. And tell Grace, 
next time you're in touch with her, how much I benefited 
from her office visit and how I hope she'll stay in touch 
with her impressions and advice. 
Perhaps, life and Bundys permitting, I'll get back up 
to Princeton during the winter. 
Once again, many, many thanks.
