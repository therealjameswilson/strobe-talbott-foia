1. I HAD LUNCH AT SPASO 9/24 WITH YUMASHEV AND WENT OVER A 
WIDE RANGE OF TOPICS. YUMASHEV DISMISSED RUMORS OF YELTSIN'S 
RESIGNATION AND STRESSED THAT BOTH SETS OF ELECTIONS WOULD TAKE 
PLACE AS SCHEDULED. HE KNEW YELTSIN HIMSELF SET GREAT STORE BY 
THE NEED TO EFFECT A PEACEFUL HAND-OVER TO HIS SUCCESSOR THROUGH 
THE BALLOT BOX. FURTHERMORE ANY EARLY ELECTIONS WOULD MAKE IT 
MORE DIFFICULT FOR PUTIN TO ACHIEVE THE PRESIDENCY AS HE NEEDED 
TIME TO GARNER POPULARITY AND DEVELOP A TRACK RECORD. 
YUMASHEV WAS VERY, VERY HIGH ON PUTIN. HE CLAIMED PUTIN 
HAD SHOWN HIMSELF TO BE A WELL-ORGANIZED, DECISIVE LEADER IN HIS 
PREVIOUS POSITIONS AND HAD A SPECIAL TALENT FOR ACQUIRING THE 
TRUST OF HIS INTERLOCUTORS WHETHER THEY BE GENERALS, GOVERNORS OR 
THE STRANGE FOLK WHO LEAD THE SPECIAL SERVICES.• STEPASHIN, IN 
CONTRAST, WHILE A DECENT MAN, COULD NEVER SAY NO TO PEOPLE AND 
PROVED TOO WEAK. YUMASHEV FELT THE'BUSINESS COMMUNITY WOULD SEE 
THAT PUTIN SUPPORTS STABLE ECONOMIC POLICIES THAT PROMOTE GROWTH. 
MORE IMPORTANTLY, HE THOUGHT PUTIN WOULD GARNER THE SUPPORT OF 
THE NEXT GENERATION OF RUSSIAN VOTERS -- PEOPLE, LIKE HIMSELF 
BORN IN THE EARLY FIFTIES (HE NOTED YAVLINSKIY, STEPASHIN AND 
PUTIN WERE ALL BORN IN 1952). ALREADY HIS POPULARITY RATINGS ARE 
RISING'ESPECIALLY AS MORE AND MORE RUSSIANS APPROVE OF HIS 
HANDLING OF THE NORTH CAUCASUS AND SECURITY ISSUES. HE 
CONSIDERED THE SPONTANEOUS APPLAUSE THAT GREETED PUTIN'S DUMA 
PERFORMANCE ON DAGESTAN THIS WEEK A RARE AND SIGNIFICANT 
ACHIEVEMENT.
