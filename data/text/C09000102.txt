United States Department of State

RELEASE IN PART 
B6 
Thanks so much for your letter. I will be sure to mention the connection to Kirk. I 
would also like to thank you for your kind invitation to address the Rotary Club. It is 
always tempting to find a good excuse to get to Cleveland for a day or two. 
Unfortunately, my schedule has been very unpredictable. When Secretary Christopher is 
away my presence is required at the State Department. As you have probably noticed he 
has been traveling a great deal. My own travel plans often change at a moment's notice. 
I would hate to make a commitment that I was unable to keep. For these reasons I'm 
afraid I must decline your offer, tempting as it is. If , however, you would like to look 
into inviting another State Department official to speak, please contact Ms. Galit 
Zolkower in my office. She will be happy to put you in touch with the Public Affairs 
office here. Her telephone number is (202) 647-8930. 
Thank you again for the invitation. 
Best regards, 
Strobe Talbott 
David S. Miller 
Evarts Capital, Inc. 
Two Commerce Park Square 
Cleveland, Ohio
