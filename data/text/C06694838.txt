2. SUMMARY. DURING A BRIEF STOP IN BUJUMBURA ON 
OCTOBER 21, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND HIS 
DELEGATION MET WITH BURUNDI'S FOREIGN MINISTER 
JEAN MARIE NGENDAHAYO (FRODEBU, TUTSI-GANWA) AND 
MINISTER OF PLANNING SALVATOR NZIGAMASABO 
(UPRONA, TUTSI). THE MINISTERS THANKED THE USG 
FOR SUPPORT OF THE DEMOCRATIC PROCESS, OFFERED AN 
ASSESSMENT OF THE SITUATION IN RWANDA, AND ASKED 
FOR ASS/STANCE WITH THE ZAIREAN GOVERNMENT TO HELP 
DISARM EX-FAR TROOPS. ACTION REQUESTED BY FOREIGN 
MINISTER IS IN PARAGRAPHS 10 AND 11 BELOW. END 
SUMMARY.

OTHER MEMBERS OF THE DELEGATION, MET WITH FOREIGN 
MINISTER JEAN MARIE NGENDAHAYO AND MINISTER OF 
PLANNING SALVATOR NZIGAMASABO ON OCTOBER 21 DURING 
A BRIEF STOP IN BURUNDI. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
CONGRATULATED THE BURUNDIANS FOR HAVING PUT A 
GOVERNMENT IN PLACE AND HAVING PRESERVED 
DEMOCRATIC INSTITUTIONS, AND ASKED HOW THE U.S.

THE REGION. THE FOREIGN MINISTER AND MINISTER FOR 
PLANNING RESPONDED WITH THEIR VIEWS ON U.S. AID, 
BOTH ECONOMIC AND POLITICAL, DESCRIBED HOW U.S. 
ASSISTANCE WITH THE ZAIRIAN GOVERNMENT TO HELP 
DISARM EX-FAR TROOPS WOULD CONTRIBUTE TO SECURITY 
IN BURUNDI, AND OFFERED THEIR ASSESSMENT ON THE 
DANGERS POSED TO THE REGION BY RWANDA'S 
INSTABILITY.
