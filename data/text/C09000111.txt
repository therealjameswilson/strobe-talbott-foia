RELEASE IN PART 
B6 
Toria I agree with you. IfH were an American citizen, that would be 
one thing, i.e., that we %vele pushing him in an official capacity because we 
want to get an Amcit into a World Bank position. But in this case, I think it 
raises broader ethical questions that L could advise on. That is, when the 
Deputy Secretary of State calls up to try to get a personal friend a job, is 
he using his official position for the possible private gain of another 
person? I think the best and most ethical way to handle this is that  
should list Strobe as a personal reference, and then, when Strobe gets a phone 
call from the Bank checking out the reference, Strobe is free to say whatever 
he wants to. I don't think that Strobe should initiate any call, however. 
Likewise, as I recall the ethical guidelines, he should not write a letter of 
recommendation (or at least, not on official stationery) for anyone. Jim 
Thessin in L/M is the expert on all of this.
