Dear Brigid: 
Thanks for your letter about the latest peculiarity in my 
life. There have been several moments in the last couple of weeks 
when, all things considered, but for the honor, I'd just as soon 
have dispensed with the whole goddamn thing. I'm sure you've seen 
how the Jewish community has come after my scalp for some of my 
writings in the '80s. In dealing with the whole thing, I've tried 
to thread the needle: sticking to my guns on the basic-quarrel I 
had with the Begin/Shamir/Sharon government (annexation, 
irredentism, etc.) while backing off some of my more provocative, 
intemperate forensic devices (notably, Israel = Iraq). AnywaY, 
it's been quite un-fun. But the worst of it seems to have passed. 
I got through the Senate Foreign Relations Committee fairly 
comfortably (17-2), and even though Helms & Co. kept the'nomination 
from coming to a vote on the floor before Congress went off on 
recess, I'll probably get confirmed on Feb. 22, and then I can get 
serious about the'fairly daunting policy issues that await: Bosnia 
and North Korea first and foremost. 
It's a strange thing to be dealing with issues like that. 
Actually, I often start from very much the same point as though I 
were writing a column: that is, I try to figure out what the facts 
are; who's right and who's wrong; where our interest lies. I often 
think of HAG's admonition that good writing is good thinking. So 
is good policymaking 
I wouldn't even blather on in this note about the trouble with 
the Jewish community except that you worked with me on so many of 
those pieces (a fact I promise to keep secret if you're ever 
nominated to a high post in the American government!). 
Yes, by the way, I did indeed read;-and savor, every word of 
the Tsvetayeva piece in The New Yorker. I hope you also had a 
chance to read Remnick's terrific Solzhenitsyn piece this past 
week. 
Next time you write give me word of what you're doing at the 
magazine. 
And try to find an excuse to come to DC this spring. Stay 
with us. There's a lot to catch up on. 
.All the best,
