2. THE FRIENDLY AND BUSINESS-LIKE MEET/NG OCTOBER 25 
BETWEEN DEPUTY SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT AND IVORIAN 
PRIME MINISTER DANIEL KABLAN DUNCAN FOCUSED ON ECONOMICS. 
DUNCAN STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF ADDITIONAL U.S. PRIVATE 
SECTOR ENGAGEMENT IN COTE D'IVOIRE; HE ASKED OBLIQUELYFOR 
PUBLIC DEBT FORGIVENESS AND SOME RENEWED ASSISTANCE; HE ALSO 
EXPRESSED COTE D'IVOIRE'S DESIRE TO PLAY A LEADERSHIP ROLE 
IN WEST AFRICAN ECONOMIC INTEGRATION. 
3. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY PRAISED COTE D'IVOIRE'S "ECONOMIC 
WISDOM AND POLITICAL COURAGE" REGARDING CFA FRANC 
DEVALUATION AND ITS EFFECTS; REAFFIRMED USG SUPPORT FOR 
AMERICAN PRIVATE SECTOR EFFORTS IN COTE D'IVOIRE; SAID THE 
DEBT RELIEF ISSUE IS NOW UNDER REVIEW IN WASHINGTON; AGREED 
TO EXAMINE REFUGEE-RELATED ASSISTANCE NEEDS; AND LAUDED THE 
IVORIAN DESIRE TO ENHANCE REGIONAL ECONOMIC INTEGRATION 
EFFORTS. ASSISTANT AID ADMINISTRATOR HICKS SAID THE USG 
COULD PROVIDE ASSISTANCE IN THIS LAST AREA. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY ALSO REITERATED USG WILLINGNESS TO HELP IN EFFORTS 
TO ENSURE THAT THE 1995 ELECTIONS ARE FREE, FAIR, AND OPEN 
TO INTERNATIONAL OBSERVERS. END SUMMARY

4. DURING HIS DAY-LONG VISIT TO COTE D'IVOIRE, DEPUTY 
SECRETARY OF STATE STROBE TALBOTT'S THIRD OF THREE FORMAL 
MEETINGS WITH GOCI OFFICIALS WAS A 40-MINUTE CALL ON PRIME 
MINISTER DANIEL KABLAN DUNCAN. THE TWO OFFICIALS HAD 
CONVERSED EARLIER IN THE DAY AT A LUNCH HOSTED BY PRESIDENT 
BEDIE; DUNCAN SAID THAT HE WANTED TO USE THE LATE AFTERNOON 
MEETING FOR A REVIEW OF A NUMBER OF BILATERAL ISSUES WHICH 
HAD NOT BEEN DISCUSSED EARLIER. DUNCAN IS ALSO MIN/STER OF 
ECONOMY, FINANCE AND PLANNING, SO THE CONVERSATION FOCUSED 
ON TOPICS RELATED TO DUNCAN'S SUBSTANTIVE AREAS OF 
RESPONSIBILITY. 
5. IN OPENING THE MEETING, PRIME MINISTER DUNCAN AFFIRMED 
THAT U.S./IVORIAN BILATERAL RELATIONS WERE VERY GOOD, BUT HE 
SAID THAT EVEN SUCH GOOD RELATIONSHIPS COULD BE IMPROVED. 
IN THAT REGARD, HE BEGAN WITH A REFERENCE GOVERNMENT-TO-
GOVERNMENT COOPERATION, AND TO THE RECENTLY TERMINATED U.S. 
BILATERAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAM. HE SAID THAT PROJECTS SUCH AS 
THE ONE WHICH HAD PROVIDED TRAINING AND HUMAN RESOURCE 
DEVELOPMENT WOULD HAVE BEEN OF PARTICULAR RELEVANCE IN COTE 
D'IVOIRE NOW. DUNCAN THEN SAID THAT THE REAL FOCUS OF OUR 
BILATERAL ECONOMIC RELATIONSHIP SHOULD BE ON PRIVATE SECTOR
