NAGORNO-KARABAKH. I HAVE MET TWICE WITH PRESIDENT CLINTON 
AND HAVE TALKED TO YOU ABOUT THIS AS WELL. WE HAVE TRIED TO 
START PEACE NEGOTIATIONS. WE HAVE HAD TALKS FOR TWO YEARS, 
BUT ARMENIA HAS NOT TAKEN A ONSTRUCTIVE POSITION. THE 
CO-CHAIRS WORKED WITH OTHERS ON A DRAFT TEXT FOR A PEACEFUL 
SETTLEMENT IN NAGORNO-KARABAKH. IT WAS NOT TOTALLY 
ACCEPTABLE TO US, BUT WE DECIDED TO TRY FOR A COMPROMISE. BUT 
ARMENIA BLOCKED IT. AND ARMENIA BLOCKED A SOLUTION, 
AGAIN, HERE IN LISBON. THEN PEOPLE TRIED TO REACH A RESOLUTION 
THROUGH OTHER LANGUAGE BUT, UNFORTUNATELY, IT DID 
NOT RECEIVE ACCEPTANCE. 
MR. QULUZADE: THE DECISION WAS TO DESCRIBE WHAT HAD 
HAPPENED, BUT THIS WAS NOT ACCEPTABLE. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: LET ME REPEAT. WE FEAR THAT 
AZERBAIJAN WILL BE ISOLATED AMONG ALL OF THE OSCE COUNTRIES 
AND WILL RECEIVE THE BLAME FOR A FAILURE OF THE CONFERENCE. 
THERE IS AN ALTERNATIVE, THAT IS FOR THE FINAL DECLARATION 
TO BE ADOPTED WITH VAGUE LANGUAGE -- NOT THE LANGUAGE YOU 
WANT -- BUT WE, THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA, WOULD ISSUE AN 
OFFICIAL STATEMENT SUPPORTING YOUR LANGUAGE ON 
NAGORNO-KARABAKH, THUS SHIFTING THE BLAME TO ARMENIA FOR 
THE FAILURE TO ADOPT THE LANGUAGE. IF I WERE IN YOUR POSITION, I 
WOULD SEE TWO ALTERNATIVE COURSES. FIRST, AZERBAIJAN COULD 
BLOCK AGREEMENT OF THE 54 NATIONS. THE ADVANTAGE WOULD BE 
THAT YOU WOULD STRONGLY EXPRESS YOUR OPPOSITION TO 
LANGUAGE THAT DOES NOT SAY WHAT YOU WOULD LIKE. THE 
DISADVANTAGE IS THAT YOU WOULD GET THE BLAME FOR THE 
FAILURE, AND THAT COULD HAVE AN EFFECT ON YOUR RELATIONS 
WITH OTHER OSCE COUNTRIES, INCLUDING THOSE THNI' SUPPORT YOUR 
POSITION. THE SECOND COURSE IS TO SAY YOU DISAGREE WITH THE 
LANGUAGE BECAUSE IT DOES NOT GO FAR ENOUGH BUT THAT YOU 
UNDERSTAND THAT YOU ARE A PART OF EUROPE AND, TO BE 
STATESMANLIKE, YOU WILL NOT OBJECT TO THIS LANGUAGE. THEN 
THE UNITED STATES WILL SAY THAT WE PREFER THE AZERI LANGUAGE, 
AND IT WOULD BE CLEAR WHY THIS WAS NOT ADOPTED. THE 
ADVANTAGE OF THE SECOND COURSE IS OBVIOUS; YOU ARE IN A 
STRONGER POSITION.
