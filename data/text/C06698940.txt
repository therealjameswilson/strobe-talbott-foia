ACTING SECRETARY EMPHASIZED THE RELEVANCE OF THIS FACTOR 
BECAUSE MANY OF THE PARTICIPANTS IN THE MINSK GROUP HAVE 
A CERTAIN AMOUNT OF MISTRUST FOR THE RUSSIANS. THE 
ARMENIANS SUPPORT THE U.S. AS CO-CHAIR, AND THOUGH THEY 
CANNOT PUBLICLY ACKNOWLEDGE THIS, THE AZERIS DO AS WELL. 
6. (51er THE ACTING SECRETARY STRESSED HIS DESIRE FOR A 
RESOLUTION TO THE EXTREMELY DANGEROUS AND EXPLOSIVE 
SITUATION IN NAGORNO-KARABAKH AND HIS BELIEF THAT U.S. 
CO-CHAIRMANSHIP OF THE MINSK GROUP IS THE BEST WAY TO 
ACHIEVE POSITIVE RESULTS. THE STAKES INVOLVED PEACE AND 
STABILITY THROUGHOUT THE REGION AND WOULD HAVE 
CONSEQUENCES FOR OVERALL REFORM PROSPECTS IN RUSSIA AND 
THE N.I.S. 
7. SZ) FM COTTI RESPONDED THAT HE BELIEVES IT IS 
ESSENTIAL FOR THE U.S. TO PLAY A CENTRAL ROLE IN 
RESOLVING CONFLICT AROUND THE WORLD. HE CONTINUED THAT 
U.S. INVOLVEMENT IS VITAL, NOT ONLY BECAUSE THE UNITED 
STATES IS THE ONLY GREAT POWER, BUT ALSO BECAUSE IT 
PLAYS A RESPONSIBLE ROLE. FM COTTI EXPLAINED THAT HAD 
HE BEEN AWARE OF THE U.S. VIEW ONE OR TWO MONTHS 
EARLIER, HIS POSITION WOULD HAVE BEEN MUCH CLEARER. 
NOW, HOWEVER, THE SITUATION WAS MORE COMPLICATED. WHILE 
THE SWISS HAD MADE NO FORMAL GUARANTEES TO FRANCE, FM 
COTTI EXPLAINED HIS GOVERNMENT HAD ALLOWED THE FRENCH TO 
BELIEVE THEY WOULD BE WELL DISPOSED TOWARD FRENCH 
CANDIDACY FOR THE CO-CHAIRMANSHIP OF THE MINSK GROUP.
