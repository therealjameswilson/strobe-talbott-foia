Dear Strobe: 
It has come to my attention that.Mr. Jacob Saliba, Chairman 
of Katy Industries, recently contacted you to express his 
interest in serving in an advisory capacity to the State 
Department. 
As you know, under Jake's leadership, Katy Industries has 
developed into a corporation with a significant economic presence 
throughout most of the Middle East and Eastern Europe. As a 
result of his experience at Katy, Jake has established valuable 
business and political contacts in these regions over the past 
twenty years, as well as gained a thorough knowledge of the 
regional economic, political and cultural systems. I feel that 
this experience would be of great value to the State Department, 
or any number of other departments or agencies dealing with that 
region of the world. 
In addition to his unique background in, and understanding 
of, Eastern Europe and the Middle East, I personally know Jake to 
be a man of great ability and dedication to his work. I heartily 
recommend him to you. 
I have enclosed a copy of Jake's references for you 
consideration, and sincerely hope that you will give Jake's 
interest in serving the Administration the serious consideration 
it merits. 
With best wishes, 
Sincerely, 
Ge g J. Mitchell
