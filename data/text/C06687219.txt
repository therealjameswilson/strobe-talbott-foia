Dear Mr. Ambassador: 
On behalf of Secretary Christopher,,I would like to thank 
you for your letter of July 16, as well as your your kind words 
regarding American assistance efforts in Azerbaijan. 
In recognition of the growing population of refugees, 
displaced persons, and other vulnerable groups within 
Azerbaijan, Ambassador Thomas Simons, Coordinator for U.S. 
Assistance, has approved $1 million for, the International 
Committee of the Red Cross to expand its emergency humanitarian 
assistance activities in Azerbaijan. 
We believe that a swift and peaceful resolution of the 
Nagorno-Karabakh conflict must be found in order to prevent the 
continuation of the humanitarian tragedies in Azerbaijan and 
Armenia. We were pleased to hear of.the recent cease-fire 
agreement worked out between the Government of Azerbaijan and 
the Nagorno-Karabakh Armenians, and encourage further contact 
between the parties. We continue to support the efforts of the 
CSCE Minsk Group to help the parties find a peaceful solution 
to the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Adviser to the Secretary on 
the New Independent States 
His Excellency 
Hafiz Pashayev, 
Ambassador of the Republic of Azerbaijan.
