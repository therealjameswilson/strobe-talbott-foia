2. SUMMARY. GYARMATI TOLD TALBOTT THAT, CONFIDENT THAT 
ITS CASE WAS WELL-UNDERSTOOD, HUNGARY NO LONGER FEELS IT 
NECESSARY TO CAMPAIGN FOR NATO MEMBERSHIP IN WASHINGTON. 
HE SAID HUNGARY WANTED TO PLAY A RESPONSIBLE ROLE IN 
ENHANCING PFP, HELPING ITS NEIGHBORS PREPARE FOR NATO 
MEMBERSHIP, AND MAKING ENLARGEMENT MORE PALATABLE TO 
RUSSIA. , HE ALSO SUPPORTED ROMANIA, SLOVAKIA AND 
SLOVENIA'S INCLUSION IN THE ALLIANCE. WHEN ASKED TO 
SUPPORT THE TRAIN AND EQUIP PROGRAM, GYARMATI MADE CLEAR 
DOMESTIC INHIBITIONS AGAINST THE PROVISION OF LETHAL AID, 
BUT TOLD TALBOTT THAT HUNGARY WAS QUIETLY WORKING WITH 
BOSNIA. TALBOTT OUTLINED THE U.S. VIEW OF AN ENHANCED 
NATO-RUSSIA RELATIONSHIP BASED ON A CHARTER. GYARMATI AND 
TALBOTT AGREED THAT MODERNIZATION OF THE CFE TREATY WAS AN 
IMPORTANT POTENTIAL ROUTE FOR DEALING WITH RUSSIAN 
CONCERNS ABOUT NATO ENLARGEMENT. END SUMMARY. 
NATO AND NATO/RUSSIA 
3. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH HUNGARIAN MOD STATE 
SECRETARY IN THE DEPARTMENT ON SEPTEMBER 25. 'GYARMATI 
BEGAN BY SAYING THAT HE DID NOT FEEL IT NECESSARY ANY 
LONGER TO "CAMPAIGN" FOR HUNGARIAN MEMBERSHIP IN NATO, AT 
LEAST NOT IN WASHINGTON. RATHER,' HE WANTED TO DISCUSS 
NATO'S ROLE IN THE LARGER EUROPEAN SECURITY CONTEXT, AS 
WELL AS THE QUESTION OF HOW TO DEAL WITH THOSE COUNTRIES 
THAT DO NOT GET INTO NATO RIGHT AWAY. GYARMATI SAID THAT 
HUNGARY WOULD BE nA RESPONSIBLE NATO MEMBER," AND WOULD 
SEEK TO HELP ITS NEIGHBORS PREPARE FOR MEMBERSHIP. IT IS 
IN HUNGARY'S INTEREST, HE ADDED, THAT ROMANIA AND SLOVAKIA 
IN PARTICULAR REACH THE LEVEL WHERE THEY ARE ABLE TO JOIN 
THE ALLIANCE. 
4. TALBOTT REVIEWED THE PRESIDENT AND THE SECRETARY'S 
RECENT DISCUSSIONS WITH PRIMAKOV. A PROPOSED NATO-RUSSIA 
CHARTER WOULD, IN THE U.S. VIEW, LAY OUT THE TERMS OF 
ENGAGEMENT AND COOPERATION BETWEEN NATO AND RUSSIA. THE 
CHARTER WOULD AMPLIFY AND ARTICULATE MANY OF THE THINGS 
THAT NATO HAS LONG BEEN SAYING: THE WORLD HAS ENTERED A 
NEW POST-COLD WAR ERA, NATO HAS A NEW MISSION, AND 
ENLARGEMENT IS NOT DIRECTED AGAINST ANY COUNTRY. TALBOTT 
SAID THE RUSSIAN LEADERSHIP UNDERSTANDS, BUT DOES NOT YET

ACCEPT, THESE PRINCIPLES. MOSCOW IS TRYING TO KEEP ALIVE 
TWO CONTRADICTORY STRATEGIES TOWARD NATO ENLARGEMENT. ONE 
TRACK FOCUSES ON STALLING OR BLOCKING ENLARGEMENT, WHILE 
THE OTHER SEEKS TO LIMIT THE DAMAGE FROM ENLARGEMENT. 
RUSSIA IS UNLIKELY TO ABANDON THE FIRST STRATEGY UNTIL 
ENLARGEMENT BECOMES A FACT. 
5. OUR TASK, TALBOTT CONTINUED, IS TO CONVINCE MOSCOW 
THAT THE FIRST TRACK HAS NO PROSPECT OF SUCCESS. TO THIS 
END, THE PRESIDENT AND THE SECRETARY HAVE TRIED TO 
CONVINCE THE RUSSIANS THAT THE ALLIANCE IS COMMITTED TO 
ENLARGEMENT, AND THAT THERE SHOULD BE A PARALLEL 
NATO-RUSSIA TRACK. HOWEVER, "IT TAKES TWO TO TANGO;" IF 
THE RUSSIANS ARE NOT WILLING, WE WILL FORGE AHEAD WITH 
ENLARGEMENT AND FIND OTHER WAYS TO DEAL WITH MOSCOW. HE 
ADDED THAT THE U.S. REGARDS THE SECOND TRACK AS THE ONLY 
TRACK, BUT DOES NOT WANT A LOT OF VISIBILITY. "THERE IS 
MORE HOPE FOR THE SECOND TRACK IF IT HAPPENS QUIETLY," 
TALBOTT TOLD GYARMATI. WITH REGARD TO THE ALLIES, TALBOTT 
SAID HE WAS QUITE SATISFIED WITH THE TONE OF ONGOING 
EXCHANGES, BUT NOTED THAT U.S. LEADERSHIP WILL LIKELY BE 
TESTED IN THE COMING MONTHS. 
ROMAN IA AND SLOVEN IA 
6. ON ROMANIA, TALBOTT NOTED THAT THE FRENCH SUPPORT 
BUCHAREST'S INCLUSION IN THE FIRST GROUP OF NEW NATO 
MEMBERS, BUT THE U.S. HAS NO OFFICIAL POSITION ON 
ROMANIA'S (OR ANY OTHER COUNTRY'S) INCLUSION. HOWEVER, 
NATO DOES NOT WANT TO APPEAR TO BE RUSHING TO GET EVERYONE 
IN BEFORE THE DOOR CLOSES. NATO ENLARGEMENT IS AN ONGOING 
PROCESS, AND COUNTRIES THAT ARE, NOT IN THE FIRST GROUP 
HAVE NOTHING TO TEAR. GYARMATI SAID THAT HUNGARY SUPPORTS 
SLOVENIA'S INCLUSION IN THE ALLIANCE FOR TWO REASONS: 
FIRST, IT HAS MADE AT LEAST AS MUCH PROGRESS ON REFORM AS 
THE OTHER POTENTIAL ENTRANTS; AND SECOND, SLOVENIA'S 
INCLUSION WOULD SEND A POSITIVE SIGNAL TO THE OTHER STATES 
OF THE FORMER YUGOSLAVIA. *TALBOTT SAID THAT THE 
"GEOGRAPHIC CONTIGUITY" ARGUMENT (I.E., A LAND BRIDGE 
BETWEEN HUNGARY AND NATO) IS ALSO A VALID ONE. THE 
SLOVENES, HOWEVER, ARE NOT HELPING THEIR CAUSE BY TRYING 
TO WALK AWAY FROM THE PROBLEMS IN THE FORMER YUGOSLAVIA;

SUPPORT OF THE TRAIN AND EQUIP PROGRAM. GYARMATI REPLIED 
THAT THERE ARE SEVERAL OBSTACLES STANDING IN THE WAY.OF 
HIS COUNTRY'S SUPPORT FOR TRAIN AND EQUIP. FIRST IS THE 
EU POLICY. HUNGARY IS IN THE EU "WAITING ROOM," AND IS 
THEREFORE MORE SENSITIVE TO THE EU POSITION THAN IT MIGHT 
BE IF IT WERE A MEMBER. INTERNAL HUNGARIAN POLITICS ALSO 
PLAY A ROLE. THE PARLIAMENTARY CONSENSUS BEHIND HUNGARY'S 
PARTICIPATION IN IFOR IS A DELICATE ONE, AND SUPPORT FOR 
TRAIN AND EQUIP MIGHT CAUSE SOME LEGISLATORS TO OPPOSE 
RENEWING THE MANDATE OF HUNGARY'S IFOR BATTALION. HE 
STRESSED, HOWEVER1 THAT HUNGARY IS QUIETLY DISCUSSING 
TRAIN AND EQUIP WITH BOSNIA. 
CFE 
8. IN CLOSING, GYARMATI SUGGESTED THAT SOME OF THE ISSUES 
RAISED BY RUSSIA IN THE CONTEXT OF NATO ENLARGEMENT COULD 
BE DEALT WITH UNDER CFE ADAPTATION, THUS SEPARATING THE 
"FOREIGN FORCES" QUESTION FROM THE QUESTION OF NATO 
ENLARGEMENT. TALBOTT REPLIED THAT THE U.S. HAD PRIVATELY 
RAISED CFE MODERNIZATION WITH THE RUSSIANS. HE AGREED 
THAT CFE REPRESENTS THE SINGLE MOST PROMISING TRACK FOR 
DEALING WITH RUSSIAN CONCERNS ABOUT MILITARY FORCES IN THE 
NEW MEMBER STATES, AND SAID CFE MODERNIZATION COULD BE 
SUPPORTIVE OF THE CHARTER BETWEEN NATO AND RUSSIA. 
HOWEVER, CFE MODERNIZATION WILL NOT BE A CONDITION FOR 
ENLARGEMENT.
