B6 
Congratulations on your appointment. I have a suggestion 
for you which will assist you in solving the problem of 
Vladimir Zhirinovsky's rise to power in Russia. 
I will be writing to Stevep Spielberg also who is the 
director of Schindler's List. 
This is my suggestion: That the United States begin a 
project of showing this movie in Russia with Russian subtitles. 
In addition, a prologue to the movie would be designed to 
show the resemblance of this man to Adolf Hitler and Naziism. 
As an expert in Russian politics and history you could easily 
devise such a prologue. I feel that if the Russians could 
understand what a man like this will eventually bring them, they 
would think twice about following this man. 
There are many other projects which will help the Russian 
situation like encouraging a more competitive natural resource 
industry, etc. but you well understand what is needed than I. 
Very truly yours, 
Mary S. Carpenter, M.Ed.
