--t51- THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE HAD CALLED ON THE 
PRESIDENT TO DISCUSS A SUBJECT OF GREAT IMPORTANCE TO 
AFRICA, THE UNITED STATES AND THE WORLD; NAMELY THE 
SELECTION OF THE NEXT SECRETARY GENERAL.OF THE UNITED 
NATIONS. 
(1.3th AS PRESIDENT MASIRE HAD NOT BEEN BRIEFED ON THE 
SUBJECT, THE ACTING SECRETARY OFFERED THE FOLLOWING 
OVERVIEW. AFTER MUCH CONSIDERATION THE PRESIDENT OF THE 
UNITED STATES, ALONG WITH THE SECRETARY OF STATE AND 
AMBASSADOR ALBRIGHT, WILL NOT BE ABLE TO SUPPORT SYG 
BOUTROS-GHALI'S BID FOR A SECOND TERM. THEIR DECISION 
IN NO WAY REFLECTS DISCREDIT ON BOUTROS-GHALI. HOWEVER, 
WITH THE IMMENSITY OF THE JOB AHEAD, THE UNITED NATIONS 
IS IN NEED OF A CHANGE AT THE TOP. 
4-59- THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THE UNITED STATES 
SUPPORTED SYG BOUTROS-GHALI'S ORIGINAL INTENTION TO 
SERVE ONLY ONE TERM, AND BELIEVED THAT VIEW SHOULD 
PREVAIL, DESPITE THE SYG'S CHANGE OF MIND. 
--(-5+ THE ACTING SECRETARY STRESSED THE U.S. DID NOT HAVE 
A PARTICULAR CANDIDATE IN MIND, BUT BELIEVED THAT THE 
CANDIDATE SHOULD HAVE STRONG ADMINISTRATION AND 
MANAGEMENT SKILLS, AS WELL AS CONSIDERABLE DIPLOMATIC 
TALENT. THE NEXT SYG SHOULD BE A RESPECTED STATESMAN 
WITH ADEQUATE STATURE AND SUPPORT IN ORDER TO ACHIEVE 
REFORMS WITHIN THE UN. 
-(-8-)- THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THE U.S. BELIEVED A 
QUALIFIED CANDIDATE FROM AFRICA IS VERY MUCH IN ORDER. 
HE ALSO ASKED THAT THE OAU REFRAIN FROM ENDORSING SYG 
BOUTROS-GHALI OR ANY OTHER CANDIDATE AT ITS UPCOMING 
MEETING IN YAOUNDE. 
4g4 PRESIDENT MASIRE THANKED THE ACTING SECRETARY FOR 
HIS REMARKS. 
(U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1404 EST.
