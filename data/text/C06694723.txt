SUBJECT: ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT'S SEPTEMBER 8 MEETING 
WITH BRAZILIAN AMBASSADOR FLECHA DE LIMA 
1. SUMMARY: ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT AND BRAZILIAN 
AMBASSADOR FLECHA DE LIMA EXCHANGED VIEWS ON HAITI AND 
CUBA DURING A MEETING SEPTEMBER 8. FLECHA DE LIMA SAID 
BRAZIL HAD DIFFICULTY SUPPORTING UN RESOLUTION 940 AND 
NOTED OTHER RIO GROUP MEMBERS ALSO WERE UNRECEPTIVE. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY SAID THAT THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY 
SPEAKING WITH A CLEAR, SINGLE VOICE, WOULD CREATE THE BEST 
CHANCE THAT CEDRAS AND COMPANY WOULD DEPART PEACEFULLY. 
FLECHA DE LIMA SAID THIS MESSAGE WAS APPEALING AND 
PROMISED TO PASS IT ON TO THE RIO GROUP. ON CUBA, FLECHA 
DE LIMA NOTED THAT THE RIO GROUP WOULD PROBABLY ISSUE A 
RESOLUTION URGING DEMOCRATIC REFORM AND NEGOTIATIONS TO 
WORK ON OUTSTANDING PROBLEMS. FLECHA DE LIMA EXPLAINED 
THAT BRAZIL HAD LONGSTANDING, WARM RELATIONS WITH CUBA 
BASED ON ETHNIC AND CULTURAL AFFINITIES AND NOTED THAT 
FIDEL WAS A POPULAR FIGURE IN BRAZIL. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY NOTED THAT FIDEL WAS NOT, HOWEVER, VERY POPULAR 
WITH HIS OWN PEOPLE. AT THE CLOSE OF,THE MEETING, FLECHA 
DE LIMA DELIVERED A LETTER FROM PRESIDENT FRANCO TO 
PRESIDENT CLINTON ON SIVAM AND SEEKING US CONSIDERATION 
FOR EMBRAER'S BID ON THE 7 BILLION DOLLAR DEPARTMENT OF 
DEFENSE TRAINER AIRCRAFT (JPATS) CONTRACT. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY PROMISED TO PASS FRANCO'S LETTER TO THE WHITE 
HOUSE AND TO CONVEY TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OF DEFENSE 
BRAZIL'S INTEREST IN THE CONTRACT. END SUMMARY.

2. BRAZILIAN AMBASSADOR FLECHA DE LIMA CALLED ON ACTING 
SECRETARY TALBOTT SEPTEMBER 8 TO DELIVER A LETTER FROM 
PRESIDENT FRANCO TO PRESIDENT CLINTON. THE MEETING BEGAN, 
HOWEVER, WITH AN EXCHANGE OF VIEWS ON HAITI AND CUBA. 
HAITI WAS A COMPLICATED ISSUE FLECHA DE LIMA SAID, AND 
BRAZIL HAD DIFFICULTY SUPPORTING UN RESOLUTION 940. HE 
NOTED THAT BRAZIL HAD PROBED OTHER RIO GROUP MEMBERS AND 
ALSO FOUND THEM UNRECEPTIVE ON THIS ISSUE. STILL, FLECHA

WOULD FACILITATE COMMUNICATION ON THE HAITI ISSUE, BUT DID 
NOT BELIEVE THE RIO GROUP MEMBERS WOULD BE FORTHCOMING.

6. AT THE END OF THE MEETING, FLECHA DE LIMA PRESENTED A 
LETTER TO THE ACTING SECRETARY FROM PRESIDENT FRANCO TO 
PRESIDENT CLINTON. THE SIVAM CONTRACT, HE SAID, WAS THE 
FIRST LARGE GOVERNMENT CONTRACT THAT A U.S. COMPANY LANDED 
IN BRAZIL DURING THE PAST 25 YEARS. BRAZIL TRADITIONALLY 
DIRECTED SUCH CONTRACTS TO EUROPE. HE ACKNOWLEDGED THAT 
RAYTHEON HAD THE BEST BID BASED ON THE MERITS BUT SAID THE 
US SHOULD NOT IGNORE THE BROADER POLITICAL SIGNIFICANCE OF 
BRAZIL'S DECISION. HE SAID THIS DEAL WOULD ENABLE THE 
RETURN OF US TECHNOLOGY TO THE BRAZILIAN MARKET.
