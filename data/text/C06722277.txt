1. 4Q4- SUMMARY. ARAB AMBASSADORS IN WASHINGTON CALLED ON 
ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT JUNE 24 TO EXPRESS THEIR EXTREME 
CONCERN ABOUT THE "RECKLESS BEHAVIOR" OF ISRAELI PM 
NETANYAHU, HIS RECENT DECISION ON JERUSALEM AND THE DANGER 
OF AN OUTBREAK OF VIOLENCE IN THE REGION AS A RESULT OF 
SUCH ACTIONS AND THE ABSENCE OF PROGRESS IN THE MEPP. THEY 
PRAISED U.S. REACTIONS TO THE GO1 DECISION AND URGED THE 
U.S. TO HOLD THE LINE. A FAIR U.S. POSITION, THEY SAID, 
WAS AN INSURANCE POLICY AGAINST INSTABILITY IN THE REGION 
AND IT STRENGHTHENED THE ABILITY OF ARAB LEADERS TO "HOLD 
THEIR PEOPLE TOGETHER." ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT URGED THE 
ARABS NOT TO FORCE THE JERUSALEM ISSUE INTO THE UNSC 
BECAUSE, HE EXPLAINED, IT NEGATIVELY AFFECT THE U.S.' 
ABILITY TO REACH AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE ISRAELIS AND THE 
PALESTINIANS. END SUMMARY. 
2. fr.C.L ON JUNE 24, ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH THE 
ARAB AMBASSADORS IN WASHINGTON. ALSO PRESENT WERE NEA A/S 
INDYK, SMEC DENNIS ROSS AND NEA DAS VERSTANDIG. SPEAKING 
ON BEHALF OF ALL THE ARAB AMBASSADORS, PRINCE BANDAR, 
AMBASSADOR OF SAUDI ARABIA AND DEAN OF THE ARAB DIPLOMATIC 
CORPS IN WASHINGTON, BEGAN BY REAFFIRMING THE COMMITTMENT 
OF ALL ARAB GOVERNMENTS AND LEADERS TO PEACE AS A STRATEGIC 
OPTION. HE THEN EXPRESSED THE VERY SERIOUS CONCERN OF ARAB 
GOVERNMENTS ABOUT DEVELOPMENTS IN THE MIDDLE EAST AND, IN 
PARTICULAR, RECENT ISRAELI DECISIONS ON JERUSALEM. 
3. BANDAR EXPLAINED THE EXTENT TO WHICH ARAB LEADERS' 
WERE DISTURBED BY THE "RECKLESS" BEHAVIOR OF ISRAELI PM 
NETANYAHU, BEHAVIOR THAT THEY SAW AS SLOWLY BUT SURELY 
DESTROYING THE PEACE PROCESS. SUCH RECKLESSNESS HAD 
CULMINATED, BANDAR SAID, IN CURRENT GO1 ACTIONS ON 
JERUSALEM, WHICH REPRESENTED UNILATERAL ACTION ON AN ISSUE 
SPECIFICALLY LEFT FOR PERMANENT STATUS NEGOTIATIONS AND 
WHICH CHIPPED AWAY AT THE ENTIRE SPIRIT AND MEANING OF THE 
MEPP. A CHANGE IN ISRAELI GOVERNMENT, HE POINTED OUT, 
SHOULD NOT CHANGE ISRAELI OBLIGATIONS. THE REGION WAS VERY 
CLOSE TO EXPLODING, BANDAR STATED, AND THE GO1 NEEDED TO BE 
SAVED FROM ITS OWN SELF. OTHERWISE, HE CONTINUED, ISRAELI 
ACTIONS WOULD BACKFIRE ON EVERYBODY IN THE REGION, A VERY 
HEAVY PRICE FOR ALL TO PAY. BANDAR TOLD THE ACTING 
SECRETARY THAT THE REGION COULD NOT SUSTAIN AN EXPLOSION OF 
VIOLENCE AND THAT SUCH AN EXPLOSION WOULD MARK THE DEATH OF 
THE PEACE PROCESS AND OF STABILITY.

BANDAR' PRAISED,.ON BEHALF OF ALL ARAB LEADERS, THE FAIRNESS OF THE U.S. REACTION, WHICH HE DESCRIBED AS AN INSURANCE POLICY TO DELAY AND POSSIBLY PREVENT THE REGION FROM EXPLODING. THE WATERING DOWN OF USG STATEMENTS, IN CONTRAST, .WOULD BE HIGHLY UNHELPFUL, HE ADDED. 
5. -1-E4-BANDAR SUPPORTED THE ARAB PERSPECTIVE OF NETANYAHU'S BEHAVIOR BY SAYING THAT EVEN THE AMERICAN PRESS, NOT KNOW FOR ITS PRO-ARAB STANCE, HAD CRITICIZED THE ISRAELI LEADER'S ACTIONS, REFERRING SPECIFICALLY TO AN EDITORIAL IN TODAY'S N.Y. TIMES STATING THAT NETANYAHU HAD GONE TOO FAR. WHEN THE U.S. ACTED FIRMLY AND FAIRLY, BANDAR SAID, IT ENHANCED BOTH SIDES' POSITIONS EVENTUALLY. DRAGGING THINGS ALONG, IN CONTRAST, INCREASED THE CHANCES OF INSTABILITY. REFERRING TO A SAYING BY THE PROPHET MOHAMMED, BANDAR SAID 
THAT WHEN A FRIEND IS WRONG, ONE CAN ONLY HELP HIM BY CORRECTING HIM. 
6. TM,TALBOTT THANKEWTHE ARAB REPRESENTATIVES FOR THEIR GOVERNMENTS' SUPPORT OF THE PEACE PROCESS AND OF U.S. EFFORTS SPECIFICALLY. HE SAID PEACE SHOULD BE VIEWED AS A STRATEGIC IMPERATIVE, AS OPPOSED TO A STRATEGIC OPTION. THE U.S., TALBOTT SAID, HAD ITS OWN CONCERNS ABOUT EVENTS OF THE PAST WEEK AND HAD RELAYED ITS CONCERNS FRANKLY AND DIRECTLY TO THE GO1 BOTH THROUGH DIPLOMATIC CHANNELS AND PUBLICLY. THE U.S., HE STATED, WAS UNWAVERING IN ITS STRATEGY AND WAS WATCHING VERY CLOSELY FOR ANY ACTION ON EITHER SIDE THAT WOULD PREEMPT PERMANENT STATUS ISSUES. HE EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THE ARABS' ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF THE POSITIVE NATURE OF THE SECRETARY'S STATEMENTS. HER WORDS, HE POINTED OUT, HAVE BEEN CHOSEN VERY CAREFULLY AND MADE IN LIGHT OF CLARIFICATIONS RECEIVED BY THE GO1 REGARDING ITS ACTIONS ON JERUSALEM.

HE EMPHASIZED THAT BRINGING THE ISSUE TO THE SC WOULD BE COUNTERPRODUCTIVE BECAUSE IT 1) UPSET THE DELICATE BALANCE IN ISRAEL THAT THE USG WAS TRYING TO STRIKE AND 2) COMPLICATE OUR EFFORTS TO ADVANCE THE PEACE PROCESS.
