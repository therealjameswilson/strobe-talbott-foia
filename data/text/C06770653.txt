2. -(-S} ON FEBRUARY 19, ARGENTINE DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER 
ANDRES CISNEROS MET WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY. WHA A/S 
ROMERO ALSO PARTICIPATED, AS DID WHA/BSC DIRECTOR ROGUS, D 
SPECIAL ASSISTANT DOHERTY AND WHA/BSC ARGENTINA DESK 
OFFICER SANDERS (NOTETAKER). 
3. 424 CISNEROS EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY'S RECEIVING HIM, AND SAID THAT HE WANTED TO BRIEF 
'THE USG ON THE STATUS OF ARGENTINE-BRITISH CONVERSATIONS 
REGARDING THE FALKLANDS/MALVINAS, PRIOR TO DISCUSSIONS HE 
HAD PLANNED FOR THE FOLLOWING DAY WITH HMG FCO AMERICAS 
DEPARTMENT DIRECTOR PETER WESTMACOTT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
NOTED THAT HE HAD MET EARLIER WITH FORMER FOREIGN MINISTER 
AND CURRENT OPPOSITION FOREIGN POLICY ADVISOR DANTE CAPUTO 
ON THIS SAME ISSUE.

7. —(-9-) THE DEPUTY SECRETARY RESPONDED THAT HE WAS SURE 
THAT CISNEROS UNDERSTOOD THAT THE FALKLANDS/MALVINAS ISSUE 
REMAINED EXTREMELY DELICATE FOR HMG. FOR ITS PART THE USG 
WOULD BE VERY PLEASED IF ITS TWO GOOD FRIENDS COULD REACH A 
SOLUTION. THE USG ROLE HAD BEEN LARGELY TO PASS CERTAIN 
MESSAGES BETWEEN THE PARTIES. IT WOULD CONTINUE TO USE ITS 
"GOOD OFFICES" AND BE "A FRIEND OF THE PROCESS." THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY, WHO HAD ANOTHER COMMITMENT, THEN LEFT THE 
MEETING, AFTER TURNING IT OVER TO ACTING A/S ROMERO. 
8. 464- ACTING A/S ROMERO SAID THAT FROM HIS OWN 
CONVERSATIONS WITH WESTMACOTT, HE UNDERSTOOD THAT HMG 
APPRECIATED THE ATTITUDE WITH WHICH THE GOA WAS UNDERTAKING 
ITS NEGOTIATION: ACTING WITH DISCRETION, AVOIDING PUBLIC 
STATEMENTS. WESTMACOTT HAD TOLD HIM THAT HMG HAD TAKEN 
NOTE OF THE GOA'S ABILITY TO WORK WITH THE POLITICAL 
OPPOSITION. IT NOW WANTED THE GOA TO PROVIDE A FORMAL, 
HIGH LEVEL WRITTEN OFFER THAT COULD BE TAKEN UP AT THE 
POLITICAL LEVEL AND LEAD TO A DEFINITIVE YES OR NO. (NOTE: 
ROBIN COOK MADE A SIMILAR REQUEST TO DI TELLA ON JANUARY 
18. SEE REFTEL. END NOTE.) 
11 -I;S.) THE MEETING CONCLUDED WITH CISNEROS PROMISING TO 
KEEP ACTING A/S ROMERO APPRISED OF FURTHER DEVELOPMENTS.
