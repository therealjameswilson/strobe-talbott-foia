SUBJECT: ACTING SECRETARY'S MEETING WITH FOREIGN MINISTER 
STEIN OF GUATEMALA 
1. CLASSIFIED BY ARA ACTING DAS JOHN R. HAMILTON. REASON 
1.5 (D). 
2. SUMMARY. IN AN APRIL 26 MEETING WITH ACTING SECRETARY 
TALBOTT, FOREIGN MINISTER EDUARDO STEIN SAID THE ARZU 
GOVERNMENT IS CONFIDENT THAT PEACE NEGOTIATIONS WITH THE 
URNG WILL BE COMPLETED BY MID-SEPTEMBER. STEIN EXTENDED 
AN INVITATION TO PRESIDENT CLINTON-TO ATTEND THE SIGNING 
CEREMONY OF A FINAL ACCORD. THE ACTING SECRETARY WELCOMED 
STEIN'S POSITIVE ASSESSMENT, EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR 
THE INVITATION TO THE PRESIDENT WHILE MAKING NO 
COMMITMENT, AND STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE TO THE U.S. OF 
POSITIVE GOG ACTION ON HUMAN RIGHTS CASES. A/S SHATTUCK 
UNDERSCORED THE LATTER POINTS. STEIN EMPHASIZED THE ARZU 
GOBERNMENT' S COMMITMENT TO ENDING IMPUNITY, AND 
ACKNOWLEDGED THE IMPORTANCE OF PRIORITY HUMAN RIGHTS CASES 
INVOLVING U.S. CITIZENS, SUCH AS THE ORTIZ, DEVINE, AND 
HARBURY/BAMACA CASES. END SUMMARY. 
3. FM STEIN TOLD THE ACTING SECRETARY THAT THE PURPOSE OF 
HIS VISIT TO WASHINGTON WAS TO CONVEY TO USG OFFICIALS AND 
MEMBERS OF CONGRESS ALIKE THE SENSE OF FORWARD MOVEMENT 
AND CONSTRUCTIVE ATMOSPHERE SURROUNDING THE PEACE TALKS 
BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT AND THE URNG GUERRILLAS. STEIN 
NOTED THAT A COMBINATION OF FACTORS - INCLUDING THE 
POSITIVE RESPONSE OF THE URNG TO THE ELECTION OF PRESIDENT 
ARZU, THE POSITIVE ATTITUDE OF GROUPS IN GUATEMALA SUCH AS 
THE PRIVATE SECTOR AND THE CHURCH, AND THE STRONG SUPPORT 
OF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY - HAD CONVERGED TO PROVIDE 
THE MOMENTUM TO ACCELERATE THE•PEACE NEGOTIATIONS. HE 
EMPHASIZED THAT PRESIDENT ARZU WAS CONVINCED THAT THE 
NEGOTIATIONS WERE MOVING FORWARD, AND PREDICTED THAT ONCE 
QUESTIONS IN THE SOC10-ECONOMIC BASKET ARE RESOLVED, OTHER 
OUTSTANDING ISSUES WOULD FALL QUICKLY INTO PLACE. STEIN 
ANTICIPATED IT WOULD BE POSSIBLE TO SIGN THE FINAL 
AGREEMENT IN MID-SEPTEMBER. HE ADDED THAT AN OFFICIAL 
INVITATION FOR PRESIDENT CLINTON TO ATTEND THE CEREMONY 
WHEN THE ACCORD IS SIGNED WOULD BE SENT THROUGH THE 
SECRETARY. 
4. THE ACTING SECRETARY EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR 
PRESIDENT ARZU'S EFFORTS TO MOVE THE PEACE PROCESS 
FORWARD, TERMING ARZU'S ACTIONS "A QUIET SUCCESS STORY." 
HE ASSURED FM STEIN THAT THE INVITATION TO THE PEACE 
ACCORD CEREMONY WOULD BE GIVEN CAREFUL CONSIDERATION, BUT

U.S. REPRESENTATION AT THE CEREMONY WILL BE AT A LEVEL TO 
INDICATE THE CONSIDERABLE IMPORTANCE WE PLACE ON ACHIEVING 
PEACE IN GUATEMALA. 
5. STEIN ACKNOWLEDGED THE CONTINUING IMPORTANCE OF THE 
HUMAN RIGHTS ISSUE, NOTING GUATEMALA'S "HORRENDOUS TRACK 
RECORD." NONETHELESS, HE INSISTED, THE BROADER TRENDS IN 
GUATEMALA ARE FAVORABLE. ALLUDING TO THE EVENTS IN 
PARAGUAY THIS WEEK, THE ACTING SECRETARY NOTED THAT 
DEMOCRACY IN THE HEMISPHERE IS FRAGILE, AND REQUIRES 
CONTINUED SUPPORT FROM THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY. . 
AGAINST THAT BACKGROUND, WE APPRECIATE THE POLITICAL WILL 
AND THE HUMANITY THE ARZU GOVERNMENT HAS INJECTED INTO 
GUATEMALA'S POLITICAL LEADERSHIP. WE UNDERSTAND THAT 
k GUATEMALA FACES THREATS TO DEMOCRACY NOT ONLY FROM CIVIL 
WAR, BUT ALSO FROM CRIME. ALTHOUGH RESOURCES ARE LIMITED, 
THE U.S. WANTS TO HELP THE GOG DEAL WITH THE CRIME 
THREAT. ONE OF THE LIMITING FACTORS IN OUR ABILITY TO 
ASSIST IN LAW ENFORCEMENT EFFORTS, HE NOTED, IS THE NEED 
TO BE ABLE TO CERTIFY TO CONGRESS THAT PROGRESS IS BEING 
MADE IN RESOLVING HIGH-PROFILE HUMAN RIGHTS CASES. 
6. A/S SHATTUCK REITERATED TO FM STEIN USG CONCERN THAT 
PROGRESS BE MADE IN RESOLVING INDIVIDUAL HUAAN RIGHTS 
CASES, MENTIONING IN PARTICULAR THE CASES OF SISTER DIANNA 
ORTIZ, THE CASES OF JENNIFER HARBURY/EFRAIN BAMACA AND 
MICHAEL DEVINE. A/S SHATTUCK EMPHASIZED THAT THE U.S. 
RECOGNIZES THE CONSIDERABLE PROGRESS MADE BY THE ARZU 
GOVERNMENT IN ADDRESSING THE PROBLEMS OF IMPUNITY AND THE 
TERRIBLE LEGACY OF HUMAN RIGHTS ABUSES IN THE PAST. EVEN 
SO, HE ADDED, IT IS IMPORTANT TO CARRY OUT ARRESTS AND 
PROSECUTIONS IN INDIVIDUAL CASES. FM STEIN TOOK THE 
OPPORTUNITY TO REPORT THAT, JUST YESTERDAY, THERE WAS A 
CONVICTION IN A HIGH-PROFILE ROBBERY/MURDER CASE IN 
GUATEMALA (THE ORTEGA DEL CID CASE) WHICH HE FELT SET AN 
IMPORTANT PRECEDENT. ALTHOUGH THIS WAS A STRAIGHTFORWARD 
CRIMINAL INCIDENT IN WHICH A TEENAGE GIRL WAS KILLED IN.AN 
ARMED CAR ROBBERY, IT IS PRECISELY THE TYPE OF CASE IN 
WHICH THE CRIMINAL, A RICH KID INVOLVED IN DRUGS, WOULD 
HAVE ESCAPED PROSECUTION.IN THE PAST. THE FACT THAT HE 
WAS FOUND GUILTY IN THIS HIGHLY PUBLICIZED CASE SENDS A 
SIGNAL THAT THE CRIMINAL JUSTICE SYSTEM CAN, IN FACT, 
'FUNCTION IN GUATEMALA. 
7. FM STEIN SAID THAT HE REGRETTED THAT HE WAS UNABLE TO 
BRING GOOD NEWS ABOUT ANY OF THE CASES WHICH A/S SHATTUCK 
HAD MENTIONED (ORTIZ, DEVINE, AND HARBURY/BAMACA). STEIN 
RAISED THE POSSIBILITY THAT U.S. DOCUMENTS SOON TO BE 
DECLASSIFIED AND RELEASED CONCERNING THESE AND OTHER CASES

8. FM STEIN EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR U.S. SUPPORT IN 
THE RECENT DISCUSSION OF GUATEMALA IN THE UN HUMAN RIGHTS 
COMMISSION (UNHRC) IN GENEVA, NOTING THAT THE INTERVENTION 
OF THE U.S. DELEGATE HAD BEEN KEY IN PERMITTING MINUGUA 
DIRECTOR FRANCO TO ADDRESS THE COMMISSION. A/S SHATTUCK 
SAID THAT HE WAS VERY PLEASED TO HAVE PARTICIPATED IN THE 
UNHRC'S DISCUSSION OF GUATEMALA, IN WHICH THE COMMISSION 
RECOGNIZED THE PROGRESS MADE IN ADDRESSING SERIOUS HUMAN 
RIGHTS PROBLEMS IN GUATEMALA. THE RESOLUTION ADOPTED THIS 
YEAR BY CONSENSUS MOVES GUATEMALA FROM THE POSITION OF 
BEING THE SUBJECT OF GREAT SCRUTINY TO THE POSITION OF 
RECEIVING ADVICE AND ASSISTANCE FROM THE UNHRC TO IMPROVE 
ITS HUMAN RIGHTS SITUATION. 
9. FOREIGN MINISTER STEIN WAS ACCOMPANIED BY HIS SPECIAL
