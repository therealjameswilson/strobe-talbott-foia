2. SUMMARY: IN HIS OCTOBER 24 MEETING WITH GHANA'S 
PRESIDENT RAWLINGS, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT UNDERSCORED 
U.S. ADMIRATION FOR GHANA'S PROGRESS IN ECONOMIC AND 
POLITICAL REFORM, AND ITS IMPORTANT REGIONAL SECURITY 
ROLE. HE ENCOURAGED RAWLINGS TO STAY THE COURSE IN 
LIBERIA AND THANKED HIM FOR GHANA'S PEACEKEEPING ROLE IN 
RWANDA. RAWLINGS, WHO WAS IN AN EXPANSIVE MOOD AND 
PUNCTUATED HIS REMARKS WITH SWEEPING THEATRICAL 
GESTURES, RANGED OVER A SPECTRUM OF ISSUES: HE 
CRITICIZED U.S. TACTICS IN THIRD WORLD OPERATIONS FOR 
USING TOO MUCH MILITARY INTIMIDATION BUT NONETHELESS 
SAID HE SUPPORTED U.S. ACTIONS IN HAITI; STRESSED THAT 
CHARLES TAYLOR WAS NOT THE CENTRAL PROBLEM IN LIBERIA; 
AND POUNDED THE TABLE WHEN SPEAKING OF FRANCOPHONE 
AFRICANS' RELIANCE ON THEIR FORMER COLONIAL MASTERS. 
THE HEART OF HIS REMARKS, HOWEVER, WAS A PROPOSAL THAT 
HE, AS THE NEWLY-CHOSEN HEAD OF ECOWAS, ARRANGE A NEW 
PEACE CONFERENCE ON LIBERIA. HE URGED THE USG TO 
INCLUDE NIGERIA IN ITS LIBERIA CONSULTATIONS, TO ADVISE 
THE NIGERIANS NOT TO PULL OUT OF ECOMOG AND TO REALIZE 
THAT ELIMINATING TAYLOR IS NOT THE ANSWER. END SUMMARY.

3. IN'A ROLLICKING DISCUSSION PUNCTUATED BY THE HIGHLY 
THEATRICAL MANNER OF GHANAIAN PRESIDENT RAWLINGS; DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT ESTABLISHED THE MAIN THEME OF THE 
MEETING WHEN HE ASKED, "DO YOU SEE ECOWAS AS HAVING THE 
POTENTIAL TO BECOME AN EFFECTIVE AND COHERENT 
ORGANIZATION FOR PRESERVING PEACE IN THE WEST AFRICAH 
REGION?" IN RESPONSE, RAWLINGS AND HIS ADVISERS 
STRESSED THE NEED TO AVOID THE FAILURE OF ECOMOG IN 
LIBERIA. SUCH A FAILURE, THEY CLAIMED, COULD UNDERCUT
REGIONAL PEACEKEEPING EFFORTS IN THE FUTURE. EVERYONE 
IS TALKING ABOUT THE WORLD AS A "GLOBAL ECONOMIC 
VILLAGE," HE DECLARED, MAYBE THE TIME HAD COME FOR A 
"GLOBAL M/LITARY VILLAGE?" THE USG IS ALSO CONCERNED, 
MOOSE SAID, ABOUT SUSTAINING ECOMOG, NOW THAT THE 
NIGERIANS ARE REDUCING THE SIZE OF THEIR CONTINGENT, AND 
THE UGANDANS AND TANZANIANS DO NOT WANT TO STAY. 
RAWLINGS RESPONDED THAT HE TOO WAS DISAPPOINTED UGANDA 
AND TANZANIA WANTED TO PULL OUT OF LIBERIA. ASSISTANT 
SECRETARY MOOSE RESPONDED THAT HE ALSO THOUGHT IT 
IMPORTANT THAT TANZANIAN AND UGANDAN TROOPS NOT BE 
WITHDRAWN. THE USG AND RAWLINGS HAD INTERVENED TO ASK 
UGANDA AND TANZANIA NOT TO WITHDRAW IN ORDER TO GIVE 
RAWLINGS' DIPLOMATIC EFFORTS TIME TO SUCCEED.
