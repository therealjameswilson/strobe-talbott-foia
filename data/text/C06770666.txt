a. (U) ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT WELCOMED DEFENSE MINISTER 
JORGE DOMINGUEZ ON SEPTEMBER 22, THANKED DOMINGUEZ FOR 
INCLUDING THE STATE DEPARTMENT ON HIS WASHINGTON SCHEDULE, 
AND MENTIONED THAT SECRETARY ALBRIGHT WAS FULLY OCCUPIED 
THIS WEEK IN NEW YORK WITH UNITED NATIONS GENERAL ASSEMBLY 
EVENTS., DEFMIN DOMINGUEZ RESPONDED THAT HE HAD SEEN 
SECRETARY ALBRIGHT THE PREVIOUS DAY IN NEW YORK, ALONG WITH 
ARGENTINE FOREIGN MINISTER DI TELLA, IN CONNECTION WITH THE 
CLINTON-MENEM MEETING AT THE UN. HE DESCRIBED THE MEETING 
OF THE TWO PRESIDENTS AS SHORT (15 MINUTES)', BUT VERY WARM 
AND WITH EXTENSIVE PRESS COVERAGE. 
2. (U) TALBOTT ASKED DOMINGUEZ TO CONVEY HIS PERSONAL BEST 
WISHES TO FORMIN GUIDO DI TELLA, SAYING HE HAD HIGH REGARD 
FOR HIM FROM THE MANY SENSITIVE ISSUES THEY HAD WORKED ON 
TOGETHER. TALBOTT ASKED WHEN DOMINGUEZ WOULD MEET WITH 
U.S. DEFENSE SECRETARY COHEN (ON THURSDAY), AND SAID HE HAD 
HEARD MANY GOOD THINGS ABOUT DOMINGUEZ FROM COHEN.

3. (U) THE ACTING SECRETARY THEN ADDRESSED U.S.-ARGENTINE 
BILATERAL RELATIONS, EXPRESSING ADMIRATION FOR WHAT THE 
ARGENTINE GOVERNMENT HAS DONE IN RECENT YEARS TO TRANSFORM 
THE NATION'S POLITICAL SYSTEM AND ECONOMY, SAYING THAT THE 
CHANGES IN ARGENTINA BORDER ON THE MIRACULOUS. IN THE 
SECURITY SPHERE, CONTINUED TALBOTT, ARGENTINA'S NAME COMES 
UP REGULARLY IN CONNECTION WITH PEACEKEEPING ALL OVER THE 
WORLD; IT IS IN THE VANGUARD OF NATIONS CONTRIBUTING TO 
THAT IMPORTANT WORK. HE STATED THAT SOME YEARS AGO HAITI 
HAD BEEN ONE OF HIS FIRST IMPORTANT PROJECTS, AS DEPUTY 
SECRETARY, AND HE HAD SEEN ARGENTINE PEACEKEEPERS IN ACTION 
THERE. TALBOTT ALSO CONGRATULATED DOMINGUEZ ON THE RECENT 
PROGRESS MADE BY ARGENTINA AND THE UK ON THE 
MALVINAS/FALKLANDS SITUATION. LOOKING AHEAD TO THE OCTOBER 
ARGENTINE ELECTIONS AND DECEMBER PRESIDENTIAL TRANSFER, HE 
ASKEP DOMINGUEZ WHAT HIS OWN PLANS FOR THE FUTURE WERE. 
4. 4.4-DOMINGUEZ REPLIED THAT HE INTENDED TO RETURN TO THE 
PRIVATE SECTOR AFTER LEAVING OFFICE, BUT WARMING TO THE 
SUBJECT OF THE ARGENTINE ELECTIONS, WENT ON TO DESCRIBE THE 
PERONIST PARTY'S CURRENT POSITION. HE SAID THAT EVEN IF 
HIS PARTY LOST THE PRESIDENCY, IT WAS STILL VERY STRONG IN 
CONGRESS AND THE PROVINCES. DOMINGUEZ POINTED OUT THAT 
PERONISTS HAD WON TEN OF THE 14 PROVINCIAL GOVERNOR 
ELECTIONS SO FAR, AND WOULD ALMOST CERTAINLY RETAIN 
MAJORITIES IN THE NATIONAL SENATE AND CHAMBER OF DEPUTIES 
AS WELL. FURTHERMORE, HE STATED THAT PRESIDENT MENEM'S' 
POPULARITY REMAINED HIGH, ESPECIALLY OUTSIDE THE CAPITAL, 
AND THAT HE WOULD BE ELIGIBLE TO RUN AGAIN IN 2003. 
5.—(-C4- TALBOTT THEN ASKED IF PRESIDENT MENEM WOULD VISIT 
COLOMBIA SOON, ADDING THAT THE U.S. GOVERNMENT WAS 
CONCERNED ABOUT THE SITUATION THERE. DOMINGUEZ REPLIED 
AFFIRMATIVELY, GIVING THE VISIT DATE AS SEPTEMBER 29. HE 
AGREED THAT THE,COLOMBIAN SITUATION WAS VERY DIFFICULT, 
ADDING THAT MENEM AND PASTRANA HAD TALKED FRANKLY IN ilEW 
YORK. HE CHARACTERIZED MENEM'S POSITION AS OFFERING ALL 
POSSIBLE SUPPORT TO PASTRANA, WHILE INSISTING THAT IT WAS A 
COLOMBIAN PROBLEM REQUIRING A COLOMBIAN SOLUTION. OUTSIDE 
MILITARY INTERVENTION, SAID DOMINGUEZ, WAS NOT THE ANSWER. 
TO THE ACTING SECRETARY'S QUERY ABOUT CLINTON-MENEM 
DISCUSSIONS ON COLOMBIA THE PREVIOUS DAY, DOMINGUE.Z 
RESPONDED THAT THERE HAD NOT BEEN MUCH TIME FOR SUBSTANCE. 
6. -te+- PETER ROMERO, ACTING ASSISTANT SECRETARY FOR WESTERN 
HEMISPHERE AFFAIRS, EXPLAINED THAT THE U.S. WAS SUPPORTING 
A GLOBAL STRATEGY ON COLOMBIA, WHICH INVOLVED ELEMENTS OF

7. (C) TALBOTT THEN ASKED DOMINGUEZ ABOUT VENEZUELA, 
VOICING A U.S.. CONCERN ABOUT DRIFT 'IN THE COUNTRY'S 
POLITICAL PROCESS. THE DEFENSE MINISTER RESPONDED THAT

POINTED OUT THAT PRESIDENT CLINTON HAD MADE THE SAME POINTS 
ABOUT STAYING WITHIN THE PARAMETERS OF DEMOCRACY. 
8. 4E4 TURNING TO PERU, TALBOTT MENTIONED HAVING A POSITIVE 
MEETING WITH THE PERUVIAN FOREIGN MINISTER IN NEW YORK, 
THEN EMPHASIZED THE USEFUL ROLES THAT THE U.S. AND 
ARGENTINA HAD PLAYED IN HELPING PERU AND ECUADOR RESOLVE 
THEIR AMAZON BORDER CONFLICT; DOMINGUEZ AGREED WITH THE 
ACTING SECRETARY, AND RECALLED THAT THE PERUVIANS HAD 
PARTICIPATED CONSTRUCTIVELY LAST YEAR IN THE CARTAGENA 
MEETINGS WITH SECRETARY COHEN AND PRESIDENT PASTRANA. HE 
RECALLED THAT AT THAT TIME THE PARTIES HAD STRONGLY 
SUPPORTED GOC PEACE TALKS WITH THE COLOMBIAN GUERILLAS, AND 
MUSING, ASKED WHAT HAD GONE WRONG. HE OPINED THAT PASTRANA 
SEEMED TO BE LOSING GROUND, AND THAT UNFORTUNATELY THE 
SITUATION NOW WAS WORSE THAN A YEAR AGO. HE ASKED WHAT 
COULD.BE DONE TO HELP PASTRANA. 
9. —(44- TALBOTT ARGUED FOR CONTINUED STRONG SUPPORT OF 
PASTRANA, AND ROMERO ADDED THAT ALL POSSIBLE TOOLS HAD TO 
BE USED IN COLOMBIA. HE PROPOSED A STRONGER DIPLOMATIC 
EFFORT WITH THE FARC AND OTHER GROUPS AS WELL, STATING THAT 
ONCE THEY CAME BACK TO THE TABLE, OPPORTUNITIES WOULD EXIST 
TO EMPLOY INCENTIVES AND DISINCENTIVES. WITH AGREEMENT ON 
A DEMILITARIZED ZONE, THE INTERNATIONAL DONOR COMMUNITY 
COULD BE MOBILIZED TO SUPPORT PASTRANA. ROMERO SAID THAT 
ARGENTINA COULD BE HELPFUL IN THAT CONTEXT BECAUSE OF 
PRESIDENT MENEM'S SPECIAL RELATIONSHIP WITH THE BRITISH.AND 
ITALIANS.

10. (U) DOMINGUEZ AGREED THAT ARGENTINE RELATIONS WITH 
BRITAIN HAD BEEN EXCELLENT SINCE MENEM'S TRIP TO LONDON 
LAST YEAR. HE MENTIONED THAT A JOINT MILITARY SEARCH-AND-
RESCUE EXERCISE AT SEA WOULD TAKE PLACE SOON, AND PRAISED 
BRITISH AIR FORCE COOPERATION IN THE SOUTH OF ARGENTINA. 
HE ALSO SPOKE POSITIVELY OF THE VISIT TO ARGENTINA OF 
BRITAIN'S FIRST SEA LORD. 
11. 4G+ ARGENTINE AMBASSADOR TO THE U.S. DIEGO GUELAR . 
ENTERED AT THAT POINT, WITH APOLOGIES FOR HIS LATE ARRIVAL. 
ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT EXPLAINED THEY HAD BEEN MAKING A 
TOUR D'HORIZON OF THE REGION, SINCE THE EXCELLENT 
ARGENTINE-U.S. BILATERAL RELATIONS HAD NOT REQUIRED MUCH • 
DISCUSSION. GUELAR SAID HE HAD JUST COME FROM A LUNCH 
MEETING AT THE INTER-AMERICAN DEVELOPMENT BANK, WHICH 
FEATURED AS THE SPEAKER PRESIDENT CHAVEZ, WHO HAD GONE ON 
AT SOME LENGTH (CAUSING HIS LATE ARRIVAL). TO TALBOTT'S 
INQUIRY WHETHER THE CHAVEZ SPEECH HAD BEEN SUBSTANTIVE, 
GUELAR REPLIED THAT IT HAD BEEN A BIT CONFUSING 
IDEOLOGICALLY. TALBOTT SAID THAT HE HAD HEARD ABOUT 
CHAVEZ' RELATIVE LACK OF PREVIOUS INTERNATIONAL EXPERIENCE, 
HOPED THAT THE UN MEETINGS HAD PROVIDED CHAVEZ WITH HEALTHY 
EXPOSURE TO DIVERSE VIEWS, AND F,SKED IF HE APPEARED TO BE 
LISTENING TO OTHERS.' ROMERO COMMENTED. THAT ALREADY THERE 
WAS A NOTICEABLE CHANGE, SINCE CHAVEZ HAD PREVIOUSLY 
COMPLAINED ABOUT LESS-THAN-FAVORABLE INTERNATIONAL PRESS; 
THIS SHOWED HE WAS ACQUIRING ABEALTHY INTEREST IN THE 
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY. 
12. 4G+ ADDRESSING THE ARGENTINE AMBASSADOR, TALBOTT 
RECALLED THAT HE HAD BEEN SAYING HOW IMPRESSIVE ARGENTINA'S 
ACHIEVEMENTS WERE; NOT ONLY DOMESTICALLY BUT AS AN EXAMPLE 
FOR THE WHOLE HEMISPHERE AS WELL. HE MENTIONED PRESIDENT 
MENEM'S DRAMATIC SUCCESSES AS A LEADER AND RETURNED TO HIS 
UPCOMING VISIT TO COLOMBIA, STRESSING THAT THE U.S. HAD 
SEVERAL LAYERS OF INTERESTS THERE (NOT JUST NARCOTICS 
ISSUES). THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID THAT A FAILURE IN 
COLOMBIA COULD HAVE OMINOUS IMPLICATIONS FOR THE WHOLE 
REGION, AND REQUESTED THAT MENEM SHARE A CANDID ASSESSMENT 
WITH THE U.S. AFTER HIS TRIP. ROMERO POINTED OUT THAT 
PEACE-MAKING (OR MILITARY INTERVENTION) WAS NOT ON ANYONE'S 
AGENDA, AND DOMINGUEZ AGREED.

FOCUS, WAS A REMARKABLE ACCOMPLISHMENT. HE ASKED HOW THE
