Dear Valery: 
Thanks for your letter. I'll accept any reason for 
being in touch with you, even a strong admonishment! 
I, too, am concerned about the future of the radios. 
I've been in fairly regular contact with Kevin Klose, in-
cluding when we were both in Moscow two weeks ago. I'm as-
king an excellent colleague of mine, Bill Danvers, to put 
together a response to your letter that will address your 
specific concerns. You'll be hearing from him shortly. 
Meanwhile, all the best to you both. 
Yours, 
PS: As for your criticism of our 'loud" support for fast-
track reform, I understand your misgivings. I really do 
hope in the New Year you'll be able to come down and meet 
with several colleagues and me and give us the benefit of 
your sense of what's happening and what we should do about 
it.
