From: Strobe Talbott 
Re: Ca11 from the Secretary 
The Secretary called this morning. He had received a 
memo from the President about our issue. In the course of 
his economic-package discussions with congressional leaders, 
the President had found Senator Dole supportive of the idea 
of assistance to the FSU. The President was sharing this with 
the Secretary, along with his interest in what the interagency 
process is generating by way of policy initiatives. I told 
the Secretary that the FSU Policy Group, at its meeting this' 
Thursday, would be discussing a package of in.itiatives that 
would be available for the President's consideration in 
advance of his meeting with Yeltsin. Th.e purpose of the package 
will be to send a clear message to Yeltsivand his government 
that the Clinton Administration is committed to assisting 
economic and political reform. The Secretary said he was 
pleased. He asked if the package would require consultation 
with the Hill. I replied that we should consult closely with 
key congressional figures on all aspects and at all stages, 
but that the package we are working on i.s based on already-
allocated funds and does not require new legislation. (If 
that statement requires correction, 1et me know right away, 
so I can give the Secretary a memo accordingly and Promptly.) 
I told the Secretary that when I catch up with him in Jerusalem 
next week, I'll give him concrete suggestions on how he can, 
in his conversations with Kozyrev, foreshadow the assistance 
issue as it will play in the Clinton-Yeltsin meeting. 
Another subject that came up was venue for Clinton-Yeltsin.
