2. SUMMARY. DEPSEC TALBOTT STRONGLY REAFFIRMED THE U.S.-
ROK PARTNERSHIP, STRESSING TEAMWORK IN IMPLEMENTING THE 
AGREED FRAMEWORK DURING JAN. 26-27 MEETINGS WITH THE ROK 
FOREIGN MINISTER AND DEPUTY PREMIER. AGREEING THAT 
"HARMONY AND PARALLELISM" SHOULD GOVERN OUR APPROACHES TO 
NORTH KOREA, HE GOT ROK ENDORSEMENT OF SENIOR BILATERAL 
CONSULTATIONS ON PROMOTING S-N DIALOGUE. TALBOTT ALSO 
DISCUSSED HOW WE COULD MANAGE LIAISON OFFICES TO OUR 
MUTUAL ADVANTAGE. ROK OFFICIALS FORCEFULLY SUPPORTED THE 
AGREED FRAMEWORK AND PROMISED TO CARRY THAT MESSAGE TO 
BOTH LEGISLATURES AND PUBLICS. JUNE ROK LOCAL ELECTIONS 
ADD A POLITICAL DIMENSION TO THE TIMING OF LIAISON 
OFFICES--SOMETHING PYONGYANG SEES AND EXPLOITS, THEY 
SAID. URGING CAUTION AND FIRMNESS, THEY SUGGESTED THAT 
PROGRESS IN LWR SUPPLY TALKS MIGHT GOVERN TIMING OF 
LIAISON OFFICES, SINCE PROLONGED DPRK RESISTANCE TO A ROK 
MODEL LWR WOULD MAKE LIAISON OFFICES POLITICALLY DIFFICULT. 
OTH SIDES AGREED TO COUNTER DPRK EFFORTS TO UNDERMINE THE 
ARMISTICE AND EXPEL POLISH OBSERVERS FROM THE DMZ. DEPSEC 
COMMENDED ROKG EMPHASIS ON GLOBALIZATION AND WITH FM GONG 
RAISED ISSUES INCLUDING HAITI, UNHRC RESOLUTION ON CHINA, 
MEXICAN FINANCIAL CRISIS, KOREAN FINANCIAL LIBERALIZATION, 
AND RUSSIAN/CHINESE ROLES ON THE PENINSULA. END SUMMARY,-

5. HARMONY AND PARALLELISM: GONG SAID WE WERE FACING THE 
CHALLENGE OF RECONCILING THE DISPARITY IN PROGRESS BETWEEN 
OUR TWO TRACKS WITH PYONGYANG. SEOUL'S MAJOR CONCERN WAS 
THAT THE ABSENCE OF SOUTH-NORTH DIALOGUE WOULD JEOPARDIZE 
FULL IMPLEMENTATION OF THE FRAMEWORK. THE ROKG WANTED 
VISIBLE PROGRESS IN THE NORTH-SOUTH TRACK BEFORE PYONGYANG 
RECEIVED FURTHER TANGIBLE BENEFITS FROM THE AGREEMENT 
(I.E. LIAISON OFFICES). STRESSING THE NEED FOR "HARMONY 
AND PARALLELISM" BETWEEN THE TWO TRACKS. -GONG SUGGESTED - 
THAT THE TIMING OF THE OFFICES MIGHT BE BASED ON PROGRESS 
IN THE LWR CONTRACT TALKS. IF THE DPRK HAD NOT ACCEPTED 
THE SOUTH KOREAN MODEL BY LATE APRIL--THESIX-MONTH-TARGET 
DATE IN THE FRAMEWORK--OPENING OFFICES WOULD BE AN 
UNPOPULAR DECISION IN SOUTH KOREA. DPRK-ACCEPTANCE OF THE 
ROK MODEL, HOWEVER, WOULD HAVE A FAVORABLE IMPACT ON THE 
POLITICAL EQUATION. GONG EXPLICITLY NOTED THAT THE 
DOMESTIC POLITICAL CALENDAR, SPECIFICALLY THE JUNE LOCAL 
ELECTIONS, WAS A KEY FACTOR.
