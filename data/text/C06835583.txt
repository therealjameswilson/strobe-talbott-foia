2. ~ THIS IS AN ACTION REQUEST. SEE PARA 5.
3. ~ DURING A TRIP TO ASHGABAT LAST OCTOBER, 5/NIS
AMBASSADOR SESTANOVICH AND OSD A/S WARNER INFORMALLY
PROPOSED INITIATING A SECURITY DIALOGUE WITH THE GOT IN
1998. PRESIDENT NIYAZOV APPEARED RECEPTIVE TO THIS IDEA.
THIS PROPOSAL FOLLOWS UP ON THESE DISCUSSIONS WITH A FORMAL
LETTER FROM SECRETARY ALBRIGHT. THE DIALOGUE WOULD CONSIST
OF MEETINGS BETWEEN AN INTERAGENCY U.S. TEAM AND A
COUNTERPART INTERAGENCY TEAM FROM TURKMENISTAN LED BY THE
RESPECTIVE FOREIGN MINISTRIES. THE VENUE FOR THESE
MEETINGS COULD ALTERNATE BETWEEN ASHGABAT AND WASHINGTON.
WE ANTICIPATE PM ACTING ASSISTANT SECRETARY ERIC NEWSOM
WOULD LEAD THE U.S. DELEGATION.
4. ~ CREATION OF THIS CHANNEL WOULD GI'VE US A USEFUL
FORUM FOR ADDRESSING POLICY-RELATED BILATERAL AND REGIONAL
SECURITY AND ARMS CONTROL ISSUES OF MUTUAL INTEREST AND
CONCERN. IT ALSO SHOULD ENCOURAGE GREATER INTERAGENCY
COORDINATION IN TURKMENISTAN ON SECURITY ISSUES AND
STRENGTHEN THE ROLE OF THE FOREIGN MINISTRY.
5. ~ ACTION REQUEST: THE LETTER FROM SECRETARY ALBRIGHT
PROPOSING THE DIALOGUE SHOULD BE DELIVERED TO FOREIGN
MINISTER SHIKHMURADOV BY THE AMBASSADOR AT THE EARLIEST
APPROPRIATE OCCASION. EMBASSY IS REQUESTED TO REFER TO THE
LETTER IN DISCUSSIONS WITH GOT OFFICIALS AS APPROPRIATE AND
TO ENCOURAGE THE GOVERNMENT OF TURKMENISTAN TO TAKE FULL
ADVANTAGE OF THIS OPPORTUNITY. THE TEXT OF THE LETTER
FOLLOWS IN PARA 6. THERE WILL BE NO SIGNED ORIGINAL.
6. ~ BEGIN TEXT:
DEAR MR. MINISTER:
AS YOU KNOW, THE UNITED STATES AND TURKMENISTAN HAVE BEEN

EXCHANGES DURING HIS TRIP TO ASHGABAT LAST OCTOBER. DURING
THIS VISIT, PRESIDENT NIYAZOV EXPRESSED INTEREST IN
INITIATING REGULAR BILATERAL DISCUSSIONS ON POLITICAL-
MILITARY ISSUES.

CONTACTS ON ARMS CONTROL AND REGIONAL SECURITY ISSUES IN
REGULARLY SCHEDULED DISCUSSIONS. THESE TALKS WOULD
STRENGTHEN OUR BILATERAL RELATIONS AND ENSURE CLOSER
COOPERATION IN MULTILATERAL FORA AND'IN REGIONAL CONFLICT
RESOLUTION. IN THIS WAY THE UNITED STATES ALSO CAN
DEMONSTRATE ITS SUPPORT FOR TURKMENISTAN'S SECURITY,
TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY AND SOVEREIGNTY.
SOME OF THE ISSUES FOR DISCUSSION COULD INCLUDE REGIONAL
SECURITY, NONPROLIFERATION, EXPORT CONTROLS, SECURITY
ASSISTANCE AND BUILDING ON TURKEMENISTAN'S ACTIVE
PARTICIPATION IN THE PARTNERSHIP FOR PEACE AND THE OSCE.
OVER THE LAST YEAR, WE HAVE SUCCESSFULLY INITIATED SUCH A
DIALOGUE WITH THE CAUCASUS NATIONS AND DISCUSSED SIMILAR
TOPICS IN JOINT COMMISSIONS WITH KAZAKHSTAN AND UZBEKISTAN.
WE ARE NOW PROPOSING A SIMILAR SECURITY DIALOGUE WITH
KYRGYZSTAN AS WELL AS WITH TURKMENISTAN.
WE PROPOSE THAT THE FIRST ROUND OF THESE NEW DISCUSSIONS
TAKE PLACE IN LATE MAY OR JUNE IN ASHGABAT. WE STRONGLY
SUGGEST THAT THEY BE LED BY APPROPRIATE REPRESENTATIVES
FROM OUR MINISTRIES, BUT ALSO INCLUDE BROAD PARTICIPATION
BY OTHER MINISTRIES. I LOOK EORWARD TO YOUR REPLY TO THIS
PROPOSAL. IF YOU ACCEPT, WE WILL RESPOND WITH A DETAILED
DESCRIPTION OF HOW THESE NEW DISCUSSIONS MIGHT OPERATE AND
OUR THOUGHTS ON THE TIMING FOR THE FIRST MEETING.
SINCERELY,
STROBE TALBOTT
