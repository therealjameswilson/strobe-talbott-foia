Congratulations on your recent confirmation as Ambassador-At-Large and Special Advisor to 
the Secretary for the Newly Independent States. 
As a member of the "fraternity" of Soviet specialists (Harriman Institute, '69) I find it very 
encouraging that President Clinton decided to create a position with this extraordinary scope 
and to appoint someone of your qualifications to the post. 
Thank you for directing the letters I sent you in January and February (with an assist from 
Barry Seaman) to Bill Taylor at State. Those letters concerned the sale of $70 million of U.S. 
agricultural equipment and supplies to Ukraine with Exim insurance. The transaction was 
successfully completed, and is providing the basis for a farm management project that will 
involve up to 100 U.S. farmers and 300 Ukrainian state farms. 
I enjoyed seeing you on Meet the Press- in Vancouver, and I was particularly pleased to hear-,_--_ 
your enthusiastic response to the question about investing in Russia. The Ukraine transaction 
we have just completed is a pr6totype, I believe, for many mid-sized investment project§ thal---1
can be done in the former Soviet Union at little direct expense to the U.S. government. I am 
preparing an "op-ed" piece about this transaction, and will send you a copy if and when it is 
published. 
Best wishes for continued success in the incredible challenge that you have undertaken. I hope 
that we have the opportunity to meet in the not too distant future, and I will be glad to be of 
assistance from my private sector vantage point in any way that I can. 
Best regards,
