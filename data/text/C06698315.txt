MEETINGS WITH RUSSIAN DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER MAMEDOV,

EUROPEAN SECURITY, AS WELL AS BOSNIA, AND tUBA. THEIR

PACIFIC SALMON AND THE RECENT CANADIAN KILLING OF

PROTECTING ITS MOST VITAL INTERESTS, INCLUDING KEEPING 
NATO INFRASTRUCTURE FROM ITS BORDERS. IN HIS JUDGMENT, 
PRIMAKOV IDENTIFIES WITH TRACK ONE, AND IS LETTING 
MAMEDOV CARRY OUT TRACK TWO. IF TRACK TWO SUCCEEDS, 
PRIMAKOV COULD. EMBRACE IT; IF IT LEADS TO A SETBACK OR 
FAILS, HE CAN DISTANCE HIMSELF FROM IT. 
5. WHAT THIS MEANS, CONTINUED D/S TALBOTT, IS THAT WE ALL 
HAVE TO BE PROTECTIVE OF THE SECOND TRACK AND HEAR 
MAMEDOV OUT, MAKING SURE HE UNDERSTANDS OUR "OBJECTIVES, 
OUR REb LINES, AND OUR BOTTOM LINES." WHEN HE LAST SAW 
PRIMAKOV, THEY DISCUSSED HOW TO "SQUARE THE CIRCLE" AS 
NATO PURSUES ITS STRATEGY OBJECTIVES AND ITS SOVEREIGN 
RIGHT TO TAKE NEW MEMBERS. AT THE SAME TIME, PRESIDENT ' 
CLINTON DOES NOT WANT TO SEE THE CREATION OF NEW DIVIDING 
LINES IN EUROPE, WHICH MEANS WE MUST BE PARTICULARLY 
SENSITIVE TO NATIONS WHO ARE NOT PART OF ENLARGEMENT. 
6. HOW THE RUSS/ANS PROCEED RAISES QUESTIONS. THEY GO ON 
THE RECORD AGAINST NATO ENLARGEMENT, SEEK LEGALLY BINDING 
COMMITMENTS AGAINST DEPLOYMENT OF INFRASTRUCTURE IN THE 
NEW NATO MEMBERS, AND ASSERT THAT NO FORMER REPUBLICS OF 
THE USSR BE ALLOWED TO JOIN NATO. THESE ARE THE RUSSIAN 
"RED LINES." THE RUSSIANS ARE TESTING TO SEE HOW MUCH 
UNITY AND AGREEMENT EXISTS AMONG THE ALLIES ON THESE 
ISSUES. THUS, THE MORE SOLIDARITY AND UNITY THE U.S., 
CANADA, AND OTHER ALLIES PRESENT ON THESE ISSUES THE 
BETTER.

9. IN RESPONSE, SMITH STATED THAT THIS ANALYSIS

IT SHOULD BE. IN RESPONSE, D/S TALBOTT STATED THERE WERE

11. SMITH RESPONDED THAT CANADA DOES NOT WANT A NEW L/NE 
DIVIDING LINE IN EUROPE SINCE THAT IS-NOT IN ANYONE'S 
INTEREST. HOWEVER, IT IS IMPORTANT THAT GOVERNMENTS START 
THINKING ABOUT THESE ISSUES BEFORE THE NEXT NATO 
MINISTERIAL. BY OCTOBER/NOVEMBER, WE NEED TO KNOW WHERE 
WE WANT TO GO. "MY BIGGEST NIGHTMARE" SAID SMITH, IS THAT 
WE WILL WORK OUT ENLARGEMENT AND AWAKEN TO FIND THAT THE 
U.S. SENATE (OR ANOTHER NATIONAL PARLIAMENT) IS NOT 
PREPARED TO EXTEND ARTICLE V TO NEW MEMBERS% WHICH WOULD 
BE A DISASTER. ON THAT POINT, D/S TALBOTT STATED THAT HE 
HAD BEEN CONSULTING WITH SENATOR LUGAR AND OTHERS, AND 
THAT WHILE THERE IS A NEED TO LAY THE GROUNDWORK IN THE 
SENATE, HE BELIEVES IT IS A WINNABLE DEBATE. ERIC 
EDELMAN ADDED THAT INITIAL DOD ESTIMATES INDICATE THAT, 
TAKING ACCOUNT THE INFRASTRUCTURE THAT ALREADY EXISTS IN 
MANY OF THESE COUNTRIES, THE COST OF NATO ENLARGEMENT MAY 
NOT BE AS HIGH AS ORIGINALLY THOUGHT. 
12. RALPH LYSYSHYN COMMENTED THAT, WITH RESPECT TO NATO 
ENLARGEMENT AND OTHER SECURITY ISSUES, LIKE NATO 
ADAPTATION, THEY NEEDED TO BE DEALT WITH IN AN INTEGRATED 
MANNER. HE EXPRESSED CONCERN THAT, TO DATE, THERE HAD 
BEEN A TENDENCY TO ADDRESS THEM IN A PIECEMEAL MANNER, 
WHEN WHAT WE NEED TO DO IS BUILD A "PACKAGE." D/S 
TALBOTT RESPONDED THAT WITHIN THE USG THIS PROBLEM WAS 
BEING ADDRESSED BY HAVING THE SAME PEOPLE IN STATE AND 
DOD DEAL WITH THESE ISSUES, AND HE RECOGNIZED THAT ALL 
THE INSTITUTIONS NEEDED TO PLAY A ROLE IN THE ENLARGEMENT 
QUESTION.

CUBA 
16. BEFORE THE MEETING CONCLUDED, SMITH TURNED BRIEFLY 
TO THE ISSUE OF CUBA, NOTING THE PLANNED AUGUST 30 VISIT 
TO OTTAWA OF AMBASSADOR EIZENSTAT TO DISCUSS HELMS/BURTON 
AND DEMOCRACY IN CUBA. ON THAT POINT, SMITH SAID THAT 
THE U.S. SHOULD REMEMBER THAT IF IT WANTS CANADA TO HAVE 
INFLUENCE ON CASTRO AND TO PUSH HUMAN RIGHTS. THAT "OUR 
INFLUENCE DIMINISHES IF WE APPEAR TO BE OPERATING ON 
BEHALF OF THE UNITED STATES." IN RESPONSE, D/S TALBOTT 
NOTED THAT IT IS POSSIBLE FOR ALLIES TO CONSULT WITH ONE 
ANOTHER WITHOUT BEING SUBSERVIENT. 
17. SMITH THEN ADDED THAT WHEN JIM BARTLEMAN (ASSISTANT 
SECRETARY TO THE CABINET FOR FOREIGN AND DEFENCE POLICY) 
RECENTLY MET WITH CASTRO, ONE REFRAIN HE HEARD WHICH U.S. 
AUTHORITIES MIGHT WISH TO THINK ABOUT WAS CASTRO'S 
COMPLAINT THAT HE HAD NO ONE HE COULD TALK TO WHO IS 
"PLUGGED INTO THE USG AT A HIGH LEVEL." SMITH SAID THAT 
THE U.S. SHOULD NOT UNDERESTIMATE THE DEGREE TO WHICH 
CASTRO IS ISOLATED FROM BOTH HIS OWN PEOPLE AND 
OUTS/DERS.
