SUBJECT: GREECE AGREES To PURSUE PROTECTING POWER ARRANGEMENT
FOR U.S. AND FRY
REFS: (A) STATE 30144 (B) ATHENS 873
CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR R. NICHOLAS BURNS, REASONS 1.5 (B,D)
1. ~ DURING HIS MEETING WITH FM PAPANDREOU ON FEB. 24 (BALKAN
AND GREECE-TURKEY ISSUES REPORTED SEPTELS), THE'MBASSADOR TURNED
DOWN GREECE'S OFFER (REF B) TO HOST A U.S. INTERESTS SECTION 1N
THE GREEK EMBASSY IN BELGRADE THE U S g HE STATED WOULD BE
INTERESTED IN PURSUING A'ROTECTING POWER ARRANGEMENT WITH GREECE
To PROTECT U.S. INTERESTS IN THE FRY. THE AMBASSADOR EXPLAINED
THE ADVANTAGES OF THIS ARRANGEMENT. THE U.S., HE ADDED, WOULD
AGREE To LET GREECE BE THE FRY'S PR'OTECTING POWER IN THE UNITED
STATES.
3.~THE AMBASSADOR AGREED ON THE POTENTIAL MUTUAL BENEFITS
THAT BOTH THE U.S. AND GREECE WOULD RECEIVE- HE EXPLAINED THAT,
IF THE FRY AGREES To THE PROPOSED ARRANGEMENT, THIS WOULD
NECESSARILY BE MADE PUBLIC. PAPANDREOU REPLIED THAT THIS WOULD
BE FINE.
4. COMMENT: PAPANDREOU DID NOT SPECIFY HOW SOON OUR OFFER
WOULD BE CONVEYED To BELGRADE NOR DID HE SPECULATE WHEN THE FRY
MIGHT RESPOND. RONDOS, THE LIKELY INTERMEDIARY, WILL BE IN
WASHINGTON ON FEBRUARY 29. WE HAVE OFFERED To HELP ARRANGE
MEETINGS FOR HIM WITH DEPARTMENT OFFICIALS AND WILL REPORT WHEN
WE HAVE FURTHER INFORMATION ON RONDOS'S SCHEDULE. END COMMENT.
BURNS
.2.~ FM PAPANDREOU ENTHUSIASTICALLY AGREED To PASS THE MESSAGE
TO THE FRY GOVERNMENT (COMMENT: PROBABLY THROUGH HIS SENIOR
BALKAN ADVISOR, ALEX RONDOS, WHO SAT IN ON THE MEETING.)
PAPANDREOU COMMENTED THAT THE PROPOSED PROTECTING POWER
ARRANGEMENT WOULD FACILITATE CLOSER COOPERATION BETWEEN U.S. AND
GREEK DIPLOMATS DEALING WITH FRY/BALKAN ISSUES. HE ESPECIALLY
APPRECIATED THE TRUST THAT THE U.S. PLACED IN GREECE IN PROPOSING
THIS ARRANGEMENT To DEAL WITH THE MILOSEVIC REGIME.
