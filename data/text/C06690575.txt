Dear Dan: 
Your letter was especially meaningful to me. Many 
of the sentiments that you so kindly express about me I 
can reciprocate -- and then some. You've had quite a 
"trajectory" of your own. I've greatly admired you 
since we first got to know each other at Yale. 
I hope our paths,can cross from time to time now 
that I am in this new line of work. I have two 
professional reasons for wanting to see you. One is 
that I would appreciate your counsel on how we are 
proceeding in our attempt to develop a post-Cold War 
strategyntowards Russia. The other is that we are 
going to be giving special emphasis to the energy 
sector in Russia and the New Independent States. As 
you may have read, Vice President Gore is going to be 
heading up a special project on the subject. Maybe 
there is some way that we could get youcinvolved in 
that. Please let me know when you have a little time 
to get together and chat. 
Thanks again for your wonderful letter, and I hope 
to see you soon.
