6. ~ TRUBNIKOV SAID THAT HE WAS NOT YET FAMILIAR WITH THE
OFFICIAL GOR POSITION ON ALL SUCH DETAILED MATTERS.
HOWEVERi ON A PERSONAL BASISi HE COULD OBSERVE THAT THE
MOLDOVAN PARLIAMENT'S PASSAGE OF A CONSTITUTIONAL
ARRANGEMENT MAKING MOLDOVA A PARLIAMENTARY REPUBLIC, AS
OPPOSED TO A PRESIDENTIAL REPUBLICi AND EFFECTIVELY
"THROWING PRESIDENT LUCINSCHI OUT OF OFFICE" COULD ONLY
COMPLICATE MATTERS. HE REGRETTED THAT THE MOLDOVAN
NATIONALISTS AND COMMUNISTS HAD GOTTEN TOGETHER "TO GET RID
OF" PRESIDENT LUCINSCHI AND THIS WOULD HAVE SERIOUS
CONSEQUENCES. HE NOTED THAT LUCINSCHI WAS SUPPOSED TO SEE
PRESIDENT PUTIN JULY 28-29 IN MOSCOW, BUT HE WAS NOT SURE
IF THAT HAD HAPPENED. TRUBNIKOV SAID THE CONSEQUENCE OF
THE MOLDOVAN PARLIAMENT'S ACTION WAS TO STRENGTHEN THOSE
FORCES WITHIN MOLDOVA WHO WANTED TO JOIN HANDS WITH A
GREATER ROMANIA, EVEN AS THE DECISION ALSO ENCOURAGED THOSE
GROUPS LOOKING FOR TRANSNISTRIAN INDEPENDENCE. THIS
POL'ITICAL CHANGE MADE THINGS MORE DIFFICULT FOR PRIMAKOV
AND THE COMMISSION, PRIMAKOV .("NOW IN AN AWKWARD
POSITION" ) HAD AGREED TO LEAD THE COMMISSION WHEN THERE WAS
A STRONG PRESIDENTIAL POWER IN MOLDOVA, BUT NOW THERE WAS
NO PRESIDENTIAL POWER ON WHICH TO RELY AND THIS MADE
PRIMAKOV'S TASK TO HAMMER OUT A SETTLEMENT MUCH MORE
DIFFICULT.
7. ~ TALBOTT THANKED TRUBNIKOV FOR HIS INSIGHTS. HE

MAINTAINED THIS PRINCIPLE AND THE RESULT WAS THAT THE
FORMEF. SOVIET UNION DID NOT GO DOWN THE VIOLENT PATH OF THE
FORMER YUGOSLAVIA. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID WE HOPED
RUSSIA WOULD MAINTAIN THIS PRINCIPLE.. HE THEN ASKED TWO

SECOND, WHAT WAS THE TRUBNIKOV ASSESSMENT OF THE FORCES IN
MOLDOVA AND ROMANIA THAT OSTENSIBLY ADVOCATED FOR A GREATER
ROMANIA?

PRIMAKOV PLAN
11. ~THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THEN TURNED TO PRIMAKOV'S
PROPOSAL FOR AN OSCE ROLE IN RESOLVING THE
MOLDOVAN/TRANSNISTRIAN CONFLICT. HE SAID HE LIKED
PRIMAKOV'S IDEA OF THE OSCE AS AN UMBRELLA, BUT THE HANDLE
OF THE UMBRELLA HAD TO BE ROOTED FIRMLY IN THE OSCE.
MOREOVER; THE UMBRELLA HAD TO HAVE A GENUINELY
INTERNATIONAL COMPONENT. HE NOTED THAT WE HAD HEARD
THROUGH THE AUSTRIANS THAT PRIMAKOV MIGHT BE SUGGESTING
THAT NATO COUNTRIES BE EXCLUDED FROM ANY OSCE
PEACEKEEPING/STABILIZING PRESENCE IN SUPPORT OF A POLITICAL
SETTLEMENT. THIS WOULD BE A PROBLEM. TALBOTT STRESSED
THAT WE DID NOT WANT TO IMPLY THAT NATO AS AN ORGANIZATION
WANTED SUCH A ROLE. HE DOUBTED ANY WESTERN CAPITAL WOULD
URGE A NATO ROLE. HOWEVER, INDIVIDUAL NATO COUNTRIES
SHOULD NOT BE EXCLUDED FROM PARTICIPATION AND ANY SUCH OSCE
EFFORT SHOULD NOT BE COMPOSED EXCLUSIVELY OF CIS COUNTRIES.
