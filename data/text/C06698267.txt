2. "')N.,,Summary: Acting secretary Talbott met with 
Spanish Defense Minister Serra on September 4. serra 
emphasized that Spain wanted a new role in the military 
command structure of NATO in recognition of its efforts in 
Bosnia, the Gulf War, and elsewhere. He argued that the 
main threat to NATO was shifting from the East to the 
South. Talbott welcomed Spain's greater interest in 
NATO. On Bosnia, Serra said IFOR must be replaced with 
something, possibly a trained•Bosnian police force, and he 
called for redoubled work on reconstruction after the 
election. On Iraq, Serra said that Spain "completely 
supportsll U.S. actions and,offered future military support 
in similar cases, while adding that more notice to allies 
before action would be preferable.' End Summary 
3. (U) ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH SPANISH DEFENSE 
MINISTER EDUARDO SERRA ON SEPTEMBER 4. ACCOMPANYING SERRA 
WERE SPANISH AMBASSADOR ANTONIO DE OYARZABAL; PEDRO 
ARGUELLES,THE DEFENSE MINISTER'S CHIEF OF CABINET; AND 
SPANISH EMBASSY POLITICAL COUNSELOR CARLOS ROBLES. ON THE 
U.S. SIDE WERE EUR DAS ANTHONY WAYNE; D STAFFER PHILIP 
GOLDBERG; MADRID POL/ECON MINISTER HARRY JONES (NOTETAKER). 
4.'144 MINISTER SERRA, WHO HAD BEEN INVITED TO WASHINGTON 
BY DEFENSE SECRETARY PERRY, SAID HE HAD COME PRIMARILY TO 
DISCUSS TWO SUBJECTS: THE NATO ALLIANCE AND THE 
PROFESSIONALIZATION OF THE SPANISH MILITARY. SERRA SAID 
THAT A CONSERVATIVE PARTY NOW HELD POWER IN SPAIN, AFTER 
MANY YEARS OF LEFTIST GOVERNMENT, AND SPAIN NOW WANTED A 
NEW ROLE AT THE LEVEL OF THE MILITARY COMNAND STRUCTURE OF 
NATO IN RECOGNITION OF ITS CONTRIBUTIONS IN BOSNIA, THE 
GULF WAR, AND ELSEWHERE. 
5. N,1 AS HE HAD TOLD SECRETARY PERRY THE DAY BEFORE, 
SERRA SAID HE WANTED TO VOICE SPAIN'S OPINION THAT THE 
MAJOR THREAT TO EUROPE WAS SHIFTING FROM THE EAST TO THE 
SOUTH. SPAIN BELIEVES THAT THE MEDITERRANEAN IS BECOMING 
THE STRATEGIC REGION FOR NATO AND PROTECTING EUROPE'S 
SOUTHERN FLANK SHOULD BE OF PRIME CONCERN. THE SITUATION 
IN ALGERIA IS FRAGILE. HE SAW PRESSURE BUILDING IN

6. (r*-ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT SAID HE WAS AWARE OF THE 
IMPORTANCE OF SERRA'S VISIT (THE FIRST OFFICIAL VISIT BY A 
MINISTER OF THE NEW GOVERNMENT), AND OF SPAIN'S 
CONTRIBUTION TO IFOR. HE MENTIONED WORKING WITH JAVIER 
SOLANA BOTH IN HIS FORMER ROLE AS FOREIGN MINISTER AND NOW 
AS SECRETARY GENERAL OF NATO, MOST RECENTLY ON 
RESTRUCTURING AND RELATIONS WITH RUSSIA. TALBOTT 
COMMENTED THAT U.S. -SPAIN BILATERAL RELATIONS WERE 
EXCELLENT AND SAID THAT SPAIN'S ASSISTANCE IN THE 
EVACUATION OF LIBERIA MADE A GRIM TIME SOMEWHAT LESS 
PAINFUL. HE WELCOMED SPAIN'S PLANS TO EQUIP FRIGATES WITH 
THE LOCKHEED AEGIS SYSTEM WHICH WOULD ENSURE A HIGH LEVEL 
OF MILITARY INTERACTION BETWEEN OUR TWO COUNTRIES. 
MINISTER SERRA SIMPLY SMILED AND NODDED AT THIS. 
7. -1`6.1. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID HE WANTED TO REITERATE 
THAT THE U.S. WELCOMED SPAIN'S DESIRE FOR A NEW ROLE IN 
NATO. HOWEVER, BECAUSE WE WERE THINKING LONG-TERM AND 
STRATEGICALLY, HE WOULD LIKE TO TALK MORE ABOUT THE IDEA 
OF FOCUSING NATO'S ATTENTION SOUTHWARD. HE ASKED THE 
MINISTER TO EXPLAIN WHAT CONCRETELY HE MEANT BY HAVING 
NATO LOOK SOUTHWARD. HE ADDED THAT WE WANT TO BE SURE 
THAT SUCH INCREASED ATTENTION WOULD NOT BECOME A 
SELF-FULFILLING PROPHECY, I.E. CONVINCING NORTH AFRICANS 
THAT THEY HAD A NEW ENEMY IN NATO. WE ARE ALREADY HARD AT 
WORK CONVINCING RUSSIA THAT NATO IS NOT A THREAT TO IT'OR 
TO ANY PARTICULAR COUNTRY. MINISTER SERRA SAID THAT HIS 
POINT ABOUT THE SOUTH IS THAT NATO SHOULD STRENGTHEN AND 
REINFORCE THE POSITION OF THE NATO COUNTRIES OF SOUTHERN 
EUROPE. IT IS NOT JUST A LOGISTIC PLATFORM FOR NATO. 
8. "1'6,1. TURNING TO BOSNIA, TALBOTT SAID WE WERE MORE 
OPTIMISTIC THAT ELECTIONS WOULD TAKE PLACE PEACEFULLY, 
ALTHOUGH WE WERE LESS CONFIDENT ABOUT WHO WOULD WIN. 
SEPARATISTS AND THOSE FAVORING PARTITION MAY WIN MANY 
VOTES. THE U.S. IS PAYING MORE ATTENTION TO BOSNIA THAN 
TO ANY OTHER ISSUE, WITH THE EXCEPTION OF IRAQ OVER THE 
LAST FEW DAYS. MINISTER SERRA SAID THAT IN DECEMBER IFOR 
MUST END, BUT THE ALLIES COULD NOT SIMPLY LEAVE. IFOR 
MUST BE REPLACED WITH SOMETHING, SUCH AS TRAINED BOSNIAN

SAID THE COMMENT WAS NOT MEANT AS A CRITICISM BUT ONLY TO 
POINT OUT THE NEED TO BRING ALLIED OFFICIALS AND PUBLICS
