1. SUMMARY: THE SECOND DAY OF THE OSCE BUDAPEST 
MINISTERIAL, DECEMBER 8, WAS DEVOTED TO BOSNIA. ALL 
WHO SPOKE RECOGNIZED THAT BOSNIA WAS A TEST WHICH 
WOULD DETERMINE THE FUTURE OF THE OSCE. THEY 
REAFFIRMED SUPPORT FOR THE DAYTON AGREEMENTS AND 
OSCE'S EFFORTS IN THE FIELDS OF ELECTIONS, HUMAN 
RIGHTS MONITORING AND ARMS CONTROL. MANY ANNOUNCED 
CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE OSCE START UP FUND, AND MANY 
ADDRESSED THE ISSUE OF BELGRADE'S PARTICIPATION IN 
OSCE. SEVERAL DELEGATIONS CALLED FOR CLOSE 
CONSULTATIONS WITH THE PERMANENT COUNCIL IN CONNECTION 
WITH THE BOSNIA MISSION. THE CROATIAN FM CALLED FOR 
AN OSCE MISSION TO CROATIA -- LIMITED TO FORMER SECTOR 
EAST (SEPTEL). THE BOSNIAN FONMIN ADDRESSED THE ISSUE 
OF THE UNITY OF SARAJEVO. ALBANIA CALLED ATTENTION TO 
KOSOVO AND OBJECTED TO (RE)ADMISSION OF THE "FRY." 
RUSSIA IDENTIFIED PRINCIPLES FOR ARMS CONTROL. FRANCE, 
SUPPORTED BY THE NETHERLANDS, SAID A EUROPEAN HEAD OF 
MISSION WOULD BE JUSTIFIED. THE STATEMENT BY DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT IS REPORTED REF A. MINISTERIAL 
DECISION ON BOSNIA IS REPORTED REF B. END SUMMARY. 
RUSSIA 
2. RUSSIAN REP USHAKOV, "ON INSTRUCTIONS FROM FM 
KOZYREV," SAID WITHOUT OSCE IT WAS DOUBTFUL PEACE 
WOULD SUCCEED. THE POLITICAL RESPONSIBILITY OF THE 
PERMANENT COUNCIL FOR OSCE ACTION IN BOSNIA SHOULD BE 
ENSURED. IT WOULD BE COUNTERPRODUCTIVE TO KEEP THE 
FEDERAL REPUBLIC OF YUGOSLAVIA (FRY) OUT OF OSCE. 
EXISTING LEGAL AND PROCEDURAL PROBLEMS COULD BE 
SOLVED; OSCE SHOULD AGREE TO RESC/ND THE PERMANENT 
COMMITTEE DECISIONS OF MAY 12 AND JULY 8, 1992 
(REGARDING SUSPENSION OF "FRY" PARTICIPATION IN OSCE 
SEPTEL). THE LONG DURATION MISSIONS (LDM) TO 
KOSOVO, SANDJAK AND VOJVODINA SHOULD BE RETURNED; 
ALONG WITH MISSIONS TO BOSNIA AND CROATIA, THEY WOULD 
HELP RESOLVE THE HUMAN RIGHTS PROBLEMS.
