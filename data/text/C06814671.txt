1. 4C-* PRIME MINISTER SIMITIS AND FOREIGN MINISTER PAPANDREOU 
BOTH ISSUED STATEMENTS ON APRIL 6 RESPONDING POSITIVELY TO THE 
SERBIAN DECLARATION OF A UNILATERAL TRUCE OVER ORTHODOX EASTER. 
INTENSIVE INTERVENTIONS BY DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND 
AMBASSADOR BURNS MANAGED TO SHIFT THE GREEKS MORE TOWARD THE NATO 
MAINSTREAM VIEW OF THE SERB OFFER, ALTHOUGH THE RESULT WAS 
NOWHERE NEAR THE CATEGORICAL REJECTIONS ANNOUNCED BY THE LEADERS 
OF FRANCE, GERMANY, THE UK, AND THE US. THE GREEK PRESS ALSO 
REPORTED POSITIVELY ON THE SERBIAN DECLARATION. FM PAPANDREOU 
PRESENTED A TOUGHER STATEMENT, MUCH CLOSER TO NATO'S, THE MORNING 
OF APRIL 7. 
2. (U) IN A BRIEF STATEMENT ISSUED LATE ON APRIL 6, PRIME 
MINISTER SIMITIS CALLED THE SERBIAN DECLARATION "A FIRST STEPi" 
ADDING THAT WHAT SHOULD FOLLOW IS AN EFFORT TO OPEN "POLITICAL 
DIALOGUE", WITH YUGOSLAVIA. THE PRIME MINISTER PROPOSED THAT THE 
YUGOSLAV DECLARATION BE EXAMINED BY NATO "WITH THE AIM OF 
ACHIEVING A SATISFACTORY SOLUTION OF THE ENTIRE'PROBLEM." THE 
PRIME MINISTER ALSO STATED THAT BELGRADE MUST NOW "GUARANTEE, 
AMONG OTHERS, THE UNIMPEDED RETURN OF REFUGEES, THE EVACUATION OF 
THE YUGOSLAV ARMY AND PEACE IN KOSOVO THROUGH THE EMPLOYMENT OF 
AN INTERNATIONAL PEACEKEEPING FORCE." 
3. 4E74- INCLUSION OF NATO'S THREE CONDITIONS (RETURN OF 
REFUGEES, WITHDRAWAt OF YUGOSLAV SECURITY FORCES, AND PROTECTION 
PROVIDED BY AN INTERNATIONAL FORCE) IN THE PRIME MINISTER'S 
STATEMENT,CAME ONLY-AFTER TWO INTERVENTIONS WITH FOREIGN MINISTER 
PAPANDREOU BY AMBASSADOR BURNS AND A THREE-WAY CONVERSATION AMONG 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, THE AMBASSADOR, AND THE FOREIGN 
MINISTER. 
4. -tei- PAPANDREOU AND ALTERNATE FM KRANIDIOTIS BOTH ASSURED THE 
AMBASSADOR THAT GREECE WOULD NOT ATTEMPT TO CONDUCT A DIALOGUE 
WITH SERBIA ON ITS TRUCE PROPOSAL. THEY BOTH ASSURED US THAT 
GREECE WILL STAY WITH NATO IN THAT RESPECT. 
(U) FOREIGN MINISTER PAPANDREOU, IN A STATEMENT ISSUED 
.EARLIER ON APRIL 6, NOTED THAT HE HAD STRESSED TO NATO SYG SOLANA 
THAT GREECE WAS INTERESTED IN "UTILIZING ANY POSSIBILITY AND ANY 
OPENING, INCLUDING THIS OPENING BY THE YUGOSLAV GOVERNMENT" TO 
EXAMINE THE POSSIBILITIES FOR A POLITICAL DIALOGUE AND A 
POLITICAL SOLUTION. 
6. (U) SPEAKING ON A RADIO TALK SHOW ON APRIL 7, PAPANDREOU 
ELABORATED A MORE MODIFIED POSITION, STRESSING THAT WHILE THE 
SERBIAN OFFER WAS A FIRST STEP, IT WAS NOT ENOUGH. NOTING THAT 
SYSTEMATIC SERBIAN ETHNIC CLEANSING HAD PRACTICALLY EMPTIED

8. —(-E9— COMMENT. IT IS A MEASURE OF THE GREEK GOVERNMENT'S 
DESPERATION FOR A PEACEFUL SOLUTION OF THE KOSOVO CRISIS THAT 
GREECE WAS THE FIRST, IF NOT ONLY, NATO GOVERNMENT TO JUMP AT THE 
SERBIAN BAIT. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S TIMELY VISIT AND 
INTERVENTION WERE INSTRUMENTAL IN KEEPING THE GREEKS ON MESSAGE 
THIS TIME, BUT WE DOUBT THAT THE SIMITIS GOVERNMENT WILL EVER 
RISE ABOVE BEING A RESTIVE PARTNER IN THIS ENDEAVOR.
