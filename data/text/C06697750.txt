REPORTS OF AN AGREEMENT IN MOSCOW BY PRESIDENT CLINTON 
AND RUSSIAN PRESIDENT YELTSIN ON CERTAIN ASPECTS OF 
CFE. OYMEN REQUESTED THE TEXT OF ANY DRAFT AGREEMENT SO 
THAT THE TURKISH FOREIGN MINISTRY COULD WORK ON THE 
FINAL TEXT. THE ACTING SECRETARY REPLIED THE USG DOES 
NOT REGARD THE PROGRESS MADE IN MOSCOW AS A FINAL DEAL, 
. BUT OTHER AS AN UNDERSTANDING TO RESOLVE THE CFE FLANK 
ISSUE AT THE HIGHEST LEVEL. THE ACTING SECRETARY ADDED 
THE TWO PRESIDENTS AGREED PRIVATELY THAT THE RUSSIAN' 
BLACK SEA PORT OF NOVOROSSISK WOULD NOT BE EXEMPTED FROM 
CFE GEOGRAPHIC RESTRICTIONS. 
- OYMEN.NOTED THIS WAS A BIG BREAKTHROUGH IN THE CFE 
NEGOTIATIONS. THE ACTING SECRETARY AGREED AND ADDED HE 
HAD ALREADY DISCUSSED THIS WITH RUSSIAN DEPUTY FOREIGN 
MINISTER MAMEDOV. THE ACTING SECRETARY RECOUNTED HOW 
YELTSIN ORDERED RUSSIAN DEFENSE MINISTER GRACHEV TO 
REMOVE ALL ARMORED PERSONNEL CARRIERS FROM NOVOROSSISK. 
HE ADDED THE RUSSIAN MILITARY HAD SWALLOWED VERY HARD ON 
THIS ORDER AND THEY WOULD NOW FIGHT ON THE NUMERIC 
RESTRICTIONS UNDER CFE. 
- OYMEN AGAIN REQUESTED THE TEXT OF ANY AGREEMENT AND 
STATED HE WAS CONCERNED ABOUT LOSING MOMENTUM ON THIS 
ISSUE DUE TO THE UPCOMING ISLAMIC HOLIDAYS. THE ACTING 
SECRETARY PROMISED TO SEND FULL INFORMATION ON THE 
PRESIDENTIAL CONVERSATION TO THE TURKISH FOREIGN 
MINISTRY THROUGH OUR EMBASSY IN ANKARA. 
OYMEN THEN TURNED THE CONVERSATION TO TURKISH 
' PARTICIPATION IN RESOLUTION OF THE CURRENT SITUATION IN 
LEBANON. OYMEN PRAISED THE USG INITIATIVE IN THE 
REGION. OYMEN SAID HE WOULD MEET APRIL 23 IN CAIRO WITH 
EGYPTIAN FOREIGN MINISTER MOUSSA AND ADDED THAT TURKEY 
AND EGYPT WERE PREPARED TO COOPERATE WITH EACH OTHER TO 
USE THEIR LEVERAGE IN THE REGION TO HELP BRING ABOUT A 
CEASE-FIRE. THE ACTING SECRETARY THANKED OYMEN FOR HIS 
OFFER AND SAID HE WOULD PASS IT ON TO SECRETARY 
CHRISTOPHER.
