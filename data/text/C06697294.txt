RELEASE IN FULL 
1. CLASSIFIED BY : LEGAL COUNSELOR D. STEPHEN MATHIAS REASON: 1.5(B), (D).) 
2. -fE4-' PER REF, EMBASSY REITERATED TO DEPUTY PROSECUTOR BLEWITT (GOLDSTONE LEFT THE HAGUh 3/30 UNTIL 4/10) USG CONCERNS ABOUT TRANSFER OF KRSMANOVIC TO BOSNIAN AUTHORITIES AND POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCES. BLEWITT REITERATED THAT THE PROSECUTOR HAD INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE TO INDICT KRSMANOVIC, AND THAT UNDER TRIBUNAL RULES THERE IS NO ALTERNATIVE.TO REMANDING KRSMANOVIC TO BOSNIAN AUTHORITIES UNLESS HE EITHER CHANGES HIS MIND AND BEGINS TO COOPERATE AS A WITNESS OR THE BOSNIAN AUTHORITIES TRANMIT AN ORDER FOR HIS RELEASE (RULE 90 BIS (E)) 
3. WITH RESPECT TO GENERAL ISSUE OF RELEASE OF AIL PRISONERS HELD BY THE PARTIES ABSENT TRIBUNAL REVIEW OF THE CHARGES AGAINST THEM, BLEWITT SAID THAT THE PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE WAS OF THE VIEW THAT RELEASE REQUIREMENT SHOULD NOT APPLY TO THOSE ALREADY TRIED AND SENTENCED BY NATIONAL COURTS. HE SAID THAT THE TRIBUNAL WAS NOT PREPARED TO SERVE AS, IN EFFECT, AN APPELLATE TRIBUNAL FOR THOSE CONVICTED IN NATIONAL COURT PROCEEDINGS. HE SAID THAT THE TRIBUNAL HAD RELEASED A PRESS STATEMENT ON THIS POINT. POST WILL FAX THIS TO L/EUR SULZYNSKY AS SOON AS IT IS AVAILABLE. 
4. -K4- TRIBUNAL PRESIDENT CASSESE (PROTECT) INFORMED EMBASSY THAT REGISTRAR WILL WAIT UNTIL MID-AFTERNOON APRIL 1 TO HEAR FROM SHAPE RE TRIBUNAL REQUEST FOR NATO ASSISTANCE IN RETURNING KRSMANOVIC TO SARAJEVO. REGISTRAR THEN INTENDS TO APPROACH DUTCH MFA TO ASK FOR DUTCH ASSISTANCE. CASSESE SAID THAT, IN VIEW OF POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCES OF RETURN, HE WILL ATTEMPT TO ENSURE THAT REGISTRAR DOES NOT SCHEDULE TRANSFER IN ADVANCE OF TRIBUNAL-IMPOSED DEADLINE OF APRIL 3. BLEWITT REQUESTED THAT IF NATO WILL NOT BE PREPARED TO ASSIST IN RETURN OF KRSMANOVIC TO SARAJEVO, IT INFORM TRIBUNAL AS SOON AS POSSIBLE. 
5. (U) DEPARTMENT PLFARE REPEAT TO SARAJEVO, USNATO, BELGRADE, ZAGREB, AND USUN.
