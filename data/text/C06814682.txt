1.164- SUMMARY. FOO MINISTER FOR EUROPE HOON TOLD DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT 10/6 THAT THE UK: 
-.WILL BE FULLY TRANSPARENT WITH THE U.S. ON ESDI AS HMG 
PREPARES FOR THE NOVEMBER 25 ANGLO-FRENCH SUMMIT, BEGINNING 
WITH DAS ASMUS'S OCT. 11 VISIT; 
- "WANTS TO HELP" ON THE NATIONAL MISSILE DEFENSE ISSUE, AND 
WILL WORK OTHER EU MEMBER STATES ON THIS; AND 
-- SHARES OUR VISION THAT EU ENLARGEMENT NEEDS TO BE-AN OPEN 
PROCESS FOR THE COUNTRIES IN EASTERN EUROPE, POTENTIALLY ALSO 
INCLUDING RUSSIA. END SUMMARY. 
2. (U) DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT DISCUSSED ESDI, EU 
ENLARGEMENT, RUSSIA AND NMD WITH FCO MINISTER FOR EUROPE 
GEOFFREY HOON DURING THEIR HOUR-LONG MEETING ON OCTOBER 6 IN 
LONDON. CHARGE GLYN DAVIES, D STAFFERS PHILIP GOLDBERG AND 
JOHN BASS, ACTING POLCOUNS RIES AND ECONCOUNS (NOTETAKER) 
ACCOMPANIED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY. HOON WAS SUPPORTED BY 
DEPUTY POLITICAL DIRECTOR PETER RICKETTS AND HIS PRIVATE 
SECRETARY. 
ESDI - HMG WILL BE FULLY TRANSPARENT 
3. -t6,- THE EUROPEAN SECURITY AND DEFENSE INITIATIVE (ESDI) 
•WAS THE MAIN TOPIC OF THE MEETING. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
EMPHASIZED THE UNITED STATES IS "GENUINELY SUPPORTIVE" OF 
HAVING THE UK "AT THE CENTER OF EUROPE" AND UNDERSTANDS ESDI 
IS A "REVOLUTIONARY ADJUSTMENT" IN UK-EUROPE RELATIONS. WE 
DID NOT WANT ESDI TO BE ZERO-SUMS, IT WOULD NOT BE EFFECTIVE 
IF IT WAS. BUT THE PROCESS NEEDS TO BE FULLY TRANSPARENT TO 
ALLAY CONCERNS WASHINGTON HAS WITH THE VERY REAL, PRACTICAL, 
DETAILS OF MILITARY PREPAREDNESS. BEING TOLD BY THE UK TO 
"TRUST US" SIMPLY ISN'T SUFFICIENT. DEVELOPMENT OF ESDI ALSO 
HAD TO BE FULY COMPATIBLE WITH OUR EFFORTS TO STRENGTHEN NATO 
AND MAINTAIN THE TRANSATLANTIC LINK. 
4.-(-Ej THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THE U.S. WAS CONCERNED BY 
THE OUTCOMES AT ST. MALO AND COLOGNE. THE COLOGNE 
DECLARATION IN PARTICULAR WAS TROUBLING; SOME OF ITS 
CONCLUSIONS APPEARED TO CONTRADICT OR WALK BACK PRINCIPLES 
REAFFIRMED AT THE WASHINGTON SUMMIT. HE CAUTIONED AGAINST 
OVEREMPHASIZING "AUTONOMY." IT CONTRIBUTED TO A POTENTIALLY 
VERY SINISTER FORM OF SUPPORT FOR ESDI IN THE U.S. AMONG 
THOSE WHO FAVORED DECOUPLING. 
5. -(e) HOON STATED CATEGORICALLY THAT HMG WOULD BE FULLY 
OPEN WITH THE U.S. "I'D BE VERY DISTURBED IF YOU DIDN'T KNOW 
WHAT'S GOING ON .. I DO NOT WANT MY NEGOTIATORS TO GO INTO 
MEETINGS WITH THE FRENCH WITHOUT (THE UNITED STATES) 
UNDERSTANDING WHAT WE'RE DOING... IT MUST BE CLEAR YOU'RE 
COMFORTABLE WITH WHAT WE WANT TO ACHIEVE." HE ADDED THAT IF 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY HAD FURTHER CONCERNS HE SHOULD FEEL FREE 
TO "RING ME UP."

TALKING DEFENSE ISSUES WITH THE FRENCH. "IT'S NOT SOMETHING 
WE HAVE DONE BEFORE." ONE OF THE UK'S OBJECTIVES, HOON 
STATED, IS TO BUILD THE SAME SORT OF "COMFORTABLE" 
RELATIONSHIP WITH THE FRENCH AS IT HAS WITH WASHINGTON. 
7. -tC--)-NOTING THE UK WANTS THE HEADLINE OUT OF THE LATE 
NOVEMBER ANGLO-FRENCH SUMMIT TO BE ABOUT CAPABILITIES, DEPUTY 
POLITICAL DIRECTOR RICKETTS PROMISED "MAXIMUM TRANSPARENCY" 
IN THE SIX WEEKS RUNNING UP TO THE SUMMIT, BEGINNING WITH DAS 
RON ASMUS'S VISIT TO LONDON MONDAY, OCTOBER 11. RICKETTS WAS 
FOREWARNED HE'D BE SHOWN "FIVE DISCREPANCIES" BETWEEN THE EU 
COLOGNE COUNCIL STATEMENT AND THE NATO BERLIN AND WASHINGTON 
MEETINGS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WELCOMED FRANK UK COMMENTS ON 
WAYS WASHINGTON MIGHT MORE EFFECTIVELY EXPRESS U.S. CONCERNS 
AS WELL. RICKETTS SUGGESTED THERE ALSO BE A SENIOR UK VISIT 
TO WASHINGTON PRIOR TO THE SUMMIT. 
NMD: "WE WANT TO HELP" 
8. -fel AFTER A LENGTHY OPENING DISCUSSION ABOUT RUSSIA 
(HOON VISITED FOR THREE DAYS IN SEPTEMBER), THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY NOTED THAT NMD (NATIONAL MISSILE DEFENSE) IS A 
"HUGE" ISSUE WITH MOSCOW AND WELCOMED THE UK'S RECENT "VERY 
SENSIBLE" DEMARCHE ON THE SUBJECT. HMG, HOON EMPHASIZED, IS 
CONSCIOUS THE UK TOO COULD BE A VICTIM OF A TERRORIST ATTACK, 
AND INDEED WORRIES IT COULD BE A "SURROGATE" (TARGET) FOR THE 
UNITED STATES. HE NOTED THERE WAS CONSIDERABLE NERVOUSNESS 
IN EUROPE REGARDING THE U.S. INITIATIVE. THE UK THUS "WANTS 
TO HELP" AND RECOGNIZES IT WILL "NEED TO WORK WITH OUR 
EUROPEAN COLLEAGUES" ON THIS. TO THE EXTENT THE U.S. COULD 
PREVENT RUSSIA FROM PROTESTING LOUDLY AND PUBLICLY, THE 
BRITISH WOULD HAVE AN EASIER TIME WITH THE EUROPEANS. THE 
TWO PRINCIPALS AGREED THAT THE U.S. AND UK/EU SHOULD KEEP IN 
CLOSE CONTACT ABOUT DEVELOPMENTS IN RUSSIA IN COMING MONTHS. 
EU ENLARGEMENT - A SHARED LONG-TERM VISION 
9.-(e)-HOON FULLY AGREED WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
COMMENT THAT THE EU NEEDS TO REACH OUT "EVEN FURTHER EAST' OF 
THE ENLARGEMENT COUNTRIES. THE UK, HOON STRESSED, IS "VERY 
KEEN" ON ENLARGEMENT AND SEES IT AS AN ON-GOING PROCESS. IF, 
AS HE HOPES, THE HELSINKI EUROPEAN COUNCIL CONFIRMS THE NEXT 
SIX "PRE-INS" (INCLUDING TURKEY), UKRAINE AND OTHER COUNTRIES 
WILL START TO THINK SERIOUSLY ABOUT THE PROSPECT OF JOINING. 
INDEED, HOON WOULD "LOVE" TO HAVE RUSSIA ASPIRE TO BECOME AN 
EU MEMBER. 
10.-{e)-THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INDICATED HE'D LIKE TO DISCUSS 
NATO ENLARGEMENT AT THEIR NEXT MEETING, NOTING DIFFERENT 
EMPHASIS ON FURTHER ENLARGEMENT, WASHINGTON AND LONDON'S, 
ESPECIALLY WITH RESPECT TO THE BALTICS. 
11. (U) THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S PARTY HAS CLEARED THIS 
MESSAGE.
