RELEASE IN FULL 
Dear Mrs. Kugel (I have to address you that way!): 
Of all the letters that have come in recently (and there 
have been quite a few), none means more to me than yours. It's 
amazing how a few kind words can obliterate the distance of 
years. Your family has figured prominently in my life -- and 
not just because Jim presided at Brooke's and my wedding. I 
miss seeing Jim more often. Please convey that to him next time 
you talk. 
Perhaps in my new job I'll have reason to go to Jerusalem. 
If so, I'll try, perhaps through you, to track him down. Best, 
also, to the girls (I have to refer to them that way!). 
Thanks again for writing. 
Skhcerely, 
,----"t Strobe Talbott 
Mrs. AdelaideCku979 
151 Central Park West, 
New York, NY.
