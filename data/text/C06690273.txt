Dear Nellie: 
It`was a special pleasure getting your letter, coming as it 
did in an envelope with such a pleasing title in front of your 
name. I'm very proud of you indeed. I was also very moved by 
your article for The Episcoplian. I appreciate your comforting 
and supportive words about the job in front of me. Yes, it is a 
bit on the scary side, but I realize that isn't so special: most 
of us have tough jobs in this life -- in fact, perhaps the 
luckies of us do, and I certainly feel lucky in many ways, 
including in having a friend like you plugging for me. 
It pleases me to think of you being with your folks when 
they celebrate their 50th in August. It was certainly fun to be 
with mine when they hit that landmark. 
Give my special best to‘Nancy next time you're in touch with 
her. 
Cheers. 
Sincerely, 
C F
