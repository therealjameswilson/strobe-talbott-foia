2. DURING AN OCTOBER 25 CALL ON MINISTER OF 
DEFENSE/ACTING FOREIGN MINISTER KOFFI, DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT FOCUSSED ON THE ROLE OF ECOWAS AND THE UNITED 
NATIONS IN REGIONAL PEACEKEEPING. HE URGED COTE 
D'IVOIRE TO SUPPORT PRESIDENT RAWLINGS' INITIATIVES TO 
END THE LIBERIAN CONFLICT AND ASKED THE GOCI TO RENEW 
ITS OWN DIPLOMATIC-EFFORTS TO RESOLVE THE CRISIS NEXT 
DOOR. THE USG STOOD READY TO HELP TRAIN A MODEST 
NUMBER OF IVORIAN OFFICERS IN PEACE-KEEPING OPERATIONS, 
THE DEPSEC INFORMED MINISTER KOFFI, AND ALSO RECOGNIZED 
THAT THE INFLUX OF LIBERIAN REFUGEES STRAINED IVORIAN 
RESOURCES. END SUMMARY. 
INCREASING IVORIAN SUPPORT FOR PKO, DIPLOMATIC EFFORTS 
3. IN HIS OCTOBER 25 MEETING WITH DEFENSE 
MINISTER/ACTING FOREIGN MINISTER LEON KONAN KOFFI, 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT RAISED THREE ISSUES, ALL OF 
WHICH CENTERED ON COTE D'IVOIRE'S POTENTIAL INVOLVEMENT 
IN RESOLVING THE LIBERIAN CONFLICT: THE ROLE OF THE UN 
AND ECOWAS IN PEACE-KEEPING OPERATIONS (PKO), RENEWAL 
OF IVORIAN DIPLOMATIC EFFORTS TO END THE LIBERIAN C/VIL 
WAR, AND THE OFFER OF USG TRAINING IN PEACE-KEEPING. 
THE IVORIAN MILITARY AND POLITICAL LEADERSHIP HAD 
RECENTLY BEGUN TO CONSIDER A POSSIBLE ROLE IN PKO, 
OBSERVED THE DEPSEC. THE UNITED NATIONS ACCORDED THE 
SEARCH FOR PEACE GREAT IMPORTANCE; HE NOTED. IN 
ADDITION, FOREIGN MINISTER ESSY'S PRESENCE IN NEW YORK 
TO PRESIDE OVER THE 49TH UNGA UNDERSCORED THE 
IMPORTANCE OE AFRICA IN THAT BODY.

REGIONAL CRISIS RESOLUTION, THE DEPSEC ASKED THAT COTE 
D'IVOIRE SUPPORT GHANAIAN PRESIDENT RAWLINGS' ENDEAVORS 
TO IDENTIFY AN ECOWAS SOLUTION TO THE LIBERIAN 
CONFLICT. THE DEPSEC ALSO SUGGESTED THAT THE GOCI 
"HARNESS THE ENERGY" OF YAMOUSSOUKRO I-IV, THE COTONOU 
ACCORD, AND THE AKOSOMBO AGREEMENT IN ORDER TO 
REVITALIZE ITS OWN DIPLOMATIC PEACEMAKING EFFORTS. ONE 
SHOULD NOT BELIEVE TALK ABOUT FRANCOPHONE/ANGLOPHONE 
WEST AFRICAN DIFFERENCES, COMMENTED THE DEPSEC.
