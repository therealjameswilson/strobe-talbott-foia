February 2, 1994 
Dear Strobe, 
Last Saturday night while watching the Tennis 
Australian Open Finals, I was flicking the TV from one 
station to another, and there you were! 
I just want to say I was very impressed with 
what you had to say about Russia, as well as how you said 
it. There was only one problem; At was twelve o'clock at 
night, and you kept me up beyond my bed time. 
It is very comforting to know that you are as 
involved as you are in some of the most important aspects of ' 
our government's decisions. As you are well aware, I am a 
staunch Republican, but I am also a great supporter of Strobe 
Talbott. 
As a friend, it might be helpful to exchange 
thoughts with the so-called proletariat of our country. 
Anytime you have a chance to do that, either over lunch, 
dinner or telephone, I would be delighted to be,in touch with 
you. 
Kindest regards'-
The Honorable Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador-At-Large 
S/NIS 
Department of State 
Washington, D.C. 20520
