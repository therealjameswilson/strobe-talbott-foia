SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S JULY 30 MEETING WITH DECLASSIFIED 
FOREIGN MINISTER DI TELLA 
TEXT -- CLASSIFIED BY STROBE 1. CONFIDENTIAL ENTIRE 
TALBOTT, REASON 1.5(B). 
2. SUMMARY. ARGENTINE FOREIGN MINISTER DI TELLA TOLD 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT IN A JULY 30 MEETING THAT THE 
TRANSITION FROM ECONOMY MINISTER CAVALLO TO ECONOMY 
MINISTER FERNANDEZ WAS SMOOTH AND SUCCESSFUL. HE STATED 
THAT ARGENTINA'S MERCOSUR PARTNERS REACTED POSITIVELY, AS 
DID THE INTERNATIONAL FINANCIAL COMMUNITY. THE ARGENTINE 
,STOCK MARKET IS STABLE, AND THE ECONOMY IS POISED TO MOVE 
FORWARD, WITH GROWTH ANTICIPATED FOR THE SECOND SEMESTER 
1996. DI TELLA RAISED CONCERNS OVER PERCEIVED 
DETERIORATION IN THE LATIN AMERICAN-U.S. RELATIONSHIP 
OCCASIONED BY U.S. POLICIES TOWARD CUBA AND COLOMBIA. 
THE GOA WILL SEEK TO ENGAGE THE U.S. ON THESE ISSUES IN 
EARLY 1997. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STATED THAT REPORTS OF 
THE SMOOTH TRANSITION AT THE ECONOMIC MINISTRY TRACK OUR 
ANALYSIS OF THE SITUATION. HE EXTENDED AN INVITATION FOR 
MINISTER FERNANDEZ TO VISIT WASHINGTON. THE DgPSEC 
REMARKED THAT THE U.S. WELCOMES THE OPPORTUNITY TO 
CONSULT WITH THE 'GOA ON HEMISPHERIC AND GLOBAL ISSUES At 
THE EARLIEST APPROPRIATE TIME. END SUMMARY. 
3. FM DI TELLA OPENED THE MEETING BY DISCUSSING THE 
RESIGNATION OF MINISTER DOMINGO CAVALLO AND THE NAMING OF 
MINISTER ROQUE FERNANDEZ AS HIS REPLACEMENT. THE 
PRESIDENT HAD DECIDED THAT IT WAS NO LONGER POSSIBLE TO 
CONTINUE WITH CAVALLO; HIS RELATIONSHIP WITH CONGRESS HAD 
DETERIORATED AND NO LAWS EMERGING FROM THE ECONOMY 
MINISTRY WERE BEING APPROVED. THE PERCEIVED DEPENDENCY 
OF ARGENTINA ON CAVALLO WAS NOT HEALTHY. THE TRANSITION 
WAS HANDLED IN A MANNER TO GUARANTEE CONTINUITY; CAVALLO 
WAS PRESENT AT FERNANDEZ' SWEARING-IN AND RECEIVED A LONG 
OVATION FOLLOWING PRAISE BY PRESIDENT MENEM. MINISTER 
FERNANDEZ IS MORE ORTHODOX THAN CAVALLO, AND HIS POLICIES 
MAY IN FACT BE MORE CONSERVATIVE AS HE SEEKS TO PROVE 
HIMSELF. FERNANDEZ' STAFF IS WELL KNOWN AND WELL 
REGARDED BY. THE INTERNATIONAL FINANCIAL COMMUNITY.

SMOOTH AND SUCCESSFUL TRANSITION TRACK WITH OUR 
INTERPRETATION OF EVENTS. HE INVITED MINISTER FERNANDEZ 
TO VISIT WASHINGTON ONCE ACCOMMODATED IN HIS NEW POST.

5. DI TELLA REMARKED THAT IN A MORE OPPORTUNE MOMENT --
PERHAPS IN EARLY 1997 FOLLOWING U.S. ELECTIONS -- THE GOA 
WOULD LIKE TO ENGAGE THE U.S. ON THE ISSUES OF CUBA AND 
COLOMBIA. IT IS THE ARGENTINE IMPRESSION THAT THERE HAS 
BEEN A DETERIORATION IN U.S.-LATIN AMERICAN RELATIONS AS 
A RESULT OF U.S. POLICIES TOWARD THESE COUNTRIES. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED HOW MUCH OF THE PROBLEM IS A 
RESULT OF HELMS-BURTON. DI TELLA REPLIED THAT IT 
CONTRIBUTED SIGNIFICANTLY. COLOMBIA IS A NEW AND SERIOUS 
ISSUE. ARGENTINA ATTEMPTED TO LOWER THE TONE OF THE RIO 
GROUP DECLARATION RESPONDING TO THE REVOCATION OF 
SAMPER'S VISA, AND SUCCEEDED PARTIALLY. 
6. THE DEPSEC STATED THAT THE U.S. ACCEPTED ARGENTINA'S 
SUGGESTION. WE WILL CONSULT WITH THE GOA, AND DI TELLA 
PERSONALLY, ON HEMISPHERIC AND GLOBAL ISSUES AT THE 
EARLIEST APPROPRIATE TIME.
