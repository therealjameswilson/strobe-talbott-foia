WASHINGTON

Chris: 
I finally have reason to be glad you 
thrust me into this job --'or at least into this 
office. (Just kidding!) I actually got out and 
ran today, taking full advantage afterward of the

used to do the same. I derive more than a little 
satisfaction from all this. Now, back to work.
