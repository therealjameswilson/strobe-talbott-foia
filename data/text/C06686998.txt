Dear Mr. Talbott: 
The World Affairs Council officers and directors cordially invite you to address a major meeting of the council 
in your honor and at your convenience. 
Council programs are generally in the early evening, with a reception followed by the guest speakers presentation 
and a discussion period. Press interviews may be arranged prior to the program. An informal, invitational 
dinner with council sponsors at a diplomat member's home, would follow the formal program. 
Our World Affairs Council is in its 28th season, presenting programs on international and national issues. The 
membership of about 1,000 includes leaders of business, academic, government and civic affairs in the region 
from Claremont and Pomona through Riverside and San Bernardino Counties. Students from area colleges and 
universities are also invited to hear our speakers. The Council is a nonpartisan, nonprofit educational 
organization similar to World Affairs Councils in Los Angeles, San Francisco and other major cities. 
Speakers have included senior diplomats and officials of the United States and other nations, as well as scholars, 
business leaders and journalists. 
Because of the rapidly changing international scene, I would not even try to predict what subject area you might 
want to discuss when you are with us. However, whatever your choice, any topic specific to a major international 
situation would be both enlightening and insightful. 
Thank you so much for considering our invitation. I know you have many requests, however, I hope you will 
accept our invitation as we would be pleased to be your hosts. 
Sincerely,
Marylin Jacobsen
