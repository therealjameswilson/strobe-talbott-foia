SUBJECT: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S JANUARY 13 MEETING WITH 
RUSSIAN DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER MASLYUKOV 
DECLASSIFIED 
CLASSIFIED BY S/NIS, ROSS WILSON, REASON 1.5(D). 
1. (-E4 SUMMARY: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY MET ON JANUARY 13, 
1999 WITH RUSSIAN FIRST DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER YURIY 
MASLYUKOV. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF 
A REALISTIC GOVERNMENT BUDGET TO FURTHER IMF SUPPORT FOR 
RUSSIA'S ECONOMY. MASLYUKOV COUNTERED THAT RUSSIA IS DOING 
ALL IT CAN TO CLOSE THE BUDGET GAP, AND THAT ANY FURTHER 
CUTS IN EXPENDITURES OR TAX INCREASES ARE LIKELY TO CAUSE A 
"SOCIAL EXPLOSION". HE PLEADED WITH THE USG TO HELP FIND A 
WAY FOR FOR RUSSIA TO OBTAIN HELP TO PAY ITS MASSIVE 
INTERNATIONAL DEBTS COMING DUE IN 1999. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TOLD MASLYUKOV THAT THE USG WANTS TO HELP RUSSIA, 
AND THAT HE BELIEVES RUSSIA CAN REDUCE ITS PROJECTED 
DEFICIT AND OBTAIN SUPPORT IF IT WORKS VERY HARD WITH THE 
IMF. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THE 
COMMITMENTS TO STRONG ENFORCEMENT OF EXPORT CONTROLS 
CONTAINED IN MASLYUKOV'S RECENT ARTICLE ON THE SUBJECT. 
HOWEVER, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD MASLUYKOV, THE GOR MUST 
SHOW THAT IT IS TAKING CONCRETE STEPS TO STOP TRANSFERS OF 
WEAPONS TECHNOLOGY TO IRAN AND OTHER ROGUE STATES. END 
SUMMARY.

DESPITE THE POLITICAL DIFFICULTIES, IF THE BUDGETARY 
ARITHMETIC DOES NOT ADD UP, THERE IS NOTHING THE IMF CAN DO 
TO SUPPORT RUSSIA.

SAID, IT IS NECESSARY TO SEE -1CONCRETE, VERIFIABLE 
INFORMATION" THAT THE VIOLATIONS OF RUSSIAN AND AMERICAN 
LAW HAVE STOPPED.

GOR'S BEST EFFORTS TO CLOSE THE BUDGET GAP, HE SAID, RUSSIA

THE GOVERNMENT'S ACTIONS ARE RIGHT AND TO GAIN THE SUPPORT
