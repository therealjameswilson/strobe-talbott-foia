During his most recent visit to Washington (April 17-21), Minister Boris Saltykov, the 
Minister of Science and Technology Policy, strongly endorsed CERDP at a meeting 
with John Gibbons. He had an appointment with you which unfortunately you had 
to cancel at the last minute. Subsequent to his Washington meetings we were 
informed by the Deputy Minister Zurab Yakobashvili that the Russian Ministry of 
Science was prepared to offer some "partial" funding support for CERDP if the U.S. 
would agree to go forward. 
In January of this year ARPA agreed to be the government agency that would 
administer and perform the contracting for the U.S Department of State for CERDP. 
We would like to ask ARPA to contact you about this pending your review of 
CERDP. SAIC believes that CERDP is the kind of program that is "on the ground 
and visible to the Russian people", that you said in your luncheon address was going 
to be an important criteria for the Clinton Administration's Russian support. 
William Chadsey (SAIC Group Senior Vice President, and architect of the CERDP 
concept) and I would like to meet with you to discuss the merits of CERDP. We are 
most eager to present to you what we believe is a blueprint for how defense 
conversion and privatization can be turned from policy into action in a way that 
insures that taxpayer dollars will be wisely spent to help stabilize the Russian 
economy and create, hopefully, a new ally and economic partner.
