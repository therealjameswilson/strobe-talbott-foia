Dear Senator Dodd: 
RELEASE IN FULL 
Thank you for your letter forwarding to my attention a 
report prepared by your constituent, William Dunkerley of New 
Britain, Connecticut. That report, "The Role of Newspapers and 
Magazines in Russian Economic Reform," is especially helpful in 
describing the issues involved during this period of 
transition. It certainly makes the point well that without 
marketing, distribution, and advertising skills the Russian 
"free press" may die aborning. In fact, we have already 
included a request for funding activities addressing these 
issues in our fiscal year '94 proposal now before Congress, 
under the Independent Media Initiative. 
Ambassador Simons' office is aware of the report and will 
be working with A.I.D. to ensure that periodical publishing 
reform is addressed appropriately in the upcoming fiscal year 
programs. Thank you for your interest in providing assistance 
to the NIS. 
The Honorable 
Christopher J. Dodd, 
United States Senator. 
Sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott
