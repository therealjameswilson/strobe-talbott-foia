Dear Jim: 
Thanks for your letter. I'm afraid all I can 
do is mention, very informally, to Mamedov that I 
know of your project and think it sounds like a 
worthy venture. I can't frankly, see writing him a 
letter on the subject. I've just got too much 
official business I'm doing with him for that to be 
profitable or even entirely appropriate. But he'll 
be in town this week to work with me on preparations 
for the Summit, so I'll have this all much in mind 
when I see him. 
I just don't know about the Musgrove meeting. 
My new job will probably make it harder to get free, 
but we'll see. 
Best regards.
