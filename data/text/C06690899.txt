RELEASE IN FULL 
Dear Mr. President: 
I was deeply touched by your kind letter 
about my appointment. Let me assure you that I 
will make it one of my highest priorities to 
consolidate the excellent relations between our 
countries and, as part and parcel of that goal, 
to make sure that the US uses its influence to 
enhance Estonia's security and prosperity. 
I, too, hope that I will have a chance to 
visit Tallinn, a city that I associate with hope, 
bravery and, thanks to you, with friendship. 
Happy New Year! 
Sincerely,
