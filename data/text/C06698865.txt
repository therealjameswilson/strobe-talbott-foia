THE VICE PRESIDENT: THANKS. LET ME GET TO ONE IMPORTANT POINT 
ON NATO ENLARGEMENT. IN MY MEETING WITH CHERNOMYRDIN, HE 
REFERRED TO FRANCE AS SUPPORTING HIS VIEW THAT THERE NOT BE A 
PARALLEL PROCESS OF NATO ENLARGEMENT AND DEEPENING OF NATO-
RUSSIA RELATIONS, BUT A SEQUENTIAL STRUCTURE -- I.E., THAT 
NATO-RUSSIA RELATIONS MUST PRECEDE NATO ENLARGEMENT. 
WE HAVE FELT FROM THE BEGINNING THAT A SEQUENTIAL APPROACH 
WOULD GIVE THE RUSSIANS VETO POWER. THE CENTRAL 
EUROPEANS, WHO ARE DESPERATE TO JOIN NATO, WOULD BE — 
OPPOSED. THAT IS WHY ALL OF US CONCLUDED THAT THE TWO 
PROCESSES SHOULD PROCEED IN PARALLEL. OVER THE NEXT SIX OR 
SEVEN MONTHS, WE SHOULD ENGAGE IN AN INTENSIVE EFFORT TO 
DEEPEN AND FORMALIZE THE NATO-RUSSIA RELATIONSHIP. BUT 
WE SHOULD NOT GIVE THE IMPRESSION THAT THERE IS ANY SUPPORT 
WITHIN NATO FOR PROCEEDING IN A DIFFERENT WAY -- ONE THAT 
WOULD REQUIRE MOSCOW TO BE SATISFIED WITH THE TERMS OF THE 
NATO-RUSSIA RELATIONSHIP BEFORE NATO WOULD HAVE THE 
RIGHT TO TAKE IN NEW MEMBERS. I MENTION THIS BECAUSE YOU, 
THE PRESIDENT, HELMUT KOHL AND JOHN MAJOR ALL NEED TO WORK 
INTENSIVELY WITH YELTSIN AND CHERNOMYRDIN TO HELP RUSSIA 
THROUGH THIS PERIOD. SOMEHOW CHERNOMYRDIN IS GETTING THE 
MISTAKEN IMPRESSION THAT FRANCE SUPPORTS THE RUSSIAN VIEW 
ON A SEQUENTIAL APPROACH. SO I ASK YOU, WHEN YOU SEE AN 
APPROPRIATE OCCASION, TO CORRECT HIS MISIMPRESSION AND TO 
CONFIRM THAT WE ARE ALL UNITED. WE ALSO NEED TO MAKE CLEAR 
THAT NATO DOES NOT SEE RUSSIA AS AN ENEMY. NATO 
ENLARGEMENT IS NOT'MEANT AS A HOSTILE MOVE TOWARD RUSSIA S 
BORDERS, BUT IS AN EFFORT TO INTRODUCE A NEW ELEMENT OF 
STABILITY IN A REGION IN WHICH RUSSIA HAS AN INTEREST IN 
STABILITY. WE SHOULD PROCEED AS WE DID IN BOSNIA, ENSURING 
THAT THE RUSSIANS UNDERSTAND THAT NATO S PURPOSE IS 
DIFFERENT THAN IN THE PAST. NATO ENLARGEMENT IS NOT TAKING 
PLACE OUT OF FEAR OF AGGRESSION. 
PRESIDENT CHIRAC: FIRST, LET ME SAY THAT I NEVER TOLD 
CHERNOMYRDIN THAT HE HAD A RIGHT OF VETO. SECOND, OVER THE 
PAST 18 MONTHS I HAVE TOLD BILL CLINTON SOMETHING THAT I WILL 
REPEAT TO YOU. YELTSIN S AND CHERNOMYRDIN S POSITIONS ON 
NATO ENLARGEMENT CANNOT BUT GET HARDER AS TIME GOES BY. THE 
PROBLEM IS NOT THE POSITIONS OF RUSSIA S POLITICAL LEADERS 
(WHICH ARE ALWAYS NEGOTIABLE) BUT THE STRENGTH OF RUSSIAN 
PUBLIC OPINION. POPULAR RESISTANCE TO NATO ENLARGEMENT RUNS 
DEEP. I AM OFTEN STRUCK BY HOW SOME IN THE WEST ACT AS IF 
RUSSIAN PUBLIC OPINION DOESN T EVEN.EXIST. YELTSIN AND 
CHERNOMYRDIN ARE IN A FRAGILE SITUATION. IF THEY ACCEPT A 
POSITION THAT IS CONTRARY TO PUBLIC OPINION, OR IF THEY IMPOSE 
AN UNSATISFACTORY RESULT ON THE RUSSIAN PUBLIC, THEY WILL BE 
IN A VERY DIFFICULT SITUATION. YOU SHOULD TAKE ONE OF YOUR 
RUSSIAN-SPEAKING ASSISTANTS (NOT SOMEONE FROM THE STATE 
DEPARTMENT) TO MOSCOW OR ST. PETERSBURG AND HAVE HIM TALK 
WITH ORDINARY PEOPLE AND BABUSHKI. YOU WOULD BE SURPRISED 
BY THE REPORT THAT HE WOULD SEND BACK. IF YOU WALK ALONG THE 
STREETS OF RUSSIA, YOU ARE TOLD THAT "WE DID NOT VOTE FOR 
YELTSIN, BECAUSE HE HAS HUMILIATED RUSSIA." THEY ASK WHAT 
YELTSIN WILL DO WHEN AMERICAN BOMBS START DROPPING ON 
RUSSIAN CITIES. I ACTUALLY HEARD THESE THINGS. THAT IS 
RUSSIAN PUBLIC OPINION, ANDYOU CANNOT CONFUSE THAT WITH 
THE POSITIONS OF THE HEADS OF STATE OR GOVERNMENT. THE 
REGIME IS VERY FRAGILE AND WE NEED TO BE CAREFUL ABOUT 
STIMULATING RUSSIAN NATIONALISM. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: WE HAVE A SLIGHTLY DIFFERENT ASSESSMENT. 
WE AGREE THAT PUBLIC OPINION IS A VERY POWERFUL FACTOR IN THE 
RUSSIAN STATE. DEMOCRACY THERE IS IMPERFECT, BUT VERY ROBUST. 
WE RESPECT RUSSIAN PUBLIC OPINION AND, IN FACT, HAVE CONDUCTED 
OUR OWN OPINION POLLS. THOSE POLLS SHOW THAT CONCERN OVER 
NATO ENLARGEMENT IS LESS STRONG AMONG THE PUBLIC THAN 
CONFIDENTIAL

I BELIEVE IT IS POSSIBLE TO HANDLE THIS ISSUE IN A WAY THAT WOULD MAKE NATO ENLARGEMENT ACCEPTABLE IN PARALLEL WITH A NEW STATEMENT OF PURPOSE BY NATO AND A NEW DEEPENING OF ITS RELATIONSHIP WITH RUSSIA. 
PRESIDENT CHIRAC: I FULLY AGREE. THAT IS ALSO MY POSITION. THAT IS WHY I PROPOSED THAT THE NATO SUMMIT BE POSTPONED UNTIL JULY, IN ORDER TO GIVE A LITTLE MORE TIME TO WORK THINGS OUT WITH THE RUSSIANS. THE SUMMIT WOULD INVOLVE TWO DAYS. ON THE FIRST DAY, WE WOULD FOCUS ON REFORM OF THE ALLIANCE AMONG THE 16 MEMBERS. ON THE SECOND DAY, WE WOULD FIRST HAVE AN ACCORD WITH THE RUSSIANS AND THEN A SESSION WITH ALL THE PARTNERS ON ENLARGEMENT, WITHOUT ANY RIGHT OF VETO FOR ANYONE. BUT WE STILL HAVE TO BE CAREFUL ABOUT THE SITUATION INSIDE RUSSIA. I AM NOT QUITE SURE YOUR ASSESSMENT OF RUSSIAN PUBLIC OPINION IS RIGHT. WE ARE CONSCIOUS OF THE STRONG"DEMAND OF THE CENTRAL AND EAST EUROPEAN COUNTRIES FOR NATO MEMBERSHIP. THEY ARE OUR FRIENDS, AFTER ALL. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: CAN WE ANNOUNCE AT THE NAC MINISTERIAL THAT THE SUMMIT WILL TAKE PLACE IN JULY? 
PRESIDENT CHIRAC: NO PROBLEM.

PRESIDENT CHIRAC: I PROPOSED THIS SCHEME TO HIM LAST WEEK BUT HE REJECTED IT, SAYING IT IS UNACCEPTABLE. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: BUT HE MAY HAVE RECEIVED .THE IMPRESSION THAT FRANCE WAS EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR THE SEQUENTIAL APPROACH. EVEN THOUGH YOU ONLY SUGGESTED THIS IN TERMS OF THE TWO-DAY SUMMIT MEETING, HE MAY HAVE INTERPRETED THIS AS APPLYING TO THE WHOLE PROCESS. 
PRESIDENT CHIRAC: I DON T THINK THAT THIS WAS THE CASE. I WAS STRUCK BY HOW FIRM CHERNOMYRDIN S POSITION WAS. WHEN I MADE MY PROPOSAL FOR A TWO-DAY SUMMIT, HE GOT ANGRY, REJECTING THE IDEA THAT NATOEXPANSION COULD BE IMPOSED ON RUSSIA. CHERNOMYRDIN EXPLAINED HOW HE HAD VISITED A WEAPONS FACTORY THAT WAS LOSING MONEY. THE MANAGER OF THE FACTORY HAD TOLD HIM THAT, WITH NATO,ENLARGEMENT, HE WOULD BE IN A POSITION TO RESUME FULL ARMS PRODUCTION AS SOON AS POSSIBLE. (AT THIS POINT THE DISCUSSION WAS INTERRUPTED AS THE VICE PRESIDENT AND PRESIDENT CHIRAC JOINED OTHER OSCE LEADERS FOR THE FAMILY PHOTO FOR THE SUMMIT.), 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: WE WANT TO SOLVE THE PROBLEM OF NATO ADAPTATION IN A WAY THAT WOULD PERMIT FRANCE TO REINTEGRATE. WHATEVER THE RESULTS OF THIS MAY BE, WE WANT OUR BILATERAL RELATIONSHIP TO IMPROVE. THE INFORMAL PEACE TREATY BETWEEN TONY LAKE AND JEAN-DAVID LEVITTE HAS_BEEN WORKING, BUT MAINLY ON OUR SIDE. THERE HAVE BEEN A FEW CONTINUING TRANSGRESSIONS ON THE PART OF SOME FRENCH SPOKESMEN. 
PRESIDENT CHIRAC: SOME DIFFERENCES ARE.UNAVOIDABLE, BUT I WILL ASK JEAN-DAVID LEVITTE TO BE IN TOUCH WITH TONY LAKE EVERY DAY TO AVOID MISUNDERSTANDINGS.
