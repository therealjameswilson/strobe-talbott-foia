ACTING SECRETARY OF STATE TALBOTT TELEPHONEDPRESIDENT MASIRE OF BOTSWANA THIS 
AFTERNOON TO DISCUSS USG VIEWS ON A NEW UNSYG AND TO ENGAGE MASIRE IN 
DISCUSSION RE HIS GOVERNMENT' VIEWS ONTHE SAME. MASIRE WAS IN A LISTENING 
MODE AS HE ADVISED THAT HE HAD NOT BEEN BRIEFED ON THE ISSUE. ST REVIEWED OUR 
SENSITIVITY TO THE ISSUE OF AN AFRICAN CANDIDACY, AND STRESSED OUR PREFERENCE 
THAT OAU MEMBER STATES REFRAIN FROM ENDORSING ANY UNSYG CANDIDATE AT THE 
UPCOMING OAU MEETING. 
IN TALBOTT'S SUBSEQUENT TELEPHONE CONVERSATION WITH ZIMBABWE'S PRESIDENT 
MUGABE, THE LATTER NOTED THAT ZIMBABWE HAD "NO PROBLEM" WITH BBG BUT WOULD 
KEEP AN OPEN MIND ON THE SUBJECT. MUGABE BALKED AT THE PROSPECT OF THE OAU. 
NOT ENDORSING A CANDIDATE FOR THE POSITION OF UNSYG AT THE UPCOMING OAU 
MEETING AND SUGGESTED THAT AN INFORMAL SOUNDING OF OAU MEMBER STATES SUGGESTED 
THAT THE MAJORITY OF MEMBERS WERE IN FAVOR OF A SECOND TERM FOR BBG.
