OF STATE. REASON: 1.5(B) AND (D). 
2. N.LSUMMARY: ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT CONVEYED TO 
BWANALI USG CONCERN ABOUT THE PROSPECT OF DEVELOPING 
RELATIONS BETWEEN MALAWI AND LIBYA. THE ACTING SECRETARY 
ASKED BWANALI TO CONVEY OUR STRONG VIEWS TO PRESIDENT 
MULUZI AND THE CABINET. BWANALI AGREED TO DO SO. END 
SUMMARY. 
3. -1-6.). THE ACTING SECRETARY MET SEPTEMBER 5 WITH MALAWI 
MINISTER OF IRRIGATION AND WATER DEVELOPMENT (AND FORMER 
FOREIGN MINISTER) EDWARD BWANALI. BWANALI WAS ACCOMPANfED 
BY AMBASSADOR TO THE U.S. WILLIE CHOKANI; THE ACTING 
SECRETARY BY AF/S DIRECTOR BLANEY, MALAWI DESK OFFICER 
GATTO (NOTETAKER), AND D SPECIAL ASSISTANT SULLIVAN. 
4.-444_ AFTER OPENING PLEASANTRIES, THE ACTING SECRETARY 
EXPRESSED U.S. CONCERN ABOUT THE PROSPECT OF DEVELOPING 
RELATIONS BETWEEN MALAWI AND LIBYA. NOTING THE VISIT OF A 
LIBYAN DELEGATION TO LILONGWE IN AUGUST, THE ACTING 
SECRETARY CONVEYED OUR STRONG CONVICTION THAT THE TRIPOLI 
REGIME IS IRRESPONSIBLE, SUPPORTIVE OF TERRORISM, AND DOES.
NOT PLAY A CONSTRUCTIVE ROLE IN AFRICA. 
5. 4et BWANALI RESPONDED THAT THE MULUZI GOVERNMENT'S 
CONTACTS WITH LIBYA HAD BEGUN AS A RESULT OF ITS EFFORTS 
TO MEDIATE THE DISPUTE BETWEEN UGANDA AND SUDAN. HE 
DESCRIBED CONTACTS BETWEEN MULUZI AND HIMSELF WITH LIBYAN 
REPRESENTATIVES IN KAMPALA AND AT THE U.N. IN CONNECTION 
WITH THIS EFFORT. TURNING TO THE LATEST CONTACTS, BWANALI 
SAID THAT LIBYA IS "REPRESENTED EVERYWHERE" IN SOUTHERN 
AFRICA EXCEPT IN MALAWI. AS PART OF THE ASSESSMENT OF 
MALAWI'S FOREIGN RELATIONS AFTER THE BANDA REGIME, THE GOM 
HAD DETERMINED THAT MALAWI SHOULD DEVELOP "NEW FRIENDS" --
E.G., THE NORDICS AND EGYPT. HOWEVER, THE GOM WAS "NOT IN 
A HURRY" TO TALK WITH LIBYA,‘HE SAID. 
6. 0 IN AUGUST, A LIBYAN DELEGATION (THAT ALSO VISITED 
OTHER AFRICAN COUNTRIES, BWANALI NOTED) HAD VISITED MALAWI 
TO EXTEND THE INVITATION FOR MULUZI TO ATTEND THE 
SEPTEMBER 1 CELEBRATIONS IN TRIPOLI. THE FOREIGN AFFAIRS 
RELEASE IN FULL

8. 'TS. BWANALI THEN TOOK THE OPPORTUNITY TO DESCRIBE THE

BOYCOTT. NOTING THAT THE SUBJECT HAD BEEN RAISED IN 
VIRTUALLY ALL OF HIS WASHINGTON MEETINGS, BWANALI ASSURED 
THEaACTING SECRETARY THAT THE GOM AND THE OPPOSITION WOULD 
"WORK IT OUT." STABILITY IN MALAWI IS ASSURED, HE SAID. 
TURNING TO CORRUPTION, HE NOTED THE FORMATION OF THE 
ANTI-CORRUPTION BUREAU AS EVIDENCE OF THE GOM'S COMMITMENT 
TO INVESTIGATE ABUSES. 
9. 04-BWANALI APPEALED FOR CONTINUED U.S. SUPPORT. THE 
ACTING SECRETARY EXPRESSED U.S. ADMIRATtON FOR THE 
GON'S EFFORTS TO BRING DEMOCRACY AND PROSPERITY TO MALAWI 
AND ASSURED BWANALI THAT THE U.S. WILL CONTINUE TO "STAND 
BY YOU." 
10.'S.I,LCOMMENT: THE ACTING SECRDTARY HAD MET BWANALI 
DURING HIS OCTOBER 1994 VISIT TO MALAWI. THIS 30-MINUTE 
RENEWAL OF THEIR ACQUAINTANCE WAS CORDIAL AND PRODUCTIVE. 
ALTHOUGH BWANALI WAS SOMEWHAT DEFENSIVE ON THE SUBJECT OF 
LIBYA, HE TOOK FULLY ON BOARD THE ACTING SECRETARY'S 
STRONG MESSAGE AND READ CAREFULLY THE TEXTS OF THE UNSCR 
RESOLUTIONS WE PROVIDED. THE ACTING SECRETARY WAS CLEAR 
ABOUT U.S. CONCERNS, AND WE TRUST BWANALI AND CHOKANI WILL 
RELAY OUR VIEWS TO MULUZI AND HIS COLLEAGUES. END COMMENT.
