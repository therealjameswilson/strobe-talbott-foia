2. -(e.)- IN A MARCH 10 DISCUSSION, BRITISH POLITICAL 
DIRECTOR EMYR JONES PARRY SUGGESTED IT MIGHT BE BETTER THAT 
THE ALBANIAN KOSOVARS HAD NOT YET SIGNED UP TO AN AGREEMENT. 
OTHERWISE, IT WOULD BE DIFFICULT TO COAX THEIR ACCEPTANCE OF 
ANY LAST-MINUTE ADJUSTMENTS REQUIRED TO BRING THE SERBS ON 
BOARD SHOULD BELGRADE ACTUALLY DECIDE TO NEGOTIATE 
SERIOUSLY. BRITISH FOREIGN SECRETARY COOK HAD HEARD 
POSSIBLY ENCOURAGING SIGNALS FROM FM IVANOV ON KFOR 
PARTICIPATION, BUT IT WOULD REMAIN DIFFICULT TO PREDICT 
WHETHER RUSSIA WOULD ACTUALLY PARTICIPATE. JONES PARRY WAS 
OPTIMISTIC THE UK COULD DEVISE A FORMULA FOR NON-EU ALLIES' 
PARTICIPATION IN ESDI WHICH WOULD PERMIT ENDORSEMENT OF 
BASIC PRINCIPLES AT THE WASHINGTON SUMMIT. FORMAL NATO/EU 
LINKS WOULD HAVE TO WAIT UNTIL THE EU APPOINTED A "MR. 
CFSP." HE INDICATED THE UK BASICALLY COULD ACCEPT THE U.S. 
OPEN DOOR LANGUAGE AND SUGGESTED FCO PERMANENT UNDER-
SECRETARY KERR WAS BEING "TOO THEOLOGICAL" IN OPPOSING 
NAMING ALL THE ASPIRANTS. JONES PARRY EXPECTED MANDATES TO 
REMAIN AN ISSUE FOR AWHILE. HE WAS OPTIMISTIC (THOUGH FCO 
DEFENSE DIR MANNING WAS LESS SO) THAT THE GERMANS HAD FALLEN 
OFF THEIR PUSH FOR SUMMIT BLESSING OF SUBSEQUENT ALLIANCE 
DISCUSSION OF NUCLEAR ISSUES. END SUMMARY. 
3. 4c..)- KOSOVO. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD BRITISH 
POLITICAL DIRECTOR EMYR JONES PARRY MARCH 10 THAT THE 
ABSENCE OF A KOSOVO SETTLEMENT AND CONTINUATION OF THE 
CURRENT SITUATION ON THE GROUND WOULD CAST A PALL OVER THE 
NATO SUMMIT; A "NO/NO" RESPONSE FROM THE ALBANIAN KOSOVARS 
AND THE SERBS WOULD PRESENT US WITH A QUANDARY. JONES PARRY 
REPEATED HIS ARGUMENT FROM THE MARCH 9 CLOSE ALLIES' POL 
DIRS MEETING (REF A) THAT IT MIGHT BE BETTER THAT THE 
ALBANIAN KOSOVARS HAD NOT YET SIGNED UP TO AN AGREEMENT. 
OTHERWISE, IT WOULD BE DIFFICULT TO COAX THEIR ACCEPTANCE OF 
ANY LAST-MINUTE ADJUSTMENTS REQUIRED TO BRING THE SERBS ON 
BOARD SHOULD BELGRADE ACTUALLY DECIDE TO NEGOTIATE 
SERIOUSLY.

HAD STARTED OFF TELLING THE RUSSIANS THAT, IF THEY COMMITTED 
NOW TO JOINING KFOR, IT WOULD BE EASIER TO PERSUADE THE 
SERBS TO ACCEPT THE FORCE. RUSSIAN FM IVANOV HAD TOLD COOK

' 5. ESDI NONDISCRIMINATION. JONES PARRY BRIEFED THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY ON THE RESULTS OF THAT MORNING'S CLOSE 
ALLIES' PETERSBERG MEETING (REF B). JONES PARRY SAID HMG 
HOPED TO BE IN A POSITION TO OFFER SPECIFIC LANGUAGE (FOR 
THE NATO SUMMIT COMMUNIQUE) OUTLINING CORE PRINCIPLES TO 
GUIDE FURTHER DEVELOPMENT OF ESDI IN THE FOURTH WEEK OF 
MARCH. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THE RISING TIDE OF ' 
APPREHENSION AMONG NON-EU ALLIES OVER UK-FRENCH PROPOSALS 
FOR FURTHER ESDI DEVELOPMENT, A GROUP THAT WOULD SOON GROW 
WITH THE INVITEES' ACCESSION. JONES PARRY SAID THE UK 
WANTED TO SETTLE THIS BEFORE THE SUMMIT. HE EXPECTED THE 
TURKS WOULD BE THE PROBLEM; THE UK HAD KEPT THE NORWEGIANS 
BRIEFED. HE SAID THE UK STRATEGY OF AVOIDING DEBATES ABOUT 
INSTITUTIONAL CHANGES HELPED. 
6. tet JONES PARRY SAID HE ENVISIONED A "BREAK LINE": 
ABOVE THE LINE WOULD BE EU-ONLY INTERNAL DELIBERATIONS OVER 
WHETHER TO TAKE ON A PARTICULAR TASK. ONCE THE EU DECIDED 
TO EMBARK ON A MISSION, AN INVITATION WOULD AUTOMATICALLY GO 
TO NON-EU ALLIES TO PARTICIPATE IN PLANNING AND 
IMPLEMENTATION; THESE ACTIVITIES WOULD OCCUR "BELOW THE 
LINE." NON-EU ALLIES COULD HAVE THE SAME RIGHTS AS THEY HAD 
NOW IN THE WEU TO SIT AT THE TABLE AND, IF THEY CHOSE TO 
PROVIDE TROOPS, PARTICIPATE IN OPERATIONAL DECISIONS. 
HE DOUBTED IT WAS NECESSARY TO SPELL THIS ALL OUT BEFORE 
WASHINGTON. IN ANY CASE, AGREEMENT ON ALL THE DETAILS WAS 
PROBABLY NOT ACHIEVABLE. WHAT WAS NEEDED WAS AGREEMENT ON A 
MINIMUM SET OF CORE PRINCIPLES THAT WOULD ENSURE TURKISH 
SUPPORT FOR THE SUMMIT LANGUAGE ON ESDI. 
7. (-e-} SPEC. ASST. BASS ASKED JONES PARRY TO CLARIFY HIS 
MODEL: WOULD NON-EU ALLIES HAVE ANY INPUT TO DELIBERATIONS 
"ABOVE THE LINE" OR OTHER OPPORTUNITY'TO EXPRESS THEIR VIEWS 
ON THE DESIRABILITY OF UNDERTAKING AN OPERATION, OR WOULD 
THEY HAVE TO AWAIT ACTION BY THE EU? JONES PARRY SAID 
TURKISH PARTICIPATION WAS A MATTER OF GRADATION; THE IDEA OF 
THE INITIATIVE WAS TO EMPOWER THE EU COUNCIL. IF THE 
COUNCIL ASKED FOR MILITARY PLANNING BEFORE IT DECIDED TO 
DEPLOY TROOPS, NON-EU ALLIES COULD BE INVOLVED. BUT NON-EU 
MEMBERS COULD NOT PARTICIPATE IN THE BASIC DECISION WHETHER 
THE EU WOULD DEPLOY A MISSION; THAT WOULD EFFECTIVELY LET 
NON-EU MEMBERS INTO THE EU'S SECOND PILLAR. (EMBASSY 
COMMENT: WE TOOK JONES PARRY TO MEAN THE TURKS COULD BE 
INVOLVED IN MILITARY PLANNING FOR AN EU MILITARY MISSION

8. 02-)- EU/US, EU/NATO. JONES PARRY COMMENTED THAT THE EU 
NEEDED TO DEVELOP THE SAME KIND OF RELATIONSHIP WITH THE 
U.S. THAT THE UK HAD NOW, WHERE THERE WERE NO SURPRISES. HE 
COMPLAINED THAT THE EU NOW TENDED TO WAIT TO ENGAGE UNTIL IT 
HAD A POSITION CAST IN CONCRETE-AT 15. WHEN ASKED ABOUT 
FORMAL NATO/EU LINKS, JONES PARRY SAID THESE WOULD HAVE TO 
WAIT UNTIL THE EU APPOINTED A HIGH REP, A "MR. CFSP"; NATO'S 
SEC GEN COULD THEN DEAL DIRECTLY AND CONSTANTLY WITH THE EU 
HIGH REP. 
9. .-E-E4 OPEN DOOR. JONES PARRY SAID THE UK COULD ACCEPT 
THE TY.S. TEXT (WITH SOME MINOR MODIFICATIONS THAT HE 
UNDERSTOOD DID NOT GIVE THE U.S. PAUSE) - PROVIDED THE U.S. 
LANGUAGE WOULD BE THE END PRODUCT. FRANCE OR OTHERS COULD 
NOT USE THE U.S. DRAFT LANGUAGE AS A "LAUNCHING PAD" TO PUSH 
FURTHER AT THE WASHINGTON SUMMIT. HE SAID THE UK WOULD NO 
LONGER "BARK" ABOUT THE MEMBERSHIP ACTION PLAN PROVIDING 
MORE FOR NATO ASPIRANTS THAN OTHER PARTNERS, THOUGH HE 
THOUGHT MUCH OFFERED IN MAP WOULD BE AVAILABLE ELSEWHERE. 
HE THOUGHT FCO PERMANENT UNDER SECRETARY JOHN KERR WAS BEING 
"TOO THEOLOGICAL" IN OPPOSING NAMING ALL ASPIRANTS. JONES 
PARRY THOUGHT ONE COULD RECOGNIZE, WITHOUT IMPLYING 
MEMBERSHIP WAS'AUTOMATIC, THAT SOME COUNTRIES HAVE SAID THEY 
WANT TO JOIN NATO. 
10. 4G4- MANDATES/NUKES. JONES PARRY EXPECTED MANDATES 
WOULD REMAIN UNRESOLVED UNTIL THE END-GAME PRIOR TO THE 
SUMMIT. HE SAID UK NATO PERM REP GOULDEN HAD COME AWAY 
REASSURED FROM A CONVERSATION WITH GERMAN POL DIR PLEUGER 
(AND A/S GROSSMAN), AFTER THE MARCH 10 CLOSE ALLIES' 
MEETING, ON WHETHER THE GERMANS WOULD PUSH FOR A SUMMIT • 
MANDATE FOR SUBSEQUENT ALLIANCE DISCUSSIONS OF NUCLEAR 
POLICY. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY INDICATED WE HAD A DIFFERENT 
ASSESSMENT OF THE CONVERSATION AND REMAINED CONCERNED 
REGARDING GERMAN INTENTIONS; A/S GROSSMAN HAD TOLD THE 
GERMANS THAT THE U.S. WAS STICKING BY THE RESULTS OF THE 
DECEMBER NATO MINISTERIAL. FCO DEFENCE DIRECTOR MANNING 
AGREED THIS ISSUE KEPT REEMERGING, EVEN AFTER WE HAD BEEN 
ASSURED IT HAD BEEN RESOLVED. JONES PARRY SAID THAT THE 
GERMANS WERE FOCUSING ON THE WRONG NUCLEAR ISSUE: THE 
GOVERNMENT'SHOULD ENCOURAGE THE GREENS TO FOCUS ON HELPING 
RUSSIA CLEAN UP ITS NUCLEAR WASTE.
