April 27, 1993 
Dear Mrs. Carter: 
Thank you for your note about the Friendship 
Force. You can count on me to follow up on this 
matter. I had the unexpected pleasure of jogging 
with your husband, along with Warren Christopher, in 
Tokyo last week. Please give him my very best and 
tell him how much I admire what good shape he is in. 
This is simply one more respect in which he is an 
inspiration to me! 
Very sincerely, 
Strobe Talbott 
Ambassador-at-Large and 
Special Adviser to The Secretary 
on The New Independent States.
Mrs. Rosalyn Carter, 
The Carter Center, 
P.O. Box 350 
Plains, GA.
