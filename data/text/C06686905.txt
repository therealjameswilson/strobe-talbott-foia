We are writing to you to request a meeting to discuss 
Sovlink-American Corporation sponsorship of the first substantial 
fund to invest in the New Independent States which, at a minimum 
size of $50 million, will facilitate over $1 billion of new 
investment in the NIS. 
The bulk of the investors in the Fund are expected to be 
American pension funds and other financial institutions. The 
Fund will co-invest primarily in projects sponsored by American 
corporations. 
Sovlink-American is the first substantial merchant bank 
owned by significant shareholders in the West and the NIS. 
Established in 1989, with U.S. dollar capitalization, it is 
majority owned by Western partners, with substantial o'wnership by 
NIS participants. Sovlink, which has offices in New York and 
Moscow and representation in Tyumen (Western Siberia) and 
Tashkent (Central Asia), is primarily engaged in providing 
financial and advisory services to clients in North America and 
the.NIS•in the areas of energy, natural resources, financial • 
institutions, transportation and defense\conversion. It has 
substantial Western and NIS clients, and has made several 
investments in the NIS with its own funds.
