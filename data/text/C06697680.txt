2. -*et-SUMMARY: DURING A CORDIAL 35 MINUTE MEETING, 
ACTING SECRETARY TALBOTT ASSURED ECUADORIAN PRESIDENT 
DURAN BALLEN OF U.S. COMMITMENT AS A GUARANTOR IN THE 
PERU-ECUADOR BORDER DISPUTE AND URGED ECUADOR TO MAINTAIN 
MAXIMUM EFFORT TO ACHIEVE PROGRESS IN RESOLVING THE 
DISPUTE. TALBOTT REITERATED THE POINTS MADE DURING DURAN 
BALLEN'S WHITE HOUSE MEETING REGARDING THE IMPORTANCE THIS 
ADMINISTRATION ATTACHES TO PEACEKEEPING EXIT STRATEGIES. 
HOWEVER, HE NOTED THAT FUTURE CONSIDERATION ON THE U.S. 
ROLE IN BORDER OBSERVATION EFFORTS MIGHT BE INFLUENCED BY 
MEASURABLE PROGRESS IN THE DIPLOMATIC NEGOTIATIONS AS 
OPPOSED TO A SITUATION OF CONTINUED STALEMATE. DURAN 
BALLEN EMPHASIZED THE IMPORTANCE THAT ECUADOR PLACES ON 
GIVING RECOGNITION TO THE GEOGRAPHIC INCONSISTENCIES OF 
THE RIO PROTOCOL, AND THE GOE'S STRONG DESIRE TO SEE THE 
MILITARY OBSERVER MISSION (MOMEP) CONTINUE AFTER 
DECEMBER. END SUMMARY. 
3.-ift THE ACTING SECRETARY OPENED THE MEETING BY 
CONVEYING SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER'S GREETINGS AND STATING 
THAT PRESIDENT CLINTON AND NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISER LAKE 
HAD BEEN VERY SATISFIED WITH THEIR JUNE 25 MEETING WITH 
DURAN BALLEN. TALBOTT STRESSED THAT DEMOCRACY, PEACE AND 
FAIRNESS ARE THREE PRINCIPLES THE U.S. VALUES AND THAT 
THESE THEMES HAD BEEN STRESSED BY BOTH U.S. AND ECUADORIAN 
PARTICIPANTS IN THE WHITE HOUSE MEETING. TALBOTT THEN 
ASKED WHAT STEPS DURAN BALLEN WOULD RECOMMEND TO HIS 
SUCCESSOR TO MOVE THE PEACE PROCESS FORWARD. 
4.-{e} DURAN BALLEN SAID THAT HE HOPED EITHER CANDIDATE 
WOULD FOLLOW THE DIRECTION THAT HE (DURAN BALLEN) HAD 
STARTED. HE TOOK CREDIT FOR BEING THE FIRST ECUADORIAN 
PRESIDENT TO ACCEPT THE RIO PROTOCOL, CALLING THIS A 
GAMBLE THAT HAD PAID OFF WHEN THE ECUADOR IAN CONGRESS AND 
PEOPLE SUPPORTED HIM. DURAN BALLEN NOTED THAT 
FRONT-RUNNER ABDALA BUCARAM HAD OFFERED TO KEEP CURRENT 
FOREIGN MINISTER LEORO IN PLACE, INDICATING BUCARAM'S 
SUPPORT FOR THE CURRENT BORDER'PROCESS. DURAN BALLEN ALSO 
FELT THAT THE OiliER PRESIDENTIAL CONTENDER, JAIME NEBOT, 
WOULD FOLLOW ESTABLISHED LINES EVEN THOUGH THERE HAD BEEN 
SOME DIFFERING VIEWS ON THE BORDER OFFERED BY HIS 
VICE-PRESIDENTIAL RUNNING MATE -- A FORMER FOREIGN 
RELEASE IN FULL

5. -(.64- DURAN BALLEN ALSO STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF 
MAINTAINING THE GUARANTOR MILITARY OBSERVER MISSION 
(MOMEP) IN PLACE AFTER THE CURRENTLY ESTABLISHED DECEMBER 
DATE FOR WITHDRAWAL OF THE U.S. SUPPORT GROUP. DURAN 
BALLEN SAID THAT BOTH SIDES WOULD FEEL OBLIGED TO REENTER 
THE DISPUTED AREA IF MOMEP WERE WITHDRAWN, RESULTING IN 
RESUMED HOSTILITIES, WHICH BOTH COUNTRIES WOULD LIKE TO 
AVOID. 
6.-(4+ TALBOTT REITERATED THE U.S. POSITION AS OUTLINED 
BY PRESIDENT CLINTON AND NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR LAKE. 
THE U.S. IS PROUD OF ITS ROLE IN MOMEP AND COMMITTED TO 
ITS STATUS AS GUARANTOR, BUT WE CANNOT MAKE AN OPEN-ENDED 
COMMITMENT TO MILITARY INVOLVEMENT IN PEACEKEEPING. IT IS 
IMPERATIVE FOR THE U.S. TO HAVE AN EXIT STRATEGY FOR THESE 
TYPES OF OPERATIONS. TALBOTT STRESSED THAT ACHIEVING REAL 
PROGRESS IN DIPLOMATIC NEGOTIATIONS TO RESOLVE THE DISPUTE 
COULD MAKE IT EASIER FOR THE U.S. TO LOOK AT NEW IDEAS FOR 
SUPPORTING THE PEACE PROCESS. IF MATTERS REMAINED AT AN 
IMPASSE, IT WOULD BE MORE DIFFICULT BOTH POLITICALLY AND 
TACTICALLY FOR US TO CONSIDER NEW OPTIONS. IN THIS 
REGARD, DAVIDOW ASKED DURAN BALLEN WHAT SPECIFIC STEPS THE. 
NEW PRESIDENT MIGHT BE ABLE TO TAKE IMMEDIATELY UPON 
TAKING OFFICE IN ORDER TO PRESERVE MOMENTUM. 
7.-{e}- DURAN BALLEN SAID IT WAS DIFFICULT TO PREDICT THE 
CONSTRAINTS THAT MIGHT FACE THE NEW PRESIDENT, BUT HE 
WOULD STRONGLY URGE THAT THE SCHEDULE OF TALKS CONTINUE. 
DURAN BALLEN SAID THAT HE WOULD THOROUGHLY BRIEF THE NEW 
PRESIDENT AND PASS ALONG ALL OF HIS ADMINISTRATION1 S 
INFORMATION. HOWEVER, HE FELT THAT A NEW PRESIDENT COULD 
TAKE UP TO TWO MONTHS TO DEVELOP NEW IDEAS. DURAN BALLEN 
SAID THAT IT WOULD ALSO BE CRITICALLY IMPORTANT FOR THE 
GUARANTORS TO CONVINCE PERU THAT THE ISSUE A GEOGRAPHICAL 
INCONSISTENCIES MUST BE KEPT ON THE TABLE DURING FUTURE 
TALKS, AS ECUADOR HAS MADE CLEAR IN ITS LIST OF IMPASSES. 
IF PERU SHOWS NO FLEXIBILITY HERE, THE TALKS WILL FAIL. 
NEITHER DURAN BALLEN NOR ANY OTHER ECUADORIAN PRESIDENT 
COULD ACCEPT A BOUNDARY THAT RAN ALONG THE CORDILLERA DE 
CONDOR. CONVEYING A FRESH INSIGHT, DURAN BALLEN SAID THAT 
HE HAD ASSURED PERUVIAN PRESIDENT FUJIMORI DURING THEIR 
MARCH MEETING IN TRUJILLO, PERU, THAT ECUADOR WOULD ACCEPT 
THE BORDER AS DEMARCATED NORTH OF HITO 13 AND WEST OF THE 
SAN FRANCISCO, BUT THAT PERU HAD TO BE FLEXIBLE.

THE PROTOCOL. BUT UNCERTAINTY OVER MOMEP PLACES GREAT

9. 4G4- DURAN BALLEN REITERATED HIS CONCERNS OVER THE 
GEOGRAPHIC INCONSISTENCIES OF THE PROTOCOL, DRAWING 
SCHEMATIC MAPS ON THE BACK OF PRESS CLIPS TO ILLUSTRATE 
HIS VIEWS. ONE MAP SHOWED TWO RIVERS AND ONE WATERSHED, 
AS DESCRIBED IN THE PROTOCOL, THE OTHER MAP SHOWING THREE 
RIVERS AND TWO WATERSHEDS, AS WAS SUBSEQUENTLY 
DISCOVERED. HE FELT THAT.ECUADOR AND PERU WILL NEVER BE 
ABLE TO RESOLVE THIS ISSUE ALONE AND URGED THE GUARANTORS 
TO HELP FIND A SOLUTION. DURAN BALLEN STRESSED THAT THE 
U.S. GOAL OF HEMISPHERIC UNITY BY THE YEAR 2005 WOULD BE 
CONTINGENT OF ACHIEVING PEACE BETWEEN PERU AND ECUADOR --
TWO MEMBERS OF THIS HEMISPHERIC COMMUNITY. DURAN BALLEN,
EMPHASIZED THAT THE PERU-ECUADOR DISPUTE IS NOT LIKE THE 
PEACE PROCESSES IN HAITI OR BOSNIA AND THAT WE MUST 
APPROACH THIS DISPUTE DIFFERENTLY. TALBOTT COUNTERED THAT 
ONE SIMILARITY TO BOSNIA WAS THE DAYTON MEETINGS, WHERE 
THE U.S. DID NOT IMPOSE A SOLUTION, BUT CREATED AN 
ATMOSPHERE WHICH ALLOWED THE PARTIES TO REACH THEIR OWN 
ACCORDS. 
10. 46* THE ECUADORIANS THEN MADE THEIR MOST POINTED 
APPEAL: WOULD THE GUARANTORS ENSURE A DISCUSSION OF THE 
GEOGRAPHIC PROBLEM? ElNAUDI CONFIRMED THAT THE GUARANTORS 
STAND READY OFFER SUGGESTIONS ON SPECIFIC ISSUES, BUT ONLY 
AFTER THE PARTIES RAISED THEM, DURING THEIR TALKS. LEORO 
EXPRESSED CONCERN THAT THE GUARANTORS WOULD STAND BACK 
UNTIL THE BILATERAL TALKS BETWEEN PERU AND ECUADOR HAD 
STALLED BEFORE BECOMING INVOLVED. ElNAUDI REPLIED,THAT 
ACCORDING TO THE PROCEDURES DEVELOPED IN BUENOS AIRES THE 
GUARANTORS WOULD HAVE TO ADDRESS THE CONCERNS OF BOTH 
ECUADOR AND PERU AND PROVIDE RECOMMENDATIONS WELL BEFORE 
THE TALKS BECOME STALLED, IF THERE WAS STILL NO 
AGREEMENT, THEN A METHOD COULD BE DEVISED TO RESOLVE THE 
SPECIFIC ISSUE IN A WAY THAT RESPECTED THE LAW AND PAST 
ARBITRAL DECISIONS.
