1. ~ SUMMARY: IN A JULY 11 MEETING PRIOR TO THE G-8
MINISTERIAL, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND FOREIGN MINISTER
KONO FOCUSED ON BILATERAL ISSUES. BOTH EXPRESSED HOPE FOR
RESOLUTION OF THE NTT INTERCONNECTION ISSUE BY THE TIME OF
THE OKINAWA SUMMIT. TALBOTT ALSO HOPED FOR SUCCESS IN :HE
HOST NATION SUPPORT (HNS) TALKS AND AGREED ON THE
IMPORTANCE OF A DIALOGUE ON BASE-RELATED ENVIRONMENTAL
ISSUES. KONO AGREED TO ASK FOR ACCELERATED EFFORTS, AND
SOUGHT USG HELP IN IMPROVING EFFICIENCY AND ELIMINATING
UNNECESSARY EXPENDITURES. TALBOTT ASKED FOR GOJ ACTION ON
THE HAGUE CONVENTION ON CHILD ABDUCTIONS AND THE PRISONER
TRANSFER TREATY. KONG AGREED TO MOVE FORWARD ON THE
LATTER, BUT NOTED THE LOW PRIORITY THE JUSTICE MINISTRY
GAVE TO THE CHILD ABDUCTION PROBLEM. KONO EXPRESSED HIS
HOPE THAT SECRETARY ALBRIGHT WOULD BE ABLE TO VISIT JAPAN,
PARTICURLARLY GIVEN MIYAZAKI CITY'S KEEN INTEREST IN HER
VISIT. TALBOTT STRESSED THE SECRETARY'S DETERMINATION TO
VISIT JAPAN AS SOON AS POSSIBLE. END SUMMARY.
DECLASSIFIED
2. (+ DURING OPENING REMARKS, KONO SAID HE HAD BEEN
DISAPPOINTED TO HEAR FROM .SECRETARY ALBRIGHT THAT SHE COULD
NOT ATTEND THE MINISTERIAL, BUT HIS REGRET WAS ASSUAGED BY
LEARNING THAT TALBOTT WOULD BE THE U.S. REPRESENTATIVE. HE
FULLY UNDERSTOOD THE IMPORTANCE OF THE MEPP AND HOPED THE
SECRETARY WOULD BE SUCCESSFUL IN THE DIFFICULT
NEGOTIATIONS. MIYAZAKI CITY ALSO HAD HIGH EXPECTATIONS FOR
HER VISIT, SO TALBOTT WAS ASKED TO CONVEY THEIR HOPES FOR A
VISIT IN THE NEAR FUTURE. TALBOTT EXPRESSED APPRECIATION
FOR KONO'S UNDERSTANDING, AND NOTED THAT SECRETARY
ALBRIGHT, WHO REGARDED KONO AS A PERSONAL FRIEND AS WELL AS
IMPORTANT PARTNER, REGRETTED HER ABSENCE ON BOTH A PERSONAL
AND OFFICIAL BASIS. SHE WAS DETERMINED TO VISIT JAPAN AS
SOON AS POSSIBLE, TALBOTT SAID, AND HAD INSTRUCTED HER
STAFF TO FIND A SPECIFIC TIME. THOUGH DETERMINED TO COME
TO MI YAZAKI ~ SHE NEEDED TO PIN DOWN THE FOLLOW UP SCHEDULE
FOR THE MIDEAST TALKS BEFORE COMMITTING TO A DATE. KONO
POINTED OUT THAT THE WORKING DINNER ON JULY 12 WOULD FOCUS
ON THE MIDDLE EAST AMONG OTHER REGIONAL ISSUES, AND A U.S.
UPDATE ON THE STATUS OF PEACE TALKS WOULD BE APPRECIATED.
3. ~ MOVING TO THE BILATERAL AGENDA, KONO EXPRESSED
JAPAN'S DEEP APPRECIATION FOR PRESIDENT CLINTON'S
ATTENDANCE AT THE OBUCHI FUNERAL, AND SAID THE GOJ LOOKED
FORWARD TO COOPERATING WITH THE USG TO MAKE THE PRESIDENT'S
HISTORIC VISIT TO OKINAWA A SUCCESS. TALBOTT REPLIED THAT
CLINTON ATTACHED GREAT IMPORTANCE TO THE U.S.-JAPAN
RELATIONSHIP AND WAS PROUD THAT HIS TRIP NEXT WEEK WOULD BE
HIS FIFTH VISIT TO JAPAN.
4. ~ ADDRESSING THE NTT INTERCONNECTION ISSUES, KONO
SAID BOTH SIDES NEEDED TO WORK FOR A RESOLUTION IN THE
TALKS THAT BEGAN MONDAY IN TOKYO. JAPAN HAD MADE A
PROPOSAL WHICH THE U.S. WAS NOW STUDYING. HE EMPHASIZED
THAT JAPAN WAS DETERMINED TO RESOLVE THE ISSUE AND HOPED TO
DO SO BEFORE THE G-B SUMMIT. KONO SAID HE FULLY UNDERSTOOD

5. ~TALBOTT SHARED KONO'S HOPES FOR A RESOLUTION OF
THE NTT INTERCONNECTION ISSUE IN THE DAYS AHEAD, HE
REFERRED TO THE PRESIDENT'S DEEP INTEREST IN IMPROVING 'HE
COMPETITIVE ENVIRONMENT, ESPECIALLY IN TELECOMMUNICATIONS,
WHICH HE SEES AS AN IMPORTANT OPPORTUNITY FOR JAPAN IN THE
AGE OF GLOBALIZATION. THE AMERICAN SIDE AS WELL HAD BEEN
INSTRUCTED TO ACCELERATE THEIR WORK ON HNS, AND WE GREATLY
HOPED FOR A BREAKTHROUGH BY NEXT WEEK. HOWEVER, TALBOTT
WOULD NOT MAKE ANY PUBLCI STATEMENTS ABOUT A TIMETABLE, AS
WE WOULDN'T WANT TO COMPLICATE WORK BY GOING PUBLIC.
(COMMENT: THE JAPANESE HAD, BEFORE THE MEETING, VOICED
CONCERN ABOUT PREMATURE AND COUNTERPRODUCTIVE PUBLIC
COMMENTS ON THE CHANCES OF A DEAL.) THE USG WAS PREPARED TO
WORK ON ALL OF THE ISSUES IN OUR SECURITY RELATIONSHIP,
INCLUDING A DISCUSSION ON BASE-RELATED ENVIRONMENTAL
ISSUES, WHICH HE KNEW WAS IMPORTANT TO KONO. THE SECURITY
RELATIONSHIP IS A VITAL CONSTANT IN THE BILATERAL
RELATIONSHIP, AND IT IS APPROPRIATE,TO UNDERSCORE TO THE
REGION THE STRENGTH OF THE SECURITY TIES. KONO AGREED,
POINTING TO THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SECURITY TREATY.
DEVELOPMENTS ON THE KOREAN PENINSULA WERE POSITIVE, AND
WHILE ALL HOPED FOR AN EASING OF TENSIONS, THE NORTH-SOUTH
'UMMIT DID NOT ADDRESS ALL THE ISSUES; WE WOULD HAVE TO
CONTINUE OUR IMPORTANT TRILATERAL CONSULTATIONS.
6. ~ KONO,RETURNED TO THE BASE-RELATED ENVIRONMENTAL
QUESTION, STRESSING THE IMPORTANCE OF THESE ISSUES, AND
HOPED THAT THE TWO SIDES COULD REACH A JOINT AGREEMENT TO
INCLUDE SUCH POSITIVE STEPS AS ENHANCED COOPERATION ON
ENVIRONMENTAL QUESTIONS. HE THEN BROUGHT UP THE RECENT
OKINAWA INCIDENTS, AND SAID HE HAD EXPRESSED HIS CONCERN
THE DAY BEFORE TO AMBASSADOR FOLEY. WHILE MOST SERVICEMEN
WERE DISCIPLINED, A MINORITY CAUSES INCIDENTS WHICH GREATLY
AGITATED THE OKINAWAN PEOPLE. THE AMBASSADOR HAD EXPLAINED
THE MEASURES TAKEN TO STRENGTHEN DISCIPLINE AND KONO HOPED
THEY WOULD HAVE RESULTS. HE ALSO ASKED FOR USG COOPERATION
IN FURTHER IMPLEMENTATION OF SACO.
7. ~TALBOTT REPLIED THAT THE USG INTENDED TO BE
PROACTIVE AND RESPONSIBLE ON ENVIRONMENTAL ISSUES.
REGARDING THE REGRETTABLE INCIDENTS, AMBASSADOR FOLEY HAD
BRIEFED HIM ON HIS MEETING WITH AND APOLOGY EXTENDED TO
KONO, AND KNEW THAT GENERAL HAILSTON HAD PUBLICLY
APOLOGIZED TO THE PEOPLE OF OKINAWA. USFJ WAS TAKING
CONCRETE MEASURES TO AVOID A REPETITION OF THE PROBLEM.
WHILE SOME PEOPLE IN BOTH COUNTRIES MIGHT FEEL APPREHENSIVE
ABOUT PRESIDENT CLINTON'S VISIT TO OKINAWA AT THIS TIME,
THE VISIT WAS ALSO AN OPPORTUNITY FOR HIM TO DEMONSTRATE
HIS STRONG POSITIVE FEELINGS TOWARD THE OKINAWAN PEOPLE,
AND TO HEAR THEIR CONCERNS.
