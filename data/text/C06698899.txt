PRESIDENT CONSTANTINESCU: THANK YOU. THESE WERE THE FIRST
ELECTIONS THAT WERE NOT CHALLENGED BY ANYONE. AFTER 60 YEARS 
OF RECENT ROMANIAN HISTORY, I AM THE FIRST PRESIDENT ELECTED IN 
A DEMOCRATIC MANNER. MOREOVER, THIS IS THE FIRST TIME IN 
WHICH THE PERSON WHO IS LEAVING OFFICE CAN REMAIN aN 
POLITICAL LIFE. WE HAVE TRIED TO ENSURE A CERTAIN ELEGANCE IN 
THE TRANSITION. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: YOU ARE CERTAINLY SUCCEEDING IN THAT. WE 
LOOK FORWARD TO WORKING CLOSELY WITH YOU AND YOUR 
GOVERNMENT TO ACCELERATE POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC REFORM:—
WE FULLY SUPPORT ROMANIA S EFFORTS TO'INTEGRATE ITSELF INTO 
EURO-ATLANTIC STRUCTURES AND WE BELIEVE THAT THESE STEPS WILL 
STRENGTHEN POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC PROGRESS IN ROMANIA. AS 
YOU KNOW, NO DECISIONS HAVE BEEN MADE CONCERNING WHICH 
COUNTRIES WILL BE INCLUDED IN THE FIRST GROUP OF STATES INVITED 
TO JOIN NATO NEXT YEAR. BUT WE URGE THAT ROMANIA CONTINUE ITS 
EFFORTS TO QUALIFY. IN ANY EVENT, THE FIRST TO JOIN NATO WILL 
NOT BE THE LAST. OF COURSE, SOME OF THE BIGGEST CHALLENGES 
YOU FACE ARE IN THE ECONOMIC AREA. WE HOPE THAT YOU AND THE 
NEW GOVERNMENT WILL DO EVERYTHING POSSIBLE TO INSTITUTE 
REFORMS IN THIS AREA. YOU HAVE THE EXPERIENCE OF OTHERS IN THE 
REGION TO FOLLOW. IT IS CLEAR THAT THOSE WHO HAVE MOVED THE 
FASTEST ARE DOING THE BEST. THOSE KINDS OF REFORMS TAKE 
COURAGE, BUT EXPERIENCE SHOWS THAT IT IS THE BEST WAY TO 
PROCEED. IF THERE IS ANYTHING WE CAN DO TO HELP, LET US KNOW. 
WITH RESPECT TO POLITICAL MATTERS, WE URGE YOU TO WORK ON 
IMPLEMENTING THE HUNGARIAN-ROMANIAN TREATY AND EXPANDING 
THAT MODEL TO YOUR RELATIONS WITH OTHERS, PARTICULARLY 
UKRAINE AND MOLDOVA. THE UNITED STATES WANTS TO SEE ROMANIA 
SUCCEED IN EVERY WAY IN MODERNIZING AND INTEGRATING WITH 
THE WEST. WE WILL DO EVERYTHING WE CAN IF ROMANIA PURSUES 
SOUND POLICIES. WE WANT THE CLOSEST POSSIBLE RELATIONSHIP. 
PRESIDENT CONSTANTINESCU: MR. VICE PRESIDENT, I AM VERY HAPPY 
TO HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY TO MEET WITH YOU. LET ME MAKE A FEW 
SPECIFIC POINTS, SINCE COMMUNICATION BETWEEN US AND THE 
EXECUTIVE BRANCH HAS NOT ALWAYS BEEN GOOD. THAT IS ONE 
REASON WHY NO ONE IN THE U.S. ADMINISTRATION FORESAW OUR 
VICTORY. LET ME THANK YOU FOR GREETING ME IN WASHINGTON A 
FEW YEARS AGO. I STILL HAVE A PICTURE OF OUR MEETING IN MY 
LIBRARY. THE PROBLEMS OF DEMOCRATIZATION IN ROMANIA ARE AT 
AN END. THE SYSTEM IS STABLE. WE HAVE WON THE LOCAL, 
PARLIAMENTARY AND PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS. WE HAVE A 
COALITION WITH THE SOCIAL DEMOCRATICS AND A STABLE MAJORITY 
IN THE PARLIAMENT. AT MY INITIATIVE, THE HUNGARIAN PARTY WILL 
BE IN THE GOVERNMENT. FROM THIS POINT OF VIEW, ONE CAN EXPECT 
THAT MINORITY PROBLEMS IN ROMANIA WILL EASE SINCE THE . 
HUNGARIANS WILL BE REPRESENTED IN THE POWER'STRUCTURES. WE 
HAVE ALREADY HAD EXPERIENCE IN DEALING WITH THEM BECAUSE 
THEY WERE PART OF THE COALITION THAT I LED. OUR RELATIONSHIP 
WITH HUNGARY WILL BE BASED ON CONFIDENCE AND TRUST. I HAVE 
,GOOD RELATIONS WITH THE HUNGARIAN PRESIDENT AND WITH OTHER 
FIGURES IN THE HUNGARIAN GOVERNMENT. IN THIS REGARD, TRUST 
WILL BE EVEN MORE IMPORTANT THAN OUR POLITICAL TREATY. THERE 
ARE ONE MILLION ETHNIC HUNGARIANS WHO VOTED FOR ME BOTH IN 
THE PAST.AND IN THE RECENT ELECTION. I AM THE PERSON THAT 
THEY TRUST. REGARDING UKRAINE, MY FIRST MEETING TODAY WAS 
WITH PRESIDENT KUCHMA. TOGETHER WE DECIDED TO INSTRUCT OUR 
FOREIGN MINISTERS TO FINALIZE A POLITICAL TREATY BETWEEN OUR 
TWO COUNTRIES. THERE ARE STILL SOME REMAINING ISSUES THAT 
DERIVE FROM HISTORY, AND THESE DO NOT NECESSARILY FAVOR 
ROMANIA. BUT I WILL ASK MY SUPPORTERS AND ALL ROMANIANS TO 
LOOK TO THE FUTURE AND NOT TO THE PAST. I HAVE THE MORAL 
AUTHORITY TO DO SO. IN SUM, WE INTEND TO SOLVE OUR INTERNAL 
' PROBLEMS AND OUR RELATIONS WITH OUR NEIGHBORS, THEREBY 
MEETING ALL THE REQUIREMENTS OF NATO. BUT I AM NOT NAIVE. I 
AM AWARE THAT THE ECONOMIC AND SOCIAL CONDITIONS MUST ALSO 
BE MET. • REFORM IN ROMANIA HAS LARGELY.BEEN COSMETIC SO FAR.

d WE NEED REAL REFORMS AND THESE WILL INCUR SOCIAL COSTS. NEVERTHELESS, I WILL ASK MY GOVERNING MAJORITY TO DRAW UP EV TWO PROGRAMS OF REFORM. THE FIRST WILL BE A SHORT-TERM PROGRAM (FOR THE NEXT FIVE MONTHS) TO ENSURE THE SOCIAL SECURITY OF THE MOST DISADVANTAGED CATEGORIES OF THE POPULATION. THEN, IN MAY, WE WILL DRAFT A CLEAR PROGRAM FOR THE LONGER TERM IN CONSULTATION WITH THE WORLD BANK, THE IMF, AND U.S. SPECIALISTS. IN ORDER TO MAINTAIN A CLIMATE OF POLITICAL STABILITY IN ROMANIA AND IN THE REGION, I WILL NEED SUPPORT IN THE NEXT FIVE MONTHS, INCLUDING ECONOMIC SUPPORT. ' WE DO NOT WANT TO CONDUCT OUR REFORM IN A ROUNDABOUT WAY. WE DON T WANT TO ASK FOR RESTRUCTURING CREDITS AND THEN USE THE MONEY TO PAY GOVERNMENT SALARIES, AS HAS BEEN DONE IN THE PAST. AS WE CARRY OUT THESE REFORMS, WE WILL NEED U.S. SUPPORT. THERE IS MUCH PRO-AMERICAN FEELING IN ROMANIA. I AM SEEN AS THE REPRESENTATIVE OF THAT FEELING (IN 1992, I WAS EVEN ACCUSED OF BEING AN AMERICAN SPY!). BUT I BELIEVE THAT FOR REAL REFORM,WE NEED THE CONFIDENCE OF THE PEOPLE, SO THAT THEY CAN SEE THAT GOOD TIMES ARE COMING. AFTER WORLD WAR II, 1.2 MILLION ROMANIANS WERE LEFT IN COMMUNIST PRISONS . WAITING FOR THE AMERICANS TO COME TO THEIR RESCUE. TWO HUNDRED THOUSAND DIED IN PRISON. THEN THE AMERICANS DID NOT COME, BUT NOW AMERICANS CAN COME FOR REAL . COOPERATION. WE QLL ADOPT A CQAR LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM. IN ONE MONTH WE WILL PASS LAWS GUARANTEEING THE PROPERTY RIGHTS OF FOREIGN INVESTORS, INCLUDING THE REPATRIATION OF PROFITS. WE ALSO INTEND TO DECENTRALIZE, WITH A LAW ON LOCAL BUDGETS, TO STIMULATE THE INTEREST OF FOREIGN INVESTORS. WE ARE READY TO GIVE A PRIVILEGED POSITION TO U.S. INVESTORS, SINCE WE WANT THEM TO STAY FOR THE LONG TERM. WE WOULD LIKE TO HAVE AN OPEN, TRANSPARENT POLICY BASED ON THE REALITIES OF THE SITUATION. I WAS ONCE A VISITING PROFESSOR AT DUKE UNIVERSITY AND I WAS ABLE TO LEARN HOW TO TALICTO AMERICANS NAMELY, IN A DIRECT AND OPEN WAY. I KNOW THAT IF ONE LIES ONCE TO AN AMERICAN, ONE WILL NOT GET ANYWHERE THE SECOND TIME. 
THE VICE PRESIDENT: WE ARE VERY EXCITED BY WHAT LIES AHEAD. WE WILL WORK CLOSELY WITH YOU AND YOUR GOVERNMENT. AGAIN, CONGRATULATIONS.
