SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY RAISES ARMS SALES TO IRAN WITH 
GREEK DEFENSE MINISTER 
1. (U) CLASSIFIED BY THE ACTING SECRETARY. REASONS: 
1.5 (A), (B) AND(D), 1.6 X6. 
2. 48} DURING A JUNE 23 MEETING WITH VISITING GREEK 
DEFENSE MINISTER TSOHATZOPOULOS (OTHER SUBJECTS REPORTED 
SEPTEL), THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE WISHED TO RAISE A 
MATTER.OF ACUTE CONCERN TO THE USG, ARMS SALES TO IRAN. 
WE CONSIDERED IT LIKELY THAT THE GREEK DEFENSE MINISTRY 
WOULD BE APPROACHED BY THE IRANIANS ABOUT PURCHASING U.S. 
MILITARY EQUIPMENT OR FOR SPARE PARTS FOR AGING 
U.S. -ORIGIN MILITARY EQUIPMENT ALREADY IN THEIR 
POSSESSION. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID WE WOULD VIEW ANY' 
ARMS TRANSFERS TO IRAN AS A MAJOR PROBLEM IN OUR BILATERAL 
RELATIONS. NOT ONLY COULD THEY TRIGGER AUTOMATIC 
SANCTIONS UNDER U.S. LAW, BUT THEY WOULD RAISE CONCERNS 
ABOUT VIOLATIONS OF GREECE'S WASSENAAR ARRANGEMENT 
COMMITMENTS TO PREVENT SUCH EQUIPMENT FROM FALLING INTO 
THE HANDS OF STATE SPONSORS OF TERRORISM. 
3. --(-St TSOHATZOPOULOS REPLIED THAT HE HAD NOT HEARD OF 
THIS ISSUE, NOR WAS HE AWARE OF ANY APPROACH FROM THE 
IRANIANS. HE SAID IT WAS GOOD OF THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TO 
TELL HIM OF THE ISSUE. HE ADDED THAT GREECE HAD ITS OWN 
PROBLEMS WITH IRAN AT THE MOMENT, FOLLOWING IRANIAN 
COMPLAINTS ABOUT THE DELAY IN THE RETURN OF THE GREEK 
AMBASSADOR TO TEHRAN.
