Dear Mr. Lesawyer: 
Thank you for your letter and kind words about my briefing 
at the International Club. Events such as those offer a 
valuable forum for explaining the often-confusing dynamics of 
the New Independent States and America's interests and 
objectives there in promoting democracy, economic reform, peace 
and stability. I try to take advantage of as many of these 
opportunities as I can, and I am encouraged by your letter that 
my remarks are finding their target. 
I applaud the efforts of the Ukrainian Institute of 
America and the Ukrainian American Veterans in sponsoring 
Ukrainian military officers' travel to the United States tp 
attend the various service institutes. These programs are an 
important element in promoting Ukraine's transition from its 
Soviet past, and would not be possible without the partnership 
and support of public organizations such as the UIA. Colonel 
'Grynenko's paper provides ample evidence of the unique 
perspectives Ukrainian officers can gain through these valuable 
exchange programs. 
I wish you every success in your organization's endeavors 
to forge greater links between Ukraine and the United States. 
Thank you again for your letter. 
Since 
Joseph Lesawyer,

Scotch Plains, NJ.
