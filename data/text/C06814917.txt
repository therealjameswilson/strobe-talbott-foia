DEAR YEVGENIY MAXIMOVICH, DECONTROLLED
I WOULD LIKE TO CONGRATULATE YOU ON YOUR APPOINTMENT AS
CHAIRMAN OF THE RUSSIAN COMMISSION ON THE TRANSNISTRIAN
DISPUTE. THIS IS A TOUGH AND THANKLESS JOB — BUT THE
APPOINTMENT OF A VETERAN OF YOUR DIPLOMATIC TALENTS SIGN]
THE SERIOUSNESS OF RUSSIA'S RENEWED ENGAGEMENT AS ONE OF
THE MEDIATORS, ALONG WITH UKRAINE AND THE OSCE, IN SEEKIN
A JUST AND DURABLE POLITICAL SETTLEMENT TO THE
TRANSNISTRIAN DISPUTE. I WISH YOU ALL THE BEST IN YOUR
WORK TO ACHIEVE THIS IMPORTANT GOAL; THE U.S. SUPPORTS THE
EFFORTS OF ALL THE MEDIATORS TO THAT END, INCLUDING THOSE
OF YOUR COMMISSION.
SUCCESS OF THIS DIFFICULT ENDEAVOR WILL DEPEND IN NO SMALL
PART ON THE ACTIVE SUPPORT OF THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY
AS A WHOLE, ESPECIALLY AS MANIFESTED THROUGH THE OSCE
WHICH, THROUGH ITS MISSION RESIDENT IN MOLDOVA, HAS BEEN
DIRECTLY ENGAGED ON THIS COMPLEX OF ISSUES FOR A NUMBER OF
YEARS NOW. WE WILL WANT TO WORK CLOSELY WITH BOTH THE
RUSSIAN FEDERATION AND THE OSCE TO ENSURE AN OUTCOME THAT,
IN SETTLING THIS INTERNAL DISPUTE IN A WAY THAT PROTECTS
THE LEGITIMATE INTERESTS OF ALL OF THE PEOPLE OF MOLDOVA
WHEREVER LOCATED, IS SEEN TO REINFORCE THE INDEPENDENCE,
SOVEREIGNTY AND TERRITORIAL INTEGRITY OF MOLDOVA. THIS
LATTER POINT IS ABSOLUTELY ESSENTIAL IF, IN THE FACE OF
SEPARATIST ASPIRATIONS, SUCH A SETTLEMENT IS TO BE
GENUINELY DURABLE AND STABILIZING FOR THE REGION.
AT THE OSCE .SUMMIT LAST NOVEMBER IN ISTANBUL, THE RUSSIAN
FEDERATION UNDERTOOK A NUMBER OF IMPORTANT STEPS WITH
REGARD TO MOLDOVA THAT WERE WARMLY WELCOMED BY THE OSCE
COMMUNITY NOT LEAST WERE ITS CFE-RELATED COMMITMENTS TO
THE COMPLETE WITHDRAWAL OF ITS FORCES, EQUIPMENT AND
AMMUNITION FROM MOLDOVA BY SPECIFIC DEADLINES IN 2001 AND
2002. DURING THE RECENT MEETING IN MOSCOW, PRESIDENT
CLINTON URGED PRESIDENT PUTIN TO FULFILL THESE COMMITMENTS.
VISIBLE PROGRESS OVER THE COMING MONTHS IN THE PRACTICAL
IMPLEMENTATION OF THESE COMMITMENTS WOULD SEND A CLEAR AND
POSITIVE SIGNAL OF RUSSIA'S INTENTION TO FULFILL ITS
FREELY-ASSUMED INTERNATIONAL OBLIGATIONS, DESPITE REPEATED
EFFORTS BY SOME TRANSNISTRIAN AUTHORITIES TO ASSERT A DE
FACTO V'TO OVER RUSSIAN ACTIONS TO THAT END. THIS WOULD IN
TURN FACILITATE THE EFFORTS OF THE MEDIATORS TO FIND A
RESOLUTION OF THE SEPARATIST CONFLICT IN TRANSNISTRIA.
FOR ITS PART, THE U.S. IS WILLING TO ASSIST FINANCIALLY,
THROUGH THE OSCE, IN THE WITHDRAWAL AND DESTRUCTION OF

MILITARY EQUIPMENT. BUT IT WILL BE IMPORTANT TO MOVE
EXPEDITIOUSLY IF SUCH INTERNATIONAL ASSISTANCE IS TO REMAIN
POSSIBLE. TO MAKE A GENUINE CONTRIBUTION TO STABILITY, ANY
POSSIBLE REQUIREMENTS FOR LONGER-TERM PEACEKEEPING IN
MOLDOVA SHOULD BE DEPENDENT UPON THE MUTUALLY AGREED
CONDITIONS OF A COMPREHENSIVE POLITICAL SETTLEMENT — AND
NOT VICE VERSA. DECISIONS ON THE EVENTUAL SHAPE OF
PEACEKEEPING SHOULD NOT BE PREJUDGED NOW. HERE TOO, WE
WILL WANT TO WORK CLOSELY TOGETHER TO ENSURE THE MOST
EFFECTIVE OSCE ROLE.
AS A SIGN OF OUR INTEREST IN ASSISTING YOUR COMMISSION'S
EFFOPTS, I WILL BE HAPPY TO HAVE CAREY CAVANAUGH, SPECIAL
NEGOTIATOR FOR NIS REGIONAL CONFLICTS, AND CRAIG DUNKERLEY,
SPECIAL ENVOY FOR CFE, CONSULT WITH YOU ON THIS ISSUE.
ONCE AGAIN, I CONGRATULATE YOU ON YOUR NEW APPOINTMENT AND
LOOK FORWARD TO THE SUCCESSFUL OUTCOME OF YOUR COMMISSION'S
EFFORTS.
MADELEINE, WHO IS HARD AT WORK ON'THE MIDDLE EAST PEACE
PROCESS AT CAMP DAVID, SENDS HER PERSONAL REGARDS.
SINCERELY,
