2. IN A MEETING ON OCTOBER 27 IN WINNIPEG, FOREIGN 
MINISTER ANDRE OUELLET RAISED THREE ISSUES REGARDING 
HAITI WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT. IF POSSIBLE, 
AMBASSADOR IS REQUESTED TO CONVEY THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
RESPONSE TO FM OUELLET. IF THIS CANNOT BE DONE ON A 
TIMELY BASIS, POST MAY RELAY RESPONSE TO APPROPRIATE DFAT 
COUNTERPART, WITH THE REQUEST THAT THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S 
RESPONSE BE CONVEYED TO THE FOREIGN MINISTER- _ 
-- WILL THE U.S. ALLOW FRAPH TO CONTINUE„TOTOPERATE/7-
THE MULTINATIONAL FORCE HAS ALREADY CRACKED DOWN HEAVILY 
ON FRAPH BOTH IN PORT AU PRINCE AND THE PROVINCES,__THE. 
MNF HAS DETAINED FRAPH LEADERS AND CONFISCATED:THEIR:7 
ARMS. ULTIMATELY, HOWEVER, THE DECISION_WHETHER_ERAPH. _ _ 
WILL CONTINUE TO OPERATE RESTS WITH THE7GOERNMENT OF 
HAITI. THE HAITIAN SENATE PASSED A BILL IN MID-OCTOBER 
ABOLISHING PARAMILITARY ORGANIZATIONS LIKE FRAPH. THUS, 
WE WOULD ANTICIPATE THAT, ONCE THE CHAMBER OF DEPUTIES-_ 
ACTS ON THE LEGISLATION, FRAPH WOULD NO LONGER OPERATE. 
-- IS THE WEAPONS BUY BACK PROGRAM A FAILURE? NO, IT_IS 
A SUCCESS. THE USG HAS ALREADY BOUGHT BACK MORE THAN 
4900 WEAPONS AND SPENT ABOUT 600,000. IN ADDITION, MORE 
THAN 7100 WEAPONS HAVE BEEN SEIZED BY THE MNF. THE BUY 
BACK PROGRAM WILL CONTINUE. IT IS FULLY FUNCTIONAL IN 
PORT AU PRINCE AND CAP HAITIEN. WHILE LABOR-INTENSIVE, 
THIS PROGRAM HAS THE SUPPORT AND ADMINISTRATIVE PERSONNEL 
TO CONDUCT ITS OPERATIONS IN THESE TWO LOCATIONS. IT IS, 
OF COURSE, ONLY PART OF A BROADER SET OF POLICIES 
DESIGNED TO DISARM PARAMILITARY GROUPS AND LIMIT ACCESS 
TO AUTOMATIC/HEAVY WEAPONRY. 
-- ARE WEAPONS COMING ACROSS FROM THE DOMINICAN 
REPUBLIC? WE ARE NOT AWARE OF THE SMUGGLING OF ARMS INTO 
HAITI FROM THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC. WE WILL CONTINUE TO 
MONITOR THIS CLOSELY. 
3. ON A SEPARATE SUBJECT, REFTEL, WHICH HAS BEEN

HAITI MENTIONED THAT ED BROADBENT, DIRECTOR OF THE

DEVELOPMENT, WOULD BE PROVIDING PRESIDENT ARISTIDE A 
60-PAGE DOCUMENT ON THE TRUTH COMMISSION. WE WOULD 
APPRECIATE POST OBTAINING A COPY OF THE BROADBENT REPORT 
FOR THE DEPARTMENT (ARA/HWG). PLEASE ALSO POUCH A COPY 
TO EMBASSY PORT AU PRINCE'S POLITICAL SECTION.
