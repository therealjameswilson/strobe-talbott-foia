April 8, 1993 
I shall miss you at the Carnegie Endowment meetings, but am delighted that 
you are where you are now. The new position that was created for you will be 
awesomely challenging, but its creation was most timely, and I am sure that you will 
meet the challenges very well indeed -- even if you don' t get much sleep in doing 
so. 
Judging by the results so far, which include last week' s summit, but more 
importantly the changes in attitude and policy that led to that summit, have been 
most welcome and most excellent, and indeed most unexpected in view of our prior 
approach to foreign policy in Russia. As a member of the Russian American 
Banking Forum who has seen some of the problems the Russians are struggling with 
at ground level, I m very pleased that our country has finally come around to focus 
on what is important. 
We all wish you well. 
Congratulations, best wishes, and good luck -- lots of good luck. 
Sincerely, 
Richard A. Debs
