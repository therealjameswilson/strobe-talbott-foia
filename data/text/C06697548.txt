13. -46+-THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THIS IS INCORRECT. 
AT THE MOSCOW NUCLEAR SUMMIT MEETING, CHIRAC PUT 
PRESIDENT CLINTON IN A DIFFICULT POSITION BY STATING 
PUBLICLY THAT PARIS SUPPORTS RUSSIAN ENTRY INTO THE G-7, 
WHILE HINTING THAT THE USG STILL NEEDS TO APPROVE THIS

CONTINUE TO EXIST AS A DISTINCT ENTITY BECAUSE IT HAS 
IMPORTANT ISSUES TO ADDRESS. THE PRESIDENT STRESSED THE 
G-7 NEEDS TO SURVIVE AND PROSPER, INCLUDING AT LYONS. 
WE HAD THOUGHT YELTSIN UNDERSTOOD THE PRESIDENT. 
14. -(a)--SMITH SAID THE GOC AGREED WITH THE USG ON THIS 
POSITION. HOWEVER, THE GERMANS AND JAPANESE MAY NOT YET 
BE CLEAR ABOUT THE RUSSIAN ROLE AT LYONS. IT WOULD BE 
UNHELPFUL FOR BONN AND TOKYO TO BE SURPRISED ON THIS 
MATTER. 
15. 48+ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THE USG WILL REMIND

16. -(44- SMITH SAID GERMAN FM KINKEL, WHO WAS IN OTTAWA,

SECRETARY ADDED THE SECRETARY FEELS THE 
SIXTEEN-PLUS-ONE FORMAT SHOULD NOT BE USED AT BERLIN. 
IT WOULD PLACE THE NATO ENLARGEMENT ISSUE AT FRONT AND 
CENTER DURING THE RUSSIAN ELECTION SEASON. WE SHOULD 
PLACE AN EMPHASIS ON BOSNIA AT BERLIN, AS, IT IS AN ISSUE 
ON WHICH THE U.S. AND RUSSIA ARE WORKING WELL TOGETHER. 
17. *04- SMITH ASKED WHETHER THE USG IS PERMANENTLY 
OPPOSED TO THE SIXTEEN-PLUS-ONE FORMAT, WHICH THE GOC

TO SIXTEEN-PLUS-ONE BUT WE DO NOT WANT TO CONVENE ONE 
BEFORE THE RUSSIAN ELECTION.'
