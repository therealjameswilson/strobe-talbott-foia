SUBJECT: DEPSECSTATE VISIT: BURMA -- AUSSIE FM EVANS

Declassification Authority: Geoffrey W. Chapman, Senior Reviewer, 
A/GIS/IPS 5/15/2019 
2. SUMMARY: IN TALKS FEB. 1, DEPSEC TALBOTT AND PM 
EVANS EXCHANGED NOTES ON THE DISCOURAGING SITUATION IN 
BURMA. EVANS SAID HIS LONG TALK IN JANUARY WITH AUNG 
S.AN SUU KYI'S HUSBAND INDICATED THE SITUATION IS 
SOMEWHAT ENCOURAGING BUT MORE EXTERNAL PRESSURE IS 
NEEDED. THUS, EVANS HAS DECIDED NOT TO VISIT BURMA AS 
PLANNED, AND THINKS A NEW ROUND OF DEMARCHES IS NEEDED 
AROUND THE REGION. HE HAS THE IMPRESSION ASSK SUPPORTS 
HOLDING SLORC TO BENCHMARKS AND IS PREPARED TO BE 
CONCILIATORY IF SHE IS RELEASED. HE SOLICITED INFO ON 
CORRUPTION SO IT CAN BE USED TO DISCREDIT THE SLORC, 
AND WAS CRITICAL OF FORMER PM HAWKE'S COMMERCIALLY-
INSPIRED VISIT TO RANGOON LAST MONTH. EVANS SAID HE 
HAS NO IMPRESSION THE ASEANS WANT TO UPGRADE BURMESE 
ATTENDANCE AT THE MINISTERIAL. END SUMMARY. 
3. DURING A MEETING AND DINNER IN CANBERRA ON FEB. 1, 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND EAP A/S LORD DESCRIBED 
STRONG USG DISAPPOINTMENT THAT THE BURMESE REGIME HAD 
ONLY ALLOWED A NARCOTICS FIELD SURVEY BUT HAD NEITHER 
FREED AUNG SAN SUU KYI (ASSK) NOR GIVEN THE RED CROSS 
PRISON ACCESS, AND HAD ATTACKED MANERPLAW -- ALL IN THE 
WAKE OF THE HUBBARD MISSION. AGREEING, FM GARETH EVANS 
SAID HE HAD DECIDED NOT TO VISIT BURMA LATER IN FEBRUARY 
AS PLANNED. THIS DECISION GREW IN PART OUT OF HIS LONG, 
ONE-ON-ONE, PROBING TALK WITH ASSK'S HUSBAND, MICHAEL 
ARIS, IN JANUARY AT OXFORD. EVANS ASKED THAT THIS 
SOURCE, AND ARIS'S VIEWS THAT FOLLOW, BE STRICTLY 
PROTECTED.
