SUBJECT: RADISIC PROPOSES, DODIK AGREES TO COMPROMISE TO 
BREAK RS GOVERNMENT DEADLOCK 
1. (U) CLASSIFIED BY: SPECIAL REPRESENTATIVE ROBERT S 
GELBARD. REASON: 1.5(B) AND (D) 
2. SUMMARY: IN A MEETING WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT, SPECIAL REPRESENTATIVE GELBARD, AND HIGH 
REPRESENTATIVE WESTENDORP, SERB PRESIDENCY MEMBER RADISIC 
PROPOSED A COMPROMISE AIMED AT BREAKING THE DEADLOCK IN 
FORMING A NEW GOVERNMENT IN THE RS. RADISIC SAID SLOGA 
HAD WORN DOWN POPLASEN AND THE SDS/RADICALS, AND SAID 
THEY WOULD PROBABLY SETTLE FOR THREE OR FOUR POSITIONS IN 
A DODIK-LED GOVERNMENT, BUT ONE (KNOWING ONE OF THE USG'S 
RED LINES) THAT WOULD BE MULTIETHNIC, INCLUDING.THE 
FEDERATION PARTIES. RADISIC SAID ONLY ACCEPTABLE SDS 
'TECHNOCRATS' WOULD BE CONSIDERED. GELBARD NOTED THAT 
THE BOSNIAKS WOULD LIKELY DEMAND POSITIONS IN THE 
GOVERNMENT IN EXCHANGE FOR THEIR SUPPORT. RADISIC AGREED 
THIS WAS ACHIEVABLE. GELBARD TOLD RADISIC THAT THE U.S. 
WOULD SUPPORT THIS MOVE ONLY IF DODIK WAS PM, IF THE 
RADICALS WERE EXCLUDED FROM GOVERNMENT, THE USG AND OHR 
COULD VET POTENTIAL SDS TECHNOCRATS, AND IF THE 
GOVERNMENT WAS MULTIETHNIC WITH BOSNIAK SUPPORT AND NON-
SERB MINISTERS. RADISIC AGREED. TALBOTt CALLED ON 
RADISIC TO EXERCISE LEADERSHIP IN OVERCOMING THE INERTIA 
THAT HAD SET IN BETWEEN POPLASEN AND SLOGA, GELBARD 
LATER BRIEFED DODIK ON RADISIC'S PROPOSAL. DODIK SAID HE 
COULD ACCEPT THIS COMPROMISE, BUT SAID IT WOULD REQUIRE 
SOME EFFORT TO CONCLUDE A DEAL. END SUMMARY. 
3. l'64. DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, SPECIAL REPRESENTATIVE 
GELBARD, AND HIGH REPRESENTATIVE CARLOS WESTENDORP MET 
WITH BOSNIAN PRESIDENCY MEMBER ZIVKO RADISIC DECEMBER 16 
ON THE MARGINS OF'THE PEACE IMPLEMENTATION COUNCIL 
MINISTERIAL. WESTENDORP UNDERSCORED THE IMPORTANCE 
PLACED BY THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY ON BREAKING THE 
DEADLOCK IN THE RS AND FORMING A DODIK-LED GOVERNMENT. 
TALBOTT REPEATED HIS MESSAGE OF DECEMBER 15 THAT THE 
UNITED STATES SUPPORTS AND WILL CONTINUE TO SUPPORT ONLY 
DODIK AS PM. WESTENDORP AND RADISIC BOTH SAID THE 
CURRENT NOMINEE FOR RS PRIME MINISTER, KALINIC, WILL 
LIKELY RETURN HIS MANDATE SOON SINCE HE HAS INSUFFICIENT 
SUPPORT IN THE RS ASSEMBLY TO FORM A GOVERNMENT. 
4. TN, RADISIC SAID HE AND SLOGA CONTINUE TO SUPPORT ONLY 
DODIK FOR RS PRIME MINISTER. THE PROBLEM, HE CONTINUED, 
IS GETTING DODIK ELECTED, SINCE ONLY POPLASEN HAS THE 
'LEGAL AUTHORITY TO NOMINATE A CANDIDATE FOR PM. SO FAR 
POPLASEN HAS RESISTED AND WILL LIKELY CONTINUE TO RESIST 
NOMINATING DODIK. RADISIC IS IN REGULAR CONTACT WITH 
POPLASEN URGING HIM TO RECONSIDER, ARGUING THAT THE 
GOVERNMENT FUNCTIONS WELL AND HAS BEEN SUCCESSFUL, HAS 
BEGUN A NEEDED REFORM PROGRAM, AND HAS THE SUPPORT OF THE

5.  (C) RADISIC SAID THAT POPLASEN, THE SDS AND RADICALS 
ARE SLOWLY GIVING,UP ON BREAKING SLOGA. HE BELIEVES THEY 
ARE READY TO ACCEPT A DEAL WHERE DODIK WOULD BECOME PRIME

THE SDS RECEIVING MINISTERIAL POSITIONS IN THE RS 
GOVERNMENT, NAMING THE DIRECTOR OF THE REFINERY IN SRPSKI

SUCH A DEAL COULD BREAK THE CURRENT DEADLOCK BETWEEN 
POPLASEN AND SLOGA. RADISIC SAID HE HAD DISCUSSED WITH 
DODIK THE POSSIBILITY OF HAVING THE SDS NAME TEN. POSSIBLE 
CANDIDATES AND LETTING SLOGA CHOOSE AMONG THEM. (DODIK 
LATER,SAID HE HAD NOT BEEN CONSULTED ON THIS IDEA.) 
RADISIC SAID BEFORE THEY COULD PURSUE THIS PLAN, SLOGA'S 
LEADERSHIP WOULD NEED TO DISCUSS AND APPROVE IT. RADISIC 
PLANS TO SEE PLAVSIC IMMEDIATELY UPON HIS RETURN TO BANJA 
LUKA. 
SEPARATELY, RADISIC SAID HE AND PLAVSIC HAVE 
AGREED ON A SERB CANDIDATE FOR THE CO-CHAIR OF THE 
COUNCIL OF MINISTERS (WHICH WAS LATER CONFIRMED WITH 
DODIK AS SVETOZAR MIHAJLOVIC. HE SAID HE HAD BEEN 
UNWILLING TO ANNOUNCE THIS AT MADRID SINCE THE CURRENT 
SERB CO-CHAIR, BORO BOSIC, WAS ATTENDING AND IT WOULD BE 
UNDIPLOMATIC TO ANNOUNCE HIS REPLACEMENT AT THE PIC. HE 
ASKED THAT GELBARD ATTEND THE NEXT PRESIDENCY SESSION 
DECEMBER 23. GELBARD SAID HE WOULD BE UNABLE TO DO SO, 
BUT SAID HE WOULD ASK AMBASSADOR-KAUZLARICH TO ATTEND. 
7. -4.7*-RADISIC SAID THAT POPLASEN HAS BEEN ASKED BY 
THIRTY-THREE RS ASSEMBLY DELEGATES TO DISMISS THE CURRENT 
(DODIK) GOVERNMENT, SOMETHING HE IS OBLIGED TO DO WHEN 
JUST TWENTY DELEGATES CALL FOR HIM TO DO SO. RADISIC 
SAID THE QUESTION IS SIMPLY WHEN POPLASEN WILL MOVE. IN 
THE MEANTIME, RADISIC URGED THAT THE HIGH REPRESENTATIVE 
NOT USE HIS POWERS TO REMOVE POPLASEN, SAYING THAT IF NEW 
ELECTIONS WERE HELD, SLOGA MIGHT DO WORSE THAN IN 
SEPTEMBER.

INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY UNDERSTANDS THE DIFFICULT 
POSITION IN WHICH RADISIC AND SLOGA FIND THEMSELVES, BUT 
THAT THE IC AND THE USG HAVE GREAT CONFIDENCE IN RADISIC. 
GELBARD SAID IT IS DIFFICULT TO OVERSTATE THE IMPORTANCE 
OF'GETTING THE RIGHT GOVERNMENT IN THE RS. BEFORE DODIK 
AND SLOGA, THE RS WAS RECEIVING NO ASSISTANCE AND WAS - 
DEMONIZED BY THE WEST. NOW, THANKS TO THE LEADERSHIP OF 
RADISIC, PLAVSIC, AND DODIK, THE RS HAS MOVED ON TOWARDS 
A DEMOCRATIC SOCIETY. GELBARD SAID THAT JUST AS THE U.S. 
RECOGNIZES THAT SERBS ARE NOT ALL THE SAME, WE RECOGNIZE 
THAT NOT ALL SDS MEMBERS ARE IDENTICAL. THEREFORE, THE 
U.S. WOULD NOT OPPOSE SDS PARTICIPATION IN THE RS

10. (-e) GELBARD TOLD RADISIC THAT ANY DEAL MUST SATISFY 
FOUR U.S. CONDITIONS. IT MUST: 
- CONTINUE WITH DODIK AS PRIME MINISTER; 
- NOT INCLUDE ANY MEMBERS OF THE SERB RADICAL PARTY; 
- HAVE USG AND OHR APPROVAL OF SDS TECHNOCRATS; AND 
- BE MULTIETHNIC,.WITH SUPPORT FROM FEDERATION PARTIES 
AND INCLUDE BOSNIAK MINISTERS PLUS A BOSNIAK DEPUTY 
ASSEMBLY SPEAKER. 
RADISIC AGREED, AND SAID KEEPING THE RADICALS OUT OF THE 
RS GOVERNMENT WAS POSSIBLE. WESTENDORP POINTED OUT THE 
IMPORTANCE OF GIVING THE BOSNIAKS GOVERNMENT POSITIONS TO 
ENSURE THEIR SUPPORT FOR THE GOVERNMENT. WITHOUT THEIR 
SUPPORT, HE SAID, THE NEW GOVERNMENT WILL BE FRAGILE, 
READY TO FALL AS SOON AS THE SDS AND RADICALS WITHDRAW 
THEIR SUPPORT.

THE DEAL.
