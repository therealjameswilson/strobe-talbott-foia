SUBJECT: DEPUTY SECRETARY RAISES POSSIBLE VISIT OF YUGOSLAV 
DEFENSE MINISTER TO ATHENS 
RELEASE IN FULL 
CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR R. NICHOLAS BURNS. REASONS 1.5 (B,D). [DECLASSIFIED] 
1., {-S-) DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT INFORMED GREEK DEFENSE MINISTER 
TSOHATZOPOULOS IN THEIR APRIL 5 MEETING THAT WE HAD PICKED UP 
FROM THE BULGARIANS NEWS OF A POSSIBLE VISIT OF THE YUGOSLAV 
DEFENSE MINISTER TO ATHENS. 
2. (-E3-) SPECIFICALLY, BULGARIAN AUTHORITIES INFORMED US THAT THE 
YUGOSLAV MINISTER, ACCOMPANIED BY HIS DAUGHTER AND OTHERS, WAS IN 
SOFIA AND EN ROUTE TO ATHENS. HE PLANNED TO MEET WITH GREEK 
DEFENSE MANUFACTURERS HERE TO PURCHASE SUPPLIES FOR THE SERB WAR 
EFFORT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED TSOHATZOPOULOS TO DO 
EVERYTHING POSSIBLE TO PREVENT THE SERB FROM ANY CONTACTS WITH 
THE GREEK GOVERNMENT AND TO LET US KNOW IF HE DID TURN UP IN 
ATHENS. 
, 3. 4S4 TSOHATZOPOULOS REPLIED THAT THIS WAS THE FIRST HE HAD 
HEARD OF SUCH A.VISIT. GREECE WOULD HAVE NOTHING TO DO WITH IT 
AND HE WOULD BE IN TOUCH WITH AMBASSADOR BURNS SHOULD THE 
INDIVIDUAL APPEAR IN GREECE.
