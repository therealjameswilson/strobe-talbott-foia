JEWISH COMMUNITY IN RUSSIA. ANTI-SEMITIC RHETORIC 
HEATED UP DURING THE ELECTION CAMPAIGN, ESPECIALLY ON 
THE PART OF THE COMMUNIST PARTY. THE VICTORY BY 
PRESIDENT YELTSIN SHOULD GO A LONG WAY TOWARD ENDING 
THAT. HE WAS PLEASED WITH JEWISH COMMUNITY RELATIONS 
WITH THE ORTHODOX CHURCH, THOUGH HE SAID THE JEWISH 
COMMUNITY RECOGNIZES THAT ORTHODOXY IS ON ITS WAY TO 
BECOMING RUSSIArS "STATE RELIGION." HE EXPRESSED 
CONFIDENCE IN THE ABILITY OF THE RUSSIAN JEWISH 
COMMUNITY TO BEGIN TO TAKE CARE OF ITSELF. WEALTHY 
JEWISH BUSINESSMEN AND BANKERS, UNITED BY MOST BANK 
PRESIDENT VLADIMIR GUSINSKIY IN THE NEWLY-FOUNDED 
RUSSIAN JEWISH CONGRESS, ARE NOW INVESTING MONEY IN THE 
DEVELOPMENT AND REVIVING OF COMMUNITIES THROUGHOUT 
RUSSIA. "WHEREAS ONLY A FEW YEARS AGO JEWS STILL HID 
THEIR BACKGROUNDS, NOW THEY ARE PROUD ABOUT IT. THIS 
IS A VERY POSITIVE STEP FOR THE COMMUNITY," HE SAID. 
8. ASKED WHETHER THE RECENT COMMENTS BY RUSSIAN 
SECURITY COUNCIL SECRETARY GENERAL ALEKSANDR LEBED ON 
RELIGION IN RUSSIA HAD CAUSED HIM CONCERN, THE RABBI 
ASCRIBED LEBED'S OMISSION OF JUDAISM TO HIS LACK OF 
EXPERIENCE WITH SUCH QUESTIONS AND HIS IGNORANCE OF 
JUDAISM. RABBI GOLDSCHMIDT SOUGHT TO DISTINGUISH 
BETWEEN LEBED'S PUBLIC RELATIONS -- WHICH HE THOUGHT 
WOULD NOW BE CORDIAL TO THE JEWISH COMMUNITY -- AND HIS 
PRIVATE PERSONA. IT IS QUITE LIKELY THAT LEBED HARBORS 
PREJUDICES AGAINST JEWS, HE SAID. BUT AS LONG AS THAT 
DOES NOT TRANSLATE INTO ADVOCACY OF ANTI-SEMITIC 
POLICIES, THEN HE IS NOT WORRIED. THE RABBI SAID HE 
AND OTHER JEWISH COMMUNITY LEADERS HOPE TO OPEN A 
DIALOGUE WITH LEBED, A MAN THEY BELIEVE MAY SOMEDAY BE 
THE PRESIDENT OF RUSSIA.
