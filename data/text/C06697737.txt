4. -ttl- AGNELLI OPENED BY REPORTING SHE HAD HEARD THE 
SECRETARY WAS GOING TO THE MIDDLE EAST. THE SECRETARY 
RESPONDED THAT PRESIDENT CLINTON HAD ASKED HIM TO TRAVEL 
TO THAT REGION, AND THAT HE WAS JUST ABOUT TO CALL 
AGNELLI TO INFORM HER. THE SECRETARY CONTINUED THAT HE 
WAS ALSO CALLING BECAUSE HE THOUGHT IT WOULD BE PRUDENT 
TO POSTPONE THE LUXEMBOURG MEETING, AS HE WANTED TO 
DEVOTE ALL OF HIS ENERGIES TO REACHING AN AGREEMENT 
ENDING THE FIGHTING IN THE MIDDLE EAST. AFTER AN 
AGREEMENT, HE WOULD TURN HIS ATTENTION TO THE FOLLOW UP 
TO THE SHARM AL-SHEIK SUMMIT, THOUGH NOT TOO MUCH IN THE 
FUTURE, HE EMPHASIZED. 
5. 4.-11' AGNELLI RESPONDED THAT SHE THOUGHT THE.BEST 
THING WOULD BE FOR HER TO JOIN THE SECRETARY IN THE 
MIDDLE EAST TO "TRY TO TALK SOME SENSE INTO THOSE 
PEOPLE", THEN CONTINUE TO LUXEMBOURG AS PLANNED. THE 
SECRETARY SAID HE DID NOT THINK AN AGREEMENT ENDING THE 
FIGHTING COULD BE REACHED SOON ENOUGH. HE SAIDTHAT HE 
WAS MAKING PHONE CALLS TO ATTEMPT TO ACHIEVE A TEMPORARY 
CEASE-FIRE, BUT THAT A LONGER TERM SOLUTION WOULD TAKE 
TIME. HE WORRIED THAT THE LUXEMBOURG MEETING WOULD NOT 
BE EFFECTIVE IN THE CURRENT ATMOSPHERE. 
6. 44} AGNELLI THEN ASKED IF THE SECRETARY THOUGHT SHE 
SHOULD JOIN HIM IN THE MIDDLE EAST, SO THAT JOINT 
U.S./EU PRESSURE COULD BE APPLIED. THE SECRETARY 
REQUESTED HE BE GIVEN TIME TO REFLECT ON HER REQUEST. 
HE EXPLAINED THAT HE HAD MUCH EXPERIENCE IN THE REGION, 
AND THAT THE PARTIES WERE USED TO DEALING WITH HIM. HE 
NOTED THAT THE ISRAELIS HAD RESISTED WORKING WITH FRENCH 
FOREIGN MINISTER DE,CHARETTE DURING A RECENT VISIT. 
HOWEVER, THE SECRETARY SAID HE DID NOT WANT TO TURN AWAY 
AGNELLI'S OFFER WITHOUT THINKING 'ABOUT IT FIRST, SO HE 
WOULD CALL HER APRIL 19. 
7. 4S4.- AGNELLI ASKED THE SECRETARY IF SHE SHOULD 
CANCEL THE LUXEMBOURG MEETING IMMEDIATELY, OR WAIT UNTIL 
THE SECRETARY HAD BEEN IN THE REGION A DAY. THE SEC-
RETARY RESPONDED THAT, WHILE THE SHOOTING OF TOURISTS 
IN CAIRO THAT MORNING HIGHLIGHTED THE IMPORTANCE OF 
LUXEMBOURG, HE THOUGHT THE ODDS WERE "A HUNDRED TO ONE" 
OF HAVING A SUCCESSFUL MEETING. AGNELLI SAID SHE 
UNDERSTOOD THE SECRETARY'S POINT. SHE SAID SHE WOULD 
EXPECT A CALL FROM HIM ON APRIL 19, AND THAT, AS SHE 
WOULD BE TRAVELING IN ALBANIA THAT DAY AND MIGHT NOT BE 
AVAILABLE, THE SECRETARY COULD TALK TO ANY OF HER 
DEPUTIES. THE SECRETARY SAID HE APPRECIATED THE 
CONFIDENCE HE AND AGNELLI SEARED IN EACH OTHER. 
(U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1326 EST. 
8. END TEXT.
