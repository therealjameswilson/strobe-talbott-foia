2. SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARIES TALBOTT AND DEUTCH, 
DEPUTY NAT/ONAL SECURITY ADVISOR BERGER, VICE 
PRESIDENTIAL NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR FUERTH, AND 
AMBASSADOR DOBBINS MET W/TH ACTING PRIME MINISTER MALVAL 
AND THE LEGITIMATE MINISTERS OF FINANCE, EDUCATION AND 
PUBLIC WORKS AT MALVAL'S HOME ON OCTOBER 7. DEPSEC 
TALBOTT TOLD MALVAL THAT THE DELEGATION WAS IN PORT AU-
PRINCE TO SEEK ADVICE FROM HAITIANS ON THE STEPS WHICH 
NEED TO BE TAKEN IN THE DAYS AND WEEKS AHEAD TO PREPARE 
FOR THE RETURN OF PRESIDENT ARISTIDE AND TO HELP HAITIAN 
GOVERNMENT INSTITUTIONS TO FUNCTION AGAIN. REGARDING THE 
LEGITIMATE GOVERNMENT, MALVAL SAID HE WAS AWARE OF 
PROPOSALS THAT A FEW MEMBERS OF THE CABINET MOVE BACK 
INTO THEIR OFFICES WHILE PROVISIONAL PRESIDENT JONASSAINT 
AND OTHER DE FACTO MINISTERS REMAIN. MALVAL SAID THAT IT 
WAS UNACCEPTABLE FOR ANY LEGAL CABINET MEMBER TO RETURN 
TO HIS OR HER MINISTRY BEFORE JONASSAINT LEAVES THE 
PALACE. MALVAL, SUPPORTED BY THE ACCOMPANYING CABINET 
MINISTERS, INSISTED THAT THIS WAS A MORAL ISSUE, RATHER 
THAN A POLITICAL ONE, AND THEREFORE NOT SUBJECT TO 
COMPROMISE. MALVAL SUGGESTED THAT U.S. FORCES COULD TAKE 
OVER THE PALACE TO PREPARE IT FOR PRESIDENT ARISTIDE'S 
RETURN, THEREBY GIVING JONASSAINT A FACE-SAVING WAY TO 
ARGUE THAT HE HAD NOT RESIGNED BUT FOUND IT IMPOSSIBLE TO 
CARRY ON AS PRESIDENT. REGARDING ACTION IN PARLIAMENT, - 
MALVAL WAS OPTIMISTIC THAT THE SENATE WOULD COMPLETE WORK__ 
ON THE AMNESTY LAW AND THAT ALL FORMALITIES WOULD BE 
COMPLETED EARLY NEXT WEEK, THUS REMOVING ANY OBSTACLES TO - 
CEDRAS'S RET/REMENT AND DEPARTURE. DEPUTY NATIONAL 
SECURITY ADVISOR BERGER WRAPPED UP THE SESSION ASSURING --
MALVAL AND CABINET MEMBERS THAT THE U.S. COMMITMENT TO 
THE RESTORATION OF HAITIAN DEMOCRACY IS DEEP-ROOTED AND 
LONG-LASTING. END SUMMARY

9. MALVAL WELCOMED DEPSEC TALBOTT'S ASSURANCE THAT

AND FOR U.S. AND MULTINATIONAL FORCES. DEPSEC TALBOTT 
NOTED GENERAL SHELTON'S PROMISE THAT HE WOULD DO HIS BEST 
TO CREATE THOSE CONDITIONS.

ACTION IN THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY 
14. IN A BRIEF DISCUSSION OF DEVELOPMENTS IN THE 
NATIONAL ASSEMBLY, MALVAL SAID THE CABINET SHARED THE 
DELEGATION'S PLEASURE AT THE QUICK ACTION BY THE CHAMBER 
OF DEPUTIES ON THE AMNESTY LAW. MALVAL SAID THE CABINET 
HAD LOBBIED PARLIAMENTARIANS HARD TO TAKE ACTION, AND 
THEY WERE PLEASED TO SEE THE RESULTS. MALVAL PREDICTED 
(CORRECTLY) THAT THE SENATE WOULD MOVE QUICKLY ON THE 
LAW, PERHAPS VOTING ON IT BEFORE THE END OF THE DAY OR AT 
THE LATEST OVER THE WEEKEND. ONCE PASSED, HE THOUGHT THE 
PRESIDENT WOULD SIGN IT QUICKLY WITH PUBLICATION_IN THE 
OFFICIAL GAZETTE (THE MONITEUR) IMMEDIATELY THEREAFTER. 
PERHAPS, MALVAL THOUGHT, ALL ACTION ON THE-LAW WOULD BE 
COMPLETED EARLY NEXT WEEK. 
15. CLOSING THE MEETING, DEPUTY NATIONAL SECURITY 
ADVISOR BERGER TOLD MALVAL AND HIS CABINET MEMBERS-THAT 
THE U.S. IS PLEASED BY THE PROGRESS MADE SO_FAR IN - 
HELPING HAITIANS RESUME THEIR LEGITIMATE DEMOCRATIC - 
INSTITUTIONS. THE COMMITMENT OF THE GOVERNMENT AND 
PEOPLE OF THE UNITED STATES TO THIS PROCESS IS, HE ADDED, 
A DEEP-SEATED ONE. OURS IS A LONG TERM COMMITMENT, HE 
CONCLUDED. 
16. THE DEPUTY SECRETARIES' PARTY DID NOT HAVE AN
