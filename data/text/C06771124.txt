2. -1.S1— SUMMARY: IN A MARCH 11 MEETING BETWEEN DEPSEC TALBOT 
AND UK SECDEF ROBERTSON THE TWO: 
- DISCUSSED WHAT KIND OF FORCE WOULD BE APPROPRIATE FOR 
DEPLOYMENT IN ALBANIA; 
- AGREED THAT FURTHER DISCUSSION OF DEVELOPING CAPABILITIES 
FOR MULTINATIONAL SPECIALIZED UNITS AMONG NATO ALLIES 
IS DESIRABLE;
- REVIEWED THE STATE OF PLAY ON KOSOVO AND AGREED ON THE 
NEED TO BE READY TO USE FORCE;
- DISCUSSED THE NATO SUMMIT MANDATE ISSUE; AND 
- AGREED ON THE IMPORTANCE OF AVOIDING TARGETING DUAL USE 
FACILITIES IN IRAQ. END SUMMARY. 
3. (C) ON MARCH 11, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOT MET WITH UK 
SECRETARY OF DEFENSE GEORGE ROBERTSON TO DISCUSS KOSOVO, OTHER 
BALKAN AREA CONCERNS, AND NATO-RELATED ISSUES. THE AMBASSADOR 
PHILIP LADER, DEPUTY POLITICAL COUNSELOR MARCIE RIES, 
EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT PHILIP GOLDBERG, SPECIAL ASSISTANT JOHN 
BASS AND POLOFF (NOTETAKER) ACCOMPANIED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY. 
MOD POLICY DIRECTOR RICHARD HATFIELD, MOD ASSISTANT UNDER-
SECRETARY EDGAR BUCKLEY, AND PERSONAL PRIVATE SECRETARY TO THE 
SECDEF TOM MCKANE ALSO PARTICI.PATED. 
4. ROBERTSON APPLAUDED A NEW ATTITUDE AMONG EUROPEAN 
DEFENSE MINISTERS TOWARDS ENHANCING DEFENSE CAPABILITIES. HE 
JUDGED ITALY'S COMMITMENT TO SPENDING 1.5% OF ITS GNP ON 
DEFENSE, ANNOUNCED AT WEHRKUNDE, TO BE "BREAKTHROUGH" EVIDENCE 
OF NEW ITALIAN SERIOUSNESS, CITING ITALIAN FEARS OF 
ALBANIAN REFUGEE INFLUXES AND SIMILAR GERMAN FEARS OF KOSOVAR 
REFUGEE FLOWS TO BE POWERFUL MOTIVATORS. 
5. -(-S-)- ROBERTSON NOTED THAT THE ITALIANS ARE KEEN TO DISCUSS 
DEPLOYMENT OF NATO TROOPS TO ALBANIA. THE DEPSEC EXPLAINED 
THAT THE U.S. THOUGHT AN OSCE-BASED OPERATION, PERHAPS TIED TO 
SUPPORTING KVM DEPLOYMENTS IN KOSOVO, IS A BETTER ALTERNATIVE. 
AT LEAST SOME OF THE PERSONNEL WOULD NEED TO BE ARMED, BOTH TO 
ESTABLISH A DETERRENT AND TO PROVIDE SOME CAPABILITY FOR SELF 
DEFENSE. 
6. 4-G4- BUCKLEY SAID HE THOUGHT THE ITALIANS WOULD NOT AGREE 
TO AN OSCE ALTERNATIVE. HE MENTIONED THAT THE UK IS TRYING TO 
BE MORE SUPPORTIVE OF ITALY IN LIGHT OF CURRENT ITALIAN

ALLIES DEVELOP SUCH CAPABILITIES, BUT EXPLORING WHETHER THOSE 
ALLIES WHO DO HAVE THEM COULD HELP TRAIN AND DEVELOP SIMILAR 
CAPABILITIES AMONG INTERESTED ALLIES AND PARTNERS. THE 
SPANISH ARE WILLING TO PURSUE THE IDEA, ALTHOUGH THEY HAVE 
BUDGETARY CONCERNS. 
8. (4:+)- ROBERTSON NOTED THAT THERE IS A SIMILAR TRAINING 
SCHEME IN BOSNIA, UNDER THE ITALIANS AND ARGENTINES. THE 
FRENCH HAVE SAID NO TO THE IDEA, REASONING THAT THEIR 
GENDARMERIE, ALTHOUGH LARGE, IS HEAVILY TIED UP IN FRANCE, AND 
THUS NOT AVAILABLE FOR SUCH SCHEMES OUTSIDE NATIONAL BORDERS. 
BUCKLEY SAID THAT OTHER COUNTRIES, LIKE THE UK, HAVE 
CONSTITUTIONAL CONCERNS ABOUT MSU'S. ROBERTSON AND TALBOTT 
AGREED THAT THE MATTER MERITS FURTHER DISCUSSION. 
9. i-S,) THE DEPSEC INFORMED ROBERTSON THAT SPE HOLBROOKE'S 
MARCH 10 SESSION WITH MILOSEVIC WAS "A TOTAL STONEWALL." 
ALTHOUGH THERE WAS NO DETAILED READOUT YET, THE USG WAS 
CONCERNED THAT EACH DAY THE KOSOVO ALBANIANS FAILED TO GIVE A 
DEFINITIVE YES REDUCED. CHANCES THAT AN AGREEMENT WOULD BE 
REACHED. THE DEPSEC EXPRESSED CONCERN THAT IN THE EVENT OF A 
NO-NO RESULT GOING INTO THE MARCH 15 PARIS MEETINGS, WE WOULD 
BE IN A DIFFICULT POSITION. NEVERTHELESS, SPE HOLBROOKE GAVE 
MILOSEVIC AN UNEQUIVOCAL MESSAGE THAT THE PENALTY FOR FAILING 
TO AGREE IS "ACTORD." DESPITE LACK OF CLARITY AS TO WHAT 
WOULD COME NEXT, BACKING OFF, OR SUSPENDING PLANS WOULD BE 
WORSE. 
10. f2i ROBERTSON AGREED THAT THE ALLIES MUST "SING A STRONG,

NO PAUSE TO GIVE HIM'TIME TO EITHER RECONSIDER, OR DRAG THE 
PROCESS OUT. ROBERTSON STRESSED THATHMG STILL BELIEVES THAT 
ACTION MAY BE NECESSARY, SAYING THAT THE STRONGER THE ALLIES 
ARE IN ADVANCE, THE LESS LIKELY IT IS THAT FORCE WILL BE USED.

13. (44 ROBERTSON MENTIONED HIS CONCERN THAT BY MARCH 15

HAD NO IDEA OF THE MASSIVE AMOUNTS OF MILITARY HARDWARE IN 
THEIR COUNTRY. NOW AWARE OF IT, THEY HOPE TO PLACE ALL 
PROCUREMENT - AND, CONSEQUENTLY SIGNIFICANT MONETARY FLOWS - 
THROUGH THE MINISTRY OF DEFENSE. IN THE MEANTIME, THEY ARE 
"DEAD SCARED" NATO WILL CHOOSE TO USE FORCES STATIONED IN 
MACEDONIA FOR KOSOVO-RELATED MATTERS, MAKING MACEDONIA A 
TARGET. AMAZINGLY, THE MACEDONIANS ARE UNEXERCISED ABOUT 
UNPREDEP WITHDRAWAL, BELIEVING THAT WITH KVCC, XFOR, ETC., 
THEY WILL NOT MISS THE UNPREDEP TROOPS.

TIMING OF AMERICAN TROOP WITHDRAWAL.
