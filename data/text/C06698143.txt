2. SUMMARY: IN A DECEMBER 6 MEETING IN HIS PARLIAMENT 
OFFICES, PRIME MINISTER HORN TOLD DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT THAT HE WELCOMED THE PEACE ACCORD IN FORMER 
YUGOSLAVIA AS A JOINT AND COMMON CAUSE WHICH COULD NOT 
HAVE BEEN DONE WITHOUT U.S. DIPLOMACY. WHILE AGREE/NG 
THAT THE WORK OF THE WAR CRIMES TRIBUNAL WAS JUSTIFIED, 
PARTICULARLY REGARDING BOSNIAN SERBS, HORN WARNED OF 
NEGATIVE CONSEQUENCES FOR THE PEACEMAKING PROCESS IF 
SERBS IN BOSNIA OR SERBIA PROPER FELT THREATENED BY THE 
PROCESS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CONFIRMED STRONG USG 
SUPPORT TO LET THE TRIBUNAL WORK WITHOUT POLITICAL 
INTERFERENCE, AND THANKED THE HUNGARIANS FOR THEIR 
ASSISTANCE IN THE PEACE TREATY IMPLEMENTATION. 
3. HORN AND THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED ON THE NEED TO 
EXPAND NATO EASTWARDS, DESPITE ANY RUSSIAN CONCERNS. 
RUSSIAN FOREIGN INTELLIGENCE SERVICE DIRECTOR PRIMAKOV 
RECENTLY WARNED HORN THAT RUSSIA WOULD NEVER ACCEPT AN 
EASTWARD EXPANSION OF NATO: IF IT OCCURS, RUSSIA MAY 
RENEGE ON THE INTERMEDIATE NUCLEAR FORCE (INF) 
AGREEMENT. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY REASSURED THE PRIME 
MINISTER THAT THE USG AT THE HIGHEST LEVELS WAS 
DETERMINED TO LET THE NATO EXPANSION PROCESS PROCEED. 
END SUMMARY '

ASSERTED THAT SERBIAN PRESIDENT MILOSEVIC'S BELIEF THAT 
HE WOULD NOT BE CONSIDERED A WAR CRIMINAL HAD PLAYED A 
MAJOR ROLE LEADING UP TO PEACE TREATY. WHILE CLAIMING 
THAT POLITICAL LEADERS IN ALL OF THE FORMER YUGOSLAV 
REPUBLICS COULD BE CONSIDERED AS WAR CRIMINALS, 
PARTICULARLY THE BOSNIAN SERBS, HORN WARNED THAT LETTING 
THE WAR CRIMES TRIBUNAL RUN ITS FULL COURSE COULD LEAD 
TO NEGATIVE CONSEQUENCES FOR THE PEACE TREATY 
IMPLEMENTATION, PARTICULARLY IF THE BOSNIAN SERBS 
BELIEVE THAT MORE THAN THE TOP TWO OR THREE LEADERS 
WOULD BE INDICTED. 
6. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ANSWERED IN STRONG TERMS THAT 
THE USG INTENDS TO LET THE TRIBUNAL COMPLETE ITS WORK 
WITHOUT POLITICAL INTERFERENCE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
SAID THAT HE DID NOT KNOW IF MILOSEVIC WOULD BE INDICTED 
AS A WAR CRIMINAL, BUT THAT THE USG WOULD ACCEPT ANY 
TRIBUNAL DECISION. 
7. HUNGARIAN ETHNIC MINORITY IN VOJVODINA: WHEN ASKED 
ABOUT THE CONDITIONS OF THE HUNGARIAN ETHNIC MINORITY IN 
VOJVODINA IN NORTHERN SERBIA, HORN REPLIED THAT THE 
ABILITY OF ALL REFUGEES TO RETURN TO THEIR HOMELANDS 
WOULD EASE THE SITUATION, BUT THAT THE HUNGARIANS WOULD 
OFTEN HAVE NO HOMES TO GO BACK TO IN EITHER SERBIA OR 
CROATIA. ON THE POSITIVE SIDE, THE ENDING OF 
CONSCRIPTION HELPED ETHNIC HUNGARIANS, AS WOULD THE 
SUSPENSION OF ECONOMIC SANCTIONS. HE HAS URGED ETHNIC 
HUNGARIANS IN SERBIA NOT TO MAKE A 
UTONOMY THEIR TOP 
PRIORITY, BUT WITH LITTLE SUCCESS. IN ANY CASE, THE 
ETHNIC MIX OF VOJVODINA WAS CHANGING IN PERMANENT WAYS 
TO THE DETRIMENT OF THE ETHNIC HUNGARIANS.
