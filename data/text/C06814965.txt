SUBJECT: MOLDOVAN AMBASSADOR-DESIGNATE CESLAV CIOBANU: 
PRESENTATION OF COPIES OF CREDENTIALS 
CLASSIFIED BY MARY WARLICK, DIRECTOR, EUR/WST. REASONS: 
1.5 (B) AND (D). 
RELEASE IN FULL 
1. MOLDOVAN AMBASSADOR-DESIGNATE CESLAV CIOBANU 
PRESENTED COPIES OF HIS CREDENTIALS TO ACTING SECRETARY 
TALBOTT IN A MEETING AT THE DEPARTMENT ON DECEMBER 21. 
CIOBANU (NOW "APPOINTED AMBASSADOR") IS LIKELY TO BEGIN 
SCHEDULING INITIAL CALLS WITH OTHER STATE DEPARTMENT 
OFFICIALS, INCLUDING S/NIS AMBASSADOR SESTANOVICH AND 
COORDINATOR FOR ASSISTANCE TO THE NIS BILL TAYLOR, EARLY IN 
THE NEW YEAR. S/CPR IS WORKING TO SCHEDULE A POSSIBLE 
APPOINTMENT SOMETIME IN JANUARY FOR CIOBANU AND SEVERAL 
OTHER AMBASSADORS TO FORMALLY PRESENT THEIR CREDENTIALS AT 
THE WHITE HOUSE. CIOBANU WAS ACCOMPANIED BY MOLDOVAN DCM 
VLAD SPANU. TALBOTT WAS JOINED BY D SPECIAL ASSISTANT 
KATHLEEN DOHERTY, PROTOCOL OFFICER LAWRENCE DUNHAM AND 
EUR/WST DIRECTOR MARY WARLICK. 
2. tei- TALBOTT CONGRATULATED CIOBANU ON HIS APPOINTMENT 
AND WELCOMED HIM TO THE UNITED STATES, NOTING HIS 
ADMIRATION FOR MOLDOVA AT A TIME WHEN IT IS COPING WITH 
SIGNIFICANT ECONOMIC PROBLEMS. HE ALSO NOTED THE RECENT 
U.S. DECISION TO PROVIDE USD FIVE MILLION IN HUMANITARIAN 
ASSISTANCE TO MOLDOVA TO HELP NEEDY GROUPS COPE WITH THE 
DIFFICULT WINTER. HE ADDED THAT HE HAD MENTIONED MOLDOVA, 
AS ONE EXAMPLE OF THE NEED FOR US ASSISTANCE, IN A MEETING 
TODAY AT THE WHITE HOUSE WITH THE PRESIDENT AND VICE 
PRESIDENT ON THE STATE DEPARTMENT'S BUDGET. HE ALSO 
PRAISED,MOLDOVA FOR ITS COURAGEOUS DECISION TO SELL ITS 
MIGS TO THE U.S., AND ITS CONTINUED COMMITMENT TO ECONOMIC 
REFORM DESPITE THE HARDSHIPS IT.IS FACING AS A RESULT OF 
THE RUSSIAN ECONOMIC CRISIS. ON TRANSNISTRIA, TALBOTT SAID 
MOLDOVA ,COULD COUNT ON THE U.S. TO CONTINUE TO WORK BOTH 
BILATERALLY WITH THE RUSSIANS AND MULTILATERALLY TO SEEK 
FURTHER PROGRESS. 
3. . CIOBANU EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THE OPPORTUNITY 
TO MEET WITH TALBOTT AND CONVEYED REGARDS FROM PRESIDENT 
LUCINSCHI, WHO REMEMBERED THE ACTING SECRETARY'S LAST VISIT 
TO MOLDOVA. HE ALSO CONVEYED AN INVITATION FROM LUCINSCHI 
TO TALBOTT AND THE SECRETARY TO VISIT MOLDOVA IN 1999. 
CIOBANU SAID MOLDOVA WAS PARTICULARLY GRATEFUL FOR THE 
OPPORTUNITY TO PARTICIPATE IN THE SOUTHEAST EUROPE ACTION 
PLAN, NOTING THIS WOULD BE A TOP PRIORITY ON HIS AGENDA. 
HE ADDED THAT A VISIT TO WASHINGTON BY PRESIDENT LUCINSCHI 
NEXT YEAR WOULD HELP TO ADVANCE BILATERAL RELATIONS AND 
PROGRESSON THE WORKPLAN. CIOBANU ALSO EXPRESSED HIS
GRATITUDE FOR USAID SUPPORT IN ADVANCING LAND PRIVATIZATION 
IN MOLDOVA DURING HIS TENURE AS MINISTER OF PRIVATIZATION.
