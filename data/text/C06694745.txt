2. SUMMARY: IN THEIR 4 OCTOBER BILATERAL WITH THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY, INDIAN COMMERCE MINISTER MUKHERJEE AND HIS 
FOREIGN AFFAIRS COLLEAGUES EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT 
PAKISTAN'S EFFORT TO STIR UP THE KASHkIR ISSUE IN THE UN. 
MUKHERJEE SAID INDIA IS READY TO DISCUSS ANYTHING WITH 
KISTAN AND THAT GOI HAS MADE SIGNIFICANT STRIDES IN 
HUMAN RIGHTS AND HAS INVITED THE UN HIGH COMMISSIONER FOR 
HUMAN RIGHTS TO VISIT KASHMIR. TALBOTT SAID HE HAD TRIED 
TO DISSUADE PAKISTAN FROM PUSHING KASHMIR IN NEW YORK; HE 
HAD TOLD THE GOP. THE U.S. OPPOSED LINKING SOUTH ASIAN 
SECURITY ISSUES WITH KASHMIR AND URGED ISLAMABAD TO TRY 
QUIET DIPLOMACY WITH INDIA. THE MINISTER'S SENIOR FOREIGN 
MINISTER COLLEAGUES SAID INDIA IS BEGINNING A MASSIVE 
DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM IN KASHMIR IN PREPARATION FOR 
ELECTIONS NEXT SPRING AND THAT INDIA WILL NOT AGREE TO AN 
EXPANDED UNMOGIP ROLE. TALBOTT CALLED ON INDIA NOT TO 
PRODUCE OR DEPLOY THE PRITHVI MISSILE. SRINIVASAN ASKED 
FOR EXPANDED MILITARY COOPERATION. MUKHERJEE DISCUSSED 
INDIA'S CONCERNS ON TEXTILE IMPORTS FROM THE U.S. BUT WAS 
RELIEVED INDIA WAS NOT PUT ON THE SUPER 301 WATCH LIST. 
END SUMMARY. 
INDIA DEPLORES PAKISTANI TACTICS 
3. INDIAN COMMERCE MINISTER PRANAB MUKHERJEE, FLANKED BY 
SENIOR FOREIGN MINISTRY COLLEAGUES, MET WITH DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT IN NEW YORK 4 OCTOBER. MUKHERJEE SAID 
INDIA IS CONCERNED OVER PAKISTAN'S BEHAVIOR AT THE UN, 
PARTICULARLY FONMIN ALI'S SPEECH TO UNGA EARLIER IN THE 
DAY. HE SAID INDIA WAS CALLING THE GOP'S BLUFF ON HUMAN 
RIGHTS. THE GOI, AT SOME RISK, HAS ESTABLISHED A NATIONAL 
HUMAN RIGHTS COMMISSION WITH WIDE INVESTIGATIVE POWERS. 
IN HIS OWN SPEECH TO THE UNGA, MUKHERJEE ANNOUNCED THAT 
THE GOI IS INVITING THE UN HIGH COMMISSIONER FOR HUMAN 
RIGHTS TO VISIT INDIA, INCLUDING KASHMIR. THE ICRC IS 
ALSO BEING ALLOWED TO VISIT. MEANWHILE, PAKISTAN CLAIMS 
INDIA WILL NOT TALK, BUT IT IS PAKISTAN WHICH REFUSES TO 
TALK. FOR EXAMPLE, THE GOP RENEGED ON A PLANNED BILATERAL 
BREAKFAST IN NEW YORK A FEW DAYS AGO -- EVEN THE MENU HAD 
BEEN AGREED TO. THE SAME THING HAPPENED AT THE HRC IN 
GENEVA LAST MARCH. MUKHERJEE ASKED IF THE U.S. WOULD BE 
WILLING TO HELP PERSUADE PAKISTAN THAT THE CHANNELS OF 
COMMUNICATION ARE OPEN.

8. TALBOTT SAID WHILE HE WAS FRUSTRATED WITH THE LACK OF 
PROGRESS ON REGIONAL ISSUES, HE WILL CONTINUE TO PROMOTE 
PEACE AND ECONOMIC PROGRESS IN THE SUBCONTINENT WITH INDIA 
AS A CENTRAL INTERLOCUTOR. PAKISTAN CLEARLY HAS A ROLE TO 
PLAY AS WELL. THE U.S. CONTINUES TO ADVOCATE RESTRAINT BY 
BOTH COUNTRIES IN DEALING WITH WELL-KNOWN, SENSITIVE 
SECURITY ISSUES. FOR INDIA, THE U.S. URGES CONTINUED 
RESTRAINT ON THE ISSUE OF PRODUCTION AND DEPLOYMENT OF THE 
PRITHVI MISSILE. SRINIVASAN RESPONDED BY QUOTING WHAT 
PRIME MINISTER RAO HAD TOLD AMBASSADOR WISNER, I.E. "THERE 
HAVE BEEN NO NEW DEVELOPMENTS SINCE THE LAST DISCUSSION."

DEFENSE COOPERATION 
9. SRINIVASAN SAID INDIA VERY MUCH LOOKED FORWARD TO 
SECDEF PERRY'S PLANNED VISIT IN JANUARY. HE SAID INDIA 
WAS KEEN TO EXPAND MILITARY COOPERATION, AND WOULD GO 
BEYOND THE U.S. EXPECTATIONS IN INDIA'S REPLY. HE NOTED 
"LIMITATIONS" OF SOME EQUIPMENT OFFERED BY THE U.S. AND 
SAID THE PROVISION OF CERTAIN SYSTEMS WOULD MAKE A 
"QUANTUM JUMP" IN THE RELATIONSHIP. HE HANDED TALBOTT_A______ 
SMALL "NON-PAPER:" WHICH REFERRED TO: 
-INERTIAL NAVIGATION SYSTEMS 
-LASER GUIDED BOMBS, WHICH HAVE BEEN DOWNGRADED IN QUALITY 
AND ARE OF NO USE TO INDIA NOW. 
TEXTILE ISSUE 
10. MUKHERJEE TOLD TALBOTT HE WAS PLEASED -THATIINDIA 
NOT FIGURE ON THE SUPER 301 "WATCHLIST." TALBOTT AGREED - - 
THAT /NDIA HAD MADE CONSIDERABLE PROGRESS AND ESSENTIALLY 
DID NOT DESERVE TO BE ON THE WATCH LIST BUT WE CONTINUE TO 
SEEK GREATER ACCESS TO INDIAN MARKETS. MUKHERJEE 
ACKNOWLEDGED THAT NEGOTIATIONS OVER U.S. TEXTILE IMPORTS 
MUST CONTINUE; HE ARGUED FOR HOLDING OFF ONFURTHER 
RESULTS UNTIL DECEMBER, AFTER THE STATE ELECTIONS NEXT

DISCUSSIONS. HE ADDED HE HAD MENTIONED THE PROBLEM OF 
INDIAN TEXTILE EXPORTS TO THE U.S. WITH THE SECRETARY AT 
THE SAARC TEA.
