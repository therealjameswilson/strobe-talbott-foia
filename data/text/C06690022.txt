Dear Murray: 
Many thanks for your kind letter. My apologies for 
not being more prompt in replying. As perhaps you can 
imagine, my life has been pretty crazy in recent weeks. 
That's no excuse, but it is an explanation. Over the years 
you-'ve been a very special mentor of mine.. I'm sorry that 
it hasn't worked out for us to see more of each other this 
past year, but I'm hoping that I'll have a tad more control 
over my life in the new job than in the old one -- and that 
I'll be able to organize myself sufficiently to have an 
occasional lunch with you. Among other things, I'd profit 
from getting your view of some of the issues I'll have to 
cope with. But most important, I want just want to be able 
to hang out with you from time to time. Let me know when 
you can come by for a sandwich or something. 
A11 the best. 
Sincerely,
