3. SUMMARY: ANDRES CISNEROS, ARGENTINE MFA UNDER 
SECRETARY FOR FOREIGN AFFAIRS AND DE FACTO DEPUTY FOREIGN 
MINISTER, PAID A COURTESY CALL ON THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ON 
SEPTEMBER 10. CISNEROS UNDERSCORED THE GOA COMMITMENT TO 
THE U.S.-ARGENTINE PARTNERSHIP AND CALLED FOR A FORAAL 
CONSULTATIVE MECHANISM TO REINFORCE COOPERATION. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY STRESSED U.S. APPRECIATION FOR THE 
EXCELLENT RELATIONS WITH ARGENTINA, INCLUDING CLOSE 
PERSONAL RELATIONS WITH SENIOR GOA OFFICIALS. HE WELCOMED 
THE SPIRIT OF CISNEROS'S IDEA FOR A CONSULTATIVE MECHANISM 
BUT CAUTIONED THAT WASHINGTON WOULD NEED TO CONSIDER THE 
SPECIFICS. END SUMMARY. 
4. DURING HIS FIRST VISIT TO WASHINGTON SINCE ASSUMING 
THE NUMBER TWO PORTFOLIO IN THE ARGENTINE MFA, UNDER 
SECRETARY CISNEROS CALLED ON DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT 
SEPTEMBER 10. CISNEROS WAS ACCOMPANIED BY SANTOS GONI, 
ARGENTINE EMBASSY DCM AND PABLO TETTAMANTI, ARGENTINE 
EMBASSY POLITICAL COUNSELOR. ARA/BSC ACTING DIRECTOR HEG, 
D STAFF OFFICER SULLIVAN AND ARGENTINE DESK OFFICER DEASY 
ALSO ATTENDED THE 15 MINUTE MEETING. 
5. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CONGRATULATED CISNEROS ON HIS NEW 
POSITION. HE OBSERVED THAT HE HAD WORKED CLOSELY IN 
RECENT YEARS WITH FOREIGN MINISTER DI TELLA AND WITH 
CISNEROS'S PREDECESSOR, FERNANDO PETRELLA, AND LOOKED 
FORWARD TO A SIMILARLY CLOSE AND PRODUCTIVE RELATIONSHIP 
WITH CISNEROS. 
6. CISNEROS OUTLINED HIS NEW RESPONSIBILITIES. HE SAID 
THAT IN HIS PREVIOUS POSITION AT THE ARGENTINE FOREIGN 
MINISTRY (SECRETARY GENERAL), A PRINCIPAL RESPONSIBILITY 
HAD BEEN TO EXPLAIN AND DEFEND THE MENEM GOVERNMENT'S 
PRO-U.S. REORIENTATION OF ARGENTINE FOREIGN POLICY AFTER A 
CENTURY OF BAD RELATIONS BETWEEN THE TWO COUNTRIES. NOW, 
POLLS SHOWED THAT THIS POLICY HAS BECOME VERY POPULAR. 
7. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED THAT U.S.-ARGENTINE 
RELATIONS ARE NOW EXCELLENT. HE NOTED THAT HIS 
RESPONSIBILITIES OVERLAPPED WITH THOSE OF UNDER SECRETARY

8. CISNEROS SAID HE HAD MET DURING HIS VISIT WITH S/CT 
COORDINATOR WILCOX AND ARA DAS ROMERO. OVERALL, ECONOMIC 
INTEGRATION WAS GENERATING INCREASED POLITICAL 
INTERCHANGE. QUICK REACTIONS WERE SOMETIMES NECESSARY, 
IN SOME AREAS, SUCH AS SECURITY RELATIONS, THE U.S. AND 
ARGENTINA HAVE CONSULTATIVE MECHANISMS IN PLACE. CISNEROS 
SAID IT WOULD FACILITATE COOPERATION IF THIS PATTERN WERE 
EXTENDED TO OTHER AREAS. 
9. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID THE USG VERY MUCH FAVORED 
COORDINATION AND CONSULTATION WITH OUR CLOSEST FRIENDS. 
HE ADDED THAT WASHINGTON WOULD NEED TO REVIEW THE 
SPECIFICS. CISNEROS HAD MADE A GOOD START AND HE WOULD 
FOLLOW THE PROPOSAL AS IT WAS DEVELOPED SUBSTANTIVELY. 
CISNEROS UNDERSCORED THAT SOME KIND OF MECHANISM WOULD BE 
IMPORTANT FOR ARGENTINE PUBLIC OPINION. 
10. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY CLOSED THE MEETING BY 
REITERATING HIS DESIRE FOR A CLOSE COLLABORATIVE 
RELATIONSHIP. PETRELLA HAD NOT HESITATED TO CALL DIRECTLY 
WHEN NECESSARY AND THIS HAD BEEN HELPFUL ON SEVERAL 
OCCASIONS. SIMILARLY, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED, HE 
WOULD NOT BE BASHFUL ABOUT CALLING. CISNEROS NOTED THAT 
INTERIOR MINISTER CORACH, DEFENSE MINISTER DOMINGUEZ AND 
JUSTICE MINISTER JASSAN WERE ALL VISITING WASHINGTON IN 
SEPTEMBER. THE TWO AGREED THAT FREQUENT HIGH-LEVEL VISITS 
SERVED TO CEMENT THE U.S.-ARGENTINE PARTNERSHIP. ANOTHER 
'NEW REALITY IN THE REGION WAS MERCOSUR, WHICH, THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY COMMENTED, HAD PROVEN OVER THE PAST YEAR THAT 
ITS AGENDA WENT BEYOND ECONOMIC INTEGRATION.
