SUMMARY 
2. MEMBERS OF THE HAITIAN PRIVATE SECTOR TOLD THE 
HIGH-LEVEL DELEGATION LED BY DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOT 
THAT SUSTAINABLE ECONOMIC GROWTH IN HAITI WILL DEPEND 
ON THE CREATION OF A STABLE POLITICAL ENVIRONMENT. 
SEVERAL WERE SKEPTICAL THAT THE UN COULD SUCCESSFULLY 
REFORM THE HAITIAN ARMY AND URGED THE U.S. MILITARY 
TO REMAIN ENGAGEO IN THIS PROCESS UNTIL IT IS 
COMPLETE. THE GROUP SAID THAT SINCE THE INTERVENTION 
BY THE MULTINATIONAL FORCE (MNF), AUTHORITY HAS 
BROKEN DOWN AND THE MNF HAS NOT BEEN ABLE TO FULLY 
FILL THIS VACUUM. LOOTING -- PRIMARILY OF FOOD 
WAREHOUSES -- CONTINUES ON A SMALL/SCALE. THEY URGED 
THE USG TO QUICKLY REMOVE THE JONASSAINT REGIME AND 
RESTORE THE MALVAL GOVERNMENT, SAYING THIS WOULD HELP 
REESTABLISH ORDER. 
3. ON ECONOMIC ISSUES, THE ENTREPRENEURS COMPLAINED 
ABOUT THE DELAY IN LIFTING OUR UNILATERAL 
FINANCIAL SANCTIONS AND HOPED IT WILL SWIFTLY 
TERMINATE THE REMAINING TRADE SANCTIONS ONCE THE UN 
SANCTIONS ARE GONE- SEVERAL BUSINESSMAN STRESSED 
THAT IT IS CRITICAL THAT THE FIRST OIL TANKER ARRIVE 
ALMOST IMMEDIATELY AFTER THE TRADE SANCTIONS ARE 
REMOVED. THE GROUP SAID THE HAITIAN PRIVATE SECTOR 
WILL NEED A PACKAGE OF INCENTIVES TO RECOVER FROM THE 
DEVASTATION SUFFERED OVER THE LAST THREE YEARS AND 
ASKED THAT A SPECIAL CREDIT FACILITY BE SET UP TO 
PROVIDE THEM WITH WORKING CAPITAL. THEY ALSO 
PROPOSED THAT THE WHITE HOUSE HOST A HAITI TRADE AND 
INVESTMENT DAY TO SIGNAL THAT HAITI IS BACK IN 
BUSINESS. THE DELEGATION NOTED THE ENTREPRENUERS' 
CONCERNS AND ASSURED THEM THAT THE USG IS COMMITTED 
TO PROMOTING DEMOCRATIC INSTITUTIONS AND ECONOMIC 
GROWTH IN THE COUNTRY. END SUMMARY. 
4. DURING THEIR OCTOBER 7 VISIT TO PORT-AU-PRINCE, 
THE DELEGATION LED BY DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOT 
AND NSC DEPUTY SANDY BERGER MET WITH SEVENTEEN 
ENTREPRENEURS FROM THE HAITIAN MANUFACTURERS 
ASSOCIATION (ADIH) AND THE CENTER FOR FREE ENTERPRISE 
AND DEMOCRACY (CLED). ADIH CONSISTENTLY OPPOSED THE 
1991 MILITARY COUP, AND ITS LEADERSHIP HAS CLOSE TIES 
WITH MEMBERS OF PRESIDENT ARISTIDE'S ECONOMIC TEAM. 
ITS MEMBERSHIP CONSISTS PRIMARILY OF ASSEMBLY SECTOR 
FIRMS AND FIRMS PROVIDING SERVICES TO THIS SECTOR.
