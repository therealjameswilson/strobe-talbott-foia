THE SECRETARY NOTED THAT HE FULLY AGREED THAT THIS. IS 
AN ISSUE THAT CAN BE PUSHED FORWARD BY GORE AND 
CHERNOMYRDIN TOWARD A SOLUTION. "HOWEVER, IF IT IS NOT 
RESOLVED BY THE SUMMIT, WE COULD WELL END UP IN A 
'CHICKEN SUMMIT' AND NONE OF US.WOULD LIKE THAT VERY 
MUCH." 
PRIMAKOV SAID, "I TOLD THE SECRETARY OF STATE THAT.IF 
PEOPLE CALL IT THE 'CHICKEN SUMMIT', THEY MAY WELL BE 
ACCUSED OF HAVING CHICKEN BRAINS." 
YELTSIN SAID, "LET GORE AND CHERNOMYRDIN SOLVE IT. LET 
THEM GET TO WORK AND SOLVE IT. DECISIONS ON THIS ONE 
ARE NOT PRESIDENTIAL." 
YELTSIN CONTINUED: "ON THE ISSUE OF THE INTEGRATION OF 
THE COMMONWEALTH OF INDEPENDENT STATES, WE WILL DISCUSS 
WITH BELARUS TOMORROW AND SIGN ON THE 29TH OF MARCH A 
TREATY WHICH WILL MANDATE PROFOUND INTEGRATION, BUT NOT 
A CONFEDERATION, OF THE TWO COUNTRIES. THERE ARE SOME 
NEW IDEAS THAT WE HAVE BROUGHT FORWARD. IT WILL BE 
IMPORTANT. THIS STEP WILL ASSURE THE PEOPLE THAT WE 
CAN DELIVER IMPORTANT AGREEMENTS. THIS WILL BE A 
REASONABLE INTEGRATION AND,MUCH BEYOND WHAT PEOPLE 
CURRENTLY EXPECT. KAZAKSTAN AND KYRGYZSTAN WILL ALSO 
JOIN US. THEY'RE NOT READY YET TO SIGN THE BELARUS 
TREATY, BUT THEY WILL PARTICIPATE AND WE WILL SEE 
WHETHER THEY CAN BE PERSUADED TO ACCEPT IT. THIS IS A 
PROFOUND INTEGRATION, SUCH AS THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITY 
HAS UNDERTAKEN LEADING TO THE UNION." 
THE SECRETARY SAID HE HAD A SECOND POINT HE WANTED TO 
MAKE AT THE END. HE SAID, "REGARDING YOUR COMMENTS 
ABOUT THE AMBASSADOR, IT'S IMPORTANT THAT WE CLEAR 
THESE UP. I SUGGEST THAT THE AMBASSADOR SPEAK WITH 
FOREIGN MINISTER PRIMAKOV AS SOON AS POSSIBLE ABOUT 
WHAT THE ISSUES ARE AND WORK TOGETHER WITH HIM TO CLEAR 
THOSE UP." 
YELTSIN SAID, "SURE, I'D BE GLAD TO DO THIS. THERE ARE 
MANY EXAMPLES. WE HAVE AN ENTIRE LIST OF ISSUES THAT 
WE HAVE PREPARED ON THIS." 
THE SECRETARY THANKED,THE PRESIDENT FOR AN EXCELLENT 
MEETING. "WE HAVE A DEEP AND EXTRAORDINARY 
RELATIONSHIP, AND WE NEED TO WORK TO PRESERVE AND 
PROSPER IT. YOU AND WE ARE VERY MUCH WORKING TOGETHER 
ON ALL OF THIS AND THIS RELATIONSHIP NEEDS TO BE KEPT 
AT THE TOP OF OUR MINDS." 
YELTSIN SAID, "I ABSOLUTELY AGREE." 
AFTER HAVING STOOD UP, AND ABOUT TO LEAVE THE ROOM, 
YELTSIN TOLD THE SECRETARY HE HAD NOT RAISED ONE ISSUE 
THAT HAD BEEN ON HIS MIND, AND THAT HE HAD NOT DONE SO 
ON PURPOSE. THAT WAS NATO ENLARGEMENT. YELTSIN SAID, 
"IT'S NOW CLEAR THAT WE HAVE AT LEAST AN AGREEMENT TO 
DISAGREE ON THIS POINT." (END TEXT)
