1. -(-1=9- SUMMARY. AT AMBASSADOR KHOKHAR'S REQUEST, ASSISTANT 
SECRETARY INDERFURTH DELIVERED A READOUT ON THE TALBOTT-
JASWANT SINGH ROME TALKS OF NOVEMBER 19-20. KHOKHAR 
REVIEWED THE PRIME MINISTER'S SCHEDULE. INDERFURTH WARNED 
AGAINST EXPECTATIONS OF FURTHER SANCTIONS RELIEF, NOTING WE 
WERE NOW EXPECTING STEPS ON THE PAKISTANI SIDE. KHOKHAR 
SAID THE PRIME MINISTER'S REMARK IN FAVOR OF TALIBAN 
JUSTICE WAS TAKEN OUT OF CONTEXT. INDERFURTH DENOUNCED THE 
TALIBAN'S INNOCENT VERDICT ON BIN LADEN, AND NOTED THIS 
WOULD LEAD TO FURTHER ISOLATION FOR THE TALIBAN. HE SAID 
HIS MEETING WITH FORMER KING ZAHIR SHAH IN ROME WAS PART OF 
A POLICY OF MAINTAINING DIALOGUE WITH ALL AFGHAN FACTIONS, 
AND THAT OUR POLICY REMAINS GROUNDED IN THE POINTS OF 
COMMON UNbERSTANDING OF THE SIX PLUS TWO. END SUMMARY. 
ROME READOUT 
2. -tE4-ON NOVEMBER 23, ASSISTANT SECRETARY INDERFURTH 
BRIEFED AMBASSADOR KHOKHAR ON THE NOVEMBER 19-20 TALBOTT-
JASWANT SINGH TALKS IN ROME. THE INDIANS OPENED WITH A 
GOOD REPORT ON THE EXPORT CONTROL TALKS HELD IN DELHI 
NOVEMBER 9-10. "WE HAVE SOME WORK TO DO UP TO JANUARY," 
INDERFURTH SAID, "BUT WE EXPECT TO SEE STEPS TAKEN TO PUT 
THESE EXPORT CONTROL MEASURES INTO PLACE EARLY IN 1999. 
THE INDIANS VIEW THIS AS A NON-CONTENTIOUS, COOPERATIVE 
EFFORT, AS DO WE AND WE ARE PLEASED TO NOTE A SIMILAR 
SPIRIT IN EXPORT CONTROL TALKS WITH PAKISTAN." HOWEVER, 
INDERFURTH NOTED THE INDIANS STRONGLY OBJECTED TO THE 
PUBLICATION OF THE ENTITIES LIST, WHICH THEY SAW AS A 
"BLACKLIST," AND THE "DISCRIMINATORY" USG IFI POLICY. "THE 
INDIANS HAVE NO OBJECTION WITH AID TO PAKISTAN, AND I 
EXPECT THEM TO VOTE FOR IT," INDERFURTH NOTED. "THEIR 
OBJECTION IS THAT THESE AVENUES ARE CLOSED TO THEM." THEY 
SEE OUR DECISION AS EVIDENCE OF "TILT." INDERFURTH 
EXPLAINED HOW WE REFUTED THESE ARGUMENTS AND RECOGNIZED 
THAT WITH ONGOING STATE ELECTIONS, THIS WAS A POLITICALLY 
SENSITIVE TIME FOR NEW DELHI. "THERE WERE NO 
BREAKTHROUGHS, AND THE TIMING WAS SUCH THAT TO EXPECT 
FURTHER STEPS TAKEN IN ROME WOULD HAVE BEEN UNREALISTIC." 
INDERFURTH REMARKED THAT IN THE COMING WEEKS, AS INDIA 
UNDERTAKES ITS STRATEGIC DEFENSE REVIEW (NOTING THE 
ESTABLISHMENT OF A NATIONAL SECURITY COUNCIL), WE WANT THEM 
TO COME TO THE RIGHT DECISION WITH RESPECT TO NUCLEAR AND 
MISSILE RESTRAINT, INCLUDING NONDEPLOYMENT.

5. 1€4...KHOKHAR WAS UNDER INSTRUCTIONS TO RAISE NAWAZ 
SHARIF'S GAFFE, IN WHICH HE PRAISED TALIBAN JUSTICE. HE 
SAID THE PRIME MINISTER'S REMARK WAS TAKEN OUT OF CONTEXT. 
HE WAS REFERRING ONLY TO THE NEED FOR SPEEDY JUSTICE IN 
CERTAIN'CRIMES, SUCH AS TERRORISM. "WE DO NOT LOOK TO THE 
TALIBAN AS,A ROLE MODEL," SAID KHOKHAR. INDERFURTH REPLIED 
THAT THE AMERICAN PRESS WOULD CERTAINLY QUESTION THE PRIME 
MINISTER ON THE TALIBAN AND THEIR ABHORRENT SOCIAL 
POLICIES. 
6. tei- INDERFURTH FOLLOWED ON BY EXPRESSING OUR VIEWS ON 
THE 'TALIBAN'S "INNOCENT" VERDICT ON BIN LADEN. "WE WILL BE 
LOOKING AT STEPS TO ISOLATE THEM. IF THIS DECISION IS 
FINAL, IT MEANS THE TALIBAN ARE COLLABORATORS IN BIN 
LADEN'S TERRORISM. THE TALIBAN MAY BE DESIGNATED A 
TERRORIST ORGANIZATION. THERE ARE' OTHER STEPS WE ARE 
CONSIDERING." THE INFORMATION CONTAINED IN THE INDICTMENT 
IS SUFFICIENT TO DEMONSTRATE BIN LADEN'S CULPABILITY. 
7. -(154- INDERFURTH CHARACTERIZED THE TALIBAN/IRAN TALKS AS A 
GOOD SIGN, BUT SAID SERIOUS IRAN-PAKISTAN DISCUSSIONS ON 
AFGHANISTAN ARE ALSO NEEDED. INDERFURTH THEN REVIEWED HIS 
MEETING WITH FORMER AFGHAN KING ZAHIR SHAH. THE KING, HE 
SAID, FULLY SUPPORTS THE NEED FOR A BROAD-BASED MULTI-
ETHNIC GOVERNMENT AND IS WILLING TO SERVE IN ANY CAPACITY 
TO HELP HIS COUNTRY. .KHOKHAR ASKED WHETHER THE KING HAD 
NEW IDEAS FOR A PEACE PLAN. WHILE THE KING BROUGHT UP THE 
LONGSTANDING NOTION OF A LOYA JIRGA, INDERFURTH SAID, THE 
KING DID NOT OFFER NEW PRESCRIPTIONS. WE BELIEVE IN OPEN 
COMMUNICATIONS WITH ALL FACTIONS, INDERFURTH SAID, AND THE 
MEETING WITH THE KING SHOULD. BE SEEN WITHIN THIS CONTEXT.
