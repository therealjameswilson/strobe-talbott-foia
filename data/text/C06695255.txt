2. SUMMARY. THE SECOND BRIEFING OF THE MULTINATIONAL 
FORCE AMBASSADORS TOOK PLACE ON SEPTEMBER 19. DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT DESCRIBED THE WEEKEND'S NEGOTIATION 
EFFORTS AND SAID SANCTIONS WOULD NOT BE LIFTED UNTIL 
HAITIAN PRESIDENT ARISTIDE WAS RETURNED TO POWER IN 
ACCORDANCE WITH THE RELEVANT UN RESOLUTIONS. JCS ADMIRAL 
WILLIAMSON DESCRIBED THE MILITARY SITUATION, NOTING THAT 
1,500 TROOPS WERE ASHORE, WITH ANOTHER 5,500 TO FOLLOW BY 
THE END OF THE FOLLOWING DAY. POINTS OF CONTACT FOR 
SPECIFIC QUESTIONS FOR COALITION COUNTRIES WERE ALSO 
GIVEN. END SUMMARY. 
DIPLOMATIC MISSION; WEEKEND NEGOTIATIONS 
3. THE SECOND BRIEFING OF THE MULTINATIONAL FORCE 
COALITION AMBASSADORS TOOK PLACE SEPTEMBER 19 1994. IN 
ADDITION TO DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, AMBASSADORS 
MCNAMARA, WATSON, GELBARD, DOBBINS, IO PDAS WARD, AND JCS 
ADMIRAL WILLIAMSON §J-3,6 MET WITH COALITION MEMBERS. 
DEPSEC TALBOTT OPENED THE MEETING BY THANKING COALITION 
AMBASSADORS FOR THEIR SUPPORT, NOTING THAT THE RECENT 
EVENTS WOULD NOT HAVE BEEN POSSIBLE WITHOUT THE SUPPORT OF 
THE ASSEMBLED COUNTRIES. 
4. TALBOTT SAID THE EVENTS SET A NEW STANDARD FOR 
TRANSPARENCY IN DIPLOMACY, AND NOTED THE SECRETARY'S 
DESCRIPTION OF THE NEGOTIATIONS AS A CLASSIC EXAMPLE OF 
DIPLOMACY BACKED UP BY POWER. HE INDICATED FORMER 
PRESIDENT CARTER'S GROUP WENT TO HAITI WITH A LIMITED 
MANDATE: TO DISCUSS ONLY IMPLEMENTATION OF UN SECURITY 
COUNCIL RESOLUTION 940; TO ADDRESS THE DEPARTURE FROM 
POWER OF THE THREE LEADERS; AND TO ENSURE ADHERENCE TO THE 
TIMETABLE OF THE PENDING MILITARY OPERATION. THE 
NEGOTIATORS REACHED FINAL AGREEMENT ONLY WHEN GENERAL 
CEDRAS SUDDENLY LEARNED FROM GENERAL BIAMBY THAT THE 82ND 
AIRBORNE DIVISION WAS LIFTING OFF FROM FORT BRAGG. 
CLINCHING THE AGREEMENT 
5. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY HIGHLIGHTED GENERAL POWELL'S 
VITAL ROLE IN EXPLAINING TO.THE HAITIAN MILITARY - IN 
PRECISE, CLINICAL DETAIL - EXACTLY WHAT THE MNF DEPLOYMENT 
WOULD BE LIKE IN TERMS OF NUMBERS OF TANKS AND EQUIPMENT, 
THE TECHNOLOGY INVOLVED, AND THE CHANGED SECURITY 
ENVIRONMENT THAT WOULD RESULT, ACCORDING TO DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT, GENERAL CEDRAS SEEMED TO PHYSICALLY 
DIMINISH IN STATURE LISTENING TO THE DESCRIPTION.

6. MR. TALBOTT, RESPONDING TO A QUESTION, CALLED THE 
AGREEMENT A QUOTE ENABLING UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN TWO 
PARTIES UNQUOTE THAT ENABLED THE MNF TO ENTER HAITI IN 
BROAD DAYLIGHT WITH EVERY HOPE THAT THE RISKS COULD BE 
KEPT CLOSE TO ZERO, AVOIDING BLOODSHED AND VIOLENCE. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY CONTINUED, SAYING HE HOPED PRESIDENT 
ARISTIDE WOULD SEE THE AGREEMENT AS A POSITIVE DEVELOPMENT 
THAT WILL FACILITATE HIS RETURN TO HAITI WITH MINIMAL RISK 
OR POTENTIAL FOR BLOODSHED. 
7. REGARDING SANCTIONS, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED UNSCR 
917, WHICH REQUIRES PRESIDENT ARISTIDE TO BE FULLY 
RETURNED TO POWER BEFORE LIFTING SANCTIONS. {NOTE: 
CONSULTATIONS REGARDING TIMING ARE ALREADY UNDERWAY.) 
8. CONCERNING THE DE FACTO LEADERS, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
EXPLAINED THE THREE U.S. NEGOTIATORS DID NOT SEE ENSURING 
THE DEPARTURE FROM HAITI OF THE TOP THREE DE FACTO HAITIAN 
LEADERS AS PART OF THEIR MANDATE. THE NEGOTIATORS DID 
INSIST ON THEIR DEPARTURE FROM POWER, AND BELIEVE THE "NEW 
CONDITIONS" CREATED IN HAITI COULD BE AN INCENTIVE_FOR 
THEM TO LEAVE THE COUNTRY.
