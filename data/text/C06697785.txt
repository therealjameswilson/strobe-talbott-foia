ARGENTINE DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER PETRELLA 
I. C HFIDENTIAL ENTIRE TEXT -- CLASSIFIED BY JEFFREY 
R. DAVIDOW, REASON 1.5(B). 
2. SUMMARY: ARGENTINE DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER PETRELLA 
PAID AN INTRODUCTORY CALL ON ACTING ASSISTANT SECRETARY 
DAVIDOW ON MAY 9. PETRELLA INQUIRED AS TO PROGRESS ON 
THE VISA WAIVER PROGRAM, AND INDICATED THAT PRESIDENT 
MENEM WAS INTERESTED IN AN INFORMAL MEETING WITH 
PRESIDENT CLINTON DURING HIS VISIT TO THE U.S. FOR THE 
OLYMPICS. THE ARGENTINE OFFICIAL NOTED THAT MUCH 
PROGRESS HAS BEEN MADE IN ARGENTINA'S LONG-STANDING 
DISPUTE WITH GREAT HRITAIN OVER THE FALKLANDS/MALVINAS, 
AND THAT THE GOA IS HOPEFUL OF REACHING A PEACEFUL 
RESOLUTION. DAVIDOW RESPONDED THAT HE HAD RECEIVED THE 
SAME MESSAGE FROM FOREIGN MINISTER DI TELLA IN CARACAS, 
WHO CONVEYED THE IMPRESSION THAT FURTHER PROGRESS MUST 
AWAIT THE BRITISH ELECTIONS. 
3. ON THE PERU-ECUADOR CONFLICT, ACTING A/S DAVIDOW 
STATED THAT WE NEED TO MOVE QUICKLY FOLLOWING THE 
ELECTIONS IN ECUADOR AND SEEK A RESOLUTION. HE NOTED 
THAT USG HEAVY LOGISTICAL PARTICIPATION IN MOMEP WOULD 
LIKELY NOT EXTEND BEYOND THIS YEAR. PETRELLA FELT THAT 
RENEWED PRESSURE MUST BE BROUGHT AGAINST ECUADOR, AND 
SUGGESTED THAT THE SOLUTION LIES IN A THIRD PARTY 
"CONCILIATOR" TO RESOLVE THE DISPUTE. DAVIDOW STATED 
THAT THE USG WAS PLEASED THAT ARGENTINA WAS CONSIDERING 
PARTICIPATION IN A POST-JUNE 30 HAITI SECURITY FORCE. 
PETRELLA REPLIED THAT IT WAS AT ONCE EXPENSIVE AND 
IMPORTANT FOR ARGENTINA TO PARTICIPATE, BUT THAT THE GOA 
INTENDED TO DELIVER. ON IPR, DAVIDOW NOTED THAT THE USG 
DECISION NOT TO SANCTION ARGENTINA WAS CLOSE AND 
DIFFICULT, AND SUGGESTED THAT MERCOSUR MAY WANT TO 
CONSIDER HARMONIZATION OF ITS PATENT LAWS IN THE WAKE OF 
APPROVAL OF THE NEW BRAZILIAN REGIME. PETRELLA RESPONDED 
THAT THE MENEM ADMINISTRATION MUST FOCUS ITS EFFORTS AT 
PRESENT ON TWO OTHER KEY PIECES OF LEGISLATION BEFORE 
CONGRESS, BUT THAT THE GOA WILL CONSIDER PRESSING FOR 
FURTHER IPR REFORMS IN AUGUST. END SUMMARY.

IS EXTREMELY PLEASED WITH STATE OF THE BILATERAL

4. PETRELLA STATED THAT SINCE SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER'S 
FEBRUARY VISIT TO BUENOS AIRES TO ANNOUNCE ARGENTINA'S 
ELIGIBILITY FOR THE VISA WAIVER PROGRAM, THERE HAS BEEN 
NO FURTHER WORD. HE ALSO NOTED THAT PRESIDENT MENEM
PLANS TO ATTEND THE OLYMPICS IN ATLANTA AND INQUIRED INTO 
THE POSSIBILITY OF A MEETING, PERHAPS INFORMAL, WITH 
PRESIDENT CLINTON. DAVIDOW STATED THAT HE WOULD LOOK 
INTO BOTH MATTERS. 
GREAT BRITAIN 
5. WITH RESPECT. TO THE RELATIONSHIP WITH GREAT BRITAIN, 
PETRELLA NOTED THAT ARGENTINA IS WORKING TO RESOLVE THE 
CONFLICT PEACEFULLY. HE STATED THAT THE PARTIES ARE 
WORKING TOWARD COMPLETION OF A LONG-TERM FISHING ACCORD 
FOR THE WATERS AROUND THE MALVINAS, AND THAT A MAY 17 
MEETING WITH FCO LATIN AMERICAN OFFICE DIRECTOR MARSDEN 
WAS SCHEDULED. DAVIDOW COMMENTED THAT FONMIN DI TELLA 
HAD MADE THE SAME POINTS TO HIM IN CARACAS, LEAVING THE 
IMPRESSION THAT FURTHER SUBSTANTIVE PROGRESS WILL AWAIT 
THE BRITISH ELECTIONS. HE NOTED THAT MARSDEN WILL BE 
HERE ON THE 20TH, AND STATED THAT AN ADVANCE READOUT ON 
HIS DISCUSSIONS WITH THE GOA WOULD nE HELPFUL. 
PERU-ECUADOR 
6. DAVIDOW REMARKED THAT tOLLOWING THE ELECTIONS IN 
ECUADOR, THE GUARANTORS MUST MOVE QUICKLY TO SEEK A 
RESOLUTION, PERHAPS AN AGREEMENT ON,ARBITRATION. HE 
NOTED THAT WE WERE CONCERNED THAT THE BRAZILIANS WERE 
MOVING TOO SLOW. PETRELLA WAS HESITANT TO ACCUSE THE 
BRAZILIANS OF FOOT-DRAGGING, BUT AGREED THAT MORE 
PRESSURE TO RESOLVE THE MATTER NEEDED TO BE BROUGHT.' HE 
OPINED THAT ECUADOR REQUIRED MORE PRESSURE,THAN PERU. 
7. THE ACTING ASSISTANT SECRETARY NOTED THAT OUR HEAVY 
LOGISTICAL PARTICIPATION IN MOMEP WILL PROBABLY NOT 
EXTEND BEYOND THE END OF THIS YEAR. HE STATED THAT WE 
CANNOT AFFORD TO LET THE SITUATION DRAG ON BEYOND THEN. 
PETRELLA REMARKED THAT WHAT WAS REQUIRED WAS A 
"CONCILIATOR" TO HELP MOVE THE PROCESS. PETRELLA AGREED 
TO CONSULT WITH THE BRAZILIANS ON THIS MATTER.

HAITI 
8. THE USG IS PLEASED, DAVIDOW NOTED, THAT ARGENTINA IS 
CONSIDERING PARTICIPATION IN A POST-JUNE 30 INTERNATIONAL 
SECURITY FORCE IN HAITI. PETRELLA RESPONDED THAT 
ARGENTINE PARTICIPATION HAS BEEN SUCCESSFUL, EXPENSIVE 
AND IMPORTANT. COMMENTING THAT THE U.S. CANNOT BE LEFT 
TO DO EVERYTHING, HE NOTED THAT IF ARGENTINA WANTS 
RECOGNITION AND RESPONSIBILITY IN WORLD AFFAIRS, IT HAS 
TO DELIVER. HE REPEATED HIS PREVIOUS ASSERTION THAT 
PRESIDENT MENEM NO LONGER WANTS ARGENTINA TO BE A "FREE 
RIDER". 
IPR 
9. DAVIDOW TOLD PETRELLA THAT THE DECISION BY THE 
PRESIDENT NOT TO IMPOSE SANCTIONS AGAINST ARGENTINA FOR 
ITS INADEQUATE IPR PROTECTION HAD BEEN VERY CLOSE AND 
DIFFICULT. HE STATED THAT WE HAVE TO WORK TOGETHER TO 
ELABORATE A PLAN TO IMPROVE THE ARGENTINE REGIME, AND 
THAT PERHAPS THE RECENT CHANGES TO THE BRAZILIAN LAW AND 
INTEREST IN HARMONIZATION THROUGHOUT MERCOSUR COULD 
PROVIDE THE SOLUTION. PETRELLA REMINDED DAVIDOW THAT 
MENEM HAD GONE TO THE MAT ON THIS ISSUE AND THE PROBLEM 
WAS WITH CONGRESS_ HE STATED THAT THE GOA HAS TO ,GIVE 
CONGRESS TIME BEFORE PRESSING FOR FURTHER CHANGES, NOTING 
THAT THE PRESIDENT NEEDS TO PASS TWO IMPORTANT PIECES OF 
LEGISLATION OVER THE NEXT FEW MONTHS, BOTH OF WHICH ARE 
SUBJECT TO NATIONALIST ATTACKS. ONE CONCERNS THE 
BOUNDARY AGREEMENT WITH CHILE, AND THE OTHER DEALS WITH 
REFORMS TO THE HYDROCARBON LAWS NECESSARY TO IMPLEMENT 
THE OIL EXPLORATION AGREEMENT WITH GREAT BRITAIN. HE 
STATED THAT THESE BILLS MUST BE PASSED BY AUGUST, AFTER 
WHICH FURTHER IPR REFORMS CAN BE CONSIDERED. 
10. THE ACTING ASSISTANT SECRETARY SUGGESTED THAT IN THE 
INTERIM PERHAPS INFORMAL TALKS WITH THE BRAZILIANS COULD 
BE INITIATED. PETRELLA REPLIED THAT THE GOA CANNOT 
AFFORD TO RISK IRKING ITS CONGRESS. HE CONCLUDED BY 
STATING THAT THE ARGENTINE GOVERNMENT WILL NOT FORGET THE 
NEED TO PURSUE'THIS ISSUE.
