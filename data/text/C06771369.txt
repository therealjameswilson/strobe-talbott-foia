6.--(4P THE ACTING SECRETARY NOTED THE SECRETARY HAD MET 
WITH RUSSIAN FM IVANOV IN BONN MAY 6. IT WAS IMPORTANT TO 
IDENTIFY AS MUCH COMMON GROUND AS POSSIBLE BETWEEN 
RUSSIA'S CONCEPT OF HOW A PEACE SHOULD BE IMPLEMENTED AND 
THE UNITED STATES'. SUCH A CONCEPT WOULD INCLUDE THE MILITARY DIMENSION. THE U.S. WAS WORKING DAY AND NIGHT ON 
THIS ISSUE, NOT JUST IN THE U.S., BUT IN BRUSSELS AS WELL. THE ACTING SECRETARY OFFERED TO HAVE IN-DEPTH 
CONVERSATIONS WITH CHERNOMYRDIN'S COLLEAGUES THE FOLLOWING WEEK DURING HIS VISIT TO MOSCOW. ' 
7. -(44-CHERNOMYRDIN WONDERED ABOUT THE STATUS OF THE UN 
ENVOYS. THE ACTING SECRETARY EXPLAINED ANNAN'S IDEA OF 
APPOINTING TWO ENVOYS WHOSE JOB WOULD BE TO BEGIN WORK 
IMMEDIATELY ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF A PEACE AGREEMENT, WITH PARTICULAR FOCUS ON THE ROLE OF THE UN. ANNAN HAD 
NOT MADE A FINAL DECISION ON WHO HE WOULD APPOINT, BUT IT 
WAS THE ACTING SECRETARY'S EXPECTATION HE WOULD APPOINT 
SLOVAKIAN FM KUKAN AND FORMER SWEDISH PM BILDT. 
8. THE ACTING SECRETARY SAID ANNAN HAD AGREED CHERNOMYRDIN WAS DOING IMPORTANT WORK WITH BELGRADE, AND 
THERE WAS A FEELING THAT CHERNOMYRDIN DESERVED SOME SPACE IN WHICH TO WORK. ANNAN ALSO UNDERSTOOD, AS DID THE U.S., 
THAT WHEN CHERNOMYRDIN WAS PREPARED TO MAKE A CLIMACTIC VISIT TO BELGRADE IT WOULD BE HELPFUL TO BE ACCOMPANIED BY 
ANOTHER INTERNATIONAL FIGURE ALL AGREED AHTISAARI WOULD 
BE THE BEST FOR SUCH A MISSION. AHTISAARI'S OFFICIAL POSITION WAS STILL BEING DISCUSSED. ANNAN WAS PREPARED TO 
GIVE HIM A UN DESIGNATION; THE EU WAS PREPARED TO GIVE HIM 
AN EU DESIGNATION, AND RUSSIA AND THE U.S. COULD DECIDE ON HOW TO GIVE HIM THEIR OWN BLESSINGS. THE ACTING SECRETARY SUGGESTED CHERNOMYRDIN THINK OF AHTISAARI AS A COMPANION WHO WOULD WEAR THREE HATS. HE URGED CHERNOMYRDIN TO SPEAK 
DIRECTLY WITH ANNAN. 
9. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 0928 EDT.
