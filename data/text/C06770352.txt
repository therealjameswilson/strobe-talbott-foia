SUBJECT: 'YAVLINSKIY CLAIMS YELTSIN ORDERING STUDY 
COOPERATIVE U.S.-RUSSIA NMD CONCEPT 
(U) CLASSIFIED BY AMBASSADOR JAMES F. COLLINS. REASON 
1.5 (B) AND (D). 
1. 4G* IN MY MEETING WITH YAVLINSKIY MARCH 16, HE, 
REPORTED THAT DURING HIS MARCH 10 MEETING WITH 
PRESIDENT YELTSIN HE RAISED HIS NOW WELL-KNOWN PROPOSAL 
FOR A JOINT-U.S.-RUSSIA MISSILE DEFENSE SYSTEM. 
YAVLINSKIY COMMENTED THAT YELTSIN WAS "REALLY 
INTERESTED." YAVLINSKIY HAD A PAPER FOR YELTSIN ON THE 
PROPOSAL, SETTING OUT ITS DETAILS AND YAVLINSKIY'S 
THINKING ON IT. ACCORDING TO YAVLINSKIY, WHEN YELTSIN 
READ IT, HE ASKED THAT YAVLINSKIY SIGN IT AS A LETTER. 
2. 444 AS FOLLOW UP, YAVLINSKIY CLAIMS, YELTSIN HAS NOW 
ASKED HIS STAFF TO PREPARE AN INSTRUCTION THAT WILL 
TASK AGENCIES TO ANALYZE AND REPORT ON THE PROPOSAL. 
YAVLINSKIY SAID HE UNDERSTANDS YELTSIN IS READY TO SIGN 
SUCH AN INSTRUCTION TO THE MINISTRIES OF DEFENSE AND 
FOREIGN AFFAIRS, AS WELL AS TO THE SECURITY COUNCIL AND 
PRESIDENTIAL ADMINISTRATION. HE IS ALSO REPORTEDLY 
CONSIDERING AN INSTRUCTION TO PRIMAKOV TO-PREPARE FOR 
SUBSTANTIVE TALKS ON THE YAVLINSKIY IDEA, AND HE MAY 
ASK THAT PRIMAKOV RAISE IT WITH EITHER THE PRESIDENT OR 
VICE PRESIDENT WHEN HE GETS TO WASHINGTON. 
3. 4s4- YAVLINSKIY IS VIGOROUSLY PURSUING HIS PROPOSAL 
FOR JOINT DEVELOPMENT AND EXPLOITATION OF A MISSILE 
DEFENSE SYSTEM FOR THE U.S. AND RUSSIA IN EUROPE. THE 
IDEA HAS ALSO TAKEN ROOT WITH KEY YABLOKO ARMS CONTROL 
THINKERS. WHEN CONGRESSMAN WELDON AND-HIS DELEGATION 
BRIEFED MEMBERS OF THE DUMA ON THE RUMSFELD COMMISSION 
REPORT AND LEGISLATION BEFORE THE CONGRESS IN SUPPORT
