SUBJECT: D/S Talbott Urges "Strategic Optimism" in Meeting 
with Romanian Presidential Advisor Petre 
Classified by EUR/NCE Dir. Jim Swigert 1.5 (B) AND (D)

DECLASSIFIED 
1. {Ei The Deputy Secretary urged Romanian Presidential 
Advisor Zoe Petre to embrace "Strategic Optimism'l in the 
run-up to the Washington Summit. Petre outlined the GOR's 
concerns about being left in the gray zone after the first 
round of enlargement. D/S Talbott assured Petre of the 
U.S. commitment to a robust Open Door package, but said 
precise timelines for a new round were problematic. Petre 
claimed the GOR emerged from January's violent miners' 
strikes more committed to reform and with its democracy 
tested and intact. End Summary. 
Miners 
2. 444 Senior Romanian Presidential Advisor Zoe Petre 
called on the Deputy Secretary February 8, offering 
assurances the violent miners' protests in January had not 
deterred the GOR from the path of economic reform. Rather, 
she said reform in Romania had reached the "point of no 
return" and that the miners' illegal tactics had only 
strengthened the GOR's resolve to do what it has to do, 
including reaching a deal with the IMF. 
3. -1(e) Petre credited President Constantinescu with helping 
end the miners' crisis by getting support from all 
parliamentary parties, except the ultra-nationalist PRM, 
for a statement calling for the miners to desist. This 
served to isolate the ultra-nationalists, she said. Petre 
said the miners' tactics sparked a pro-democracy reaction 
in Bucharest and elsewhere that should strengthen Romania' 5 
civil society. Petre acknowledged, however, the protests 
had underlined the need for more attention to the social ' 
impacts of reform. She said the GOR is working with the 
World Bank on an initiative targeted at the social sector. 
Washington Summit Jitters 
4. (4-.÷) Petre stressed the GOR hopes to see a concrete 
"Madrid-plus" package come out of the Washington Summit to 
counter the lack of new invitations. She appealed for 
public expressions of support for Romania to help bolster 
Romanian public opinion. Petre reiterated the GOR's hope 
for a concrete timeline for the next round and for an Open 
Door package that would offer specific programs to move 
Romania closer to membership. Petre passed the Deputy

Activism in Region 
5. +ei- Petre pledged Romania would "be there whenever 
called to help" on Kosovo. She said Romania had extra 
troops trained for SFOR duty that it could deploy to Kosovo 
on short notice, with more able to follow. She reiterated 
Romania's desire to play a stabilizing role in the region. 
She said Romania would like to use its hosting of both the 
Southeast Europe Summit and the Southeast Europe Defense 
Ministerial (SEDM) in the fall of 1999 to promote regional 
cooperation. Petre offered to consult closely with the USG 
in planning these events. She also said the expected May 
1999 visit of the Pope to Romania would offer the 
opportunity to engage regional religious leaders of key 
faiths in helping broker peace in Kosovo. She said 
President Constantinescu was very interested in pushing 
this idea. 
Open Door: Working on the "Beef" 
6. +et The Deputy Secretary thanked Petre for her overview. 
He acknowledged Romania' 5 economic difficulties, noting 
that as painful as the IMF prescriptions are, the 
alternatives are worse. He agreed with Petre that 
democratization is the cure to the "diseases" of the 
region. He commended Romania' 5 contributions to SFOR and 
its willingness to help in Kosovo, saying Romania was the 
"paradigm of a.regional good citizen."

Strategic Optimism and Tactical Realism 
8. -44- Referring back to the pre-Madrid Summit period, 
Talbott recounted how, because of Romania's public push, 
we had had to work hard to counter the perception Romania 
had been rejected at Madrid. The Deputy Secretary urged 
the GOR to avoid making public demands the Alliance is not 
able to meet and which would convey the impression NATO is 
rebuffing Romania and that Bucharest had therefore 
"failed." He reiterated that the Open Door package would 
give Romania something very important from the Summit. He 
encouraged Petre to convey "strategic optimism" and embrace 
"tactical realism" rather than doubt.
