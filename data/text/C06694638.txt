2. SUMMARY: IN A JAN. 25 MEETING WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT, 
FM KONO EXPRESSED BROAD SATISFACTION WITH THE STATE OF BILATERAL 
RELATIONS AFTER PM MURAYAMAIS SUCCESSFUL VISIT TO WASHINGTON. 
TALBOTT AGREED BUT EMPHASIZED THE UNFINISHED TRADE BUSINESS AND 
URGED THE GOVERNMENT OF JAPAN (GOJ) TO WORK WITH US TO MAKE 
PROGRESS IN THE AUTO SECTOR. HE ADVISED THAT AMBASSADOR MONDALE 
WOULD BE PERSONALLY ENGAGED ON OKINAWA BASE ISSUES. KONO SAID PM MURAYAMA LIKEWISE SOUGHT TO FOLLOW UP QUICKLY ON THIS PROBLEM.
