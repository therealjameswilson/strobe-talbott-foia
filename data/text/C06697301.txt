APRIL NUCLEAR SUMMIT 
4. —Mt FOREIGN MINISTER PRIMAKOV SUGGESTED THE PLENARY 
BEGIN WITH MATTERS RELATING TO THE APRIL 19-20 NUCLEAR 
SAFETY SUMMIT IN MOSCOW. HE TURNED THE FLOOR OVER TO 
DFM MAMEDOV, WHO SAID THAT TEAMS FROM THE TWO SIDES HAD 
HAD EXCHANGES ON SUMMIT PREPARATIONS EARLIER THAT DAY 
(MARCH 22) AND MARCH 21. SOME ISSUES STILL REMAINED, 
INCLUDING COMMUNIQUE LANGUAGE REGARDING UKRAINE AND 
CHORNOBYL. MAMEDOV SAID THE RUSSIANS WERE PREPARED TO. 
"UNDERSCORE THE IMPORTANCE OF UKRAINE" WITH A SEPARATE 
'STATEMENT, AND AFFIRMED THAT CONTACTS HAD ASSURED "GOOD 
CONTENT" FOR THE NUCLEAR SAFETY SUMMIT.- HE SAID RUSSIAN 
EXPERTS WERE PREPARED TO DISCUSS THE TREATMENT OF FIRST 
GENERATION AND OTHER OLDER NUCLEAR PLANTS. IN 
CONCLUSION, MAMEDOV RESTATED THE RUSSIAN POSITION ON 
TRANSFORMING THE G-7 INTO A G-8, WITH RUSSIA A FULL 
PARTICIPANT.
