5. ONE-ON-ONE/TWO-ON-TWO MEETINGS. SUMMARIZING THE PREVIOUS TWO 
HOURS AND FORTY MINUTES OF MEETINGS FOR THE LARGER GROUP, THE 
SECRETARY SAID HE AND PRIMAKOV HAD HAD A PRODUCTIVE AND EXTENSIVE 
DISCUSSION OF EUROPEAN SECURITY ISSUES. THEY AGREED WORK SHOULD 
CONTINUE -- PRINCIPALLY BETWEEN THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND MAMEDOV, 
WHO HAVE BEEN DEALING WITH THE ISSUES. THE U.S. AND RUSSIA HAVE 
DIFFERING PERSPECTIVES ON NATO ENLARGEMENT BUT WOULD WORK TOWARD 
A COMPREHENSIVE RELATIONSHIP. THE SECRETARY SAID HE AND PRIMAKOV 
HAD BRIEFLY DISCUSSED ABM/TMD. HE NOTED THE JOINT STATEMENT 
BEING ISSUED THAT DAY CONFIRMS THE TWO SIDES REMAIN ON THE TRACK 
LAID OUT BY PRESIDENTS CLINTON AND YELTSIN IN APRIL. THE 
SECRETARY NOTED THE U.S. AND RUSSIA AGREED ON RECONVENING THE SCC 
(SPECIAL CONSULTATIVE COMMISSION) ON OCTOBER 7; THAT THE SIDES 
WILL CONFORM AND PREPARE THE PART ONE DOCUMENTS AND BEGIN 
'DISCUSSIONS OF PART TWO; AND THAT DEPUTIES WOULD SIGN THE PART 
ONE DOCUMENTS BY THE END OF OCTOBER. THE SECRETARY SAID THE 
SIGNING OF PART ONE IS A BIG STEP AND THAT THE U.S. IS COMMITTED 
TO COMPLETING PART TWO AS QUICKLY AS POSSIBLE. PRIMAKOV 
CONCURRED THAT THE JOINT STATEMENT ON ABM/TMD IS GOOD, A STEP 
FORWARD. 
6. FORMER YUGOSLAVIA. PRIMAKOV SAID THE SITUATION IS COMPLEX. 
WHAT MATTERS NOW IS THAT THE DAYTON ACCORD BE IMPLEMENTED IN ALL 
ITS PARTS. THE SYSTEM OF GOVERNMENTAL AUTHORITY NEEDS TO BEGIN 
FUNCTIONING. RUSSIA IS DOING ALL IT CAN UNDER THE CIRCUMSTANCES 
TO PREVENT ANY FORMER YUGOSLAV SIDE FROM PUTTING UP OBSTACLES. 
NOW, WE NEED TO PREPARE THE NOVEMBER MUNICIPAL ELECTIONS. IT IS 
GOOD THAT IFOR CONTINUE IN ITS CURRENT SCOPE AND FORMAT THROUGH 
THE MUNICIPAL ELECTIONS. THE MILITARY PRESENCE IS NECESSARY IN 
ANY CASE. DUE TO CONSIDERATIONS ON THE U.S. SIDE, THERE COULD BE 
MODIFICATIONS IN IFOR'S STRUCTURE, TERMS OF REFERENCE, AND SIZE. 
IF.THE MILITARY PART OF THE OPERATION IS MAINTAINED, RUSSIA WILL 
CONTINUE TO TAKE PART. 
7. THE ISSUE OF THE MOMENT, PRIMAKOV CONTINUED, IS THE LIFTING 
OF SANCTIONS. THAT SHOULD HAPPEN, AS WAS AGREED BEFORE, 10 DAYS 
AFTER THE NATIONAL ELECTIONS. IT IS COUNTERPRODUCTIVE TO 
ANTAGONIZE MILOSEVIC NOW. PRIMAKOV PASSED OVER TO THE SECRETARY 
THE TEXT OF A DRAFT UNSC RESOLUTION ON THE LIFTING OF SANCTIONS 
HEADED "AS MAY BE ACCEPTABLE TO THE RUSSIAN FEDERATION." HE SAID

SANCTIONS WAS APPROACHING. HOWEVER, THE REMOVAL OF SANCTIONS IS

MEANWHILE, RUSSIA SHOULD WORK TO ENCOURAGE THE THREE PRESIDENTS 
TO MEET IN SARAJEVO TO DEMONSTRATE THE EFFECTIVENESS OF THE 
NATIONAL ELECTIONS. THE SECRETARY NOTED IN THE RUSSIAN DRAFT 
REFERENCES TO FROZEN ASSETS AND FRY ELIGIBILITY TO PARTICIPATE IN 
INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS, WHICH ARE NEW AND DIFFERENT ISSUES. 
WE WILL TRY TO WORK THEM WITH AMB. LAVROV, BUT THEY ARE COMPLEX. 
THE SECRETARY EXPRESSED APPRECIATION FOR THE COOPERATION WHICH 
HAS BEEN DEVELOPING BETWEEN AMBS. ALBRIGHT AND LAVROV. 
9. PRIMAKOV RESPONDED IT WAS AGREED IN LYON THAT THE LIFTING OF 
SANCTIONS WOULD OCCUR 10 DAYS AFTER THE ELECTIONS. ACCORDING TO 
MILOSEVIC, THE SECRETARY HAD SAID THE SAME TO HIM -- REMOVE 
SANCTIONS 10 DAYS AFTER ELECTIONS -- WHICH WAS ONE REASON 
MILOSEVIC HAD SIGNED DAYTON. THE SECRETARY SAID THE AGREED 
PROCEDURE IS CLEAR: THE,LIFTING OF SANCTIONS OCCURS THROUGH A 
UNSC VOTE ON THE CERTIFICATION OF FREE AND FAIR ELECTIONS, AND 
THE UNSC DETERMINATION CANNOT BE MADE UNTIL THE ELECTION 
PROCEDURES ARE COMPLETED. PRIMAKOV CLAIMED CERTIFICATION IS A 
NEW ELEMENT. HE WAS NOT AWARE OF ANY UNSC AGREEMENT ON 
CERTIFICATION; RATHER, IT WAS AGREED THAT LIFT WOULD OCCUR 10 
DAYS AFTER ELECTIONS. PRIMAKOV SAID THE SECRETARY'S ARGUMENT 
OFFERED THE DILEMMA THAT SANCTIONS COULD BE LIFTED, OR THAT 
ABSENCE OF LIFT WOULD DENY THE VALIDITY OF THE ELECTIONS AND 
DAYTON. THE SECRETARY SUGGESTED HE AND PRIMAKOV TRY TO AVOID 
POLARIZING THE ISSUE. PRIMAKOV COMPLAINED THAT AMB. FROWICK HAD 
TOLD HIM THREE DAYS PREVIOUSLY THAT -THE ELECTIONS HAD BEEN FREE 
AND FAIR. THE SECRETARY NOTED THAT FROWICK WAS WORKING ON A 
RECOUNT. PRIMAKOV ASKED IF THE CONTACT GROUP COULD ESTABLISH A 
DEADLINE. THE SECRETARY URGED THAT A SHORT PERIOD WAS AT ISSUE; 
HE AGAIN ASKED THAT RUSSIA HELP IN GETTING THE THREE PRESIDENTS 
TO SARAJEVO; HE UNDERSCORED THAT FROZEN ASSETS AND INTERNATIONAL 
ORGANIZATIONS WERE DIFFICULT ISSUES. AMB. LAVROV NOTED THE 
LIFTING OF SANCTIONS WAS ON THE UNSC'S AGENDA FOR SEPTEMBER 24. 
10. START-2. THE SECRETARY EXPRESSED HIS HOPE RUSSIA WOULD 
RATIFY START-2 SOON AND NOT SEEK TO EXTEND THE TIMELINE FOR 
REDUCTIONS BEYOND 2003. HE APPRECIATED THAT THERE WILL BE COSTS 
FOR RUSSIA, BUT THESE SHOULD BE MANAGEABLE GIVEN REDUCTIONS 
ALREADY ACCOMPLISHED. THE SECRETARY ASKED FOR PRIMAKOV'S 
PREDICTION. 
11. PRIMAKOV SAID THE ISSUE IS CLOSELY RELATED TO ABM/TMD 
DEMARCATION. AFTER THAT IS ACHIEVED, PROSPECTS IN THE DUMA FOR 
RATIFICATION WOULD BE BETTER. THE TIMELINE IS A SERIOUS MATTER 
FOR RUSSIA, WHICH SEEKS A FIVE-YEAR INCREASE IN THE 
IMPLEMENTATION PERIOD. PRIMAKOV NOTED THIS WAS THE SUBJECT OF A 
PRESIDENTIAL EXCHANGE. 
12. PRIMAKOV CONFIRMED HE WOULD BE SIGNING THE CTBT 
SEPTEMBER 24.

ACTIONS, NOT HIS WORDS, AND WE ARE WATCHING CAREFULLY. AS

BUT WE MUST PROTECT OUR AIRCREWS. MEANWHILE, WE RECOGNIZE THERE 
ARE DIFFERENCES IN THE U.S. AND RUSSIAN APPROACHES TO IRAQ, AND
