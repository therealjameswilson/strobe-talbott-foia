SUMMARY 
1. 4-81 MEETING WITH THE AMBASSADOR ON MARCH 16, 
RUSSIA'S "YABLOKO" PARTY LEADER GRIGORIY 
YAVLINSKIY REVEALED THAT, DURING HIS MUCH-TALKED-
ABOUT 90-MINUTE MEETING WITH THE PRESIDENT ON 
MARCH 10, YELTSIN MADE CLEAR HE WAS LOOKING FOR A 
SUCCESSOR AND THAT HE CONSIDERS YAVLINSKIY A 
DESIRABLE CANDIDATE. YELTSIN ALSO CONFIRMED THAT 
PRIMAKOV IS RESISTING YELTSIN'S DEMANDS TO MAKE 
CHANGES TO THE GOVERNMENT'S ECONOMIC TEAM (I.E., 
MASLUYKOV AND KULIK) AND THAT THE PRESIDENTIAL 
ADMINISTRATION STAFF IS PRESSING THIS MATTER MORE 
AGGRESSIVELY THAN YELTSIN HIMSELF. YAVLINSKIY 
CONCLUDED THAT YELTSIN INTENDS TO REMOVE PRIMAKOV, 
BUT NOT IN THE NEAR TERM. YAVLINSKIY ALSO WARNED 
THAT CHECHNYA COULD BECOME A BIG PROBLEM FOR 
PRIMAKOV, BUT NOT NECESSARILY A POLITICAL PROBLEM. 
END SUMMARY

8. 48+ IN REPLY TO THE AMBASSADOR'S QUESTION ON 
THE CONSEQUENCES OF REMOVING MASLYUKOV AND KULIK 
IMMEDIATELY, YAVLINSKIY MADE THE POINT THAT 
REACTION TO THEIR REMOVAL SHOULD NOT BE

IT IS FAR MORE IMPORTANT TO STOP THE PENDULUM FROM

REITERATED HIS CONVICTION THAT MASLYUKOV AND KULIK 
ARE CORRUPT.

CHECHNYA 
10. -(11:1-} ON CHECHNYA, YAVLINSKIY EXPLAINED THAT 
THE PROBLEM IS BOTH EASY AND COMPLICATED. 
,CHECHNYA IS A CRIMINAL TERRITORY, WITHOUT CONTROLS 
OF ANY KIND. IT CANNOT EXIST AS A STATE, NOR CAN 
IT EXIST AS A CONSTITUENT PART OF A STATE -- IT 
CAN ONLY EXIST AS A TERRORIST ENVIRONMENT. RUSSIA 
SIMPLY HAS NO ABILITY TO ADDRESS THE PROBLEMS IN 
CHECHNYA AT THE PRESENT TIME. 
11. -(-8-)- IN REPLY TO THE AMBASSADOR'S QUESTION ON 
WHAT WOULD HAPPEN IF NOTHING IS DONE, YAVLINSKIY 
SAID THAT, IN THE WORST CASE, CHECHNYA'S BRAND OF 
TERRORISM WOULD SPREAD MORE QUICKLY THROUGHOUT THE 
REGION AND BEGIN TO CREATE "DANGER ZONES" IN MANY 
PARTS OF RUSSIA -- EVEN IN MOSCOW. IN ANY 
SCENARIO, CHECHNYA CAN ONLY SPREAD TERRORISM -- IT 
IS THE ONLY WAY FOR CHECHNYA NOW; IT HAS NO 
GOVERNMENT THAT CAN EXERCISE ANY CONTROL AND 
RUSSIA IS NOT ABLE TO CONFINE THE CRIMINAL 
TERRORISM TO ITS BORDERS. CHECHNYA IS BECOMING 
"PART OF OUR LIVES," YAVLINSKIY UNDERSCORED, AND 
IT IS "DANGEROUS." 
12. -I.-Si-NEVERTHELESS, DESPITE THE FACT THAT AT 
ANY DAY DEVELOPMENTS IN CHECHNYA CAN QUICKLY 
BECOME A BIG PROBLEM FOR PRIMAKOV, IT PROBABLY 
WILL NOT BECOME A "POLITICAL" PROBLEM. YAVLINSKIY 
GOVERNMENT, CHECHNYA, AND PRESIDENTIAL POLITICS 
EXPLAINED THAT THIS IS A "FAMILY KIND OF. PROBLEM," 
AND NOT A PROBLEM THAT SHOULD AFFECT PRIMAKOV 
POLITICALLY. THIS IS A PROBLEM THAT NO ONE CAN 
SOLVE.
