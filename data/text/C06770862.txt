  THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE 
HAD BEEN FOLLOWING THE SITUATION CLOSELY AND REGARDED THE 
RECENT AGREEMENT REACHED BY OSCE CIO VOLLEBAEK WITH FRY 
PRESIDENT MILOSEVIC AS LESS THAN SATISFACTORY. 
PRINCIPALLY, THE AGREEMENT GAVE MILOSEVIC THE DIPLOMATIC 
EQUIVALENT  OF AN ACTORD, ENABLING HIM TO EXPEL WALKER AT 
ANY TIME. 
5. tst THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, EMPHASIZING THAT HE HAD NOT 
YET SPOKEN WITH THE SECRETARY, SAID HE FELT MILOSEVIC'S 
MINIMAL RESPONSE MADE IT IMPERATIVE THAT THE NATO ALLIES 
PUSH FOR ACTORD THIS WEEKEND. VOLLEBAEK HAD A SHORT TIME 
EARLIER TOLD THE SECRETARY THE THREAT OF ACTORD HAD BEEN 
INSTRUMENTAL IN CONVINCING MILOSEVIC TO FREEZE WALKER'S 
EXPULSION, NOTED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY. EVEN THOUGH THIS 
CONCESSION WAS PROVOCATIVE AND PROBLEMATIC, IT, WAS BETTER
THAN FORCING WALKER TO LEAVE THE REGION. 
6. 4.S4- THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE WOULD MEET WITH THE 
SECRETARY SHORTLY WOULD DISCUSS CONTACTING ALLIES AND 
CONTACT GROUP CAPITALS TO EXPLAIN MILOSEVIC HAD GIVEN 
NOTHING ON ANYTHING OTHER THAN THE WALKER ISSUE. HE ADDED 
THAT GIVEN MILOSEVIC'S DEFIANT, PROVOCATIVE, AND 
UNACCEPTABLE BEHAVIOR, THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY SHOULD 
PRESS IMMEDIATELY FOR ACTORD. 
7. (S) THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID EVEN IF MILOSEVIC HAD 
GIVEN UP COMPLETELY ON WALKER, THIS WOULD STILL BE 
INSUFFICIENT BECAUSE MILOSEVIC FAILED TO COMPLY IN OTHER 
SIGNIFICANT AREAS. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
EMPHASIZED A SUCCESSFUL NATO SUMMIT WOULD BE IMPOSSIBLE IF 
THE SITUATION IN KOSOVO WORSENED.
