2. -f-g.).._SUMMARY: OMAN'S MINISTER OF COMMERCE, MAQBOOL 
BIN ALI SULTAN, LED, FOR THE OMANI SIDE, ON ACCESSION TO 
THE WTO DURING A LUNCHEON DISCUSSION WITH DEPUTY 
SECRETARY TALBOTT, MARCH 14 (SEE SEPTELS FOR REPORTING 
ON OTHER DISCUSSION ITEMS). MAQBOOL ONCE AGAIN FOCUSSED 
ON THE CONTRAST BETWEEN THE NEGOTIATIONS WITH OMAN AND 
WTO ACCESSION AGREEMENTS WITH THE FOUR GCC STATES 
(KUWAIT, BAHRAIN, QATAR, AND UAE) ALREADY IN THE WTO. 
HE DID ACKNOWLEDGE THAT OMAN'S REQUEST FOR A FIVE-YEAR 
TRANSITION PERIOD FOR IMPLEMENTING REGULATIONS ON 
SOFTWARE PROTECTIONS IS A NON-STARTER, AND CLAIMED THAT 
OMAN WOULD IMPLEMENT REGULATIONS PROTECTING AUDIO AND 
VIDEO PRODUCTS IN 1998. BUT HE REITERATED THE REQUEST 
FOR A TWO-YEAR TRANSITION FOR SOFTWARE PROTECTIONS AND 
REPEATED EARLIER ARGUMENTS IN SUPPORT OF OMAN'S OFFERS 
ON GOODS AND SERVICES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, HANDING 
OVER A LETTER TO MAQBOOL FROM USTR BARSHEFSKY, 
REITERATED U.S. SUPPORT FOR OMANI ACCESSION TO THE WTO, 
BUT EMPHASIZED THAT U.S. POSITIONS ON ACCESSION WERE 
GROUNDED IN FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES AND EQUITIES. 
CUTTING TO THE BOTTOM LINE, MINISTER RESPONSIBLE FOR 
FOREIGN AFFAIRS BIN ALAWI PLEADED FOR U.S. SUPPORT ON 
WTO ACCESSION REGARDLESS OF THE RULES. TALBOTT REPLIED 
THAT THE U.S. COULD ONLY SUPPORT OMAN WITHIN THE 
FRAMEWORK OF WTO AULES, END SUMMARY 
3. "Tcl-- MINISTER OF COMMERCE MAQBOOL BIN ALI SULTAN 
RAISED OMAN'S ACCESSION TO THE WORLD TRADE ORGANIZATION 
(WTO) DURING A LUNCH DISCUSSION WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT, MARCH 14. INDICATING THAT THE OMANI DELEGATION 
IN GENEVA HAD MET WITH U.S. TRADE NEGOTIATORS TWO WEEKS 
EARLIER, MAQBOOL ALLEGED THAT THE U.S. MISUNDERSTANDS 
OMAN'S POSITION ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS 
PROTECTION AND WTO ACCESSION. HE EVEN CRITICIZED 
EMBASSY INTERVENTION AT RECENT IPR SEMINAR (REFTEL). 
IPR IS A "VAST ISSUE," MAQBOOL ARGUED, AND OMAN HAS 
ALREADY AGREED TO MEET ALL OF THE REQUIREMENTS. IN THE 
AREA OF TRADEMARKS, OMAN HAS HAD LAWS IN PLACE SINCE 
1988. OMAN, WITH THE OTHER GCC STATES, WILL IMPLEMENT A 
PATENT LAW THIS YEAR, AND WILL ESTABLISH A SUB-OFFICE OF 
THE GCC PATENT ADMINISTRATION IN MUSCAT. AS FOR 
COPYRIGHT PROTECTIONS ON AUDIO, VIDEO, AND COMPUTER 
SOFTWARE PRODUCTS, OMAN WILL IMPLEMENT REGULATIONS FOR 
AUDIO AND VIDEO MATERIAL THIS YEAR. IN FACT, HE SAID,

4. (b.), TURNING TO OMAN'S MARKET ACCESS OFFER, MAQBOOL 
OPENED DISCUSSION BY NOTING THAT THE U.S. HAD SURPASSED 
THE UK FOR THE FIRST TIME, IN 1997, AS-A NET EXPORTER OF 
GOODS TO OMAN (UAE AND JAPAN OCCUPY THE TOP TWO SLOTS). 
REVISITING ARGUMENTS ALREADY TESTED WITH U.S. TRADE REPS 
IN GENEVA, MAQBOOL ASSERTED THAT OMAN, AS A DEVELOPING 
STATE, REQUIRES A HIGHER TARIFF RATE TO PROTECT INFANT 
INDUSTRIES. COMPARING OMAN'S PROPOSAL WITH ITS GCC 
NEIGHBORS, MAQBOOL NOTED THAT OMAN IS SEEKING ONLY 
TREATMENT COMPARABLE TO BAHRAIN'S THIRTY-FIVE PERCENT 
TARIFF. IN RESPONSE TO AMBASSADOR COOK'S INTERVENTION, 
POINTING OUT THAT KUWAIT, BAHRAIN, UAE, AND QATAR HAD 
ENTERED THE WTO UNDER DIFFERENT NEGOTIATING STANDARDS, 
MAQBOOL DISAGREED, ARGUING THAT THE WTO IS JUST A FORUM 
FOR BARGAINING: "IF YOU SHOUT LOUDER, YOU GET MORE."

7. I'S& COMMENT: THE MEETING WITH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
PROVIDED THE OMANIS WITH THE OPPORTUNITY TO REITERATE 
THEIR WELL-KNOWN POSITIONS ON THE WTO. IT APPEARS FROM 
MAQBOOL SULTAN'S COMMENTS THAT THE OMANIS ARE BEGINNING 
TO RECOGNIZE THAT THEIR POSITION ON IPR IMPLEMENTATION 
IS UNTENABLE, AS DID THEIR DEFENSIVE PRESS COMMUNIQUE, 
EARLIER IN THIS WEEK, DURING THE MOTION PICTURE 
ASSOCIATION-ORGANIZED IPR SEMINAR AT THE CHAMBER OF 
COMMERCE (REFTEL). BUT FURTHER EVOLUTION IS REQUIRED 
BEFORE THEY ESTABLISH AN ACCEPTABLE POSITION AND, GIVEN 
THEIR TRACK RECORD OF BROKEN PROMISES ON IPR 
ENFORCEMENT, WE REMAIN SKEPTICAL ABOUT THEIR WILLINGNESS 
TO UNDERTAKE SERIOUS ENPORCEMENT MEASURES THIS YEAR. WE 
APPRECIATE USTR'S EFFORT TO PROVIDE THE LETTER FROM 
CHARLENE BARSHEFSKY (GIVEN OUR CONCERN THAT MINCOMMERCE

PROVIDED A COPY OF THAT LETTER TO SULTAN QABOOS AS
