My firm is advising a small group of audited Russian commercial banks on how to provide hard 
currency financing to support foreign and domestic long-term investments inside Russia. The goal is 
to recapture Russia's flight capital and put it to work inside Russia in place of U.S.-owned dollars. 
I would like to visit with you to explain who is involved in the project, what our goals are, and how 
we are going to achieve them. We believe that this initiative will compliment where you are likely to 
want Exim to be headed with respect to Russia, but it is best that we be sure about your goals. Also, 
if the banks involved can make course corrections that complement Exim's own resources, we ought 
to take the time now to find out.
