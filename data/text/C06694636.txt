Declassification Authority: Geoffrey W. Chapman, 
Senior Reviewer, A/GIS/IPS 5/15/2019 
2. DURING A CALL ON KING SIHANOUK JANUARY 29 
(SEPTEL), DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT ASKED THE 
KING FOR HIS INSIGHTS INTO THE SITUATION IN 
NORTH KOREA. THE KING REPLIED THAT, BEFORE 
RETURNING TO CAMBODIA FROM BEIJING, HE HAD 
SOUGHT TO TRAVEL TO PYONGYANG TO PAY A VISIT ON 
KIM JONG-IL. HOWEVER, THE NORTH KOREANS 
INFORMED HIM THAT BECAUSE THE MOURNING PERIOD 
FOR KIM IL-SUNG WAS STILL CONTINUING, THE SON 
COULD NOT MEET SIHANOUK YET. HE SAID THE 
CURRENT STATE OF AFFAIRS IN THE DPRK, THEREFORE, 
"REMAINS MYSTERIOUS EVEN TO ME. EVEN SIHANOUK, 
THE BEST FRIEND OF NORTH KOREA, CANNOT GO 
THERE." SIHANOUK HOPED TO PAY A VISIT THIS 
COMING JULY OR AUGUST BUT THE DPRK HAS NOT 
AGREED TO THIS YET. 
3. THE KING TOOK ISSUE WITH WHAT HE CALLED A 
TENDENCY BY WESTERNERS TO SEE THE SITUATION IN 
NORTH KOREA AS A POWER STRUGGLE BETWEEN THE 
POLITICAL LEADERSHIP AND THE MILITARY. IN HIS 
VIEW THERE IS NO PROBLEM BETWEEN KIM JONG-IL AND 
THE MILITARY. THE NORTH KOREANS ARE MORE 
ROYALIST THAN COMMUNIST. IN ANCIENT KOREAN 
TRADITION, A YEAR-LONG PERIOD OF MOURNING 
FOLLOWED A KING'S DEATH, DURING WHICH TIME A NEW 
KING COULD NOT BE CROWNED. KIM IL-SUNG WAS A 
VIRTUAL KING AND THE NORTH KOREANS SEEM TO BE 
FOLLOWING THIS TRADITION. 
4. SIHANOUK SAID THE NORTH KOREANS ARE AWARE OF 
THE DISADVANTAGES OF ISOLATION; DESPITE THEIR 
PROPAGANDA THEY DO NOT WANT TO GO IT ALONE. 
THEY ARE, THEREFORE, VERY PROUD TO BE IN 
DISCUSSIONS WITH THE UNITED STATES. KIM IL-SUNG 
SENT SIHANOUK A SPECIAL NOTE WHEN JIMMY CARTER 
VISITED PYONGYANG TO SAY HOW HAPPY HE WAS ABOUT 
THE VISIT. IMPROVED RELATIONS WITH THE U.S. ARE 
OF THE UTMOST IMPORTANCE TO THE NORTH KOREANS. 
KING SIHANOUK SAID THAT, AS A FRIEND OF BOTH 
NORTH KOREA AND THE UNITED STATES, HE IS PLEASED 
BY THIS DEVELOPMENT AND HOPES TO SEE FURTHER 
IMPROVEMENT OF OUR TIES. 
5. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED KING SIHANOUK TO 
PASS ON A MESSAGE TO PYONGYANG THAT POSITIVE 
RELATIONS WITH THE UNITED STATES CANNOT DEVELOP 
FURTHER UNTIL THE DPRK TAKES STEPS WITH THE 
SOUTH. "U.S. RELATIONS WITH NORTH KOREA WILL
