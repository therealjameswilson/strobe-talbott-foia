2. SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET OCTOBER 23 
WITH PRESIDENT MUGABE TO REVIEW PEACEKEEPING AND 
REGIONAL ISSUES. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPRESSED 
CONCERN ABOUT REPORTS OF HIDDEN ARMS CASHES IN 
MOZAMBIQUE, AND UNDERSCORED U.S. SUPPORT FOR FREE AND 
FAIR ELECTIONS. MUGABE SAID THAT IS JUST THE MESSAGE 
HE INTENDS TO SEND MOZAMBIQUE'S REPRESENTATIVES AT 
SADC'S EXPANDED MEETING IN HARARE OCTOBER 25. MUGABE 
SAID THE REGION IS FIRMLY OPPOSED TO ANY EFFORT TO 
DESTABILIZE ELECTED GOVERNMENTS, AND PRAISED U.S. 
INTERVENTION IN HAITI TO RESTORE DEMOCRATIC RULE. 
DEPSEC TALBOTT PRAISED MUGABE'S REGIONAL LEADERSHIP 
WITHIN SADC AND OFFERED TO EXPLORE HOW WE COULD HELP 
THE SOUTHERN AFRICAN DEVELOPMENT COMMUNITY (SADC) 
MEET /TS LOGISTICS NEEDS AS IT EXPANDS ITS 
PEACEKEEPING FUNCTION. END SUMMARY. 
3. DEPSEC TALBOTT AND PARTY FLEW BACK TO ZIMBABWE 
FROM MALAWI FOR A SPECIAL SUNDAY EVENING MEETING ON 
OCTOBER 23 WITH PRESIDENT MUGABE TO EXCHANGE VIEWS ON 
REGIONAL ISSUES AND PEACEKEEPING ACTIVITIES. 
SECRETARY TALBOTT THANKED THE PRESIDENT FOR HIS 
EFFORTS IN RESTORING DEMOCRATIC RULE IN LESOTHO, BUT 
EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT HIDDEN ARMS CACHES IN 
MOZAMBIQUE THAT COULD THREATEN THE ELECTORAL PROCESS 
THERE. MUGABE WAS NOT AWARE OF THE MOST RECENT 
REPORTS ON ARMS VIOLATIONS, BUT SAID HE WAS CONCERNED 
ABOUT THE UPCOMING ELECTIONS AND WOULD DO WHAT HE 
COULD TO ENSURE THAT DEMOCRACY DID NOT SUFFER A 
SETBACK. 
4. DEPSEC TALBOTT OFFERED TO SHARE INFORMATION WITH 
MUGABE ON THE RECENT ARMS CASHE DISCOVERIES TO HELP 
THE GOZ PREPARE FOR THE SADC MEETING. MUGABE 
ACCEPTED THE OFFER, AND EXPLAINED THAT WITH THE 
DEMISE OF APARTHEID IN SOUTH AFRICA, THE FLS WAS 
BEING EXPANDED TO INCLUDE ALL THE COUNTRIES IN THE 
REGION. THE FOCUS NOW WAS ON STRENGTHENING 
DEMOCRACY, PARTICULARLY FOLLOWING SOUTH AFRICA'S 
SUCCESSFUL TRANSITION. MUGABE SAID THAT'THE NATIONS 
IN SOUTHERN AFRICA WERE UNITED BEHIND THE PRINCIPLE 
THAT DEMOCRATIC GOVERNMENTS SHOULD NOT BE 
DESTABILIZED. 
5. MUGABE TOLD THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THAT HE HAD 
SCHEDULED SADC MEETING (INCLUDING MALAWI, LESOTHO, 
AND SWAZILAND) IN HARARE FOR OCTOBER 25 TO UNDERSCORE 
THE MESSAGE THAT THE MOZAMBICAN ELECTIONS BE FREE AND 
FAIR, AND WITHOUT VIOLENCE. IF THE ELECTIONS PROVE
