piece of January 10, 1949). Also your mention of Einstein and Camus, both of whom 
identified with and supported our claim to world citizenship during those hectic days when 
the UN GA sat at the Palais de Chaillot, further encouraged rne. But your new assignment 
at the State Department prompts me now to communicate some ideas for your 
consideration.

suggested. It is fact. Viewing our century in retrospect with its insane national and 
international wars has impelled concemed citizens throughout the world to act by claiming 
that highest citizenship which alone can address planetary problems. 
This sovereign action - the exercise of the inalienable right of political choice - has resulted 
in the World Govemment of World Citizens.

those stitching nations together or those tearing them apart?" as your Time piece asked, but 
how do people commit themselves to a higher allegiance capable of addressing their new 
global problems? "Stitching nations together.." typifies the nation-centric thinking which

Thus, if indeed legal world order as you claim is the sine qua non of human survival as we 
approach the 2nd millenium, then the only government able to save the human race is the 
world government already declared despite its present minimal if evolving power. 
As a former joumalist, you of course reported daily on nations tearing themselves apart. 
Ironically, your assignment in the state diplomatic process is part of that breakdown 
metaphor. I am wondering, therefore, how you equate being a national diplomat with your 
advocacy of "a union of separate states that allocate certain powers to a central government 
while retaining many others for themselves..." and with statements like "..perhaps national 
sovereignty wasn't such a great idea after all.." and "..it has taken the events in our own 
wondrous and terrible century to clinch the case for world government." 
In your capacity as Deputy-Secretary of State then are you going to advise your president - 
as this writer did during his campaign - that his only mandate as chief executive of 
constitutional laws, given the anarchic world community, is to endorse and promote the 
evolution of a world constitutional process so as to protect his own electorate as well as that 
of all national electorates? 
President Clinton will meet President Yeltsin in Russia and President Havel in Prague next 
week. (Incidentally, the latter aiso is a firm world government advocate and has a World 
Passport from our government). His new "Partnership for Peace" is literally meaningless 
so long as nations enjoy the legal right to make war. Without law, "Partnership for Peace" 
is a mere sop for a public desperate for sane leadership and a insipid coverup for the ° 
perpetuation of the anarchic state between nation-states. It is moreover a signal co the world 
public of a confused and threadbare foreign policy. In short, it is a travesty. 
Today's news stories report that Vladimir Zhirinovsky ridiculed President Clinton for 
refusing to xneet him in Moscow. As,a national legislator, he is of course dangerous....but 
no less so than Senator Nunn. Only by raising the level of legislative power to represent 
humanity can the Nunnahirinovsky's of the world be neutralized. But they are legion, 
Mr. Talbott, as you well know. They reside in the mind and heart of every national soldier 
from the generals on down the ranks to the lowliest foot soldier. They reside in the offices 
of state departments, intelligence services, national security councils, congresses and 
parliaments which allocate funds for so-called national defense, the metaphor for world 
lawlessness and ultimate omnicide. 
They permeate the national world. And, a.s Toffler pointed out in The Third Wave, they are 
obsolete. 
However, I find it heartening, if puzzling, that a world government advocate will find 
himself the Deputy-Secretary of State in the world's so-called remaining superpower. 
Maybe it is a sign that the injunctions of the Declaration of Independence re the protection 
of inalienable human rights by "government with the consent of the governed" will finally 
bear global fruit.
