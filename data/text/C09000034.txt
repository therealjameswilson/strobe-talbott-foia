Dear Strobe: 
RELEASE IN FULL 
I appreciate ever so much the detailed docurnentation I recently received from 
Frank Machak of the meetings Tom Watson, Bob Galvin, and I had with President 
Brezhnev, Foreign Minister Gromyko and Defense Minister Ustinov. 
I found that the minutes of our meetings and the full documentation of press 
conferences, etc... were far more detailed than I thought they would be. I am deeply 
grateful to you, Frank Machak, and Larry Napper, and I know the material will be of 
great interest to Bob Galvin. I told him about it at his and Mary's 50th wedding 
anniversary celebration last week, and I sat next to Olive Watson at the dinner they had 
and told her about the papers as well. She was very pleased indeed. 
Warmest personal regards, 
Charles H. Percy
