FOLLOWING IS A BRIEF MESSAGE REPORTING ON THE DEPSEC'S 
MEETING WITH SOMMARUGA FOR YOUR CLEARANCE. 
1.   SUMMARY: IN AN APRIL 28 MEETING WITH ICRC PRESIDENT 
SOMMARUGA IN GENEVA, DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT EXPRESSED U.S. 
SUPPORT FOR AN EARLY RETURN OF ICRC TO ALL PARTS OF THE FRY, 
INCLUDING KOSOVO. SOMMARUGA DESCRIBED HIS MEETINGS IN 
BELGRADE AND MILOSEVIC'S AGREEMENT TO ALLOW ICRC UNIVERSAL, 
UNIMPEDED ACCESS TO ALL THOSE IN NEED IN THE FRY. ICRC IS 
CONDUCTING DETAILED DISCUSSIONS WITH FRY OFFICIALS AND WITH 
NATO. SOMMARUGA EXPRESSED CONCERN ABOUT COLLATERAL DAMAGE 
IN BELGRADE FROM THE BOMBING CAMPAIGN AND ITS AFFECT ON THE 
CIVILIAN POPULATION. TALBOTT EXPRESSED U.S. APPRECIATION TO 
SOMMARUGA FOR THE BREAKTHROUGH HE HAD ACHIEVED WITH REGARD 
TO ACCESS TO THE THREE U.S. PRISONERS. END SUMMARY. 
2. (U) ON APRIL 28, DEPUTY SECRETARY STROBE TALBOTT MET WITH 
ICRC PRESIDENT CORNELIO SOMMARUGA AT ICRC HEADQUARTERS IN 
GENEVA. SOMMARUGA WAS ACCOMPANIED BY DIRECTOR OF OPERATIONS 
JEAN DANIAL TAUXE, EUROPEAN DIRECTORS ANGELO GNAEDINGER AND 
BEATRICE MEGEVAND ROGGO. TALBOTT WAS ACCOMPANIED BY 
AMBASSADOR MOOSE, EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT PHIL GOLDBERG AND 
MISSION NOTETAKER. 
3.-tel- TALBOTT EXPRESSED U.S. APPRECIATION FOR ICRC EFFORTS 
IN THE BALKANS, AND TO PRESIDENT SOMMARUGA PERSONNALLY FOR 
THE BREAKTHROUGH HE HAD ACHIEVED WITH REGARD TO ACCESS TO 
THE THREE U.S. PRISONERS. HE REITERATED U.S. SUPPORT FOR AN 
EARLY RETURN OF THE ICRC TO ALL PARTS OF THE FRY, INCLUDING 
KOSOVO, BOTH FOR POLITICAL AND HUMANITARIAN REASONS. BASED 
ON HIS RECENT DISCUSSIONS IN BOTH BERLIN AND MOSCOW, TALBOTT 
SAID HE BELIEVES THAT RUSSIA IS WORKING IN A GENUINELY 
CONSTRUCTIVE DIRECTION TO ACHIEVE A POLITICAL SETTLEMENT TO 
THE CONFLICT. UNFORTUNATELY, IT REMAINS UNCLEAR WHETHER 
MILOSEVIC CAN BE BROUGHT ONBOARD. AS NATO INTENSIFIES THE 
AIR CAMPAIGN IN THE COMING DAYS, AND REITERATES THE TERMS 
FOR ENDING THE BOMBING, TALBOTT STRESSED, IT WILL BE 
IMPORTANT TO INCLUDE BOTH THE UN AND THE RUSSIANS IN OUR 
EFFORTS. 
4. --te+- TALBOTT ASKED SOMMARUGA TO DESCRIBE THE TERMS OF 
REFERENCE UNDER WHICH THE ICRC WOULD RETURN TO KOSOVO. 
SOMMARUGA EXPLAINED THAT HIS DISCUSSION WITH MILOSEVIC 
REVOLVED AROUND A SPECIAL AGREEMENT FOR "SECURITY" -- BOTH 
FOR ICRC PERSONNEL/OPERATIONS AND FOR THE CIVILIAN 
POPULATION THEY WOULD BE AIDING. MILOSEVIC'S BROAD 
AGREEMENT TO ALLOW ICRC UNIVERSAL ACCESS TO ALL THOSE IN 
NEED, WITHOUT RESTRICTIONS MUST NOW BE MADE IN WRITING AND 
MUST INCLUDE DETAILS SUCH AS AGREEMENT ON VISAS FOR LARGE 
NUMBERS OF INTERNATIONAL STAFF, FREE CIRCULATION,

5. ON THE SPECIFICS OF HOW ICRC WILL OPERATE IN THE FRY, 
SOMMARUGA PROMISED TO PROVIDE AMBASSADOR MOOSE WITH A 
COMPLETE BRIEFING (SEPTEL). HE SAID HE HAD ALREADY SPOKEN 
WITH NATO SYG SOLANA AND WITH SACEUR CLARKE ABOUT HIS PLANS 
"IN GENERAL TERMS," AND WOULD BE SENDING A TEAM TO BRUSSELS 
EARLY NEXT WEEK TO ESTABLISH A MODUS OPERANDI WITH NATO. HE 
WANTS TO ENSURE THAT A GOOD LIAISON RELATIONSHIP IS 
ESTABLISHED. 
6. (C)  TURNING TO THE CURRENT SITUATION ON THE GROUND, 
SOMMARUGA SAID THAT HE REGRETTED THAT MILITARY FORCES, 
ESPECIALLY NATO, HAD TO BE CALLED IN TO SUPPORT HUMANITARIAN 
ACTIVITIES.' WHILE HE DOES NOT DISPUTE THAT IN THIS CRISIS, 
ONLY THE MILITARY HAS THE KIND OF ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE, 
RESOURCES AND MANPOWER TO DO THE URGENT TASK OF PROVIDING 
FOR THE REFUGEES, ICRC "IN PRINCIPLE" PREFERS A CLEARER 
SEPARATION'OF HUMANITARIAN AND MILITARY ACTIVITIES. 
7. H:L=l- HAVING JUST RETURNED FROM BELGRADE, HE WAS 
PARTICULARLY STRUCK BY THE COLLATERAL DAMAGE IN BELGRADE AND 
ITS AFFECT ON THE CIVILIAN.POPULATION. DAMAGE FROM THE 
BOMBING CAMPAIGN IS CLEARLY HAVING A PROFOUND IMPACT ON 
.LARGE PARTS OF THE CITY AND AVERAGE CITIZENS ARE NOW FEELING 
BOTH THE MATERIAL AND PSYCHOLOGICAL AFFECT.. TALBOTT AGREED 
THAT THERE HAS BEEN SUFFERING AS A RESULT OF THE AIR 
CAMPAIGN. IN ATTEMPTING TO COUNTER THE REGIME WHICH HAS 
CARRIED OUT PERHAPS THE WORST ATROCITIES SINCE WWII, SOME 
SUFFERING IS ALMOST INEVITABLE. THE ALLIES ARE WORKING HARD 
TO MINIMIZE IT AND ARE PURSUING EFFORTS To ALLOW THE 
REFUGEES AND IDPS TO RETURN AND TO ALLOW VITAL HUMANITARIAN 
WORK TO PROCEED UNIMPEDED. HE ASSURED SOMMARUGA THAT THE 
U.S. WILL SUPPORT THE ICRC'S EFFORTS IN THIS REGARD.

PRISONERS, AND NOTED THAT GNAEDINGER HAD BEEN ABLE TO SPEAK 
WITH THEIR FAMILIES BY TELEPHONE SUBSEQUENTLY. THE ICRC HAS 
REQUESTED WEEKLY VISITS AND WILL EXPEDITE DELIVERY OF 
REPORTS ON THE VISITS FOR THE U.S. ENGLISH LANGUAGE BOOKS 
AND MAGAZINES ARE ALSO BEING PROVIDED TO THE PRISONERS. 
SOMMARUGA CLOSED BY NOTING THAT HE WILL BE-IN WASHINGTON MAY 
5-7 FOR MEETINGS WITH THE AMERICAN RED CROSS AND THE ' 
AMERICAN JEWISH COMMITTEE AND WOULD BE AVAILABLE TO MEET 
WITH U.S. OFFICIALS ABOUT ICRC PLANS FOR THE FRY OR ABOUT 
THE PRISONERS.
