4. ~SUMMARY. THE SECRETARY PRESSED FM IVANOV TO REACTIVATE
THE OSCE ASSISTANCE GROUP IN THE NORTH CAUCASUS, GRANT ICRC
ACCESS TO DETAINEES AND WORK OUT TIES BETWEEN HUMAN RIGHTS
OMBUDSMAN KALAMANOV AND INTERNATIONAL EXPERTS. IVANOV WAS
RESPONSIVE ONLY ON ADDING TWO COE EXPERTS TO KALAMANOVS OFFICE
IN CHECHNYA. IVANOV WAS NONCOMMITTAL ON ICRC ACCESS TO
DETAINEES; ON AN OSCE AG PRESENCE, HE SAID FURTHER DISCUSSION
WITH THE OSCE WAS NEEDED. IVANOV AND THE SECRETARY AGREED
EXPERT DISCUSSIONS ON NMD DURING HIS APRIL VISIT TO WASHINGTON
WOULD BE HELPFUL. IVANOV WAS OPTIMISTIC ABOUT START II
RATIFICATION SHORTLY AFTER RUSSIAN PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS. ON
KOSOVO, THE SECRETARY URGED IVANOV TO SUPPORT MODERATE SERBS AND
STOP BLOCKING THE OSCE ELECTION REGISTRATION BUDGET. IVANOV
SAID HE NEEDED MORE INFORMATION ON THE LATTER. THE SECRETARY
AND IVANOV AGREED TO RESUME CONTACT GROUP MEETINGS AT THE
POLITICAL DIRECTOR LEVEL IN THE NEAR FUTURE. HE WAS NON-
COMMITTAL ON HER SUGGESTION FOR A HIGH-LEVEL RUSSIAN PUBLIC
STATEMENT„TO HELP ACCELERATE RESOLUTION OF THE EX-IM TYUMEN OIL
CASE. THE SECRETARY SAID SHE WOULD FOLLOW UP WITH A LETTER ON
THE SUBJECT. END SUMMARY.
CHECHNYA
5.~ THE SECRETARY HOSTED A WORKING BREAKFAST FOR FOREIGN
MINISTER IVANOV IN LISBON ON MARCH 3, IN ADVANCE OF THE U.S.-EU-
RUSSIA TRILATERAL MEETING (SEPTEL). (IVANOV MET WITH THE EU ON
MARCH 2.) THE SECRETARY OPENED BY TALKING ABOUT THE EFFECT OF
CHECHNYA ON U.S,-RUSSIA RELATIONS. SHE NOTED THAT RUSSIAN
SECURITY COUNCIL SECRETARY SERGEY IVANOVS FEBRUARY 16-19 VISIT
TO WASHINGTON HAD HELPED KEEP UP MOMENTUM IN BILATERAL RELATIONS
AFTER HER TRIP TO MOSCOW. ARMS CONTROL, NON-PROLIFERATION AND
ECONOMICS REMAINED AT THE TOP OF OUR AGENDA.
6. ~ THE ADMINISTRATIONS RUSSIA POLICY, HOWEVER, WAS COMING
UNDER INCREASING CRITICISM BECAUSE OF RUSSIAS CONDUCT IN
CHECHNYA. THE SECRETARY URGED RUSSIA TO SHOW IT WAS MOVING
TOWARD INTERNATIONAL NORMS AND REITERATED THE THREE STEPS
PROPOSED 'TO IVANOV IN HER FEBRUARY 29 LETTER: FIRST, REACTIVATE
THE OSCE ASSISTANCE GROUP IN THE NORTH CAUCASUS UNDER ITS
EXISTING. MANDATE; SECOND, GRANT ICRC ACCESS TO MONITOR
CONDITIONS IN DETENTION AND REFUGEE CAMPS IN CHECHNYA; AND,
THIRD, ARRANGE FOR INTERNATIONAL INVOLVEMENT WITH VLADIMIR
KALAMANOV, ACTING PRESIDENT PUTINS NEW HUMAN RIGHTS OMBUDSMAN
FOR CHECHNYA. THE GOAL WAS AGREEMENT ON THESE THREE OBJECTIVES
IN ORDER TO ANNOUNCE THEM WITH RUSSIA AND THE EU AT THE
TRILATERAL 'MEETING LATER THE SAME MORNING.
7. ~ THE SECRETARY STATED THAT, SINCE THE ISTANBUL SUMMIT LAST
NOVEMBER, THERE HAD BEEN PROMISES FROM THE RUSSIAN SIDE THAT
POSITIVE STEPS WERE ABOUT TO BE TAKEN ON CHECHNYA.
UNFORTUNATELY, THOSE POSITIVE ACTIONS HAD NOT MATERIALIZED AND
THAT WAS PART OF THE PROBLEM. THE ANNUAL UNHRC COMMISSION
MEETING WAS COMING UP THIS MONTH AND RUSSIAS TREATMENT WOULD
DEPEND IN PART ON THE OUTCOME OF THE U.S.-EU-RUSSIA TRILATERAL.
