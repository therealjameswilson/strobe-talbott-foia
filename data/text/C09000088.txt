Dear Strobe: 
I thought at this moment you might be glad to hear from someone 
who believes that US policy with respect to Russia has been 
constructive and that it is based on a realistic assessment of 
Russia's situation and our possibilities to influence it. Maybe we 
feel the way we do because our mutual mentor inspired us with 
affection for Russia and Russians. 
What troubles me at the moment is that democrats in Russia as 
well as concerned parties here are wholly engaged in election post 
mortems and Duma politics: they are ignoring the important local 
elections which are scheduled for the next three months. Perhaps the 
United States can offer democratic politicians (Burbulis?) appropriate 
practical assistance (including visits by Americans with hands-on 
experience in organizing political parties and campaigns) so that they 
can vigorously contest these elections for oblast and republic dumas. 
With regard to US assistance, in Moscow in November I met the 
Cecci assessment team formulating the AID program to promote rule of 
law in Russia. It seemed to me that they were floundering and ,need to 
find reliable and effective Russian partners. But that is the subject 
of another letter, after Jill and I return from Virgin Gorda on 
February B. 
Best Regards,
