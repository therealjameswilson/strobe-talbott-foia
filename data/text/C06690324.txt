Dear Hugh: 
I was deeply touched by your letter, which just caught 
up with me on the road. I've been with the President on 
his Europe/Russia trip. It was a hectic but good week. It 
would have been better if you'd been along. Briefing the 
WH press corps was a surreal experience for me, as you can 
imagine. On one occasion, I briefed them on a private 
dinner Yeltsin gave Clinton at a dacha. I had the good 
sense to swipe .the menu so that I could give the reporters 
newsmagazine-type color. 
I really would like to catch up with you on all kinds 
of things. Please give Valerie a call (647-3112) if you've 
ever got an excuse to be at the State Department, and we'll 
grab a few minutes together. 
,All the best -- and thanks again for your letter. 
Sincerely, 
(--Strobe Talbott
