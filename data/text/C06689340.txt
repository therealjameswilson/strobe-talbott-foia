RELEASE IN FULL 
June 29, 1993 
To: Ken Propp 
From: Strobe Talbot 
As I mentioned on the phone yesterday, my father, 
who is associated with a quartz manufacturing firm 
outside Cleveland, called to inform me that he had 
been making some inquiries about possibe USG agency 
financing for a joint venture of some kind with a 
Russian quartz firm. Since I chair the interagency 
Policy Steering Group on the Former Soviet Union 
and since the Eximbank and OPIC are both represented 
on that committee -- I wanted immediately to flag you 
of this matter. Naturally, I will take any steps you 
feel appropriate, by way of recusal or whatever, to 
avoid even the appearance of a conflict of interest. 
As I told you, the Steering Group does NOT pass on all 
or even most actions by Exim and OPIC, but it does) have 
overall policy—guidance supervisory responsibility for 
the activities of both in the FSU. 
Attached is a memo from my father recounting his 
calls on this subject.
