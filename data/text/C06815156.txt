2. THIS CABLE CONTAINS A MEMORANDUM OF THE DEPUTY
SECRETARY'S TELEPHONE CONVERSATION WITH FORMER FINNISH
PRESIDENT AHTISAARI ON SEPTEMBER 7, 2000. FORMER
PRESIDENT AHTISAARI, IN HELSINKI, INITIATED THE CALL TO
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY IN NEW YORK. SPECIAL ADVISOR FOR
KOSOVO JAMES O'RIEN ALSO PARTICIPATED -IN .THE CALL. THEY
DISCUSSED THE UPCOMING FRY ELECTIONS. I ASK THAT YOU NOT
PERMIT COPIES TO BE MADE OF THIS MESSAGE, AND THAT YOU NOT
DISTRIBUTE IT OUTSIDE OF THE MISSION'S EXECUTIVE OFFICE.
3. ~ BEGIN TEXT:
MEMORANDUM OF TELEPHONE CONVERSATION
DATE:
TIME:
SEPTEMBER 7, 2006
1322 EDT
~ PARTICIPANTS: THE DEPUTY SECRETARY, FORMER
FINNISH PRESIDENT AHTISAARI AND SPECIAL ADVISOR FOR KOSOVO
JAMES O'RIEN.
5. ~AHTISAARI TOLD THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THAT, BASED ON
HIS RECENT TRIP TO THE REGION AND RELIABLE, PROFESSIONAL
POLL RESULTS, HE WAS CONVINCED SERBIAN OPPOSITION LEADER
KOSTUNICA WOULD WIN THE UPCOMING FRY PRESIDENTIAL
ELECTIONS BY A WIDE MARGIN. MILOSEVIC WOULD HAVE TO STEAL
AT LEAST 30 PERCENT OF THE VOTE TO WIN THE ELECTION, HE
ADDED. HE URGED THAT ALL POSSIBLE MEASURES BE TAKEN TO
SUPPORT THE OPPOSITION, AND PARTICULARLY TO PREPARE FOR
THE POSSIBILITY OF MASS DEMONSTRATIONS BY THE OPPOSITION
AFTER 'LECTIONS.
6. ~IN RESPONSE TO THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S QUESTION AS
TO WHAT MEASURES THE EUROPEANS WOULD TAKE IF IT BECAME
CLEAR MILOSEVIC HAD STOLEN THE ELECTIONS, AHTISAARI
,RESPONDED HE DID NOT KNOW. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ADVISED
AHTISAARI THE U.S. HAD RECEIVED SOME INDICATIONS THE
EUROPEANS WOULD SUPPORT THE EXPULSION OF THE FRY FROM THE
UNITED NATIONS. O'RIEN NOTED THAT IF MILOSEVIC SOMEHOW
MANAGED TO SURVIVE THESE ELECTIONS AND THE EUROPEANS STILL
INSISTED ON CONSIDERING A LIFT OF SANCTIONS, THERE WOULD
BE'SOME FRICTION WITH THE U.S.; WE HAD EVIDENCE THAT
SANCTIONS WERE MAKING LIFE CONSIDERABLY HARDER FOR
MILOSEVIC AND HIS CRONIES.
7. fS,''RIENAND AHTISAARI AGREED THAT IT WAS
IMPERA IVE TO MAKE THE EXTENT OF MILOSEVIC'S'ELECTION
FRAUD PUBLIC QUICKLY AFTER THE POLLING WAS OVER. O'RIEN
MENTIONED ELECTION MONITORING WOULD BE DONE BY THREE
ENTITIES: DOMESTIC GROUPS, NGOS FROM NEIGHBORING
COUNTRIES~ AND THE OSCE MISSIONS IN BELGRADE 0 BRIEN
