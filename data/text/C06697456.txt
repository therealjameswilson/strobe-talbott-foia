5. -054- BOUTROS GHALI PLAYED DOWN HI8 ROLE NOTING THAT 
HE ADDRESSED THE CONFERENCE AND THEN HELD NUMEROUS 
BILATERAL MEETINGS. OF THOSE MEETINGS, HE SAID, HE 
SPENT OVER AN HOUR WITH UKRAINIAN PRESIDENT KUCHMA. 
KUCHMA INDICATED HE DOES NOT OPPOSE THE CIS. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY SAID OUR SOUNDINGS INDICATE CONSIDERABLE 
AMBIVALENCE IN THE UKRAINE TOWARD THE COMMONWEALTH, BUT 
THERE ARE TWO KEY REALITIES OF THE GOU'S CURRENT 
SITUATION. FIRST, UKRAINE IS TIGHTLY INTEGRATED 
ECONOMICALLY WITH RUSSIA. SECOND, KUCHMA DOES NOT WANT 
TO COMPLICATE YELTSIN'S POSITION DOMESTICALLY ON THE EVE 
OF THE RUSSIAN PRESIDENTIAL ELECTION. 
6. 484— TURNING TO THE REGION AS A WHOLE, THE UNSYG 
BRIEFED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ON PREPARATIONS UNDERWAY 
FOR THE MAY 30 CIS MIGRATION CONFERENCE IN GENEVA. HE 
SAID THAT UNHCR, THE INTERNATIONAL COMMISSION FOR 
MIGRATION, THE CIS, OSCE, AND VARIOUS NGOS WILL DISCUSS 
THESE IMPORTANT ISSUES. THE CONFERENCE COMES OUT OF A 
RESOLUTION OF THE UNGA. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED 
WHETHER THE CIS WILL BE REPRESENTED AS SUCH; THE SYG 
CONFIRMED THAT IT WOULD BE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ALSO 
NOTED THAT UN HIGH COMMISSIONER FOR REFUGEES, MRS. 
OGATA, HAD JUST BEEN IN HIS OFFICE DISCUSSING ISSUES 
BELATED TO AFRICA, BOSNIA AND, TO A LESSER EXTENT, THE 
MAY 30 CONFERENCE. 
7. -034- TURNING TO AFRICA, BOUTROS GHALI INDICATED THAT 
YELTSIN AND PRIMAKOV TOLD HIM THAT THE GOR WILL SUPPORT 
BURUNDI, INCLUDING GREATER UN ACTIVITY WITH AIR AND 
LOGISTICAL SUPPORT. THE PROBLEM FOR THE GOR IS 
FUNDING. STRESSING THE NEED FOR PARTICIPATION ON THE 
PART OF THE PERMANENT MEMBERS OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL, 
BOUTROS GHALI SAID HE ALSO RECENTLY PRESSED FRENCH 
PRESIDENT CHIRAC ON THIS ISSUE. THE FRENCH, BOUTROS 
GHALI SAID, ARE DIFFICULT BASED ON THEIR EXPERIENCE IN 
OPERATION TURQUOISE. GHALI STRESSED THE FRENCH MUST AT 
LEAST SUPPORT UN EFFORTS, EVEN IF THEY SEND NO TROOPS. 
CHIRAC SAID PUBLICLY THAT THE FRENCH WILL PROVIDE 
LOGISTICAL SUPPORT. PARTICIPATION OF THESE TWO 
COUNTRIES, BOUTROS GHALI SAID, MAY ENCOURAGE OTHER 
COUNTRIES TO SEND TROOPS IF NEEDED. 
8. -09+--ON HIS TWO HOUR MEETING WITH YELTSIN, BOUTROS 
GHALI NOTED ONE POTENTIAL PROBLEM -- YELTSIN'S APPARENT 
OVER-CONFIDENCE THAT HE WILL WIN THE ELECTION. SPEAKING 
FRANKLY WITH YELTSIN, BOUTROS GHALI SAID THAT WHEN HE 
WAS A DEPUTY, HE WAS OFTEN APPREHENSIVE ABOUT ELECTION 
RESULTS UNTIL MUCH CLOSER TO THE FINAL TALLIES. 
YELTSIN, HE SAID, DECLARED HIMSELF "100 PERCENT SURE" OF 
VICTORY. PRIMAKOV, TOO, GHALI SAID, WAS VERY SURE THAT 
YELTSIN WILL WIN THE ELECTIONS. 
9. *St— DURING HIS MOSCOW VISIT, BOUTROS GHALI ALSO MET 
WITH THE PRIME MINISTER, THE DEFENSE MINISTER, AND HELD 
A ROUND TABLE DISCUSSION WITH THE DUMA. HIS MESSAGE IN 
MOSCOW WAS THAT RUSSIA IS A GREAT POWER AND THEREFORE 
HAS AN IMPORTANT ROLE TO PLAY IN THE UN. BOUTROS GHALI 
CLOSED THE CONVERSATION BY NOTING THAT WE ALL AWAIT THE 
OUTCOME OF THE JUNE ELECTIONS. 
10. (S) THE CALL ENDED AT 1030 EDT.
