Dear Ken: 
RELEASE IN FULL 
I just got back from a two-week trip that took me to 
Moscow and Tokyo. I was distressed to discover, on my 
return, a letter from you inviting my family and me to a 
July 4th event on the Mall. I apologize for my rudeness in 
not having replied. I'm sure it was a fun evening. As it 
happens, I spent the 4th on a United Airlines flight from 
Frankfurt to San Francisco where I was going to join the 
Presidential party for the G-7 expedition. The G-7, I'm 
sure you'll agree, went very well from the standpoint of 
our Russia agenda -- as well as in other respects. The $2 
billion ExIm oil and gas facility featured prominently in 
our announcements of what we are doing bilaterally. 
I look forward to our lunch. 
Best regards, 
Strobe .Talbott,,
