4-Er)- SUBJECT: UN SECURITY COUNCIL RESOLUTION ON IRAQ 
4. N\THE ACTING SECRETARY TOLD MAMEDOV THAT HE HAD AN ANSWER TO HIS EARLIER QUESTION ABOUT A PENDING UN SECURITY COUNCIL RESOLUTION ON IRAQ. MR. TALBOTT SAID THAT THE U.S. AGREED WITH THE GOR THAT IT WAS OF OVERRIDING IMPORTANCE THAT RUSSIA AND THE U.S. NOT END UP AT LOGGERHEADS OVER THE RESOLUTION, FOR TWO REASONS: TO AVOID ADDED TENSION IN THE CURRENT CRISIS IN IRAQ, AND ALSO FOR THE SAKE OF THE GENERAL RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE U.S. AND RUSSIA. 
5. 04 THE ACTING SECRETARY TOLD MAMEDOV THAT HE HAD SPOKEN TO AMBASSADOR ALBRIGHT TWICE SINCE HIS PREVIOUS CONVERSATION EARLIER IN THE DAY WITH MAMEDOV. MR. TALBOTT NOTED THAT AMBASSADOR ALBRIGHT WAS WORKING WITH THE OTHER P5 MEMBERS, ESPECIALLY RUSSIAN AMBASSADOR TO THE UN LAVROV, TO AVOID A VETO OF THE BRITISH-SPONSORED RESOLUTION. MR. TALBOTT SAID THE ISSUE CAN BE SOLVED EITHER BY WITHDRAWING THE RESOLUTION OR NEGOTIATING CHANGES TO ITS WORDING. THE BOTTOM LINE, HE STRESSED, IS THAT THE U.S. AND RUSSIA ARE TOGETHER ON THIS MATTER. 
MAMEDOV SAID HE WAS RELIEVED TO HEAR THE U.S. POSITION. HE SAID HE LOOKED FORWARD TO SEEING MR. TALBOTT IN PARIS ON SEPTEMBER 11, NOTING THAT THE GOR MAY HAVE SOME NEW IDEAS THEN. THE ACTING SECRETARY AGREED THAT THEY WOULD SHARE MORE INFORMATION IN PARIS. 
7. (U) THE CALL ENDED AT 1054 EDT.
