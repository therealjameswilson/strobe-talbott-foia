international institutions. In the East, cooperation institutions,

political, economic and security-related developments in_ the NIS 
region. These misunderstandings have in turn encouraged tendencies 
of repolarization and fragmentation within the CIS, in Eastern 
Europe, and between Russia and Ukraine,-and between East and West
