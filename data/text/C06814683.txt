IMPORTANT THAT HUMAN RIGHTS BE ON THE AGENDA OF NEXT MONTH'S 
IBERO-AMERICAN SUMMIT IN HAVANA. MATUTES RESPONDED THAT MADRID 
WAS PRESSING HAVANA ON THE HUMAN RIGHTS QUESTION; HE CITED HIS 
OWN TELEVISION INTERVIEW OF THE PREVIOUS WEEK, CONDEMNING THE 
"POLITICAL IMPASSE" IN CUBA. OPENING CUBA'S POLITICAL SYSTEM I 
WOULD BE NEITHER QUICK NOR EASY, BUT SPAIN WOULD CONTINUE TO WORK 
TOWARDS THIS GOAL.

WITH RUSSIA.
