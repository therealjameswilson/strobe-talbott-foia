1. ~ SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND NSC SENIOR
DIRECTOR HILL MET WITH GENERAL SIR RUPERT SMITH, DEPUTY
SUPREME ALLIED COMMANDER EUROPE (D/SACUER) ON APRIL 18 TO
DISCUSS RUSSIA-NATO RELATIONS AND THE BALKANS. TALBOTT
EMPHASIZED THE POLITICAL IMPORTANCE OF'USSIA'S ROLE IN
KFOR/SFOR AND NOTED THAT HE AND SYG ROBERTSON SHARE THE
VIEW THAT PUTIN WANTS TO COOPERATE MORE BROADLY WITH NATO.
SMITH ASKED ABOUT U.S. DOMESTIC POLITICAL CURRENTS IMPACT
ON TH" BALKANS DURING THE REMAINDER OF THE CLINTON
ADMINISTRATION. TALBOTT INDICATED THAT MORE EFFECTIVE
CIVIL IMPLEMENTATION IN BOSNIA AND KOSOVO AND A STABLE
MONTENEGRO ARE. KEY GOALS. AMBASSADOR HILL ADDED THAT UNMIK
MUST MOVE FROM "DIRECT RULE" TO "INDIRECT RULE" BY GIVING
LOCAL PEOPLE IN KOSOVO THE OPPORTUNITY TO RUN THEIR OWN
INSTITUTIONS. FINALLY, TALBOTT NOTED THAT THE OPEN DOOR ON
NATO ENLARGEMENT CANNOT CLOSE. END SUMMARY.
2. ~ SMITH ASKED ABOUT NATO-RUSSIA RELATIONS, AND TALBOTT
RESPONDED THAT HE SHARED SYG ROBERTSON'S VIEW THAT PUTIN
WANTS TO COOPERATE WITH NATO. SMITH DISCOUNTED REPORTS
THAT THE RUSSIAN CONTINGENT MAY LEAVE ORAHOVAC, NOTI'NG THAT
IT DOESN'T APPEAR THAT THE SERBS, THEMSELVES, ARE TRULY
INTENT ON DEPARTING FROM THERE. TALBOTT EMPHASIZED THE
POLITICAL IMPORTANCE OF THE RUSSIAN PRESENCE IN THE BALKANS
BUT CAUTIONED THAT WE CANNOT BECOME SUPPLICANTS IN ORDER TO
KEEP THEM THERE. SMITH ADDED THAT THE GENERALLY GOOD
RUSSIAN MILITARY PERFORMANCE IN KFOR AND SFOR CONTINUES,
ESPECIALLY WHEN RUSSIAN OFFICERS EXERT EFFECTIVE
LEADERSHIP.
3. ~ TURNING TO THE BALKANS, HILL SUMMARIZED U.S.
MONTENEGRQ STRATEGY AS KEEPING MILOSEVIC GUESSING ON NATO'S
RESPONSE TO ANY VJ ACTION. IN KOSQVO, THE U.S. WANTS TO
CREATE CONDITIONS FOR LOCAL INSTITUTIONALIZATION TO THRIVE.
OTHERWISE, THERE IS NO "PROTOPLASM FOR DEMOCRATIC LIFE
FORMS."
4.~HILL NOTED THAT HE WAS ENCOURAGED BY HIS RECENT TRIP
TO BOSNA. MUSLIM FAMILIES ARE SLOWLY RETURNING TO BRCKO,
AND THE TEXAS NATIONAL GUARD IS PERFORMING WELL, THEREBY
RELIEVING PRESSURE ON U.S. ACTIVE FORCES.
5, ~ SMITH ASKED IF THERE IS AN IMPETUS FOR A MORE
COHERENT POLICY TOWARDS THE BALKANS AS A WHOLE. HILL NOTED
THAT THE EU HAS A MA JOR ROLE TO PLAY HERE I AND HE PRAISED
CHRIS PATTEN'S DYNAMISM. HOWEVER, HILL CAUTIONED THAT THE
STABILITY PACT HAD BEEN OVERSOLD AS A POTENTIAL EXIT
STRATEGY.
