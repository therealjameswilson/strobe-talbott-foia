2. (C) ON JANUARY 8, ARMENIAN FOREIGN MINISTER VARTAN 
OSKANIAN, IN THE U.S. PRIMARILY FOR PRIVATE MEETINGS IN 
CALIFORNIA AND WASHINGTON, MET THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AT THE 
DEPARTMENT. OSKANIAN WAS ACCOMPANIED BY ARMENIAN 
AMBASSADOR TO THE U.S. ROUBEN SHUGARIAN, ARMENIAN 
AMBASSADOR TO THE OSCE JIVAN TABIBIAN, AND EMBASSY FIRST 
SECRETARY LILIT TOUTKHALIAN (NOTETAKER). THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY WAS ACCOMPANIED BY S/NIS AMBASSADOR SESTANOVICH, 
S/NIS DEPUTY NULAND, NAGORNO-KARABAKH SPECIAL NEGOTIATOR 
KEYSER, NSC CAUCASUS DIRECTOR ELKIND, AND ARMENIA DESK 
OFFICER CARLINO (NOTETAKER). SESTANOVICH CONDUCTED A 
FOLLOW-ON MEETING AT WHICH THE DEPUTY SECRETARY WAS NOT 
PRESENT. 
3. 4-ei THANKING OSKANIAN FOR HIS ROLE IN PERSUADING 
ARMENIAN PRESIDENT KOCHARIAN TO ACCEPT ISTANBUL AS THE SITE 
FOR THE 1999 OSCE SUMMIT, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY ASKED 
OSKANIAN FOR HIS VIEWS ON THE CURRENT STATUS OF TURKISH-
ARMENIAN RELATIONS. OSKANIAN SAID HE HAD TWO USEFUL BUT 
INFORMAL MEETINGS WITH TURKISH FM CEM. THE TWO FM'S 
PROBABLY WILL MEET AGAIN, PERHAPS MORE FORMALLY, 
POTENTIALLY IN MARCH. MEANWHILE, CONTACTS CONTINUE AT THE 
POLITICAL DIRECTOR OR HEAD OF DEPARTMENT LEVELS. WHILE 
THESE DIALOGUES ARE USEFUL, OSKANIAN ACKNOWLEDGED, HE HAS 
DETECTED NO CHANGE IN ANKARA'S INSISTENCE THAT PROGRESS BE 
MADE ON NAGORNO-KARABAKH BEFORE TURKEY WILL NORMALIZE 
RELATIONS WITH ARMENIA.

RUSSIAN MILITARY DELIVERIES 
6. 4-5- IN THE FOLLOW-ON MEETING, SESTANOVICH UNDERSCORED 
THAT RUSSIAN ARMS DELIVERIES TO ARMENIA COULD COMPLICATE 
PROGRESS ON NAGORNO-KARABAKH AND THE SHARED U.S.-ARMENIAN 
GOAL OF PROMOTING REGIONAL COOPERATION. SESTANOVICH NOTED 
THAT TURKEY AND AZERBAIJAN ARE CONCERNED ABOUT REPORTED 
DELIVERIES AND THE U.S. CONGRESS INEVITABLY WILL QUESTION 
THEIR IMPACT ON THE REGIONAL MILITARY BALANCE. OSKANIAN 
CONFIRMED THE DEPLOYMENT OF FIVE MIG-29'S TO A RUSSIAN BASE 
IN ARMENIA. HE SAID THEY ARE NOT REPLACEMENTS FOR THE MIG-
23'S ALREADY IN COUNTRY BUT ADDITIONAL ASSETS. THE PURPOSE 
OF THE DEPLOYMENT, HE CLAIMED, IS TO STRENGTHEN ARMENIA'S 
DEFENSE CAPABILITY. HE ASSERTED THAT AS LONG AS N-K 
REMAINS UNRESOLVED AND NORMAL RELATIONS WITH TURKEY DO NOT 
EXIST, ARMENIA WILL BE "SECURITY CONSCIOUS." UNDER THESE 
CONDITIONS, HE SAID, ARMENIA WILL CONTINUE TO FEEL A NEED 
FOR MEANS OF DETERRENCE. SESTANOVICH REPLIED THAT ALL 
NATIONS.HAVE LEGITIMATE SECURITY CONCERNS AND THAT ARMENIA 
IS NO EXCEPTION. HE ASKED OSKANIAN TO CLARIFY IF THE MIG-
29'S ARE NOW IN THE ARMENIAN INVENTORY. OSKANIAN REPLIED 
THAT ARMENIA'S TACIT UNDERSTANDING WITH RUSSIA IS THAT THE 
MIGS WILL BE LEFT IN ARMENIA WHEN RUSSIAN BASES IN THE 
COUNTRY EVENTUALLY ARE CLOSED AND RUSSIAN FORCES WITHDRAWN, 
WHENEVER THAT TIME MAY COME. HE CONFIDED'THAT ARMENIA IS 
"NEGOTIATING" FOR 5-T0-10 ADDITIONAL MIG-29'S. HE STRESSED 
THAT THE DEPLOYMENT WAS CONDUCTED WITH TRANSPARENCY AND IN
