SUBJECT: GOJ POSITION ON INDIA SANCTIONS 
CLASSIFIED BY ROBIN L. WHITE, DIRECTOR, OFFICE OF JAPANESE 
AFFAIRS. REASON: 1.5 (B) AND (D). 
1. (S)  ACTING ON INSTRUCTIONS, AMBASSADOR SAITO TOLD 
DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT THAT JAPAN HAD JOINED THE USG IN 
ABSTAINING ON THE MORNING'S WORLD BANK VOTE ON INDIA'S 
ANDHRA PRADESH POWER PROJECT. THE GOJ DECIDED TO ABSTAIN 
OUT OF RESPECT FOR THE USG POSITION, AND IN HOPES THAT THE 
VOTE WOULD•ENCOURAGE INDIA TO SIGN THE CTBT BY THE END OF 
MAY. SECOND, REGARDING OTHER NON-BASIC HUMAN NEEDS , 
PROJECTS, JAPAN WANTS TO CONTINUE CONSULTATION AMONG THE 
G-8 AND PARTICULARLY WITH THE USG. THIRD, JAPAN WOULD' 
LIKE TO DISCUSS THIS MATTER AT THE G-8 MEETING IN POTSDAM 
NEXT WEEK. IF THE G-8 CHOSE TO CHANGE ITS POLICY ON IFI 
LENDING, A NEW G-8 AGREEMENT IN WRIT.ING SHOULD REFLECT THE 
CHANGES SINCE LAST YEAR'S G-8 COMMUNIQUE. AS CHAIR OF THE 
ENLARGED G-8 TASK FORCE, JAPAN IS READY TO PLAY AN 
APPROPRIATE ROLE.
