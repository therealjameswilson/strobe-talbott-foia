FROM THE PUTIN MEETING
4. ~ CHECHNYA. COOK SPENT 50 MINUTES OF A 90-MINUTE
MEETING WITH PUTIN ON CHECHNYA. PUTIN TRIED TO THROW COOK
OFF GUARD AT THE BEGINNING OF THE MEETING BY CHARGING LONDON
WAS ALLOWING GROUPS IN THE UK TO RAISE FUNDS FOR CHECHEN
"TERRORISTS." COOK OBJECTED TO RUSSIA'S EXCESSIVE USE OF
FORCE AND THE SITUATION OF THE CHECHENS, THOUGH HE DID NOT
SPECIFICALLY MENTION THE FILTRATION CAMPS.
5. ~ BABITSKIY. PUTIN CLAIMS CAPTIVE JOURNALIST
BABITSKIY HAS GONE OF HIS OWN "FREE WILL" TO HIS CHECHEN
"FRIENDS" AND IS NOW IN THE MOUNTAINS. RATHER BIZARRELY, IN
THE BRITISH VIEW, PUTIN CLAIMED BABITSKIY HAD SAID THE
CHECHENS WERE CUTTING OFF PEOPLE'S HEADS NOT BECAUSE THEY
WERE "ANIMALS" BUT TO SHOW THE TERROR OF WAR. PUTIN APPEARED
TO BE IMPLING BABITSKIY WAS AN ECCENTRIC ABOUT WHOM WE SHOULD
NOT BE SO CONCERNED.
6. tS).PUTIN CLAIMS A POLITICAL SETTLEMENT (WHICH COOK
PUSHED FOR) IS IMPOSSIBLE IN THE SHORT TERM BECAUSE HE HAS NO
ONE TO NEGOTIATE WITH ON THE CHECHEN SIDE. HE DID NOT RULE
OUT A POLITICAL SOLUTION OVER THE LONGER TERM. IN THE
MEANTIME, RUSSIA WOULD BUILD GRADUAL CONTROL OVER THE AREA,
THROUGH BOTH FEDERAL AND LOCAL ACTIONS.
7.~ ECONOMIC REFORM. PUTIN "PRESSED ALL THE RIGHT
BUTTONS" ON ECONOMIC REFORMS AND ATTRACTING FOREIGN
INVESTMENT, HE PROMISED HE WOULD ALLOW NO SPECIAL BENEFITS
TO VESTED INTERESTS, "INCLUDING THE SECURITY SERVICES." THE
FCO TAKES THIS TO MEAN PUTIN DISAVOWS ANY SPECIAL ECONOMIC
FAVORS FOR THE KGB.
