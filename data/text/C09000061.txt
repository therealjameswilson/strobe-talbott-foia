RELEASE IN FULL 
I would like to take this opportunity to introduCe myself, My name 
is Jacob Saliba and I am the Chairman of Katy Industries, Inc., a 
New York Stock Exchange Company. I will shortly be retiring as 
chairman but will remain on the Board of Directors. 
Katy Industries is a conglamorate with more than twenty 
subsidiaries in the United States and in Europe. Through our 
German Subsidiary, Schon A.G. I have had wide contacts in the 
former Soviet Union and in the Middle East. Schon manufactures 
shoe and die cutting machinery and our equipment is installed in 
more than 100 cites of the former Soviet Union from the Baltic Sea 
to the Pacific. Our machinery is presently in operation in every 
one of the republics of the former Soviet Union., We know of no 
company in Europe and America that has established a greater 
economic presence in that area and because this is primarily in the 
field of shoe production, this activity continues to have a high 
priority in state planning. Our Schon sales and service 
organizations are maintaining important contacts in all areas,of 
the former Soviet Union' even though presently there is great 
confusion and disorganization. 
These contacts also include a continued presence in the former East 
Bloc Countries. ,In 1992, we were one of the first western 
companies to establish a private enterprise in Hungary in 
partnership with two state agencies. Schon is the controlling 
partner in a manufacturing operation at Eger, Hungary , where we 
employ some two hundred persons. This is an ongoing successful 
venture.

We have installed various types of industrial machinery for the 
production of consumer products in almost every country of the 
Middle East extending from Morocco to Yemen and including Saudi 
Arabia, Kuwait and Iraq. 
I am of Arabic (Christian) origin and my mother and father were 
born in Mardin, Turkey. They first emigrated to Canada where I was 
born and we finally emigrated tosthe United States in 1923. 
I am a graduate of Boston University where I have served as a 
Trustee for more than ten years. I am also a member of the 
corporate board of the Massachusetts General Hospital and I am 
listed in WHO'S WHO IN AMERICA. 
I was an assistant to Nelson Talbott on two assignements at Wright 
Patterson Air Force Base in 1942 and in 1952. On both assignments 
I was acting as a special consultant to General Edwin Rawlings. In 
1952 I was the Co-Chairman of the Air Force Spares Study Group and 
the implementation of our recommendations resulted in savings of 
over one billion dollars. Secretary of the Air Force, Harold 
Talbott, later presented meritorious citations to the writer, 
Gordon Reed and Harry O. King. 
I am listing a few references and I would greatly appreciate the 
opportunity to meet you at your convenience.
