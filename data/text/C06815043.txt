2. ~SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET ON MARCH 16
WITH FRENCH MFA POLITICAL DIRECTOR GERARD ERRERA TO
DISCUSS NATIONAL MISSILE DEFENSE, NAGORNO-KARABAKH,
BALKANS POLICY AND THE CHINESE HUMAN RIGHTS RESOLUTION.
LIKE OTHER RECENT FRENCH OFFIC1AL VISITORS, ERRERA
REPEATED THE GOF'S DESIRE TO HOLD A FRANK DISCUSSION WITH
THE UNITED STATES REGARDING CONCERNS OVER NMD DEPLOYMENT.
HE DISCUSSED RECENT EVENTS IN NAGORNO-KARABAKH, CHRIS HILL
AND JAMIE RUBIN'S TRIP TO THE BALKANS AND CONGRESSIONAL
SUPPORT FOR OUR KOSOVO EFFORT.'N CHINESE HUMAN RIGHTS,
ERRERA SAID THAT THE EU WOULD REACH A CONSENSUS DECISION
THAT WOULD PRECLUDE CO-SPONSORSHIP OF THE UNCHR RESOLUTION
BUT ULTIMATELY FRANCE'S POSITION WOULD BE CLOSE TO THE
UNITED STATES. END SUMMARY.

4.~! ERRERA ADMITTED THAT THE USG WAS WELL AWARE OF
FRENCH MISGIVINGS ON NMD, BUT TOLD TALBOTT THAT HE WANTED
TO MOVE THE DEBATE BEYOND THREATS TO STRATEGIC CONCERNS.
ERRERA RECOMMENDED THAT BOTH COUNTRIES SIT DOWN QUIETLY IN
A RESTRICTIVE FORMAT TO PUT THE ISSUES ON THE TABLE. HE
ADDED THAT THE DEBATE SHOULD BE AT A POLITICAL, NOT ONLY
EXPERT, LEVEL, AND THAT IT WAS TIME TO HOLD A NORMAL
DIALOGUE ON THE ISSUE, BETWEEN ALLIES.
5. ~ TALBOTT AGREED TO THE CONCEPT, AND REPORTED MORE
FULLY ON HIS MEETINGS WITH CHINESE LEADERS IN BEIJING ON
NMD DEPLOYMENT. TALBOTT COMMENTED THAT THE CHINESE WERE
LESS ALARMED BY NMD BECAUSE THEY WERE UNABASHEDLY
PREOCCUPIED WITH TAIWAN AND THE UPCOMING ELECTIONS THERE.
THE CHINESE DID NOT VOICE CONCERN THAT NMD MIGHT BLUNT
CHINESE STRATEGIC NUCI EAR DETERRENCE, AS ANTICIPATED, BUT
RATHER WERE CONCERNED THAT NMD MIGHT SOMEHOW BE EXTENDED TO
PROTECT TAIWAN VIA TMD. TALBOTT DISCUSSED A PAPER BEING
PREPARED ON CHINA AND PROMISED TO DISCUSS CHINESE THINKING
ON NMD WITH THE FRENCH THEREAFTER. I
6.~TALBOTT ALSO DISCUSSED HIS CONVERSATIONS WITH THE
RUSSIANS IN ROME ON NMD. THE RUSSIANS'OSITION REMAINS
UNCHANGED, BUT LIKE THE UNITED STATES, THE RUSSIANS DID NOT
WANT THE DECISION OVER NMD DEPLOYMENT TO BE A STARK CHOICE,

HE COMMENTED THAT MANY IN THE RUSSIAN GOVERNMENT WERE
AFRAID F'R THEIR JOBS, AWAITING THE RESULTS OF THE RUSSIAN
ELECTIONS. TALBOTT AND ERRERA AGREED TO DISCUSS NMD AT
THEIR NEXT MEETING IN APRIL.
NAGORNO-KARABAKH
7. ~ ERRERA DESCRIBED THE AMBIVALENCE AND FRAGILITY OF
THE PEACE PROCESS IN NAGORNO-KARABAKH AFTER A RECENT
MEETING OF POLITICAL DIRECTORS IN LISBON. ERRERA SAID THAT
FRANCE WAS ANXIOUS TO ENCOURAGE THE PEACE PROCESS, BUT NOT
TO ANTICIPATE IT PRECIPITOUSLY'EST THE MINSK GROUP BE
SEEN AS IMPOSING AND FORCING AN AGREEMENT ON THE PARTIES.
ERRERA ADDED THAT FRANCE WANTS TO EXPAND THE ROLE OF
INTERNATIONAL INSTITUTIONS IN THE PEACE PROCESS, INCLUDING
THE OSCE. FRANCE THEREFORE AGREES THAT THE MINSK CO-CHAIRS
SHOULD BRIEF THE AUSTRIAN CIO ON THE N-K PEACE PROCESS.
TALBOTT WELCOMED THIS, AGREEING THAT THE CIO HAS AN
IMPORTANT ROLE TO PLAY.
8.~ON THE TOPIC OF A NEEDS ASSESSMENT MISSION TO THE
REGION, ERRERA WANTED TO WAIT UNTIL AFTER THE RUSSIAN
ELECTIONS, ADDING THAT FRANCE DID NOT HAVE A PROBLEM
CONCEPTUALLY WITH THE MISSION. ERRERA ADDED THAT THE
SITUATION ON THE GROUND WAS STILL BLOCKED. TALBOTT
RESPONDED THAT THE SITUATION WAS NOT PERMANENTLY BLOCKED,
BUT PALPABLE PROBLEMS REMAIN, ARISING FROM THE
ASSASSINATIONS LAST OCTOBER. TALBOTT SAID THAT THE TWO
PRESIDENTS WERE ANXIOUS TO HAVE THE NEEDS ASSESSMENT
PROCEED AS QUICKLY AS POSSIBLE, AND THAT THE USG AND FRANCE
NEEDED TO CLOSE ANY GAP QUICKLY.
9. ~ TALBOTT AND ERRERA AGREED THAT THE DIPLOMATIC
PROCESS NEEDED TO RESUME AFTER THE CIO BRIEFING IN rVIENNA.
STEVE SESTANOVICH URGED THAT THE CO-CHAIRS MEET IN GENEVA
WITH THE RELEVANT INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS IN THE FIRST
WEEK OF APRIL TO PLAN AN ASSESSMENT MISSION TO ADDRESS
POST-CONFLICT RESETTLEMENT AND RECONSTRUCTIONS NEEDS IN THE
REGION. SESTANOVICH NOTED THAT KOCHARIAN PLAINLY WANTS TO
SOLVE THE PROBLEM, TO WHICH ERRERA RESPONDED THAT THE
CURRENT DEAL SERVES HIS INTERESTS, OTHER THAN THE ISSUE OF
ACCESS TO IRAN. SESTANOVICH RESPONDED THAT KOCHARIAN
PLACES GREAT IMPORTANCE ON THE RUSSIAN ELECTIONS, AND WiLL
WANT TO MOVE FORWARD ONLY AFTER PUTIN'S THEN PRESUMED
ELECTION. SESTANOVICH ADDED THAT TIME IS A PROBLEM FOR
ALIYEV. WITH UPCOMING PARLIAMENTARY ELECTIONS IN
AZERBAIJAN, AND PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS FOLLOWING
THEREAFTER, ALIYEV WANTS PROGRESS IN THE PEACE PROCESS TO
BLUNT POSSIBLE CRITICISM. ERRERA COMMENTED THAT THE
RUSSIANS WANT STABILITY, AND THAT A WORKABLE SOLUTION FOR
KOCHARIAN AND 'ALIYEV IS ENOUGH.

10. ~SESTANOVICH SAID THAT PUTIN WANTS TO REVERSE THE
PERCEPTION THAT RUSSIA LOSES IN ANY PEACE PROCESS FRAMED BY

NO REAL CONFLICT IN THE REGION. RELATIONS WITH ARMENIA ARE

PUTIN'S GOAL IS TO SHOW THAT RUSSIA IS THE DOMINANT PLAYER

11. (W TALBOTT ADDED THAT IRAN HAS BEEN A FACTOR IN THE

MAY PLAY IN THE REGION. ERRERA RETURNED TO THE ISSUE OF
THE GENEVA CONFERENCE, CONCLUDING THAT THERE WAS NO ISSUE
OF PRINCIPLE, ONLY ONE OF OPTIMAL SCHEDULING.
BALKANS
12- ~ ERRERA DESCRIBED GOOD MEETINGS IN LISBON AND
BRUSSELS ON DEVELOPMENTS IN KOSOVO, SAYING THAT THE EU
REACHED A MEETING OF THE MINDS ON BASIC POLICY, NOT JUST ON
HOW TO DEAL WITH PROBLEM AREAS, LIKE MITROVICA. ERRERA
SAID THAT THE WESTERN AND INTERNATIONAL ACTORS ARE
NARROWING PRIVATE DIFFERENCES ON HOW BEST TO PROCEED.
ERRERA REPORTED THAT KOFI ANNAN'S RECENT MEETINGS IN PARIS
REFLECTED THE EMERGING CONSENSUS NOT TO PUSH FOR
CLARIFICATION OF FINAL STATUS .OF KOSOVO. PUSHING
CLARIFICATION, ERRERA WARNED, COULD LEAD TO FURTHER
AGGRAVATION OF THE TENSE STANDOFF IN MITROVICA. ANY
AGREEMENT ON SUBSTANTIAL AUTONOMY WILL BOLSTER THE
DEMOCRATIC IMPULSE IN KOSOVO/ AND RATTLE THE SERBS ~ 'HO ARE
CONVINCED THAT NATO IS ACTING AS A COVER FOR THOSE WHO
FAVOR INDEPENDENCE FOR KOSOVO. TALBOTT AGREED THAT WE MUST
RESIST THE TEMPTATION TO FORCE THE ISSUE~ LEST WE CREATE A
STARK SITUATION WITH RESPECT TO INDEPENDENCE FOR KOSOVO.
ERRERA ADDED THAT MUNICIPAL ELECTIONS ARE VERY IMPORTANT
AND AN ENTIRELY SEPARATE SUBJECT. HE URGED STRONG QUINT
COORDINATION ON PREPARING FOR ELECTIONS IN THE FALL.
13. ~ ERRERA RAISED THREE FURTHER ISSUES WITH RESPECT TO
KOSOVO: LEVEL OF ARMED FORCES, POLICE AND FINANCIAL
SUPPORT. ERRERA SAID THAT THE GOF WAS CONCERNED ABOUT
ONGOI NG CONGRESS IONAL SUPPORT FOR THE KOSOVO EFFORT ~ FUELED
IN PART BY BERNARD KOUCHNER'S WIDELY PUBLICIZED FRUSTRATION
AT THE SLOWNESS OF EU FUNDING MECHANISMS. TALBOTT RESPONDED
THAT SECRETARY OF DEFENSE COHEN WAS TRYING TO SECURE
CONGRESSIONAL SUPPORT, BUT THAT WE NEEDED REAL EVIDENCE
THAT THE EUROPEANS WERE INTENSIFYING THEIR OWN EFFORTS TO
GET FUNDS TO THE REGION. THE BOTTOM LINE, TALBOTT POINTED
OUT, WAS TO PROTECT OUR PEOPLE NOT JUST THE CONCEPT.
ERRERA AGREED THAT IT IS CRITICAL TO GET THE FUNDING
FIGURES AND THE TROOP LEVEL ISSUES WORKED OUT RIGHT.
ERRERA SAID THESE ISSUES WERE ADDRESSED IN FRENCH DEFMIN
ALAIN RICHARD S RECENT VISITS'O WASHINGTON~ BRUSSELS AND
EU CAPITALS.
14. ERRERA COMMENDED AMBASSADOR CHRIS HILL AND
SPOKESPERSON JAMIE RUBIN'S RECENT TRIP TO THE REGION,
SAYING AS LONG AS THE UCK FELT THEY JIHAD UNFETTERED SUPPCRT
FROM THE UNITED STATES AND NATO WE WERE ALL HEADED FOR
TROUBLE. IT WAS CRITICAL, HE ADDED, TO DELIVER THE MESSAGE
TO THE KOSOVAR ALBANIANS THAT WE WOULD NOT BE DRAWN INTO
THEIR ADVENTURE, AND HE EXPECTED OUR ONGOING GOOD
COOPERATION AND COORDINATION ON KOSOVO POLICY TO CONTINUE.
