1. ~SUMMARY: MEETING WITH DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT AND
DEPUTY NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISOR STEINBERG ON FEBRUARY 15,
FOREIGN MINISTER KONO STRESSED THAT U.S.-JAPAN COOPERATION ON
INTERNATIONAL ISSUES IS VITAL, ESPECIALLY IN ASIA. HE
HIGHLIGHTED THE IMPORTANCE OF WORKING TO ENSURE CHINA ACTS
PEACEFULLY KONO POINTED OUT THE DIFFICULTY OF USING "SOFT
POWER" TO RESOLVE THE(PROBLEM OF JAPANESE NATIONALS BELIEVED
ABDUCTED BY NORTH KOREA. HE ARGUED THAT ASIA'S "CLOCK" IS
DIFFERENT THAN THAT OF THE WEST, AND URGED GREATER PATIENCE
WITH COUNTRIES LIKE BURMA. KONO ALSO RAISED THE IMPORTANCE
OF INDONESIAN STABILITY, ASEAN DISSATISFACTION WITH THE
RESULTS OF GLOBALIZATION, AND HIS REGRET OVER THE FAILURE TO
BEGIN A NEW WTO ROUND IN SEATTLE:, IN RESPONSE, TALBOTT
EMPHASIZED THE VALUE WE PLACE ON THE U.S.-JAPAN RELATIONSHIP,
AND CONVEYED REGRETS FOR THE DIFFICULTY IN SCHEDULING'
MEETING WITH THE SECRETARY. HE CONFIRMED THEIR COMMON VIEW
ON CHINA AND CONVEYED OUR APPRECIATION FOR CLOSE GOJ
COOPERATION ON DPRK ISSUES. TALBOTT URGED THE GOJ TO ACCEDE
TO THE HAGUE CONVENTION ON CHILD ABDUCTION. HE NOTED THE
SECRETARY'S DEEP INTEREST IN INDONESIA AND BURMA, HIS CLOSE
COORDINATION WITH GOJ OFFICIALS ON RUSSIA, AND THE IMPORTANCE
OF HOST NATION SUPPORT. TALBOTT SAID WE SHOULD CONTINUE TO
ALIGN CLOSELY OUR APPROACHES ON NUCLEAR NONPROLIFERATION, AND
UNDERSCORED THAT THE U.S. WOULD DO EVERYTHING POSSIBLE TO SEE
THAT INDIA, PAKISTAN, AND THE U.S. SIGNED THE CTBT. END

3. (W)KONO OPENED BY DISCUSSING THE U.S.-JAPAN RELATIONSHIP,
AND WELCOMED THE OPPORTUNITY TO REVIEW ITS FUNDAMENTAL BASIS.
U.S. JAPAN COOPERATION ON INTERNATIONAL ISSUES IS VITAL,
ESPECIALLY IN ASIA. SINCE HE WAS LAST FOREIGN MINISTER,
JAPAN'S RELATIONSHIP WITH THE ROK HAS SIGNIFICANTLY IMPROVED.
HOWEVER, THE JAPAN-CHINA RELATIONSHIP HAD NOT MADE AS MUCH
PROGRESS, AND IT IS IMPORTANT TO DEEPEN MUTUAL UNDERSTANDING.
ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT TASKS FOR OUR TWO COUNTRIES IS TO
HELP MAKE CHINA A FUNDAMENTALLY PEACEFUL COUNTRY. WHILE THEY
SAY THEY BELIEVE IN PEACE, THEY ATTACH CONDITIONS WHEN IT
COMES TO TAIWAN, AND THERE ARE PROBLEMS WITH TIBET AND OTHER
AREAS. NONETHELESS, KONO BELIEVES THEY DO BELIEVE IN PEACE.
NATURALLY, WE MUST KEEP STABILITY FOR THE TIME BEING THROUGH
A BALANCE OF POWER.
4. ~ KONO SAID THAT JAPAN CONSIDERS THE DPRK A KEY
PROBLEM. THE ABDUCTION ISSUE IS ESPECIALLY DIFFICULT; HE
HIMSELF HAD MET WITH GRIEVING PARENTS, AND THOUGH THE DPRK
C
DENIED ANY CONNECTION TO DISAPPEARANCES, THE ISSUE COULD NOT
BE AVOIDED. THERE WERE ALSO UNRESOLVED ISSUES RELATED TO WW
II FIFTY YEARS AGO THE GOJ PROVIDED COMPENSATION TO THE ROK,
WHICH CONTRIBUTED TO THE FUNDAMENTALS FOR THEIR ECONOMIC
GROWTH, HE SAID, WITHOUT SPECIFYING WHAT THE GOJ HAD IN MIND
FOR THE DPRK. JAPAN RECOGNIZED THAT IT DID NOT HAVE THE
MILITARY LEVERAGE OF THE U.S. OR ROK. THEREFORE THEY WOULD
HAVE TO USE FOOD AND ECONOMIC ASSISTANCE. STILL, IT WAS HARD
TO USE SUCH "SOFT POWER" TO GET RESOLUTION OF THE ABDUCTEE
ISSUE. KONO WELCOMED PROGRESS BETWEEN THE U.S. AND DPRK.
5.~WHILE NOTING THE HUMAN RIGHTS PROBLEMS IN EAST TIMOR,
KONO STRESSED HOW IMPORTANT INDONESIAN STABILITY WAS FOR
JAPAN AND ASIA. HE BELIEVED THAT WAHID WAS OFF TO A GOOD
STARTr THOUGH IT WAS HARD TO SAY IF THE SITUATION HAD
STABILIZED, GIVEN THE COMPLICATION OF HIS DEALINGS WITH
WIRANTO, AND THE QUESTION OF HOW WAHID WOULD WORK WITH
MEGAWATI.
6. (~ KONO DESCRIBED PM OBUCHI'S VISIT TO THAILAND LAST
WEEK FOR UNCTAD, WHERE ASEAN NATIONS SHOWED A STRONG SENSE OF
DISQUIET AND DISSATISFACTION WITH THE PACE AND RESULTS
OF'LOBALIZATION.
REFERRING TO HIS DIFFICULT EXPERIENCE IN
SEATTLE, KONO REGRETTED THE MEETING'S FAILURE TO BEGIN A NEW
WTO ROUND. IT WAS IMPORTANT FOR THE U.S, AND JAPAN TO
ANALYZE WHY THE MEETING HAD FAILED, AND FIND WAYS TO DEAL
WITH THE ANXIETIES OF LDCS AND NGOS.
7. ~ ARGUING THAT ASIA'S "CLOCK" WAS DIFFERENT FROM
WESTERN IDEAS OF TIME, KONO SAID THAT THE U.S. NEEDED TO BE
MORE PATIENT AS ASIAN NATIONS CHANGED. FOR EXAMPLE, THE
PROBLEMS OF BURMA WOULD NOT BE RESOLVED BY THE U.S. OR UK
APPROACH. IT WILL TAKE A LONG TIME TO BRING THEM IN THE
RIGHT DIRECTION.

VISIT AND REGRETTED THE SCHEDULING DIFFICULTIES. HE NOTED
ALSO THE PRESIDENT'S PLAN TO MEET WITH KONO (WHO WAS VISIBLY
PLEASED) AND REFERRED TO FORMER SECRETARY CHRISTOPHER'
REMINISCENCES OF HIS EXCELLENT RELATIONSHIP WITH THE FOREIGN
MINISTER. OF ALL THE TOPICS TO BE DISCUSSED DURING THE FM'S
VISIT TO WASHINGTON, THE MOST IMPORTANT WAS THE NATURE AND
PROSPECTS FOR THE U.S.-JAPAN RELATIONSHIP. THE .USG PLACES
GREAT VALUE ON THE STABILITY OF THE RELATIONSHIP, A STRONG
SECURITY ALLIANCE AND A -PARTNERSHIP OF TWO ENDURING
DEMOCRACIES AND STRONG ECONOMIES.
