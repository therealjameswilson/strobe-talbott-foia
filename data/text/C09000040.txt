DEAR YOSSI, 
OUR MEETING ON TUESDAY PROVIDED A USEFUL OPPORTUNITY 
TO RESUME THE DIALOGUE WE BEGAN IN JUNE AND I LOOK FORWARD

ARE SOME ADDITIONAL THOUGHTS ON THREE OF THE SUBJECTS WE 
DISCUSSED. 
I WANT TO ASSURE YOU THAT WE WILL MAKE EVERY EFFORT AT 
THE UPCOMING IAEA GENERAL CONFERENCE IN VIENNA TO WORK

WITH ISRAEL FOR THE RESTORATION OF IAEA TECHNICAL 
ASSISTANCE TO ISRAEL. I AGREE WITH YOU THAT, IN LIGHT OF 
THE REMARKABLE PROGRESS TOWARD A COMPREHENSIVE PEACE IN 
THE MIDDLE EAST, NOW IS THE TIME TO ELIMINATE THIS 
RESIDUAL, UNHELPFUL ISSUE FROM THE IAEA, AND PUT IT 
FINALLY TO REST. AS YOU ARE AWARE, WE HAVE BEEN URGING 
IAEA MEMBER COUNTRIES -- THROUGH OUR REPRESENTATIVES IN 
THE IAEA IN VIENNA AND OUR EMBASSIES IN CAPITALS -- TO 
SUPPORT AN ACTION RESTORING TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE TO 
ISRAEL. OUR DELEGATION WILL BE WORKING CLOSELY WITH YOURS 
DURING THE CONFERENCE AS WE COORDINATE OUR EFFORTS ON THIS 
ISSUE. 
AS I MENTIONED DURING OUR MEETING, I HAVE A STRONG 
INTEREST IN CSCE AND OTHER POTENTIAL REGIONAL 
SECURITY/POLITICAL MECHANISMS. THIS WILL BE AN IMPORTANT 
THEME IN AN UPCOMING SPEECH I WILL GIVE AT OXFORD. 
ACCORDINGLY, I LOOK FORWARD TO RECEIVING YOUR PAPER ON 
CSCME. AN EVOLVING MULTILATERAL PROCESS COVERING 
SECURITY, ECONOMIC AND HUMANITARIAN AREAS CAN PLAY A KEY 
ROLE AS PART OF THE CHANGING LANDSCAPE BROUGHT ABOUT BY A 
COMPREHENSIVE PEACE IN THE MIDDLE EAST. 
FINALLY, I HAVE CHECKED WITH AMBASSADOR SIMONS ON OUR 
APPROACH TO COOPERATION WITH ISRAEL AND TURKEY ON 
ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS IN CENTRAL ASIA AND WANT TO ASSURE YOU 
THAT WE REMAIN HIGHLY COMMITTED•TO THE PROCESS, BOTH 
BECAUSE OF OUR CLOSE RELATIONSHIP WITH YOUR GOVERNMENT AND 
THE GOVERNMENT OF TURKEY, AND THE POTENTIAL BENEFITS WE 
SEE ARISING FROM OUR MUTUAL COOPERATION. IT IS IMPORTANT 
TO REMEMBER, HOWEVER, THAT NEId RESOURCES ARE NOT LIKELY TO 
BE FORTHCOMING. OUR IMMEDIATE OPERATIONAL OBJECTIVES 
INCLUDE SIGNING THE DRAFT MOU NEGOTIATED IN ANKARA AND 
FIELDING A JOINT MISSION TO DISCUSS OUR PLANS WITH THE 
UZBEKISTAN AND TURKMENISTAN GOVERNMENTS. WE LOOK FORWARD 
TO INTENSIFIED COOPERATION OVER THE COMING MONTHS. 
SINCERELY, 
/S/ 
STROBE TALBOTT 
HIS EXCELLENCY 
YOSSI BEILIN, 
DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER OF ISRAEL.
