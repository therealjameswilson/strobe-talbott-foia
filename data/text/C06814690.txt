1. fey SUMMARY: DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT MET WITH PRESIDENT 
DEMIREL OCTOBER 27 TO BRIEF HIM ON HIS MEETINGS IN BAKU AND 
YEREVAN. THIS FOLLOWED ONLY BY HOURS THE TRAGIC EVENTS IN 
YEREVAN. AGAINST THIS SOBER BACKDROP, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
REVIEWED IN BROAD TERMS THE EFFORTS OF PRESIDENTS ALIYEV AND 
KOCHARIAN TO MOVE TOWARD PEACE. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD 
DEMIREL PRESIDENT ALIYEV IS MORE PREPARED TO MOVE AHEAD 
QUICKLY AT THIS POINT THAN PRESIDENT KOCHARIAN. IT WAS 
UNCLEAR WHAT IMPACT THE TRAGEDY IN YEREVAN WOULD HAVE, 
HOWEVER. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EMPHASIZED THE CONCERN SHARED 
IN BOTH BAKU AND YEREVAN FOR CONFIDENTIALITY, AND OUR - 
SUPPORT FOR THIS. DEMIREL ALSO AGREED. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY NOTED SOME AREAS WHERE KOCHARIAN THOUGHT TURKISH-
ARMENIAN TIES MIGHT MAKE PROGRESS; DEMIREL RESPONDED, 
AGREEING THAT A MEETING OF FOREIGN MINISTERS SHOULD TAKE 
PLACE AND SENDING A CONDOLENCE LETTER TO YEREVAN AT THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY'S URGING. DEMIREL SAID HE WOULD ENCOURAGE 
ALIYEV TO CONSIDER NORMALIZATION OF RELATIONS WITH ARMENIA 
AS PART OF A PEACE DEAL. DEMIREL ALSO UPDATED THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY ON PROSPECTS FOR CLINCHING A BAKU-CEYHAN OIL 
PIPELINE DEAL IN TIME FOR SIGNING AT THE OSCE SUMMIT IN 
ISTANBUL. END SUMMARY.

BRIEF DEMIREL ON HIS CURRENT TRIP. WITH RESPECT TO HIS JUST-
CONCLUDED CONTACTS IN YEREVAN, HE TOLD DEMIREL THAT, IN HIS 
MEETING WITH PRESIDENT KOCHARIAN, PRIME MINISTER SARKISIAN 
HAD FOR THE FIRST TIME HEARD SOME OF THE DETAILS OF THE 
KOCHARIAN-ALIYEV DISCUSSIONS. THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
EXPRESSED HOPE THE VIOLENCE IN ARMENIA AND DEATH OF 
SARKISIAN AND OTHERS WOULD NOT TAKE WITH THEM THE PROSPECTS 
FOR PEACE. DEMIREL AGREED EVENTS IN YEREVAN WERE "VERY 
UNFORTUNATE."

4.,(Q/ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT ON THIS TRIP HE HAD 
STOPPED IN GENEVA, BAKU, AND ARMENIA. IN GENEVA, HE MET 
WITH KNUT VOLLABECK TO ENLIST THE INVOLVEMENT OF THE OSCE 
CIO IN IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PEACE PROCESS, SINCE HE COULD 
PLAY A VERY IMPORTANT ROLE. IN BAKU, HE HAD A HOPEFUL 
SESSION WITH PRESIDENT ALIYEV. THERE WAS NO QUESTION OF 
ALIYEV'S SERIOUSNESS OR THAT. HE IS READY TO SEIZE THIS 
UNIQUE OPPORTUNITY. IT HAD BEEN POSSIBLE TO HAVE A DETAILED 
CONVERSATION WITH ALIYEV ABOUT WHAT A SETTLEMENT MIGHT LOOK 
LIKE. THERE WAS, HOWEVER, A CAUTIONARY NOTE: ALIYEV WAS 
INSISTING ON A VIRTUAL "DIVORCE" FROM ARMENIA, IN THAT HE 
WOULD NOT EVEN ENTERTAIN A TIMETABLE FOR RESUMING NORMAL 
STATE-TO-STATE RELATIONS. BASED ON HIS CONVERSATION IN 
BAKU, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID HE LEFT BAKU THINKING IT 
MIGHT EVEN BE POSSIBLE TO HAVE AN EVENT IN THE NEXT FEW 
WEEKS. 
5. te)-THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID PRESIDENT KOCHARIAN HAD 
BEEN MORE CAUTIOUS THAN ALIYEV BECAUSE HIS SITUATION IS MORE 
COMPLEX. EVEN BEFORE THE TRAGEDY IN YEREVAN, KOCHARIAN HAD 
CAUTIONED THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGAINST PUSHING TOO HARD. 
BASED ON THIS CONVERSATION, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY TOLD 
DEMIREL HE WAS NOT AS OPTIMISTIC AS HE HAD BEEN ABOUT 
ORGANIZING A MAJOR ARMENIA-AZERBAIJAN EVENT AT THE OSCE 
SUMMIT IN ISTANBUL. NEVERTHELESS, HE SAID HE HAS TOLD 
PRESIDENT CLINTON AND SECRETARY ALBRIGHT TO KEEP OPEN A SPOT 
FOR SUCH AN EVENT, JUST IN CASE. 
6.-(et THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPLAINED THAT PART OF 
'KOCHARIAN'S WARINESS WAS BASED ON HIS CONCERN THAT THE 
RUSSIANS MAY NOT BE FULLY INFORMED ABOUT THE PROGRESS MADE 
IN HIS DISCUSSIONS WITH ALIYEV. KOCHARIAN WOULD SEE 
PRESIDENT YELTSIN AT SOCHI ON NOVEMBER 5 AND TALK TO HIM. 
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY SAID KOCHARIAN WAS CONCERNED LEST 
MOSCOW BELIEVE HE WAS GOING BEHIND ITS BACK -- OR THAT THE 
AMERICANS WERE DOING SO. 
- 7. -(-C-)- BOTH KOCHARIAN AND ALIYEV ARE DEEPLY CONCERNED ABOU 
MAINTAINING CONFIDENTIALITY, THE DEPUTY SECRETARY EXPLAINED' 
IT WILL BE IMPORTANT FOR ALL OF US TO TAKE ACCOUNT OF THESE 
CONCERNS. DEMIREL SAID HE UNDERSTOOD FULLY; TURKEY COULD BE 
DEPENDED ON TO KEEP WHAT IT LEARNS TO ITSELF. LATER IN THE 
CONVERSATION, WHEN DISCUSSING HOW TO CHARACTERIZE THEIR 
MEETING TO THE MEDIA, DEMIREL AND THE DEPUTY SECRETARY 
AGREED THEY WOULD NOTE HAVING DISCUSSED THE BAKU-CEYHAN OIL 
PIPELINE AND THE DEPUTY SECRETARY'S BRIEFING THE PRESIDENT 
ON HIS CURRENT TRIP. 
8. (C) KOCHARIAN NOTED OTHER CONCERNS. HE TOLD THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY ARMENIA WANTED TO MAINTAIN ITS GEOGRAPHIC AND 
POLITICAL CONNECTIONS WITH IRAN. KOCHARIAN SAID HE WANTED 
TO FIND SOME WAY VIA DIALOGUE WITH AZERBAIJAN AND/OR TURKEY 
TO ENSURE HIS IMPORTANT TRADE LINKS WITH IRAN. DEMIREL 
AGREED THERE SHOULD BE WAYS FOR AZERBAIJAN TO ADDRESS THIS 
ARMENIAN CONCERN. "WE'LL TELL ALIYEV ARMENIA SHOULD NOT BE 
CUT OFF FROM IRAN," DEMIREL SAID. 
9. -{e)-KOCHARIAN ALSO PRESSED HARD FOR U.S. FINANCIAL • 
ASSISTANCE IN CONNECTION WITH A SETTLEMENT. THE DEPUTY 
SECRETARY SAID HE EXPLAINED CURRENT BUDGET UNCERTAINTIES IN 
THE U.S. WHICH MADE IT DIFFICULT TO BE SPECIFIC. HOWEVER, 
HE TOLD KOCHARIAN THE U.S. WOULD SEEK ADDITIONAL HELP FOR 
BOTH ARMENIA AND AZERBAIJAN SHOULD THERE BE A SETTLEMENT.

HAPPEN. KOCHARIAN:

12. -(Qj THANKING THE DEPUTY SECRETARY FOR HIS BRIEFING, 
PRESIDENT DEMIREL SAID THE PICTURE HE PRESENTED WAS SIMILAR 
TO THE ONE HE HAS FROM HIS DISCUSSIONS WITH ALIYEV. HE 
AGREED ON THE IMPORTANCE OF ARMENIA'S AVOIDING LINKING 
PROGRESS WITH AZERBAIJAN AND ITS RELATIONS WITH TURKEY. WE, . 
CAN SUPPORT THE TWO LEADERS' EFFORTS, DEMIREL SAID. AND 
ONCE A PEACE HAS BEEN AGREED, WE CAN OPEN BORDERS, OPEN RAIL 
LINES, OPEN AIRPORTS, AND ALLOW ISTANBULTO BECOME ARMENIA'S 
COMMERCIAL HUB -- BUT NOT UNTIL THERE IS PEACE. DEMIREL 
ALSO RECALLED WARNING ARMENIAN PRESIDENT TER-PETROSSIAN 
AGAINST ANCHORING ITS POLICY TOWARD TURKEY IN THE PAST. 
13. -te)-DEMIREL SAID HE HAS EXPRESSED HIS PERSONAL CONCERN 
TO ALIYEV ABOUT A "KOCHARIAN-ALIYEV PEACE DEAL." HE WORRIED 
THAT SUCH AN AGREEMENT WOULD BE DIFFICULT FOR THE TWO 
LEADERS TO SELL IN THEIR CAPITALS. DEMIREL SAID HE HAD 
COUNSELED ALIYEV TO LET THE MINSK GROUP PUT THE LEADERS'. 
PROPOSAL FORWARD SO THE TWO PARLIAMENTS COULD WEIGH IT AS A 
"MINSK GROUP DEAL." 
14.-(e)-CONCERNING ALIYEV, DEMIREL SAID TURKEY SHARES THE 
AMERICAN VIEW THAT HE IS THE ONLY AZERI WHO CAN SELL A DEAL. 
HE HAS THE POLITICAL SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE TO DO SO. HE IS 
A SURVIVOR. BUT, DEMIREL CONTINUED, TIME IS OF THE ESSENCE. 
"I WORRY ABOUT HIS HEALTH." IF ALIYEV IS OLIT OF THE 
PICTURE, I WORRY ABOUT AZERBAIJAN'S ABILITY TO MAKE PEACE, 
HE SAID. 
15. -fe)-THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AGREED THE MINSK GROUP HAD AN 
IMPORTANT ROLE TO PLAY. AT THIS POINT, THE U.S. KNOWS MORE 
THAN ITS PARTNERS ABOUT THE DEAL. HE TOLD DEMIREL HE WOULD 
BRIEF HIS RUSSIAN AND FRENCH PARTNERS, AND THEN WE WILL 
APPLY OURSELVES TO DEVELOPING A MINSK GROUP APPROACH 
CONSISTENT WITH THE AZERI AND ARMENIAN LEADERS' TALKS.

TODAY." (NOTE: AND HE DID. SEE REFTEL.)
