2. SUMMARY. IN A SEPTEMBER 23 MEETING DEPUTY SECRETARY 
TALBOTT AND MALTA PRIME MINISTER EDDIE FENECH ADAMI 
bISCUSSED IMiROVED U.S.-MALTESE COOPERATION, AIR MALTA'S 
PLANNED EXPANSION, AND MALTA.S INTEREST IN EU MEMBERSHIP 
AND JOINING THE PARTNERSHIP FOR PEACE. FENECH ADAMI 
STRESSED MALTESE CONCERNS ABOUT MEDITERRANEAN SECURITY AND 
THE SPREAD OF ISLAMIC FUNDAMENTALISM IN THE MAGHREB. THE 
DEPUTY SECRETARY WELCOMED THE RENEWAL IN BILATERAL 
RELATIONS AND STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF CONTINUED 
COOPERATION IN LIBYAN SANCTIONS ENFORCEMENT AND 
COUNTERTERRORISM ISSUES. END SUMMARY.

EUROPEAN INTEGRATION.
