2. f9+ SUMMARY: DURING A ONE-HOUR MEETING ON JANUARY 27.
THE DEPUTY SECRETARY AND UK DEFENSE SECRETARY GEOFF HOON
DISCUSSED RUSSIA, NMD AND THE BALKANS. ON RUSSIA, THE
DEPUTY SECRETARY NOTED THAT HIS RECENT DISCUSSIONS WITH
SENIOR RUSSIAN OFFICIALS SUGGEST MOSCOW IS LOOKING FOR
WAYS TO "DECLARE VICTORY" IN CHECHNYA IN THE NEXT FEW
MONTHS AND THEN RESUME BROAD COOPERATION WITH THE WEST.
HE JUDGED AS OVERLY OPTIMISTIC DFM MAMEDOV'S SUGGESTION
THAT RUSSIA COULD SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETE THIS STRATEGY IN
TIME FOR THE JULY G-8 SUMMIT IN OKINAWA. THOUGH THE
ULTIMATE OUTCOME REMAINS UNCERTAIN, THE DEPUTY
SECRETARY'MD
DISCUSSIONS WITH THE RUSSIANS HAVE QUALITATIVELY
IMPROVED, WITH MOSCOW PERHAPS PREPARED TO CONCEDE THAT THE
STRATEGIC ENVIRONMENT HAS CHANGED. RUSSIAN CONCERNS OVER
DECLASSIFIED
NMD'S IMPACT ON THEIR OWN DETERRENT REMAINS A HUGE
STUMBLING BLOCK, BUT THEY NOW HAVE THEIR "TECHNICAL MINDS"
ENGAGED WITH OURS TO TRY TO ADDRESS SPECIFIC ISSUES.
3. ~ HOON SAID THE UK GOVERNMENT WANTS TO BE HELPFUL ON
NMD IF A DECISION ON PHASE 1 OR PHASE 2 DEPLOYMENT IS
MADE, BUT HE ALSO NOTED THREE POTENTIAL PROBLEM AREAS
REQUIRING ATTENTION: UK AND EUROPEAN PUBLIC OPINION,
MA'INTAINING THE VIABILITY OF THE UK'S INDEPENDENT
DETERRENT'ND THE BUILDING PERMIT PROCESS FOR
FYLINGDALES. ON KOSOVO, NEITHER THE U.S. NOR UK SEES A
SOLUTION THAT WOULD ALLOW A WITHDRAWAL OF ALL ALLIED
FORCES EVEN IN THE MEDIUM-TERM. WITH REGARD TO
MONTENEGRO, HOON PREDICTED THAT, HAVING LEARNED THE LESSON
OF KOSOVO, MILOSEVIC WILL USE HIS MANY RESOURCES IN
MONTENEGRO TO TRY To PROVOKE DJUKANOVIC INTO MISSTEPS BUT
IN SUCH A MANNER AS TO MINIMIZE THE CHANCE OF AN
INTERNATIONAL REACTION. END SUMMARY.

NMD
8. ~THE NUMBER ONE AGENDA ITEM FOR THE RUSSIANS WAS

MINDS" NOW FINALLY ENGAGED. IT REMAINS AN OPEN QUESTION
WHETHER THE RUSSIANS WILL IN THE END AGREE TO AMEND THE
ABM TREATY. ALL THE SAME, THE RUSSIANS, HE SAID( NOW
APPEAR ON TRACK TO SOFTEN THEIR GENERAL STANCE AND

CATEGOPICALLY MEANS THE END OF DETEREENCE, THEY STILL NEED
A LOT MORE CONVINCING, AND THE MILITARY REMAINS STAUNCHLY
OPPOSED: GENERAL ZLENKO OF THE MOD'S INTERNATIONAL
COOPERATION DEPARTMENT TOLD THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THAT NMD
IS BY CONSEQUENCE AND INTENT ANTI-RUSSIAN. THE NEXT
TALBOTT/MAMEDOV MEETING IS SCHEDULED FOR MARCH, WITH JOHN
HOLUM MEETING WITH RUSSIAN COUNTERPARTS IN GENEVA IN
FEBRUARY.

THE CASE IF THE U.S. WERE SEEN AS BEHAVING AGGRESSIVELY

16. ~ THE DEPUTY SECRETARY THANKED HOON FOR BRITISH
FINANCIAL AND OTHER CONTRIBUTIONS IN KOSOVO. HOON IN TURN

UNDERSTANDING FOR THE UK'S NEED TO CUT BACK ITS DEPLOYMEN

MINISTER IS EAGER TO SEE FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE TAKE HOLD, BUT

COULD AT LEAST BE GREATLY REDUCED. HE POINTED UP THE
PRESSING NEED FOR GREATER CONTRIBUTIONS TO BUILDING UP THE
POLICE AND THE UNMIK BUDGET IN KOSOVO, AT THE SAME TIME

KOSOVO BUDGET.

ENGAGEMENT, BILLED AS A KEY TEST OF EUROPEAN LEADERSHIP, A
SUCCESS.
